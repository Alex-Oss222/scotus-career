At a Court of General Sessions, convened on Novem- 
ber 11, 1996, the Grand Jurors of Lexington County pre- 
sent upon their oath: 


COUNT ONE - MURDER 


That WILLIAM ARTHUR KELLY, did in Lexington 
County on or about January 5, 1996, willfully, feloniously 
and with malice aforethought kill one SHIRLEY S. 
SHEALY by means of stabbing, cutting and wounding the 
victim numerous times with a knife, and the said SHIR- 
LEY S. SHEALY died in Lexington County on or about 
January 5, 1996, as a result of exsanguination due to 
incised wounds of the neck and multiple stab wounds to 
the back and chest. 


COUNT TWO - KIDNAPPING 


That WILLIAM ARTHUR KELLY did in Lexington 
County on or about January 5, 1996, unlawfully and 
feloniously seize, confine, inveigle, decoy, kidnap, abduct 
or carry away SHIRLEY S. SHEALY, in that WILLIAM 
ARTHUR KELLY did unlawfully deprive SHIRLEY S. 


SHEALY of her freedom without authority of law and in 
violation of § 16-3-910 of the South Carolina Code of 
Laws (1976), as amended. 


Against the peace and dignity of the State, and con- 
trary to the statute in such case made and provided. 


/s/ Donald Myers 
SOLICITOR 


COUNT THREE - ARMED ROBBERY 


That WILLIAM ARTHUR KELLY did in Lexington 
County on or about January 5, 1996, while armed with a 
deadly weapon, to wit: a knife, unlawfully and feloni- 
ously take from the person or in the presence of SHIRLEY 
S. SHEALY by means of force or intimidation goods, 
monies or other personal items of the said SHIRLEY S. 
SHEALY and/or Kentucky Fried Chicken at 431 West 
Columbia Avenue in Batesburg-Leesville, such goods, 
monies or other personal items being described: Lawful 
U.S. coins and/or currency, or other personal property 
items, all in violation of § 16-11-330 of the South Carolina 
Code of Laws (1976), as amended. 


COUNT FOUR - POSSESSION OR DISPLAY OF A 
KNIFE DURING THE COMMISSION 
OF A VIOLENT CRIME 


That WILLIAM ARTHUR KELLY did in Lexington 
County on or about January 5, 1996, willfully and 
unlawfully have in his possession, or did visibly display, 
a knife during the commission of a violent crime or 
crimes, to wit: Murder and/or Armed Robbery, as 


defined in § 16-1-60, all in violation of § 16-23-490 of the 
South Carolina Code of Laws (1976), as amended. 


Against the peace and dignity of the State, and con- 
trary to the statute in such case made and provided. 


/s/ Donald V. Myers
