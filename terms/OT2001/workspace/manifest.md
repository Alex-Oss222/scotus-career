## Chronology and related-matter controls

All uncoordinated matters on the same date use the common entering-law baseline immediately before that date, including across chunk boundaries. Processing or assignment order does not make another same-day decision entering law. No coordinated same-day sequence is established by the supplied neutral scheduling fields. Conditional references to Leventhal in Drayton and to Harris in Ring do not establish priority. Any later established coordination requires an express, validated sequence.

Companion and related-matter references identify scheduling relationships only. A later-calendar matter is unavailable as entering law until its own event date; proposed positions and historical dispositions supply no current-term holding. Earlier current-term law enters only through validated, effective Records. The same limits apply to dependencies elsewhere in the neutral packets.
