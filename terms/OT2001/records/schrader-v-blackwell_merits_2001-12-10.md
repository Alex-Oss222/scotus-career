## Event, record and chronology

Schrader v. Blackwell. Merits decision, December 10, 2001. Review of the Sixth Circuit judgment reported at 241 F.3d 783, reversing relief for the requested Libertarian ballot designation. Schrader qualified for the 1998 congressional ballot through independent nomination; the party had actually nominated him but lacked state recognition. A preliminary injunction permitted the label for that election. The party later regained recognition. The question is whether the First and Fourteenth Amendments permit Ohio to condition the official party designation on recognized-party status. The continuing party plaintiff, recurring recognition requirement and short election cycle keep the dispute reviewable; the earlier filing-deadline objection is outside review.
