- 5 U.S.C. §§7503(a), 7513(a) authorize covered adverse actions for cause promoting the efficiency of the service.
- Sections 7503(b) and 7513(b) provide advance notice, an opportunity to respond orally and in writing, representation, and written reasons.
- Sections 4303(e), 7513(d), and 7701 provide Board review for the covered personnel actions.
- Section 7701(c) separates the agency's evidentiary burden from employee defenses based on harmful procedural error, prohibited personnel practices, and other legal defects.
- Section 7703(c) permits setting aside a Board decision for arbitrary or capricious action, abuse of discretion, legal error, failure to follow required procedures, or lack of substantial evidence. This is the statutory court-to-Board review framework; it is distinct from the Board's own proof and collateral-review rules.
