## 27. Owasso Independent School District No. I-011 v. Falvo

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for eight Justices. Scalia concurred in the judgment, declining the broader suggestion that protected records must be kept in a central repository.
* **Historical Holding:** The peer-graded papers were not education records maintained by the institution or a person acting for it. The Court assumed without deciding the private-action question and did not resolve teacher-gradebook coverage.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Owasso Independent School District No. I-011 v. Falvo](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/534/426/).
