## Independent statutory check

The previously saved official 2000 Code chapter reproduces §1681p's unamended 1970 limitation text and identifies §1681n's later amendment history. The historical note confirms that before the 1996 amendment the actual-damages paragraph covered actual damages sustained as a result of the failure; the alternative $100–$1,000 statutory award was added later. The original introductory provision applied to a consumer reporting agency or user willfully failing to comply with a requirement concerning a consumer. Punitive damages the court may allow and successful-action costs/fees were separately listed components. No post-1994 statutory-damages alternative is applied to these disclosures.

This confirms the neutral addendum's distinction between actual damages and the complete willful-noncompliance remedy. Whether that structure permits a sufficiently complete willful claim before later actual losses is a legal question for modeling, not a fact assumed by this receipt. No punitive award, fault finding or medical/emotional injury date is established.
