## 127. Balderas v. Texas

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court summarily affirmed on June 17, 2002, No. 01-1196, reported at 536 U.S. 919. No individual vote allocation is recorded in the published disposition.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Summary order without an explanatory merits opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** The order affirmed the congressional judgment. It supplies no articulated rule resolving every premise of the lower court's analysis and no independent explanation of electoral capacity.
* **Historical Authority:** Balderas v. Texas, 536 U.S. 919 (2002). The historical disposition does not displace the different governing Valdespino rule stated in Section I.
* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court No. 01-1196 docket, June 17 and July 17, 2002 entries; published order at 536 U.S. 919; underlying congressional opinion of November 14, 2001.
