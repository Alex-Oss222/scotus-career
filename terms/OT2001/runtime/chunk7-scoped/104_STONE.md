# OT_2001CHUNK7

## 104. Atkins v. Virginia

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Law through June 28, 2001; position for 2002-06-20, subject to any actually effective intervening decision.
* **Conditions:** Any intervening controlling decision must be applied or expressly addressed before the vote.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the judgment sustaining death eligibility and remand under a categorical prohibition on executing persons with intellectual disability.
* **Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Require a reliable, individualized determination of the claimed disability under procedures capable of applying the constitutional rule. If established, impose a lawful noncapital disposition. Preserve the convictions and do not declare the contested diagnosis proved or prescribe an entire state sentencing code.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Death is disproportionate for the established clinical class

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The categorical claim is preserved. The constitutional rule can be decided while the fact-dependent membership determination remains for remand.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether intellectual disability bars execution rather than merely supplying discretionary mitigation.
* **Stone’s Legal Position:** Adopt a categorical Eighth Amendment restriction and expressly overrule Penry v. Lynaugh only insofar as it permits execution of persons with established intellectual disability. Penry v. Johnson’s requirement of effective mitigation remains applicable where capital punishment is otherwise lawful; it does not already supply this exemption.
* **Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A person whose intellectual disability is established may not be executed. The relevant status requires significantly impaired intellectual functioning together with adaptive limitations and developmental onset; a bare score, diagnosis label, or courtroom impression is insufficient by itself.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The restriction concerns death eligibility. It does not immunize criminal conduct, decide incompetence or insanity, prohibit lawful imprisonment, or automatically classify other impairments.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The developed legal and clinical evidence supports a durable judgment that the class’s diminished capacities reduce the culpability needed for the most severe punishment and impair the reliability of the process selecting it. Individualized mitigation does not fully answer those problems: the same limitations may distort apparent remorse, assistance to counsel, and the evaluation of testimony. State legislative movement provides objective support, but the conclusion is a proportionality judgment with stated reasons, not a vote count or a free-standing medical decree. Reliance on a power to execute cannot outweigh this constitutional limit; the consequence for existing judgments and collateral review must still follow their distinct rules. The conflicting experts here require a reliable determination rather than appellate factfinding. The decisive method is Eighth Amendment proportionality informed by objective legal developments: eighteen death-penalty States had enacted exemptions, the direction of change since Penry was consistent, and execution of the class was uncommon. Those facts support, but do not mechanically determine, the judgment that diminished culpability and impaired adjudicative reliability make death excessive for this class. Clinical evidence explains the class’s relevant limitations; it does not independently dictate constitutional law. A categorical rule is warranted because individualized mitigation leaves the same class-wide deficits capable of being treated as aggravation. No later diagnostic cutoff or later judicial rule is imported.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The precise allocation of proof, detailed diagnostic procedure, retroactivity in a separate collateral case, and other disability categories are not presented for final resolution.
