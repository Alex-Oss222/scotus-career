## 109. Utah v. Evans

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg; O’Connor joined the introductory and standing portions and otherwise dissented. Thomas, joined by Kennedy, agreed on jurisdiction and statutory permission but dissented on constitutional validity. Scalia dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Utah had standing; hot-deck imputation violated neither the Census Act nor the enumeration requirement.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only. The historical account of Department of Commerce v. United States House of Representatives is incompatible with that case’s simulated disposition and is not imported.
