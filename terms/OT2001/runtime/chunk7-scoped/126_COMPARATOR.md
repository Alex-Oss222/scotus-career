## 126. Robertson v. Bartels

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court summarily affirmed January 22, 2002, No. 01-721, 534 U.S. 1110. No individual vote allocation is recorded for this matter.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Summary order; no reasoned opinion or recorded separate writing.
* **Historical Holding:** The judgment was affirmed. The order does not expressly adopt every rationale below. The district court had relied on virtual representation and absence of racial predominance; those grounds must not be converted into an explanatory Supreme Court opinion.
* **Historical Authority:** The order concerns the judgment and issues necessarily resolved. It does not replace Section I's governing territorial-injury rule.
* **Historical Sources:** Robertson v. Bartels, 534 U.S. 1110 (2002), No. 01-721, order of January 22, 2002; Robertson v. Bartels, 148 F. Supp. 2d 443 (D.N.J. 2001).
