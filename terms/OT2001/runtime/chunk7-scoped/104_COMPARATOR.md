# OT_2001CHUNK7

## 104. Atkins v. Virginia

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for O’Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Rehnquist and Scalia each dissented, joined by the other and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Execution of intellectually disabled offenders violates the Eighth Amendment; the States were left to develop appropriate implementation procedures.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only. Later clinical-procedure and retroactivity decisions are outside the event-date record.
* **Historical Sources:** [Atkins v. Virginia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/536/304/).
