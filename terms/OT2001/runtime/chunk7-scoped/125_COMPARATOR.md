# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The three-judge District of New Jersey court entered judgment for defendants after trial and denied injunctive relief. No final Supreme Court disposition or individual Supreme Court vote has been verified for this appeal.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** A per curiam district-court opinion explains the trial judgment. The earlier Third Circuit opinion required a three-judge court; it did not decide that the map violated federal law.
* **Historical Holding:** The district court principally found the necessary opposing-bloc proof absent under § 2 and rejected the constitutional claims. Its racial-predominance reasoning is not identical to the controlling territorial-injury doctrine used in Section II. Its alternative Fifteenth Amendment ground was failure to establish discriminatory purpose.
* **Historical Authority:** The lower-court judgment and reported appeal are historical anchors, not an invented Supreme Court merits holding.
* **Historical Sources:** Page v. Bartels, 144 F. Supp. 2d 346 (D.N.J. 2001); Page v. Bartels, 248 F.3d 175 (3d Cir. 2001); Robertson v. Bartels, 148 F. Supp. 2d 443, 449 n.3 (D.N.J. 2001).
