## 61. Thompson v. Western States Medical Center

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling law through June 28, 2001. Apply any subsequently completed decision effective before this matter is decided.
* **Conditions:** The stated jurisdictional, preservation and record limits apply. No alternative voting instruction is supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the constitutional judgment without reopening nonseverability.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Leave the challenged speech conditions unenforceable and the circuit’s unreviewed treatment of the exemption intact. Do not enact a substitute compounding regime, order approval of a drug, or authorize false promotion.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Commercial speech and the compounding exemption

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The pharmacies challenge restrictions on truthful promotion within a statutory exemption. Apply the ordinary commercial-speech test, resolving the constitutional question actually reviewed.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether the Government has adequately justified drug-specific silence as the means of distinguishing permissible compounding from manufacturing that must undergo approval.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the existing reasonable-fit standard. Do not adopt a new categorical ban on commercial-speech regulation or demand proof of an absolutely least restrictive alternative.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The challenged restrictions fail the required fit between the asserted interests and the burden on truthful commercial information. Drug-specific promotion is an insufficiently justified proxy for impermissible manufacturing where it also suppresses useful information about individualized lawful preparations, and the Government has not adequately explained why direct controls on the conduct would not serve the asserted distinction with materially less speech burden.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The holding covers these conditions on truthful, nonmisleading promotion. It leaves room for restrictions on false claims, unlawful products, actual manufacturing conduct, and appropriately supported narrower commercial-speech measures.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Central Hudson and Fox require material advancement and reasonable fit; 44 Liquormart and Lorillard preserve the separate inquiry into the burden actually imposed on lawful communication. Stone applies that intermediate commercial-speech framework, with fit decisive. Drug safety and preservation of individualized compounding are substantial interests, but truthful information about a particular preparation does not reliably identify manufacturing at a scale requiring full approval. Direct limits on production, wholesale distribution or prescription practice bear on fit because they target the conduct creating the concern. The Government has not adequately explained why those alternatives leave a need for this broad speech condition. That is not a demand for the least imaginable restriction or a controlled study. The existing record supports invalidation now, while the unpresented severability issue and design of a replacement regime remain outside review.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Severability was not brought within review, and the choice of any replacement regulatory scheme belongs outside this case.

## 62. US Airways, Inc. v. Barnett

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling law through June 28, 2001. Apply any subsequently completed decision effective before this matter is decided.
* **Conditions:** The stated jurisdictional, preservation and record limits apply. No alternative voting instruction is supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm reversal of summary judgment and remand for the individualized inquiry stated below.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Allow Barnett to establish a reasonable request and require US Airways to prove any particular undue hardship under the statute. Consider actual employee reliance and operational effects. Award no job, damages, or fees at this stage.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Accommodation and an employer-created seniority system

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The employer seeks a rule ending the claim from the policy conflict alone. That issue can be decided without finally determining every eligibility or remedial predicate.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether a unilateral seniority system creates an automatic or presumptive exception to the ADA’s accommodation obligation.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Decide the open accommodation question under the enacted framework. Reject both a categorical defense and a newly created general presumption for this noncontractual employer policy.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Conflict with an employer-created seniority policy does not by itself make a requested accommodation unreasonable or create a presumption that ends the employee’s claim. The employee must identify a facially reasonable accommodation consistent with qualification and the actual position; the employer may establish undue hardship through concrete operational burdens and legitimate employee reliance. The policy’s duration, consistency, retained exceptions, and practical effects matter, but its label is not a statutory exemption.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule covers the unilateral policy presented and permits reasonable accommodation to require an exception to a neutral practice. It does not require eliminating essential functions, creating a job, displacing an incumbent, or disregarding an independently binding federal labor obligation.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The enacted allocation between reasonable accommodation and demonstrated undue hardship is decisive. Garrett confirms that reassignment is a statutory accommodation example and preserves the employer's actual significant-difficulty-or-expense defense; it does not establish an extra defense for every neutral employment policy. Stone would resolve the open seniority question by rejecting a new presumption for this unilateral system. The employee must first identify a facially reasonable accommodation, and the employer must then substantiate the particular hardship it asserts. A consistent seniority practice can generate concrete employee reliance even when the employer reserves power to change it; that reliance is evidence, not a conclusive exemption. The combination is textual and functional, with the statutory allocation controlling how operational evidence counts. Barnett's ability to perform the vacant mailroom job permits further adjudication, not automatic assignment or a damages award.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A binding collective-bargaining agreement, actual displacement of an incumbent, and the final amount of any damages are outside this record or require further findings.

## 63. City of Los Angeles v. Alameda Books, Inc.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling law through June 28, 2001. Apply any subsequently completed decision effective before this matter is decided.
* **Conditions:** The stated jurisdictional, preservation and record limits apply. No alternative voting instruction is supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the appellate judgment and remand for the evidentiary and classification inquiries under Renton and City of Erie.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Permit the City to defend a supported inference from the study while requiring resolution of the preserved expressive-targeting ground and the applicable advancement, fit, and expression-access questions. Do not enter final judgment validating the ordinance. Any existing injunction is subject to prompt lawful reconsideration on remand, not automatic enforcement permission from this merits vacatur alone.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Classification and evidence for the zoning restriction

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The circuit’s evidence-based affirmance can be reviewed without assuming the unresolved neutrality question in the City’s favor. Both must remain distinct on remand.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether the study is categorically incapable of supporting the concentration inference and what legal conditions govern further review of the ordinance.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply City of Erie’s controlling anti-targeting proposition and the existing requirement of supported predictive judgment. Clarify the evidentiary application without adopting the historical opinion’s new burden-shifting language as prior law.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The City may reasonably rely on the concentration study to support the asserted connection between concentrated adult operations and neighborhood crime; the absence of a separate within-building comparison does not justify summary judgment for the challengers on that evidentiary ground. This does not establish neutrality or ultimate validity. The preserved targeting and advancement-and-fit grounds must be adjudicated under City of Erie and the scrutiny warranted by the ordinance’s actual operation and justification.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule permits evidentiary inference, not automatic deference to any asserted secondary effect. It preserves challenges based on weak causal support, suppressed access, actual communicative targeting, and the mismatch between the demonstrated harm and chosen restriction.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Renton permits reasonable evidentiary inference; City of Erie separately prevents incidental-burden treatment when justification and operation principally target expression. Stone reconciles those rules rather than making a study an automatic constitutional defense. A concentration study need not test every internal building configuration to support the inference that several adult operations together can produce the identified harm. On the stated evidentiary objection, that inference is sufficient to defeat the businesses' demand for summary judgment. It is not enough merely to name crime: the City retains the ultimate burden under the applicable scrutiny, and unsupported inferences may be rebutted by ordinary evidence and reasoning. The evidentiary question is decided now; the preserved, unreached targeting and fit grounds remain for adjudication. This bounded answer avoids both a mandatory new study and a new presumption of constitutional validity.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Final factual conclusions about the rule’s operation, alternative expressive locations, and the properly supported causal inference require further adjudication. No general adult-business exemption or categorical ban is proposed.

## 64. Henderson v. Norris +

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling law through June 28, 2001. Apply any subsequently completed decision effective before this matter is decided.
* **Conditions:** The stated jurisdictional, preservation and record limits apply.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the Eighth Circuit judgment granting conditional sentencing relief.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Preserve the conviction. Require Arkansas to conduct lawful resentencing within the conditional-writ period set below, subject to ordinary mandate administration. Do not impose a judicially invented term of years or require immediate unconditional release while a lawful resentencing remains available.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Sentence-specific proportionality

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the preserved punishment claim under the pre-AEDPA framework. Lindh excludes amended §2254(d), and Teague does not convert independent legal review into acceptance of every reasonable state interpretation. Separately apply Teague’s finality-era dictated-rule requirement; for the reasons below, this sentence-specific result applies existing law and does not depend on a new rule or a retroactivity exception. No separate guilt defect is before the Court.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether this offender’s permanent imprisonment bears a constitutionally defensible relation to this proved offense.
* **Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply the surviving gross-disproportionality principle and propose its concrete application to this sentence; this application is the holding Stone proposes.
* **Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A life sentence carrying no ordinary parole eligibility is grossly disproportionate for this first conviction of delivering 0.238 grams of cocaine base on the record established here. Legislative authority to punish distribution severely does not permit the aggregate harms of an industry to replace the individual’s offense and culpability.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The holding governs imprisonment for this offense and materially equivalent first-offender conduct. It does not classify every life sentence for a drug offense as invalid or equate sale with possession.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Solem's comparison of offense gravity and penalty, informed by Harmelin's narrow proportionality principle, supplies the governing standard. Stone applies that standard rather than creating a categorical immunity from imprisonment for drug sales. Permanent incarceration for this first conviction involving 0.238 grams produces an inference of gross disproportionality; the available intra- and interjurisdictional comparisons confirm the exceptional mismatch. Legislative authority over punishment deserves real weight, but abstract descriptions of the social harm of drugs cannot erase the actual offense and offender. The combination uses contextual proportionality with comparative evidence; the demonstrated mismatch, not any single numerical ratio, is decisive.

  Teague requires more than identifying an old general principle. Finality, not the offense date or the date of this habeas decision, fixes the relevant law. Rummel, Solem, and Harmelin all preceded Henderson’s 1995 direct-review affirmance; this conclusion depends on no subsequent expansion. Solem already distinguished discretionary commutation from ordinary parole and required attention to the actual offense and culpability. Rummel’s repeat-conviction and parole predicates are absent. Harmelin preserved a narrow proportionality inquiry and sustained punishment for a quantity capable of extensive distribution; its reasoning does not equate every retail sale with that magnitude of harm. Applying those settled distinctions, together with the required comparisons, Stone finds the exceptional mismatch dictated by existing law. Evidence of two additional rocks does not establish Harmelin’s large-scale predicate, even if considered; the appellate record also supplies no sentencing instruction permitting punishment for the separate witness-interference evidence. Neither the State’s disagreement nor the absence of a prior case involving exactly 0.238 grams makes this application new. The holding changes no threshold, creates no drug-weight cutoff, and depends on no new categorical exemption or retroactivity exception. This confines relief to the existing constitutional violation and leaves the lawful replacement sentence to Arkansas.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The exact permissible replacement term requires sentencing facts and a lawful state sentencing proceeding that this record does not supply.
