## 61. Thompson v. Western States Medical Center — April 29, 2002

### Components, governing terms and available paths

The reviewed component is the First Amendment validity of the prescription-solicitation and particular-drug/class/type-promotion conditions of section 503A. The Ninth Circuit's separate nonseverability determination is not independently before the Court. Each Associate accepts merits reach on that bounded question and distinguishes truthful promotion of lawful activity from deceptive promotion or an unlawful underlying transaction. Each rejects the proposition that the Government wins simply because it calls the exemption voluntary. Lanphere's speech-selected-condition rule supplies that limited point; its particular records-access application is not this case.

The controlling rule is Central Hudson and Fox, as applied in Edenfield, Rubin, Greater New Orleans, 44 Liquormart and Lorillard: actual harm, material advancement and reasonable proportional fit. There is no universal least-restrictive-means rule or required empirical-study design. The analysis must preserve the statute's actual alternatives. An identified patient's prescription can satisfy subsection (a) through unsolicited receipt or the prescribing practitioner's approved necessity notation. Limited advance compounding rests on the specified prescription history and established relationships. Subsection (c) separately prohibits promotion of a particular drug, class or type while allowing promotion of the service. Neither clause is accurately described as an exceptionless prohibition on all solicited prescriptions or all compounding advertisements.

The substantive controls are real but distinct: permitted substances, source registration and certificates; applicable monographs; withdrawn unsafe or ineffective products; regular or inordinate commercial copying, with the significant patient-specific difference exclusion; demonstrable compounding difficulties; and interstate distribution through the State memorandum route or the five-percent limit measured against total prescription orders. There is no enacted twenty-percent memorandum ceiling. The three specified federal exemptions, PET and radiopharmaceutical exclusions, and manufacturer-directed-mixing exclusion retain their actual terms. No Justice's conclusion authorizes exemption from every drug law or finds compounded drugs generally safe.

**Path A — affirm the constitutional judgment for inadequate fit.** Assume the Government's combined interests in approval integrity, health and individualized access are substantial and that promotion can increase production. The complete particular-drug/class/type speech condition and solicitation limitation nevertheless burden useful professional and patient information without adequate justification for using speech as the boundary. Direct controls on quantities, distribution, patient prescriptions and commercial copying address production itself. The strongest objection is that a direct production cap can exclude legitimate specialized compounding and existing controls do not prevent growth in unapproved products that are not commercial copies. The answer must therefore concern this scheme's disproportionate informational burden and available conduct distinctions, not a judicially selected numerical cap or a finding that every conceivable alternative is equally effective.

**Path B — reverse the constitutional predicate.** The combined statutory purpose differs from suppressing information merely to manipulate an otherwise unregulated lawful purchase. Congress permits patient-specific preparation while requiring ordinary commercial manufacture to obtain approval. Product promotion can be both an incentive and a workable indicator of manufacture organized around demand for particular preparations; service advertising and the necessity-notation alternative retain meaningful individualized access. The strongest objection is that useful truthful communications to physicians can trigger the same restriction without showing industrial production, while general service promotion can also expand demand. This path must accept that burden and justify the rough boundary under reasonable-fit review, rather than deny the speech interest or treat the classification as proof of its own constitutionality.

## 62. US Airways, Inc. v. Barnett — April 29, 2002

### Components and statutory work

All eight Associates reach only the reviewed seniority/accommodation issue. The en banc judgment also contains cargo-accommodation and interactive-process matters, while retaliation summary judgment was affirmed. None is silently resolved by this seniority disposition. The disputed coverage-preservation account supplies no present finding that Barnett is or is not disabled. Injury in 1990 does not make a claim concerning the post-effective-date request and denial retroactive.

Sections 12111(8)–(10) and 12112(b)(5)(A) do separate work: qualification for essential functions; a possible reasonable accommodation, including reassignment to a vacant position and policy adjustment; and the employer's demonstrated significant-difficulty-or-expense defense under the statutory operational and resource factors. An effective preference is not automatically reasonable. Conversely, identical application of a policy does not itself answer a duty to accommodate. No Associate imports Hardison's more-than-de-minimis threshold into the ADA or treats this employer-created policy as an authenticated collective-bargaining obligation.

**Path A — ordinary individual inquiry and affirmance of further proceedings.** Barnett must show a suitable and reasonable accommodation, not merely improvement in his position. Feasibility, qualification, vacancy and the character of the requested change perform independent work. The employer then must demonstrate actual significant difficulty or expense; seniority reliance and consequences for other workers are relevant but no special presumption reallocates the inquiry. The strongest objection is that displacing predictable seniority ordering gives a substantial preferential benefit and makes the system less reliable even where an individual monetary cost cannot be shown. The answer is that real noncash operational burdens remain provable under the statutory factors; the existence of a policy cannot replace that statutory showing.

**Path B — ordinary unreasonableness with a case-specific opportunity to show otherwise.** An established seniority system normally creates expectations about positions and advancement for many employees. Displacing that ordering is ordinarily an unreasonable requested accommodation even if effective for this employee. The claimant can show circumstances under which that objection lacks its usual force, such as relevant actual departure practices or applicable exceptions undermining the claimed expectation. Those are illustrations, not mandatory elements. The employer's undue-hardship defense retains separate work when the accommodation is shown reasonable. The strongest objection is that a judicial seniority presumption may duplicate hardship while weakening Congress's express allocation. This path locates the ordinary obstacle in the character of the requested preference, not in a presumed showing of significant difficulty or expense.

**Path C — bona fide seniority conflict outside required reassignment.** Reassignment preserves an opportunity for which the employee is eligible; it does not require superior priority to a position allocated to more-senior workers under an established system. The strongest objection is the express possibility of policy modification, the absence of an ADA seniority exemption and the lack of an incumbent removed from this mailroom position. A categorical position must answer those features through reasonable accommodation's independent limit and the nature of the allocation system, not through a nonexistent statutory exemption or the proposition that every neutral rule is immune.

## 63. City of Los Angeles v. Alameda Books — May 13, 2002

### Components and adversarial paths

Each Associate accepts the bounded evidentiary question, recognizes the preserved classification defense of the existing decree, and rejects final validation merely because the appellate evidentiary rule is set aside. El Paso Natural Gas permits the businesses to defend their existing judgment on the preserved ground without a cross-appeal. It does not authorize a broader decree.

Renton permits reasonably relevant evidence and genuine land-use regulation directed to noncommunicative effects. Erie controls the precise boundary against incidental-burden treatment when actual justification and operation principally target communication. Its entire strict-scrutiny explanation and its alternative intermediate-scrutiny insufficiency explanation are not holdings of five Justices. Turner permits supported prediction with independent judicial review; Playboy preserves the Government's ultimate burden where content-selected suppression is established. Thomas v. Chicago Park District's neutral event-coordination rule does not classify this law.

**Path A — nonvalidating vacatur and remand.** A study associating concentrations of adult businesses with crime can support a reasonable inference that concentrating separately defined uses in one building may create similar noncommunicative harms; no rule requires a study isolating every architecture. The study's treatment of bookstore/arcade combinations as one unit prevents claiming a direct measured comparison, but does not alone negate every inference from concentration. The strongest objection is that splitting a small, physically undivided operation may suppress a protected use without reducing actual patron concentration. This remains material on remand: the City must explain its inferential bridge and meet contrary evidence under the proper classification and scrutiny. The rule does not shift the ultimate burden or establish that every adult use has identical effects.

**Path B — affirm this summary judgment for insufficient support.** The actual study measured a different concentration unit and does not establish that adding a use within one small operation adds the harmful concentration that justified dispersing distinct establishments. The answer is not a new mandatory-study rule: the asserted inference must still address the change actually compelled, and this record's unit mismatch and undivided operations leave that material bridge unsupported. Classification need not be finally resolved if the evidence fails even assuming the less demanding applicable land-use scrutiny.

The circuit expressly recognized that Renton permits outside studies and reasonable inference. It nevertheless held this particular inference unreasonable, including the proposed increase in frequency or regularity of activity from adding an arcade. Path A disagrees with that actual inferential ruling; it does not attribute to the circuit a literal rule requiring a newly commissioned or configuration-specific study in every case. The distinction between permissible inference and conclusive constitutional proof remains essential on both paths.

### Adversarial paths

That is a different ground from concluding that the sentence passes narrow proportionality. It is not controlling simply because other Justices would also deny relief.
