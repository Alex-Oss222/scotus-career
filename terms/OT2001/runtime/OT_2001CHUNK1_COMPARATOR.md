## 1. United States Postal Service v. Gregory

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded; unanimous judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for eight Justices. Thomas also concurred. Ginsburg concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** Pending grievances did not categorically prohibit considering prior discipline. The adequacy of the Board’s review standard and the consequences of the expunged letter of warning were not finally resolved.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; no simulated holding or vote is supplied.
