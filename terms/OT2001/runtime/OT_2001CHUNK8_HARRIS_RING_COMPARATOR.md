## 112. Harris v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist, O’Connor, Scalia, and Breyer joined Kennedy’s Parts I, II, and IV; Rehnquist, O’Connor, and Scalia also joined Part III. O’Connor concurred; Breyer concurred in part and in the judgment. Thomas dissented with Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** Brandishing was a sentencing factor, and the judicial finding could raise the minimum without changing the maximum.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only. Later reversal of that historical rule is not event-date authority.
* **Historical Sources:** [Harris v. United States](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/536/545/).


## 113. Ring v. Arizona

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7-2 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas. Scalia concurred with Thomas; Kennedy concurred separately. Breyer concurred in the judgment. O’Connor dissented with Rehnquist.
* **Historical Holding:** The Sixth Amendment required jury determination of a death-eligibility aggravator; Walton v. Arizona was overruled to the contrary extent.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; the proposed simulated change is stated independently in Section II.
* **Historical Sources:** [Ring v. Arizona](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/536/584/).
