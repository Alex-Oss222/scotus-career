## 118. Hope v. Pelzer

### Source and premise comparison

The verified historical judgment is **reversed**, 6-3, not an express reversal-and-remand decree. Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer supported reversal; Scalia and Thomas supported affirmance with the historical Chief Justice. The historical majority's Part V already reserved ultimate individual responsibility and defenses other than the appellate court's stated immunity ground. Its legal assumption covered both occasions: extended attachment apparently inflicting gratuitous pain or discomfort after immediate danger and any continuing refusal to work ended. The June aggravations were not indispensable to that assumption and were not attributed to May-only officers.

Thomas and Scalia accepted obvious-illegality notice without a factual twin. Their objection was that this law did not clearly prohibit the actual conduct attributed to these defendants, and that the Eleventh Circuit had not imposed the categorical prerequisite the majority attributed to it. Their application disagreement therefore survives agreement on the general notice rule.

The entering law supplies no material change warranting the frozen eight-Associate vacatur. Lanier and Wilson already preserve particularized notice without a twin; this was also accepted in the historical dissent. Saucier establishes no controlling general sequencing rule, but that difference does not alter this granted notice question. Delaney is a new public commitment to assumed-fact review and individual responsibility; it concerns six months without exercise and comparatively direct preconduct exercise precedents. It neither supplies 1995 notice nor equates missing allegations of a defendant's role with a factual dispute about a sufficiently alleged role. Its unanimous judgment does not oblige either historical dissenter to deny immunity here. Denius likewise distinguishes settled applications from uncertain extensions. Leventhal confirms that a Justice's merits disagreement cannot eliminate objective immunity; it supplies no new restraint rule. The narrower Hudson holding still prohibits the established nontrivial malicious beating and leaves conditions to Farmer; neither changes the accepted constitutional premise or removes Gates and Ort.

### Components and resolved alternatives

**A. Notice rule.** Apparent unlawfulness may follow from an obviously covered application of established preconduct law without identical facts. A general moral condemnation alone is insufficient. An earlier decision expressly reserving the particular application may demand greater factual specificity. Contrary authority, context and the relevant hierarchy must be assessed. No official must prove actual consultation of authorizing precedent; later law supplies no retroactive warning.

**B. May.** McClaran allegedly ordered attachment; Pelzer and Gates allegedly attached Hope. Two hours, quarter-hour water and bathroom offers and the activity log are retained. Those protections distinguish May from June but do not answer whether the assumed extended, painful attachment was gratuitous after necessity ended. The six reversers find notice on that legal assumption; the two affirmers regard the identified initial attachment after disruption, authorizing regulation and contrary district decisions as insufficiently clear for personal damages.

**C. June.** Gates is the only remaining respondent connected to this episode. The alleged seven-hour suffering and deprivations are not findings about his supervision or conduct. The six reversers find fair warning for the assumed knowing, unjustified punitive attachment; the two affirmers reject denial of immunity based on other guards' prolongation, clothing, water or taunting acts not attributed to Gates.

**D. Individual responsibility and remedy.** None finds final responsibility, damages, causation or every officer's knowledge. The six reverse the reviewed summary judgment on its similarity-based legal ground, with ordinary further proceedings and independent defenses preserved. They do not merely order an open-ended repetition of the notice question already resolved on the stated assumptions. The two affirm personal immunity for these respondents on the alleged conduct, without declaring every hitching-post use constitutional or adopting a factual-twin requirement. Withdrawn claims against absent guards remain withdrawn.

The strongest argument for the frozen vacatur is that legal correction can precede officer-specific application. It does not justify treating all eight as favoring that disposition: the historical majority already preserved attribution while deciding the legal notice application, and the historical dissent already accepted the abstract legal correction while finding affirmance warranted. No changed grant, record or preconduct authority supplies the missing bridge. The historical disagreement therefore remains persuasive evidence after independent review.

## 121. Board of Education v. Earls

### Comparator, changed precedent and components

The historical Court reversed 5-4. Of the current Associates, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer supported reversal and the program; Stevens, O'Connor, Souter and Ginsburg favored affirmance. Breyer fully joined the historical Court opinion and separately emphasized counseling, demand reduction, school responsibility, participatory adoption and the concerns with individualized targeting. The four dissenters disputed the need/intrusion fit; O'Connor and Souter also preserved their Vernonia disagreement. These positions are evidence, not assignments.

The current Vernonia has distinct controlling parts: unanimous search/no-waiver; a five-Justice contextual athlete-program holding; and an eight-Associate facial prescription ruling expressly allowing only a textually possible confidential method, without finding actual use or approving intrusive implementation. The full prior public entry confirms that distinction and each Associate's join. Chandler supplies actual need and fit without mandatory catastrophe, quantified efficacy, universal safety sensitivity or exhaustion. Those governing limits permit consideration of this program but do not make a paper safeguard a finding of actual compliance. Earls seeks relief both on the policy and its operation, at summary judgment. The neutral packet preserves supported contrary implementation evidence; no new evidence is invented here.

Components: **A**, urinalysis and acquired information are protected and participation is no waiver; **B**, concrete school need and the relation to this nonathletic competitive population; **C**, collection, confidentiality, restricted uses and counseling/participation consequences; **D**, supported actual-operation disputes and formal remedy. All eight retain A and require ordinary treatment of disputed evidence. Four find B/C insufficient even with the written safeguards and affirm. Four find the defined program constitutionally permissible on the specified protective operation but vacate/remand to correct the categorical group-specific prerequisite and address material actual-operation evidence. None supports unconditional reinstatement of summary judgment by assuming implementation away.

The written-policy distinction matters at its actual terms: the first positive permits continued participation with counseling proof within five days of the parental meeting and retesting two weeks later; a second produces fourteen days' exclusion, four counseling hours and monthly testing; a third produces exclusion for the remaining school year or eighty-eight school days, whichever is longer. No law-enforcement use or ordinary academic discipline is authorized. These are real restrictions and benefits, not no consequences. Prescription information allegedly exposed and result disclosure beyond need remain allegations subject to ordinary support/materiality rules; no other student's actual viewing is established.
