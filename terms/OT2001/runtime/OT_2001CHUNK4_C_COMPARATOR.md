## 61. Thompson v. Western States Medical Center

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas. Thomas concurred. Breyer dissented with Rehnquist, Stevens, and Ginsburg.

## 62. US Airways, Inc. v. Barnett

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O’Connor, and Kennedy. Stevens and O’Connor concurred separately. Scalia dissented with Thomas; Souter dissented with Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** A conflict with a seniority system ordinarily makes an accommodation unreasonable, subject to special circumstances shown by the employee.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; neither the presumption nor either opposing dissent is treated as an earlier simulated rule.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/535/391/).

## 63. City of Los Angeles v. Alameda Books, Inc.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote a plurality joined by Rehnquist, Scalia, and Thomas. Scalia concurred separately. Kennedy concurred in the judgment. Souter dissented with Stevens and Ginsburg, with Breyer joining Part II.
* **Historical Holding:** The City’s study and permissible inferences sufficed to overcome the businesses’ summary-judgment theory. Kennedy separately emphasized reducing harms without substantially reducing speech.
* **Historical Authority:** Quarantined comparator. The plurality’s full evidentiary formula is not automatically a majority holding, and the historical Erie premise differs from the supplied simulated rule.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/535/425/).

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits decision exists for this added matter. The Eighth Circuit reversed and remanded for conditional habeas relief; the published panel opinion contains no panel dissent.
