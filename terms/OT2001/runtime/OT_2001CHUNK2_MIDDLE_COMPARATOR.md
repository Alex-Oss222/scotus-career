## 23. National Cable & Telecommunications Assn., Inc. v. Gulf Power Co.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded. Six Justices joined the mixed cable-Internet jurisdiction ruling; all eight participating Justices agreed on wireless attachments.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, Scalia, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Thomas, joined by Souter, concurred in part and dissented in part, seeking further agency classification for mixed services. O’Connor did not participate.
* **Historical Holding:** The general pole-attachment grant covered the challenged mixed-service and wireless attachments.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [National Cable & Telecommunications Assn., Inc. v. Gulf Power Co.](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/534/327/).

## 25. Kansas v. Crane

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O’Connor, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Scalia dissented with Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Due process required proof of serious difficulty controlling behavior, but not complete lack of control. The historical premise that Kansas v. Hendricks permitted civil commitment differs from the authenticated simulation.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Kansas v. Crane](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/534/407/).

## 24. Lee v. Kemna

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, and Breyer. Kennedy dissented with Scalia and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The state procedural ground was inadequate in the exceptional circumstances; the Court did not finally grant habeas relief on the continuance claim.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Lee v. Kemna](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/534/362/).

## 26. United States v. Microsoft Corp. +

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The en banc D.C. Circuit affirmed monopoly-maintenance liability in part, reversed attempted-monopolization liability, vacated tying liability and the remedy, and remanded with reassignment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam opinion of Edwards, Williams, Ginsburg, Sentelle, Randolph, Rogers, and Tatel.
* **Historical Holding:** The software tie required rule-of-reason treatment; the press contacts required disqualification and reassignment, but the court did not vacate all independently reviewed liability findings.
* **Historical Authority:** Lower-court judgment under simulated review; no Supreme Court merits outcome is supplied.
* **Historical Sources:** [United States v. Microsoft Corp.](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/253/34/576095/).
