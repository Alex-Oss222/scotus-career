## 111. BE&K Construction Co. v. NLRB

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, unanimous in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for Rehnquist, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Scalia concurred with Thomas. Breyer concurred in part and in the judgment with Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** The Board’s standard impermissibly reached reasonably based unsuccessful litigation with retaliatory purpose; the Court adopted a limiting construction and remanded.

* **Historical Sources:** [BE&K Construction Co. v. NLRB](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/536/516/).

## 115. United States v. Ruiz

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for eight Justices; Thomas concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The Constitution did not require the challenged pre-plea impeachment disclosure and, in this agreement’s context, affirmative-defense disclosure. The Court addressed the statutory appellate-authority issue before rejecting the merits theory.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [United States v. Ruiz](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/536/622/).
