# United States v. Emerson — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: one signed opinion of the Court with component-specific partial joins and separate dissents.

The case applies settled individual coverage, interprets the statutory alternatives, extends Napier to a precisely defined threat-order predicate, and independently resolves the Fifth Amendment dismissal ground. The new constitutional application and distinct grounds warrant a signed merits opinion with four parts and exact partial joins. The coverage and statutory premises belong to the arms component; disagreement with its judgment is not cured by agreement on those premises. Stevens assigns that component as senior Associate in its six-Justice majority. The Fifth Amendment dismissal ground is a separate judgment component: the Chief joins its reversal through the approved fallback and may assign it, but cannot author its reasoning through that fallback. One author fully joining all four parts can carry the opinion; there is no need to split counted leads. Form and propositions are fixed on these legal grounds before the recount.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
