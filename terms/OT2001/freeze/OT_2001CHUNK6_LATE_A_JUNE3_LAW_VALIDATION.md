## Echazabal

**Delegation and statutory authority.** Zandford independently construes the securities connection requirement and expressly leaves additional controlling-deference analysis unnecessary. It changes neither Mead, Haggar nor Walton, and does not decide EEOC authority. Echazabal's ground still rests on the general qualification defense, the nonexclusive threat-to-others example, the express rulemaking grant and the bounded regulation. No Associate's Zandford join contradicts that ground.

**Proof and relief.** Zandford separates a corrected statutory rule from civil proof, preclusion and actual relief; reversal does not automatically restore the former civil judgment. That is consistent with Echazabal's already stated distinction between regulatory authorization and Chevron's disputed medical showing. It creates no medical presumption, new hearing duty or remedy. Compaq's tax-characterization review and Mackby's constitutional proportionality review supply no universal appellate rule for every mixed question and do not displace ordinary summary-judgment treatment of the competing medical evidence.

**Review channel and preservation.** Holmes Group's counterclaim-only patent rule concerns a particular specialized appellate allocation. Its transfer holding depends on established section 1631 predicates. Echazabal presents neither that jurisdictional defect nor a wrong appellate destination. Holmes creates no ground to reopen Irwin's judgment or the unpreserved Chevron accommodation argument. Compaq's profit and sham holdings change no original-ADA coverage, accommodation or objective safety condition.

**Conclusion:** All eight original reversal/remand commitments and their limits remain unchanged. The medical application, assumed coverage and motion-specific preservation boundaries remain as frozen.

**Justice-specific continuity.** None of the new holdings resolves the actual disagreement between the five Associates distinguishing the mandatory own-right objector from Felzen and the three retaining intervention as necessary. **Conclusion:** The five reversal/remand and three affirmance commitments remain unchanged, including the timely-objection, mandatory-class and own-right limits. Settlement merits and the existing intervention ruling retain their separate posture.

**Conclusion:** All eight retain the same three reviewed-component dispositions: reverse the Franconia contract limitations ground; reverse the specified Grass Valley contract limitations ground; vacate and remand the Franconia taking limitations disposition for its distinct inquiry. ## McKune

**The material penalty analogy does not change the constitutional inquiry.** Mackby holds that a mixed compensatory and punitive civil monetary demand is a covered fine and then requires a separate gross-disproportionality inquiry. Its recognition that a civil label and legitimate remedial purpose do not alone defeat independent constitutional protection is consistent with every McKune commitment. None treats the treatment label, beneficial purpose or absence of an entitlement to preferred privileges as automatically dispositive.

Mackby's partly punitive coverage test is not the Fifth Amendment compulsion standard. Its proportionality rule compares an established sovereign monetary exaction with the actual offense; it is not a general test for pressure to surrender the privilege. McKune concerns disclosure-linked housing and privilege changes, with the sentence, credit and disputed-danger limits already preserved. No monetary exaction or finding of compelled testimony follows from Mackby's terminology. Its eight-Associate appellate-review paragraph expressly creates no universal rule for every mixed question.

**The actual compulsion division remains.** The four affirming Associates still regard the combined automatic restrictions as substantial pressure under the penalty authorities. O'Connor still rejects an exclusive Sandin hardship prerequisite but regards these established consequences as insufficiently severe. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas still support the same sufficient actual-consequences conclusion with their additional prison-context explanation. Their Mackby joins perform different constitutional work and do not decide the disputed degree of Fifth Amendment pressure. The governing Sandin rule, Woodard's automatic-penalty limit, Mitchell's actual exposure and limited waiver, and Reiner's no-innocence-forfeiture rule remain unchanged.

**Other new law.** Zandford rejects using a criminal conviction label as a substitute for every element of a subsequent civil claim. McKune already preserves genuine additional incriminating exposure rather than treating conviction as universal waiver. Zandford supplies no compulsion rule or change to the testing claim's procedural posture. Holmes Group's patent forum/transfer rules and Compaq's tax recognition rules change no present threshold or remedy.

**Conclusion:** The four-to-four Associate division, sufficient grounds and join barriers remain unchanged. No final Court tally or controlling rationale is inferred from this comparison.
