# Carey v. Saffold — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: signed opinion of the Court with a dissent.

The case resolves the open statutory meaning of pendency for ordinary inter-court intervals and California's original-petition procedure, with a distinct state-timing limit and a contested application-level reading. The six-Justice coalition shares the full rule and bounded remand. A signed merits opinion explains that reusable statutory construction; the three dissenting Justices retain their contrary reading. The form and propositions follow the legal work, independently of any count or author.
