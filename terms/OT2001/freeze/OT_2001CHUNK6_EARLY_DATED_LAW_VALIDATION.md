Its controlling rules require consistent gross-dividend and legally available credit treatment, preserve independent review of legal tax characterization and clear-error treatment of subsidiary facts, recognize this arrangement's actual return, acquired rights, market exposure and sufficient business purpose, and preserve substance scrutiny for different records.
