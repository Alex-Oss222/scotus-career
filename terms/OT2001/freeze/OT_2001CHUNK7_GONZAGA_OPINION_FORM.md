# Gonzaga University v. Doe — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-20.

Form: Signed opinion announcing the judgment, with four Justices on the institution-directed no-right explanation and a two-Justice judgment concurrence on the narrower FERPA-specific ground. The common statutory conclusion does not create a majority general rights formula. Three dissent on the enforceability question..

### These FERPA nondisclosure provisions do not support the asserted Section 1983 claim

**Holding and operative rule:** FERPA's education-record nondisclosure provisions do not establish the individual judicially enforceable entitlement asserted in this Section 1983 action. The FERPA-based federal judgment must therefore be reversed; the state-action determination, excluded from review, and independent state-law judgments are unaffected.

**Authority:** Six Justices agree on this statute-specific conclusion. @AUTHOR@'s lead opinion is joined by @JOINS@; Breyer, joined by Souter, concurs in the judgment on the separate FERPA-specific explanation below. No majority adopts a new general formula equating a statutory right with intent to create a private remedy.

**Controlling explanation:** Section 1983 enforces individual federal rights, not every beneficial federal funding condition. Blessing requires examination of the particular provision; Bruneau separately preserves the enforcement route once a right is established. The four-Justice lead reads FERPA's policy-or-practice funding condition as an institutional obligation administered through federal oversight, rather than the asserted individual nondisclosure right. Mandatory language, identified records and consent instructions do not alone change that statutory object. Breyer and Souter reach the same statute-specific conclusion because the broad records coverage, uncertain practical reach and centrally administered institutional obligation do not establish the asserted judicial entitlement; they reject a universal heightened formula collapsing rights into remedial intent. All six reject this particular enforcement claim. That conclusion neither categorically excludes spending legislation nor makes absence of an implied FERPA action dispositive. The disagreement with the dissent concerns whether this statute creates the underlying individual entitlement in the first place. Administrative machinery does not independently foreclose Section 1983 whenever a right otherwise exists. Because the threshold fails here, no separate damages holding is necessary.

**Precedent treatment:** Blessing and Bruneau remain distinct provision-specific right and enforcement inquiries; Owasso's reservation of enforcement is resolved for these nondisclosure provisions. No general rule supported only by the four-Justice lead is elevated into controlling law.
