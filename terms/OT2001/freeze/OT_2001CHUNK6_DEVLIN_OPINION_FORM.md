# Devlin — form fixed before assignment

One signed majority opinion states the limited appellate-party rule and its bounded remand, with one dissent stating the formal-intervention alternative. Stone, Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support the full mandatory-class, own-right, timely-objector ground. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support affirmance because represented binding and heard objections do not themselves confer independent litigating status. The majority preserves Felzen's derivative-shareholder rule rather than overruling it; both writings leave the existing intervention denial and settlement merits untouched.

There is one controlling proposition, including its procedural implementation, with no need to divide authorship or manufacture additional grounds. The six complete majority joiners are eligible for the lead; the three dissenters are not. No Stone fallback or conditional position is used. This form is recorded before counts or author selection.
