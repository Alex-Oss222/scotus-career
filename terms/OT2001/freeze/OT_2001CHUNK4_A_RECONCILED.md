## 50. Barnhart v. Walton

The complete official report, 535 U.S. 212–227, was read, including Scalia's complete separate writing. Historical argument was January 16, 2002. The lower court's statement that the 2000 rule was not retroactive is therefore not a final answer to that different interpretive-use issue.
