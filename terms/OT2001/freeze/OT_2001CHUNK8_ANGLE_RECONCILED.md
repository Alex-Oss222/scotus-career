## Rule and reach retained

The five-Justice position is a new decision of questions Apprendi reserved. It covers a fact concerning the commission or consequences of the present offense that the mandatory Guidelines make legally necessary to increase either the binding upper limit or the binding lower limit of punishment. In this federal prosecution the covered fact requires the applicable grand-jury charge and, absent a valid element admission or lawful jury waiver, jury determination beyond a reasonable doubt. A sentencing admission, notice of exposure or evidentiary strength does not itself meet those requirements. Neither a numerical replacement range nor an unproved waiver is supplied.

The operative distinction is between a fact changing a legal limit and a consideration informing choice within limits already lawfully established. The floor component makes less punishment legally unavailable; it is not merely advice favoring a harsher choice. A ceiling-only rule remains a lawful competing position, but none of the eight adopts it for the reasons assessed below. The five's common rule is full rationale agreement at this scope, not a sum of different grounds or a judgment-only coalition.

McMillan's received rule remains binding until expressly displaced. The proposed floor holding therefore must expressly overrule its contrary permission to establish a covered present-offense aggravator by judicial preponderance proof merely because the outer statutory maximum remains unchanged. Calling this an application of Apprendi would be inaccurate. This reconciliation retains that express displacement and does not turn the prior Apprendi reservation into an earlier overruling.

The five preserve lawful sentencing choice and relevant-conduct consideration inside the range constitutionally established. Witte and Watts retain their actual within-authority and successive-prosecution holdings; neither previously supplied a universal exemption for every binding Guidelines constraint. Mistretta's institutional approval does not settle allocation of a new aggravating fact. Mitigation reducing otherwise authorized punishment, valid departures under their own conditions, and prior-conviction questions retain their distinct footing. The commitments choose neither advisory Guidelines nor wholesale statutory severance. The three contrary Justices accept that the Guidelines bind but regard them as regulation of authority already conferred by the statutory offense; their disagreement concerns constitutional classification.

## Disposition and plain-error reconciliation

All eight preserve the convictions and Angle's independently required resentencing. The five's constitutional directions govern that real forthcoming sentencing proceeding and reverse the legal premise under which the court below directed it. They do not rest relief from Angle's former sentence on an unperformed Rule 52(b) analysis: the independent findings defect already removed that sentence. The three would preserve only the ordinary findings remand. No new trial, acquittal, enhanced charging proceeding or numeric replacement sentence follows automatically.

Phifer's count excess remains a constitutional error regardless of available aggregate exposure. Apprendi's rejection of unused consecutive authority as an answer to error does not itself decide Rule 52(b) prejudice. The three opposing Justices therefore have a coherent third-condition ground if their Guidelines rule is accepted: a valid mandatory total and obligatory stacking would yield identical imprisonment, and no other substantial-rights effect is established. The five reject that premise because the legal validity of the mandatory total depends on the very quantity classification they reject. Neither side treats possible cumulative statutory maxima alone as dispositive.

The five vacate only the appellate sentence-affirming judgment and remand for the remaining Rule 52(b) application under the lawful-range rule. Failure of the en banc court's sufficient rationale does not establish every condition for correction. It does not itself vacate Phifer's sentence, command resentencing, reduce imprisonment or authorize a replacement aggregate. The defendant ordinarily bears the substantial-rights burden; the independent fairness, integrity or public-reputation condition remains necessary. A genuinely lawful inevitable identical outcome may bear on effect, but its lawfulness and inevitability must be established rather than assumed from the original quantity calculation.

Vonn permits the relevant existing record to inform the inquiry. Johnson and Cotton govern their settled-contrary-trial-law/intervening-law setting; the new Guidelines proposition is not automatically declared clear at trial, and initially unsettled trial law is not decided. Scalia's preserved reservation requires no separate writing if the common opinion adds no broader timing holding. The official Angle source's general evidence descriptions do not establish those circumstances. Neder's preserved-error actual-verdict review neither replaces Rule 52(b) nor supplies a hypothetical quantity verdict.
