# Gisbrecht: formal mandate clarification

**Scope:** Supplemental reconciliation clarification only. The completed partial reconciliation and all frozen commitments remain unchanged. No private position or assembly proposal has been read. This clarification concerns the object of reversal or vacatur, not a new fee-method or disposition choice.

## Supported mandate envelope

The seven remand supporters' frozen G4 commitment directs vacatur of the three reviewed fee determinations and remand through the court of appeals. It does not state an objection to reversal of the appellate judgment that affirmed those determinations under the erroneous exclusive-lodestar rule. The final historical judgment reversed that appellate judgment; each of these seven Associates joined that result. No actual simulated premise changes the relevant methodological error, the need to remove its affirmance, or the reservation of individual fee amounts.

Each of those seven may therefore join this precise ordinary formulation: reverse the Ninth Circuit judgment insofar as it affirmed the fee determinations for Gisbrecht, Miller and Sandine under an exclusive lodestar method, and remand through that court for the three determinations to be set aside and redetermined under G1–G3. Reversal refers to the appellate affirmance; vacatur or setting aside refers to the dependent fee determinations. This formulation implements the frozen G4 relief and does not convert it into an award, a fixed amount, a performance finding or relief for Anderson. A vacatur-and-remand formulation achieving the same correction also remains permissible. The words are not interchangeable for a different object or broader relief.
