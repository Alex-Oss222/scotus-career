# United States v. Fior D'Italia, Inc. — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: Signed opinion of the Court resolving contested aggregate tax-assessment authority on full merits review..

One six-Justice coalition joins the entire statutory authority, substantive limits and stipulated remedy; the three dissenters have a distinct statutory method objection. A signed merits opinion explains the generally applicable method and its indispensable limits without splitting propositions to affect counts. Form is selected before any count or lead author.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
