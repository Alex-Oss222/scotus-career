Respondent timely filed a Notice of Appeal from the 
District Court’s judgment; however, instead of appealing to the 
Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit, Respondent directed its 
appeal to the Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit. 
Respondent contended that the Federal Circuit had appellate 
jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1295(a)(1), not based on anything 
contained or pleaded in Petitioner’s complaint or decided by 
the District Court, but based on Respondent’s having included, 
in its Answer, a counterclaim against Petitioner for alleged 
patent infringement. The Respondent’s counterclaim was 
extraneous to the District Court’s jurisdiction to hear Petitioner’s 
suit and formed no part of the judgment from which Respondent 
had appealed. In its brief filed with the Court of Appeals for the 
Federal Circuit, Petitioner urged that Respondent’s appeal be 
dismissed for lack of appellate jurisdiction or, alternatively, that 
the appeal be transferred to the Tenth Circuit pursuant to 
28 U.S.C. § 1631.
