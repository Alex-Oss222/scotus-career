## Components and supported alternative paths

**T1 — Scope of censorship safeguards.** A genuinely content-neutral system allocating park space and coordinating activities need not adopt Freedman's entire procedure for censorship of expressive content. Narrow, objective grounds, action within the established bounded period, written explanation and an available avenue for judicial review still matter. Freedman's requirement that the censor initiate judicial proceedings and carry the censorship burden is not automatically a requirement for every administrative refusal of a neutral park permit. This conclusion does not exempt a licensing mechanism from review merely because its officials describe it as logistical.

The strongest contrary path applies the full censorship safeguards to any permit prerequisite, because delay can defeat speech regardless of the ground of denial. The pertinent distinction is the official function actually authorized: deciding acceptable ideas poses a different recurring danger from applying definite use, scheduling and safety limits. Existing neutral-permit authority permits the latter subject to meaningful constraints. No ruling is needed that every neutral scheme may dispense with all timing protection, or that an applicant can never challenge actual delay.

**T2 — Standards and discretion in this ordinance.** The two prior-conduct predicates and eleven further grounds confine denial to the stated subjects. The prior-conduct language operates only to the extent permitted by law. The ordinance MAY permit denial on a listed ground; it does not require denial on every occurrence. Permission to excuse an otherwise disqualifying circumstance is not permission to invent an additional ground of denial. The actual grounds concern completion and prescribed submissions, material falsehood, legal capacity, unpaid damage or debt, competing earlier applications and scheduled District programs, designated uses, unreasonable danger, required licenses and legal prohibitions. They are sufficient for the facial standards challenge when applied as park-management criteria, not as authorization to select favored ideas.

The strongest invalidity path treats permissive exceptions, danger assessment and cross-references as open-ended power to discriminate. Forsyth requires real constraints and does not accept presumed goodwill. Here the specified grounds, order of receipt and written explanation allow a court to identify a reason outside the authorized criteria or a discriminatory application. No holding approves hostile-audience pricing, an unpresented fee amount, every financial condition in every application, viewpoint favoritism, or a refusal justified merely by anticipated objection to a message. A materially different implemented discretion remains challengeable.

**T3 — Timing, review and remedy.** The District must act within 14 days, unless it gives written notice extending that period for another 14 days. Denial must state its grounds in writing; where feasible the District must propose measures to cure application defects. When denial rests on a prior competing application, it must suggest other times or places. The applicant has seven days to appeal in writing; the General Superintendent must act within seven days. Common-law certiorari review is available in state court. Together these protections support rejection of the facial challenge without importing every Freedman procedure. There is no established automatic-issuance rule and no supplied adjudication deadline for the reviewing court; neither is invented.

All commitments affirm the Seventh Circuit's facial judgment. No application-specific discrimination finding, damages, injunction against a particular denial or new substitute permit ordinance is entered. The actual constitutionality of delayed, selective or content-based administration remains for a proper claim and record. The disposition does not presume that such an independent claim is pending or establish its elements by allegation alone.

## Proposed common proposition support and source limits

Each of the eight non-Stone Justices supports the T1 distinction at its stated level, the T2 facial standards conclusion and the T3 actual procedural sufficiency and affirmance. This is not a final Court count, an assignment or an assertion about Stone.

Forsyth remains controlling on objective standards, facial challenge and hostility-based fees. Cox remains authority for neutral coordination; Shuttlesworth and City of Lakewood require bounded licensing. Ward supports genuinely content-independent time, place and manner administration. Freedman retains its censorship safeguards in its own setting and is distinguished here, not overruled. Hill and Schenck supply the individual positions identified above without treating unlike mechanisms as interchangeable.

No source gap prevents this bounded disposition. A proposed broader holding about automatic issuance, fee amounts or exemptions, a fixed judicial decision deadline, actual expedited relief, or a specific discriminatory denial would require additional sanitized evidence. No such holding is part of these commitments.


## Historical reconciliation and limits

Historical Thomas was unanimous on the distinction between this neutral park-management permit system and censorship, and on the sufficiency of the ordinance's bounded grounds and review. It confirms the independent result and component-level joins of Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Its historical authorship does not assign the opinion here.

For Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter, their actual Forsyth joins require standards rather than official goodwill. The list of authorized grounds, receipt order, bounded action period, written explanation and available review answer that concern here. Scalia and Thomas's actual Forsyth dissent joins recognize neutral cost regulation without approving an unbounded licensor; nothing in their narrower earlier position requires invalidating this enumerated system. Ginsburg and Breyer's own Hill and Schenck positions support the mechanism-specific approach identified in their rows, and the direct historical Thomas joins confirm it. Neither the actual Hill nor Schenck decision supplies a blanket exception for rules described as neutral.

The Court distinguishes Freedman's content-censorship machinery; it does not overrule that decision or FW/PBS's application to a licensing system directed at sexually explicit businesses. Under the actual scheme presented, the District need not itself commence judicial proceedings upon each denial or provide Freedman's special judicial timetable. Because that premise is absent, the Court need not decide whether Freedman's expeditious-review requirement in its own setting means prompt commencement of court proceedings or a prompt judicial determination. This is a ground-based limit on the question decided, not a holding that every neutral permit system may allow indefinite administrative delay or ineffective judicial review.

The ordinance permits denial on a specified ground; it does not require denial whenever a technical defect occurs. Permission to excuse a defect is not permission to invent a disqualifying ground or discriminate by viewpoint. A rigid no-exceptions construction is not required. The historical Court also cited a specific oral-argument explanation of how the District exercised that permission; the approved neutral packet does not certify that concession, and the final commitments do not rely on it as established practice. The constitutional and textual limits on permissible administration are sufficient for this facial ruling. No new fee amount, waiver system, actual expedited court order or automatic grant on silence is added.

The actual Forsyth audience-hostility rule continues to govern any covered fee; this judgment does not approve an unpresented financial condition, assess a particular denial or decide every element of a later application claim. The historical footnote identifying unpressed additional time-place-manner objections does not expand review or supply a new finding of narrow tailoring for every provision. No heightened numerical-pattern prerequisite to challenging actual viewpoint discrimination is created.

There is no historical departure and no material change to the provisional commitments. No new neutral fact is necessary.
