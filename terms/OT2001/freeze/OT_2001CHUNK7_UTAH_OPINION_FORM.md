# Utah v. Evans — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-20.

Form: one signed multipart plenary Opinion of the Court and two separate mixed opinions.

Parts I, II and III resolve standing, statutory permission and constitutional validity respectively. One member of the six-Justice full coalition can carry every part, so no author split is warranted. O'Connor, joined by Scalia, gives the statutory dissent with standing concurrence and constitutional reservation. Thomas gives the constitutional dissent while joining standing and statutory permission. These forms follow the different legal grounds before any counts are examined.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
