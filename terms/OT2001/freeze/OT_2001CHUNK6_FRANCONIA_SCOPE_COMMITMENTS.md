## Unchanged legal commitments applied at the corrected scope

**Contract rule for both reviewed contract components.** Under the assumed unrestricted-prepayment undertaking, the promised performance is acceptance of permitted prepayment when the borrower exercises the option. Legislation refusing that future performance is repudiation rather than, by itself, an overdue performance. A legally effective election to treat repudiation as present breach may begin the claim; without that election, the claim accrues when the obligation becomes due and is refused or otherwise breached. Actual request, refusal, effective election, any legally sufficient tender excuse, and each filing interval require their own supported determination. Neither implementation on May 23 nor enactment on February 5 makes that election compulsory. The premise remains assumed for this accrual review, not a final determination of formation, authority, contractual scope, liability or damages.

Roehm supplies election principles; Mobil's actual and anticipatory process-promise components require separating failed performance from repudiation; Winstar preserves an actual monetary undertaking while Congress remains free to legislate. Bay Area Laundry supports the distinction between an obligation and a completed enforcement claim without importing its ERISA timetable. TRW does not turn this accrual inquiry into a discovery extension, and Young supplies no stay-based suspension here. These sources retain the Justice-specific qualifications recorded in the original commitments.

The strongest contrary characterization is a continuously available option destroyed immediately by legislation. That ground remains rejected on the accepted future-performance premise. An actually different present-performance promise would require its own established contractual basis. The change in which lower court selected which date does not establish such a promise or a universal election date.

**Separate taking rule for Franconia only.** The as-applied taking claim does not become complete merely because enactment repudiates an assumed contract. The court must identify the property interest, challenged governmental act and completed injury; where meaningful agency choices remain, a sufficiently definitive application matters. Suitum and Palazzolo distinguish procedures capable of changing the burden from futile repetition, and Tahoe-Sierra preserves the appropriate contextual property inquiry. The Federal Circuit's characterization of the interest as a contract option must be examined against the actual asserted as-applied property theory; it cannot make contract accrual mechanically dispositive. Conversely, a genuinely complete appropriation or destruction theory must receive its own timing analysis and is not automatically postponed until a contract-performance refusal.

Franconia's taking disposition therefore returns for its own accrual/finality and remaining lawful inquiries. Its statutory release routes do not establish either universal eligibility or universal futility. The original commitments' complete paragraph (4) negotiation requirements, paragraph (5) sale process, conditional 180-day permission, distinct paragraph (5)(G) exceptions and paragraph (5)(F) budget exception remain operative. None is reduced to an absolute ban or a guaranteed right to immediate release.

**Competing scope paths.** A disposition of both reviewed judgments may resolve both legal questions without treating both questions as present in each judgment. The strongest argument for broader Grass Valley relief is that the later trial court expressly followed the Franconia limitations rationale and may be affected by the same new governing rule. That supports possible application through the separate proceeding's lawful procedures; it does not supply an appellate judgment, petition or order placing the January 2 takings ruling within this review. The narrower mandate is required by the corrected record, rather than adopted merely for caution. No new Grass Valley takings vote is modeled. A separate appeal, petition or enlargement order would require a new scoped procedural input, not inference from the common parties or theory.
