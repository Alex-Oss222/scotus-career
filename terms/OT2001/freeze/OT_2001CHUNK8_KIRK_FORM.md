# Kirk — form and governing propositions fixed before assignment

The decision will issue per curiam. It corrects a direct failure to apply Payton's settled physical-home-entry requirement on the submitted record, without plenary oral argument, an enlarged exception or a final evidence determination. This form follows that legal work. No signed lead assignment or balance count is required, and this event earns no lead-opinion credit.

The Court's rule is that probable cause alone does not authorize warrantless nonconsensual home entry: an appropriate warrant or a valid exception must independently authorize crossing the threshold. Arrest and search-incident authority do not substitute for that authority; a later warrant does not cure the earlier entry. The receiving court must address the actual exigency question and any properly remaining independent-source and evidence-consequence issues under their existing requirements.

All eight Associates support reversing the erroneous state appellate judgment and remanding. Stone's approved formal disposition is vacatur and remand; it cannot be recast as reversal. He joins the home-entry propositions and fact-bound remand inquiry but dissents from reversal to the extent he would vacate instead. His short concurrence in part and dissent in part states that remedial difference without adding a ground beyond his approved position. The judgment and opinion table must distinguish this eight-to-one formal disposition from nine-Justice agreement on the rule and need for further proceedings. No majority rationale is manufactured by counting disposition agreement.
