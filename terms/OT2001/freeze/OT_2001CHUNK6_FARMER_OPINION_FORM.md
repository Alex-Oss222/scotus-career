# Farmer — form fixed before assignment

One signed lead contains three legally distinct parts: I, immediate review and the supported hearing rule (all nine); II, the applied Mathews explanation (all except Scalia); III, Farmer's complete release burden and conditional remedy (eight Associates, excluding Stone-Zsela). Scalia's separate concurrence in part and in the judgment preserves his sufficient later-adversary-process ground. Stone's concurrence in part and dissent in part preserves his express government-taint-burden objection. The judgment is unanimous as to hearing relief and 8–1 as to the adopted release allocation.

Seven Associates can carry every part: Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. A single author therefore remains legally possible and divided authorship is unwarranted. Stone assigns the hearing component, including Parts I–II, but cannot assign from the release-allocation coalition he opposes. Stevens, its senior Associate, assigns Part III. The eventual choice must satisfy both actual assignment bases and the balance requirement governing the parts assigned by the Chief; all part-specific eligible sets and pools must be recorded after the recount. No fallback applies. These propositions and this form are independently adopted before counts or author choice.
