- **Leventhal.** Seven Justices support the entire opinion. Scalia joins Parts I and III and Stevens joins Part I; neither is eligible for the whole lead. Stone's nine-count self-assignment is within the seven-person pool. The 9-0 judgment includes eight merits votes and Stevens's immunity-only vote.

- **Drayton.** O'Connor is the most senior Associate in the five-Justice reversal coalition and assigns the whole opinion to herself. The four dissenters' Part I agreement does not make them members of the judgment coalition or eligible authors of Part II. The Associate assignment is not bound by the Chief's pool rule, and its lead counts.

- **Emerson.** Stevens assigns Parts I-III from the six-Justice arms coalition; the Chief assigns the independent Part IV from the unanimous Fifth Amendment judgment coalition. Both assignments select Souter from a fresh unchanged vector, with Drayton/O'Connor last. The arms eligible set has six full joiners. The 6-3 arms judgment, unanimous Fifth Amendment judgment, nine Parts I-II joins, six Part III joins and eight Part IV joins reconcile without a synthetic majority.

- **Gonzaga.** O'Connor assigns as senior Associate in the six-Justice judgment coalition. Four Justices fully support Thomas's lead rationale; Breyer and Souter agree only in judgment on their narrower statutory ground and are not eligible for that lead. The four-Justice opinion announcing the judgment counts once. The public topology correctly distinguishes the four-Justice rationale from the six-Justice judgment.

- **Page and Robertson.** The full-opinion eligible set in each is Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Page's four remaining Justices withhold different portions: O'Connor and Kennedy do not join Part I; Scalia and Thomas do not join Parts I-II. Its 5/7/9/9 part coalitions and 9-0 judgment reconcile. Robertson has nine Part I joins but five Parts II-III joins; unanimous rejection of preclusion supplies no additional merits vote. Its 5-4 judgment reconciles. Each Stone assignment compares a full-join alternative and remains within the pool; neither relies on office alone.

- **Utah.** Nine Justices join standing, seven join statutory permission and six join the constitutional application. Thomas joins Parts I-II but opposes Part III; O'Connor and Scalia join Part I, oppose Part II and do not reach Part III. Only the six full-opinion joiners are eligible for the whole lead. Their counts are eleven except Ginsburg's twelve, so all six belong to the balance pool. Breyer's eleven-count assignment produces the 6-3 judgment and raises his count to twelve.

- **Other assignments.** Carey, Ticknor, Fior D'Italia, Hooper, Atkins, Harbury, Columbus, Dadian and Rush each state the actual complete-rationale eligible set.
