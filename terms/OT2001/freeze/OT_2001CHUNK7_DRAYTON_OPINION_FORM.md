# United States v. Drayton — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: signed opinion of the Court and a dissent.

The parties share the contextual seizure framework but divide on its application to two successive passenger encounters. A signed merits opinion distinguishes the common rule from the five-Justice no-seizure application and preserves respondent-specific proceedings and the consent boundary. The Chief is outside the judgment majority; O'Connor, the senior Associate in that coalition, assigns the complete opinion. Shared framework agreement by dissenters creates no separate judgment component for a different assigner.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
