# Leventhal v. Knapek — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: signed opinion of the Court with precise partial joins and two concurrences.

The case directly establishes the workplace inception-and-scope rule through seven votes and applies it to four distinct computer inspections. The privacy question, general framework and actual application have different coalitions; an opinion divided into those legally distinct parts preserves Scalia's narrower merits agreement and Stevens's independent immunity ground. A signed merits opinion is warranted by that doctrinal and record work. The parts do not multiply counted leads or manipulate eligibility; a full-rule Justice can write the complete opinion.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
