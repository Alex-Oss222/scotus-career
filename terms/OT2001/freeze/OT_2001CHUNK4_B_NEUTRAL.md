* **Existing Rule's Reach:** Drye already determines which sovereign supplies which part of the analysis. Its inheritance-control application does not itself decide the aggregate of present rights in an entireties estate or their value.

The circuit treated the absence of a separate state-law interest reachable by an individual creditor as dispositive. The Government points to the husband’s actual powers and economic benefits and the federal statute’s breadth. The Government cannot collect the wife’s independent share as if it belonged to the husband; the wife cannot answer the federal classification question solely with Michigan’s different choice about ordinary creditors.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**Plausible Holding Scope, Judgment Consequences, and Uncertainty:** Reversal could recognize attachment to the taxpayer’s rights and remand valuation and remaining statutory questions.
