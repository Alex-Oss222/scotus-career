## Actual new law compared

- **Compaq, May 28:** The actual ADR transaction satisfies economic substance and sufficient business purpose; gross dividend and legally available credit must be treated consistently. Historical facts retain clear-error review while legal tax characterization receives independent review. Recognition removes only the sham-dependent adjustment and penalty within the actual stipulation. The decision creates neither an ownership-only safe harbor nor a universal nominal-profit test.
- **Holmes Group, June 3:** Under the then-operative patent-jurisdiction statutes, a patent counterclaim alone does not supply the incorporated arising-under basis when the complaint supplies none. The proper regional appeal must receive transfer once the statutory conditions are established. The district court's independent federal jurisdiction survives. Ginsburg and O'Connor instead would permit specialized review of an actually adjudicated compulsory patent counterclaim; they join the transfer ruling and agree the unadjudicated counterclaim here does not suffice.
- **Zandford, June 3:** Actual sales executing a contemporaneous concealed appropriation plan can satisfy the securities-transaction connection, with duty, scienter, deception, interstate means and the actual transaction connection independently required. Later independently conceived theft after an honest sale is different. The unanimous ruling reverses categorical dismissal without automatically establishing civil liability, preclusion or the former relief.
- **Mackby, June 3:** The combined FCA fixed penalties and treble damages are at least partly punitive and subject to the Excessive Fines Clause. Gross disproportionality to the actual offense governs, with legislative respect and relevant compensatory functions retained. The eight Associates join independent constitutional appellate review and clear-error review of underlying findings. Coverage does not establish excessiveness, authorize a remedy under another statute, impose every criminal-procedure protection or create a fixed ratio or government-cost ceiling.
