# Rush Prudential HMO, Inc. v. Moran — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-20.

Form: one signed plenary Opinion of the Court and one dissent.

The six-Justice Court opinion decides saved insurance regulation and compatibility with the existing federal benefits remedy. A single author can carry every proposition; no division or combination is made for assignment. Thomas, joined by Scalia and Kennedy, gives the actual-conflict dissent. The two necessary legal inquiries are independently explained without creating separate countable leads.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
