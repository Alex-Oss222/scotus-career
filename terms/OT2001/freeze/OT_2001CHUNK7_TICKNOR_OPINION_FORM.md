# Ticknor v. Choice Hotels International, Inc. — opinion form

Event: 2002-06-17.

Form: signed opinion of the Court with a dissent.

The merits disposition applies a complete state-law choice rule and genuinely general contract defense to a developed but disputed commercial record. A signed Court opinion explains the fact-specific application and avoids a categorical federal symmetry rule; a dissent preserves the opposing accurate-application rationale. All five in the judgment majority share the complete opinion, so there is no need to divide authorship.

Form and governing propositions were fixed on their legal grounds before examining the assignment count or choosing an author.
