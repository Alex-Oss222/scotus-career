The clause otherwise requires arbitration in Maryland and application of Maryland law. The addenda were presented by Choice; the majority accepted the evidentiary finding that Ticknor did not negotiate the clause, while the dissent emphasized experience, alternatives and the distinct substantive duties behind the carve-outs. The actual motel is in Bozeman, Montana; Ticknor signed while in Mexico as a Colorado resident, and the guarantors lived in South Dakota. Performance and the subject property, not an invented common Montana domicile, support Montana's interest.

Montana's adopted conflict rule requires its materially greater interest and applicable law absent choice, plus a fundamental-policy conflict before overriding the chosen law. Adhesion is not dispositive. The state rule further asks whether the provision lies outside reasonable expectations or, if within them, is unduly oppressive, unconscionable or against public policy; identical obligations are not invariably required. A state label cannot insulate a disguised arbitration-specific disability. Conversely, the FAA does not displace a genuinely general and supported defense merely because its application defeats arbitration.

Casarotto, First Options and Green Tree remain governing at their exact scopes. Waffle House (January 15) applies the employment exclusion and independent agency rights, neither of which describes this commercial franchise agreement. The Court's judicial antecedent inquiry remains applicable; no actual clear delegation is established merely by the broad clause and AAA reference. Affirmance may sustain the supported general-defense application; reversal may identify reliance on mere form, asymmetry or an erroneous account of the carve-outs and require a proper conflicts-and-enforceability inquiry. No path adopts a universal invalidity rule for asymmetric commercial contracts or decides the franchise merits.
