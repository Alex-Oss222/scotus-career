## 77. Federal Maritime Commission v. South Carolina State Ports Authority

### Questions, record and distinct components

This review concerns the private Shipping Act complaint against the admitted state arm, not a Commission-initiated enforcement proceeding. The Commission's own-motion review of the ALJ's dismissal does not change the complaint's initiator. The complaint seeks reparations, cessation of alleged discrimination and Commission pursuit of an interim judicial injunction. No violation, award, waiver, officer substitution or independent enforcement action has been established.

The judgment below ordered dismissal after extending immunity to private administrative adjudication and treating Article I as incapable of sustaining this enforcement against a State. Actual Florida Prepaid and Garrett make the latter categorical premise unavailable. The two issues must therefore be separated even by a Justice who supports extending immunity to this tribunal.

The separable components are:

- **F1 — Tribunal boundary:** Whether the State's immunity from private judicial suit also bars this private complaint before the Commission.
- **F2 — Legislative authorization and power:** If immunity reaches the proceeding, whether the operative statute unmistakably authorizes the State's exposure to this particular adjudication and remedy, and whether that enforcement falls within valid enumerated power. Current law permits that inquiry; it does not settle its Shipping Act application.
- **F3 — Actual consent:** Ordinary port operations are not consent. No authorized voluntary removal, express waiver or other established submission supplies consent here.
- **F4 — Prospective-only alternative:** Whether the cease-and-desist request survives even if the monetary component encounters immunity. The government's administrative distinction must be addressed; it is not already a judicial Young claim against an identified connected officer.
- **F5 — Later proceedings:** Commission investigation on its own initiative, governmental enforcement, private judicial enforcement of nonreparation orders, private reparation collection and the two interim-injunction channels retain their separate predicates.
- **F6 — Disposition:** Reversal on the agency-boundary ground, or vacatur with a remand under the controlling legislative-authorization framework. Neither supplies liability or an award.

### Operative grounds available for joins

**Agency-boundary ground.** The Commission may adjudicate this statutory maritime complaint against the state arm without treating the administrative proceeding itself as a private judicial suit barred by sovereign immunity. Private initiation and a reparation request do not by themselves remove an adjudication from the Commission's public regulatory function: the agency determines the statutory violation, reviews the ALJ's determination and issues its own order through the enacted scheme. This conclusion does not convert the complainant into the United States, make the complaint a government-filed action or decide immunity from a later private judicial collection action.

The supported explanation is confined to this Commission process. California establishes federal authority to regulate public terminal operations and the described corrective order; it does not answer the distinct immunity issue. The distinction rests on the nature and statutory control of this federal administrative function, not merely the name of the branch, the absence of immediate execution, the political cost of noncompliance or a general denial that adjudication burdens States. Waffle House identifies the independent character of an agency's own action, but this complaint is not that action. Seminole's judicial-function holding establishes that a court's conclusive determination can remain judicial despite later remedial steps; it does not classify every executive adjudication of statutory public obligations as a private court suit. Later judicial proceedings remain subject to their own immunity, authority and remedy requirements.

**Private-compulsory-adjudication ground.** Immunity protects a State from this private litigant's compulsory adjudication before an ALJ and Commission, subject to valid displacement under the actual legislative-power rules. The pleadings, demanded response, civil discovery, adversarial hearing, determination of individual liability and personal reparation request do legal work comparable to a private suit. The agency's review and inability to execute its own order do not eliminate the adjudicative burden or the order's specified later evidentiary consequences. The conclusion is not an immunity from substantive maritime duties, federal investigation or government enforcement.

This ground does not sustain the judgment below by itself. Florida Prepaid and Garrett independently permit Congress, within enumerated power, to authorize the State's particular exposure unmistakably. A Justice adopting this ground returns the separate authorization question for consideration under that controlling rule instead of reinstating the displaced categorical Article I prohibition. That return is a decision about the scope of this review and the lower court's erroneous legal premise, not a declaration that an unavailable research document establishes a litigant's failure.

**Authorization reserved on the returned path.** General coverage and statutory exposure to a particular private proceeding are distinct. The original person and terminal definitions, the complaint and investigation provisions, the separate enforcement provisions, California's public-terminal construction and the actual statutory continuity all bear on that distinction. The clear-authorization requirement is not reduced to a demand for a single formula; nor does the word person, public-terminal coverage or federal regulatory power alone satisfy it. The lower judgment's categorical constitutional premise prevented the inquiry from doing its required work. The returned proceeding must identify the operative statute and actual relief, assess unmistakable authorization, and apply the valid Article I framework. No present affirmative or negative abrogation holding is joined by these commitments.

The source-version limit does not prevent deciding F1. The original and 1998 texts are separately supplied; the 1998 changes generally operate from May 1, 1999 with their savings clause, while the government appendix retains older cross-references. The commitments decide no version-sensitive violation, enhanced reparation, transition fact or particular award. The actual complaint and conduct dates must govern any later conclusion that depends on them. They are not invented or converted into untimeliness.

The two grounds are not logical subsets: one denies the immunity barrier here, while the other recognizes it and requires a separate authorization determination. Their shared removal of the dismissal cannot establish a common tribunal-immunity holding. Assembly must count actual proposition joins after the Chief's position enters and must preserve each remand's scope.

No proposed path depends on whether the 1941 California proceeding began privately or on agency initiative; that historical fact remains unestablished. No finding concerns every contention in the 1944 briefs. Neither uncertainty is an open disposition blocker. The statutory applicability and remedy questions deliberately left for ordinary proceedings are judicial reservations, not missing current commitments. These eight rows are complete for separate reconciliation.
