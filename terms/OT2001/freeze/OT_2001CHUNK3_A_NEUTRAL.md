Any holding must distinguish the scope of exhaustion from timeliness, procedural default within a grievance system, and substantive liability. Booth governs exhaustion of actually available and responsive procedures even when they cannot award the particular damages requested; the excessive-force category remains the issue here.

**Plausible Holding Scope, Judgment Consequences, and Uncertainty:** Affirming the transmission component would recognize federal authority over interstate service independently sold to a retail customer while preserving local distribution and retail-sale authority. Reversal would exclude that category from Order No. 888. ## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The taxpayers emphasize that the statute measures backward from the new petition and contains no express exception for the earlier stay; Congress provided extensions elsewhere. The United States argues that the rule preserves a collection opportunity that the automatic stay itself removed. The circuit accepted suspension during the prior proceeding. The Government’s strongest rule is limited to a legally disabling stay, not ordinary collection inconvenience; the taxpayers’ textual argument must explain why the Code would make its own prohibition consume the protected period.

**Plausible Holding Scope, Judgment Consequences, and Uncertainty:** Affirmance could recognize traditional tolling for the period of the prior stay and leave this debt enforceable, without finding abusive filing.
