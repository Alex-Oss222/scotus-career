## The two retained applications

The four affirming Associates accept the actual general-defense application. Nonnegotiability was supported after an evidentiary hearing; the addenda were presented by Choice and do not compel a contrary finding. In their view, reservation of the drafter's principal practical enforcement remedies, together with the required distant forum and formation circumstances, establishes oppression even though Choice must arbitrate other claims. The strongest contrary argument is that experienced franchisees made a commercial choice and the three carved-out categories reflect different underlying duties. The four give those points weight but find them insufficient on this combined record. They adopt no rule that every asymmetric arbitration clause, distant forum or commercial form contract is invalid.

The four reversing Associates conclude that the legal application gives form and unequal obligations too much work without adequately examining the actual commercial alternatives, reasonable expectations and practical effect of the limited carve-outs. They preserve supported findings rather than declaring negotiations occurred merely because addenda exist. Indemnification and trademark protection may have distinct functions; Choice remains obliged to arbitrate other contract claims. Their objection is to the application and its consequent conflicts conclusion, not to all generally applicable unconscionability defenses. The strongest contrary point is the completed hearing and the State's own recognition of general defenses in commercial transactions. The bounded return respects that evidence and law while requiring an accurate application. It does not substitute a federal preference for the State's law or order automatic arbitration of all parties.

Current Green Tree requires a substantiated actual statutory-access obstacle and a focused opportunity on its particular undeveloped cost record. Its divided general burden positions do not create a universal initial-proof allocation or discovery entitlement for every contract defense. Here actual formation and clause evidence exists; no new cost finding, binding cost allocation or federal statutory claim is invented. The prior positions remain useful personal evidence about proof and effective access, not a rule mechanically deciding this different unconscionability application.
