## Provisional result and exact scope

Each Associate supports entering a conforming decree on the submitted A–G terms and unchanged original Exhibit A at the implementation scope below. For Scalia and Thomas this is acceptance of implementation of the Court's settled judgment and the present joint proposal; it is not a new join in the 1997 federal-retention reasoning or the Range geographic determination. ### A — Counterclaim leave

Grant formal leave to file Alaska's counterclaim. The title questions have already been adjudicated; formal completion of the pleading posture does not grant a new merits hearing or reopen the 1997 decision.

### B — Fixed offshore boundary and qualified reciprocal protection

Enter the submitted continuous intersovereign line from Point Hope to the international boundary, subject to the actual agreed terms. The memorandum supports the westward extension through the parties' agreement, resolved issues and uncontroverted baseline principles; it does not supply new adjudicated historic-title findings concerning Harrison Bay, the Arco pier or other features. The normal-baseline and Dinkum rulings remain intact. No Dinkum-generated belt is restored.

Recognize the United States' seaward exploration and natural-resource rights against Alaska and persons claiming under it, with the express §1337(g) and Paragraph C exceptions. Recognize Alaska's corresponding shoreward rights against the United States and persons claiming under it, except within the identified Petroleum Reserve and Refuge, subject to §1313 and Paragraph C. The reciprocal noninterference injunctions protect these qualified rights, not an unqualified grant on either side.

Preserve §1313's actual categories, including expressly acquired or retained federal lands and interests, proprietary acquisitions and federal-use reclamation, actual federal occupation under claim of right, Indian trust interests, and navigational-servitude structures and improvements. Enter no new parcel-specific exception finding. Preserve §1337(g)'s actual revenue-sharing regime, including the Alaska partial-tract and disputed-escrow provisions, interest and payment qualifications. A statutory share is not seaward title, and this decree does not calculate a universal 27-percent payment or decide every statutory application.

### C — Lease accounting, administration and preserved rights

Maintain all three categories independently:

1. Section 7 agreement leases and their escrow are governed by the agreements. Distribution is required within 180 days after actual decree entry, with administration under the applicable agreement terms.
2. Existing nonagreement leases intersected by the fixed line remain administered by their original lessor, who retains past and future lease revenue. Paragraph B governs the area after expiration, relinquishment or termination. Fixing the line does not change distributions from former leases in this category that expired before entry.
3. Existing leases that are both covered by a Section 7 agreement and intersected by the line retain that agreement's escrow-distribution and administration rules. Paragraph B governs the area after expiration, relinquishment or termination. The fixed line does not change distributions from former intersected agreement leases that expired before entry.

Paragraph C(2) separately preserves the sovereigns' rights and obligations to lessees and third parties, whether under Section 7 agreements or otherwise, and rights and obligations under present or future unitization, operating, enhanced-recovery, commingling and similar agreements. No damages amount, cancellation, new third-party burden, present accounting dispute or expired deadline is inferred. The filing date itself starts no 180-day clock.

### D–F — Reservation coastlines and continuing change

The Petroleum Reserve description follows highest highwater westward from the western bank of the Colville River, with the specified small-lagoon and estuary closures, ocean-side barrier islands and sandspits within three miles, and Plover Islands, to Icy Cape. The Refuge description follows extreme low water from the international boundary near Monument 1 to Brownlow Point, closing the described lagoon entrances and including the specified bars, reefs, islands and separating lagoons. Those different measures remain different.

The Refuge description's reference to the 1960 order is descriptive. It does not give the order retroactive title effect, replace the legally operative 1957 segregation or use the 1980 enlargement as the original boundary. The Petroleum Reserve's identity and legally operative estate come from the settled 1997 judgment and the named boundary description, not a new finding about the number of its executive instrument. See the specific citation discrepancy below.

These reservation coastal boundaries migrate with relevant physical features. Negotiation, alternative dispute resolution and the Court's retained jurisdiction remain available; the parties may jointly submit an agreed location for a supplemental decree. Permission is not an obligation to use a particular dispute mechanism. No current movement or new survey finding is made.

### G — Finality and retained jurisdiction

Retain jurisdiction to effectuate and supplement the decree and the parties' rights. In other respects the decree is final. Retained jurisdiction is an implementation mechanism, not permission to treat settled title as undecided or to import future disputes into this event.

## Enhanced alternative-path test

**Entry path.** The recovered joint proposal supplies the implementation requested in 1997; its legal structure follows the settled baseline, island and retention rules, adds the parties' supported continuous-line settlement, and preserves statutory and contractual exceptions. Section 1301(b) authorizes the legal effect of coordinate fixation. The three lease categories address the consequences of the fixed line without altering the title entitlement. The strongest objection is that agreement alone cannot authorize a decree inconsistent with the judgment or statutes, and an unverified coordinate should not be declared a new judicial survey. The answer is a conformity-based entry, incorporating the original submitted schedule without claiming independent surveying or modifying a coordinate. The packet identifies no concrete legal or numerical departure requiring a different line.

**Withhold or require correction path.** A materially nonconforming reservation, a changed Dinkum entitlement, an unsupported title allocation, an altered coordinate or an unqualified revenue/lease provision would justify withholding the affected provision and requiring a conforming proposal. This is a lawful alternative, not an invented present objection. Its strongest difficulty on the supplied record is the absence of such a substantive departure after the clause-specific check. The citation-number conflict described below does not identify competing land or a competing boundary. It therefore does not justify reopening title or refusing all implementation. If further work reveals a real geographic ambiguity, only that affected commitment must return for neutral correction and renewed modeling.

**Renewed-title-objection path for Scalia and Thomas.** Their 1997 separate writing rejected inferred express retention of the Petroleum Reserve submerged estate under the Submerged Lands Act and rejected treating a still-pending Refuge application as an established refuge. Their acceptance of the decree mechanism was expressly subject to those title positions. Those reasons remain their published legal view, and agreement with implementation cannot be counted as a newly adopted federal-title holding. The strongest ground for renewing partial opposition is that Paragraphs B, D and E give enforceable form to the title outcome they rejected.

**Implementation-with-reservation path, provisionally selected by each.** The present proceeding asks whether the joint terms implement the now-settled judgment; it does not present the old exceptions for redetermination. The prior Court directed a decree, the sovereigns now submit conforming terms jointly, and no newly adverse title contention or specific conformity defect is established. Accepting entry in that procedural setting does not require withdrawing either Justice's merits analysis or supplying a new geographic assent. This is a case-specific inference from their accepted implementing mechanism, the final operative judgment and the new joint posture, not a rule that dissenters always join later decrees. No new published dissent is necessary to preserve their prior position, and none is invented here.
