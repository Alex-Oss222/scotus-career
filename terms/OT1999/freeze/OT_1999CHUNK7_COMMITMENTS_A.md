## Statutory-source completion for Vermont Agency

The [official 1994 §3729 text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-1994-title31/html/USCODE-1994-title31-subtitleIII-chap37-subchapIII-sec3729.htm) confirms the cooperation exception already preserved above. Its full operative conditions are these: the violator furnishes all known violation information to the United States officials responsible for investigating false claims within thirty days of first obtaining it; fully cooperates with any Government investigation of that violation; and, when furnishing the information, no criminal prosecution, civil action or administrative action under the title concerning the violation has begun and the person has no actual knowledge of an investigation into it. If the court finds those conditions, it may assess damages of no less than twice the Government's loss. The statute separately makes the violator liable for costs of the civil action to recover the penalty or damages. This is a source-specific completion of the earlier preserved exception, not a changed commitment, eligibility finding or award. No long quotation is transcribed.

**Term-effective source limit:** Original Rule 103(a)'s substantial-right, timely-objection and specific-ground requirements govern. The definitive-ruling/no-renewal amendment effective December 1, 2000 supplies no rule on this date. Rule 609(a)(1)'s accused-specific requirement is that qualifying felony impeachment's probative value outweigh its prejudicial effect to the accused; its other-witness and dishonesty provisions remain separate. The 1990 removal of the cross-examination-only restriction permits direct disclosure but does not expressly resolve the effect on appellate review.

**Frozen disposition division:** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas affirm on the self-introduction ground. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer reverse that preservation ruling and remand for ordinary review of admissibility and any harmless error. The latter position supplies no finding of evidentiary error, reversible prejudice, automatic new trial or vacatur of the trial convictions. The former does not approve the unreviewed Rule 609 balance.

**Adversarial test and exact disagreement:** The affirmance path rests on a recognized distinction between receiving adverse evidence over objection and choosing to offer that evidence oneself. Its strongest point is that the prosecution might have withheld impeachment, while the defense's first disclosure changes the trial and secures a tactical advantage. Its four supporters treat intentional introduction as the relevant surrender of the exclusion claim, without inventing an express appellate-waiver colloquy or invoking plain-error review for mere inaction. They distinguish Martinez-Salazar because its retained cause ruling and absence of peremptory deprivation did not decide appellate treatment of self-introduced evidence; they distinguish Old Chief because an offered adequate alternative did not itself put the contested prejudicial conviction details before the jury.

The reversal path rests on the preserved objection to a definitive judicial ruling and the difference between mitigating expected damage and approving that ruling. Its strongest answer to trial uncertainty is that Ohler actually testified, the conviction actually reached the jury and the prosecutor actually used the felony characterization, so Luce's absent testimony/possible impeachment problem is materially reduced. Remaining uncertainty belongs to the ordinary review of the actual record. These four do not read Olano as forbidding all waiver by conduct; they find this defensive conduct inconsistent with inferring abandonment of the already asserted objection. Neither side treats the prosecutor's felony question as proof of every unasked contingency.

**Common boundaries:** All eight preserve Luce's holding for a defendant who never testifies; distinguish this case's actual testimony; retain Rule 609's actual balancing requirement without deciding it; and reject reliance on the later Rule 103 amendment. Hill's express scheduling agreement creates no general waiver presumption. Martinez-Salazar leaves the initial cause error intact and establishes no universal compelled-choice doctrine. Old Chief retains its status-only adequate-substitute scope and its independent nonconstitutional harmlessness rule. No Justice adopts a new trial solely from preservation, invents an express abandonment, or decides unrelated constitutional claims. Research cutoff: May 22, 2000.
