# Carter — form before assignment

Before fresh counts or author selection, one signed opinion will resolve the plenary Rule 31(c) statutory appeal. The integrated majority ground is that the charged completed-taking branch of §2113(a) does not require §2113(b)'s separate intent to steal or purloin and carrying-away requirements; each independently defeats inclusion of either larceny grade. Value exceeding $1,000 is an additional element independently defeating the requested felony subset. The opinion applies legally construed elements, preserves appropriate knowledge for the robbery offense, excludes the entry paragraph and unpresented configurations, and leaves the conviction intact without deciding disputed force evidence or a separate indictment theory.

Stone, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support this full ground. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer agree on felony value but support basic-inclusion and a bounded return for request scope and rational-jury entitlement. They will issue one common dissent that states their common-law/contextual construction, basic-grade operation, unresolved request and evidence conditions, and reservation of an automatic higher-grade election. Agreement on value will be recorded as an express join in that severable proposition, without converting the dissenters into judgment supporters or full-lead author candidates. The sections reflect genuine different proposition coalitions; no author division is necessary because five Justices support the integrated lead in full.

The choice of signed form, substantive propositions and common dissent precedes counts and reflects the legal controversy alone. No historical authorship or fictional circulation is used.
