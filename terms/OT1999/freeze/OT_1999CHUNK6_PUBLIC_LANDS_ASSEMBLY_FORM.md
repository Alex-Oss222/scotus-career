# Public Lands Council — form before count

One signed opinion after plenary review addresses the three distinct facial statutory questions in separate holdings, with one common nine-Justice judgment and full-join coalition. Their common Act and regulatory scheme warrant one opinion; their distinct qualifications require separate holdings, not separate assignments. No free-standing deference or general rangeland-policy holding is added.

Stone's priority and prospective-title positions support the corresponding common grounds. His eligibility position is expressly subject to lawful qualifications and reserves arrangements evading statutory eligibility. It does not require the Court to grant a permit to an ineligible stock-free applicant. He may join the actual deletion of the commercial-business requirement and the retained statutory categories in full. A concurrence preserves his additional approved point about personal livestock ownership at every moment only for an otherwise lawfully eligible applicant under actual program conditions, and refuses paper permits disconnected from grazing. The Court does not adopt that point as an unrestricted eligibility rule. This preserves rather than substantively narrows his approved qualifications. No fallback is used.

O'Connor and Thomas's common emphasis on real active/suspended-use priority, later applications and the unpresented APA question supports a concurrence with full Court joins. No Associate commitment changes and no fracture is manufactured. The Chief belongs to all three judgment coalitions and assigns one opinion. These form and scope choices precede count and author selection.
