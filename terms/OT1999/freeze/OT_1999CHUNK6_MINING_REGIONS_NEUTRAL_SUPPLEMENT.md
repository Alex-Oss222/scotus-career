## Mining's actual rules

Ordinary §510(c) permit blocking requires actual current ownership/control, including qualifying downstream control, and a current violation. The separately authorized former-control route requires every stated willfulness, pattern, nature, duration, irreparable-damage and intent condition. Anti-evasion policy cannot erase those requirements. The three-year notice schedule does not supply a universal age cutoff; satisfactory correction in progress can suffice, so completed correction is not invariably required.

The limited temporal holding concerns present eligibility for prospective operations on actual current control and a current violation. The antecedent dates of both acquisition and violation do not alone defeat that application. A present application date or current label cannot sanitize a new disability resting solely on completed conduct. Smiley's qualified use of authoritative interpretation where clear contrary guidance is absent does not backdate a regulation or displace Bowen's limit on retroactive legislative rules. The holding does not establish that nobody relied on anything, decide displacement of clear official guidance, or settle a particular application. The common ground does not merge the distinct earlier Landgraf approaches.

The correction holding rests on express suspension, revocation and withholding authority, with lawful substantive/temporal predicates and the applicable state-primacy procedures. It does not adopt an unlimited implied reopening power or determine the force of a particular final adjudication. The imminent-danger procedure remains distinct from ordinary notice, abatement opportunity, hearing opportunity, written finding and cessation-order requirements. Ungranted control presumptions, burden rules, section 2462 and the separate procedural litigation were not newly decided.

## Regions: Fischer's program context and individual public positions

Regions permits correction of the lawful 1984 graduate-medical-education base for later formula payments under the statute at issue, while preserving finality of the closed 1984 payment and each independently closed later determination. The Court's operative permission and temporal/finality holding have six adherents; its construction/deference, reasonableness and preclusion explanations have the separately identified five-Justice support. The partial joins cannot be collapsed into a single nine-Justice endorsement of general Medicare discretion.

Its majority explains a structured program in which allowable cost data and sustained institutional reimbursement matter, but that program account is not a holding that every provider receipt is a criminal-statute benefit. It creates no proof that West Volusia received special GME or accelerated payments. Good Samaritan, Guernsey and the exact program record retain their respective roles.

Scalia's dissent, joined by O'Connor and Thomas, argues that Congress selected the cost amount previously recognized through the existing machinery, so administrative accuracy cannot authorize a new base determination beyond the statute. That is a text-and-delegation position about a particular formula. It is not a rejection of Medicare's institutional purpose, a provider-benefits classification under §666 or a current Fischer vote. The majority's contrary construction likewise supplies no automatic Fischer vote. Each Justice's actual writing must be connected to the different statutory phrase and proved program facts.
