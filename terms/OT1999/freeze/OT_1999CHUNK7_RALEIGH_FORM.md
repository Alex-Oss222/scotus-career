# Raleigh — form before assignment

One signed plenary opinion resolves the actual source-of-persuasion question, distinguishing production and prima-facie effect from ultimate persuasion and preserving express federal changes, state elements and the limited appellate remedy. All nine support the complete rule and affirmance; no separate writing or artificial part is needed. The opinion does not determine an amount or reopen the lower court's separate tax, timeliness and subordination issues. Form and propositions are fixed before count or author selection.
