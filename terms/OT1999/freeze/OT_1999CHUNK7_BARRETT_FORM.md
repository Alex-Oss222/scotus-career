# Barrett — form before assignment

One signed plenary lead resolves the reviewed ordinary collateral judgment. Part I distinguishes that judgment from the unreviewable authorization refusal and rejects the savings-clause route for an available but omitted claim. All nine support that ground. Part II independently rejects the residual-writ substitute under section 1651; the eight Associates join its ground, while Stone supports that component's disposition under his standing fallback without adding a reason. The statutory routes, not counts, require this scope distinction. Stone publishes a concurrence in part and in the judgment to state his affirmative intervening-statutory-innocence rule, which the eight Associates reserve. These forms and propositions are fixed before counting; no artificial split or shared authorship is needed because each Associate can deliver the entire lead.
