# Henerey - form and governing propositions before assignment

A single signed plenary Court opinion is warranted by the actual constitutional question and common grounds. Parts I-II state the supervised-election Hazelwood framework and permissibility of genuine, clear, viewpoint-neutral prior-approval conditions for this campaign. Those propositions have all nine joins. Part III applies the actual-ground and summary-judgment rule, sustaining the process enforcement and reasonable disqualification on this record, without the appellate sexual-content alternative or a blanket validation of the complete policy. Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas join that part and affirm; Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would vacate and remand, joining Parts I-II and dissenting from III.

The scope and partial-join form are fixed on legal grounds before counts. Each of the five affirmers can carry the complete Court opinion; no division of authorship is needed. No Justice changes a reconciled commitment. Stone's approved insistence on viewpoint-neutral campaign administration and no pretext is implemented as a case-specific condition, not expanded into a universal rule for all curricular content choices.
