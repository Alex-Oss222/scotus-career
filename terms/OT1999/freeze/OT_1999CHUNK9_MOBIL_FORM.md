# Mobil / Marathon - form before count

The two dockets arise from the same lease-process dispute and receive one signed plenary Court opinion, with docket-specific restitution and amount proceedings preserved. Part I states the actual clear continuing undertaking, incorporated conditions, reserved powers and breach of the submitted-plan approval obligation; all nine join. Parts II-V state material repudiation of that process, the fifth-lease anticipatory ground, restitution distinct from expectation damages, rejection of the supplied election defense, and bounded restoration/amount remand; eight join, Stevens dissenting. One of the eight full supporters can carry every proposition, so no divided authorship is warranted. These form and scope decisions precede count consultation.

The full coalition is Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Their common clear-undertaking and actual allocated-risk ground leaves the competing noncontrolling Winstar canon explanations unnecessary. No unresolved necessary join or new accommodation requires expansion, and no reconciled commitment changes. Stone's approved restitution instruction includes proved payments, lawful credits/offsets and independently authorized interest, not an immediate rounded sum or an expectation-profit award.
