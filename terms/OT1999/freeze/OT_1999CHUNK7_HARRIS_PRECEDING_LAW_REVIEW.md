# Harris Trust: June 5 law refresh before June 12 assembly

The [five June 5 public decisions](../entering-law/OT_1999CHUNK7_HARRIS_EFFECTIVE_JUNE5_PUBLIC.md) are mechanically copied from the actual completed Records and supplement the [six earlier decisions](../entering-law/OT_1999CHUNK7_B_EFFECTIVE_A_PUBLIC.md). The assembly context authored and validated the former five and applies their actual public holdings, not predicted outcomes or private grounds. This refresh precedes the Harris assignment and Record.

Castillo's former-firearm classification and ordinary element consequences do not establish a general statutory defendant rule. Mixon's bounded section 2 coverage, genuine-appointment and defendant-specific immunity holdings do not decide ERISA transferee eligibility, violation or remedy. Sims's actual Appeals Council notice/form review rule neither adjudicates an ERISA forfeiture nor changes the distinction between an existing claim and a supporting legal argument. Troxel's shared parental-care and anti-substitution propositions, its fractured further standards and its case-specific dismissal do not supply an ERISA equitable category. Hubbell applies an express immunity scheme and Kastigar's established government-borne independent-source burden; it does not allocate the purchaser-for-value defense reserved in Harris.

Hartford's earlier named-plaintiff construction remains recognized: section 506(c) designates the trustee, while section 502(a)(3) identifies authorized plaintiffs without a categorical fiduciary-only defendant class. Raleigh preserves the substantive tax burden absent a federal change and does not resolve the independent traditional purchaser-defense allocation here. No new majority holding concerning ERISA remedy adequacy, compensation, nonfiduciary transferees or tracing has intervened.

The actual new law changes no material frozen factual, legal, remedial or Stone-core premise in Harris. Fresh modeling or reconciliation is therefore unnecessary. The purchaser-for-value notice qualification, actual statutory-violation requirement, traditional-equity limit, unresolved purchaser-defense burden and ordinary remand remain intact. None of the prior decisions supplies the missing transaction, notice, exemption or recoverable-property findings.
