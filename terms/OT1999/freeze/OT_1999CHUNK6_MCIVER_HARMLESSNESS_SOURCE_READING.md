Chapman supplies the beneficiary's burden beyond a reasonable doubt that the constitutional error did not contribute to the verdict. Stewart's separate opinion disputes applying a uniform harmlessness approach and specifically distinguishes potentially different Fourth Amendment considerations; Harlan's dissent proposes a state-procedure/federalism approach.
