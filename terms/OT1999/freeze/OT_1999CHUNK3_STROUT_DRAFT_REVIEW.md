## Corrections before finalization

1. **Retain the student-transfer condition in section 2951.** The draft repeatedly reduces the final condition to requested transfer of records. The supplied statute requires, upon a school unit's request, release of copies of all student records **for students transferring from the private school to that school unit**. A request alone does not trigger transfer of every pupil's records. State the transferring-student condition in the public Event and corresponding internal statutory summaries.

2. **Complete the statutory summary where it purports to enumerate conditions.** The assessment calculation is the **previous year's October and April average enrollment**, not an unspecified preceding pair of months. Section 5204(4)'s actual alternatives are an approved private school, a public school in an adjoining unit that accepts tuition students, or a school approved for tuition in another state or country, subject to the receiving-school permission language. Replace the opaque reference to “its listed receiving schools” with these alternatives. Preserve the resident-unit/no-school/no-contract predicate and mandatory payment up to the legal rate. The frozen appendix already supplies all these terms; no new research or neutral premise is required.

3. **Preserve the first granted question's asserted discrimination.** The public Event should expressly retain that the challenged exclusion affects otherwise eligible sectarian schools **and their students**, from a generally available program, **solely because the schools are religious**. The draft's shorter formulation obscures the allegation the funded-function answer addresses. The second question should retain independent parental choice. This is clarification of the existing grant, not enlargement of it.
