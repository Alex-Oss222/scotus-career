## Procedure, individual assistance and remedy reconciliation

The independent procedure-valid group gives constitutional significance to the complete Wende arrangement: counsel's review and cited narrative, communication and notice, continued appointment and availability, personal submission, full judicial record review, and required briefing if a nonfrivolous issue is found. These four do not deem judicial review alone a substitute for counsel or excuse a lawyer's unreasonable failure to identify an available nonfrivolous ground. Their distinction between this retained-counsel process and Penson's denial of counsel supports an individual Strickland inquiry, not automatic relief or automatic rejection of Robbins's claim.

The independent procedure-invalid group identifies a missing function: counsel must provide meaningful legal assistance that allows the tribunal to evaluate the diligent search for possible grounds and the conclusion of frivolousness. A factual narrative and availability after the court discovers an issue do not, for these four, furnish an equivalent means of testing that conclusion. They acknowledge counsel's real work and continued appointment; their constructive-denial analysis concerns the asserted absence of the advocate's legal-screening function, not literal withdrawal or a claim that counsel performed no work at all. Neither exact Anders wording nor merits advocacy for frivolous grounds is required.

The preservation supplement controls the original freeze's overstatement concerning a presently established nonfrivolous appeal. The State's library-preservation argument is unresolved, and the two possible grounds have no established probability of success. The counsel-waiver-withdrawal ground is separate. For the procedure-invalid four, restoration of adequate screening is itself the corrective process: requiring Robbins first to establish the available issue that the missing process should identify would defeat that function. Anders's restoration of adequate consideration supplies the direct remedial basis; Penson and Strickland/Cronic distinguish actual or constructive loss of necessary assistance from deficient performance within an otherwise supplied function. This reconciles the frozen remedy with the historical dissents without importing Penson's additional fact of an actually identified arguable appeal.

For the procedure-valid four, reverse the existing categorical appellate-counsel basis and remand for ordinary performance/prejudice adjudication and the unresolved trial claims. No adequate-performance finding or no-prejudice finding follows now. For the procedure-invalid four, preserve conditional adequate appellate screening if relief on the eleven remaining trial claims does not make it unnecessary. Proper screening may still establish that every available issue is frivolous. Neither group directs a winning appeal, compulsory briefing of an unavailable claim, immediate release, a new thirty-day clock or a mandatory evidentiary hearing.
