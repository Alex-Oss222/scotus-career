## Common reach, components and source limits

All eight Associates reach the presented federal speech and association question through certiorari. No supported jurisdictional, preservation, finality or participation obstacle warrants a threshold dismissal. New Jersey's construction of its own statute controls coverage: BSA is a public accommodation; the distinctly-private, religious-educational-facility and parental/in-loco-parentis exceptions do not apply; adult leadership is a statutory privilege or advantage. Those state-law conclusions do not decide the federal expressive burden. No definitive waiver follows from the state court's discussion preceding its merits ruling.

The components are (1) the existence and scope of protected expressive activity, (2) whether compelled retention in this adult teaching role materially burdens that activity, (3) the State's justification for any established burden, (4) intimate association as a distinct possible ground, and (5) the disposition and permissible relief. The same formal judgment need not imply agreement on all propositions. Neither BSA's federal charter nor public assistance alone establishes state action. No Associate would revive the dismissed common-law claim or award an unestablished damages amount.

Hurley supplies independent First Amendment review, with credibility deference, and protection for a private expressive composition without demanding a single theme or unanimity. It also expressly preserves ordinary identity-based access law where compelled expression is not materially established. Jones protects an association's central selection choice, but its official-nomination injury is not a categorical rule governing every association's admission decision. Each Associate's own Hurley join and Jones position is the immediate date-eligible public foundation below. O'Connor's identified Roberts concurrence additionally supports her distinction between instruction of the young and commercial activity; no unsupplied quotation or additional proposition from it is adopted.

## Justice-specific enhanced commitments

### Stevens

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Affirm the rejection of the federal objection to the LAD's application to Dale's adult position. BSA undertakes protected moral education, but this record does not establish that Dale's retention, without contrary Scouting advocacy, materially alters expression attributable to the organization. Broad recruitment alone does not remove constitutional protection; the decisive failure is the connection between the asserted exclusion and the expression actually conducted. His Hurley join protects demonstrably expressive composition and reserves ordinary access. His Jones dissent distinguishes governmental access rules from actual interference with private leadership or advocacy; it does not deny the latter protection when established.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Role-specific reversal has force because an assistant scoutmaster teaches by example and a court may not replace BSA's values with its own. Nevertheless the ordinary program's silence about homosexuality, the referral of sex education to parents and advisers, diversity of sponsors, and the absence of a proposed contrary message in Scouting prevent an assertion of exclusion from proving its own expressive necessity. The strongest objection to affirmance is that positive moral teaching need not enumerate forbidden conduct. He accepts that proposition, but does not treat it as proof that the existing general phrases communicate this particular exclusion through every adult leader's identity.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join affirmance grounded in the absence of a demonstrated material expressive alteration and the State's concrete equal-access interest. Will not join judicial approval or disapproval of BSA's moral beliefs, an absolute requirement of explicit public advocacy, or a rule that publicly known identity can never affect expression. The supplied size and openness defeat a separate intimate-association exemption; individual troop relationships do not establish a different judgment here. Ordinary state proceedings on remand remain available without a new damages award. Will write or join a separate position explaining the record-based distinction from Hurley if the Court reverses; no additional independent writing is necessary if that distinction is fully stated.

### O'Connor

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Reverse the judgment insofar as it compels BSA to retain Dale as an assistant scoutmaster and remand for relief consistent with that role-specific constitutional limit. Instruction of children through chosen adult example is genuine expressive activity. The oath, law, adult declaration, official statements and the role's instructional responsibilities sufficiently connect BSA's asserted moral position to this leadership choice, even though its youth materials do not catalogue the position. Her Roberts concurrence's treatment of education and quiet persuasion, her Hurley join, and her Jones opinion protecting central association choices supply the grounds.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Affirmance would preserve important equal access where a large organization's claimed message is only a conclusory exclusion. Its strongest evidence is the absence of homosexuality from ordinary materials and Dale's lack of a plan to advocate within Scouting. Those matters require genuine scrutiny, but they do not eliminate the independently supported teaching-by-example function or authorize a court to narrow the association's expression to public verbal instruction. The strongest objection to reversal is that an organization could manufacture immunity by labeling every exclusion expressive. Her answer confines the rule to this established moral-instruction role and record, rather than ordinary membership, employment, customers or goods.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a role-specific expressive-association holding requiring a demonstrated material burden and treating the State's access interest as important but insufficient to compel this expressive leadership selection. Will not join automatic deference to any asserted exclusion, a general exemption for public accommodations, or a rule equating status alone with a message. Jones supports central selection autonomy without resolving the separate evidentiary inquiry here. No intimate-association ground is necessary or adopted. Willing to join a complete opinion carrying these qualifications; a concurrence is needed only if the lead rationale omits them. No membership-only restoration or damages resolution is imposed without a supported separate basis.

### Scalia

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Reverse the compelled adult-leadership application and remand. BSA's moral instruction through adult example is protected expression, and the State cannot prescribe who must carry that instruction when the organization's supported view is that the appointment contradicts its values. His full Hurley and Jones joins protect the association's own expressive composition and central representative choice. The legal ground is compelled alteration of demonstrated expression, not an assessment of the merits of the moral view.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Affirmance could follow if the policy is an unsupported status exclusion unrelated to expression; Hurley itself preserves that category. The strongest such argument is that Dale would neither speak against BSA's values within Scouting nor be identified in the newspaper as its representative. It does not answer the distinct claim that BSA selects adults to exemplify its moral instruction. Reversal's strongest difficulty is that acceptance of every self-description would extinguish ordinary antidiscrimination law. The record's actual educational activity and this particular adult responsibility supply the limiting connection; the organization's assertion alone is not the whole proof.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join protection for this compelled appointment without requiring that BSA repeatedly announce an anti-homosexuality message or secure agreement from every member. Will not join a rationale allowing courts to reject a supported expressive claim because they find the values inconsistent or insufficiently publicized. The antidiscrimination interest cannot justify compelling this identified expressive role. The decision need not rest on intimate association, and it does not overturn New Jersey's statutory construction. Willing to join a suitably stated common opinion; no separate writing is required merely to restate it. Relief is confined to the unconstitutional application, with no factual declaration about every member or future claimant.

### Kennedy

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Reverse the compelled-leadership application and remand. His Hurley join and Jones concurrence distinguish an association's voluntary development of its own message and constituency from state alteration of the people entrusted to express it. An adult scoutmaster's teaching by example provides the connection that an ordinary customer's identity would lack. Protecting that choice neither approves the exclusion nor diminishes Dale's own freedom to speak and associate.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Affirmance has force because protecting a person's status from exclusion need not require the group to endorse his views. The record nonetheless makes this a teaching appointment, not merely public access or an audience member's presence. The strongest objection to reversal is the danger that vague moral phrases become a universal license to discriminate. He would require the actual educational undertaking, leadership role and supported organizational position present here, rather than a generalized declaration of expressive purpose. The absence of planned contrary instruction does not fully remove the communicative significance of selecting an adult exemplar.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a role-specific material-burden analysis and rejection of the asserted justification for this compulsion. Will not join a broad rule of group sovereignty over all regulated relationships, a claim that gay identity always conveys a political message, or a holding against ordinary nondiscrimination laws. No intimate-association or state-action ruling is needed. Willing to join a complete common opinion; a separate explanation may emphasize freedom to change voluntarily and the distinct freedom of the excluded individual, but is unnecessary if the lead opinion already does so. Remand must remove the unconstitutional leadership compulsion without inventing a membership-only entitlement.

### Souter

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Affirm the rejection of BSA's federal challenge on this record. His own Hurley opinion requires identification of what expression is compelled and preserves identity-based access when the material expressive alteration is missing. BSA's educational activity qualifies as expression, but the further claim that retaining this adult necessarily alters its message is not sufficiently established by the policy statements and broad moral terms when considered with the program, sex-education instructions and Dale's proposed activity. His Jones join involved an established transfer of decision-making power over the official representative; it does not remove Hurley's threshold inquiry here.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Reversal is supported by the particular adult's teaching-by-example role, which makes the case stronger than an ordinary customer-access dispute. Yet selecting an instructor is not invariably selecting every message associated with that person's identity. BSA's supported power to insist on its actual teaching remains distinct from a demonstrated need to exclude this instructor for the fact presented. The strongest objection to affirmance is that courts must not demand a narrow, consistent or publicly repeated message. He disclaims all such requirements: the problem is attribution and material impairment on the actual record, not insufficient ideological consistency.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a record-based affirmance preserving the protection of real expressive leadership choices. Will not join automatic deference to BSA's legal characterization, nor an absolute rule that protected expression must be explicit or public. The state court's constitutional characterizations require independent review and are not adopted merely because it made them. No separate intimate-association exemption follows from the supplied openness and scale. Willing to explain the precise Hurley distinction in a separate writing if needed; willing to join another writing that states it accurately. Existing state remand continues, without a new liability amount or an invented membership-only remedy.

### Thomas

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Reverse the compelled adult-leadership application and remand. His Hurley and Jones joins protect the selection of those who constitute an organization's own expressive activity. BSA's moral education and the assistant scoutmaster's function establish more than the identity of a recipient of services. The supported institutional view, coupled with teaching by example, makes compulsory retention a material expressive burden.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Affirmance could follow under the surviving Roberts access principle if the group merely asserted a policy without connecting it to an expressive function. The strongest evidence for that path is the absence of a specific instruction about homosexuality in the ordinary materials and Dale's absence of contrary Scouting advocacy. Neither requires treating adult example as nonexpressive; a group may convey positive standards without spelling out every exclusion. The strongest objection to reversal is a limitless exemption based on the association's own label. He confines the holding to the evidenced moral-instruction undertaking and this leadership role, not all asserted expressive affiliations.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the material-burden and justification analysis described above and the narrow remedial consequence. Will not join a rule allowing government to define BSA's moral meaning, or one requiring members' unanimous agreement before expression receives protection. No broader intimate-association ruling is needed. Willing to join a common opinion that carries the substantive protection without an unsupported universal exemption. No independent concurrence is required. Statutory coverage and the three statutory exceptions remain as construed by New Jersey.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Affirm the rejection of the constitutional objection to retaining Dale in the presented adult position. Her Hurley join protects actual expressive composition; her Jones dissent preserves contextual examination of the asserted injury and governmental access interests. BSA's educational character does not automatically establish that this adult's status changes its expression. The ordinary program, its treatment of sex education, institutional diversity and lack of proposed contrary Scouting advocacy support application of the access law without materially directing the organization's speech.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Role-specific reversal correctly emphasizes that an instructor may speak through example and that neutrality in statutory form does not cure compelled expression. But the constitutional issue remains whether this particular inclusion carries the claimed message, not whether instructors can ever embody an organization's principles. Reversal's strongest difficulty is that the exclusion itself would become the evidence making it constitutionally immune. The strongest objection to affirmance is that it undervalues nonverbal moral education; she accepts that mode of expression while rejecting the proposed automatic inference about this person's retention.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a record-specific absence-of-material-burden rationale, without declaring public associations incapable of protected expression or requiring an express denunciation of each disfavored practice. If a real material burden were established, a separate justification analysis would be necessary; generalized invocation of equality would not mechanically dispose of it. No intimate-association exemption is established. Will join a substantive separate position if the Court reverses; no additional writing is needed if another opinion fully states the distinction. Preserve the state remand and remedy limits; do not treat public assistance as a finding of state action.

### Breyer

**Commitment and sufficient ground.** Affirm the rejection of the federal objection on this record. His Hurley and Jones joins protect a materially demonstrated loss of control over expression or central representative selection. Jones's compulsory participation directly changed who made the party's official choice. Here the supplied evidence does not sufficiently establish that retaining Dale changes the content or control of BSA's teaching, rather than protects access to an existing role subject to its legitimate instructional requirements. The State's concrete antidiscrimination interest is therefore sufficient for the burden actually established.

**Alternative and strongest counterarguments.** Reversal offers substantial protection against government choosing an association's moral teachers. Its strongest point is that leadership example need not take verbal form. But that general point does not decide the degree of expressive interference produced by this person's presence under this program. The strongest objection to affirmance is that requiring proof of altered expression can force an association to surrender its own understanding of its message. The inquiry here accepts BSA's right to choose its values and examines the claimed effect of the compelled appointment in the supported record, rather than deciding whether those values deserve approval.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join affirmance that addresses the particular instructional role and does not rest on BSA's size alone, a public-speech requirement, or a universal equation of identity with silence. Will not join a mechanical balancing rule that dilutes Jones's severe-burden protection or Hurley's expressive-composition holding once their material triggers are satisfied. No intimate-association disposition is independently necessary, and the supplied facts do not establish that exemption. Willing to join a separate explanation of these distinctions if needed; no automatic additional writing. Allow the existing state remand, without ordering an unverified membership-only compromise.
