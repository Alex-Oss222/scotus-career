## Common issue, rule and remedy

All eight Associates support granting certiorari for summary correction, reversing the appellate mootness judgment and remanding for the remaining live issues. Their common sufficient ground is that this record does not establish elimination of the challenged competitive disadvantage: Adarand remains able and ready to bid with continuing exposure to the incentive, while the offered state certificate's federal acceptance and durable effect remain unsettled. An existing certificate does not by itself resolve those questions. This is a reasoned threshold disposition, not a merits ruling about racial classifications or a pure discretionary GVR.

The June 12, 1995 Adarand v. Peña standing holding, joined by every Associate, supplies equal-opportunity injury without proof of a lost award and preserves concrete, sufficiently imminent renewed exposure for prospective relief. Northeastern Florida (June 14, 1993), authored by Thomas and joined by Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy and Souter among the current Associates, distinguishes an alteration continuing the challenged injury from a change actually ending it. Its application of City of Mesquite supplies the surviving voluntary-cessation principle. The proponent of mootness must adequately establish that the challenged disadvantage will not recur; this does not relieve a plaintiff of initial standing or stage-appropriate proof. Lesage (November 29, 1999), unanimously joined, preserves the distinction between unequal-process injury and entitlement to particular relief, and requires concrete continuing or imminent exposure for prospective relief.

DOT has not approved Colorado's altered certification procedure. Colorado accepts self-certification without investigation, while the federal individual-disadvantage route requires an individual determination. The federal government does not endorse this particular certification and reserves review if a third-party challenge reaches DOT. DOT may require withdrawal proceedings upon reasonable cause to believe federal eligibility criteria are unmet. These facts prevent treating the certificate as established, durable removal of the disadvantage. They do not establish an actual third-party challenge, a withdrawal order, present ineligibility, inevitable revocation, or unconstitutional administration.

The existing June 12, 1995 vacatur and bounded remand remain operative. The common substantial-justification standard governs remaining constitutional questions; this threshold decision neither readopts nor reopens that standard. Reversal removes the appellate mootness disposition as the reason for terminating the controversy and vacating the favorable district judgment. It does not affirm the district judgment's merits or automatically approve or reinstate every element of its disputed relief. The lower court must address remaining preserved issues and the decree's lawful effect under ordinary procedure and the existing mandate. No new complaint, injunction, damages, subcontract award, program redesign or special evidentiary procedure is ordered.

## Material alternative paths

**Return for live issues — adopted.** The strongest counterargument is that Adarand already holds the very eligibility it sought; a merely possible future administrative challenge cannot supply imminent injury. The response is confined to this record: initial competitive injury and continuing bidding exposure are established premises, and the federal defendant relies on a certificate whose governing procedures it has not approved and whose correctness it expressly leaves unresolved. The holding does not infer an imminent withdrawal from the existence of review machinery alone. It finds the offered cessation showing inadequate to end this already established controversy.

**Sustain mootness — rejected on this record.** This would be lawful if the certificate or another supported change actually and adequately eliminated the prospective competitive disadvantage. Its strongest objection here is the unresolved mismatch between the state certificate and federal recognition, alongside continuing exposure to the challenged program. No finding or binding federal acceptance supplied in the packet resolves that mismatch. Nominal status alone cannot carry the result. Conversely, not every revocable license or reviewable certificate necessarily leaves a case live; an adequately supported future mootness determination remains possible.

**Vacate solely for additional factfinding — unnecessary.** A narrower return could be appropriate if an unknown fact prevented decision of the current mootness basis. Here the admitted reservations suffice to reject that basis without deciding the certificate invalid or predicting its fate. Ordinary further proceedings remain available for genuinely unresolved issues; no additional hearing or factfinding command follows merely from unavailable research materials.
