## Commerce: common grounds and the precise precedent disagreement

### Four supporting affirmance

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas retain the actual Lopez ground. The class is private gender-motivated violence; its serious effects through injury, curtailed travel, employment, treatment, consumption and productivity establish neither a market-directed statutory object nor another independently eligible mechanism. Their conclusion does not deny the findings' empirical force. It distinguishes the existence and magnitude of economic consequences from the constitutional relationship of the enacted class to interstate commerce.

The strongest contrary argument is that widespread gender violence forms a concrete barrier to economic participation, rather than merely producing isolated crime costs. The four answer at the enacted class: section 13981 covers the specified assault whether or not it is directed at provision or receipt of a commercial service or participation in a market. American Life League and Bird concern force and obstruction whose object and operation directly impair actual medical services. Robinson concerns operating business assets and its actual commerce element. Condon concerns the identified interstate information-disclosure market. The economic aspect of a selected example does not replace the class Congress enacted.

The Necessary and Proper alternative also fails on this supported record. Hale, Kirk and the recipient/supply decisions sustain a class whose exclusion would enable evasion or materially impair an identified valid interstate regime. Gillespie's domestic-violence predicate did not create a general violence power; the supply/recipient relationship supplied its connection. The surrounding Act's breadth does not itself show that this private civil claim is necessary to the operation of a specified interstate restriction. No anti-evasion necessity is invented.

Home Builders supplies an independent noneconomic route, which O'Connor and Kennedy joined: a materially supported substantial cross-border effect which individual States cannot reasonably internalize or control, with reasonable fit of the federal prohibition. National prevalence, similar injury in many States and desirability of a nationwide remedy are insufficient by themselves. The present findings support serious economic consequences but do not supply the distinct cross-border mechanism and state inability that route requires. Scalia and Thomas examine this controlling route without being falsely assigned personal assent to a Home Builders part they did not join. Their refusal of that earlier join does not itself establish a categorical objection to every externality theory.

Hinds supplies a separate statutory property-use inquiry, not an added commerce element in section 13981 or a replacement for the constitutional mechanism. Neither it nor Home Builders authorizes reconstruction of this action around its strongest economic example. The four find no concrete potentially eligible mechanism awaiting a genuinely unresolved application below, and therefore decline another existing-record inquiry. Home Builders' particular remand did not create an entitlement to a second chance to invent a connection.

The four reject neither all noneconomic regulation nor all regulation within traditional state fields. No universal jurisdictional element, individual border crossing, individual substantial effect, formal-findings checklist or numerical threshold is adopted. A jurisdictional phrase would have to perform actual constitutional work; inserting one here would change the enactment. Their grounds confirm the historical disposition while using the materially developed actual architecture, rather than importing every historical formulation about economic categories or state spheres.

### Four supporting reversal: proposed rule, not current application

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer rely first on their own actual public Lopez dissent. Congress may rationally assess the substantial interstate effects of the actual regulated class, including the documented obstruction of work, travel and economic participation, and choose reasonable means against the conduct causing that obstruction. They retain judicial review, substantiality, a rational class-wide relationship and reasonable means. They do not accept every conceivable consequence, conclusive congressional labels, national importance alone or an individual-crossing requirement.

The decisive obstacle is the existing controlling rule: aggregate downstream consequences cannot themselves supply the antecedent commerce connection. The four confront that obstacle candidly. Their preferred theory would displace that prerequisite to the extent it forecloses this supported class-effects rationale. Calling the statute a national economic barrier is not presented as a way to satisfy the prerequisite without changing it. Nor do they claim a new externality or necessary-regime fact.

Their later joins in decisions sustaining market and supply regulation—including Condon and Gillespie—establish those sufficient grounds and applications. They do not establish that these four withdrew their earlier broader view whenever an additional ground was unnecessary. Ginsburg and Breyer's Home Builders position is particularly concrete: they joined Parts II–IV given controlling Part I while expressly preserving disagreement with Part I's enacted-class restriction. They cannot now call mere national prevalence the cross-border externality those joins required, and they do not. Stevens and Souter's absence from that coalition supplies no invented separate ground.

**Stare decisis limit:** A continued constitutional dissent is a reasoned proposal to change the governing restriction, not authority to treat it as already displaced. These four maintain the class-effects and deference reasoning in their published Lopez position, confirmed by the historical comparator's central Commerce disagreement. No new reliance, workability or changed-fact finding is manufactured to support an overruling. If an eventual controlling coalition adopts their proposed ground, the decision must identify the precise restriction it displaces and its effect on later authorities that apply that restriction. It cannot both sustain this action on aggregation alone and claim to leave the connection-before-magnitude prerequisite unchanged. The affirmative market, regime and externality holdings remain sufficient routes; their exclusivity as a prerequisite to this proposed broader theory is the point in dispute. This handoff neither overrules a case nor predicts that coalition.

## Section 5: operative fit, not categorical shortcuts

The underlying constitutional injury is discriminatory state protection, not every private gender-motivated assault. The verified Civil Rights Cases and Harris (1883) retain that boundary. They also recognize relevant official administration, judicial action and customs carrying legal force; state action is not confined to discriminatory statutes. The Civil Rights Cases contemplate prospective corrective legislation and remedies that can reach a private party when appropriately related to the state wrong. Harris's mob conspiracy did not become state action merely because its victims were held by an officer; Harlan's jurisdictional dissent supplies no contrary merits position. Harris (1883) is not the simulated 1995 Commerce decision.

Actual Boerne permits preventive coverage beyond acts independently provable unconstitutional and rejects a universal requirement of individualized violations, proof in every jurisdiction, a particular number or form of findings, geographical limits or sunsets. Actual Florida Prepaid upheld the fitted patent-property remedy: the States' own deprivations, exclusive federal infringement jurisdiction and uncertain alternative redress supported preventive inclusion of ordinary infringement. College Savings separately requires a real constitutional injury rather than a federal statutory tort alone. Actual Kimel reserved section 5; its historical section 5 result cannot be borrowed here.

The strongest case for section 13981 is that a federal civil avenue can substitute for biased state protection, and that forcing every claimant first to prove discriminatory denial could defeat prevention. Congress's express account of discriminatory justice matters, as do civil relief, serious violence and gender animus. The four rejecting authority confront this argument rather than treating the nineteenth-century cases as a prohibition on every private defendant.

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas nevertheless find the specific remedial relation insufficient. Liability and relief follow the private assault whether the State denies equal protection or faithfully supplies it. The assailant's gender animus does not identify discriminatory official administration. The action neither replaces a specified constitutionally defective procedure nor defines a supported class of official practices to which its coverage and relief are fitted. The remedial explanation therefore does not bridge the relationship between the private wrong and the state's constitutional denial on this design.

This is not a disguised individual-violation prerequisite. The four accept prophylaxis but distinguish Florida Prepaid's fitted remedy concerning the States' own property deprivation and the redress structure from nationwide liability for a different actor's underlying private assault. Breadth is assessed with trigger, defendant and relief, not counted as a defect by itself. The statutory reference to persons acting under color of state law remains meaningful: the disposition concerns the private-assault cause presented and adjudicates no hypothetical color-of-law application, statutory severance or newly pleaded state-action claim.

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer reserve the alternative. Their commitment rejects categorical private-defendant and per-case constitutional-violation rules, but does not adopt a dispositive section 5 justification. Their actual Florida Prepaid support makes genuine prophylactic latitude a material consideration; their Boerne positions and, for Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer, College Savings joins preserve the substantive-right boundary. No additional vote is inferred from their Commerce result.

Actual Saenz helps distinguish an official's own attributable intervention from private harm the government did not prevent. It supplies no official act or causal removal of protection in this complaint and no decision about congressional prophylaxis. Its direct constitutional-claim predicates cannot become universal predicates for every person reached by valid preventive legislation. Its immunity application supplies no controlling rule for these power questions. Mining supplies no Commerce or section 5 authority.

**Historical departure:** No Associate changes the historical judgment alignment. Material reasoning qualifications follow from changed in-world law: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas must apply actual Lopez and its developed market, regime and externality routes, while actual Boerne and the upheld Florida Prepaid preventive remedy foreclose categorical private-defendant, individualized-violation or geographic requirements as substitutes for section 5 fit. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer retain their own broader Lopez position but must describe it as a proposed revision of the explicit controlling prerequisite; Ginsburg and Breyer preserve their actual Home Builders conditional joins. These are identified legal premises, not invented changes in the assault allegations or legislative record.

Affirmance maintains the constitutional dismissal of the presented private-assault action. Reversal would remove that power-based dismissal and return the statutory claim to ordinary adjudication, without an added commerce element, new state-failure allegation or guaranteed hearing, trial or award. Neither path decides Title IX, state remedies, an alleged assailant's factual liability, every color-of-law application or an unpresented severability question. No broad proof reopening or litigant default is inferred from the source limits. This handoff creates no law, supplies no final Court tally and makes no correction to a completed adjudication.
