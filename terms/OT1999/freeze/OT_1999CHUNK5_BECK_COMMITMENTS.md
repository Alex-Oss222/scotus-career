## Component definitions and the two supported constructions

The granted component is the §1962(d)/§1964(c) claim for retaliatory termination, treated on this review as neither a racketeering predicate nor otherwise independently wrongful under RICO. The independent substantive fraud, stock, debenture and guarantee rulings are not reopened. The existing appellate vacatur of the pendent-state-claim dismissal and remand remain effective under either proposed Supreme disposition; sanctions, costs and service rulings likewise retain their existing scope.

**Affirmance construction:** Civil recovery for injury by reason of a RICO conspiracy requires injury caused by an act of racketeering or another act independently wrongful under RICO. Agreement can make a conspirator responsible for a qualifying injurious act committed by another; it does not turn every act advancing the agreement into an independently compensable RICO injury. Retaliatory termination of the kind established for this question therefore cannot support this civil claim, even when it directly advances or conceals the alleged scheme. The exact coverage of other nonpredicate substantive RICO wrongs need not be decided to reject this non-RICO termination injury.

**Best objection to affirmance:** Section 1964(c) expressly reaches violations of all §1962, including its separate prohibition of conspiracy. Requiring an injury-causing RICO wrong arguably transfers a substantive-offense limitation into a distinct agreement offense. Salinas makes agreement a distinct basis of responsibility, and no borrowed common-law principle may erase the enacted reference to subsection (d).

**Affirmance answer:** The unlawful agreement and the private compensable injury answer separate questions. Civil conspiracy's background links recovery to an independently actionable injurious act rather than agreement alone. Requiring a RICO wrong preserves subsection (d)'s work in attributing that injury to agreeing defendants who did not personally perform the act. It does not require personal commission of two predicates, personal enterprise direction by every conspirator, a prior conviction or a special injury beyond the injury from the actionable conduct. Holmes's contextual causal rule remains independently required.

**Reversal construction:** A direct business or property injury intentionally caused by an act carrying out a proved agreement to violate §1962 may be injury by reason of the conspiracy, even when the injurious act is not itself a racketeering predicate or an independent RICO violation. Subsection (d) is an enacted wrong; importing the civil-tort rule does not justify choosing the substantive subsections as the only injury-generating violations. This does not permit every foreseeable consequence: factual causation and Holmes's contextual directness remain necessary, and the agreement must satisfy Salinas's actual unlawful-object requirement.

**Best objection to reversal:** This could allow recovery for employment or contract disputes that substantive RICO excludes, simply by alleging a larger scheme. **Reversal answer:** Proving a qualifying knowing conspiracy and direct injury from an act implementing it is substantive work, not a label. This record's asserted direct retaliatory firing differs from Steamfitters's intervening smoker/illness/treatment chain. The broader construction removes the categorical legal exclusion but proves no conspiracy, causation or damages and automatically revives no rejected substantive fraud theory.

## Justice-specific commitments

### Stevens

**Commitment:** Reverse the categorical conspiracy-injury ground and remand for ordinary proceedings on that claim. He supports the reversal construction, including the requirement of a direct injury from an act furthering the qualifying conspiracy. His Salinas join recognizes the agreement's distinct legal work; his Holmes and Steamfitters joins require directness without special racketeering injury or imported antitrust injury. Reves's substantive direction requirement, which he joined, governs the conspiracy's object but does not supply this additional private-injury restriction.

**Counterpath and join barrier:** Civil-conspiracy background is relevant, but the enacted conspiracy prohibition itself supplies the wrong here. He cannot join the requirement that the injury-producing act independently violate RICO; its attribution function does not exhaust subsection (d)'s text. He can join explicit limits against recovery on agreement alone, derivative injury, unproved schemes or unrelated employment harms. Rotella's limitations rule, which he joined, does not answer cognizability or create a new time bar here.

### O'Connor

**Commitment:** Affirm conspiracy summary judgment under the affirmance construction and preserve every independent lower-court component. Her Holmes, Reves, Salinas, Steamfitters and Rotella joins recognize statutory breadth together with independently operative civil injury, conduct and causation conditions. The common-law distinction between agreement and actionable civil harm supplies a bounded interpretation of §1964(c), rather than an extra criminal element.

**Counterpath and barrier:** The reference to all §1962 violations gives subsection (d) real effect, but does not require transforming every non-RICO injury advancing an agreement into treble-damage liability. Conspiracy still reaches agreeing participants who do not personally commit the injury-producing RICO wrong. She would reject a narrower formulation requiring the particular defendant personally to perform a predicate and a broader declaration that only §1962(c), never (a) or (b), can generate qualifying injury.

### Scalia

**Commitment:** Affirm on the civil-conspiracy injury boundary. His separate Holmes position accepted proximate cause, and his full current-term Steamfitters join accepts the directness rule and its rejection of imported antitrust injury. His Salinas join preserves an agreement offense without personally promised predicates. The legal meaning of civil recovery for conspiracy supplies the independent-wrong requirement without rewriting those criminal elements.

**Counterpath and barrier:** The literal reference to subsection (d) does not remove the background distinction between unlawful agreement and compensable civil harm. He joins an injury rule, not an overt-act requirement for criminal liability. He would not join a special judicial test of sufficiently blameworthy retaliation or allow state-law wrongfulness alone to satisfy the federal injury condition.

### Kennedy

**Commitment:** Affirm the termination-based conspiracy judgment, with common limits. His Holmes, Reves, Salinas and Steamfitters joins support giving agreement, substantive enterprise conduct, business/property injury and causation distinct work. The agreement expands responsibility for qualifying RICO harm; it does not alone create a federal damages remedy for every retaliatory act.

**Counterpath and barrier:** Direct intentional retaliation can plainly satisfy a causal account, but causal closeness does not decide the separate cognizable-injury question. He would preserve the possibility of nonpredicate acts independently wrongful under other RICO substantive provisions and refuse a rule restricting all claims to §1962(c) or personal predicate performance.

### Souter

**Commitment:** Reverse the categorical exclusion and remand under the reversal construction. His Holmes opinion and Steamfitters join require a direct relation, not injury uniquely associated with racketeering; his Salinas join recognizes the separately unlawful agreement. His Reves disagreement with the added direction requirement supplies additional date-eligible evidence against transferring restrictions beyond the statutory work they perform, while the controlling Reves rule remains binding for the substantive object.

**Counterpath and join barrier:** The civil-conspiracy analogy cannot displace Congress's express inclusion of subsection (d). A knowing agreement to conduct the unlawful enterprise and direct injury from an act implementing it sufficiently distinguish an ordinary contract dispute. He cannot join an independent-RICO-act prerequisite, but would join rigorous proof of agreement, direct cause and business/property loss. His Rotella opinion concerns a complete stale claim and supplies no disposition here.

### Thomas

**Commitment:** Affirm conspiracy summary judgment on the independent-RICO-wrong requirement. His Salinas opinion distinguishes the conspirator's undertaking from personal predicate performance; his Steamfitters opinion distinguishes real property loss from a legally sufficient claim. His Holmes and Reves joins likewise preserve separate statutory conditions. The civil injury limitation gives those distinctions effect without changing Salinas's actual holding.

**Counterpath and barrier:** Congress's separate conspiracy subsection is not surplusage when an agreeing defendant may answer for qualifying harm committed by another. The competing construction would treat non-RICO wrongs as treble-damage injuries despite the civil-conspiracy background. He refuses any claim that this case settles a general criminal overt-act rule, a question Salinas expressly reserved in this timeline.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment:** Affirm at the common confined scope. Her Salinas and current-term Steamfitters joins support preserving the distinction between the prohibited agreement, the claimant's injury and the required link. Conspiracy can expand responsible defendants without converting an injury outside RICO into a RICO injury. The employment and contract interests keep their own lawful remedies.

**Counterpath and barrier:** The alleged firing is more direct than the funds' medical expenditures, so Steamfitters cannot itself justify affirmance for remoteness. The separate civil-conspiracy injury boundary does the decisive work. She joins no finding that Beck lacked a real property loss, no categorical shareholder/creditor exclusion and no reinstatement of the vacated state-claim dismissal.

### Breyer

**Commitment:** Affirm the termination-based conspiracy judgment under the independent-wrong construction. His Salinas, Steamfitters and Rotella joins preserve RICO's effective reach while enforcing distinct statutory conditions. The construction allows liability against knowing participants for another conspirator's injurious RICO act but does not convert every related employment or contract harm into federal treble damages.

**Counterpath and barrier:** Direct intentional retaliation is causally stronger than the funds' downstream costs; practical directness alone does not define which civil harms the conspiracy provision compensates. He joins the common-law-informed civil injury rule only with the express reservations for other substantive RICO wrongs, nonpersonal predicate commission and distinct state rights. No general criminal overt-act rule follows.

## Remedy, precedent and complete qualifications

Six Associates provisionally support affirmance of the granted conspiracy ground; Stevens and Souter support reversal and remand. No full-Court tally is inferred. The six share the same operative injury condition; the two opposing positions share the distinct text-and-directness construction. Neither ground is a logical subset of the other, and no fractured-opinion synthesis is attempted.

Holmes continues to require factual causation and contextual proximate cause. Steamfitters is distinguished as an intervening-injury causal chain; it supplies neither a universal target exception nor the additional civil-conspiracy injury rule. Sedima retains rejection of special racketeering injury and prior conviction. Salinas retains knowing agreement without personally promised predicates and its general-overt-act reservation. Reves governs the substantive enterprise object without imposing personal direction on each conspirator. Rotella's complete-claim/known-injury limitations rule remains separate and is not applied to decide timeliness here.

Under affirmance the federal termination-conspiracy summary judgment stands. The Eleventh Circuit's state-claim vacatur and common-law pendent-jurisdiction remand remain operative because §1367's later regime did not apply to this May 1990 action. The lower court must retain consideration of limitations prejudice; a limitations-defense waiver remains a permissible protective condition if dismissal is chosen, not a mandatory Supreme Court requirement. Independent sanctions, taxable-costs and Sparber service dispositions remain at their existing scope. No fraud finding, veil-piercing ruling, damages award, new sanctions or automatic discovery follows.

The two reversing Associates would remove only the granted categorical ground and permit ordinary consideration of the conspiracy claim's remaining elements and defenses; they would leave the same independent components intact. Neither position awards treble damages or treats the allegations as proved.

The later §1964(c) text's distinct securities qualification remains: conduct actionable as securities purchase-or-sale fraud cannot establish the RICO violation, except in an action against a person criminally convicted in connection with the fraud; in that exception the limitations period starts when conviction becomes final. This termination question decides neither the amendment's temporal applicability nor a securities theory, and restores no securities claim. Common-law conspiracy is interpretive support, not enacted statutory text; this handoff claims no newly recovered historical briefs or factual findings.
