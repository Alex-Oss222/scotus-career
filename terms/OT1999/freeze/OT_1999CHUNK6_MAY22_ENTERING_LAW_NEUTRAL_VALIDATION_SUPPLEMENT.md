## Jones: actual Morrison law alongside Hinds

**Morrison's controlling Commerce rule.** The five-Justice Court opinion preserves Lopez's sequence: identify the class Congress actually regulated and a constitutionally eligible interstate connection before aggregate magnitude can perform its work. Serious downstream effects on work, travel, treatment, consumption or productivity do not create a missing connection. The private gender-motivated-assault action presented in Morrison lacked an actual interstate-market direction, a supported reasonably necessary anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship to an identified valid interstate regime, and a materially supported substantial cross-border externality that individual States cannot reasonably internalize with a reasonably fitted prohibition. Its nationwide prevalence and large economic consequences did not fill those distinct gaps.

The rule preserves the established commerce, channels and instrumentalities routes and the developed market, necessary-regime and externality routes at their actual scopes. It adopts no categorical prohibition on noneconomic conduct or traditional state subjects, universal jurisdictional-element requirement, personal border-crossing rule, numerical threshold or findings checklist. A court must identify and assess the supported mechanism; it may neither assume that every national problem qualifies nor invent a missing market, spillover or anti-evasion necessity. Morrison distinguishes Hinds's statutory property-use inquiry rather than overruling it or converting it into the constitutional test.

**The published broader dissent is distinct.** Breyer, Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg would permit a rationally supported substantial-class-effects account of the regulated conduct's obstruction of interstate labor, travel, education and service markets, with reasonable means and judicial review. That is an express proposal to change the connection-before-magnitude prerequisite where it excludes that ground, not an application leaving the prerequisite intact. It retains substantiality, a rational class-wide relationship and reasonable means; congressional labels, national importance or any conceivable economic consequence do not alone suffice even under that proposal. Their joins in cases with independently sufficient market or supply connections did not abandon this broader position. The dissent is relevant published prior reasoning, not governing law and not a forecast of any Justice's vote or statutory construction in Jones. Its reservation of Section 5 is not a vote sustaining that alternative.

**Hinds continues to govern the statutory inquiry.** Its eight-Justice complete construction asks whether the damaged property's actual function and use connect it to interstate commerce or an activity affecting it. An owner's affiliation, purchases, insurance or payments do not establish the element merely by existing; their relation to the property's use must be shown. Hinds rejects a universal profit-making, rental, sales or exclusively business-purpose prerequisite, as well as categorical religious or nonprofit exemption. No individual-fire substantiality or interstate-travel condition is added. The separate narrower active-commercial-use view does not become the Court's construction through Morrison.

Jones's supplied evidence remains an owner-occupied residence, an out-of-state mortgage lender and insurer, and interstate natural gas. No former rental use, current home business or other unproved function is added. The exclusion path must explain why these transactions do not establish the building's required use, while answering the breadth of the enacted affecting-commerce language. The inclusion path must identify the actual property function and relation the words require; naming a mortgage, policy or utility contract cannot substitute for that explanation. Constitutional power to regulate some broader class would not establish that Congress covered this use in §844(i).

Only if the properly construed statute covers the established use does the constitutional question become necessary. Then the enacted class and supported interstate mechanism must be assessed under actual Lopez/Morrison law, confronting the Government's economic-property and express-element account and the objection that incidental household transactions do not supply the required connection. The express commerce element matters only to the extent the connection it requires and the evidence establishes perform constitutional work. Its presence is neither a safe harbor nor a defect. Morrison's disposition of a differently framed civil action does not establish the result here, require a categorical private-home exception, or authorize a court to insert a different element.

**Remedy remains tied to the existing record.** Hinds distinguishes an erroneous appellate premise from actual insufficiency of the complete existing proof. Existing-record review may identify a lawful use basis, but an actual failure of proof under the correct element requires acquittal under Burks and bars another evidentiary opportunity to repair the failure. Morrison's refusal to invent a constitutional connection or remand for new legislative proof reinforces no general entitlement to a second chance; its civil remedy does not replace Hinds's criminal remedy. Any consequence for §924(c) or the separate firearm-making conviction must follow actual dependence and procedural scope, not an automatic all-count disposition. Neither the broader dissent nor the presence of an express element supplies new record facts.
