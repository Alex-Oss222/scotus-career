# Eisenberg — form before assignment

One signed plenary lead resolves the preliminary-relief review and both actual appellate injunction directions. Part I applies the existing common substantial-justification standard and describes the actual individual burden. Part II holds that supported educational benefits from meaningful K–12 integration and avoidance of serious racial isolation can be an important lawful objective; six support it: Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Part III vacates both the immediate-admission and final race-free-reconsideration directions and remands under ordinary, period-appropriate procedure; Kennedy joins Parts I and III while reserving Part II. The other six join throughout. Scalia and Thomas publish one dissent preserving the bounded application-specific appellate relief, with both their stricter position and alternative conclusion under binding law. Their recognition that Adarand binds does not invent a new join in the lead. Kennedy's substantive reservation warrants a brief concurrence in part and in the judgment. These real question and join differences fix the parts and form before any count or author choice; no divided authorship is needed.
