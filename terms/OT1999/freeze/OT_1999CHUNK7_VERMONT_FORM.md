# Vermont Agency — form before assignment

One signed plenary lead contains Part I's assignment-standing holding, supported by all nine, and Part II's bounded statutory exclusion of this privately maintained State-defendant action, supported by Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Part II uses the shared no-affirmative-authorization ground: the generic person term, section-local investigative definition and distinct private-relator structure do not authorize this sovereign defendant. It does not add historical-original-text, punitive-remedy, sister-statute or displaced constitutional-doubt reasons. Ginsburg's frozen bounded ground therefore supports the complete Part II; no broader join is inferred. The two real issues require these parts, not separate authors. Stevens and Souter publish a common partial concurrence/dissent joining standing and disputing statutory exclusion. Form and propositions precede counts and author selection.
