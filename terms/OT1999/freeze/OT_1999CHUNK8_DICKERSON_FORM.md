# Dickerson v. United States — form and propositions before assignment

**Event:** No. 99-5525, June 26, 2000; October Term 1999, chunk 8.

**Sequence:** Prepared after the final C law/reconciliation release and the Apprendi revalidation stop, before opening any current count or considering an author. Apprendi supplies neither a Record nor a counted opinion.

**Form fixed on legal grounds:** One signed lead opinion with two severable parts, plus one dissent. Part I supplies the constitutional authority of Miranda, the inadequacy of voluntariness alone as the proposed substitute, the statutory qualifications and restoration of the statement-suppression order with a bounded remand. Part II rejects the separate request that the Court itself discard the core case-in-chief safeguard, on the six Associates' additional stare-decisis ground. The separation reflects different grounds and Stone's actual authorization, not workload or a desired author. No division among authors is necessary if one Justice can carry both parts.

**Tentative final scope:** Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support reversal and restoration of statement suppression and join Part I. Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer additionally join Part II. Stone's supplement supplies no independent stare-decisis ground for the alternative judicial-reconsideration request: his standing fallback supplies agreement with the majority's rejection of that separately presented request, adding no ground or Part II join. Scalia and Thomas dissent on their reconciled judicial-reconsideration premise and would sustain the statutory admissibility basis on a sustainable voluntariness determination with all mandatory statutory circumstances considered.

**Limits fixed before counting:** Genuinely equally effective safeguards remain possible; no exact verbal formula is mandated; unwarned does not invariably mean actually coerced; existing impeachment, public-safety, derivative-evidence and later-warned-statement doctrines retain their own conditions. Sections 3501(c), (d) and (e) are not invalidated; the independent coercion inquiry survives. Review does not reach the separate physical-evidence, reopening or circuit-issue-raising disputes. Suppression concerns case-in-chief use subject to a properly preserved exception. No new factual finding, new hearing, dismissal, conviction vacatur, acquittal or release follows.

**Eligibility criterion:** A whole-lead author must support both parts in full. This excludes Stone from whole-lead eligibility because his agreement on Part II's disposition supplies no additional rationale. Eligibility and the balance pool will be determined from the actual final proposition commitments and fresh Record-derived counts. No author or count is recorded here.
