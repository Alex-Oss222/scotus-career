# Pegram — form before assignment

Before fresh counts or author selection, this plenary appeal will issue one signed integrated lead with three genuine parts: I, function-specific fiduciary status and inseparable treatment; II, ordinary compensation/risk sharing and the separate plan-asset predicate; III, the independent conditional ground that even assuming genuine administration of nonroutine claims, the pleaded general incentive arrangement alone supplies no independently disloyal act or breach. These are different substantive tasks in the frozen reconciliation, not divisions for counting. One full-ground author can carry the entire lead.

All eight Associates support every part and reversal with dismissal of the reviewed amended count. Stone supports the same judgment and Part I's mixed-treatment rule. His approved core expressly leaves pure administrative decisions and plan-asset misuse open and supplies no independent no-breach holding under assumed administrative status. He will not be assigned or counted as a full-lead joiner. Parts II–III have eight direct supporters; no Stone position is supplied by inference or fallback. His brief concurrence in part and in the judgment preserves his actual treatment ground and reservations while declining to join the additional asset/business and assumed-administration grounds. It changes neither the Associates' grounds nor the disposition.

The judgment leaves the $35,000 malpractice judgment and unreviewed counts undisturbed. It decides no general preemption, independent disclosure claim, particular plan-asset ownership, direct-denial reward, fee or amendment entitlement. No historical authorship or fictional circulation is used.
