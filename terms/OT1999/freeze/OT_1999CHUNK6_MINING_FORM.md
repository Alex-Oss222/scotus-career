# National Mining: form and governing propositions fixed before counting

Assembly chooses one signed opinion after plenary review. The question joins statutory current/former control, the temporal operation of an otherwise lawful construction, and the express correction of improperly issued permits. Distinct component votes require three severable parts, but the eight reconciled Associates can carry every part; no substantive ground requires divided authorship.

- Part I affirms that ordinary section 510(c) permit blocking requires current control of a currently violating operation. Actual indirect downstream control remains possible; former control requires the separate complete willful-pattern conditions or another enacted basis. The current-rule cure alternatives and schedule/other-information distinction remain.
- Part II reverses the categorical exclusion of pre-November 2, 1988 violations and control acquisitions only for present eligibility involving actual current control and a current violation. It applies the confined current-condition proposition and Smiley's qualified interpretation rule, without adopting Landgraf's entire general methodology anew. Completed-conduct new disabilities, displaced clear official guidance and particular finality claims remain distinct.
- Part III affirms express statutory authority to correct improperly issued permits under section 1211(c), with actual lawful predicates and required procedure; no specific finally adjudicated permit is decided and no unlimited implied reopening power is adopted.

The opinion's remedy follows each part: preserve the former-control limitation and express correction authority; reverse the categorical temporal bar at Part II's scope; conform the existing remand through the district court to OSM without new merits judgments on ungranted components.

Stone's fixed affirmance supports Parts I and III, but cannot be converted into support for Part II's reversal by isolating his reservation of prospective regulation and current-control enforcement. His approved position supplies a concurrence in part and dissent in part preserving the conclusion that the challenged application attaches a new disability to completed pre-rule transactions. The writing retains all his prospective-regulation, current-control, statutory-reopening and independently valid willful-pattern reservations. It neither asserts a particular final adjudication nor introduces another ground. No standing fallback is used.

The Chief assigns Parts I and III from their nine-Justice judgment coalitions. Stevens is the most senior participating Associate in Part II's eight-Justice reversal coalition and assigns that part. One Associate can deliver all three; the balance rule governs because the Chief assigns portions of the same lead. Eligibility and counts will be assessed only after this form and scope are fixed. No author or count was selected in making these choices.
