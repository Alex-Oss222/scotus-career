O'Connor authored that Court holding; Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas joined it. That restriction was already clearly established before the June 1997 state decision. The historical Terry Court had to explain the narrow proper operation of Fretwell against the broader lower-court reading; here the limitation is explicit existing majority law and must be applied as such.
