## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Guam Organic Act counts all general-election ballots

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Reverse the Ninth Circuit and remand for recognition of the certified slate. The statutory majority is calculated from votes cast for gubernatorial slates, not all ballots submitted in the general election. This honors the enacted denominator for a gubernatorial majority rather than count ballots expressing no vote for that office.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The statutory majority is calculated from votes cast for gubernatorial slates, not all ballots submitted in the general election.
