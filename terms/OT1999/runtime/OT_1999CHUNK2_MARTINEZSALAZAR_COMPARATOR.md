*United States v. Martinez-Salazar*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg, J., delivered the opinion of the Court, in which Rehnquist, C. J., and Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, Thomas, and Breyer, JJ., joined. Souter, J., filed a concurring opinion, post, p. 317. Scalia, J., filed an opinion concurring in the judgment, in which Kennedy, J., joined, post, p. 318.
* **Historical Holding:** A defendant’s exercise of peremptory challenges pursuant to Rule 24 is not denied or impaired when the defendant chooses to use such a challenge to remove a juror who should have been excused for cause.
* **Historical Authority:** Fractured decision; controlling authority must be identified issue by issue from the official joins.
