## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Medicare payments are federal benefits received

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** An organization receives program benefits when continuing federal payments materially support its operations and advance an ongoing federal programmatic purpose. This applies § 666's enacted program-benefit element to the actual structure and purpose of the Medicare payments, not to adopt a general pro-enforcement presumption. Ordinary procurement, one-time market transactions, and payments lacking a programmatic relationship remain outside.
