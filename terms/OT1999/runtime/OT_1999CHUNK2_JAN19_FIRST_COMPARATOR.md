# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Gutierrez v. Ada*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter, J., delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** The Guam Organic Act does not require a runoff election when a candidate slate has received a majority of the votes cast for Governor and Lieutenant Governor of the Territory, but not a majority of the number of ballots cast in the simultaneous general election. Section 1422 contains six express references to an election for those offices, two of them preceding the phrase “in any election,” and four following.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at the scope supported by the joins stated above.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-528/USREPORTS-528-250); Official U.S. Reports, 528 U.S. 250 (2000); SCDB case-centered vote metadata for the reported judgment tally.

# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Smith v. Robbins*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas, J., delivered the opinion of the Court, in which Rehnquist, C. J., and O’Connor, Scalia, and Kennedy, JJ., joined. Stevens, J., filed a dissenting opinion, in which Ginsburg, J., joined, post, p. 289. Souter, J., filed a dissenting opinion, in which Stevens, Ginsburg, and Breyer, JJ., joined, post, p. 292.
* **Historical Holding:** 1. The Anders procedure is only one method of satisfying the Constitution’s requirements for indigent criminal appeals; the States are free to adopt different procedures, so long as those procedures adequately safeguard a defendant’s right to appellate counsel.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at the scope supported by the joins stated above.
