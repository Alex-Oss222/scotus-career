# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Reno v. Condon*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist, C. J., delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** In enacting the DPPA, Congress did not run afoul of the federalism principles enunciated in New York v. United States, 505 U.S. 144, and Printz v. United States, 521 U.S. 898. The Federal Government correctly asserts that the DPPA is a proper exercise of Congress’ authority to regulate interstate commerce under the Commerce Clause, U.S. Const., Art. I, § 8, cl. 3.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at the scope supported by the joins stated above.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-528/USREPORTS-528-141); Official U.S. Reports, 528 U.S. 141 (2000); SCDB case-centered vote metadata for the reported judgment tally.

# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Martinez v. Court of Appeal of California, Fourth Appellate District*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9–0 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens announced the Court’s opinion; Justice Scalia concurred in the judgment, and Justice Kennedy filed a concurrence joined by Justice Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The Constitution does not provide a right to self-representation on direct appeal from a criminal conviction.
* **Historical Authority:** The Court’s judgment and the propositions supported by the controlling opinion.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-528/USREPORTS-528-152); Official U.S. Reports, 528 U.S. 152 (2000).
