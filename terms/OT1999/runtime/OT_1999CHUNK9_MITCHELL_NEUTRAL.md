### Issue 1: Does Chapter 2 violate the Establishment Clause

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Agostini rejects religious-location presumptions and incidental-benefit objections, applying modified Lemon to actual governmental function, attribution and legally relevant control. Its approval of the specified safeguarded secular public service does not immunize demonstrated devotional financing. Jackson v. Benson (March 16, 1999) rejects neutral parental routing as a sufficient defense where the funded educational work demonstrably integrates devotional instruction without reliable separation. Applied here, those decisions require identification of what Chapter 2 actually finances; identity, fungibility and recipient control alone do not establish the same funded devotional function found in Jackson. Rosenberger preserves its distinct privately attributed printing benefit.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Identify the operational control and instructional use of Chapter 2 materials. Fungibility or institutional religious identity alone cannot establish a violation; any invalidity must rest on the actual funded function, legally relevant control or supported diversion, not a displaced presumption.
