## Stevens: precise reconciliation and correction

The historical dissent, 530 U.S. at 624?639, is not merely an election reservation. At 625 and 629 Stevens expressly agrees that the Government breached the incorporated thirty-day approval obligation. At 630?631 he denies that the breach destroyed the essential object of the contract or that the statute and letters amounted to anticipatory repudiation. At 634?639 he evaluates materiality in the continuing transaction at the time of breach, giving weight to North Carolina's independently assumed obstacle, the Government's completed evaluation and continued work, preservation of lease time, and both sides' continuing performance. He permits at most proved delay damages rather than restoration of the initial investment. His footnote 3 distinguishes genuine consideration already furnished from the withheld legal approval; the majority's claim that the companies received nothing does not express his view.

The initial model correctly identified his recognition of a real contract and his concern about continued performance, but incorrectly converted the latter into an election-only reservation while joining total repudiation. That changes his decisive legal ground. The exact Winstar law here does not require that change: it expressly separates breach from contract-specific causation and relief, preserves qualifications and ordinary defenses, and awards no automatic recovery for every legislative impairment. His own Winstar joins support contractual accountability but do not equate every actionable withdrawal with total breach or rescission. His additional sovereign-acts and unmistakability positions do not answer the materiality question.

The June 2, 1995 letter is a genuine qualification of the historical account, but its consequences are confined. It requires rental payments and limits the new suspension to five years; the packet establishes no later renewal or continuation through June 2000. Thus neither a continuously rent-free benefit nor a still-operative suspension at decision may be assumed. Stevens's actual dissent does not make free rent its premise. Its essential conclusion rests on the nature of the bargain and circumstances when approval was withheld in 1990, the independent state obstacles, actual evaluation already performed, and the documented extensions and continuing efforts. A rent-bearing extension still preserves lease time. Absence of a recovered later continuation is not proof that no continuation occurred. Correcting those later descriptions does not transform the breach into total repudiation for this Justice, and does not justify replacing his materiality holding with an election remand.

**Reconciled Stevens commitment:** Recognize the Government's actual breach of the submitted-plan approval undertaking, but deny restoration of the bonus consideration because this failure, viewed with the contractual risks and continuing performance, does not amount to a total breach or repudiation. Reject the fifth-lease anticipatory theory; no plan was submitted there and no actual missed approval obligation is found. Sustain the appellate rejection of restitution for both petitioners on this ground, not on a theory that state objections erased every breach. Any separate delay-damages issue remains subject to ordinary preservation, causation and proof; no damages amount, automatic damages hearing or new claim is ordered. Do not declare that the lessees waived rights, attribute one petitioner's every act to another, or adjudicate disputed correspondence preservation.

His strongest contrary argument is that the purchased approval procedure is itself valuable, so a categorical thirteen-month-or-longer prohibition materially removes an essential part of the bargain regardless of ultimate success. He accepts the procedure as a real undertaking, but distinguishes existence of a promise from the severity needed to undo the whole transaction. The Government continued assessment, the contracted project faced independent known barriers, and keeping the opportunity alive supplied performance relevant to that narrower question. He may therefore agree on the incorporation and actual breach portions while refusing the total-breach, anticipatory-repudiation, restitution and majority-remedy portions. A separate writing would explain that substantive disagreement and the limited damages alternative; it must not be summarized as disagreement only about waiver or arithmetic.

No concrete changed premise supports the initial departure on materiality. The correction follows complete source review and the same surviving own-Justice contract principles, rather than a mechanical historical vote replacement. The careful source limitations above remain even though the ultimate anti-restitution position agrees with history.

## Seven confirmed Associate commitments

The seven remaining Associates share the sufficient substantive ground in the frozen model: the leases sold a future conditional opportunity under identified procedures, not simply existing regulatory permission; OBPA materially withdrew that process. A promise of approval upon the incorporated conditions is not a guarantee of all permits, state concurrence, oil, profits or freedom from future sovereign regulation. Sections 1, 9?10, 13 and 19 and the OCSLA predicates remain real qualifications. A regulation permitting specified environmental suspension does not automatically incorporate new statutory prerequisites excluded by the contractual allocation. For the fifth lease, the statutory prohibition supplies the anticipatory process ground without an invented application.

Their historical full joins confirm this result, but current Winstar and the more precise packet control its articulation. All seven accept that genuine legislative authority and monetary contractual responsibility can coexist; actual allocated risk is sufficient even assuming public and general legislation. No present waiver of a sovereign-acts defense is inferred from the historical opinion's procedural statement, and no comprehensive new sovereign-acts or unmistakability rule is required.

The seven reject the supplied election defense at its actual scope. Urging performance or seeking to meet other conditions does not itself surrender restitution; acceptance of significant substituted performance could. The documented plan submission, failed override pursuit and MOU-related extensions do not establish such acceptance here. Their holding may acknowledge actual administrative work and the value of preserved time without pretending these delivered the repudiated process. It need not claim every lease was rent-free or use a disputed future renewal. The 1992 suit is an affirmative restoration election; no retraction, settlement or abandonment is found.
