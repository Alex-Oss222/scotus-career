## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Did the Government materially breach offshore leases

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Reverse and remand. Refusal to provide the contracted approval process because of a later statutory condition outside the lessees’ assumed risk constitutes material repudiation supporting restitution.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Refusal to provide the contracted approval process because of a later statutory condition outside the lessees’ assumed risk constitutes material repudiation supporting restitution.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Reverse and remand. Refusal to provide the contracted approval process because of a later statutory condition outside the lessees’ assumed risk constitutes material repudiation supporting restitution. Return the proved up-front payments subject to lawful credits and offsets; interest requires independent authorization. Winstar’s financial-responsibility principle means Congress may change the governing law while the Government remains responsible for the risk it contracted to bear. The lessees’ restitution claim depends on this promised process and its repudiation, not a free-standing guarantee of regulatory approval or profits.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No guarantee of plan approval, state concurrence, permits, successful production, or speculative profits follows.
