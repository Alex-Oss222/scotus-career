## Commitments by component

**A. Section 9.** All eight Associates provisionally hold its award-district venue permissive and supplementary to otherwise applicable venue law. Where the contract has not specified a court for judgment, §9 authorizes application in the district where the award was made without saying that an application can be made only there. This construction leaves the location words operative: they independently authorize that venue even when ordinary venue might not. It does not hold that the word may invariably resolves exclusivity in every statute.

The preceding agreed-court clause, the permission to apply within one year and the command to confirm unless the award is vacated, modified or corrected as §§10–11 prescribe perform different tasks. The command to grant qualifying relief does not convert the subsequent venue permission into an exclusive-location command. This case requires no determination of the enforceability or exclusivity of a genuine specified-court agreement, whether the one-year language is a mandatory limitations bar, or the present entitlement to confirmation. Section 9's notice and personal-jurisdiction clauses do not independently create federal subject-matter jurisdiction or excuse their own procedural requirements.

**B. Sections 10 and 11.** All eight provisionally reach the same nonexclusive venue construction for the parallel award-district language authorizing vacatur and modification/correction. Their formulation places the identified court before may and the remedy, whereas §9 speaks of an application. That grammatical difference makes the exclusive reading plausible, but it does not supply an exclusive territorial command or show that Congress wanted confirmation and attacks on the same award confined by different venue regimes. The specified district retains meaningful affirmative authority; otherwise applicable venue is not displaced merely because a remedial grant identifies an additional court.

Neither the grounds for vacatur and modification nor their qualifications are changed. Section 10's corruption, partiality, prejudicial misconduct and excess-of-power grounds are not decided. Its authorization of an arbitral rehearing when the agreement's time has not expired remains distinct. Section 11's material calculation/description mistakes, matters not submitted with its exception for a matter not affecting the merits of what was submitted, and nonmerits formal imperfections remain distinct statutory grounds. No deficiency in this award is established by choosing a venue. Section 12's notice/service rules and its three-month provision are not enlarged or resolved here.

**C. Structure, legal review and disposition.** All eight support vacatur of the Eleventh Circuit's judgment and remand because the courts treated the Alabama venue as exclusive when considering the request to dismiss, transfer or stay. Alabama remains an authorized venue. The remand permits consideration of the competing proceedings under the correct legal premise, including any properly presented ordinary venue and case-management grounds. It does not direct transfer, enforce a mechanical first-file rule, grant the Mississippi vacatur request, decide the merits of the award, or enter a replacement monetary judgment. No independent basis establishes that the venue error was necessarily harmless; the courts' categorical rationale cannot answer the discretionary request on its own.

Sections 3, 4 and 8 confirm that the FAA operates within ordinary judicial proceedings rather than establishing a single universal supervisory court. Section 3 contemplates a stay by the court with the pending action; §4 expressly limits the place of ordered arbitration to the petition district; §8 expressly preserves the admiralty court's jurisdiction to enter a decree on the award. Section 8's notwithstanding clause could function as an exception to an exclusive rule, so it is corroborating structure rather than an irreconcilable textual contradiction. The contrary argument also gains something from §4's use of any district court and §§9–11's use of a specified district. The decisive answer is that these provisions regulate different acts and supply no negative exclusion from ordinary postaward venue. No new rule concerning §4 extraterritorial compulsion or §7 subpoenas is announced.

The possibility of parallel actions does not add an exclusivity term to Congress's text; ordinary coordination, transfer and other applicable doctrines address that problem on their own requirements. The Eleventh Circuit's obligation to follow its former Fifth Circuit precedent explains its decision but does not bind this Court's construction. Naples and Sutter remain the competing lower interpretations being evaluated, not controlling Supreme Court rules. The Court does not invoke a free-standing policy favoring arbitration to override statutory words.

## Reach, joins and remaining matters

The eight Associates support the nonexclusive §§9–11 venue rule and the bounded vacatur/remand. Their shared result arises from separate review of the textual fork; no earlier arbitration coalition determines it. No compelled separate writing or author assignment is made. The text-and-structure ground is sufficient for each. Scalia and Thomas require omission of any free-standing policy rationale; all eight can join an explanation satisfying that limit. None supports importing a rule from current-case history or applying a later jurisdictional doctrine.

A narrower lawful ruling could decide §9 alone because the reviewed judgment confirmed the award. That would fail to answer the expressly granted coordinated §§9–11 question and leave the court's treatment of the pending vacatur/modification action under an unresolved legal premise. The common text permits resolution of all three venue clauses without deciding the substantive grounds. A broader order choosing which district must proceed lacks the needed lower-court consideration and record. An affirmance on exclusive venue is textually arguable but is rejected by each Justice for the reasons recorded; affirmance as harmless would require an independent supported basis not supplied here.

No material neutral-source gap prevents this disposition. Subject-matter jurisdiction, personal jurisdiction, service, statutory timing, contractual forum restrictions and the merits retain their independent requirements. Nothing in the freeze assumes they have been litigated and resolved merely because venue is permissive.
