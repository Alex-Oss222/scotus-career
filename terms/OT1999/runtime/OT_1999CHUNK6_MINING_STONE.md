## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Permit-blocking authority after ownership ends

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Affirm the D.C. Circuit's relevant holdings. The statute's ownership-and-control language does not authorize an agency to continue a permit block after the legally relevant relationship has ended unless another enacted provision, including the rule for a demonstrated pattern of willful violations, supplies that consequence. The agency also may not attach new permit-blocking consequences to completed pre-rule transactions without clear statutory authorization for retroactive effect.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Interior may not continue ownership-and-control permit consequences after the statutory relationship ends or attach new consequences to completed pre-rule transactions without enacted authority that clearly supports those applications.
