## Components, fixed premise and common legal boundaries

All eight Associates reach the federal Chapter 2 question. No supported jurisdictional, participation or preservation obstacle is supplied. The other programs in the larger litigation are not automatically within this grant. Any treatment of a Louisiana counterpart must follow its actual legal dependence on the reviewed federal disposition; its mere appearance in the same opinion supplies no jurisdiction or substantive ruling.

The components are (1) the governing financing rule, (2) Chapter 2's neutral, secular and controlled statutory design, (3) the established actual financed-use application, (4) the Fifth Circuit's categorical equipment/library-book declaration, and (5) corrective relief. The adopted premise establishes actual financing of religious instruction or demonstrated religious use under recipient control in this application. It is neither merely possible diversion nor a finding about every school, item or use. No Justice may replace it with neutrality, potential divertibility or institutional savings. No hypothetical filing, audit or new proof is required to establish the premise already adopted.

Agostini's unified Lemon inquiry is current law; its former independent prongs do not return. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas joined both its governmental-function rule and the actual secular-control qualifications. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer later joined Jackson's actual funded-function rule, while retaining the differences in their earlier Agostini writings. Strout applies the rule to the integrated ordinary-tuition application. Rosenberger protects an actual independently edited printing benefit under its collective safeguards; it is not a neutrality-only spending exemption. Southworth concerns a genuine independent-expression program and does not decide this instructional financing. Santa Fe's official-function and private-expression distinction remains; neither its school-event holding nor its dissent supplies an automatic answer about equipment.

Public title and administration, secular/neutral/nonideological materials, independent providers and supplement-not-supplant restrictions are legal commands, not findings that every local use satisfies them. Religious affiliation, ordinary monitoring and incidental savings alone establish no constitutional violation. Conversely, retaining title while actually financing the prohibited religious function does not cure that defect. No universal religious-provider exclusion, independent-governance requirement, complete personnel separation or new audit bureaucracy is adopted.

## Justice-specific enhanced commitments

### Stevens

**Commitment.** Vacate the reviewed categorical Chapter 2 declaration and remand for relief against the established actual religious financing, preserving lawful severable applications. The actual financed-use application violates the Establishment Clause. His full joins in Souter's Agostini substantive dissent and O'Connor's Jackson opinion, and his Strout join, make actual public support of the religious instructional function a sufficient ground without reducing the issue to who speaks for government. His Southworth concurrence distinguishes the independent distribution of expression from this instructional expenditure.

**Competing paths.** Affirming the existing categorical declaration has force under Meek and Wolman's concern that equipment supports an integrated religious school. Its strongest difficulty is the decree's sweep over equipment and library loans based on the recipient's character, irrespective of genuinely separable secular use recognized by current law. He would preserve scrutiny of institutional support without using it to exclude all religious providers. Reversing for an unconditional declaration of validity has the opposite strongest argument—neutral eligibility and public ownership—but cannot survive the fixed actual-financing premise. The premise strengthens rather than weakens his constitutional objection.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the actual-use prohibition, rejection of affiliation alone, and conflict-specific remand. Will not join a declaration that all direct aid is permissible whenever access is neutral, or an unnecessary wholesale overruling of Meek/Wolman beyond the categorical equipment rationale actually at issue. No return to the independent Lemon prongs is required for his sufficient ground. Willing to join a complete opinion that respects those limits; a concurrence may preserve his institutional-support concern only if the lead opinion purports to reject it. No automatic recoupment, school closure or remedy against unreviewed programs.

### O'Connor

**Commitment.** Vacate the reviewed categorical declaration and remand for relief that prevents the demonstrated religious financing and preserves lawful severable loans. Her Agostini join permits reliably controlled secular instruction; her own Jackson opinion requires inquiry into what the expenditure actually buys, with a confined remedial determination; her Strout join confirms that nominal neutrality and private choice do not themselves separate integrated devotional work. The adopted fact makes a constitutional defect established, rather than a mere suspicion requiring proof on remand.

**Competing paths.** A blanket affirmation would answer the actual violation but preserve a rule that equipment at a sectarian school is forbidden even when reliably used for independently identifiable secular work. Agostini rejects that categorical location/inseparability inference. A blanket reversal would correctly reject the equipment/textbook category as conclusive but wrongly treat public title and eligibility as conclusive approval of administration. Rosenberger supports its actual controlled private benefit, not this latter shortcut. The best objection to her narrower disposition is that it leaves implementation work; the answer is that quantity and lawful severability are not established by the fixed premise and may not be invented to enlarge the remedy.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a common opinion stating the actual-use violation, rejecting the lower court's categorical rationale at its inconsistent scope, and requiring sufficient relief against that use without all-provider exclusion. A remand cannot authorize continuation of the established unconstitutional financing, merely reopen whether the adopted premise is true, or demand a new trial when the existing record suffices. It may determine the actual provider/item/application scope, severability and prospective relief. No independent concurrence is necessary if these distinctions are preserved.

### Scalia

**Commitment.** Vacate the categorical equipment declaration and remand for confined correction of the established actual religious financing under Agostini. His own Agostini join accepts enforceable secular-use controls and rejects neutrality as an excuse for devotional instruction or diversion. His Jackson and Strout dissents concern genuine individually selected educational benefits and their attribution; they do not establish that every transfer of publicly titled instructional equipment is such a benefit. The present record supplies neither an independently edited expression program nor an individual beneficiary's independent direction of this financed religious use.

**Competing paths.** Reversal of the entire adverse declaration has a strong argument insofar as the Fifth Circuit uses an equipment/textbook distinction and religious affiliation instead of actual use. It does not support approval of the administered religious financing once that fact is fixed. Conversely, affirming the broad prohibition on the strength of actual misuse would transform a bounded use defect into exclusion of lawful secular loans. His Santa Fe dissent insisted on supported operation rather than presumed official devotion; the adopted actual-use premise supplies the operational fact whose absence mattered there, without importing Santa Fe's different event rule.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a confined actual-function opinion and neutral-access protection for genuinely secular uses. Will not join an inference that control, fungibility or religious identity alone proves unconstitutional subsidy, a general separation-of-institutions rule, or displacement of Jackson/Strout merely to avoid their established present scope. The statutory violation and constitutional defect overlap here but the sufficient constitutional ground is actual financing, not every breach of a funding condition. A concurrence is warranted only if needed to preserve genuine independent-benefit distinctions; no required separate writing otherwise. Remedy may address the demonstrated uses without inventing provider-wide exclusion or compulsory new supervision machinery.

### Kennedy

**Commitment.** Vacate the categorical declaration and remand with the actual financed-use defect established and lawful secular applications preserved. His Rosenberger opinion protects independently edited expression on its actual program terms; his Agostini join protects separately controlled secular work. His Jackson and Strout dissents rest on genuinely directed individual educational benefits, not the proposition that neutral allocation makes every expenditure private. Those distinctions do not supply a sufficient basis for approving actual religious instruction financed through this program of publicly acquired and administered materials.

**Competing paths.** The strongest route to complete reversal treats the per-student allocation and private-school choice as placing the resource within the same genuinely independent private benefit he defended in Jackson. The decisive obstacle is the program's direct purchase and loan of secular instructional resources, coupled with the fixed administered-use fact; school choice alone does not establish independent beneficiary direction of that religious use. He does not abandon his prior dissents or adopt a universal rule against private educational benefits. Affirmance of the entire declaration has the serious opposite defect of denying legitimate secular resources solely by recipient character or material type.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the governmental-funded-function distinction, the present violation and tailored remand. His Santa Fe join reinforces that formal private choice does not erase an actually established governmental religious function, but it adds no rule forbidding private religious expression. Will not join characterizations that declare every privately selected curriculum government speech or erase Rosenberger's financing holding. Willing to join the common opinion with these limits; an independent-benefit concurrence is optional only if it adds a needed qualification. No immediate blanket funding cutoff follows from a premise establishing no quantity or prevalence.

### Souter

**Commitment.** Vacate the categorical judgment at the reviewed scope and remand for effective relief against the established religious instructional financing. His Agostini dissent supplies a stronger concern about direct institutional instructional support, but his full Jackson join and own Strout opinion support this sufficient actual-function ground without reviving independent Lemon prongs. The government cannot purchase the integrated devotional work merely because its materials have public title and are allocated neutrally.

**Competing paths.** Affirming the whole lower declaration is supported by Meek/Wolman's historic concerns and by his own institutional-support objection. The strongest counterargument is that the decree makes the equipment category and sectarian identity decisive, beyond the actual financing established, and would reach the reliably separable secular work current law protects. He can preserve the concern without maintaining that overbroad remedial rule. Complete reversal relies on facial statutory controls; it fails because those commands do not negate the adopted fact about actual administration. His Southworth concurrence involves an independent-expression distribution program and does not erase that fact.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join a common actual-use opinion with explicit reliable-accountability and funded-function qualifications. Will not join a declaration that direct institutional aid is constitutionally harmless whenever neutral, that nominal secular labels suffice, or that every religious activity must be attributable as government speech before expenditure can be prohibited. Nor does his ground require segregated corporate governance or entirely separate personnel in every case. A separate concurrence is needed only if a lead opinion unnecessarily rejects his preserved structural concern. Relief must actually prevent the supported defect; ordinary remand implementation supplies no permission to keep financing it indefinitely.

### Thomas

**Commitment.** Vacate the categorical equipment declaration and remand to confine correction to the demonstrated actual religious financing. His Agostini and Rosenberger joins reject affiliation-based presumptions and protect genuinely secular services and independent private expression, while retaining the prohibition on actual devotional financing. His Jackson and Strout joins in dissent preserve genuinely directed individual benefits, not a categorical immunity for all neutral material programs. There is no supported need to overrule those intervening financing decisions to resolve this dispute.

**Competing paths.** Complete reversal is persuasive against the lower rationale that ordinary instructional equipment is constitutionally different from textbooks solely because it can be diverted. The fixed actual-use fact forecloses turning that criticism into approval of this administered application. Affirming all equipment exclusions is equally unsound because actual abuse in an unspecified subset does not prove every religious provider or lawful secular loan unconstitutional. His Santa Fe dissent's objection to anticipated rather than established operation does not eliminate an expressly established operative premise here.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the actual-use rule and correction at its supported scope, with neutral access for otherwise lawful secular applications. Will not join presumed inculcation, a general divertibility test, universal attribution of a religious school's work to government, or a revival of Lemon's independent prongs. No statutory noncompliance rule alone becomes a constitutional holding. A common opinion can carry his position without a separate writing; if broader institutional-support theory is included, he withholds that portion and may state the narrower basis. No damages, recoupment or unreviewed state-program determination is authorized.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment.** Vacate the categorical declaration and remand for relief against the established financed religious use, preserving lawful severable applications. Her Agostini substantive join and separate procedural dissent do not convert this ordinary merits review into a Rule 60 dispute. Her Jackson and Strout joins supply the current sufficient financing and remedial rules. Public administration in law does not eliminate recipient-controlled religious use in fact.

**Competing paths.** Affirming the broad lower judgment offers a simple means of preventing direct religious support and accords with part of her prior structural concern. It nevertheless extends the relief beyond demonstrated religious use into otherwise lawful secular loans, contrary to the limits she joined in Jackson. Complete reversal would correctly reject sectarian identity as conclusive but leave the established constitutional defect unanswered. Her Santa Fe join similarly distinguishes actual official function from private labels without making school location alone dispositive.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the actual-function violation, rejection of the lower categorical scope and confined remand. Will not join an opinion declaring all institutional aid presumptively valid, replacing the inquiry with neutrality alone, or using this application to erase the distinct existing rules governing genuinely private expression. No need for a separate writing if the sufficient ground and limits are fully carried; otherwise she may preserve the direct-support concern with Stevens and Souter without enlarging the judgment. The remedy must prevent the proved financed function while allowing ordinary determination of item, provider and severability scope; missing quantity supplies no all-program presumption.

### Breyer

**Commitment.** Vacate the reviewed categorical declaration and remand for confined correction of actual financed religious use. His Jackson and Strout joins directly support that result. His Agostini joins were limited to precedent and procedure; he did not adopt Souter's affirmative direct-aid objection and does not now acquire it by grouping. His Southworth concurrence and Santa Fe opinion emphasize what the program actually does rather than using a general funding or private-speaker label as the answer.

**Competing paths.** Categorical reversal has substantial force because a neutral program of useful secular resources need not be forbidden by the recipient's identity or an assumed risk of diversion. The adopted actual-use premise supplies a materially different defect that must receive relief. Categorical affirmance avoids that defect but sweeps beyond it and can exclude lawful secular work without examining the actual function. The strongest criticism of a remedial remand is additional delay; bounded implementation is nevertheless required because the premise fixes the prohibited use, not its quantity, every provider's conduct or a ready-made statewide remedy.

**Joins, limits and remedy.** Will join the funded-function holding, preservation of genuine secular and private-expression relationships, and precise remand without an automatic trial, new audit system or complete institutional separation. Will not join a revived independent-prong Lemon rule or the unadopted Agostini structural dissent as an alternative controlling rationale. No independent separate writing is required if those limits are preserved. The lower court may use the existing record when sufficient and must provide effective relief against the actual violation, not reopen the premise as an unanswered hypothetical.

## Proposition and remedy boundaries for later stages

The eight commitments independently permit a common core: actual public financing of the religious function established here cannot be sustained by neutral eligibility, nominal public title or secular-use commands; religious identity and equipment classification alone do not justify the Fifth Circuit's full categorical declaration; the reviewed declaration should be vacated and the case remanded for effective relief against the actual defect while preserving lawful severable applications. This is no claim about Stone or a final Court count.

Meek and Wolman's equipment/library-book distinction cannot control as a conclusive affiliation-based prohibition inconsistent with the actual funded-function inquiry and protected secular relationships. This does not reopen their unrelated holdings or displace every safeguard. Any stronger proposed overruling requires its own supported analysis and the individual join limits above. Textbooks are not automatically made unlawful, and equipment is not automatically approved.

The remand concerns the lawful scope and implementation of relief, not whether the adopted actual-use premise occurred. It should identify the reviewed federal declaration and any genuinely dependent state-law consequence without assuming that every program in the larger litigation is before the Court. No invented incident, material count, school, waiver, administrative regime, recoupment or damages is authorized. Different broader theories remain separate and cannot be aggregated into controlling law.
