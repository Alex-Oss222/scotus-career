## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Ninth Circuit.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Apply Rule 24 and the impartial-jury guarantee; separate the for-cause error from denial of allotted peremptories. Leave the conviction undisturbed as against this jury-selection claim.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Defendant is denied a constitutional or Rule-based right

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** A defendant who preserves the for-cause objection, uses an allotted peremptory challenge to remove the juror, and is tried by an impartial jury suffers no constitutional or Rule 24 deprivation merely because the remaining challenges are exhausted. This separates a mistaken for-cause ruling from the constitutional question whether the jury actually seated was impartial and the prescribed peremptory allotment remained available. Do not approve the for-cause error, compel curative use, or decide a case involving a biased seated juror, denial of the prescribed allotment, or an independent *Batson* violation.
