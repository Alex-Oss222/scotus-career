* **Respondent’s or appellee’s strongest supported position:** A State may use a procedure different from the precise *Anders* format, but it may not permit appointed appellate counsel to withhold every arguably supportive issue and shift the search for such issues to the court or the indigent defendant. California's *Wende* procedure therefore fails to provide the meaningful adversarial representation required on a first appeal as of right.
* **Necessary concessions and limiting principles:** A State remains free to adopt a genuinely equivalent alternative that preserves counsel's active advocate role and gives the reviewing court a focused basis for determining frivolousness.
