# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Joint motion granted and decree entered; no vote tally was announced.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** The Court entered the decree without a separately reported merits opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** The decree implemented the Court’s 1997 submerged-lands judgment and established the operative boundary and lease-revenue terms.
* **Historical Authority:** Decree in an original action.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-530/USREPORTS-530-1021); Official U.S. Reports, 530 U.S. 1021 (2000); United States v. Alaska, 521 U.S. 1 (1997).
