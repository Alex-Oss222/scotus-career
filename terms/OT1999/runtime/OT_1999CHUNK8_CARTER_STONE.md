## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Elements comparison under Rule 31(c)

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the elements question because it is dispositive of the requested instruction.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Bank larceny is not a lesser included offense of bank robbery because § 2113(b) contains elements that § 2113(a) does not require.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** An offense under § 2113(b) is not included within § 2113(a) under Rule 31(c), because the larceny provision requires proof of additional statutory elements.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The elements test protects notice, charging responsibility, and the jury’s lawful choices. Carter’s factual theory cannot erase § 2113(b)’s separate intent, taking-and-carrying-away, and value requirements. A court may not rewrite the statutes into a common-law hierarchy because both concern bank property.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Stone does not decide whether the evidence supported an instruction on another offense, whether the indictment alleged additional elements, or any independent trial error.
