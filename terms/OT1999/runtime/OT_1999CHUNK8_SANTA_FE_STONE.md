## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Does the District’s policy permitting student-led student-initiated prayer

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Affirm. The invocation occurs at a school event, through school equipment, under school supervision, pursuant to a policy designed to produce prayer, and after a majority vote determining whether the religious exercise occurs. Routing the decision through students does not remove public attribution or the minority student's coercive exclusion.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A public school may not use a student election to authorize an invocation institutionally structured and delivered as part of a school event.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Affirm. The invocation occurs at a school event, through school equipment, under school supervision, pursuant to a policy designed to produce prayer, and after a majority vote determining whether the religious exercise occurs. Routing the decision through students does not remove public attribution or the minority student's coercive exclusion. Lee’s official-sponsorship ground applies without demanding proof that every spectator felt coerced. Unlike Pinette’s open forum, the school organizes the invocation through its policy and event machinery; a student majority cannot make that official religious exercise private.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Voluntary private prayer, spontaneous student expression, and genuine neutral-access forums without school selection, control, financing, or attribution remain private speech.
