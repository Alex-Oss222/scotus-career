### Issue 1: May California require political parties to select their

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Reverse. Selection of a party's official nominee is a central associational act, and California compels each party to let nonmembers, including adherents of rival parties, determine that choice. Stone finds that difference severe enough to demand the most exacting justification; he does not infer that every primary regulation has that effect.
