## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Does an HMO physician act in an ERISA

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits of this issue. No separate threshold or vehicle bar identified in Section I prevents decision.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Reverse and direct dismissal of the ERISA fiduciary claim. A treating physician does not act as an ERISA fiduciary when making an inseparable mixed decision about medically appropriate treatment and the benefit delivered through that treatment. This respects ERISA's fiduciary boundary and avoid federalizing ordinary medical-treatment decisions merely because an HMO structures payment incentives.
