## Common undertaking, breach and scope commitments

**1. Actual promise and allocated risk.** All eight Associates would reject the Federal Circuit's basis for denying contractual relief. The Government sold a conditional opportunity to pursue exploration and development through the incorporated legal process over the lease term. The incorporated OCSLA exploration-plan procedure is a material part of that opportunity. Following necessary modifications, it calls for approval within thirty days of submission unless the specified serious-harm ground, not avoidable by modification, requires disapproval. This is neither a free-standing implication from regulation nor a guarantee of approval, drilling, state concurrence, discovery or profit: the executed leases expressly incorporate the identified statutory and regulatory regime as the terms of the purchased rights.

Section 1 identifies existing regulations and specifically includes future regulations for prevention of waste, conservation of OCS resources and protection of correlative rights. Its other-applicable-law language, read with the transaction's operative rights and reservations, does not authorize replacement of the contracted process by every future statute without monetary consequences. Sections 9–10 require approved plans and regulatory compliance; §13 reserves actual OCSLA suspension/cancellation and the distinct wartime/national-emergency route; §19 preserves ungranted rights with its particular noninterference and defense limits. These are real qualifications, not a promise of immunity from future law or a cancellation of the identified process undertaking.

**2. Material displacement and repudiation.** OBPA imposed new, geographically defined prerequisites and a mandatory bar on six activities, including plan approval, irrespective of whether an individual proposal satisfied the original approval criteria. Its minimum interval and new reporting/review conditions materially displaced the purchased process. The Secretary could disagree with the scientific panel through the prescribed explanation; that feature does not eliminate the additional statutory gate. Nor does the Act merely reproduce the original suspension authority: national-interest suspension under §1334(a)(1)(A) requires a lessee request; serious-harm suspension under (B) has its own ground. The packet establishes no cancellation findings and does not equate environmental concern with the required harm finding.

For the four unit leases the submitted final plan brings the actual process deprivation into view. For the fifth, OBPA's categorical refusal to allow the original approval route supplies an anticipatory-repudiation theory; no nonexistent application or denial is invented. An eventual possibility of approval under altered terms is not performance of the original undertaking. This conclusion depends on the substantial statutory displacement, not a rule that every short administrative delay is total breach. Repeal in 1996 does not retrospectively erase the deprivation or automatically undo an intervening election to seek restoration.

**3. Independent permits.** North Carolina's objections and Commerce's September 1994 refusal to override remain legally effective obstacles to operations. They do not prove that the Government supplied the distinct approval process it sold. A claim for restoration of consideration after material repudiation differs from expectation damages requiring proof of drilling success and resulting profits. The absence of a guaranteed profitable outcome therefore does not by itself defeat restitution. Materiality must still concern a real part of the bargain: here the contracted ability to obtain timely consideration, within the original conditions, is substantive rather than an inconsequential procedural preference.

**4. Sovereign authority and defenses.** Congress remained free to enact OBPA, and the lessees had to obey it. The case concerns contractual monetary responsibility for an assumed risk, not an injunction or regulatory veto. Winstar's common assumed-risk ground supplies a sufficient answer even assuming the legislation is public and general. The additional unmistakability and substantial-effect accounts remain distinct as described below; neither is imported as a majority rule from Winstar. Actual leasing authority and executed contract terms supply the undertaking; the Tucker Act alone and fairness do not.

## Enhanced alternative-path analysis

### Promise, suspension and materiality

**Government-judgment path.** The leases conveyed a priority to pursue uncertain development under extensive existing environmental and permitting powers, with suspensions and extensions preserving the opportunity. Independent state objections prevented drilling, and a changed review interval might produce no compensable loss. The strongest support is the whole-contract regulatory structure and the lessees' negotiated 1989 MOU. The strongest objection is that this approach collapses the promised consideration process into eventual drilling and reads a new statutory prohibition as though it were an exercise of the original conditional powers. All eight decline that collapse. Their Winstar positions require enforcing the actual undertaking with its limits, rather than either a guarantee of profits or a right to retain consideration without the purchased performance.

**Breach/process path, adopted.** The express incorporation and specified future changes identify a contracted legal route. OBPA withholds that route for a substantial period and substitutes new conditions; this materially changes the paid-for opportunity even if state concurrence might ultimately fail. The strongest objection is that the Secretary already could conduct environmental analysis or suspend, and the panel did not have a veto. Those points preserve genuine original discretion, but do not establish that the categorical legislative prohibition rested on or complied with the predicates of that discretion. No actual cancellation entitlement is presumed. All eight accept liability at this scope without determining what later delay after the congressional period was independently caused by the Secretary. The established initial displacement is sufficient; uncertain later history is not needed to enlarge it.

### Fifth lease: anticipatory repudiation or wait for an application

**Wait-for-performance path.** No plan was submitted for the fifth tract, the legislation could end, and the Government did not announce that it would never permit drilling. The strongest objection to recovery is therefore the absence of a presently denied individual application.

**Anticipatory path, adopted at the process level.** Roehm recognizes an immediate claim when a party unequivocally disables the promised future performance; the legally significant promise here is performance under the contracted route, not eventual performance under any substituted conditions. The enacted prohibition covered the fifth lease as well as the four unit leases and materially withheld that route. Its duration was qualified but not an ordinary processing interval. The petitioners need not submit a futile application merely to demonstrate that statutory bar. This does not establish a fifth-tract permit entitlement, actual drilling plan, particular benefit received, or identical dollar recovery. Restitution and any election inquiry must still treat the tract and each petitioner's interest at their supported scope.

### Election: restoration available now or further adjudication first

**Restoration-entitlement path, adopted by seven Associates.** A request that the breaching promisor perform or retract does not itself accept substitute performance or surrender the response to a material breach. The plan submission and efforts to overcome a separate state obstacle are consistent with seeking the original bargain. The promised MOU suspensions and preservation of time while the dispute proceeds do not, on these supplied facts, establish acceptance of the withdrawn process as performed. The 1992 suit gives an affirmative restoration election; subsequent preservation of contested rights is not automatically its abandonment. No new promise, settlement, retraction, successful approval, or equivalent substitute performance is found.

The strongest objection is substantial: extensions are contractual rights of value, and the companies affirmatively sought them after knowing of OBPA, including during litigation. Calling the requests mere protest would understate the defense. The seven distinguish the value of preserved time, which may bear on restoration and cognizable benefit accounting, from acceptance of the repudiated performance sufficient to foreclose the remedy. They resolve the legal defense even allowing the documented requests and extensions; they do not depend on the broader, contradicted claim that all suspensions were continuously rent-free. They do not hold that actual acceptance of materially useful substituted performance could never be an election.

**Election-first path, adopted by Stevens.** Requests to compel an original promise do not themselves waive a remedy, but obtaining and retaining extensions of the very leased opportunity can amount to continued contractual performance. The preexisting MOU character does not remove their contractual nature. The combination of the post-enactment application, pursuit of authorizations and accepted extended term therefore requires an actual election determination before a present entitlement to undo the bargain is declared. The strongest objection is that the suit made the restoration choice clear and the extensions merely preserved the dispute's subject. Stevens finds that explanation legally possible, but not conclusive from the packet's recitation alone. He would reject the no-breach rationale while remanding the properly presented election issue, including its preservation and actual scope for each petitioner and lease, before deciding entitlement. He does not find waiver established, rely on an unrecovered letter, or impute Mobil's individual conduct to Marathon without a basis.

### Restitution, expectation and amount

Restoration of transferred consideration for the unperformed bargain does not require proof that the venture would have yielded profits. This does not make the bonuses an automatic measure of every loss or excuse restoration of retained rights and legally cognizable benefits. Behan distinguishes proven outlay from speculative profits, but its damages analysis is not itself a complete restitution formula. Pence concerns election after fraud, not this contract's performance, and supplies only the caution against treating demand, delay or continued conduct without context as an automatic election. Roehm supplies the anticipatory framework, not an exact refund amount.

The trial court held a benefits hearing, awarded bonus consideration and denied rentals; the Federal Circuit left the Government's amount objections undecided. Seven Associates would recognize restitution entitlement and remand for those genuinely unresolved, properly preserved monetary issues. Stevens would reach that stage only after resolving election. No Associate supplies an exact award from rounded reports, declares no offset possible, reopens every evidentiary issue, or mandates a trial/hearing regardless of the existing record. Any award must account for restoration of the corresponding contractual rights. Profits, annual rentals and interest are separate; interest requires its own authority. Statutory cancellation compensation under §1334(a)(2), with its hearing, harm, duration and formula qualifications, is not the remedy entered here.

## Compatibility and remedy handoff

All eight reject the Federal Circuit's independent-permit/no-operative-causation reason for denying the actual-process contract claim. Seven (O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer) support present restitution entitlement with ordinary remand of unresolved amount/restoration matters. Stevens supports correction of that liability rationale but requires a preceding election determination and therefore withholds the present entitlement/remedy join. These are provisional Associate positions, not a final Court count.

A shared Court rationale can rest on the actual authorized undertaking, its materially displaced process, the fifth lease's anticipatory posture, assumed risk, the distinction between restitution and speculative expectation, the confined rejection of the documented election defense, and a bounded monetary remand. Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Ginsburg require satisfaction of unmistakability; Souter, Stevens, O'Connor and Breyer permit their narrower no-effective-sovereign-surrender analysis. No forced fusion of those accounts is authorized. A rationale that says simply that all money suits avoid unmistakability is not compatible. Nor is a new substantial-effect test necessary to any provisional result.

The judgment must address both docketed petitioners and distinguish the four unit leases from the fifth. The remand is to the Federal Circuit for remaining lawful proceedings, not a drilling directive or automatic new hearing. The Court need not decide contested waiver preservation on material outside the record to reject the supplied legal defense; Stevens's alternative preserves that question if his remedy is selected. The lower bonus award, rental denial and unaddressed Government calculation objections are not rewritten as an affirmed exact sum. No current lease status, later renewal, payment history, quantified benefit, offset, or statutory cancellation finding is supplied by inference.
