## Reach, independent components and common commitments

All eight Associates participate and reach all three granted constitutional questions. Gillespie has concrete personal injury from the federal disability and consequent exclusion from armed employment. Removing the challenged federal restriction would remove that legal obstacle, even though the City retains independent employment authority. His standing does not depend on proving an unlimited arms right; nor does a claim concerning the constitutional allocation of federal power become unavailable merely because the City defends the enactment. This conclusion concerns a directly regulated person, not a general license to litigate every State interest.

The Police Department and Chief Zunk remain dismissed in their redundant capacities. ## Governing propositions and qualifications

**Personal right and practical burden.** The register *Personal arms protection, incorporation and necessary acquisition*, effective through Love, December 11, 1995, and Hale, February 12, 1996, identifies the individual holder and keeps coverage distinct from lawful regulation. Wright, October 5, 1998, supplies *Core-Preservation Review*. Stage 1 identifies protected keeping, bearing, acquisition, necessary ammunition or transportation through constitutional text, history and precedent. Stage 2 examines the restriction's practical operation. Core deprivation requires a compelling interest, narrow tailoring and no comparably effective materially less restrictive alternative; substantial regulation requires an important objective, substantial relationship and no unnecessary or effective elimination of protected activity; genuinely incidental regulation without meaningful interference receives deferential reasonableness review, retaining prohibitions on arbitrary administration, discrimination and cumulative destruction. The independent boundary against abolishing meaningful ordinary defensive home possession remains. Routine labels do not determine the tier.

FOP, October 7, 1998, already applies that framework to this qualifying disability and official-use exclusion, with all eight Associates joining Souter's opinion. Preventing armed recurrence is important. The adjudicated violent conduct, defined domestic relationship, counsel and jury-where-entitled safeguards, and conviction-relief qualifications connect the disability substantially to that objective. A service firearm remains accessible to its possessor; long service and governmental use do not eliminate that connection. This is a substantial burden, not incidental paperwork, but the present record supplies no distinct home-possession application or general abolition of defensive arms. The personal right supplies no automatic official-use exemption or fundamental entitlement to a public armed job.

**Commerce mechanism and separate nexus.** The register *Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects* retains Lopez's identification of the enacted class and its supported interstate mechanism before aggregation. Hiley, October 9, 1998, applies that sequence to the same recipient class and section 922(d)(9) supply prohibition. January 18 FOP unanimously reaffirms that precise connection. Inclusion of recipients' acquisition and retention prevents evasion or material impairment of the valid supply prohibition. The mechanism is neither generalized domestic-violence costs nor a remote firearm journey. The enacted shipment, possession and receipt branches remain distinct. The accepted interstate premise suffices for the civil challenge presented; the Court does not determine every prosecution element or transfer section 922(d)(9)'s knowledge-or-reasonable-cause term as the complete culpability rule of section 922(g)(9).

**No administrative command.** Hiley and January 18 FOP distinguish a substantive restriction from a command to govern other persons. New York and Printz retain their actual anti-compulsion limits; no need exists to enlarge their assistance rules. Garcia and Condon support the difference between direct regulation and conscription but valid commerce authority alone does not answer every possible structural objection. This particular staffing theory fails because its asserted command is absent. Neither the City nor the Court is required by this judgment to choose termination over another independently lawful employment response.

**Statutory limits retained.** Section 921(a)(33)'s qualifying offense is a federal or state misdemeanor with an element of use or attempted use of physical force or threatened use of a deadly weapon, committed by a current or former spouse, parent or guardian; a person sharing a child; a current or former cohabitant as spouse, parent or guardian; or a similarly situated person. Counsel or knowing and intelligent waiver is required; a jurisdictional jury entitlement requires trial or knowing and intelligent waiver by plea or otherwise, without creating a new entitlement. Expungement, set-aside, pardon and qualified civil-rights restoration retain their statutory operation. The express-firearm reservation names pardon, expungement and restoration, not separately set-aside; nothing here enlarges it. The source's (a)(33)(A) cross-reference to (C) is not silently repaired into a new exception.

Section 925(a)(1)'s governmental channels concern items imported for, sold or shipped to, or issued for use of the specified federal, state and local entities; it is not an exemption for every private weapon of a public employee. Its exceptions for sections 922(d)(9), 922(g)(9) and section 922(p) remain distinct. Section 922(d)'s separate exception for the specified section 925(b) licensees or recipients of section 925(c) relief is not an officer exemption. Section 925(c)'s discretionary public-safety/public-interest procedure and actual-denial review establish no funded process, actual denial, jurisdiction or relief entitlement here. The present application disputes neither conviction classification nor a statutory safeguard.

## Adversarial comparison and Justice-specific basis

The meaningful alternative is vacatur for an individual application under Wright, not reliance on the displaced militia-only theory. Its strongest argument is that a personal plaintiff's serious loss of armed work deserves independent constitutional justification rather than automatic association-case treatment. The answer is that the substantive justification has already been adopted for the same official-use disability, and the supported facts disclose no differing predicate, safeguard failure, private-home burden or restoration claim. Wright remanded an unresolved machinegun application, expressly without establishing automatic remand. A second hearing is not itself constitutional protection where the accepted facts and controlling rule decide the preserved claim.

The competing commerce path would either sustain the law because the gun once crossed a border or invalidate possession as necessarily local. The former conflicts with Lopez and Hiley's separation of constitutional mechanism from enacted nexus; the latter disregards Hiley's necessary-inclusion holding. The competing staffing path treats the practical inability to retain an armed officer as a command. That accounts for the injury but not for the statute's actual operation; the law neither selects the employment response nor directs state enforcement. These arguments preserve meaningful future boundaries without supplying a contrary disposition here.
