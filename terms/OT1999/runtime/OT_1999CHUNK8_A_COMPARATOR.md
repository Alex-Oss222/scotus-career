# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor, J., delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court. Ginsburg, J., filed a concurring opinion, post, p. 154.
* **Historical Holding:** 1. A plaintiff ’s prima facie case of discrimination (as defined in McDonnell Douglas Corp. v. # STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Carter v. United States*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas delivered the Court’s opinion, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Scalia, and Kennedy; Justice Ginsburg dissented, joined by Justices Stevens, Souter, and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** Bank larceny under § 2113(b) is not a lesser included offense of bank robbery under § 2113(a) because it requires proof of elements not contained in the robbery offense.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-530/USREPORTS-530-255); Official U.S. Reports, 530 U.S. 255 (2000).

# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Bradley v. Arkansas Department of Education*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits decision. The historical anchor is the Eighth Circuit’s affirmance of the IDEA immunity ruling on the funding-waiver ground.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** No Supreme Court merits opinion applies. The historical comparator is the published Eighth Circuit panel decision in the consolidated appeals; the Bradley IDEA disposition is distinct from the separate Jim C. Section 504 disposition.
* **Historical Holding:** The Eighth Circuit allowed the IDEA actions on the funding-consent ground while rejecting blanket §5 abrogation and recognizing prospective officer relief. In the related Jim C. appeal, the panel rejected the separate §504 immunity theory. These distinct historical procedural results do not merge the two claims or make the §504 component part of the simulation grant.
* **Historical Authority:** Published lower-court panel opinion only; it is the historical comparator and is not current simulated law.
* **Historical Sources:** Published Eighth Circuit opinion, 189 F.3d 745, submitted September 24, 1998, and filed August 31, 1999.
