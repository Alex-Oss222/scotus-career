## Components and common legal boundary

The additional authorized public reading supplement, `../entering-law/OT_1999CHUNK5_DOE_CURRENT_TERM.md`, was read before freeze. Carter's eight-Associate opinion preserves injury, attribution, culpability and causation as distinct questions and supplies a directly established Ginsburg position against equating a deprivation with governmental responsibility. Its municipal-pleading holding does not require reopening summary judgment here. Powell's distinct statutory-right and private-remedy inquiries concern an effects-only Title VI theory, not this constitutional claim; neither its controlling rule nor the four Associates' public dissent supplies the missing private-actor attribution predicate. Those current-term decisions leave the commitments below unchanged.

The relevant choices are Rosenberg's referral and hospital-security transport, and the hospital physicians' §9.27 certification and confinement. The actors established by this record are private. A state official's enforcement or joint decision is not established. Later judicial review ordering release is a separate public act and does not retroactively make the initial private choices the State's decisions.

The common affirmance ground is choice-specific attribution: permission to invoke a regulated commitment procedure, medical licensing and review do not alone supply governmental compulsion, significant encouragement, joint participation or a traditionally exclusive public function. The particular confinement function must be examined rather than described merely as medicine; the record's private-commitment history and absence of governmental participation defeat the exclusive-function and joint-action theories here. A State's restriction of private confinement to specified circumstances is not itself a direction to confine this patient. No blanket immunity for physicians or all privately run institutions is adopted.

All eight Associates provisionally would affirm the Second Circuit on attribution for both identified components. They would leave the federal summary judgment and §1367(c)(3) dismissal of pendent claims in place, without adjudicating the constitutional or state-law merits. Each commitment adopts only the stated fact-bounded rationale and reservations below.

## Justice-specific commitments and adversarial alternatives

### Stevens

**Commitment:** Affirm both attribution components on the identified private conduct; join the common rule only with an express distinction between authorization and actual delegation of an exclusively governmental confinement function. His recorded Sullivan position declined the unnecessary attribution ground rather than deciding that all carriers were state actors. That reservation does not prevent deciding attribution when it is the granted, dispositive question and no alternative merits ground has been adjudicated. His participation in Edmonson's public-jury-function inquiry supplies the relevant distinction between private choice and private exercise of a governmental institutional power.

**Strongest opposite path:** Treat involuntary detention, unlike withholding a disputed payment, as exercise of sovereign coercion delegated by statute. West prevents describing professional judgment as an automatic answer to delegation. **Barrier:** Edmonson involved actual governmental jury machinery; West involved delivery of a governmental custodial duty. Here the hospital-security transport and private certification precede judicial intervention, and the record supports a history of private confinement. Statutory permission without the necessary governmental function or participation remains insufficient under Sullivan. He would not join language declaring transportation constitutionally insignificant, dangerousness a purely medical question, or every licensed confinement private.

### O'Connor

**Commitment:** Affirm both components under the Sullivan attribution proposition she joined. Blum and Rendell-Baker distinguish regulation from responsibility for the challenged decision. The actual state-conduct predicates are absent and the private-history evidence defeats the proposed exclusive-function characterization at this scope.

**Strongest opposite path:** Statutory certification confers immediately effective power over liberty and therefore significant encouragement. **Barrier:** A permission bounded by safeguards is not a State command, and the resulting power is not shown to be historically exclusive to government. The opposite path would erase the separate attribution inquiry Sullivan and Lugar retain. She can join an express reservation for actual official direction, joint confinement or a materially different governmental contract; no new inquiry is ordered on merely hypothetical facts.

### Scalia

**Commitment:** Affirm referral/transport and certification/confinement. His Sullivan join supports distinguishing state-created legal permission from state responsibility. A general liberty deprivation does not itself establish the state actor required for the federal constitutional claim; regulation and subsequent judicial availability cannot replace that element.

**Strongest opposite path:** Any legally authorized coercive confinement is a public function. **Barrier:** That formulation expands the exclusive-function category beyond the supported historical predicate and would transform numerous permitted private actions into constitutional action. Lugar's separate requirements and the actual record foreclose the expansion. He would not join a newly created balancing test measuring the degree of liberty loss against the density of regulation, nor dicta deciding actual police participation not present here.

### Kennedy

**Commitment:** Affirm both components. His Sullivan attribution opinion supplies the governing particular-choice inquiry, and Edmonson's government-administered jury function supplies a real counterexample rather than a universal state-permission rule. The hospital licensing relationship described does not establish permanent public control or a command concerning Doe.

**Strongest opposite path:** West shows a discretionary professional can perform a delegated governmental obligation, and detention invokes authority unavailable in ordinary private relationships. **Barrier:** Doe was not already in government custody receiving the State's constitutionally required care; the demonstrated institutional and decisional facts do not establish the missing duty delegation or public participation. Join requires preserving the possibility of attribution on those different facts and refusing to equate dangerousness with medical treatment alone.

### Souter

**Commitment:** Affirm both components at the same record-bounded level as his Sullivan attribution join. The governmental relationship must explain responsibility for this decision, not simply the availability of a statutory procedure. The supplied record identifies neither a compelled choice nor actual official enforcement.

**Strongest opposite path:** Certification and confinement are sufficiently integrated with statutory judicial oversight to make the system governmental. **Barrier:** Post-deprivation review ending the confinement does not show State approval of its initiation, and the neutral record's history prevents treating this confinement function as exclusively public. He would reserve materially different contracts, official initiation or actual joint action, but would not remand to seek facts supported only by speculation or revive the ungranted discovery issue.

### Thomas

**Commitment:** Affirm each component under his Sullivan attribution join. Statutory permission does not satisfy the actor element, and neither the degree of regulation nor importance of liberty permits replacing the exclusive-government-function predicate with a function merely regulated by government.

**Strongest opposite path:** Private confinement requires legal immunity and therefore is itself a governmental delegation. **Barrier:** A state-created privilege is only one part of Lugar's attribution inquiry; no joint public actor or exclusive governmental activity is established. He can join the actual-record exceptions but not a categorical new delegation rule covering every private invocation of state protective legislation.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment:** Affirm both components on the confined attribution ground. Her Sullivan reservation was a decision not to reach that additional question, not an affirmative attribution ruling. Here state action is directly granted and dispositive, so that reservation affords no alternative disposition. She applies the Court's existing particular-conduct rule while preserving meaningful relief for a proved official deprivation.

**Strongest opposite path:** The statutory confinement power, licensing arrangement and judicial release mechanism together constitute state responsibility for restraint. **Barrier:** The established relationship does not show governmental direction of this patient's confinement, and later review is not initiation. The rule preserves actual joint action and governmental duty delegation; it does not deny that confinement substantially affects liberty. She would withhold assent from the district court's separate characterization of transport as insignificant, from treating Doe's disputed dangerousness as established, or from deciding the underlying Fourth/Fourteenth Amendment claims.

### Breyer

**Commitment:** Affirm both components under his Sullivan attribution join, examining the practical operation as well as the legal description of this procedure. Actual certification came from private doctors and hospital security performed the transport; licensing and review supplied limits rather than a governmental selection of Doe for confinement.

**Strongest opposite path:** A system of immediate private restraint backed by state-created authority can function as delegated coercive government. **Barrier:** The necessary delegation or governmental participation has not been established here, and the private-function history matters. West remains available when the State entrusts its own custodial duty to a private physician. He would join an explicit confined ruling, not a universal assertion that medical discretion defeats attribution or that regulatory detail is always irrelevant.

## Operative qualifications, remedy and unresolved matters

The §9.27(a) authorization, §9.27(d) alternatives requirement, §9.01 definitions, and New York decisions requiring dangerousness remain distinct. The commitment does not transform the lower court's dangerousness construction into words of §9.27(a), decide the truth of the disputed medical accounts, or decide whether the statutory conditions were actually satisfied. Unused conditional restraints supply no separate use-of-restraints holding. Sections 9.39, 31.02 and 31.27 do not replace the actual §9.27 basis.

Sullivan is applied to its actual attribution rule; Blum and Rendell-Baker supply the connection between regulation and the particular act; Lugar preserves distinct legal-authorization and governmental-attribution elements. Edmonson and West are distinguished by governmental participation and duty delegation, retaining full force in their proper settings. No earlier authority is overruled or narrowed into a blanket private-hospital exemption.

Affirmance leaves Doe's federal judgment adverse and her pendent claims dismissed without a decision of their merits. It orders no damages, new confinement, release, mandatory discovery or hearing. The Supreme Court's granted review ends; any otherwise available state-law proceeding rests on its own procedural footing. A different conclusion depending on unestablished official enforcement, different contract terms or an independent discovery grant would require a corrected neutral predicate and fresh affected stages.

No Associate requests a separate writing solely to repeat these common limits. A formulation omitting a material reservation would require the corresponding join to be reconsidered; this handoff contains no fictional circulation history.
