# STONE PRE-VOTE CASE BRIEF

*Boy Scouts of America v. Dale*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist, C. J., delivered the opinion of the Court, in which O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas, JJ., joined. Stevens, J., filed a dissenting opinion, in which Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer, JJ., joined, post, p. 663. Souter, J., filed a dissenting opinion, in which Ginsburg and Breyer, JJ., joined, post, p. 700.
* **Historical Holding:** Applying New Jersey’s public accommodations law to require the Boy Scouts to readmit Dale violates the Boy Scouts’ First Amendment right of expressive association. Government actions that unconstitutionally burden that right may take many forms, one of which is intrusion into a group’s internal affairs by forcing it to accept a member it does not desire.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at the scope supported by the joins stated above.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official U.S. Reports source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-530/USREPORTS-530-640); Official U.S. Reports, 530 U.S. 640 (2000); SCDB case-centered vote metadata for the reported judgment tally.
