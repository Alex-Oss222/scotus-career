## Review and component separation

The certiorari writ concerns the Seventh Circuit's affirmance of the §844(i) conviction and, only if statutory coverage exists, the constitutionality of that coverage. No supplied threshold defect prevents reaching statutory construction. The fire damaged the cousin's owner-occupied residence. The government's identified interstate connections are an Oklahoma mortgage lender, Wisconsin insurer and interstate natural gas. The validated packet supports no former-rental, home-office or other qualifying property use. No personal injury occurred. The separate §924(c) and §5861(f) convictions are not independently submitted for decision on all their elements.

The statutory words require property used in interstate or foreign commerce or in an activity affecting that commerce. The function of the damaged property and the connection of that function to commerce must both be identified. Hinds rejects a universal profit-making, exclusively commercial, rental or sales prerequisite and rejects categorical religious/nonprofit exclusion. It does not render every consumer relationship sufficient or eliminate the independent property-use requirement.

## Proposed common statutory proposition

All eight Associates would hold that an owner-occupied dwelling used as a private residence is not brought within §844(i) solely because its owner has an out-of-state mortgage, insurance or gas supply. Those relationships finance, protect or support the residential use; without a further function connecting the building's actual use to commerce, they do not establish that the building is used in the lender's, insurer's or utility's interstate business. The breadth of activity affecting commerce does not erase used in or substitute every economic incident of ownership for the enacted property function.

This is a conclusion about the asserted connections and established residential use, not an exemption for all owner-occupied homes or all noncommercial property. Rental use remains sufficient in Russell's setting, but is not the exclusive permitted use. A genuinely different use must be assessed under Hinds; the Court neither invents such a use here nor rules on hypothetical home businesses, mixed-use premises or institutional functions. The holding does not add an individual-fire substantial-effect requirement, an interstate-travel condition or a missing statutory element.

## Adversarial paths and constitutional reservation

**Coverage path tested:** The house serves as mortgage security and insured property and consumes interstate gas; Congress used broad affecting-commerce language with a case-specific element. This argument supplies a genuine textual alternative and cannot be dismissed simply because the house is owner occupied or the arson is local. It fails at the property's actual function: the supporting transactions are the asserted connections, and the record establishes residential use without an additional qualifying role. The statutory term used in must do work independent of the breadth of affecting commerce.

**Exclusion path adopted:** Apply Hinds to the actual residential function and reject those three incidental relationships as sufficient by themselves. This preserves the clause's broad reach when a qualifying function is established and preserves Russell's rental holding, but gives the separate use condition effect. The answer does not depend on declaring every home exempt, proving that this particular fire had no national economic consequences, or imposing a universal income-production requirement.

**Constitutional path not reached:** If coverage existed, controlling Lopez/Morrison would require the regulated class and eligible interstate mechanism before aggregation, including actual market, necessary-regime or supported noninternalizable-externality routes as applicable. The mere existence of the element would not establish that mechanism. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer preserve their expressly proposed broader substantial-class-effects approach; it is not governing law and supplies no separate statutory coverage vote. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas retain their actual Lopez/Morrison joins. No Justice is assigned a new conditional constitutional judgment on a question unnecessary to the adopted statutory resolution.

## Existing-record remedy and its limits

Eight Associates support reversal of the §844(i) conviction, with remand for entry of acquittal on that count. The supported government theory identifies only the mortgage, policy and gas connections to a private residence. This is not Hinds's situation in which correction of an appellate rationale left the complete existing property-function evidence to be assessed. The identified proof here cannot establish the properly construed element; the government receives no further opportunity to prove a previously unestablished commercial or other qualifying use. Burks and Hinds supply that consequence, and Carmell preserves the distinction between an assumed evidentiary gap and a resolved insufficiency.

The remand may address any actual legal dependence of the §924(c) judgment and any necessary sentence consequence within ordinary procedural authority. It does not presume that §844(i) was the only possible firearm predicate or that every other count necessarily survives. The §5861(f) conviction is not independently reversed, and no new finding concerning its elements is made. No release order, fixed sentence, new trial, substitute conviction or compensating punishment is prescribed.

All eight can join the actual-use statutory proposition and these limits. Four published Commerce dissenters need not join constitutional avoidance as a sufficient ground, and the other four need not surrender their controlling Commerce positions to agree on the statutory answer. Residual lenity may be stated separately only at Scalia's specified limited scope. No author, final Court coalition including Stone, or historical comparison is supplied. No material source gap prevents the bounded statutory commitments.
