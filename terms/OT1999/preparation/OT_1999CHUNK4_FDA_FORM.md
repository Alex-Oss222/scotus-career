# FDA: opinion form before assignment

One signed plenary lead will affirm the statutory-authority judgment for tobacco as customarily marketed through the asserted drug/device regime. The four affirming Associates' reconciled integrated-statutory ground is compatible with Stone's approved whole-statute ground. The opinion will state the applicable statutory version and preserve the distinction between the general authority question and unresolved particular advertising or constitutional issues. No independent ban, recall or enforcement order is issued.

One common dissent will preserve Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer's independent coverage-versus-lawful-exercise ground and bounded remand. It will not import the historical dissent's broader reading making an otherwise mandatory cessation command elective. There is no recorded disagreement among these four requiring multiple dissents.

The lead is one undivided opinion: its statutory-version and delegation explanations belong to the same authority judgment. Form and governing propositions are selected for that legal work, before the fresh count and author choice. No new substantive component is added for balance.
