# Southworth: legal form before assignment

One signed plenary lead will resolve the university's compulsory-fee question through the actual educational expression program, its viewpoint-neutral allocation requirements, and a distinct referendum remand. The five reconciled Associates accepting that complete constitutional ground and Stone's approved core supply six direct rationale votes. The grant does not reopen an Establishment, free-exercise, RFRA or foreclosed policy claim, and the operative remand concerns the referendum.

Stevens, Souter and Breyer support the same reversal and remand on a distinct judgment rationale: the actual stipulated neutral ordinary program suffices without deciding a newly necessary viewpoint-neutrality test for every such fee arrangement. One common concurrence in the judgment will preserve that disagreement and its limits. No opinion is divided into separately assigned parts, and no new doctrinal component is added for counting.

Assignment remains pending the earlier FDA Record. Counts and author selection will occur only after that Record exists, in the required effective-date and same-day alphabetical sequence.
