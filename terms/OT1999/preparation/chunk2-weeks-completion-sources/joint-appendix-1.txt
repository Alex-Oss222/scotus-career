Staff Writer 


When he was evaluating his returning talent a fev 
weeks ago, Seventy-First High School boys’ basketball 
Pete House made no secret of the fact Lonnie Weeks was 
going to have to play a pivotal role if the Falcons were to 


do well. 


67 


Just two games into the season, Weeks has more than 
lived up to his coach’s expectations. 


Seventy-First opened the season last week with victo- 
ries over Richmond Senior and Scotland. Weeks scored a 
total of 58 points, grabbed 29 rebounds and shot 64 
percent from the field in the two games. 


His performance earned him this week’s Fayetteville 
Observer-Times Athlete of the Week honors. 


“I just hope we haven't jinxed ourselves” House said. 
“As young as we are, we've got to have so many guys 
play well. But Lonnie had an excellent week. He’s such a 
fierce competitor.” 


House felt attitude had a lot to do with Weeks’ fast 
start. “He played like a senior, played with leadership 
qualities,” House said. “We've been trying to prime him 
to be a leader for us this year and to be our captain. 


“I thought he did that better than anything. The other 
strong point for the week was the other kids did a good 
job of getting him the basketball.” 


Weeks, 6-foot-3, is playing the low post for the Fal- 
cons this year, so most of his shots come inside. But even 
though he was shooting from close range, he admitted 
concern about his accuracy last week. 


“I knew that my shot wasn’t really on,” he said. “I 
tried to do the best I could and it started falling. 


“People are expecting me to do a lot, and I’m trying 
to do the best I can, I don’t feel too much pressure, 
because I just go out there and do what I have to do, I

think I can keep it up as long as I get support from 
everybody.”
