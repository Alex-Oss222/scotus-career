# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia, J., delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** In a § 43(a) action for infringement of unregistered trade dress, a product’s design is distinctive, and therefore protectible, only upon a showing of secondary meaning.
* **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at the scope supported by the joins stated above.
