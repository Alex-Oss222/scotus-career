# Michael Wayne Williams — form before count

One signed plenary opinion separately states Part I's section 2254(e)(2) applicant-fault/diligence construction and cumulative statutory exceptions, Part II's report affirmance, Part III's two concealed-relationship applications, and Part IV's limited cause conclusion and preserved remand conditions. All eight Associates can carry the entire opinion. Stone joins Part I's legal construction but expressly remands diligence and hearing questions rather than joining the Court's final claim-specific diligence findings. His separate writing preserves that choice. The report affirmance has eight votes against Stone's vacatur; all nine set aside the two relationship barriers, with eight reversing the stated legal determinations and Stone seeking vacatur for the specified further inquiry. His silence on the separate procedural-default cause component supplies only the approved fallback vote supporting rejection of the lower no-cause ground, not a rationale or author eligibility. These distinctions must be published. There is no need for divided authorship, a per curiam form or a combined opinion with Terry Williams. Form and propositions are fixed before any count or author is considered.
