# Reform Party — form and propositions fixed before assignment

One signed plenary opinion addresses the two joined appellate tracks. Part I affirms the Rule 60(b)(6) denial on finality and the absence of an extraordinary circumstance; Part II sustains Childress's timely pre-primary equal-protection entitlement under calibrated burden, interest and fit review; Part III confines relief lawfully within Childress review and preserves the separate final Eshenbaugh obligations. The parts reflect distinct legal grounds and remedial coalitions, not workload considerations. The common propositions are separately stated so agreement with them does not imply assent to remedial confinement.

All eight Associates join Parts I and II. Stone joins Part II and votes to affirm the Rule 60 denial under the standing fallback, adding no Rule 60 ground. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer join Part III. Stone, Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg would affirm the existing breadth; their separate writing preserves the party-status objection to narrowing and accepts otherwise lawful neutral regulation. The Chief assigns Parts I and II; O'Connor, the senior Associate in the narrowing coalition, assigns Part III. All five narrowing Associates can carry every governing proposition, so one author delivers the opinion. No divided authorship is justified. No counts or author have been considered in fixing this form.

The author must preserve the uncertain relationship between decrees, the limited review of Eshenbaugh, the distinction between Rule 60(b)(5) and (b)(6), and the absence of a universal fusion right. There is no automatic restoration of post-primary enforcement. No commitment changes in assembly.
