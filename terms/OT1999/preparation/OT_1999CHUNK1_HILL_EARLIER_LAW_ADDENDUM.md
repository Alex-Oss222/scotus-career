# Hill: additional effective earlier law

This neutral handoff supplies only the public law of two earlier completed events. It contains no private position, provisional commitment, comparator or audit annex for Hill. The event-specific cutoff is immediately before January 11, 2000; same-day Kimel and January 12 decisions do not enter Hill's substantive baseline.

**Paul P. v. Verniero, December 6, 1999.** The Court recognizes a limited Fourteenth Amendment privacy interest against unjustified deliberate official dissemination of compiled nonpublic residential information exposing the home and household. A sufficient connection among the information, its safety audience and protective function, with reasonably fitted safeguards against unnecessary disclosure and redistribution, governs the notification at issue. Public conviction information remains outside that secrecy claim. The judgment affirms the challenge to the notification provisions as a matter of law while retaining an application-specific safeguards remand. No injunction or interim restraint issues. Doe remains an assumed-right decision about confined administrative use; Sheets is extended at the stated scope; W.P.'s separate state-entitlement process, class and proof limits remain intact. [Public law source](../records/paul-p-v-verniero_merits_1999-12-06.md).

**Los Angeles Police Department v. United Reporting Publishing Corp., December 7, 1999.** Lanphere governs a speech-selected condition on access to current arrest addresses and commercial use. Government ownership does not exempt the condition from ordinary commercial-speech review. A maintained claim of the plaintiff's own injury is not defeated by the facial label, and alternative record grounds may defend a favorable judgment without enlargement under X-Citement Video. The appellate judgment is set aside and the commercial application remains open; the Court neither dissolves nor continues the existing relief. The Ninth Circuit addresses that relief. No general right to records, new Article III test, universal confidentiality rule, or final commercial-validity conclusion is established. [Public law source](../records/los-angeles-police-department-v-united-reporting_merits_1999-12-07.md).
