# Early assembly form decisions

## Baral v. United States
Before counts or author selection: one signed merits opinion of the Court resolving the two specified deemed-payment rules and their independent amount-window application. The statutory operations are related parts of one refund dispute, not separate opinions. No separate writing is needed when claim timeliness and other remittances remain outside the decision.

## Hunt-Wesson, Inc. v. Franchise Tax Board of California
Before counts or author selection: One signed Court opinion on constitutional territorial limits, the qualified section 24344 mechanism and its failure under either proposed state-law reading. Both Due Process and Commerce grounds belong to this one disposition. No separate writing is needed if reasonable allocation and the taxpayer burden are retained.

## Weisgram v. Marley Co.
Before counts or author selection: One signed merits Court opinion extends preserved Rule 50 appellate authority to insufficiency following correction of erroneous evidence admission, with fair-opportunity/new-trial discretion and the limited grant integral. One disposition; no separate writing because the supported qualifications answer the recorded reservations.

## Rice v. Cayetano
Before counts or author selection: One signed Court opinion on ancestry as a racial franchise qualification, the independent public-state-election ground and rejection of limited-purpose/beneficiary defenses. The five who adopt that ground can support one lead; Souter and Breyer receive a distinct judgment concurrence explaining actual trust/electorate fit. Stevens dissents on federal political/fiduciary relationship and contextual ancestry grounds; Ginsburg shares only the former and separately states its sufficiency under both Amendments. No separate Court parts are created to alter eligibility or counts.
