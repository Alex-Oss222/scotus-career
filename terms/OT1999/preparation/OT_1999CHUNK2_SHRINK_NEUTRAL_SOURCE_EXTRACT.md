The $1,075 statewide/state-auditor ceiling is the provision these litigants challenge. The statute also contains other office-based ceilings; mention of them is not a separate party or office-specific adjudication. Indexing is statutory. The fundraising figures below include contrary evidence and must be assessed together: the 97.62 percent figure measures the proportion of contributors, not the proportion of dollars. The comparative spending and candidate-count changes do not themselves establish what caused them. The government retains the entering law's burden; research incompleteness proves no litigant default.

Additional numerical evidence reported at pages 394 and 396: Proposition A received 74 percent support. For the 1994 state-auditor elections, 97.62 percent of contributors gave $2,000 or less; this is a count of contributors, not a fraction of total funding. The statewide vote is evidence of voters' expressed preferences, with its significance open for assessment under entering law. These source recitations are paraphrased to omit the target Court's evaluation.

The last source passage begins mid-footnote.
