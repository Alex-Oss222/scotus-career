# Terry Williams — form and propositions before assignment

Fixed on the legal grounds before any count or author selection: one signed lead opinion with distinct join scopes, two separate statutory-construction accounts preserving the four-Associate/four-Associate general fork, and the two-Associate dissent from application and relief. No per curiam form, combination with Michael Williams or division among lead authors is legally required. The independent same-day Michael event supplies no entering law.

The lead's Part I states the direct common minimum supported by all eight Associates: the clauses perform independent work; a contrary legal rule need not match facts as well as law; materially indistinguishable facts with an opposite result also qualify; objective unreasonableness is not an actual judicial poll; clearly established law consists of this Court's holdings at the state merits decision; Strickland's general form does not disqualify it. Part I also preserves ordinary lawful-mitigation prejudice, the narrow controlling Fretwell limit and Virginia's actual unanimity consequence. Stone is not assigned a general contrary-to construction or exclusive clause allocation through silence.

Part II supplies the seven-Justice independently sufficient objective-unreasonableness application, actual investigation deficiency, whole-record reasonable-probability prejudice and sentence-only conditional writ. Stone and the six relief Associates support these propositions. The application must fall outside the range permitted by governing law; mere disagreement is insufficient. The statutory gateway and actual Sixth Amendment violation remain separate. The adverse and mitigating records, actual trial evidence, preserved performance defense, parole qualification and disputed diagnosis/experts are all retained at their established scope.

Part III preserves the six relief Associates' alternative contrary-rule gateway ground under the already binding Fretwell limitation, including assessment of the State's ordinary-probability alternative. It does not expand into their incompatible exclusive general clause allocations. Stone need not join that alternative to support the complete relief required by Part II. Six Associates can carry the entire lead, so no artificial division is needed to secure an eligible author.

The broader governing-rule account is supported by Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer; the distinct-clause account by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Neither complete exclusive formulation has a fifth vote, and no Marks or logical-subset claim is made. Scalia and Thomas accept the common rules but would affirm both because the actual state application remains reasonable and because no actual prejudice is established on independent review; they assume performance rather than holding it adequate.

Relief concerns only the death sentence: a constitutionally adequate new sentencing proceeding within a reasonable period fixed below, or a lawful noncapital sentence. No guilt reversal, immediate release, life-without-parole direction, new evidentiary hearing or revival of independent cross-appeal claims follows. Authorship and counts remain open until the earlier Michael Record is actually written.
