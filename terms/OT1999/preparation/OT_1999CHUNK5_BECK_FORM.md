# Beck — form and propositions before assignment

Fixed on the legal grounds before consulting counts: one signed Court opinion on civil conspiracy injury and a dissent preserving the competing text-and-directness interpretation. Stone, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer support the independent-RICO-wrong condition and confined affirmance; Stevens and Souter would reverse that categorical ground. No part division or combination with another case is warranted.

Civil recovery requires business/property injury caused by a racketeering act or another act independently wrongful under RICO, rather than any non-RICO act advancing the agreement. This does not require personal commission by every conspirator, establish a criminal overt-act rule, or restrict every qualifying injury to subsection (c). Reserve the investment-injury question and whether a completed actionable substantive violation is required or agreement plus an injury-producing predicate suffices. The presented termination is assumed nonracketeering and not otherwise independently wrongful under RICO; no alleged scheme or injury amount is found.

Affirm the granted termination-conspiracy summary judgment, preserving the existing vacatur and common-law pendent-state-claim remand, its discretionary protective conditions, independent federal substantive dispositions, sanctions, costs and service rulings. The later securities-fraud exclusion, criminal-conviction exception and final-conviction limitations trigger retain their terms without an application or temporal ruling.
