### Morgan Stanley & Co. v. Pacific Mutual Life Insurance Co., No. 93-609

Merits decision, 1994-05-23

#### Chronology and Posture

Reported at 511 U.S. 658. Argued April 26, 1994; decided May 23, 1994. On certiorari to the Fifth Circuit, 997 F.2d 39, which reversed denial of reinstatement under §27A(b). The question is whether Congress may require reopening of this completed private federal dismissal.

Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Blackmun, Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg participate. Justice O'Connor takes no part; no reason is stated.

#### Judgment

The Fifth Circuit's judgment is affirmed by an equally divided Court, 4–4. The judgment states the alignment: Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas would reverse; Justices Blackmun, Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg would affirm. No opinion issues.

**Affirmed by an equally divided Court, 4–4.**

**Supporting:** Blackmun, Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg.
**Opposing:** Stone-Zsela, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas.
**Not participating:** O'Connor.

The Fifth Circuit's judgment reversing the district court's refusal to reinstate Pacific Mutual's action and remanding under §27A(b) stands between the parties, and proceedings continue under it. The securities claims, defenses and remedies remain for ordinary judicial adjudication. No fraud finding, liability judgment or damages award is entered.

#### Opinion Topology

No opinion issues. The judgment is unsigned; it states the equal division and the alignment and gives no reasons. No Justice writes for the Court, for a plurality or separately.

#### Precedent Treatment and Current-Law Effect

The affirmance establishes no precedent on whether Article III permits §27A(b) to require reinstatement of a final, unappealed federal dismissal; it binds only the parties to this case, and no Supreme Court rule results. No earlier decision is applied, extended, distinguished or overruled. Robertson v. Seattle Audubon Society's reservation of the final-judgment question remains open. Klein, McCullough, Wheeling Bridge, Sioux Nation, Lampf, Beam, Harper, Landgraf, Rivers and Chase Securities retain their existing force.

Subsection (a) separately prescribes the jurisdiction's June 19, 1991 limitations law, including retroactivity principles, for the specified private actions commenced on or before that date. Nothing is decided under subsection (a), and nothing is decided on due process, fraud, liability or damages.
