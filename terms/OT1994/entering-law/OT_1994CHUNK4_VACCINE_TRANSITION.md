## Law After Decision

The revised Vaccine Injury Table and Qualifications and Aids to Interpretation apply to petitions filed on or after March 10, 1995. Earlier petitions remain governed by section 2114(a) and (b) of the Public Health Service Act as in effect February 8, 1995. Both the Table and its interpretive qualifications follow this express petition-date boundary.
