## Law After Decision

The amendments operate subject to section 14's former-law preservation for earlier certificates and pending applications, permitted refiling with its original-date rule, and amended-protection labeling and sanctions requirements. The complete enacted instrument supplies all qualifications. Section 9's contract-producer clause has its own enumerated crop and contractual conditions; section 10 removes the former farmer-to-farmer sale proviso for the amended regime.
