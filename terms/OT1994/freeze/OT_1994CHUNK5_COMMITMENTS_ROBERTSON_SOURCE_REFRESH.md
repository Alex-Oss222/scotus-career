## Competing grounds after the opposing record

**Narrow direct-engagement path.** Section 1962(a) separately reaches an enterprise engaged in interstate commerce and one whose activities affect it. American Building Maintenance, 422 U.S. 271, supplies the direct acquisition distinction: an enterprise's own interstate acquisition of goods or services differs from a local transaction involving goods whose interstate movement ended earlier. The common ground here includes supplies sent interstate for the mine's establishment and people traveling interstate to perform the mine's work. The panel's legal exclusion of those business inputs as incidental cannot stand merely because gold was sold locally, most equipment was bought locally, or the enterprise was small. A business need not export its output as well as obtain inputs across state lines. This is an application to the acknowledged, business-directed movements and the lower court's procurement account, not a finding of all the Government's stronger allegations.

**Respondent's strongest path.** The enterprise-commerce element cannot be replaced with the investor's separate interstate conduct or the predicates' commerce connections. Section 1962(a)'s reference to investment in establishment does not itself make every establishment act the enterprise's interstate commerce. The Government's references to a wrecked car, an unconnected plane, communications, financing, general gold markets and unspecified recurring travel contain different attribution and proof issues. The general verdict does not resolve those disputes individually, and the appendix's lack of testimony prevents an independent finding about each disputed addition. Furthermore, a generalized effects rationale must answer respondent's class-definition, federalism and substantiality arguments rather than treat the word 'affecting' as eliminating every constitutional limit.

**Why the selected path survives that objection at this baseline.** Attribution is retained as a requirement. Actual supplies moved to establish this operating mine and identified persons traveled to perform its work; these are not merely the proceeds' unlawful source or the investor's private acquisitions. Calling those movements establishment does not categorically strip them of their business character. The indictment and instructions describe an enterprise encompassing the partnership and its successor; neither the existence of formal partnership dates nor the change to a sole proprietorship warrants ignoring the enterprise's acknowledged setup activity. The continuing enterprise still must be established under the charge; the Court does not find every allegation proved merely from the indictment. Respondent's majority-local-equipment and local-sales account does not contradict the narrower actual interstate input account. No output-export premise is needed.

**Alternative remedy if the attribution premise fails upon a genuine neutral record correction.** A Justice unable to attribute even the acknowledged inputs to the charged enterprise could accept correction of the incidental-activity rule while remitting application to the existing trial record, instead of declaring the commerce element satisfied. That narrower course would not authorize new evidence to repair the Government's proof after an insufficiency determination, decide the preserved special-verdict claim, or direct an automatic new trial. It is an explicit alternative, not the provisional disposition selected below. If the existing trial evidence proves no qualifying engagement and the eventual actual law supplies no valid alternative, the appropriate insufficiency consequence must be confronted rather than hidden in an unconditional reinstatement.

The Government's broad aggregation argument and respondent's contrary constitutional theory remain unnecessary to the selected statutory path. The supplied briefs' quotations or descriptions of post-October-1991 Supreme Court decisions do not replace actual simulated public law. In particular, the reply's Allied-Bruce argument is assessed against the authorized actual January 18 public holding and its reservation about remote object history. References to historical Lopez oral argument supply no rule. This document does not use historical Lopez or Robertson as law.
