### Reachability and component boundaries

All eight recognize that the **renewed age-eighteen and lascivious-wording objections are available defenses of respondents' existing favorable judgment**. American Railway Express, 265 U.S. 425, 435–436, permits that use of a record ground rejected below without a cross-petition when no enlargement of the prevailing party's rights or reduction of its opponent's rights is sought. This does not undo Izumi's rule about seeking review of a separate adverse judgment. It also does not compel decision of every alternative: Dandridge, 397 U.S. 471, 475 n.6, preserves a proper remand option, and an independently sufficient disposition may leave an alternative unnecessary.

The six who reject facial invalidity on the scienter ground **reach and reject both renewed alternatives**, permitting reversal/remand without leaving these two facial objections unresolved. No vote from those two is counted for rejection or acceptance of either new alternative.

The other definition challenge concerning actual-or-simulated bestiality and sadistic-or-masochistic abuse is not established as renewed.
