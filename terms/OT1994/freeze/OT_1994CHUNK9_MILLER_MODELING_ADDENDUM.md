## Substantive source boundaries

The reconstructed lower-court judgment and its record stipulations, not historical Georgia adjudication, fix this case. Racial predominance and subordination of ordinary districting considerations are admitted findings. The source packet admits maps, legislative evidence, and preclearance materials as described, but establishes no new map measurement, witness quotation, document passage, discriminatory service event, campaign injury, or DOJ communication. An independent model may assess the legal consequence of the admitted finding without re-creating an evidentiary trial or pretending to have independently verified unprovided exhibits. It must not reject that finding merely to obtain a nonracial explanation for the map.

Wright v. Rockefeller resolved failure to prove racial purpose, not this admitted-purpose record. United Jewish Organizations v. Carey is fractured and supplies no universal rule. Gomillion addresses racially engineered municipal exclusion; Anderson v. Martin addresses official racial ballot labels without requiring a changed winner; McLaurin shows why formal access alone need not mean equality. Those examples preserve genuine noneconomic injuries without deciding whether racial territorial design alone produces one. Croson concerns state/local racial allocation of opportunities; the model must explain any proposed analogy or distinction rather than import a uniform tier into this different setting by assertion.

Adarand, June 12, 1995, preserves Metro Broadcasting's important-objective/substantial-relationship rule for the supported federal remedial classification at issue, with Fullilove's limited remedial judgment, and leaves Croson's state/local setting intact. Its concrete unequal-competitive-opportunity standing holding distinguishes injury from the ultimate legality of the preference. It does not supply a universal benign-program defense, convert a state map into congressional classification, or displace Shaw.

Jenkins, June 12, 1995, permits violation-linked educational repair and retains the complete Freeman release requirements. Its racial considerations follow an independently established segregation injury. It does not make every use of race forbidden or every inclusive aim an independently sufficient justification. Nor does its retained-control remedy authorize a new districting supervisor or reopening of settled claims here.

The exact reusable titles in the law copy are **Territorial racial design and independently cognizable injury** and **The designated channel for preclearance review** (present operation effective June 28, 1993, Shaw); **Section 2 fragmentation, state districting choice and influence claims** (with the actual current wording and De Grandy update); **Section 2 challenges to governing-body size** (present operation effective June 30, 1994, Holder); and the concrete-personal-interest and unequal-opportunity standing entries. Their specific triggers, proof burdens, exceptions, and authority limits are copied. De Grandy's proportional-opportunity evidence neither guarantees proportional officeholding nor creates a safe harbor or offset for proved local dilution. Holder requires a legally and practically grounded comparison, independent statutory proof, and a remedy for an actual violation. Neither supplies an extra constitutional-injury requirement for a statutory claim, and neither creates a statutory claim in this case where none is independently pleaded.

## Questions and genuinely competing paths

All six questions remain the scope if jurisdiction permits merits review. A threshold disposition cannot be disguised as six substantive holdings. Where a question is conditional, identify that status and do not present the conditional analysis as necessary to a judgment supported on another ground.

| Question | Supported paths and the central counterarguments |
|---|---|
| 1. Predominance without an independently identified deprivation | Apply Shaw's substantive boundary and reject a design-only theory; the strongest contrary argument is that race-based assignment itself classifies and injures personally, like other official unequal treatment. Alternatively, expressly reconsider Shaw and adopt a predominance scrutiny trigger; that path must identify why stare decisis permits replacing the recent controlling distinction and why the present record changes the legal premise rather than merely repeating the rejected theory. No silent overruling is possible. |
| 2. Sorting, stereotypes, representation, and political membership | Determine whether these characterizations actually identify unequal personal treatment in the reconstructed common constituency or simply restate its demographic design. The strongest objection to rejection is that equality injuries need not await loss of a vote or service; the strongest objection to acceptance is that hypothetical representative behavior or a racial purpose does not by itself prove the asserted official inequality. Formal eligibility and absence of dilution are not dispositive answers to every possible equality claim. No denied service or eligibility restriction may be invented. |
| 3. Voluntary inclusion versus statutory compulsion | Distinguish a State's constitutional permission from whether federal law commands the particular map: lack of a mandate is not itself a prohibition. The counterargument is that a voluntary goal cannot excuse actual unequal racial treatment if such treatment is established. Conversely, a strict-scrutiny view must not treat an administrator's demand as self-proving necessity. Neither path can turn absence of a statutory duty into universal permission or universal invalidity. |
| 4. Shape and other evidence | Under Shaw as adopted, shape and purpose may illuminate the challenged conduct but predominance alone does not establish the rejected wrong. Under a proposed racial-classification approach, unusual shape can be evidence rather than an indispensable gate when direct evidence supplies predominance. The objection to an aesthetic trigger is that a compact district can still reflect purposeful racial assignment; the objection to dispensing with the injury inquiry is that proof of purpose cannot silently change Shaw's substantive rule. State the function of each kind of evidence, without inventing a shape finding beyond the supplied map description. |
| 5. The admitted predominance finding | Accept the reconstructed finding unless a supported review ground within the actual supplied record warrants a contrary conclusion; it cannot be demoted to an assumption or overcome with invented neutral explanations. Its consequence can differ under the competing legal rules: relevant but insufficient to the particular deprivation under current Shaw, versus a scrutiny trigger only after an express doctrinal change. A disagreement about the legal consequence is not factual clear error. The packet contains no witness-level materials justifying a new evidentiary reversal. |
| 6. Trigger and conditional statutory-compliance justification | First decide whether strict scrutiny governs under the current rule or a expressly adopted change. Independently give the requested conditional answer on the asserted VRA/DOJ justification. Beer limits section 5 to nonretrogression; Pleasant Grove preserves the separate purpose inquiry; Gingles and the actual Holder/De Grandy rules preserve section 2's distinct requirements without maximization. The reconstructed court found that section 5 did not require this district and that DOJ's demands did not establish reasonable necessity under the substantive statutory obligation. A compliance defense cannot rest solely on those demands. The strongest competing legal argument invokes legitimate compliance, uncertainty, and the independent statutory purpose/results duties, but requires an actual showing of necessity and fit; no new supporting factual finding is supplied. Do not substitute the general permission to seek inclusion for the specifically asserted federal-compulsion justification. No additional independently supported justification or separate statutory cause of action is created. |

For a merits judgment rejecting the theories, the prescribed procedural consequence is affirmance of the reconstructed defendant-favorable judgment, with no remand to hunt for an unpleaded injury. For a conclusion recognizing a deprivation, identify its actual legal ground, governing test, sufficient justification inquiry, and the record-supported relief. Conditional failure of a justification does not establish liability absent the predicate triggering that justification burden. If any part requires additional proceedings, name the necessary unresolved issue and explain why it is actually presented despite the reconstructed judgment's statement that no independent claim remains. Do not order a new district map, particular racial proportion, election timetable, or permanent supervision without an established basis.
