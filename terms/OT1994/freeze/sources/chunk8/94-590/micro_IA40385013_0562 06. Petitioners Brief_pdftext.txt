                             CONCLUSION

   The Policy satisfies both requirements of the T.L.O.
reasonableness test. It also satisfies the requirements
common to those cases in which this Court has upheld
searches without individualized suspicion. Searches pur-
suant to the Policy are constitutionally reasonable. The
District, therefore, respectfully requests that the judg-
ment of the circuit court be reversed and the judgment
of the district court in favor of the District be reinstated
on the grounds that the District's Policy is not an unrea-
sonable intrusion under the Fourth Amendment.
