                     QUESTIONS PRESENTED

   1. Whether an individual who endeavors to ob-
struct a grand jury proceeding by making false and
misleading statements to prospective witnesses may
be prosecuted for obstruction of justice, within the
meaning of 18 U.S.C. 1503.

   2. Whether an individual who knows of an appli-

cation for authorization to intercept telephone con-
versations and who discloses its existence to a target
in order to obstruct the interception of the target's
conversations may be found guilty of violating 18
U.S.C. 2232(c), regardless of whether the authoriza-
tion had expired by the time the disclosure was made.
