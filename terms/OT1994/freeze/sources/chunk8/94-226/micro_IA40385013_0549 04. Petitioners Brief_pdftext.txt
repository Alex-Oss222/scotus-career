          QUESTIONS PRESENTED FOR REVIEW
                                        I

WHETHER THE COURT SHOULD RETURN TO THE
STATES A REASONABLE RANGE OF AUTHORITY TO
DETERMINE WHETHER ATTORNEY ADVERTISING
ADVERSELY AFFECTS THE ADMINISTRATION OF JUS-
TICE, AND TO REGULATE IT ACCORDINGLY.

                                        II
WHETHER THE LIMITS PLACED UPON STATE
AUTHORITY BY THE DECISION BELOW EXTEND
BEYOND ANY HERETOFORE RECOGNIZED BY THIS
COURT AND ESTABLISH ATTORNEY ADVERTISING
AS A PRIVILEGED CLASS OF COMMERCIAL SOLIC-
ITATION WITH A GREATER DEGREE OF INSULATION
FROM REGULATION THAN OTHER CLASSES.

      A. Whether the challenged rules meet the
      requirements for valid commercial regulations
      because they are narrowly tailored to serve sub-
      stantial governmental interests.
      B. Whether the challenged rules are valid time,
      place or manner regulations.
