     I. Under Shapero v. Kentucky Bar Association, does a
Florida Bar 30-day ban on truthful, non-misleading, personal
injury direct mail letters which fully comply with the Bar's
extensive regulation of the content, delivery, and style of the
letters, violate the First Amendment?

     II. Has The Florida Bar met its burden, under Central
Hudson or any First Amendment test, to justify a 30-day ban on
lawyers' truthful, non-misleading, personal injury targeted direct
mail letters?
