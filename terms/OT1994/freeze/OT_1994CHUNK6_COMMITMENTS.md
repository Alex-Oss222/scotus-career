## 61. City of Edmonds v. Oxford House

Reach merits. The only judgment component is whether the family definition is absolutely exempt under §3607(b)(1). An affirmance leaves liability, reasonable accommodation and the merits of any particular asserted safety justification for remand. A reversal sustains the exemption, without a separate holding that unrelated disabled residents possess no other rights.

**Path A — affirm:** Maximum occupancy restrictions regulate total crowding. Edmonds allows related occupants without numerical limit while restricting unrelated persons to five; that is a family-composition rule rather than a dwelling's maximum occupancy. The exception does not conclusively immunize it. Strongest contrary argument: five is a genuine numerical maximum for the class actually regulated and the exception contains no express requirement that every family receive the same cap. Answer: read “maximum number of occupants permitted to occupy a dwelling” as a restriction on dwelling occupancy, not authorization to exempt every membership-based zoning rule that contains a number. Statutory context preserves meaningful coverage of disability-related discrimination and reasonable accommodation without forbidding actual crowding codes.

**Path B — reverse:** An actual numerical limit applicable to an unrelated household remains a restriction regarding maximum occupants; the text does not require a universal cap or make the city's underlying purpose dispositive. Strongest contrary argument: this construction converts unlimited related-family occupancy into a maximum-occupancy rule and drains coverage from group homes. Answer: exemptions can be broad; the five-person rule genuinely limits the group home, and courts should not insert a qualification absent from the words. This ground must stay confined to a reasonable restriction and cannot equate all family definitions with valid exemptions regardless of their operation.

### Component 1 — expenditure-triggered increases and subsidies

**Invalidity path:** Spending triggers a governmental advantage for the candidate the speaker seeks to oppose: an increased spending allowance equal to the expenditure and, for an eligible recipient, a subsidy equal to half. The trigger makes exercising political expression a condition for funding the adverse electoral response. That targeted burden is distinct from ordinary equal public financing. Buckley's refusal to suppress independent speech to equalize electoral resources and NCPAC's independent-expression protection support invalidation. Strongest response: the speaker remains free to spend, and more opposing speech is not suppression. The invalidity position answers that a public benefit expressly and proportionally triggered by disfavored independent advocacy changes the legal consequences of speaking even without a ceiling, but does not claim immunity from all counter-speech.

**Validity path:** The statute neither caps nor taxes the independent speaker, nor orders it to surrender resources. Candidates voluntarily accepting expenditure limits may obtain resources to respond; facilitating additional political advocacy is within Buckley's recognized public-financing interests. Strongest response: inducement to abstain can exist without formal prohibition and the funding is specifically adverse. The validity position answers that this consequence is the anticipated response of another speaker supported by a voluntary program, not a reduction in the challenger's legal opportunity to communicate. It rejects the proposition that government may condition an unrelated entitlement on silence. It also requires an honest account of actual subsidy eligibility and payment timing; participation percentages do not alone prove constitutional voluntariness or cure a coercive condition.

His NCPAC separate position and Austin concurrence distinguish financing regulation and differing electoral contexts rather than treating every monetary consequence as suppression. His California Medical plurality join recognizes permissible restrictions on indirect contributions, although this component concerns expenditures and cannot be upheld merely as a contribution limit. His public McIntyre join preserves financing and organizational questions rather than extending individual anonymous-leaflet immunity. Barrier: a holding that independent expenditure caps are valid, or that equalization alone justifies direct suppression. Minimum shared rationale: no direct restriction here; public-financing responsiveness facilitates additional speech, with independent expression still legally unlimited. Narrow alternative: reject facial invalidity while preserving a separately proved coercive application.

### Component 3 — $100 annual contributions to committees and funds

All eight retain invalidation of the $100 annual ceiling on this established record. This is a contribution restriction, not an expenditure ceiling. Buckley's contribution/expenditure distinction remains. California Medical supplies a lawful anticircumvention route for a multicandidate committee that makes candidate contributions, but its fragmented reasoning does not conclusively govern an exclusively independent-expenditure organization. Blackmun's reserved distinction cannot be silently converted into a blanket right of every committee to receive unlimited funds.

Path A invalidates this exceptionally restrictive ceiling because it impedes practical association: one-quarter to one-third of MCCL-CSPC's contributions exceeded the cap, and the record supplies no proportionate corruption/circumvention mechanism justifying such a low universal ceiling. Path B sustains a carefully fitted contribution cap where money may be routed to candidates and available collective advocacy remains effective. Strongest objection to A is that courts cannot require corruption of every individual donor or impose an arbitrary numerical minimum. Answer: A requires neither; the relationship between the statutory burden and the State's actual theory must be sufficient. Strongest objection to B is that a nominally nonzero amount can disable meaningful pooling. Answer: the State must show a fitted burden in the class regulated, which it has not done here. No national permissible dollar floor follows.

Individual anchors: Stevens's California Medical plurality join permits real circumvention controls, but does not establish that $100 leaves meaningful association on these facts. O'Connor's NCPAC join and MCFL organizational-burden concurrence require attention to the actual impairment, even while allowing lawful contributions rules. Scalia's MCFL III-A join and Austin dissent support robust political association, but the common holding need not adopt a general rule against all contributions limits. Kennedy's Austin dissent and public McIntyre join support the narrowly tested burden; he reserves genuinely fitted anticircumvention limits. Souter and Ginsburg's public McIntyre joins distinguish financing regulation from anonymous individual leaflets, yet do not immunize it from constitutional fit; Souter's Norman opinion and Ginsburg's Northwest Airlines opinion support testing actual operation. Thomas's public McIntyre concurrence supplies expressive-protection support but he can join this narrow result without a general balancing test or a decision of all contribution caps. Breyer's public McIntyre join and practical contextual method in Allied-Bruce support the record-specific association conclusion rather than a universal numerical prohibition.

Common join barrier for every Justice: a Court opinion must not state that California Medical categorically validates limits on every committee, that contributions are expenditures, that $100 is unconstitutional solely because it is less than Buckley's historical amount, or that no limit can ever govern a conduit committee. Minimum common formulation: the present universal ceiling and established practical burden lack the sufficient fitted justification required by the governing contribution doctrine; ordinary candidate limits and supported circumvention rules remain open.

## 64. U.S. Term Limits / Bryant — disclosed fallback

Reach merits in both consolidated dockets; no separate petition-stage decision. The same disposition applies to §3's House and Senate ballot restrictions, preserving the issue of congressional qualifications apart from the state-office portions not presented.

**Path A — affirm invalidity:** The Article I qualifications for membership in Congress are fixed nationally. Powell establishes that otherwise qualified electors and candidates are not subject to additional substantive disqualifications imposed by Congress; the constitutional allocation and representative character extend that exclusivity to States. A restriction expressly keyed to prior congressional service does what an additional qualification would do through the ordinary ballot. The Elections Clause regulates election mechanics, not the permissible identity of otherwise qualified members. Strongest objection: Powell involved Congress, not a State exercising retained power; write-in eligibility means Arkansas has not prohibited service. Answer: federal offices arise from the Constitution's plan, and making prior service a barrier to the ordinary route deliberately imposes an additional substantive disability. A genuine neutral administrative rule remains distinguishable.

**Path B — reverse:** Powell limits Congress's own exclusion power and does not expressly hold that the people of each State lack additional eligibility choices. States' reserved authority and election administration permit this ballot limitation; a candidate remains eligible by write-in. Strongest objection: allowing different membership qualifications makes the national constitutional list incomplete and subordinates national representation to state alteration. Answer: national minimum qualifications can coexist with local voter-imposed rules absent an express prohibition, and this rule determines ballot placement rather than actual membership. This position must confront the deliberate service-based exclusion and cannot defend it merely by labeling it procedure.
