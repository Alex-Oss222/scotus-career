## Exact reconciled disposition

### Decided commercial-insurance and HMO applications

Reverse the reviewed judgment insofar as it holds the 13% hospital differential, additional 11% commercial-insurer measure and conditional HMO assessment up to 9% preempted on the rejected economic-effects theory in the decided commercial-insurance/HMO coverage applications. Set aside the associated ERISA hospital-surcharge injunction to that extent and require the lower courts to conform the actual operative decree. These are distinct charges with the qualifications retained in final B; there is no newly imposed flat 24% or 9% rate.

### Reserved direct self-funded applications

**Insofar as the reviewed judgment or associated ERISA hospital-surcharge injunction adjudicates or affords protection on a direct self-funded application that the Court leaves unresolved, vacate that portion and remand for consideration under the announced law and any properly presented independent ground.** This includes the presented 13% direct-payor application and any necessary relation, insurance-saving or deemer inquiry. It does not newly declare the 11% commercial-insurer charge or the HMO assessment directly payable by self-funded plans.

The vacatur removes the vacated adjudication as a continuing final entitlement to direct-plan protection. It is not a merits rejection, dismissal of the claim, holding that deemer applies, or holding that deemer cannot apply. The reserved claim remains available for proper adjudication. Neither the broad theory rejected in the decided applications nor the mere fact that an alternative was preserved is by itself sufficient to maintain final direct-plan relief after this express vacatur.

### Independent grounds and orders

Leave the independent FEHBA and Actuarial Letter dispositions undisturbed. The reviewed amended FEHBA setting involves experience-rated coverage; community-rated coverage remained reserved. The district court did not decide a FEHBA challenge to 9%. The stop-loss Actuarial Letter relief concerns paragraphs 1, 2, 3 and 5, with paragraph 4 additionally held preempted on appeal; paragraphs 6–7 were not challenged on that appeal. Stop-loss insurance relief is not itself direct-plan hospital-surcharge relief.

Where independently surviving FEHBA protection concerns the same payment, removing the reviewed ERISA ground does not destroy the FEHBA basis. The mandate must therefore identify the application and legal ground, rather than dissolve all relief associated with an ERISA claimant or with the lower litigation.

### Interim, monetary and temporal implementation

The selected disposition creates no distinct direct-plan interim injunction, automatic stay, mandated escrow continuation or disbursement, reimbursement, accrued-liability finding, or new collection/payment direction. It supplies no particular mandate-issuance or stay-termination date. It does not assert that collections change automatically on the decision's announcement date.

The lower courts must conform existing implementation orders to the component-specific mandate, identify any independent surviving basis, and resolve properly presented requests for interim protection or fund administration under applicable law and supported facts. A renewed interim request on the reserved claim is neither guaranteed nor foreclosed. Its merits must not be resolved through an assumption that the old final relief necessarily survives, or that vacatur proves the direct-plan claim fails. The statutory-extension, rate-year and amount limits in final B remain in force.

## Rejected alternatives and the reason they do not create another gate

Temporary preservation could be a lawful procedural choice on a supported record and need not affirm the reserved merits. Here it would need an identified protected arrangement, terms and supporting present grounds. The clean model has instead chosen not to impose it. No source or historical vote requires this context to replace that grounded choice with compulsory protection. Lower courts retain the ability to consider a supported request; the selection is not a prediction that such relief will be denied.

Merits reversal of every direct-plan application would be inconsistent with the condition and with final B's express reservation. By contrast, procedural vacatur followed by consideration of a preserved claim does not decide that claim adversely. The scope clause also prevents the instruction from manufacturing a direct-payor application of charges imposed on different statutory categories.

An unexplained remand might be legally administered by the lower courts, but would leave the very mandate question now submitted unresolved. The new handoff supplies express vacatur under §2106 after independent modeling. It does not declare a universal rule that every reversal automatically dissolves every injunction, or that remand alone automatically preserves one.

The original later payment order and subsequent implementation record would be necessary for a direction promising continued protection, computing a sum, ordering distribution, fixing collection dates or specifying how a particular existing escrow immediately operates. None is adopted here. Requiring those documents before this application-limited vacatur would expand the source inquiry without resolving any predicate of the chosen remedy. There is therefore no remaining genuine blocker for this supplement's stated scope.
