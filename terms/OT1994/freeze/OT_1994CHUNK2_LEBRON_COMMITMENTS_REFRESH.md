On the reachable entity question, constitutional responsibility cannot be settled solely by the statutory nonagency label. The bounded affirmative ground is the combination of special federal creation for national transportation purposes and continuing governmental selection of the corporation's governing majority. The nine-member structure is the Secretary of Transportation, five other direct Presidential selections in statutory categories, two governmental preferred-shareholder selections, and the corporation's president chosen within its structure. Funding, regulation, a federal charter, ownership of property, or public benefit alone is insufficient under this proposed ground. The 1994 recodification is not a new constitutional ruling and does not retroactively change the legal character of the 1992 rejection.

Board authorization of advertising arrangements and delegation to the rejecting officer do not establish that the board considered this advertisement or that an external federal official ordered its rejection.

*Red Cross* supplies charter jurisdiction rather than constitutional actor status. *McCollum* attributes the particular use of governmental jury machinery; it is no general corporation rule.
