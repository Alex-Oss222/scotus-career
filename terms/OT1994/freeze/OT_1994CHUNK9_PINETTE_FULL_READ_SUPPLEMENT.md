This is an action for declaratory and injunctive relief and for damages brought by members of the Ku Klux Klan because the Capitol Square Review and Advisory Board and its members and employees have rejected the application of the Plaintiffs to erect a free-standing, privately owned cross on the Statehouse grounds. In addition, the suit seeks to void a bill from public officials for security services related to prior use of the Statehouse grounds and to require the Defendants to approve a permit to hold a January rally on Capitol Square. The action seeks declaratory and injunctive relief and damages for these deprivations and for a pattern of denial of rights of the plaintiffs. II. For more than a century, Capitol Square has been a traditional, public forum located in downtown Columbus. It is the seat of government of the State of Ohio. It is surrounded on all sides by streets and by public and private offices and business establishments and places of accommodation or amusement. Capitol Square traditionally has been used by office holders, office seekers, non-profit enterprises, commercial enterprises, and individuals for speeches, gatherings, and displays. 13. | For many years during the period stretching from approximately Thanksgiving until the new year, Capitol Square has been adorned with festival lights and a Christmas tree.
