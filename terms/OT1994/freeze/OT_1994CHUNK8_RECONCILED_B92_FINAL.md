## Rule, constitutional qualification and remedy precision

For ?1503, the five require a relationship in time, causation or logic to pending court/grandjury work and knowledge that the endeavor is likely to affect it; natural/probable effect can exist even if the endeavor fails. Generic false agency statements and potential future testimony do not themselves satisfy it here. No categorical statutory subpoena requirement, witness-related immunity or right to lie follows. Three opposing associates retain actual pending justice and specifically intended corruption without the added known-probable-effect requirement. They reject implied wholesale repeal from ?1512; 1988 amendments do not retroactively add elements to this conduct.

For ?2232, seven require actual application/authorization knowledge, purpose directed to obstructing its interception, and notice or attempted notice of that possible interception. Expiry unknown to the accused is not alone an automatic defense; no actual subsequent-order knowledge is imputed. Notices to nontarget persons can qualify. The statute does not require successful warning/interference, and mechanical failure/factual impossibility does not itself establish a defense. A genuinely imaginary authorization lacks the first element. Liability decades later and a possible limit at official inventory notification remain reserved; the scheme's notification duty does not extend interception authority. The frozen suggestion that knowledge interception was impossible would negate purpose is limited to an actual lack of that purpose, not a new objective-success condition overriding the adopted rule.

As-applied FirstAmendment objection is rejected with support from all eight, including Stevens's separate agreement. Obstructive purpose confines the statute, and this official learned sensitive judicial interception information through his judicial office. The government can protect voluntarily assumed official confidentiality without ordinary-public-speaker standards; DailyMail, SeattleTimes and Snepp supply that distinction. This is constitutional support for the statute's enacted elements, not an independent confidentiality offense, a source-of-knowledge element, or a command that any historic wiretap discussion is criminal. It does not approve a general prohibition on ordinary public reporting. All associates can adopt this narrow treatment without abandoning frozen refusal of an independent official-confidentiality ground.

The comparator's phrase 'reinstated the separate conviction' is materially incomplete. Official final paragraph leaves unresolved instruction challenge for remand. A change in statutory construction does NOT automatically restore a final conviction/sentence, mandate retrial or resolve harmless error. On ?1503 five-associate path the insufficient charged judicial connection preserves reversal; on ?2232 seven-associate path the expiry ground is rejected and ordinary preserved instruction/harmlessness issues return.
