## Independent sufficient grounds and adverse-path review

### Access and the governing six-step rule

The five remanding associates correctly distinguish legal error from an established merits violation. Casey directly adopted the complete rule; Kennedy's retained-choice join and the other Justices' votes against the old trimester timetable cannot be added to create a different replacement. The lower court's substantial-obstacle endpoint and strict facial alternative do not answer remaining demonstrated meaningful interference and the corresponding justification inquiry. Permission to telephone authorized information and complete certification at the procedure eliminates an assumed statutory second visit. It does not by itself decide whether the mandatory interval adds consequential delay or other concrete nontrivial interference to actual Thursday/Friday physician availability, travel, employment or appointment constraints.

The contrary affirmance path has real force: ordinary scheduling may accommodate an advance call, the State did not create every travel or provider constraint, and no substantiation burden arises merely from regulating abortion. It fails as a conclusive disposition for these five because the supplied lower analysis did not assess all evidence-supported incremental effects under the actual lesser-interference branch. The substantial affidavit/deposition record and the appellate assumption that evidentiary facts were true remain acknowledged. Lack of district fact findings alone is not the summary-judgment error, and remand does not require a hearing or trial by default.

The remand preserves the complete sequence: identify the actual duty, construction, exceptions and consequences; determine function and objectively supported purpose; establish the stable affected population, causal comparison and triggering interference; apply the independent baseline, obstructive-purpose and substantial-obstacle prohibitions, then residual severe and meaningful lesser-interference scrutiny; respect clinical and accurate-information protections; and tailor relief. The State's justification and fit obligations arise after the applicable trigger. The rule permits reliable experience and reasonable prediction, does not demand numerical benefit calculations or a new study in every case, and requires an answer to demonstrated concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternatives rather than hypothetical perfection. Below meaningful interference the heightened access inquiry ends without extinguishing independent protections.

Automatic final invalidation is also unsupported. Pennsylvania's record does not prove the North Dakota trigger, inadequate justification, indivisibility or severance result. The telephone/agent permissions are genuine distinctions. North Dakota's absence of Pennsylvania's distinct disclosure-health defense and rape-related permission to omit paternal-support information is equally real. Those omissions prevent treating every North Dakota compulsory-information application as already sustained by Casey; they do not establish a new preserved claim, an obligation to omit information, or automatic invalidity. Accurate content, optional materials and lawful documentation remain separately assessable, with dependent forms and sanctions addressed if their predicate fails.

The three access-affirmance positions rest on actual public Casey disagreement, not a reconstructed historical Fargo vote. Kennedy retained protected choice and clinical boundaries but expressly rejected the general lesser-burden allocation. Scalia and Thomas joined the more fundamental constitutional objection and reasonable-regulation ground. The present telephone/agent construction supplies a concrete application of those published positions. The record establishes no third-party veto or adjudicated necessary-care denial on the preserved access claim that contradicts their chosen bounded ground. Their disagreement cannot authorize a lower court to disregard the complete rule. A move to remand by applying that rule would be a new judicial choice, not an unspoken conditional vote already in this freeze.

### Abortion definition: textual notice and enforcement

All eight retain the independent ground that the complete enacted phrase defines pregnancy termination with the stated purpose, excluding production of a live birth and removal of a dead embryo or fetus. The intention qualifies the pregnancy termination being defined. An intentional diagnostic procedure is not, solely because it produces an unintended loss, a termination undertaken with that statutory purpose. The amniocentesis illustration is answered by the category's own words, without relocating a default mental state from another title. The strongest contrary reading detaches the intentional diagnostic act from the unintended termination and treats its nonexcluded diagnostic aim as the aim of that termination. The frozen construction reasonably rejects that severance of the phrase.

X-Citement's complete-category analysis supports contextual treatment for Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Scalia and Thomas's dissent there supplies a construction limit, not a prior join in that category holding: they cannot add a fault term the text will not bear. Their Fargo commitment meets that limit because intention is actually present in this definition and the whole phrase identifies its object. The construction is more than a general appeal to constitutional avoidance.

Kolender independently requires an enforcement boundary. This definition identifies a medical act and its purpose rather than authorizing an enforcer to demand additional compliance according to personal satisfaction. An enforcer must identify the defined purposeful termination; an unwanted diagnostic result alone does not satisfy that category. The ground does not depend on some clear elective-abortion applications or a universal rule requiring uncertainty in every application. It creates neither blanket diagnostic immunity nor a knowledge-of-illegality requirement, and it decides no completed prosecution or civil award. A materially different authoritative state construction or concrete enforcement remains reviewable. No presently indispensable missing culpability source defeats this limited ground.

### Emergency definition: intelligibility is distinct from constitutional breadth

All eight retain rejection of the preserved challenge to the terms grave, immediate and major bodily function, read as part of the enacted clinical forecast. The alternatives are an immediate abortion necessary to avert death, or a twenty-four-hour delay that will create grave peril of immediate and irreversible loss of major bodily function, assessed through the physician's prospective best clinical judgment. The standard specifies the medical consequences and timing to be assessed. It requires neither certainty nor a numerical prognosis and does not require waiting for the threatened harm to occur. These are notice and enforcement conclusions, not a finding that this restricted range of emergencies satisfies every Casey life-and-health requirement.

Vuitch and Doe support using intelligible professional judgments; their broader health constructions, prosecution burden and statutory details do not become North Dakota law. Colautti is the strongest contrary authority because subjective medical judgment does not itself cure an uncertain trigger or establish culpability for error, especially amid conflicting duties. The independent ground survives that objection at the claim's specified scope: these challenged severity and timing terms identify a comprehensible professional forecast rather than the conflicting viability formulations and uncertain technique priorities adjudicated in Colautti. The narrower emergency category may be clear even if an application later proves substantively insufficient to protect necessary care. Preservation therefore matters: the Court may decide the vagueness claim actually adjudicated below without deciding the unpressed emergency-access claim.

Kolender's distinct enforcement concern is met by the specified pregnancy complication, two outcomes, twenty-four-hour forecast and prospective clinical assessment. Officials may test clinical evidence against those criteria; they cannot replace them with disapproval of abortion or an invented condition. The source does not establish virtually complete enforcement discretion merely because physicians may reasonably differ. The qualification concerning reasonable later disagreement means disagreement alone does not establish every element of a punishable offense or damages claim. It does **not** mean that disagreement is never relevant, that a physician's assertion is conclusive, or that only proven bad faith permits liability.

The federal lower court's construction excluding significant life-or-health threats retains its prospective, nonbinding-on-state-courts status. It does not amend the statute or delete immediate, irreversible or major. The sufficient notice/enforcement ground rests on the enacted clinical criterion, rather than assuming the protective construction universally expands it. Best clinical judgment is not silently rewritten as good-faith judgment or professional immunity. The emergency explanation is due before the abortion **if possible**; separate written certification is no authority to delay necessary care. Materially different clinical applications, authoritative constructions and enforcement remain open.

If a later proposed opinion requires a definitive element-specific fault allocation or a broader substantive health ruling to sustain this component, the existing handoff does not provide that ground. It must not enlarge these affirmance commitments by implication. A precisely bounded reconsideration remains the recorded alternative if the independent clarity ground is rejected; erasing the exception while leaving consent and delay absolute is not the alternative selected by any associate.

### State fault and the distinct civil remedy

Mueller and North Dakota Education Association defeat the bare assertion that §12.1-02-02 supplies willfulness to every silent North Dakota criminal enactment. Goetz construed an expressly present securities-law fault term and did not abolish the title boundary. This correction does not show that every Title 14 offense has strict liability or that every lack of an express fault term is facially unconstitutional. The abortion definition's own intention language remains operative; the penalty's first misdemeanor sentence remains distinct from its expressly willful rule-or-regulation infraction sentence. Neither that second sentence nor Title 12.1 automatically supplies the first sentence's element-specific fault.

The separate punitive and treble civil-damages provisions do not acquire a criminal default. No associate supplies a civil defense, constitutional actual-knowledge floor, bad-faith-only rule, burden of proof or general knowledge-of-illegality condition absent its own lawful source. The definitions' limited text and clinical grounds remain sufficient without those additions. Thus the failure of an additional lower-court rationale does not itself require vacating both separately sustainable definition dispositions or independently invalidating the general penalty. Actual liability and any different challenge remain subject to their own elements, source and preservation.

### Remedy and interim protection

The five remanding associates select legal-error vacatur at the access component's scope, preserve both definition dispositions, and leave renewed assessment and appropriate proceedings below. They direct prompt consideration of a properly presented interim request under the applicable ordinary standard and the then-current record. The three others would affirm the access dismissal and deny restoration. Neither acknowledgment of remedial authority nor common insistence on relief fitting an established violation joins the opposing remand.

Immediate restoration is unsupported because the full prior decree and current equitable predicates are missing. Conversely, absence of a final invalidity determination does not bar a properly supported interim application. Vacatur does not itself reinstate the dissolved 1991 injunction, find irreparable injury, compel a hearing or award a permanent injunction. Casey's case-specific denial of extra materials or attendance relief creates no universal remedial bar; Benten's appellate-stay setting supplies no substitute ordinary standard. Transcon's separation of authority, violation and fit supports precision, without transferring its public-enforcement predicates. Final relief must identify the failed duty or application, dependent certifications and sanctions, surviving lawful duties and applicable severability basis.
