## 71. Wellons — vehicle, personal luggage, and suppression remedy

**Common bounded rules.** The rental agreement's omission of a driver does not alone decide that driver's personal Fourth Amendment interest. The inquiry evaluates his own possession and control with the renter's established permission, the right to exclude and other relevant circumstances; theft, fraud or an actual termination of possession may matter when established. Contract breach is relevant but does not automatically answer the constitutional question. A claimed personal closed container must be evaluated independently. Neither rule establishes final suppression, automatic privacy in every object, or an unrestricted right to use a rental car contrary to the owner's contract.

All eight vote to vacate the categorical privacy ground supporting the affirmance and remand for the separate vehicle and luggage inquiries and any lawfully available exceptions. The mandate addresses the suppression ruling and resulting conviction affirmance; **it is not resentencing**.

## 72. Koray — statutory credit and remedy

**Majority proposed sufficient ground.** Reverse the conditions-inquiry remand. In the coenacted scheme, official detention is a legal detention status rather than restrictive conditional release under section3142(c). Established release status excludes this period without converting disputed conditions into findings. Judicial review of BOP's calculation survives Wilson, and statutory credit for qualifying detention remains mandatory; no double credit is allowed. An internal program statement is not independently controlling merely because BOP calculates credit. No general deference holding is necessary.

Stevens answers that this context need not dictate an exclusive definition in a separate mandatory credit provision. Residual lenity is his supporting alternative only if ordinary construction leaves a genuine unresolved ambiguity; no universal lenity presumption is adopted.

**Remedy and omitted issues.** The seven reverse commitments require rejection of credit for the established conditional-release period, not resentencing or denial of every possible detention period. The repeated accounting, visitor restrictions and medically related departure remain allegations. Equal protection was abandoned and receives no merits disposition.
