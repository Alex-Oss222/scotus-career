## Issue 1: authorized formulation

Casey's complete six-step standard, rather than Salerno's independent no-valid-applications barrier, governs the abortion-access challenge. For each provision, determine the enacted duty and protected baseline; actual function and purpose; concrete additional interference, its causal comparison and the independently defined affected population; the applicable substantive protection in the required order; clinical and communication safeguards; and relief corresponding to the demonstrated violation. The relevant population consists of persons for whom the particular duty actually operates as a restriction, defined before deciding injury and kept stable, with evidence-supported subgroups. It is neither every pregnant person nor only successful claimants.

The existing phrase “a substantial obstacle ... in a large fraction of the relevant cases” is not retained as an independent numerical prerequisite for review or relief. It is replaced by the actual controlling inquiry: a protected-baseline violation, objectively established obstructive purpose, or substantial obstacle independently invalidates the duty; remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring; remaining meaningful interference requires the State to substantiate its permissible justification and fit and answer a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective. Challengers establish the duty, population, causation and triggering interference; the State bears only the applicable justification burden. Absence of a substantial obstacle does not end the inquiry. Below meaningful interference, heightened access scrutiny ends, while prohibited-purpose claims, independent constitutional protections and ordinary legal requirements remain.

Actual access conditions matter through the additional burden attributable to state law. Compare the system with and without the challenged duty or identified interacting package, holding other circumstances constant; account for cumulative effects without double counting. Independently existing poverty, distance or limited provider schedules is not itself a constitutional violation. Nor is the availability of telephone information an answer to every additional delay or clinical burden. Exceptions and alternatives count only if usable. Reliable evidence and reasonable prospective predictions may establish injury without waiting for lost access; the existing submissions are not converted into Supreme Court findings.

Relief follows Step 6. Individual injury is not defeated by another person's compliance. Broader relief needs a corresponding showing. An indivisible mandatory condition may fail when its ordinary operation produces demonstrated meaningful interference and the State fails to justify it; otherwise relief remains confined to established applications or groups. Preserve independently lawful provisions under applicable severability law and address dependent certification and sanctions. There is no universal numerical facial threshold and no categorical ruling that every twenty-four-hour interval is valid or invalid.
