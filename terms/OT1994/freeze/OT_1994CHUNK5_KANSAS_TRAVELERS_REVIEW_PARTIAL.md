## Comparison and conclusion

All ten provisional Kansas component conclusions remain unchanged for Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer.

The ERISA holdings distinguish the reviewed economic influences from a command to plan benefits or administration, while preserving possible acute indirect effects and rejecting categorical or numerical safe harbors. That statute-specific inquiry does not establish that a small hydrologic depletion is legally immaterial, that an indirect physical effect cannot violate IV-D, or that conventional state water regulation is immune from an interstate Compact. It changes neither Kansas's proof obligation nor the need to examine actual timing and usability. The WWSP ground therefore remains the particular model's inadequate operational comparison and nonparallel errors, not a generalized proposition that indirect effects never count. Pumping liability remains supported by the distinct record of material usable loss.

Travelers's separate analysis of the 13%, 11% and conditional HMO measures does not turn a label or a common program into a substitute for component-specific treatment. The Kansas handoff already keeps the pre-Compact baseline, actual Plan operation, claimed free-standing legal credit, Trinidad command theory, WWSP depletion, usability, prior approval and two counterclaims distinct. No calculation or burden moves between those components as a result of Travelers. Its ERISA presumption concerning traditional health regulation supplies no new presumption against enforcing the Compact's terms.

Travelers expressly chooses a limited appellate vacatur under §2106 for an unresolved direct-plan application while preserving the claim and properly supported independent grounds. It does not establish a universal remand rule, automatic interim protection, automatic dissolution of every order, or an original-jurisdiction disposition rule. Kansas presents recommendations of a Master in an original liability proceeding, not the appellate judgment and injunction reviewed in Travelers. The planned further remedy proceedings on established pumping liability therefore continue to rest on the Compact, the existing original-action framework and the date-eligible water authorities. They do not borrow §2106 as the source of power or silently vacate a final Kansas decree that has not been entered.

The reservation of independent Trinidad contract/beneficiary claims remains exactly that: no determination that those claims succeed, no injunction or payment entitlement, and no alteration of the proposed dismissal of the presented Compact theory. Travelers does not require deciding those independent claims now or treating their possible availability as a basis to sustain unproved Compact liability. Similarly, preserving remedy-stage equities and actual physical offsets does not announce a final depletion amount, monetary cutoff, payment obligation or prospective engineering order.
