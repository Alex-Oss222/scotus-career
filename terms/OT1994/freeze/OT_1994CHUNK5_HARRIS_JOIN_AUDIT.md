## Operative proposition and boundary

Congress may protect an instrumentality of interstate commerce against its direct forcible seizure even when the particular taking is intrastate. Original §2119 is constitutional as applied here because it protects possession and control of the motor vehicle itself, the object is a functioning means of transportation, and the offense requires proof that this particular vehicle was transported, shipped, or received in interstate or foreign commerce. The Buick's Michigan manufacture and Arkansas/Mississippi titling establish the supplied vehicle connection. The occupants' displacement and the taking of the car concern the transportation instrumentality itself, rather than conduct merely happening in or near it.

No present interstate journey, shipment, or planned interstate resale is found or required. The Court does not infer a trip from the title history. The constitutional mechanism is protection against seizure of the transportation instrumentality; the history condition restricts the vehicles the statute reaches. Neither the jurisdictional wording nor old interstate movement alone establishes constitutional sufficiency. This application does not make every crime involving a vehicle federal, classify every vehicle for every federal purpose, or extend automatically to possession of any previously shipped object. Profit motive is not a statutory or constitutional condition of this direct protection. The asserted retaliatory motive does not remove the taking from the rule.

The opinion should state that the Fifth Circuit's generalized sequence through violence, insurance, travel, and national economic costs is not the ground sustaining this prosecution. Lopez supplies the governing limit on that sequence. The Harris opinion need not demand that the four Lopez dissenters renounce their broader class-effects position or declare their dissent unsound. No economic-class precedent is displaced, and the direct-taking holding does not imply that economic-acquisition authority is unavailable.

## Asymmetry and strongest contrary path

The strongest objection is that transportation capability plus old interstate movement could let Congress regulate almost every local incident involving a car. That objection would defeat an opinion merely labeling all automobiles instrumentalities. The supplied formulation instead identifies a specific prohibited interference: forcible seizure of the transportation instrumentality's possession and control. Its application is consequently different from using a car's presence or manufacturing history to federalize an unrelated offense. The rule is an adjudication of this particular application, not a claim that Lopez already decided carjacking.

The contrary four/four division is not supported by the existing individual evidence. All eight Harris commitments leave a separately justified instrumentality ground open. Allied-Bruce's remote-history qualification applies to O'Connor and Kennedy just as it applies to Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. The latter four's economic ground does not logically compel an instrumentality join, but neither does having a sufficient alternative establish a refusal. Their own published acceptance of concrete interstate relationships, together with the distinct direct-protection mechanism, supports the affirmative join. No source identifies current interstate service as their constitutional prerequisite. Inventing that requirement would give a general analytical concern the force of a Justice-specific commitment it does not possess.

## Authority and source limits

The governing authority and principal individual evidence are the complete safe [Lopez Public Projection](../entering-law/OT_1994CHUNK5_LOPEZ_PUBLIC.md), particularly its direct-instrumentality explanation and published joins, and the [Allied-Bruce public holdings and writings copied in the entering-law slice](../entering-law/OT_1994CHUNK5_B.md). The [original enactment](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-106/pdf/STATUTE-106-Pg3384.pdf) supplies the express particular-vehicle condition. The validated Harris facts and frozen commitments supply the application; no factual premise is added.

Primary-source checks confirm the distinction between a general doctrinal route and this application. [Southern Railway v. United States, 222 U.S. 20, 26–27](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-222/pdf/USREPORTS-222-20.pdf), protects interstate traffic from intrastate dangers because traffic shares an operating railway; it does not decide the status of every automobile. [Brooks v. United States, 267 U.S. 432, 438–39](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-267/pdf/USREPORTS-267-432.pdf), sustains punishment of interstate stolen-car transportation and closely connected disposition; it does not hold that old movement alone sustains every later taking. These opinions confirm the need for an operative connection rather than supplying an unmade fact about Harris. Their complete opinions were read and checked against the official Reports.

[Scarborough v. United States, 431 U.S. 563](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-431/pdf/USREPORTS-431-563.pdf), joined by Stevens, construes the firearms statute's nexus to include prior interstate travel without contemporaneous movement. Its complete opinion, dissent, and notes were read at Cornell; the official Report confirms the lineup. It is limited individual evidence against assigning Stevens a universal current-movement requirement, not a holding that dispenses with the independent constitutional work required here. None of these confirmatory sources changes the validated record or replaces actual Lopez.
