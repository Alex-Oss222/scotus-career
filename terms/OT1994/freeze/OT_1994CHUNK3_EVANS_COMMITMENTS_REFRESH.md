## Components and common boundaries

**J — jurisdiction and reach.** Section 1257(a) review of the final state appellate suppression disposition, following dismissal without prejudice; adequate independent state ground under Long and actual Sochor/Middlebrooks; discretionary reasons to decline the merits remain distinct from absence of jurisdiction.

**E — exclusionary-rule proposition.** Whether a court employee's negligent failure to report a quashed warrant is outside Leon merely because it is ministerial, and whether reasonable police reliance can therefore avoid federal exclusion. No constitutional-validity ruling is needed: the State concedes the Fourth Amendment violation.

**F — factual application.** Who caused the entry to remain; whether reliance was objectively reasonable in the legally relevant circumstances; how to treat the rare-error estimate together with the three additional irregularities. The trial judge did not resolve attribution or system reliability. The narrower legal rule may be settled without finding that the record establishes every premise for ultimate admission.

**R — remedy.** Reversal and remand for application of a conditional rule, affirmance of suppression, or discretionary dismissal. Reversal does not restore a conviction, direct final admission without necessary findings, or adjudicate guilt. All eight accept these restrictions.

The other three irregularities are not erased by the estimate of one occurrence every three or four years in this particular court. Conversely, they do not establish a statewide error rate, repeated arrests of all affected persons, chronic police neglect, or knowledge of unreliability by Sargent. The merits filing's same-day-quashing account remains advocacy; the transcript and attribution dispute control the factual qualification. The post-arrest I-Bureau call is not a prior independent court-record check. The January 7 notification and fax are responsive acts, not proof of the precise time of database clearance.

## Analytical annex: authorities, two lawful paths and minimum formulations

These are concise conclusions and legal support, not conference dialogue. The corrected record is common to every row; different judgments do not receive different facts.

**Stevens — restraint and the remedial function.** His full Long dissent, 463 U.S. at 1065–1072, supplies the discretionary objection to using scarce federal review to narrow a state court's protection. The controlling Long rule nevertheless remains operative, so this commitment distinguishes a DIG preference from a false jurisdictional dismissal. His Leon separate, 468 U.S. at 960–980, and Krull dissent join, 480 U.S. at 361–369, supply the substantive concern: the ordinary officer's blamelessness does not settle systemic incentives or the consequence of withdrawn authority. His actual Dickerson application join accepts suppression on the real operative facts. The strongest opposing path is Leon's conclusion that neutral judicial actors lack the prosecution incentive needed for deterrence. Stevens distinguishes a judge's constitutional judgment from the unrepaired ministerial record, while accepting that this distinction does not resurrect automatic exclusion for every constitutional violation. His narrower conditional merits ground is this administrative chain and this conceded invalid arrest, not overruling all good-faith doctrine.

**O'Connor — the responsible actor and objective limits.** Her Leon join provides direct support for an exception concerning reasonable reliance upon the judicial process. Her own Krull dissent is substantial contrary material: it rejects an extension based solely on the enforcing officer's good faith, emphasizes the different institutional function, and recognizes the larger effects of systemic action. The minimum formulation satisfying both is a court-employee exception expressly conditioned on objectively reasonable reliance, without resolving police responsibility or the disputed reliability facts. The rare estimate does not establish isolation; the three other cases do not alone establish the sort of systemic defect that makes reliance unreasonable. Her actual Dickerson suppression join requires honoring a supported finding, but here there is no corresponding attribution finding. Remand thus preserves, rather than contradicts, her treatment of the record.

**Scalia — extension without erasing the actor distinction.** His Krull majority join, especially 480 U.S. at 349–355 and 360 n.17, directly supports extending Leon when the relevant actor differs from the executing officer while keeping objective limits and the outside-scope police question distinct. The competing institutional path argues that warrant-record clerks, unlike judges deciding probable cause, maintain a mechanism police depend upon and can respond to exclusion. Scalia's conclusion is that this argument does not justify ignoring the source of error in every case; it may matter to a distinct police or mixed failure. His actual Dickerson full join likewise distinguishes lawful information acquisition from an unauthorized additional search and does not dispense with actual findings. No current-case vote, separate or author is used as support.

**Kennedy — supported findings and bounded remedial correction.** His own actual Sochor Part II-A and Middlebrooks joins support the threshold treatment, while his actual Dickerson application join is the closest verified current Fourth Amendment position in the permitted materials. That record-based commitment is compatible with correcting a categorical federal rule and returning an unresolved application. The strongest alternative is affirming because the hearing already demonstrates state negligence and no valid warrant. Leon forecloses treating those two facts alone as the complete exclusion inquiry, and the explicit absence of an attribution finding distinguishes Dickerson's accepted manipulation finding. The court-employee conditional rule, not a general computer exception, is the sufficient ground. No direct predecision Kennedy database writing is claimed.

**Souter — a legal distinction with genuine factual limits.** His Sochor opinion and Middlebrooks join require a real independent state ground, not a possible future one. His actual Dickerson application join supports adjudication on accepted facts and supplies a strong reason not to convert this record into a finding of isolated error. The alternative suppression path is substantial because three further irregularities make the problem concrete and ministerial records differ from judging probable cause. He nevertheless applies Leon's actor-sensitive remedial framework to reject the conclusion that the identity of the responsible institution can never matter. The least broad corrective disposition leaves the cause and objective-reliance application to the state court. That does not hold that three or four errors are tolerable or require a quantified rate before an objection can be heard.

**Thomas — correct the rule, then obtain its factual predicate.** His actual Dickerson position was a join in Blackmun's concurrence/dissent seeking findings under the correct tactile-recognition rule, not a vote authorizing the challenged search. That distinction is controlling for the remedial analogy here: deciding that court-employee errors may fall within Leon does not establish the predicate that this error did. The competing affirmance path would regard the existing state-negligence proof as sufficient without attribution. He rejects that rule as neglecting the actor whose future conduct exclusion could influence. His actual Sochor and Middlebrooks joins support jurisdiction on the real basis of the lower judgment. The narrower alternative of remanding without announcing any legal distinction would leave the original finding gap unexplained; his commitment resolves only the conditional legal rule needed to make the fact question relevant.

**Ginsburg — distinguish the function, without invented empirics.** Her actual Middlebrooks join supports the threshold distinction between a genuinely independent state basis and this interwoven opinion. No own date-eligible opinion establishing a database-error rule was located in the bounded sources, and her merits commitment is not presented as compelled by a different Justice's prior vote. Applying Leon itself, including footnote 24's refusal to isolate the last executing officer, she treats the function of maintaining live arrest authority as the legally significant distinction. The strongest opposite path is that ordinary court clerks lack any stake in prosecution and therefore share judges' weak response to exclusion. The record's three additional irregularities and the unlocated communication failure leave an insufficient basis to make that new functional exemption categorical here. Her affirmance requires neither a finding of pervasive unreliability nor a general rule that all negligence requires exclusion; it rests on declining the proposed extension on this record and preserving the institutional maintenance incentive. This is a provisional substantive commitment requiring historical reconciliation, not a claim of prior settled Ginsburg doctrine.

**Breyer — apply governing law without inherited preferences.** No own predecision database opinion is claimed. His membership begins August 3, 1994; Blackmun's Leon concurrence is not his personal precedent. He applies the binding Leon/Krull remedial framework to the evidence and the legally relevant actor. The best affirmance argument is that actual repetition and a ministerial information chain make systemic deterrence materially different from a magistrate's warrant judgment. The paired evidence also includes a limited estimate of rarity and responsive efforts, but neither side has an established factual account of cause or general reliability. A rule allowing a confined court-employee exception while returning the factual application gives effect to the distinction without resolving unsupported empirical propositions. His support ends where a draft presumes system reliability, discounts all repeated errors, or decides police/mixed cases categorically. The absence of an individually on-point writing is a source limitation to be weighed at reconciliation, not a reason to manufacture one.
