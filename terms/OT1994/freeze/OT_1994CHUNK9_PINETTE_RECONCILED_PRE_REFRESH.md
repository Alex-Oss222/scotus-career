### Stevens

**Conclusion and historical test.** Retain reversal. The comparator at 515 U.S. 797–815 confirms the independent distinction between identifiable attended private expression and a stationary religious object appearing to speak from the seat of government. **Strongest competing path.** Equal terms, a diverse actual forum, private funding, and an offered sign substantially support access under Widmar and Lamb's Chapel. Those are real facts rather than mere formal labels. Stevens nevertheless concludes that placing the unaccompanied cross at this governmental site communicates approval beyond private title. His view protects reasonable nonadherents without requiring mastery of forum doctrine or the site's full administrative history.
