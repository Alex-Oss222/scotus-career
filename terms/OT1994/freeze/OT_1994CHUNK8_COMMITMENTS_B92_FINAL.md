## Adversarial paths

1503A: affirm relief on narrow nexus ground. Defendant must know a pending proceeding and intentionally undertake conduct whose natural and probable relation is to obstruct that proceeding; independent agency lies do not meet the judicial-proceeding requirement merely because an agent might later testify. Pettibone requires knowing pending justice, and source-lower Wood/Grubb distinction shows concrete connection matters. No actual success required. Strongest contrary argument: 'endeavor' encompasses an unsuccessful corrupt purpose, and knowledgeable lies intended to reach grandjury are enough even if agents lack subpoenas. Answer: unsuccessful endeavor need not succeed, but must concern the statutory proceeding rather than a speculative possible transmission. Affirms1503 reversal without adopting implied wholesale witness repeal, blanket safe harbor for FBI lies or a privilege to lie.

1503B: reverse statutory exclusion/remand. Unchanged omnibus text plus purposeful endeavor to corrupt a known grandjury can encompass lies to potential agent witnesses; no statutory subpoena/authorization condition is enacted. Russell's distinction of endeavor from completed obstruction and Batchelder's permissible overlapping offenses answer implied repeal. Strongest objection is overbreadth swallowing every agency lie. Requires actual proven knowledge AND corrupt purpose toward a pending judicial proceeding; generalized desire to avoid investigation is insufficient. Remand retained instruction issue is indispensable.

2232A: reverse expiry bar/remand. Text's completed-application knowledge and subjective obstructive purpose, notice of possible interception and attempted-notice coverage do not make undisclosed expiration an automatic defense. Where defendant believes interception resulting from known application possible and warns in order to impede it, knowledge of actual application plus purposeful notice can suffice. Strongest objection: POSSIBLE describes objective authorized interception, and SUCH binds intent to the expired specificorder rather than some unknown later wiretap. Answer: this narrow result addresses subjective obstruction of the interception sought in the known actual application, not knowledge of any historic tap plus generalized investigative hostility. Actual knowledge that interception impossible would negate asserted purpose. No independent official-confidentiality ground adopted: source/source-of-knowledge instruction was contested and government eschewed it below.

2232B: affirm expiry bar. POSSIBLEinterception and SUCHinterception objectively limit notice offense to legally available interception from known authorization/application; attempted notice does not redefine prohibited information. No physically successful interference required, but completed expiry without known pending successor puts noticed information outside class. Strongest objection: completed application language and intent/attempt provisions make factual impossibility irrelevant. Distinguish inability to deliver prohibited notice from legal absence of protected interception. Lenity supports bounded reading if ambiguity persists; not an independent ruling that all disclosure protected.

Current Ratzlaf's text-specific mensrea/lenity, Staples' factual-knowledge construction and Hubbard's actual court-entity construction inform interpretive discipline, not substitute their elements here. Hubbard does not remove executive FBI false statements from1001 or decide1503. No obligation to know statutes' illegality grafted onto either charged offense.
