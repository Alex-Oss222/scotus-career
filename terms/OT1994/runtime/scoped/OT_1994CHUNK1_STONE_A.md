- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone finds no jurisdictional, preservation, or vehicle bar and reaches the question presented.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the enacted text and the settled common-law meaning of conspiracy. Section 846 criminalizes the agreement and omits the overt-act language Congress used in neighboring conspiracy statutes. The Court may not add that element.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Section 846 does not require allegation or proof of an overt act; the unlawful agreement is the actus reus, and lenity does not apply because the statutory omission is not ambiguous.

- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not reduce the Government’s burden to prove the unlawful agreement and the defendant’s knowing participation beyond a reasonable doubt.

## 3. Hess v. Port Authority Trans-Hudson Corp.

<!-- BEGIN_SECTION II -->

### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## Position and scope

**Approval Status:** USER-SUPPLIED POSITION. The supplied disposition, reasoning and express alternatives are recorded as preparation instructions. No Court judgment, vote, rationale or coalition is created by this supplement.

**Build:** Commerce Clause and Federalism.

## Case-specific reasoning

Identify the entity's actual legal relationship to the states and responsibility for its debts. A governmental connection is not enough to make every public corporation an arm of the state. Keep this institutional-status inquiry separate from Congress's power to abrogate immunity where immunity otherwise exists.

Do not ask merely whether the entity was created by States or performs a public function; ask about separate legal identity, treasury responsibility, control, assets, debts, authority to sue and contract, fiscal independence, and the practical source from which judgments will be paid.

## Disposition, application and preserved limits

- **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** REVERSE the Third Circuit and REMAND.
- **Stone's Remedy and Remand Instruction:** The FELA actions may proceed against PATH in federal court, subject to ordinary defenses and merits adjudication.

#### Issue 1: Merits

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone finds no jurisdictional, preservation, or vehicle bar and reaches the question presented.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies and clarifies Lake Country Estates. State-treasury exposure receives great weight, but it is not an exclusive test. The compact, separate corporate identity, independent revenues, power to sue and be sued, and absence of state responsibility for PATH judgments show that the suits do not operate against either State.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A bistate compact entity is not entitled to the States’ Eleventh Amendment immunity when the compact creates a distinct enterprise responsible for its own obligations and a judgment will not run against either State’s treasury or sovereign dignity.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** PATH is financially self-sufficient and legally distinct. The capped provisions for possible state contributions do not convert every PATH liability into a state debt. Reading the compact as a whole gives no good reason to treat PATH as the States themselves.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Treasury liability is highly probative, not categorically dispositive; a materially different compact that makes an entity a direct alter ego of the States remains open.

## 4. United States v. X-Citement Video, Inc.

<!-- BEGIN_SECTION II -->

### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## Approval and scope

**Approval Status:** APPROVED IN SUPPLIED SOURCE. The existing approval label is preserved; consolidation supplies no new approval, vote or Court holding. Revalidate only against the actual event-date record, lawful scope and effective law.

- **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** REVERSE the Ninth Circuit and REMAND.
- **Stone's Remedy and Remand Instruction:** The case returns for proceedings under the construction requiring knowledge of the facts that make the depictions unlawful; the facial First Amendment invalidation is set aside.

#### Issue 1: Merits

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone finds no jurisdictional, preservation, or vehicle bar and reaches the question presented.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the strong mens rea presumption to the facts separating innocent from criminal conduct. “Knowingly” reaches the sexually explicit character of the material and the use of a minor. That fair construction also avoids an unnecessary facial constitutional ruling.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Sections 2252(a)(1) and (2) require proof that the defendant knew the depiction was sexually explicit and knew the performer was a minor, though not knowledge of the statute or the precise legal age definition.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** Ratzlaf requires knowledge that structuring is unlawful under its particular willfulness provision; Staples requires knowledge of the weapon characteristics making it a machinegun, not knowledge of the registration requirement or illegality. Those offense-specific holdings distinguish legal knowledge from factual knowledge; neither automatically construes §2252. Staples supports the factual-knowledge analogy when otherwise familiar conduct can be innocent. The grammatical distance does not overcome text, structure, settled mens rea principles, and constitutional avoidance.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The Government need not prove that the defendant knew the legal classification or the exact age threshold; unmistakable child pornography and other distinct offenses remain governed by their own text.
