## 49. Stone v. INS

### Historical comparison and additional review

History affirmed the original-order timeliness dismissal. Its associate support was Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Ginsburg; O'Connor, Souter and Breyer supported reversal. Kennedy's historical authorship and Breyer's historical dissent authorship are documentary facts only. The provisional model agreed with six of those eight judgment positions but placed Stevens and Ginsburg with the original-order-review supporters.

The dispute turns on former §1105a(a)'s incorporation of chapter 158 **subject to its specified modifications**, particularly the 1990 command in (a)(6) to consolidate separately sought review of the original order and reconsideration. Locomotive Engineers, 482 U.S. at 279–285, supplies the ordinary rule that a timely administrative reconsideration motion postpones finality for judicial review; it also distinguishes the original order from a refusal to reopen for material error. Actual Ibarra supplies the analogous timely criminal reconsideration rule, and actual Darby forbids extra exhaustion requirements in the specified APA setting. Neither holds that Congress cannot alter the ordinary rule in an express agency-specific review scheme.

The incorporation argument remains a lawful and substantial alternative. Consolidation can have work when judicial review begins before agency reconsideration; mandatory consolidation differs from an ordinary discretionary ability to manage related reviews. But that argument requires choosing how the INA's specific exception coordinates two independently pursued review objects. It does not establish that the no-tolling construction contradicts Locomotive Engineers. Conversely, the separate-finality position cannot accurately say that consolidation has literally no conceivable application under the competing construction. Its sufficient ground is that the structure of the **amended** review statute calls for original-order review within its own period while separately permitting later review of reconsideration, with mandatory consolidation when both are before the court. The shortening of the original period corroborates that construction; no-stay regulations and delay policy alone do not establish it.

Additional review of Stevens's entire Locomotive Engineers writing matters. His objection there concerned a judicially created exclusion from review of material-error reopening denials where the enacted text did not make that distinction, 482 U.S. at 287–295. He recognized that restrictive review language can control and distinguished statutory settings containing it, id. at 292 n.7. That is not a commitment that an express modification in another statute must leave the ordinary finality rule untouched. His actual Ibarra and Darby joins preserve ordinary rules in their own domains. His public Gustafson join accepts the relevance of statutory context. Together those sources support a serious incorporation argument, but supply no changed premise that would explain rejecting this particular INA construction when his historical position accepted it.

For Ginsburg, the provisional reliance on her public Gustafson dissent identifies a method, not a new rule about incorporation or finality. Her insistence that section 10 should not erase an express section 2 definition leaves contextual qualification available when the enacted provisions warrant it. Former §1105a(a) expressly introduces its enumerated exceptions; treating (a)(6) as a modification does not disregard an unqualified incorporation. She was not a member of the Ibarra or Darby Courts, and no personal join in them is attributed. Her actual Gustafson position supplies no new INA-specific legal, factual, procedural or remedial premise.

The exact March 25, 1993 filing date, verified in the lower opinion rather than the comparator's approximate account, changes no side of the timeliness issue: it remains within ninety days of February 3, 1993 and outside ninety days of July 26, 1991. The motion, scope, statute and relief remain materially the same. After this additional legal and source review, revise Stevens and Ginsburg to affirm the original-order dismissal. This is a correction of an inference drawn too broadly from their earlier writings, not adoption of historical votes as a command. The other six rows remain supported on their individual grounds.

## 51. Rubin v. Coors Brewing Co.

### Historical comparison and reconciliation of the narrower provisional rationale

History affirmed the label judgment with all eight associates supporting the result. Seven—O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer—joined Central Hudson reasoning rejecting both material advancement and reasonable fit; Stevens concurred in the judgment on a distinct truthful-information rationale while agreeing that the statute did not advance the stated objective. History also rejected the particular unsupported claim that federal suppression was needed to facilitate state policy. Thomas's historical authorship supplies no assignment.

The provisional model selected a third-prong-only opinion joined by all eight, found no necessary separate writing and left fit undecided. Its advancement analysis is sound on this bounded record, but that did not complete reconciliation of the distinct doctrinal choices. The packet expressly makes fit an available path and the parties presented it. Neither the Tenth Circuit's nonreach of fit nor the fact that advancement suffices prohibits affirmance on the district court's independently litigated fit ground. Nothing in the admitted scope excludes it.

Further review of the official statutory unit, complete appellate opinion, competing fit submissions, actual Edge and April 18 Lanphere supports a separate reasonable-fit conclusion. Fox does not demand the least restrictive imaginable means. Nor does the existence of one alternative automatically invalidate speech regulation. The relevant defect is the unexplained breadth of this ban on accurate percentages in relation to the harm actually shown and the concrete means directed at that harm: direct limits on strength, controls on promotion emphasizing high strength, and a restriction confined to the allegedly affected market segment. The Government answers that national strength limits could displace state choice, numerical claims can promote strength, and a narrower malt-liquor restriction might miss competition elsewhere. Those points deserve an answer, not dismissal. But they do not adequately justify suppression of every accurate numerical label in the challenged scheme, including information enabling comparison of weaker products, when proportionate direct or promotional measures remain available. No replacement program is constitutionally prescribed. No state-by-state enactment is found without verification.

Actual Lanphere reinforces, rather than displaces, the separation of advancement from reasonable fit. Kennedy, Stevens, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer joined both grounds there. O'Connor and Scalia's dissent rests on governmental disclosure of official information and privacy, while accepting review; their objection does not establish a power to suppress independently possessed accurate product information. Actual Discovery Network likewise retains proportionate fit without an all-sources requirement. The record therefore supplies a legal basis for the seven associates to adopt the fit ground already identified as live in the packet. Their provisional nonreach expressed no substantive objection to fit, and no concrete changed premise supports deleting history's independently sufficient ground after that ground is examined.

### Common scope and the two supported paths

The charged enactment is original 18 U.S.C. §922(q)(1)(A), not a later commerce-element statute. **Bounded invalidation position.** The stand-alone original possession prohibition is not sustained by the proffered chain from gun possession through crime costs or school disruption to national productivity. Neither its regulated class nor its enacted elements supplies the commercial connection that makes the aggregation precedents applicable in their settings. The absence of a case-specific nexus is relevant but not a universal requirement for every commerce statute. This position preserves Congress's broad authority over economic activity and comprehensive interstate commercial regulation, including appropriate regulation of intrastate activity essential to such a scheme; this original provision is not sustained as such an essential component. It rejects a universal formal-findings requirement and declines to overrule Wickard or Perez, revive traditional-governmental-function immunity, or decide every noncommercial federal offense. It would affirm the judgment directing dismissal of this charge.

**Bounded sustaining position.** Congress could rationally find that the class of school gun possession substantially affects national commerce through its effect on education, workforce preparation and travel, and could reasonably address that class by the original prohibition. Wickard and Perez permit assessment of class effects without treating this individual's conduct as independently substantial. The position preserves judicial review, the need for a substantial class relationship and reasonable means, and the absence of a universal formal-findings prerequisite. It does not confer commerce power merely because safe schools are desirable or because this object once crossed a state line. It would reverse the constitutional dismissal and remand for the unresolved sentencing objections, rather than validate the entire conviction and sentence without further review.
