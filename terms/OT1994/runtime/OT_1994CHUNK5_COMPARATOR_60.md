- **Historical Holding:** A federal court is neither a “department” nor an “agency” under 18 U.S.C. § 1001; the Court overruled Bramblett to that extent and reversed the § 1001 judgment in part.
- **Historical Authority:** The official opinion structure stated above controls only as historical comparator; it is not simulated law entering Stone’s vote.
