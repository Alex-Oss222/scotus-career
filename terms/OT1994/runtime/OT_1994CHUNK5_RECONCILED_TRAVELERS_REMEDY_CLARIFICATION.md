# Travelers — Stone-blind clarification of the reserved direct-plan remedy

**Scope and status:** Clarification of the existing final reconciliation for the April 26, 1995 Travelers / Pataki / Hospital Association matters, Nos. 93-1408, 93-1414 and 93-1415. This document does not reopen Lopez, alter the final B freeze, add a Chief Justice position, select an author or Court judgment, or claim a newly modeled interim-relief vote. No Stone or assembly material was received or read. No Git command was executed.

**Conclusion:** The frozen eight-associate position leaves the direct self-funded application, including the need for relief on that application, for lower-court consideration on remand. It does **not** determine that a separately identifiable direct-plan injunction remains operative pending remand, and it does **not** expressly direct immediate vacatur or dissolution of every existing direct-plan restraint. Neither conclusion may be inferred from the reservation alone. The existing freeze supports remand without a present direct-plan merits determination. It leaves the precise immediate effect on any existing injunction or stay unresolved. No associate should be recorded as opposing either unmodeled interim course.

This distinction matters because reversal of the broad economic-effects rationale is not itself a decision that the direct-plan application is valid, and leaving that application open is not an affirmance of all relief previously resting on the rejected rationale. An interim preservation order and a procedural vacatur permitting renewed relief can each coexist with merits nonreach, but they are different operative directions. The final handoff did not choose between them.

The neutral alternative-remand path, actual X-Citement's preservation of remand discretion and the historical reservation support it. | This allocation of further adjudication does not by itself say what happens to each existing injunction/stay in the interval. The three options should not be treated as three opposing merits votes. The third is the existing remand commitment; the first and second are additional immediate-remedy choices that could be attached to a reserved merits application if supported. No broad ERISA injunction may be justified on remand merely by reasserting that ordinary economic influence necessarily triggers preemption. Conversely, no new merits rejection of the reserved direct-plan claim follows from rejecting that broad rationale. Independent FEHBA and Actuarial Letter relief remains outside the decided review and cannot be swept away by an undifferentiated instruction dissolving the entire lower injunction.

## Exact clean clarification needed if an immediate direction is required

The bounded remand can be stated faithfully as: **the direct self-funded application and appropriate relief on it remain for consideration on remand; the Court makes no present merits determination of that application.** This wording reports the existing substantive reservation. It must not be followed by an unsupported assertion that a particular injunction necessarily continues or necessarily dissolves.

If assembly requires a concrete immediate instruction, the necessary clean process is limited:

1. Neutral validation must establish the exact original injunction/judgment provisions applicable to direct self-funded payors; the 13% stay's terms and duration; the April 27, 1993 payment order identified in the existing sources; and any admitted lower-court modification, mandate or stay controlling immediately before April 26, 1995. Identify separately which relief rests on ERISA, FEHBA or the Actuarial Letter. Do not import historical Supreme Court stay or mandate action as this Court's action. The currently supplied broad references are insufficient to certify that a single operative direct-plan injunction exists in the assumed form.
2. Identify any party-requested interim disposition and the date-eligible rule needed for the proposed mandate effect. Only sources material to choosing preservation, procedural vacatur or ordinary lower-court administration are necessary; no full reopening of the surcharge merits is requested.
3. Send only the clean order/status facts and neutral remedial alternatives, with actual entering law, to a clean model context. Ask each associate to specify the immediate treatment of the identified relief **conditional on reservation of the direct application**, keeping that question distinct from direct-plan preemption on the merits. Preserve the result and return for historical reconciliation. Do not frame a desired vote or pass private Stone material.

If the clarified source establishes a routine consequence compelled by the specific operative orders and governing mandate rule, identify that consequence and its authority rather than inventing discretionary disagreement. If a genuine choice remains, record the narrow remedial commitments. Until then, the absence of such a choice is an unresolved detail, not an eight-Justice opposition to any proposed course.
