- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** 5 F. 3d 806, reversed and remanded. Vote: 5-4.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter, J., delivered the opinion of the Court, in which Stevens, O’Connor, Ginsburg, and Breyer, JJ., joined. J., and Kennedy and Thomas, JJ., joined, post, p. 456.
- **Historical Holding:** 1. Under United States v. Bagley, 473 U. S. 667, four aspects of materiality for Brady purposes bear emphasis. First, favorable evidence is material, and constitutional error results from its suppression by the government, if there is a “reasonable probability” that, had the evidence been disclosed to the defense, the result of the proceeding would have been different.
