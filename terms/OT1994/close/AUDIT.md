### F02 — Low — The checker misclassifies source identifiers as commit references

[tools/check_term.py](../../../tools/check_term.py), lines 167–174, scans every seven-to-forty-character hexadecimal substring in Records and close files without requiring a commit-reference context. The documented object checks found the asserted references. The unresolved defect is diagnostic: source identifiers can produce false warnings and obscure a genuinely missing reference. Source context must therefore accompany a warning.

### F03 — Low — Candidate cleanup is triggered by audit wording, not completed publication

[tools/check_term.py](../../../tools/check_term.py), lines 176–181, treats the adjacent words `no` and `unresolved` anywhere in AUDIT as proof that close has been completed, then reports the presence of candidate files as an error. A failed audit can trigger the same predicate as a completed close. Candidate status therefore requires independent examination of publication and file state. The predicate alone establishes neither completion nor a cleanup obligation.
