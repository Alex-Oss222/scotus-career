### Demore v. Kim; No. 01-1491; judgment below, 276 F.3d 523 (9th Cir. 2002). — 2003-10-14

Effective October 14, 2003, section 1226(e) preserves independently proper habeas review of the legality of the mandatory framework, with effective relief and the ordinary limits on discretionary review and initial agency factfinding. Section 1226(c) may operate before a final removal order, subject to actual consideration of disputed statutory membership. Lawful permanent residents with a category question that is not insubstantial and not interposed solely for delay have a statutory entitlement to individual flight-risk and danger consideration, with each independently sufficient mandatory ground addressed.

These jurisdiction and statutory-threshold propositions are direct majority holdings. Kim's remand applies the detainee clear-and-convincing risk showing and preserves his current release through the threshold determination and any consequent bond decision under a five-vote judgment. The burden rationale has four joins and supplies no general majority proof rule. The independent constitutional hearing and continued-detention formulations remain without a majority at the necessary level of generality; a distinct duration claim also requires findings about the course of detention. Classification and individual-risk findings on remand are applications of the stated instructions, not unresolved statutory standards.

[Full controlling holdings and authority](../records/demore_v_kim_merits_2003-10-14.md#holdings); [precedent treatment](../records/demore_v_kim_merits_2003-10-14.md#precedent-treatment).

### United States v. Holston; Second Circuit No. 02-1292; judgment below reported at 343 F.3d 83. — 2003-10-30

Effective October 30, 2003, the supported necessary-inclusion doctrine sustains the defined local production class when actual-child-abuse images constitute the reproducible, concealable and substitutable supply of the identified interstate prohibition. Lower courts must identify the regime and require material support for the production class's anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship, independently examine constitutional eligibility and fit, and give Congress latitude on supported empirical judgments. Proof of an individual interstate sale is unnecessary to that established class-and-regime ground. The statutory materials-movement element continues to govern the prosecution independently.

This decision adds a controlling application to the existing rule. Lopez's connection requirement, Home Builders's support obligation and Griffin/Langley's separation of statutory nexus from constitutional authority retain their force. The equipment-only theory is insufficient; the supported production mechanism sustains the challenged application.

[Full controlling holdings and authority](../records/united_states_v_holston_merits_2003-10-30.md#holdings); [precedent treatment](../records/united_states_v_holston_merits_2003-10-30.md#precedent-treatment-1).

### Rancho Viejo, LLC v. Norton; D.C. Circuit No. 01-5373; judgment below reported at 323 F.3d 1062. — 2004-02-05

Effective February 5, 2004, the Home Builders class-and-mechanism rule continues to govern equivalent intrastate-taking applications involving commercial projects. Courts must identify and materially evaluate either supported necessary inclusion in a valid interstate regime or a substantial interstate externality satisfying the cross-border, territorial-control and fit requirements. The Government's support responsibility, independent judicial inquiry and bounded legislative latitude remain unchanged.

The commercial-project rationale cannot alone sustain this application. The lower courts must resolve its constitutional validity through the existing-record inquiry and enter relief according to the supported mechanism or its absence. This decision applies the existing doctrine without creating a new Commerce category; the unresolved work concerns the record's support and the resulting application-specific judgment.

[Full controlling holdings and authority](../records/rancho_viejo_llc_v_norton_merits_2004-02-05.md#holdings); [precedent treatment](../records/rancho_viejo_llc_v_norton_merits_2004-02-05.md#precedent-treatment-1).

### Lomont v. O’Neill; judgment below reported at 285 F.3d 9 (D.C. Circuit). — 2004-03-11

Effective March 11, 2004, officers presenting a concrete, traceable and redressable challenge to federal burdens on their own work may litigate without separate authorization to represent the State. The official-injury inquiry remains distinct from whether the challenged measure imposes an unconstitutional command.

Federal certification arrangements of this kind remain voluntary when officers can refuse unconditionally without federal penalty, compelled investigation or substitute work. Courts examine legal consequences and actual enforcement, with the challenger required to identify an actual command or adverse federal consequence; constituent pressure alone does not supply one. This decision adds a controlling application of official-injury and genuine-choice principles. The existing bounded compulsory-assistance rule, its unresolved fiscal rationale and Condon's direct state-activity rule retain their distinct scope.

[Full controlling holdings and authority](../records/lomont_v_oneill_merits_2004-03-11.md#holdings); [precedent treatment](../records/lomont_v_oneill_merits_2004-03-11.md#precedent-treatment-1).

### Environmental Defense Center, Inc. v. United States Environmental Protection Agency; judgment below reported at 344 F.3d 832 (Ninth Circuit). — 2004-03-25

Effective March 25, 2004, municipal own-discharge regulation may employ the challenged permit architecture when a lawful and actually available route permits direct compliance without compulsory administration of third-party rules. Government must identify that route when relying on choice; an operator may show it to be illusory. Ordinary costs of valid own-operation compliance are not converted into assistance reimbursement claims. Application requirements and final permit obligations remain distinct, and the retained administrative remands continue to govern permitting.

The decision also establishes a confined operational-information application: factual pollution-impact, reduction and disposal-hazard information may be required within the independent physical function, substantial purpose, direct practical fit, flexibility and justification-and-burden limits stated in Holding II. This governing permission extends beyond automatic classification as commercial product speech while retaining the compelled-belief and independent-criticism protections. The validity of a materially different final message, burden or permit condition is assessed under those existing limits on its own record.

Enforcement remains subject to Kansas’s genuine-practical-choice rule: an actual credible threatened or imposed funding consequence can be challenged on its scope, notice and practical effects, including competent qualitative evidence. Statutory discretion supplies lawful possible applications and remains reviewable when exercised or credibly threatened.

## Material Published Noncontrolling Positions

### Demore v. Kim; No. 01-1491; judgment below, 276 F.3d 523 (9th Cir. 2002). — 2003-10-14

**Chief Justice Stone-Zsela, concurring in part and concurring in the judgment.** He joins Parts I, II-A and II-B and accepts the entire vacatur, remand and interim-release remedy so Kim can receive the individual assessment available under the statutory pathway. He withholds assent from the allocation of persuasion to Kim. In his view, when genuinely contested inclusion or constitutionally insufficient continuing authority requires individual review, the Government should prove by clear and convincing evidence that reasonably available conditions, separately or together, cannot adequately address flight risk or danger; he would also accept governmental preponderance with the same object of proof and effective relief. Mathews directs attention to liberty and error; Salerno and Addington offer regime-specific support for the preferred quantum; Cooper illustrates the consequences of a burden that wrongly allocates uncertainty. Krimstock supports meaningful review and consideration of alternatives, while his separate preponderance position there remains his own. Kim seeks liberty in a civil immigration proceeding, not release pending appeal of his criminal convictions, so section 3143(b) does not persuade him to place the risk of uncertainty on Kim. The neutral decisionmaker should receive contrary evidence, assess residence, family and community ties, convictions, release conduct, appearance evidence and feasible conditions, and give reviewable reasons; failure of the governmental showing should permit conditional release.

He also explains that lawful initial detention does not justify continuation after its nature, duration or circumstances lose a reasonable relationship to removal proceedings pursued with reasonable diligence. A resident would identify a concrete continuing-custody concern; the Government would supply the chronology, basis, delay explanations and anticipated course and prove disputed diligence facts by preponderance. Legitimate substantial defenses are not automatically misconduct; solely dilatory conduct may count against the resident, and unjustified governmental or institutional delay weighs against categorical continuation. If that authority fails, individualized justification and effective relief are required; material change permits renewed review. Gilbert, Fairley, Foucha and Jackson support the continuing-authority inquiry; Flores preserves lawful temporary custody, and Zadvydas contributes the removal-purpose relationship without its separate calendar or burden. The present statutory holding leaves this constitutional limit open. His proposals are noncontrolling, supply no finding that Kim's past detention crossed the limit, and neither condition the entered judgment nor alter its remand instructions.

**Justice Souter, concurring, joined by Stevens and Ginsburg.** They join the entire lead opinion because its substantial-question pathway affords genuine individual consideration and protects Kim's existing release during the inquiry. They retain the broader view that a lawful permanent resident must receive a reasonably prompt, impartial opportunity to contest the necessity of custody on flight-risk and danger grounds. A past criminal category cannot conclusively establish present need throughout the proceeding. Carlson retained individual discretion, Flores retained real release and review avenues, and Zadvydas ties detention to its purpose; National Center and the continuing-deprivation decisions support effective inquiry into the actual justification. A rebuttable initial category presumption may be permissible, but meaningful consideration and effective release authority are essential. Their statutory join accepts Part III's allocation within that pathway, without making it a universal burden or approving categorical constitutional validity outside the threshold. That broader constitutional position is noncontrolling.

**Justice O'Connor, concurring in part and dissenting in part, joined by Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas.** They accept pre-order operation of section 1226(c), but would reverse the resident-wide constitutional hearing requirement and remand for implementation in the actual custody and removal posture. Congress may make a properly established criminal category sufficient during reasonably dispatched removal proceedings. Flores supports temporary categorical arrangements; the statutory classification, rather than risk alone, supplies the substantive predicate. Gilbert, Krimstock and Fairley require testing the actual legal justification, not adding a new risk exception to a valid category. Joseph and the proper review channels must address genuine membership disputes. A substantial challenge does not itself suspend Congress's custody rule, and the express witness-protection exception does not authorize the Court's implied bond pathway. The disputed charges require their own lawful treatment; the requested continuance and elapsed six months do not establish the exceptional constitutional defect asserted here. O'Connor and Kennedy join the Court's jurisdiction holding; Scalia and Thomas reach these merits on the Court's jurisdiction determination. This proposed reversal is noncontrolling.

**Justice Kennedy, additionally concurring in part and dissenting in part.** His disagreement with the statutory pathway preserves individual protection against unreasonable, unjustified or arbitrary continuing detention and denial of prescribed review. The Government must have actual support for mandatory category membership; unreasonable delay or failure of that justification can require individual flight-risk and danger consideration. His Flores and Zadvydas positions and Krimstock's inquiry into continuing authority support that distinction. The present record, including Kim's requested continuance and incomplete findings, does not establish that constitutional defect from elapsed time alone. This qualification of his reversal position is noncontrolling.

**Justice Scalia, additionally concurring in part and dissenting in part, joined by Thomas.** They read section 1226(e)'s second sentence as a bar on setting aside an executive detention or release action, including through this challenge to the mandatory framework. A legal or constitutional label cannot evade the action-based language. Their application of retained channels under other statutes does not settle this text. They decline Part I and proceed to the statutory merits on the Court's contrary jurisdiction determination, without announcing a comprehensive Suspension Clause rule. Their jurisdictional objection is noncontrolling.

### Rancho Viejo, LLC v. Norton; D.C. Circuit No. 01-5373; judgment below reported at 323 F.3d 1062. — 2004-02-05

**Stevens, dissenting, joined by Souter.** They would affirm because the activity directly producing the alleged habitat injury is commercial housing construction and land conversion. Sweet Home identifies that physical habitat alteration as the conduct reached by the taking prohibition. The dissent therefore looks to the actual economic application, rather than to the developer's collateral equipment purchases or a generalized claim that species preservation benefits the national economy.

Wickard and Perez permit class-wide regulation of connected economic activity, and Hodel v. Virginia Surface Mining & Reclamation Association permits regulation of economic land use for environmental ends. On that reasoning, the actual construction supplies a sufficient economic connection without a market in the toad or a newly found ecological spillover. The dissent accepts the continuing statutory requirements of actual habitat-caused injury and ordinary causation; its proposed affirmance concerns the Commerce Clause challenge.

Home Builders's contrary enacted-class holding directly obstructs this ground. Stevens and Souter would reconsider it to the extent it excludes reliance on the actual injury-producing economic activity in this application. They regard treating the prohibition's broader coverage as a reason to disregard this commercial application as a mistaken class inquiry. They neither claim that their earlier dissent already controls nor distinguish Home Builders through a new factual premise. Their ground would leave this judgment effective and make the mechanism remand unnecessary; it would not make every noneconomic taking an economic activity.

Noncontrolling. [Published writing and scope](../records/rancho_viejo_llc_v_norton_merits_2004-02-05.md#opinion-topology).

### West Virginia v. U.S. Department of Health & Human Services; judgment below, 289 F.3d 281 (4th Cir. 2002). — 2004-06-16

**Chief Justice Stone-Zsela, concurring.** He joins the Court’s complete opinion and the facial affirmance. He adds that review must provide effective relief: a court acting through an available channel must promptly restrain an established coercive sanction. A timely challenge to an actual or sufficiently imminent consequence must examine the threatened funds, notice and practical effects under Kansas. This additional remedial qualification explains how he would give effect to the preserved coercion limit; it does not alter his vote to affirm the present judgment.

Noncontrolling. [Published writing and scope](../records/west_virginia_v_us_department_of_health_and_human_services_merits_2004-06-16.md#opinion-topology).

### Standing referral practice

**Referral of applications, effective October 7, 1991; signed explanations and certification, effective October 2, 1995.** By the Court's internal practice adopted at the opening of October Term 1991, an application for a stay, injunction, or other interim relief in a matter on the Term's inventory is referred by the Circuit Justice to the full Court and decided by the participating Justices under the participating-majority rule. The order issues in the Court's name and recites the presenting Circuit Justice ("presented to Justice ___ and by him or her referred to the Court"). From October 2, 1995, referral does not prevent the Circuit Justice, or the substitute the term-effective Rules designate, from taking a necessary temporary action in his or her own name while the Court considers the application. Each action is explained in short signed writings, including the Circuit Justice's own explanation at final disposition, whether prevailing or dissenting, and a statement by each participating Justice in each Court action deciding relief, and is certified for issuance by the Chief Justice or the Chief's substitute, as Engine §10 provides. A Justice may participate in a referred application from the date of the judicial oath. An application not on the Term's inventory remains subject to ordinary Circuit Justice practice.
