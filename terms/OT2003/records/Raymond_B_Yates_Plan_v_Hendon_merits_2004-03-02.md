**Controlling explanation:** ERISA's integrated provisions recognize overlapping capacities. Section 1108(c)(1) permits a fiduciary's participant benefit when computed and paid consistently with the plan as applied to all other participants and beneficiaries; subsection (c)(3) recognizes fiduciary service alongside service for a party in interest. Subsection (d) separately restricts owner-employee transactions. These provisions regulate the risks of dual capacities rather than declare them impossible.

The regulation likewise defines employee-plan coverage. Section 2510.3-3 contrasts owner-only arrangements with plans covering common-law employees as well as self-employed individuals, and confines its owner exclusion to that section's purpose.
