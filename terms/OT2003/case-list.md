# OT_2003_CASE_INDEX

119 merits matters in eight chunks. Chunk 1 contains Demore v. Kim, restored to the calendar for reargument and the first case of the Term, and the six priority cases; Chunks 2–8 contain sixteen cases each. Grouping does not change event dates or the chronological force of a decision. Consolidated companions count once.

**+** identifies the thirty-eight added lower-court matters. Their listed citations identify the judgments under review.

All 119 Section II positions are approved by the user. Approval establishes Stone’s positions; it does not establish a judgment or votes of the Court.

Only five briefs carry a special conditional writing direction. In McConnell v. Federal Election Commission, Vieth v. Jubelirer, Cheney v. United States District Court for D.C., and Roeder v. Islamic Republic of Iran: with a lawful quorum, if at least five Justices including Stone actually join his reasoning supporting the judgment on an issue decided, Stone writes and articulates those joined portions. A judgment-only vote does not adopt his reasoning; different propositions require their actual joins. Unjoined reasoning remains his own. In Demore v. Kim: if Breyer joins a five-vote judgment that secures Kim an individualized custody hearing, including the practical-effect judgment Stone's position describes, Breyer writes the opinion of the Court. No outcome, vote, or future precedent is assigned by these conditions, and they do not extend to the other briefs.
