TENNESSEE, PETITIONER v. GEORGE LANE

ON WRIT OF CERTIORARI TO THE UNITED STATES
COURT OF APPEALS FOR THE SIXTH CIRCUIT

[May 17, 2004]

    Justice Thomas,
dissenting.

     I join the Chief Justice’s
dissent.  I agree that Title II of the Americans with
Disabilities Act of 1990 cannot be a congruent and proportional
remedy to the States’ alleged practice of denying disabled
persons access to the courts.  Not only did Congress fail to
identify any evidence of such a practice when it enacted the
ADA, ante, at 6, 10, Title II regulates far more than
the provision of access to the courts, ante, at
15—16.  Because I joined the dissent in Nevada Dept. of
Human Resources v. Hibbs, 538 U.S. 721 (2003),
and continue to believe that Hibbs was wrongly decided,
I write separately only to disavow any reliance on Hibbs
in reaching this conclusion.
