(1) Any person who votes in an election after the regular poll-closing time

envelope. The election official witnessing the voter’s subscription and affir-

All such provisional ballots shall remain sealed in their envelopes and trans-
mitted to the supervisor of elections.

may be cast in accordance with the provisions of s. 101.048(4).
