## II. The proposal's actual doctrinal change

The proposed right concerns control of an elected chamber by an already existing party or coalition despite reliably demonstrated opposition majorities across recorded conditions, caused materially and avoidably by the assignments. It is narrower than a general proportional-representation entitlement: it uses an absolute majority, a real governing body, persistence beyond an isolated condition, personal causal exposure, and an independently justified comparison. Its exclusion of congressional delegations is meaningful. The proposal should not be rejected by simply pretending those limits are absent or by calling every majority-control claim a demand for an exact proportional seat share.

Those limits nevertheless do not make this an application of existing Vieth. The new formulation would establish a separate constitutional entitlement to avoid the defined retained-control result without a prohibited-purpose showing. Its defense asks whether consistently applied lawful constraints made that result unavoidable. Vieth instead requires a material electoral disadvantage purposefully imposed on identified voters because of their political affiliation or activity, followed by a State showing that materially the same lawful decision would have occurred without the forbidden purpose. A plan can be one permissible, independently justified choice among alternatives without its partisan-control consequence being unavoidable. The two defenses therefore have different reach.

Vieth's unanimous Article I rejection does not itself decide the proposed distinct House equal-protection theory. Nor does the Court's adoption of a purposeful equal-treatment cause necessarily hold that no other independently established constitutional right can ever operate without purpose. Population equality is a familiar example of a different right with different elements. But recognizing a new governing-control entitlement requires an additional constitutional judgment; the existence of population equality, §2 results liability or a valid statutory effects regulation does not perform that work. The proposed cause cannot acquire its missing authority merely by borrowing their absence of an intent requirement.

The comparison requirement helps separate assignment effects from geography, turnout, candidates and lawful requirements. It does not by itself establish why a court may require one lawful control result over another. Likewise, standing, reliable measurement and repeated effects identify important factual limits; they do not independently create the asserted substantive right. The audit preserves this distinction rather than treating additional proof detail as a substitute for adopting the new constitutional principle.

### Measurement clauses at their actual scope

The absolute-majority requirement is more than half, not merely the largest share. A coherent coalition must be independently established; unrelated votes cannot be combined simply because their aggregation exceeds half. The whole-membership requirement for a staggered body is material, though no staggered-body finding is supplied here. Disclosed and tested treatment of uncontested races is essential because candidate votes are not automatically a reliable measure of all voters' preferred governing coalition. Recorded conditions beyond an isolated point address persistence, but their representativeness and actual limits still require adjudication. No fixed election count is added by this audit.

The proposal does not require a completed election under the challenged map, and the four Vieth majority Associates already accept that actual participation injury need not await electoral defeat. That existing proposition does not establish that old elections or another map automatically prove this new chamber-control injury. Applying recorded votes to a comparison requires a supported inference about candidate choice, turnout, coalition coherence and the scope of the changed assignments; the source must establish the inference rather than assume fixed behavior. No such evidence is fabricated here.

These are recognizable proof disciplines and do not themselves supply additional-rule joins. A Justice can agree that any proposed theory would require such care while declining the theory's constitutional premise, its defense or its adoption in this case.

## III. Individual join audit

### Stevens

**Actual join:** Refuses adoption of the circulated purpose-free rule as an additional Martinez ground. Retains the frozen congressional and House remand under the complete Vieth rule, with the Senate and other affirmances unchanged.

**Own date-eligible basis:** His full actual Vieth join; his Shaw/Miller and Cano independent-injury joins; his Bandemer position; his Cook opinion at its official-ballot-penalty scope; his Holder and De Grandy joins; and his Brooks statutory-opportunity and purpose joins. The earlier case 70 reconciliation explains why the historical predominance and inferred-representation theory cannot substitute for his actual in-world commitments.

**Strongest reason to join:** A reliably established loss of a coherent majority's ability to replace a governing chamber could be a practical democratic injury distinct from a mere hope for more favorable constituents. The proposal's governing-body boundary and independent comparison address genuine weaknesses in a delegation-ratio theory. His concern with arbitrary political obstruction gives those distinctions weight.

**Recorded objection:** The source does not establish that this particular injury is presented, and the exact proposal makes an avoidable control result constitutionally sufficient without the prohibited-purpose and identified-capacity elements he joined in Vieth. His rejection of design alone does not turn a newly selected control threshold into an already established right. Cook's actual governmental ballot penalty supplies no general chamber-majority entitlement, and statutory opportunity decisions cannot supply a constitutional results-only rule by transfer.

**Supportable accommodation:** He may join an explanation that reliably proved control insulation can be evidence of an actual Vieth injury, mechanism, prohibited purpose and causation, with the existing comparison and same-decision rules intact. He may reserve the independent governing-control question in an appropriately presented case. Neither position adopts the exact new cause or changes his Martinez vote. Replacing the proposal's no-purpose clause and unavoidability defense with Vieth would be a material revision, not a clarification of this proposal.

### O'Connor

**Supportable accommodation:** She can join the proposition that neither a projected ratio nor an unproved new theory changes this judgment, and can preserve independently established population, racial, exclusion or retaliation causes. 1. **Clarifications consistent with the proposal:** Preserve its whole-body limitation; real absolute majority; independent coalition identification; actual vote measurement with tested treatment of uncontested races; transparent causal inference; personal exposure and redressability; and no automatic liability from a ratio. It cannot be simulated by adding a conditional phrase to the opinion. Any material new record must go through the clean source process. The original House Vieth remand is complete without it.
