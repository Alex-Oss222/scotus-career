## Exact common and contested law

FOIA places the burden on the agency to establish an enacted exemption. Exemption 7(A) permits functional categories when disclosure could reasonably be expected to interfere with pending or reasonably anticipated enforcement proceedings. Robbins Tire permits categorical prediction; it does not require actual intimidation or a public account for each person. Landano's separate 7(D) rule preserves supported inferences and an agency burden; its rejection of a universal confidentiality label does not prohibit every 7(A) category. Klamath confirms that a substantial confidentiality purpose cannot substitute for the particular exemption's conditions.

The six remand supporters find the submitted explanation insufficient for the entire withholding scope. Information may identify an ongoing source, investigative direction, vulnerability to coordination, intimidation or manufacture of misleading evidence. The agency must connect its actual functional categories to those harms and address reasonable segregation; a past connection to the investigation does not itself establish a continuing enforcement connection for every immigration detainee, material witness, attorney and withheld item. A released or deported person may remain a source, and neither release nor lack of criminal culpability automatically defeats protection. Sensitive reasons may be presented through supported shared categories and appropriately protected submissions. No public individualized statement, fixed number of categories or mandatory in-camera hearing is imposed.

Scalia and Thomas accept the narrower factual inference that official confirmation of the comprehensive investigative map reveals the inquiry's selections, contacts, gaps and potential sources even where individuals' roles differ. The recorded declarations' mechanisms make that class functional in their view. They reject an exemption based merely on a national-security label, and their result does not establish that every future investigation list or every attorney's name is protected. Their affirmative 7(A) ground makes the alternative protections unnecessary to their judgment. This is a substantive dissent from the six's statutory remand, alongside agreement on the separate access components.

## Independent access, all eight

The First Amendment does not require release of this executive investigative compilation merely because some underlying information concerns arrests or adjudicative hearings. Under North Jersey, access attaches to an identified kind of historically and functionally open adjudication; that proceeding-specific holding does not turn an executive investigative list into the hearing itself. United Reporting preserves the absence of a general records entitlement while separately scrutinizing speech-selected access conditions, which are absent here. All eight join this limited ground without abandoning their actual North Jersey positions.

Congress's FOIA disclosure-and-exemption scheme supplies the governing access regime for these very agency records. The asserted common-law route cannot override its valid exemptions or create a second balancing mechanism for the same executive compilation. Milwaukee supplies displacement where Congress addresses the subject; Nixon supports attention to the applicable statutory access route. ## Complete remand and remedy

For the six statutory remand supporters, vacate the blanket FOIA disposition and require adjudication of the actual categories and reasonably segregable material. Independently invoked protections retain their own elements: under 7(C), the cognizable personal-privacy interest is balanced against disclosure's illumination of government conduct; under 7(F), a reasonable expectation of danger to life or physical safety must be supported; Exemption 3 and Rule 6(e) require a connection to protected grand-jury matters; court sealing requires determination of an actual order and its coverage. An attorney does not automatically inherit every client protection and is not categorically excluded from safety protection because of professional status.

The requested wholesale disclosure does not follow before those protections are decided. The earlier district-court release command is not automatically reinstated. Lower courts may protect claimed sensitive information during adjudication and must release nonexempt reasonably segregable information after applying the stated standards. No new deadline or invented production order is supplied.

This resolves the legal standards and both independent-access components. What remains is category-specific factual application and residual-exemption/segregability adjudication under the stated rules, within Engine section 8(c), not a bare reservation of governing law. The existence and actual coverage of sealing orders must be decided through protected review where appropriate; a material-witness designation alone does not answer them. No detention legality, guilt, actual counsel-access or entitlement to unseal a court record is inferred.
