# United States v. Carnes — frozen neutral non-Stone commitments

**Event:** Merits, 2004-02-09; Sixth Circuit No. 00-2103; judgment below 309 F.3d 950.
**Inputs and cutoff:** Approved neutral packet, C preflight and entering-law slice, official 1994 and 2002 §§2510 and 2511, and the lower opinion's commerce discussion and mandate. February 9, 2004. No historical target Supreme Court action or private position supplied.

## Common judgment and rule

All eight Associate Justices would affirm the rejection of the Wiretap Act commerce objection, leaving the lower court's independent reversal of the wiretap conviction, new-trial direction and associated sentencing proceedings in effect. This is not wholesale affirmance of the conviction or unrelated appellate components.

Congress may protect the interstate telephone system by prohibiting unauthorized interception of covered wire communications carried through it, even when the intercepted call begins and ends in one State. The relationship is present use of and intrusion into an interstate communications instrumentality, not remote interstate manufacture of a recorder or a generalized effect of local crime. Congress need not prove that the particular conversation crossed a border or separately had a substantial interstate effect. The statutory definition, intentional interception requirement and applicable statutory exceptions remain independently necessary; the constitutional ruling supplies no missing element or finding.

## Alternative-path comparison

Lopez preserves channels and instrumentalities as direct bases of regulation. Harris applies instrumentality protection without reducing every individual event to an independent substantial-effects inquiry. Guillen likewise permits confined protection of an actual interstate transportation mechanism. Unauthorized acquisition of communications carried through the interstate telephone network directly implicates the network's secure operation, including communication confidentiality; physical damage or interruption of a call is not an additional constitutional prerequisite.

Carnes's strongest objection is that personal local conversations and a locally installed recording device are not commercial transactions. That objection would defeat a theory relying only on the device's manufacturing history, which Holston rejects, or on an unsupported chain from crime to national economic costs. It does not sever the intercepted wire communications from the actual network carrying them. Section 2510(1)'s facilities/provider relationship supplies the statutory limit relevant here. No new element requiring a completed interstate call is inserted.

A second lawful framework would ask whether inclusion of local calls is integral to a valid interstate telephone regime under Home Builders and, by February 9, Rancho Viejo. That route remains available on its own predicates, but the direct instrumentality ground already resolves this case; the opinion need not turn practical enforcement concerns into an independently sufficient holding. The constitutional power over face-to-face speech or stand-alone recorders is outside this narrow question. The Roving Interception matter concerns the conditions on a Government assistance order and supplies no exception to Congress's power to protect covered telephone communications from private interception; no uncompleted Roving decision is treated as law in this commitment.

## Remedy and record limits

The constitutional challenge supplies no additional ground to dismiss the wiretap charge. The existing appellate order suppressing specified tapes and requiring a new trial on that count is not disturbed, and the wiretap conviction is not reinstated. Other convictions, sentencing and search issues retain their actual lower-court mandate without a new Supreme Court merits endorsement.

The pre-1997 statutory text is separately saved: the 2002 definition's change concerning electronic storage is not retrospectively used to define the charged interception, and neither version's stored-content treatment is decided. The present rule concerns live telephone communications intercepted through the described line. No independent factual finding about network technology beyond the supplied covered telephone-system facts is added. Authorship, final opinion joins and reconciliation remain for their authorized later stages.
