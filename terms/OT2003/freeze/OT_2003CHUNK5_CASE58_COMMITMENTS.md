## Provisional disposition and propositions

The eight Associates provisionally reject the proposed pollutant-origin requirement, vacate the judgment sustaining summary judgment and remand for the material distinct-waters inquiry. Their agreement is on the supplied neutral record: the packet identifies a genuine hydrological dispute rather than an undisputed finding this Court may replace. Preservation and review-scope qualifications are set out below and must remain available for reconciliation against any properly sanitized record clarification.

The Act's point-source definition includes discernible, confined and discrete conveyances. A pump can discharge pollutants it transports even though it did not generate them. Borden Ranch already rejects an offsite-origin prerequisite while requiring actual statutory conveyance and deposit; neither machinery movement nor environmental harm substitutes for the discharge elements.

An addition remains necessary. Moving pollutant-containing water entirely within one water body does not, by that movement alone, add pollutants to that water body. Whether C-11 and WCA-3 are meaningfully distinct cannot be resolved by their names or by the bare existence of hydrological connections. The lower court must resolve the material factual dispute under the proper discharge rule. Deaton's coverage of a connected tributary system does not transform every covered water into a single body for the addition inquiry. Arkansas's downstream standards likewise preserve waterbody-specific pollution consequences without deciding this record.

The Government's broader proposition that all navigable waters must be treated as one was not developed below in its present form. It is not adopted or rejected as a controlling merits rule in this review. Its proper presentation and statutory/regulatory setting may be considered in ordinary remand proceedings; it receives no assumed agency deference. The origin argument can be decided without that broader theory, and the present permit judgment cannot stand without a sound addition premise.

## Adversarial alternatives and remedy

**Affirm the permit judgment.** The strongest ground is that S-9 carries phosphorus from polluted canal water into the conservation area and the lower courts treated them as distinct. The origin argument fails under the text and Borden Ranch. The obstacle is the packet's express material dispute over the hydrological premise and the difference between a litigated assertion and an undisputed fact. An affirmance confined to the point-source question is a supported review-scope alternative, particularly for Stevens; it becomes decisive only if the broader attack is outside the granted question or the record establishes no genuine factual dispute. Neither condition is supplied as established.

**Vacate after rejecting the origin argument.** The strongest objection is that the question granted centers on origin and that a broad remand would give a late unitary theory unwarranted significance. The sufficient answer is to confine the remand to the disputed addition predicate litigated below and make clear that the new theory has received no merits approval. Addition is necessary to the judgment sustaining the permit obligation, so a source-only legal answer does not settle the complete statutory claim on this record.

**Accept an all-waters rule or an origin requirement.** The origin requirement conflicts with the conveyance definition and the operative Borden Ranch rule. The all-waters rule raises distinct text, regulatory and presentation issues; its undeveloped form cannot be adopted simply as a consequence of rejecting pollutant generation. No Justice presently commits to it.

The case returns for proceedings on whether the pumping adds pollutants between meaningfully distinct waters and the resulting permit obligation. The Court does not replace the lower court's hydrological work with a factual conclusion. A single signed lead opinion may carry the common propositions. No mandatory separate writing or author assignment is established; Stevens's stated scope alternative must be preserved for reconciliation rather than converted into a presumed final join on a changed record.
