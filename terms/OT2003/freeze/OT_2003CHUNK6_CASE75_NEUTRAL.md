### Issue 1: Final state judgment and preserved federal review

* **Controlling Law:** Florida v. Thomas requires dismissal where substantive state proceedings remain, later federal review is available and no Cox exception applies. It did not decide the underlying search question.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** A decided federal issue alone does not make an entire state judgment final. Test the actual proceedings against the four Cox categories; exceptional urgency cannot merely repeat the ordinary importance of the asserted right.
* **Other Governing Law:** 28 U.S.C. §1257(a); Supreme Court Rules 14.1(g), 14.1(i), 15.2 and 24.1(e) concerning jurisdiction and relevant opinions.
* **Material Other Authority:** Cox Broadcasting Corp. v. Cohn preserves limited finality exceptions. Batson v. Kentucky governs the underlying constitutional issue only after lawful jurisdiction exists.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** Florida v. Thomas governs this procedural structure; the remaining inquiry is application to the particular remand.
* **Open Point:** Whether an exceptional finality category applies despite available later review and unresolved grounds for reversal.
