## Reconciled components

All eight Associates retain D1–D4 and affirmance in `freeze/OT_2003CHUNK2_DEATON_COMMITMENTS.md`, at their stated operative scope. The four components remain separate holdings rather than a general approval of federal jurisdiction or environmental benefit.

**D1 — statutory and regulatory authority.** Sections 1311, 1344 and 1362 supply the enacted discharge/permit program, expanded water definition and actual assignment to the Army and Corps. Binding regulations exercise that assignment. The Court independently enforces statutory boundaries before deferring to a permissible delegated choice; ambiguity, expertise or the regulation's label alone does not confer authority. The connected tributary/adjacent-wetland application is reasonable within that authority. SWANCC's bird-use-only isolated-pond limit remains effective, as does Riverside Bayview's adjacent-wetland construction; neither makes every nonnavigable branch excluded nor approves every isolated-water criterion.

The separate interpretation of the regulation receives Auer's controlling weight because the responsible Corps construction of tributaries as the feeder system is fair, considered and consistent with the text. The older primary/secondary/tertiary usage supports that reading. An earlier headwaters coverage cutoff does not mean more remote streams were linguistically never tributaries. The opinion must not merge deference to a delegated statutory interpretation with interpretation of the agency's regulation, or treat either as proof of disputed hydrology. Section 328.3(a)(7)'s predicate excludes waters that are themselves wetlands; no different adjacency chain is inferred here.

**D2 — channel protection.** Congress may protect the identified navigable receiving water from pollutant effects carried through this actual tributary system and adjacent wetlands. The agreed route to the Wicomico and the reported supporting class evidence establish the concrete connection. The protection of channels extends to injurious uses, not solely obstructions to boat passage. The rule requires neither a separately substantial effect from this individual fill nor proof tracing a particular particle downstream. Preventive regulation follows the supported connection; aggregation cannot manufacture it.

Rio Grande Dam, Appalachian Electric Power, Oklahoma ex rel. Phillips and Grand River Dam support protection of navigable receiving waters through relevant nonnavigable reaches. Lopez's connection-before-magnitude discipline remains controlling. The direct channel ground resolves the constitutional application; no independent Home Builders interstate-externality holding, inferred market in development services or necessary-inclusion holding is adopted. Scalia and Thomas's objection to independent ecological externality remains intact. Virginia's compact-specific exemption from Maryland's unilateral licensing preserves applicable federal authority and gives these owners no CWA exemption.

**D3 — delineation.** The Manual's express visual root-zone indicator, usually saturation within twelve inches, answers the asserted surface-only interpretation objection. The regulatory definition still requires frequency and duration sufficient to support, and under normal circumstances actually supporting, the required prevalence of vegetation. The undisputed soil and vegetation criteria remain part of the determination. One shallow-water observation does not replace those conditions or establish that every such parcel is a wetland.

The owners' recorded-well evidence was offered to prove inadequate saturation to the surface. The published account supplies no finding that their root-zone measurements contradicted the actual indicator. The Manual ranks recorded data above field indicators, and affirmance does not erase that ranking or permit ignoring genuinely contrary data. The Court rejects the preserved claim that the Corps contradicted the Manual; it does not decide an unpresented invalidity challenge to the Manual, announce a fourteen-day requirement or invent groundwater facts.

**D4 — restoration.** Independently affirm the refilling and previolation-elevation remedy under abuse-of-discretion review. The lower findings explain why moving sidecast soil away would retain the drainage benefit of the unlawful project and risk further environmental harm. The potentially lawful alternative before excavation does not establish an equivalent restorative remedy after the violation. Practical achievability, environmental benefit and equitable relation to the wrong inform the analysis; Cumberland Farms supplies persuasive factors, not a new compulsory checklist. The restoration objective does not create unlimited jurisdiction or a newly prohibited excavation offense. The order remains tied to this established discharge and its effects, without punitive reconstruction or unrelated redesign.
