## Freier: regulatory fit and the distinct judicial obligation

It covers state-law personal-injury or property-damage actions caused or contributed to by exposure to a hazardous substance, pollutant or contaminant released into the environment from a facility. It substitutes the federal date only when state law starts the applicable period earlier, otherwise retains state limitations law, and excludes actions under section 9607. The general date is actual or reasonable knowledge of the causal link; paragraph (b)(4)(B) supplies separate later-date rules for minors and incompetent plaintiffs.
