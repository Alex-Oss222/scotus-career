## Exact statutory operation and remedy

The statute covers state-law personal-injury or property-damage actions caused or contributed to by exposure to a hazardous substance, pollutant or contaminant released into the environment from a facility. It replaces a state commencement date only when that date is earlier than the federally required commencement date; otherwise the applicable state limitations law remains. Actions under section 9607 are excluded. The ordinary federal date is when the plaintiff knew or reasonably should have known that the covered injury or damage was caused or contributed to by the substance concerned.

For a minor, the federal date is the later of that ordinary causal-discovery date or the date on which the minor reaches the age of majority under state law or has a legal representative appointed. For an incompetent plaintiff, it is the later of the ordinary date or the date the person becomes competent or has a legal representative appointed. These are the statute's express qualifications, not newly adjudicated applications to particular plaintiffs. ## Official qualification text, mechanically copied

(B) Special rules
In the case of a minor or incompetent plaintiff, the term “federally required commencement date” means the later of the date referred to in subparagraph (A) or the following:
(i) In the case of a minor, the date on which the minor reaches the age of majority, as determined by State law, or has a legal representative appointed.
(ii) In the case of an incompetent individual, the date on which such individual becomes competent or has had a legal representative appointed.
