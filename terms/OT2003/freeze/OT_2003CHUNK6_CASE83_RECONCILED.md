## Grounds, contrary argument and authority limits

The seven-Associate common ground is that Spending and Necessary and Proper powers permit Congress to protect the integrity and effective operation of genuinely federally assisted institutions against corrupt influence over their agents' substantial institutional transactions, without tracing the particular bribe to particular federal dollars. The recipient, agency, transaction and corrupt-intent requirements supply the operational relationship. Money's fungibility reinforces that relationship; it is not an independent source of federal authority. McCulloch supplies appropriate means; Salinas supplies the statutory rejection of traced federal-dollar effects; Fischer supplies genuine assistance and preserves compensation distinctions. Dole and Kansas concern grant conditions and do not foreclose direct regulation of the briber. Lopez and Morrison prevent generalized consequences from creating missing enumerated power; they do not impose a Commerce element on this spending protection.

The strongest objection is that a modest grant must not nationalize every local offense. The common rationale answers through the statute's actual recipient-agent-business relationship and genuine assistance, rather than declaring federal money anywhere sufficient for any offense. The decision rejects this facial demand for an express tracing element. It supplies no invented facts resolving a concrete materially different application and does not use Salinas's statutory construction as a prior resolution of the constitutional question.

Thomas's narrower ground is analytically sufficient to reject this facial objection: its premise that no prosecution can be valid without an express tracing element fails where the pleaded corruption directly targets the federally assisted development institution and its responsible agent. He does not add a new statutory nexus element or decide guilt. His plainly-adapted test asks for an evident protective relationship rather than a chain from any federal benefit to all local integrity. The institutional allegations here supply that relationship at the pretrial stage. His judgment agreement is not a direct join in every possible application of the seven-Associate rule.

**Historical departure:** Thomas historically relied on the Commerce Clause while questioning a broad rational-means spending rationale. Here his own simulated Lopez and unanimous Rancho Viejo joins supply an explicit identifiable-undertaking, covered-class and genuine-functional-relationship method, and the proposed reasoning is confined to the pleaded directly assisted development institution rather than an assertion that fungibility validates any covered local bribe. That changed legal premise and narrower rationale answer his objection to attenuated means-ends reasoning sufficiently for his plainly-adapted concurrence in this facial judgment; they do not establish his support for general rational-basis spending review. His historical Commerce rationale is not imported without identifying the actual interstate mechanism required by current law.

Kennedy and Scalia preserve structural facial challenges when the enacted class exceeds Congress's power. Nothing in the judgment converts their objections to a generic afterword into disagreement with this statute's supported program-protection application. No historical author or joinder is selected merely because it occurred in the comparator.

## Trial and statutory precision

The Government must prove corrupt giving, offering or agreement to give value, the intent to influence or reward a covered agent, covered institutional business or a transaction or series involving at least $5,000, and more than $10,000 in qualifying program benefits during the statutory continuous one-year period. The one-year period may begin up to twelve months before or end up to twelve months after the offense and may straddle it. Transaction value and bribe amount are distinct. Section 666(c)'s bona fide salary, wages, fees, other compensation and ordinary-course expense exception remains effective. No charge, guilt finding or sentencing outcome follows merely from affirmance of the constitutional ruling.
