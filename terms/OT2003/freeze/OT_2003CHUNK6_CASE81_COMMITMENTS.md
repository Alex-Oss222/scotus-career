Lane alleges that inaccessible courtrooms required him to crawl to a criminal proceeding and that he was later arrested after refusing another inaccessible appearance. Jones alleges that courthouse barriers excluded her from court-reporting opportunities. These are allegations at the immunity stage. Lane's participation as a defendant and Jones's participation as a professional serving court proceedings implicate different individual interests. Neither their allegations nor the constitutional-power decision establishes the statutory elements or an award.

Popovich is prior controlling law and Kennedy's published opinion, joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer as well as the Chief Justice then participating. It authorizes bounded preventive accommodation and anti-retaliation duties enabling meaningful participation in custody adjudication, beyond an independently provable constitutional tort. It does not establish a universal rule for every public service. Thomas's separate writing, joined by Scalia, objects to the fit of the preventive damages regime beyond an actual denial or unconstitutional penalty. Garrett and Hibbs retain their actual, different statutory and constitutional scopes.

## Components and common legal premises

All eight Associates accept that section 12202 expressly authorizes State exposure to Title II enforcement. Sections 12131 and 12132 retain public-entity coverage, disability, qualification, essential eligibility and disability-based exclusion or discrimination requirements. Section 12133 supplies the actual remedial cross-reference. Clarity alone is not constitutional power, and an Article I enforcement rule alone cannot identify a substantive enumerated-power connection for this court-access application.

The substantive question is whether bounded court-access accommodation is congruent and proportional enforcement of constitutional access to judicial proceedings. The constitutional anchors include a criminal defendant's meaningful opportunity to attend and defend, and the legally protected opportunity for meaningful participation in adjudication. Disability rationality review is not converted into heightened scrutiny. Title II may prevent the predictable denial of those opportunities without making every claimant prove a completed independent constitutional tort.

The six affirming Associates define the relevant class by access to judicial proceedings and the services through which those proceedings operate. The fit rests on the practical relationship between inaccessible hearing facilities and exclusion from adjudication, together with the statutory qualification condition and the limits requiring reasonable modification, effective alternatives, and protection against fundamental alteration and undue financial or administrative burdens. A required accommodation need not be the claimant's preferred physical arrangement or technology. This class is more directly connected to a constitutionally guaranteed opportunity than Title I's general employment regulation in Garrett or the minimum family-care entitlement disputed in Hibbs.

The class encompasses the pleaded courthouse-access claims of both Lane and Jones at this stage, but it does not convert Jones's work claim into Lane's criminal-procedure right. Accommodation of persons participating in and serving judicial proceedings can prevent exclusion of court users and preserve accessible judicial services without proof that every covered beneficiary personally possesses the same constitutional claim. The remand must distinguish a denial of access to judicial proceedings or their services from a freestanding employment-benefit claim; the latter is outside the court-access basis established here. Jones must prove her actual service participation, qualification, disability-based denial and remedial entitlement under the statute. The existing allegations support adjudication under that rule rather than categorical dismissal of her courthouse-access claim.

## Enhanced comparison of the two supported paths

**Court-access prevention.** Affirm denial of categorical immunity for the pleaded court-access actions. Apply Popovich's meaningful-hearing principle to physical access needed for judicial participation, retaining the statute's actual scope and accommodation limits. The strongest objection is that court buildings serve many persons and activities without an individual fundamental entitlement, so the label courthouse cannot make every Title II duty or damages request valid Section 5 enforcement. The six answer by defining the functional judicial-access class, preserving essential eligibility and reasonable-accommodation limits, and requiring ordinary proof that each asserted statutory claim actually belongs to that class. They do not rely on an invented incident count or expand the decision to unrelated public services.

**Constitutional-injury-linked enforcement.** Vacate the broad immunity denial and remand to confine the available Title II application to enforcement of an actual denial of meaningful judicial participation or an unconstitutional penalty for seeking it, on the preserved allegations and record. Thomas and Scalia maintain the particular-fit objection they stated in Popovich. The strongest objection is that Popovich already upheld prevention beyond individually provable constitutional violations in a closely related hearing setting. They acknowledge that holding and would reconsider its preventive-damages extension to the extent necessary; they cannot treat it as absent or adopt a universal individual-tort condition for all Section 5 legislation. Their objection here concerns the insufficient fit of damages exposure for broader courthouse-access duties, particularly Jones's professional opportunity, rather than a numerical deficiency in legislative findings.

The two paths differ over the legally sufficient scope of prevention and the remand standard, not the existence of express authorization or the gravity of an actual constitutional exclusion. A conclusion that Congress may enact preventive duties does not itself prove every accommodation or award. Conversely, a claim's need for factual application does not permit categorical dismissal of a valid statutory action.

## Proposition-level arithmetic and remedy

All eight Associates accept express statutory authorization. Six support the Section 5 court-access prevention ground and affirmance: Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Scalia and Thomas support vacatur and the narrower remand described above. No current-Chief vote is inferred and no author is assigned.

The six-Associate remedy preserves the denial of Tennessee's categorical immunity motion and returns both pleaded courthouse-access claims for adjudication under the defined court-access rule. The court must resolve the actual statutory coverage, effective access, accommodation, causation and remedial requirements on the record. Lane's and Jones's allegations remain distinct and neither is treated as an adjudicated constitutional violation. The same boundary governs Jones: a proven denial of participation in the court's judicial services falls within this application; a claim that proves only an unrelated employment benefit does not acquire Section 5 support from this decision.

The two dissenters would vacate and require the lower court to determine whether the preserved statutory applications enforce an actual constitutional denial of meaningful judicial participation or an unconstitutional penalty for seeking it. They would confine enforcement to those supported applications and reject relief resting solely on the broader preventive theory. The supplied preliminary record does not justify their ordering dismissal of every possible Lane claim or finding an actual constitutional denial; their remand resolves that work under their stated rule.

## Precedent treatment and source limits

Popovich is applied and extended from the established custody-hearing application to the defined physical judicial-access class, preserving its distinction between statutory prevention and an individual constitutional tort. Garrett remains controlling at the full Title I employment scope it decided; it neither forecloses this closer hearing connection nor validates every Title II service. Boerne supplies congruence and proportionality without a numerical or formal-findings checklist. Hibbs supplies a separate example of bounded preventive enforcement and the Associates' differing fit analysis; its family-care rules are not transferred here. Frew supplies no State-damages authority for this Title II case.

No incident count, congressional finding, physical configuration, offered alternative, or damages fact absent from the packet is invented. The commitments rest on the supported Popovich principle, statutory qualifications and the present preliminary allegations. A broader rule covering unrelated Title II services or a decision requiring a particular accommodation would need its own supplied legal and factual basis and fresh affected analysis. No unresolved source gap prevents the bounded court-access commitments.
