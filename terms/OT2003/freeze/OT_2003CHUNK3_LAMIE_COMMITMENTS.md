# Lamie v. United States Trustee — frozen neutral non-Stone commitments

**Event:** Merits, 2004-01-26; No. 02-693; argued 2003-11-10.
**Inputs and cutoff:** Approved neutral packet, C preflight, C entering-law slice, official 2002 Code §§327 and 330 including the former text and 1994 amendment note. January 26, 2004. No target historical disposition, comparator or private position was read.

## Common judgment and operative rule

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer each would affirm denial of estate compensation for the postconversion services. Under amended §330(a)(1), a debtor's lawyer in Chapter 7 cannot receive an estate award on that status alone; the attorney must be a professional employed under §327 with the required court approval to use that route. Lamie acknowledges that employment is absent. The retained reference to attorney in subparagraph (A) describes services of a professional eligible under the opening list and does not add a deleted recipient.

This rule does not hold that the services lacked value or forbid personal payment. Individual Chapter 12 and Chapter 13 compensation under §330(a)(4)(B) is a distinct express authorization. Prepetition retainer ownership, independently available payment routes and an award calculation are outside the eligibility issue; no retainer transfer or fee amount is adjudicated.

## Alternative-path comparison

**Textual eligibility path:** The recipient list is grammatically imperfect but legally workable. Its express employment reference matches §327's supervised selection mechanism. An omitted conjunction can be understood without inserting an entire additional recipient category. The services list remains meaningful because retained attorneys are professional persons. Hartford Underwriters unanimously enforced a specifically named bankruptcy claimant despite beneficial estate work; Patterson requires the enacted rather than preferred restriction. The result therefore rests on eligibility, not equity or factual worthlessness.

The strongest objection is the combination of the deleted category, retained attorney reference and drafting history, which may indicate an inadvertent amendment. That contention warrants examination of the former and current provisions. It does not require restoring the category where the enacted list has a coherent operation and Congress expressly supplies a separate debtor-attorney allowance for individual Chapter 12 and 13 cases. Neither hardship nor the general purpose of professional compensation establishes that the opposite rule is legally impossible.

**Corrective-construction path:** Restore debtor's attorney to preserve former eligibility, then remand for reasonable compensation review. Its strongest support is the visible drafting awkwardness and claimed absence of a deliberate policy change. Its decisive obstacle is that it changes a workable statutory distribution rule and makes the employment condition ineffective for this additional recipient class. The Code does not make every useful service a charge against the estate. Dewsnup's confined contextual resolution of lien language creates no general judicial authority to restore deleted recipients.

All eight reject the corrective construction on this record. They need not hold that courts can never recognize a genuine scrivener's error or that legislative history is always inadmissible.

## Remedy and source limits

Affirm the Fourth Circuit's denial of the requested postconversion Chapter 7 estate compensation. No new calculation hearing is ordered because statutory eligibility fails. No new fact, amendment, historical Supreme Court holding or generalized bankruptcy policy supplies the disposition. The official enactment note identifies the 1994 change and its applicability to cases commenced on or after October 22, 1994; the supplied case proceeds under that amended provision. No additional date-dependent exception is invented. A separate writing is not necessary if the Court's reasoning preserves the specified contextual limits; authorship and final joins remain for assembly.
