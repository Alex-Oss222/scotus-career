## Complete rule and mandate

Congress may protect the interstate telephone system by prohibiting unauthorized interception of covered wire communications carried through it even when the particular call's endpoints are in one State. The direct intrusion into communications carried by that instrumentality supplies the constitutional connection; neither a separate interstate journey by the call nor physical network damage is necessary. A recorder's remote manufacture would not suffice on its own. The statutory definition, intentional interception requirement, exceptions and criminal burden remain independently operative.
