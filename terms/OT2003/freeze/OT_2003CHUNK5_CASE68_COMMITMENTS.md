## Common proposed judgment and grounds

All eight Associates would reverse the Sixth Circuit's judgment and remand for rejection of the claim that these separately disclosed over-limit fees necessarily had to enter the finance-charge subtotal. The remedy adjudicates that classification theory. It does not award damages, declare compliance with every disclosure provision, or dismiss a distinct preserved claim whose resolution depends on another duty.

The Board exercised actual delegated authority through a binding implementing regulation. The court must first enforce the statutory boundaries. The phrase incident to the extension of credit reaches costs connected with credit, but it does not unambiguously settle how a fee triggered by exceeding an agreed credit limit must be classified. Section 1604(a)'s express classification authority is especially important where that boundary is left open. The fact that an issuer knowingly honors an over-limit purchase does not itself turn every resulting fee into the agreed price for a new credit limit. Pfennig's contrary connection to the extension of additional credit is substantial, but establishes a permissible competing reading rather than statutory compulsion.

The Board may distinguish the agreed price of credit from contingent charges incurred when the borrower exceeds the agreement's limit. Uniform treatment of that specified category facilitates intelligible comparison of credit prices and compliance without excusing disclosure of the charge. This is a reasonable classification within the stated statutory purposes. Administrative ease alone would not authorize contradiction of §1605(a), and ordinary remedial-purpose language alone would not eliminate §1604(a). Neither side's principle replaces the enacted provisions.

Smiley's treatment of certain charges as interest under the separate national-bank statute does not compel their classification under TILA. Each agency's authority, statutory definition and legally operative category must be examined separately. No new retroactivity holding is necessary: the packet establishes that the operative regulation was already in force when these fees were charged.

## Handoff limits

Eight votes support the stated reversal and the bounded statutory rationale. No disagreement requires a separate writing at this stage, and no author is selected. Reconciliation must preserve the distinction between delegated classification and an exemption under another subsection, between the two separate regulatory exclusions, and between rejecting this claim and adjudicating every disclosure obligation. A newly supplied record showing that the charge was for a different agreed credit product would require revisiting application openly; that unestablished possibility is not a reason to postpone the presented legal question.
