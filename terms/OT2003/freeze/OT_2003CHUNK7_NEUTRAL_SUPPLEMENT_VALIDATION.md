## Public Citizen environmental effects and conformity

Validated. The contemporaneous official appendix supplies regulatory text, not a holding or adoption of the government?s argument. Section 1508.8 distinguishes direct effects from later or more distant but reasonably foreseeable indirect effects; both remain effects caused by the action. Section 1508.7 adds the action?s incremental impact to other past, present and reasonably foreseeable actions regardless of actor. The presence of another actor does not eliminate cumulative consideration, but the provision does not itself establish that every consequence of that actor?s independent decision is an effect of the reviewed agency action.

The separate conformity definition in section 93.152 requires both causal and foreseeability conditions and the agency?s practicable control maintained through continuing program responsibility for indirect emissions. Its explicit control requirement must be analyzed on its own terms, rather than silently copied into NEPA as identical text. Direct emissions retain their own caused-or-initiated and same-time-and-place definition. The supplement adds no finding about which actual effects satisfy any definition, no new regulatory alternative and no preservation conclusion.

**Readiness:** Ready for independent modeling. Apply the then-operative text with the packet?s allocation of presidential and agency authority and its preserved procedural limits.

## Norton supplementation

Validated. Section 1502.9(c)(1) requires supplementation upon either qualifying trigger: substantial agency changes in the proposed action relevant to environmental concerns, or significant new circumstances or information relevant to environmental concerns and bearing on the proposed action or its impacts. These are alternatives, not cumulative prerequisites. Section (c)(2) separately permits supplementation when the agency determines that it would further NEPA?s purposes; it does not convert that permission into a mandatory duty.

The surviving predivergence Marsh quotation and contemporaneous official reproduction provide the stated regulatory evidence. The excerpt alone does not establish that a proposed action remains, that a management plan is continuing major federal action, that qualifying changes or information exist, or that a particular supplementation duty has been unlawfully withheld. Those predicates remain for analysis under the packet?s separate nonimpairment, plan-compliance and NEPA theories. Neither discretionary supplementation nor the mandatory triggers may be omitted from any eventual proposition that depends on them.

**Readiness:** Ready for independent modeling with this regulatory supplement. No new factual reconstruction or enacted-law predicate requiring a user choice is identified; no later regulatory version has been imported.
