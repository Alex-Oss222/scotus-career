# Dretke v. Haley: independent commitments

Event and research cutoff: May 3, 2004. Sources: frozen case 73 neutral packet, controlled objective-record addition and entering-law slice; effective public projection; the published Monge entry in OT1997 chunk 12 and Sawyer separate positions in OT1991 chunk 13. No current controlled Chief Justice position, comparator, reconciliation or raw Decision Record was consulted. These commitments select neither authorship nor the final Court coalition.

## Distinct questions and available dispositions

The acknowledged sequencing defect establishes Haley's ineligibility under Texas's habitual-offender provision. It does not by itself establish a federal constitutional violation or an entitlement to a writ. The defaulted sufficiency claim, the possible innocence gateway, constitutional assistance as cause, the separately pleaded assistance claim, the section 2254 restriction on that state-adjudicated assistance claim, and the available sentence remedy remain distinct.

The state court rejected assistance on its merits; the federal court left that claim unadjudicated. The federal application was filed in August 2000. Thus a remand to that claim requires application of amended section 2254 and Strickland, not an assumed finding that counsel's missed objection automatically establishes all requirements. A favorable assistance disposition can independently support sentence relief and can supply qualifying cause if the requirements for that use are satisfied.

Two disposition paths receive support. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer would vacate the appellate judgment and require adjudication of the available assistance route before deciding whether to extend the innocence gateway. Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg would affirm recognition and satisfaction of a narrowly confined factual-ineligibility gateway, but vacate the existing conditional-writ disposition for adjudication of the independent constitutional basis and applicable habeas conditions. No Associate commits to a writ resting only on the conceded state-law defect.

## Enhanced issue-specific comparison

**Assistance-first path.** The sufficient reason for remand is that a properly presented, unresolved constitutional claim may supply the complete requested sentence remedy without a new noncapital gateway. Edwards preserves that claim's independent requirements; Trest and Lambrix establish that default is nonjurisdictional and permit reasoned sequencing. The strongest objection is that Haley's statutory ineligibility is conceded and an additional round of litigation can prolong custody beyond any punishment the enhancement law permits. The response supporting this path is a prompt, bounded remand on the existing assistance claim, rather than a final rejection of relief or an assumed extension of Jackson to every recidivist finding. This is a case-specific sequencing disposition, not a command that every habeas claim follow the same order.

**Gateway-now path.** Sawyer distinguishes eligibility from discretionary selection. Its core miscarriage concern can support a narrow gateway where reliable, conclusive chronology establishes that no reasonable factfinder could find a necessary statutory condition for the additional custody. Haley satisfies even Sawyer's clear-and-convincing quantum on the admitted chronology. The strongest objections are Sawyer's capital setting, the risk of converting every sentence error into innocence, and the absence of a completed constitutional adjudication. The confined response is factual ineligibility for the habitual-offender status, rather than mitigation, sentence discretion, an ordinary legal mistake or a free-standing state-law writ. Constitutional cognizability and entitlement must still be decided. This path settles the submitted gateway question but does not presume its distinct merits predicates.

The constitutional-proof question cannot silently be answered by the gateway. Monge preserves independent due-process questions and adopts only a bounded noncapital renewed-proof classification. Its dissenters' element-function reasoning supports a possible constitutional route but is not controlling law. Later Limon and Gatewood propositions cannot simply be moved backward to the relevant state adjudication or finality date. Winship and Jackson supply the earlier proof principles; their application to this enhancement and any independently applicable nonretroactivity issue require express adjudication if the sufficiency route becomes necessary.

## Remedy and source limits

The five assistance-first commitments support vacatur of the existing judgment and remand for prompt adjudication of the separately pleaded assistance claim under section 2254 and Strickland, including its actual state merits treatment. If that route establishes entitlement, the federal court must implement a lawful sentence remedy; if it does not resolve the request, the court must address any remaining properly available cause and prejudice and gateway/constitutional questions under their distinct requirements. These commitments reject neither the preserved assistance claim nor noncapital innocence categorically.

The three gateway-now commitments would bind remand to the availability and satisfaction of the eligibility gateway on the admitted chronology. They still require an actual federal constitutional ground before restoring a writ. They can join the neutral statement of remaining assistance, statutory and remedial requirements, but not a mandate treating the gateway as wholly open or requiring assistance adjudication as an exclusive prerequisite.

Any eventual sentence writ must allow the State a reasonable, concrete period to impose a lawful sentence without the invalid enhancement, or require termination of custody attributable to that invalid punishment. The theft conviction survives. The lower court must establish the actual present custody and judgment status and account for service already completed; neither a new state resentencing event nor a separate current custody basis is supplied. No unconditional present release or automatic sixteen-and-one-half-year custody continuation is committed here.

The missing precise state merits date matters to any proposed use of a later substantive proof holding. It does not block the stated assistance-first or gateway-plus-merits-remand paths, both of which expressly require the actual dated constitutional and statutory inquiries. These commitments are ready for separate reconciliation; they make no finding that the independently unreviewed federal assistance claim succeeds.
