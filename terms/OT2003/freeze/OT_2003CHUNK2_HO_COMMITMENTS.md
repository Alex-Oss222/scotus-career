## Proposed propositions

P1. The reviewed class is asbestos-removal work during regulated facility renovation and the advance notice supporting regulation of that work, under §§7412(h), 7414(a), 7413(c)(1)/(c)(2)(B) and 40 C.F.R. §61.145. It is not pollution in general or every local activity causing health costs. The regulation specifies kinds and amounts of asbestos; coverage of this hospital is uncontested. The facility definition includes institutional, commercial, public, industrial and specified residential structures, ships and active or inactive waste sites, while excluding apartment buildings with four or fewer units and preserving coverage of previously covered structures. The breadth of that definition is acknowledged, not silently rewritten as a commercial-building element.

P2. This work-practice class regulates the actual activity supplied in an established interstate asbestos-abatement service market. The removal operation, including owner-procured substitute labor, directly concerns the production and cost of that service, rather than an owner's unrelated commercial surroundings. Ho's solicited licensed bids, rejection on cost grounds, paid substitute workers and renovation operation concretely support that market relationship. Repeated evasion of required handling precautions alters the competitive cost of providing the very service regulated. Once this economic connection and statutory fit are identified, Congress may assess substantial aggregate effects without proving this single project's effect was independently substantial. Wickard and Perez preserve that economic-class principle; Hodel permits regulation of economic operations for environmental ends.

P3. The preventive requirements may apply without proof that asbestos actually escaped into ambient air or crossed a State boundary. Work standards operate before release; advance notice makes those standards enforceable at the removal stage. Congress need not wait for the feared injury before regulating an economic operation within its commerce power. Notice is directly ancillary to the valid work regulation, not an independently aggregated class of all failures to report. No actual-release element is added to or removed from another offense. The present constitutional conclusion accepts the lower court's no-release assumption for this question and does not borrow the sentencing finding made under a different evidentiary standard.

P4. Lopez requires connection before magnitude and forbids curing a deficient enacted class with an invented element. Home Builders rejects treating a species-taking class as economic merely because a developer undertook it. Here the regulated operation itself is the abatement service, and the record identifies the direct service-market mechanism. This distinction does not create a universal rule that commercial projects validate any regulation touching them. Holston's supported regime-inclusion rule remains distinct: this direct economic-service ground needs no assumption that asbestos removal has the same concealment, reproducibility or supply mechanism as the images in Holston. No independent interstate-externality ruling is required, and generalized health expenditures do no constitutional work.

P5. Affirm the two convictions against the preserved Commerce Clause challenge. The Fifth Circuit's distinct sentence vacatur and remand remain operative on their existing grounds and scope; the constitutional decision does not reinstate the original sentence or resolve an ungranted enhancement issue. The other dismissed or acquitted counts are unaffected. This as-applied judgment resolves the commercial removal and related notice operation presented; materially different nonmarket applications outside this grant require their own supported mechanism, without a facial judgment on the entire Act.

## Enhanced alternative-path comparison

Ho's strongest objection is that the regulation's facility coverage extends beyond commercial buildings and contains no commerce element; Home Builders prevents selecting a commercial application to hide a noneconomic enacted class. The answer is not the hospital's price or Ho's general business ownership. The object of these particular standards is the performance of a defined asbestos-removal operation, an activity actually supplied and procured in the identified national service market. The paid work and competing licensed bids support that operation-level classification; property value is corroboration, not the bridge. Direct performance substituted for purchasing abatement does not remove an economic service from its market class. No commercial-transaction element is judicially inserted.

The alternative reversal path would treat work practices and notice as purely local noneconomic conduct until a release crosses state lines. It cannot explain why production and procurement of the regulated service lose their commercial character before an emission occurs, and it imports a harm element absent from these preventive offenses. The strongest narrower remand path demands proof of the actual interstate pollution mechanism; that evidence is unnecessary to the sufficient service-market ground. Courts still independently test the eligible mechanism and fit, and the Government must support its claimed market rather than invoke environmental importance.
