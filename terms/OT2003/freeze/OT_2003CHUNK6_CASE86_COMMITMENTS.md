## Competing legal paths

Preiser supplies habeas for the core attack on custody; Heck and Edwards require comparison with what success actually invalidates. Randell's unanimous compatible-claim rule and the effective February 25, 2004 Muhammad holding reject categorical conversion based on prison context. Here the pleaded claim leaves the sentence and a lawful lethal-injection implementation available. Temporary delay to resolve that civil claim is not itself a declaration that the sentence is invalid. A claim whose necessary relief forecloses every lawful implementation would present the different habeas path; that consequence is not pleaded or established here.

Alabama's strongest contrary argument is that injunctive relief immediately before execution obstructs the sentence just as effectively as habeas. The distinction is between temporary litigation relief and the claim's necessary legal consequence. Courts can enforce statutory exhaustion, examine the medical merits and apply ordinary equity without redefining every execution-related injury as a successive habeas claim. Gomez supplies an independent equitable objection to unjustified withholding of a long-available claim. It creates neither an automatic late-filing bar nor an automatic capital stay.

## Remedy and scope

All eight support reversal of the channel dismissal and remand. The civil court must adjudicate the claim through its ordinary process, address any properly raised available-remedy exhaustion question on evidence, determine the medical and constitutional merits, and decide any actual renewed interim request under the applicable equitable and PLRA conditions. The allegations' truth for pleading purposes is not a merits finding. The channel holding supplies no new execution stay, protocol injunction or judgment of constitutional injury. No blanket claim-wide ruling on all execution-method challenges is necessary.
