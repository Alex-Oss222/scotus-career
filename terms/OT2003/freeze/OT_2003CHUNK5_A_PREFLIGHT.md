## Case-specific source adequacy and live paths

**56. Karaha Bodas.** The record is sufficient for review of the preliminary foreign restraint and surviving civil-contempt consequence. The separate award-recognition appeal remains outside this judgment. The live alternatives are affirmance for lack of demonstrated necessity; reversal if the shown foreign interference is itself sufficient; or a confined remand if an identified threat requires findings. The packet establishes pursuit of annulment and anti-enforcement relief, not that the American court has lost practical ability to enforce. The validity of a particular foreign annulment and primary annulment jurisdiction need not be settled to resolve the necessity of this restraint. No categorical lack of all anti-suit power follows.

**57. Batzel.** The record is sufficient to decide the category's federal appealability. The probability-of-success inquiry and claimed litigation immunity are identified; no finding of email falsity, actual intent or reasonable perception is supplied. The alternative appealability path would require an independently important immunity, separateness and effective unreviewability. A finality disposition avoids all three competing section 230 constructions. The independently final sponsor judgment supplies no bootstrapping and remains outside the granted issues.

**58. Miccosukee.** The pump transports rather than creates pollutants. The packet expressly supplies a disputed hydrological premise, litigation below on that premise, and a dispute over the scope of further review. Those facts support a conveyance ruling and either a remand tied to a genuine material distinct-water dispute or a scope-confined affirmance. They do not establish that a whole watershed is one water body. The Government's broader unitary-waters theory has not been developed below in its present form; no binding agency position or later transfer exemption is admitted. A definitive affirmance that treats distinctness as conclusively established would require more than this packet supplies. A separate-writing objection to remand can rest on the grant's limited focus, rather than a newly found hydrological fact.

**59. Galletti.** Timely assessment of the partnership tax is supplied. Independent state-law partner liability is the alleged collection basis. The Court can decide the repeated-assessment question without fixing every partner's liability, claim amount or administrative collection entitlement. A tax-specific interpretation and a partner-specific taxpayer reading are both sufficiently presented; Updike's actual transferee statute and obsolete six-year period must not become the present rule. The modern collection period remains governed by section 6502's operative text and any independently applicable limitations provisions.

**60. Nixon.** The packet identifies a state restriction on municipal telecommunications and the local-authority withdrawal argument. It permits statutory resolution of whether section 253 supplies sufficiently clear authority without determining Congress's ultimate constitutional power. A competing ordinary-preemption path can treat an already empowered municipality as an entity and return for the state-law capacity determination; it cannot invent affirmative authority. The broader construction must explain the statute's prohibition-effect language rather than rely on a formal withholding/withdrawal distinction alone.
