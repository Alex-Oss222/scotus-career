## Defects identified and repairs verified

1. **Groh: distinguish warrant defect from unconstitutional search.** The initial Record characterized the particularity issue as 7–2 and stated only the warrant's facial deficiency in Holding 1. The frozen case 43 reconciliation shows that all nine accept the facial defect, while Thomas and Scalia reject the conclusion that the execution was unconstitutional. The repaired kernel and Public Projection expressly hold the search unconstitutional, identify that holding's seven direct joins, preserve all-nine agreement on facial deficiency, and retain the six-Justice immunity disposition. The public topology now names the search proposition rather than assigning a 7–2 division to facial defect alone.
2. **Doe: remove an unnecessary public nonadoption statement.** The initial Holdings said the Court need not infer its answer from deleted bill language. That was an unnecessary public account of an excluded rationale under Engine section 8. The repaired kernel and Public Projection state the enacted monetary authorization directly; the excluded drafting-history material remains only in the internal compatibility/reconciliation account.
3. **Cline: preserve the regulation's conditional qualification.** The initial Scalia summary described section 1625.2(b) as permitting additional benefits when the employer reasonably concludes they counteract discrimination. The primary supplement says such benefits may be lawful if the employer has a reasonable basis to conclude they counteract problems related to age discrimination, and forbids their use to accomplish otherwise prohibited practices. The repaired summary preserves both that conditional formulation and the separate prohibition. It does not turn agency permission into automatic legality.
4. **Olympic: identify the speaker correctly.** The initial Holdings attributed an invitation to seek a seat exchange to the third request. The corrected neutral sequence attributes it to the attendant's response. Both kernel and public Holdings now say the attendant's response to the third request. This repair changes no holding, vote or factual inference.
