## Historical verification and reconciliation

The complete Cornell majority and Thomas dissent, including their notes, were read. The official report is saved separately for cross-checking. Historically six Associates joined reversal and remand; Thomas dissented with Scalia. The independent judgments, required-content distinction and relation-back grounds match those positions. Historical authorship does not determine assignment.

The six permitting amendment distinguish mandatory content from an absolute prohibition on later completion. A timely identified fee request already invokes a proceeding ancillary to an action within the court's jurisdiction. Becker and Edelman support this distinction; relation back predates Rule 15 and is not confined to papers within that rule's definition of pleadings. The statutory allegation shifts the burden to the Government without adding the operative facts or a new demand. Irwin and the canonical Franconia holding support ordinary limitation principles after the relevant immunity waiver; neither turns every missing statutory condition into a curable defect. O'Connor's insistence on lawful authorization is satisfied by the independent construction of this fee procedure, rather than a free-standing power to award money on equitable grounds. This is the same sufficient premise as her historical join.

Thomas and Scalia preserve the mandatory timely-content objection. Becker had an express cure rule; Edelman had an authorized regulation; they do not accept that those sources authorize completion of this statutory fee application. Kontrick's nonjurisdictional classification does not answer their timely asserted mandatory-processing objection. No jurisdictional label is needed to preserve the historical dissent's operative construction.

## Complete rule and assembly boundaries

For this timely application, the missing allegation that the Government's position lacked substantial justification may be supplied after the thirty-day period by relation back. A demonstrated prejudice from the amendment precludes relation back; the Government asserted none here. Prejudice is not merely another merits defense. The separate statutory special-circumstances exception permits denial where an award would be unjust. The statutory command is that the court shall award qualifying fees unless substantial justification or special circumstances defeats the award, subject to the statute's coverage, eligibility, amount and other operative conditions. Permission to correct the allegation does not itself award fees.

The independent commitments remain frozen as written. The comparator verifies three precise limits already implicit in their confined rule: the Court need not decide whether the second sentence's allegation independently shares the first sentence's thirty-day deadline; it does not adjudicate late cure of the first sentence's required showings; and it does not apply equitable tolling to this application. These are limits arising from the actual submitted omission, not categorical exclusions from all future EAJA cases. Assembly must preserve them where needed to prevent the rule from becoming broader than the recorded joins.

Remedy is reversal and remand for acceptance of the corrected allegation and ordinary fee adjudication. The veterans court must decide substantial justification and any properly presented remaining entitlement or amount issue. The dissent would leave the application dismissal in place. No material changed premise, historical departure, missing choice or stopped issue remains. Final Chief compatibility, opinion form and balanced assignment await assembly.
