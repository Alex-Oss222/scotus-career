Matter 112, Rasul and Al Odah, Nos. 03-334 and 03-343; June 28, 2004. This is a narrow historical-reconciliation wording audit of the civil branch only. It supplements, without rewriting, the [original B commitments](OT_2003CHUNK8_B_COMMITMENTS.md) and [B reconciled commitments](OT_2003CHUNK8_B_RECONCILED.md). ## Proposed form and six-Associate conclusion

> The categorical dismissal of Al Odah's pleaded federal civil claims is reversed, and those claims are remanded for separate adjudication of their actual jurisdictional, cause-of-action, immunity and remedial predicates.

**Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support this exact sentence on their existing R-C grounds.** For these six, replacing vacated with reversed is a formal clarification, not a material change. Their frozen positions already determine that the categorical ground for dismissing the civil branch is legally erroneous and require adjudication of the actual claims under their independent predicates. They do not merely request clarification of an uncertain lower-court rationale. The proposed reversal identifies that existing error while preserving the same remand.

The sentence reverses the categorical dismissal; it does not establish jurisdiction over every pleaded theory, recognize a new cause of action, waive immunity, determine liability, or award relief. Actual statutory reviewability, independent jurisdictional objections and the merits remain for adjudication at their proper stages. Listing the principal predicates in the sentence does not resolve or eliminate those existing defenses. The complete frozen opinion-level qualifications remain operative.

This conclusion is specific to the error and remedy already adopted. It does not treat every position described as setting aside a judgment as interchangeable with reversal. In particular, Scalia and Thomas's affirmative disposition of the constitutional-dependent branch is a substantive disagreement with the six-Associate sentence, not a terminological difference.

## Scalia and Thomas: preserve the divided disposition

Neither Scalia nor Thomas supports the proposed sentence as an unqualified disposition of all the pleaded civil claims. Each would affirm dismissal insofar as a claim necessarily depends on the extraterritorial constitutional entitlement rejected in his frozen R2 position. Each separately rejects universal alien incapacity and supports remand of genuinely independent statutory claims. A label such as statutory or APA cannot convert an identical rejected constitutional entitlement into an independent claim.

The precise civil-disposition form supported by each is:

> Affirm the dismissal insofar as a pleaded civil claim necessarily depends on the rejected extraterritorial constitutional entitlement. Reverse the categorical dismissal of the remaining genuinely independent statutory claims, and remand that statutory branch for separate determination of its actual jurisdictional, cause-of-action, immunity, reviewability and remedial predicates.

For the civil branch as a whole, this is **affirm in part, reverse in part, and remand** at the specified scopes. The reversal of the statutory branch is a formal clarification of the already frozen limited vacatur/remand: its sufficient ground is the established error of applying universal alien incapacity instead of the source-specific analysis those Justices already accepted. It is not a finding that any particular statutory claim survives every defense. Because the packet does not enumerate every claim's complete predicates, no count-by-count allocation or new finding that a particular claim is independent is supplied here.

This audit neither creates an additional historical departure nor removes those explanations. Their historical votes to affirm the entire judgment are not recharacterized as historical partial reversals.

## Agreement and unchanged limits

- Six Associates support reversal and remand of the entire categorical civil dismissal: Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.
- Scalia and Thomas support partial affirmance on the constitutional-dependent branch and partial reversal/remand on the genuinely independent statutory branch.
- All eight reject universal alien incapacity as a substitute for the actual claim-specific inquiry. That common legal proposition is not eight votes for the six-Associate civil disposition.
- The habeas result, Kennedy's preserved Eisentrager rationale barrier, and Scalia and Thomas's habeas opposition are unaffected. No civil remand establishes habeas jurisdiction by relabeling a foreclosed claim.
- No new cause, sovereign-immunity waiver, finding of unlawful detention, immediate release or damages entitlement is added. The historical note about withdrawal of ATS theories does not erase the claims retained in the approved neutral packet.

Adopting the unqualified six-Associate sentence for Scalia or Thomas would be a material and unsupported revision because it would displace their affirmative disposition on the constitutional-dependent branch. Replacing vacated with reversed only within their specifically identified statutory branch is supported by the existing legal-error conclusion and identical remand. These two different assessments preserve the actual commitments rather than manufacturing agreement from vocabulary.
