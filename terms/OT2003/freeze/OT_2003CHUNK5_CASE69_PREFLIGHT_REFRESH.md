## Material refresh and sufficiency

All six rules contain commercial-availability-related qualifications, but the operative triggers, certificate procedures, residual duties, dates and covered vehicles differ. The District's general description of the rules as available-product selections is an argument whose reach must be tested against their terms. The petitioners' reply, printed pages 9–10 and note 7, argues that the four clauses using availability and possible use permit the Executive Officer to demand a theoretically usable but nonexistent product. That is a disputed legal construction; neither that claim nor the District's contrary blanket description is adopted as a finding about actual administration.

Rule 1186.1's purchase route and service-contract route must be distinguished. Its technical-infeasibility route to controlled diesel purchases has an express July 1, 2004 cutoff; the service-contract/no-alternative-bids route has distinct terms. April 28 precedes the cutoff, but a challenge to the enacted prospective rule cannot silently assume an uncodified extension. Nor does the cutoff alone prove that an unavailable product will actually be demanded. No rule is invalidated merely because a future operational fact is unknown.

Section 246 is interpretive context, not an independent live authorization defense for these Fleet Rules. The approved addendum establishes that the Rules did not satisfy all requirements of that program. The District's merits brief, printed pages 36–41, uses the federal fleet-purchase program structurally and contests petitioners' inference from it; it does not ask for affirmance because these Rules satisfy section 246. No section-246-compliance remand is supplied. The District's alternative section 209(d) use argument, printed page 43, is expressly described as not litigated below; remand leaves any procedurally available distinct application issue to the ordinary rules and creates no preserved merits ruling by assumption.

The supplied questions and exact rules are sufficient to decide the categorical purchase exemption and specify the remand's boundaries. The missing opening-brief pages do not determine the rules' text; the complete reply states the pertinent opposing construction. No aggregator supplies the disposition. Genuine proprietary activity, statutory newness, the scope of any actually applicable waiver and severability remain source-specific questions rather than inferred exceptions. No additional docket or grant verification is required.
