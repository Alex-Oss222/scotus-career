# Scarborough v. Principi: independent commitments

Event and research cutoff: May 3, 2004. Authorized inputs are the frozen case 74 neutral packet, controlled objective-record supplement, entering-law slice and effective neutral public projection. No current controlled Chief Justice position, comparator, reconciliation or raw Decision Record was consulted. No authorship is assigned.

## Components and common premises

The application was timely and identified the applicant, prevailing status, financial eligibility, requested amount and itemized work. The sole omitted item was the allegation that the Government lacked substantial justification; counsel supplied it promptly after objection. The Government did not argue prejudice from relation back. That litigation position is not a general factual finding about every possible amendment.

The allegation is required. The question is whether it may be supplied by amendment relating back to this timely application. Permission to amend neither proves entitlement nor transfers the Government's substantial-justification burden to the applicant. Section 2412(d)(1)(A) makes fees available to a prevailing eligible party in its covered action unless substantial justification or special circumstances defeats the award; amount and the other actual statutory conditions remain for the fee court. Pierce supplies the merits meaning of substantial justification, not a new pleading default.

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would reverse and remand to permit the amendment and adjudicate the fee request. Scalia and Thomas would affirm because the timely invocation must include this expressly required allegation and no applicable cure provision authorizes its post-deadline addition over the Government's timely objection. All eight distinguish this application-content question from a lack of subject-matter competence over the covered action. The dissenting ground does not depend on calling every EAJA requirement jurisdictional.

## Enhanced comparison

**Relation-back construction.** A timely, identified application invokes the existing fee proceeding; the later allegation completes that request rather than commencing a different claim. Becker and Edelman demonstrate that mandatory content and permitted later cure can coexist. The fee statute regulates a request ancillary to an action already within the court's authority, and adding an allegation on an issue the Government ultimately must substantiate does not expand the class of authorized monetary liability. Ordinary relation-back principles inform this construction without directly transforming the application into a civil complaint governed by Rule 15.

The strongest objection is that Congress placed the application contents beside an express thirty-day deadline, and cure can appear to make a mandatory condition optional. The six allowing cure answer that the allegation remains obligatory and is actually supplied; the timeliness and claim identity already exist, and the enacted text does not expressly prohibit completion by relation back. This record does not ask for an equitable extension of the filing period.

**Complete-application construction.** The linked directions require the statutory contents in the thirty-day application. Neither a timely incomplete request nor lack of surprise substitutes for a requirement Congress chose. The strongest objection is the disproportionate forfeiture of an otherwise complete timely request for an allegation adding no new facts or fee demand. Scalia and Thomas answer that Becker had an express rule authorizing cure and Edelman had an authorized regulation operating within its statute; neither supplies a freestanding power to disregard a mandatory fee-application condition. Kontrick separates jurisdiction from mandatory processing but preserves timely objections and does not decide relation back.

## Remedy and completeness

The six reversal commitments require the veterans court to accept the corrected allegation and adjudicate the fee application under section 2412(d), including substantial justification on the relevant litigation and agency record, special circumstances if properly raised, eligibility and reasonable amount. The two affirmance commitments leave dismissal in place for failure to meet the mandatory application condition. Neither position makes fee entitlement the consequence of the procedural ruling alone.

The Government's timely objection prevents a Kontrick forfeiture disposition; its earlier response extension does not itself waive the objection. Becker's represented-litigant rule defeats a distinction based only on counsel rather than a lay applicant. The precise issue of an entirely absent or entirely late application lies outside this submitted timely-application case; no general equitable-tolling disposition is made. The actual initial filing is accepted as timely under the controlled record, without treating the earlier prematurely returned paper as the operative application.

No material factual gap blocks these commitments. Same-day matters use the common pre-May-3 baseline. The distinct positions are ready for historical reconciliation and later compatibility testing.
