## Shared rule and the actual division

A genuine checkpoint seeking possible witnesses' information about another person's specific completed crime is not automatically invalid under Edmond. The actual purpose and operation must distinguish the request from suspicionless screening of motorists for their own ordinary offenses. The label information gathering supplies no authority by itself. Brown then requires evaluation of the gravity of the public concern, the degree to which the seizure advances it, and the full interference with individual liberty, including actual duration and limits on officer discretion. Neither a presumption of validity nor a universal quantified-efficacy or exhaustion-of-alternatives requirement follows.

The five sustaining Associates conclude that the recent fatal incident, specific location and approximate weekly timing, systematic treatment, narrowly limited question and flyer, and limited total delay satisfy that rule. The practical connection to people who may repeat their travel patterns supports the information request without a finding that every stopped driver witnessed the accident. The brief compulsory wait and nighttime apprehension are real but proportionate to this confined inquiry. Their remedy reverses suppression on this checkpoint ground and remands for proceedings consistent with the checkpoint's validity; no genuinely independent preserved issue is invented or decided.

The three remand Associates accept the same purpose distinction and Brown framework but withhold assent from the final application. They give the compulsory delay and nighttime encounter greater weight, consider the useful-information inference plausible without regarding its constitutional sufficiency as obvious, and would have Illinois apply the contextual standard that its categorical Edmond approach bypassed. That is a case-specific choice of appellate disposition. It neither requires a factual dispute before every remand nor makes remand mandatory whenever a lower court used the wrong rule. It does not hold the checkpoint unreasonable or affirm categorical suppression. No evidentiary hearing, proof of the least intrusive method, efficacy study or particular new finding is commanded.
