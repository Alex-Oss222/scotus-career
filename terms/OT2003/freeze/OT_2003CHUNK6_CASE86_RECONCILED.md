## Complete channel and interim-relief qualifications

The holding concerns this pleaded unnecessary cut-down and supported alternative venous access. It does not decide how every challenge to an execution method should be classified. If ordinary factual development establishes that the challenged procedure is necessary to carry out the lawful execution, the lower court must confront the different channel question on that actual premise; it must not treat the present pleaded compatibility conclusion as conclusively resolving that different claim. Likewise, a future request to enjoin execution irrespective of whether the State uses the cut-down exceeds a merely lesser-included temporary restraint of the challenged procedure and requires its own channel and scope analysis.

A properly limited temporary restraint needed to adjudicate a cognizable civil claim does not become habeas merely because it produces delay. Conversely, cognizability supplies no entitlement to a stay. The district court must consider likelihood of success, relative harms, the State's enforcement interest and whether the applicant unnecessarily delayed. The eight accept a strong equitable presumption against a stay where the claim could have been brought in time for merits consideration without requiring one, with the actual reasons for timing and irreversible harm still considered. This is not an automatic bar on any late filing, an automatic capital stay or a finding that Nelson delayed abusively.
