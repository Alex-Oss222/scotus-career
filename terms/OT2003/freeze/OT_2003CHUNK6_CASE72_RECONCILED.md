## Assembly boundary and completeness

The shared rule includes §1658(a)'s exception where law otherwise supplies a limitation, the four-year period from accrual, the post-December 1, 1990 enactment criterion, and the requirement that the enactment made this claim against this defendant possible. An amendment immaterial to an already available claim does not shift that claim's limitation. The described hostile-environment, termination and transfer claims receive the amendment-enabled classification; remaining claim-specific accrual, timeliness and discrimination adjudication are ordinary remand work. Do not turn a definition of new claim into a rule that every action citing a revised statute receives four years.

Goodman remains the borrowing rule outside a displaced limitation category; Patterson remains the earlier-text construction; Rivers establishes new coverage and its prospective temporal operation, rather than a separate limitation rule. No independently sufficient alternative holding, split component or material historical departure is established. No stopped determination remains.
