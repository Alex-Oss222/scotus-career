Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/02-1657.ZO.html, factual Part I.B and objective litigation-position statement near the end. After an earlier prematurely returned filing, the application at issue was retained by the CAVC clerk and filed after the appeal period expired. It alleged prevailing-party status, net worth below $2 million, the requested sum and itemized attorney work. The required lack-of-substantial-justification allegation was the sole missing content item; compliance with the other content requirements was undisputed. After the Secretary sought dismissal, counsel promptly amended and opposed dismissal. The Secretary had exhausted a response extension before moving. The Government did not argue that relation back would prejudice it. This is an account of its litigation position, not an adjudicated universal absence-of-prejudice rule.
