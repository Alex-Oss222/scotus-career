## Rule, record and remedy retained

Section 2518(4) permits necessary technical assistance only when performed unobtrusively and with a minimum of interference with the services supplied to the person intercepted. The least disruptive feasible method does not meet that condition when it displaces the ordinary contracted information, roadside and emergency-assistance functions and substitutes an unassured signal to investigators for the provider's emergency response. The rule allows incidental interruption; it does not require zero interference.

The expiration exception rests on both predicates already established: a thirty-day order cannot undergo full review before expiry, and the Government anticipates further requests involving this same provider. The provider is within the assistance class. The particular order nevertheless fails its independent execution condition. Affirm the appellate reversal. A later request must satisfy the statute on its actual technology and record.

New York Telephone retains its All Writs scope; Bach retains its valid-warrant technical-execution scope.
