## Shared legal propositions and disposition

All eight Associates would reverse the Ninth Circuit and remand to implement rejection of this instructional habeas claim, restoring the District Court's denial on this ground. Each supports the same sufficient statutory proposition: the state court's contextual determination falls within the objectively reasonable range allowed by the governing whole-charge rule, so section 2254(d)(1) bars relief on this claim. This is a judgment and rationale coalition among the eight Associates, not a statement of the final Court vote.

Cupp requires examination of the instruction in its full setting; Boyde supplies reasonable likelihood, and Estelle adopts that standard for an asserted instructional bypass of elemental proof. Williams keeps contrary-rule and unreasonable-application review separate; Visciotti requires examination of substance rather than isolated language or formal citation; Andrade preserves the substantive-law cutoff. The state court neither stated a rule requiring the fear to be reasonable nor ignored the actual contradiction. Its reading rests on the repeated, emphatic actual-but-unreasonable distinction, the express reasonable-person contrast, and the surrounding explanation. Those features support a contextual judgment rather than an automatic cure from the number of correct sentences.

The strongest competing path treats the erroneous definition as a controlling qualification of every reference to imminent peril, thereby withdrawing the very defense the charge otherwise states. Such a reading could satisfy the constitutional reasonable-likelihood inquiry and, if the state court's contrary assessment were objectively indefensible, the statutory gateway. Here the direct statement that the defense applies even when a reasonable person would not share the belief provides a reasonable basis for the state interpretation. Ordinary instructional error, a plausible competing reading, and independent disagreement therefore do not suffice. No alternate harmlessness determination is necessary to the statutory rejection.
