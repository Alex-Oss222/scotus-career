## Neutral issue for affected independent assessment

If the legal method begins with prime and adjusts upward for plan risk, which party must produce evidence supporting the adjustment? One supported path places the initial evidentiary responsibility for a requested increase on the creditor, because lender knowledge of repayment and collateral markets may exceed the debtor's information. The competing path retains the plan proponent's ordinary responsibility to establish statutory confirmation conditions, including adequate present value, and requires supported risk findings without making creditor silence proof of adequacy. The bankruptcy filings, opportunity for both parties to present evidence, and distinction between production and ultimate persuasion require separate consideration. Neither path permits a rate inadequate to preserve the allowed secured claim or dispenses with the independent feasibility condition.

These are analytical alternatives, not assertions about a party's actual filing, preservation, concession or conduct. This receipt selects neither a rule nor a Justice's position. It introduces no historical Court holding. The exact role of prime, deferred cash versus a distributed note, and the need for findings remain for the affected independent stage under the existing statutory question.
