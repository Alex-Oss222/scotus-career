This is a statutory-source handoff, not an adjudication. Both saved official source texts were read in full during neutral validation. The 2002 Code text supplies subsection (g)(1) through (g)(4), preserving the distinct grounds for suit, distinct remedial provisions and complete damages formula. ## 5 U.S.C. section 552a(g)(1)–(4), 2002 edition

Source: [official Code PDF](../sources/chunk4/privacy-code-2002.pdf), page 39; [saved text](../sources/chunk4/privacy-code-2002.txt), lines 270–349, right column. The column was selected by script; source wording and line-break hyphenation are unchanged. The selection includes subsection (g)(1)(D) and the complete subsection (g)(4), with the surrounding civil remedies for context.

The comparison issue remains the statutory relationship among adverse effect, intentional or willful action, actual damages, a person entitled to recovery, and the minimum amount. This source handoff adopts no construction. The frozen neutral packet and MacArthur entering law continue to identify the granted issue and the separate reserved definition of compensable actual damages.

## Verification and source boundary

The 2002 Code source has 1,074 text lines and the original Act source has 1,139 text lines; both were read through the end, including their adjacent source-page material. Their notes show the original civil-remedy enactment and the later amendment to subsection (g)(5)'s effective-date wording, not a replacement of the selected subsection (g)(4) formula. The original Act's section 5 study direction was checked in the complete statutory context. No target Supreme Court opinion or target result was opened in preparing this handoff. This supplement is ready for the fresh independent case 45 modeling context.
