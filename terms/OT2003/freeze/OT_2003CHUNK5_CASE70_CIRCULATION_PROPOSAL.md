# Vieth proposed public formulation for isolated compatibility audit

This is an uncommitted formulation for a source-grounded compatibility audit. It supplies no vote, assignment or adopted Court rule. The audit receives the frozen neutral record, commitments and comparator but no private position supplement.

## Proposed common equal-protection holding

A voter has a cognizable constitutional claim when electoral boundaries materially impair an existing or demonstrably attainable opportunity for effective political organization, participation, nomination or election because officials substantially seek to suppress identifiable political opposition and preserve governing power against electoral challenge. The claimant must establish the identifiable opportunity, a substantial practical obstruction to it, the prohibited purpose and material causation by the challenged assignments. Ordinary political disadvantage, partisan composition, unusual shape, a projected seat disparity or a desire for a more favorable map does not alone establish injury. The claim concerns an opportunity to undertake political activity; a lost election or prior electoral victory is unnecessary to an injury independently proved at organization, participation or nomination.

The claimant proves these elements by a preponderance. The State may establish by the same standard that materially the same lawful assignment would have occurred without the prohibited objective. The defense requires an actual independent account of the decision, including genuinely applied lawful requirements or an independently justified and consistently applied fairness policy. A merely conceivable explanation is insufficient. Relief corrects the proved deprivation and gives the State the first opportunity to draw lawful corrective boundaries.

## Comparison formulation requiring specific audit

An independently justified lawful comparison is a means of identifying the protected opportunity and distinguishing the boundaries' contribution from geography, electoral competition and lawful constraints. The benchmark cannot be chosen solely because it produces the claimant's preferred partisan allocation. Evidence may include a feasible alternative district, actual prior political activity, the operation of the challenged reassignment and other supported causal comparisons. No particular drawing, fixed map count, factor list or election history is invariably required. The court must explain the actual legal and factual basis of the comparison it uses.

This formulation preserves a comparative injury-and-causation inquiry but does not impose production of a specific alternative map as a universally necessary element. The exact question is whether the latter qualification fits Souter's and Ginsburg's frozen insistence on an independently grounded lawful comparison and their date-eligible writings, or materially changes a necessary condition they retain. A possible inference must be supported by each Justice's actual sources; the usefulness of a coalition supplies no reason to accept it. Apply the same test to Stevens and Breyer and preserve any substantive additional objection. Kennedy's present insufficiency position is not changed by this proposal.

## Scope and disposition

The proposed remand concerns the live Act 34 equal-protection claim, personal standing and any amendment permitted through ordinary procedure. The existing map remains operative pending properly supported relief. An independent First Amendment claim is not deemed preserved. There is no direct Article I entitlement to a delegation-wide proportional or majority seat allocation. The proposed rule neither establishes actual liability nor substitutes the stayed, malapportioned Act 1 map as a lawful benchmark.
