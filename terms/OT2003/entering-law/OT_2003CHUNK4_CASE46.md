#### Zicherman v. Korean Air Lines Co., Ltd.; Korean Air Lines Co., Ltd. v. Zicherman, 516 U.S. 217

**Docket or dockets:** Nos. 94-1361 and 94-1477.

**Decided:** January 16, 1996.

##### The Convention leaves compensable loss to applicable domestic law

**Holding:** The Warsaw Convention's reference to damage does not supply an independent, comprehensive code identifying every compensable loss or beneficiary. Domestic law supplies those matters, subject to the Convention's own conditions and limits; the parties' accepted use of United States law permits decision here without choosing a general international conflicts rule.

**Proposition-level authority:** Opinion of the Court (O'Connor), unanimous.

**Limits and questions not reached:** No general international choice-of-law rule or determination of Convention exclusivity in every setting is announced. Removing a Convention liability cap does not create a category of loss excluded by applicable domestic law.

**Treatment of earlier authority:** Saks and Floyd are applied for treaty interpretation bounded by the Convention's actual conditions; neither supplies a general damages code.

##### DOHSA excludes loss-of-society damages for both relatives

**Holding:** When United States law governs damages for a death on the high seas beyond a marine league from a United States shore, within the Death on the High Seas Act's geographical field, the Act's restriction to pecuniary loss excludes loss-of-society recovery. Dependency cannot revive that excluded category, and general maritime law cannot supplement the Act to permit it.

**Proposition-level authority:** Opinion of the Court (O'Connor), unanimous.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Other properly preserved damages remain undisturbed. The Court decides no general dependency rule, survival claim, predeath suffering claim, mental-distress claim, beneficiary-standing issue or jury-trial issue.

**Operative remedy or transition:** Affirm exclusion of the mother's loss-of-society claim; reverse the sister's dependency-based remand. Remand for a conforming damages judgment, without vacating carrier liability or unrelated award components.

**Treatment of earlier authority:** Higginbotham and Offshore Logistics govern the statutory exclusion; Miles and Yamaha preserve its field. Gaudet is distinguished by its territorial setting, and American Dredging by its procedural subject.

#### El Al Israel Airlines, Ltd. v. Tsui Yuan Tseng

**Docket or dockets:** No. 97-475
**Decided:** 1999-01-12.

##### Part I — Local law cannot avoid the Convention's conditions within its covered field

**Holding:** When a passenger's personal-injury occurrence falls within Article 17's temporal and substantive field, Article 24 subjects an action against the carrier, whatever its legal foundation, to the Convention's conditions and limits. Failure to satisfy the accident or bodily-injury condition does not itself remove the occurrence from that field or permit an independent local damages action; occurrences outside that field remain outside this holding.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part I of O'Connor's Opinion of the Court has eight direct joins: Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer and controls this rule and its application.

##### Part II — The Article 25 argument does not change the result on this record

**Holding:** The asserted Article 25 route cannot alter the disposition where the trial's finding of no treaty willful misconduct remains undisturbed. Intentional touching alone does not establish that misconduct; this application decides neither contested preservation nor Article 25's full effect when its misconduct predicate is proved.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part II has seven direct Associate joins: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Stone-Zsela does not join it.

**Treatment of earlier authority:**

- **Air France v. Saks, 470 U.S. 392:** Applied to distinguish the external event from injury; its flexible accident rule is unchanged and the classification of this search is not decided.
- **Eastern Airlines, Inc. v. Floyd, 499 U.S. 530:** Applied for Article 17's bodily-injury requirement. Its reserved local-law question is now answered within the treaty's covered personal-injury field.
- **Zicherman v. Korean Air Lines Co., Ltd.:** Preserved as allowing domestic loss categories and beneficiaries subject to treaty conditions; its reserved general-exclusivity issue is resolved in the stated field. It supplies no domestic liability despite a failed treaty condition.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Within Article 17's actual personal-injury field, a claimant cannot obtain local damages merely because the Convention's accident or bodily-injury condition is unsatisfied. Domestic law continues to supply losses and beneficiaries where permitted by Zicherman, subject to treaty liability conditions. Outside-field occurrences, baggage questions outside this review, and Article 25's full operation on proved willfulness remain unresolved here.

**Operative remedy or transition:** **Reversed and remanded, 8–1.**

**Supporting:** Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer.

**Opposing:** Justice Stevens.

The New York personal-injury claim must be dismissed. The separate baggage award remains undisturbed; the Court orders no further tort trial or baggage relief.

### Domestic compensable loss under the Warsaw Convention

**Governing question:** The source of damage categories and beneficiary rights in a Warsaw Convention action.

**Current rule:** The Convention’s damage language does not supply an independent comprehensive code of compensable loss or beneficiaries. Applicable domestic law supplies those matters subject to the Convention’s conditions and limits. Uniform carrier-liability conditions do not require a judicially created uniform damages code, and lifting a Convention cap does not create a category domestic law excludes.

**Authority by component:**

- Zicherman — Convention and domestic-loss rules, 1996-01-16; O’Connor for all nine.

**Present operation effective:** Zicherman, January 16, 1996.

**Open question:** The holding assumes the parties’ accepted use of United States law and supplies no general international conflicts rule or comprehensive decision on treaty exclusivity. “Territorial-water death remedies and the exclusive high-seas field” states the independently governing DOHSA exclusion.

### Warsaw Convention exclusivity within the Article 17 field

**Governing question:** Whether local damages law can avoid unsatisfied Convention conditions for a covered passenger injury occurrence.

**Current rule:** When a passenger’s personal-injury occurrence lies within Article 17’s temporal and substantive field, Article 24 subjects the carrier action, whatever its legal foundation, to the Convention’s conditions and limits. Failure to satisfy the accident or bodily-injury condition does not itself remove the occurrence from that field or allow an independent local damages action. Domestic law may identify compensable losses and beneficiaries subject to treaty conditions; it cannot create liability simply because a treaty condition fails.

- Field-based exclusivity and distinction between conditions and coverage: Canonical Decision Record `el_al_israel_airlines_v_tseng_merits_1999-01-12.md`, Public Projection / Holdings / Part I — Local law cannot avoid the Convention’s conditions within its covered field, 1999-01-12; eight direct joins.
- Intentional touching alone does not establish Article 25 willful misconduct: the same Record, Public Projection / Holdings / Part II — The Article 25 argument does not change the result on this record, 1999-01-12; O'Connor with Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer, seven direct Associate joins. Stone-Zsela does not join Part II.

**Present operation effective:** 1999-01-12.

**Exceptions and limits:** Events outside covered embarking, carriage or disembarking remain outside this rule. Article 25’s full operation when willful misconduct is proved remains open; intentional touching alone does not establish that predicate. No categorical rule about every physical manifestation of emotional injury or every intentional accident is adopted.
