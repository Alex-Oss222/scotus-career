## 29. Verizon Communications Inc. v. Law Offices of Curtis V. Trinko, LLP

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, unanimous in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for six Justices. Stevens, joined by Souter and Thomas, concurred in the judgment on antitrust-standing grounds.
* **Historical Holding:** The complaint failed under historical §2 refusal-to-deal principles; the saving clause preserved established antitrust standards without creating a new claim.
* **Historical Authority:** Verizon Communications Inc. v. Law Offices of Curtis V. Trinko, LLP, No. 02-682; 540 U.S. 398.
* **Historical Sources:** Verizon Communications Inc. v. Law Offices of Curtis V. Trinko, LLP, No. 02-682; 540 U.S. 398.

## 30. Illinois v. Lidster

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded. All nine agreed that Edmond did not automatically invalidate the checkpoint; six sustained its reasonableness.

* **Historical Sources:** Illinois v. Lidster, No. 02-1060; 540 U.S. 419.

## 37. Lamie v. United States Trustee

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9-0, January 26, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote the Court’s opinion; Scalia joined except for Part III. Stevens concurred in the judgment, joined by Souter and Breyer, who also joined the Court’s opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** The amended provision did not authorize estate compensation for a Chapter 7 debtor’s attorney unless employed under §327.
* **Historical Authority:** Lamie v. United States Trustee, No. 02-693; 540 U.S. 526.
* **Historical Sources:** Lamie v. United States Trustee, No. 02-693; 540 U.S. 526.
