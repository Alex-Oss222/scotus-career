## 58. South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 8 to 1 on the disposition, March 23, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote the Court’s opinion. The rejection of a pollutant-origin requirement was unanimous; Scalia dissented from the broader remand while concurring in that source ruling.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court rejected the origin requirement, remanded the distinct-water factual question, and left the unitary-waters theory open on remand.
* **Historical Authority:** South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians, No. 02-626; 541 U.S. 95.
* **Historical Sources:** South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians, No. 02-626; 541 U.S. 95.

## 60. Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 8 to 1, March 24, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for six Justices; Scalia, joined by Thomas, concurred in the judgment. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 253 did not preempt the Missouri limitation on its municipalities’ telecommunications authority; the historical majority also relied on the absence of an unmistakably clear congressional direction.
* **Historical Authority:** Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League, Nos. 02-1238, 02-1386 and 02-1405; 541 U.S. 125.
* **Historical Sources:** Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League, Nos. 02-1238, 02-1386 and 02-1405; 541 U.S. 125.

## 70. Vieth v. Jubelirer

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4, without a five-Justice categorical nonjusticiability rationale.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia’s four-Justice plurality would overrule Bandemer; Kennedy concurred in the judgment while reserving future manageable standards. Stevens, Souter joined by Ginsburg, and Breyer dissented separately.
* **Historical Holding:** The Pennsylvania challenge failed. Four Justices would make partisan-gerrymandering claims nonjusticiable; Kennedy declined that categorical conclusion.
* **Historical Authority:** Vieth v. Jubelirer, No. 02-1580; 541 U.S. 267.
* **Historical Sources:** Vieth v. Jubelirer, No. 02-1580; 541 U.S. 267.
