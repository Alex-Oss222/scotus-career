# EDC incorporated small-construction waiver: neutral exact-text supplement

The source below is the complete operative 40 C.F.R. §122.26(b)(15), including the incorporated paragraph (i), paragraph (ii)'s designation category and the accompanying summary exhibit. It is copied by script from the saved official December 8, 1999 rule, 64 Fed. Reg. 68722–68851, document 99-29181, `runtime/B_sources/FR_1999_12_08_99_29181.txt`. Source URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1999-12-08/html/99-29181.htm. The source states effectiveness on February 7, 2000; its exact Dates paragraph is also copied below. This is the historical rule text incorporated by the supplied EDC provision, not a later regulatory revision.

The waiver is permissive for the Director. Its two alternatives concern the rainfall erosivity factor with operator certification, or an approved or established TMDL or qualifying non-impaired-water equivalent analysis with the required operator certification. Their full predicates, the pollutant definition, the construction-size/common-plan coverage, and the routine-maintenance exclusion are in the exact text. No fact is supplied that a particular site meets either route or has received a waiver.

Section 122.34(b)(4)(i)'s exemption from the municipal construction-site program follows only if the permitting authority waives the construction-discharge requirements in accordance with the incorporated provision. This is distinct from the alternative individual-permit route under section 122.33(b)(2)(ii) and from the separate exceptions for particular non-stormwater flows. No waiver is converted into an obligation to grant one, and no statutory or regulatory exception is inferred merely from a site's small size.
