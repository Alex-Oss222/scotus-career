## Genuine alternative: the controlling distinction and source precision

Condon distinguishes regulating the government's own activity from directing it to regulate others. New York requires a lawful alternative to compulsory state responsibility. Lomont requires examination of actual legal consequences and operation rather than an optional label. Its unconditional refusal rule addressed assistance to private applicants; it creates no right to refuse valid control of a municipal operator's own pollution.

The different-program provision permits use of section 122.26(d)'s individual application. It allows omission of the specified legal-authority information unless the applicant wants the permit writer to consider it in developing other conditions. That is permission to omit, not a requirement to withhold. Section 122.34(a) expressly excepts that application route from its listed minimum controls. Neither provision guarantees a particular final permit or eliminates all information requirements. The City of Abilene experience is record-based evidence of availability, not controlling law or proof concerning every future permit.

The strongest contrary path recognizes that the listed measures contemplate local ordinances, inspection and enforcement and argues that the alternative is too demanding to be real. The first premise is accepted, but the supplied actual route addresses a different program and the existing record does not establish a universal compulsory-third-party condition. Expense and inconvenience of valid own-discharge controls differ from conscription. Conversely, a concrete final permit that imposes the same third-party administration cannot be sustained solely by its individual-permit label; the present judgment supplies no advance approval of it.

## Speech: meaningful independent justification, not automatic NEMA transfer

The public education provision permits distribution of educational materials or equivalent outreach about stormwater effects and public pollution-reduction steps. It does not demand both methods. The distinct illicit-discharge provision requires information to public employees, businesses and the public about illegal-discharge and improper-disposal hazards. These actual messages and their physical operating purpose, rather than general support for environmental protection, define the holding.

NEMA permits truthful, noncontroversial commercial product and disposal facts on its stated justification and burden conditions. It does not establish that a municipality's general outreach is commercial product speech. The eight Associates knowingly adopt a confined operational application here on additional actual grounds: a valid physical discharge-control program exists independently of communication; community inputs and disposal affect the system's discharge; the required information directly addresses those effects and practical reduction; the rule prescribes no ideological profession and permits meaningful choice of factual means; actual justification and undue burdens remain reviewable. Calling information factual is not by itself sufficient.

The operation may identify the municipality as the information source. No invented nonattribution guarantee supplies the result. Factual information concerning the operator's own system differs from compelled adoption of a political or ideological judgment. Glickman's particular private-subsidy and attribution conditions are not erased or treated as a literal test for a different direct-information duty. United Foods requires an independent function and prevents the selected message from justifying its own compulsory support; the physical pollution controls supply the distinct function here. Barnette and Wooley continue to protect against ideological affirmation.

The strongest contrary path notes that direct communication is speech, potentially more intrusive than a fee, and that even a lawful program cannot compel unrelated advocacy. All eight accept those premises. They reject facial invalidation because the defined duties concern the actual regulated system's impacts and harmful inputs, with independent physical justification, limited factual content, practical fit and flexibility. The provisions require no endorsement of EPA's policy judgments and restrict no contrary criticism. Existing-material options bear on burden without establishing that all implementation is cheap. There is no supported facial showing of an unjustified or undue burden on the actual limited communication. A materially different message or burden must satisfy the same real limits, not be deemed valid by today's outcome.
