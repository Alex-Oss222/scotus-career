# West Virginia: correction of the section 1396c helper description

The earlier B source addendum's explanatory phrase about confining **withholding** to unaffected plan categories reverses the direction of the quoted statute. The exact statutory quotation was correct and is unchanged. For all further use, replace that helper description with the following:

**Section 1396c permits the Secretary, in discretion, to limit continued payments to categories under or parts of the State plan not affected by the failure, instead of making no further payments to the State. It does not describe withholding payments from the unaffected categories.**

The complete conditions are:

- The Secretary must give reasonable notice and an opportunity for hearing to the State agency administering or supervising the approved plan.
- The Secretary must find either that the plan has been changed so that it no longer complies with section 1396a, or that administration of the plan fails to comply substantially with such a provision.
- Upon that finding, the Secretary shall notify the State agency that further payments will not be made, or, in the Secretary's discretion, that payments will be limited to categories or parts not affected by the failure.
- That payment cessation or limitation continues until the Secretary is satisfied that there will no longer be any such failure to comply. Until then, the Secretary shall make no further payments or shall limit payments to unaffected categories or parts, as stated in the statute. The statutory stopping condition ends on that satisfaction; the quoted section supplies no separate retroactive-payment amount, new payment date or automatic administrative-funds-only ceiling.

The less extensive alternative remains discretionary; the text does not require selecting it or establish which categories would qualify in a particular case. No notice, hearing, finding, selected sanction, satisfied condition or actual payment is newly found here.

This is a correction to the helper prose only, not a new legal rule, decision, default or blocker. Earlier frozen artifacts remain preserved.
