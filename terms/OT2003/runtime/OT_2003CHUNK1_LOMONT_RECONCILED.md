## Reconciled rule and direct joins

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer each join both propositions and affirmance in full:

**Official injury.** An officer may challenge a concrete burden directed at his own office by the federal certification arrangement without separate authorization to represent the State. The own-office work and demands, their connection to the specific federal arrangement and relief removing that arrangement supply the stake here. Official title or abstract opposition to policy does not suffice. Whether the alleged federal obligation is ultimately compulsory is the distinct merits inquiry, and an adverse merits answer does not retroactively eliminate a concrete controversy. The officials do not obtain standing from the applicants' separate difficulty securing a certification. Because the officials support the same requested relief, no separate private-applicant standing determination is required.

**Unconditional refusal.** Requiring a private federal applicant to obtain a local certification does not commandeer the local officer when federal law leaves refusal unconditional and imposes no penalty, mandatory investigation or substitute compulsory task on the officer or office. The supplied arrangement satisfies that condition. Constituent pressure and political unpopularity do not turn the applicant's federal requirement into an official federal duty when the officer retains an actual freedom to refuse. The rule sustains these historical certification regulations against the presented Tenth Amendment claim; it does not authorize compelled investigations, create a compensation rule or cure an independent statutory-authority defect.

The judgment is affirmed. The challenged arrangement remains effective against this attack, with the same unconditional option to decline that supports its validity. There is no injunction, fiscal proceeding, severance, mandatory new hearing or merits remand.
