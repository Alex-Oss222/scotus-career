## 66. United States v. Lara

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for five; Stevens also concurred; Kennedy and Thomas separately concurred in the judgment; Souter dissented with Scalia.
* **Historical Holding:** Congress could recognize inherent criminal jurisdiction over nonmember Indians; successive federal prosecution was not barred as a second exercise of the same sovereign power.
* **Historical Authority:** United States v. Lara, No. 03-107; 541 U.S. 193.
* **Historical Sources:** United States v. Lara, No. 03-107; 541 U.S. 193.
