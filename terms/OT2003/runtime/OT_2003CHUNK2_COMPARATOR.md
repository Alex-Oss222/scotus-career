## 20. McConnell v. Federal Election Commission

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed in part and reversed in part. The principal soft-money and electioneering rulings were 5-4; there was no single vote governing every provision. The forced choice and minors prohibition were invalidated.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens and O’Connor jointly wrote on Titles I and II, joined by Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Rehnquist wrote on Titles III and IV with varying joins. Breyer wrote the five-Justice Title V opinion. Scalia, Thomas, Kennedy, Rehnquist and Stevens wrote separate opinions addressing different components.
* **Historical Holding:** The principal party-money and electioneering restrictions survived, along with disclosure and broadcaster records; §213 and §318 failed, and specified challenges were nonjusticiable.
* **Historical Authority:** McConnell v. Federal Election Commission, 540 U.S. 93; Nos. 02-1674, 02-1675, 02-1676, 02-1702, 02-1727, 02-1733, 02-1734, 02-1740, 02-1747, 02-1753, 02-1755, and 02-1756.
* **Historical Sources:** McConnell v. Federal Election Commission, 540 U.S. 93; Nos. 02-1674, 02-1675, 02-1676, 02-1702, 02-1727, 02-1733, 02-1734, 02-1740, 02-1747, 02-1753, 02-1755, and 02-1756.
