## 82. Grupo Dataflux v. Atlas Global Group, L.P.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 30, 2003, subject to any controlling decision effective before this case's event date.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand for dismissal for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Vacate the jurisdictionally unsupported federal merits disposition; dismiss without a federal merits judgment.
* **Remedial Reach:** This federal action and its verdict. No determination of the underlying state-law debt or of the timeliness of another authorized action.
* **Practical Relief:** The district court must enter a jurisdictional dismissal and remove any judgment depending on the unsupported verdict. Any renewed action must establish its own jurisdiction and limitations compliance; the verdict is not preserved by this mandate.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Time of filing and the limited cure doctrine

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The preserved dispute permits this legal determination; no missing merits finding is assumed.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether a partnership’s post-filing change in membership cures the absence of complete diversity at filing when the same partnership remains a party.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the time-of-filing rule and preserve Caterpillar’s actual party-dismissal limit.
* **Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The continuing party’s citizenship change presents the necessary distinction. Eliminating every recognized cure would overrule an established holding unnecessarily. Preserving this verdict solely because trial occurred would make expenditure a source of judicial power.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A post-filing change in a partnership’s membership does not cure diversity absent at commencement when the partnership remains the same party throughout the action. The party invoking federal jurisdiction must establish the necessary citizenship; later membership changes do not satisfy that burden. Caterpillar’s lawful dismissal of a nondiverse party is a different cure and does not save this action.
* **Reach Within the Question:** Changes in citizenship of continuing parties, including partnerships; lawful dismissal of a dispensable party remains governed by its separate requirements.
* **Next-Case Guidance:** None.
* **Transition:** Not applicable.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Caterpillar preserved a judgment after the jurisdictional party was removed; it did not make all defects harmless whenever diversity exists before trial. Carden identifies Atlas’s citizenship through its partners but does not turn those partners into separately named litigants whose dismissal cured the complaint. The original absence of jurisdiction therefore remains decisive.
* **Precedent Treatment:** Caterpillar Inc. v. Lewis is distinguished and its cure confined to its actual rule. Carden v. Arkoma Associates is applied.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** None.
