## 47. Banks v. Dretke

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, with a 7-2 division on the Farr sentence-relief analysis; the Cook appellate issue drew all nine Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote the Court’s opinion. Thomas, joined by Scalia, concurred in the Cook portion and dissented from the Farr materiality determination.
* **Historical Holding:** Concealment and reasonable reliance excused the Farr default, whose suppression was material; the Court also required proper appellate consideration of the Cook claim.
* **Historical Authority:** Banks v. Dretke, No. 02-8286; 540 U.S. 668.
* **Historical Sources:** Banks v. Dretke, No. 02-8286; 540 U.S. 668.

## 54. Crawford v. Washington

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously in judgment, March 8, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for seven Justices. Rehnquist, joined by O’Connor, concurred in the judgment and opposed replacement of the received reliability framework.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court required unavailability and a prior opportunity for cross-examination for testimonial statements, including this police interrogation, and rejected reliability as its substitute.
* **Historical Authority:** Crawford v. Washington, No. 02-9410; 541 U.S. 36.
* **Historical Sources:** Crawford v. Washington, No. 02-9410; 541 U.S. 36.

## 55. Iowa v. Tovar

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, March 8, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote the unanimous opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** Neither specified warning was invariably required by the Sixth Amendment; the historical Court applied a stage-sensitive understanding requirement and rejected the Iowa court’s mandatory formulation.
* **Historical Authority:** Iowa v. Tovar, No. 02-1541; 541 U.S. 77.
* **Historical Sources:** Iowa v. Tovar, No. 02-1541; 541 U.S. 77.
