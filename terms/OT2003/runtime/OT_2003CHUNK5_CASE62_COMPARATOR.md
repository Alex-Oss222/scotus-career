## 62. United States v. Flores-Montano

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, March 30, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote the unanimous opinion; Breyer added a concurrence.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held that the fuel-tank removal and reassembly required no reasonable suspicion and reserved materially destructive or offensive methods.
* **Historical Authority:** United States v. Flores-Montano, No. 02-1794; 541 U.S. 149.
* **Historical Sources:** United States v. Flores-Montano, No. 02-1794; 541 U.S. 149.
