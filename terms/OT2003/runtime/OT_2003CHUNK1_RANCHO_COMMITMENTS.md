## Record and separable decisional questions

The proposed 202-acre housing development in San Diego County threatens the arroyo toad; the species' range is intrastate and the development's commercial character is established. The court below sustained the application by treating the commercial housing project as the regulated activity. Those facts do not themselves establish an interstate wildlife market, cross-border ecological consequences, a mechanism of market evasion or a need for uniform federal species protection.

The questions for the Court are: (1) whether the commercial-project ground can sustain this application consistently with Home Builders's enacted-class holding; (2) whether an eligible, materially supported alternative mechanism permits affirmance; and (3) whether the proper consequence of the defective inquiry is a bounded remand or final relief against the challenged application. The record description does not establish exhaustion of the entire relevant legislative and adjudicative record. Its brevity is neither evidence against the Government nor permission to invent the required connection.

The case presents an as-applied commerce challenge. It does not present facial invalidation of all species protection or an adjudicated entitlement to a section 10 permit. Sweet Home's actual injury, proximate causation and ordinary foreseeability requirements remain operative statutory law. A commerce determination cannot itself establish an actual taking, its imminent occurrence or entitlement to incidental-take permission.

## Proposed common remand propositions

**1. The lower court used an insufficient ground.** Under Home Builders, section 9's enacted class is the taking of protected wildlife, including the habitat injury the statute reaches. The commercial attributes of the particular housing development do not by themselves establish that class's constitutionally eligible connection to interstate commerce. The lower judgment therefore cannot stand on its commercial-project ground. Neither an as-applied label nor the project's actual commercial purpose removes the governing class inquiry.

**2. The established alternative mechanisms remain available on their actual predicates.** The Government may sustain this challenged application by materially supporting either that inclusion of the relevant local taking class is reasonably necessary to prevent evasion or material impairment of an identified valid interstate regulatory regime, or that the class generates a substantial interstate externality that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize and the federal prohibition reasonably fits that demonstrated cross-border effect. The externality route requires an identified concrete cross-border ecological, commercial or comparable effect, substantiality beyond speculation or general national interest, a supported reason ordinary territorial authority cannot reasonably contain or internalize it, and a reasonable fit between the taking prohibition and that effect.

The Government bears responsibility for identifying and supporting its invoked mechanism with a materially adequate legislative or adjudicative basis. Courts independently determine constitutional eligibility and reasonable fit. Congress receives substantial latitude concerning supported predictions, magnitude and practical design once the eligible mechanism exists. Neither universal formal findings, a fixed numerical threshold, defendant-specific congressional findings nor clear-and-convincing proof is required. Aggregation measures magnitude after the connection is established; it does not create that connection.

**3. This record posture requires the Home Builders remand.** The lower court's incorrect commercial-project ground leaves the supported-mechanism inquiry unperformed at the required scope. The supplied materials do not establish an alternative sufficient to affirm or a complete-record failure requiring final invalidation. Vacate the sustaining judgment and remand for examination of the relevant existing legislative and adjudicative record under the full rule. If that record supports an eligible mechanism and the necessary fit, the application may be sustained on that identified ground. If it cannot, the lower court must grant relief confined to the unsupported application actually challenged. It cannot preserve the original judgment by repeating the project's commercial attributes or invoking national importance alone.

**4. Holston supports the method, not a transferred factual premise.** Holston sustained local production because it created the reproducible supply of the identified prohibited interstate image market. The identity between the produced material and that prohibited supply supported the anti-evasion inference. Nothing in this packet establishes that Rancho's taking creates or retains comparable prohibited interstate stock. A valid necessary-inclusion analogy must identify and support the relationship specific to the wildlife regime; the statute's breadth and a general conservation objective do not supply it.

## Enhanced alternative-path comparison

### Economic application versus the enacted class

The strongest affirmance ground is not that Rancho purchases tools or materials in commerce. It is that housing construction and habitat conversion are the conduct directly regulated in this application, making the application an economic class under Wickard, Perez and Hodel. Stevens and Souter retain precisely that position from Home Builders, informed by Sweet Home's identification of habitat modification as the injury-causing conduct. They do not need to invent a market in toads or an ecological effect across a State line.

The six remand supporters answer that the same distinction was presented in Home Builders and rejected by its controlling Part I: the enacted prohibition covers taking without making commercial development its defining condition. A court cannot substitute the economically convenient subset for the class Congress enacted. That answer does not deny the project's commercial character or treat it as an owner's collateral expenditure; it holds that the fact cannot independently supply this statute's constitutional connection. An as-applied challenge remains available, but the asserted mechanism must fit the enacted prohibition and the actual application.

For Stevens and Souter, this is an express renewal of their legal disagreement, not a claim that their old dissent became law or that Home Builders is distinguishable on an invented fact. They would reconsider that class holding insofar as it bars reliance on the injury-producing economic activity and affirm at this limited scope. The principal objection is the force of settled directly applicable precedent. Their response is their source-grounded view that the class rule mistakenly prevents ordinary regulation of actual economic habitat conversion. They do not offer a new empirical premise or claim Holston overruled Home Builders. Their choice remains a minority position unless the necessary Court coalition expressly adopts it; it cannot silently enter the controlling opinion.

### Final sustainment on another mechanism versus bounded remand

A supported anti-evasion or substantial-externality ground could permit affirmance even though the lower court relied on the wrong rationale. Holston confirms that a concrete source-and-regime relationship may be established without a defendant's individual interstate transaction. But this packet supplies no analogous toad-market relationship, ecological spillover or territorial-control fact. Neither a comprehensive species-protection law nor biodiversity's general value proves an eligible mechanism. Accordingly, no Associate commits to affirming on a newly found alternative mechanism.

The strongest objection to remand is that the Government already had an opportunity to defend the application and the grounds summarized here are insufficient. That does not establish the content or exhaustion of the complete relevant record. Home Builders directs an actual inquiry when the lower judgments have not performed it. Remand performs that existing judicial task; it is not a finding that an adequate mechanism exists or an order to invent and develop new grounds indefinitely. The instruction concerns the relevant existing legislative and adjudicative record and ordinary lawful proceedings on the preserved challenge. No special discovery entitlement or permission to fabricate evidence follows.

### Confined invalidation versus a complete-record inquiry

The developer's intrastate-range argument identifies an important factual limit but cannot establish categorical immunity: both Home Builders mechanisms can reach local noneconomic conduct when their actual predicates are supported. Conversely, the absence of a commerce element in section 9 is not cured by judicial insertion or by the project's commercial attributes. Final relief against this application would be warranted if the complete relevant record supplied no legally sufficient connection. That premise is not established by the supplied record description.

Scalia and Thomas's earlier Home Builders preference for confined relief does not mechanically dictate the same disposition here. At that decision they opposed the Court's newly announced independent-externality route and its chosen remand. In this later case the route and remand inquiry are established law, while the current packet supplies no exhaustive record determination. Their supported choices are to apply that existing disposition rule or affirmatively reopen it; neither supplies a factual finding of whole-record failure. They choose the former, retaining their underlying objection as a reservation. Their recent unanimous Holston join reinforces that an actually demonstrated necessary-regime relationship must receive consideration; it does not prove such a relationship for these toads.

**O'Connor.** She joined all Home Builders controlling parts and Kennedy's concurrence, as well as the complete Holston opinion she delivered. Her consistent ground requires a supported actual mechanism, with the externality route confined by ordinary territorial-control limits. She joins all four remand propositions. She refuses an opinion asserting that all species losses necessarily spill across borders, that a conservation statute's comprehensiveness establishes necessity, or that the housing project's economic character decides the class question. The full existing standard answers her objection without a new separate opinion.

**Scalia.** He joined Home Builders's class and regime holdings and, with Thomas, rejected its original externality rationale. **Kennedy.** His Home Builders concurrence with O'Connor makes the cross-border character and limits of ordinary territorial authority essential. **Souter.** His authorship of Sweet Home and join in Stevens's Home Builders dissent support treating actual habitat-changing construction as the economic conduct in this application. **Thomas.** His Home Builders joins and dissent track Scalia's stated class/regime and externality distinctions. ## Precedent treatment and operative remand

- Home Builders is applied directly: the enacted taking class, rejection of commercial-project features as a sufficient substitute, two actual eligible mechanisms, support responsibility, independent constitutional inquiry, reasonable fit and bounded remand retain their existing force. The proposed controlling application overrules no portion. Stevens and Souter's contrary proposal remains separate.
- Lopez supplies connection before magnitude, fit to the enacted class and the limit on judicially added elements. The Court neither supplies a commerce element for section 9 nor treats the absence of one as automatic invalidity.
- Holston applies the same necessity method to an actually supported image-supply relationship. It is distinguished factually; it establishes no toad supply, wildlife market or ecological spillover.
- Hale and Kirk retain supported necessary inclusion of local conduct within an identified valid regime. Their specific prohibited-stock relationship cannot be presumed for habitat injury.
- Wickard and Perez retain aggregation within properly connected economic classes. Under the governing Home Builders rule, they do not create the missing connection through the housing project's commercial character.
- Sweet Home retains statutory habitat-caused injury, proximate causation, ordinary foreseeability and distinct conditional permissions. It identifies what the taking prohibition reaches but supplies no independent commerce power or permit entitlement in this proceeding.

Vacate the D.C. Circuit judgment insofar as it sustains this challenged application on the insufficient commercial-project rationale. Remand through that court for the lower courts to examine the relevant existing record and decide the preserved challenge under both Home Builders mechanisms, giving an actual reasoned determination of the invoked connection, its support and fit. Rancho's request for immediate final relief against application is not granted at this stage. If the required connection cannot be supported, relief must address this challenged application; if it is supported, the lower court may sustain the application on that ground.

The statutory take prohibition remains in force during the remand; vacating the sustaining judgment does not itself grant permission to undertake the proposed taking or establish an incidental-take permit. No unsupported interim injunction or existing-stay change is supplied. This Court's disposition ends its present review while the lower-court commerce inquiry remains pending. The case does not remain on this Court's open docket merely because lower-court work follows its mandate.
