## 73. Dretke v. Haley

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 30, 2003, subject to any controlling decision effective before this case's event date.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the availability of a gateway for this kind of factual ineligibility; vacate to the extent the writ rests on gateway innocence alone and remand for explicit resolution of the independent federal basis for relief.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Require prompt adjudication of ineffective assistance and any properly reached sufficiency claim under applicable habeas law. If a constitutional defect is established, condition continued custody on a lawful resentencing within a short period set by the district court, accounting for time already served.
* **Remedial Reach:** Haley’s sentence and custody. His theft conviction remains unless an independently established ground invalidates it.
* **Practical Relief:** On mandate, the district court must obtain current custody and sentencing information, decide the preserved federal grounds on a priority schedule, and identify whether any lawful sentence supports continued imprisonment. A conditional writ must specify the resentencing deadline and release consequence.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Factual innocence of statutory sentencing eligibility

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The preserved dispute permits this legal determination; no missing merits finding is assumed.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether actual innocence of a noncapital habitual-offender enhancement permits review of a procedurally defaulted constitutional claim challenging that enhancement.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Propose extending the eligibility gateway, while retaining the independent constitutional and statutory requirements for habeas relief.
* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Statutory eligibility supplies an administrable distinction tied to authorized punishment. A gateway for any sentencing error would undo default rules. A death-only line would leave conceded factual ineligibility for years of imprisonment without the same safeguard.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Clear and convincing proof that no reasonable factfinder could find the statutory facts authorizing a noncapital recidivist enhancement excuses procedural default of a federal constitutional challenge to that enhancement. The prisoner bears that gateway burden; discretionary sentencing disagreement is insufficient. Haley’s undisputed chronology meets it, but a writ still requires an independently established constitutional violation and satisfaction of applicable habeas restrictions.
* **Reach Within the Question:** Factual eligibility for a statutory recidivist enhancement; it does not create a federal remedy for every state-law sentencing error or revise ordinary sentencing discretion.
* **Next-Case Guidance:** Until this Court decides the unresolved recidivist-proof question, courts apply existing constitutional proof rules and §2254 without treating gateway innocence as a new substantive right; a properly available Strickland claim remains independently adjudicable.
* **Transition:** Not applicable.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Sawyer separates legal eligibility from discretionary selection and applies a demanding factual burden. That distinction remains usable when the unauthorized additional punishment is imprisonment. Schlup’s offense-innocence standard need not be transplanted wholesale. Edwards preserves a separate counsel-based route but does not make an unadjudicated claim an established violation. The admitted chronology opens review; it does not supply the missing constitutional analysis.
* **Precedent Treatment:** Sawyer v. Whitley is extended beyond capital eligibility. Schlup v. Delo and Edwards v. Carpenter retain their distinct functions.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The precise underlying constitutional basis requires adjudication not adequately established by the gateway finding; no independent freestanding innocence claim is presented.
