## United States v. Holston: ready neutral handoff

Read `terms/OT2003/entering-law/OT_2003CHUNK1_HOLSTON.md`. The exact current standard is **Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects**. Its present-operation dates are October 9, 22 and 29, 1998 for the later recipient/supply applications and nexus distinction; its architecture remains Lopez, April 26, 1995, developed through Hale, February 12, 1996, and Home Builders, June 8, 1998. The slice contains these full controlling entries and later Kirk, Hiley, Franklyn/Gonzalez and Griffin/Langley applications, plus the prior public topology and separate positions in Lopez, Hale and Home Builders. Those later applications prevent a mistaken assumption that earlier split votes made necessary inclusion unsettled.

The neutral packet presents a reserved constitutional challenge after a conditional guilty plea, concerning actual minors, local production and recording materials that previously crossed state lines. It presents neither a dispute about factual guilt nor a virtual-image First Amendment claim. The Court may reject the equipment-only constitutional rationale while independently considering whether including actual-child-image production is a reasonably adapted, integral means of executing the identified interstate image-market prohibition, as by preventing its evasion or material impairment. These are distinct grounds: proof of the enacted materials element cannot by itself establish constitutional eligibility, and a sufficient constitutional mechanism does not eliminate the element Congress enacted.

Lopez requires the enacted class and a constitutionally eligible interstate mechanism before magnitude, deference or aggregation. The class cannot be redefined by a defendant's incidental equipment purchases or through a general account of crime's social costs. Hale supplies a real regime relationship: the prohibited machinegun-stock rule would be impaired by excluding retained and concealed locally held stock. Kirk applied it to completed prohibited stock produced by local conversion, without a proved sale prerequisite. Hiley and Franklyn/Gonzalez extend the method to identified supply/recipient relationships, and Griffin/Langley unanimously separates the enacted past-movement nexus from the constitutional justification. None holds that every manufactured object, marketable image or comprehensive statute automatically satisfies that method.

Home Builders directly controls the identification inquiry. The Government must identify the valid interstate regime, the covered local-production class and a genuine functional relationship between them, such as that excluding this class would permit evasion or materially impair regulation, on the statute's operation and any supporting legislative or adjudicative basis. The mechanism proposed here is that local production creates substitutable actual-abuse images readily copied, concealed and circulated in that market. Its sufficiency must be evaluated from the supplied record and supported inference, not certified merely because the Government advances it. No universal formal findings, defendant-specific transaction, numerical market threshold or clear-and-convincing burden is required. If the model regards the supplied factual basis as insufficient for final resolution, the authorized alternatives remain a confined remand for the actual mechanism or relief against this application, with the conditional-plea consequence expressly addressed. The preflight does not choose among those alternatives.
