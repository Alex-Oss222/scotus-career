## 58. South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 8 to 1 on the disposition, March 23, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote the Court’s opinion. The rejection of a pollutant-origin requirement was unanimous; Scalia dissented from the broader remand while concurring in that source ruling.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court rejected the origin requirement, remanded the distinct-water factual question, and left the unitary-waters theory open on remand.
* **Historical Authority:** South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians, No. 02-626; 541 U.S. 95.
* **Historical Sources:** South Florida Water Management District v. Miccosukee Tribe of Indians, No. 02-626; 541 U.S. 95.
