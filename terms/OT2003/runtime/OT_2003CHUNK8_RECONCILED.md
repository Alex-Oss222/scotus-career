AMD's recognized participation and review rights make it an interested person. The Commission's described first-instance adjudicative function qualifies; evidence for a reasonably contemplated dispositive proceeding need not await pending or imminent adjudication. Section 1782 imposes no categorical foreign-discoverability prerequisite or requirement that analogous domestic litigation permit the same discovery.
