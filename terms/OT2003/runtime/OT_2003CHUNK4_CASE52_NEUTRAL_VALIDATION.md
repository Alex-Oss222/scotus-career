It does not claim independent inspection of its PDFs or renderings; the source preparer's full-text and image-check receipt remains the provenance for those checks. ## Regulatory text

The quoted 2003 text of 29 C.F.R. section 2510.3-3(a)-(c) preserves the title I coverage purpose, the exclusion for a plan with no covered employees, the apprenticeship or training-program exception, the distinction between owner-only and mixed plans, and the provision-specific owner/spouse and partner/spouse definitions. The limiting words concerning the purpose of the section must accompany any use of subsection (c); the text is not independently characterized here as settling every individual participant-status question.

Subsection (b) references the covered-participant definition in subsection (d), which is not part of this selected quotation. This addendum does not invent its terms. If a proposed path disputes whether another employee is actually a participant covered under the plan, subsection (d) and the plan record must supply that predicate. The selected text is sufficient to present the stated distinction between plans covering no employees and plans covering common-law employees as well as self-employed persons, without deciding the present case's legal consequences.

## Statutory text and timing

The section 1108(c) quotation preserves the separate provisions for receipt of plan benefits, reasonable compensation and proper expenses, and service in dual roles. The equal-plan-terms condition and the full-time-pay compensation exception remain operative text, not optional qualifications. The exceptions concerning compensation and expense reimbursement must not be collapsed into a prohibition on receiving all plan benefits.

Subsection (d) supplies transaction-specific restrictions on the stated exemptions, its enumerated counterparties and transactions, its employer-security-sale exception, and its own shareholder-employee definition. Reproduction adopts no interpretation of working-owner coverage and makes no finding that a particular loan, transfer, benefit, or party satisfies an exemption. The subsection (d)(2)(B) exception concerns the stated transaction under paragraph (1)(C); it is not generalized to every transaction listed in paragraph (1).

The supplement expressly retains the 1997 amendment's application to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1997. The later Code text therefore cannot silently govern an earlier transaction. Any transaction-specific reliance must identify the applicable earlier or later version, the actual transaction date and taxable-year predicate, and the relevant classification. The quotation's structural relevance does not establish retroactive operation or a valid exemption for the actual transaction.
