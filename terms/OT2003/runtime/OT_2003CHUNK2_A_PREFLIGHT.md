**Material addition.** The trial was presented as a single-offender robbery and shooting, with confession testimony from Esparza's sister and a fellow inmate. ## 11. Boca Investerings Partnership — November 6, 2003

**Entering law.** Gitlitz protects enacted tax consequences within their actual statutory prerequisites; it is not a general immunity for an arrangement that fails entity recognition. Culbertson and Tower ask whether the parties genuinely intended to join in conducting the enterprise; Gregory distinguishes qualification under the statute from form alone. Apply §§761 and 7701(a)(2), the partnership provisions and the then-effective contingent-installment rule. Do not import later economic-substance legislation.

**Record and competing grounds.** The D.C. Circuit expressly accepted that differing findings in another taxpayer's litigation do not establish clear error and rejected the effort to import that other case's evidence. It treated the district court's 392 findings after a seventeen-day trial as supported but applied a legal requirement of a nontax reason for the elaborate partnership with specially created foreign entities. The district court had found genuine sharing of income, expenses, gains and losses; the circuit held that insufficient under its entity-recognition rule. The government's trial memorandum had raised the nontax-purpose issue, so the appellate preservation dispute cannot be treated as an established forfeiture.

The two IRS adjustment theories are materially different: entity nonrecognition allocated the transactions' gains and losses to AHP; transaction-level economic sham eliminated them. The lower judgment rests on entity recognition. Real investments, interest risk and profit potential cannot be equated automatically with genuine partnership intent, but a tax motive likewise cannot replace the enacted qualification inquiry. The source gives evidence of specially formed foreign participants, premium payments, substantial structuring costs, planned shifts in interests and allocations, while preserving the trial findings about actual transactions.

**Paths.** Affirmance must connect nonrecognition to a valid joint-enterprise requirement, addressing the factual findings rather than condemning tax reduction alone. Reversal or remand must explain whether the lower court imposed an extra-statutory necessity requirement or failed to defer to legally relevant findings. Any remand specifies the recognition inquiry and resulting allocations; it cannot silently adopt the government's separate economic-sham ground or award an invented final tax figure.

**Sources and limits.** `boca_lower.txt`, 314 F.3d 625 (D.C. Cir. 2003), is the reviewed opinion, with its quotations from the district judgment at 167 F. Supp. 2d 298. The latter was not independently read in full; the appellate account supports the bounded recognition dispute, not replacement findings about witness credibility. Gitlitz's prior public positions identify Breyer's distinct statutory account and the other Justices' actual joins without predicting this issue. Research cutoff: November 6, 2003. ## 12. Barnhart v. Thomas — November 12, 2003

**Objective record correction.** Thomas's particular elevator-operator position was eliminated on August 25, 1995. The ALJ found capacity for the former work and declined the proposed inquiry into whether that occupation still existed. The lower opinion expressly states in note 3 that no finding resolves nationwide occupational obsolescence. Therefore the packet's statement that the occupation had become obsolete must be understood as the claimant's asserted circumstance for the legal question, not a new adjudicated vocational fact. Neither the existence of a job listing nor the present record establishes an available job for this claimant.

**Entering law.** Read Mead, Walton and Heimmermann and their actual public joins. Walton establishes §405(a)'s delegation, its incorporation into SSI through §1383(d)(1), and the formal binding-regulation path; expertise, administrative complexity or a ruling label alone do not establish controlling force. Heimmermann preserves actual delegated interpretation consistent with the statute. The lower opinion reproduces §423(d)(1)–(2), the equivalent SSI definition and the then-effective step-four/five rules. Read the previous-work condition separately from other substantial gainful work existing in significant numbers nationally, along with the statutory exclusion of individual vacancy, hiring probability and the claimant's immediate locality.

**Paths and strongest objections.** The Commissioner relies on the separate previous-work screen, the syntax of the national-economy qualifier and a capacity proxy within an administrable sequence. Thomas relies on the statutory relation between previous and other work and the mismatch between ability to perform vanished work and present earning capacity. The Third Circuit majority found an unambiguous statutory conflict and alternatively rejected an irrational cutoff; Rendell, joined by Sloviter and Roth, defended separate capacity and vocational stages and delegated implementation. Independent modeling must decide first whether the statute resolves the issue and, if not, whether this binding implementation is authorized and reasonable. Administrative convenience cannot override enacted limits; a preferred judicial reading cannot by itself defeat an authorized permissible construction.

**Remedy and limit.** Sustaining step four resolves the legal prerequisite on the established capacity finding. Requiring an existence inquiry yields reconsideration and, when appropriate, step five; it does not award benefits or establish actual obsolescence. Research cutoff: November 12, 2003. Source: `thomas_lower.txt`, 294 F.3d 568 (3d Cir. 2002), majority and dissent. A broader change to delegated interpretive authority could affect later Deaton and completed later administrative cases; an application of Walton's existing rule need not.

## 13. Washoe County — November 17, 2003

**Entering law.** Read Palazzolo and Tahoe-Sierra, the definitive-restriction and contextual-takings standards, and surviving Penn Central, Dugan and International Paper principles. State water-use permission creates no federal easement. Residual agricultural use answers total-deprivation treatment but is not a universal answer to a genuine partial taking.

**Record precision.** The parties agreed below that no material facts were disputed. The source describes Nevada usufructuary rights, meaning rights to use water rather than own every molecule. The State Engineer authorized a different location and manner of use of existing rights; the claimants retained and exercised agricultural use because they had not perfected the proposed municipal use. Federal action did not divert water, reduce the quantity accessible from the ranch, restrict pumping or access to wells, or supply a prior right to cross federal land. The sole feasible municipal pipeline route crossed that land. Army and tribal opposition was fixed, and the government identified no remaining administrative step other than a distinct suit to compel agency action. These facts support deciding finality without imputing a failure to complete the permit process.

**Paths.** The government's strongest merits ground concerns ownership and action: refusing a new easement across its own land did not appropriate or regulate the claimant's existing water entitlement. The claimants' strongest account treats conditioning the sole route on adverse third parties' assent as functional deprivation and reservation of water for federal use. Assess those claims against the retained access and actual rights, not merely the project's profitability. If a supported and preserved Penn Central theory survives the property/action determination, it needs contextual analysis; the supplied materials do not themselves supply a valuation finding. Do not invent a separate municipal-only property slice or reject all partial takings merely because something valuable remains.

**Remedy and source.** Affirmance may preserve summary judgment on the supported absence of appropriation/interference with the actual entitlement. A remand must identify a legally cognizable preserved burden and the specific factual inquiry remaining. Compensation or an easement does not follow automatically from project frustration. `washoe_lower.txt`, 319 F.3d 1320 (Fed. Cir. 2003), verifies the entire appellate opinion and its three arguments. Research cutoff: November 17, 2003. No direct same-group dependency is identified.

## 14. United States v. Ho — November 20, 2003

**Entering law and chronology.** Read Lopez, Home Builders, the complete Commerce connection standard and Holston's October 30 public projection. Home Builders's supported anti-evasion and externality routes remain distinct from direct regulation of economic activity. No February 2004 decision enters this event.

**Verified application.** Ho purchased the former hospital after asbestos disclosure, rejected licensed commercial bids and hired workers for removal in his renovation project. The convicted counts were notice and work-practice violations. The separate ambient-air danger count was dismissed; another release-reporting count was dismissed at trial. The Fifth Circuit assumed no release for the Commerce question and considered actual release separately under a sentencing evidentiary standard. The government there invoked aggregate effects on interstate abatement services and commercial real estate, not an individually substantial effect or the channels/instrumentalities categories. The source's commercial-services evidence identifies actual transactions rather than downstream national health costs.

**Paths and limits.** One lawful path treats the regulated removal/renovation operations and notice integral to them as directly commercial activity whose supported aggregate market effects justify preventive regulation without proving an actual interstate escape. The alternative asks whether the enacted class is broader than the market mechanism and whether the application can be sustained without adding an element Congress did not enact. Test statutory fit; Home Builders prevents borrowing the project's commercial features without identifying the actual work-practice class. A true noneconomic application would need its own supported regime or externality route, not a general pollution label. No individual-residence application is presented for decision.

## 15. Valeria v. Davis — November 25, 2003

**Material entering-law addition.** Coalition for Economic Equity v. Wilson, November 10, 1998, is directly relevant. Its five-Justice political-process holding rejects the premise that voluntary or otherwise lawful racial remedies lose protection merely by being called preferences. Hunter and Seattle survive; Crawford's neutral policy-withdrawal scope does not erase their structural inquiry. Its remedy was confined preliminary protection, not a universal entitlement to a program. Adarand's common substantial-justification standard governs an actual racial classification; the lower opinion's historical strict-scrutiny vocabulary is not the operative simulated standard. Read these entries, Romer and the prior public positions.

**Record and statutory precision.** Proposition 227 shifts ordinary bilingual instruction to structured English immersion, normally intended as a transition not exceeding one year, and restricts ordinary local policy change. Waivers require parental consent and apply when the child already knows English; when the child is at least ten and the school finds an alternative better serves English education; or after thirty days of immersion where the school finds special physical, emotional, psychological or educational needs and an alternative better serves educational development. Twenty waivers in a grade at a school require an alternative class. Amendment requires voter approval or a two-thirds legislative enactment furthering the act's purpose signed by the Governor. The trial finding rejects discriminatory pedagogical purpose; demographics and campaign references are in the appellate record but do not themselves establish a contrary motive finding.

**Enhanced comparison required.** Test the strongest state account—neutral centralization of pedagogy applicable to all families—against the strongest structural claim—selective removal of a policy especially serving a racial minority to a more remote process while other educational choices remain locally obtainable. Coalition materially changes the legal background of the lower opinion's reliance on its own earlier Coalition ruling. Distinguish rejection of a structural claim merely because the underlying program was not a remedy for proved discrimination from a supported finding that race did not define this decisionmaking allocation. The trial purpose finding and a distinct political mechanism are related but not interchangeable. No right to prevail on a bilingual-education policy and no automatic immunity for every statewide initiative follows from existing law.

**Paths and remedy.** Affirmance requires applying the actual structural rule to this record, not simply using the absence of animus or of a prior remedial decree as a universal bar. Reversal or remand requires identifying a racial decisionmaking mechanism and the legal or factual error affecting it; disparate beneficiaries alone do not establish it. Relief targets any unlawful political barrier, without judicially prescribing curriculum or reopening independent statutory language-access claims outside the grant. Source: `valeria_lower.txt`, 307 F.3d 1036 (9th Cir. 2002), including its statutory recitation and notes. Research cutoff: November 25, 2003. Later election-law matters may need any new structural holding, but do not transfer this doctrine to ordinary party eligibility or funding rules without its racial mechanism.
