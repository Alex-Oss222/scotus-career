## Reconciled disposition and propositions

Every Associate Justice — Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer — retains the frozen vote to reverse the Ninth Circuit's challenged environmental-review requirements and remand consistently with PC1–PC4. Each joins those four propositions in full, with their operative limits. The present judgment does not order a new search for an unsupported alternative or effect.

The full-source verification confirms, rather than changes, the neutral allocation of authority and the grounds of the frozen commitments:

- **PC1, NEPA:** A reasonably close causal relationship is required; foreseeable but-for implementation alone does not attribute the effects of the President's independent entry decision to FMCSA. Its statutory duty to register qualified carriers leaves it no environmental admission veto. Metropolitan Edison supplies the causal limit; Robertson supplies informed procedure within the agency's actual decision. The informational function does not enlarge the action's legally attributable effects.
- **PC2, cumulative effects and alternatives:** Section 1508.7 still requires evaluation of the rules' incremental impact in the setting of other actions regardless of actor. That obligation does not transform the President's action into an effect of the rules. The specific unpresented alternatives objection is forfeited under Vermont Yankee. An obvious flaw could dispense with a specific comment, but no supported obvious omitted alternative is established here. Neither that forfeiture nor the authority limitation extinguishes every environmental objection.
- **PC3, conformity:** The Clean Air Act regulations have their own terms. Unlike NEPA's close-causation analysis, section 93.152's caused-by definition permits but-for causation. Even assuming that connection, truck emissions do not occur at the same time and place as promulgation and are not direct emissions; the agency lacks practicable continuing program control over the independent entry emissions, so those emissions also fail the indirect-emissions definition. Reasonable foreseeability does not replace that second condition. The frozen commitment is confirmed on those separate grounds and must not be rewritten as a CAA rejection of but-for causation.
- **PC4, remedy:** The challenged requirement to prepare a full EIS and conformity determination for the independently authorized increase is removed. Remand implements that disposition. Ordinary duties concerning effects of actual agency choices remain, but the present record supplies no independent preserved omitted alternative or controllable effect requiring another ordered inquiry.

The source's congressional-ratification discussion supplies no additional controlling proposition necessary to these frozen commitments. It confirms that reenactment of the appropriations condition does not decide interpretation of the different environmental statutes. No new litigant concession, fact, filing, waiver or remedy predicate is added.
