## Reconciled propositions

All six eligible Associates retain P1–P3 of the frozen model, subject to the following precise scope clarification. A genuinely neutral policy against rehiring employees separated for workplace misconduct is a legally sufficient nondiscriminatory explanation at the production stage. Its legal sufficiency establishes neither its truth nor the actual motive. The employee retains ultimate persuasion and may use the entire record, including meaningful falsity and circumstantial evidence, without a mandatory additional category of proof. Courts may not convert an untimely impact theory into intentional treatment or treat a facially lawful label as conclusive evidence.

Vacate and remand for the proper pretext, knowledge and intentional-discrimination sufficiency inquiry. The lower court's rejection of the policy as legally unavailable prevented that inquiry under the correct rule. The Court does not decide the ultimate sufficiency result or award either party judgment.

**Scope clarification:** The frozen model's references to a qualification inquiry do not authorize reopening an issue outside the supplied grant or disturbing the unchallenged prima facie ruling. The historical opinion expressly records that qualification was not disputed in this Court and leaves the prima facie ruling unreviewed. On the approved question, the remand concerns the legitimate explanation and remaining motive/pretext inquiry; the 1999 examination cannot become a new basis for rejecting the 1994 application here. The assumed disability premise remains an assumption of this litigation, not a classification of every former drug user. This is a limit on the model's general remand language, not a newly adjudicated qualification question or a new factual premise.
