## 89. Republic of Austria v. Altmann

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote with O’Connor, Scalia, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer; Scalia concurred; Breyer concurred with Souter; Kennedy dissented with Rehnquist and Thomas.

## 94. Elk Grove Unified School District v. Newdow

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 8 to 0 in the judgment. Five Justices resolved the case on prudential standing; three concurred in the judgment on other reasoning.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Rehnquist concurred in the judgment, joined by O'Connor and in part by Thomas; O'Connor and Thomas also wrote separately. Scalia did not participate.
* **Historical Holding:** Newdow could not maintain this challenge given the custody and prudential-standing concerns. The majority did not decide the Pledge's constitutionality.
* **Historical Authority:** Elk Grove Unified School District v. Newdow, No. 02-1624; 542 U.S. 1 (2004).
