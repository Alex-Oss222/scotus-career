## 64. BedRoc Limited, LLC v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist plurality joined by O’Connor, Scalia and Kennedy; Thomas concurred in the judgment with Breyer; Stevens dissented with Souter and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** Ordinary sand and gravel were outside the Pittman Act reservation; the plurality emphasized valuable minerals, while the concurrence declined to extend Western Nuclear.
* **Historical Authority:** BedRoc Limited, LLC v. United States, No. 02-1593; 541 U.S. 176.
* **Historical Sources:** BedRoc Limited, LLC v. United States, No. 02-1593; 541 U.S. 176.
