## Law After Decision

Rule 53(a)(1)(C) permits appointments for pretrial and posttrial matters that an available district judge or magistrate judge cannot address effectively and timely. The appointment order specifies duties, including investigation or enforcement duties, and authority limits. Under Rule 53(g)(3), objections to factual findings receive de novo determination unless the parties stipulate, with court consent, to clear-error review or, for appointments under Rule 53(a)(1)(A) or (C), finality. The rule's other appointment, notice, disqualification, review and compensation requirements operate according to their text. Its application to pending proceedings remains subject to the just-and-practicable transition.
