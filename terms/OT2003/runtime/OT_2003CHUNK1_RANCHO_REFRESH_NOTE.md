## Rancho Viejo: material addition, baseline inquiry retained

Holston adds a unanimous application of the necessary-inclusion doctrine. Its first holding separates the statutory materials-in-commerce condition from constitutional authority: equipment or media provenance alone does not justify the later local production class. Its second holding sustains local production of actual-child-abuse images because that production creates the reproducible, concealable and substitutable supply of the identified prohibited interstate image market. The Government must identify the valid prohibition, the covered production class and their actual anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship; constitutional eligibility and reasonable fit receive independent judicial review, with substantial legislative latitude on reasonable empirical judgments and regulatory design.

This addition is material to the method and Justice-specific authorities in Rancho, but establishes no new wildlife fact. Holston expressly applies Home Builders's necessary-inclusion and identification rules and rests on its own supported product-and-source relationship, not on the wildlife scheme Home Builders sustained. Rancho's housing project is not thereby treated as the source of prohibited interstate wildlife stock. A commercial project, an input that crossed state lines, the value of biodiversity, or the breadth of a conservation program does not independently establish the enacted taking class's eligible mechanism. Neither a defendant-specific interstate transaction nor universal formal findings, a fixed numerical benchmark or clear-and-convincing evidence is required merely because the relationship must be identified.

The existing Home Builders rule remains controlling: identify the enacted taking class; test necessary inclusion, under which the Act's integrated system regulating interstate exploitation, movement and commercial disposition of listed wildlife may include the unauthorized taking of listed wildlife without species-by-species, border-crossing or independent-effects proof, subject to outside-class, invalid-regime and pretext showings, or the independently recognized substantial-interstate-externality route and its territorial-control limits; preserve the Government's identification responsibility and the proper disposition when the lower court used the insufficient economic-project rationale. Holston does not resolve an ecological externality or establish a complete Rancho record. A short packet's failure to demonstrate a species-specific mechanism is not a factual adjudication about the relevant record.

All eight Associates join O'Connor's entire Holston opinion, both holdings and their application. Each therefore has a new public commitment to the distinction between an enacted nexus and the constitutional mechanism, and to a materially supported actual product-and-source rationale in that defined setting. Stevens and Souter's earlier Home Builders economic-application view is not withdrawn by the Holston opinion. Scalia and Thomas's agreement with the demonstrated necessary-regime ground does not establish their agreement with Home Builders's necessary-inclusion holding or its independent externality route. O'Connor and Kennedy's territorial-control position, and Ginsburg and Breyer's acceptance of the governing class premise and available mechanisms, remain as set out in the baseline public authorities. The fresh model must consider the new unanimous application together with those actual earlier positions, without importing a disposition or coalition for Rancho.

**Readiness:** Rancho is ready for independent modeling on the unchanged approved neutral entry, the B baseline and source limits, the baseline Rancho slice, and this refresh.
