## 60. Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 8 to 1, March 24, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for six Justices; Scalia, joined by Thomas, concurred in the judgment. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 253 did not preempt the Missouri limitation on its municipalities’ telecommunications authority; the historical majority also relied on the absence of an unmistakably clear congressional direction.
* **Historical Authority:** Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League, Nos. 02-1238, 02-1386 and 02-1405; 541 U.S. 125.
* **Historical Sources:** Nixon v. Missouri Municipal League, Nos. 02-1238, 02-1386 and 02-1405; 541 U.S. 125.
