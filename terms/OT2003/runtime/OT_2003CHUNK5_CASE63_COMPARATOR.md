## 63. National Archives and Records Administration v. Favish

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed unanimously and remanded for summary judgment for the custodian as to the four photographs, March 30, 2004.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote the unanimous opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court recognized surviving-family privacy and required more than bare suspicion when alleged government misconduct supplied the asserted disclosure interest.
* **Historical Authority:** National Archives and Records Administration v. Favish, No. 02-954; 541 U.S. 157.
