* **Question Granted:** The question presented above; review is confined to the preserved issues and judgment identified here.

### Issue 1: Delegated classification of over-limit charges

* **Controlling Law:** Heimmermann v. First Union Mortgage Corp. and Mead require an actual delegation and an authorized exercise before an agency interpretation has controlling force; Haggar preserves enforcement of the statutory limits. Section 1604(a)'s express classification authority, rather than ambiguity alone, is material here.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** A valid delegated regulation cannot contradict unambiguous enacted meaning; a reasonable choice within the delegation and genuine statutory ambiguity receives the governing deference.
* **Other Governing Law:** 15 U.S.C. §§1604(a), 1605(a), 1637 and 1640; Regulation Z’s finance-charge exclusion for over-limit fees.
* **Material Other Authority:** Chevron U.S.A. Inc. v. Natural Resources Defense Council supplies the retained framework; a remedial purpose does not itself nullify an authorized classification.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** The entering rules determine how to review Regulation Z but do not settle this fee category.
* **Open Point:** Whether the statutory phrase incident to the extension of credit unambiguously includes all over-limit fees, and whether the categorical exclusion is permissible.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Reasoning below:** The Sixth Circuit distinguished agreed over-limit extensions from unilateral default and treated the former fee as a finance charge.
* **Petitioner or appellant:** The issuers defend a uniform distinction between the price of credit and penalties for exceeding agreed terms, within the Board’s express classification authority.
* **Respondent or appellee:** Pfennig argues that knowingly permitting purchases beyond the limit extends credit and makes the related charge part of its cost.
* **Alternative or unreached grounds:** Other disclosure duties remain even if the fee is outside the finance-charge subtotal.
* **Concessions and limiting principles:** No unverified oral-argument concession is used.
* **Solicitor General or material amicus position:** None separately verified.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**Plausible holding scope and consequences:** Sustaining the regulation reverses this classification-based claim while preserving independent disclosure requirements. Invalidating it remands for TILA liability and statutory defenses rather than automatically awarding damages. A transaction-specific construction would require legally administrable criteria identifying which authorizations change the fee’s category.
