## Holdings

**Operative rule:** Mosley's death after submission ends this personal criminal certiorari proceeding on the record presented, which establishes no surviving substituted claim or separate collateral controversy. The Court therefore vacates its grant and dismisses the petition without deciding whether bank larceny is a lesser included offense of bank robbery or vacating the judgment below.

**Authority:** The Court's per curiam disposition supplies this limited procedural ruling; it announces no tally or individual lineup.

**Controlling explanation:** Dove v. United States supplies the treatment of death during criminal certiorari review and displaces Durham's inconsistent automatic remedy extending to the prosecution below. The fact that review was granted and submitted requires disposition of the grant as well as the petition; it does not restore a live offense-elements controversy or revive that displaced remedy. U.S. Bancorp recognizes authority under §2106 to dispose of a pending review after merits adjudication becomes unavailable, but makes available relief distinct from an entitlement to vacatur. Arizonans likewise permits supported corrective action without deciding the merits. No separate ground is established here to erase the completed appeal below. This action leaves questions involving an appeal of right, a substituted estate claim, fines, restitution and independent collateral rights outside the decision.

## Precedent Treatment

- Dove v. United States, 423 U.S. 325: applies its criminal-certiorari death disposition.
- Durham v. United States, 401 U.S. 481: its automatic contrary all-proceedings-abatement remedy remains displaced to the extent specified by Dove; the grant does not revive it.
- U.S. Bancorp Mortgage Co. v. Bonner Mall Partnership: applies the distinction between §2106 dispositional authority and entitlement to judgment vacatur; no merits authority follows from that statute.
- Arizonans for Official English v. Arizona: preserves supported corrective action after loss of a live controversy; its civil judgment-vacatur result does not require erasure of this completed criminal appeal.

## Law After Decision

The bank-larceny and bank-robbery elements question remains undecided by this Court. The order terminates this certiorari proceeding and leaves the Third Circuit judgment undisturbed. It establishes no general rule governing death during appeal of right, substituted estate litigation, fines, restitution or independent collateral consequences.
