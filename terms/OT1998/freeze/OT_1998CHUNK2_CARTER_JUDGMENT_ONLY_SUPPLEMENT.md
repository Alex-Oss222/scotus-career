## Exact proposition and operative mandate tested

Vacate the two reviewed Minnesota Supreme Court judgments to the extent they dispose of the federal suppression claims through the challenged observation determination, and remand for lawful further proceedings under the directly adopted personal-interest rule and the existing Fields limits. The vacatur removes the adverse federal suppression judgments. It does not reverse the personal-interest holding, declare the observation a search or no search, restore the convictions automatically, or direct admission or exclusion of every item. Because no search application is adopted as Court law, lawful further consideration of that question remains available below.

The source constraints already stated in the proposal remain: distinguish trial findings, testimony and appellate characterization; do not replace historical facts by photographic inference; address factual-review and preservation objections if rejecting an appellate characterization is necessary; alternatively explain an answer that assumes the disputed additions. These conditions create no new hearing, reconstruction, discovery, burden, factual finding, state-law disposition or additional claim. Ordinary procedures govern independent remaining issues. They describe the permissible remand; a judgment-only join does not adopt O'Connor's explanation that renewed actual-vantage adjudication is necessary in preference to deciding search now.

This is the exact legal distinction left insufficiently tested before. A Justice may conclude this observation was not a search and publish that complete conclusion, while accepting removal of the erroneous suppression judgments as lawful and sufficient operative relief. Acceptance does not require him personally to reserve search. It does require him actually to tolerate a mandate under which the lower courts remain free to reach a properly supported result not foreclosed by a Court search holding. That consequence is expressly accepted in each provisional judgment-only join below.

## Why the earlier Fields comparison does not settle this test

The earlier analysis treated the four Justices' opposition to actual-vantage vacatur in Fields as strong evidence against any similar Carter vacatur. Fields vacatur, however, removed suppression denials and dependent conviction affirmances that these four considered correct on the unaided-view and entry grounds. Carter vacatur removes federal suppression reversals that these four consider erroneous on the qualified unaided-view ground. Their substantive views remain continuous while the direction of the corrective relief differs.

This difference does not automatically establish four joins. The individual question remains whether the particular mandate gives legally sufficient relief without requiring a forbidden substantive concession or imposing an objectionable obligation. The frozen Carter positions seek removal of the observation-based adverse judgments, retain independent later-evidence questions, and expressly disclaim automatic admission or unconditional reinstatement. Those positive remedial limits support accepting the present judgment's actual corrective work while withholding assent from the remand rationale. Nothing in the supplied own-ground reasoning makes conclusive no-search instructions the only legally acceptable relief.

The earlier test correctly found no reason for these Justices to abandon their conclusive no-search answer. It went too far when it treated that fact as sufficient to reject judgment-only assent to a disposition that does not require abandoning it. This supplement tests the operative relief, not the strength of the unchanged personal merits conclusion.

## Antonin Scalia

**Provisional response: adopts the judgment only.** He retains his rejection of the accepted personal-interest basis and, under the adopted contrary premise, his complete qualified no-search application. He does not join O'Connor's view that a fresh actual-vantage adjudication is necessary.

**Recorded objection and the exact answer:** His objection was that the supported accessible setting and actual unaided view settle search even assuming climbing and crouching; a remand cannot be justified by treating that factual dispute as indispensable. The revised object of assent is removal of the federal suppression judgments, not factual uncertainty or a personal reservation of search. On his own view those judgments rest on a constitutional error and should not remain operative. The bounded vacatur supplies that correction now. His separate writing can state why no search occurred and why he would conclusively reverse on that ground.

**Own date-eligible support:** Scalia's actual Fields Part I treats exposed natural viewing as sufficient notwithstanding an occupant's protected relationship and possible trespass, while accepting that distinct remaining remedies require their own predicates. His Pinson opinion distinguishes otherwise concealed information from actual exposed information, and its Part III confines correction to the reviewed suppression ground without automatically reviving or deciding every other claim. These are case-specific substantive and remedial principles, not a general inference about willingness to compromise. His Carter commitment likewise directs no blanket admission and retains genuine separate claims.

**Mandate consequence accepted:** He accepts that, absent a Court no-search holding, Minnesota may lawfully reconsider the federal search question and may reach a result his separate opinion would reject. That institutional effect does not make the present removal unlawful or require him to endorse a search finding. His position is not that every permissible future result is constitutionally correct in his view; it is that this Court's bounded correction need not purport to establish his nonmajority answer as law. Unlike Fields, vacatur here does not unsettle the suppression disposition he believes correct.

**Operative objections and limits:** He would refuse a mandate declaring a search, requiring exclusion, opening the record anew, manufacturing a protected enclosure or shifting the privacy burden. None is present. The source-review conditions apply only if historical factual rejection matters and do not adopt the review extension to which he objected in Ornelas/Fields. He tolerates the stated remand with those limits; he does not join its necessity rationale or withdraw his sufficient no-search ground.

## Anthony M. Kennedy

**Provisional response: adopts the judgment only.** He retains the personal-interest disagreement and the qualified actual-view conclusion. He refuses O'Connor's rationale for leaving the application undecided as his own reasoning.

**Recorded objection and the exact answer:** His no-search answer rests on the information actually acquired, ordinary access, absence of enclosure, natural visibility and lack of enhancement, with disputed posture assumed. The prior proposal offered no reason to abandon that answer. This limited proposition asks whether the observation-based judgments may be removed without making his answer a binding instruction. Removal corrects the particular adverse judgments he considers unsupported; it does not tell him that ordinary access or the exposed information is constitutionally irrelevant.

**Own date-eligible support:** His actual Fields substantive join favors the particular unaided-view application while preserving distinct personal interest and later remedial issues. His Pinson/Myers position focuses on acquired information and method, rather than deriving search from the domestic label or investigatory purpose. His Ornelas and Fields review joins preserve factual findings while permitting independent constitutional judgment. Together they support a lawful corrective disposition on his view of these judgments without factual rewriting, automatic admission or a mandatory evidentiary proceeding.

**Mandate consequence accepted:** He accepts lawful reconsideration below even though his published view supplies a conclusive no-search answer. His judgment-only assent does not convert the separate answer into Court law, and the absence of that direction does not make removal of the erroneous judgments legally insufficient. The remaining stop, warrant, arrest and statement issues already preclude an unconditional final admission or reinstatement mandate under his frozen position. Permitting ordinary further proceedings is therefore consistent with his remedial limits, even though he would resolve the antecedent search more conclusively.

**Operative objections and limits:** He would refuse compulsory new factfinding, a rule treating every off-walkway observation as a search, a statutory-violation finding or instructions that ordinary natural visibility cannot matter. The proposed mandate imposes none. The possibility of a later disagreement with a lawful lower-court application is not treated as a present obligation to adopt that result. He accepts the bounded correction while preserving all contrary merits grounds.

## Clarence Thomas

**Provisional response: adopts the judgment only.** He continues to reject the accepted privacy basis and, under the controlling contrary premise, to find no search on the complete qualified facts. He does not adopt O'Connor's reason for requiring another constitutional application.

**Recorded objection and the exact answer:** His substantive objection is that protected personal privacy does not convert the exposed natural view from the actually used unenclosed ground into a search. Assuming the disputed additions avoids an unresolved factual premise. The judgment-only proposition leaves that ground intact while removing the adverse suppression judgments it establishes as erroneous in his view. It requires no agreement that search is genuinely undecidable for him.

**Own date-eligible support:** His actual Fields I-A and Scalia Part I joins separate personal relationship from the particular exposed view. His Pinson/Myers joins protect concealed home information and distinguish correction of an antecedent suppression premise from deciding all evidence or reopening unavailable claims. His Ornelas and Fields review joins preserve historical facts independently of constitutional assessment. His frozen Carter remedy expressly leaves later arrest, vehicle, statement and warrant claims separate and gives neither a new claim nor automatic admission.

**Mandate consequence accepted:** He accepts that the Court's operative disposition leaves the search question available on remand because his own conclusive answer is not a controlling Court proposition. That is less conclusive relief than he would independently give, but it performs the supported corrective task of setting aside the judgments against the State. The inference of assent rests on that concrete legal effect and the actual preserved remedy limits; it does not arise merely from counting vacatur as less than reversal.

**Operative objections and limits:** He would refuse a mandate presuming search, crediting the key proffer as established control, giving Johns Carter's evidence, replacing facts through photographs, imposing a public-access burden or commanding admission/exclusion. The proposed mandate does none of those things. It also adds no doctrine allowing criminal purpose alone to decide personal protection. With those bounds, he accepts the judgment while withholding its nonconclusive-application rationale.

## Stephen G. Breyer

**Provisional response: adopts the judgment only as to the observation disposition, while retaining his full affirmative join in the separate personal-interest proposition.** He continues to find no search on the complete actual-use record and the favorable assumption of climbing/crouching. He does not adopt O'Connor's need-for-reconsideration rationale.

**Recorded objection and the exact answer:** His existing opinion already performs the proposed remand's permissible assumption method and explains why the additions do not change the answer. No reason to abandon that explanation has emerged. The revised object of assent instead preserves the personal-interest determination he affirmatively supports while removing the erroneous federal observation judgments. It does not turn his personal privacy conclusion into an assumption or erase the actual-use basis for no search.

**Own date-eligible support:** His actual Fields Part I join distinguishes personal room privacy from actual natural exposure and rejects a categorical shared-yard rule. His Pinson/Myers information approach examines the acquired information and method. The effective Carter commitment specifically disclaims blanket admission, unconditional reinstatement and final treatment of separate vehicle or statement issues; it also accepts the Fields IV conditional remedies if the Court does not adopt his no-search ground. That last conditional agreement alone would not establish a vacatur vote, but in combination with the removal of the specific erroneous judgments and retained independent proceedings it supports this express judgment-only correction.

**Mandate consequence accepted:** He accepts that a lawful lower-court reconsideration remains available and that his separate no-search conclusion does not bind it as Court law. The present mandate nevertheless accomplishes two concrete legal tasks he supports: it respects the adopted personal entitlement and removes the adverse judgments resting on the observation application he rejects. It neither leaves those judgments intact nor requires him to describe search as unresolved in his own view. Unlike Fields, vacatur moves the operative judgment in the direction warranted by his substantive conclusion.

**Operative objections and limits:** He would refuse remand instructions that discredit the assumed appellate additions, create a common-area immunity, make the uncertain blind geometry dispositive, require new proof of already supported natural visibility, or order a particular evidence outcome. The bounded mandate contains none. His separate writing preserves the exact favorable factual assumptions, ordinary nonresident-use requirement, distinction between the first window-side view and an unproved sidewalk view, and exclusion of manipulated blinds, protected enclosures, dwelling intrusion or technological acquisition from his rationale.

## Proposed revision and historical-audit question

The material proposed revision is confined to the four Justices' final formal remedy: each can accept bounded vacatur/remand as sufficient correction while still publishing his preference for conclusive no-search reversal. It does not change their merits conclusions, their objections to O'Connor's analysis, any factual premise or the scope of a privacy join. It is therefore a judgment-only accommodation, not a new doctrinal coalition.

The audit should test each inferred assent against the prior reconciled commitment, own dated positions and historical comparator. The concrete premise differs from the rejected Fields analogy: these are adverse suppression reversals, not favorable suppression denials. The Court's directly adopted personal-interest proposition and unresolved search application define the bounded institutional mandate, while each Justice's own sufficient no-search ground supplies his reason for removing the judgments. Those legal and remedial differences are the proposed basis for accepting a less conclusive Court disposition without withdrawing a conclusive separate view.

This receipt supplies proposed adoptions for review, not certification. If the isolated audit finds that any Justice's source-grounded commitment requires conclusive no-search instructions as an indispensable remedial condition, that Justice's proposed adoption must be rejected or narrowed accordingly. No assignment or final judgment is entered before that audit.
