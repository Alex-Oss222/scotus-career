**Scope:** Supplemental validation of the already-preserved section 5 exceptions and coordinate immobilization. This receipt does not replace any frozen neutral, commitment or reconciliation file and does not supply a new adjudicative fact, result or title determination.

**Sources actually read:** The complete official 1997 Code extractions at `../runtime/preflight-sources/43usc1313-1997.txt` and `../runtime/preflight-sources/43usc1301-1997.txt`, corresponding to the saved PDFs. The complete terms of 43 U.S.C. § 1313(a)–(c) explain the section 5 reservation already expressly included in the proposed decree and every frozen handoff. Stating those terms in the Public Projection is within that existing preservation, without a new model premise or affected-stage return, provided no parcel or claimant is newly found to satisfy an exception.

Section 1313 excepts from section 1311:

- Under (a), land tracts or parcels, with their accretions, resources and improvements, whose title the United States lawfully and expressly acquired from a State or from a person with title vested under state or federal law; lands the United States lawfully holds under state law; lands expressly retained by or ceded to the United States at state admission, apart from a general retention or cession of lands beneath the marginal sea; lands acquired by the United States in a proprietary capacity through eminent domain, purchase, cession, gift or otherwise; lands it filled, built up or otherwise reclaimed for its own use; and federal rights in lands then presently and actually occupied by the United States under claim of right.
- Under (b), submerged lands, or interests in them, held by the United States for the benefit of an Indian tribe, band, group or individual.
- Under (c), structures and improvements constructed by the United States in exercising its navigational servitude.

The temporal and title qualifiers remain operative. The reservation is not a new finding of federal title, a determination of an Indian interest, a parcel description, or general authority to claim land based solely on future occupation. Section 1314's neighboring retained regulatory powers are not silently substituted for section 1313's ownership exceptions.

Section 1301(b)'s 1986 amendment confirms that a State–United States boundary fixed by coordinates in a final Supreme Court decree remains immobilized at those coordinates and is not ambulatory. This verifies the already-frozen statutory ground; it adds no new coordinate, survey conclusion or future-coastline adjustment.

**Conclusion:** Faithful statutory recitation completes the existing express preservation and does not materially change any commitment.
