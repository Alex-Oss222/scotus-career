## Issue-specific alternatives and supported dispositions

### Racial/ethnic promotions

Path A: affirm the invalidation on independent failure of a sufficiently connected remedial predicate and fit, without adopting an egregious-resistance or formal-adjudication prerequisite. The 1972 DOJ findings concern discriminatory entry barriers, while the 1976 agreement principally concerns recruitment/hiring and does not confess every allegation. Their force is real but does not automatically establish the necessary scale of present preferences in driver-engineer, lieutenant and captain promotions. The City's actual neutral efforts distinguish Engineering Contractors's unperformed alternatives inquiry. Nevertheless, continuing imbalance alone does not explain why these measures cannot address the relevant surviving effects; common population-based goals across ranks do not establish the relevant remedial need. Qualification, banding, a yearly ceiling and recurring five-year limits do not by themselves cure that missing connection. The burden on employees bypassed in promotion is real even though less severe than job loss.

Strongest objection to A: the original DOJ letter supplies identified departmental exclusion rather than societal discrimination; a closed internal ladder transmits earlier hiring exclusion upward. Using only the already depleted feeder ranks can hide the very effect to be repaired. The Fifth Circuit emphasized absence of egregious resistance and treated good-faith alternatives as a reason to reject further relief; neither is a constitutional prerequisite or dispositive. A limited remand can correct that analytical shortfall without finding the preferences valid. Response supporting A: those facts may establish a permissible remedial purpose, but independent review still requires support for these particular preferences. No complete exhaustion or discriminatory intent in current examinations is demanded; the present record's disconnected goals and inadequacy explanation remain insufficient.

Path B: vacate the racial three-rank constitutional judgment and remand for independent application of Adarand/Engineering Contractors to the combined evidence and actual policy. Do not declare the required justification proved or uphold the promotions. DOJ's identified entry exclusion, the closed ladder and actual responsive alternatives are material distinctions from Engineering Contractors's common ground. The lower court's emphasis on egregiousness and resistance, coupled with its inference that alternatives merely need more time, does not adequately settle whether surviving cohort effects justify these bounded measures. The rank goals, relevant pools, duration and actual burdens remain genuine objections to be assessed together, rather than sufficient grounds to invent a numerical remedy.

Strongest objection to B: the Court may affirm a correct judgment on an independent record ground; the trial court identified goals common to differing feeder ranks and promotion burdens, while the City retains the burden of justification. Response supporting B: those features cannot be resolved by assuming feeder composition is unaffected by the identified entry exclusion, nor may mere continued disparity establish the City's opposite conclusion. The record supports a lawful reconsideration under the correct inquiry, not a Supreme Court finding about the exact counterfactual workforce. Remand gives no new-evidence entitlement and is not a general chance to build a different program.

An outright reversal validating every challenged racial promotion is rejected by all eight: the supported record does not establish each rank's remedial connection and fit. A categorical ban on local remedial preferences or requirement of obstinate resistance is also rejected as the ground of judgment.

### Sex-conscious promotions

Path A: affirm on the independently inadequate sex-specific justification under demanding intermediate scrutiny. The racial DOJ findings cannot be relabeled findings of sex discrimination. The agreement's sex commitments and underrepresentation may be relevant, but on the stated record they do not establish the connected disadvantage needed to justify these preferences in the three ranks. This is a failure of the particular justification, not a racial-alternatives-exhaustion holding, a demand for a prior liability judgment, or a rule requiring statistical proof.

Strongest objection: correction of historical exclusion can be an important purpose; a closed ladder and an earlier agreement addressing women could warrant combined-evidence reconsideration, particularly in summary-judgment posture. Response: unlike Engineering Contractors's sex remand position, this packet supplies no comparable combined showing of sex-specific entry barriers, surviving adjusted opportunity disparity or accounts of exclusion. The unavailable underlying archive is not presumed empty; rather, the supported evidence and arguments actually identified do not supply the constitutional link. The Court does not infer a female-discrimination finding from the documented racial practices.

Path B: vacate for separate intermediate review if the judgment below actually applied racial strict scrutiny or ignored identified sex-specific evidence. That route is lawful in principle but its factual/legal predicates are not established here: the appellate opinion separately invoked intermediate scrutiny and identified the absence of the necessary sex justification. Merely stating that it found even less sex evidence does not itself import the racial test. No Associate adopts a remand solely to search for a missing evidentiary case. Sex fit is not independently resolved once this justification fails.
