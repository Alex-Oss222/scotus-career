Root restored the source's nonspecific referent and explicitly retained the secured-account payment condition. The explanation denies any guaranteed loan, and Source Notes leaves construction of the unavailable future-credit promise open. **Resolved.** The entry does not decide the source's referent more specifically than the source itself.
