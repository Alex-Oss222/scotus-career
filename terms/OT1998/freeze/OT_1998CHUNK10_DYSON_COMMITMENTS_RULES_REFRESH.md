## Clean new source and express correction

Read OT_1998CHUNK10_DYSON_RULES_NEUTRAL_ADDENDUM.md; the linked official1998 Code reproductions research/chunk10_root/rule15_1998.txt and rule16_1998.txt; and their official retrieval receipt. The supplied edition was adopted January16,1997, effective May1,1997, amended through January5,1999. No later rule is substituted. The new material adds procedural law, not an actual response, service, waiver, distribution or deadline event.

The original commitment incorrectly treated a requested response as a categorical prerequisite to a grant and adverse summary merits disposition. That proposition is withdrawn. Dated Rule15.1 says an opposition brief may be filed but is mandatory only in a capital case or when the Court orders one. Dyson is the supplied noncapital drug-prosecution matter. There is no mandatory response order established here. The absence of an archived opposition does not establish nonfiling, waiver, default or breach of a Court order.

Rule15.5 separately governs distribution: receipt of an express waiver; expiration of the allowed filing time where neither waiver nor opposition was filed; or, after timely opposition, distribution no less than10days after its filing. Those alternatives cannot be collapsed into a response-request requirement. Rule15.3 supplies the ordinary30day opposition period after docketing and permits extensions; it supplies no actual docketing date, extension or service history here. Rule15.2's possible nonjurisdictional waiver is qualified and issue-specific; no waiver is found from the research gap. Rule15.6 does not defer distribution/consideration awaiting a possible reply; no reply fact is inferred.

Under Rule16.1 an order after consideration of documents distributed under Rule15 may be a summary disposition on the merits. Rule16.2's ordinary briefing/argument route upon a grant does not eliminate that express option. A response request remains a discretionary institutional choice and, if actually ordered, would impose an obligation on the respondent; it is not an already-entered step or a mandatory cure for an unavailable research copy.
