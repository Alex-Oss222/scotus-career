## 3. What the actual tax verdict establishes

The final charge did not allow the jury to convict for an unspecified false entry anywhere in either return. Its second tax element required finding that the reported annual total income was false: $53,625 for 1985 and minus $980,377 for 1986. The first element concerned a signed return declared under penalties of perjury; the third required knowledge of the falsity; the fourth required a purposeful statement rather than accident, negligence, or inadvertence. The general instructions required proof beyond a reasonable doubt, separate consideration of each count, and unanimity. The trial judgment records guilt on both counts 86 and 87.

Those are findings about the particular annual-income statements. The indictment's approximately $1,361,361.79 and $4,268,767.83 of additional income are not transformed into separately required numerical findings. Neither the instruction nor the general judgment establishes that every juror accepted every transaction, calculation, witness, or alleged dollar. This commitment needs no such finding.

The judge then expressly told the jury not to decide materiality. His finding outside its presence remains his finding, not the jury's. The preserved objection and this express withdrawal establish the Gaudin error. The charge's earlier use of the word material in other contexts does not silently undo that direction.

The defense contested taxable character, fraudulent purpose, knowledge, and good faith. Counsel argued that the proceeds were repayable loans rather than current profit, that development expenditures and personal guarantees supported repayment intent, and that professional advice justified the transactions and returns. Those defenses were actually presented; they were not stipulated away. A guilty verdict under the specified tax elements nonetheless rejects those defenses insofar as they would make the charged annual-income totals truthful or their falsity unknown or innocent. It does not establish that every subsidiary part of the advice narrative was false. Counsel's proposed intent-never-to-repay formulation was argument, not an additional element supplied by the Court's charge.

The critical distinction is between a dispute over whether the income figures were knowingly false, which these actual findings resolve, and a remaining dispute about whether such annual-income information could bear on the IRS's determination of income and administration of the return. The latter is not supplied by a judicial finding that Neder received every alleged dollar. The charged annual totals themselves directly serve that determination. No unpaid-tax amount, actual revenue loss, successful deception of the IRS, or particular agency response is necessary to their capacity to influence that function.

## 4. Statutory commitments common to all eight

### A. Separate construction of the fraud provisions

For the charged money-or-property fraud schemes under §1341 and §1343, materiality of the falsehood is an element. A scheme to defraud or obtain property by false or fraudulent pretenses is not established merely by finding some knowingly false but inconsequential statement associated with a transaction. The fraud language incorporates the established requirement that the deception matter to the decision it is designed to affect. The mail or wire use remains a separate statutory requirement; the communication itself need not contain the deception if it executes the fraudulent scheme.

Section 1344 likewise requires materiality in the charged scheme to obtain a covered institution's property through false or fraudulent pretenses. The statute separately describes executing or attempting a scheme to defraud the covered institution and executing or attempting a scheme to obtain its property by false or fraudulent pretenses. Reading those routes together preserves materiality in the deception at issue; the second route's use of false alongside fraudulent does not convert the whole provision into a general prohibition of every false statement to a bank. This decision does not settle every possible distinction between the two routes, their intent requirements in other settings, or the treatment of a nonrepresentational scheme absent here.

Morissette's borrowed-term principle supports reading established legal content in statutory words instead of treating the absence of an extra adjective as dispositive. Durland rejects restricting the federal scheme offense to misrepresentations about past or existing facts; fraudulent future promises can qualify. That holding does not eliminate the significance of the deception to the proposed transaction or require importing every feature of a completed common-law tort. These are specific applications of the statutes' words, not judicial creation of a new crime.

Wells remains controlling for §1014: knowing false statements or willful overvaluation plus the statutorily required purpose of influencing a covered institution do not require an additional objective-materiality element there. The seven Associates who joined Thomas's Wells construction distinguish that text from the present fraud terms. Stevens's disagreement with Wells supports materiality here but supplies no vote to overrule Wells or reopen §1014 convictions in this case.

### B. Operative materiality and jury rules

For these alleged financial deceptions, a falsehood is material when, in the transaction's context, it has a natural tendency or capacity to influence the decision of the person or institution to which it is directed. The inquiry concerns the decision and context actually targeted, not whether the lie would interest someone in the abstract. It does not require proof that the scheme succeeded, that the recipient actually relied, that property was ultimately lost, or that the falsehood was the only reason for the transaction. Those completion requirements would contradict the statutes' coverage of schemes and, under §1344, attempts. A defendant's fraudulent purpose and the representation's capacity to influence remain distinct questions.

The record calls for no exhaustive ruling on every common-law fraud qualification, every omission duty, or every unusual recipient preference. A generic statement that all criminal statutes incorporate every tort element is expressly excluded. The preserved §7206(1) materiality element rests independently on its express statutory words. The natural-tendency formulation supplies the relevant functional inquiry; Kungys's civil allocation and civil evidentiary burden do not replace Gaudin's criminal jury allocation or proof beyond a reasonable doubt.

Gaudin requires the jury to apply materiality to the facts beyond a reasonable doubt. The trial judge states the legal test but cannot withdraw its application because he regards the evidence as conclusive. Thus the tax and bank directions are erroneous; the mail and wire charges also fail to supply the required determination on the reviewed basis. Their cross-references and actual findings matter to harmlessness, but cannot sustain the court of appeals' conclusion that the statutes contain no materiality requirement.

## 5. Reviewability, method, and the strongest competing paths

### A. Common reviewability commitment

All eight distinguish this case from Sullivan's destruction of the reasonable-doubt standard governing every factual finding. The remaining specified elements here were submitted under an otherwise valid reasonable-doubt charge. Removing a particular element is unconstitutional, but it does not by itself eliminate every valid finding on which an actual-verdict inquiry can operate. Roy unanimously classified its preserved personal-purpose omission as trial error and expressly separated classification from method and result. The same distinction applies to these particular materiality errors on preserved direct review, where Chapman independently supplies the Government's burden.

The strongest contrary path is that an express direction not to decide materiality is a directed verdict on an essential component and therefore leaves nothing the reviewing court may evaluate for that component. This objection rules out appellate replacement of the missing finding. It does not require disregard of actual independent findings that effectively resolve the materiality question, or of other inferences allowed by Roy's qualified method. Express withdrawal makes the required connection harder to establish; it is not automatically equivalent to Sullivan's invalidation of the standard for all findings. This holding does not classify every omitted element, every constructive amendment, or a total denial of jury trial.

Neither Gaudin nor Richardson settles this classification by itself. Gaudin reserved universal structural-error treatment. Richardson requires unanimity on constituent CCE violations and leaves both the availability and application of harmlessness open for its distinct defect. Its reservation is preserved, not converted into a result for either case. Johnson's unpreserved-error disposition is also distinct: a Rule 52(b) fourth-condition judgment does not reduce the Government's Chapman burden here.

### B. Six-Justice qualified actual-verdict method

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, and Breyer accept that Chapman permits supported examination of the whole trial record to decide beyond a reasonable doubt whether the error contributed to the actual verdict. Necessary equivalent findings are sufficient but are not invariably the only permissible proof. The inquiry must test the charge's actual focus, the findings it required, the evidence those instructions put before the jury, and the record's material conflicts. A court may not replace this work with an assertion that enough evidence existed for a different, properly instructed jury to convict.

Uncontested and powerful evidence can support an inference; those descriptions are not an automatic safe harbor. In particular, where the omission kept the relevant question away from the jury so that the actual-verdict connection cannot reliably be made, strong evidence alone fails. Neither a defendant's silence on an element nor absence of a separately labeled defense shifts the burden. An actual dispute concerning a premise must be assessed, including whether the verdict resolved it; merely calling a dispute something other than materiality does not make it irrelevant.

Stevens's agreement here does not retreat from his Roy position. There he accepted that the lower court's general necessary-findings rule was too restrictive, but found the State's actual showing inadequate and no useful unfinished task justifying remand. Here the tax charge supplies the specific annual-income findings missing from Roy's distinct-purpose problem, and the fraud question has never received the required appellate review. His join excludes a rule authorizing independent appellate conviction on a supposedly overwhelming record.

### C. Scalia and Ginsburg's narrower method

Scalia and Ginsburg retain their published Roy constraint. The Government must establish beyond a reasonable doubt that the jury's other determinations effectively embrace the omitted element, or that the evidence and findings show the jury could not have made its actual findings without resolving what establishes that element. Their method permits review of the record to identify the meaning and necessary implications of the verdict; it does not permit a substitute appellate verdict on a remaining factual issue.

They therefore withhold assent from a general proposition allowing harmlessness without the required effective equivalent finding. They can join the common trial-error classification and the tax disposition because the narrow route succeeds there. Their strongest objection to the broader method is that an inference from unchallenged proof can cease to describe what this jury found once the instruction excluded the element from its task. Their narrower position requires the remand court to confront that objection count by count in the fraud case.

## 6. Tax application and adversarial test

**Path supporting affirmance:** The actual jury found knowing, purposeful false annual-income totals on the two identified returns. Those findings concern the very quantity the IRS must ascertain in administering the returns; the relevance is inherent in the particular subject actually found falsely stated. The findings do not leave the court to choose between an immaterial collateral statement and a statement bearing on income. The defense's loan and professional-advice arguments were directed to whether the reported totals were knowingly false, and those elements were submitted and resolved. The materiality withdrawal consequently added no unresolved factual choice necessary to these verdicts. On this specific record the Government establishes beyond a reasonable doubt that it did not contribute to the tax verdicts.

**Strongest path against affirmance:** Materiality was expressly withdrawn, taxable character and advice were contested, and no special finding states the exact amount of omitted income or the IRS's response. An appellate court cannot presume complete acceptance of the prosecution's multimillion-dollar account, or equate knowledge of some falsity with the importance of an unspecified statement. If those were the predicates needed, the Government's proof of a valid equivalent finding would fail and a new trial, not judicial factfinding, would be the remedy.

**Resolution:** The affirmative ground does not require those extra predicates. The express second element fixed the false statement as the annual total itself. No independent disputed fact about what the statement concerned remains for the reviewing court to resolve. Its capacity to influence determination of annual income follows from that actual finding's subject, whether or not the jury accepted every alleged dollar, whether or not Neder owed additional tax after other calculations, and whether or not the IRS was actually deceived. The court is identifying the legal significance of a finding already made, not deciding for itself that the proceeds were taxable. A truthful or innocently mistaken total would not satisfy the verdict's submitted elements.

This is also why the express withdrawal does not defeat the narrower Scalia–Ginsburg route: the needed equivalent is the independent finding of knowingly false annual income, not a supposition that the jury disobeyed the withdrawal and secretly answered a separate materiality question. Their rule does not require magic words where the findings actually made establish the material proposition. It would not permit affirmance merely because a generic false-return verdict and an impressive Government case existed.

All eight therefore affirm as to the preserved materiality-instruction challenge to counts 86 and 87. They reject the broader proposition that any unchallenged omitted element is harmless. The million-dollar allegations support the trial context but are not relabeled unanimous numerical findings or made indispensable to the affirmance.

## 7. Fraud application and exact remedial limits

**Path supporting immediate affirmance:** The alleged inflated appraisals and prices, concealed relationships, down payments, presales, and requests for advances plainly concern lending decisions; the jury found fraudulent schemes and use of the instrumentalities. Many such findings may effectively embrace materiality, and the same evidence may support qualified actual-verdict inferences. Receipt of funds and the scheme's scale reinforce the Government's position.

**Strongest contrary path:** The charge expressly removed bank materiality, incorporated various definitions in the mail and wire portions, permitted conviction without all details of the alleged schemes, and required neither success nor actual reliance. The guilty verdicts do not automatically identify every misrepresentation accepted by every juror, or prove the capacity of each legally available scheme theory to affect the relevant decision. Purpose to deceive alone is not objective materiality. The separate §1014 findings cannot automatically supply a missing fraud element because Wells distinguishes purpose to influence from objective capacity. The defense also disputed the lenders' asserted reasons for making loans and the significance of price and relationship information. Those arguments must be read for what they actually dispute rather than erased by a blanket uncontested-evidence label.

**Resolution:** Reverse the court of appeals' no-materiality construction and vacate its affirmance of the affected mail, wire, and bank fraud convictions. Remand for the court of appeals to apply the proper constitutional harmlessness inquiry in the first instance, by affected count or by a genuinely common finding whose coverage is demonstrated. The tax result supplies no categorical answer for those different verdicts. The charge's cross-references may support a real equivalent finding, but the explicit materiality withdrawal and alternative scheme descriptions must be addressed together. The Government may rely on legally available evidence and findings; no new factual stipulation or admission is imposed on Neder.

For the six Justices accepting the qualified whole-record method, the court may sustain an affected fraud conviction only if the Government meets Chapman through a reliable actual-verdict analysis. Scalia and Ginsburg would require the effective equivalent finding described above. All eight allow this initial appellate task because the court below disposed of the statutory element question against Neder and never performed it; unlike Roy's record-specific dissenting position, there is an identified unresolved adjudicative task.

If an affected conviction cannot lawfully be sustained under the applicable harmlessness rule, it must be set aside, with retrial permissible under correct instructions. This is instructional-error relief, not a ruling of evidentiary insufficiency or an acquittal. No automatic new trial is ordered for every fraud count, and no automatic reinstatement, release, restitution amount, or sentence calculation follows. Any lawful resentencing or dependent disposition must follow the surviving convictions and the actual mandate; the Court does not decide those questions in advance.

The mandate neither overturns nor newly validates the separate §1014, conspiracy, or racketeering counts. Claims about a fraud predicate's consequences for another count remain subject to their own availability, actual dependence, and proper disposition below. No acquittal is reopened. The judgment entry is used for its validated tax linkage, not to resolve peripheral OCR discrepancies or reconstruct unvalidated individual fraud-count results. The affected fraud convictions are identified by the three statutory categories and the judgment under review; assembly must not invent an additional count disposition from an OCR range.

**First sources:** His Wells dissent; his Roy concurrence in part and dissent in part; his Gaudin join; his Jones joins and published broader jury position; his Mitchell join. ### O'Connor

**First sources:** Her Wells and Gaudin joins, Roy Part III join, Jones dissent join, and Johnson joins. Her Wells position prevents assuming materiality simply because a statement is criminalized; the operative fraud vocabulary supplies the additional ground here. Her Jones position concerns legislative classification, not permission to remove an admitted or properly construed element.

### Scalia

**First sources:** His Gaudin and Sullivan opinions; his Roy separate writing; his Wells join; his Jones opinion and relevant joins; his Richardson join. His statutory method reads fraud as a legal term with established content. His own Gaudin opinion prevents treating materiality as a judge-only legal issue, and Sullivan prevents replacing a missing valid jury determination with an appellate verdict. ### Kennedy

**First sources:** His Gaudin and Wells joins; Roy Part III join; Jones dissent; Mitchell join; Richardson dissent. His Richardson view that the series could be the relevant element does not imply that a judge may decide statutory materiality or that the unanimity error there is harmless.
