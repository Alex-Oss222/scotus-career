## Reconciled commitments and precise disposition

Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer retain Path A: the coordinated enacted provisions authorize EEOC compensatory awards, with all applicable substantive damages conditions preserved. They reject the Seventh Circuit's no-power ground and support **vacating the reviewed compensatory-damages judgment and remanding** for consideration of whether Gibson's remaining arguments were properly raised and, if so, their resolution. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas retain Path B and would **affirm the reviewed component** because the new damages authorization does not unambiguously permit an administrative award divorced from the action and any-party jury conditions. Neither group awards damages or resolves the disputed exhaustion and counseling predicates.
