## Competing statutory paths

**Individual-violation elements:** The statute requires a felony violation that belongs to a continuing series of violations. Each violation is independently unlawful conduct, rather than merely a piece of evidence explaining how an agreed single act occurred. Reading the series to require agreement on its constituent offenses preserves the ordinary jury protection of elements and avoids allowing different jurors to convict on materially different criminal episodes. The enterprise features add requirements; they do not make the unlawful episodes interchangeable evidence. No agreement on every evidentiary detail of each agreed violation follows.

The strongest contrary argument is that Congress punished the continuing drug enterprise, not a specified collection of separately charged transactions, and the supervisory and substantial-income requirements already distinguish it from isolated offending. That argument explains the statute's target but does not establish that the independently criminal violations disappear into one undifferentiated means element. It has particular force against requiring a detailed organizational chart or agreement about every person, but those questions are not presented.

**Series as one course-of-conduct element:** Congress can make a continuing criminal business the object of an offense while permitting transactions to prove its existence. A unanimous finding that the defendant committed the required continuing series, with the separate management, concert and resources elements, may therefore be enough without a unanimous selection of transactions. Schad permits genuine alternative means, and Gaudin governs actual elements rather than deciding the antecedent classification.

The strongest objection is that this aggregation allows the jurors to disagree about every concrete offense. Schad's historically grounded single-homicide alternatives supply no automatic authorization for modern, separate crimes on different occasions. The Associates taking the course-of-conduct path nevertheless regard the substantive, temporal and enterprise connections as a sufficient legislative definition of the series here; they do not endorse unlimited legislative relabeling or a generic offense embracing unrelated crimes.

## Error classification, application and mandate remain separate

The preserved error is not governed by Johnson's fourth plain-error condition. Gaudin supplies the jury right for elements but reserves general structural classification. Roy rejects automatic reversal merely from omission of an element and permits a qualified actual-verdict inquiry for its personal-intent category. Sullivan concerns destruction of the governing reasonable-doubt standard for all findings. Neither case by itself resolves the distinct express permission to disagree about the series here. Griffin's supported-lawful-alternative rule expressly leaves an independent unanimity defect outside its operation.

Two supported remedial paths must be distinguished. Richardson proposes reversal for a new Count Two trial because express permission to disagree leaves no valid unanimous series finding; the absence of an equivalent finding is his additional argument. The Government proposes initial appellate consideration of harmlessness. A third path—this Court declaring the error harmless from the evidence summarized in the briefs—lacks adequate support. Evidence of many drug sales is not a finding that the jury unanimously chose the same sales, and the section 846 agreement is not three completed section 841 transactions.

The remand path survives because the Seventh Circuit decided no harmlessness issue, the governing decisions do not automatically classify this precise defect, and the positive materials neither identify a unanimous triplet nor establish every possible implication of the complete charge and verdicts. The mandate can permit first consideration of availability and, if available, application of harmlessness without directing that the defect is harmless-reviewable. It does not decide that an inquiry necessarily will fail, require a new evidentiary proceeding, or make research incompleteness a failure by the Government to meet its litigation burden.

## Authority treatment and source limits

- Section 848 is construed to require common agreement on the violations composing the charged series; its other conditions and the distinction between elements and evidence remain intact.
- Schad is distinguished at the specific boundary between permissible means for a traditional offense and independent episodes composing a modern federal series; neither its result nor an exclusive universal test is overruled.
- Gaudin is applied to identified elements and its classification reservation preserved.
- Rutledge's separation of the agreement and series requirements is applied; its duplicate-conviction remedy is not an unpresented ground for reopening Count One.

- Jones supports careful statutory identification of elements without supplying a universal constitutional maximum-fact rule. Mitchell reinforces the distinction between sufficient lawful evidence and proof of no actual contribution; its sentencing holding is not converted into a unanimity rule.
