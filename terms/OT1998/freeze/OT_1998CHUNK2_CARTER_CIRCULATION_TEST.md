## Formulation actually tested

Keep the proposed directly supported personal-interest holding fixed: both respondents possess their own interest in the resident-authorized private use of the deliberately screened home; short duration, commercial purpose and illegality do not automatically defeat it. Test vacatur of the two reviewed state judgments insofar as they conclusively dispose of the federal suppression claims through the observation determination, with remand for source-accurate actual-vantage adjudication under that personal-interest rule and Fields.

The mandate would keep trial findings, testimony, appellate characterization and the factual-correction arguments distinct. When rejecting an appellate factual characterization would matter, the deciding court must address the governing factual-review and preservation objections. It may instead state why the constitutional answer remains the same even assuming the disputed additions. It would command no new hearing, photographic reconstruction, discovery, public-access burden, factual finding, curtilage classification or automatic evidence outcome. Ordinary state procedure would govern remaining independent issues.

This proposed disposition reopens the federal search application. It does not conclusively decide that application against respondents, conclusively affirm it, find an independent state ground, or resolve any evidence-specific causal question. Those distinctions are material to every join test below.

**Own support and scope:** Fields I-B/II-A and his plurality join distinguish personally shared home privacy from publication; Katz and his actual Pinson join permit protected acquisition without physical entry. His Carter commitment specifically applies those grounds to a more intrusive window examination. He retains affirmative privacy, search and bounded affirmance. He accepts the relevant conditional factual-review and evidence-specific principles, but neither a reasoning join nor a judgment-only join in the proposed vacatur is supported. His general Evans/Long reservation remains separate and does not supply dismissal or a new state ground.
