## Public authorities requiring examination

**Tryforos v. Icarian Development Co., 518 F.2d 1258 (7th Cir. 1975).** Decided July 3, 1975; rehearing denied September 5. Circuit Judge John Paul Stevens wrote the opinion. It allowed a shareholder who had appeared to object under a Rule 23.1 notice to challenge dismissal with prejudice. Footnote 22 states the objecting shareholder need not have formally become a party to appeal; it separately excludes a shareholder who failed to appear. The body distinguishes a limited objection to the form of dismissal from taking over further prosecution, and evaluates which appearance requirements serve that limited role. Its treatment of a defective appeal notice and its merits conclusion that the corporation had not been served are separate. This is a direct predivergence own-writing source, not merely unnamed circuit practice. Assess its force after Marino and the actual Izumi holding, including the limited-objection/prosecution distinction.
