## Sutton v. United Air Lines — June22,1999; No.97-1943

**Posture and requested relief correction.** TenthCircuit130F.3d893 affirmed Rule12(b)(6) dismissal. The twin pilots seek reversal and remand; United seeks affirmance. The supplied “Relief Requested: Affirm” is the respondent's position, not the petitioners'. In this pleading posture factual allegations receive the appropriate favorable treatment; no safety finding is made.

**Record.** The applicants allege severe myopia: uncorrected right-eye vision20/200 or worse and left-eye20/400 or worse, corrected vision20/20, first-class FAA medical certificates and commercial experience. United uses a20/100 uncorrected-acuity hiring cutoff. The complaint alleges disability substantially limiting seeing and that United regarded applicants as substantially limited in working. Below, the court assumed exclusion from all United pilot positions for the appeal, despite United’s asserted narrower position; other pilot employment and the meaning of a class of jobs remain important. FAA qualification does not itself prove that every higher employer standard is unlawful, while an employer safety assertion does not establish actual risk.

**Issue1 — original ADA coverage and mitigating measures.** Original42USC12102(2) separately identifies actual impairment substantially limiting an individual's major life activity, a record of such impairment, and regarded-as impairment. Sections12111(8),12112 and applicable defenses separate coverage from qualification and unlawful discrimination. Bragdon(June25,1998) requires individual limitation and reserves the general mitigating-measures question. Its exact Standards headings, “Actual disability and substantial limitation of reproduction” and “Objective assessment of an ADA direct threat,” both effective June25,1998, retain their activity and TitleIII settings. Seeing is the pleaded activity here; reproduction-specific disagreements cannot be transferred without analysis.

Petitioners argue that impairment and substantial limitation should be assessed without corrective devices, supported by EEOC Appendix guidance, Rehabilitation Act continuity, legislative explanations and the statute's separate accommodation/qualification framework. Otherwise effective self-management can remove protection against impairment-based exclusion. United emphasizes the individualized present substantial-limitation text and actual normal corrected functioning; uncorrected hypothetical inability is distinct from the person's functioning. It does not press the separate contention that myopia cannot be an impairment. A corrected-state inquiry must consider any actual remaining limitations or burdens and cannot be converted into a universal rule that every treatment eliminates coverage. An uncorrected-state inquiry does not establish qualification, liability or damages.

**Issue2 — authority and persuasive weight of agency material.** The regulation’s definition and appended interpretive guidance must be distinguished. Petitioners invoke delegated TitleI authority, notice/comment handling and consistent administrative interpretation; United disputes authority to control the general definition and says guidance contradicts the statute. Chevron and current Haggar(April21,1999) require actual delegation, statutory boundaries and genuine ambiguity before deference; notice/comment and an agency label alone do not supply authority or validity. Haggar's nine-Justice framework remains governing, while its tariff-rule application is not an ADA answer. Guidance may have persuasive force even when not controlling. Do not assume a post-event deference doctrine or silently dismiss the parties’ agency argument.

**Issue3 — regarded-as working and remedial scope.**29CFR1630.2(j),(l) distinguish exclusion from a class or broad range of jobs from a single specialized job. The parties dispute whether exclusion from United’s global-airline-pilot positions shows the broader perception. Applicants point to common training and the exclusion's breadth; United points to other pilot jobs and the specialized position. Perception of an impairment is not automatically perception of substantial limitation. At pleading stage the legal adequacy of alleged scope must be separated from new evidentiary conclusions. Petitioners focus on regarded-as working; no new regarded-as-seeing theory or amended complaint is invented.

**Strongest paths and limits.** Affirmance can rest on a supported corrected-state interpretation and an independently insufficient regarded-as theory; it must answer statutory/guidance and pleading arguments. Reversal/vacatur may follow an uncorrected-state rule, a narrower conclusion that the pleaded correction burdens need examination, or a properly supported regarded-as interpretation. Each route must state which actual theory survives and why; it cannot decide qualification or safety simply from disability or FAA certification. A remand can leave justified business requirements, direct threat, accommodation, causation and relief to ordinary proceedings without ordering hiring or a trial on unsupported new facts.

**Justice-specific continuity.** Bragdon's Kennedy opinion, joined by Stevens/Souter/Ginsburg/Breyer, recognizes the impairment/activity/individual-limitation sequence while leaving mitigation open. Ginsburg’s concurrence identifies broader life effects without replacing that sequence. Stevens/Breyer favored the prior lower risk disposition but joined a bounded remand. Scalia/Thomas separately objected both to the reproductive-activity category and proof of present individual limitation; O’Connor’s narrower activity objection reserved separate limitation issues. Their shared demand for objective risk assessment does not establish a particular visual-acuity rule. All eight Associates joined Haggar’s actual-authority framework.
