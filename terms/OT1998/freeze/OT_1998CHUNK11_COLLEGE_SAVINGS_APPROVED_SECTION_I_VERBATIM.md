### Issue 1: Commercial enforcement against States

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Union Gas remains governing at its actual scope; Seminole Tribe leaves the broader Article I amenability issue unresolved and does not establish a universal prospective-only rule.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The Trademark Remedy Clarification Act expressly subjects States to the relevant Lanham Act actions. Extending retained commerce-power reasoning to this remedy is available but must be justified.
* **Other Governing Law:** Commerce Clause; 15 U.S.C. §§1122,1125(a).

### Issue 2: Waiver and constitutional property

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Welch rejects constructive consent as a substitute for a valid waiver. Boerne requires a constitutional predicate for §5 legislation.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Florida’s commercial participation is not an unequivocal litigation waiver. Injury from a competitor’s allegedly false advertising is not automatically deprivation of constitutional property.
* **Other Governing Law:** Eleventh Amendment; Fourteenth Amendment §5; Parden as limited by later waiver authority.
