## Governing law and chronology screen

**Collective Brady materiality and investigating-team responsibility**, effective Kyles,April19,1995, supplies the standard: all lawful combined impeachment, preparation, corroboration and investigation uses in the whole record; reasonable probability sufficient to undermine confidence; no requirement of probable acquittal, an itemwise threshold or insufficiency of remaining evidence. The same principle applies to the distinct punishment result. A completed material violation cannot be defeated by a second duplicative harmlessness inquiry.

**Independent psychiatric-assistance injury after surviving capital eligibility**, effective Tuggle,October30,1995, supplies its bounded eligibility/selection distinction. It is not a Brady holding, a new burden of proof or automatic sentence relief. The four relief votes and four denial votes each assess the death choice independently of capital eligibility.

**Temporal reach of ordinary AEDPA habeas amendments**, capital extension effective Carriger,April16,1998, retaining Lindh's June23,1997 rule, excludes amended§2254(d) and replacement§2254(e)(1) for the established March5,1996 application absent demonstrated Chapter154 qualification. None is supplied. Other provisions and hearing rules are not categorically decided. Carrier, Amadeo, Coleman and current O'Sullivan retain the actual default/gateway distinctions.

The now-effective **West,June14**, permits the EEOC remedy and preserves properly presented procedural alternatives; it neither defines habeas cause nor changes Brady materiality. Its separation of power, preservation and entitlement is consistent with keeping these issues separate but supplies no new gate or relief here. **Schleifer,June15**, supplies a facial-vagueness holding and a divided substantive curfew judgment, not a capital-error standard or a five-Justice general liberty test. Its public writings change no relevant Justice commitment to Kyles or Tuggle. The actual earlier-date decisions therefore require no change in cause, guilt, penalty standard or remedy. No June17 peer holding is used.

Neder, Lilly and Calderon v.Coleman's distinct error categories likewise supply no replacement for the governing Brady test. A Justice's own Coleman writing may illuminate attention to the actual penalty record; its harmlessness burden is not transferred here.

## Independent alternative-path assessment

### Cause and capital guilt

**Cause established:** case-police suppression, actual open-file use and the later completeness representation while state proceedings continued constitute the supported external impediment. Knowledge of interviews, a lineup or evolving recollection differs from access to these specific favorable contradictions. The strongest contrary path relies on counsel's existing state-habeas allegations and an unpursued discretionary discovery mechanism. Seven Justices still find those warning signs insufficient to defeat reasonable reliance in this particular represented-disclosure setting. Thomas still finds them sufficiently concrete to require the available inquiry, without assuming a request certainly would have succeeded. None treats any Brady allegation as automatic cause or a local research gap as a litigant's failure.

**Guilt confidence retained:** the five documents would substantially improve preparation and challenge the supposedly disinterested mall witness. Counsel's closing concessions are not sworn proof or a limit on the disclosure counterfactual. Nevertheless the independent evidence of joint active fatal participation, vehicle/property connections and Tudor's contested account remains sufficiently distinct from that witness's mall reconstruction to retain confidence under the actual instruction, which did not require Strickler alone to drop the rock. The strongest guilt-relief argument is that changing the abduction narrative changes the whole structure of the case. All eight continue to reject a sufficient probability of a different guilt result after considering that use, not merely counting remaining evidence. The invalid instructed predicate is not converted into a newly reviewed standalone instruction claim. The corrected post-guilt and penalty references neither strengthen nor weaken what the guilt jury heard.

### Jury penalty selection: materiality

The **relief path** begins with the witness's trial account of spontaneous aggression and relative roles and the separately verified jury-closing use of grabbing a stranger and abducting her in the danger presentation. Disclosure would allow a substantially different challenge to the witness's identification confidence, reconstruction, chronology and observations, together with different preparation concerning whether her apparently independent account reliably assigned Strickler that role. This is an actual evidentiary-and-argument connection, even though the jury-directed references were brief and the more extensive20R15 passage was not jury advocacy.

The qualification that the criminal record contained no convictions involving actual violence prevents reliance on a prior-violent-conviction history as an independently established foundation for the same aggression inference. It does not affirmatively prove low dangerousness. The convicted crime's brutality, the69-pound rock, joint active participation, victim-property evidence and Tudor's account remain weighty evidence supporting death. The relief path nonetheless finds a reasonable probability of a different individualized punishment when the jury's distinctive account of an aggressive encounter with a stranger is assessed with the five documents available. That result may differ from guilt because participation sufficient for capital conviction does not settle relative culpability or the discretionary death choice.

The strongest objection is that the specific jury references were brief, the killing itself establishes extraordinary violence, and the suppressed materials concern observations before the killing rather than direct evidence of the fatal act. The four relief positions accept those facts. They do not infer juror reliance from20R15, require the State to disprove every effect, or treat absence of prior violent convictions as dispositive. Their conclusion rests on the combined change to the supported trial account and its documented penalty use, sufficient to undermine confidence in punishment without requiring that a lesser sentence be more likely than death. No particular childhood-abuse testimony, jury rejection of mitigation or independent judicial sentence is part of that conclusion.

### Jury penalty selection: no materiality

The **denial path** gives the same collective impeachment and preparation uses full effect, including the qualified criminal-history characterization. It treats the actual jury closing as making the supported brief abduction references rather than the longer leadership presentation. The jury's independent evidence of active fatal conduct and the manner of killing, with the supported surrounding evidence, still retains enough force in the individualized punishment assessment that the changed mall-witness presentation does not undermine confidence in the death result. Criminal history is considered only within its established, qualified content; it is not characterized as a series of violent convictions.

The strongest objection is that the apparently disinterested account supplies a distinctive basis for inferring the defendant's aggression and future danger, especially without proved prior violent convictions, and the effect of impeachment cannot be measured by closing length. The four denial positions agree that these are material considerations and that selection differs from eligibility. They find the additional uses insufficient in the whole supported case, rather than treating brevity, one valid aggravator or legally sufficient guilt as an automatic answer. No unspecified jury mitigation or curative judicial role supplies their conclusion.

### Judicial sentencing and the proposed remand alternative

A distinct theory based on20R22 or20R57–58 would require the relevant state sentencing procedure, the scope and preservation of that application of the existing Brady claim, the judicial evidence and the judge's actual function. The remarks alone do not establish a new violation, lawful independent reweighing, or cure of a jury injury. All eight decline to select this theory as a sufficient ground. That limited conclusion does not adjudicate every properly presented judicial-sentencing contention on a complete record.

The alternative of a new in-world proceeding merely to obtain missing transcript or mitigation information is rejected. No modeled disposition relies on the exact unverified jury mitigation, complete history exhibits or independent judge-role proposition. The record correction therefore does not itself warrant vacatur for source collection, discovery, a hearing or remand. The four proposed remands below implement an independently found jury-selection Brady violation; the four denial positions require no further proceedings on that claim. If assembly proposes a materially broader ground, the exact missing predicate must first be supplied through a new clean source handoff.

## Remedy, joins and source-dependent limits

The four relief commitments identify a **jury death-selection injury**. Their proposed mandate reverses the reviewed penalty Brady denial and returns for a conditional writ directed to the death sentence: within a reasonable period fixed by the district court, Virginia must provide a lawful sentencing disposition that corrects that injury or a lawful nondeath disposition. If the condition is not met, the death sentence must be vacated under the writ. The valid convictions and independent imprisonment remain; no acquittal, guilt retrial, immediate release, fixed deadline or automatic life term follows.

This mandate does not prescribe a particular jury or judge procedure or hold that a judge-only reconsideration necessarily can or cannot cure the established injury. The governing state procedure and constitutional requirements determine the permissible corrective mechanism. Nothing in20R22 or20R57–58 supplies that law. A final opinion may not add an independently sufficient judicial-sentencing violation, claim that the original judge cured the jury injury, or mandate a particular new tribunal without further legal and factual support. The actual jury injury is the legal basis for relief; further source acquisition is not.

The other four would affirm denial of the penalty claim on their stated grounds. All eight reject a new proceeding solely to solve the operator's source gap. None reopens proportionality review, a standalone instruction claim, withdrawn ineffective assistance, disputed disclosure of2/7/8 or the unverified recantation through this refresh.
