# Franklyn — relevant-conduct completeness validation

Section 1B1.3(a)(1)'s offense connection applies to both the actor/aiding route in (A) and the joint-activity route in (B). The attributed conduct must occur during the offense of conviction, in preparation for it, or while attempting to avoid detection or responsibility for it. The [official archived 1995 Chapter 1 text](https://www.ussc.gov/guidelines/2015-guidelines-manual/archive/1995-ch1web) confirms that shared condition and the distinct scope of subsection (B).

The matching ammunition, coordinated possession and supported assistance concern the same offense episode. Part V requires actual assistance for subsection (A), and agreed scope, furtherance and reasonable foreseeability for subsection (B). Neither presence nor foreseeability alone suffices.

Franklyn's commerce holding in Part II supplies the section 922(d)(1)/section 922(g)(1) supply-and-recipient mechanism that enters Griffin. Part V's sentencing rule supplies no additional Griffin premise, vote or remedy. The Public Projection contains the complete rules and their applications.
