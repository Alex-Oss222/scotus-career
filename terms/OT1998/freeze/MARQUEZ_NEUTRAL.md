## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Ninth Circuit affirmed summary judgment for the Screen Actors Guild on the fair-representation claim and treated the separate thirty-day employment-period contention as committed to the National Labor Relations Board.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Marquez argues that membership language conceals employees’ statutory rights and that a federal representation claim permits resolution of the grace-period issue. SAG answers that statutory wording has a settled limited meaning and that an independent NLRA dispute belongs to the Board. Actual misleading notice or coercive collection differs from the clause’s facial wording.
