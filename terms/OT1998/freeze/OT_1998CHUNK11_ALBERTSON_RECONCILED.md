## Reconciled result and reach

All eight non-Stone Justices retain the judgment commitment to **reverse the Ninth Circuit and return the case for proceedings consistent with the employer’s entitlement to summary judgment on the reviewed claim**. The dispositive ground is the employer’s right under the ADA to insist on the otherwise applicable federal vision criterion despite this experimental waiver program. No trial, renewed accommodation hearing, damages determination or employment order follows. “Return” implements the reversal; it does not reopen the sufficient legal ground or command additional factfinding.

All eight support the federal-standard rationale below. O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg also support a severable correction of the lower court’s actual-disability method. Stevens and Breyer withhold that adjudicative disability-method join. The disability discussion is unnecessary to the judgment; it creates no independent employee-loss judgment and no freestanding remand. The regarded-as alternative remains undecided and cannot overcome the sufficient qualification ground.

The judgment and these joins match the historical non-Stone alignment. Full-source review adds the material qualification preference and reservation Thomas actually expressed; it does not erase his full support for the common dispositive rationale. No supported changed premise warrants changing a judgment vote.

## Exact propositions and join limits

The following formulations control this handoff. They retain the independent propositions while clarifying their historically supported boundaries.

**Q1 — Separate statutory elements.** Actual disability, regarded-as coverage, factual driving skill, the lawful application of a federal qualification, and reasonable accommodation are separate inquiries. Disability does not establish qualification; good performance of one job does not negate every substantial limitation in seeing. Cleveland preserves ordinary ADA qualification proof and the statutory accommodation inquiry. Its SSDI explanation requirement has no trigger here. This proposition does not assign every challenge to an employer’s qualification standard to the employee’s prima facie burden.

**Q2 — The experimental-waiver ground.** The applicable bilateral acuity regulation remained a substantive federal safety standard. The 1992 waiver scheme collected data to determine whether that standard should be changed; it did not rest on a completed evidentiary determination establishing a substantively revised safety standard. In this setting the ADA did not require Albertson’s to participate in the experiment or justify the Government’s own otherwise applicable standard afresh merely because an individual experimental waiver was available or later issued. This is a legal distinction between the general standard and this program, not a finding that Kirkingburg was an unsafe driver. It establishes permission to insist on the standard; it does not hold that every waiver holder was legally forbidden to drive or that every employer had to reject a waiver.

**Q3 — Application and remedy.** Kirkingburg did not meet the ordinary acuity standard. His driving history, the initial erroneous certifications, and the eventual actual waiver do not remove the employer’s Q2 entitlement. Leave to obtain this experimental waiver would not require the employer to abandon that lawful criterion. Reverse the appellate judgment for implementation of employer summary judgment on the reviewed claim. The conclusion does not depend on treating an unpossessed waiver instrument, incomplete accommodation terms or an unreported pleading as a failure of litigant proof. It does not resolve a separately enlarged post-waiver cause of action or find the alternative-job offer reasonable.

**Q4 — Direct threat, time and reservations.** The employer need not independently prove a direct threat merely to invoke Q2. That conclusion does not decide whether every employer-created safety standard must satisfy the statutory direct-threat test or every relationship between §§12112(b)(6), 12113(a) and 12113(b). Bragdon’s objective, individualized method remains intact in its proper setting. A study authorization is not itself a conclusive determination that a participant poses no safety threat. Neither later success nor later invalidation supplies knowledge the employer possessed in November 1992. The program’s contemporaneous substance suffices without declaring it invalid, resolving Hobbs Act jurisdiction, or invoking the separate defense in 29 C.F.R. §1630.15(e). Other waiver systems, the later exemption regime, and an amended substantive federal standard remain open.

**D1 — Individual substantial limitation.** Seeing is an accepted major life activity and amblyopia an impairment in this case. Section 12102(2)(A) still requires substantial limitation of this individual’s seeing. A significant difference in manner is not necessarily a significant restriction. Complete blindness or utter inability is unnecessary. Monocularity covers persons with different residual vision, adjustment and practical restrictions and cannot itself replace the individual inquiry. This imposes ordinary statutory proof, not an onerous new evidentiary burden or a presumption against monocular claimants.

**D2 — Natural compensation.** Actual functioning includes the body’s own compensating mechanisms and remaining restrictions. Natural adjustment is neither categorically irrelevant nor necessarily sufficient to defeat disability. Depth perception, field of vision and the condition, manner, severity and duration of the actual limitation matter. Successful truck driving does not alone establish unrestricted seeing. This case decides natural compensation; it supplies no governing general rule about medication, corrective devices or every external mitigating measure. The historical opinion’s same-day Sutton premise is unavailable here, while Bragdon’s individual method and express mitigation reservation remain governing.

**D3 — Application not finally resolved.** D1–D2 correct categorical method without determining Kirkingburg’s ultimate actual-disability status. The record’s visual limitations are retained; no final finding of inadequate proof is entered. The lower court’s separate regarded-as route is not adjudicated. A manager’s impairment description is not converted into a conclusive statutory finding or a separate defeat of that route. Both disability paths remain immaterial to the judgment because Q2–Q3 suffice. EEOC definitional regulations may be used as presented without settling their validity or deference. No new quantitative test, expert-evidence mandate or disability remand is adopted.

**T — Thomas’s additional qualification preference and reservation.** Thomas prefers the alternative conclusion that the federal requirements bear directly on whether the employee is a qualified individual: lawful interstate truck driving is essential to the position, the ordinary federal regulation binds driver and employer, and requiring participation in this experimental program is not a reasonable accommodation that the ADA compels. He also joins Q1–Q4, provided the rationale leaves open that threshold significance of federal requirements. Q1’s separation of inquiries cannot be used to foreclose his alternative. This is Thomas’s supported additional ground, not a shared eight-Justice holding, a new proof default, or a holding that later-issued waiver participants could never lawfully drive. Its legal work should remain represented if the assembled rationale otherwise suggests that federal requirements matter only as an employer defense. This handoff neither assigns nor requires a particular writing form.

## Alternatives, uncertainties and assembly boundary

The employee-favorable path remains substantial: an actual waiver authorizes driving, good performance supplies individual evidence, and a private qualification ordinarily requires ADA justification. It fails here because this particular program had not substantively replaced the Government’s safety criterion; the legal entitlement does not depend on proving the employee unsafe. That answer is shared by all eight and does not require universal resolution of direct-threat defenses or invalidation of the waiver program.

A disability-only employer victory is unsupported: the individual method does not decide the full evidentiary application, the separate regarded-as route remains, and qualification already disposes of the case. An employee liability or hiring order is equally unsupported. Missing originals cannot establish pleading defects, lack of preservation, absence of proof, waived defenses or unknown accommodation terms.
