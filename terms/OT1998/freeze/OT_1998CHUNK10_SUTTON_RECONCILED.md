## Reconciled disposition and rule

Retain the frozen six-Associate affirmance: O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg would affirm dismissal of the actual-seeing and regarded-as-working theories. Stevens and Breyer would reverse and remand on actual disability, leaving qualification, business necessity, direct threat, causation and relief for ordinary proceedings. They do not join dismissal of the separate regarded-as theory and need not decide it to obtain their disposition.

The affirming six assess the individual’s actual condition with measures actually used, including their beneficial and adverse effects. Correction is no categorical exclusion and does not erase the impairment. Here the complaint alleges20/20-or-better vision and functioning identical to those without the impairment when corrected. The pleaded constant dependence on lenses does not establish an additional actual substantial limitation inconsistent with those allegations. On working, assume the activity’s status and require the alleged perceived restriction to reach a class or broad range of jobs; exclusion from the specialized global-airline-pilot category does not suffice on the presented allegations, even accepting the lower court’s assumption that United rejected them for all its pilot positions. The absence of broader alleged perception is the defect; actual work elsewhere does not conclusively establish what United believes.

Stevens and Breyer assess the severe underlying visual limitation without the lenses, distinguish coverage from qualification and rely on the statute’s protection/accommodation structure with persuasive administrative and legislative materials. They retain individual substantiality; not everyone who benefits from glasses automatically qualifies. Their remand reinstates the actual-disability theory and orders neither hiring nor damages.
