## Event

UNUM Life Insurance Co. of America v. Ward, No. 97-1868. Argued February 24, 1999; decided April 20, 1999. On writ of certiorari to the Ninth Circuit, 135 F.3d 1276. Ward sought insured disability benefits under ERISA after late proof of claim and communications through his employer. The questions concern California's notice-prejudice rule and the separate employer-agency ground used in reversing UNUM's summary judgment.

## Participation

Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer participated in the consideration and decision.

## Public Action

The judgment is affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded.

## Judgment & Remedy

| Component | Disposition | Vote |
|---|---|---|
| Notice-prejudice and reversal of insurer's summary judgment | Affirmed; remand for actual prejudice | 9–0 |
| No-relation premise for the employer-agency instruction | Reversed; any material agency issue must be reconsidered under the corrected rule | 9–0 |

**Supporting both components:** Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer.

UNUM must establish actual prejudice; lateness alone or possible prejudice does not suffice. No benefits award or actual-agency finding is entered.

## Opinion Topology

Justice Ginsburg delivers the unanimous Opinion of the Court, joined in full by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer. The common coalition controls both holdings and the complete remand.

## Holdings

### Notice-prejudice is saved insurance regulation

**Holding and operative rule:** California's insurance-specific rule prevents an insurer from forfeiting coverage for late notice unless it proves actual prejudice and is saved by ERISA §514(b)(2)(A) as applied to this insured policy. The rule informs Ward's federal benefits action under §502(a)(1)(B); it creates no independent state enforcement remedy and establishes neither absence of prejudice nor entitlement to benefits.

**Authority:** Ginsburg's unanimous Opinion of the Court controls this holding and the agency holding below, including their qualifications and remedy.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court applies the insurance-saving clause to the rule's operation. Metropolitan Life preserves regulation of the insurer-insured bargain. Notice-prejudice restricts an insurer's forfeiture defense and thus directly governs that bargain. The common-sense insurance inquiry and the McCarran-Ferguson considerations support that conclusion: the rule is directed to insurance and integral to the policy relationship. Those considerations are guideposts, not three indispensable conjunctive elements; the Court need not decide whether this rule independently transfers or spreads risk. Pilot Life rejects a competing state enforcement remedy, whereas Ward invokes ERISA's own benefits action. The written deadline cannot nullify a substantive insurance rule that Congress saved. Travelers and De Buono preserve federal control of plan administration but do not erase that express reservation. John Hancock likewise requires respect for compatible insurance obligations while preserving genuinely conflicting federal commands; no command requiring absolute forfeiture is shown here. UNUM's uniformity argument therefore does not justify preemption of this policy term. The record still requires the actual-prejudice inquiry. Self-funded plans, the deemer clause's distinct protection and other timing rules are not decided.

### Mandatory administrative agency cannot rest on a no-relation premise

**Holding and operative rule:** A state rule assigning the employer an insurer-agency role because it performs benefit-plan administration directly connects with that administration under ERISA §514(a); the Ninth Circuit's contrary no-relation premise and contingent instruction cannot stand. The categorical administrative reassignment may not be used here, but the decision does not preempt every ordinary agency principle or resolve a properly presented voluntary or fact-specific agency undertaking.

**Controlling explanation:** Travelers and De Buono distinguish remote economic effects from direct commands to administration. A mandatory rule making an employer the insurer's agent for notice and claims processing reallocates administrative responsibility itself. That is materially different from the saved content of an insurance contract. Ward disputes the breadth of Elfstrom and invokes ordinary agency principles; the Court accordingly does not declare that every application of California agency law is an irrebuttable employer-agency rule. Nor does it find actual authorization, apparent authority or their absence. Ward did not press insurance saving for this agency theory, so the notice-prejudice holding cannot silently supply that distinct argument. Reversal corrects the legal premise, not the disputed agency facts. If actual prejudice makes agency material, the lower courts must assess any properly presented theory under the corrected rule, giving the policy's written-authorization provision and the legal source of the asserted agency their proper effect. No general federal agency code or automatic ineffectiveness of notice to the employer follows.

## Precedent Treatment

- Metropolitan Life Insurance Co. v. Massachusetts preserves substantive insurance-contract regulation; the saving clause covers this forfeiture limitation.
- Pilot Life Insurance Co. v. Dedeaux excludes a competing state enforcement scheme; Ward's §502 benefits action presents a distinct question.
- Travelers and De Buono distinguish indirect economic effects from direct regulation of benefits or administration; that distinction separates the two state rules here.
- John Hancock Mutual Life Insurance Co. v. Harris Trust preserves compatible insurance obligations while requiring attention to actual federal conflict.
- Hughes Aircraft Co. v. Jacobson distinguishes entitlement, administrative function and remedy; it supplies neither automatic benefits nor an additional state cause of action.

## Law After Decision

Insured-plan benefits may incorporate California's saved actual-prejudice requirement through ERISA's federal enforcement channel. A separate mandatory reassignment of plan-administration agency cannot survive on the ground that it has no connection with the plan. Actual consensual or fact-specific agency, when properly presented, remains a distinct inquiry. No precedent is overruled.

## Separate Writings

No separate writing is issued.

## Procedure After Action

The case returns for determination of actual prejudice from late notice. If prejudice makes agency material, the lower courts must apply the corrected relation rule to the actual theory and policy provision. They may not reuse the rejected categorical allocation. The Court awards no benefits, finds no agency and authorizes no independent state damages action.
