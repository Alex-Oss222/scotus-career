## Event

Maryland v. Dyson, No. 98-1062. Certiorari granted June 21, 1999, to review 122 Md. App. 413, 712 A.2d 573 (1998), following denial of discretionary review by Maryland's highest court on October 9, 1998. The accepted question is whether the Fourth Amendment automobile exception requires an additional case-specific exigency, including insufficient time to obtain a warrant, when a readily mobile automobile is supported by probable cause. There was no plenary argument.
