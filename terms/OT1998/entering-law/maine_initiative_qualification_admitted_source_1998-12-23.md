## Event

Maine Secretary of State determination on the initiative petition at issue in Hart v. Gwadosky, December 23, 1998. This is an administrative source event.

## Participation

The Secretary of State made the determination. The Court takes no action through this source entry.

## Public Action

The Secretary found the resubmitted petition valid for submission to the Legislature.

## Judgment & Remedy

No judicial judgment or remedy is entered. The determination accepts 47,406 signatures against the applicable 42,101 threshold.

## Opinion Topology

No judicial opinion or vote accompanies the administrative determination.

## Holdings

The source establishes no Supreme Court holding.

## Precedent Treatment

No precedent is altered.

Existing constitutional and statutory law remains unchanged; the initiative's administrative qualification is established.

No judicial separate writing accompanies the determination.

## Procedure After Action

Qualification permits submission to the Legislature under Maine Constitution Article IV, part 3, section 18. Later legislative or electoral action is not established by this determination. Its effect on the pending judicial controversy remains for judicial resolution.
