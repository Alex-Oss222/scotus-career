## Event

Central State University v. American Association of University Professors, Central State University Chapter, No. 98-1071. Certiorari order, March 22, 1999, on review of the Ohio Supreme Court's federal equal-protection judgment concerning public-university faculty workload standards and collective bargaining. No oral argument precedes this order.

## Participation

Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer participate.

## Public Action

The petition for a writ of certiorari is granted on whether Ohio's exclusion of public-university faculty workload standards from collective bargaining lacks a rational basis under the Equal Protection Clause. No vote count is announced.

## Judgment & Remedy

The grant brings the stated federal question before the Court. It does not itself reverse the judgment below or determine an independent state-law question.

## Opinion Topology

Unsigned certiorari order; no opinion accompanies this access action.

## Holdings

The grant establishes no substantive holding.

## Precedent Treatment

No precedent is altered by the grant.

## Law After Decision

The governing law remains unchanged pending disposition of the federal question.

## Separate Writings

No separate writing accompanies this order.

## Procedure After Action

The case proceeds to the Court's separate summary merits decision of the same date. The grant itself orders no argument or further briefing schedule.

## Source Notes

The reviewed Ohio decision is reported at 83 Ohio St. 3d 229, 699 N.E.2d 463. The federal question does not include adjudication of independent Ohio constitutional protections.
