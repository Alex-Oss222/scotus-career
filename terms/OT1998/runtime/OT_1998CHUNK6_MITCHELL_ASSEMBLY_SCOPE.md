# Mitchell — form and propositions fixed before the assignment count

One signed plenary lead is appropriate. Part I states the bounded rule that the accepted plea and trial-limited colloquy do not waive privilege concerning expressly reserved quantity while genuine sentencing incrimination remains. The plea and actual admissions remain usable; Brown's voluntary-testimony rule and the factual-basis inquiry retain their distinct operation. All nine positions support that operative rule and explanation. No general immunity, permanent privilege or new waiver finding is supplied.

Parts II and III state the six-Justice crime-facts no-inference rule, the contribution-based Chapman assessment and the bounded sentencing remedy. Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support those parts. O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas join only Part I; they defend this inference independently of waiver and do not join error or relief. Their conditional acceptance of lawful relief under a contrary controlling error rule adds no reversal vote. Stone's affirmative-evidence remedy fits the actual reliance and existing Chapman rule; it supplies no new burden or separate jury/classification ground.

The formal reviewed appellate judgment is reversed in the sentencing respect; the sentence is vacated and the cause remanded. The guilty pleas remain intact. There is no fixed replacement term, automatic floor, acquittal, jury order or automatic elimination of unaffected components. Stone's lawful acceptance-reduction burden qualification is retained without deciding an actual remorse or acceptance claim; immunity-backed testimony remains unresolved and voluntary statements remain usable.

One partial dissent states the three inference-permitted positions while joining Part I. A distinct Thomas dissent preserves his willingness to reconsider Griffin and Carter in an appropriate case, a position Scalia does not share here. No extra Scalia concurrence is created from his rejected provisional departure; his published Jones range position remains intact without an unsupported new inference application. The common lead can be delivered by any full six-Justice supporter, and is not split to affect eligibility or counts. No author is selected in this pre-count determination.
