## Component commitments and sufficient grounds

All eight Associates reach the developed official-use claim through the chiefs' concrete operational injury and exposure under the supply prohibition. The association satisfies Hunt for this prospective relief. United Food preserves concrete member injury; it does not itself waive any participation condition in this different statute. No individualized damages or unproved private-possession injury is necessary. Craig's directly regulated supplier relationship supports assertion of the affected officers' equality interest; compliance by the chiefs is the very act that withholds the challenged benefit. No universal third-party or associational standing rule follows.

All eight reject the asserted irrationality of withholding section 925(a)(1)'s government-use exception from the domestic-violence-misdemeanor provisions while retaining it for the felony provision. Domestic-violence misdemeanants are not a suspect class. Congress may address a practical gap left by the older felony scheme and state felony restrictions without rewriting every pre-existing exception at the same time. The enacted class consists of offenses involving force or attempted force or threatened deadly-weapon use within specified domestic relationships, with the counsel, jury-where-entitled, conviction-relief and express-firearms-reservation qualifications retained in the neutral supplement. It is rational to prevent governmental issuance from defeating this newly enacted disqualification. Official employment does not logically eliminate the conduct-based concern.

The strongest contrary path is the lower court's comparative-culpability conclusion: a less serious domestic offense produces a heavier official-use disability, some States still allow felons to receive official weapons, and relief practices differ. Heller and Romer require a relationship to that actual disadvantage; repeating public safety in the abstract is insufficient. Here the regulatory gap and the newly covered category explain why Congress removed this route of access when it extended the disability. Congress need not deem misdemeanants more dangerous than felons, and imperfection at the older boundary does not establish irrational prejudice or a bare wish to harm. Variations among States limit the completeness of the solution without defeating the fit of the targeted rule. This is an analytical defense available under conceivable-ground review; it is not attributed to the government's original brief or to invented legislative findings. Beach and Lee Optical permit incremental action; Heller's actual confinement-burden holding and Romer's blanket exclusion remain controlling within their materially different applications.

All eight reject the association's claim that personal arms protection itself requires access to the government-use exemption. Love's personal-right rule governs; the government's collective-only premise is rejected. The supplied claim seeks an exception permitting government issuance to persons with qualifying adjudicated domestic violence, not recognition that ordinary private keeping is categorically outside the guarantee. Neither the personal right nor necessary acquisition establishes an unconditional right to armed public employment, to receive a government's weapon, or to exemption from an otherwise applicable conduct-based restriction. The government's restriction here is tied to the qualifying violent conduct and closes the official-issuance route for that category; the fact of employment does not establish immunity from that justification. The Court may sustain the refusal of this particular official-use exception without deciding every application of section 922(g)(9) to an individual's private arms, every misdemeanor disqualification, or a universal scrutiny tier.

**Arms alternative tested:** Personal bearing can occur at work, so an official setting alone cannot extinguish the right, just as Rybar's commercial setting did not. A categorical assertion that all government-related arms activity is unprotected is therefore rejected. The adopted limited ground concerns the asserted entitlement to an exemption for the qualifying conviction category and its supported public-safety justification; it does not deny personal invocation, treat all convicted people identically, or rest only on the absence of a property right to employment. No independent individualized private-possession claim or record is developed in the source, and none is created for remand.

All eight would reverse the appellate judgment extending the official-use exemption and remand for a judgment consistent with sustaining the challenged exclusion on the developed official-use claim. No exception is judicially expanded or removed. Section 928 does not select a different result after the claimed constitutional defect is rejected. The unadjudicated question whether never-lost civil rights count as restored is unnecessary and remains unresolved; no statutory restoration construction is imposed. The original undeveloped Commerce, Tenth Amendment and broader private-possession theories are neither substantively decided nor revived by a generic remand. No tally or remedy is supplied for a claim not reached.
