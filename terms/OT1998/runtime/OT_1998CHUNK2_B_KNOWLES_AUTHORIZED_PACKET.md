## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Iowa Supreme Court affirmed Knowles’s conviction under a state rule authorizing a full search incident to issuance of a citation.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Iowa relies on arrest authority for the traffic offense and officer-safety concerns during the encounter. Knowles distinguishes the citation actually issued from a custodial arrest and denies that speeding evidence could justify the full car search. A particular protective search requires its own grounds.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

Authority to arrest may or may not suffice for the claimed exception; a holding requiring actual custody defeats this search absent another established justification. Reversal addresses the search basis, not immunity or all roadside protective measures.
