### Issue 1: Administration of state-mandated changes

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** *Lopez v. Monterey County*, 519 U.S. 9 (1996), holds that a covered subdivision must obtain preclearance before administering a change even when a noncovered State mandates the policy and leaves no local discretion. *Young v. Fordice* confirms that state-mandated changes remain governed by *Lopez*.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Administration of state-mandated voting changes in covered jurisdictions; Section 5 coverage designations and prospective nonuse.
* **Other Governing Law:** Voting Rights Act §§ 4 and 5.
* **Material Other Authority:** *Bossier Parish* concerns substantive preclearance questions not reached here.

### Issue 2: Prospective nonuse and a genuinely judicial interim plan

* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Judicial interim plans under Section 5.
* **Other Governing Law:** Voting Rights Act § 5 and the district court's equitable authority.
* **Material Other Authority:** *Connor v. Johnson* supplies the limited comparator for a necessary plan actually formulated by the court.
