# Humana Inc. v. Forsyth

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the Ninth Circuit’s judgment.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The RICO action may proceed subject to proof of every statutory element, causation, injury, and authorized remedy.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: McCarran-Ferguson reverse preemption

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits; no dispositive threshold issue is identified.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone reads impairment as conflict with the State’s chosen operation, not every addition of federal liability. Parallel enforcement is permissible when state law remains fully operative and no exclusive remedial bargain is displaced.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A federal RICO claim does not “invalidate, impair, or supersede” state insurance law when the federal claim targets conduct the State also forbids, leaves the state scheme intact, and adds compatible remedies rather than frustrating an exclusive state mechanism.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Nevada prohibited the alleged deception and did not grant insurers immunity or make administrative remedies exclusive. RICO therefore reinforces the same substantive policy. Greater damages alone do not establish statutory impairment. Barnett Bank’s insurance-specific exception cannot do the work here because RICO has no comparable insurance-specific subject matter. Applying Fabe therefore requires examination of Nevada’s actual policy. Nevada condemns the alleged concealed discount arrangement and does not make its administrative sanctions an exclusive substitute for private redress; the additional federal remedy on these allegations reinforces that policy rather than disabling it.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A federal claim would be barred if it required conduct state law forbids, nullified a state-approved practice, or displaced an exclusive remedial structure demonstrated by enacted law.
