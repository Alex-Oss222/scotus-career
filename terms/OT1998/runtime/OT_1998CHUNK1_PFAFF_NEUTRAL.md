## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Federal Circuit held Pfaff’s patent claims invalid because the invention was commercially offered more than one year before the patent application and was sufficiently complete for patenting.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Pfaff argues that an invention cannot be on sale until reduced to practice. Wells relies on the definite purchase order and sufficiently complete drawings as commercial exploitation of an invention ready for patenting. The dispute distinguishes an unfinished research undertaking from a completed enabling design.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

A prototype prerequisite would require reconsideration of invalidity; an offer-plus-readiness construction can sustain it on the established drawings and order. Negotiations and incomplete conceptions require distinct treatment.
