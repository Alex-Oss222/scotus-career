### Issue 1: First Amendment

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Forbes (May 18, 1998) treats a genuinely selective public-broadcaster debate as a nonpublic forum: exclusions must be reasonable for the journalistic purpose and viewpoint neutral. Supported public-interest and electoral-support judgments can satisfy that rule; ballot qualification alone does not require admission, and prospective publication of criteria is not universally required. Apply that rule to Iowa’s actual selection criteria and treatment of comparable candidates, not to a presumed policy of favoring major parties.
* **Other Governing Law:** First and Fourteenth Amendments; the broadcaster’s actual debate-selection terms and record.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** Eighth Circuit en banc, 150 F.3d 924 (8th Cir. 1998), sustained Iowa Public Television’s exclusion of two minor-party candidates from a 1996 congressional television debate using objective newsworthiness criteria.
* **Material Positions:** Candidates argue ballot status and equal speaking rights require inclusion when government hosts a debate. Broadcaster argues journalistic discretion and objective viability criteria prevent the forum from becoming a compulsory equal-time platform for every aspirant.
