## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Credible pre-enforcement injury

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Speech self-censorship qualifies only with an objectively credible threat.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** A credible threatened penalty or privately enforceable cause can chill protected speech without a completed prosecution. But no abstract suit may force state judges to predictively adopt federal statutory interpretations. The appellate opinion appears to have conflated the alleged facial relief with interpretive supervision; clarification and evidentiary review, not an invented finding of imminent threat, is the narrow remedy.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Speech self-censorship qualifies only with an objectively credible threat.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A credible threatened penalty or privately enforceable cause can chill protected speech without a completed prosecution. But no abstract suit may force state judges to predictively adopt federal statutory interpretations. The appellate opinion appears to have conflated the alleged facial relief with interpretive supervision; clarification and evidentiary review, not an invented finding of imminent threat, is the narrow remedy. The officials’ long-standing narrowing position and absence of action against WRTL weigh against an official threat, while the actual Travis proceedings and private suits against other speakers explain why the concern is not purely imagined. Maine Right to Life requires a credible application to this association’s planned speech and relief that removes the identified burden. On remand WRTL must identify that communication and remedial connection; neither self-censorship alone nor an order telling officials to obey Buckley establishes jurisdiction.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No assumption that the organization necessarily faces enforcement, no invalidation of all Wisconsin registration law, and no regulation of abortion as such is decided.

### Issue 2: Redressability and form of decree

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Facial relief differs from a federal instruction directing a state judiciary’s construction.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** A credible threatened penalty or privately enforceable cause can chill protected speech without a completed prosecution. But no abstract suit may force state judges to predictively adopt federal statutory interpretations. The appellate opinion appears to have conflated the alleged facial relief with interpretive supervision; clarification and evidentiary review, not an invented finding of imminent threat, is the narrow remedy.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Facial relief differs from a federal instruction directing a state judiciary’s construction.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A declaration invalidating an enforceable penalty provision may redress a credible threat to planned speech. An order requiring state judges to adopt a particular interpretation in hypothetical future litigation poses different causation, redressability and institutional questions. Stone would require the court to identify the defendants who can enforce the challenged obligation and the precise relief sought, then decide the live constitutional challenge if those requirements are satisfied. No federal instruction to control an unpresented state case follows. Maine Right to Life’s completed statutory holding also prevents using Stone’s preferred exclusive verbal formula as the governing rule. Context may count only within the holding’s cumulative restrictions. That boundary bears on the feared enforcement but does not itself decide Wisconsin’s distinct statute or supply a general federal injunction against future state interpretation.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No assumption that the organization necessarily faces enforcement, no invalidation of all Wisconsin registration law, and no regulation of abortion as such is decided.
