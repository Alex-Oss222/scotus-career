* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Simulated punitive-damages holdings require lawful punitive considerations and meaningful review but do not define Title VII’s statutory mental state. Title VII liability and remedy remain separate.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The D.C. Circuit denied a punitive-damages instruction because Kolstad had not shown sufficiently egregious discrimination.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Kolstad argues that knowledge of federal discrimination rights can establish reckless indifference without additional outrageous conduct. The Association seeks a more demanding threshold and invokes its compliance policy and limits on vicarious punitive liability. Liability, punitive mental state and employer attribution are distinct.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

The correct statutory standard may require remand without a present punitive award. Good-faith prevention efforts must be assessed in operation, not inferred solely from a written policy; punitive liability still requires the applicable agency and culpability predicates.
