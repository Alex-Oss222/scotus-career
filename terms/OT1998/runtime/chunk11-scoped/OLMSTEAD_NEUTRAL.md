### Issue 1: Unnecessary institutionalization and reasonable modification

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Pennsylvania Department of Corrections v. Yeskey applies Title II to all state services, programs, and activities without an institutional-custody exception. Bragdon requires individualized disability analysis and objective treatment of asserted risks.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Title II coverage of state prisons and Objective individualized direct-threat assessment, under Disability Law, confirm unqualified public-entity coverage and individualized justification.
* **Other Governing Law:** ADA Title II; 28 C.F.R. § 35.130(d) integration regulation and reasonable-modification rule.
* **Material Other Authority:** School Board of Nassau County v. Arline and the ADA’s statutory design reject exclusion based on stereotype while preserving legitimate program administration.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Eleventh Circuit held that unnecessary institutionalization can violate Title II but remanded for consideration of the State’s fundamental-alteration defense.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** The plaintiffs seek community placement after professional approval and consent; Georgia invokes institutional service responsibilities and the cost of rearranging limited resources. Title II coverage does not itself dictate every placement, and a fundamental-alteration defense requires the actual program and obligations to others.
