# College Savings Bank v. Florida Prepaid Postsecondary Education Expense Board

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Third Circuit’s immunity dismissal and remand for adjudication of the Lanham Act claim.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Permit the preserved statutory claims and remedies to be considered on their own elements; neither damages nor prospective relief is awarded at this stage.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Article I remedy

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Decide the preserved question within this proceeding; the proposed result is a position, not an entered Court judgment.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** May the Lanham Act claim proceed?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Give effect to the express congressional authorization of suit against States participating in the regulated interstate market.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Give effect to the express congressional authorization of suit against States participating in the regulated interstate market.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The alleged misrepresentations concern competing college-financing products offered in interstate commerce. A federal rule against misleading commercial representations can bind state sellers, and an expressly provided enforcement remedy prevents the regulated State from enjoying an immunity unavailable to private competitors. Union Gas permits this commerce-power starting point; Seminole does not supply the opposite categorical rule. The extension concerns enforcement of this federal commercial obligation, not unlimited power to abolish every state immunity.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Falsity, materiality, commercial injury, causation and relief remain unproved.

### Issue 2: Consent and §5

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Decide the preserved question within this proceeding; the proposed result is a position, not an entered Court judgment.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does market participation waive immunity or create constitutional property?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Reject constructive waiver and the proposed automatic constitutional-property theory.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Reject constructive waiver and the proposed automatic constitutional-property theory.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Entering a commercial market is conduct regulated by Congress, not an unequivocal election to be sued. The action therefore rests on valid congressional power and unmistakable legislation. Nor does every injury caused by false advertising deprive the competitor of property without due process. Boerne cannot be satisfied by relabeling the statutory claim as a constitutional tort.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual consent and a properly supported §5 remedy remain distinct routes in other cases.
