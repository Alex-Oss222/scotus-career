### Issue 1: Individualized disability and natural compensation

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** *Bragdon v. Abbott* requires claimant-specific proof of substantial limitation and rejects categorical treatment of every person sharing a diagnosis.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Asymptomatic impairment and individualized disability, under Disability Law, supplies the individualized inquiry.
* **Other Governing Law:** ADA definition of disability; major life activity of seeing.
* **Material Other Authority:** Actual adaptation, depth perception, field of vision, and practical visual effects are relevant to application but do not create a categorical rule.

### Issue 2: Operative federal safety qualification and experimental waiver program

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** No supplied simulated holding requires an employer to disregard an operative federal safety qualification. *Bragdon* separately governs individualized disability and employer-created direct-threat judgments.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Objective individualized direct-threat assessment, under Disability Law, remains available when an employer relies on its own risk assessment; a binding federal qualification has independent legal force.
* **Other Governing Law:** ADA qualification provisions; Department of Transportation commercial-driving vision regulation and experimental waiver program.
* **Material Other Authority:** The experimental program did not amend the operative regulation or itself confer a waiver on Kirkingburg.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Ninth Circuit reversed summary judgment for Albertson’s and allowed Kirkingburg’s disability and qualification claims to proceed.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Kirkingburg invokes his monocular condition and the federal waiver program. Albertson’s relies on the actual DOT qualification applicable to the driving job. A diagnosis, individual substantial limitation, valid safety requirement and an operative waiver are separate matters.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

A genuine qualification defect may resolve the case without finally classifying disability. A valid effective waiver would require its actual terms and timing; an experimental opportunity to seek one is not necessarily an existing qualification. Employer-created exclusions retain their own justification requirements.
