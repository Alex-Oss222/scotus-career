C. Career Development Planning Services. 

In order to promote the upward mobility of qualified 
women employees the ADA will take the following steps: 

JA-16 

1. The ADA will develop a program of Career 
Development Planning Services. The purpose of this program 
will be to assist each interested employee in achieving the 
and interests. A woman employee in the Department of 
Personne! will be designated to implement this program. This 
designated employee will be available to counsel employees 
on career development paths and potential opportunities for 
upward mobility at the ADA. Where appropriate, employees 
may be referred for management training or for other training 
opportunities that will broaden their employment potential at 
the ADA. 

The person designated to implement this program will 
either be the same person as the EEO Officer, or will work in 
close contact with the EEO Officer (see Section VI below). 
In either event, a primary focus of her work will be to 
encourage the upward mobility of women. 

The services of this program will be available to both 
non-exempt and exempt employees. 

2. Information concerning the salary grades assigned 
to each ADA position and the salary ranges associated with 
each grade will be made available by the Personnel 
Department to any ADA employee upon request. ADA 
employees will also have the right, upon request, to review 
the current job description for any position at the ADA. 

3. The ADA will continue its current policy of 
internal posting of all position vacancies prior to external 
advertisements or formal or informal recruitment. All 
persons involved in the process of selecting candidates to fill 
vacancies will be advised that pre-selection of a favored 
candidate is contrary to the ADA’s firm policy of giving full 
and fair consideration to each application. Violations of this

to provide promotional opportunities to qualified staff, all 
position openings are posted internally through inter-office 
memoranda to all employees.

staff members to apply for open positions before outside 
recruitment begins. Each ADA employee applying for a new 
position must have at least six (6) months experience in his or
