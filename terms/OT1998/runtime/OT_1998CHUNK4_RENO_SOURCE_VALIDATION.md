This source validation supplements the Reno section of [C_NEUTRAL](OT_1998CHUNK4_C_NEUTRAL.md#reno-v-american-arab-anti-discrimination-committee). It supplies no commitment, vote, new finding, procedural event or interpretation of withheld material.

The [bounded order and status extract](../sources/chunk4_c/RENO_ORDERS_AND_STATUS_EXACT.md) has been checked against that supplement's conditional source questions. The extract identifies the original appendix and predecision briefs as its sources and separates actual court language from advocacy. Its source status and legal significance are:

| Material | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| January 5, 1994 order, appendix 151a | The court preliminarily enjoined the defendant “from conducting further deportation proceedings against plaintiffs.” Read with the already validated procedural account, this is the Six's injunction. | It contains no quoted, separately articulated ruling about particular later charges, their legal basis, or application following legalization. Such applications require construction of the actual words and posture. |
| April 1996 passage, appendix 75a | The district court denied dissolution of the Six's injunction and granted the Two's renewed preliminary-injunction motion on its stated preliminary findings. It therefore supports both injunction strands and their different procedural origins. | It is not a final adjudication of discrimination or deportability. The excerpt ends during a footnote; that incomplete sentence cannot establish a complete concession, present immigration status or a final merits premise. |
| Government's legalization report | The Government states that Barakat and Sharif were granted legalization and consequently were no longer subject to deportation **based on the original status violations**. Respondents agree with that bounded status consequence. This supported agreement is sufficient under Engine §14 without demanding possession of each original legalization instrument. | Legalization here is described as adjustment to temporary resident status, not a grant of permanent residence. The report neither determines all other possible removal grounds nor establishes a currently pending replacement charge. |
| Respondents' charge-history account | It separately describes the Six's visa-related charges and the Two's political-association and later material-support charges. It asserts that the injunction bars new charges against Barakat and Sharif and that the INS has not disavowed seeking all eight persons' deportation. | The claimed new-charge protection is respondents' interpretation of the order, not additional operative language. Alleged absence of disavowal is not a government concession of a concrete planned charge, a filing, or a finding of an imminent individualized threat. The contention that all plaintiffs could reside permanently absent the political charges remains advocacy. |

**Effect on readiness:** The actual January injunction wording, retention/extension of the two strands, and the agreed loss of deportability on Barakat and Sharif's original status grounds are now sufficiently supported. The earlier conditional need to retrieve those particular terms and that bounded status consequence is resolved. No originals-possession requirement remains for them.

The sources do not conclusively resolve individualized mootness or the injunction's application to hypothetical new charges. A model may analyze the actual wording and the parties' supported positions, and may choose a lawful jurisdictional or preliminary-relief disposition without deciding those remaining questions. It must not invent replacement charges, treat respondents' non-disavowal allegation as proof of an imminent threat, or declare legalization dispositive of every possible live claim. If an ultimate disposition requires a finding of an actual new charge or particular threatened action, the precise charge or threat evidence remains the material source needed; this extract supplies none. That limited reservation does not block the already supported jurisdictional alternatives or require further retrieval merely to preserve issues not reached.

All operative-law, chronology-refresh and participation requirements in C_NEUTRAL remain. This validation adds source support only and changes no adjudication or commitment.
