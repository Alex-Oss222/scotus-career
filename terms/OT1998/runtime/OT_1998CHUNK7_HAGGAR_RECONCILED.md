## Reconciled commitments

**All eight Associates agree on the review framework:** The Federal Circuit may not categorically exclude the duly promulgated tariff regulation from Chevron review. The Court of International Trade's own-record factfinding and correct-decision duties do not remove the legal force of a regulation adopted within Congress's delegation. Sections 1500(b) and 1502(a), the tariff delegation provisions, and the distinction between judicial facts and governing legal norms support the framework. Section 10.11's preservation of judicial review does not direct courts to disregard a lawful regulatory construction. Agency identity and notice and comment alone do not prove statutory authority or ultimate validity.

**Six Associates — O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer — vacate and remand:** The regulation's actual reasonableness and its application remain for the Federal Circuit or Court of International Trade under the proper framework. These six do not join the remodel's direct qualified-validity holding. A court must enforce the statute's enacted conditions and protected incidental operations, then determine whether the agency reasonably filled the remaining category. The lower courts' mistaken categorical framework left that inquiry unresolved. The operation's significance, primary purpose, relation to assembly, chemical improvement and treatment of ordinary pressing bear on the regulatory/statutory boundary itself, not merely on a separable duty calculation. These are the identified unfinished inquiries; the remand is not justified by an omitted grant or a generic preference for narrow decisions.

**Stevens and Ginsburg sustain the regulation and its application and would reverse outright:** In their view, the agency permissibly identified significant chemical treatment imparting a new characteristic as outside incidental assembly operations. On the supported facts, ovenbaking the resin-treated fabric activates the lasting permanent-press property. That is a meaningful category distinction from ordinary pressing and a permissible application of the delegated line. The introductory regulatory terms and statutory examples remain operative; neither any chemical effect nor the mere use of heat is an automatic disqualification. But no concrete changed legal or record premise supports requiring a fresh application adjudication of the denial already under review. They reject the categorical no-deference judgment and would reverse it, sustaining the challenged denial rather than directing a new validity/application inquiry.

This is a six/two remedial difference among the Associates, not an eight-Associate vacatur or a ruling that the regulation is invalid. All eight reject categorical exclusion of Chevron. The six-member route leaves regulation validity open; the two-member position approves validity and the actual denial. No particular duty amount, new documentary defect, universal permapressing prohibition or unrelated shipment issue is adjudicated here.

## Why the independent remodel changes on reconciliation

The remodel offered a substantial direct-decision argument: the legal texts distinguish ordinary finishing from significant production of permanent chemical characteristics, so a court could sustain that category while returning application. That argument must be considered, but it is not a materially different premise from the one the six historical remanding Associates had. The historic Court knew that the second question was granted, quoted the same introductory regulatory terms, acknowledged the ambiguity and the agency's delegated authority, and still found the statutory reasonableness and process-specific objections intertwined. Correcting this repository's scope omission cannot explain why a Justice would now change that assessment.

Nor does describing the new validity ruling as qualified establish a changed premise. The historical remand already preserved statutory incidental operations, the relation between the regulation's general rule and examples, and the possible inconsistency of particular applications with the statute. The remodel's distinction between significant chemical improvement and ordinary pressing is legally serious; it does not remove the dispute about where this actual operation falls or establish that the agency's category fares reasonably under the statute in all relevant respects. Resolving the category before examining that interaction could prejudge a portion of the returned inquiry.

The earlier simulated decisions were tested for a genuine change. MCI and NCUA enforce actual statutory limits rather than displacing Chevron. NationsBank recognizes bounded, reasoned classification; the historical Haggar Court expressly relied on NationsBank's reasonable-gap-filling proposition, so invoking that proposition does not identify a newly displaced premise. AT&T supplies both direct decisions and corrected-framework remands: its distinct superior-quality and new-combination components return unresolved rule-specific objections after the authority question. It creates no presumption that a granted legal question must be decided immediately and no rule that validity is always separable from application. NFFE requires a real agency choice after a particular legal premise changes; there is no corresponding finding here that Customs thought the statute compelled this rule or that its explanation must be discarded. It therefore supplies neither automatic validity nor a compulsory new agency proceeding. Each Justice's own positions on these authorities are addressed below.

For Stevens and Ginsburg, the independent application-only remand rests on an asserted lack of every shipment-level or regulatory-predicate finding in the supplied account. That is an operator-source limitation, not evidence of a changed actual record, unsettled additional claim or different Customs determination. The established process facts support the precise chemical-treatment line they historically accepted. The second question is now confirmed rather than removed. Their actual historical separate position, evaluated against those facts and their simulated respect for reasoned agency classification, remains stronger than an unspecified need for more application work. Outright reversal does not require this Court to calculate duty amounts or resolve unknown collateral controversies.

This conclusion does not mechanically adopt a historical count. It rejects the claimed bases for divergence after testing them against the actual earlier positions, record and rule. The legal permissibility of a proposed independent route is not alone sufficient evidence that a particular Justice would take it when the same-matter historical position directly addresses the same premises.

## Operative qualifications and authority

The six Associates' controlling-ready framework retains every tariff condition: United States fabricated components exported ready for assembly without further fabrication, retention of physical identity, and no foreign advancement in value or improvement except through assembly and incidental operations such as cleaning, lubricating and painting. The benefit is a partial duty reduction based on qualifying component cost or value, with the enacted proportional treatment for specific or compound rates, not duty-free entry of the whole article.

Section 10.16(b) permits incidental operations before, during or after assembly and includes final pressing and folding. Section 10.16(c) addresses a significant nonassembly process whose primary purpose is fabrication, completion or physical/chemical improvement, or a significant process unrelated to assembly. The alternatives are not collapsed. Its chemical-treatment examples cannot silently erase its introductory terms, and the statutory examples remain binding. The six do not decide the ultimate interaction as applied to this operation; Stevens and Ginsburg find the actual chemical-treatment distinction reasonable and sufficient here.

No new hearing, agency proceeding, burden, deadline or evidentiary demand is imposed. The six return the unresolved validity/application questions through the ordinary appellate channel under the correct framework. They neither approve the claimed exemption nor uphold Customs' denial in advance. Stevens and Ginsburg's stronger disposition sustains that denial on the identified ground without new duty arithmetic.

Chevron supplies the framework; NationsBank supplies reasonable gap filling within actual statutory limits. MCI and NCUA remain limits, not general anti-deference rules. AT&T and NFFE retain their distinction between authority, particular validity and actual lawful agency choice without importing their distinct statutory remedies. No precedent is overruled. The eight-Associate framework should be separated from the six-Associate remedial ground and the two-Associate direct-validity/application position. A substantive separate writing is needed for the latter if the Court adopts the six-Associate disposition; no author is assigned here.
