Subject: 
TRANSFER OF PATIENTS FROM THE CRITICAL 
CARE COMPLEX TO ANOTHER FACILITY 
PURPOSE: 
To provide guidelines for the safe and efficient transfer of 
patients from the critical care complex to another facility. 
To assure that all patients who have an emergency medi- 
cal condition are transferred in accordance with the 
Cobra requirements. 
POLICY STATEMENT: 
All patients who transfer from the Critical Care Complex 
to another facility and who are considered to have an 
emergency medical condition, will be transferred in 
accordance with the requirements established by COBRA. 
Transfers will be made using good medical practice and 
for the benefit of the patient. No patient will be trans- 
ferred arbitrarily. Transfers will be made for medical, not 
economic, reasons. 
Transfers can take place only after the transferring physi- 
cian has received approval for transfer by the patient/ 
patients’ family and the accepting physician. 
The Critical Care Complex will secure 
a written, 
informed consent prior to transfer. This consent must 
include the risks and benefits of the transfer. 
When transferring a patient to another facility, the follow- 
ing must accompany the patient: 
1. 
copies of all medical records related to the emer- 
gency condition and available at the time of 
transfer; 
2. 
Copy of the patient’s request for transfer or the 
physician’s transfer certification; and, 
3. 
The name and address of any on-call physician 
who refused or failed to appear within a reason- 
able amount of time to provide necessary stabi- 
lizing treatment. 
All patient requests for transfer must be in writing and 
must include a statement of the Hospital’s obligations 
and the risks of transfer. 
Social Service will be contacted prior to the transfer of 
any patient from the Critical Care Complex to another 
facility. Social Service will make the ambulance arrange- 
ments. 
Air ambulance transfers are arranged through Social Ser- 
vice, using only authorized medical air transport services 
who supply the medical flight personnel and the equip- 
ment/supplies.
