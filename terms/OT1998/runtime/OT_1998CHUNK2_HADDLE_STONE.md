# OT1998 chunk 2

# Haddle v. Garrison

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Eleventh Circuit’s judgment and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The district court shall adjudicate the remaining conspiracy, intent, causation, and damages elements. No liability finding is made.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Statutory injury from retaliatory interference with at-will employment

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits; no dispositive threshold issue is identified.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the statute according to its own elements and rejects conversion of a state-law at-will rule into immunity for a federally forbidden retaliatory conspiracy.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Loss of at-will employment caused by a conspiracy to deter or retaliate against federal testimony is an injury to person or property within § 1985(2), even though the employee lacked a constitutional entitlement to continued employment.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** At-will status defined the employer’s ordinary power to terminate; it did not authorize third parties to procure termination for a federally prohibited object. Haddle alleged concrete lost wages and employment caused by the conspiracy, which is enough at the pleading stage.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not create tenure, eliminate causation and intent requirements, or make every wrongful discharge a federal civil-rights claim.
