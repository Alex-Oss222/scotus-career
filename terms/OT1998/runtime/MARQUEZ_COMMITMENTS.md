## Component commitments

All eight Associates would affirm the judgment on the two questions under review, while leaving the separate existing notice and actual-enforcement remands intact. The Court does not affirm summary judgment on every fair-representation claim and orders no new remand on the reviewed facial theory.

**1. Facial membership wording.** Negotiating a union-security clause that uses the statute's membership language, without restating every controlling limitation in its text, does not by itself establish arbitrary, discriminatory or bad-faith representation. General Motors limits the enforceable membership condition to financial obligations rather than compelled full formal membership; Beck limits the exaction from objecting nonmembers to the costs of collective bargaining, contract administration and grievance adjustment. Those established constructions remain part of the clause's lawful meaning. The provision cannot lawfully authorize greater demands merely because those qualifications are not printed in the agreement.

Vaca and O'Neill govern negotiation as well as administration. They preserve three distinct grounds of breach: arbitrariness, discrimination and bad faith. Under the arbitrariness ground, the negotiated product must fall outside the broad range of reasonable choices assessed against the facts and law at the time. Reliance on statutory terminology with an established limited meaning falls within that range; the clause alone establishes neither discriminatory treatment nor dishonest purpose. Courts need not prescribe an exhaustive code of employee advice in every collective-bargaining provision.

**Strongest contrary path:** Ordinary employees may read membership in good standing as requiring full membership and all dues, and the union's exclusive position gives that misunderstanding practical force. That concern supports meaningful enforcement of actual notice and collection duties; it does not make every use of Congress's language inherently irrational or prove deception in this case. The result therefore provides no safe harbor for misleading demands, concealment of rights, coercion or actual collection beyond lawful limits. No ruling that the text necessarily supplies sufficient notice is joined.

**2. Thirty-day grace-period jurisdiction.** Affirm refusal to adjudicate the separate statutory timing theory on the supplied framing. Garmon assigns an independent section 8 unfair-labor-practice question initially to the Board. Vaca and Beck permit a court with an independently supported fair-representation claim to decide an NLRA issue necessary to resolving that claim; a party cannot create that independent basis solely by labeling a disputed statutory interpretation arbitrary. The asserted misapplication of the thirty-day period, without an identified independent arbitrary, discriminatory or bad-faith feature of representation supporting this particular theory, remains a statutory administration dispute. The Court does not decide whether the period restarts after a break, whether prior employment counts, or whether SAG's reading is correct.

**Strongest contrary path:** Beck permits courts to decide statutory questions embedded in fair-representation litigation, and actual enforcement is still being litigated here. The answer is claim-specific: an existing independent enforcement dispute does not automatically transfer every collateral statutory question from the Board. If resolving a properly supported independent representation claim requires the timing issue, Vaca and Beck remain available at that actual scope. No universal jurisdictional bar applies whenever statutory construction is involved, and no intent-only version of fair representation displaces O'Neill's separate arbitrariness ground.

**3. Existing actual-enforcement proceeding.** All eight leave untouched the Ninth Circuit's reversal of summary judgment on actual enforcement. The source reports a genuine dispute about whether SAG told Marquez she had to join or had to pay. Neither version is now found true; facial validity of the terminology supplies no factual resolution or liability defense for unlawful implementation. No damages, lost-job causation or restitution award is entered.

**4. Existing notice proceeding.** All eight separately preserve the Ninth Circuit's remand for consideration of the unaddressed Beck notice claim. The district court had not ruled on that claim. The Court neither finds adequate notice nor orders a particular notice or determines liability. The source specifically grounds this procedural remand in Beck; no additional General Motors notification judgment is invented. This component is not the thirty-day theory and is not absorbed into the enforcement factual dispute.
