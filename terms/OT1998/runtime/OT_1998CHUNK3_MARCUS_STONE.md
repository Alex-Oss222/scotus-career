## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: First Amendment

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Nonpublic designated election debate; selection must be viewpoint-neutral and reasonable as to purpose, grounded in actual indicators not arbitrary party status alone.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Received *Forbes* directly governs the official network’s editorial debate. Selecting realistic viable participants using reasonable indicators, without excluding a candidate because of her policy views, preserves expressive format rather than opening a mandatory public forum. Neither public ownership nor ballot qualification alone converts every debate into open access.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A state-owned broadcaster may exclude ballot-qualified minor-party candidates from a nonpublic candidate debate on reasonable, genuinely viewpoint-neutral evidence of electoral viability.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Received *Forbes* directly governs the official network’s editorial debate. Selecting realistic viable participants using reasonable indicators, without excluding a candidate because of her policy views, preserves expressive format rather than opening a mandatory public forum. Neither public ownership nor ballot qualification alone converts every debate into open access.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No permission for hostility-based exclusions, manipulation of criteria to favor incumbent parties, or blanket exclusion of all minor-party speakers where a particular debate’s facts show genuine viability.
