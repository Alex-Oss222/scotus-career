# OT1998 chunk 2

# Knowles v. Iowa

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Iowa Supreme Court’s judgment and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The Iowa courts shall suppress the fruits of the citation-only search and conduct any further proceedings consistent with the remaining admissible record.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Full search after a traffic citation

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits; no dispositive threshold issue is identified.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the exception according to its justifications. A citation permits the limited precautions supported by particularized safety concerns, but not an automatic evidentiary search.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Issuing a traffic citation, without custodial arrest, does not authorize a full search incident to arrest; an officer needs consent, probable cause, particularized protective grounds, or another recognized exception.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Knowles faced no custodial transport, the speeding offense supplied no destructible evidence in the car, and the officer identified no particularized danger. The state rule therefore converted a narrow exception into a general search license.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding leaves intact protective orders to exit a vehicle, a Terry frisk based on reasonable suspicion of danger, and searches supported by independent probable cause.
