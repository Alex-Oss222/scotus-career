## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Tenth Circuit invalidated the registered-voter, badge, and disclosure provisions challenged here.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Colorado invokes traceability, fraud prevention and voter information to support registration, contemporaneous name badges and individual paid-circulator reports. The challengers identify reduced speakers and exposure of advocates, and point to affidavits, addresses, subpoena authority and sponsor reporting. Each requirement’s additional enforcement value and speech burden require separate treatment.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

One provision’s validity does not decide the others. The court may invalidate unsupported exclusions or disclosure burdens while retaining lawful sponsor, aggregate-finance and verification measures. Any replacement must follow statutory severability rather than a judicially drafted initiative code.
