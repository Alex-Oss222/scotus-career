# NAACP neutral supplemental source receipt

**Event:** November 5, 1998; No. 97-1840. No outcome or commitment is supplied here.

The full Ninth Circuit opinion, including all six footnotes, was read through the published FindLaw reproduction: https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-9th-circuit/1257053.html (December 16, 1997; 131 F.3d 1317; No. 96-56455). Its procedural recitation identifies a Rule 12(b)(6) complaint seeking declaratory and injunctive relief. Lindner is a former judicial candidate; the other plaintiffs are voters and voter associations claiming limited wealth. It describes no damages demand or concrete future candidacy. The District Court dismissed Jones for lack of pleaded acts and County defendants for lack of injury in fact. The Ninth Circuit read the statewide challenge as encompassing Jones and expressly assumed standing to reach the merits (footnotes 3–4). This is the lower court's stated method, not an established Article III finding.

The challenged sample-ballot statement has 200 words. Section 13307 permits the Board to decide each election cycle whether participating candidates reimburse actual printing costs, permits estimated advance payment, forbids profit, and requires adjustment after the election as necessary. It does not fund the election itself. Section 13309 excuses indigents from advance payment but permits later billing of their pro rata costs. These plaintiffs expressly exclude an indigent claim; state-law claims are unpreserved. No discriminatory racial intent or viewpoint favoritism is alleged in the published account. No compulsory ballot-access fee is challenged.

**Material threshold limit:** The supplied record does not establish whether the actual complaint adequately alleges a named plaintiff's continuing or impending personal injury redressable by prospective relief. Former candidacy must not become an invented planned candidacy; likewise incomplete published recitation is not proof that the complaint contains no such allegation. This uncertainty affects adjudicative reach rather than the constitutional validity of the arrangement.
