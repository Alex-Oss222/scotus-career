## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Criminal Law and Sentencing

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Threshold death/abatement precedes statutory lesser-included-elements analysis; the two questions must not be conflated.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** The live-controversy defect is dispositive. The merits were briefed and submitted but the Court cannot announce a binding §2113 rule solely to resolve a dead petitioner’s appeal. This is not a reversal of the Third Circuit or an acquittal on the merits.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The Court does not decide whether bank larceny is a lesser-included offense of bank robbery in a matter in which the petitioner died before the Court could issue a merits judgment.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The live-controversy defect is dispositive. The merits were briefed and submitted but the Court cannot announce a binding §2113 rule solely to resolve a dead petitioner’s appeal. This is not a reversal of the Third Circuit or an acquittal on the merits.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The statutory elements question, conviction-abatement consequences in other proceedings, and any collateral rights remain outside this limited certiorari dismissal.
