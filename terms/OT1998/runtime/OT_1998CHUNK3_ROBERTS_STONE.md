## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Health-Care Programs

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Text’s conditions: emergency medical condition, hospital awareness/stabilization, safe transfer exceptions, statutory remedy and causation.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Unlike ordinary medical malpractice, EMTALA creates express stabilization and transfer duties once statutory conditions are met. Its stabilization text does not demand improper purpose; Congress specified actual transfer exceptions instead. Inferring a motive limitation from anti-dumping purpose would add a missing element and alter burden of proof.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** An EMTALA stabilization claim does not require proof that the transferring hospital acted with an improper motive; liability follows only if the enacted duty and remedy predicates are proved.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Unlike ordinary medical malpractice, EMTALA creates express stabilization and transfer duties once statutory conditions are met. Its stabilization text does not demand improper purpose; Congress specified actual transfer exceptions instead. Inferring a motive limitation from anti-dumping purpose would add a missing element and alter burden of proof.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No decision on whether this hospital had an emergency condition within the statute, actual stabilization, whether an exception permitted transfer, or causation and damages.
