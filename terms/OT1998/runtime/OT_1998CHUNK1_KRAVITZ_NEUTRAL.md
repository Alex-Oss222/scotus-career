### Issue 1: Securities Regulation

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Central Bank (April 19, 1994) preserves separate primary and culpable-assistance routes within the private securities action, requiring the underlying fraud, qualifying scienter, reliance, causation and loss. It does not convert a broker’s breach of care into fraud. Here the direct theory concerns the implied representation made when accepting customer orders, so actual best-execution obligations and contemporaneous knowledge must be proved.
* **Other Governing Law:** Securities Exchange Act §10(b); Rule 10b-5; Ernst & Ernst v. Hochfelder; Basic v. Levinson; actual contemporaneous best-execution duties and market practices. Later SEC rules do not govern completed 1992–1994 trades retroactively.
