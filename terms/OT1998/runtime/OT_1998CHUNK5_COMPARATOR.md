# Federal Republic of Germany, et al. v. United States, et al.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Leave to file the original complaint and preliminary relief denied, 7–2; printing requirements waived.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam. Souter, joined by Ginsburg, concurred without relying on Eleventh Amendment principles and considered the Solicitor General’s position. Breyer, joined by Stevens, dissented and would grant a temporary stay for fuller briefing.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Certiorari granted, Ninth Circuit judgment summarily reversed, and its injunctive order vacated; 8–1 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam.
