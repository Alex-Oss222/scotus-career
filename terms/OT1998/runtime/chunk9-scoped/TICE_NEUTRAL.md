### Issue 1: Nonparty preclusion through shared interests

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Richards v. Jefferson County (June 10, 1996) rejects preclusion of nonparties through shared interests and awareness alone, absent a legally recognized relationship and adequate, accountable representation. Here common employment, a similar challenge and shared counsel do not themselves establish control, an authorized representative relationship or a valid class. The earlier result’s precedential force is distinct from preclusion against these pilots.
