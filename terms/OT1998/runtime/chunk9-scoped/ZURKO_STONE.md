# Dickinson v. Zurko

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Federal Circuit's judgment and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The Federal Circuit shall identify and apply the proper APA § 706 category to the PTO's factual findings on the whole record. The Court does not decide whether § 706(2)(E) or § 706(2)(A) governs this particular proceeding or the ultimate patentability question.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: APA review of PTO factual findings

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the standard-of-review assignment for direct appellate review of the PTO.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** The PTO is an agency, and §§ 559 and 706 establish the general court-agency review scheme. The Federal Circuit may not substitute the clearly-erroneous standard used for district-court findings merely because pre-APA patent practice used a different label.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Direct Federal Circuit review of PTO factual findings is governed by the applicable APA court-agency standard, not the clearly-erroneous standard for district-court findings. The lower court must determine and apply the proper § 706 category on the whole record.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** *Allentown Mack* confirms the whole-record discipline once the correct APA category is selected. Enacted administrative-law text displaces the Federal Circuit's court-to-court analogy.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not decide ultimate patentability, the precise § 706 category, legal conclusions, or review of district-court findings made on a new evidentiary record.
