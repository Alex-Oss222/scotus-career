# Richardson v. United States

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the Seventh Circuit's judgment and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Richardson's continuing-criminal-enterprise conviction shall be set aside unless it may lawfully be sustained through proceedings consistent with the element-specific unanimity rule, subject to ordinary double-jeopardy and retrial principles.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Unanimity on the continuing-series violations

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches whether the particular violations are elements or merely alternative means.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Section 848 uses 'violations' to refer to independently criminal acts and makes a continuing series of them part of the greater offense. The jury must therefore agree on the same qualifying violations, though not on every evidentiary detail or means of committing each agreed predicate.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Each violation necessary to constitute the continuing series under § 848 is an offense element. The jury must unanimously identify the same qualifying violations beyond a reasonable doubt, though it need not agree on every evidentiary detail or means of committing an agreed predicate.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A conviction without agreement on concrete predicate crimes would permit jurors to find different criminal series and still return one verdict. Statutory text, traditional unanimity, and constitutional avoidance reject that result.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not require unanimity on every overt act, item of evidence, participant, or means used to commit an agreed predicate violation.
