# Cunningham v. Hamilton County

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the Sixth Circuit’s judgment.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The interlocutory appeal remains dismissed. Counsel may obtain review after final judgment or through another independently authorized extraordinary route if its demanding requirements are met.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Collateral-order appeal of a Rule 37 sanction

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the merits; no dispositive threshold issue is identified.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the final-judgment rule without creating a professional-status exception for attorneys. The sanction’s factual and legal basis overlaps the discovery dispute, and payment or reputational concern does not destroy later review.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** An order imposing a Rule 37 sanction on counsel during ongoing litigation is not immediately appealable under the collateral-order doctrine when the ruling is intertwined with case management and can be reviewed effectively after final judgment.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The sanction did not conclusively resolve an immunity or right whose value would be lost by trial. Later appellate review can vacate the order and direct repayment or other correction.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not foreclose § 1292(b), extraordinary mandamus, immediate review after counsel becomes a genuine nonparty through final withdrawal in materially different circumstances, or review of contempt carrying independent finality.
