## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Nonparty preclusion through shared interests

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Richards forbids binding unrepresented nonparties solely by shared litigation interests.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** The identity of the employer policy does not create a representative relationship between separate claimants. Under *Richards*, foreclosing these pilots by a prior loss requires legally meaningful control, authorized representation, an appropriate class or recognized privity. Shared work, concern or legal theory is not enough, and later plaintiffs remain subject to the substantive prior holding’s persuasive force without becoming parties to it.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Richards forbids binding unrepresented nonparties solely by shared litigation interests.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The identity of the employer policy does not create a representative relationship between separate claimants. Under *Richards*, foreclosing these pilots by a prior loss requires legally meaningful control, authorized representation, an appropriate class or recognized privity. Shared work, concern or legal theory is not enough, and later plaintiffs remain subject to the substantive prior holding’s persuasive force without becoming parties to it.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Does not decide the airline’s BFOQ defense, deny effect to judgments against actual parties, erase properly certified class judgments, or create a universal venue presumption.

### Issue 2: Separate ADEA merits and venue defenses

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Rejecting preclusion leaves the statutory employer defenses and §1404 venue ruling separate.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** The identity of the employer policy does not create a representative relationship between separate claimants. Under *Richards*, foreclosing these pilots by a prior loss requires legally meaningful control, authorized representation, an appropriate class or recognized privity. Shared work, concern or legal theory is not enough, and later plaintiffs remain subject to the substantive prior holding’s persuasive force without becoming parties to it.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Rejecting preclusion leaves the statutory employer defenses and §1404 venue ruling separate.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Once virtual representation is rejected, the airline remains entitled to prove its actual ADEA defenses concerning the position, qualifications and any legally sufficient BFOQ. The pilots’ right to be heard is not a finding that the staffing rule violates the statute. Venue likewise requires the actual §1404 considerations and review of the decision made below; shared counsel or a prior case elsewhere does not establish preclusion and cannot substitute for the transfer analysis. Stone would leave the separately resolved venue issue undisturbed absent a preserved legal error and remand the live discrimination claims for their own elements.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Does not decide the airline’s BFOQ defense, deny effect to judgments against actual parties, erase properly certified class judgments, or create a universal venue presumption.
