### Issue 1: APA review of PTO factual findings

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** *Allentown Mack Sales & Service, Inc. v. NLRB* confirms whole-record review of agency factfinding against the agency's announced substantive standard and does not create a patent-law exception to the APA.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Whole-record review of agency factfinding.
* **Other Governing Law:** Administrative Procedure Act §§ 559 and 706; patent-review statutes.
* **Material Other Authority:** Pre-APA patent cases do not unmistakably preserve a court-to-court clearly-erroneous standard for direct review of agency findings.
