### Issue 1: Truthful casino advertising

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** *Rubin v. Coors Brewing Co.* treats statutory and regulatory contradictions leaving comparable incentives untouched as evidence that suppression of truthful commercial information does not materially advance the asserted interest. *Ibanez v. Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation* likewise requires evidence and fit. 44 Liquormart retains ordinary Central Hudson review, with separate majority support for material advancement and proportional fit. It rejects a categorical demand for numerical studies or the least restrictive alternative. Edge Broadcasting remains a record-specific approval of a meaningful restriction and must be distinguished rather than treated as overruled.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Truthful commercial information and evidence-based fit.
* **Other Governing Law:** First Amendment; 18 U.S.C. § 1304; *Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corp. v. Public Service Commission*.
* **Material Other Authority:** The challenge is as applied to truthful advertisements for lawful private-casino gambling in Louisiana.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Fifth Circuit upheld the federal broadcast restriction against the casino broadcasters’ as-applied challenge.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Broadcasters challenge suppression of truthful lawful casino advertising while comparable gambling promotion is permitted. The government invokes gambling-related harms and support for state policy. The scheme’s actual exceptions and alternatives bear on material advancement and fit.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

A failure of justification permits relief for the described advertisements without invalidating every gambling regulation or every application of the broadcasting statute. A coherent, supported restriction requires its own analysis; inconsistent exceptions are evidence, not an automatic formula.
