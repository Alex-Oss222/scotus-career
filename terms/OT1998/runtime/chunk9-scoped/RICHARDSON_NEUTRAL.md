* **Controlling Simulated Law:** No supplied simulated holding construes 21 U.S.C. § 848's continuing-series requirement. *United States v. Gaudin* preserves jury determination of every offense element.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Controlling Reasoning Below:** The Seventh Circuit affirmed Richardson’s conviction despite an instruction that the jury need not agree on the same three predicate violations.
* **Material Positions and Limits:** Richardson seeks jury agreement on the specific violations comprising a continuing series. The government treats the individual violations as means of a single series element. The statutory structure and unanimity protection determine whether jurors may rely on different predicate crimes.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

An elements construction requires a verdict satisfying predicate unanimity and the appropriate response to any instructional error. It does not require every detail of each offense to be an independent element or decide all possible continuing-criminal-enterprise predicates.
