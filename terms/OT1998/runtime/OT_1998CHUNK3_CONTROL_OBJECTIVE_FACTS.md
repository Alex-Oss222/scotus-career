## Lopez v. Monterey County

California submitted its 1983 additional-judgeships law to the Attorney General and included a copy of the County's 1983 consolidation ordinance. The Attorney General did not object to that submission. The preceding county consolidations and the State's 1979 consolidation law were not thereby shown individually submitted.
