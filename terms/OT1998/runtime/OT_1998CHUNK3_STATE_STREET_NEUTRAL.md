### Issue 1: Claim construction and technical findings

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Markman (April 23, 1996) assigns legal claim meaning and embedded technical-usage disputes to the judge while reserving external infringement facts to the proper factfinder. It does not decide universal de novo review of subsidiary findings. Warner-Jenkinson preserves material claim boundaries.

### Issue 2: Mathematics and business uses

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Benson, Flook, Diehr and Chakrabarty supply the inherited §101 framework: abstract ideas and mathematical principles are not patentable by themselves, while a qualifying application is assessed as a whole. No completed simulated decision resolves this financial-computation claim.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The calculation of a share price may be practically useful but remains a numerical result. The question is whether the claimed implementation supplies a patentable application rather than appropriation of the underlying financial calculation.
* **Other Governing Law:** 35 U.S.C. §§100(b),101–103; then-effective patent law.
