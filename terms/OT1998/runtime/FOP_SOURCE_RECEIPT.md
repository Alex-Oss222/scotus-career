**Verified objective source facts:** The opinion reviews summary judgment for the government. The record identifies chief law-enforcement members' operational injury and exposure for supply under section 922(d)(9). The opinion's standing analysis reaches the official-use equal-protection claim, considers third-party rights through the chiefs, and says broader theories were not developed. It states that the association concedes domestic-violence misdemeanants are not a suspect class, argues a fundamental arms right, and that the United States disputes that right's individual character. The opinion does not resolve the arms character because its equal-protection disposition uses rationality review. Its actual remedy extends the official-use exception by treating section 925 as invalid only insofar as it withholds the exception from domestic-violence misdemeanants; it invokes section 928. It leaves the separate restoration interpretation unresolved. These are descriptions of the lower court, not proposed current Court decisions.
