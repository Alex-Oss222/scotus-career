## Component-level competing paths, sufficient grounds and limits

### 1. Commerce authority and the derivative Tenth Amendment objection

**Commitment C:** Reject the challenges. Hale's possession/transfer relationship and Kirk's application to retained completed stock control. The completed .223-caliber automatic weapon is admitted prohibited stock. Wright's acquisition, retention and intended concealment do not escape the national stock regime because no sale or particular interstate movement is proved. The possession component prevents evasion or material impairment of that valid transfer-and-stock restriction. No commerce element is added to the statute. The disassembled .50-caliber weapon is not redescribed as an operable completed gun; the plea and the record identify it as a machinegun being brought for reassembly. There is no preserved claim that these admitted objects fall outside the enacted machinegun class, and no new statutory branch finding is made.

**Competing path:** Invalidate the count because possession is local, no interstate element exists and formal findings are missing. Its strongest point is that national regulation cannot create its own constitutional power merely by being comprehensive. That objection is correct in principle but fails here: Hale independently identifies the transfer/stock regime and the fitted anti-evasion mechanism, while Kirk rejects a universal transaction prerequisite for the admitted completed stock. Mere marketability, dangerousness, prior interstate movement or social costs would not suffice. No independent commandeering claim is presented; the derivative Tenth objection fails with the enumerated-power objection. No general rule that every exercise of commerce authority defeats every Tenth Amendment claim is adopted.

**Limits:** The section 922(o)(2)(A) exception covers transfers to or by, and possession by or under authority of, the United States or its departments/agencies, or a State or its departments/agencies/political subdivisions. Section 922(o)(2)(B) separately covers lawful transfer or possession of a machinegun lawfully possessed before May 19, 1986; earlier manufacture alone is insufficient. No exception is newly established. This ruling does not decide every receiver, conversion part, incomplete assembly, readily restorable weapon or wear-induced conversion. Commerce authority supplies no answer to the arms claim.

### 2. Machinegun possession and the personal arms claim

**Commitment A:** Recognize personal invocation; vacate the appellate affirmance of the single machinegun count and remand for the actual weapon-and-restriction application. The lower court required a connection to a militia actively maintained and trained by Georgia. Love and Hale forbid that prerequisite. The original opinion expressly declined to decide what justification could sustain restriction of otherwise protected activity. It therefore supplies no independent application that can survive correction of its affiliation premise.

**Tested immediate-affirmance path:** The completed .223 automatic weapon's repeated fire and the statutory restriction on additions to civilian machinegun stocks supply a substantial argument for the validity of this application. A functioning automatic weapon differs materially from an ordinary semiautomatic; the preserved older-stock exception need not imply that older guns are safer. Government channels and the stock limitation can be evaluated as actual features of the restriction. The objection to remand is strongest here: the Court can resolve law on settled facts and need not demand an empirical study or a hearing merely because a lower court used a wrong ground.

**Why that path is not adopted by these Justices here:** Each joined Rybar Part III, which considered identified completed automatic weapons, their known functional characteristics, the same stock restriction, and the argument for immediate affirmance. Each withheld that merits resolution when the lower court had not performed the personal-right application. Wright supplies no materially stronger independent adjudication of that issue: footnote 18 expressly reserves justification, and the single count also includes a disassembled .50-caliber weapon whose exact component configuration is not supplied. Wright's proposed burial and private group's stated preparedness do not establish an adjudicated criminal purpose beyond the possession offenses. The pipe bombs belong to the independent registration count and cannot substitute for justification of this possession prohibition. No prior private position is used to change these published commitments.

**Tested immediate-invalidation path:** The personal right and evidence of military usefulness favor coverage, and the prohibition substantially restricts possession. But Love, Hale and Miller do not make military suitability immunity or decide that every possession restriction violates the guarantee. Reversal with acquittal would decide the open regulatory application without a sufficient controlling premise. It is rejected.

**Remedy and joins:** The remand requires assessment of the actual completed weapon and the disassembled weapon under their proper statutory branches where necessary; constitutional coverage and the justification of this restriction remain separate. It does not reopen admitted possession or impose a new offense element. The existing record may suffice; further ordinary proceedings are permitted only as needed, with no compulsory empirical production, evidentiary hearing, new trial, acquittal, release or return of weapons. Because one count covers the machineguns, no invented second possession count or unsupported severance of the conditional plea is created. The alternative jury-instruction request returns only insofar as it remains procedurally material after lawful resolution; the Court orders no trial or particular charge. No universal scrutiny tier, categorical dangerous-arms exclusion, categorical military-arms immunity or automatic-remand rule is joined.

### 3. Independent destructive-device registration count

**Commitment R:** Affirm the section 5861(d) conviction concerning the three pipe bombs. Hale Parts II and IV supply an independent tax-administration and arms ground: personal possession and military usefulness do not exempt the holder from identification and registration supporting lawful making and transfer. This is a registration conviction, not a machinegun-possession prohibition disguised as one. The supported record establishes no inability lawfully to approve or register these destructive devices and no denied application. No hypothetical machinegun-registration conflict is transferred to the pipe bombs.

**Competing path:** Vacate this conviction too because the lower court's militia prerequisite infected its discussion of both statutes. The objection is substantial as to that reasoning, but not sufficient as to this judgment. Hale's independent registration holding reaches the precise presented objection without determining whether every pipe bomb is outside constitutional coverage. Unlike the possession application, the legal ground sustaining the registration duty is already controlling and was joined by every Associate Justice. Thus the lower court's invalid affiliation rationale is rejected while the registration judgment is affirmed.

**Limits:** Section 5841 uses registration of a transferred firearm to the transferee through the transferor; it is not a universal obligation for a possessor to seek late registration. Section 5812 requires advance transfer approval and denial when transfer, receipt or possession would violate law; section 5822 separately requires advance making approval and denial when making or possession would violate law. No statutory element, tax exemption, knowledge question or registration-impossibility defense outside the preserved issues is decided. The Court need not classify all explosive devices as constitutionally unprotected.

### 4. Independent Ninth Amendment defense

**Commitment N:** Reject the claimed independent exemption for this possession and nonregistration. The Ninth Amendment prevents the enumeration of rights from denying or disparaging other rights retained by the people; the assertion of privacy and natural self-defense does not itself establish an immunity from these otherwise applicable weapons laws. Love and Hale already distinguish personal protection from unrestricted possession and from the registration obligation. The personal-arms claim receives its own adjudication and is not rejected on the ground that a listed right exhausts all constitutional liberty.

**Competing path:** Treat the Ninth Amendment's reservation and asserted natural self-defense as an independent ground for reversal even if the specific arms application does not establish invalidity. Its strongest point is that enumerating one right cannot extinguish others. That principle does not identify the scope of the claimed immunity or establish that keeping these particular machineguns and unregistered pipe bombs is exempt from lawful regulation. The narrow rejection of the presented independent defense is sufficient. No holding that the Ninth Amendment is meaningless, that unenumerated rights do not exist, or that every privacy claim must fail is joined. No new substantive-due-process claim is invented.

### 5. Acceptance of responsibility: both independent rehearing grounds

**Commitment S1 — legal ground:** The court may not deny an otherwise warranted section 3E1.1 adjustment solely because a defendant presents a nonfrivolous legal or constitutional challenge unrelated to admitted factual guilt. Application note 2 expressly permits acceptance in the unusual case tried to preserve such a legal issue; a guilty plea reserving that issue cannot itself supply automatic disqualification. Notes 1 and 3 require examination of acceptance, not a reduction as a reward for abandoning legal review. Stinson makes consistent interpretive commentary authoritative. The legal-challenge-only alternative ground in the rehearing opinion cannot stand.

**Strongest contrary path:** Denial of a reduction is not necessarily punishment for invoking a constitutional right, and conduct accompanying litigation may reveal nonacceptance. That proposition does not authorize an exclusion inconsistent with the commentary. False factual denials, sham factual assertions or conduct inconsistent with acceptance can matter even when attached to a constitutional claim. Neither the unsuccessful legal position alone nor insistence on appellate review establishes such conduct. Wright's personal-right argument is especially not frivolous under Love and Hale; its registration implication can fail on the merits without making the preserved claim a denial of facts.

**Commitment S2 — asserted factual alternative:** The quoted record does not establish that the same denial rested on a sufficiently independent, legally valid remorse finding. The sentencing judge evaluated whether Wright's group was a lawful state-protective militia, and earlier remarks expressly focused on counsel's claimed constitutional entitlement. The rehearing majority's conclusion that these remarks amount to a lack-of-remorse finding is its interpretation of those statements, not an additional historical finding. The Court cannot convert the same legally displaced militia premise into a conclusive independent acceptance finding.

**Strongest affirmance path:** The sentencing judge saw Wright testify, doubted that he sought to carry out rather than resist the law, and could find a lack of recognition of wrongdoing notwithstanding admissions and cooperation. Genuine acceptance findings receive substantial deference, and neither the guilty plea nor the prosecutor's recommendation binds the judge. That path remains lawful if supported findings actually establish it. On these published passages, however, the judge's acceptance analysis is intertwined with the invalid affiliation premise. Koon preserves discretion within lawful rules and gives no deference to legal error. Its unanimous error/remedy treatment rejects appellate replacement reasons and mere speculation that the same sentence might recur. Resentencing therefore remains necessary independently of the still-open machinegun-count application.

**Remedy and limits:** Vacate the sentence and require reconsideration under the correct rule, coordinated with the disposition of the machinegun count. No two- or three-level reduction, particular prison term or finding that Wright has conclusively accepted responsibility is ordered. Admissions, cooperation, timely plea and the stated cessation of weapons possession are significant evidence; supported contrary conduct may outweigh them. The judge may evaluate genuine lack of remorse or insincerity on lawful grounds. This is an application of the commentary and the legal-error/deference distinction, not a new constitutional holding barring every adverse sentencing consequence connected to litigation.

## Final compatibility and joins

### Framework adopted by O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg

**Holding and operative rule:** Stage 1 asks whether the law interferes with protected keeping, bearing, acquisition, necessary ammunition or transportation of arms, with constitutional coverage determined from text, history and the Court's precedents. If protected activity is burdened, Stage 2 requires justification corresponding to the severity of the restriction's practical operation, subject to the independent categorical rule that government may not abolish meaningful possession of ordinary functional defensive arms in the home.

**Standards and operative qualifications:** A core deprivation receives strict scrutiny: the government must establish a compelling interest, narrow tailoring and the absence of a comparably effective, materially less restrictive alternative. A substantial regulation receives intermediate scrutiny: an important governmental objective, a substantial relationship between the regulation and that objective, and a restriction that does not unnecessarily or effectively eliminate the protected activity. An incidental regulation imposing no meaningful interference receives deferential reasonableness review; arbitrary administration, discrimination and cumulative destruction of the right remain unconstitutional. Routine equipment specifications, administrative procedures and minor requirements qualify for that last category only when they do not materially obstruct the right. Actual operation controls: calling an effective prohibition a safety measure or administrative rule cannot reduce its level of review.

**Controlling explanation:** Love identifies the personal holder, incorporates the guarantee and protects acquisition necessary to exercise it. Hale requires attention to the actual weapon and keeps coverage distinct from lawful regulation; military usefulness decides neither immunity nor exclusion. Rybar requires a genuine application instead of disposing of the claim through militia affiliation. Those decisions leave the measure of justification open. The Court now supplies that measure because a remand must identify how protected activity and permissible regulation relate. Text and history inform coverage and regulatory limits without creating a closed catalogue. A real individual guarantee requires protection against practical destruction, while regulation that leaves its exercise meaningfully available calls for a justification fitted to its actual burden. Public safety is important, but its invocation alone does not establish that fit. Conversely, recognizing a personal right does not subject every equipment rule or administrative step to strict scrutiny. Registration and a possession prohibition must be examined separately. The home boundary protects a meaningful core; it does not grant immunity to every weapon, person or use. Necessary ammunition and transportation receive protection as incidents of protected keeping or bearing, not through coverage of every item or transport method. That boundary, the graduated standard and the application to particular facts perform different functions.

**Reserved questions:** Prohibiting ordinary functional handguns in the home and effectively eliminating all lawful public carry illustrate core burdens; no particular public-carry law is before the Court. Public-carry licensing, safety instruction, examinations, demonstrated proficiency, refresher training and recertification, including their classification and justification, are expressly reserved. No universal numerical rule, militia exclusion, military-weapon immunity or comprehensive prohibited-arms list is adopted.

## Separate positions and their scope

**Stevens, concurring in part and concurring in the judgment.** Part A, joined by all seven other Associates, independently sustains the pipe-bomb registration conviction under Hale's tax-administration and registration rules. Registration identifies and traces lawful making and transfer; personal possession and military usefulness do not establish an exemption. No denied application or legal inability to register these devices is established, and no universal late-registration duty is imposed. The eight adopt that sufficient ground without requiring exclusion of every explosive device from coverage.

In Part B, joined by Breyer, Stevens joins the commerce, personal-invocation, Ninth Amendment and sentencing grounds and agrees that the machinegun affirmance must return for the actual arms application. Love, Hale and Rybar already require that result; they would leave selection of a comprehensive severity framework until a developed application requires it. They do not join the categorical home rule or the three tiers. Stevens emphasizes that supported legislative predictions and the particular statutory operation must receive their proper weight. Breyer shares that reservation because the competing individual and public interests must be examined in their concrete setting. Neither treats public safety as conclusive, restores a militia prerequisite, requires an evidentiary study or would disturb the separate registration conviction. Their disagreement concerns the governing framework, not any judgment component.

**Scalia, joined by Thomas, concurring in part and concurring in the judgment.** They join the same specified common portions and all dispositions, but withhold assent to Part II-B and its applications. Love's personal-holder rule and Hale's weapon-specific inquiry require a constitutional answer grounded in text, history and the actual restriction. ## Preliminary and governing commitments

**F — Fault and facial review.** All eight reject the lower court's inference that the absence of an express mental state in section 2323.31(A) itself establishes strict liability. Section 2301.21(B) expressly supplies recklessness unless the silent provision plainly indicates strict liability. Deletion of an earlier knowing requirement and retention of knowing possession in the neighboring magazine provision show different wording; they do not, without more, plainly negate the enacted default. The present prohibition therefore must be read with that default: the city must prove that, with heedless indifference to the consequences, the person perversely disregarded a known risk that the relevant prohibited circumstance was likely to exist. No federal knowledge-of-illegality requirement, universal actual-knowledge minimum or transplant of Staples's particular NFA construction follows.

The source's alternative suggestion that recklessness cannot matter is also too broad. Fault cannot repair an undefined legal boundary, but it can address an owner's ignorance of an intelligibly defined physical characteristic. Fargo and Women's Medical, each joined on these notice distinctions by all eight Associates, require separate examination of definition, fault and substantive validity. Grayned and Kolender require understandable guidance for people and officials; Hoffman Estates supplies contextual rigor, not automatic invalidity whenever criminal liability is involved. ## Definition-by-definition conclusions

**G1 — Rifle/carbine and detachable magazine.** All eight reject facial invalidation on the grounds presented. A weapon that accepts an existing detachable magazine of twenty rounds or more has the specified physical capacity whether or not its owner happens to possess that magazine. A merely imaginable magazine not shown to exist does not establish that statutory characteristic. The owner's original four-round purchase neither makes a compatible existing larger magazine impossible nor establishes awareness; the default mental state remains separate. Walker's affidavit identifies an actual thirty-round magazine compatible with his M1; his preference for five or fifteen rounds does not make the physical criterion unintelligible. Smolak's hypothetical susceptibility does not establish the actual larger-magazine fact or the required fault. The Court does not adjudicate either plaintiff's liability and supplies no duty to predict future manufacture.

The strongest contrary path combines future magazine production with a strict-liability trap. Removing the unsupported strict-liability premise and retaining proof of an actual compatible magazine answers that categorical objection. A disputed factual capacity or lack of awareness is not necessarily uncertainty about the legal rule. Neither grandfather status nor the arms merits is resolved by this notice ruling.

**G2 — Shotgun capacity.** All eight reject facial invalidation. Capacity exceeding six rounds refers to actual ammunition compatible with the shotgun and its magazine; the fact that compatible shells come in different lengths does not erase that measurable criterion. The default fault condition prevents liability merely because the owner unknowingly encountered a shorter compatible shell. The strongest alternative is that the city must specify a single measuring shell; the present physical-capacity language instead covers more than six compatible rounds of an actual length. No imaginary shell, alteration of the weapon or unknown-risk strict liability is approved. A particular disputed application remains subject to its own proof, but no remand to create a measuring standard is necessary to reject the stated facial ground.

**G3a — Modification lineage.** All eight would affirm invalidity of the two modification branches as written. The ordinance does not say whether a particular handgun must itself have been physically converted from the referenced rifle or automatic firearm, or whether a manufacturer's derived model or design resemblance suffices. The record's expert describes this concrete uncertainty; the city offered no provision-specific defense that establishes a limiting criterion. The conclusion is based on the absent boundary, not the city's silence as a litigant default. Recklessness cannot supply the missing legal relationship that makes something a modification. A person may know every observable feature and its history yet lack a stated rule selecting physical conversion, design descent or resemblance. This differs from a bounded original-design criterion and does not invalidate every statutory use of modification.

The contrary path treats every form of alteration or design derivation as clearly included. But neither an objective threshold of design inheritance nor a limitation to actual conversion appears, and selecting one would add the very criterion that criminal enforcement requires. The Court does not write such a criterion or decide a new hypothetical converted-weapon prosecution. The automatic-firearm branch also retains the ordinance's own definition: a firearm designed or specifically adapted to fire a succession of cartridges by one trigger function, and separately a semiautomatic firearm designed or specifically adapted to fire more than thirty-one cartridges without reloading, except one chambering only .22-caliber short, long or long-rifle cartridges. It is not silently confined to federal machineguns.

**Scope of G3a relief:** Physical conversion may furnish an apparently clear example, but this challenge is not resolved by listing that example and leaving the same undefined central relationship to govern every descendant design. Kolender's independent minimum-enforcement-guidance requirement addresses that defect: neither an owner nor an officer has a rule identifying which design inheritance, alteration or resemblance makes the entire handgun a prohibited modification. The ambiguity is the classification's operative criterion, not merely an unknown historical fact within an otherwise bounded rule. Its application to protected ordinary keeping and its criminal consequences justify relief against these two undefined branches as written. A federal limitation to personally converted individual weapons would select and substitute one contested category; no authoritative state construction supplies it. Thus a narrower hypothetical exemption would leave the same deficient classification governing others. This is the stated affirmative basis for branch-wide relief, not an imported Casey facial test, First Amendment overbreadth, or a universal rule invalidating every statute with an unclear edge. Ohio severability still determines whether the remaining independent definitions and dependent references can operate after those branches are removed.

**G3b — Original handgun design for more than twenty rounds.** All eight reject the lower court's derivative invalidation. This text fixes the inquiry at the handgun's original design and a detachable magazine exceeding twenty rounds, rather than every subsequent conceivable magazine. Historical proof may be difficult, but an objective design fact plus the enacted fault requirement is not constitutionally meaningless merely because an ordinary owner does not already know it. Posters 'N' Things supplies the distinction between an article's objective design and a person's relevant knowledge, without importing its complete federal knowledge rule. The claim that all design-history inquiries are necessarily vague is rejected. This is not permission to convict on ownership alone or a finding concerning any plaintiff's particular handgun.

**G4 — Restoration.** All eight reject blanket invalidation of this category, while limiting its reach to independently valid predicate definitions. Restoration concerns returning an existing firearm to a former operable condition, not treating any raw material or wholly new construction as restoration. The word may expresses actual capability, and the default fault requirement remains. The absence of a readily qualifier makes the provision broader than one confined to easy repair; breadth alone is not an absent standard. The city must prove the former covered condition and actual capability of return, rather than an unsupported theoretical possibility. A disputed level of work presents an application issue; it does not establish that every restorable firearm is unclassifiable. References to invalid G3a modification definitions cannot independently sustain enforcement, and the Court does not invent a universal time, cost, owner-skill or machine-shop threshold.

**G5 — Parts.** All eight keep the branches distinct. The first addresses parts designed or intended to convert a firearm into a weapon within a valid G1, G2 or G3b predicate. Objective conversion design or the identified conversion purpose supplies a bounded connection; ordinary unrelated possession of generic parts does not establish it. The second requires a combination from which such a weapon may readily be assembled and which is possessed or controlled by the same person. Readily imposes a practical assembly condition; it does not include every remote possibility achievable by rebuilding components. Neither branch is facially meaningless on the lower court's categorical reasoning. References dependent on invalid G3a criteria cannot independently supply liability. No NFA parts-package result is imported, no personal use or general gunsmithing immunity is added, and the Court makes no actual parts attribution or fault finding.

The competing objection is that readily has borderline applications. Grayned does not require a numerical measure for every practical standard; the stated conversion design/purpose, actual combination, practical assembly and common possession/control requirements constrain both notice and enforcement. This differs from the unresolved lineage relation in G3a. The city must establish every applicable condition and fault; it may not select arbitrary combinations across unrelated owners.

## Equal protection and relief

**E1 — Former-city-registration firearm exemption.** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer would reverse the invalidation of section 2323.31(B)(3). It exempts a person who lawfully possessed an assault weapon and registered that particular assault weapon pursuant to former section 2323.05 in 1989; registration of some other weapon does not suffice. Protecting that documented set rationally preserves a distinct formal reliance interest while limiting the exempt stock. The earlier ordinance's invalidity defeats punishment under its unclear requirement; it does not make all actual registration and reliance nonexistent. Newly covered owners could not have known to register, and the old window was only thirty days, but ordinary review permits a bounded exception rather than requiring every pre-existing owner receive the same benefit. The city need not prove every registrant actually studied the law or that this was the best grandfather rule. The finite records and actual prior registration supply the distinction; a bare label of deserving compliance with an invalid law would not.

Stevens would affirm invalidation of that registration selector. His Nordlinger disagreement with unequal entrenched ownership advantages, coupled with his Heller/Romer fit positions, gives decisive weight to the indistinguishable lawful pre-existing possession and to owners newly covered after the only window closed. For him the invalid notice regime and expansion disconnect the old registry from protecting legitimate ownership reliance; a clear prospective cutoff or an opportunity for all covered prior owners would present a different case. He does not demand that every grandfather rule be universal or decide its permissible replacement.

**E2 — Magazine exemption.** All eight sustain the NFA-registration and inert-object branches against the presented ordinary equality challenge. The NFA branch covers a large-capacity magazine belonging to an NFA-registered firearm or possessed by the owner of such a firearm; the latter alternative does not require the magazine itself to be registered or to fit that firearm. That breadth is an imperfect but rational protection of the identified regulated-ownership reliance. The city-registration alternative requires the magazine to belong to or be part of an assault weapon registered under former section 2323.05(C). The inert-object alternative likewise requires the magazine's relationship to the qualifying assault weapon: that weapon must have been rendered totally inoperable or inert, cannot readily be rendered operable or activated, and must be kept as a trophy, souvenir, curio or museum piece. Ownership of an unrelated inert object is insufficient. No operative exception is omitted or rewritten. The seven named above would also sustain the former-city-registration magazine clause for E1's reason; Stevens would affirm invalidity of that clause for his E1 reason. Section 2323.11(F)'s more-than-twenty definition and .22-rimfire-only exclusion, and section 2323.32(A)'s express knowing-possession requirement, remain distinct.

**R — Mandate.** All eight would affirm the threshold judgment and the invalidation of G3a's two modification branches, reverse the stated categorical vagueness rulings for G1, G2, G3b, G4 and G5, and preserve invalid-predicate dependencies. Seven would reverse the old-city-registration equality invalidations; Stevens would affirm them. All eight agree to preserve the otherwise sustained magazine exceptions. Remand is confined to conforming relief and determining actual Ohio severability of the invalid modification branches and dependent references, consistently with Women's Medical's ordinary state-law severability rule. No statewide weapons code, automatic arrest, reopened registration window or new offense is judicially created. No independent substantive arms judgment is entered, and no lower-court finding that every affected weapon is constitutionally unprotected survives.

## Components and supported conclusions

**C1 — Franklyn's machinegun commerce challenge.** All eight reject the objection and affirm the section 922(o) conviction against it. The admitted category and actual fully automatic .45-caliber weapon fall within the national transfer-and-stock mechanism sustained in Hale, Rybar, Kirk and now Wright. Possession permits retention and circulation of stock that would defeat the valid supply restriction if omitted; no individual sale or interstate journey is constitutionally required by that rationale. The alternative — a local possession offense beyond Lopez — correctly rejects dangerousness, remote manufacture and generalized crime costs as sufficient reasons, but does not defeat this identified anti-evasion connection. Neither a machinegun's actual interstate journey nor a new jurisdictional element is added. No grandfather exception or its burden is newly adjudicated, and no Second Amendment claim is presented.

**C2 — Gonzalez's independent section 922(g) commerce objection.** All eight reject the objection and affirm his separate ammunition conviction, in light of Hiley's effective method at its actual scope. Unlike section 922(o), the prohibited-person statute regulates a disqualified recipient's acquisition and retention in connection with the express prohibition on supplying that recipient under section 922(d)(1). The counterpart recipient restriction closes that route of evasion within the national supply regime. The stipulated interstate movement of the ammunition satisfies the statute's separate nexus on this record; the constitutional mechanism is not the mere fact that any object once crossed a boundary. This preserves Lopez and the distinction between the two provisions. No unraised arms theory, new convicted-person classification, or guilt determination for a different count is supplied.

**C3 — Batson.** All eight affirm rejection of the presented discriminatory-strike claim on the actual alternative merits determination. Three of the government's six strikes removed three of four Black panel members; those figures properly call for examination and are not dismissed as legally irrelevant. The trial court heard the prosecutor's explanations and separately found no purposeful discrimination; the appellate court found no clear error in that ultimate determination. Batson and Hernandez preserve the difference between production of a neutral explanation and the court's ultimate assessment of discriminatory purpose, including credibility. Trevino and McCollum preserve ordinary staged burdens; J.E.B. reinforces that a neutral label cannot defeat supported pretext, without changing the racial inquiry into a cause-challenge requirement.

The strongest contrary path is a remand if the trial court merely accepted facial neutrality without deciding genuineness. The unreproduced individual explanations must not be invented, assumed inherently adequate, or characterized as a detailed comparator finding. ## Proposed result and rule

Affirm the Federal Circuit's patent-invalidity judgment on the reviewed claims. An invention is on sale for section 102(b) when, before the one-year critical date, it is the subject of a commercial offer and is sufficiently complete to be patented. Physical reduction to practice is sufficient evidence of that completeness but is not indispensable: drawings or descriptions that enable a person skilled in the art to practice the invention can establish it. A promise to undertake future inventive research and a mere incomplete conception do not meet that second requirement merely because a customer offers money.

The substantial Texas Instruments purchase order supplies the commercial commitment; the supplied record establishes drawings complete enough for production before the critical date. The later manufacture of the first physical unit therefore does not postpone the bar. No new factfinding, infringement remedy or damages award follows from this affirmance. This disposition does not invalidate unreviewed claims or decide a distinct experimental-use dispute.

## Competing constructions

**Prototype prerequisite:** A physically proven device gives a clear completion date and avoids loss of rights during uncertain development. Its strongest defect is statutory: section 102(b) concerns an invention being on sale, not delivery of a manufactured specimen. The Telephone Cases recognize an operative invention shown in an enabling specification despite imperfect early physical demonstrations. Making an inventor's manufacturing schedule decisive would permit commercial exploitation of a completed design before the statutory clock begins.

**Commercial offer plus demonstrated completeness:** This construction gives effect to both the sale and invention requirements and Pennock's limit on prolonged commercial exploitation. Its strongest objection is uncertainty about how developed a design must be. Requiring enabling completion answers that objection better than an unbounded assessment of whether work is 'substantially complete': the record must show that the actual claimed invention can be practiced, not just that development is likely to succeed. Elizabeth's controlled experimental use remains distinct; ordinary commercial delivery of a finished design is not made experimental merely by the absence of an earlier prototype.

# OT1998 chunk 1 — Kravitz provisional Associate commitments

## Proposed action and sufficient rule

Affirm the Third Circuit judgment reversing summary judgment and remand for ordinary adjudication of the customers' primary fraud claims. An accepting broker-dealer's implied best-execution undertaking can constitute a material representation in connection with a securities transaction. On this record, a factfinder could find that the firms accepted uninstructed customer orders while knowing they intended to use NBBO alone despite better terms reasonably available to those customers, contrary to the undertaking the firms understood themselves to make. That is a potentially deceptive undertaking, not merely a later breach of a duty of care.

Neither NBBO execution nor widespread industry practice conclusively defeats deception or scienter. Practical availability and net customer benefit depend on contemporaneous order size, trading characteristics, execution speed, clearing costs, market-access costs and difficulty, as well as quoted price. Firms may prove those costs offset nominal price improvement, that the customer knew the actual limited undertaking, or that the required culpability is absent. The contrary evidentiary materials cannot be resolved for the moving firms at summary judgment.

The same confined reasoning covers alternative electronic quotations, crossing opposing customer market orders, and matching customer orders with in-house limits. It makes no venue mandatory, creates no best-possible-price guarantee and does not require a legally unsupported search of every quotation for every order. SOES use is relevant to practical circumstances but is not an established universal safe harbor here.

## Adversarial paths

**Restore summary judgment:** The strongest argument is that 1992–1994 industry practice defined the accepted undertaking as NBBO execution and neither law nor contemporary technology clearly required more. Santa Fe forbids turning every breach of agency duty into federal fraud, and Hochfelder forbids negligence liability. The record could ultimately support that defense. It does not support a categorical judgment now: the parties acknowledge an implied best-execution undertaking; evidence of actual alternative use, other firms' practices and specific trades supports knowing inconsistency with that undertaking. No authoritative contemporaneous safe harbor establishes that NBBO exhausted reasonably available terms for all these trades.

**Permit the proved-fraud theory to proceed:** First Jersey recognizes that a broker's transactional implied representations can be deceptive when the broker knowingly conceals their falsity. Its established findings cannot be imported here, and a dealer's profit alone proves nothing. The present materials nevertheless create a genuine dispute over the actual undertaking, practical alternatives and contemporaneous knowing falsity. This ground preserves Santa Fe and Hochfelder. Central Bank's assistance theory is unnecessary and does not convert professional status into primary liability.

**Scienter boundary:** The proposed common sufficient ground is the triable knowing-deception theory. Neither a universal primary-fraud recklessness standard nor automatic transfer of Central Bank's accessory standard is required to sustain the remand. The lower opinion's recklessness references are not represented as already controlling Supreme Court doctrine. Fraud still requires legally sufficient culpability; negligence and uncertainty alone are not enough.
