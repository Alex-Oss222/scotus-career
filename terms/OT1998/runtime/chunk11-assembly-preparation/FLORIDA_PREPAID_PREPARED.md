**The four-Justice lead commercial-consent portion — Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, Breyer.** Independently of §5, these four would hold that a State accepts a clear prospective federal-court condition by continuing specified commercial activity it can realistically choose not to operate. **The Patent Clause judgment concurrence — Stone-Zsela.** The concurrence joins the affirmance and precise common Part I propositions, while relying on the approved independent Article I ground.
