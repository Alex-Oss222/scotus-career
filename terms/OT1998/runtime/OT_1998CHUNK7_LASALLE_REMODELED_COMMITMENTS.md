## Revised adversarial paths

### L1: reverse the exclusive-eligibility plan on the preserved legal question

The opportunity reserved to old holders to acquire the reorganized debtor is property received under the plan because they hold the old interest. Offering new capital accounts for the price of new ownership but does not independently account for old holders alone receiving the chance to purchase it. When the impaired senior unsecured class is not paid in full and objects, section 1129(b)(2)(B)(ii) forbids that preferred allocation. The proposition does not depend on proving that the Bankruptcy Judge undervalued the real property or misstated the contribution's present value.

The strongest objection is now more substantial than in the initial packet: the debtor has a lawful, unappealed proposal-exclusivity order; the court made the valuation and confirmation findings; and no independent challenge to those findings remains. The response is a legal distinction, not a claim of missing proof. Section 1121 controls who proposes a plan at that procedural stage; section 1129 controls whether its distribution of property may be confirmed. A refusal to terminate proposal exclusivity does not independently adjudicate the plan's old-equity-only investment eligibility. An accurate valuation of the underlying property and contribution does not by itself eliminate that legal eligibility preference. Ahlers treats ownership and related economic rights as property even when valuation arguments minimize their worth. Nobelman likewise illustrates that valuing an asset and respecting protected legal rights are separate tasks, although its mortgage rule is not extended here.

Under L1, meaningful exposure to competing investment or another lawful process eliminating the old-interest preference is relevant because of the adopted legal rule. It is not an existing factual finding and not a free-standing judicial demand that every bankruptcy asset be auctioned. The Court need not decide that a universal new-value corollary survives, prescribe its full elements, fix an auction, or directly reverse the unappealed exclusivity order.

### L2: affirm on a permissible new-capital construction

The phrase on account of distinguishes ownership received as consideration for new capital from ownership distributed for the old interest. On this view, old owners' eligibility to submit and fund the confirmed arrangement is not itself a separately distributed property interest requiring another exchange. The Code deliberately supplies a proposal-exclusivity mechanism; treating every old-equity-only investment arrangement during that period as forbidden adds a competitive-process condition not expressed in section 1129. Where the property valuation and remaining confirmation findings are unchallenged, the preserved legal question does not independently establish that the former owners receive a gratuitous distribution.

The strongest objection is that statutory proposal exclusivity does not compel exclusive investment eligibility, and excluding other investors can have value despite accurate underlying-property valuation. L2 must address that distinction, not characterize section 1121 as blanket immunity or find the priority challenge waived. Its answer is that the statutory receipt at issue is the reorganized ownership purchased with specified new consideration; old eligibility is part of the lawful plan-making setting rather than an additional distribution to which a judicial market-test requirement attaches. The rule would not protect an actual distribution for old ownership, unsupported nominal capital or a properly challenged valuation. The present source does not permit findings that every conceivable new-value criterion was separately litigated and satisfied, or that the five-year contribution was immediately paid.

Los Angeles Lumber supplies the historically recognized distinction between retained ownership for old interests and participation based on money or money's worth. Ahlers rejected promises of personal labor and management while expressly reserving the survival of that capital route. Its holding does not treat the specified cash contribution here as personal services or conclusively reject L2. The remaining choice is statutory construction, not discretionary disregard of priority. The contrary L1 reading is substantial and must be resolved expressly.

### L3: reject all new-value participation under the Code

A categorical construction would treat any junior holder's retention while a senior class remains unpaid as prohibited notwithstanding offered new capital. Its strongest support is the enacted absolute-priority command and Ahlers's limit on equitable innovation. Its strongest difficulty is the statutory phrase on account of, the distinction between old and new consideration, and Ahlers's explicit reservation. Noland and Reorganized CF&I forbid replacing a statutory rank but preserve actual exceptions and require attention to the particular legal operation. They do not dictate categorical rejection here.

L1 can resolve this plan while reserving L3; L2 rejects the categorical bar as applied to a qualifying new-capital exchange. None of the following commitments requires a complete L3 holding. A narrower L1 choice does not silently imply recognition of the general new-value doctrine. Likewise, L2 does not announce that every deferred cash promise satisfies every requirement of that doctrine.
