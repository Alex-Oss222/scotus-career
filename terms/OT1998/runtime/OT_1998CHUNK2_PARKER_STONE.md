## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Bankruptcy

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the presented question within the established review posture and the stated preservation limits.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Read statement/perform-intention obligation together with nonalteration savings clause and actual contract default rules; bankruptcy discharge of personal liability does not eliminate surviving lien.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Congress required a disclosure of intentions and listed important options but added the explicit nonalteration clause. A reading that automatically accelerates a compliant debtor’s lien debt or forfeits collateral would contradict that clause without a separate default or statutory remedy. Lienholder security and ordinary foreclosure upon later breach remain intact.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Under then-effective §§521(2) and 521(2)(C), a debtor who remains current and otherwise honors the security agreement may retain property without mandatory redemption or reaffirmation solely to satisfy the statement-of-intention rule.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Congress required a disclosure of intentions and listed important options but added the explicit nonalteration clause. A reading that automatically accelerates a compliant debtor’s lien debt or forfeits collateral would contradict that clause without a separate default or statutory remedy. Lienholder security and ordinary foreclosure upon later breach remain intact. Parker’s execution of an agreement does not compel its approval. Including unsecured debt in exchange for modestly reduced car payments makes the debtor-protection inquiry concrete. Recognizing the credit union’s pecuniary stake permits review; it does not displace §524(c)(6) or convert §521(2)’s notice requirements into a right to renewed personal liability.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No nullification of secured lien, payment default remedies or contract covenants; later Bankruptcy Code amendments cannot be imported retrospectively.
