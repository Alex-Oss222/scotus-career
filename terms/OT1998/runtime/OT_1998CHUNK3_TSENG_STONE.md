# El Al Israel Airlines, Ltd. v. Tsui Yuan Tseng

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** September 28, 1998; no prepared OT1998 position is a completed Court decision.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Second Circuit's judgment as to the personal-injury claim and reinstate dismissal of that claim.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The New York personal-injury claim shall be dismissed. The separate baggage judgment and questions concerning occurrences outside the Convention or Article 25 remain untouched.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Exclusivity of the Warsaw Convention remedy

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches Convention exclusivity for the personal-injury claim. He does not revisit whether the search was an Article 17 accident or decide the baggage and Article 25 matters outside the grant.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Article 24's reference to actions 'however founded,' read with Article 17 and the Convention's structure, makes Article 17's conditions limits on recovery rather than invitations to replace an unsuccessful Convention claim with local tort law.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** When a passenger-injury occurrence falls within Article 17's temporal and substantive field, Article 24 permits an action, however founded, only subject to the Convention's conditions and limits. Local law cannot supply recovery solely because the claimant fails one of those conditions.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Tseng's claim arose during the international carriage setting governed by Article 17, but she alleged no qualifying bodily injury. *Saks* and *Floyd* support enforcing that boundary, while *Zicherman* does not authorize local law to create liability when the Convention's liability conditions are absent.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not govern occurrences outside embarking, carriage, or disembarking; baggage matters outside the grant; or the operation of Article 25 and other treaty-preserved consequences.
