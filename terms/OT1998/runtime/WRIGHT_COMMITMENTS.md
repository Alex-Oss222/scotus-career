## Component-level competing paths, sufficient grounds and limits

### 1. Commerce authority and the derivative Tenth Amendment objection

**Commitment C:** Reject the challenges. Hale's possession/transfer relationship and Kirk's application to retained completed stock control. The completed .223-caliber automatic weapon is admitted prohibited stock. Wright's acquisition, retention and intended concealment do not escape the national stock regime because no sale or particular interstate movement is proved. The possession component prevents evasion or material impairment of that valid transfer-and-stock restriction. No commerce element is added to the statute. The disassembled .50-caliber weapon is not redescribed as an operable completed gun; the plea and the record identify it as a machinegun being brought for reassembly. There is no preserved claim that these admitted objects fall outside the enacted machinegun class, and no new statutory branch finding is made.

**Competing path:** Invalidate the count because possession is local, no interstate element exists and formal findings are missing. Its strongest point is that national regulation cannot create its own constitutional power merely by being comprehensive. That objection is correct in principle but fails here: Hale independently identifies the transfer/stock regime and the fitted anti-evasion mechanism, while Kirk rejects a universal transaction prerequisite for the admitted completed stock. Mere marketability, dangerousness, prior interstate movement or social costs would not suffice. No independent commandeering claim is presented; the derivative Tenth objection fails with the enumerated-power objection. No general rule that every exercise of commerce authority defeats every Tenth Amendment claim is adopted.

**Limits:** The section 922(o)(2)(A) exception covers transfers to or by, and possession by or under authority of, the United States or its departments/agencies, or a State or its departments/agencies/political subdivisions. Section 922(o)(2)(B) separately covers lawful transfer or possession of a machinegun lawfully possessed before May 19, 1986; earlier manufacture alone is insufficient. No exception is newly established. This ruling does not decide every receiver, conversion part, incomplete assembly, readily restorable weapon or wear-induced conversion. Commerce authority supplies no answer to the arms claim.

### 2. Machinegun possession and the personal arms claim

**Commitment A:** Recognize personal invocation; vacate the appellate affirmance of the single machinegun count and remand for the preserved arms application. The lower court required a connection to a militia actively maintained and trained by Georgia. Love and Hale forbid that prerequisite. The original opinion expressly declined to decide what justification could sustain restriction of otherwise protected activity. It therefore supplies no independent application that can survive correction of its affiliation premise.

**Tested immediate-affirmance path:** The completed .223 automatic weapon's repeated fire and the statutory restriction on additions to civilian machinegun stocks supply a substantial argument for the validity of this application. A functioning automatic weapon differs materially from an ordinary semiautomatic; the preserved older-stock exception need not imply that older guns are safer. Government channels and the stock limitation can be evaluated as actual features of the restriction. The objection to remand is strongest here: the Court can resolve law on settled facts and need not demand an empirical study or a hearing merely because a lower court used a wrong ground.

**Why that path is not adopted by these Justices here:** Each joined Rybar Part III, which considered identified completed automatic weapons, their known functional characteristics, the same stock restriction, and the argument for immediate affirmance. Each withheld that merits resolution when the lower court had not performed the personal-right application. Wright supplies no materially stronger independent adjudication of that issue: footnote 18 expressly reserves justification, and the single count also includes a disassembled .50-caliber weapon whose exact component configuration is not supplied. Wright's proposed burial and private group's stated preparedness do not establish an adjudicated criminal purpose beyond the possession offenses. The pipe bombs belong to the independent registration count and cannot substitute for justification of this possession prohibition. No prior private position is used to change these published commitments.

**Tested immediate-invalidation path:** The personal right and evidence of military usefulness favor coverage, and the prohibition substantially restricts possession. But Love, Hale and Miller do not make military suitability immunity or decide that every possession restriction violates the guarantee. Reversal with acquittal would decide the open regulatory application without a sufficient controlling premise. It is rejected.

**Remedy and joins:** The remand requires assessment of the actual completed weapon and the disassembled weapon under their proper statutory branches where necessary; constitutional coverage and the justification of this restriction remain separate. It does not reopen admitted possession or impose a new offense element. The existing record may suffice; further ordinary proceedings are permitted only as needed, with no compulsory empirical production, evidentiary hearing, new trial, acquittal, release or return of weapons. Because one count covers the machineguns, no invented second possession count or unsupported severance of the conditional plea is created. The alternative jury-instruction request returns only insofar as it remains procedurally material after lawful resolution; the Court orders no trial or particular charge. No universal scrutiny tier, categorical dangerous-arms exclusion, categorical military-arms immunity or automatic-remand rule is joined.

### 3. Independent destructive-device registration count

**Commitment R:** Affirm the section 5861(d) conviction concerning the three pipe bombs. Hale Parts II and IV supply an independent tax-administration and arms ground: personal possession and military usefulness do not exempt the holder from identification and registration supporting lawful making and transfer. This is a registration conviction, not a machinegun-possession prohibition disguised as one. The supported record establishes no inability lawfully to approve or register these destructive devices and no denied application. No hypothetical machinegun-registration conflict is transferred to the pipe bombs.

**Competing path:** Vacate this conviction too because the lower court's militia prerequisite infected its discussion of both statutes. The objection is substantial as to that reasoning, but not sufficient as to this judgment. Hale's independent registration holding reaches the precise presented objection without determining whether every pipe bomb is outside constitutional coverage. Unlike the possession application, the legal ground sustaining the registration duty is already controlling and was joined by every Associate Justice. Thus the lower court's invalid affiliation rationale is rejected while the registration judgment is affirmed.

**Limits:** Section 5841 uses registration of a transferred firearm to the transferee through the transferor; it is not a universal obligation for a possessor to seek late registration. Section 5812 requires advance transfer approval and denial when transfer, receipt or possession would violate law; section 5822 separately requires advance making approval and denial when making or possession would violate law. No statutory element, tax exemption, knowledge question or registration-impossibility defense outside the preserved issues is decided. The Court need not classify all explosive devices as constitutionally unprotected.

### 4. Independent Ninth Amendment defense

**Commitment N:** Reject the claimed independent exemption for this possession and nonregistration. The Ninth Amendment prevents the enumeration of rights from denying or disparaging other rights retained by the people; the assertion of privacy and natural self-defense does not itself establish an immunity from these otherwise applicable weapons laws. Love and Hale already distinguish personal protection from unrestricted possession and from the registration obligation. The personal-arms claim receives its own adjudication and is not rejected on the ground that a listed right exhausts all constitutional liberty.

**Competing path:** Treat the Ninth Amendment's reservation and asserted natural self-defense as an independent ground for reversal even if the specific arms application does not establish invalidity. Its strongest point is that enumerating one right cannot extinguish others. That principle does not identify the scope of the claimed immunity or establish that keeping these particular machineguns and unregistered pipe bombs is exempt from lawful regulation. The narrow rejection of the presented independent defense is sufficient. No holding that the Ninth Amendment is meaningless, that unenumerated rights do not exist, or that every privacy claim must fail is joined. No new substantive-due-process claim is invented.

### 5. Acceptance of responsibility: both independent rehearing grounds

**Commitment S1 — legal ground:** The court may not deny an otherwise warranted section 3E1.1 adjustment solely because a defendant presents a nonfrivolous legal or constitutional challenge unrelated to admitted factual guilt. Application note 2 expressly permits acceptance in the unusual case tried to preserve such a legal issue; a guilty plea reserving that issue cannot itself supply automatic disqualification. Notes 1 and 3 require examination of acceptance, not a reduction as a reward for abandoning legal review. Stinson makes consistent interpretive commentary authoritative. The legal-challenge-only alternative ground in the rehearing opinion cannot stand.

**Strongest contrary path:** Denial of a reduction is not necessarily punishment for invoking a constitutional right, and conduct accompanying litigation may reveal nonacceptance. That proposition does not authorize an exclusion inconsistent with the commentary. False factual denials, sham factual assertions or conduct inconsistent with acceptance can matter even when attached to a constitutional claim. Neither the unsuccessful legal position alone nor insistence on appellate review establishes such conduct. Wright's personal-right argument is especially not frivolous under Love and Hale; its registration implication can fail on the merits without making the preserved claim a denial of facts.

**Commitment S2 — asserted factual alternative:** The quoted record does not establish that the same denial rested on a sufficiently independent, legally valid remorse finding. The sentencing judge evaluated whether Wright's group was a lawful state-protective militia, and earlier remarks expressly focused on counsel's claimed constitutional entitlement. The rehearing majority's conclusion that these remarks amount to a lack-of-remorse finding is its interpretation of those statements, not an additional historical finding. The Court cannot convert the same legally displaced militia premise into a conclusive independent acceptance finding.

**Strongest affirmance path:** The sentencing judge saw Wright testify, doubted that he sought to carry out rather than resist the law, and could find a lack of recognition of wrongdoing notwithstanding admissions and cooperation. Genuine acceptance findings receive substantial deference, and neither the guilty plea nor the prosecutor's recommendation binds the judge. That path remains lawful if supported findings actually establish it. On these published passages, however, the judge's acceptance analysis is intertwined with the invalid affiliation premise. Koon preserves discretion within lawful rules and gives no deference to legal error. Its unanimous error/remedy treatment rejects appellate replacement reasons and mere speculation that the same sentence might recur. Resentencing therefore remains necessary independently of the still-open machinegun-count application.

**Remedy and limits:** Vacate the sentence and require reconsideration under the correct rule, coordinated with the disposition of the machinegun count. No two- or three-level reduction, particular prison term or finding that Wright has conclusively accepted responsibility is ordered. Admissions, cooperation, timely plea and the stated cessation of weapons possession are significant evidence; supported contrary conduct may outweigh them. The judge may evaluate genuine lack of remorse or insincerity on lawful grounds. This is an application of the commentary and the legal-error/deference distinction, not a new constitutional holding barring every adverse sentencing consequence connected to litigation.

## Proposed proposition and remedy boundaries for reconciliation

All eight would join the same narrow propositions: the established commerce regime reaches this admitted stock; the presented derivative Tenth objection fails; affiliation is not required to invoke the arms guarantee; the unperformed machinegun application returns under Love/Hale/Rybar; the separate pipe-bomb registration conviction survives the presented objection under Hale; the asserted Ninth defense establishes no independent immunity; legal challenge alone is not a lawful section 3E1.1 disqualifier; and these quoted remarks do not furnish a sufficiently independent acceptance determination to save the sentence.

There is no proposed separate writing among the eight necessary to express a recorded disagreement at these levels. Their stated reservations must remain in any assembled common opinion. None joins a broader automatic-weapons exclusion, a right to every military weapon, a universal scrutiny tier, a complete Ninth Amendment theory, a mandatory acceptance reduction, or an automatic arms-remand rule. No author is selected at this stage.
