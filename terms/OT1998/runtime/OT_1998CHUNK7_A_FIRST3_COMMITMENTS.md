## 1. In re Sealed Case — No. 98-93 — April 13, 1999

### Shared decisional propositions and remedy

Reach the preserved Rule 501 question through the accepted third-party privilege appeal. Affirm the order compelling testimony insofar as challenged by the asserted protective-function privilege. Rule 501 does not create the proposed privilege for information protective personnel acquire by their proximity to the President on this record. Presidential protection is an exceptionally important public function; that importance does not establish either a confidential communications relationship or a sufficient reason-and-experience basis to withhold this broad class of observations from a federal grand jury.

The proposed exception for an observation or statement that, at the time made, gave reasonable grounds to believe a felony had been, was being, or would be committed does not cure the recognition problem. It would make the privilege turn on an agent's contemporaneous legal and factual perception, while excluding other probative observations; agents and the President could not know in advance that all relevant observations would remain protected. This is an objection to the proposal's justification and boundaries, not a finding about any particular agent's knowledge or criminal conduct. Jaffee authorizes principled common-law development and does not make historical recognition an indispensable prerequisite. Its demonstrated treatment relationship and widespread recognition materially differ from the supported premises here. Swidler preserves an established communication privilege and likewise does not create protection for independently observed facts.

The strongest opposing path recognizes a qualified protection because anticipated compulsion could cause a President to limit protective access, then demands extraordinary investigative need. Director Merletti's declaration and former President Bush's April 15, 1998 letter affirmatively support the confidence-and-proximity prediction. Those submissions deserve consideration; they are not adjudicated findings of impaired access, and the supported prediction and proposed scope together do not establish a sufficient basis to outweigh the evidentiary cost of this proposed class. Actual impairment is not made a mandatory prerequisite for recognition of every privilege. Neither a presumption that Presidents will compromise their own security nor a presumption that disclosure can never affect security is adopted. An absolute privilege is still less supportable. Once recognition fails, no special overcoming test should be announced or applied. Actual independently supported security accommodation and other properly asserted privileges remain available under their own law; this judgment creates neither an automatic protective order nor a special necessity threshold.

## 2. UNUM Life Insurance Co. of America v. Ward — No. 97-1868 — April 20, 1999

### Notice-prejudice component: common decisional ground

California's insurance-specific notice-prejudice rule falls within ERISA §514(b)(2)(A)'s saving clause as applied to this insured disability policy. It limits the insurer's contractual forfeiture defense: late notice does not relieve the insurer without proof of actual prejudice, and neither lateness itself nor possible prejudice suffices. It is part of the substantive insurer-insured bargain, not merely a state-created damages remedy for administering an ERISA claim. Its application in Ward's §502(a)(1)(B) suit leaves the federal enforcement channel intact. The self-funded-plan question and the deemer clause's distinct protection are not decided.

The common-sense insurance inquiry and the McCarran-Ferguson guideposts address actual operation. The rule specifically governs insurance, directly regulates the policy relationship and preserves the insurer's assumed coverage absent actual prejudice. The parties may dispute whether that last feature independently transfers or spreads risk in a technical sense; these guideposts do not require transforming every consideration into an indispensable conjunctive element. The decision need not invent a replacement saving-clause test or decide every timing rule.

The strongest opposing argument is that permitting state variation in claim timing defeats uniform administration and rewrites an express plan deadline. Metropolitan Life preserves substantive insurance terms despite their effect on insured plans; applying §502(a)(1)(B) to those terms does not create Pilot Life's competing remedy. Travelers and De Buono preserve uniform administration but do not repeal the insurance saving clause. John Hancock preserves both compatible insurance regulation and a concrete-conflict inquiry; no contradictory ERISA command requiring this absolute forfeiture has been identified.

Affirm reversal of the insurer's summary judgment on the notice-prejudice ground and remand for the actual-prejudice inquiry. No finding that prejudice is absent, no award of benefits, and no automatic finding of employer agency follows.

### Employer-as-agent component: corrected legal premise and bounded remand

The primary contemporaneous United States merits filing confirms that the agency question was litigated as a second question; the short neutral grant statement was incomplete. Reach the question at the scope the actual dispute permits. A state rule assigning an employer the insurer's agency role because the employer performs specified benefit-plan administration has a connection with that administration under §514(a). It directly reallocates notice and claim-processing responsibility; it is not merely an external cost of buying insurance. The Ninth Circuit's unconditional no-relation rationale cannot support its contingent Elfstrom instruction on that understanding.

Ward disputes that characterization and describes ordinary agency principles applied to actual circumstances. That distinction matters. The Court cannot turn the disputed doctrine into a universal irrebuttable employer-agency rule, declare every ordinary agency principle preempted, or find a voluntary insurer designation absent or present. Ward did not press insurance saving for this agency theory; the notice-prejudice holding does not silently save it. The actual policy's written-authorization clause and the legal source of any asserted agency must receive their proper effect without treating the employer's administrative participation alone as conclusive.

Vacate the contingent agency direction insofar as it rests on the erroneous premise that the described mandatory allocation does not relate to a plan. If UNUM proves actual prejudice and the agency issue therefore remains material, the lower courts must identify and consider the actual rule invoked under the corrected relation standard, preserving any properly presented voluntary or fact-specific agency theory. This is not an automatic ruling that notice to MAC was ineffective, and no broad federal agency doctrine is created. The notice-prejudice remand remains independently necessary and is affirmed; the complete disposition is **affirm in part, vacate in part, and remand**.

## 3. United States v. Haggar Apparel Co. — No. 97-2044 — April 21, 1999

### Shared decisional ground and remedy

Reverse the Federal Circuit's categorical exclusion of Chevron review for the noticed Customs regulation and remand for the proper legal and factual inquiries. Congress's delegation to Treasury/Customs to implement tariff classifications can coexist with the Court of International Trade's decision on its own trial record under 28 U.S.C. §2640(a)(1). The latter provision establishes the court's evidentiary-review function; it does not itself withdraw the delegated interpretive authority or instruct the court to disregard a valid regulation.

The court must first determine the statute's meaning and actual bounds; clear statutory terms control. Where those terms leave the delegated issue open, the formally promulgated construction receives Chevron treatment only within that lawful discretion. Section 10.16(c)'s significant nonassembly-processing rule and (c)(4)'s chemical-treatment/permapressing example must be tested against the partial exemption for assembly and incidental operations. The record must then support application to the actual 12–15 minute baking process. Recognizing the review framework does not itself find the regulation valid, find the factual predicates satisfied, or direct a final duty amount.

The exemption retains all three component conditions: the United States fabricated component was exported ready for assembly without further fabrication, retained physical identity, and was not advanced in value or improved abroad except through assembly or incidental operations. The amount is a partial duty reduction for qualifying United States component cost/value, with the enacted proportional treatment for specific or compound rates; the Court computes none. Section 10.16(b) permits incidental operations before, during or after assembly, including ordinary final pressing and folding; that permission is distinct from paragraph (c)'s significant improvement and chemical-treatment exclusions. Timing after sewing alone therefore establishes neither inclusion nor exclusion, and ordinary pressing must not be equated with resin-activating permapressing.

The strongest contrary path relies on de novo trade review and §10.11's assurance of judicial review to demand wholly independent tariff construction. Judicial review remains effective precisely because the court determines statutory boundaries and permissible interpretation; preserving that right does not negate the promulgation and delegation. Conversely, the Government's strongest broader request could sustain the exclusion at once because permapressing is named. The proper remand preserves the difference between regulatory authority, statutory validity and factual application. The importer's commercial sequencing explanation is an argument bearing on incidental processing, not a judicial finding that the resin activation is necessary assembly. No generalized rule about informal classification letters, no later administrative doctrine, and no conclusory equation of every notice-and-comment rule with lawful authority is adopted.
