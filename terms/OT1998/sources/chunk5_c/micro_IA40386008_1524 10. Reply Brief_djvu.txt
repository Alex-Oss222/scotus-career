REPLY BRIEF OF PETITIONERS 


Briefs in response to our Petition for Certiorari have 
been filed by respondents State of Wisconsin (“State”), 
Parents for School Choice et al. (“PSC”), and Marquelle 
Miller et al. (“Miller”). Although the three respondent

parties are united in disputing our legal contentions, they 
are not of one mind with regard to the question at issue 
here—whether this case warrants the attention of this 
Court. While the State and the Miller respondents oppose 
granting the Petition—notwithstanding the State’s ac- 
knowledgement that the issue it presents “is of profound 
legal and practical importance, both to Wisconsin and to 
the Nation,” State Br. at 1—PSC urges this Court to grant 
review, contending that this is the “ideal” case for clari- 
fying the constitutional boundaries applicable to private 
school tuition voucher programs, PSC Br. at 13, and for 
removing the “constitutional cloud,” id. at 4, that shrouds 
both those programs that have been enacted and those that 
are under consideration in various legislative forums. 


The State and the Miller respondents oppose the Peti- 
tion for two reasons: First, they deny that the decision 
below is—as we contend—in irreconcilable conflict with 
the decision of this Court in Committee for Public Educa- 
tion v. Nyquist, 413 U.S. 756 (1973). Second, they sug- 
gest that this Court should wait for some other case to 
address the issues presented here. We reply briefly to both 
points.
