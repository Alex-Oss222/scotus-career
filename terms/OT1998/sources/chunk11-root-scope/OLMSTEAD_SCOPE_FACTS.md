# Olmstead, No. 98-536 — objective grant-scope certificate

This handoff records only archived pre-merits procedural facts. It neither enters a new simulated Court action nor supplies a target merits disposition, rationale, vote or opinion authorship.

The docket initially records a grant on December 14, 1998. Its December 17, 1998 entry expressly amends that order: the petition is granted **“limited to Question 1 presented by the petition.”** The original unqualified grant entry therefore cannot establish that every petition question remained within the amended grant. The docket does not reproduce Question 1; its precise text must be taken from the petition already obtained by neutral preparation.

The docket sets argument for April 21, 1999, and page 2 independently records that the case was argued on April 21, 1999. Direct inspection of the scanned second page confirms the full date, which the OCR partly omits. It also records the March 29, 1999 grant of the Solicitor General's motion to participate as amicus and for divided argument. This establishes counsel's participation only; the docket supplies no merits-participation roster of Justices.

No entry in the reviewed docket expands the amended grant to Question 2. Whether a separate question could be reached on an independently lawful and preserved basis is a legal issue for the scoped analysis; this certificate makes no such determination. In particular, the original broad grant is not evidence for silently treating Question 2 as separately granted.
