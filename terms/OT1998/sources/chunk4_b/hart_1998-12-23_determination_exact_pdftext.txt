STATE OF MAINE
           OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY OF STATE

            DETERMINATION OF THE VALIDITY
                OF A PETITION FOR INITIATED
                     LEGISLATION ENTITLED:

    “An Act to Permit the Medical Use of Marijuana”

      1. On November 24, 1998, petitioners resubmitted to
the Secretary of State for review, pursuant to the Consti-
tution of Maine, Article IV, Part Third, Section 18, peti-
tions containing 68,330 signatures which were already on
file, plus new petitions containing an additional 1,024
signatures, for a combined total of 69,354 signatures, on
behalf of the initiated legislation entitled, “An Act to
Permit the Medical Use of Marijuana.”

     2. The number of valid signatures required for an
initiative petition to qualify for submission to the Legisla-
ture pursuant to the Constitution of Maine, Article IV,
Part Third, Section 18, is 10% of the total vote cast for
Governor in the most recent gubernatorial election pre-
ceding this filing. That election occurred on November 3,
1998, and the qualifying number is now 42,101 signatures.
Although, as petitioners requested, I have based this
determination of validity on a review of all of the signa-
tures submitted (both newly submitted and previously
filed), it is clear that petitioners already had in excess of
the minimum number of signatures to qualify under the
new threshold, without submitting any additional signa-
tures for review.

     3. Based upon a consolidated review of the petitions
which were previously submitted, and the additional
petitions submitted for the first time on November 24,

     4. For the reasons set forth above, I find that a total
of 21,948 signatures are invalid. Petitioners have there-
fore submitted a combined total of 47,406 valid signa-
tures. As noted in paragraph 2 above, the minimum
number of signatures required for validity is now 42,101.

Because the number of valid signatures exceeds the mini-

mum required number by 5,305 signatures, I find the

petition to be valid.
