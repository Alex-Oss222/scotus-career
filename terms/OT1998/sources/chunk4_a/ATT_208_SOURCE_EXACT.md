(a) Any person, any body politic or municipal organiza-

complaint or to answer the same in writing within a rea-

common carrier within the time specified shall make rep- 
aration for the injury alleged to have been caused, the 
common carrier shall be relieved of liability to the com-

plained of. If such carrier or carriers shall not satisfy the 
complaint within the time specified or there shall appear

plaint, it shall be the duty of the Commission to investi- 
gate the matters complained of in such manner and by 
such means as it shall deem proper. No complaint shall 
at any time be dismissed because of the absence of direct 
damage to the complainant. 


(b)(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), the Com- 
mission shall, with respect to any investigation under this 
section of the lawfulness of a charge, classification, regu-

tigation within 5 months after the date on which the 
complaint was filed. 


(2) The Commission shall, with respect to any such 
investigation initiated prior to the date of enactment of 
this subsection, issue an order concluding the investigation
