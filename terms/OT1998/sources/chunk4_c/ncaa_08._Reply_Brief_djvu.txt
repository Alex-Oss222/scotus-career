IV. THE AMENDED COMPLAINT FAILS TO STATE 
A TITLE IX CLAIM AGAINST THE NCAA. 


Finally, contrary to the suggestion of respondent (Resp 
Br. 33-34) and the Solicitor General (U.S. Br. 17), the 
judgment below may not be affirmed solely on the basis 
of the amended complaint’s allegation that: “ “The NCAA 
is a recipient of federal funds because it is an entity

recipient and operates an educational program or activity

allegation at all, but a legal conclusion that

no allegations about the NYSP. In fact, the “bare allega-

and the Solicitor General can point to in the complaint—
