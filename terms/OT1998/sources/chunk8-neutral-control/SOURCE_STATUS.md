- **White supplemental extract (526 U.S. 561–563):** The accepted factual recitation supplies probable cause that the vehicle had been used for cocaine delivery. It does not supply present probable cause that cocaine remained inside. The vehicle seizure and later inventory search are distinct. The quoted statute permits seizure and directs forfeiture; these verbs are not interchangeable. The quoted section 932.703(2)(a) ties seizure to notice of the right to a postseizure adversarial probable-cause hearing. The source quote contains its own ellipsis, which is retained and not represented as a complete copy of the entire section. The proceeding is the possession prosecution and suppression ruling, not a final adjudication of forfeiture.
