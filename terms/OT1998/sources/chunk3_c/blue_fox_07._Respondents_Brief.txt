                               STATEMENT

           In September, 1993, pursuant to Section 8(a) of the
Small Business Act, 15 U.S.C. § 637(a), the United States
Department of the Army awarded a contract to the United
States Small Business Administration. Pet. App. 2a. The
contract involved the installation and testing of a telephone
switching system and the construction of a facility to house
the switching system at the Umatilla Army Depot, Oregon.
Id. SBA agreed to furnish the supplies and services requested
by the Army by subcontracting with Verdan Technology, Inc.
Id.

          Verdan subcontracted the construction portion of its
contract to Respondent Blue Fox. Pet. App. 4a. The total
cost of the work to be performed by Blue Fox was
$186,347.80. Jd. Blue Fox did not know until it had
completed its performance under the contract that Verdan had
not furnished a payment bond. /d. Verdan failed to pay Blue
Fox its full contract price -- the sum of $46,586.14 remained
due. /d. Blue Fox informed the Army and SBA in writing
on May 26, 1994, and again on June 15, 1994, that it had not
been fully paid. /d. In its June 15, 1994 letter to the Army,
Respondent requested “suspension or reduction of payment be
administered by your agency.” J.A. 33.' Nevertheless, the
Army subsequently disbursed a total of $86,132.33 to Verdan
between July and October, 1994. /d.
