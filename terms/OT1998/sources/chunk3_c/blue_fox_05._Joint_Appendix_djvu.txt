INTERROGATORY NO. 1: State the reason(s), if 
any there be, why no Miller Act bond was in place for 
Plaintiffs protection under Contract Number DAEA 


08-93-C-0056. 


ANSWER: The reason is not clear from the record. 
It may have been because the contract was classified 
under Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) Code
