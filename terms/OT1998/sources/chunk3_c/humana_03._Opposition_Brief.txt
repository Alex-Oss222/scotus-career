         QUESTION PRESENTED

Whether this Court should grant the
Petition for Writ of Certiorari submitted
by Humana, Inc. and Humana Health
Insurance of Nevada, Inc., which asks
this Court to decide whether the
application of RICO anti-fraud
provisions to insurance kickback schemes
is pre-empted by the McCarran-Ferguson
Act, when the inter-circuit split is
negligible and insufficiently developed,
and the only Court of Appeals cases
deeming RICO pre-empted by
McCarran-Ferguson are wrongly and
inadequately reasoned or squarely
conflict with this Court’s bedrock pre-
emption jurisprudence.
