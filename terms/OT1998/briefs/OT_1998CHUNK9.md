* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies *Landgraf*, *Rivers*, and the enacted structure. * **Stone's Proposed Holding:** AEDPA § 440(d) does not apply to a former § 212(c) application that was pending when AEDPA took effect, absent a clear congressional direction making the new categorical restriction applicable to such pending applications.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Goncalves had invoked the discretionary process, obtained an immigration-judge decision on its merits, and pursued an administrative appeal before enactment. The new law did not merely change a tribunal's procedure; it removed eligibility for the discretionary judgment already underway. * **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not govern applications first filed after AEDPA's effective date, guarantee discretionary relief, or decide the distinct temporal operation of IIRIRA's later repeal in another posture.

### Issue 3: Interpretive weight owed the Attorney General

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone works within the then-operative statutory-deference framework and does not silently replace it.
