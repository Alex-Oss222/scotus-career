OBJECTION TO PROPOSED SETTLEMENT 
OF DERIVATIVE ACTIONS 
COME NOW, Charles W. Pauly, Jr. and Elizabeth D. 
Pauly, joint tenants and file this Objection to Proposed 
Settlement of Derivative Actions and would show to the 
Court as follows: 
1. If the Plaintiffs’ case has merit, then the financial 
settlement is grossly inadequate to compensate the Plain- 
tiffs for the injuries suffered as a result of the actions~by 
the Defendants. 
2. If the Plaintiffs’ case is without merit, then the case 
is being setiled for nuisance value which compensates 
Plaintiffs’ attorneys, but does nothing to enhance share- 
holder value. 
In fact, it only demonstrates the company 
is a “soft” target for future frivolous litigation. 
3. Plaintiffs should be put to trial and demonstrate the 
merits of their claim. 
If Plaintiffs are successful, then 
damages will be substantially higher than the nominal 
consideration being received in the settlement. 
If Plaintiffs 
are unsuccessful, 
the suit was without merit and the 
Plaintiffs and their counsel should receive nothing. 
WHEREFORE, premises considered, movant requests 
the Court deny the proposed settlement as being unfair 
and inadequate to the shareholders of Archer Daniels 
Midland Company.
