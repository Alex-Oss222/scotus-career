ORDER 


WHEREAS, Plaintiffs moved on February 19, 1998 (1) 
pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 56(c) for an award of sum- 
mary judgment on their claim against Defendants for 
breach of contract in the amount of at least $80,194,054 
as of December 12, 1997 together with accruing pre- 
judgment interest and (2) pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 
12(b)(6) to dismiss with prejudice Defendants’ counter- 
claims asserted in their Answer and Counterclaims, dated 
January 8, 1998; 


111 


WHEREAS, Defendants submitted no opposition to the 
aforesaid motions to the extent the motions seek a money 
judgment in Plaintiffs’ favor and dismissal of the counter- 
claims with prejudice; 

WHEREAS, Plaintiffs also moved for an order directing 
Defendants to irrevocably assign or transfer to Plaintiffs 
a sufficient amount of toll road Receivables or Government 
Notes (as those terms are defined in the moving papers) 
to satisfy the judgment or, alternatively, for an order con- 
verting the Court’s preliminary injunction order of Decem- 
ber 23, 1997 to a permanent injunction; 


WHEREAS, the Court issued a preliminary injunction 
on December 23, 1997 enjoining Defendants from assign- 
ing or otherwise transferring their interests in the toll road 
Receivables or Government Notes pending the outcome of 
this action; 

NOW, THEREFORE, upon consideration of the filings 
and evidence submitted by the parties, the arguments of 
counsel, and all the pleadings and prior proceedings herein, 
and due deliberation having been had, it is hereby OR- 
DERED that: 


1. Plaintiffs’ motion for summary judgment on their 
breach of contract claim is granted; and plaintiffs are 
awarded judgment in the amount of $82,444,259.00; 


2. Plaintiffs’ motion to dismiss Defendants’ counter- 
claims with prejudice is granted; 

3. Plaintiffs’ motion for an order directing Defendants 
to irrevocably assign or transfer to Plaintiffs a sufficient 
amount of toll road Receivables or Government Notes to 
satisfy the judgment is granted; 

4. Pending such assignment or transfer, the restraints 
as stated in the preliminary injunction order issued on 
December 23, 1997 shall continue and are incorporated 
herein by reference; and

5. The preliminary injunction ordered December 23, 
1997 is converted to a permanent injunction effective until 
the defendant meets its obligation set forth in paragraph 
3. 


6. Defendants’ obligation in paragraph 3 is stayed for 
10 days.
