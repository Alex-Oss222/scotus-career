There is no inferred severable join, fallback, changed vote or separate writing.

The two statutory conditions remain cumulative: the claimed invention must have been subject to a commercial offer in the United States and ready for patenting before the critical date, one year before the application for patent in the United States. The official 1994 Code extraction and [statutory validation](../freeze/PFAFF_STATUTORY_VALIDATION.md) confirm both territorial qualifications. Readiness can follow from reduction to practice or sufficiently enabling drawings or descriptions; a prototype is not indispensable. The application uses the supported purchase order and enabling drawings without inventing claim numbers or additional transaction dates. Later embodiment does not postpone an already satisfied bar, and commercial strength does not substitute for readiness.

The opinion retains the limits for preliminary negotiations, research proposals, incomplete conceptions and genuine controlled experimentation; it does not decide an unpresented experimental-use dispute. Its treatment of Pennock, The Telephone Cases, Elizabeth, Markman and Warner-Jenkinson does not transfer an older statutory period or attribute this exact test to those precedents. ## Kravitz

The [Record](../records/merrill_lynch_v_kravitz_merits_1998-11-12.md) correctly affirms the Third Circuit's reversal of summary judgment, 9–0. Kennedy authors one signed plenary opinion, joined in full by the other eight Justices. Stone's unjoined concurrence performs separate legal work by preserving his broader knowing-or-reckless deception rule.

The frozen Associates adopt a sufficient knowing-deception ground, not a general primary-fraud recklessness rule. Stone expressly approves that knowing branch and every material qualification of the common opinion. The opinion neither declares recklessness insufficient nor forecloses its consideration under otherwise applicable law; the concurrence preserves Stone's additional proposed rule. This is complete support for the entire common opinion, not an unauthorized partial join or substantive narrowing of Stone's position. No Associate is attributed an unsupported join in his broader concurrence.

The actual entering-law Central Bank rule concerns culpable affirmative assistance and does not supply a universal primary-recklessness standard. First Jersey supplies knowing primary deception through a broker's implied transaction representation; its established findings and SEC-enforcement posture do not prove private reliance or injury here. O'Hagan's separate source-deception theory is not expanded. The Record preserves those distinctions and each Associate's frozen join barriers.

The direct claim requires a materially false undertaking and qualifying culpability, together with legally sufficient reliance, causation and actual loss. Fiduciary breach, inferior execution, professional status or customary practice alone establishes neither fraud nor immunity. Both holdings retain the actual undertaking, customer instructions and disclosed limitations. Contemporary practical availability includes size, trading characteristics, speed, clearing costs and market-access expense and difficulty. The three alleged alternatives remain distinct. No mandatory venue, universal quotation search, best-possible-price guarantee or automatic SOES safe harbor is imposed.

The later-study qualification is explicit: reliable retrospective evidence may illuminate contemporary feasibility, while later rules or technology do not themselves create earlier duty, access or culpability. Particular-trade evidence is not a finding of injury in every transaction, class certification or a reliance presumption. The limited affirmance returns the claims for ordinary proceedings and supplies no automatic trial, liability, damages or regulatory injunction.
