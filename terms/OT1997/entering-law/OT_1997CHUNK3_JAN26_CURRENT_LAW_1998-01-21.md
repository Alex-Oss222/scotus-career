## Case-specific determinations

### Allentown Mack — no material alteration

[Neutral packet](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_ALLENTOWN.md); [initial law](OT_1997CHUNK3_ALLENTOWN.md). The four new decisions neither change the NLRA polling threshold or Auciello nor decide the actual scope of the petition, whole-record substantial-evidence review, or this Board's treatment of employee reports. Lee concerns prospective jurisdiction and gives no general right to a remand where an agency rationale is challenged. Buchanan's contextual assessment of a different capital charge supplies no agency evidence rule or authority to infer the content of an unreproduced record.

LaChance preserves actual administrative charges, the governing proof burden, statutory procedural safeguards and a distinct appellate substantial-evidence standard. That is compatible with the packet's separation of a permissible policy from a supported application; it does not transfer CSRA preponderance, service nexus or personnel-specific safeguards into NLRA polling law. Its eight-Associate statutory joins and all-nine constitutional joins provide no present Allentown position. Lunding's Article IV substantial-reason/close-relationship inquiry is distinct from the NLRA's agency-policy and evidence inquiries. Its actual-operation reasoning and the Ginsburg–Kennedy dissent supply no substitute standard or employee-count inference. All existing lawful paths and limits remain.

### Oubre — no material alteration

[Neutral packet](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_OUBRE.md); [initial law](OT_1997CHUNK3_OUBRE.md). None of the four decisions changes OWBPA's actual minimum waiver conditions or its express validity burden. Lee's personal injury and jurisdictional correction do not convert the existing release defense into a speculative dispute or require a hearing. Buchanan holds that statutory permission to introduce mitigation does not itself create a particular instructional entitlement; it does not weaken the different express OWBPA minimum commands or create a universal rule that statutory safeguards are optional.

LaChance's preservation of lawful contest alongside rejection of knowingly false denials supplies no finding that retention of consideration is deception, no estoppel elements, and no permission to bypass an enacted waiver condition. Its preserved valid-privilege rule is not a general waiver doctrine. Lunding preserves the separation between substantive invalidity and a remedy whose actual prerequisites must be established, but its payment and tax-correction predicates do not decide tender back, ratification, restitution, recoupment or setoff under the ADEA. The Ginsburg–Kennedy tax dissent and Buchanan's mitigation writings provide no material new Justice-specific position on this release. The existing alternative paths and unadjudicated accounting questions remain.

### Yankton Sioux — additional Lee threshold authority; no change to diminishment law

[Neutral packet](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_YANKTON.md); [initial law](OT_1997CHUNK3_YANKTON.md). Lee now supplies direct current authority for continuing personal controversy and bounded corrective jurisdiction. Its claim-specific relief means that a threshold disposition cannot be treated as a diminishment ruling, and a correction of the declaration cannot revive the Tribe's unappealed injunction/regulatory-authority branch.

Lunding's geographic source-allocation dispute supplies no reservation-boundary presumption or Article IV test for congressional diminishment. Lee's Scalia–Thomas disagreement over an untriggered provider duty and the Chief's separate unformed-chain ground remain public noncontrolling applications, not a rule that every unresolved threshold requires dismissal or that this existing declaration has lost effect.

## Exact additional Lee rules relevant to Yankton's threshold boundary

The following rule and authority paragraphs are copied verbatim from [Lee's Holdings](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_PUBLIC_DELTA_LEE.md#holdings). ### Continuing controversy, representation and the proposed class

**Holding and operative rule:** Dutson's personal prospective controversy does not continue after his death on this record, which establishes no surviving personal claim, substitute or certified class. A provider's relationship to patients and a proposed class never certified cannot supply the represented patients' missing injury or an independently continuing controversy.

**Authority:** Stevens's Part II, joined by all seven other Associate Justices, controls with eight votes; Stone-Zsela agrees in these dispositions only.

### The District Court's declaration and injunction remain vacated

**Holding and operative rule:** Limited corrective jurisdiction permits clearing a constitutional merits judgment unsupported by a live injured claimant, and the equities here require leaving vacated the District Court's patient-based equal-protection declaration and permanent injunction. Reconsideration of a distinct, previously undecided provider claim does not restore that judgment or establish a new entitlement to relief.

**Authority:** Stevens's Part V has all eight Associate Justices; Stone-Zsela agrees in leaving the judgment and injunction vacated only.
