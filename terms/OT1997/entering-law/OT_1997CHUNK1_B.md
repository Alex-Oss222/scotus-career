## Commerce and personal arms: sources for fresh Rybar validation

- [Constitutional Structure](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md), lines 1282–1324, **United States v. Lopez**, April 26, 1995: original school-zone offense; insufficient remote-effects connection; no insertion of an unexpressed element. Neither all noneconomic conduct nor every statute without findings is excluded.
- Same volume, lines 1681–1698, **United States v. Hale**, February 12, 1996, **Congress may include possession in this regulation of the machinegun market**: integral transfer/private-stock regime; no transaction-specific interstate element; marketability alone insufficient. Government-related channels and lawful pre-May 19, 1986 possession remain statutory exceptions; manufacture date alone is insufficient.
- [Civil Rights](../../../state/holdings/civil-rights.md), lines 2424–2489, **Love v. Pepersack**, December 11, 1995: personal holder, incorporation, necessary acquisition, and actual weapon/conduct/restriction assessment; no universal scrutiny tier or completed infringement finding. Read the separate remedy paragraph.
- Same volume, lines 2493–2530, **United States v. Hale**, February 12, 1996: personal invocation without militia affiliation; military usefulness neither exclusion nor automatic immunity; actual object and statutory branch matter. Thirteen possession counts returned for arms application; separate registration disposition does not decide the possession prohibition.
- [Standards](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md), line 5726, **Required commercial connection and distinct economic-class regulation**: Present operation effective Mussari/Schroeder, June 30, 1997, for support-payment component; Lopez April 26, 1995 and Hale February 12, 1996 retain their component dates. No exhaustive six-route test or automatic instrumentality rule.
- Standards, line 9982, **Personal arms protection, incorporation and necessary acquisition**: Present operation effective Hale, February 12, 1996; Love incorporation/acquisition effective December 11, 1995. No separate Current wording authority is stated. Read open questions and the distinction between coverage and permissible regulation.
- Surviving pre-divergence sources identified by those holdings: *Miller*, 307 U.S. 174; *Wickard*, 317 U.S. 111; *Perez*, 402 U.S. 146; *Hodel*, 452 U.S. 264; *Bass*, 404 U.S. 336. Their stated holdings survive only at the scopes retained by the published simulated decisions.

## Parker

- [Constitutional Structure](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md), lines 2192 onward, **United States v. Mussari; United States v. Schroeder**, June 30, 1997: direct protection of a legally fixed cross-border payment obligation; essential different-state residence; private regulation does not command state government. No flight, bank wire, evasion motive, or voluntary crossing element.
- [Criminal Procedure](../../../state/holdings/criminal-procedure.md), lines 5476 onward, same case, **Whether conviction may rest on failures before §228's enactment** and **What each trial remand and any later restitution determination permit**: only post-October 25, 1992 willful failures; every element beyond reasonable doubt; older arrears may establish background; restitution treatment of older arrears remains open.
- [Federal Courts](../../../state/holdings/federal-courts.md), lines 7093 onward, **Domestic-relations boundaries**: enforcing an operative order is distinct from issuing or changing support, custody, parentage or status. Cognizable challenge to existence or continuing force of the predicate order survives.
- Standards, line 4327, **Post-enactment interstate child-support failures**: Present operation effective Mussari/Schroeder, June 30, 1997, construing offense effective October 25, 1992. Amount exceeding $5,000 OR nonpayment longer than one year; existing support order; interstate residence; willfulness; ordinary defenses; no pretrial guilt or restitution adjudication. No separate Current wording authority.
- Standards, line 5726, commerce module above; line 5708, **Compulsory state regulation or ownership of private obligations**, Present operation effective *New York*, June 19, 1992. [Constitutional Structure](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md), lines 2162–2190, *Printz/Mack*, June 27, 1997, preserves its qualified temporary-assistance rule and limited fiscal remand; neither categorical invalidation nor blanket validation of executive commands may be imported.
- Review channel: 28 U.S.C. §1254(1) permits certiorari before or after rendition of judgment in a court of appeals. The pretrial return of a prosecution is not itself a §1257 final-state-judgment problem. No merits conviction is under review.

## Salinas

- [Criminal Procedure](../../../state/holdings/criminal-procedure.md), lines 4866–4884, **United States v. Wells**, February 26, 1997: no court-added §1014 objective-materiality element; all enacted conduct, purpose, institutional and transaction requirements remain. This is an interpretive analogy, not a holding construing §666.
- Same volume, lines 2329–2370, **Ratzlaf**, January 11, 1994: context-sensitive willfulness and independently sufficient residual lenity after ordinary interpretation. Not universal ignorance-of-law protection.
- Same volume, lines 3591–3616, **Shabani**, November 1, 1994: §846 requires knowing agreement but no overt act; mere awareness or association insufficient. Offense-specific; §1962(d) needs its own construction.
- [Business and Commercial Law](../../../state/holdings/business-and-commercial-law.md), lines 129–167, **Reves**, March 3, 1993: §1962(c) direction requirement; expressly leaves conspiracy liability unresolved. Do not transfer a substantive participation condition automatically to §1962(d).
- Standards: line 4134, **False statements and influencing purpose under §1014**, Present operation effective Wells, February 26, 1997; line 3081, **Knowing unlawfulness in willful currency structuring**, Ratzlaf, January 11, 1994; line 3416, **Knowing narcotics agreement without an overt-act requirement**, November 1, 1994; line 7462, **Direction of enterprise affairs under §1962(c)**, Reves, March 3, 1993. None states a separate Current wording authority. Their statutory limits remain operative.
- Statutory anchors: offense-date 18 U.S.C. §§666(a)(1)(B), (b), (c), (d); §§1961(1), (4), (5), 1962(c), (d). Surviving pre-divergence agreement authorities to consult if needed: *Nash*, 229 U.S. 373; *Singer*, 323 U.S. 338; *Pinkerton*, 328 U.S. 640. Do not replace actual agreement with vicarious-liability shorthand.

## Foster

- [Election Law](../../../state/holdings/election-law.md), lines 559–608, **Voting Rights Coalition v. Wilson**, February 26, 1996: Article I, §4 allocation for congressional election administration; separate presidential authority; qualifications/administration distinction; limits on extending its public-enforcement remedy to other suits.
- [Constitutional Structure](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md), lines 1619–1640, **Thornton / Bryant**, May 22, 1995: exclusive congressional qualifications; genuine time/place/manner administration remains distinct. Lines 1553–1584, **Freightliner v. Myrick**, April 18, 1995: operative law and actual conflict required; overlap alone insufficient; express clause does not foreclose all independent conflict.
- Standards, line 5664, **Federal election registration duties through state agencies**: Present operation effective Voting Rights Coalition, February 26, 1996; retained statutory qualifications at enacted operation. Line 5964, **Motor-vehicle standards and independent actual conflict**, Present operation effective April 18, 1995. Neither establishes an election-day result or a private-action notice requirement; neither states a separate Current wording authority.
- Statutory anchors: then-operative 2 U.S.C. §§1, 7 and the distinct §8 vacancy/failure-to-elect provision; Article I, §4. Presidential timing is outside the supplied congressional controversy. Surviving authorities: *Smiley*, 285 U.S. 355; *Ex parte Siebold*, 100 U.S. 371; *Storer*, 415 U.S. 724, within preserved electoral-administration scope.
