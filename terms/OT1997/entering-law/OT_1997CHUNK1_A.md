## State Oil Co. v. Khan — November 4, 1997

The received rule is *Albrecht v. Herald Co.*, 390 U.S. 145 (1968), including its maximum vertical-price holding. No simulated replacement was found in the canonical antitrust volume or relevant Standards. *Continental T.V. v. GTE Sylvania*, 433 U.S. 36 (1977), addresses nonprice vertical restrictions; *Business Electronics*, 485 U.S. 717 (1988), preserves the significance of an actual price agreement; *Atlantic Richfield v. USA Petroleum*, 495 U.S. 328 (1990), preserves antitrust injury, including the competitor/dealer distinction. *Brunswick*, 429 U.S. 477 (1977), and Sherman Act §1 remain relevant. Lower courts' inability to overrule controlling precedent is distinct from this Court's power to reconsider it; see *Rodriguez de Quijas*, 490 U.S. 477, 484 (1989).

- [Antitrust](../../../state/holdings/antitrust.md) lines 274–298, **Brown v. Pro Football, Inc. — Whether joint implementation of the last wage proposal after bargaining impasse remains exempt** (1996-06-20), and [Standards](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md) lines 7716–7730, **Joint implementation in an ongoing multiemployer bargaining process** (same present-operation date): a limited ongoing collective-bargaining exemption. It expressly excludes product-market collusion and nonlabor agreements and does not classify this restraint.
- Standards lines 7610–7626, **Equipment competition and single-brand aftermarket power** (present operation *Eastman Kodak*, 1992-06-08): *Kodak*'s evidence-sensitive aftermarket inquiry does not answer per se classification here. Lines 7671–7683, **Below-cost pricing and prospective recoupment** (present operation *Brooke Group*, 1993-06-21), concern that decision's distinct predatory-pricing prerequisites.
- Standards lines 3589–3604, **Federal courts outside offense-date §1001’s entity definition** (present operation *Hubbard*, 1995-05-15), explicitly preserves *Hubbard*'s absence of a controlling extended stare decisis framework. Do not create a mandatory generalized test from separate rationales in that case.

Retaining, distinguishing or expressly reconsidering *Albrecht* requires an actual precedent analysis. Neither reclassification nor retention establishes market power, actual competitive effect, injury, damages or ultimate liability. No minimum-price or horizontal-price rule is automatically altered.

## Jeffries v. Harleston — November 17, 1997

Prior-law selection only. Fresh neutral validation must supply current-case framing and any necessary procedural-predicate treatment.

- [First Amendment](../../../state/holdings/first-amendment.md) lines 697–750, **Waters v. Churchill** (1994-05-31), contains three exact majority propositions: **A genuine, reasonable account of disciplinable speech supplies a limited safe harbor** (seven express supporters); **An unreasonable false account cannot defeat actually protected speech** (six supporters, including two judgment dissenters); **The motive dispute requires remand without restoring defendants' summary judgment** (seven supporting the limited disposition). There is no complete controlling investigation framework under *Marks*. Genuine reliance, protected conduct, actual causation and individual immunity remain distinct. The employee's substantial/motivating-factor burden and employer's preponderance same-decision defense survive.
- Same volume, lines 1366–1385, **United States v. National Treasury Employees Union — A broad prospective honoraria ban requires justification for its actual speech burden** (1995-02-22): six-Justice authority concerns a broad prospective burden, not an automatic rule for every individual personnel action. Its employment balance and burden on listeners preserve their stated setting.
- Same volume, lines 1977–2014, **Umbehr — Whether an existing independent contractor can challenge speech-retaliatory termination; What causation and same-decision requirements govern the remand** (1996-06-28): seven direct joins apply the actual-relationship *Pickering* balance and *Mt. Healthy* sequence. Lack of property entitlement is not dispositive; an imaginable lawful reason is insufficient.
- Same volume, lines 2016–2051, **O'Hare Truck Service**, particularly **How affiliation and speech or materially mixed claims remain distinct; What causation, defenses and remedy predicates remain** (1996-06-28): seven direct joins preserve the distinction between functional affiliation requirements and speech balancing. Personal responsibility and relief require independent support.
- Same volume, lines 2102–2135, **Settle v. Dickson County School Board** (1996-06-25): unanimous genuine-pedagogy and actual-causation holdings concern a student assignment. They do not establish a general university-employee capacity rule or import an employment investigation code.
- [Civil Rights](../../../state/holdings/civil-rights.md) lines 2237–2262, **Kimberlin v. Quinlan — Circumstantial proof remains available in motive-dependent claims** (1995-06-12), and lines 2264–2304, **Crawford-El v. Britton**, all three component headings (1997-06-16): unanimous rejection of direct-only proof and a special clear-and-convincing motive requirement; ordinary preponderance, competent circumstantial proof, lawful case management and actual same-decision proof remain. Conduct-date immunity is separate; later merits law supplies no retrospective notice.

Applicable [Standards](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md): **Public-employee speech and the employer's mistaken factual account**, lines 6578–6596 (present operation *Waters*, 1994-05-31); **Prospective restrictions on employees' citizen expression**, lines 4635–4651 (*NTEU*, 1995-02-22); **Political retaliation in existing government service relationships**, lines 4826–4847 (*Umbehr/O'Hare*, 1996-06-28, combined only prospectively); **Genuine pedagogy and religious perspectives in graded assignments**, lines 5068–5082 (*Settle*, 1996-06-25); **Circumstantial proof of unconstitutional motive despite qualified immunity**, lines 2050–2068 (*Crawford-El*, 1997-06-16, retaining *Kimberlin*). No separate current-wording override is stated.

Surviving authorities: First Amendment; 42 U.S.C. §1983; *Pickering*, 391 U.S. 563 (1968); *Connick*, 461 U.S. 138 (1983); *Givhan*, 439 U.S. 410 (1979); *Mt. Healthy*, 429 U.S. 274 (1977); *Harlow*, 457 U.S. 800 (1982), and *Anderson v. Creighton*, 483 U.S. 635 (1987), for conduct-date immunity. Actual verdict wording, Rule 49/50 posture, defendant roles and the relief on review must control any verdict-reconciliation remedy; this source map makes no new finding about them.
