### Hetzel v. Prince William County, No. 97-954. — 1998-03-23

#### Event

Hetzel v. Prince William County, No. 97-954. Certiorari granted, March 23, 1998; October Term 1997, chunk 5. The petition seeks review of the Fourth Circuit's mandamus command requiring an unconditional reduced compensatory judgment and staying a new damages trial. The limited question is whether that command may deny the plaintiff the option of accepting a properly determined remittitur or obtaining a new jury trial. The separate appeal from a later $15,000 District Court judgment is not included in this grant. No oral argument occurs on the petition.

#### Participation

All nine Justices participate. The internal certiorari positions are not disclosed.

#### Public Action

The petition for a writ of certiorari is granted, limited to the challenged mandamus restriction on the plaintiff's remittitur election. The certiorari poll is not publicly disclosed.

#### Judgment & Remedy

The grant changes the matter to the limited merits stage. It does not vacate a judgment, restore an award, decide the separate damages appeal or itself require retrial.

#### Opinion Topology

No opinion or separate writing accompanies this grant.

#### Holdings

The grant resolves no merits question and changes no governing law. The limited mandamus/election question proceeds to the separate summary-merits action of March 23, 1998; the grant itself neither selects damages nor orders a new trial.

#### Precedent Treatment

The grant makes no precedential disposition.

#### Law After Decision

Governing law is unchanged by the grant.

#### Separate Writings

No separate writing is issued.

#### Procedure After Action

The limited question is addressed in the separate summary-merits action of March 23, 1998. No broader question or additional appeal is taken up.
