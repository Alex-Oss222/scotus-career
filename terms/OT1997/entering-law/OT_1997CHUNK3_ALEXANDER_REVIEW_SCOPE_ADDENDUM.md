# Alexander — bounded review-scope clarification

The authorized review concerns constitutional personhood before live birth and the live-birth line for wrongful-death and survival remedies, as stated in the [clean packet's review scope](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_ALEXANDER.md#review-scope-and-participation). The [source-facts handoff](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_ALEXANDER_SOURCE_FACTS.md) expressly certifies no broader review scope; its [account of the lawyers' own-right and representative rulings](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_ALEXANDER_SOURCE_FACTS.md#reported-lower-arguments-and-rulings-without-fresh-target-framing), item 5, establishes that those components existed below, not that the lawyers' distinct independent constitutional entitlement to bring clients' tort suits is separately included in this Supreme Court review. The packet's instruction to distinguish claimants and its analytical discussion of their lower dispositions do not enlarge that authorization. That distinct unreviewed component may therefore remain undisturbed as a matter of scope, without adopting its lower-court merits reasoning or assuming the lawyers' Article III injury.

As the packet's [threshold section](../freeze/OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_ALEXANDER.md#thresholds-before-constitutional-merits) states, the mother's own alleged denied-letter obstacle may support her concrete personal remedial challenge, subject to the actual proper-defendant, live-relief and jurisdictional predicates. If that independent controversy permits resolution of the authorized questions, deciding the lawyers' separate standing is unnecessary; they cannot supply a missing maternal or represented-claim controversy. Any directly adjudicated child-representative component retains its own necessary threshold inquiry, distinct from whether the asserted constitutional right exists. This clarification selects no jurisdictional finding, merits result, requested judgment or vote. It infers no waiver or default from unrecovered originals and leaves every unrelated lower disposition outside the bounded review unchanged unless its treatment is actually necessary to implement a lawful resolution of the authorized questions.
