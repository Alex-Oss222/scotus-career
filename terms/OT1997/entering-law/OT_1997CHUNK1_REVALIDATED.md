## Brown — October 20, 1997

**Controlling holdings.** In [Federal Courts](../../../state/holdings/federal-courts.md), read the complete named entries: Zatko (November 4, 1991; lines 592–609); Martin (November 2, 1992; 2122–2165); Demos (March 8, 1993; 2087–2120); Day and Sassower (October 12, 1993; 3337–3394); Anderson (May 2, 1994; 3396–3431); Attwood (January 22, 1996; 4802–4830); Jones v. The last two are essential current authority, not optional historical illustrations.

**Distinct inquiries and burdens.** Rule 39.1 financial eligibility requires its prescribed motion and Form 4 affidavit or §1746 declaration, subject to the rule's actual appointed-counsel exception. Current means matter; particular supported clarification remains permissible. Incomplete research descriptions prove neither eligibility nor disobedience. Rule 39.8 permits, but does not compel, denial upon the particular paper's frivolousness or malice. Previous losses, unsupported pleading, lack of writ entitlement and an access label are each insufficient alone. Examine independent review-access content. Vey v. Clinton permits a supported current-paper denial despite assumed indigence; In re Vey preserves an arguable particular access contention despite other defects. Martin's lead and companion applications cannot be collapsed into an invariably favorable or unfavorable rule.

Future fee-and-form restrictions separately require demonstrated applicant-specific abuse, fit to the relevant filing category, and support for inadequacy of treating individual papers. Lower-court abuse, aggregate totals, a present denial, paid access or preserved criminal access do not supply missing predicates. There is no numerical threshold, universal warning prerequisite, categorical ban on future restrictions, or duty to exhaust every conceivable lesser measure. McDonald, Sindram and the earlier extraordinary-writ Demos retain targeted authority at their own scope. A fee order determines no certiorari outcome; another case's deadline does not transfer.

**Standards.** No freestanding Rule 39 module was found in [Standards](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md); use the direct Holdings. **Factual frivolousness and review of fee-screening dismissals** (lines 156–174), present operation *Denton*, May 4, 1992, within former §1915(d)'s scope, with the April 26, 1996 PLRA transition, preserves the distinction between clearly baseless facts and ordinary disbelief. It is not the whole Rule 39 test. No separate current-wording override is stated. Liteky's complete Federal Courts entry (March 7, 1994; lines 3515–3547) supplies objective appearance from actual circumstances if bias matters; adverse rulings alone are insufficient.

**Public Justice-specific continuity.** [In re Vey public decision](../../OT1996/output/OT_1996CHUNK6.md#in-re-vey-no-96-8005) records all eight Associates joining the particular arguable-paper grant and independent future-condition refusal. ## Jeffries — November 17, 1997; prior-law navigation only

This section supplies no final current-case framing. Use the separately routed objective facts extraction and the additional fresh validator's packet.

- [First Amendment, Waters](../../../state/holdings/first-amendment.md#waters-v-churchill-511-us-661), May 31, 1994 (lines 697–750): seven Justices expressly accept a genuine, reasonable account of disciplinable speech as a sufficient safe harbor. Six expressly agree that an unreasonable false account cannot defeat actually protected, causally relevant speech. Only four accept the complete objective-investigation framework. Genuine reliance and pretext remain material; the judgment leaves defendants' summary judgment displaced. No unified Marks investigation code follows.
- [NTEU](../../../state/holdings/first-amendment.md#united-states-v-national-treasury-employees-union), February 22, 1995 (1366–1385): six direct joins require justification commensurate with a broad prospective citizen-speech burden and its effect on listeners. O'Connor's separate case-specific balance is not a join in that complete formulation. The rule does not automatically govern every individual personnel action.
- [Umbehr and O'Hare](../../../state/holdings/first-amendment.md), June 28, 1996 (1977–2051): seven direct joins in each require the appropriate actual-relationship speech or affiliation inquiry and preserve causation, personal responsibility, entity attribution and relief. Lack of property entitlement is not dispositive. A hypothetical lawful reason is not proof of the same-decision defense.
- [Civil Rights, Crawford-El](../../../state/holdings/civil-rights.md#crawford-el-v-britton), June 16, 1997 (2264–2304), retaining Kimberlin, June 12, 1995 (2237–2262): unanimous ordinary competent direct/circumstantial proof and preponderance, without a special clear-and-convincing motive requirement. Claimant shows protected conduct and substantial/motivating causal role; defendant proves by a preponderance that the same action would occur without it. Lawful specificity, discovery management, summary judgment and conduct-date immunity remain.

## Rybar — November 24, 1997

**Commerce.** [Constitutional Structure, Hale](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md#united-states-v-hale-judgment-below-978-f2d-1016), February 12, 1996, Part I (1681–1700), unanimously permits possession control integral to §922(o)'s direct transfer/private-stock regime without a transaction-specific interstate element. Marketability, remote interstate history and generalized crime costs alone are insufficient. Its government-related and prior-lawful-possession exceptions retain their text. Earlier manufacture alone is not prior lawful possession. [Lopez](../../../state/holdings/constitutional-structure.md#united-states-v-lopez), April 26, 1995 (1284–1322), rejects the original school-zone law's remote cost chain and judicial insertion of an unexpressed commerce element; it supplies no exhaustive commerce formula.

**Exact Standards module:** [Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md#required-commercial-connection-and-distinct-economic-class-regulation), lines 5726–5750. **Present operation effective:** Mussari/Schroeder, June 30, 1997, for its interstate-support component; original dates remain for the earlier components. The relevant machinegun component is Hale, February 12, 1996; Lopez remains April 26, 1995. No separate current-wording override is stated. The support-obligation, vehicle-taking and medical-services components supply neither new elements nor an arms answer here.

**Arms.** [Civil Rights, Love and Hale](../../../state/holdings/civil-rights.md), lines 2424–2530. Love, December 11, 1995, Parts III-A–C, has six direct joins for personal holding, state incorporation and necessary acquisition. Miller's actual weapon/record inquiry survives; no formal militia affiliation is required. Hale, February 12, 1996, Part III-A, unanimously preserves personal invocation while rejecting both automatic military-utility exclusion and immunity. Part III-B, joined by all eight Associates, returns the thirteen possession counts because the rejected affiliation rule and inadequate independent application had controlled below. It requires the actual weapon, conduct and restriction inquiry, not automatic remand of every later case. Part IV's eight-Associate registration rationale concerns lawful making/transfer administration and does not validate every §922(o) prohibition or decide impossible registration.

**Exact Standards module:** [Personal arms protection, incorporation and necessary acquisition](../../../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md#personal-arms-protection-incorporation-and-necessary-acquisition), lines 9982–9998. **Present operation effective:** Hale, February 12, 1996; Love's incorporation/acquisition components date from December 11, 1995. No separate current-wording override. No universal scrutiny tier, closed regulatory list, exhaustive weapon classification, universal defensive-purpose rule or categorical §922(o) validity/invalidity exists. State incorporation is unnecessary in this federal prosecution. Section 921(a)(23)'s incorporation of §5845(b) preserves distinct completed-weapon, receiver, conversion-part and assembly branches.

**Public continuity.** [Love](../../OT1995/output/OT_1995CHUNK2.md#love-v-pepersack-fourth-circuit-no-94-1582) records O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas joining the controlling arms rules; Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer's state-nonapplication objections do not answer a federal prosecution. ## Jefferson — December 9, 1997

**Text and received framework.** [1994 §1257(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-1994-title28/html/USCODE-1994-title28-partIV-chap81-sec1257.htm) requires a final judgment or decree of the highest state court in which decision could be had. State certification and litigation importance are insufficient. [Cox Broadcasting, 420 U.S. 469, 477–487](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/420/469), supplies four bounded routes after a federal question is finally decided: practically concluded proceedings; an issue necessarily surviving further proceedings; no later effective review whichever outcome; or reversal precluding the relevant further litigation plus threatened serious erosion of federal policy. Category four is not satisfied merely because a litigant might win on nonfederal grounds.

**Direct simulated applications, all in [Federal Courts](../../../state/holdings/federal-courts.md):**

|---|---|
| Sullivan — **The sentencing remand does not prevent review of this completed conviction ruling**, June 1, 1993; 2708–2720 | Category two: conviction issue necessarily survives a separate sentencing outcome. Scalia's unanimous opinion; no general interlocutory exception. |
| Labron/Kilgore — **Whether the binding state suppression judgments are final enough for §1257 review**, July 1, 1996; 5463–5473 | Category three: State cannot obtain effective later review after either conviction or unappealable acquittal. O'Connor with Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer among the seven joining. Finality does not cure an adequate independent state ground or compel discretionary review. |
| Casarotto — **A finally decided federal notice-preemption obstacle is reviewable despite further merits proceedings**, May 20, 1996; 5498–5506 | Category four: litigation threatens the claimed federal arbitration forum right; possible unadjudicated contract defenses are not actual independent grounds. No separate §1257/Cox Standards module was located. Kokkonen concerns settlement-enforcement jurisdiction; De Grandy concerns the particular prior judgment's legal effect. Neither substitutes for these direct applications.
