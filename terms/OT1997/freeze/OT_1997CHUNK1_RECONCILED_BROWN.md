## Concrete changed premise and the later Vey qualification

Simulated **Martin, November 2, 1992**, requires particular-paper assessment and independently supported prospective control; its lead grant and companion denial have different established predicates. **Demos, March 8, 1993**, confirms that filing history cannot replace examination of current access content. **Jones, February 26, 1996**, forbids converting incomplete financial descriptions into either eligibility or default, while preventing defects in underlying claims from swallowing separate access content. Those are actual public holdings, not a general assumption that fees must always be waived.

Their [operative holdings](../../../state/holdings/federal-courts.md#vey-v-clinton) preserve the same distinction.

The June denial therefore remains a serious counterexample to any automatic grant.
