## June 25 changes relevant to this case

**Diamondstone: retained immunity and personal responsibility.** Holdings 3 and 4 retain Harlow/Anderson's objective inquiry into the particular right, the actual official's conduct and information, and law sufficiently established at the time. The eight Associates join the complete framework; the separate proposal to replace it with an official-burden contextual defense is not Court law. Actual judicial communications and superior advice may matter, but neither an adverse state ruling nor superior approval automatically settles federal immunity. No factually identical precedent is invariably required. Personal receipt, content, authority, opportunity, acts and causation remain individual inquiries; title, silence or another official's knowledge alone is insufficient.

Applied as entering law, this reinforces Bauchman's existing distinction between a viable underlying claim, each administrator's own role, and an independent immunity defense. It does not find that the reported April 1994 reprimand was received by every defendant, determine its content or legal effect, or transfer the teacher's knowledge to administrators. New June 25 legal explanation cannot itself supply notice for alleged 1994-95 conduct. A correction of a constitutional or pleading premise may require reconsideration of a dependent ruling without proving personal liability or directing a trial. Bauchman's appellate court did not decide immunity; Diamondstone does not decide it for this case. Proper pleading-stage assumptions remain distinct from eventual proof.

**Finley: selective arts grants remain distinct from school-directed religious activity.** The five-Justice narrow facial holding permits the mandatory decency/respect consideration in that particular competitive arts program because it imposes no categorical viewpoint veto or fixed disqualifying weight. It preserves review of invidious applications, conditions suppressing independent expression and the actual boundaries of Rosenberger and Forbes. It does not reclassify the supported private art as government speech. The four-Justice supplementary spending discussion and the broader subsidy concurrence do not supply a universal controlling exemption; the dissent's different viewpoint analysis remains published noncontrolling material.

This may inform the need to identify the precise government program and expressive role, but it supplies no new curricular test and does not displace Agostini, Lee, Ingebretsen or Settle. A school-taught graded choir is not automatically the same legal arrangement as selective support for independent art. Artistic selectivity does not prove official worship, and the artistic label does not establish constitutional immunity. The existing alternatives remain: genuine academic treatment and performance of sacred music versus adequately alleged school-directed devotional activity, resolved under the actual governmental-attribution/function inquiry and the proper procedural burdens. Finley's vagueness holding concerns comparative grant standards and does not adjudicate any new vagueness claim here.

**Bragdon: bounded method review, without a transferred medical rule.** Its controlling source-remand holding distinguishes an actual source's content, a judicial inference from it, and the special weight owed within its medical context. It leaves the lower court free to reach the same result and imposes no automatic trial or new-evidence entitlement. It does not create special legal deference to pedagogical assertions, an immunity for professional sincerity, or a rule that the Bauchman allegations are true. A possible methodological analogy must respect that Bauchman's amendment dispute is not an already-entered summary-judgment adjudication; the previously frozen notice-pleading, evidentiary-relevance and limited-discovery distinctions remain.

**Clinton and the remaining decisions.** Clinton's actual-action standing analysis preserves a personal supported injury and useful relief without a guaranteed final downstream result; it does not supply a new parent, sibling, capacity or damages claim to Bauchman, revive moot student-specific prospective relief, or decide nominal-damages availability. Its section 692 route is statute-specific. Eastern's property rule and divided retroactivity judgment, Balsys's independent-foreign-risk privilege rule, and Swidler's post-death attorney-client privilege have no new predicate in the supplied choir dispute. They change none of its governing claims or remedies. All published positions remain available in the shared snapshot at their actual noncontrolling scope; no position predicts a vote here.

## Reviewed questions and posture

1. Whether the credited public-school choir's selection, rehearsal and performance of religious music, including religious venues, violates the Establishment Clause where a secular artistic/pedagogical explanation is asserted.
2. Whether the alleged performance/rehearsal requirement, with the reported grade-neutral option not to sing objectionable songs, violates Free Exercise or protection against compelled expression.
3. Whether graduation moots prospective claims and what controversy a legally available damages claim preserves.
4. Whether affirming denial of leave to amend the Establishment Clause claim as futile was erroneous, including the categorical exclusion of pre1994-95 teacher conduct.

These are supplied analytical questions, not purported verbatim petition text. The amendment question is pleading-stage review: allegations are not established facts. The lower futility reasoning considered supporting materials and made an alternative summary-judgment forecast, but no actual summary-judgment judgment or trial findings should be invented. Review of that legal/procedural reasoning is distinct from a Supreme Court factual resolution of the proffered materials.

The original complaint sought declarations, an injunction and damages under section1983, RFRA and Utah constitutional law. The district court dismissed it, denied Rule59(e) reconsideration, permitted limited discovery on a proposed amendment, then denied leave as futile and alternatively untimely. The Tenth Circuit affirmed the original federal dismissal and denial of amendment, dismissed prospective relief as moot after graduation, and remanded state constitutional claims for dismissal without prejudice. It did not reach individual immunity or decide the district court's untimeliness alternative. The initial emergency-injunction appeal has its own history and was dismissed as subsumed/resolved, not silently merged into a merits award.

## Parties and actual controversy

Rachel Bauchman, who is Jewish, was a sophomore in1994-95. She auditioned for and joined Richard Torgerson's elective, credit-bearing a cappella choir at West High School, Salt Lake City. Her mother Cheryl sued solely as parent/guardian; no individual parental theory or younger-child allegation was pleaded, and the appellate court treated Rachel as the only plaintiff. Defendants included the teacher, high school, district and individual administrators; students/parents intervened to defend. The separate parties and capacities matter to damages and attribution; the appellate opinion does not supply each individual's sued capacity.

Rachel moved to private school for1996-97, declined an invitation to audition for the school choir, and graduated in June1997 after appellate argument. She could no longer enroll in district secondary schools. Mere ongoing ideological interest or concern for siblings does not create a new pleaded claimant. A surviving damages claim may support adjudication of past injury but does not itself preserve student-specific prospective relief. Conversely, graduation does not erase legally available damages. RFRA dismissal was not appealed and is outside the supplied continuing case; the high-school defendant's separate dismissal also appears unappealed in majority note5. Neither should be revived by this review.

## Original allegations, defenses and emergency history

Bauchman alleged a preponderance of Christian devotional songs in required graded rehearsal/performance, many by contemporary Christian composers, and performances at Christian locations including the Church of the Madeleine, First Presbyterian Church and Temple Square, with crosses or other religious imagery. She alleged Torgerson criticized her before classmates, emphasized her Jewish difference, blamed her family for cancellation of the spring tour, distributed a family letter through another parent intending to provoke ridicule, and rebuffed questions concerning an alleged alternative tour organized under a Boy Scout Explorer Post. Fellow students allegedly subjected her to religious/racial epithets and humiliation. These are allegations, not findings; teacher religious identity, unpopular curricular choices and actual official religious exercise are distinct propositions.

In connection with the Christmas concert series, Torgerson offered her the option not to sing songs she found religiously objectionable and assured her grade would not suffer. That is the supported opt-out account, not a finding that every rehearsal, venue, group activity, grade or practical consequence was voluntary. Utah statutory/regulatory waiver protections are recited in majority note11. Their existence does not resolve actual administration or every asserted participation pressure. The claims must confront the actual option and not recast the record as an established grade penalty.

The original complaint's challenged repertoire/venues did not expressly allege that selection was for religious promotion; the proposed amendment added that theory expressly. The original complaint did not allege actual participation by Bauchman in past tours or enough concrete activity for the proposed covert1995 tour. Those omissions do not mean historical conduct can never be relevant evidence of a present claim.

Emergency litigation addressed planned performances of Friends and The Lord Bless You and Keep You at1995 graduation. The district court denied the emergency request on those songs without deciding the broader requested injunction. The appellate court enjoined the songs pending appeal; some students/audience members later sang one. A contempt petition was dismissed after the appellate court adopted a district recommendation. No contempt liability finding is supplied. The first appeal's graduation issues were treated as resolved and broader issues subsumed into the second appeal. The Court must not convert that prior episode into new injunctive relief, a finding of governmental complicity, or an adjudicated contempt violation.

## Proposed amendment and limited discovery

The amendment specifically alleged religious promotion in selection of songs/venues, a continuing practice spanning approximately two decades, and each individual administrator's claimed responsibility. It included eight affidavits; two concerned1994-95/graduation, others earlier events. Both parties later submitted deposition/exhibit material. Discovery was restricted to1994-95 unless Bauchman established a clear and concise nexus between earlier conduct and her injuries; the district judge deferred amendment pending that inquiry. That restriction and the legal theory behind it matter to any proposed evidentiary-sufficiency disposition.

Murphy's separate opinion reproduces concrete earlier allegations: required participation in LDS prayer/sacraments in1977; an asserted1980 religious inquiry used for choir admission; approximately monthly LDS worship performances in1992-93; classroom use of devotional lyrics to advocate beliefs;1993-94 acknowledgement/rejection of limits on classroom religious advocacy; rehearsal with lights out and visualization of Jesus dying for students' sins; and an Oregon fireside at which the choir allegedly gave religious testimony as an LDS choir. Separate note11 identifies an April1994 reprimand for a prayer before a performance at Mormon worship. The later continued repertoire/venue pattern could support, but does not prove, a connection to1994-95 conduct.

These acts are allegations used to support a current claim, not claims for nonparties' injuries or judicially established historical violations. Bauchman did not argue that religious beliefs alone, even joined with a religious identity inference, establish liability. The teacher and district may contest the acts, temporal connection, admissibility and significance. Rule403 and other ordinary evidentiary restrictions remain; rejecting categorical irrelevance does not order admission of every past incident or unrestricted discovery.

## Exact lower reasoning, with source status retained

The majority recognized original-pleading review under Rule12(b)(6), accepting well-pleaded facts and using the then-applicable notice-pleading standard. It nevertheless identified plausible secular explanations itself: serious choral literature's religious content, teaching musical skills, acoustics/seating and public exposure of the choir. Majority note9 acknowledges that it could not evaluate defendants' own statements of purpose. Thus those explanations are appellate inferences at the pleading stage, not proven actual reasons or trial findings about these venues. The majority rejected establishment liability and treated retaliation/ridicule allegations as insufficient to repair its view of the underlying claim. It rejected Free Exercise/compelled-speech claims based on the opt-out and grade assurance. Each ground remains open to review under the actual current constitutional framework and proper pleading burdens.

For amendment, the majority excluded pre1994-95 conduct as irrelevant, emphasizing no lack of a clear secular purpose, nonrepresentative standing and limits on psychological-motive inquiry. It held that remaining current-year allegations/materials did not cure the pleading defect. Paragraph70 permits futility based on a forecast that an amendment would fail summary judgment and rejects treating this as actual conversion of the case into summary judgment. Murphy's note12 describes the majority as not separately deciding the merits of that alternative forecast. A precise disposition must not assume a completed summary-judgment adjudication, read the restricted record as unrestricted, or let a faulty legal exclusion become a finding of no evidence.

The district judge's untimeliness alternative remained unaddressed on appeal; the majority questioned it after court-authorized discovery but did not decide it. Murphy would allow amendment and relevant discovery, not order final liability, an automatic trial or an exclusively secular repertoire. He agrees with dismissal of the original complaint, but distinguishes concrete earlier acts from religious identity and reasons that actual purpose/current function may be illuminated by earlier conduct. His different legal analysis is a lower-court alternative, not a Supreme Court vote or holding.

The appellate court did not reach immunity after finding no adequately pleaded underlying deprivation. The amendment's detailed administrator allegations likewise were not held sufficient to establish liability, policy, personal action or a municipal cause. Section1983 does not impose respondeat superior on the district. The state-law disposition concerns discretionary supplemental jurisdiction and comity after federal dismissal, although the mandate uses want-of-jurisdiction language; it is not a determination that section1367 never gave power or that Utah supplies no private remedy.

## Controlling current law and conduct-time limits

Agostini retains Lemon as one inquiry using purpose, effects and entanglement as evidence: a specific prohibition or distinct doctrine controls conduct it resolves; an established permissible relationship defeats the claim on that ground; otherwise a legally sufficient unresolved allegation is examined through governmental attribution and function under ordinary procedure. These are three routes in that inquiry, not freestanding independent prongs or a fallback endorsement test. Government cannot organize worship, prescribe religious profession/observance, adopt doctrine as public truth or solicit adherence through public authority. A secular end does not cure prohibited religious means. Conversely, a teacher's faith or a religious location does not itself make secular musical instruction devotional. The actual governmental act and its function must be identified, without a rule that every song with religious text is worship or that every choir performance is exempt pedagogy.

Lee and Ingebretsen preserve official-worship prohibitions independently of coercion, with Lee's separate pressure holding operating within its scope. Tanford and Chaudhuri apply official-worship principles to adult university functions and distinguish genuine silence/private expression. They do not establish that these actual choir events were worship. Sherman permits its actual voluntary civic Pledge while preserving protection against compulsory words/gestures and distinguishing worship; it does not say abstention cures all establishment violations. Settle protects genuine curricular method while denying constitutional immunity to an impermissible religious exclusion or merely possible lawful causal account. No case grants a pupil unrestricted curricular control.

Bronx Household distinguishes genuinely private after-hours worship from governmental adoption and preserves actual forum boundaries. This school-taught graded choir cannot automatically be characterized as private forum expression from location or religious content. Rosenberger/Pinette likewise preserve private attribution on their actual arrangements; they do not resolve curricular devotional instruction by slogan. Hinds supplies no new Establishment Clause rule.

Arizonans/Hodge and the actual Spencer holding preserve continuing personal injury and keep legal availability of damages separate from prospective mootness. No nominal damages are invented against an entity/capacity where unavailable. Leatherman rejects heightened municipal pleading while preserving Monell/Canton's substantive responsibility requirements. Foman and the date-effective Rules8,12,15/59 govern amendment and its proper procedural stage; do not import later pleading standards. A permissible summary-judgment futility inquiry must still honor ordinary evidentiary rules, actual opportunity, supported assumptions and the legal relevance of materials.

Most alleged personal conduct occurred1994-95. Lee(1992) was then in force, as were applicable earlier official-worship/compelled-affirmation precedents. Later Settle/Sherman(1996), Agostini/Ingebretsen(1997), Tanford/Chaudhuri/Bronx and current procedural holdings can govern present adjudication but cannot themselves furnish retroactive notice. Any individual immunity analysis must identify the actual act, date, preserved defense, defendant's responsibility and then-existing federal right; no general holding of all-defendant liability follows from allowing a claim.

## Lawful alternatives and precise remaining limits

A lawful analysis can uphold a genuinely secular musical curriculum with a meaningful opt-out on the actual supported/pleaded setting, or find a legally sufficient claim of governmental devotional activity notwithstanding musical form. It must confront each side's strongest point: sacred art can be taught/performed academically, yet labeling conduct artistic and offering abstention do not cure government-organized worship. Musical predominance, venue, asserted pedagogical explanation, past acts, actual direction, current function and practical compulsion each matter at their proper evidentiary status, with no single proxy dispositive by itself.

The Court can distinguish dismissal of the original complaint from whether the amendment cures it; correct a categorical exclusion of historical evidence without deciding its admissibility or ultimate inference; preserve/reconsider the unaddressed timeliness or other genuinely independent grounds; and decide Free Exercise, compelled speech, prospective mootness and the surviving damages controversy separately. A conclusion on one claim does not automatically resolve the others. No grant of amendment itself proves unconstitutional conduct, individual liability, municipal policy, actual damages or entitlement to trial. No new claim for earlier students or younger siblings is supplied.

A remand may direct application of the correct legal/pleading framework without mandatory unrestricted discovery, an injunction, a specified repertoire, a new grade or damages. If relief rests on a concrete grade penalty, compulsory religious exercise, actual administrator knowledge/capacity or an asserted unreported filing beyond the recitations, the original complaint, proffered amendment, affidavits or relevant record is needed. The published appellate accounts suffice for reviewing the reported legal categories and pleading/procedural error without inventing those facts. Do not make missing research a finding of waiver, nonproduction or lack of evidence.
