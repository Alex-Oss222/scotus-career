## Statutory connection to federal funds

An express reservation of **whether §666 requires a distinct connection between the bribe and expenditure of federal funds** is compatible with the supported question whether the bribe must demonstrably affect federal funds. Those are not necessarily identical requirements. Resolving the proposed demonstrable-effects requirement need not decide every other possible statutory nexus. The supported connection between the charged jail conduct and the jail's housing of a federal prisoner permits the distinction to remain express without inventing a traced-dollar finding.

The statutory reservation and an outer constitutional reservation do different work and must remain separate. Neither declares an additional nexus necessary, identifies its elements, allocates a new burden, or resolves its precise satisfaction. Language declaring that §666 can never require any further connection to federal expenditures would exceed this bounded treatment. All expressly enacted agency, corrupt-intent, transaction, federal-benefits, timing and exception requirements remain operative.
