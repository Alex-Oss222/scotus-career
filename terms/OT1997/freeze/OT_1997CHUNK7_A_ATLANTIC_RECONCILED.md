## Common propositions and explanation for assembly

All eight Associates join: Section 1023(e)(3)(B) does not unambiguously restrict reserve strengthening to increases caused by changed methods or assumptions. Within that interpretive room Treasury reasonably defines strengthening through its specified net-addition calculation. Chevron, applied consistently with Smiley and Sweet Home, supplies the sufficient ground. Cottage Savings supports reasonable tax implementation; it does not permit disregard of the statute. Prior legislation and claimed industry usage do not establish the exclusive technical meaning urged here. Linguistic breadth and the transitional exclusion permit additions reflecting revised loss information to count even without a change in computational method. Fairness, administrability and avoidance of manipulation support the specified rule without making taxpayer wrongdoing a prerequisite. No artificial-reserve finding is made.

For each unpaid-loss reserve attributable to an accident year before 1986, compare the reserve at the end of the taxable year beginning in 1986 with the preceding year-end reserve reduced by attributable claims and loss-adjustment payments during the 1986-beginning year. Reinsurance ceded that year counts as a loss payment. Net strengthening is outside the fresh-start exclusion and is treated as occurring in the first taxable year beginning after December 31, 1986. Reasonableness of the reserve and taxpayer discretion are not additional computational prerequisites.

Both actual exceptions remain integral: exclude a nondiscretionary loss amount reported by a mandatory state or federal assigned-risk pool; exclude payments concerning reinsurance assumed during a 1986-beginning year and reserve additions for a line's reinsurance assumed then, only up to a hypothetical reserve computed with the assumptions, other than assumed interest rates, used for that line's reinsurance assumed in a 1985-beginning year. A 1985-beginning-year assumed or ceded reinsurance transaction is treated as occurring the succeeding year if the appropriate unpaid-loss reserve is not adjusted until that succeeding year. Section 845 separately governs a reinsurance transaction having avoidance of the strengthening limitation as one purpose. Neither exception is asserted here and no new avoidance finding follows.
