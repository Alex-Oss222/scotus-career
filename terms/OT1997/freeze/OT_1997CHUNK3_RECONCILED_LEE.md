**D mandate for the six remanding Associates:** Vacate the appellate judgment only insofar as it directs dismissal of the claimed compelled-participation challenges, including their injury and ripeness dispositions, and remand for ordinary claim-specific analysis. Both paths leave all assisted-death constitutional and statutory merits unadjudicated. The unadjudicated First Amendment, due-process, ADA, Rehabilitation Act and RFRA theories acquire no merits resolution here.
