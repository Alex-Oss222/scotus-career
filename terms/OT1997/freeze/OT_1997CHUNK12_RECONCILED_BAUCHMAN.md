## Common propositions and join boundaries

1. **Actual governmental function governs.** Agostini's current Lemon inquiry uses purpose, effects and institutional relationships as evidence within one inquiry. A specific prohibition or distinct doctrine governs conduct it resolves; an established permissible relationship resolves that relationship; otherwise a legally sufficient unresolved allegation is examined through governmental attribution and function under ordinary procedure. Genuine academic use of sacred music is distinct from organizing worship, prescribing religious profession or observance, adopting doctrine as public truth or soliciting adherence through public authority. Content, location, religious identity and an artistic label are not individually conclusive. A secular aim cannot justify prohibited devotional means.
2. **The original claim is bounded by its actual reviewed theory.** Both majority paragraphs 9, 37 and 56 and Murphy paragraphs 120–122 distinguish the original content/location objection from the amended allegation of a continuing official promotional practice. The original dismissal does not establish a requirement to plead particular words about subjective purpose. A properly alleged official devotional function is actionable without such a formula. Nor does the affirmance adopt the lower court's broader suggestion that religious targeting or retaliation can never illuminate function: the reported ridicule remains part of the context, but the particular original curricular and compelled-singing theories are insufficient at their stated scope. The case adjudicates no independent retaliation cause of action.
3. **Abstention has a limited role.** Sacred lyrics performed as choral literature do not by themselves establish personal religious affirmation. The reported grade-neutral song option defeats the original contention that singing the objectionable songs was itself compulsory. It neither cures adequately alleged school-organized worship nor establishes absence of every practical religious pressure. Sherman, Lee and Ingebretsen retain their distinct civic, compulsory-affirmation, sponsorship and actual-pressure rules. No formal-penalty or quantified-pressure requirement is added to governing law.
4. **Ordinary proof reaches relevant earlier acts.** The amendment alleges a present continuing practice and supports it with concrete earlier conduct, including religious exercises, religious admission inquiries and classroom devotional direction. These remain allegations. They differ from an inference based solely on a teacher's religion or uncommunicated belief. Temporal connection, truth, weight, admissibility and personal attribution remain contestable. Rule 403, Rule 404(b)'s distinction between legitimate evidentiary uses and character propensity, and ordinary proportionate discovery limits remain available. The Court orders admission of no particular incident. A categorical calendar cutoff cannot create the absence of evidence then used to defeat the proposal.
5. **A forecast is not an entered summary judgment.** Majority paragraph 70 allows a legally proper summary-judgment futility forecast but expressly denies that the district court entered summary judgment. Murphy note 12 disputes the forecast's merits and the adequacy of discovery; it describes the majority as not independently deciding that forecast. Under either account, the same erroneous constitutional exclusion cannot supply an independent affirmance ground. Proper legal screening remains available after the error is removed; this is not a blanket prohibition on summary-judgment futility.
6. **Procedure and liability remain separate.** Foman permits supported reasons for denying leave; the unaddressed timeliness alternative is not resolved here. Settle and Crawford-El distinguish actual supported grounds from possible lawful explanations and preserve ordinary case management. Their claim-specific causation rules are not transplanted as a new Establishment Clause burden shift. Leatherman retains ordinary municipal pleading without removing Monell/Canton's substantive requirements. Diamondstone retains Harlow/Anderson, objective particularized conduct-time notice and individual causal responsibility. Later law cannot supply 1994–95 notice. The April 1994 reprimand does not establish every defendant's knowledge, its content, its federal legal significance, or later liability.

Each Associate joins these six propositions at this scope. No commitment supports a universal curricular exemption, an exclusively secular repertoire, automatic amendment, an adjudicated current devotional practice, automatic damages, administrator liability by title, or reopening unrelated claims. No separate writing is required by the reconciled differences, and no author is selected.

## Historical comparison and competing routes

The comparator is the Tenth Circuit judgment, not a historical Supreme Court merits decision. Its original-complaint affirmance and mootness account are supported by the source; they are not votes attributable to any Associate. Its account of institutional authority requires correction under the supplied current law.

The lower majority used separate endorsement-purpose, endorsement-effect and entanglement inquiries. It imagined plausible academic reasons at the pleading stage, acknowledged in note 9 that defendants' actual explanations were not before that original-complaint inquiry, and treated the older conduct as irrelevant through standing and motive/purpose categories. Current Agostini replaces that independent-prong method with the governmental-function inquiry. Settle and Crawford-El independently disallow turning a possible lawful ground or a categorical proof barrier into an established answer to adequately supported allegations. Neither current law nor the record compels a finding of unconstitutional worship.

Murphy supplies a substantial lower-court alternative, not the controlling rationale. He agrees with dismissal of the original complaint, disputes the temporal evidentiary barrier, and would allow amendment and relevant discovery. His own free-standing endorsement-purpose theory is not adopted. His proposed rejection of timeliness is also not silently imported; the reconciled remand leaves that unaddressed alternative for ordinary adjudication. His note 10 preserves ultimate admissibility, and his note 13 leaves other dispositive issues undecided.

**Complete affirmance was tested.** Its strongest bases are real academic freedom, the distinction between artistic performance and personal profession, the actual abstention option, and the distance of some earlier incidents. Those support the original-claim result and lawful limits on proof. They do not justify treating every prior concrete act as irrelevant to an expressly alleged continuing practice or validate a futility forecast constructed on that exclusion. Each Associate's actual prior positions below support that distinction.

**Vacating the original dismissal as well was tested.** The strongest contrary argument is that repertoire, venues and targeted hostility together might provide fair notice of a devotional function under ordinary pleading. That argument prevents a general rule disregarding context or requiring an express intent formula. It does not compel reopening the original reviewed content/location and compelled-singing theories, whose narrower scope is independently described by both lower opinions. The supported new continuing-practice allegation is addressed through amendment. No preserved independent retaliation claim is invented to alter that route.

**Automatic amendment and trial were tested and rejected.** Removing one legal error neither resolves the unaddressed timeliness alternative nor establishes responsibility, immunity, evidentiary sufficiency or entitlement to relief. The bounded remand corrects the actual error while preserving genuinely independent defenses.

**Historical departure:** The Establishment Clause amendment/futility disposition and dependent state-claim direction depart from the historical lower judgment because Agostini's governmental-function inquiry and the ordinary-proof rules the Associates adopted in Settle and Crawford-El do not permit categorical exclusion of earlier concrete acts bearing on an alleged continuing present devotional practice. The Justice-specific support is stated below. This is no departure from an invented Supreme Court merits vote, and it does not adopt Murphy's entire theory or remedy.
