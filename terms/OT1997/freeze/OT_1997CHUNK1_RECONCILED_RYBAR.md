## Historical comparator and controlling law

There is **no corresponding historical Supreme Court merits lineup** for this assigned event. This lower-court division supplies no vote attributed to any of the eight Associates. Nor is the dissent attributed to an Associate by analogy or by later judicial service.

The lower court affirmed possession Counts I and III. Separate NFA transfer Counts II and IV had been dismissed and were not appealed by the Government. The reviewed possession charges involve a Chinese Type 54 7.62-millimeter submachine gun sold on April 4, 1992, and a U.S. Military M-3 .45-caliber submachine gun sold the next day to the same purchaser at the Pennsylvania gun show. The record identifies two completed automatic weapons. Neither their designations nor age establishes lawful pre-May 19, 1986 possession or another §922(o)(2) exception.

The majority treated §922(o) as regulation of the machinegun market, including possession as a demand-side restriction on acquisition and private-stock expansion. It separately rejected the arms objection through a required militia relationship. Alito's dissent challenged the existing possession prohibition on commerce grounds, stressing the lack of a jurisdictional connection and adequate legislative support while identifying jurisdictional language or supporting findings/evidence as possible alternatives.

Entering simulated law controls first. Rybar's completed sales strengthen the direct commercial setting but are not a new offense element. The lower dissent therefore supplies no ground to disregard Hale; none of the eight commitments proposes reconsidering it. The historical market reasoning corroborates the existence of a direct commercial subject, but does not enlarge C into authority over every marketable possession, remote interstate history, or generalized crime costs.

The lower militia premise cannot survive Love and Hale. The personal holder need not establish organized militia enrollment or service. Military usefulness neither excludes an arm from the guarantee nor confers immunity from regulation. Commerce authority remains distinct from the arms application. Hale's lawful registration holding does not sustain every possession prohibition and cannot restore dismissed transfer counts.
