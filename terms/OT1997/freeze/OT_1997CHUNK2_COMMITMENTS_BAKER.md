## Adversarial alternatives and record application

**Recognition without the requested evidence exclusion — selected.** The decree's binding operation between GM and Elwell is acknowledged, while the requested command to the Missouri tribunal is denied. The strongest objection is that a valid personal command can operate beyond Michigan, and compelling the enjoined witness to testify may accomplish indirectly what Elwell cannot do voluntarily. A judgment may also have lawful effects on third persons without formally naming them. Those points rule out treating the Bakers' nonparty status or Missouri policy as a complete answer. They do not establish that a broad promise or personal restraint against assisting GM's opponents becomes a judgment governing each opponent's evidentiary rights. The materials identify no adjudication of the Bakers' claim or of an item-specific privilege that the Missouri tribunal is being asked to relitigate. Recognizing what was decided therefore does not require the distinct exclusion GM requests.

This ground assumes neither that Michigan courts would annul the injunction nor that its extraterritorial personal operation is invalid. Even accepting a continuing personal command against Elwell, the present judicial act is the Missouri court's administration of evidence in a suit it is empowered to hear. The decree is not an appellate mandate to that court. The separate agreement's court-order protection further prevents treating the record as an unqualified prohibition in every relevant respect, but that agreement is not deemed to amend the judgment and is not the indispensable legal ground.

**Recognition as requiring exclusion until Michigan relief — rejected.** This path has support in finality, the rendering-state-first rule, and the continuing force of valid personal injunctions. Its best version would preserve the Bakers' tort claim in name while requiring Michigan relief before they could obtain this witness. It fails to identify why §1738 requires that consequence for this separate proceeding, rather than recognition of the decree's actual adjudicative scope. Celotex cannot supply the missing cross-proceeding authority: its actual bankruptcy relation, statutory allocation, supported restraint and concrete review structure did legal work. The Bakers' participation in their own tort action does not itself make them parties to the GM–Elwell dispute or establish a mandatory intervention duty. Affirmance on this path would convert a general private testimony restriction into an evidence rule without an adjudication of the affected claim or particular privilege.

**Instrument-construction-only reversal or exploratory remand — not selected.** A court could seek a Michigan construction of the injunction together with the settlement, but the selected ground does not require an unknown Michigan rule, an unquoted contractual term, incorporation of the agreement, or a finding that Michigan would modify its decree. Remanding merely to investigate those matters would defer the supplied legal question without resolving a fact necessary to the chosen rule. Those issues remain available in a proceeding where actual personal enforcement or a genuine instrument ambiguity is material. The distinction avoids both fabricated state law and automatic remand whenever original papers were not separately recovered.
