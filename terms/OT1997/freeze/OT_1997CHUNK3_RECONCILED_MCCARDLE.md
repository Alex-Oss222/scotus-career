**Rule and its source:** The 1997 Rule 50(a)(2) requires a motion specifying the judgment sought and its supporting law and facts. Rule 50(b) permits renewal of the legal questions raised by the trial motion made at the close of all evidence, with filing no later than ten days after entry of judgment; it supplies no general right to add a new ground after verdict. The official 1991 Advisory Committee explanation confirms both the opportunity to address an identified deficiency before submission and the ability to supply required information by explicit reference to materials and argument already given to the court.
