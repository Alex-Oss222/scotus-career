# Waste Management — assembly status

**Matter:** Waste Management, Inc. of Tennessee v. Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, May 27, 1998.

**Status:** Completed. The [Canonical Decision Record](../records/waste_management_v_nashville_merits_1998-05-27.md) states the Court's action. Both reviewed Commerce Clause determinations are vacated and remanded, 5–4, under the five-Justice public-facility and diversion-cost rules. The takings and ripeness rulings outside the grant remain undisturbed.

**Assignment:** Stone-Zsela authors the Opinion of the Court, joined by O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. This signed plenary lead contributes one Stone-Zsela opinion to the term's Section 8 count. Separate writings contribute no lead count.

**Legal compatibility:** The [assembly validation](OT_1997CHUNK9_WASTE_ASSEMBLY_VALIDATION.md) and [scoped reconciliation audit](OT_1997CHUNK9_WASTE_PUBLIC_GROUND_AUDIT.md) state the supported joins and validation results.
