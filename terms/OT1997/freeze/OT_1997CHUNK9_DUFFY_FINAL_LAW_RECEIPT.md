## Florence: relevant limits preserved; no changed commitment

Florence actually affirms two narrowly defined individual claims: knowing coerced transfer into an appreciated serious danger, and purposeful arbitrary enforcement against an actually comparable driver. It preserves actual awareness, each defendant's information and causal role, relevant comparison, purpose and effect, ordinary proof, and conduct-time immunity. These limits are consistent with Duffy's refusal to infer equal discriminatory knowledge or motive from membership on the three-judge hiring panel. The Duffy remand requires each defendant's own participation and remaining defenses to be evaluated; it orders neither collective liability nor an automatic trial or discovery program.

The police equality holding does not become Duffy's employment rule. Florence separates an objectively lawful arrest from a distinct equality objection but also preserves supported legitimate enforcement differences. Duffy likewise treats substantial lawful qualifications and references as serious evidence, while distinguishing their existence from a conclusive finding of the decisionmakers' actual motive. It does not use Florence to presume that two applicants are equally qualified, to make every hiring disparity unconstitutional, or to import the police comparator and burden formulation into the Davis claim. The frozen decision rests on the specific reported appointment statement and the treatment of the actual Rule 56(f) request, not on a mere differential result.

Florence expressly declines an orientation scrutiny tier, a general conscience framework and an Armstrong prosecutor-discovery threshold; it preserves Crawford-El without imposing a universal burden sequence. Duffy's received Fifth Amendment sex-discrimination claim and its ordinary civil proof/discovery analysis therefore remain distinct. No new heightened discovery hurdle or guarantee of a deposition follows. Scalia and Thomas's concurrence joins the narrow Court opinion while reserving broader direct-restraint/conscience questions; it neither repudiates the established Davis employment claim nor announces a rule against adjudicating every new constitutional application. No additional join barrier appears for their Duffy commitments.
