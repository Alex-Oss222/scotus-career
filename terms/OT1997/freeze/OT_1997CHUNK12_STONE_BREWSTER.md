**Proposed Stone disposition:** VACATE the categorical grant of qualified immunity and REMAND for separate evaluation of the First Amendment and procedural due process claims.

**Reasoning:** The fact that *Pickering* or *Mathews* requires contextual balancing does not automatically establish that every government official confronting those rules acted reasonably. A court must identify the constitutional interests at stake, evaluate the official's actual responsibilities and knowledge, and determine whether a genuinely defensible understanding of the governing law supported the particular decision. Good-faith uncertainty can protect an official, but the existence of a balancing standard is not itself sufficient.

The proposed disposition does not establish that Brewster proved retaliation, denial of constitutionally required process, or entitlement to damages.

**Doctrinal contribution:** Contextual fair notice; interaction between balancing tests and immunity; rejection of automatic protection based solely on doctrinal uncertainty.
