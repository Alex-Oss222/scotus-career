## Common procedural and legal premises

All eight Associates are seated, and no case-specific new-run nonparticipation is established. The paired dockets present one agency-interpretation controversy and the same threshold question; the commitments apply equally to both. The Supreme Court review channel and granted questions are supplied by the neutral packet. No additional cross-petition, independent claim, forfeiture or agency action is invented.

Article III injury is undisputed in this action. The challenged approvals permit customers who otherwise might use respondent banks to use ATTF; setting aside those approvals can remove that competitive effect. All eight Associates accept injury, traceability and redressability on that supported posture. Lujan's actual personal injury and litigation-stage proof requirements remain intact. The result does not mean that every competitor alleging an unlawful expansion has constitutional standing. MacArthur's distinction between an actual personal controversy and ultimate statutory success reinforces separation of Article III from the merits; its Privacy Act action and monetary provisions do not transfer here.

APA §702's provision-specific zone of interests remains a separate question. No independent statutory review preclusion or finality defect is established. The challenged charter approvals are completed, operative agency actions. Bennett supplies the provision-specific inquiry and preserves the other APA conditions; it does not eliminate the zone inquiry merely because economic injury exists. The ESA citizen-suit language in Bennett is not this cause of action. The zone ruling is not an Article III dismissal and must not be rendered as a finding that these banks suffered no injury.

The challenged sentence limits membership through occupational or associational common bonds, or the distinct geographic route. The exact question is whether the occupational route permits addition of multiple employer groups unrelated to one another, each possessing only its own internal bond. This is not whether all members must work for one employer, whether different employers can share one occupation or association, or whether lawful geographic membership is possible. No commitment displaces an express statutory membership exception or decides individual membership-retention rights.

The complete [official 1997 §1759](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-1997-title12/pdf/USCODE-1997-title12-chap14-subchapI-sec1759.pdf) first includes incorporators and other elected persons and incorporated or unincorporated organizations to the extent permitted by Board rules. Each member must subscribe to at least one share and pay its initial installment; a uniform entrance fee is required only if the board of directors requires one. The common-bond/geographic limitation qualifies that membership authorization. The separate final sentence permits survivorship joint-tenancy shares with persons designated by a member, but forbids a joint tenant to vote, obtain loans or hold office unless within the field of membership and a qualified member. That joint-tenancy allowance is not unlimited membership or an authorization to add unrelated employer groups. No family or membership-retention exception appears in this supplied section; the present issue does not establish any separate regulatory category. These commands and permissions remain distinct on either merits path.

The pre-1982 common-bond interpretation and the 1982/1989 policy changes are supported administrative history. Change alone neither invalidates an interpretation nor creates ambiguity. The geographic concession is attributed to NCUA: groups qualifying through the geographic clause must come from the same neighborhood, community or rural district. It is a pertinent concession and structural argument, not a separately litigated geographic holding. The banks' assertion that Congress worried about competitive injury and petitioners' contrary account remain submissions; neither is converted into an independently verified legislative finding.

## Enhanced zone-of-interests comparison

**Available path allowing review.** Section 109 itself sets the membership boundary whose alleged removal directly creates the challenged competition. These banks are participants in the same market, injured by agency expansion across that boundary. Clarke and the competitor cases it applies permit review without proof that Congress intended especially to benefit the plaintiff. This is a direct relationship to the provision's operative limitation, not a claim derived from some unrelated provision of the Act. Air Courier's postal employees had a collateral employment interest; its footnote 5 and discussion at 529 preserve the difference between employees and direct competitors.

**Strongest objection to that path.** A statute's restriction of entry or eligibility will often reduce competition incidentally. Treating that effect alone as sufficient can collapse the statutory zone into injury in fact, precisely what Air Courier forbids. The membership bond can protect the cooperative's members without protecting established commercial lenders against competition. Clarke found a statutory purpose beyond mere incidental benefit; Bennett found a particular protective data requirement.

**Available path denying review.** The occupational limitation concerns the character of a qualifying membership group. The banks' interest in keeping potential customers away from the credit union lacks the necessary protective connection to that particular membership condition. A shared preference for enforcing a restriction is not itself a sufficient statutory interest. This path does not require an express right of action in the FCUA, special-beneficiary status, or proof that Congress named banks; it requires an arguable relationship to the provision's purposes beyond economic consequences.

**Strongest objection to that path.** Clarke expressly rejects a special-intended-beneficiary requirement, and its discussion of Investment Company Institute accepts competitor challenges to restrictions primarily aimed at financial safety. A bank need not share every purpose of a statute to challenge agency removal of a statutory limit. The banks' direct market position provides a more immediate relationship than the postal employees' derivative interest.

## Enhanced common-bond and deference comparison

**Available path invalidating the policy.** Read as a limit on a federal credit union's membership, §109 requires the relevant occupational groups to share the common bond rather than letting each unrelated employer group supply a different one. The plural permits multiple groups sharing the bond; it does not render the bond requirement meaningless. The parallel geographic alternative supplies the same collective structure, consistent with NCUA's concession. On this reading, the agency's service-area requirements cannot replace the enacted occupational connection. MCI enforces a statutory boundary before deference, while NationsBank leaves permissible classifications inside it.

**Strongest objection to invalidation.** Ordinary plural grammar permits each group to have its own bond. Group-by-group qualification still excludes unaffiliated individuals and therefore need not make the statute surplusage. The occupational and geographic clauses use different grammatical forms, and NCUA retains group membership and administrative service-area requirements. Its policy therefore can be described as applying the condition to groups rather than abolishing it.

**Available path sustaining the policy.** The plural groups admits a distributive reading: every qualifying group must possess an occupational bond, but distinct groups need not share one bond with each other. Congress did not expressly say that one bond must unite the credit union's entire occupational membership. The agency may choose among the genuinely permissible readings within its express rulemaking authority over membership, and changed administrative policy alone is not a defect. That authority remains subject to the common-bond exception, whatever its proper unit of application. Clarke, NationsBank and Allentown Mack preserve agency policy and classification discretion within the statute.

**Strongest objection to sustaining it.** If every unrelated group can be added, the occupational clause ceases to connect the membership of the credit union as a whole. The geographic concession and the earlier decades of consistent common-bond administration reinforce the collective reading. An agency cannot use general access or viability objectives to remove a concrete statutory limitation; MCI controls that boundary even where the replacement has policy advantages.

## Remedy, opinion compatibility and limits

For the four Justices reaching the merits, affirmance preserves the D.C. Circuit's rejection of the challenged interpretation in both dockets. Their shared ground is the occupational common-bond boundary, not independent proof that the 1989 service-area rule is unreasonable, an APA procedural violation, or a new restriction on all future agency policy. No merits holding is made on membership through the geographic alternative, the breadth of a genuine common occupation or association, membership retention, or individual statutory exceptions.

For the four denying the statutory zone, reverse the reviewed judgment and remand to terminate this APA challenge on that statutory ground. Dependent merits relief cannot remain based on an action these plaintiffs may not maintain. This result would not adjudicate the agency construction's legality or convert the threshold dismissal into an Article III lack-of-injury finding. Stevens, O'Connor and Breyer's conditional merits agreement with the agency and Ginsburg's conditional disagreement remain noncontrolling unless actually reached and joined in assembly.

The source establishes that a nationwide injunction followed the appellate merits ruling, but its full wording has not been independently recovered. No Justice commitment expands that injunction, orders removal of existing members, liquidates a credit union, awards money, fixes a transition date, or approves every term of the unexamined decree. A bounded appellate disposition can resolve the presented legal questions without those additions. Any proposed new detail about the injunction's scope requires its actual text and appropriate neutral refresh.

Shared statutory-zone reasoning is possible separately within each threshold coalition. No doctrine may be assembled by summing constitutional injury, statutory zone and conditional merits votes. No Marks analysis is needed or supplied. No separate-writing author or actual final topology is assigned; assembly may preserve threshold dissents and any expressly conditional merits discussion at its actual level of agreement.
