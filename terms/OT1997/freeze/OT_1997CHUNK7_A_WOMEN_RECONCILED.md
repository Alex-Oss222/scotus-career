## P1 — Governing previability standard: five Associates

Apply Casey's complete six-step rule, as reaffirmed in Fargo; neither numerical large fraction nor Salerno's no-valid-applications formula operates as an independent threshold. The operative sequence remains:

1. Identify the actual enacted command, authoritative construction, exceptions, sanctions and medical circumstances. A prohibition of protected previability choice, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care independently fails. Patients need not prove medical necessity for protected choice; clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable treatment, not official approval of reasons. Care cannot await imminent death or catastrophic deterioration.
2. Identify actual function and objectively supported purpose. Safety, informed consent, truthful information, proper administration and the other functions recognized in Casey may be permissible. Preference for childbirth and truthful persuasion differ from obstruction. Moral disapproval does not justify blocking access, and objectively established obstructive purpose independently invalidates the duty; a legislator's opposition alone does not prove it.
3. Challengers establish concrete, nontrivial added interference attributable to the duty or identified package, against the system without it while holding other conditions constant. Define a stable affected population by the duty's operation, with supported subgroups; do not average over all pregnancies or select only successful claims. Cumulative effects cannot be double counted. Expense, delay, provider capacity, clinical risk, coercion and disclosure count through a demonstrated causal connection. Exceptions and alternatives must be usable. Reliable evidence and reasonable prospective predictions may suffice without waiting for lost access; no universal numerical threshold applies.
4. Baseline violation, obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle independently requires invalidity; no interest rescues an established obstacle, which need not make care absolutely impossible. Remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Remaining meaningful interference requires substantiated permissible justification and fit. When challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it; inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequences may suffice. No new study, scientific certainty, numerical benefit calculation, disproof of every hypothetical or cheapest-policy command is imposed. Below meaningful interference, this heightened access inquiry ends while independent protections and lawful-regulation rules remain. Challengers bear duty, population, causation and trigger; the State bears the applicable justification, tailoring or fit burden. Ordinary factual proof and statutory-defense burdens remain distinct.
5. Protect competent individualized clinical judgment and truthful, nonmisleading material communication. Evaluate professional evidence, circumstances and substantial contrary evidence; neither a professional association, bare clinical preference nor unsupported official assertion is conclusive. Necessary care cannot depend on an unnecessary committee veto or documentation functioning as permission. Actual culpability must be respected; reasonable later disagreement alone does not prove every element, and fraud, malpractice and misconduct remain regulable. Accurate content does not itself validate burdensome delivery or certification. Distinguish confidential oversight from public disclosure and enforcement of invalid conditions.
6. Identify the proved violation and matching relief under applicable severability law, addressing dependent certifications, reports and sanctions. Others' ability to comply defeats no individual's supported claim, but broad relief requires a corresponding showing. An indivisible mandatory condition may fail when its ordinary operation causes meaningful interference that the State fails to justify; voluntary compliance does not establish the legitimacy of compulsion. Otherwise confine relief to established applications or groups. Neither replacement of the old trimester timetable nor vacatur automatically dissolves supported protection or invalidates every requirement.

The strongest contrary positions fail to apply the controlling rule: absence of a substantial obstacle does not end all review, but regulation of abortion alone triggers no State substantiation burden. The five do not adopt a new framework, numerical formula or final method-ban invalidation.

## P2 — Actual wording, notice and fault: eight Associates

Vacate the categorical facial-vagueness rationale. Ohio defines D&X by purposeful insertion of a suction device into the fetal skull to remove the brain and expressly excludes suction curettage and suction aspiration; §2919.15(B) prohibits knowingly performing or attempting that defined act. An act-defined category does not become unintelligible merely because physicians sometimes perform the described act during a procedure medically called D&E. The published testimony describes purposeful skull suction during some D&E, not merely accidental contact and not every D&E. That overlap remains relevant to substantive interference.

The postviability criteria state specified medical outcomes, good faith and reasonable medical judgment. Fargo and Vuitch support intelligible professional forecasts without certainty. Colautti requires distinct examination of the actual trigger and fault concerning error; X-Citement Video supplies no universal knowledge-of-illegality rule. An intelligible criterion and uncertainty about a subsidiary fault element are not the same defect. No universal express-scienter requirement, bad-faith-only safe harbor, professional immunity, default Ohio culpability rule or all-purpose scienter cure is decided. Actual criminal and civil elements remain separate. This holding does not affirm every concrete enforcement or materially different authoritative construction.

## P3 — Necessary mental-health care after viability: six Associates

A postviability prohibition must permit abortion necessary, in competent individualized medical judgment, to avert the presented severe, nontemporary and potentially irreversible mental or emotional injury. Casey's expressly retained physical-and-mental-health baseline supplies this constitutional protection; Doe supports individualized clinical judgment. The protection is not a right to terminate after viability for every preference, distress or transient emotional reaction, and requires competent medical support. Necessary treatment cannot await catastrophe.

An intelligible emergency definition can still be substantively inadequate. Pennsylvania's emergency-delay construction does not automatically validate Ohio's broader prohibition, and Pennsylvania's information-omission defense supplies no Ohio emergency exception. Affirm the injunction insofar as it prevents exclusion of the described necessary care. The lower court must identify whether §§2919.17(A), 2919.16(F)/(J) and dependent commands permit a fairly supported protective construction under their actual words and Ohio law. If not, the restriction remains unenforceable to the extent of the established violation. Courts may not insert an unlimited new clause, assume a construction impossible, or treat every physician preference as necessity.

Scalia and Thomas dissent from this constitutional requirement under their retained Casey/Fargo objection. They do not supply a false statutory construction or new clinical fact.

## P4 — Method prohibition: five Associates remand; three would reverse the injunction

The five vacate the conclusive whole-Act disposition insofar as categorical notice analysis or the displaced facial formula substituted for actual Casey application. The evidence of purposeful skull suction during some D&E establishes a real reach and clinical-interference issue; it does not alone settle the affected applications, magnitude, usable alternatives or corresponding relief. Those identified legal predicates require application below, not a new trial merely because original transcript files were not independently recovered. Previability and postviability applications are distinct.

The criminal health defense requires a physician's prima facie showing that every available alternative would pose greater risk; the prosecutor must then prove beyond a reasonable doubt that at least one available alternative would not. The civil affirmative defense is separate and carries no imported criminal burden shift. The lower court must assess whether these actual protections permit necessary care and whether any demonstrated additional interference satisfies the applicable rule. Usable alternatives matter for the patient and circumstances. Lack of proof that D&X is universally superior does not answer whether forbidden conduct is necessary for a supported subgroup; unsupported preference or an assumed ban of all D&E likewise proves no violation.

Kennedy would reverse the previability method injunction under his narrower reasonable-regulation position: the act-defined restriction, actual alternative procedures and health defense defeat this categorical challenge absent an established denial of protected choice or necessary care. He preserves supported necessary-care applications and P3, but does not join the five's residual-substantiation remand or find all alternatives equally safe. Scalia and Thomas also would reverse that injunction under their continuing ordinary-regulation position, without joining Kennedy's constitutional necessary-care rationale.

## P5 — Severability, mandate and interim protection

For the five, affirm P3 protection in part, vacate the categorical grounds and overbroad final disposition, and remand P4 and provision-specific relief. Ohio §1.50 preserves independently operable provisions and applications. Under Geiger and Maurer, the court must determine separability, consistency with legislative intent and whether separation requires inserting terms or fundamentally disrupts the scheme. Leavitt requires that inquiry; its distinctive Utah clause supplies no answer for Ohio.

Keep the actual commands and exceptions separate: testing begins at the twenty-second week with the medical-emergency exception; viability is rebuttably presumed at twenty-four weeks, not constitutionally fixed then. Under §2919.17(A), the physician may proceed on the stated good-faith, reasonable-medical-judgment necessity determination or a conforming determination that the fetus is not viable. The five postviability duties are attending-physician written necessity certification; second-physician written certification after reviewing records and tests; a facility with neonatal services; the survival-favoring method unless it poses significantly greater maternal death risk or a serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function; and a second physician present to care for the fetus. Their separate medical-emergency exemption permits noncompliance when its terms are met; it does not require omission. Their method-choice risk exception is not a different section's alternative-method affirmative defense. Sections 2919.16(F)/(J)'s defined immediate/delay risk and diagnosed substantial irreversible impairment remain subject to P3's constitutional limit.

The Act's felony, misdemeanor and civil channels and dependent certifications must receive separate treatment; a patient is not criminally liable under the stated method/postviability provisions. No ruling automatically sustains or eliminates every reporting, testing, certification or damages provision. No fixed constitutional week, liability award or new criminal element follows.

Conform existing protection promptly to the affirmed necessary-care holding. For unresolved applications the lower court must promptly determine interim protection under the ordinary equitable standard and present record. The mandate does not finally dissolve that protection before this determination, order a hearing without demonstrated need, or permanently affirm a blanket injunction. Kennedy joins P3-linked protection and severability but would lift previability method relief; Scalia and Thomas would reverse the constitutional injunction. Their dispositions cannot be merged with the five's interim-method protection.
