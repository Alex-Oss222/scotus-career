## Reconciled common grounds and remedy

All eight retain the original five common propositions and the refresh's express qualifications. This substantive challenge is premature because the plan authorizes no cutting, material site/method decisions and public processes remain, a concrete project can be challenged before cutting along with its causally relevant planning premise, and the supplied record establishes no sufficient present consequence requiring immediate review. Agency refinement and useful factual development support fitness; neither agency discretion alone nor hypothetical amendment automatically defeats review.
