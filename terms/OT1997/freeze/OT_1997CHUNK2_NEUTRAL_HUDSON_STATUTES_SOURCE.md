# Hudson — sanction provisions and version boundaries

This control source extraction supplies statutory text history for fresh neutral validation; it decides no classification or judgment. The February 1989 monetary notice concerns early- and mid-1980s conduct, and the August 1989 debarment notice rests on the same lending allegations. Do not apply the later enhanced monetary tiers or broadened removal grounds merely because the consent orders were signed after August 9, 1989.

## Personal monetary sanctions

The relevant authorizations are **12 U.S.C. §93(b)(1)–(2)** for the national-banking violations and **§504(a)–(b)** for the pertinent Federal Reserve Act violations. Sections 101 and 103 of Pub. L. 95-630 enacted both regimes in 1978. Each expressly describes a civil penalty and covers the bank and specified officers, directors, employees, agents, and other persons participating in its affairs who violate the covered provisions. The operative monetary maximum was $1,000 per day of continuing violation, not the later 1989 tiered amounts. The enactment defines violating broadly to include causing, participating, counseling, and aiding or abetting; its monetary authorization does not add an across-the-board willfulness requirement.

Both amount provisions require consideration of financial resources and good faith, the gravity of the violation, prior violations, and other matters justice requires. Good faith is therefore an assessment consideration, not a textual requirement that every penalized violation be willful. The provisions establish agency notice, an opportunity for an administrative hearing and court-of-appeals review, and collection of final assessments; they do not themselves decide criminal guilt. Section 93(b)(6) authorizes compromise, modification, or remission. The official 1982 amendment notes establish that assessment became discretionary (“may be assessed”) under both statutes; §504 also received express compromise authority. No individual procedural default is inferred from these general procedures.

**Sources:** [1978 enactment selections](sources/chunk2_control/PL95-630-sections101-103-selected.txt), §§101 and 103, 92 Stat. 3641–3644; [official Code selections and amendment notes](sources/chunk2_control/USCODE-1994-title12-93-504-selected.txt), especially the 1982 amendments and §907(l) effective-date note. The latter makes the 1989 enhancements prospective subject to a limited exception for pre-enactment conduct not already subject to an agency notice and occurring after the last examination report. Here the monetary notice preceded August 9, 1989. The exact reported notice and consent terms, not an invented number of violation days, control the actual assessments.

## Banking participation prohibition

The relevant administrative authority is **12 U.S.C. §1818(e)**. It authorizes the appropriate federal banking agency to initiate removal or prohibition proceedings upon the specified conduct, harm or prejudice, and culpability predicates. Its alternative culpability language distinguishes personal dishonesty from willful **or continuing** disregard for banking safety or soundness; do not turn that disjunction into a universal willfulness element. The pre-1989 removal scheme also used the willful-or-continuing formulation, as the official historical note reproducing former §1818(e)(2) shows. A final classification analysis must retain all material predicates rather than treating this isolated phrase as the whole statute.

**Version limit:** The 1989 §903(a) changes to §1818(e) apply to violations and activities after August 9, 1989. The Code's post-1989 (e)(1)(C)(ii) numbering must therefore not be represented as the complete original authority governing these earlier lending acts. [Official §1818 history](sources/chunk2_control/USCODE-1994-title12-1818-history-selected.txt) supplies that effective-date limit and the former subsection's text. This extraction does not determine that a particular later-added removal ground was used or that each substantive predicate was adjudicated in the consent proceeding. The actual prohibition and written-authorization qualification remain exactly as in the main sanitized factual packet.
