**Merits, if a lawful route and eligibility exist:** Bailey excludes passive possession, proximity, accessibility and protective storage from active use, while reserving properly available carrying grounds. **Different procedural doctrines:** Schlup's reliable-new-evidence probability gateway, §2255's statutory successive certification routes, and a substantive statutory-innocence theory are not interchangeable.
