That observation is **not** an agreed complete judgment, a common mandate, a fifth vote for either remedy, or a common controlling rationale. No additional acceptance of a bounded compromise disposition has been modeled. Reconciliation and assembly must preserve this fork unless a supported, properly staged legal change supplies an express different commitment.
