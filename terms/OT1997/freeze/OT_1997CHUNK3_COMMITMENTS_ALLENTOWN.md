## Enhanced comparison of supported paths

**Facial validity.** Sustaining a genuine objective reasonable-doubt threshold is supported by the Board's explained concern that even safeguarded employer polling can unsettle the bargaining relationship, and by polling's remaining function of confirming doubt before a more consequential withdrawal. It need not be the best policy or the only permissible one. The strongest competing path rejects requiring the same predicate for a poll and withdrawal: uncertainty is the very reason for inquiry; §§7 and 8(c), safeguards, and less consequential polling favor more accessible investigation. That objection exposes a serious practical tension, but no supplied text commands the proposed distinction, Gissel reserved its necessity, and Auciello allows a reasoned stability choice without converting it into a statutory mandate. Each Associate provisionally sustains the announced threshold; none adopts an actual-minority threshold or holds that any conceivable administration is lawful.

**Scope.** One supported path considers the evidence only to determine the legal standard actually applied, declining independent reweighing as beyond the question. The competing path regards the claimed excess of demanded evidence as including actual application of the threshold to the very order under review. The latter is available here because the petition itself links the rule to the quantity of evidence demanded; it does not depend simply on the briefs. The commitments below reach a bounded whole-record application, while preserving Hubbard's limit against adding a freestanding question or revisiting the successor and unit findings.

**Whole-record application.** An affirmance must address all the contrary evidence: seven credited or assumed individual expressions, Marsh's dissatisfaction, Bloch's night-shift report, and Mohr's informed report. Seven of 32 is neither a minimum nor a maximum lawful doubt threshold. Marsh's remarks can indicate alienation without necessarily opposing representation; the interview setting informs meaning without establishing coercion. Bloch's report has some probative force despite the absence of his basis, but its stated collective content cannot automatically become five or six additional, nonoverlapping repudiations. Mohr's steward and bargaining role supports access to employee sentiment; substantial workforce continuity gives his old-workforce report some relevance. The uncertainty about his sources, departments, group size, and retained composition can nevertheless reduce its force. His personal indifference is not a personal repudiation.

The strongest reversal path is that discounting each item separately eliminates the circumstantial evidence Auciello expressly preserves, while the Board's old-workforce explanation disregards the 32 retained employees and the reasonable-doubt question. The strongest answer is that the ALJ expressly assessed Mohr's account together with the other evidence and identified concrete deficiencies affecting its reach. The Board adopted the adverse findings and separately supplied the old-workforce rationale. Those explanations are distinct: the latter cannot be expanded into a universal exclusion, and the former must be tested for adequacy rather than treated as an automatic credibility finding. On this record, the affirming commitments regard the adopted, cumulative assessment as a permissible inference under Universal Camera. Stevens instead finds sufficient uncertainty about the operative cumulative assessment to require reconsideration. Neither conclusion treats an ALJ label as judicial truth or supplies invented demeanor evidence.

**Remedy.** Affirmance leaves the existing enforcement judgment in force. A limited vacatur for erroneous application requires renewed agency consideration under the lawful doubt standard, not judicial certification of actual minority, a new numerical rule, or compulsory use of another circuit's formula. An outright denial of enforcement would require the further conclusion that only one lawful application is possible; the provisional remanding commitment does not make it. The February 8 vote is excluded as retroactive justification for the January 25 decision. The poll's §8(a)(1) issue and refusal-to-bargain consequences remain analytically distinct; the incomplete charging allocation does not establish a separate §8(a)(5) prohibition of polling. Any correction of the predicate would require lawful treatment of its dependent order, without reaching the separately unpreserved attack on the bargaining remedy.

## Individual commitments

### John Paul Stevens

**Reach and judgment:** Reach facial validity, the question's actual-standard challenge, and the associated whole-record application. Sustain the facial reasonable-doubt rule; vacate the enforcement judgment for reconsideration of the application's cumulative basis. Leave the independent, unpreserved bargaining-remedy objection unreviewed.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** His full Auciello join accepts explained Board policy while expressly preserving circumstantial doubt and distinguishing it from actual minority. His Fall River join preserves separate review of the policy and its factual application. His published Joiner position would return a cumulative evidentiary method for consideration under the correct standard without directing its ultimate acceptance. That position is relevant to the completeness of the assessment, not a transfer of scientific-admissibility review to NLRA factfinding. Here the stewardship report, substantial retained workforce, night-shift report, and individual disaffection must be given their supported combined significance under doubt rather than minority proof. The agency's additional old-workforce reason makes it insufficiently clear that this distinction controlled the final application.

**Counterargument and boundary:** The ALJ did expressly mention combination and identified real source and coverage limitations; repetition of the word cumulative is not missing. Stevens's objection is therefore substantive and confined: the adopted analysis and additional Board explanation do not adequately resolve whether the collective reports were treated as potentially supporting reasonable uncertainty, rather than rejected because they failed to prove a majority. He would not hold every report reliable, aggregate overlapping employees, infer actual minority, or require reversal whenever a reviewing Justice prefers another inference.

**Remedy and joins:** Remand through the reviewing court for lawful reconsideration, allowing the Board to retain a supported adverse application. No required hearing, new poll, election, result, current-preference finding, or automatic decertification. Can join the facial-rule and preservation propositions; cannot join enforcement on the present application or a holding that the employer conclusively satisfied the threshold. A narrower legal-standard remand is compatible; a broader invalidation of the polling prerequisite is not his commitment. A separate writing serves the limited application disagreement if necessary.

### Sandra Day O'Connor

**Reach and judgment:** Reach the preserved facial and bounded application questions; affirm enforcement. Decline the independent unpreserved remedy objection.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** Her complete Auciello join permits explained bargaining stability within statutory boundaries and retains circumstantial doubt. Her Richenberg opinion distinguishes meaningful consideration from a requirement to accept every denial or contrary inference, while refusing invented agency reasons. The actual Allentown reasons—uncertain sources, coverage and retained composition, equivocal individual remarks, and express combined consideration—permit the Board's conclusion under Universal Camera. They are more specific than a bare invocation of its presumption. The old-workforce observation is not an independently valid categorical exclusion; the adopted contextual analysis supplies the sufficient ground.

**Counterargument and boundary:** A steward's report and a substantial disaffected group can support doubt without a majority tally. She gives them that potential force but does not find that they compel doubt here despite the documented limitations. Her Fall River dissent concerned continuity and representative complement, expressly leaving the remote-certification issue open; it supplies no present commitment against this polling rule or a basis to reopen unchallenged successorship.

**Remedy and joins:** Existing enforcement stands, without a new evidentiary proceeding or separate approval of the decertification bar's merits. Can join an opinion sustaining actual cumulative weighting and genuine reasonable doubt. Cannot join an actual-minority requirement, a categorical rule excluding steward or group reports, or a rule insulating ALJ findings from whole-record review. A properly supported legal-error remand would be lawful, but the present record does not establish its predicate.

### Antonin Scalia

**Reach and judgment:** Reach facial validity and application insofar as the question challenges the evidentiary demand actually made; affirm the enforcement judgment. Do not use that reach to decide the unpreserved remedy objection.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** His full Auciello join requires statutory construction before deference, and his Fall River join distinguishes permissible Board policy from the application inquiry. Sections 7 and 8(c) do not expressly confer the claimed right to poll on safeguards alone, and Gissel left compulsory permission undecided. Polling can still test whether supported uncertainty should lead to withdrawal, so the threshold is not logically self-defeating. On application, an authentic doubt standard is less demanding than proof of minority; the ALJ's expressly combined assessment does not become a disguised minority rule merely because equivocal, unsourced, or coverage-limited reports receive reduced weight.

**Counterargument and boundary:** The serious contrary claim is that administration, not wording, demands a headcount and nullifies circumstantial doubt. The credited seven are not sufficient by arithmetic alone, but neither are the additional reports excluded by arithmetic. On the supplied reasons, qualitative uncertainty about what the reports establish permits the adverse inference. His Joiner application join supports respect for permissible inferential judgments, without transferring its abuse-of-discretion standard or eliminating Universal Camera's independent statutory role.

**Remedy and joins:** Affirm on rational rule and supported actual application; no additional ground based on the eventual 19–13 poll or on an assumed independent bargaining violation. Cannot join a majority-proof construction, a judicially devised polling prerequisite, or an opinion treating every agency application as unreviewable. A legal-standard remand would be appropriate if the record established substitution of minority proof, but that substitution is not provisionally found. No separate writing is required for the compatible ground.

### Anthony M. Kennedy

**Reach and judgment:** Reach the facial rule and its bounded actual application; affirm. Preserve §10(e)'s separate bar to the remedy-explanation objection.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** His Auciello join preserves statutory employee choice while allowing reasoned stability policy. His Joiner opinion separates legal errors, historical facts, and permissible inferential judgments rather than converting consequence into a more stringent standard. Applying Universal Camera here, the evidence materially detracts from the Board's conclusion but does not compel a different one: the group accounts lack sufficiently established basis and present-unit coverage, and the particular remarks remain susceptible to a distinction between dissatisfaction and opposition. The adopted reasons allow these limits to be considered together without inventing credibility findings.

**Counterargument and boundary:** Polling may be a less intrusive response than withdrawal, and a rule in practice requiring proof of minority would undermine its investigative function. That objection does not defeat this facial rule where doubt remains circumstantial and the actual assessment can be sustained without proving employee support. His Richenberg join accepts that considering rebuttal evidence does not compel accepting it, while retaining the requirement that the actual reasons do the work; it supplies no blanket administrative deference.

**Remedy and joins:** Affirm the existing order through enforcement; no current-majority declaration or new procedural direction. Cannot join categorical exclusion of old-workforce evidence despite substantial continuity, a refusal to consider cumulative reports, or a scope rule allowing any issue mentioned in merits briefs. Can join an explanation relying on the adopted source and coverage limitations, with the old-workforce statement confined to that context. A broader employer entitlement to poll is not supported by his provisional position.

### David H. Souter

**Reach and judgment:** Reach facial validity and whole-record application of the challenged evidentiary threshold; affirm enforcement. Do not reach the separate remedy objection.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** His Auciello join accepts permissible policy only with the distinct factual and procedural safeguards intact. His Richenberg dissent insists that actual agency explanations give rebuttal evidence meaningful force; a reviewing court may not manufacture the missing connection. Here, unlike a premise that receipt alone suffices, the ALJ supplied particular reasons about Mohr's sources, scope and retained composition and expressly addressed the combined evidence. The Board adopted that assessment. Those reasons can account for the collective reports without treating them as inadmissible or demanding a majority count. His Joiner application join is consistent with sustaining a permissible inference despite acknowledged contrary material; Universal Camera remains the governing review rule.

**Counterargument and boundary:** Workforce continuity and Mohr's union responsibilities substantially weaken a blanket old-workforce objection. Souter would reject that objection if it were the sole ground or if adoption of the ALJ's distinct reasons were not established. The released packet establishes adoption and the actual alternative explanation, so reliance on it does not invent an agency rationale. Auciello's circumstantial-evidence qualification permits such evidence to establish doubt; it does not require every collective report to prevail.

**Remedy and joins:** Existing enforcement stands. Can join the facial rule and case-specific cumulative application; cannot join a categorical rule that unidentified group members or an older workforce make a report irrelevant, a presumption treated as proof of actual support, or remand merely to improve drafting despite sufficient actual reasons. The narrower remand path remains lawful on a materially corrected record showing categorical exclusion; it is not the present commitment.

### Ruth Bader Ginsburg

**Reach and judgment:** Reach the facial and bounded application challenges and affirm the enforcement judgment. Apply §10(e) independently to the remedy objection.

**Sufficient ground and personal authority:** Her unanimous Auciello opinion carefully preserves independent statutory limits, permissible policy, circumstantial doubt, and separately unpreserved objections. **Counterargument and boundary:** The steward's role and retained majority of the workforce make his report significant, and the Board's separate old-workforce statement cannot erase it. A remand would follow if that statement were the only operative rationale or if the combined reports were categorically excluded. On the released record, the adopted, distinct assessment gives a lawful basis for assigning limited weight. Neither her Auciello opinion nor this conclusion establishes actual employee support or makes the presumption conclusive.

**Remedy and joins:** Affirm the existing enforcement judgment without separately approving every possible bargaining remedy. Can join the announced-threshold and supported-application holdings with explicit preservation of circumstantial evidence. Cannot join generalized exclusion of group reports, a numerical threshold, or a new actual-minority determination. The broader invalidation path lacks a statutory or unexplained-policy predicate on this record; a narrower application remand remains available when an actual legal mismatch is established.

Can join an opinion preserving circumstantial doubt, meaningful whole-record review and the difference between permissible and mandatory Board policy. Cannot join a requirement of majority repudiations or an insulated presumption.
