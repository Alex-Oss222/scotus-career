## Reconciled legal grounds and material qualifications

### Statutory channel and discretionary alternative

Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg construe individual within this statute's remedial context to include an adversely affected juridical person. Congress made cancellation applicable to corporate and governmental fiscal interests and created a prompt constitutional-review mechanism with congressional notice and intervention. The contrary reading is linguistically substantial: individual ordinarily denotes a natural person; the Dictionary Act differentiates persons and individuals; section 691e(9)(B)(iii) itself distinguishes businesses/associations from individuals. Ordinary federal-question review remains available, so the broader reading is not justified by falsely claiming that entities would otherwise have no judicial remedy. The five instead conclude that the scheme's particular prompt-review design encompasses separately injured organizational claimants. This is a construction of section 692, not a universal definition of individual and not jurisdiction based on importance alone.

O'Connor and Scalia retain their objection: ordinary review makes Congress's possible preference for expedited human claims workable, and statutory purpose does not erase the chosen word. Cranney's presence cannot cure his own absent personal injury or transfer statutory eligibility to an independently injured entity. Their proposed alternative depends on a valid, distinct section 1254(1) application and an express exercise of Rule 11 discretion. Breyer accepts review without joining that objection; the reconciliation does not invent a previously unstated textual rationale for him.

Section 692(a)(2)'s prompt delivery of the complaint to both Houses and their intervention rights, subsection (a)(3)'s House-intervention protection, subsection (b)'s ten-calendar-day notice and thirty-calendar-day jurisdictional-statement periods and prohibition on a single-Justice stay, and subsection (c)'s expedition command remain separate. Section 4722(c) covers identified taxes, fees and assessments collected before June 1, 1997, for which the specified waiver was requested or otherwise would be required; when requested, administrative action including judicial review must have remained incomplete on July 23, 1997. The Court need not adjudicate the unions' separate standing. Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer accept Snake River's injury at the opportunity level. The cooperative's acquisition purpose, identified discussions, owner's expressed interest in a tax-deferred transaction, and incentive tied to purchases by eligible cooperatives permit a reasonable inference that withdrawal impaired its actual negotiating opportunity. Bryant v. Yellen supplies a predivergence example of economic opportunity injury without certainty of the eventual purchase. Investment Company Institute v. ### Article I invalidity: Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg

These five support the same controlling level of generality if the Court reaches the merits: after the underlying spending and tax provisions became law, the President's discretionary cancellation removed their operative legal force on a new appraisal of fiscal and national policy. Congress cannot authorize that partial repeal outside bicameral passage and presentment. Chadha supplies the requirement that repeal, like enactment, follow Article I; the distinction is between that later legal change and implementation of an enacted contingency. The President may return an entire bill before enactment; these cancellations select parts after enactment.

The strongest contrary theory reads the earlier cancellation Act and later fiscal enactments together as one congressionally authorized contingent scheme. Field v. Clark permits implementation of a congressional policy upon a specified factual contingency, with the prescribed consequence when that contingency is found. Here the President may decline cancellation even after making the required determinations; he chooses whether to remove the enacted item for his policy reasons. The difference concerns actual operative authority, not the title of the Act or a prohibition on every executive decision affecting a statute. The five reject the argument that any legal-force consequence is necessarily invalid; it is this cancellation power's post-enactment discretionary displacement of the provisions that supplies the ground.

The enacted restrictions are real and preserved: the President may cancel only an entire qualifying dollar amount, item of new direct spending or limited tax benefit; he must determine deficit reduction, no impairment of essential government functions and no harm to the national interest; consider legislative history, construction, purposes and specified or best available information; and send the required special message within five calendar days excluding Sundays. The message must reach both Houses for cancellation to take effect. Enacted disapproval nullifies the identified cancellation and restores the item from its original effective date, and disapproval laws are exempt from cancellation authority. These conditions do not convert the authority into a compulsory factual-contingency implementation. The tax-identification and separate judicial-review restriction, statutory exceptions to the limited-tax-benefit definition, and deficit-accounting provisions are not interchangeable conditions.

Stevens's Hechinger ground concerned a congressional agent with operative control, not a universal independent Article I rule. Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg likewise preserve that limit. Loving authorizes bounded implementation of Congress's military punishment choices and forbids contradiction of those choices; Thomas joined its judgment only on a military-specific basis and supplies no civilian-delegation rationale join. Morgan Stanley and Plaut permit Congress itself to enact a bounded new entitlement while leaving independent adjudication to courts. Their changed simulated holdings therefore do not require acceptance of presidential cancellation. For Kennedy and Thomas, their structural objections in those matters remain consistent with invalidity here; for Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg, their support for enacted legislative adjustment is not consent to executive repeal.

Kennedy additionally supports a separate substantive explanation that structural divisions protect individuals as well as institutional prerogatives: the capacity selectively to burden a visible group affects liberty, and voluntary congressional surrender does not cure it. His full join to the common Article I rule does not depend on the other four accepting every additional observation. No new author or separate-writing assignment is prescribed.

The sufficient Article I ground leaves independent nondelegation/balance-of-powers grounds undecided. It also leaves severability of discretionary-budget cancellation provisions and disputed lockbox applicability undecided. Even if the canceled items continue to affect deficit accounting, their lack of force for these plaintiffs remains the relevant alteration; no precise lockbox application is necessary to that conclusion. Affirm the reached declarations, restoring the underlying provisions according to their own terms. There is no presidential injunction, assessment, refund, completed sale, tax award, retrospective invalidation of every other cancellation or ruling on an unpresented severability question.

### New York merits opposition: O'Connor and Scalia; shared presentment ground with Breyer

O'Connor and Scalia sustain this direct-spending cancellation if lawfully reached. Enactment satisfied Article I; the subsequent choice exercised authority contained in those enacted laws, read together. Field demonstrates that authorized executive reduction of a statute's operation is not categorically forbidden by the Presentment Clause. The constitutional issue is therefore the permissible scope of the delegation, not literal noncompliance with the initial enactment steps. They accept Breyer's same execution-versus-amendment explanation, but that agreement does not establish agreement on every category's delegation validity.

For direct spending, early discretionary appropriations and Cincinnati Soap support Congress's authority to leave expenditure choices to the Executive. Bowsher distinguished execution from legislative-agent control; Hechinger preserves that distinction. Train shows that an executive may not withhold funds when Congress has required their allotment, while leaving Congress able expressly to confer discretion. This Act supplies that authority and confines its subjects, timing, required considerations and determinations. Loving's support for ancillary executive implementation is consistent with, but is not the sole source of, their conclusion. The difference between eliminating a spending item's legal effect and declining expenditure does not create an impermissible delegation on this record. They recognize constitutional limits on delegation and no inherent presidential cancellation power.

Their conditional New York merits remedy is reversal of its declaration on the presented presentment and delegation objections. Their restrictive special-route position persists. Unless an actual lawful route is established, no such merits disposition can be entered merely from this conditional conclusion. For the tax action they continue to seek jurisdictional disposition and withhold merits assent; their common presentment analysis cannot be counted as an affirmative tax-validity judgment.

### Breyer's distinct complete validity rationale

Breyer accepts standing in both actions and would sustain both challenged cancellations. He shares with O'Connor and Scalia the argument that the earlier statute's authorization is part of the law that the President executes. His further functional and nondelegation analysis is distinct. The limited budgetary choice resembles established executive expenditure and tax administration; Congress chooses the affected subject matter, may exempt provisions through legislation, retains the disapproval mechanism and supplies procedural, purposive and substantive guidance. J. W. Hampton's intelligible-principle rule, the context-sensitive delegation authorities, and Skinner v. Mid-America Pipeline's rejection of a stricter constitutional nondelegation standard merely because taxation is involved support his conclusion.

He considers the strongest objections rather than equating all delegations: the tax-cancellation history is thinner than the spending history; selective tax burdens create greater arbitrary-treatment concerns; the President has not supplied subsidiary administrative rules and ordinary agency-style review is absent. The limited budget subject, express item categories and tax restrictions, mandatory consideration of the underlying law, short cancellation period, deficit/essential-functions/national-interest determinations and presidential electoral responsibility collectively answer those concerns for these applications. This supplies neither inherent authority to rewrite taxes nor a rule sustaining every delegation outside the budget. The statutory definition's 100-or-fewer and 10-or-fewer categories and their separate exceptions remain operative; a shorthand reference to a small group never eliminates them.

The actual Plaut holding allows a bounded congressional adjustment with the judicial function retained, which is compatible with his attention to the institution's retained function but does not decide this executive power. Hechinger's congressional agent is absent. Loving supplies a bounded example, not general military exclusivity or unlimited executive discretion. Breyer reverses both reached declarations on the presented structural objections. His additional reasoning receives no assumed join from O'Connor or Scalia, and no assignment is made.
