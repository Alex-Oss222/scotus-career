## Scope and disposition screened

This internal review addresses completion of National Association of Home Builders v. Babbitt in its existing June 8, 1998 slot. The assembled disposition under review is affirmance, 5–4: Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer adopt the as-applied economic-construction ground preserved as Path A in the final reconciliation. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas retain reversal and remand confined to the challenged applications. Stone's separate anti-evasion/material-impairment and substantial-interstate-externality framework supplies no controlling proposition.

The screened rule reaches the actual habitat-altering construction and conversion in the challenged project applications, followed by aggregation. The Associates' authoritative inputs remain [frozen commitments B](OT_1997CHUNK10_COMMITMENTS_B.md) and [final reconciliation B](OT_1997CHUNK10_RECONCILED_B.md).
