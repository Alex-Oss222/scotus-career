# MacArthur — third supplemental instruction

## Verbatim controlling user instruction

Resolve MacArthur under the governing law and supported record. I am authorizing realistic consideration of Stone’s position and test, without prescribing whether either wins.
Resolve the reported blockers first. Under Engine §14, identify the exact unresolved fact or legal question affecting statutory redressability, explain why it prevents a merits decision, and conduct the targeted research needed to resolve it. Use what the published opinions and available record establish. Distinguish an unavailable research document from an actual deficiency in the litigation record. Do not assume jurisdiction, invent facts, or treat uncertainty about the ultimate remedy as automatically defeating jurisdiction. Any material reconstruction outside existing authority must be presented as a specific premise for my approval.
Stone’s conditional instruction: If the supported analysis establishes that unresolved threshold matters require a threshold-only vacatur and remand, I authorize Stone to join that disposition. Preserve his complete Continuing Pertinence Test for possible later consideration; neither adopt nor reject it at that stage. If the statutory merits are properly reachable, retain his approved merits position.
When the merits are reached, use two separate decisions under Engine §§8–9. First determine the disposition. If Stone’s proposed merits disposition commands a majority, expressly submit his complete Continuing Pertinence Test, exactly as written in the approved brief, for a separate adoption vote as the legal basis for that disposition. Agreement on a threshold-only remand does not trigger this merits-test vote.
Evaluate each Justice’s support realistically from that Justice’s date-eligible legal reasoning, the governing simulated law, and the record. An identical historical formulation is unnecessary. General agreement with the test’s objective is insufficient unless the Justice also supports its operative requirements.
The complete test requires five affirmative votes from Justices supporting both that merits disposition and that test. Agreement with the disposition alone does not count. Record support for the complete test, support for specified portions, and opposition separately. If fewer than five support the complete test, it does not become the Court’s rule; the disposition and any independently supported majority rationale must be reported accurately.
Preserve Stone’s test word for word. Identify any alternative formulation separately, and do not attribute substantive changes to Stone without my approval. Record the disposition coalition and doctrinal joins distinctly, with concise legal grounds for material disagreements.
Continue the other twelve completed decisions through rendering. Resolve MacArthur as far as the law and record permit, reporting any remaining specific blocker without treating a reserved merits question as a rejection of the test. After the vendor is complete, re-adjudicate MacArthur and then update the readiness.
