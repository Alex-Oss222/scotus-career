## Associate-specific findings

**O'Connor.** Full join and vacatur are supportable. The class holding accepts her frozen objection; Hale, Rybar and Kirk supply the regime route, and Hinds preserves independent work for the enacted predicate. The concrete interstate effect, territorial limitation and reasonable fit answer her demand for a mechanism without treating conservation's national importance as sufficient. Her join in Kennedy's historical Lopez concurrence supports practical federal authority with a judicially enforceable national/local boundary. Recognizing the expressly reserved route and testing it on the existing record is a bounded inference, not a claim that Hale already adopted it. Her Hinds remand join supports resolving the legal predicate before assuming factual insufficiency.

**Kennedy.** Full join and vacatur are likewise supportable. His American Life League, Rybar, Robinson and Bird opinions require a prohibition's actual operation and reject labels or remote cost chains. The opinion offers a concrete interstate operation, not merely a hospital label or construction expenditure. His historical Lopez concurrence supports practical analysis rather than rigid categories, while preserving an enforceable federal boundary. The record, territorial and fit conditions do the necessary limiting work. The packet attributes no ecological finding to him and supplies no commercial-transaction prerequisite that would withdraw his join from the stated independent route.

**Ginsburg.** Her economic-application objection remains intact. Sweet Home, Hinds, Robinson and Bird support her view that the habitat-injuring construction is itself economic conduct, rather than a property owner's collateral purchases. Accordingly she dissents from Part I. That disagreement is not falsely presented as answered by a repeated enacted-class assertion. Part I nevertheless has five independent votes. She chooses to accept its controlling classification as the premise for her operative votes on the remaining questions. Her Hinds existing-record remand join supports examining the record under the Court's governing predicate; her join in Breyer's historical Lopez dissent supports practical class-wide analysis and legislative latitude. With the preferred ground unavailable under Part I, supported alternative interstate mechanisms remain legally open. She affirmatively chooses inquiry into those mechanisms over invalidation unsupported by the proper inquiry, joining Parts II-IV and vacatur. She casts no concurrent affirmance vote.

**Breyer.** His Path A analysis also remains intact, and he dissents from Part I. His historical Lopez dissent and effective functional-market joins would not support simply declaring that economic development has ceased to be economic. The packet makes no such claim about his position. Instead, the independent class holding supplies the operative legal premise for his further votes. His practical concern with actual regulatory operation and legislative prediction supports both affirmative routes; Part III preserves substantial predictive latitude while requiring a reasoned legislative or adjudicative basis. His Hinds vote supports existing-record examination under the Court's adopted predicate. He therefore chooses actual vacatur/remand under Parts II-IV, without assuming that an unestablished spillover cannot exist. The concurrence must preserve his disagreement with Part I and his complete assent to the remaining parts and judgment. This is a supported institutional accommodation, not a finding that his original class objection disappeared.

**Stevens.** Affirmance and refusal to join are supported. His Hinds opinion's actual-function approach and Sweet Home, Robinson and Bird positions support the economic habitat-conversion ground. He treats that ground as dispositive and declines to accept Part I as the premise for his own remedy vote. Abstract agreement that other routes can sometimes exist supplies no inferred opinion or remand join.

**Souter.** Affirmance and the stated Stevens-dissent join are supported. His Sweet Home opinion locates liability in actual habitat-causing activity with ordinary causal limits; his Hinds and Robinson joins support examination of actual function. He continues to regard the economic application as sufficient and the alternative inquiry as unnecessary. The accommodation accepted by Ginsburg and Breyer does not compel him to accept it.

**Scalia.** Joins in Parts I and II-A, refusal of Parts II-B-IV, and confined reversal/remand are supported. His Lopez no-insertion and Hale-line commitments accept an enacted interstate-regime mechanism and reject constructing an economic predicate from the actors' circumstances. The independent externality route remains objectionable to him because territorial regulatory need does not itself establish commerce or necessity to a valid commerce regime. His partial joins do not imply assent to the Court's new route, combined support framework or remand. Sweet Home remains binding and no categorical intrastate-species immunity is attributed to him.

**Thomas.** His identical partial joins and Scalia-separate-opinion join are supported by the frozen enacted-mechanism commitments. His historical Lopez concurrence adds a Justice-specific objection: a problem affecting several States is not for that reason an enumerated subject. He accepts the governing Hale architecture while withholding assent from the independent externality route. The packet does not attribute an overruling of current law or revival of his Sweet Home statutory disagreement.

## Qualifications and integrity

The Government's support responsibility requires a reasoned basis, not a fixed quantum of proof or judicial substitution for environmental science. Courts independently determine constitutional eligibility; Congress receives substantial latitude on supported prediction, magnitude and design. National desirability, biodiversity, possible future value or comprehensiveness alone remains insufficient. Neither a valid interstate-regime relationship nor an ecological spillover is found on this record merely because the opinion recognizes an available route.

The remand examines the existing legislative and adjudicative record. It invents no market, interstate purchase, ecological linkage, actual taking, permit entitlement, default or filing event. It supplies no blanket development permit and does not reopen Sweet Home's statutory construction. Source unavailability must not be treated as a litigant's failure of proof.
