## Final commitments and exact support

**Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg, and Breyer each retain affirmance of the Fifth Circuit judgment on the two supported statutory questions. Each joins S1R, S2R, and S3 with all of the following limits.** This is a unanimous Associate commitment, not a final Court count.

**S1R — Demonstrable effect on federal funds.** Section 666 does not require proof that the bribe demonstrably affected federal funds. The corrupt-intent and agency requirements, covered business/transaction threshold, federal-benefits condition and period, and actual statutory exceptions remain operative. The federally connected jail setting permits this answer without deciding **whether the statute requires a distinct connection between the bribe and expenditure of federal funds**. That is an express statutory reservation, separate from the reservation of outer constitutional applications. Neither reservation adopts a nexus requirement, defines a new element or proof burden, or finds a traced federal dollar.

The county's receipt of qualifying benefits under a federal program is assumed for these questions, not newly adjudicated; no rule makes every federal receipt a qualifying benefit. No precise annual amount is found. General coverage of intangible benefits is outside this decision. Valuation of the covered business, transaction, or series is not reopened. The $5,000 condition applies to that statutory object, not automatically to the bribe or the watches/truck. The federal-benefits threshold exceeding $10,000 in the relevant period remains distinct. These are review limits, not exemptions from elements or a declaration that a demonstrated element defect is harmless.

**S2R — Knowing RICO agreement and the personal-two-act objection.** Section 1962(d) does not require the defendant personally to commit or personally to agree to commit two predicate acts. It requires a knowing agreement to further an undertaking that, if carried out, would satisfy the substantive enterprise offense. The agreed objective must remain unlawful and encompass the applicable substantive elements; mere knowledge, friendship, employment, or association is insufficient. *Reves*'s governing substantive direction requirement remains part of the contemplated violation rather than automatically becoming a requirement that every conspirator personally perform the substantive role.

This holding rejects the supported personal-two-act instruction objection. It does not authenticate or approve the entire charge, adjudicate an independently presented personal-direction instruction, resolve general evidentiary sufficiency or harmlessness, or announce general aiding-and-abetting liability. The substantive §1962(c) acquittal remains intact. No separate universal conspiracy code is derived from *Shabani*.

**S3 — Judgment.** Affirm on those two statutory questions. No new-rule instruction review or special remedial remand is required by these constructions. No acquitted substantive count is revived, conviction enlarged, or sentence independently reviewed. Any proposed disposition resting on a disputed predicate, complete charge, general sufficiency, or harmlessness issue needs its own authorized source and preservation basis.

The full eight-name coalition above expressly supports **each** proposition and reservation. No additional holding or broader join follows from their matching judgment positions.
