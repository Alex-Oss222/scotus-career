## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Employment discrimination — supervisory harassment and agency

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** When a supervisor’s sexual threats do not culminate in a tangible employment action, what establishes employer responsibility for an actionable hostile environment?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Title VII’s agency principles; hostile-environment threshold; actual authority, apparent authority, and negligence are distinct possible attribution routes.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** AFFIRM the rejection of categorical employer nonliability and REMAND for application of the simulated Faragher rule to the actual summary-judgment record.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone would require the court to identify how the supervisor’s actual delegated power helped create or maintain the asserted hostile environment. Employment threats can supply that connection even when not carried out; formal rank or access to a workplace cannot alone do so. Ellerth must still establish the actionable environment under Harris. Burlington’s policy and the available complaint route matter to negligence, actual prevention, and any avoidable loss, but nonuse alone cannot erase proved authority-aided responsibility. Stone would decline a blanket immunity from liability based on a paper policy and would require evidence that protection was practically available and effective before crediting a claimed protective defense. This follows Faragher’s actual holding while treating any further complete defense as a new doctrinal proposal requiring justification, not as an already approved historical rule.
* **Treatment of Controlling Precedent:** The completed simulated Faragher decision already holds that actual delegated authority materially aiding actionable harassment can establish responsibility without a tangible action or higher-management notice. Rank alone is insufficient. Faragher expressly reserves whether genuinely effective safeguards and unreasonable nonuse could supply a complete defense; the historical Ellerth/Faragher formula is not already simulated law. Harris separately requires an objectively and subjectively abusive environment.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The existing simulated Faragher holding binds Stone even where his separate writing disputed its application. A new defense must not silently overrule that judgment.
