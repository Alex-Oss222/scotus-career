# Aktepe v. United States

## A. Case, record and posture

**Identity and event:** Eleventh Circuit No. 96-2167, 105 F.3d 1400; lower judgment February 20, 1997. User-authorized plenary review scheduled March 10, 1998 under the ordinary court-of-appeals review channel. No separate Supreme Court docket or argument date is established. All nine seated Justices are eligible absent a separately established participation fact; none is supplied here.

**Reviewed judgment and relief:** The district court granted summary judgment for the United States on political-question grounds; the Eleventh Circuit affirmed. The sailors seek reversal and adjudication of two wrongful-death and 299 personal-injury claims under the Public Vessels Act, Death on the High Seas Act and maritime tort principles. Liability or damages are not established by a decision that the case is justiciable.

**Record:** During a 1992 multinational NATO drill, the Saratoga fired two live Sea Sparrow missiles at the Turkish Muavenet. All confrontations were intended as simulated attacks. Some firing-team members were not told the event was a drill; operators understood the arm-and-tune command to require live arming, while supervising officers did not appreciate that meaning. These background facts are uncontested in the published summary-judgment record. The complaint challenges communications, training and drill procedures as well as operational conduct. The lower court concludes that adjudicating those allegations would require judgments about military training, command and competing safety/effectiveness choices; that is its legal rationale, not an established fact binding this Court.

**Question:** Whether this particular negligence controversy is nonjusticiable under Baker's issue-specific political-question principles, including whether any separable operational claim can be adjudicated through manageable legal standards without selecting military policy. The lower courts did not decide Feres. No original source establishes an intentional attack, torture or a different categorical-abuse claim.

## B. Entering law and supported paths

The exact Nixon, Dalton, Weiss and Loving entries preserve constitutional and statutory review despite military or political subject matter while recognizing actual issue-specific commitments and scheme-specific preclusion. Richenberg gives substantial military weight within an independently enforceable legal standard; it supplies no blanket military immunity or tort holding.

**Nonjusticiability path:** Determining a reasonable drill could require judicial choice about realistic battle simulation, command obedience, live-weapons precautions and multinational operational arrangements for which ordinary negligence supplies no independently manageable standard. Gilligan's concern with judicial management of military training and Baker's commitment/standards inquiries support testing that asserted intrusion. The strongest objection is that this suit seeks retrospective compensation for an unintended accident rather than an injunction placing training under continuing judicial control, and that military context alone cannot establish nonjusticiability.

**Adjudication or bounded-remand path:** Accidental firing through misunderstood instructions may permit application of an existing operational duty without deciding whether the exercise, alliance or training policy was wise. A court could distinguish obeying established safe procedures from designing them, or permit genuinely severable claims. The strongest objection is that no particular controlling safety rule is supplied as an uncontested record premise, and calling conduct operational does not answer whether the proposed duty itself changes training or obedience policy. Do not invent a violated written protocol. A remand could require issue-specific consideration only if the existing record supports a separable legal inquiry; it cannot manufacture negligence or guarantee discovery.

**Prior writing leads:** Nixon records Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas joining its confined nonjusticiability ground, with Souter outside that rationale. Souter's unanimous Dalton opinion is tied to an enacted review scheme. Kennedy's Weiss and Loving writings preserve constitutional limits on military arrangements; Thomas's distinct Loving judgment ground remains distinct. Scalia's pre-divergence Stanley and Boyle opinions are possible leads for military discipline and discretionary procurement, but their statutory/remedial settings must not be transformed into a universal political-question bar.

## B. Operative record and material qualifications

Tier 2 reaches qualifying schools, child-care facilities and organizations serving children or supporting women; Tier 3 reaches members of the public likely to encounter the registrant. The thirteen-factor Scale has weighted categories and thresholds, but is not conclusive. Individual review permits challenges to facts/calculation, atypical cases and excessive notification, but does not permit attacks on the Scale itself or its weights in that proceeding. # United States v. Robinson

## A. Identity, scope and record

Fifth Circuit No. 96-11165, 119 F.3d 1205, decided August 8, 1997. User-authorized plenary review scheduled March 23, 1998 under the court-of-appeals review channel. The convictions comprise Hobbs Act conspiracy, aiding and abetting three robberies, and aiding and abetting one firearm offense. The Fifth Circuit affirmed convictions but vacated the sentence and remanded because the vulnerable-victim enhancement was unsupported on its stated race/business-habit rationale. That sentencing mandate is preserved as distinct background.

The targeted businesses sold interstate goods and cashed out-of-state, payroll and government-benefit checks. Robbers watched the merchants' banking routines and took business withdrawal money in bank drive-through lanes or store parking lots; the property was not unrelated household cash merely because seized away from a shop. The lower record reports impaired check-cashing and restocking capacity and one business closure for lack of capital. Those are published recitations, not new findings. ## B. Entering law and supported alternatives

**Direct-market-interference path:** Targeting operating businesses' working capital can directly disrupt interstate purchasing and check-cashing functions. On that defined class, an actual slight nexus plus class aggregation could satisfy the commerce power without relying on general social costs of robbery. The strongest objection is the need to identify the enacted statutory class and a manageable boundary: describing all crime victims as consumers or all stolen money as potential interstate spending would reproduce the forbidden downstream chain.

**Constitutional-limit path:** Robinson can argue that generic depletion of locally held money, combined with multiplication of local robberies, does not by itself identify a qualifying interstate mechanism. The lower court's arithmetic description of aggregate magnitude cannot substitute for that antecedent inquiry. The strongest objection is the supported relationship to ongoing interstate business operations, which distinguishes this record from undifferentiated crime-cost, household-consumption or remote-goods-history theories. **Component and remedy limits:** The commerce nexus is an offense element, not subject-matter jurisdiction.
