    Justice Souter, concurring in part and concurring in the judgment.




     I join the opinion of the Court except for its response to petitioner's argument premised on the potential for prosecutorial abuse of 
18 U.S.C. § 1001
 as now written (
ante
, 7-8).  On that point I have joined Justice Ginsburg’s opinion espousing congressional attention to the risks inherent in the statute's current breadth.
