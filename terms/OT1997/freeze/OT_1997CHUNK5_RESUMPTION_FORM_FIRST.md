# OT1997 chunk 5 resumption — form and proposition decisions before assignment

Scope: Lewis v. United States (1998-03-09) and Triestman v. United States (1998-03-12). These decisions are fixed before reading or calculating opinion counts.

Lewis receives one signed lead opinion after plenary argument. Part I states the two-stage Assimilative Crimes Act inquiry, applies federal murder grading to reject the Louisiana provision, and preserves genuinely different state conduct/interest possibilities. Its coalition is Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Part II preserves the uncontested federal second-degree conviction and vacates only the sentence affirmance for federal Guidelines resentencing, including lawful upward departure; Scalia and Thomas join this part in addition to the six. Scalia and Thomas concur in the judgment on nonassimilation through their distinct generic-offense construction, rejecting the broader policy inquiry. Kennedy dissents on assimilation and the requested federal resentencing. One author can carry both parts; no authorship division is legally needed. The parts reflect the frozen method distinction, not a count-driven split. The accepted conviction posture establishes no general appellate reformation rule.

Triestman receives a per curiam jurisdictional dismissal of attempted ordinary review. All nine adopt the section 2255 certification cross-reference and section 2244(b)(3)(E) bar. No independent application, merits judgment, transfer, release or alternative vehicle is supplied. This is a procedural threshold action without plenary merits review; it is not a counted signed lead opinion. Stone-Zsela writes separately to state his approved claim-specific savings-clause qualification, expressly as a noncontrolling view of what dismissal leaves open. No Associate's frozen threshold reservation supplies a join in that substantive qualification. The choice of per curiam form follows routine direct application of Felker to the only supported order and does not turn on workload.

The existing freezes are unchanged. No assignment has yet been made in either resumed matter. Prior stopped-matter entries are not adjudications and their abandoned assignments, if any, cannot count; the stop receipts expressly recorded none.
