## Event

Lewis v. Brunswick Corp., No. 97-288. Argued March 2, 1998; dismissed May 15, 1998. The writ sought review of the Eleventh Circuit's judgment, 107 F.3d 1494, concerning whether the Federal Boat Safety Act and the Coast Guard's treatment of propeller guards preempt the state-law design-defect claim arising from a child's death.

The parties presented the conforming all-parties dismissal agreement governed by Rule 46.1. The preemption question is not decided.

## Participation

## Public Action
