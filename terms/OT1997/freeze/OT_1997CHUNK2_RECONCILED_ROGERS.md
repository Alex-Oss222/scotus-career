Kennedy and Souter oppose dismissal and would reach the granted question. These are procedural positions, not six votes affirming the convictions or two votes reversing them. The six dismissal votes suffice regardless of the remaining Justice's position, assuming the supplied participation remains unchanged; no position for that Justice is supplied here.

Gaudin, Sullivan, and Johnson preserve their different scopes. None supplies a concrete reason why a Justice who regarded this record as an unsuitable vehicle must now regard it as suitable.
