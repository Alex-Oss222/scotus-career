## Gavin v. Branstad — May 21, 1998

**Common judgment:** Affirm the Eighth Circuit's reversal of the district court's constitutional ruling and its remand for actual statutory adjudication. Apply the November 26, 1997 current-and-ongoing amendment to the pending proceeding. Decline immediate termination by this Court: the district court never determined the preservation condition. No decree-wide continuation entitlement is established either.

**Common sufficient rule:** Under Rouse and Benjamin, Congress may change the conditions for continuing prospective relief while the court independently determines factual eligibility, federal violations and remedial scope. Section 3626(b)(2) supplies a statutory route separate from Rule 60(b)(5); the movant must establish the absence of substantive original findings, without requiring ritual wording. Section (b)(3) preserves only relief supported by written record-based findings of a current **and** ongoing federal violation, necessity, no excessive scope, narrowness and least intrusiveness. No substantive federal right is legislatively reduced. The court must determine the missing statutory application before ending or preserving the disputed future obligations.

**Constitutional and remedial limits:** No perpetual vested right to unchanged prospective supervision; no retrospective erasure of completed effects or accrued claims. The actual remedial classification rationally relates to institutional responsibility and resources while retaining meaningful federal-right remedies; nonsuspect status alone does not suffice. On the assumed-contract premise, the federal nonparty rationality rule controls without finding an independent contract or cancelling accrued liability. A supported violation excluded solely by the amended conjunction remains reserved. Benjamin's record-specific proof rule supports a proper opportunity under ordinary procedure, not compulsory reopening or a hearing in every termination case. Library relief requires the actual access injury and remedial fit Lewis identifies, with no present injury finding in either direction.
