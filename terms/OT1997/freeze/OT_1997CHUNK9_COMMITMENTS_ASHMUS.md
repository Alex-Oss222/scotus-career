## Disposition and scope

All eight Associates would reverse the appellate judgment sustaining the injunction and remand with directions to remove the declaration and injunction resting on this standalone chapter-154-qualification claim and dismiss that claim for want of an Article III controversy. This is a jurisdictional disposition, not a determination that California qualifies. The remaining complaint claims were not resolved in the interlocutory appeal; their lawful jurisdictional treatment is for the district court, without an invented wholesale merits dismissal. The jurisdictional defect bars the challenged anti-assertion injunction and its declaratory premise. Neither the granted Eleventh Amendment issue nor the First Amendment objection needs decision.

The sufficient common rule is limited: prisoners may not use a standalone declaratory action to obtain an advance adjudication of whether the State may invoke a particular procedural regime in their eventual federal habeas actions, when the action seeks neither habeas relief nor another conclusive adjudication of the substantive legal relation at issue in those proceedings. That isolated procedural controversy must be addressed in a concrete habeas proceeding with its actual claims, relevant dates and requested relief. This does not make declaratory relief generally nonjudicial, require an executable coercive decree in every case, or hold that every anticipatory suit must await completed injury.

## Enhanced alternatives

**A. Jurisdictional dismissal.** Aetna requires a definite controversy susceptible to conclusive judicial relief; Coffman and Wycoff distinguish a concrete legal relation from an isolated issue whose significance lies in another proceeding. Chapter 154 qualification here is sought as a preliminary rule for future custody litigation, without those custody claims or a standalone legal entitlement conclusively presented. Declaratory Judgment Act §§2201–2202 do not expand Article III. Steel Co. requires deciding the disputed constitutional power before contested qualification merits.

The strongest objection is present hardship: the State's position and delays in appointing counsel can pressure prisoners to file deficient petitions or risk an asserted shorter limit. That is a serious practical problem, not imaginary injury. It nevertheless does not transform every disputed potential defense into a separate controversy suitable for advance adjudication. Qualification, the applicable limitations regime and appropriate protection can be contested within the actual habeas proceeding; no particular clock, denied amendment, default or lost claim is proved by the class descriptions. The holding does not rely on the lower opinion's mistaken suggestion that every late initial petition becomes successive.

**B. Present statutory-burden declaration.** An alternative would recognize a concrete relation between identified prisoners and officials asserting an immediate shorter filing regime; an effective declaration could remove that uncertainty. Seminole Part I confirms that a conclusive declaration may be judicial despite further proceedings, while Texas and Ohio Forestry preserve review of actual present burdens. This alternative fails here because it treats the procedural predicate of the habeas dispute as the complete legal relation, using the practical importance of a litigation issue to supply the missing controversy. Seminole adjudicated an existing statutory negotiation duty and activated defined consequences; the chapter-154 dispute asks which procedures another court will apply to custody claims not adjudicated here. No immediate-execution criterion is used.

**C. Reach immunity or speech.** If an independent actual controversy existed, the ongoing-violation and connected-officer predicates would still require separate analysis. Seminole's actual IGRA officer holding and Coeur d'Alene do not turn a disputed procedural litigation position automatically into an ongoing federal violation. Conversely, the State's characterization of the injunction as speech regulation would not automatically defeat a lawful decree. Neither issue supplies Article III power, and no Associate needs to decide either once path A is adopted.

## Remedy, law and source limits

Eight modeled votes support the same jurisdictional result and sufficient rule; Stone is not counted. No Justice commitment decides the Eleventh Amendment, officials' speech/petitioning rights, any specific §2265 deficiency, the merits of a habeas petition, §1983 liability, or entitlement to fees. All retain the separate statutory counsel, continuity, transcript, 180-day and tolling predicates supplied in the exact annex. The district court must treat independently remaining claims according to their own jurisdictional basis; it cannot retain the adjudicated standalone declaration merely by relabeling it ancillary relief.

No new source fact or reconstruction premise is needed for this threshold disposition. Its legal basis is the actual posture and limited relief requested, not absence of an unrecovered filing. The formal grant's precise text remains unrecovered; the clean reported description establishes that immunity and First Amendment questions were granted and that Article III is antecedent, not the sole granted issue. Any merits ruling on the State system would require respecting the actual statutory text and grant scope, but no such ruling is made here.
