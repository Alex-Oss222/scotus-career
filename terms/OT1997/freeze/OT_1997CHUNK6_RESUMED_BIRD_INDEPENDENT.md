## 1. Fixed record, scope and governing law

The jury convicted Bird under §248(a)(1) after he threw a bottle at Dr. Herring's approaching car, shattered its windshield and threatened to kill him as he entered America's Women Clinic. Bird otherwise does not contest guilt under the statute. There was no physical injury. Herring resided in Texas, and the prosecution supplied no clinic-specific proof of interstate patients, staff or supplies. The conviction cannot be supported by inventing that proof or an additional element.

The September 14, 1995 sentence imposed one year in prison followed by one year of supervised release, restitution of $820.67 and a $50 assessment. The special condition excludes Bird from within 1,000 feet of any abortion clinic, specifically America's Women Clinic. It is not merely a no-contact condition protecting Herring or his residence. Prior clinic and provider-home trespass and resistance to restrictions are supported lower-court reasons. They are not a finding that every conceivable restraint is necessary. The November 13, 1997 appellate mandate does not establish when imprisonment or supervision began or ended, whether either was stayed or tolled, or a surviving consequence on the event date.

The verified petition principally presents Commerce, §5 and abortion-right questions and identifies the supervision condition as not pertinent to that petition. The resumed authorization separately includes supervision. The remaining lower-appeal issues—familial classification, facial overbreadth, vagueness and the written judgment's descriptive label—are preserved as record context, not silently added as independent reviewed questions. The present commitments do not enlarge that scope. There is no independent RFRA claim.

The operative sources are:

- **Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects**, present operation June 30, 1997, with Lopez's April 26, 1995 architecture and American Life League's November 13, 1995 direct service-market application. Identify the enacted class and an eligible interstate mechanism before aggregation; require reasonable identifiable fit and an enforceable federal boundary. General crime, health or productivity costs do not supply a missing connection. No mandatory interstate crossing by each actor, universal jurisdictional-element requirement or categorical immunity for local noneconomic conduct follows.
- **FACE conduct predicates and responsibility for civil relief**, November 13, 1995. Section 248(a)(1) requires force, threat of force or physical obstruction; intentional injury, intimidation, interference or attempt; and the specified service-related purpose. Interference restricts movement, intimidation requires reasonable apprehension of bodily harm, and obstruction makes ingress or egress impassable or passage unreasonably difficult or hazardous. Protected expression remains protected. The parent/guardian exception removes every FACE penalty and civil remedy only for activity directed exclusively at the actor's own minor; it creates neither an outsider exemption nor immunity under other law.
- **Presented questions, subsidiary arguments and exceptional Supreme Court review**, current wording through Lebron, February 21, 1995. Rule 14.1(a)'s strong presumption excludes distinct unpresented questions absent supported exceptional circumstances. A supporting argument for an included claim is different from an additional independent claim. The duty to notice jurisdiction is separate.
- **Spencer**, March 3, 1998. An actual continuing redressable interest must exist after the challenged restraint ends; consequences of a conviction cannot simply be presumed for a different challenged adjudication. Spencer's decided setting was an expired parole-revocation sentence, not every direct sentencing appeal. Its underlying Article III inquiry nevertheless requires identification of the particular challenged component and surviving injury. Speculative future discretionary use is insufficient. The capable-of-repetition exception requires both insufficient duration for full review and a reasonable expectation that this claimant will again encounter the action. Delay alone supplies neither jurisdiction nor an exception.
- **18 U.S.C. §3583(d)**. An additional condition must be reasonably related to the offense and offender characteristics and the specified deterrence, protection and treatment considerations; deprive liberty no more than reasonably necessary for deterrence, public protection and treatment; and accord with pertinent Sentencing Commission policy statements. The requirements are distinct. A permissible protective objective does not dispense with the liberty limit, and the limit is not a universal least-restrictive-means command.
- **Speech burdens in a content-neutral injunction**, Madsen, June 30, 1994, with Schenck's confined counseling qualification, February 19, 1997. An injunction must burden no more speech than necessary for its established remedial interest. Its greater-than-ordinary precision requirement and rejection of unsupported territorial restraints inform fit, but a criminal supervised-release condition is not automatically governed by the identical injunction test. Neither precedent establishes a universal permissible distance.
- **28 U.S.C. §2106**, effective well before this event, permits appropriate further proceedings in a case lawfully before the Court. It does not confer merits jurisdiction over a moot component. **Hinds**, April 6, 1998, preserves the independent work of §844(i)'s property-use language; it neither inserts that language into FACE nor alters American Life League's commerce holding.

## 2. Present component commitments

The following operative formulations define the references in the Justice-by-Justice table. They are commitments to propositions and dispositions, not an assembled opinion.

**C — Conviction:** Affirm the Fifth Circuit's judgment sustaining the §248(a)(1) conviction against the reviewed Commerce challenge. American Life League controls the enacted class of intentional force, threats and obstruction directed at provision or receipt of reproductive services in the interstate service market. Bird's attack on the physician arriving to provide services falls within that class. The connection is the conduct's object and operation on provision of those services, not its contribution to general national crime costs. The national market premise does not require proof that this doctor, this patient or this clinic crossed state lines. No new commerce element or evidentiary opportunity is needed; the conviction is not vacated for lack of an element Congress did not enact and current law does not require.

**N — Unnecessary and unaccepted questions:** Do not decide §5 as an alternative source of power once Commerce independently sustains this conviction. Do not reconsider protected-choice law: whether protected choice independently limits state abortion regulation does not determine Congress's valid commerce authority over the service-directed violence proved here. Leave the familial-classification, facial speech, vagueness and descriptive-label claims outside this review as independent claims. The lower court's dispositions are not converted into new Supreme Court merits rulings. No free-exercise, RFRA, worship-branch or civil-remedy issue is added.

**J — Supervision jurisdiction and remedy:** Decline unconditional affirmance or invalidation of the special condition on the present unresolved status. Support vacatur **only of the Fifth Circuit judgment insofar as it affirms that special condition**, with remand through that court for determination whether the challenged restriction presents a continuing redressable controversy and for the lawful proceedings that determination permits. The district condition itself is not presently vacated or rewritten; the remand creates no stay, new exclusion, extension of supervision, release order or resentencing of other components. No conviction, restitution or assessment disposition changes because of this inquiry.

The inquiry concerns actual commencement, any legally operative delay or tolling, expiration, continuing restraint and any identified redressable consequence. These are subjects to determine, not findings or a mandatory document checklist. Its purpose is to establish Article III authority over this distinct component. It is not premised on a finding that a paper is absent from the Court's file, a litigant withheld information, or a local archive must be completed. If actual judicial materials already resolve status, use that determination rather than require duplicative production. If no live burden or applicable exception remains, the courts must address the proper mootness disposition and any further vacatur in light of the actual timing and circumstances; this handoff establishes neither expiration nor an automatic equitable-vacatur entitlement. If a live burden remains, the authorized merits challenge is open under §3583(d) and applicable constitutional law. The present remand does not prejudge whether a lawful application will retain, modify or remove the condition.

## 3. Enhanced comparison: authority, scope and threshold

### Commerce: direct interference versus the proposed local-act distinction

**Path supporting C:** Apply American Life League to targeted violence against the physician's provision of clinic services. The strongest objection is that this prosecution lacks the clinic-specific patients, staff and supplies evidence described in American Life League and concerns a local noneconomic attack. The objection identifies a real record difference but does not overcome the precedent's class-level holding: it requires no separate crossing by each actor or each transaction. The service-directed predicate supplies the operative connection; its absence in a hypothetical different offense could matter. It is not enough to invoke any victim's eventual effect on commerce.

**Proposed contrary path:** Treat this application as outside the class and reverse for insufficient interstate proof, or demand a new trial with such proof. Its strongest support is Lopez's connection-first rule, Hinds's insistence on enacted predicates, and the lower dissent's warning against federalization of ordinary crime. Its decisive difficulty is that it substitutes an individual-crossing or clinic-use requirement for the very service-market mechanism each Associate adopted in American Life League. Hinds construes different language and provides no omitted FACE element. The lower dissent's proposed fungibility, comprehensive-regulatory-program and local-subject exclusions are not conditions of current law. On this supplied record this is not a second surviving disposition under current law; adopting it would require an expressly reasoned distinction or reconsideration that these commitments do not support.

Justice-specific consequence: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas can and do retain their Lopez joins while applying their later service-market joins. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer need not adopt a broader aggregate-cost rationale to reach the same disposition; their own American Life League joins suffice. All eight refuse a draft saying that national prevalence, ideological purpose or congressional findings alone establish authority. All eight accept a formulation that identifies the service-related class, direct interruption of provision, reasonable class fit and the exclusion of general crime-cost reasoning. No broader interstate-externality doctrine, case-specific nexus element or alteration of Hinds is accepted.

### Scope: necessary reasoning versus new independent adjudications

**Selected path N:** Resolve the accepted Commerce question and leave unnecessary §5 and protected-choice contentions without a merits ruling; leave the independent lower-only claims outside review. Strongest counterargument: a general constitutional challenge and the same criminal judgment arguably bring all supporting objections within reach, while a respondent may defend on an alternative ground. The answer is component-specific: an alternative source of authority may be available in principle but need not be adjudicated after the sufficient ground; unrelated facial and label challenges do not become fairly included merely because they target the same judgment. Nothing in this record establishes the exceptional-review circumstances needed to expand them.

**Broader path tested:** Reach §5, reconsider protected choice or decide each lower-appeal argument to dispose comprehensively of the statute's validity. Its strongest support is practical completeness and prior adversarial treatment. Its barriers are the absence of necessity or supported exceptional circumstances and the difference between a constitutional argument within an accepted claim and a distinct request for relief. Stevens's Madsen and Caspari scope objections especially favor N; the seven other Associates' scope authorities identified in the table also do not authorize the proposed extension. The separate supervision authorization supplies scope for that question alone, not for every independent constitutional claim.

If §5 ever becomes necessary in a materially changed posture, City of Boerne's June 25, 1997 **Congruent and proportional Fourteenth Amendment enforcement** rule permits genuinely preventive or remedial legislation but forbids replacement of the underlying right. The guarantee, constitutional injury and remedial relationship would need examination. The lower dissent's private-conduct discussion is not a substitute for that current rule. No §5 merits commitment, state-failure finding or new abortion-right rule is frozen here.

### Supervision: inquiry versus presumed liveness, presumed mootness or immediate merits

**Selected path J:** Separate the conviction from the special condition and obtain the jurisdictional determination needed for the latter. Its strongest counterargument is that an otherwise live direct criminal appeal and a possibly ongoing sentence make further inquiry unnecessary, or that the supplied sentencing dates make expiration apparent. Neither resolves actual commencement, tolling, restraint or redressability. A live conviction does not authorize advisory adjudication of every expired component, while simple date addition cannot establish expiration. The limited appellate-component vacatur leaves the sentencing restriction unaltered until a lawful determination and avoids issuing a merits mandate without authority.

**Alternative lawful procedural path:** Retain this component and first conduct a bounded jurisdictional inquiry at the reviewing court, reserving its disposition until actual continuing interest is resolved. Its strongest advantage is avoiding vacatur of an appellate merits ruling that may have been valid when entered. Its disadvantage is retaining a separate fact-dependent sentencing issue when a focused remand can determine status and, if live, apply the existing condition law in the ordinary sentencing channel. Each Associate prefers J but can join this narrower inquiry-first formulation if assembly confines it to actual Article III facts and leaves merits reserved. This is a permissible change in procedure, not permission to choose facts or announce a constitutional outcome.

**Non-surviving alternatives:** Immediate affirmance or reversal of the condition on an assumed live controversy; dismissal based on presumed expiration; presumed collateral consequences because the conviction survives; or an automatic capable-of-repetition exception because the supervision term is short. Each supplies an unresolved predicate. Stevens's Spencer dissent supports examination of a concretely identified surviving official injury; it does not turn this unknown temporal status into a general presumption. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer directly joined Spencer's actual-injury and exception rules and will not join those shortcuts.

## 4. Conditional merits comparison and individual join barriers

These alternatives become legally decidable only after a live redressable controversy is established. They do not freeze a future merits vote or direct a result contingent on facts not found. A new material fact must pass the appropriate clean stage before it is used. This comparison fully identifies the legal work of the competing paths so that the threshold disposition is not mistaken for approval of either.

**Retention path:** Sustain the condition as a temporary, individual criminal-supervision restriction reasonably related to Bird's violent threat, attack, repeated trespass and rejection of prior restrictions. A standard command against criminal association does not necessarily prevent Bird acting alone, and a promise not to commit a new crime need not make preventive spatial restrictions redundant. The condition need not be a universal permissible injunction for every protester to satisfy §3583(d) for this convicted offender. The strongest objection is its exclusion from every clinic and its effect on otherwise lawful movement, access and advocacy throughout 1,000 feet. Retention requires a reasoned application of the liberty limit to that actual scope; violence alone supplies the interest, not unlimited discretion. No invented finding that Bird needs to attend a clinic, has lawful employment within the area, or has threatened every clinic is permissible.

**Correction path:** If the established record does not justify that full territorial and all-clinics exclusion under §3583(d)'s reasonable-necessity limit, set aside only the offending condition and return its lawful formulation to the sentencing court. Madsen's treatment of unsupported territorial relief and Schenck's retained lawful advocacy provide relevant fit comparisons. The strongest counterargument is that these decisions concerned injunctions, whereas convicted persons may receive reasonable preventive restrictions based on individualized risk and repeated failure of lesser commands. A correction therefore cannot rest on a categorical ban against geographic conditions, an automatically controlling Madsen distance, or failure to produce a finding for every foot. It must identify the unsupported deprivation or actual constitutional defect. No replacement distance, permission to threaten or obstruct, alteration of the valid conviction, general sentence reopening or new evidence to prove criminal guilt follows.

## 5. Peripheral claims and continuity boundaries

The following limits prevent scope nonreach from being misreported as either silent acceptance or reversal of the lower court:

- **Familial exception:** The lower court found Bird lacked standing because he was unrelated to Herring and his conduct did not involve a family member's services. This handoff adds no equal-protection ruling. If a genuinely included dispute later requires reaching that subject, the standing objection must be addressed before the classification's merits; the exception's complete remedial scope must remain accurate.
- **Overbreadth and vagueness:** Each Associate previously joined American Life League's respective rationales at their actual scope. Those prior holdings remain law. Their facial holdings do not resolve every criminal application or permit describing FACE as categorically unrelated to speech. Bird's concrete attack and threat are supported facts, but no new general threat-mental-state or emotional-injury rule is created by the current scope disposition.
- **Judgment label:** The lower court treated the descriptive offense label as consistent with the indictment, statutory citation and jury verdict. This handoff neither reviews that independent objection nor finds a constructive amendment, clerical correction or invalid conviction.
- **Remedial separation:** Commerce affirmance preserves the conviction on the reviewed ground. The supervision inquiry affects only the identified appellate component and its further lawful treatment. No acquittal, retrial, revised restitution, assessment refund, general resentencing, injunction against future FACE enforcement or release directive follows.
- **Current law:** American Life League, Lopez, Hinds, Madsen, Schenck, Spencer and Boerne retain their identified limits. A remand to determine present jurisdiction does not extend Spencer's actual revocation holding to a categorical rule for all sentencing appeals. A conditional comparison is neither an alternative holding nor present governing law.
