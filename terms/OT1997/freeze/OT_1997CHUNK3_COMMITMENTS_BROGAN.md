## Enhanced comparison of available paths

**Path A — reject the categorical exception and affirm the reviewed conviction.** A deliberate negative answer is a factual statement. The false-statements clause addresses any false statement made knowingly and willfully within its agency-matter scope; it does not limit coverage to volunteered accounts, benefit applications, affirmative narratives, statements under oath or questions whose answer investigators do not yet know. Nor does the separate concealment clause's language about a trick, scheme or device automatically become a required feature of every statement offense. The asserted exception would remove a substantial category of otherwise included conduct based on its reactive form and self-exculpatory purpose without a corresponding enacted limitation.

The strongest objection is contextual rather than grammatical. A statute protecting agency functions can be read to distinguish an ordinary denial of suspected wrongdoing from an affirmative attempt to corrupt governmental action; the unsolicited criminal interview, potential added felony, privilege values, lower-court exception and executive charging policy give that contention force. Path A nevertheless finds the full statutory text and surviving authority insufficiently ambiguous to support this categorical exclusion. A federal investigation is an authorized agency function; the absence of deception's success, or investigators' existing contrary evidence, does not by itself negate every statutory element or establish a universal immateriality defense. No new materiality holding is necessary to reject the exception. Constitutional protection against compelled self-incrimination supplies no general entitlement to make a deliberately false assertion, but the absence of that entitlement is not treated as independently sufficient to establish statutory coverage.

**Path B — recognize a bounded statutory exception and reverse only the §1001 conviction.** The strongest version would exclude a bare reactive oral denial of the questioned person's own suspected wrongdoing during an unsolicited criminal investigation, while leaving volunteered deception, affirmative misdirection, benefit claims and regulatory submissions governed by the statute. It can be advanced as statutory construction informed by constitutional values, rather than a constitutional right to lie. Bryson's affirmative affidavit setting does not itself decide that version, and LaChance expressly leaves the criminal statute's construction open. The reported record would make this a serious candidate for the exception's application without treating silence after the warning as a separate new offense.

The central difficulty is the statutory basis for drawing those precise distinctions. A short negative assertion remains a statement; no enacted term makes unsolicited questioning, the speaker's wish to exculpate himself, or the investigator's prior knowledge a categorical exclusion. The proposed contrast with affirmative misdirection also risks treating differently statements that communicate the same factual proposition. A general agency-function purpose cannot displace an otherwise covered authorized investigation, and neither unadopted legislative proposals nor an internal prosecution policy amend the offense. Ratzlaf's residual lenity is applied after ordinary interpretation, not used to create an exception merely because one would reduce troubling prosecutorial possibilities. The eight provisional commitments find no remaining statutory ambiguity sufficient for this categorical exception. They do not adopt a universal rule that every broadly phrased criminal statute admits no contextual limit.

**Path C — reject the exception but remand for another issue.** A genuinely available fault, materiality, instruction, compulsion or conduct-time notice objection could require a distinct disposition. The lower court reserved whether willfulness requires knowledge of illegality, and surprise or lack of reflection can matter to actual intent. The warning sequence is particularly important to any such inquiry. But a reservation is not an adjudicated instructional error or an independently preserved claim, and the reported knowing/willful concession cannot be erased to manufacture one. Gaudin supplies no finding that an actual element was withdrawn from this jury. Buchanan supplies neither a criminal false-statement charge construction nor a new harmlessness rule.
