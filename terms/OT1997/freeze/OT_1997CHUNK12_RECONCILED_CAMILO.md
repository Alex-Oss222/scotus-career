Its verdict review did not separately adjudicate qualified immunity, and its advisory-role implications require their own application. Febus-Rodriguez, 14 F.3d 87, 91–95 (January 18, 1994), supplies the counterexample: unrelated complaints, inadequate proof of culpable training choices and speculative causal links did not establish those supervisors' constitutional wrongdoing.
