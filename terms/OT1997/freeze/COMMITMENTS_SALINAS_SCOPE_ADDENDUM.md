## Exact effect on the prior propositions

**S1 is narrowed and superseded by S1R for later assembly.** Section 666 does not require proof that the bribe demonstrably affected federal funds. That construction preserves all enacted agency, corrupt-intent, covered-business/transaction, federal-benefits, timing, and exception conditions. The supplied federally connected jail conduct permits decision of this effects question without resolving **whether the statute requires a distinct connection between the bribe and expenditure of federal funds**. That statutory question is expressly reserved. A demonstrable effect on federal funds and a distinct connection to federal expenditure are not necessarily the same requirement.

The original S1's references to an absent additional “tracing/impact element” and its complete institutional/transaction formulation must not be used to announce that §666 can never require any additional statutory connection to federal expenditure. The revised proposition rejects the demonstrable-effects requirement alone; it neither adopts a different nexus requirement nor identifies its elements, allocation of proof, or precise satisfaction. It makes no traced-dollar finding. It also preserves the independent reservation concerning outer constitutional applications. A statutory reservation does not disappear into that constitutional reservation, and neither supplies an unannounced element.

**S1R expressly leaves three predicate matters outside the reviewed questions.** The recipient-benefits predicate is assumed for the presented legal questions; no new decision classifies the county's receipts under §666(b), establishes a precise annual sum, or treats every federal payment as a statutory benefit. The clean supplement's report of assistance above $10,000 during the relevant periods is not an independent adjudication of any disputed classification. The general coverage of intangible benefits remains undecided. Valuation of the covered business, transaction, or series under the $5,000 condition is not reopened. The latter threshold remains an element and is not equated with the bribe, watches, truck, or other consideration. These nondecisions are limits on review, not exemptions or conclusions that a demonstrated element defect is harmless.

**S2's construction remains, with its instructional scope made explicit as S2R.** The supported objection would require Salinas personally to commit, or personally to agree to commit, two predicate acts. Section 1962(d)'s knowing agreement to further an undertaking constituting the substantive enterprise violation does not impose that additional personal-two-act requirement. The agreement must concern the unlawful objective; mere knowledge, friendship, employment, or association is insufficient. The substantive §1962(c) elements, including Reves's direction requirement as part of the contemplated violation, remain distinct from each conspirator's personal performance. The acquittal of the substantive charge remains intact.

S2R rejects the identified proposed personal-two-act requirement. It does not authenticate, quote, or approve the complete actual jury charge. Neither the limited objection nor its rejection supplies a general evidentiary-sufficiency or instructional-harmlessness holding. The original discussion of personal direction is retained only to explain why substantive performance conditions are not automatically transferred to the agreement offense; it is not a new adjudication of an independently presented personal-direction instruction. No general aiding-and-abetting question is decided.

**S3's judgment position is unchanged, subject to S1R and S2R.** Each Associate would affirm the Fifth Circuit judgment on the two supported questions. No new-rule instruction review, harmlessness determination, or special remedial remand follows from these refinements. No substantive acquittal is disturbed, conviction enlarged, or sentence independently examined. A proposed disposition relying on a disputed predicate, complete instruction, sufficiency, or error analysis outside these questions requires a separately supported and authorized record basis.

## Individual reassessment and joins

The strongest objection to S1R is that the statute's express institutional/transaction predicates could support deciding all possible federal-expenditure connections now. The existing text-based sources do not require that broader decision to reject the demonstrable-effects requirement in this jail setting. Conversely, reserving a distinct nexus does not imply that Salinas has established one or shown its absence. The strongest objection to S2R treats agreement to violate §1962(c) as personal commitment to every act required for substantive liability; the separate agreement offense and the adopted Shabani reasoning answer that objection without reviewing an unproduced charge.
