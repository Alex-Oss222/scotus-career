## Exact governing distinctions

1. **Capacity and benefit.** This is a §1983 challenge to governmental employment action. Public concern is supported by the curriculum and historical-oppression subjects. The off-campus, unaffiliated festival and absence of assigned or controlled speaking duties support citizen-expression treatment. Introduction by the chair title is relevant context, not conclusive governmental sponsorship. The actual chair duties include assigning courses, internal representation and meetings, budget assistance, recordkeeping and tenure evaluations. These are real responsibilities, although the published record characterizes the role as essentially ministerial and identifies no special confidentiality, loyalty or additional public-contact obligations. Neither the title nor the ministerial description decides protection. *Perry* and the existing employment doctrine prevent retained tenure or absence of a guaranteed three-year chair term from answering the retaliation claim. *O'Hare* does not convert this speech dispute into a partisan-affiliation case. *Settle* does not convert this independent public address into controlled curriculum.

2. **Protection and prediction.** *Pickering* and *Connick* require the legal balance of the citizen/public interests and the government's legitimate interests in this service relationship. The jury's reasonable-expectation finding survives alongside its no-actual-disruption finding. Neither is the other finding, and neither alone performs the constitutional balance. Recruitment, fundraising, community relationships and faculty interaction are identified interests that must be considered; they are not invented after the verdict. Predicted operational effects can justify action before they occur. Their constitutional weight depends on their relationship to actual service and expression, rather than offense at a viewpoint alone. The reported adequate-performance assessments are relevant evidence, not findings of flawless performance. No universal investigation duty, actual-harm prerequisite, fixed evidentiary checklist, quantified-loss requirement or least-severe-sanction rule follows.

3. **Waters's limited force.** The seven-Justice genuine/reasonable-account safe harbor applies only to a genuinely relied-upon account of legally disciplinable speech. Six Justices separately supported its unreasonable-false-actually-protected endpoint; seven supported its limited motive remand. Those overlapping propositions do not create a complete majority investigation framework. This case principally concerns known speech, its legal balance and actual cause. Absence of investigation into predicted effects is not an independent constitutional wrong under *Waters*. Sincere factual belief cannot itself decide the legal protection of the words actually spoken.

4. **Actual cause.** Jeffries must establish protected conduct and its substantial or motivating role. A defendant may establish by a preponderance that the same action would have occurred absent that protected conduct. *Mt. Healthy*, the exact *Waters* remand, and unanimous *Crawford-El* preserve this counterfactual; a possible lawful reason is insufficient. Competent direct and circumstantial proof remain available under *Kimberlin* and *Crawford-El*, without a special clear-and-convincing burden. Ordinary personal responsibility, injury and entity/capacity attribution remain separate. No finding of punitive culpability replaces these elements.

5. **Verdict and relief.** A speech-motivation answer, a reasonable-forecast answer, a no-actual-disruption answer and a punitive-state-of-mind answer address different inquiries. They cannot simply be collapsed into one conclusion or pronounced necessarily consistent. The later answers concerning six defendants include a disjunctive punitive formulation; they do not establish one exclusive motive. The lower opinion's conflict assessment warrants attention but does not establish the precise Rule 49/50 consequence without the actual instructions, forms and preservation record. Section 2106 permits appropriate appellate relief; it supplies no missing verdict fact.

6. **Immunity and time.** Personal damages immunity remains governed by *Harlow* and *Anderson v. Creighton* at the particular conduct date: late October 1991 for the term limitation, and a distinct March 1992 inquiry only if that later action is within the preserved claim. The later *Waters*, *NTEU*, *Umbehr*, *O'Hare*, *Kimberlin*, *Settle* and *Crawford-El* decisions do not themselves establish notice in 1991 or 1992. Bad motive or punitive answers do not eliminate objective immunity. The separate immunity revision proposed in *Crawford-El* was not adopted. Personal immunity does not by itself dispose of otherwise available prospective relief or establish entity liability.

## Supported alternative paths and their limits

**Affirm on the legal employment balance.** Accept the public-concern setting, the concrete chair functions and the jury's reasonable prediction. Give substantial weight to the identified recruitment, fundraising, faculty-interaction and community interests in administering the university. Conclude that those interests justify the chair-term decision even though realized disruption was not proved. Retention of tenure distinguishes the actual employment action; it does not extinguish a claim by itself. If this balance makes the causally relevant speech disciplinable, speech motivation and the punitive answers cannot create the missing constitutional injury. The strongest objection is that the role findings deny special loyalty or public-representation duties, while the asserted institutional effects substantially concern public reactions to independent academic expression. A reasonable forecast does not automatically give those reactions superior constitutional weight. This route must perform that balance and cannot call its result dictated by the jury or by *Waters*.

**Vacate and remand on the proper contextual balance and actual-cause rules.** The affirmative record identifies independent public-concern expression and a chair role with no demonstrated special public-contact, confidential or policymaking obligation. Those supported characteristics bear on the legal weight of the specific predicted harms. The appellate court performed a balance, but its treatment of the retained professorship as disposing of the academic-freedom objection does not adequately account for a speech penalty through a different employment benefit, and its result gives the reasonable forecast more legal force than the actual role and expression justify. That is a disagreement with the legal application, not a finding that the jury made no reasonable prediction. On this path the separate nine-vote rationale cannot sustain blanket judgment without the actual causation analysis below. The strongest objection is that *Connick* and *Waters* require room for preventive management, and ministerial employees can harm operations. The response preserves both points: the role is relevant rather than dispositive, the named institutional interests remain admissible, and ordinary further proceedings may still yield a supported defense judgment. Neither absence of realized injury nor incomplete research is the reason to vacate.

**Affirm on a genuinely independent lawful Board decision or absent personal cause.** Nine votes for the one-year term can be powerful causal evidence. If the Board independently would have imposed that same limitation without protected speech, or a particular defendant did not cause the injury, an appropriate defense judgment remains available. The strongest objection to the appellate use of this route is that the absence of the six defendants' attributed retaliatory animus in the nine successful voters does not itself establish that counterfactual. Nor does vote arithmetic answer whether the proposal was causally procured by nonvoting officials. Conversely, the officials' procurement, a defeated vote and an abstention do not establish constitutional injury merely because they occurred. All eight accept these governing distinctions; only the five vacating Associates make their application part of the proposed remand.

**Enter final plaintiff judgment or order a categorical retrial.** The speech and verdict evidence supports continued adjudication, and the appellate conflict discussion supports considering retrial if the actual verdict rules require it. But final liability, immediate restoration of the district court's awards or a mandatory new trial would decide unresolved protection, personal cause, verdict-preservation, immunity or remedial questions. No Associate selects that disposition. The surviving narrower path avoids these gaps without presuming any missing fact.

## Independent commitments by Justice

### John Paul Stevens

**Judgment:** Vacate the April 4, 1995 judgment and remand; do not restore the district court's injunction or punitive awards automatically. Reach both grounds that supported the appellate judgment.

**Own public basis:** His *Waters* dissent, including its reliance on his *Connick* and *Rankin* positions, protects actual public-concern expression while retaining *Pickering* and *Mt. Healthy*. His *NTEU* opinion identifies the citizen and audience interests in expression outside work. His *O'Hare* opinion and *Umbehr* join reject an entitlement-based answer and require actual service interests and proved cause. His unanimous *Kimberlin*, *Settle* and *Crawford-El* joins preserve ordinary proof and independent defenses.

**Two-path application:** The reasonable forecast permits preventive action to enter the balance; it does not reduce independent curriculum and historical criticism to whatever expression institutional supporters welcome. The role findings and the distinct chair benefit materially weaken an inference that taking the chair term away is constitutionally justified merely because the university's reputation or donations may suffer. Affirmance gives insufficient weight to those characteristics. Conversely, Stevens does not infer final protection simply from public concern or overturn the jury's reasonable prediction. He supports the bounded remand because personal cause and the verdict also remain unresolved.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** Faculty interaction and recruitment can be genuine service concerns even for a nonpolicymaker. He accepts consideration of their supported predicted effects and does not demand completed harm. His rejection of a reasonable-factual-error defense in *Waters* need not be reconsidered here because no different account of the words controls this dispute. He will join a common opinion that leaves *Waters*'s exact existing authority intact and decides this legal balance and causation question. He will not join a rule treating retained tenure, permissible public controversy or absence of a property right as categorical defenses, or a remand presuming every defendant liable.

### Sandra Day O'Connor

**Judgment:** Affirm the judgment for defendants on the substantive employment balance. Do not make the separate nine-vote causal rationale or a final immunity holding an alternative ground.

**Own public basis:** Her *Waters* plurality permits management to act on a genuinely and reasonably assessed threat before actual breakdown, while retaining the legal balance and cause. Her *NTEU* separate opinion applies ordinary, case-specific *Pickering* analysis without automatically importing a heightened prospective-restraint burden. Her joinder in Scalia's *Rankin* dissent, 483 U.S. 378, 394–401 (1987), rejects treating a nonpolicymaking role as protection against employment consequences regardless of foreseeable institutional impact. Her *Umbehr/O'Hare* and unanimous motive-proof joins retain the actual relationship and same-decision limits.

**Two-path application:** She accepts the citizen setting and public concern and treats the no-special-public-contact finding as relevant. Nevertheless, course assignments, tenure evaluations, internal representation and budget work give this chair a consequential administrative relationship with the institution. In that setting the identified recruitment, faculty and community concerns, supported by the reasonable-expectation verdict, carry sufficient legal weight for the chair-term action. This is her constitutional evaluation, not a new finding that any threatened loss occurred or that Jeffries discriminated in performing a duty. The narrower employment consequence is part of the actual dispute, not a constitutional requirement to choose the mildest sanction.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** The administrative assessments were adequate and the public criticism was independent. She finds that those facts do not require the university to await the forecasted operational effects. She does not infer an investigation violation from the reported absence of inquiry or import *NTEU*'s full broad-ban burden. If the Court rejects this balance, she accepts in principle ordinary defendant-specific cause, verdict treatment and conduct-date immunity; that conditional position is not a vote for vacatur. She can join severable statements rejecting actual-harm and categorical-title rules, but not the proposed finding that the appellate legal balance cannot sustain judgment.

### Antonin Scalia

**Judgment:** Affirm on the legal employment balance, without adopting the nine-vote rationale as independently sufficient and without a separate immunity determination.

**Own public basis:** His *Rankin* dissent expressly recognizes potential harm from nonpolicymaking employees and distinguishes constitutional speech protection from proportionality in civil-service sanctions. His simulated *Waters* concurrence requires a genuine legally permissible ground, rejects an independent investigation duty, and accepts remand for actual retaliatory cause. His *NTEU* dissent gives substantial weight to administrable preventive interests. His *Umbehr/O'Hare* dissents concern extending employee protection to commercial relationships; they do not erase the employee doctrine applicable here. His *Crawford-El* and *Kimberlin* joins foreclose special motive-proof barriers.

**Two-path application:** He assumes the address qualifies as public concern rather than deriving a categorical unprotected-speech exception from its offensive content. He would sustain the employer side of the balance because the speech's public identification with the chair and the actual administrative duties coexist with a jury-supported forecast of harm to the university's ability to function. Nonpolicymaking status does not neutralize those interests. His conclusion neither treats the festival as officially sponsored nor finds a special public-contact role contrary to the record. He rejects vacatur insofar as it gives the ministerial characterization and continuing professor role insufficiently defeasible force against the institution's reasonable preventive judgment.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** The forecast concerns reactions to protected public discussion, which cannot invariably become employer control over opinion. He accepts that boundary, but concludes that these identified institutional concerns in this actual employment relationship suffice. Honest belief alone is not his holding; legal disciplinability is. A rule making mere sincerity dispositive, imposing a constitutional investigation code, or converting nine votes into automatic superseding cause would not receive his join. If speech were held protected, his own *Waters/Crawford-El* positions permit a genuine causation inquiry. That is a conditional accommodation, not assent to the remand judgment.

### Anthony M. Kennedy

**Judgment:** Vacate and remand on both appellate grounds, without directing plaintiff judgment or a particular trial remedy.

**Own public basis:** His joinder in Scalia's *Waters* concurrence preserves genuine legal grounds, rejects a freestanding inquiry obligation and supports remand where actual cause remains disputed. His direct *NTEU* Part I join protects independent citizen expression against inadequately connected institutional restraints. His *Umbehr/O'Hare* joins require a function-sensitive employment/service balance and actual same-decision proof. His *Crawford-El* join retains the constitutional elements and objective immunity.

**Two-path application:** Kennedy accepts the precise reasonable-forecast finding and the specified potential harms. He gives greater legal weight than the affirmers to the public, unaffiliated address and the affirmative absence of special chair loyalty or additional public-contact obligations. Ordinary administrative responsibility does not make the chair's entire civic expression university speech or dispose of the constitutional interest in speaking. The appellate reliance on the retained professorship cannot resolve the penalty imposed through the chair position. These circumstances support reconsideration of the legal balance rather than final defense judgment on the existing rationale.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** Universities can suffer operational consequences from publicly identified officers, and *Waters* permits acting in advance. He preserves that latitude and does not impose viewpoint neutrality on all employment judgments. The remand must turn on actual service interests and constitutional protection, not on failure to interview particular people. He can join a common *Pickering* and *Mt. Healthy* opinion that expressly leaves the full *Waters* investigation theories unsettled. He cannot join a mandatory objective-inquiry procedure, an absolute academic exception or a conclusion that the six officials' motives establish causation by themselves. The two nonvoters' procurement remains material but not conclusive.

### David H. Souter

**Judgment:** Vacate and remand; no automatic reinstatement, damages or retrial.

**Own public basis:** His *Waters* concurrence distinguishes actual belief, reasonable factual assessment and the limited endpoint agreements. His *Umbehr* opinion requires adjusting *Pickering* to the real service relationship and proving the actual counterfactual. His unanimous *Settle* opinion distinguishes genuine legitimate grounds from labels or merely imaginable explanations. His *NTEU*, *O'Hare* and *Crawford-El* joins reinforce those distinctions in their stated settings.

**Two-path application:** A reasonable prediction and absence of actual harm can coexist, and Souter accepts both findings. He nevertheless rejects treating the forecast as resolving whether this independent address was disciplinable in light of the chair's supported functions. The nonpolicymaking and ordinary-public-contact findings, specific tasks and adequate-performance assessments must receive their actual contextual significance. Faculty-relations and financial concerns can matter without automatically resolving that balance. On cause, the contrast between the nine successful votes and six later answers requires examination of what the decision actually depended on, rather than a conclusion drawn from the absence of a particular culpable state of mind in the successful voters.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** The completed trial and reasonable-forecast verdict supply more than a hypothetical management concern. He gives them that weight and identifies no new investigation deficiency. His *Waters* discussion of excessive discipline does not become a least-severe-sanction holding. He can join the narrow remand while reserving final speech protection and the relation of the verdict answers. He refuses either presumed bad faith from the punitive answers or presumed lawful cause from arithmetical sufficiency. A source gap concerning instructions is addressed by leaving the actual procedural consequence to the lower courts, not by declaring that a new trial must occur.

### Clarence Thomas

**Judgment:** Affirm on the substantive employment balance. Reach no necessary alternative holding on verdict incompatibility, individual cause or immunity.

**Own public basis:** His *Waters* joinder accepts genuine legally permissible grounds without an additional constitutional investigation obligation. His *NTEU* dissent join sustains preventive public-employer judgments even when actual harm has not yet materialized. His *Umbehr/O'Hare* dissent joins preserve a boundary against extending employee doctrine to commercial arrangements, while acknowledging the existing employee rules. His unanimous *Kimberlin*, *Settle* and *Crawford-El* joins retain ordinary motive evidence, actual cause and distinct objective immunity.

**Two-path application:** Thomas applies the existing employee balance rather than a contractor-status exclusion or a categorical professor exception. He would give controlling weight in this application to the jury-supported reasonable operational forecast and the university's management of the identified chair functions, after considering the public-concern and independent setting. The threatened recruitment, faculty-interaction and institutional-relationship consequences support an administrative response even though the reported evidence does not establish completed losses. The decision concerns the chair term; continuing tenure neither ends the claim nor requires the same result for a different employment action.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** The role findings and outside speech limit the employer's interest, and institutional displeasure cannot automatically defeat the right. He considers those limits insufficient to outweigh the particular predicted service harms here. He will not join an invented finding of discriminatory performance, a policymaker presumption or a rule making offensive content categorically unprotected. His preventive judgment also does not dispense with genuine reliance or actual cause where legally protected speech is involved. If the Court finds the substantive ground insufficient, he accepts the governing *Mt. Healthy/Crawford-El* constraints on further proceedings; he does not thereby join this vacatur or the proposed adverse application of the balance.

### Ruth Bader Ginsburg

**Judgment:** Vacate and remand on the limited protection/cause grounds; retain separate consideration of personal defenses and available remedies.

**Own public basis:** Her *Waters* plurality join conditions its protection on a genuine, reasonable account of legally disciplinable speech. Her *NTEU* and *Umbehr/O'Hare* joins require attention to the actual expressive burden and service relationship. Her *Kimberlin* Court opinion distinguishes proof of unconstitutional motive from personal responsibility and qualified immunity; her *Crawford-El* join preserves ordinary preponderance and the actual same-decision defense.

**Two-path application:** The speech's independent public setting, the concrete nonpolicymaking role and the benefit actually withdrawn prevent treating retention of professorship as an answer to the First Amendment claim. Recruitment and community relationships remain legitimate possible service concerns, but their identification and the forecast verdict do not automatically sustain the appellate balance against this actual expression. The later verdict answers also cannot be reduced to a collective employer motive: the nine successful voters, three defeated voters, abstainer and two recommending officials present different personal causal questions. Both appellate grounds therefore require the bounded reconsideration.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** The verdict expressly permits reasonable prediction, and a board majority can genuinely provide an independent lawful cause. Ginsburg accepts those possibilities without assuming either established the legal defense for all defendants. She will join a remand allowing supported individual defense judgments and harmonization of the actual answers, but not a direction to award damages, reinstate the injunction, bypass conduct-date immunity or retry every issue. Circumstantial proof is available; its availability does not itself prove a violation. No contemporary constitutional rule is used as retrospective notice.

### Stephen G. Breyer

**Judgment:** Vacate and remand for the contextual balance, defendant-specific actual cause and ordinary verdict/defense treatment; no predetermined merits or trial result.

**Own public basis:** His direct *NTEU* Part I join connects the actual speech burden and justification. His *Umbehr/O'Hare* joins apply the practical service relationship and proved counterfactual, and his unanimous *Settle*, *Kimberlin* and *Crawford-El* joins require genuine grounds without inventing a special proof regime. He was not on the Court for *Waters*; no *Waters* vote or full investigative theory is attributed to him.

**Two-path application:** The duties and specific institutional concerns deserve substantial weight, and the jury found reasonable prediction rather than merely public embarrassment. But the same record distinguishes independent citizen speech, ordinary chair functions and adequate performance from institutional sponsorship or special loyalty duties. A final defense judgment requires the correct application to that actual combination. The public/faculty interest does not disappear because only the chair benefit was affected. Likewise, nine votes may prove an independent lawful decision in the actual record, but counting those votes and contrasting the six punitive answers is not itself the same-decision inquiry.

**Strongest counterargument and limits:** Remand can be unnecessary when an appellate court has already performed a permissible legal balance on a completed trial. Breyer does not remand merely for more information; he joins the legal objection to treating the retained professorship and reasonable forecast as sufficient despite the supported role and expression characteristics, together with correction of the independent causal ground. He will join a concrete application of existing rules, not a new tier of scrutiny, universal *NTEU* burden shift, investigation protocol or required retrial. Properly preserved personal defenses and live remedial questions remain available below.
