The reasonable-prediction principle remains intact under current law. The jury found both no actual disruption and a reasonable expectation of disruption; all eight preserve both findings. The choice between affirmance and remand turns on their legal weight in the actual relationship, not on deleting one finding. The chair's recordkeeping, course assignments, internal representation, meetings, budget assistance, and tenure evaluations are consequential functions. The supported nonpolicymaking characterization, ordinary-professor public contact, unaffiliated public address, and adequate-performance assessments also remain intact. Simulated *Waters* controls through its precise overlapping propositions: a genuinely relied-upon, reasonable account protects discipline only if that account describes legally disciplinable speech; actual protected conduct and actual cause retain independent significance. It supplies no complete majority investigation framework. *Perry*, *Umbehr*, and *O'Hare* prevent absence of a guaranteed chair term or retention of tenure from answering the retaliation claim, while preserving the actual service relationship. *NTEU* does not transfer its full broad-ban burden to this individual action. *Settle* supplies no general rule converting this address into controlled curriculum. These propositions constrain both sides without mechanically deciding this balance.

The five remanding Justices reject the panel's **application**, particularly the weight given to predicted institutional reactions despite the concrete role and independent expression, and its treatment of retained tenure as disposing of the academic-freedom objection. They preserve the university's particular recruitment, fundraising, faculty-interaction, and community interests. They require neither completed damage nor quantified losses. The three affirmers consider those interests, the real administrative functions, and the reasonable forecast sufficient after accounting for the same expressive interests. The changed simulated scope of *Waters* alone does not compel a different outcome: their disagreement remains a permissible, supported dispute over the legal balance.

The causal comparison is conditional on legally protected speech. If the speech was disciplinable, its role in the action does not itself establish an unconstitutional injury; the affirmers rely on that route. If protected conduct is established, *Mt. The absence of the six defendants' attributed malice does not alone prove that the nine would impose the same term absent protected speech. Conversely, procurement of a proposal, a losing vote, or an abstention does not alone prove responsibility. A supported finding that the Board independently acted on lawful grounds, that a particular actor did not influence its decision, or that another personal defense disposes of a claim remains fully available.
