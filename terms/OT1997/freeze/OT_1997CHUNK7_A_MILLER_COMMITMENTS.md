## Statutory and remedial premises common to all commitments

The denial being reviewed rests on the failure to establish the required paternal relationship within the statutory age period. The amended §1409(a) requires clear-and-convincing blood-relationship proof; United States nationality of the father at birth; the father's written agreement, unless deceased, to support the person until eighteen; and, while the person is under eighteen, legitimation under residence/domicile law, the father's written sworn acknowledgment, or competent-court paternity adjudication. The fourth requirement contains alternatives, not a mandatory legitimation route alone. The support requirement and its deceased-father qualification remain distinct from the fourth requirement's deadline.

Miller belongs to the transitional cohort permitted to elect the former provision, whose relevant legitimation age was twenty-one. The State Department's reference to eighteen therefore did not accurately exhaust the available age route, but the July 1992 decree came after her June 1991 twenty-first birthday as well. No other timely recognition event is established. This conclusion does not prove every other statutory condition unsatisfied or satisfied. Her father was originally a plaintiff, was dismissed, did not appeal, and encountered no stated unusual practical or legal obstacle to doing so.

Section 1409(c) separately gives a person born outside the United States and out of wedlock after December 23, 1952 the nationality of a citizen mother at birth if the mother previously had continuous physical presence in the United States or an outlying possession for one year. Section 1401(g)'s general citizen/alien-parent route has its separate parental physical-presence condition: the current five years, including two after fourteen, must not silently replace the earlier ten years, including five after fourteen, for a birth governed by the earlier law. No finding that Charlie Miller satisfies the applicable period is made merely because he is an American or served in the Air Force.

Concrete injury, whose equality right is asserted, judicial remedial power and ultimate entitlement are separate questions. The petitioner suffers a concrete denial traceable to the Secretary. Lujan does not permit calling the disputed citizenship claim already lost simply by labeling her an alien; nor does concrete injury automatically entitle her to assert a different person's constitutional right or obtain citizenship. Pangilinan bars conferral by equity contrary to congressional conditions. Whether invalidation leaves a statutory entitlement, entails extension, or instead requires withdrawal of a preference is a constitutional-remedial question distinct from equitable naturalization.

## Eight independent commitments

### Stevens — affirm on the narrow merits

**Result and reach:** Affirm the denial of the requested declaration. The concrete citizenship injury permits consideration of the challenged condition; he would resolve the age-limited recognition requirement on the merits without resting the judgment on the father's unpursued separate claim. No universal standing permission for every parental-right claim is adopted.

**Sufficient rule and application:** Congress may require an affirmative paternal recognition or legally established relationship during minority as a condition for this particular citizenship-at-birth route. Even applying the important-objective/substantial-relationship standard of Virginia, the condition substantially serves reliable establishment of the parental connection and an opportunity for ties to the citizen parent and country while the child is a minor. The maternal relationship is objectively established in the birth event in a way that the paternal relationship is not necessarily established. Section 1409(a)(4)'s alternative routes permit acknowledgment or adjudication, not only marriage or the father's voluntary legitimation. Miller's first identified legal recognition after both possible age endpoints fails that condition.

**Adversarial comparison:** Virginia, Kelley and Klinger forbid treating an assumed male unwillingness to care as sufficient. The justified proposition here concerns establishment of the relationship and the childhood opportunity it creates, not the proposition that fathers generally desert children or that every mother creates enduring United States ties. Later proof of biological fatherhood answers biology but does not supply the timely recognition Congress required for this route. This is the strongest basis for distinguishing the claim from Virginia's categorical denial to qualified applicants; real differences may matter under that precedent without lowering the tier.

**Join boundary/remedy:** Joins an opinion sustaining the challenged fourth condition at this scope and affirming; he need not decide whether Fiallo requires only highly deferential review. Does not uphold every father-only condition, a mandatory marriage rule, the independent support promise in every application, or all physical-presence distinctions. No remedy is due for this challenge and no citizenship declaration follows. He does not join the proposition that courts can never remedy unconstitutional citizenship conditions.

### O'Connor — affirm on claimant and standing limits

**Result and sufficient ground:** Affirm. Miller has her own concrete injury but cannot use this record to assert her father's right to heightened scrutiny of sex discrimination against him. A close parent-child relationship alone does not dispense with a genuine impediment to his own litigation. He filed his own claim, failed to appeal dismissal and identifies no unusual obstacle. This is not Powers' situation of excluded jurors facing substantial barriers; ordinary third-party-standing limits remain operative.

**Own claim and alternative:** Miller's own parentage-based citizenship claim is distinct from the father's sex-equality claim. At that personal-claim scope, the challenged requirement reasonably connects this statutory citizenship route with establishment of the citizen-parent relationship during minority; the denial may be sustained without deciding the father's intermediate-scrutiny challenge. Her Virginia opinion requires actual justifications when that sex-equality claim is properly before the Court, but does not eliminate the requirement that the litigant be entitled to assert it. She declines a broad Fiallo holding governing every citizenship classification.

**Barrier/function:** Would join Kennedy on the lack-of-hindrance ground and the limited rejection of the child's separate claim. Does not join Stevens's conclusion that the father's sex distinction passes demanding intermediate review. A separate concurrence in the judgment must keep concrete injury, prudential third-party restrictions and the substantive level of review distinct. No new rule that inability to obtain a desired citizenship decree always destroys Article III standing.

### Scalia — affirm because the requested citizenship remedy is unavailable

**Result and sufficient ground:** Affirm without deciding whether the sex distinction is constitutionally valid. Congress controls the statutory conferral of citizenship on persons born abroad; Pangilinan does not permit a court to declare a person a citizen when the governing congressional conditions have not been met by supplying its own enlarged grant. The recorded postdeadline decree does not satisfy either available age route. The requested extension cannot be justified as equitable naturalization.

**Adversarial comparison:** Heckler v. Mathews recognizes that equal treatment and benefit extension are distinct; Westcott does not make extension inevitable for every benefit scheme. Striking a restrictive condition here would not be a merely clerical recognition of a demonstrated existing entitlement: it would enlarge the class Congress chose to make citizens, without findings on the remaining statutory conditions. The Court can distinguish its power to review constitutionality from power to grant this requested status. Lujan's redressability discipline supports attention to available relief, but a sweeping declaration that all constitutional citizenship challenges are beyond Article III is unnecessary.

**Barrier/function:** Joins a remedy-based affirmance, with the naturalization/statutory-recognition distinction expressly addressed. Does not join an intermediate-scrutiny merits opinion, a citizenship grant by severance, or a universal denial of constitutional judicial review. His concurrence in the judgment may assume the claimed equality defect for purposes of this remedial conclusion. No claim that Kelley's ordinary sex-classification rule has been overruled.

### Kennedy — affirm on third-party and own-claim limits

**Result and sufficient ground:** Affirm on the same operative ground as O'Connor: concrete injury is present, but the father suffered no shown impediment to pursuing his own sex-equality claim. His own choice not to appeal cannot be converted into a hindrance giving the daughter standing to litigate that right.

**Authority and comparison:** His Lujan joins retain distinct jurisdictional elements; his Virginia join requires actual important objectives and substantial fit when the sex claim is properly reached. Powers' individual-right account does not dispense with its relationship-and-obstacle requirements. Miller's concrete personal statutory injury therefore does not by itself establish the separate third-party permission. The child's own parentage-based claim does not defeat a reasonable requirement of recognition of a citizen-parent relationship during minority; he would not announce that the father's challenged sex distinction satisfies heightened scrutiny.

**Barrier/function:** Joins O'Connor's confined concurrence in the judgment, including an explicit reservation of the father's merits and a refusal to equate the daughter's sex with her father's classification. Does not join Stevens's intermediate-scrutiny conclusion, Scalia's broader remedial theory or a broad immunity from judicial scrutiny for nationality laws. No citizenship award or new factual proceedings are required for his disposition.

### Souter — reverse the sex-based age-condition ruling and remand

**Reach and rule:** Miller may challenge the unequal condition placed on her own claim to citizenship; she is its direct subject and suffers its denial. She need not invoke her father's unpursued rights to obtain review of that condition. Virginia, Kelley and Klinger require the actual classification and actual opportunity to be examined; discrimination does not cease to be sex-based solely because the sex used to classify this applicant is her citizen parent's. Fiallo's immigration-admission preference does not settle citizenship transmitted at birth.

**Application and alternative:** Reliable proof of biological parentage is an important legitimate objective and can justify suitable proof rules reflecting actual differences between maternity and paternity. It does not establish the need for an absolute premajority formal-recognition cutoff where competent later proof can establish the relationship. The asserted child-country connection is imperfectly served by automatic maternal transmission without comparable continuing contact or support requirements; stereotyped expectations about parental willingness cannot supply the substantial relationship. He would reject the challenged fourth condition's unequal deadline at that scope, without declaring blood-relationship proof itself unconstitutional or deciding every support/presence rule.

**Remedy and limits:** Reverse and remand to address the lawful equality remedy and remaining statutory predicates before any declaration of citizenship. Pangilinan prohibits equity against a valid statute, not judicial review and violation-linked constitutional correction. Mathews requires consideration of equality's remedial alternatives; Westcott permits extension where consistent with the statutory scheme but does not compel it automatically here. A declaration that Miller already qualifies after a lawful remedy would differ from naturalization by judicial discretion. No unconditional passport, statutory presence finding or assumption of compliance with every other requirement. A dissent should explain this own-right route and limited remand if affirmance prevails.

### Thomas — affirm on the citizenship-remedy ground

**Result, authority and scope:** Affirm on the same operative Pangilinan/statutory-conferral ground as Scalia, without adjudicating the sex-classification merits. His Lujan joins require an actual legally available form of relief; his Kelley and Klinger joins do not supply authority to create a broader statutory grant of citizenship.

**Adversarial comparison and barrier:** Removing a condition may be an available equality remedy in ordinary benefits legislation, but does not automatically establish that Congress conferred citizenship on this additional class or that all other qualifications are met. Mathews's distinction between equality and extension defeats the assumption that an asserted constitutional defect necessarily produces a passport. He accepts review of the remedial question without calling every constitutional challenge nonjusticiable. Would join Scalia's concurrence at that stated scope; does not join Stevens's merits, O'Connor's full distinct claimant rationale, or an equitable conferral.

### Ginsburg — reverse the unequal age condition and remand

**Reach and merits:** Reverse and remand on the direct injury to Miller's own access to statutory citizenship. Her Virginia join and the unanimous Kelley/Klinger rules require examination of the actual sex-based criterion, not a presumption that the disadvantaged child may assert only a man's rights. Fiallo's admissions setting does not authorize unexamined sex stereotypes in the child's citizenship-at-birth claim.

**Alternatives and application:** The biological proof problem is real and can support reliable paternal proof requirements. The additional categorical age deadline does different work and is not justified by stereotypes that a mother inevitably forms lasting ties while a father ordinarily does not. Neither a mother's continued care nor actual transmission of American culture is required by the compared maternal route. Recognition after majority may establish the same parentage fact; that does not make all child-connection objectives illegitimate, but the sex-based condition must substantially serve the actual objective. She joins Souter's bounded invalidity analysis, without holding every father-only condition or presence distinction invalid.

**Remedy/function:** Joins reversal and remand for the constitutional remedy and remaining statutory conditions. Favors eliminating the unequal barrier consistently with the existing citizenship-at-birth structure over destroying the maternal protection, but does not assume that remedial choice or every qualification is already resolved by this record. Mathews and Westcott require a distinct remedy inquiry; Pangilinan bars equity alone, not constitutional correction. A dissent should explain the direct personal claim, the distinction between biology and stereotype, and the limited remedy.

### Breyer — reverse the unequal age condition and remand

**Reach and merits:** Same disposition and operative ground as Souter and Ginsburg. Virginia, Kelley and Klinger require substantial actual fit for the sex criterion governing the claimant's benefit; the daughter's direct deprivation permits that inquiry. Fiallo supplies a comparison, not a command that a citizenship-at-birth classification escapes ordinary equality review.

**Alternative and support:** A timely parent-child connection can be a legitimate objective, but actual statutory operation matters. A formal acknowledgment or judgment before a deadline is not by itself a durable relationship, and the maternal route does not demand one after birth. More accurate parentage proof may justify different evidentiary requirements without establishing the fit of this categorical age barrier. He joins invalidation of the fourth condition as applied to the unequal recognition deadline, not a least-restrictive-means requirement or a holding that Congress lacks all authority to demand early ties.

**Remedy/function:** Joins the bounded remedial remand, with no immediate citizenship or passport declaration and no assumed physical-presence/support compliance. Constitutional correction is distinct from dispensing citizenship by equitable preference; Mathews and Westcott require statutory remedial attention. He may join the Souter/Ginsburg dissent rather than invent a separate framework.

## Coalition boundaries and accommodations frozen now

Five Associates support affirmance, but on three materially distinct grounds: Stevens's narrow merits ground; O'Connor/Kennedy's claimant/standing and limited own-claim ground; Scalia/Thomas's remedial ground. No five-Associate rationale is present. These rationales cannot be combined as a single test, and one is not declared a logical subset of another merely because each supports denial. Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support reversal and the limited constitutional/remedial remand. No minority conditional view becomes current law.

A proposed opinion may obtain Stevens's join by sustaining the fourth condition under actual intermediate scrutiny without stereotypes or a universal Fiallo rule. That accommodation does not obtain O'Connor/Kennedy's merits join: their refusal is the absence of third-party permission on this record. The latter two will join a genuine hindrance analysis and leave the father's merits unresolved; concrete injury remains acknowledged. Scalia/Thomas join only the stated remedy conclusion, not a broad ruling that Article III never permits citizenship-related equality review. The three reversal supporters may join a common opinion distinguishing biological proof from the deadline and remanding remedy; they do not commit to an unconditional citizenship award or wholesale invalidation of §1409(a).
