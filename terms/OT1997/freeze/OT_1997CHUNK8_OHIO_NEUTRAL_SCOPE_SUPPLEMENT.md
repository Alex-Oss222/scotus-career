**Objective record recitations for validation:**

- The Second Amended Complaint's factual allegations, paragraphs 13–47 (Appendix 16–23), describe the plan and allege that erroneous analysis improperly favors logging and clearcutting.
- Its first claim, paragraph 49 (Appendix 24), challenges approval of the plan and the directing or permitting of below-cost timber sales accomplished through clearcutting under NFMA, NEPA and the APA. This reference to NEPA is part of the pleaded claim; it does not by itself identify a specific omitted environmental statement or other denied procedure.
- Its second claim, paragraph 52 (Appendix 24), alleges that directing or permitting below-cost sales under the plan violates public-trust duties.
- Its third claim, paragraphs 54–60 (Appendix 25–26), challenges the regulations used to identify land suitable for timber production on the ground that they fail properly to identify economically unsuitable land, under NFMA and the APA.
- The requested relief, Appendix 26–27, includes a declaration against the plan and timbering it authorizes, an injunction against further timber harvesting and below-cost sales pending plan revision, costs and fees, and other appropriate relief.
- The respondents' merits brief, page 34, also asserts present harm from allowing motorcycles and heavy machinery without further consideration of wilderness-recreation effects and from the failure to close roads or build hiking trails in areas designated for logging. The published account reports no identified earlier court document presenting those claimed harms; whether and how that recitation fixes the reviewed scope requires clean neutral validation. It is not a finding that those activities actually occurred.
