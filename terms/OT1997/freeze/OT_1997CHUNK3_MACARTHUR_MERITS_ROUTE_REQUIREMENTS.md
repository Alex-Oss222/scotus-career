# MacArthur — requirements for an actual statutory merits route

**Status:** Options note only, using the already frozen sources and final current-law checks through February 12, 1998. No source search, factual selection, Court order or adjudication is made. MacArthur remains stopped. The [final assembly validation](OT_1997CHUNK3_ASSEMBLY_RESPONSE_VALIDATION_MACARTHUR_1998-02-12.md), [neutral packet](OT_1997CHUNK3_NEUTRAL_MACARTHUR.md), [monetary clarification](OT_1997CHUNK3_SOURCE_FACTS_MACARTHUR_MONETARY_ADDENDUM.md) and [exact law](../entering-law/OT_1997CHUNK3_MACARTHUR.md) control the distinctions below.

**No currently supplied source establishes a complete route.** The reporter establishes actual associational records and an original collective demand for monetary relief. It establishes neither a preserved Lindblom Privacy Act damages claim nor its abandonment, a qualifying amendment/refusal history, actual exemption coverage or waiver, or a fully resolved independent equitable action. Those are historical-record gaps, not routine procedure created by a simulated order. Section 14 does not permit filling them by ordinary scheduling reconstruction.

## 1. Complete the existing statutory damages route, if it actually survives

**Targeted existing record needed:** The operative complaint's Lindblom Privacy Act count and relief paragraphs; the relevant district disposition; and the appellate issues/prayer showing what happened to that particular monetary demand. These specific passages could establish whether a claim under §552a(g)(1)(D)/(g)(4) remained before the reviewing court. The reporter's collective original demand is not enough, and its express abandonment of other claims does not prove abandonment of this demand.

**Necessary legal and factual predicates:** Lindblom must be an eligible individual invoking an actual agency claim for the challenged continued maintenance, with a supported personal injury and causal connection at summary judgment. The actual claim must seek legally cognizable statutory money relief, rather than direct agency constitutional damages barred by Meyer. Section (g)(1)(D)'s adverse-effect requirement must be kept distinct from Article III injury and (g)(4)'s intentional-or-willful action and actual-damages conditions. The surviving claim and its factual support must provide a legally available form of compensation for the asserted injury; a bare damages label or newly invented loss cannot do that work. An applicable exemption must be addressed within its statutory scope: (e)(7) is nonexemptible, and Doe forbids using a blanket civil-remedy exemption to evade a nonexemptible duty, but that does not dispense with the actual statutory action and relief conditions.

**What need not be assumed:** Standing does not require a present award, certainty of ultimate recovery, or a final finding of intent, violation or damages. The court must distinguish a genuine route whose ultimate merits remain disputed from relief the law cannot provide or that is not actually in the action. No later Doe v. Chao or Cooper damages construction may be imported. No new monetary amount, injury episode or preserved count is proposed here.

**Would preservation alone suffice?** No. Proof that the original Privacy Act money claim survived would resolve one critical ambiguity, but the court must still identify a supported personal injury and explain why the actual type of money relief sought can legally redress it. Conversely, once that connection is supported, uncertainty whether Lindblom will ultimately prove the violation or recover is not itself a jurisdictional bar. A viable money route could permit the retention construction to be decided while equitable relief remains separately unresolved.

## 2. Complete an existing amendment/expungement route

**Targeted existing record needed:** Lindblom's actual request to amend the identified records; the agency's refusal and administrative-review disposition, or the qualifying failure to review; the operative claim seeking judicial amendment; and the agency's actual Privacy Act exemption determination for those records. A FOIA access request is not automatically an amendment request. The already reported in camera review and FOIA exemptions do not establish these distinct predicates.

**Necessary legal predicates:** Sections (d)(2)–(3), (g)(1)(A) and (g)(2) must actually authorize judicial amendment in this action. Any exemption from (d) must be resolved on the particular records, governing rule and statutory (j)/(k) category; if a valid exemption forecloses that route, a genuine applicable agency waiver would need its own support. The court cannot infer noncoverage or waiver because (e)(7) itself is nonexemptible. Doe expressly distinguishes those questions. Nagel's no-universal-exhaustion treatment of direct (e)(7) litigation does not prove completion of this separate amendment route; Clarkson recognizes its possible expungement use while reserving actual jurisdictional facts.

**Scope:** A supported route to amendment of identified retained records can supply meaningful partial redress without establishing entitlement to destroy every file, a future-maintenance ban or damages. Actual preservation duties must be identified and accommodated; none is presumed. The court must still identify the plaintiff's supported personal injury and causal connection rather than use the Foundation's failed reputation theory as a substitute.

## 3. Establish an independently available prospective equitable action

**Targeted existing record needed:** The actual claim and requested injunction identifying the challenged continuing official conduct, the defendant's connection and an available form of prospective restraint. The existing pleadings and rulings must permit that action to be considered within this review; no new claim or defendant is silently supplied.

**Necessary legal determinations:** Reich recognizes prospective equitable review of an implementing officer's action beyond statutory authority unless Congress displaced that remedy. Section 702 waives nonmoney sovereign immunity but does not itself supply the cause or override another consent-to-suit statute's express or implied prohibition of relief. The court must decide whether an independently cognizable equitable action exists here and whether the Privacy Act's specified remedies displace the particular relief requested. Neither conclusion is already settled for this action by the supplied sources. A bare (e)(7) claim cannot bypass (g)(1)(D) simply by being called equitable or declaratory.

**Scope:** The actual injury, causal link and lawful prospective relief must satisfy Article III. An eventual mandatory decree must specify the responsible officer or successor by name or title under §702; that decree requirement is distinct from an automatic demand to add a new party before jurisdiction can exist. Reich's non-APA equitable route does not require inventing an APA pleading, and §702's waiver alone does not establish one. This route requires a real legal resolution of displacement, not just a stipulated jurisdictional label.

## Historical completion versus express limited reconstruction

Historical completion means obtaining the identified existing passages or authenticated recitations and accepting what they establish, whether favorable or unfavorable to a merits route. The current missing sources prove neither party default nor satisfaction of the predicates. No in-world production order should issue solely to repair this research gap.

An expressly selected counterfactual premise would be different. The narrow possible packages are:

- **Money package:** The existing Lindblom §552a(g)(1)(D)/(g)(4) demand remained preserved through review, and the actual threshold record contains identified evidence of a personal injury causally connected to the challenged maintenance for which that demand seeks a legally cognizable form of compensation. The evidence and injury must be specified, not replaced by “damages are available.” Preservation alone is an incomplete package; this note selects no invented loss or evidence.
- **Amendment package:** The same identified records were the subject of a qualifying preexisting amendment request and refusal/review failure; judicial amendment is actually sought in the preserved action; and a specified, legally sufficient basis makes the (d)/(g)(1)(A) route available for those records. That basis must be actual noncoverage under an identified exemption category or an expressly selected valid waiver, not a general assumption that exemptions do not matter. This package would add material administrative history and therefore is not routine reconstruction.
- **Equitable package:** The existing preserved action actually presents the identified prospective restraint against the challenged official maintenance, with the defendant connection and supported personal injury necessary for that relief. The court must still adjudicate the equitable cause and statutory-displacement questions. A factual premise alone cannot stipulate those legal questions into a favorable answer.

These are precise requirements for any proposed limited premise, not findings that such events occurred or authorization to invent them. No complete reconstruction package can be selected solely from the current reporter recitations. The minimum additional express selection must identify the particular route and the material missing facts it adopts; the court must then independently decide whether those facts establish jurisdiction and a lawful cause and remedy. Calling the package “threshold only” does not make a material new claim, injury, administrative act or waiver routine under §14. Any such material change requires clean affected-component validation, modeling and reconciliation before assembly; it cannot be inserted into the frozen commitments by this note.

**Can a threshold record premise alone remove the current obstacle?** It can supply the missing factual basis without predetermining the retention merits, if it establishes one actual, legally coherent redress route and a supported personal injury. It cannot create sovereign consent, a cause or remedy contrary to law, and it cannot compel a majority to adopt the test. Establishing a damages route would not automatically establish equitable relief; establishing an amendment route would not establish damages; establishing either would not cure the separate constitutional standing defects. The threshold-only vacatur/remand remains the supported eight-Associate disposition unless and until a real route is established, and Stone's response to that disposition remains unselected.
