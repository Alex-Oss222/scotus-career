## Disposition and precise joins

**Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg, and Breyer each retain affirmance of the appellate judgment reinstating the prosecution and join P1, P2, and P3 at the following exact scopes.** No conviction is under review, and no guilt is adjudicated.

- **P1:** Original §228 reaches willful post-enactment withholding of an existing legally fixed support payment where obligor and children reside in different States. The charged Florida-to-Pennsylvania payment relation supplies the direct interstate connection. No completed payment, flight, travel, wire, voluntary border crossing, or evasion motive is an additional element. Nonpayment longer than one year and an amount greater than $5,000 are alternatives, neither substituting for different-state residence.
- **P2:** This is direct regulation of a private obligor, not a command to state government. The existing order is accepted as a predicate; federal creation or revision of support, custody, parentage, or domestic status is not authorized. A properly presented challenge to the predicate order's existence or continuing force remains cognizable.
- **P3:** Affirm reinstatement and permit ordinary proceedings on the existing charge. Only willful failures after October 25, 1992 may constitute the crime; every element must be proved beyond reasonable doubt and lawful ability-related and other defenses remain. Earlier arrears may supply background or past-due status. Guilt, sentence, restitution amount, and the treatment of older arrears in a future restitution order are not decided.

Each of the eight joins all three propositions. No separate aggregate commerce theory, automatic state-command rule, new offense element, or restitution holding receives a join through this reconciliation.

The lower opinion employed an aggregate-effects rationale, giving weight to a national support-collection problem, congressional judgments, and normal interstate payment channels. It also described an interstate sequence beginning with a parent's movement and stated a broad proposition that action under enumerated power entails no Tenth Amendment violation. Those statements are not imported as the current Court's rule.

Current *Mussari/Schroeder*, joined by every Associate, already establishes the narrower sufficient ground: the legally fixed cross-border payment itself and regulation of the private obligor. It does not require actual travel or payment, and it preserves independent limits on implementing means. Current *Lopez* bars reliance merely on remote downstream social costs, not this direct interstate relation. *New York* and the qualified *Printz/Mack* rules remain distinct; the absence of state commandeering is not independently a grant of commerce power. Thus the reconciled disposition agrees with the lower result while resting on the precise binding grounds and qualifications actually entering this event.
