## Justice-specific commitments and adversarial comparison

### Stevens

He dissented in Agostini but applies the governing modified Lemon test's preserved official-worship prohibition here.

**Threshold and judgment:** Reach the merits on Urbanski's concrete future exposure; reverse and remand for bounded prospective relief. His Lee threshold join supports a live claim without adjudicating the other plaintiffs' individual standing.

**Sufficient ground and scope:** Apply Lee's independently sufficient official-sponsorship ground through the specific-prohibition route of Lemon as Agostini modified it to the actual state-university ceremony. The official request, selection and ceremonial placement constitute governmental presentation of worship. His Lee sponsorship join, his join in Souter's Lee concurrence concerning nonpreferential endorsement, his Ingebretsen Parts II–III joins, and his Sherman Parts I–II joins support the distinction between voluntary civic acknowledgment and an official devotional exercise. He need not establish a separate coercion violation, denominational preference, improper subjective purpose or excessive entanglement.

**Strongest alternative and response:** Affirmance has genuine support in the mature audience, abstention practices, Marsh and the long local tradition. Those facts weaken an adolescent-pressure claim, but do not make this explicit prayer a civic pledge or remove governmental attribution. Marsh's legislative institution supplies no categorical exception for every adult state ceremony. The limited holding concerns this recurring official university worship arrangement; it does not outlaw every religious reference in government speech.

**Join barriers and remedy:** Will join a contextual official-worship holding without requiring a finding of psychological pressure. Will not join a rule making coercion necessary, a disclaimer cure for official worship, or an analogy that treats this prayer as genuinely private. Relief must address the officially selected invocation and benediction; no exclusion of private religious viewpoints, generic university speech ban or damages award follows. A narrower holding confined to this program is sufficient; no broader adult ceremonial-prayer holding is needed.

**Join barriers and remedy:** She joins the concrete specific-prohibition and attribution conclusion, not a universal claim that all adult ceremonial prayer is invalid. ## Proposition and remedy compatibility

The five reversing Associates share this complete sufficient proposition: **A public university violates the Establishment Clause by selecting a religious leader to deliver actual invocation and benediction prayers as components of its official commencement program on this record. This governmental organization of worship falls within the specific-prohibition route of Lemon as Agostini modified it; the mature audience, optional attendance, ability to abstain, nonsectarian wording and sincere ceremonial purpose do not cure it.** This is a contextual application to this devotional program, not a holding that every religious reference or adult governmental ceremony is forbidden. Lee and Ingebretsen supply the sponsorship principle; applying it here reaches their previously unresolved university setting. Lemon remains governing as Agostini modified it; its specific-prohibition route supplies the five reversing Associates' ground. Marsh remains valid within its distinct legislative context. Sherman remains valid because its permitted exercise was civic allegiance rather than organized worship.

The three affirming Associates can share the narrower conclusion that **this brief, traditional adult commencement practice, with actual freedom to abstain and no demonstrated unconstitutional pressure, falls outside the established school-prayer prohibitions.** They cannot automatically share a full coercion methodology: Kennedy preserves substantial practical pressure without a legal penalty, while Scalia and Thomas's historical position is more demanding. Their agreement does not make the prayer private and does not displace the controlling secondary-school sponsorship rule.

The five reversing Associates authorize reversal and remand for declaration and appropriately limited prospective restraint of the University's official selection and inclusion of these devotional invocations and benedictions. The supported ongoing policy and Urbanski's continuing exposure permit this relief. Precise decree terms remain for ordinary implementation below; no automatic hearing, damages award, fee ruling, university-wide speech prohibition, denominational exclusion or compulsory new forum is ordered. All eight agree that independent prayer and otherwise lawful private religious expression must remain outside an injunction's reach.
