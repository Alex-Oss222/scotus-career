## Exact common propositions and judgment components

**P0 — Review and scope.** Reach the supplied merits under §1254(1). Review the presented statutory, Article III, due-process, contract-impairment and equality objections. Do not decide independently accrued claims, a separate state contract's existence or enforceability, or the conditional Rule 60(b) vacatur issue. The four circuit appeals receive the same judgment; no unsupported assignment of a particular issue to an individual appeal number is made.

**P1 — Independent statutory route and current text.** Under §3626(b)(2), a defendant is entitled to termination of covered prospective relief approved without the required substantive need, narrowness and least-intrusiveness findings, subject to the court's independent application of amended (b)(3). Actual findings can suffice without statutory formulas; the State must establish the termination predicate. The applicable preservation condition requires written, record-based findings of necessity to correct a current AND ongoing federal violation, no broader relief than necessary, narrow tailoring and least intrusiveness. Rufo's separate equitable significant-change condition is not added to this statutory route. These propositions apply Plyler and Lewis while expressly updating only the statutory text changed on November 26.

**P2 — Application and requested further inquiry.** Sustain the lower determination that the actual findings do not supply the necessary statutory predicates for retention, and reject a compulsory new inquiry based only on the described speculative deterioration. This does not establish that compliance with a decree necessarily defeats a federal claim, that missing ritual words suffice for termination, or that all possible constitutional questions are fact questions receiving maximum deference. Legal interpretation and the existence of an appropriate opportunity to present relevant evidence remain judicial questions; ordinary protection of supported historical findings does not insulate legal error. No particular new hearing or findings order is justified by a demonstrated defect on this packet. No categorical rule bars protection against sufficiently supported threatened injury in a properly presented case.

**P3 — Article III at the presented scope.** Reject the final-judgment and Klein objections to this termination. Plyler directly permits genuine alteration of the law governing continuing prospective relief while preserving independent judicial determination of statutory predicates. Robertson and Wheeling Bridge support that distinction; Rufo confirms the prospective decree's capacity for lawful modification. Congress has not commanded a contrary historical finding, legislatively adjudicated compliance, dictated a result under unchanged law or canceled accrued damages. Morgan Stanley and simulated Plaut remain separate bounded final-dismissal authorities; the historical Plaut rule quoted below is not governing law. Because the amendment changes no outcome-determinative preservation issue shown here, its application here warrants no broader constitutional holding about current-but-not-ongoing violations or elimination of otherwise necessary federal protection.

**P4 — Vested enforcement and procedural due process.** Under Plyler, beneficiaries possess no entitlement to perpetual unchanged federal enforcement of this mutable prospective decree against valid later remedial law. The actual motion, opposition and appellate review support rejection of the presented legal due-process objection; there is no established denial of an opportunity to present a preserved relevant claim. No unreported hearing is presumed. This decides neither accrued property rights nor the separate validity of all contractual or monetary claims. It does not authorize termination after a supported refusal to consider evidence required by the applicable preservation condition.

**P5 — The presented contract-impairment theory.** Assume for this legal objection, without finding, both a contract and substantial impairment. National Railroad Passenger Corp. v. Atchison (1985), as reproduced in the verified lower opinion, supplies rationality review of federal impairment where the United States is not the contracting party. Federal judicial approval and federal legislation benefiting States do not make the United States a party to their alleged bargain. Garcia's political-process account supplies no constructive federal self-dealing. Restricting continuing federal supervision beyond relief necessary to correct federal violations bears a rational relationship to lawful allocation of institutional responsibility and public resources, while retaining judicial protection of federal rights. The justification is that relationship, not the circular assertion that a law is rational because it changed the law. Ending the federal decree's future judicial force does not adjudicate a freestanding contract's existence, breach, enforceability in another forum or accrued liability. No holding is supplied about the United States impairing its own contract.

**P6 — Equal protection and access.** Reject the presented Fifth Amendment equality objection at ordinary rational review. No suspect-class theory is presented, and the challenged termination on this record does not punish pretrial detainees or deny effective adjudication and remedies for federal-right violations. Bell's substantive protection and Lewis's meaningful-access right remain. Neither supplies an entitlement to perpetual enforcement of every negotiated term. The relevant disadvantage is the special limit on continuing federal prison supervision; limiting such management to supported federal corrective needs rationally relates to administrative responsibility, public resources and safety. Those interests do not make this underlying suit frivolous or permit punishment before conviction. Beach's conceivable-ground principle operates together with Heller's requirement of fit to the actual disadvantage and Romer's Moreno/Cleburne limits. A bare desire to harm detainees would be impermissible; no separate animus finding is necessary to find irrationality, and rhetoric about criminals is not categorically incapable of bearing on a broader detention law. The reported rhetoric and operation do not defeat the concrete rational connection in this case. This is no general holding that any remedial restriction is valid whenever a courthouse remains nominally open.

**P7 — Termination, vacatur and mandate.** The statute includes judicial consent decrees within relief. Termination therefore ends the federal decree's prospective legal operation, including the obligations operating as continuing judicial relief; merely withholding one method of specific enforcement while retaining that judicial operation is insufficient. The judgment does not annul the historical decree or declare its original entry erroneous. Section 3626 supplies no command to vacate the decree retrospectively, erase its past lawful effects or cancel accrued monetary liabilities. Independent contractual or state-law claims are left to their own predicates, without an affirmative finding that one survives or may be enforced. Affirm the First Circuit judgment and leave implementation of its prospective-termination direction to the lower court. No new Supreme Court merits remand, mandatory evidentiary hearing, release order, stay, deadline, retention of jurisdiction or scheduled return is added. Because termination is obtained, honor the Commissioner's conditional setting aside of Rule 60(b); do not convert it into unconditional abandonment or adjudicate vacatur under that Rule.
