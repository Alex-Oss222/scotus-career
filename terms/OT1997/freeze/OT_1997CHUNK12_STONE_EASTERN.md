**Proposed Stone disposition: Reverse as applied to Eastern Enterprises.**

Congress generally possesses substantial authority to legislate retrospectively. Nevertheless, the unusually remote, severe and disproportionate liabilities imposed on Eastern present a substantial due-process problem.

Stone would distinguish this case from *United States v. Carlton*, which sustained a narrower retroactive tax correction. He would favor a confined due-process rationale rather than a sweeping new Takings Clause rule.

**Doctrinal development:** Even expressly retroactive legislation must satisfy independently applicable constitutional limitations.
