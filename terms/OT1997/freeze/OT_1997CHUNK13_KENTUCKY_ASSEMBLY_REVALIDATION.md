## Precise blocker: gubernatorial Fund-participant percentage branch

The approved Issue 5 identifies a candidate-wide $150,000 PAC-receipt ceiling. Its essential application says the $1,000 per-PAC limit means the candidate-wide ceiling begins restricting gifts only after sufficiently numerous committees have given; the 150-committee example supports the facial judgment, although the supplement rejects turning that example into an irrebuttable empirical finding for every race. It affirms this cap on source-specific dependency and the asserted absence of a demonstrated prohibitory effect, reserving later concrete burdens.

The corrected neutral statute has two distinct branches. For a Fund participant, permanent-committee receipts may not exceed 25 percent of qualifying contributions received, up to $150,000 per election. The percentage remains operative at lower receipts; $150,000 is a maximum rather than the universal trigger. A nonparticipant has the separately reported $150,000 ceiling. The 151st-PAC illustration assumes maximum private fundraising and maximum individual gifts.

This is a materially different operative trigger, not a citation correction or a more precise description of an unchanged $150,000 rule. A participating slate may encounter the percentage restriction before reaching the maximum-fundraising illustration. The difference could affect Stone's judgment about cumulative associational burden and the adequacy of the facial justification—the very matters his approved reasoning makes relevant. The supplement contains no express approved branch addressing that percentage rule and no discretion envelope authorizing substitution of a new sufficient rationale.
