**Boundary:** Separate reconciliation receipt. Read the frozen `OT_1997CHUNK8_BOUSLEY_PRESERVATION_VALIDATION.md` and the existing per-matter reconciled handoff. No Stone material, raw Record or assembled draft was read. The original reconciliation and all earlier commitment handoffs remain unchanged.

The clean validation confirms two limited record facts from the previously full-read Court opinion: the District Court did not address factual innocence, and the Government did not assert that the initial collateral omission waived that route. The source offers only a tentative explanation for the District Court's omission. These facts do not establish innocence, resolve plea intelligence, erase the distinct direct-review default, establish cause and prejudice, or require a particular evidentiary hearing. They support a first lower-court innocence determination if the five Associates' conditional-remand framework is adopted. They supply no general waiver rule and do not decide the full scope of judicial initiative over unasserted procedural defenses.
