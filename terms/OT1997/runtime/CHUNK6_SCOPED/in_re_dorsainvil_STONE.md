## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Fair review within the enacted collateral scheme

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Establish lawful jurisdiction and savings-clause eligibility, then examine any properly available claim and relief. This case is an approved + hearing in preparation, not a completed judgment. A screening decision must apply the actual rules to the claim; a mere prior loss is not a substitute for that inquiry.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Give effect to both Congress’s restrictions on repetitive litigation and the retained savings clause. The intervening interpretation must apply to the conviction and support a substantial statutory-innocence claim, and the absence of an effective earlier opportunity must be established. Failure to satisfy successive-motion criteria alone is insufficient.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A qualifying claim of detention for conduct outside the criminal statute may receive §2241 consideration when §2255 was inadequate or ineffective to test it in the circumstances described. Eligibility for that process and entitlement to ultimate relief remain separate determinations. This is Stone’s approved position, not a Court holding.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The user expressly approved this reasoning for both cases:

Congress may establish orderly procedures, restrict repetitive litigation, and protect the finality of judgments. Courts must respect those choices.

But the statutory savings clause must retain practical meaning. When an intervening interpretation supports a substantial claim that the prisoner’s conduct was outside the criminal statute, and no effective earlier opportunity existed to test that claim, an appropriate court must be able to examine it.

The prisoner must establish eligibility for that review and entitlement to relief. The government retains its lawful defenses. Judicial consideration guarantees an independent examination, not a favorable result.

The relevant legislative choice includes the retained savings clause as well as the restrictions on repeated §2255 motions. Its history helps explain the change of forum and preservation of an adequate means to challenge detention; it does not permit Stone to rewrite successive-motion categories or invent jurisdiction from an attractive outcome. A statutory interpretation remains different from new factual evidence and a new constitutional rule. The consequences for liberty do not change those categories. Courts independently determine whether the actual claim qualifies and whether the conviction survives, respecting lawful procedural defenses without replacing examination with generalized appeals to finality.

* **Treatment of Controlling Precedent:** Felker preserves the distinction between barred authorization review and independent habeas; the statutory and Rule 20 requirements of any original petition remain. Morgan Stanley and Plaut reinforce independent adjudication under an authorized procedure but do not create this criminal remedy. Article III supplies no freestanding warrant to hear every claim; §§2241 and 2255 supply the relevant statutory basis. Complete foreclosure of meaningful review would present a separate Suspension Clause question, not an automatic overruling of Felker. Lonchar, Schlup, and Herrera retain the distinct limits described in Section I.B; none makes access equivalent to success.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No unlimited retries, general reopening power, new exhaustion requirement, universal oral-hearing entitlement, or right to favorable treatment. The prisoner uses the procedures the law actually provides and meets the applicable burdens. A fair opportunity may end in a lawful denial.

### Issue 2: The particular firearm claim and appropriate examination

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Assess the savings-clause showing and, through a lawful proceeding when that showing is satisfied, the actual firearm record and defenses. Do not assume that rejecting use establishes innocence of every properly available statutory alternative.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Intent, protective storage, availability, and possession cannot substitute for the active employment required by Bailey. The government must establish the elements and legal availability of any carrying or intentional-assistance ground on which it relies. Whether an officer reasonably perceived danger is not itself the criminal conduct element.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The described record warrants a genuine opportunity to establish the asserted statutory-innocence claim through the applicable savings-clause process. It does not establish a right to release or resolve the disputed alternative grounds.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The first §2255 proceeding ended before Bailey, and the subsequent interpretation supplies a concrete question about whether the use theory criminalized conduct outside §924(c). The described reaching movements are evidence to assess, not an established finding of aiming, firing, or attempting to fire. The government’s carrying argument remains substantial and separate. Stone requires examination of the full evidence, charge, and legally available grounds rather than treating mere inclusion of carrying language as a complete verdict or harmlessness analysis. He does not find innocence merely because active use is unsupported. A district court may reject the claim on an adequate record and correct legal ground; it may not substitute the gun’s proximity or the mere fact of a prior §2255 motion for that examination.

Stone’s supported process choice is claim-specific examination. The proper court may resolve a question on the existing record when sufficient, require appropriate submissions, or permit necessary factual development under the governing law. The prisoner is not required to have already won the ultimate claim merely to explain why review is available; nor does an allegation alone establish every gateway or merits element. No new production or persuasion burden replaces an existing one. A lawful adverse decision remains possible after that examination.

* **Treatment of Controlling Precedent:** Apply Bailey’s active-employment holding and its preservation of a properly available carrying ground. Preserve the distinction between the majority’s remedy and Stone’s separate view. No later historical carrying definition, retroactivity decision, or amendment is imported into the assigned OT1997 event. The legal-innocence inquiry concerns the firearm offense; the separate drug convictions remain subject to their own lawful grounds for challenge.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No factual finding that the prisoner attempted to fire or was innocent is supplied. The carrying evidence, adequacy of the plea or instructions as applicable, collateral defenses, and proper remedy must be determined through an adequate record and an authorized court.
