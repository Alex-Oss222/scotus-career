## What differs and what does not

The April 9 ICJ order, the scheduled execution, the admitted or alleged notification failures, access available since 1996, the disputed plea counterfactual and the original applicants' new-order theory were present in history. None is a counterfactual fact or a newly discovered causal finding. The ICJ expressly reserved remedy and prejudice for the merits; that reservation does not create a domestic cause of action, resolve domestic jurisdiction or erase an otherwise applicable procedural rule.

The current legal setting does differ in specified respects. Souter's Seminole opinion now supplies the Court's bounded officer-enforcement rule; no controlling decision overrules Union Gas or adopts the historical categorical Article I/immunity formulation. Coeur d'Alene affirmatively preserves prospective enforcement against connected officers within real remedial limits. These rules do not establish an ongoing treaty duty, private right or injunction. Aktepe now requires the particular independently manageable duty to be examined and rejects foreign-relations subject matter as a sufficient political-question bar; it supplies neither the duty nor an automatic hearing. Blessing requires an actual provision-specific individual entitlement and keeps right, channel, defendant and relief separate. Woodard preserves a defined minimum against fundamentally arbitrary clemency process and rejects no otherwise cognizable judicial claim on the theory that mercy is its exclusive substitute. It creates no treaty cause or general extra-time entitlement.

The proposed protection also differs in kind and duration from granting the requested longer relief or conclusively enforcing the ICJ order. Each surviving preservation position, in A-767 or A-771 only, supports a domestic administrative order ending at 5 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time on April 16, 1998, unless the Court acts earlier. The domestic proceedings remain pending. A-767's actual request ends with disposition of its original habeas petition; its purposes do not convert it into an automatic stay through ICJ merits. Preliminary preservation authority remains distinct from ultimate jurisdiction and merits. The clean source refresh supplies its limits and requires a separate §2283 inquiry for the civil applications; the administrative label is no exception.

Steel Co. forbids assumption of disputed Article III power to enter a contested merits adjudication. The present denial positions are limited interim assessments; the preservation positions must identify the actual appellate or original channel being protected and cannot rest on freestanding equity. Baker confirms the bounded need to examine a nonparty's own rights, without granting Paraguay a remedy or deciding jurisdiction. The intervening Hinds decision changes none of these rules: its eight-Associate statutory construction and existing-record remand establish no treaty or emergency-relief principle, and its constitutional questions remain reserved.
