## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Legislative revision of continuing equitable standards

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the statutory and Article III questions needed to review termination; reserve factual compliance issues not resolved in the supplied summary.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply revised Morgan Stanley/Plaut and Rufo as distinct holdings. Neither absolute finality nor an unrestricted congressional reopening power describes the received law.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Congress may revise the law governing continuing equitable relief while leaving courts responsible for the findings and application the amended statute requires.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone would affirm the Eighth Circuit’s rejection of the Article III challenge and remand for the inquiry the district court never performed. Section 3626 changes the legal standard governing future coercive relief; it leaves judges to find current violations and fit relief to them. It therefore differs from legislation ordering a named party to win on unchanged law. The prisoners retain federal constitutional claims and an opportunity for the statutory findings, so termination does not deprive them of an irrevocable entitlement to the 1984 and 1988 remedial arrangements. The law’s focus on prison administration passes rational-basis review without diminishing the substantive rights of prisoners. Stone would reject a wholesale constitutional exemption for pre-PLRA decrees, but would equally reject automatic termination based only on the age of the decree. The library component must rest on the actual court-access rule, and other components on their own federal rights. No remand finding should be presumed.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The interlocutory appeal presented constitutional challenges to termination; the Rules Enabling Act argument was not renewed on appeal. The automatic stay and new-relief provisions are not decided. Apply the amended current-AND-ongoing wording at the simulated 1998 event.
