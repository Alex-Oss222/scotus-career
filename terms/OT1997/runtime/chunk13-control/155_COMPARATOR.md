# 155. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission v. Johnson & Higgins, Inc.

*Stone pre-vote case brief. Alternate-history petition and merits treatment, not an actual Supreme Court merits decision.*

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. Merits comparator

The historical Supreme Court never reached the merits. The Second Circuit upheld EEOC enforcement and injunctive relief, with a reported dissent regarding coverage of the owner-directors.

## B. Petition comparator

- **Actual treatment:** Certiorari denied **October 6, 1997**, No. **96-1743**, OT 1997.
- **Official source:** [Supreme Court Journal, October Term 1997](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/journal/jnl97.pdf), October 6 orders, p. 8.
