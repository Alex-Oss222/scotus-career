# 147. Clifton v. Federal Election Commission

**Matter:** No. 97-786, certiorari petition from the First Circuit  
**Simulation stage:** Petition conference, February 1998, October Term 1997  
**Scope:** The appellate decision and unresolved remand issues as of the OT 1997 petition, not later district-court action.

---

# SECTION III. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. Merits comparator

There is **no** historical Supreme Court merits decision in No. 97-786. The operative historical appellate judgment for comparison is the First Circuit's mixed disposition, 114 F.3d 1309. Its remand of the electioneering provisions is a lower-court procedural step, not Supreme Court approval or disapproval of them.

## B. Petition comparator

- **Historical petition treatment:** **Certiorari denied.**
- **Date:** February 23, 1998, inside OT 1997.
- **Sources:** [Supreme Court official OT 1997 Journal, February 23 orders, No. 97-786](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/journal/jnl97.pdf); [FEC procedural history](https://www.fec.gov/legal-resources/court-cases/clifton-v-fec/).
- **Limitation:** The historical denial contains no merits opinion and cannot be treated as a Supreme Court judgment on the surviving regulations or remanded clauses.
