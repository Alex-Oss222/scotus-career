## Threshold sequence and common qualified ground

1. **Identify the appealed claims and lawful appellate assumptions.** Section 1254 permits review of the federal appellate judgment but enlarges no §1291 jurisdiction. Johnson, Behrens and the actual Clanton/Smith decisions permit a legal notice defense accepting the plaintiff-favorable historical account. Here that includes alleged patronage and assumed nonpolicymaker status, alongside the reported undisputed duties and defendants' knowledge. No denial of political motivation, different employee function, nonparticipation finding or competing credibility account is necessary. The district court's characterization of the policymaker inquiry does not transform every underlying historical fact into law. Conversely, the presence of motive and status disputes does not foreclose a legal defense that assumes the claimants' side of them. No discrete unavailable factual-appeal component is established on this narrow route; no manufactured jurisdictional dismissal is entered simply because factual questions remain in the underlying suit.
2. **State the real substantive rule without deciding its ultimate application.** Branti asks whether affiliation is appropriate to effective performance of the actual office, not whether the words policymaker or confidential can be attached. Its footnote 6 rejects a nonreappointment loophole. Its footnotes 13–14 distinguish prosecutors' broader public responsibilities from public defenders' individual-client obligations without deciding that every prosecutor or employee may be selected by party. Professional confidentiality, expertise, at-will status and personal desire for trusted associates do not independently satisfy the rule. These employees' work implementing a public prosecutorial function is relevant to the possible exception; the proposition that one party produces better investigators is not.
3. **Resolve conduct-time notice on that actual rule.** Harlow/Anderson remains the objective particularized defense. Elder requires all relevant existing law, and the current Lanier, Clanton and Smith formulations permit obvious coverage without a factual twin. The date is mid-November 1995. Regan's inherent-power inquiry, Vezzetti's nonexclusive functional indicators, Savage's actual confidential offices and the pre-1995 prosecutor cases are relevant context. They do not create a numerical safe harbor or controlling decision about these exact seven positions. Together with Branti's express reservation, however, they leave room for a reasonable official to regard this known close investigative participation in confidential prosecutorial decisions as materially different from an ordinary clerk's access to sensitive files or a public defender's client confidences. The ground is that particular reasonable error, not that every uncertain job classification yields immunity.
4. **Apply that ground to every materially distinct position.** The role-specific application below is an essential limit on the all-seven result. Actual final authority to prosecute or determine broad policy is not assumed. An official could reasonably perceive the close confidential contribution to the discretionary prosecutorial process as sufficient in 1995 even if the Constitution ultimately requires more. The shared reported functions, not a title or missing description, permit the common disposition.
5. **Keep motive and other defenses separate.** Assumed patronage is not displaced by Vacco's stated wish for stronger criminal enforcement. A possible independent management reason is not Mt. Healthy proof. Crawford-El and the actual Jeffries/Valot holdings retain ordinary competent motive evidence, actual personal responsibility and a real preponderance same-decision defense; no direct-evidence or clear-and-convincing requirement is imposed. Because the notice defense is dispositive assuming adverse personal causal participation, the Court need not determine whether Vacco, Buscaglia or Page actually caused each nonreappointment. Page's letters and Vacco's office alone establish no constitutional liability. Fletcher's advocacy function does not confer absolute immunity on personnel decisions.
6. **Confine the remedy.** Personal damages immunity is the judgment, constitutional permission is not. No mandatory merits-first sequence prevents it. No merits declaration is added to guide future dismissals on a record that does not require one. Other claims and any separately supported remedies retain their own predicates. The statute and regulations describing the unit do not themselves establish affiliation's appropriateness or an official's notice; the source's quoted 1996 regulatory text is not silently deemed a separately verified November 1995 enactment.

Later Gordon, Vona, McEvoy, Bavaro and other post-November-1995 decisions cannot supply retrospective notice or validate a new 1995 documentation duty. O'Hare, Umbehr and the current-term public employment decisions govern present doctrine and provide each Justice's actual public position; they are not conduct-time notice sources. The January 21 LaChance decision supplies neither an actual falsehood nor an independent lawful firing ground here and does not turn patronage into a CSRA dispute. Lee changes no interlocutory limit, Buchanan supplies no immunity rule, and Lunding's Article IV inquiry does not become the Branti test.

## Enhanced alternative-path comparison

**Path A — affirm the particular legal notice defense without deciding constitutional permissibility.** This is the principal commitment of each Justice. Its strongest objection is that Branti already rejected confidentiality and employment labels, so a prosecutor's need for accurate, trusted staff cannot disguise party-based discrimination. That objection rules out a broad office-wide exemption. It does not eliminate the unresolved 1995 boundary where known employees worked directly and confidentially with prosecutors in the process that implements enforcement choices; Branti expressly distinguished the public prosecutor's role and reserved its application. The pre-existing functional circuit authorities reinforce that actual uncertainty. The judgment protects reasonable error while leaving the substantive governmental burden intact. It neither declares that the plaintiffs were policymakers nor needs a specific identical appellate case immunizing each job.

**Path B — vacate the immunity judgment for a genuinely functional, employee-specific legal application.** This would be lawful if the lower disposition depended only on confidentiality, exempt status, office importance or a factual inference that every employee chose prosecution policy. It would preserve denial below only subject to the proper legal analysis, not establish liability, require a trial or compel production of missing descriptions. Its strongest objection is that the published opinion already provides the shared direct confidential prosecutorial relationship and actual additional functions, assumes patronage/nonpolicymaker status, and applies an independent reasonable-error defense. The absence of complete descriptions is not an affirmative showing of purely technical work. On the authorized bounded record the principal commitments reject Path B because it would fail to give effect to that real legal defense, rather than because patronage is harmless or because every factual question has been resolved. A different supported plaintiff-favorable account severing the asserted prosecutorial connection would materially change the inquiry; it cannot be invented to obtain a remand.

**Path C — affirm for supervisory or senior positions but remand Henesey, Matthews or Angiletta.** This is the most substantial component alternative to common treatment. It properly notices the absence of supervisory authority and uneven individual descriptions. Its weakness is that the relevant reasonable perception is not confined to supervisory status: the shared close confidential involvement in the prosecutorial process is reported for all seven, and Henesey's specific investigative functions are affirmative evidence beyond his title. Dividing at rank would introduce a categorical rule the governing cases reject. Each Justice below separately evaluates the closest application; none commits to a partial remand on this record. This does not preclude a different disposition by role when the material functions differ.

**Broader merits path — declare all seven constitutionally removable for affiliation.** The argument is that prosecutors implement electoral policy and these staff materially assist them. It would give the officials a merits defense rather than immunity alone. Its strongest objection is decisive here: the detailed functional question remains unsettled, the source does not establish the full inherent authority of every office, and sensitive factual investigation is not necessarily an appropriate partisan function. All eight reject this unnecessary extension. None treats Branti footnote 13 as a holding resolving all prosecutor support employees.

**Factual-jurisdiction path — dismiss the entire interlocutory appeal.** Johnson forbids fact review, but a genuine legal notice defense accepting the proper assumptions survives. The lower opinion's legal route does not require resolving disputed patronage or final policymaker status. All eight therefore reject dismissal of the entire appeal. If an actual challenge instead contests knowledge, participation, credibility or what duties were performed, that portion would be unavailable; the present judgment does not adjudicate such a challenge or authorize connected factual review.

## Source limits, isolation and release conditions

The unreproduced pleadings, full depositions, appendix and several job descriptions do not establish waiver, default, an employee's unreported duty, a defendant's unreported knowledge or an entitlement to further discovery. Engine §14 permits bounded published recitations. The common disposition rests on affirmative reported functions; if a clean correction shows a material plaintiff-favorable dispute about that common premise, the affected role/defendant component must be remodeled.
