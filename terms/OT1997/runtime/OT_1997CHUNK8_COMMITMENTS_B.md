## Textron Lycoming Reciprocating Engine Division v. UAW — No. 97-463

### Proposed sufficient ground and scope

Reverse the Third Circuit and remand to reinstate dismissal of the presented §301 action for want of the asserted statutory jurisdiction. Section 301(a)'s category is suits for violation of covered labor contracts. A standalone formation-fraud action that alleges no violation by either party does not enter that category merely because it seeks to have the contract declared voidable. The statute permits a court to decide formation or validity when necessary to resolve a properly brought violation suit; this holding does not prevent such a defense or a genuine declaratory action concerning an actual or threatened violation. Neither predicate is established by the present complaint or reported record. The separate federal-common-law theory is outside the reviewed question and is not adjudicated.

Wooddell establishes that an individual beneficiary may enforce a qualifying interunion contract; it broadens neither the covered type of dispute nor the statute into all litigation concerning labor contracts. Kokkonen confirms that federal policy and a contract's relationship to federal law do not replace a statutory jurisdictional basis. Lexecon reinforces enforcement of the actual statutory allocation despite practical arguments for a different forum. These analogies supplement, rather than substitute for, §301's own text. No fraud, breach, contract validity, state-law remedy, or NLRA unfair-labor-practice question is decided.

### Alternative-path check

The union's strongest path treats contract validity as indispensable to uniform federal labor law and relies on §301 decisions permitting validity defenses. That establishes why a court resolving an alleged violation may decide validity; it does not establish why a standalone fraud dispute without any actual or threatened violation is a suit for violation. The declaratory label supplies no missing coercive controversy. Conversely, the textual path must not prohibit validity adjudication altogether: such an overbroad rule would frustrate the court's duty to decide whether an enforceable contract was violated. All eight commitments therefore adopt the narrow statutory distinction above, not a categorical anti-declaratory rule.

No separate writing is needed for any recorded disagreement. This is a provisional judgment coalition, not an assignment decision.

## Montana v. Crow Tribe of Indians — No. 96-1829

### Components and lawful paths

The established invalidity of the particular Montana coal taxes is not reopened. The present question is the Tribe's entitlement to money paid by Westmoreland before registry deposits began. The Ninth Circuit's direction to disgorge the entire approximately $58.2 million does not follow automatically from tax invalidity. The payment periods, Interior's refusal to approve the earlier ceded-strip tribal tax, the 1982 agreement releasing Westmoreland from the earlier tribal obligation, the actual taxpayer's failure to preserve its refund route, and the distribution of the registry deposits are independent equitable circumstances. No finding establishes that the entire preregistry amount was money belonging to the Tribe or measures its financial loss.

**Path A — reverse the mandatory whole-collection award.** Equitable restitution requires unjust enrichment at this claimant's expense on the legally relevant circumstances. It is not a punitive transfer of every dollar obtained under an invalid levy. United States v. California distinguishes a claimant's economic burden from ownership of the taxpayer's refund claim; Fulton distinguishes unconstitutional taxation from the remedial entitlement and permits lawful correction fitted to actual procedures and claimants. The strongest contrary argument is that these taxes directly impaired federally protected tribal mineral development, so the State should not retain their proceeds merely because a lessee wrote the checks. That distinguishes this from a wholly remote economic claimant, but does not establish the amount, defeat all equitable considerations, or turn preemption into an automatic assignment of Westmoreland's expired claim.

**Path B — sustain restitution to vindicate the Tribe's own protected mineral interest.** Crow II's invalidity is fixed, the United States sues as trustee, and the money was extracted from exploitation of the trust resource. Those premises support treating the Tribe's claim as its own equitable injury rather than only subrogation. The strongest objection is that whole-benefit disgorgement still requires a reason the entire benefit is unjust as against this claimant. The unapproved early tax, later agreement, lessee's separate refund position and lack of actual-loss findings prevent that conclusion as a compulsory consequence of invalidity. A claim-specific equitable remedy remains analytically possible, but the present appellate whole-collection command does not establish its predicates.

### Remedy and reservations common to the commitments

The Ninth Circuit's compulsory payment direction must be reversed. Remand restores a case-specific equitable disposition rather than ordering payment, interest, a particular refund calculation, or an actual-loss award. The current amended complaints seek restitution, not a separately established compensatory-damages claim. This Court should not declare the unverified 1993 amendment allowed, disallowed on an identified ground, or necessarily reversible. A properly available independent theory or amendment must satisfy its own procedural conditions; the present disposition creates none. Existing distribution of registry funds is undisturbed. No Justice's recorded commitment reopens the adjudicated taxes' invalidity or treats the State as entitled to impose them anew.

The commitments share rejection of the mandatory full award. The reservations above are compatible with the stated narrow opinion; no separate writing is required unless circulation proposes a categorical prohibition on every independent tribal monetary claim.

## Bousley v. United States — No. 96-8516

### Components and entering-law treatment

This is ordinary review of the denial of the first §2255 proceeding, not review of a successive-motion authorization order. Felker, Triestman and Dorsainvil therefore impose no authorization-review bar here. Their refusal to reach statutory innocence through a barred vehicle establishes no adverse merits rule. Bailey supplies the correct meaning of use but neither grants collateral relief nor establishes factual innocence. The civil finality decisions named in the packet establish no independent §2255 reopening entitlement.

Six Associates support reversal of the categorical collateral rejection and remand for the bounded inquiry below. Scalia and Thomas would affirm on the plea-intelligence claim. The components must not be flattened into agreement on every rationale.

**Remand framework supported by O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.** A plea must be voluntary and intelligent, including adequate notice of the true nature of the charge. An admission made under a materially incorrect description of an essential statutory element can fail that requirement. Bailey construes the existing substantive crime; applying that interpretation to decide whether conduct was criminal does not announce a new constitutional procedure barred by Teague. This does not mean every later favorable change makes an earlier plea unintelligent: the decisive alleged defect is misdescription of what the Government had to prove, not a disappointed prediction about punishment or litigation risk.

Failure to challenge plea intelligence on direct review defaults that claim. Cause requires an external impediment or a legal claim not reasonably available, not merely adverse circuit precedent or the expectation that an argument would lose. The statutory distinction between use and possession was available; the present record establishes no cause on the proposed novelty/futility ground. No automatic prejudice conclusion follows. The remaining innocence route concerns factual innocence under the correct statute: considering all relevant reliable evidence, it must be more likely than not that no reasonable, properly instructed juror would convict. The Government may present relevant evidence beyond the plea colloquy; the existing record's legal inadequacy alone does not establish innocence. Existing facts may be evaluated under the newly clarified substantive element; a categorical requirement to invent evidence arising after the plea would defeat the very statutory-innocence question.

Where the Government actually forgave a more serious charge as part of the plea bargain, the prisoner must also establish factual innocence of that identified charge to use this miscarriage-of-justice route. The condition requires proof of the actual bargain and greater seriousness; it does not reach every possible uncharged offense or every alternative statutory theory. Here the indictment charged use, not carrying, and no bargain to forgo carrying or any identified more-serious charge is established. The remand therefore requires proof about use, without presuming a carrying obligation or a forgone charge. A supported bargain claim, if properly raised, would need its own factual and legal determination rather than an assumption from the Code's alternatives.

Stevens supports the ordinary collateral route, the substantive application of Bailey, and remand to determine factual innocence and the actual plea's intelligence. He does not join the proposed extension of the gateway to other, uncharged offenses; his complete join can be obtained by leaving that untriggered issue open. His judgment vote does not depend on that caveat.

### Remedy, barriers and separate-writing function

The six remanding Justices would reverse the Eighth Circuit's categorical rejection and return the case for an opportunity to establish the stated innocence gateway and, if reached, the plea-intelligence claim. The lower court may use an adequate existing record, appropriate submissions or factual development legally required by the actual dispute; this is not an automatic oral or evidentiary hearing. The firearm conviction is not immediately vacated, the drug conviction is untouched, and no release, acquittal or resentencing is ordered. The Government retains legally applicable defenses and may rebut innocence with relevant evidence beyond the plea record.

Stevens's reservation concerning uncharged offenses is a genuine proposition-level limit. Scalia and Thomas require a dissent explaining contemporaneous plea intelligence and finality; their position must not be represented as holding mere possession to satisfy Bailey or as applying §2244's authorization bar. A complete opinion shared by all six on the actual case can reserve the unestablished forgone-charge issue; if the five retain the conditional rule, Stevens joins the remaining parts only.

## Stewart v. Martinez-Villareal — No. 97-300

### Separately decided components

**Ordinary review channel.** All eight Associates would reach the presented statutory question. The Ninth Circuit held that authorization was unnecessary and returned the matter for district consideration; it did not grant or deny authorization by applying §2244(b)'s substantive permission criteria. Felker, Triestman and Dorsainvil govern actual authorization decisions, not every circuit order mentioning the statute. Calderon's unanimous channel portion likewise looks to the actual judicial action. This characterization permits review of whether the circuit correctly treated this proceeding as outside the successive-application mechanism; it does not permit relabeling a true authorization refusal.

**Previously raised, premature Ford claim.** Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would affirm at the narrow scope that this particular renewed claim is not second or successive. The March 1993 application presented it; the State obtained a prematurity dismissal; the 1996 mandate expressly preserved later Ford litigation; and the claim is now presented after an execution warrant and state competency proceedings. It has never received federal merits determination. Treating the ripeness dismissal as exhausting the one opportunity for this claim would bar the merits when the only reason for earlier nonadjudication has ended. The statutory term retains its established procedural meaning and is not equivalent to counting every paper or case number.

The first three exhaustion dismissals likewise do not establish earlier merits adjudication of this claim. The holding needs no rule that every never-before-presented competency claim, every later-arising claim, or every dismissed claim escapes §2244. It concerns the actually presented and expressly preserved Ford claim. Lonchar's insistence on source-authorized dismissal supports an actual classification inquiry; it does not dispense with any applicable statutory bar. Carriger and Lindh control the identified ordinary merits/factual-presumption provisions if this is continuation of the pre-enactment application; they do not alone decide the separate successive threshold. No Arizona chapter 154 qualification or hearing predicate is supplied.

Scalia and Thomas would reverse the circuit's exemption and require the statutory successive-application process. The earlier application was finally disposed of after other claims received merits treatment. The present filing renews a claim already presented, and Congress made repeated claims in a second or successive application subject to dismissal. They do not treat the State's earlier prematurity request as misconduct or waiver of the enacted statute; a court's reservation cannot create statutory jurisdiction. They distinguish a whole first application dismissed for nonexhaustion from a resolved first merits proceeding containing one dismissed claim. Their result does not adjudicate competency or authorize execution.

### Remedy and source limits

The six affirming Associates would leave the circuit's return to the district court in place for consideration of the renewed Ford claim under otherwise applicable law. No finding establishes incompetence, invalid state competency procedures, a mandatory hearing, or entitlement to a writ or stay. Ford's substantive prohibition and its procedural requirements remain distinct from this gatekeeping question. The two dissenting Associates would reverse the exemption and return the matter for treatment under the statutory mechanism, without directing the result of any genuinely available separate proceeding.

The broader proposition that no Ford claim is ever second or successive is outside all six affirming commitments. The narrower-or-broader join barrier is therefore material. Scalia and Thomas need a substantive dissent on completed-application finality and repeated-claim text; their agreement on ordinary jurisdiction must remain visible. No Justice relies on a historical participation fact, a peer May 18 decision, or an invented execution schedule.
