## Section 8 counting and eligibility

Count each signed lead opinion after plenary merits review once per Justice delivering any part, irrespective of the assigning Justice. Recount all earlier effective events, taking same-date events alphabetically by case name, before each new assignment. Multiple parts written by the same Justice add only one count. Separate writings never count; neither do per curiam opinions, summary dispositions, procedural actions, section 10 explanations or stopped matters without a Record.

Eligibility must be determined separately from frozen reconciled commitments and Stone's actual approved position. Each eligible author must join the full proposed part, including necessary accommodations; a judgment-only, partial or fallback-only position does not qualify. Of the eligible Justices, the Chief's pool contains those no more than one above that eligible set's lowest count. The values below are available counts, not a declaration that any Justice is eligible for an unidentified chunk 6 opinion. Associate assignments are not constrained by the Chief's pool rule, but their resulting plenary merits leads count.
