## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Candidate selection for a public-broadcast debate

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the forum classification and the record-specific exclusion. He does not classify every public-broadcast debate categorically.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** A selectively composed public-television debate is a nonpublic forum when the broadcaster retains editorial selection and has not opened the program to every qualified candidate. Exclusion must be reasonable in light of the journalistic purpose and viewpoint neutral.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A public broadcaster’s selectively composed candidate debate is a nonpublic forum when the broadcaster retains editorial selection and has not opened the program to every qualified candidate. A viability-based exclusion is permissible when reasonable in light of the program’s journalistic purpose and not a pretext for viewpoint discrimination.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The Commission used public support and newsworthiness criteria and the record does not establish political pressure or disagreement with Forbes’s views as the cause. Existing forum doctrine resolves the claim; no legal source supports an additional constitutional requirement that future criteria be published.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not authorize viewpoint exclusion, incumbent favoritism, sham or selectively administered criteria, or exclusion from a debate genuinely opened to all qualifying candidates. It does not make publication of future criteria a constitutional prerequisite.
