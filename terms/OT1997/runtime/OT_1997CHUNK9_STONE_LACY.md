## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Staleness and particularity for durable digital evidence

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches probable cause and facial particularity, not an unpreserved execution challenge.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Staleness depends on the nature of the item and storage practice, not elapsed time alone. Actual downloads of durable digital files, together with supported evidence of retention, can sustain probable cause months later. A warrant may authorize searching identified computers and media for offense-related image and transaction categories, but not a general inventory of unrelated life data.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A ten-month interval does not defeat probable cause to search for downloaded child-pornography files when the affidavit establishes actual acquisition, durable digital storage, and a supported likelihood of retention, and the warrant confines the search to identified devices and offense-related categories.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Unlike consumable or fleeting evidence, digital files and disks can persist unchanged. The affidavit did not rely on mere subscription or curiosity; it documented completed downloads. The warrant tied the devices and categories to the offense sufficiently for this technology and record.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The Court reserves warrants lacking offense or category limits, indiscriminate examination of unrelated data, cloud or account searches not described, and affidavits relying only on generalized collector profiles without acquisition evidence.
