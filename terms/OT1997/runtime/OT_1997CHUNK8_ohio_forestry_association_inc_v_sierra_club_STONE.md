## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Ripeness of a programmatic forest-plan challenge

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone resolves ripeness only. He does not decide standing, NFMA compliance, the newly asserted immediate-harm theories, or any distinct completed procedural claim.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** A substantive challenge to a programmatic forest plan is unripe when the plan authorizes no immediate logging, imposes no present legal obligation or hardship, and preserves review of later site-specific decisions. Later proceedings may change or eliminate the anticipated effects and will produce a concrete record.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A substantive challenge to a programmatic forest plan is unripe when the plan authorizes no immediate logging, imposes no present legal hardship, and preserves review of later site-specific authorizations.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The plan identifies potential levels and methods but does not itself approve tree cutting. Any project requires another decision, analysis, public process, and review. Adjudication now would address forecasts rather than a concrete operation.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not bar review of an approved logging project, an immediately binding plan provision, or a properly preserved completed procedural violation. It decides no NFMA merits question or standing issue.
