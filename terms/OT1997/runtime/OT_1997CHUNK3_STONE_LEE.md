## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Standing and ripeness for assisted-suicide challenge

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone resolves Article III standing and ripeness and does not reach any assisted-death merits question.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** The patient claims depend on future eligibility, a later request, participation by unidentified physicians, failures of several statutory safeguards, and eventual ingestion contrary to the patient’s true wishes. The providers and facilities identify no present request, concrete statutory duty, current compliance cost, or credible enforcement threat.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Plaintiffs lack standing and their claims are unripe where injury depends on an unformed chain of future eligibility, requests, independent medical decisions, safeguard failures, and unidentified participants, with no present compliance burden or credible enforcement threat.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Unlike a law that directly stops existing conduct, Oregon’s statute had not been applied to any identified patient, provider, or facility in a way creating a present legal consequence. The contingent chain prevents the court from identifying injury, causation, or a ripe dispute.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not validate Oregon’s statute or foreclose a challenge by an eligible patient facing imminent application, a provider confronting a concrete duty and credible enforcement, or a facility with an identified participation conflict.
