## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Source position under the corrected governing law

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the granted and preserved issues identified in Section I.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone would preserve the supplied remand on the individual claims because the plaintiff-favorable facts describe affirmative custody and transfer, rather than a generalized right to police protection. The officers allegedly displaced the existing means of escape after receiving specific warnings and exposed Black to the identified violent driver. The lawfulness of that affirmative action and the officials’ conduct-time notice require their own analysis; Pinder cannot be restated as a general state-created-danger holding. On Stemler’s claim, intentional selection of her for arrest while disregarding the allegedly intoxicated Kritis can support equal protection if the evidence establishes discriminatory causation. No intermediate scrutiny is needed to reject naked animus. The municipal and official-capacity judgments stand absent evidence satisfying the separate entity-liability requirements. The remand does not revive every false-arrest or malicious-prosecution theory, and does not declare the disputed coercion, knowledge, or motive proved.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The sourced preferred disposition remains recorded above, but any unsupported claim that current law already compels it is withdrawn.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Pinder reserves broad affirmative-danger merits and gives immunity for its earlier conduct-time circumstances; it is not a blanket prohibition on claims based on an officer’s own seizure and transfer of a person into danger. Black’s alleged forced removal and placement with Kritis must be distinguished from a mere failure to protect her. The available time for assessment and warnings also distinguish roadside custody from a split-second pursuit. No uncompleted County of Sacramento proposal governs. Stemler’s separate equal-protection claim requires intentional selective treatment and a valid comparator; the military rational-basis cases do not establish a heightened civilian orientation tier.
