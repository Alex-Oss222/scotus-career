## Rice v. Paladin Enterprises — May 14, 1998

**Shared disposition:** Affirm the reversal of defendants' summary judgment and remand for the reserved proceedings. On the motion-only stipulations, the First Amendment supplies no absolute immunity from civil liability for purposeful, concrete assistance actually used to commit the murders. All tort elements, actual intent/assistance/causation outside the motion's assumed facts, defenses and damages remain for ordinary proceedings. No instruction manual is banned and no final liability is entered.

**Shared operative scope:** Intentional participation through operational expression in independently unlawful conduct is distinct from advocacy of lawlessness. The necessary safeguards here are the publisher's admitted purpose to assist murder for hire, the instructions' concrete assisting role, and stipulated assistance to these completed crimes, together with the applicable tort's substantive and causal requirements. Mere publication, offensiveness, abstract advocacy, knowledge of possible misuse, foreseeability or someone else's imitation does not establish that combination. The absence of knowledge of Perry's specific plot does not create an absolute defense when purposeful assistance to criminal readers and actual assistance to these crimes are stipulated. The Court does not decide the constitutional sufficiency of lesser mental states, impose a general immediacy exception to Brandenburg, or adopt the lower opinion's broad claims about a publication having no legitimate value.

## United States v. Thompson — May 21, 1998

Marriage and physical initiation alone do not establish the asserted protected premise. The constitutionality of prohibiting genuinely consensual private marital conduct is reserved. No general test asks whether a court considers an intimate act supportive of marriage.

**Procedure holding all eight can join:** Judicial entry to answer members during closed deliberations without accused or counsel violates Article 39(b). Under Article 59(a) and Allbee's rebuttable presumption of prejudice against the Government, the reported complete verbatim record rebuts prejudice: findings were already reached, questions concerned recording lesser included offenses, and no substantive instruction or objectionable answer occurred. The failure to identify an objection or desired clarification confirms the positive record; it does not shift the Government's burden. No rule makes all contacts harmless, all recorded errors nonconstitutional, or lack of a defense objection alone sufficient.

## Gavin v. Branstad — May 21, 1998

**Common judgment:** Affirm the Eighth Circuit's reversal of the district court's constitutional ruling and its remand for actual statutory adjudication. Apply the November 26, 1997 current-and-ongoing amendment to the pending proceeding. Decline immediate termination by this Court: the district court never determined the preservation condition. No decree-wide continuation entitlement is established either.

**Common sufficient rule:** Under Rouse and Benjamin, Congress may change the conditions for continuing prospective relief while the court independently determines factual eligibility, federal violations and remedial scope. Section 3626(b)(2) supplies a statutory route separate from Rule 60(b)(5); the movant must establish the absence of substantive original findings, without requiring ritual wording. Section (b)(3) preserves only relief supported by written record-based findings of a current **and** ongoing federal violation, necessity, no excessive scope, narrowness and least intrusiveness. No substantive federal right is legislatively reduced. The court must determine the missing statutory application before ending or preserving the disputed future obligations.

**Constitutional and remedial limits:** No perpetual vested right to unchanged prospective supervision; no retrospective erasure of completed effects or accrued claims. The actual remedial classification rationally relates to institutional responsibility and resources while retaining meaningful federal-right remedies; nonsuspect status alone does not suffice. On the assumed-contract premise, the federal nonparty rationality rule controls without finding an independent contract or cancelling accrued liability. A supported violation excluded solely by the amended conjunction remains reserved. Benjamin's record-specific proof rule supports a proper opportunity under ordinary procedure, not compulsory reopening or a hearing in every termination case. Library relief requires the actual access injury and remedial fit Lewis identifies, with no present injury finding in either direction.
