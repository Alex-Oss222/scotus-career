# Hinds — assembly form and governing propositions

The legal form is one signed lead opinion following full merits consideration of the authorized lower-court addition. It construes the enacted property-use condition, applies that condition to the appellate reasoning, and orders bounded review of the existing trial record. These issues belong in one opinion; no substantive division requires separate lead authors. A per curiam summary disposition would not adequately express the new statutory construction and its remedial limits.

All eight Associates support the complete statutory construction and existing-record remand. Stone's different active-commercial-use formulation, immediate acquittal remedy, and Agostini-based explanation require a separate writing concurring in the shared statutory propositions and dissenting from the remand judgment. The shared propositions are the independent work of the property-use element, the inadequacy of the owner's economic relationships merely by themselves, and the absence of a religious exemption. Stone does not join a broader construction, a new proof rule, or conditional substitution for his approved immediate acquittal.

The governing propositions and form follow the legal commitments, without using the authorship count to add, divide or combine a proposition. No author is selected in this receipt.
