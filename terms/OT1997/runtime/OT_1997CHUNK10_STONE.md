### Issue 1: Capital procedure — lesser included offenses

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Does Beck require a capital jury to receive instructions on offenses that state law does not treat as lesser included offenses of the charged felony murder?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Beck v. Alabama and due process in capital trials; state offense elements identify lesser inclusion, subject to independent constitutional review of an actual arbitrary exclusion.

Gaudin reinforces respect for actual offense elements; an additional mental-state element can defeat lesser inclusion even if the alternative crime is less severely punished.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: ESA application to the intrastate taking of the Delhi Sands fly

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The constitutional question is properly presented because the statutory “harm” construction does not itself establish Congress’s power to apply the prohibition to every intrastate taking. The existing record must be tested under the post-*Lopez* framework.
- **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether intrastate habitat modification that kills or injures a species found only within one State — conduct that is noneconomic as the take prohibition defines its class, without any commercial element — may be federally regulated when the conduct is not itself an interstate transaction and, if so, what interstate mechanism and evidentiary showing are required.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Clarify and develop *Lopez*. Noneconomic character is relevant but not categorically dispositive. *Hale* already establishes one route: inclusion genuinely necessary to prevent evasion or material impairment of a valid interstate regime. *Home Builders* adds a second route only because this case squarely presents it: a materially demonstrated substantial interstate externality that individual States cannot reasonably internalize. Stone rejects both categorical answers. He does not hold that an intrastate species is automatically outside federal power, but he refuses to sustain the law merely because biodiversity, tourism, science, or development has national economic importance. The lower courts must identify and support the actual interstate mechanism before the application can lawfully be upheld or invalidated.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Congress may reach noneconomic intrastate conduct when its inclusion is reasonably necessary to prevent evasion or material impairment of a valid federal regulation of interstate commerce, or when Congress reasonably identifies on a materially adequate evidentiary basis a substantial interstate externality generated by the regulated class that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize. General national interest, scientific value, tourism, development, biodiversity, possible future economic value, or the fact that similar problems occur in many States does not itself establish either mechanism.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** *Lopez* requires connection before magnitude and deliberately left materially different interstate mechanisms for later adjudication. *Hale* operationalized anti-evasion for an actual product-market regime. This case raises a different claim: local conduct may impose effects beyond the regulating State that cannot be practically controlled by the affected States because the ecological or economic burden crosses jurisdictional lines or operates through an inherently interstate system.

  Stone recognizes that such an externality can be a genuine “among the several States” mechanism without treating every nationally important local problem as interstate commerce.

  The externality route must therefore be bounded. The Government must identify:
  1. the regulated class;
  2. the concrete cross-border ecological, commercial, or comparable interstate effect;
  3. why that effect is substantial rather than merely speculative or nationally interesting;
  4. why the affected States cannot reasonably internalize or control the mechanism through ordinary territorial authority; and
  5. a reasonable fit between the federal prohibition and the demonstrated spillover.

  No fixed number of States, incidents, dollars, species, market percentage, or scientific threshold is constitutionally required. Congress receives substantial respect concerning predictive and empirical judgments once it identifies the mechanism, but courts may not invent the missing interstate effect.

  The existing judgments do not adequately determine whether destruction of the Delhi Sands flower-loving fly produces such an interstate externality, whether exclusion of the challenged conduct would materially impair a valid interstate species-market or ecosystem regime, or whether the record materially supports either theory. National biodiversity importance, tourism, research, development pressure, and possible future commercial value cannot substitute for those findings.

  Remand is therefore required rather than either categorical invalidation or judicial invention of the missing facts.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Stone does not hold ESA § 9(a)(1)(B) constitutional or unconstitutional in every intrastate application; does not make every ecosystem national; does not make every species a commodity; does not equate occurrence in multiple States with an interstate externality; does not require formal legislative findings in every case; and does not disturb applications involving actual interstate sale, transport, trafficking, migratory movement, or another independently sufficient Commerce mechanism.

### Issue 2: Evidentiary responsibility for a novel interstate mechanism

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Necessary because the externality theory cannot operate as a legal label without factual support.
- **Question Stone Resolves:** Who must identify and support the interstate mechanism, and what role does legislative judgment play.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Clarify *Lopez*’s division of institutional responsibility.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Courts independently determine whether the asserted mechanism is constitutionally eligible. Congress receives substantial latitude on predictive and empirical questions once an eligible mechanism is identified, but the Government must point to a materially adequate legislative or adjudicative basis supporting the mechanism on which the challenged application depends. Courts may not invent a market, externality, causal pathway, or anti-evasion necessity absent from the relevant record.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The rule prevents both extremes: judges do not redo environmental science from scratch, and congressional invocation of interstate commerce does not make the constitutional predicate self-proving. Where the lower courts have not resolved the material factual mechanism under the correct legal standard, remand is the proper remedy.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No universal formal-findings requirement, evidentiary burden of “clear and convincing” proof, numerical threshold, or requirement that Congress make defendant-specific findings is adopted.
