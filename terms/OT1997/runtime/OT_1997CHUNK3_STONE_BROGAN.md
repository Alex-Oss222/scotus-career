## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Exculpatory-no exception to § 1001

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches statutory coverage. No constitutional avoidance construction is available because the privilege protects refusal to answer, not a right to lie.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the enacted text and reject the judge-made exception. A knowingly false material denial is a statement within § 1001. Concerns about investigator tactics, trivial denials, and prosecutorial leverage belong to materiality, mens rea, charging discretion, and Congress rather than an exception the statute does not contain.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Section 1001 applies to a knowingly false material “no” given to federal investigators; the statute contains no exculpatory-no exception.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The word “any” reaches negative as well as affirmative statements, and the proposed exception lacks a stable textual boundary. The privilege allowed Brogan to remain silent. Creating a right to answer falsely would transfer the privilege’s function into a rule Congress did not enact.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The Court does not decide coercive interrogation, literal truth, immaterial statements, ambiguous questions, or abuse of charging discretion.
