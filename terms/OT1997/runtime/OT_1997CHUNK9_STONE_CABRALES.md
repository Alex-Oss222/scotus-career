## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Criminal procedure — venue for money laundering

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** May money-laundering transactions performed entirely in Florida be prosecuted in Missouri solely because the criminal proceeds originated there?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Article III, Sixth Amendment venue protection, Rule 18, and 18 U.S.C. §§1956 and 1957 as charged; identify essential conduct rather than every circumstance element.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** AFFIRM dismissal of the challenged Missouri laundering counts for improper venue.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone would distinguish the factual predicate that money is criminally derived from the act of laundering it. Missouri’s drug activity matters to proving the money’s character, but the charges described financial conduct in Florida. Permitting prosecution wherever some prior offense occurred would detach venue from the defendant’s charged activity. A properly charged conspiracy, aiding-and-abetting theory, or transportation offense may present a different geographic question; none can be assumed to rescue these counts.
* **Treatment of Controlling Precedent:** Gaudin requires respect for enacted elements, but an element describing the origin of money does not necessarily identify where the defendant committed the offense. Venue must be tied to the charged conduct; association with criminal proceeds cannot relocate a transaction across States.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The exact indictment controls; do not extend the result automatically to conspiracy or continuing transportation offenses.
