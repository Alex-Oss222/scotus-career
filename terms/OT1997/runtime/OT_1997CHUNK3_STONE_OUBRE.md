## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Mandatory statutory waiver requirements

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches enforceability of the waiver. The ADEA merits and monetary accounting are not before the Court.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the mandatory statutory conditions. A release that does not satisfy § 626(f) is not a knowing and voluntary ADEA waiver, and retention of consideration cannot supply compliance after the fact. Congress allocated the consequence of deficiency to the employer drafting the release.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** An ADEA release that fails the OWBPA’s mandatory requirements is unenforceable as an ADEA waiver and is not cured by ratification or the employee’s failure to tender back severance payments.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The statute states the minimum conditions for a valid waiver. Treating common-law ratification as a substitute would make those conditions optional whenever an employee spent the money before discovering the defect. Any equitable adjustment can be considered separately without blocking the federal claim.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not invalidate waivers complying with § 626(f), decide fraud or contract claims, or forbid a lawful setoff after liability is established.
