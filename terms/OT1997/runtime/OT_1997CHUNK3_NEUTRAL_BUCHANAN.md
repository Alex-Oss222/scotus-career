The jury returned a verdict of death, the trial court imposed that sentence, and the Virginia Supreme Court affirmed. The Federal District Court then denied Buchanan habeas corpus relief, and the Fourth Circuit affirmed. * **Record Application and Open Point:** Revised Graham and Johnson v. Texas require an effective route for the jury to give material mitigation its independent culpability significance; admission of evidence and partial relevance to dangerousness alone are insufficient. Delo allows a supporting-evidence requirement for a particular mitigating instruction, and Tuilaepa rejects mandatory numerical weights or labels. The question here is whether the whole charge already supplies the effective route that was absent in Graham and Johnson.
