## P1 — Governing previability standard: five Associates

Apply Casey's complete six-step rule, as reaffirmed in Fargo; neither numerical large fraction nor Salerno's no-valid-applications formula operates as an independent threshold. The operative sequence remains:

1. Identify the actual enacted command, authoritative construction, exceptions, sanctions and medical circumstances. A prohibition of protected previability choice, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care independently fails. Patients need not prove medical necessity for protected choice; clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable treatment, not official approval of reasons. Care cannot await imminent death or catastrophic deterioration.
2. Identify actual function and objectively supported purpose. Safety, informed consent, truthful information, proper administration and the other functions recognized in Casey may be permissible. Preference for childbirth and truthful persuasion differ from obstruction. Moral disapproval does not justify blocking access, and objectively established obstructive purpose independently invalidates the duty; a legislator's opposition alone does not prove it.
3. Challengers establish concrete, nontrivial added interference attributable to the duty or identified package, against the system without it while holding other conditions constant. Define a stable affected population by the duty's operation, with supported subgroups; do not average over all pregnancies or select only successful claims. Cumulative effects cannot be double counted. Expense, delay, provider capacity, clinical risk, coercion and disclosure count through a demonstrated causal connection. Exceptions and alternatives must be usable. Reliable evidence and reasonable prospective predictions may suffice without waiting for lost access; no universal numerical threshold applies.
4. Baseline violation, obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle independently requires invalidity; no interest rescues an established obstacle, which need not make care absolutely impossible. Remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Remaining meaningful interference requires substantiated permissible justification and fit. When challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it; inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequences may suffice. No new study, scientific certainty, numerical benefit calculation, disproof of every hypothetical or cheapest-policy command is imposed. Below meaningful interference, this heightened access inquiry ends while independent protections and lawful-regulation rules remain. Challengers bear duty, population, causation and trigger; the State bears the applicable justification, tailoring or fit burden. Ordinary factual proof and statutory-defense burdens remain distinct.
5. Protect competent individualized clinical judgment and truthful, nonmisleading material communication. Evaluate professional evidence, circumstances and substantial contrary evidence; neither a professional association, bare clinical preference nor unsupported official assertion is conclusive. Necessary care cannot depend on an unnecessary committee veto or documentation functioning as permission. Actual culpability must be respected; reasonable later disagreement alone does not prove every element, and fraud, malpractice and misconduct remain regulable. Accurate content does not itself validate burdensome delivery or certification. Distinguish confidential oversight from public disclosure and enforcement of invalid conditions.
6. Identify the proved violation and matching relief under applicable severability law, addressing dependent certifications, reports and sanctions. ## Rule, sufficient explanation, and remedy

All eight Associates join: once the federal rescission right expires under §1635(f), a borrower cannot revive it as a defense in a later foreclosure. Ordinarily it expires three years after consummation or on earlier sale, even without the required disclosure. The enactment addresses the life of the right, not merely the time for an affirmative action. Reiter and Bull preserve same-transaction recoupment against the latter kind of limitation, up to the plaintiff's asserted liability; they do not preserve a right that Congress has terminated. Section 1640(e), by contrast, expressly preserves defensive damages by recoupment or setoff in a collection action beyond its one-year action period, except as otherwise provided by State law. Giving these distinct clauses distinct effects does not repeal ordinary recoupment or erase consumer protection. Limiting the period for unwinding a secured transaction can protect title while leaving the separately authorized damages defense available on its own terms.

The agency extension remains part of the operative rule. An agency empowered to enforce the subchapter must institute a §1635 enforcement proceeding within three years of consummation, find a violation, and the obligor's rescission right must rest wholly or partly on a matter in that proceeding. The right then expires at the later of (a) three years after consummation or the earlier property sale and (b) one year following conclusion of the proceeding, any judicial review, or the period for judicial review. This is not an extension for every regulatory inquiry or private lawsuit. No qualifying proceeding is identified here.

The 1986 refinancing, cessation of payments in 1991 and foreclosure in 1992 establish that the federal rescission right had expired in 1989 on this record. Affirm the Florida Supreme Court. The $396 actual-damages and $1,000 statutory-damages offsets remain untouched. No new award, evidentiary hearing or injunction is ordered. ## Proposition-level scope and controlling explanations

**P1 — Six Associates.** A white defendant may assert excluded Black citizens' equal-protection rights against purposeful racial discrimination in selecting a voting member of the grand jury indicting him. Powers supplies three requirements: concrete injury to the accused, a sufficiently close relationship with excluded jurors, and obstacles to their asserting their own rights. Discriminatory composition undermines the integrity of the accusatory body and ensuing process; selecting only one member and the absence of a publicly visible series of strikes do not eliminate that injury. The defendant and eligible jurors share an interest in lawful selection, and the defendant has a direct incentive to litigate. The practical costs and limited individual recovery hinder jurors' separate enforcement. Evidence of prior exclusions supports the alleged discrimination in this defendant's selection; it does not change the claim into an unconnected lawsuit for earlier venires. Powers, reinforced within its respective scope by McCollum and J.E.B., supplies individual equality, not racial proportionality or an entitlement to group loyalties. Purposeful exclusion remains to be proved.

**P2 — Eight Associates.** Campbell has standing to litigate whether the selection of a member of his indicting grand jury violated his own due-process rights. ## Reconciled division and complete proposed rules

**Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg, and Breyer — vacate and remand.** Probable cause and state authorization to enforce a fine-only civil licensing ordinance do not automatically establish that taking an identified operator to a station and confining him is reasonable. The additional restraint must reasonably serve the actual enforcement function; a uniform municipal paperwork policy cannot alone supply the constitutional justification for its own restraint. The reviewing court must assess the actual continuing violation, identification, processing, duration, release, and enforcement relationship under that rule.

These four select vacatur because the lower court's categorical two-predicate premise and its consequent treatment of this custody as ordinary foreclosed the required assessment of the added restraint under the current bounded rule. They acknowledge that the lower opinion also discussed continuing operation, potential daily fines, one-hour processing, and recognizance release. Those facts must be evaluated, not ignored or treated as newly missing. Remand corrects the governing inquiry; it does not require a new hearing simply because more facts might exist.

Their rule permits custody reasonably connected to securing identification or appearance, ending an actual continuing violation, protecting persons or evidence, or necessary processing in the circumstances. These examples are nonexclusive and are not findings that any particular one occurred. The rule imposes neither a least-restrictive-means burden nor a duty to issue a citation whenever one is conceivable. Fine-only status is relevant to the reasonable extent of restraint, not categorical immunity from arrest. No present damages judgment or finding that every fine-only custody is unlawful follows.

**Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas — affirm.** On the actual record, probable cause of an admitted continuing violation, accepted arrest authority, and a short period devoted to preparing enforcement documents and recognizance release establish an adequate objective connection between custody and enforcement. The Fourth Amendment does not require proof that an on-site citation was impossible before this ordinary processing. The civil and fine-only character does not, by itself, make that limited custody unreasonable.

These four reject the lower court's broad suggestion that local authority and probable cause necessarily establish every arrest's constitutionality. Their affirmance rests on the additional supported connection and limited duration, not that formula. They preserve review of atypical force, unjustified duration, unrelated later detention, and a method lacking reasonable relation to enforcement; none of the hypothetical shackling, strip searches, deadly force, or home entries occurred here.

## Exact proposed accommodation and its limits

The operative accommodation is the combination of these draft sentences:

> The judgment of the Court of Appeals is reversed insofar as it sustains summary judgment on the categorical premise that probable cause and state or local arrest authority alone establish the reasonableness of station-house custody for this fine-only licensing violation.

> The Court does not decide whether this custody satisfies that requirement on the supported record, award damages, resolve individual immunity, or reopen the settled search and abandoned probable-cause claims.

The second sentence is essential to the four joins, as are the remand's actual-fact directions and rejection of a mandatory citation or least-restrictive-means rule. It reverses no independent search or probable-cause judgment and grants Ricci no present damages entitlement.

For implementation clarity, the eventual mandate should expressly state that **the adverse summary judgment on the preserved custody-reasonableness claim is set aside**. That makes the existing proposal's intended consequence unambiguous without changing its substance. Reversal of an abstract reason while leaving the same adverse custody judgment operative would not satisfy the four commitments. Conversely, directing judgment that this custody was unreasonable would exceed them.

The lower opinion did discuss continuing operation, potential accumulated fines, the processing purpose, and one-hour duration. The draft appropriately preserves those facts and does not describe the lower court as wholly ignoring context. The four find the legal framework insufficient despite that discussion; on remand those circumstances must receive assessment under the added-restraint rule.
