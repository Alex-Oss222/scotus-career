## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Federal taxation — insurance reserve transition

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Is Treasury’s treatment of reserve strengthening in the transition to discounted insurance-loss reserves consistent with the Tax Reform Act of 1986?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Tax Reform Act of 1986 transition provisions and the applicable Treasury regulation; interpret the actual statutory language before assessing administrative discretion.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** AFFIRM the regulation’s application on the reconstructed statutory premise.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone would first ask whether Congress fixed the narrow actuarial meaning the insurer proposes. If the transitional language leaves the treatment of reserve increases open, Treasury may adopt an administrable rule tied to preventing an artificial enlargement of the transition deduction. That explanation is stronger than a bare invocation of revenue protection, which could justify almost any tax increase. The conclusion remains confined to the transition provision and the actual regulated reserves; the agency must identify textual room for its interpretation.
* **Treatment of Controlling Precedent:** Hill requires keeping distinct tax accounts and statutory purposes separate; a commercial accounting label is not conclusive of the federal tax category. Sweet Home recognizes interpretive discretion within statutory bounds, not a power to alter clear language. The transition rule must be assessed against the specific windfall it addresses.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No free-standing judicial power to deny deductions because they appear generous.
