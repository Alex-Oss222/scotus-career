### Issue 1: Administrative review — appeal from Social Security remand

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** May a benefits claimant appeal a sentence-four remand when she sought an immediate award and received only further agency proceedings?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** 28 U.S.C. §1291 and sentence four of 42 U.S.C. §405(g); finality and aggrievement are separate questions.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** REVERSE and REMAND for the court of appeals to consider the claimant’s challenge.

That supplies finality. A claimant can prevail enough for some purposes while still being adversely affected by denial of the larger requested relief.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Sentence-six remands and cases granting all requested relief present different finality or aggrievement questions.

- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Then-§ 230(d)(3) governs actions brought and liabilities imposed after its effective date, including claims based on earlier provider conduct.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Landgraf step one controls because Congress directly addressed when inconsistent causes could be brought and liabilities imposed. Zeran filed after the effective date and sought a later judgment, so applying § 230 follows the enacted temporal command rather than attaching an inferred new consequence.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** AOL’s recordkeeping practices remain unresolved, as do claims outside the statutory definitions and exceptions.
