## Current-law and alternative-path check

Lamb's Chapel directly protects a religious perspective on an admitted subject even assuming a nonpublic forum. Rosenberger protects viewpoint neutrality within its actual program. These grounds support all eight Associates' instruction commitments: allowing religious discussion does not cure exclusion of religious instruction when comparable secular ethical and character instruction is allowed. The holding is limited to otherwise admitted subjects and does not compel an unrestricted curriculum or unlimited public forum.

The worship fork remains genuinely open. Lamb's Chapel, Good News/Good Sports and Church on the Rock reserved standalone worship; their instruction or discussion results do not themselves decide this service. The five access commitments classify the private Sunday service within the actual broad community and instructional uses and reject removal solely for its devotional form. Their strongest contrary consideration is the government's ability to create a limited forum; their answer is that a nominal activity boundary cannot merely subtract religious expression from otherwise comparable admitted activities. The three contrary commitments accept a narrower distinction between instruction and the stipulated integrated church service, including communion. Their strongest contrary consideration is the absence of an exact secular sacramental analogue; their answer rests on the service as a distinct activity, not on religious perspective alone. The record supports that disagreement without forcing it into one rationale.

All eight reject a categorical claim that the Establishment Clause requires exclusion. Agostini's modified Lemon inquiry governs: governmental organization of worship is prohibited, established permissible private access remains distinct, and actual unresolved attribution is assessed by function and context. The school did not select a devotional speaker, arrange an official pupil program or compel attendance on the supplied record. Location and religious identity alone are insufficient. Chabad's contextual holding and Pinette's bounded access result support that distinction without universal forum immunity, a mandatory sign, assumed successful disclaimer, public devotional financing or an entitlement to permanent facilities. Tanford and Chaudhuri concern officially organized prayer and preserve private forum expression. Perry supplies no traditional-public-forum status for this school and no automatic importation of a different tailoring standard.
