## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the invalidation of the nonprofit-affiliation distinction.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** The city may adopt viewpoint-neutral, speaker-neutral rules addressing congestion, traffic flow, fraud, or genuinely commercial vending, but may not condition protected solicitation or message-bearing sales on nonprofit status.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Speaker- and content-based exemptions in public-forum vending

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the tailoring defect. He does not separately decide alternative channels because the unsupported nonprofit distinction is sufficient.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** The Boardwalk rules may distinguish purely commercial vending from protected expression, but nonprofit affiliation bears no demonstrated relationship to pedestrian congestion, traffic flow, or unfair competition. Government may not make organizational form the condition for otherwise comparable expressive solicitation or sales.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Los Angeles may distinguish purely commercial vending from protected expression, but it may not condition expressive solicitation or sales on nonprofit affiliation when that status bears no demonstrated relationship to congestion, traffic flow, or unfair competition.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The speakers’ sales and donation requests were integrated with protected expression, while similarly situated nonprofit organizations were permitted to engage in the same physical conduct. The record supplies no neutral forum-management reason for that organizational distinction.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not invalidate objective rules governing space, size, safety, licensing, fraud, or purely commercial commodities, and it does not require the city to permit every sale in every location.
