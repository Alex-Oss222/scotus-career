# OT1997 chunk 8 — frozen independent Bronx commitments

See `OT_1997CHUNK8_PREFLIGHT_A_BRONX.md` for the validated source, two-path analysis and current-law qualifications. No current-case comparator, private position or assignment material was read. All eight Associate Justices participate on the supplied record.

## Instruction component

All eight Associate Justices would reverse the judgment insofar as it sustains excluding religious instruction on subjects admitted for comparable secular instruction, and remand for nondiscriminatory consideration and appropriate relief. The rule is viewpoint neutrality within actual forum limits, not an entitlement to teach every religious subject in every school. Permitting discussion does not cure banning religious instruction where secular ethical/character instruction is allowed. No separate RFRA holding or unrequested new claim is necessary.

**Proposition-level compatibility:** All eight share the instruction rule and rejection of a categorical establishment necessity. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer share the applied worship-access proposition at the following scope: in this actual broad after-hours community/educational forum, private Sunday religious expression is not disqualified solely by being conducted as worship; the school remains free to enforce otherwise lawful neutral operational and actual activity limits. They support remand for nondiscriminatory treatment, not a Supreme Court-issued permanent permit. Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg oppose that worship disposition and its characterization of this service as a comparable admitted activity. Their acceptance of private attribution does not make them join the access rule or judgment.

**Separate-position function if published:** The partial dissent explains why an integrated, stipulated religious service is a distinct permissible activity exclusion even while religious instruction on admitted subjects cannot be excluded. It does not claim that the Establishment Clause requires the ban. A majority opinion preserving actual context and neutral management needs no invented separate writing merely because its joiners reason differently about religion generally.

**Remedy boundary:** Reverse instruction; the five named Associates also reverse worship. Remand supplies appropriate declaratory/injunctive implementation under the rules actually held. No damages award, fixed disclaimer text, monopoly rental, admission-fee finding, mandatory new evidentiary hearing, new school policy or automatic Court return. Matters outside the reviewed questions remain outside the judgment's new substantive holdings.
