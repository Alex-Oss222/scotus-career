## Governing propositions and scope

1. Section 844(i) requires the damaged property to be used in interstate or foreign commerce, or in an activity affecting such commerce. The property-use requirement is distinct from whether the owner's affairs have economic consequences. Hinds stipulated the other offense elements but disputed this one. The prosecution therefore retains the burden of proving this element beyond a reasonable doubt.
2. Russell v. United States, 471 U.S. 858 (1985), sustains the rental building because renting it put the building itself into the rental market. Stevens's opinion states both Congress's broad intended coverage and the remaining use limitation. Its enactment account includes deletion of the proposed business-purpose limitation after discussion of churches and other nonbusiness buildings. Russell does not establish either categorical church coverage or a categorical commercial-owner prerequisite. Its holding does not resolve the present church's actual function.
3. The current **Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects** standard, presently operative through Mussari/Schroeder (June 30, 1997), retains Lopez's connection-before-magnitude architecture. American Life League, Hale, Rybar, Kirk, Parker and Robinson apply identifiable mechanisms with distinct predicates. A statutory commerce element is relevant but neither self-defining nor automatically constitutional. Robinson (March 23, 1998), joined by every Associate, requires the actual operating-business relationship before treating business-asset depletion as connected economic conduct. It leaves generic consumer-spending chains outside its ground.
4. The current **Lemon test**, modified by Agostini (June 23, 1997), supplies one inquiry into governmental religious authority and function. The former three independent prongs are unavailable. Everson's ordinary police/fire-protection relationship is materially different from Tanford's officially organized worship. The religious identity of a protected property owner does not itself make general arson protection an establishment.
5. Burks and the current Jacobson remedy distinguish evidentiary insufficiency from an erroneous legal standard. A finding that the trial proof is legally insufficient requires acquittal and bars another trial to repair that proof. A legal-standard vacatur is not itself such a finding, and it does not automatically authorize a retrial either. Bailey/Robinson's statutory separation and bounded remand, joined by all eight Associates, illustrate that correcting the meaning of one statutory condition does not adjudicate every alternative or remedial predicate.

## Supported alternatives and conclusion

### A. Affirm on the church's recurrent economic operations

The strongest affirmance argument is that this is an operating institution, not simply an idle building. It pays employees, buys an employee's out-of-state insurance, buys utilities and religious materials, and annually sends funds through a national denomination. The words any activity affecting commerce are broad; Russell specifically recounts removal of a proposed business-purpose limitation. The government need not prove that this one arson substantially affected the national economy. The religious use of premises does not immunize their genuine participation in interstate transactions.

The difficulty is that the panel treated the institution's economic relationships as sufficient without identifying the function the building itself performed in those relationships. Its statement of the statute omitted the connection between property use and an activity affecting commerce. Electricity consumption, procurement, an employee's insurance and an institution's payments can establish economic relationships without establishing that the building was employed in a qualifying activity. Robinson does not make every entity that spends money an operating commercial asset within every federal criminal statute. The available account identifies neither rental, sales, fee-based services nor the building's operation as a facility for interstate transfer or some other specific commerce-affecting function. No one of those examples is a newly required universal element, and their absence does not prove that a religious building can never qualify.

**Disposition selected by all eight Associates:** The panel's reasoning does not sustain affirmance under the enacted property-use requirement. Correct the legal standard and return the case for a decision on the actual trial record, with the remedy constrained as below. None supports affirmance simply because a church buys goods or sends funds, and none adopts a rule requiring substantial individual magnitude.

### B. Reverse immediately for evidentiary insufficiency

The strongest argument for an acquittal is that the government's reported evidence consists only of ordinary institutional inputs and charitable transfers; the trial was expressly confined to the commerce element. If that is the complete evidence of the building's function, the government has failed to prove the necessary use and cannot obtain another trial to find a new commercial function.

The present commitment does not convert the absence of an additional function in the published appellate account into a finding that the complete trial record contains none. The legal defect in the panel's analysis is established without that inference. Its factual account describes the church's operations but does not determine the property's function under the correct construction. All eight therefore favor a bounded appellate reconsideration on the existing trial record, rather than an immediate Supreme Court finding of insufficiency or an unqualified new-trial mandate. This is not a direction to the prosecution to supplement its trial evidence.

### C. Decide the Commerce Clause instead

If the statute were construed to reach the property solely because of ordinary purchases, an employee's insurance or generalized arson costs, the enacted class and the actual interstate mechanism would still require separate constitutional analysis. Lopez does not permit those generic consequences to create the missing connection. Conversely, proof that a building actually operates in an identified interstate transaction or commerce-affecting activity could supply a materially different application; nonprofit status alone defeats none of the current recognized routes. None of the Associates would use Hale's machinegun anti-evasion rationale without an identified federal regime whose operation depends on including this conduct.

All eight select the statutory route and reserve the outer constitutional issue. These commitments do not support a constitutional holding that all church arsons exceed federal power, that the present application is constitutionally valid, or that a jurisdictional phrase is a safe harbor. The constitutional discussion identifies the obstacle to automatic affirmance and the reason the statutory inquiry matters; it is not an independently adopted ground of judgment.

### D. Decide the Establishment objection

On the facts presented, all eight would reject the claimed inference from ordinary property protection to impermissible government regulation of religious teaching. The governmental act is prosecution for malicious destruction under a general property-protection statute. It organizes no worship, prescribes no doctrine and confers no religiously allocated civil authority. Agostini's established-permissible-relationship route, informed by Everson, supplies the relevant inquiry. No old Lemon prong, categorical religious exemption or approval of hypothetical regulation of church governance follows.

Because the statutory judgment is set aside, all eight would leave this constitutional objection unnecessary to the decision. Their conditional compatibility on this issue is not a vote to publish an advisory constitutional holding. If assembly presents a genuinely necessary alternative disposition, the issue's actual reach must remain explicit.

## Precise available joint ground and remand

**Common statutory proposition:** Section 844(i) requires identification of the function served by the damaged property and of the connection between that function and interstate commerce or a commerce-affecting activity. An owner's national affiliation, ordinary purchases, an employee's out-of-state insurance and recurring payments do not, without the required relation to the property's use, establish that element. Neither religious nor nonprofit identity creates an exclusion, and no categorical profit-making requirement is adopted.

**Application:** The Fourth Circuit sustained the conviction by treating the church's economic relationships as sufficient. It did not apply the required inquiry to the function of the damaged premises. Its rationale therefore cannot sustain the affirmance. The decision should not characterize the church as a commercial enterprise or deem the interstate source of every purchased item established.

**Judgment and stage:** Vacate the appellate affirmance and remand to the Fourth Circuit to apply that statutory construction to the existing trial record and determine the resulting disposition of the conviction. A complete existing record showing only the insufficient relationships described above requires reversal for entry of acquittal; Burks forecloses a new trial to supply missing element proof. If the record supports a legally adequate use finding, the court must address that actual basis and any necessary preserved constitutional question under current law. This mandate does not itself authorize new evidence, a new trial, acquittal, a substitute section 247 conviction, an amended penalty, release, or an independently selected sentence.

The review remains about this section 844(i) conviction. No section 247 offense, universal arson jurisdiction, hypothetical religious governance regulation or penalty-amendment question is decided. No exact offense date is assumed. No separate writing is required by these compatible commitments; any proposed opinion must retain the stated limitations to obtain these joins.
