## Why the provisional discrepancies cannot stand

Stevens's provisional row accepted the default/gateway route and withheld assent only from the rule concerning forgone charges. His complete historical writing instead treats the misinformation about the statutory element as establishing a constitutionally invalid plea, distinguishes formal Rule 11 error from constitutional invalidity under Timmreck and Hill, and rejects a requirement of a direct appeal before collateral attack on that plea. He would vacate the firearm conviction and allow pleading anew, leaving the Government its ordinary burden if the charge proceeds. The source does not merely favor a more permissive innocence gateway.

No concrete changed premise supports the provisional departure. The actual offense charged, the asserted erroneous element advice, Bailey's substantive meaning, lack of a direct-review plea challenge, and first collateral posture remain the same. Current Schlup and Carriger determine how to apply an otherwise relevant innocence gateway; they do not impose default on this plea claim or displace Stevens's Timmreck/Hill distinction. Hyde preserves knowing assent and addresses presentence withdrawal after acceptance, not this constitutional collateral claim. Godinez separates capacity from an actual informed choice. These authorities cannot turn Stevens's historical disagreement into an affirmative join in the five Associates' burden and remedy. His own source interpretation remains an individual legal conclusion, not a Court factual finding imposed on other Justices.

Scalia and Thomas's provisional ground treated a later statutory interpretation as insufficient to make the plea unintelligent because it was informed under the law then understood. Their historical dissent instead accepts lack of cause and contests the extension of Schlup to guilty pleas: a trial record normally permits a workable whole-record innocence assessment, whereas a plea supplies no equivalent record of facts thought irrelevant under the former construction; plea bargains may exchange an admission for relief from more serious exposure; stale retrials of both charged and forgone offenses burden finality and do not reproduce Schlup's rare trial-conviction setting. Their discussion criticizes the breadth of plea-intelligence doctrine, but it does not replace those sufficient procedural grounds with the provisional Brady/Broce holding. No intervening simulated authority resolves the plea-gateway extension against them or removes those practical and legal premises. Their affirmance remains, on the supported ground.

## Reconciled propositions and individual positions

O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer retain the full frozen framework. A plea must be voluntary and intelligent, with real notice of the charge's essential nature. A materially incorrect account of a statutory element can invalidate it if proved; a later disappointed prediction about the evidence or penalty is distinct. Bailey states the existing substantive crime, not a new constitutional procedure barred by Teague. This first §2255 proceeding is within ordinary review and not an appeal from a successive authorization refusal.

For these five, failure to raise the plea-intelligence claim on direct review is default. Neither an available use-versus-possession argument's expected failure nor adverse precedent establishes cause; prejudice alone cannot excuse the missing cause. A factual-innocence showing remains possible: considering the whole probative record under the correct crime, it must be more likely than not that no reasonable, properly instructed juror would convict. Mere inadequacy of the plea record is not factual innocence. The Government may offer admissible rebuttal evidence outside that record; no new post-plea event must be invented. The gateway, constitutional validity, ultimate guilt and remedy remain separate.

The five also retain the conditional rule that factual innocence must extend to an identified **more serious** charge the Government **actually forgave in the plea bargain**. Neither seriousness nor a bargain is presumed. The source's syllabus omits the more-serious qualification in one summary sentence; the complete Court opinion includes it and controls the comparison. Bousley's indictment charged use, not carrying, and no bargain relinquishing carrying or an identified more-serious charge is established. He therefore need establish only absence of use under Bailey on this record. A hypothetical charge or alternative statutory verb creates no added burden.

## Remedy and public-position consequences

Six Associates support setting aside the Eighth Circuit's rejection, but they do **not** all support the same mandate. The five direct the opportunity to establish factual innocence and, if satisfied, adjudication of the plea-intelligence claim; they do not immediately vacate the firearm conviction. Stevens would vacate that conviction and allow pleading anew without requiring the gateway. Scalia and Thomas affirm. Assembly must keep those remedial positions and proposition-level joins distinct.

No automatic oral hearing follows from the five's remand: adequate submissions or factual development required by the actual dispute may suffice. The drug conviction remains untouched.
