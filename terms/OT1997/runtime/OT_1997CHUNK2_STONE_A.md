## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Punishment classification of the OCC sanctions

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the punishment-classification question because it is necessary to the Double Jeopardy claim.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Ursery and Hendricks without alteration. Congress designated the money penalties as civil, and occupational debarment is administered by banking regulators to protect the integrity of a regulated industry. That designation is not conclusive, but the petitioners must provide the clearest proof that the sanctions’ combined design and actual operation are so punitive as to override it. Deterrence, severity, and connection to criminal conduct are relevant but insufficient by themselves.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The OCC monetary assessments and occupational debarment are civil on this record because their combined design and operation do not supply the clearest proof of criminal punishment. The later criminal prosecution therefore is not barred on a multiple-punishment theory.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The sanctions were imposed through civil banking authority and serve conventional regulatory functions: monetary accountability for unsafe banking conduct and exclusion from positions of trust in the banking system. Unlike the extraordinary tax in Kurth Ranch, they are not structured as a post-conviction exaction whose defining operation is a second punishment. Unlike the application invalidated in simulated Hendricks, the record does not show prisonlike custody or a failed civil treatment-and-release structure whose operation defeats the statutory label. The record therefore does not satisfy Ursery’s demanding override requirement.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not classify every banking sanction as civil, preclude a different result on materially different design or operational facts, resolve an Excessive Fines claim, or immunize a punitive measure merely because Congress labels it civil.

### Issue 2: Same-offense and successive-prosecution question

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone does not reach whether the OCC predicates and criminal charges constitute the same offense because Issue 1 establishes that the OCC measures were not criminal punishment. A Blockburger ruling would therefore be unnecessary.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** No separate holding.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Double Jeopardy’s same-offense inquiry becomes necessary only after the first proceeding qualifies as criminal punishment. That predicate fails here, so the Court stops.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The same-offense relationship among the administrative predicates and charged crimes remains unresolved.

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches statutory jurisdiction. No threshold requires dismissal because the federal and state claims form one constitutional case or controversy.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply § 1367 according to its text. A state-law claim does not fall outside supplemental jurisdiction merely because state law assigns it an administrative-review form. Once removed federal claims establish original jurisdiction, closely related state claims fall within the statute unless an express exception applies.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A district court with original jurisdiction over federal constitutional claims has supplemental jurisdiction over related state-law claims seeking review of the same local administrative decisions.

Characterizing the state claims as appellate does not alter Congress’s grant. The district court retains discretion to avoid needless disruption of state administration, but that question follows jurisdiction rather than defining it.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The Court does not require retention, alter state standards of review, or decide whether abstention or § 1367(c) warrants remand.

### Issue 1: University-selected commencement prayer

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the Establishment Clause merits. The official program and selection process make attribution clear, so no private-forum ambiguity remains.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Hold that a public university may solemnize commencement through secular remarks and may provide neutral access for genuine private expression, but it may not choose clergy and install prayer as an official ceremonial component. The resulting worship is the State’s act. The ability to skip graduation or remain silent does not supply authority for government to conduct the prayer.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not prohibit private religious remarks by graduates, neutral access to a genuine forum, historical acknowledgment lacking devotional operation, or a moment of silence.

### Issue 1: Material false information causing detention

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Resolve the actual threshold and reviewable legal question before deciding factual matters outside the record.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** The supplied case-specific position and proposed contextual defense control this reconstruction of Stone. Current Court law still retains the Harlow/Anderson defense: Crawford-El did not adopt Stone’s replacement. His proposed official-burden framework and rejection of a duplicative excuse for deliberate violations must therefore be identified openly as proposed doctrinal changes.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** An official cannot justify knowingly supplying material false information that causes detention merely by demanding a prior case with the identical communication method.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone would affirm on the three surviving theories as legally presented: deliberate material warrant falsity, deliberate false information prolonging detention, and use of a coerced manufactured confession to deprive Clanton of her own liberty. The last theory vindicates her due process, not a vicarious assertion of her nephew’s personal interrogation rights. A later absence of indictment and use of hearsay are not independent grounds for liability. Cooper may contest falsity, coercion, causation, and materiality at the proper stage, but cannot obtain interlocutory reversal by replacing the plaintiff-favorable account with his own. Stone’s developing contextual defense likewise cannot turn knowing fabrication into a reasonable mistake; the inherited Harlow defense remains the Court’s law until changed.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Failure of immunity does not itself prove the constitutional claim, causation, damages, supervisory liability, or a right to trial.
