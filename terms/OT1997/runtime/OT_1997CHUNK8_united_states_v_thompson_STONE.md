## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Marital privacy and conduct during an ongoing assault

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone resolves this application without deciding whether Article 125 may punish genuinely voluntary private sexual conduct between spouses or other adults.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Constitutional protection for consensual marital intimacy does not cover an act undertaken by one spouse to divert the other while the other is reloading a pistol during an ongoing deadly assault. The classification turns on the lack of free consensual intimacy, not on judicial approval of a particular marital practice.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Constitutional protection for consensual marital intimacy does not cover an act undertaken by one spouse to divert the other during an ongoing deadly assault.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Thompson’s wife acted to interrupt immediate violence and survive the encounter. The conduct therefore was not voluntary marital intimacy of the kind protected by the asserted privacy interest.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not decide whether Article 125 may punish genuinely voluntary private sexual conduct between spouses or other adults.

### Issue 2: Article 39(b) contact with the deliberating members

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the preserved statutory violation and prejudice question.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** The judge violated Article 39(b) by entering the closed deliberative session without Thompson or counsel, but the complete record may rebut prejudice when the contact was exclusively ministerial, concerned entry of findings already reached, and was fully disclosed.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The recorded, exclusively ministerial Article 39(b) violation was nonprejudicial.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The exchange did not address evidence, guilt, legal instructions, or deliberative substance. It concerned only entry of findings the members had already reached, and the record permits complete review of what occurred.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not authorize substantive, unrecorded, or otherwise unreconstructable judicial contact with deliberating members.
