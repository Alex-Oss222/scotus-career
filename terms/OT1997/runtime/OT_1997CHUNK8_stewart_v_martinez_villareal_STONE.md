**Proposed Stone disposition: Affirm the Ninth Circuit.**

A habeas claim dismissed because it was premature has not received a merits determination. Bringing the same claim back when it becomes ripe should not automatically transform it into a prohibited second or successive application.

The prisoner's claim of incompetence to be executed may therefore receive the adjudication that was previously unavailable. This ruling establishes neither incompetence nor automatic relief from the sentence.

**Doctrinal development:** Meaningful first review is different from successive litigation.
