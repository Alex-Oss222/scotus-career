## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Intentional speech assistance to murder

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the categorical First Amendment defense but not ultimate tort liability.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Reject blanket publisher immunity where the publisher intentionally designs and supplies operational instructions to facilitate a particular class of serious crimes and the instructions materially assist the crime. Do not dilute Brandenburg for abstract advocacy; this case proceeds through intentional assistance, not mere persuasion or remote audience response.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The First Amendment does not categorically bar tort liability when a publisher intentionally provides detailed operational assistance for murder, intends criminal use, and the publication materially facilitates the resulting crime.
- **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The stipulations remove the ordinary danger that liability will be inferred from unpopular content or a reader’s independent act. Paladin concedes an objective of assisting murder and a causal operational role. The law may treat that communication as integral assistance while leaving advocacy, journalism, and description protected.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding is confined to proved intent and material assistance and does not authorize liability for abstract advocacy, news, fiction, historical works, negligent foreseeability, or generalized dangerous ideas.
