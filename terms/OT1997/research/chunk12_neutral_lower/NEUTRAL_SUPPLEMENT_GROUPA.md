## Clinton v. City of New York - No. 97-1374

**Scope and posture.** Review the consolidated District of Columbia declaratory judgment invalidating the two completed cancellations. The statute's direct-review authorization, the meaning of its eligible-plaintiff language and constitutional standing are distinct antecedent issues. The district court was not established to be a three-judge court. The Government filed both a direct appeal and a protective circuit appeal. The plaintiffs sought declarations, not an injunction against the President. A Supreme Court route or the merits cannot be justified merely by expedition.

**Record corrections and limits.** The New York exposure is removal of protection from contingent recoupment/provider-assessment liability; HHS's waiver requests remained undecided. The estimated $2.6 billion is not a final assessment. Snake River's injury theory concerns a tax incentive for prospective sellers and an asserted effect on its acquisition bargaining. The declaration describes discussions, interest and abandonment after cancellation; it establishes neither a contract nor a specified lost price. Treat each plaintiff's injury, causation and redressability on its own record. An adequate plaintiff in one consolidated case does not automatically supply standing for a separate cancellation challenged only in the other. Neither a pending waiver nor an uncompleted commercial transaction categorically settles imminence; lawful reasonable inferences and the applicable burden remain material.

**Entering law.** Raines rejects individual legislators' abstract institutional dilution while reserving concrete cancellation injuries. Lujan requires the litigation-stage evidentiary showing and retains reasonable inferences. Akins distinguishes concrete shared injury from a generalized grievance. Steel Co requires resolving disputed Article III power before disputed merits within its actual holding. Loving sustains a bounded military punishment-limiting delegation in an assigned executive function; it neither gives unlimited cancellation power nor establishes that every post-enactment discretion is repeal. Hechinger invalidates congressional-agent operative control outside the constitutional allocation while preserving advice, reporting and lawful responsive legislation. Morgan Stanley and Plaut sustain their carefully bounded statutory reopening of limitations dismissals while retaining independent adjudication; neither decides this delegation or presentment question.

**Open lawful legal paths.** The cancellation can be analyzed as legally changing enacted consequences without the constitutionally prescribed enactment process, or as exercise of discretion expressly conferred through an earlier bicamerally enacted and presented law. The difference must be tested against the actual cancellation text, conditions, legal effect, underlying spending/tax provisions and enacted disapproval route. Chadha's congressional veto and Field v. Clark's contingent tariff execution offer different analogies; neither analogy substitutes for that analysis. Congress's consent cannot alone answer a structural objection, while the word cancellation alone cannot answer whether this is execution of Congress's enacted choice. Presentment and nondelegation are distinct grounds and need distinct holdings if reached.

**Remedy and gaps.** Possible lawful paths include resolution of each jurisdictional component, affirmance or reversal on a reached constitutional issue, or a bounded remand for a genuinely material unresolved issue. ## Eastern Enterprises v. Apfel - No. 97-42

**Scope and posture.** Review the First Circuit's affirmance of summary judgment enforcing Coal Act liability. Eastern asserted due process, takings and statutory claims; the supplied review question concerns constitutional retroactive liability. Do not expand it to a renewed statutory claim merely because that claim existed below. The stated requested relief is declaratory/injunctive protection from enforcement, not a Treasury compensation award. Tucker Act availability and the proper remedial channel remain distinct threshold issues; the federal respondent's abandonment of its jurisdictional objection does not confer jurisdiction.

**Record corrections and limits.** Eastern employed the assigned retirees before 1966 and signed the pre-1965 wage agreements. Its direct coal activity ended in 1965, but its ownership of the coal subsidiary continued until 1987 and it received reported dividends. Those matters are relevant without equating indirect ownership with a newly proved lifetime promise. Written pre-1974/1978 benefit terms, trustee alteration rights, the later guarantees, miners' expectations and Eastern's industry participation must remain separate. The assessed liability is tier-three section 9706(a)(3) liability for more than 1,000 former employees; do not decide tiers one and two, unassigned miners, or the separate 1992 plan by implication. Annual premiums and projected lifetime totals are different quantities; estimates are not fixed damages.

**Entering law.** Concrete Pipe sustains rational proportionate allocation of pooled pension obligations in its actual participation setting and separately rejects its takings challenge; private contribution limits alone did not defeat that application. Carlton asks whether the retrospective operation rationally serves a legitimate purpose and creates neither an absolute one-year ceiling nor an unlimited revenue safe harbor. Landgraf's presumption does not defeat explicit temporal direction and does not settle constitutional validity. Phillips establishes ownership of actually generated client interest while leaving taking and compensation separate; it creates no rule that every compelled payment is a taking. Morgan Stanley/Plaut's adjudication holdings do not establish unlimited permission for retrospective liabilities.

**Open lawful legal paths.** Due process permits testing the connection between Eastern's prior employment/benefit participation and the statutory cost allocation, including the timing, magnitude and claimed expectations. Turner Elkhorn and R. A. Gray supply economic-retroactivity baselines; they do not excuse attention to this statutory operation. A takings analysis must identify the asserted property and explain whether an obligation payable from general assets implicates it, then address the applicable framework and remedy. The competing view that liability is economic regulation without appropriation of a specific property interest must be evaluated independently of whether due process permits the burden. Invalidity or validity under one clause does not mechanically establish the result under the other.

**Remedy and gaps.** Upholding this assignment, invalidating this application on a reached ground, or remanding a properly preserved material question are open alternatives. A categorical invalidation of all Coal Act liabilities or a fixed monetary compensation award does not follow from the supplied record. Precise indemnity allocation, retained corporate control and additional evidence of implied promises remain source limits if material to a chosen rationale. Unsupported assumptions on those subjects cannot be used either for or against Eastern.

**Record corrections and limits.** HIV infection and its manifestations, the identified major life activity, substantial limitation of this individual, and the statutory alternative disability routes must not be collapsed.
