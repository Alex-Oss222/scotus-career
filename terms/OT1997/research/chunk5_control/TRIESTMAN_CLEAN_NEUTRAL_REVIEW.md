**Independent §2241 route, if actually presented:** A federal prisoner's savings-clause gateway and an appropriate habeas court would still have to be established. The strongest access argument is that an intervening substantive statutory interpretation may demonstrate criminal punishment for conduct outside the statute while the ordinary motion structure gave no meaningful earlier opportunity. Nothing here finds an original application filed or authorizes the validator to choose that procedural premise. Section1651 does not create jurisdiction. [08_triestman.md, Felker and Supreme Court original-habeas register.]

**Merits, if a lawful route and eligibility exist:** Bailey excludes passive possession, proximity, accessibility and protective storage from active use, while reserving properly available carrying grounds. Aiding and abetting requires an actual substantive offense and intentional assistance; association, awareness or a charge label alone does not suffice. These legal questions remain distinct from actual innocence, default, plea consequences, evidentiary development and remedy. Neither Bailey nor a savings-clause gateway automatically orders release or a hearing. [08_triestman.md, Bailey holdings and limits.]

**Different procedural doctrines:** Schlup's reliable-new-evidence probability gateway, §2255's statutory successive certification routes, and a substantive statutory-innocence theory are not interchangeable. The federal-prisoner evidence route is not the state-prisoner clause's distinct constitutional-error/diligence test. Trest holds only that courts have no mandatory duty to raise an omitted state default; it does not turn statutory jurisdiction into a forfeitable defense, establish waiver here, prohibit all judicial initiative, or order substantive review. Lonchar protects process actually required by law and excludes an invented equitable dismissal sanction; it does not supply a missing statutory forum.
