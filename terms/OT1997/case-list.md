# OT1997 case index

**+ = one of the 50 expressly listed simulated grants.** For those entries, the docket shown is the lower court’s own docket; it is not a historical Supreme Court identifier. Stone approval status is stated separately in Section II.

All 146 current Stone positions are user-approved as of 2026-10-21; this is approval of personal reasoning and proposed dispositions, not completed Court decisions. Existing legal/vehicle conflicts remain flagged. See the [approval record](OT_1997_STONE_APPROVALS.json) and [application clarification audit](OT_1997_APPLICATION_UPDATE_AUDIT.md).

146 matters: 136 MERITS, 1 ORIGINAL, 4 APPLICATION, and 5 DISMISSAL; 13 chunks (12 each in chunks 1–12, 2 in chunk 13). Listed applications and petition-stage events: Brown v. Williams, Arteaga v. United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit / Arteaga v. Wilson, Glendora v. Porzio, and the combined Breard / Paraguay matters. The comprehensive supplied list and authorized additions control membership, with the six already-completed disputes—Kalina, Oncale, Steel Co., Crawford-El, Akins, and Faragher—excluded from replay, and Davenport and Jean reserved for OT1998. Consolidated companions count once.

The four APPLICATION entries concern specific procedural relief. Brown, Arteaga / Wilson, and Glendora concern permission to file certiorari petitions without paying fees (in forma pauperis, or IFP), together with prospective restrictions on future noncriminal filings. Those entries concern fee access, not a decision whether to grant certiorari. Breard / Paraguay / In re Breard is a combined emergency proceeding seeking to halt Breard’s execution, with related certiorari petitions, an original habeas petition, and Paraguay’s request for permission to bring an original action. It is not a plenary merits case. A fee waiver or execution stay does not itself grant certiorari.
