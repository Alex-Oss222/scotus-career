## Holdings

### The present substantive planning challenge is premature

**Holding and operative rule:** A substantive challenge to a programmatic forest plan is unripe when the plan authorizes no immediate logging, establishes no sufficient present legal hardship, and leaves further project decisions and meaningful review before cutting. On this record that rule requires dismissal without prejudice of the plan/clearcutting, public-trust sales and timber-suitability-regulation claims, without deciding their substantive validity.

**Authority:** Scalia's opinion of the Court, joined in full by all nine Justices, supplies direct controlling authority for this rule and the limits below.

**Controlling explanation:** Abbott Laboratories directs courts to consider fitness for decision and hardship from withholding review. Texas applies that inquiry to a dispute whose operative details remain unresolved. The Wayne plan identifies logging goals, suitable areas and probable methods; it does not authorize cutting. Before logging, the Forest Service must identify a site and method, ensure consistency with the plan, provide notice and an opportunity to be heard, conduct environmental analysis and make a final project decision subject to review. Those choices can alter the anticipated effects and provide a concrete basis for adjudication. Review need not wait until trees are cut; the relevant planning premise may be challenged with the project that applies it.

The suitability regulations are completed rules, but Toilet Goods distinguishes finality from a presently fit controversy. The complaint establishes no sufficient present consequence requiring resolution of this substantive logging dispute now. Bennett shows why retained agency discretion alone is not decisive when completed action already has supported consequences. New York and Pic-A-State likewise preserve review of actual present burdens; no comparable burden is established here. Possible plan amendment is a consideration, not an automatic bar.

NEPA's inclusion identifies no particular omitted statement, denied participation or other completed procedural deprivation. The public-trust label supplies no different timing premise. The requests for an injunction, costs and fees do not establish current cutting authority or an independent ripe injury. Neither lower court's substantive forestry ruling can survive as the disposition of this unripe action.

**Limits and questions not reached:** The Court decides neither standing, APA finality, statutory or public-trust merits, nor an independent fee entitlement. It does not adopt the lower court's exceptional-circumstances formulation for even-aged management or equate an optimum-method inquiry with a resource-protection condition. A site-specific authorization, an immediately binding provision or regulation, or a properly presented completed procedural violation may receive review when its independent requirements are satisfied. Lujan's protection of a procedural right tied to a threatened concrete interest remains intact. No optional-appeal exhaustion requirement or future limitations ruling is adopted.
