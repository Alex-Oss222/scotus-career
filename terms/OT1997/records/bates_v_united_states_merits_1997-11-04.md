## Holdings

**Knowing and willful misapplication does not require an additional purpose to injure or defraud.** Under the applicable §1097(a), the Government must establish the actual knowing and willful culpable misapplication; an ordinary unpaid refund, accounting mistake, insolvency, or breach of a funding condition is not sufficient by itself. Ginsburg's opinion, joined by all nine Justices, controls this construction.

The express culpability terms perform substantial work. Wells requires fidelity to enacted elements without eliminating required fault; Ratzlaf gives willfulness its contextual statutory force; Staples and Morissette preserve culpability and the received meaning of criminal terms. These authorities do not require the same additional purpose in every offense. The bank-misapplication analogy rests on a distinct history in which earlier intent language disappeared during a technical revision. That history does not establish incorporation of the same purpose into §1097. After text, context, and that history are considered, no unresolved ambiguity justifies adding the proposed element through lenity.

The indictment alleges knowledge of refund duties and deliberate diversion, but allegations remain unproved. Rejecting an extra fraudulent purpose does not settle the complete meaning of willfulness, including knowledge of illegality, or every conversion theory. It creates no general rule that statutory silence always defeats inherited criminal meaning. The later refund-specific amendment is not applied retroactively. Aiding-and-abetting and independent indictment issues receive no expanded holding or assumed preservation.

## Precedent Treatment

- Wells is applied to the distinction between actual offense elements and a proposed additional condition.
- Ratzlaf's contextual construction and independently sufficient residual-lenity ground remain intact; they do not supply this statute's extra purpose.
- Staples, Morissette, and Liparota retain their protection against eliminating the fault required by the particular offense. No universal mental-state formula is adopted.

## Law After Decision

The applicable §1097(a) requires knowing and willful culpable misapplication, without a separate injurious or fraudulent purpose. Proof of the real elements remains necessary. The decision adds that statute-specific construction while leaving the full willfulness definition and other offenses unchanged.
