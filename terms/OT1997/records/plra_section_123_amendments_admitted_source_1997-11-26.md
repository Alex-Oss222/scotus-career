## Source, authority, and effective operation

The authenticated [GovInfo enrolled public-law text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-105publ119/html/PLAW-105publ119.htm), preserved in the [source snapshot](../freeze/sources/chunk2_control/PLAW-105publ119.html), identifies approval on November 26, 1997. Section 123(a)(2) amends §3626(b)(3) by replacing “current or ongoing” with “current and ongoing.” Section 123(b) makes the amendments effective on enactment and applicable to pending cases. These are enacted statutory terms, not judicial findings or a Court construction.

The remainder of §123(a) also changes §3626(a)(1)(B)(i) from “permits” to “requires”; revises the prisoner-release-order wording in (a)(3)(A); adds legislators to the identified officials in (a)(3)(F) and corrects its final reference to prison; supplies mandamus for failure to rule promptly under (e)(1); makes a motion under (b) operate as the stay described in (e)(2); adds a good-cause postponement of no more than 60 days, excluding ordinary calendar congestion, in (e)(3); and subjects orders blocking the automatic stay, other than the specified postponement, to interlocutory appeal under 28 U.S.C. §1292(a)(1) in (e)(4). Exact enactment text controls each component. Admission determines no application of these provisions to an individual motion, no stay start or expiration, and no unresolved constitutional question.
