A prepared position and an event's place in a chunk change no law. No later-effective event may supply law to an earlier one.

Each event uses only completed earlier-effective Records. An undecided earlier-dated event in a later chunk supplies neither law nor a blocker. A maximum date identifies the completed set, not completion of every intervening event.
