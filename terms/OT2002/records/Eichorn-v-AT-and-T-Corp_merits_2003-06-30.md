## 173. Eichorn v. AT&T Corp. — June 30, 2003

### Operative components

**E1 — antitrust.** All eight affirm dismissal of the Sherman Act claims. A genuinely ancillary no-hire covenant reasonably connected to the transfer of an operating business receives restraint-sensitive rule-of-reason examination, not automatic treatment as a naked horizontal labor allocation. The separate Preliminary Net, while operating within a wholly owned group, is not an agreement among independent actors merely because future separation was contemplated. That internal-coordination ground covers the period of common ownership; any later independent agreement requires its own agreement and effects analysis. The later Pre-Closing and 245-day Post-Closing Nets concern covered employees and consultants earning over $50,000 and the purchaser's supported workforce-continuity interest. On this record the employees have not established a viable competitive-effects claim from those limited restraints, the asserted market or the evidence of alternative employers. Losing access to employment that preserves one firm's pension advantages does not itself establish the relevant antitrust market or injury to competition.

Neither a sale label nor rejection of per se treatment proves ultimate lawfulness. California Dental requires examination fitted to the restraint and its material explanation; NYNEX requires the actual agreement and competitive harm; Microsoft supplies no immunity for a restraint merely attached to integration. Direct competitive effects can suffice without a universal full market-definition exercise. The record-specific failure here is not a categorical denial of antitrust protection to workers selling labor.

**E2 — ERISA.** All eight affirm reinstatement of the claims against AT&T and Lucent concerning the Pre-Closing and Post-Closing Nets. The eight-month return restriction crosses the six-month bridging deadline; the internal communications about that effect and the evidence of direct pension-expense savings, considered together, permit a reasonable inference of a specific interference purpose. The legitimate workforce-continuity explanation remains a competing inference. Rule 56 does not permit selecting it by weighing credibility. Knowledge alone, general corporate savings alone or benefit loss alone does not establish the prohibited purpose. Inter-Modal protects prospective attainment while retaining participant status, the actual protected right, prohibited conduct, specific purpose, causation and authorized remedy. The Preliminary Net remains excluded: the source's footnote 10 states that it did not prevent receipt of benefits while the employees remained at Lucent. It is not silently merged into the two later restrictions.

**E3 — return and relief.** The surviving ERISA claims receive ordinary adjudication under the preponderance standard. Class-related discovery must be reconsidered after reinstatement; certification requires the actual Rule 23 showing, including the claim, defense and proof differences identified in Bridgestone/Firestone. Great-West and the applicable section 502 route govern eventual relief. Texas Pacific Group is not converted into a section 510 defendant by its role in the sale.

### Enhanced paths

The employees' strongest antitrust alternative treats independently competing employers' agreement not to hire as a naked allocation or a restraint broader than the sale reasonably needs. That path would permit further antitrust proceedings if supported competitive harm or nonancillary scope created a real dispute. The actual duration, restricted participants, workforce need and alternative employment evidence support the bounded ancillary application here. The Court must not describe a legitimate commercial purpose as categorically disproving anticompetitive effect.

The employers' strongest ERISA path treats every pension effect as incidental to a legitimate sale and every communication as awareness rather than purpose. The combined timing, communications and direct savings permit a contrary reasonable inference without a direct admission. Inter-Modal does not make the inference true; it prevents treating lawful restructuring power as an answer to an independently prohibited interference purpose. Antitrust legality and ERISA intent therefore remain distinct conclusions.

## Holdings

### I. Internal coordination is distinct from independent agreement

**Holding and operative rule:** A wholly owned corporate group’s internal coordination does not constitute agreement among independent actors under Sherman Act §1 merely because separation is planned. That principle sustains dismissal concerning the Preliminary Net during its supported period of common ownership; later independent agreements require their own examination.

**Authority:** The unanimous opinion of the Court; all nine Justices directly join this proposition and its explanation.

**Controlling explanation:** Copperweld treats a parent and its wholly owned subsidiary as a single enterprise for this purpose. The Preliminary Net was imposed while the relevant companies retained that relationship and unified corporate interest. Planning a future divestiture did not itself produce an independent agreement during that period. The preliminary restriction therefore differs from the Pre-Closing and Post-Closing Nets involving the sale participants. The corporate identity and time of operation, not a blanket description of the entire transaction, control this agreement inquiry.

### II. The supported ancillary restraints do not establish an antitrust violation here

**Holding and operative rule:** A genuinely subordinate no-hire covenant reasonably connected to transfer of an operating business receives rule-of-reason examination fitted to the restraint’s actual competitive tendency and supported transaction function. The limited Pre-Closing and 245-day Post-Closing Nets, the supported workforce-continuity interest and the competitive evidence here sustain dismissal; loss of access to a preferred employer’s pension arrangements alone establishes neither the relevant labor market nor injury to competition.

**Authority:** The Court’s opinion, joined by Stevens, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer, supplies eight direct joins to this proposition and its application. Stone-Zsela dissents from the dismissal on this ground.

**Controlling explanation:** California Dental requires examination appropriate to the restraint and its material commercial explanation. NYNEX requires the actual agreement and competitive injury, and Microsoft makes a transaction label insufficient to establish justification. Here the purchaser sought the technical workforce of an operating business, the restraints covered specified employees and consultants earning more than $50,000, and the post-closing period lasted 245 days. The record identifies more than twenty alternative telecommunications employers and a broader set of jobs for more general skills. The asserted market defined by a small geographic area and preservation of these particular pensions lacks the necessary competitive support. Employees may challenge restraints on their sale of labor, and direct competitive effects may suffice without an invariably elaborate market study. But this record does not supply a viable competition-reducing effects claim. The contrary view properly demands actual ancillarity rather than deference to a sale label; the supported continuity function, limited scope and deficient competitive showing answer that objection here. The same covenant may still have been adopted with a prohibited pension-interference purpose, a separate statutory question.

### III. The combined pension evidence permits a specific-intent finding

**Holding and operative rule:** An ERISA §510 claimant may prove a specific purpose to interfere with attainment of protected prospective plan rights through circumstantial evidence. The 245-day return restriction extending beyond the six-month bridging period, internal communications about that effect and direct pension-expense savings, considered together, create a genuine dispute on the Pre-Closing and Post-Closing claims against AT&T and Lucent.

**Authority:** The unanimous opinion of the Court; all nine Justices directly join this proposition and its explanation.

**Controlling explanation:** Inter-Modal protects prospective attainment while retaining protected participant status and right, prohibited action, interference purpose, causation and an authorized remedy. Hazen Paper distinguishes pension motivation from age motivation; it does not insulate conduct aimed at pension rights. Here the combined timing, communications and direct savings go beyond the observation that a corporate transaction saves money. Workforce continuity remains a legitimate competing inference, but Rule 56 does not allow the court to choose it through credibility weighing. Awareness, incidental savings or benefit loss alone would not prove specific purpose. At trial the employees bear the preponderance burden on each required element. The Preliminary Net remains excluded from this reinstatement because it did not prevent the employees from receiving benefits while they remained at Lucent. The actual §510 defendants are AT&T and Lucent.

### IV. Class discovery and relief follow their own requirements

**Holding and operative rule:** Reinstatement of the supported §510 claims requires reconsideration of the denied class-related discovery request. Any later certification must rest on an actual workable Rule 23 arrangement addressing material differences in the claims, defenses and proof; any eventual relief must fit the invoked ERISA §502 route.

**Authority:** The unanimous opinion of the Court; all nine Justices directly join this proposition and its explanation.

**Controlling explanation:** Bridgestone/Firestone requires examination of a real proposed arrangement, rather than treating a common account as the answer to material differences. Discovery may identify the employees and employers covered by each restraint, individual bridging opportunities, common purpose evidence and the proof of injury, causation and requested relief. A common covenant may support common purpose evidence without proving each employee’s loss. A supported arrangement must fairly adjudicate those differences; the court must deny or limit a requested certification that cannot do so while preserving viable individual claims. The rule does not categorically bar this employment class. Great-West preserves §502(a)(3)’s requirement of appropriate equitable relief: a personal debt is not equitable merely because payment is requested by injunction. Genuine equitable categories retain their distinct predicates. The correct remedy therefore follows the actual right, violation and statutory channel, rather than the mere survival of a §510 claim.

## Precedent Treatment

- **Copperweld Corp. v. Independence Tube Corp., 467 U.S. 752:** Applies the single-enterprise boundary to the Preliminary Net during common ownership, preserving separate analysis of later independent agreements.
- **California Dental Association v. FTC, May 24, 1999:** Applies examination fitted to the actual competitive tendency and material explanation; an elaborate market study is not universally required.
- **NYNEX Corp. v. Discon, Inc., 525 U.S. 128:** Applies classification by the actual agreement and the independent requirement of competitive injury.
- **United States v. Microsoft Corp., June 28, 2002:** Applies by analogy the need for actual justification and competitive inquiry; its platform–browser setting does not itself classify this labor covenant.
- **Inter-Modal Rail Employees Association v. Atchison, Topeka & Santa Fe Railway Co., April 29, 1997:** Applies protection for prospective attainment and retains the remaining statutory elements.
- **Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins, 507 U.S. 604:** Distinguishes pension motive from age motive without exempting purposeful pension interference.
- **In re Bridgestone/Firestone, Inc., Tires Products Liability Litigation, November 5, 2002:** Applies actual workable-arrangement analysis to any later class request; its product and state-law differences do not dictate this case’s certification outcome.
- **Great-West Life & Annuity Insurance Co. v. Knudson, January 8, 2002:** Applies §502(a)(3)’s equitable-category limits according to the remedy actually sought.

## Law After Decision

A sale-related labor restraint must be classified and tested by its actual agreement, ancillary function and competitive effect. This record sustains antitrust dismissal while permitting a separate inference of purposeful interference with prospective pension rights. That difference follows the statutes’ different elements. The surviving claims require ordinary proof, and class treatment and relief depend on their own governing requirements.

## Separate Writings

**Stone-Zsela, concurring in part and dissenting in part.** He joins Parts I, III and IV. He would return the independent-party antitrust claims for a supported ancillarity and competitive-effects inquiry. Copperweld answers genuine internal coordination, but independent employers’ naked labor allocation remains subject to per se treatment. A restraint must be subordinate and reasonably necessary to a legitimate transaction before it receives the rule of reason. Defendants invoking ancillarity must substantiate that relationship and necessity; plaintiffs must establish agreement, cognizable injury and the applicable competitive harm, and may meet a supported justification with lack of fit or materially less restrictive alternatives. California Dental and NYNEX require the actual competitive inquiry, State Oil preserves the distinction among restraint categories, and Microsoft rejects justification by an integration label. The eight-month restriction’s relation to a six-month bridging deadline and its covered hiring opportunities require more than selection of the continuity inference on summary judgment. He would affirm dismissal if the complete record conclusively established genuine ancillarity and no cognizable anticompetitive effect under that inquiry; he does not treat those predicates as established here.

He agrees that the combined pension evidence permits a §510 trial inference without establishing liability. For requested plan restoration he would require identification of the §502 route, defendant authority over plan status, the protected right and the causal correction. Varity permits genuine individual restoration within operative plan terms, while a separate adequate ERISA remedy ordinarily makes additional §502(a)(3) equity inappropriate. Great-West distinguishes a personal debt from a lien or constructive trust in specifically identifiable property held through the required equitable connection; other equitable remedies retain their own predicates. His antitrust disagreement and additional remedial explanation do not change the unanimous ERISA return.
