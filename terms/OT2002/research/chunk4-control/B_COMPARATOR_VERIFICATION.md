## Material doctrinal boundaries verified

Scheidler's historical majority required acquisition as well as deprivation and did not categorically exclude intangible property. Footnote 6 expressly disclaimed deciding or rejecting Tropiano's intangible-property conclusion. The generic state-extortion and extortion-dependent Travel Act predicates shared the acquisition defect; failure of the RICO basis made private injunctive authority unnecessary. Stevens treated coercive appropriation of control as obtaining, relying on the established appellate construction, Carpenter and broad statutory coverage. Ginsburg and Breyer joined the majority while stressing severe RICO consequences, the risk of extending that statute beyond its terms, and the distinct FACE response.

The Government's actual position is separately extracted without comparator material in `SCHEIDLER_AMICUS_FACTS.md`. It requested reversal overall but maintained an acquisition-of-control extortion theory; it conceded remand for the jury instruction's inclusion of a patient's liberty interest as property. The original packet's description of agreement with the obtaining objection requires fresh neutral validation and affected modeling.

Moseley's historical controlling text required actual lessening of source-identifying capacity, not merely likely dilution, but did not require lost sales or profits. Mental association alone was insufficient on the nonidentical marks before the Court. Reliable circumstantial evidence could establish actual dilution; identical marks were an example, not an automatic presumption or a mandatory condition. The statutory status of every tarnishment theory was not decided because the parties did not contest its relevance and the evidence failed in any event. Kennedy's concurrence emphasized impairment of present and potential capacity and equitable protection before separate downstream economic damage; his full join does not turn mere association into proof or replace the statutory element with a general likelihood standard.
