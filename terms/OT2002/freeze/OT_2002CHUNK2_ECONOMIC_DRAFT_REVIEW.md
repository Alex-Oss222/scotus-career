## Statutory-completeness repairs: resolved

### 1. Dow Corning: community-property qualification clarified

Location: Controlling holdings / Permanent independent claims cannot be extinguished through general plan powers / first explanation paragraph, and the identical Public Projection Holdings passage.

The reviewed draft invoked section 524(e) as statutory structural support, then preserved section 524(a)(3) only with the phrase "community-property qualification intact." That reference identifies a provision without explaining what it does. Under the adopted completeness requirement, a qualification the analysis expressly uses must be stated in its own terms.

The necessary substance is already available in tmp/economic-sources/bankruptcy-524.txt: section 524(a)(3) protects qualifying after-acquired community property from collection of allowable community claims, subject to its discharge exceptions and the related spouse-discharge limits. State the qualification's operation accurately beside the section 524(e) proposition, keeping it separate from the independent nondebtor claim category adjudicated here. Do not turn that property protection into a general release of another person's liability. No new research or merits reconsideration is needed.

### 2. Sprietsma: section 4305 actor and condition clarified

Location: Controlling holdings / The Act preserves this common-law damages action / second explanation paragraph, and the identical Public Projection Holdings passage.

The reviewed draft stated the hazardous-local-condition permission accurately and kept it separate from section 4311(g), but called the other qualification only the "section 4305 permission." It does not identify the Secretary's exemption power or the required safety judgment. This is the same completeness problem, confined to an otherwise correct statutory account.

The text is already available in tmp/economic-sources/boat-4306.txt, which includes section 4305 immediately before section 4306. The Secretary may exempt a requirement of the chapter or a regulation under it if the Secretary considers recreational-vessel safety will not be adversely affected. Add that actor and condition, preserving permission rather than a duty and preserving the distinct local-condition and liability-saving provisions. No new research or changed commitment is needed.
