## 117. United States v. Haney

### Components, sufficient grounds and remedy

**H1 — Commerce.** Reject the preserved commerce objection to both completed machine guns. Hale (February 12, 1996), Kirk (February 14, 1998) and Wright (October 5, 1998), each joined on this point by all eight Associates, establish the relevant connection: a locally converted or assembled completed machine gun adds to the civilian stock Congress validly restricts, and excluding its possession would permit retention and circulation that materially defeat that regime. Commerce together with the Necessary and Proper Clause supplies the power. A particular interstate sale or individually substantial interstate effect is not an additional requirement. General gun danger, marketability, remote travel or downstream crime costs are not substitute grounds.

The exceptions remain distinct. Section 922(o)(2)(A) concerns transfer to or by, and possession by or under the authority of, the specified federal and state governmental bodies. Section 922(o)(2)(B) concerns lawful transfer or possession of a machine gun lawfully possessed before May 19, 1986. Earlier manufacture or prior possession of unassembled parts does not itself satisfy the latter condition. Neither exception is established here. The completed character of both weapons is undisputed, so no judgment about an unfinished component is necessary.

**H2 — Personal invocation.** Reject the lower court's militia-service prerequisite. The claimant may invoke the individual guarantee without membership in, service to or preparation for an organized militia. Hale and Wright require examination of the actual weapon, conduct and restriction; military usefulness alone proves neither exclusion nor immunity. This conclusion does not resolve the coverage or permissibility of this particular restriction.

**H3 — Arms judgment and remand.** Vacate the appellate affirmance of the two section 922(o) convictions insofar as it rests on rejection of the preserved Second Amendment defense, and remand for a genuine application of the governing personal-right law. The supplied judgment performed neither an adequate independent coverage inquiry nor an adequate burden-and-justification inquiry. The fact that the weapons are completed, rather than disputed parts, removes an object-classification problem; it does not answer the constitutional inquiry. Existing record materials may permit decision. Ordinary further proceedings are available only to resolve an actual remaining issue, without a mandatory study, evidentiary hearing or new trial.

Wright remains binding on the lower court. Coverage is determined through the constitutional text, history and precedents. Where protected activity is affected, the practical restriction determines the required justification: a core deprivation demands a compelling objective, narrow fit and absence of a comparably effective materially less restrictive means; a substantial burden requires an important objective, a substantial connection and meaningful preservation of protected activity; a truly incidental rule receives reasonable-regulation review while arbitrariness, discrimination and cumulative destruction remain forbidden. Government cannot eliminate meaningful possession of ordinary functional defensive arms at home. Neither that categorical boundary nor the availability of some other weapon automatically decides the present application.

The commerce resolution survives the remand. The arms claim remains to be decided under that rule; consequential treatment of the convictions and the thirty-three-month sentence follows its lawful resolution. Vacating the appellate affirmance is not an acquittal and does not establish failure of the enacted offense elements. Unrelated trial and sentencing issues are outside the granted questions.

### Supported alternatives and decisive limits

**Remand path:** The lower judgment's invalid affiliation premise and absent constitutional application bring this case within Hale and Wright. The strongest contrary argument is that completed automatic weapons, the restriction on new civilian stock and remaining defensive options permit immediate affirmance. That argument identifies relevant material, but the packet does not establish its constitutional consequences or the complete justification at the appropriate level. A lawful national stock mechanism establishes enumerated power, not satisfaction of the separate right.

**Direct-decision path:** The Court could resolve the arms defense now if the preserved facts and legal materials established the actual coverage, severity and required justification. Its strongest form is a restriction leaving ordinary defensive arms available and addressing a demonstrated mechanism-related danger. Its difficulty here is substantive, rather than a demand for possession of every trial paper: neither the lower reasoning nor the supported recitations supplies the completed Wright application. Direct invalidation has the converse defect. Personal invocation does not establish that these weapons are within the protected activity, what burden the statute imposes there, or that its justification fails. Neither direct endpoint is sufficiently supported on this handoff.

Emerson (June 17, 2002) demonstrates that a supported individual danger predicate can justify a substantial burden; it does not supply Haney with that adjudicated predicate or prove this weapon-class rule valid. Bean (December 10, 2002) concerns an actual agency denial under section 925(c), not jurisdiction over this preserved constitutional defense.

## 125. Hilton v. City of Wheeling

### Components, operative rules and remedy

**I1 — Petition Clause.** Affirm summary judgment on the claim that the First Amendment required police to furnish the requested affirmative protection or enforcement. The right to submit a grievance does not itself command investigation, citation, prosecution or a favorable response. Smith v. Arkansas State Highway Employees (1979) supplies this distinction. Police actually received and responded to the complaints. The presented service demand supplies no supported obstruction or retaliation claim. DeShaney and the unanimously joined no-custodial-duty holding of Pinder (May 21, 1996) distinguish general public protection from an independently established constitutional deprivation; their limits do not erase equal protection.

**I2 — Equal-protection rule.** Olech (February 23, 2000) permits a claim of intentional unequal treatment of materially similarly situated persons without a rational basis. Personal ill will is possible evidence, not an additional mandatory element. The challenger still must establish intentional difference and material similarity and negate reasonably conceivable legitimate grounds. The government need not have recorded a contemporaneous justification. Negligence, an ordinary mistake, bare numerical disparity or disagreement about discretionary judgment does not suffice. Police administration is subject to that rule; ordinary enforcement discretion identifies possible legitimate distinctions rather than an automatic class-wide exemption.

**I3 — Equal-protection judgment and remand.** Vacate the equal-protection summary judgment because the circuit made absence of improper personal motive decisive, and remand for the surviving elements and actual summary-judgment evidence. Eighty responses and roughly fifteen citations or arrests of Hilton do not establish similarly situated complaints. Conversely, probable cause for his citations does not establish a rational basis for every decision concerning another person's conduct. The reported opinion neither conclusively adjudicates all material comparator differences nor supplies a dispositive independent ground that this Court may treat as an established finding.

The remand court must assess the relevant choices and comparator evidence under the ordinary summary-judgment standard and retained rational-basis burden. It must also decide each defendant's actual participation, municipal attribution, any conduct-date personal immunity, causation and available relief where material. Damages require the relevant liability and loss predicates; prospective relief requires a continuing threat. McClendon (March 4, 2003) supplies the all-relevant-law, no-factual-twin immunity method, not an immunity outcome or a finding of clearly established unlawfulness in this case. The Petition Clause disposition remains final within this review.

### Supported alternatives and strongest objections

**Vacatur path:** Olech removes the ground the court below made decisive. The strongest objection is that summary judgment requires evidence and a remand must not reward a claimant who has not negated ordinary enforcement explanations. That burden remains fully operative. The remand corrects the legal criterion and permits application to actual proof; it does not find that Hilton supplied an adequate comparator or entitle him to trial on a mere count of citations.

**Independent-affirmance path:** A demonstrated failure of intent, material similarity or rational-basis proof would support affirmance despite the motive error. The strongest case for that route points to varied complaints, credibility differences, probable cause and officers' repeated attendance. Those are possible or partially established explanations, but the packet does not conclusively connect them to every material challenged difference. The circuit's decisive motive rationale cannot be replaced with invented comparator findings. The absence of such independent proof in the reported account is not proof that it is absent from the judicial record; the lower court must examine that record under the correct rule.

**Categorical-discretion path:** Excluding all discretionary policing would require a supported change to Olech's scope. None of the supplied prior writings establishes that categorical exception. The separate sufficient Petition Clause ground does not supply it, because absence of an affirmative-service entitlement differs from intentionally irrational administration of service actually undertaken.

## 126. Conant v. Walters

### Components, operative rule and remedy

**C1 — Protected recommendation and registration.** Reach the preserved merits. The stipulated credible risk of registration loss and the resulting restriction on physician-patient communication establish a present pre-enforcement dispute. The government may not revoke the class physician's DEA registration solely because the physician gives a sincere medical-marijuana recommendation in a bona fide clinical relationship, where the communication remains medical advice rather than participation in unlawful conduct. Professional payment or licensing does not by itself remove speech protection. The announced consequence selects the favorable medical judgment expressed in independent clinical communication; the government's authority to regulate controlled-substance transactions does not alone justify penalizing that judgment.

Casey's independent medical-speech holdings (June 29, 1992) distinguish accurate treatment information from ideological coercion and reject the payment shortcut. Rice (May 14, 1998) distinguishes ideas, foreseeable misuse and knowledge of possible misuse from purposeful, materially effective assistance to an independently unlawful act. Thompson (April 29, 2002) confirms that a regulatory program must justify its actual informational burden in the professional setting; its commercial-speech method and lawful-compounding context do not decide every medical recommendation. Rosenberger (June 29, 1995) supports careful separation of independently attributed expression from a defined government program, without converting this licensing case into a subsidy-forum case. Registration is not government authorship of the doctor's clinical view.

**C2 — Criminal and regulatory boundary.** Sincerity and a bona fide relationship do not immunize words that actually function as unlawful prescribing, dispensing, deliberate procurement, agreement or purposeful assistance. For aiding and abetting, the applicable elements require the intent to facilitate, the mental state required for the underlying offense, assistance or participation, and commission of the underlying offense by someone. Conspiracy requires the unlawful agreement and its required knowledge and intent. An expectation that a patient may independently obtain marijuana is not alone any of those predicates. The First Amendment distinction does not add elements to sections 841 or 846 or section 2, resolve anyone's guilt, or make a conviction the universal prerequisite to every lawful administrative registration decision.

The section 823(f) criteria retain their statutory work: the state licensing authority's recommendation, relevant dispensing or research experience, conviction record, compliance with applicable controlled-substance law, and other conduct threatening health and safety. The existence of those factors does not make the protected recommendation, standing alone, a sufficient disqualifying act. The decision concerns the announced recommendation-based policy and its actual threat, not a general license to disregard lawful professional requirements.

**C3 — Existing injunction.** Affirm the permanent decree at the existing amended class's actual scope and under the lower court's limiting construction. The decree restrains revocation or initiation of a revocation-directed investigation solely for the protected recommendation. It preserves good-faith investigation supported by substantial evidence of conspiracy, aiding and abetting or other criminal conduct. This is the construction of this decree, not a new universal constitutional threshold for beginning federal investigations. Probable cause to charge remains distinct from the described investigation limitation. A recommendation that functions as intentional unlawful participation is outside the protected recommendation category; the decree does not require officials to ignore evidence because it takes the form of words.

The government's stipulated threat supports effective preventive relief. Its enforcement objection identifies a potential concern, but no demonstrated interference with legitimate enforcement under the existing construction requires deleting the investigation provision. Absence of empirical interference is not a finding that recommendations can never help commit crimes. The class remains the narrowed illness/symptom and treating-physician class already certified; this handoff supplies no invented exhaustive list or enlargement.

### Alternative paths and strongest objections

**Affirm-as-construed path:** The defined recommendation is protected private communication, and both the decree's sole-ground limitation and criminal-participation construction preserve legitimate enforcement. ## 120. Norfolk Southern Railway Co. v. Kirby

### Reach and common provisional result

All eight Associates commit to reverse the Eleventh Circuit on the two reviewed limitation grounds and remand for further proceedings. The integrated international through bills are maritime contracts for this substantive liability-allocation question. Their nature and contemplated ocean carriage, rather than the inland place of the casualty alone, determine that classification. On this record there is no identified local interest requiring a different rule for interpreting the international carrier undertaking. The result does not extend COGSA by statute to every rail shipment.

The ICC bill's broadly described class of persons, including independent contractors performing the promised transport, reaches Norfolk Southern's contemplated inland performance. The Hamburg Süd bill independently binds the cargo interest at the limited subject of downstream carriage liability negotiated by the entrusted intermediary, and expressly protects inland carriers. These are separate sufficient contractual sources; the first bill does not acquire the second bill's numerical measure by association. The limited-authority rule does not make ICC Kirby's general agent or erase an independently enforceable promise ICC made to Kirby.

### Enhanced issue-specific alternative comparison

**K-1: Governing law.** Applying federal maritime law to this international carriage undertaking follows Kossick's contract-nature inquiry. American Dredging preserves substantive maritime obligations while allowing a distinct state forum-management rule; it does not require every incident of an international bill to change governing law at the port. The strongest contrary argument is that the accident and immediate defendant were inland and the bill contemplates distinct modal regimes. Those facts can affect an actual contractual liability measure, but do not turn interpretation of the through undertaking into a wholly local carriage contract. The chosen rule therefore applies to this maritime undertaking and actual contractual extensions, not as an assertion that efficiency displaces any statute.

**K-2: First bill beneficiary.** Herd requires contractual support before a noncarrier may take a carrier's limitation. A downstream carrier may rely on that shipment-specific undertaking without proving the intermediary's general agency for the cargo owner's affairs. The strongest contrary argument is actual contract separation: ICC called itself carrier, acted in its own name and might owe Kirby a different protection. The limited rule accepts those facts. ICC remains a principal in its own undertaking; Kirby's actual contractual recourse against it is preserved. The rule concerns allocation with the downstream carrier for the carriage the owner entrusted, not power to make unrelated contracts or abrogate all cargo rights. It is supported by the entrusted transaction and Great Northern, not by supposed efficiency alone.

The district court's partial ruling applied the Hamburg Süd bill's $500 limitation to the ten containers and fixed the invoked limit at $5,000. Reversal removes the two legal grounds on which that ruling was set aside; the partial limitation may be restored at that reviewed scope. It establishes neither negligence nor the amount of actual recoverable loss. The package issue was not material to the appellate decision and no universal container-as-package rule is adopted. There is no supported need to calculate a separate ICC figure because the Hamburg Süd limitation independently resolves the invoked cap.

The rejected alternative would require ordinary general agency for the second bill and confine the first clause to a direct relationship unsupported by its whole undertaking. It gives legitimate weight to contractual consent but treats the absence of general agency as dispositive of limited transportation authority, and treats the contractual mode of performance as a beneficiary exclusion. A confined remand for an actual missing limitation term could be lawful if that term were dispositive; it is unnecessary to invent such a term or to withhold the two legal answers supported by the supplied clauses and record.

## 123. Los Angeles v. David — May 19, 2003

### Components and operative commitment

**D1 — reach and disposition.** Reach the timing question already properly before the Court. Reverse the Ninth Circuit's timing disposition and remand with the constitutional twenty-seven-day-delay claim resolved in the City's favor on this record. The remand conforms the judgment to that conclusion; it does not reopen the separately rejected procedure claims or establish a repayment entitlement. No separate certiorari action is modeled.

**D2 — sufficient ground.** The actual continuing deprivation is temporary loss of the use of $134.50 after David recovered the automobile. Applying Mathews, Mallen and Gilbert's distinct scrutiny of continued deprivation, the limited financial loss, absence of supported additional error from this interval, and the practical work of arranging an impound hearing permit this twenty-seven-day interval. A vehicle-retention timetable cannot be transferred categorically to this returned-vehicle repayment claim. The conclusion is about this record and this interval, not a thirty-day constitutional safe harbor or a declaration that money is insignificant.

**D3 — material qualifications.** The City's annual volume, expedited share and possible interest compensation remain supported operational assertions, not adjudicated facts about every claimant or an actual interest award. Interest's potential to repair time-value loss helps identify the nature of the interest; it is not an automatic substitute for timely process. The officer's inability to attend the eventual hearing does not erase the practical relevance of scheduling attendance, but his actual attendance cannot be cited as a fact. David declined the continuance offers, and the examiner considered the officer's report and David's account. The rejected other procedure claims are not relitigated. California's forty-eight-hour command remains distinct from the federal constitutional standard. No finding of compliance with that state command is made.

### Adversarial comparison

**Path D-A: resolve the timing claim for the City.** The strongest objection is that the City invokes recurring workload to justify recurring retention of citizens' money, even though California prescribed faster hearings and this officer ultimately did not attend. This objection would be decisive if the Court treated workload as conclusive or disregarded demonstrated hardship, impaired evidence or a material factual conflict. The supplied record instead identifies a completed-payment claim, an actual hearing, the reasons for ordinary scheduling and no evidence that this interval damaged David's ability to present his sign-obstruction account. The constitutional balance can be resolved without declaring every municipal assertion conclusively true or declaring every monetary delay harmless.

**Path D-B: retain the claim for further adjudication or require earlier review.** Its strongest foundation is Gilbert's refusal to let justified initial action justify its whole duration and Newman's insistence on actual process facts. Its principal defect here is that the material nature of the continuing deprivation is already known, and neither the different injury in continued automobile detention nor the state-law deadline establishes an unresolved federal timing fact. Newman concerned an irreversible deprivation on pleadings without developed urgency or safeguard facts. David presents an actual hearing and a limited temporary monetary injury. A remand merely to search for an unshown timing dispute would not apply those precedents to this record.

## 127. Nevada Department of Human Resources v. Hibbs — May 27, 2003

### Components and exact scope of the proposed grounds

**H1 — statutory authorization.** All eight Associates conclude that §2611(4)(A)(iii)'s inclusion of public agencies, the incorporated State-agency definition, and §2617(a)(2)'s express action against any employer including a public agency in a competent federal or state court unmistakably authorize this private family-care action against the State agency. State-employer coverage and the remedy text perform different work. Kennedy and Thomas's Kimel incorporation objection does not transfer to this direct remedy text. Authorization is not waiver, constitutional power or an individual merits finding.

**H2 — Article I.** Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer adopt this independent sufficient ground: under Florida Prepaid, Kimel and Garrett, Congress may authorize the private action against a covered State employer when the employment duty and its enforcement fall within valid Commerce and Necessary and Proper Clause authority. Family-care leave regulates terms and continuity of the actual economic employment relationship. The authorized employee action is an appropriate way to enforce that duty. Section 2611's commerce designation is consistent with this analysis but is not a conclusive congressional declaration of constitutional power. Neither generalized social costs nor inferred consent supplies the ground.

O'Connor withholds H2 while sustaining H3 and affirmance. Kennedy, Scalia and Thomas oppose H2 and would expressly reconsider the contrary Article I State-suit rule to the extent necessary for their proposed immunity result. They do not assert that current law already contains a patent-only exception or categorical Article I prohibition. Rejection of H3 alone cannot sustain their proposed reversal while H2's controlling precedents remain applicable.

**H3 — Section 5.** Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer adopt the independently sufficient conclusion that the family-care entitlement and its express private enforcement are congruent and proportional prevention of unconstitutional sex-based State employment practices. The relevant constitutional wrong is sex-based exclusion and stereotyping in employment and administration of family leave, judged under demanding intermediate scrutiny; the Constitution is not redefined to require every State employer to grant twelve weeks of leave. A neutral and bounded care entitlement reduces the use of assumed domestic roles as a proxy for sex and prevents unequal discretionary leave from perpetuating those roles. Men as well as women are protected by that preventive mechanism.

The supporting five distinguish the legislative evidence's categories. Historical restrictions, contemporary public-sector accounts and testimony, private practices, pregnancy-disability rules, child-rearing provisions and care of ill relatives are not a single count of constitutional violations. The Massachusetts public-school account and testimony concerning public employment offer more specific public-sector support than private-company statistics alone. Pregnancy incapacity can warrant different treatment; the seven-regime list is not seven adjudicated unconstitutional denials of family-care leave. The pattern and mechanism support prevention notwithstanding those limits, because the care-role stereotype connects allocation of leave to hiring and advancement rather than only to the isolated wording of one policy. Later illustrations are not represented as evidence before Congress in 1993.

Kennedy, Scalia and Thomas reject H3 for the particular remedy's fit. They accept both heightened scrutiny of actual sex classifications and Boerne's permission for valid prevention beyond separately provable constitutional violations. Their objection is that the supplied differentiated evidence does not sufficiently connect unconstitutional State family-care practices to this across-the-board minimum entitlement and private damages remedy. They do not require a violation in every State, an incident quota, formal findings, a sunset or an individual constitutional tort in every valid preventive action. They distinguish Popovich's reasonable access duty, which directly secures a meaningful adjudicative hearing, from a freestanding leave benefit owed even where leave is administered equally.

**H4 — judgment and remedy.** The five Associates supporting H3 vote to affirm the Ninth Circuit's rejection of categorical immunity for the family-care statutory action and return the case for ordinary adjudication under the valid statutory duty. Kennedy, Scalia and Thomas would reverse that immunity determination, on the express doctrinal reconsideration and separate H3 rejection stated above. No Associate treats the department's exhaustion assertion as an adjudicated finding. Statutory coverage, employee eligibility, leave actually used, notice/certification, interference, causation and lawful relief receive their ordinary adjudication; the separate constitutional claim is not revived through a new constitutional cause of action.

### Statutory boundaries material to both fit and remand

These are the actual qualifications supporting the assessment of the scheme, not findings that Hibbs satisfies or violates them. The exact source text remains the statutory reading copy.

1. Public-agency employer coverage does not eliminate employee eligibility. Eligibility generally requires twelve months of employment and 1,250 service hours in the preceding twelve months. The statutory federal-employee exclusion and fewer-than-fifty-employees-within-seventy-five-miles worksite exclusion retain their own terms. The incorporated employee definition also retains its actual exclusions; the Title 5 exclusion is not a generic high-ranking-State-official exception.
2. Section 2612(a)(1)(C), subject to certification, covers care of the specified spouse, son, daughter or parent with a serious health condition. The statutory relationship, age/disability and inpatient-care-or-continuing-treatment definitions apply. The total is twelve workweeks within a twelve-month period for the combined statutory categories. It is not twelve new weeks per relative or episode. Self-care's distinct constitutional validity lies outside this family-care question.
3. Medically necessary intermittent or reduced-schedule family-care leave is permitted under its conditions, with only leave actually taken deducted. Foreseeable planned treatment requires reasonable scheduling to avoid undue operational disruption subject to provider approval and ordinarily thirty days' notice, or practicable notice when treatment must begin sooner. An employer may require a qualified temporary transfer for foreseeable intermittent treatment only to an available position with equivalent pay and benefits that better accommodates recurring leave.
4. Leave may be unpaid. The distinct accrued-paid-leave substitution permissions do not compel paid sick or medical leave where normally unavailable. The same-employer-spouse aggregation permission applies to birth/placement or sick-parent care, not all family-care leave. Provider certification, employer-paid second opinions on a supported doubt, jointly chosen employer-paid binding third opinions on conflict, and reasonable recertification retain their separate statutory conditions.
5. Restoration protects the same or an equivalent position and accrued preleave benefits, while creating neither benefit accrual during leave nor a right the employee would have lacked without leave. The highly compensated exception concerns restoration, not a general right to deny leave: a salaried employee in the top ten percent within seventy-five miles, substantial and grievous economic injury, required notice and, once leave has begun, election not to return after notice are all retained. Group-health coverage continues under the enacted terms. Premium recovery after failure to return remains subject to its exceptions for a qualifying serious health condition and circumstances beyond the employee's control. No exception is declared applicable to Hibbs.
6. The remedy follows the violation and loss. Lost wages, salary, benefits or compensation are distinct from the alternative for directly resulting monetary losses when such compensation was not lost; the latter alone has the twelve-week wage/salary cap. Interest uses the prevailing rate. Additional liquidated damages equal the compensatory amount plus interest. Reduction to principal and interest is discretionary only after the employer proves both good faith and reasonable grounds for believing the conduct lawful. No universal backpay cap or automatic good-faith reduction is adopted.
7. Appropriate equitable relief and the fees-and-costs provision retain their own terms. The usual limitations period is two years from the last event constituting the alleged violation, or three for a willful violation. A Secretary complaint seeking the specified restraint of payment delay under §2617(d), or recovery of the employee's specified damages under §2617(b), terminates the private-action right, unless the Secretary's action is dismissed without prejudice on the Secretary's motion. No such complaint is supplied here. The separate educational-agency rules do not supply a defense for this welfare employer. Greater statutory, local, contractual and plan protections retain the force provided by §§2651–2653.

### Adversarial paths

**H-A — affirm solely under Article I.** This is the narrowest sufficient ground under the existing State-employment precedents. Its strongest objection is structural: authority to regulate a State's employment practices differs from authority to permit a private treasury action. Florida Prepaid, Kimel and Garrett have squarely rejected that distinction as a categorical bar where duty, enforcement and authorization satisfy the stated predicates. No defect in the economic character of this employment regulation or direct action text is established. This ground preserves the judgment without resolving individual leave merits. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer accept its sufficiency, but also accept H3 as an independently supported answer to the presented Section 5 issue.

**H-B — affirm under Section 5.** Its strongest objection is that absence of any leave benefit can be entirely nondiscriminatory, while portions of the supporting evidence concern pregnancy, parenting or private employers rather than unconstitutional State family-care decisions. Its supporters answer through the sex-role mechanism and the concrete limits above: Congress can remove a recurring opportunity for discriminatory assumptions through equal availability, and the entitlement remains finite, qualified and claim-linked. Heightened scrutiny of actual sex classifications makes the protected right materially different from the age and disability rationality settings, without making all statutory breaches constitutional violations. O'Connor accepts this ground while declining H2.

**H-C — reverse on immunity.** This requires two distinct legal conclusions: explicit reconsideration of the Article I State-suit rule, and failure of this family-care remedy's Section 5 fit. Kennedy, Scalia and Thomas take both positions. Their strongest counterargument is that a repeated line of employment-enforcement holdings already establishes H2 and that heightened sex-equality protection plus the neutral care mechanism provides a stronger H3 connection than the rationality-based statutes previously challenged. They maintain the structural objection from their own Kimel/Garrett positions and find the present evidence-to-remedy connection insufficient, for the Justice-specific reasons below. They do not purport to preserve H2 as binding and nevertheless obtain reversal by ignoring it.

### W2. Read the authorization permission with its actual statutory conditions
