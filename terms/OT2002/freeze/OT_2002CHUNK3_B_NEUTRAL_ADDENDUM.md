## 30. Pierce County v. Guillen

The supplied public-disclosure judgment and tort-discovery ruling require separate treatment. The packet establishes the former's completed disclosure determination with only collateral or ministerial proceedings remaining, and the latter's interlocutory character without an identified exception. Consolidation does not confer finality on the tort ruling. The alternative objection to respondents' ability to raise the federalism defense is distinct from the enumerated-power question and should not silently become a new granted dispute.

The official 2001 text of section 409 verifies that the protection is tied to enumerated materials, their compilation or collection purpose, and the covered discovery or evidentiary uses. It reaches safety identification, evaluation or planning under sections 130, 144 and 152, or development of a highway-safety construction project that may use federal-aid funds. The 1995 amendment added collection; the 1991 amendment added discovery. Their existence is consistent with the packet and the selected baseline; no displaced Supreme Court predicate for either amendment was identified.

The material statutory alternatives are: protection confined to program-created material; protection of material collected for the program in the program holder's files while independent ordinary-purpose originals remain available; and an information-wide protection extending to those independent originals. The first must give the 1995 addition meaningful work. The broadest must confront the privilege's purpose and ordinary narrow construction. The intermediate reading requires identifying the actual purpose and custodian rather than assuming that any document concerning an accident is protected. No source reviewed establishes the final classification of each requested document.

The constitutional analysis must use the actual current Commerce standard, not the historical Lopez formula as a substitute. The relevant identified route concerns highway transportation channels and instrumentalities, and measures sufficiently connected to their safety administration. The supplied risk of discouraging hazard collection bears on the relationship between the information rule and that program. General national importance or a remote chain through accident costs does not independently supply power. A conclusion sustaining this particular program-related rule would not establish federal power over all evidence in state litigation. A spending-power holding is an alternative requiring its own conditions, not a necessary addition to a sufficient commerce ground.

**Material operative standard:** `Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects`. Its architecture originates in Lopez, 1995-04-26; Harris, 1995-04-27, supplies the unanimous transportation-instrumentality application. The register's current wording attributes the broader necessary-inclusion and externality details to Kirk, 1998-02-14; Robinson, 1998-03-23; and Home Builders, 1998-06-08, with firearm-specific later operations dated 1998-10-09, 1998-10-22 and 1998-10-29. Those later firearm applications do not become elements of section 409. The exact register is copied in the entering-law slice.

**Justice-specific authority already available:** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas joined the controlling Lopez propositions. All eight current Associates joined Harris's bounded instrumentality ground. The model must assess this different information protection independently; those joins supply relevant grounds without dictating a vote.

## 34. Barnhart v. Peabody Coal Co.

The dispositive comparison is between a mandatory completion duty whose authority survives default and a statutory allocation that closes conclusively on October 1, 1993. Each is a legal construction of the assignment scheme; neither implies that the agency met its deadline. The continuing-power theory must account for the as-of allocation provisions and express correction mechanisms. The closure theory must account for the absence of an enacted invalidity consequence and the continuing assignment task, without treating the word shall alone as an answer.

Brock, 476 U.S. 253, was reviewed in full from the official report. Its limitation in footnote 9 matters: it does not say that only express language can ever make an agency deadline terminate authority. Ordinary statutory indicia may establish that consequence. Its holding concerns CETA's actual scheme, and its available lesser-remedy discussion does not itself order relief for these operators. The packet's treatment of Brock and Good therefore needs the context qualification, not an irrebuttable no-express-sanction rule.

**Material operative standard:** `Customs forfeiture promptness duties and dismissal`, present operation effective Good, 1993-12-13. The exact register and holding are copied. It distinguishes mandatory reporting or commencement duties from an unstated dismissal sanction; it preserves separately sourced constitutional-delay relief and the actual limitations period. All then-sitting current Associates joined the statutory timing portion; Breyer's separate assessment must rest on his eligible jurisprudence and the governing rule rather than a nonexistent prior join.

**Other exact entering law:** Sigmon Coal, 2002-02-19, restricts the assignment classes despite a revenue rationale. Thomas's complete opinion was joined by Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg, as well as the Chief. Its related-person and successor limitations remain effective regardless of the deadline answer. Ventura, 2002-11-04, reserves unperformed statutory factual judgments initially to the agency unless the result is legally compelled. Bean, 2002-12-10, requires an actual denial under section 925(c)'s different review text. Neither supplies an unstated expiration of assignment authority; neither permits a reviewing court to perform an initial factual assignment itself.

A reversal limited to the deadline would leave properly preserved employment, related-person and amount objections for ordinary proceedings. An affirmance based on deadline closure would not resolve every Coal Act constitutional question or invalidate the scheme generally. No supplied fact proves all challenged assignments satisfy the substantive criteria.

## 38. United States v. Jimenez Recio

The genuine statutory issue is whether secret governmental frustration automatically ends the unlawful agreement, not whether any person appearing at a controlled delivery is necessarily a conspirator. Continuing agreement and knowing joinder remain factual elements. An agreement solely with government agents, actual withdrawal, abandonment and entrapment are distinct matters with their own predicates; neither the seizure nor the defendants' subsequent appearance establishes their resolution.

**Material operative standards:** `Knowing narcotics agreement without an overt-act requirement`, present operation effective Shabani, 1994-11-01; and, by bounded analogy, `Knowing agreement to further a RICO enterprise offense`, present operation effective Salinas, 1997-12-02. The exact standards and holdings are copied. Shabani's O'Connor opinion and Salinas's Thomas opinion were joined by all eight current Associates. Both preserve an actual unlawful agreement rather than mere awareness or association. Salinas's RICO coverage is not transposed into a new section 846 element.

The statutory-construction alternatives concern agreement as the continuing offense versus a legal-termination rule keyed to objective impossibility. The latter must explain why an unknown police seizure ends a still-supported private agreement under section 846. The former must preserve the requirement of actual unlawful participants and avoid replacing proof of joinder with the abstract existence of a conspiracy. The strongest controlled-delivery objection thus bears on the actual agreement and proof, even if automatic termination is rejected.

The requested appellate correction must be separated from unconditional reinstatement, a third trial, new factfinding or an acquittal. The second jury's convictions and the circuit's pre-seizure sufficiency approach are the supplied posture. Any remaining instruction, sufficiency or double-jeopardy issue must be addressed under its actual preservation, ground and remedy. This handoff supplies no new finding on those questions and does not assume that correction of a legal rule permits repairing a failure of proof.
