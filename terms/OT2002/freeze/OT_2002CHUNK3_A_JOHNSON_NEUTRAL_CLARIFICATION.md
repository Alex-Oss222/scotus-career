# Johnson — proposed neutral posture clarification

This clarification accompanies the unchanged Johnson Section I packet and authorized entering-law selections. It is for clean neutral validation before affected independent modeling. It adds no new historical event, litigant conduct, factual assumption or governing holding. The matter is properly before the Court on January 14, 2003 under the supplied premise.

The $38,500 attorney-fee award is an already entered judgment that Cincinnati seeks to reverse. The reported circuit court expressly identified that live collateral controversy and examined the federal-right predicate for the award. This is not a request to obtain fees in an otherwise ended dispute. The existing award provides an identified live component for appellate review within its lawful collateral scope. The circuit's legal explanation is part of the judgment under review, not controlling Supreme Court law.

State v. Burnett and cessation separately bear on prospective relief. Their exact effect on each prospective component and the final disposition of the complaint's damages request remain incompletely described in the reported account. Those source limits do not establish that the courts lacked the papers, that damages were abandoned, that a party defaulted, or that the live fee appeal requires a new factual premise before it can be identified. They supply no basis for an in-world production request.

Model the fee judgment, prospective declaration and injunction, and any genuinely surviving damages component separately. Apply Laidlaw to cessation and equitable vacatur principles to any component actually made moot. Determine which presented substantive questions are necessary to resolve the existing fee challenge and which preserved independent grounds may support the actual relief. The district court's separate double-jeopardy ground for Au France remains outside the listed movement-and-association grant; it may not be silently eliminated. Neither the fee award nor its review authorizes an advisory prospective decree.

A threshold remand must identify its actual legal error or factual issue and the component it affects. Uncertainty about the disposition of damages does not itself supply an error in the reported fee adjudication. No conclusion about local movement, intimate association, fee entitlement, the final remedy or any Justice's position is supplied here. The controlling opinion must resolve any question it reaches in accordance with Engine section 8, including its reach and the parties' remedy to the extent supported.
