# Garceau neutral specification source support

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/01-1862.ZO.html, footnote 1, quoting Appendix to Brief in Opposition 227. Exact quoted passage copied mechanically from saved source.

The reproduced portion of the filed specification states that “[b]ased on a preliminary review of case materials, counsel believes the following federal constitutional issues exist in this case and are among the issues that may be raised on [Garceau’s] behalf in a petition for habeas corpus.” App. to Brief in Opposition 227 (emphasis added).

The authenticated published quotation establishes the language quoted, not possession of the complete filing. The neutral alternatives must distinguish identifying prospective issues to sustain preparatory relief from a present request to adjudicate the habeas claims. The supplied brief and this quotation support the former characterization; no missing-file default is inferred.
