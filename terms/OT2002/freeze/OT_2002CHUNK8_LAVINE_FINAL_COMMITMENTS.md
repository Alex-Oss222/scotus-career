## Questions, law and component definitions

**A. Preventive authority and initial forecast.** The unsolicited poem is personal student expression. It is neither assigned nor school-sponsored, and its violent theme is not Fraser lewdness. Tinker permits temporary preventive action on concrete facts reasonably supporting a forecast of substantial disruption or injury; neither criminal-threat elements nor proof sufficient for involuntary psychiatric commitment is a prerequisite. The school must actually act on that lawful ground. The poem's narrator cannot simply be equated with the student, and violent expression does not create its own exception to speech protection. The specific reported context is sufficient to permit a genuine temporary safety response, as distinct from establishing that every adverse action here was constitutionally caused.

**B. Actual reason and summary judgment.** Settle's unanimous genuine-ground and causation rules apply; Henerey's unanimous framework preserves them. Proof that a lawful reason could exist is distinct from proof of the actual ground. If protected expression is shown to have been a substantial or motivating factor in an impermissible adverse decision, defendants may prove by a preponderance that the same decision would have occurred without that impermissible basis. Using the poem as relevant evidence in an otherwise lawful safety forecast is not itself forbidden motivation. The disputed issue is whether the school punished its expressive content as such, independently of the supported safety inquiry. The counterfactual does not require officials to blind themselves to the poem when assessing safety.

**C. Continued exclusion.** Initial authority does not license indefinite exclusion. The response must remain justified by the safety concern and assessment actually being pursued. The particular sequence is exclusion October 5, agreement through counsel on a district-funded examination, three Watson visits, a favorable recommendation at the third visit on October 26, and rescission and return shortly afterward after seventeen missed days. No exact referral, first-two-visit, rescission or return date is added. The record does not establish a separate period of unnecessary delay, failure to review, obstruction of evaluation or refusal to honor favorable clearance. No new daily-proof rule, fixed assessment deadline, automatic periodic-hearing requirement or presumption against all multiweek assessment is supported by the cited speech cases.

**D. Continuing file and fitted relief.** Documenting a lawful temporary safety response is different from maintaining a continuing adverse disciplinary characterization of protected expression after the school has accepted that the student may safely return. No general constitutional right to erase accurate history follows from return or an unpleasant entry. The specific backdated replacement omits subsequent clearance and preserves the unqualified adverse account; the six below regard that continuing characterization as an unsupported burden that can be cured through correction or removal of that document. The two contrary commitments see an insufficient constitutional predicate for treating documentation of the actual exclusion as a continuing sanction. All distinguish this issue from the reason for the original exclusion.

## Material evidence and its legal significance

Newell knew of the poem, earlier suicidal thoughts, the family conflict, recent breakup and reported stalking, disciplinary history and the nearby Springfield shooting before deciding. Dr. Dewitt's conclusion against involuntary commitment was also known to him. The deputy personally interviewed James; Dewitt received telephone information and did not personally examine him in this account. Their conclusion and the denials of weapon access or violent intent count against danger, but the commitment standard is not Tinker's standard. The earlier suicide thoughts, isolated incidents and reported stalking retain their dates and evidentiary character. None becomes an adjudicated offense or a reliable general profile of a dangerous student. Solitude, clothing and absences are not independently violent conduct.

The record supports both the actual safety rationale and the students' competing purpose inference. The teacher and counselor acted before expulsion; personnel met, checked homecoming plans, warned security and sought public-agency and psychiatric help. Newell's actual consideration of other facts is reported as undisputed. Later district funding of evaluation and prompt return after clearance also support a safety explanation. The original letter nevertheless denominated presentation of the poem a discipline violation and imposed a sanction, while Haney testified that he would expel anyone who wrote this poem. Haney participated in the investigation and supplied information to Newell. His testimony cannot be omitted, but it is not proof that Newell shared a categorical policy. The word discipline alone is not dispositive, since the state emergency mechanism itself bears the expulsion label.

Watson's retrospective view that assessment and removal were warranted is relevant to the later record, not knowledge the principal possessed when he decided. James's incident-free return is likewise not retroactive proof of the original motive. The father's and student's reactions after notice cannot justify the prior Sunday decision. Columbine and Santee do not enter the October 1998 forecast. The lower court's conclusion that safety predominated and its conclusion that no genuine material dispute existed are legal applications under review, not trial findings insulating those inferences from review here.
