## Components and common legal distinctions

**A. Substantive amount.** The actual judgment is $145 million punitive damages and $1 million compensation, a 145-to-1 ratio. The original $2.6 million compensation and the trial court's $25 million punitive disposition are different amounts and do not supply the denominator or a constitutionally approved replacement. The five reversing Associates apply *BMW*'s existing reprehensibility, actual or supported potential harm, and comparable-sanction guideposts under *Cooper*'s independent constitutional review. Supported historical facts retain their force. Meaningful state review under *Oberg* does not itself settle the separately presented substantive challenge.

**B. Territorial power.** *BMW* prohibits punishment for conduct lawful where it occurred and without an effect on the forum or its residents, and prohibits using punishment to impose the forum's policy on other States. Relevant outside transactions may prove intent, pattern or reprehensibility. This record does not establish the law and facts of every foreign transaction. The reversing Associates therefore do not find that each introduced foreign act was lawful, or that the jury demonstrably imposed a specified foreign-act multiplier. Their territorial instruction on remand applies *BMW*'s existing limits. It does not adjudicate the full extent of power over unlawful conduct elsewhere.

**C. Dissimilar conduct and nexus.** Common-program evidence is not all alike. The Utah testimony, altered Campbell file, payout pressure on Brown, Excess Liability Handbook, and reported document destruction tied to this litigation support a deliberate local wrong and rebut the honest-mistake explanation. They cannot be dismissed as unrelated merely because the program operated nationally or also reached first-party claims. Conversely, twenty years of disparate practices do not all become evidence of the reprehensibility of the particular bad-faith injury merely by sharing a corporate label. The reversing Associates require a reasoned connection between the evidence used to enhance reprehensibility and the conduct that injured these plaintiffs; dissimilar independent grievances cannot enlarge punishment simply by establishing the company's generally objectionable character. This is a due-process limit on the basis of this punitive judgment, not a comprehensive new code of evidence or an adjudication of absent persons' claims.

**D. Compensation and potential harm.** The record describes full compensation of $1 million for eighteen months of emotional distress and minor economic injury, with no newly caused physical injury to these insureds. Their preexisting illness and vulnerability remain relevant to reprehensibility. The death and disabling injury of the original accident victims cannot be counted as physical injuries inflicted by State Farm on the Campbells. The 1984 protection agreement and payment of the entire liability judgment in 1989 before this complaint constrain a claim of indefinitely continuing unprotected exposure; they do not erase the preceding wrong. Outrage and humiliation are compensable injuries. No source allocates a punitive portion of the compensation, and no Justice supporting reversal treats an invented percentage of it as duplicate punishment. The substantial and complete recovery bears on whether a large multiplier is justified, not on a universal rule that emotional-distress compensation itself punishes.

**E. Remedy.** The five reversing Associates would reverse the $145 million punitive disposition and remand. The sum cannot survive on the present showing simply through a fuller recital of the same broad national grievances, wealth and detection estimate. Utah must redetermine punishment for the adjudicated wrong under the stated limits, through a lawful appellate determination or a new punitive proceeding as its law and the reliable separability of the record permit. The compensation and established underlying liability remain intact. No mandatory retrial, $25 million reinstatement, replacement sum, single-digit presumption, one-to-one rule, or universal numerical ceiling is adopted.

## Necessary application and retained legal boundaries

For the five reversing Associates, intentional alteration of records, false reassurance, vulnerability and repeated connected claims practices materially distinguish the case from an innocent contract breach. The proper explanation must acknowledge those features before explaining why they do not justify this amount. Nonphysical injury can support substantial punishment. No consideration is assigned a dispositive numerical weight.

The $10,000 fraud fine is reported as the apparently most relevant civil comparison, not established as the sole applicable law or a maximum award. Possible licensing, disgorgement and criminal sanctions inform seriousness only at their actual legal and factual fit. Their prerequisites and any permissible aggregation are not supplied sufficiently to calculate a constitutional replacement. The earlier Texas verdict was a first-party matter, never reduced to judgment, and settled for a fraction of the verdict; it does not establish a prior final punitive judgment for this conduct. Neither comparison can be silently converted into a known amount or multiplicative formula.

The territorial question and the dissimilar-conduct question are distinct. Proof that national management caused this Utah wrong is permissible even though it crosses state lines. Proof of unrelated misconduct within Utah does not automatically bear on the reprehensibility of this injury merely because geography presents no federalism objection. The five's amount conclusion is independently sufficient on the supported record; it does not depend on an invented instruction, special-verdict allocation or finding that all out-of-state acts were lawful.

The state redetermination must preserve supported factual findings while selecting a punishment justified by permissible conduct, actual or supported potential harm, and the comparable-sanction inquiry. A reliable separation may permit appellate correction; an inseparable punitive determination may require a new proceeding under state law. This is an actual remand standard and choice of lawful route, not permission simply to restate the same invalid rationale.
