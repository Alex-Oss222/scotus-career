# Branch: sanitized chronology correction for fresh neutral validation

This source-only addendum corrects the distinction between the adoption of Mississippi's state-court plan and its submission to the Department of Justice. It carries no Supreme Court disposition, Justice lineup, or Supreme Court decisional reasoning. Do not alter frozen commitments without the affected fresh neutral and modeling stages.

**Source status:** The objective procedural history in Part I of the [primary opinion text, Branch v. Smith, Nos. 01-1437 and 01-1596](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/01-1437.ZO.html), reporting the lower-court orders and jurisdictional-statement appendix. The syllabus's introductory summary gives the adoption date as the submission date; the full opinion repeatedly distinguishes them. This correction follows the full opinion's record recitation, not its legal resolution.

| Date | Objective procedural event |
|---|---|
| December 5, 2001 | The federal District Court permitted state plaintiffs to intervene and deferred ruling on preliminary relief. Its scheduling order said that, unless it was clear by January 7 that state authorities could have a plan in place by March 1, it would exercise its jurisdiction and draft a plan if necessary. |
| December 13, 2001 | The Mississippi Supreme Court issued its Mauldin order concerning the Chancery Court's authority. |
| December 21, 2001 | Following trial, the Chancery Court adopted the state plaintiffs' redistricting plan. This is the adoption date. |
| December 26, 2001 | Mississippi's Attorney General submitted both the plan and the Mauldin order to DOJ for preclearance. This is the initial submission date. |
| February 4, 2002 | The federal District Court promulgated a plan for use absent timely preclearance of the state-court plan. |
| February 14, 2002 | DOJ sent a request for additional information concerning Mauldin, stating that the submitted information was insufficient and advising that the sixty-day review period would begin upon receipt of the requested information. This is DOJ's contemporaneous position, not a determination in this handoff that the request was legally valid. |
| February 19 and 20, 2002 | The State's Attorney General supplied additional information. Both dates are recited; later deadline arguments used February 20 as the completion date. |
| February 19, 2002 | The federal District Court set close of business February 25 as the point by which preclearance had to occur to avoid use of the federal map. |
| February 25, 2002 | DOJ had taken no action by the court-imposed deadline. |
| March 1, 2002 | Candidate qualification deadline identified in the lower-court scheduling history. |

The 60-day rule, the effect of a request for more information, and any effect of subsequent litigation on the running period remain legal questions. This addendum supplies no conclusion about them and no new assumption about filing, service, waiver, or preservation. The assigned event date is March 31, 2003.
