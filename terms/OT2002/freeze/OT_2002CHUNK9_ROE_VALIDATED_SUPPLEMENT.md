## Statutory and record distinctions

Section 248(a)(1) requires the prohibited means, intentional injury, intimidation, interference or attempt, and the enacted reproductive-services nexus. The two statutory alternatives must remain intact: conduct because a person is or has been obtaining or providing services, or conduct intended to intimidate that person, another person or a class from doing so. No anti-abortion-belief requirement or occupational exemption appears. Conversely, intentional physical restraint during treatment is not automatically proof of the required nexus.
