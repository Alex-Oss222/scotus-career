## 93. LaVine v. Blaine School District

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator is identified. On July 20, 2001 the Ninth Circuit affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded. It sustained the temporary exclusion but affirmed the injunction against placing or maintaining negative documentation in LaVine’s file.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Fisher wrote for Judges Betty Fletcher, Fisher, and District Judge Schwarzer, sitting by designation. The panel distinguished the emergency action from the permanent record.

**Historical Holding:** The panel used Tinker’s reasonable-forecast standard and did not require a true-threat finding before temporary exclusion. It treated the continuing adverse documentation differently after the district concluded the student could return.

**Historical Authority:** Historical lower-court anchor only. Its holding is the judgment being reviewed in this review, not controlling Supreme Court law.

**Historical Sources:** LaVine v. Blaine School District, 257 F.3d 981.

## 94. First Unitarian Church of Salt Lake City v. Salt Lake City Corp.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Tenth Circuit reversed and remanded on October 9, 2002, without a reported dissent. No historical Supreme Court merits decision is identified.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Seymour wrote for Judges McWilliams and Henry.

* **Historical Holding:** The retained pedestrian easement was a public forum and the ban on public expression invalid. Plaintiffs were entitled to summary judgment and declaratory and injunctive relief; remaining federal and state theories were unnecessary.

* **Historical Authority:** First Unitarian Church of Salt Lake City v. Salt Lake City Corp. is the historical lower-court anchor. Its use of airport concurrences does not alter controlling precedent.

* **Historical Sources:** Historical appellate anchor.

## 97. Boroff v. Van Wert City Board of Education

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** No Supreme Court merits comparator is identified for this added matter. The Sixth Circuit affirmed on July 26, 2000.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Wellford wrote for the majority, joined by Siler; Gilman dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The majority permitted the prohibition under its reading of school authority over vulgar or offensive expression; the dissent would have remanded the First Amendment claim because the record supported viewpoint discrimination.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported lower-court decision is the anchor, not an earlier Supreme Court judgment.
* **Historical Sources:** Boroff v. Van Wert City Board of Education, 220 F.3d 465 (lower-court identifier; no historical Supreme Court docket is assigned).
