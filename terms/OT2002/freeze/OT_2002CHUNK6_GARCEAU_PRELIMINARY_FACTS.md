# Garceau supplementary neutral judicial-action facts

Published record recitation, 538 U.S. 210-211. The selected sentences below are mechanically copied from the saved source; they contain the district-court procedural facts alone.

After Garceau filed a motion for the appointment of counsel, motion for a stay, and motion for leave to file a habeas application, the District Court stayed Garceau’s execution. Over the objection of the State, the District Court held that Garceau had identified non-frivolous issues so that a stay of the execution was appropriate.

This describes a preliminary assessment supporting protective relief, not adjudication of an application for a merits writ. The previously quoted specification identifies issues potentially to be raised in a later application. Both facts must remain available when testing the alternative transition rules.
