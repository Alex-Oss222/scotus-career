## Haney: independent power and right inquiries

Hale, Kirk and Wright unanimously establish the national transfer-and-stock connection for completed machine guns. Haney's local conversion and assembly fit the identified evasion concern: allowing newly completed local weapons to enter and remain in prohibited civilian stock defeats the national limit. This uses the Commerce and Necessary and Proper Clauses together. The lower court also used inherent interstate-commodity and broadly economic-activity rationales. Those additional descriptions are not automatically adopted; the controlling stock-regime ground must supply the connection before aggregation.

Section 922(o)(2)(A)'s government-related exception and subsection (2)(B)'s pre-May-19-1986 lawful-possession exception remain separate. Earlier manufacture, earlier ownership of parts and military usefulness are not substitutes for their enacted conditions. The completed weapons' machine-gun classification is undisputed; this case does not depend on treating an unfinished assembly as a completed weapon.

Wright directly governs the rights inquiry. Coverage is determined from text, history and governing precedent, without a militia-service gate. If protected activity is burdened, actual operation determines the justification required. A core deprivation requires compelling justification, narrow tailoring and no comparably effective materially less restrictive alternative. A substantial burden requires an important objective, substantial relationship and preservation of meaningful protected activity. A genuinely incidental burden receives reasonableness review, subject to the prohibition on arbitrariness, discrimination and cumulative destruction. The categorical protection of meaningful ordinary defensive home possession does not itself immunize every weapon.

The genuine alternatives are (a) vacatur of the arms disposition for Wright's coverage, severity and justification inquiry, with the commerce challenge resolved; and (b) a supported direct decision on the arms application if the preserved record actually supplies the necessary grounds. The strongest objection to remand is that the mechanism, limited post-1986 stock rule and retained defensive alternatives may establish justification without new proceedings. The strongest objection to direct affirmance is that the lower judgment's militia premise supplies no adequate rights application and the weapon label alone proves neither burden class nor fit. Direct invalidation likewise needs support for both a constitutional violation and the resulting criminal remedy; individual coverage alone does not compel acquittal. No automatically required new hearing, study or trial follows.

Emerson's supported individualized protective adjudication is relevant to burden-sensitive regulation, but its danger finding and civil-order safeguards are not facts of this weapon-defined restriction. Bean's actual-denial requirement for statutory disability relief does not displace an independently presented constitutional defense.

## Altman: jurisdiction, function, exercise and relief

The school-specific record must stay separated. The Ganesha and worry-doll matters arose at the elementary school, and the nature tape arose at the middle school. The Earth Day event at issue occurred at the high school. The circuit's 2001 conclusion that the DiBaris retained high-school standing is not proof of their exact May 19, 2003 status. Tiana's graduation from middle school in 1999 supplies a chronology marker; it establishes neither a later high-school graduation nor continued enrollment.

Harvey, decided February 25, 2003, unanimously requires a determination of actual remaining effectual relief before constitutional merits where the record does not establish it. Arizonans, Spencer and the existing mootness authorities permit corrective jurisdiction and a factual remand without hypothetical merits jurisdiction. A remand can require identification of each plaintiff's concrete, still-redressable claim and then either jurisdictional dismissal with separate equitable treatment of judgments or adjudication of an identified live claim. Fees alone do not preserve the merits controversy. Do not assume that a remand restores every district injunction automatically.

The contrary threshold path must rest on actual evidence that the remaining high-school claim remains live or has become moot, rather than ordinary-school-progression assumptions. The original input authorizes deciding from the available record; it does not authorize inventing a damages claim or a class action. Steele permits municipal standing on a supported probable local fiscal effect; a fixed paid sum is not invariably required. Its broader conception of probable fiscal effect does not eliminate the need for a concrete supported connection here. Paid staff and a general taxpayer contribution to the district budget do not alone supply it.

If a live high-school controversy is established, Agostini's unified attribution-and-function inquiry governs. Official school worship is prohibited under Lee and Santa Fe; sincere secular purpose and optional attendance would not cure actual official worship. Commack further confirms that official adoption of religious truth or allocation of civil power to religious office can violate the Clause without a separate conversion or coercion finding. But the identified high-school evidence consists of metaphorical speeches, drums, a globe, conservation themes and structures identified in the record as tepees; the source does not establish an altar sacrifice or teaching that Earth is divine. A statement coinciding or conflicting with Catholic teaching is not, for that reason alone, religious establishment.

New York Education Law section 810's direction to assemble pupils for conservation exercises supplies the asserted educational setting. It must not be confused with the principal's testimony that this Earth Day attendance was optional. A statutory educational command and a factual finding about attendance are different propositions. Applicable trial-finding deference must be distinguished from independent constitutional classification. The circuit's obsolete separate Lemon-prong analysis is not current law.

The exercise claim separately asks whether government targeted religious conduct, compelled affirmation, or imposed a concrete burden or practical pressure. Mere disagreement with instruction is insufficient. Lee forecloses treating the absence of punishment as universally dispositive; Lukumi and Shahar preserve targeting and general-applicability analysis. On the reported record no credited evidence establishes compulsory Earth Day observance or a penalty for religious adherence. The strongest opposing merits path would need actual devotional function or concrete exercise burden, not migration of another school's evidence.

The circuit identified independent Rule 65(d) specificity defects in the mandatory guidelines provision and overbreadth in districtwide prohibitions unsupported at every school level. These are distinct remedial grounds, not proof that the underlying activities were permissible. The fee award was $106,856.61; its final treatment depends on the surviving judgment and actual fee entitlement. Preserve the distinction between jurisdictional vacatur and merits reversal in each component.

## Hilton: legal element versus summary-judgment proof

Olech's controlling objective rule was joined by eight Justices, with Breyer concurring on the additional vindictiveness allegation. It makes personal ill will unnecessary to the objective claim; the lower court's statement that the absence of improper motive is decisive cannot stand as that rule. Intentional materially unequal treatment and lack of a rational basis remain required. The challenger must negate reasonably conceivable legitimate grounds. Animus can be evidence without becoming a mandatory element.

The current record is summary judgment, not Olech's pleading posture. About eighty responses and fifteen citations or arrests against Hilton do not establish comparability. His concession of probable cause for his own citations does not establish that every decision about a neighbor was rational; nor does it prove that the opposing complaints had identical conduct, evidence, seriousness, credibility or enforcement circumstances. The circuit speculated about legitimate possibilities but made the absence of improper motive decisive.

The genuine alternatives are (a) vacate the equal-protection judgment for application of the correct rule to actual summary-judgment evidence, or (b) affirm on a genuinely supported independent failure of intent, comparability or irrationality. The strongest objection to remand is that the challenger already bore the surviving evidentiary burden and mere unexplained differences cannot defeat summary judgment. The strongest objection to independent affirmance is that the legal error shaped the court's analysis and the reported account does not conclusively establish a substitute fact-specific ground. A categorical immunity for discretionary policing would be a new legal limitation requiring its own basis, not an application of a limitation Olech already contains.

The Petition Clause protects presentation of grievances against unconstitutional obstruction or retaliation; it does not assure favorable enforcement or an affirmative police service. Smith v. Arkansas State Highway Employees, 441 U.S. 463 (1979), supplies the distinction between protected advocacy and an obligatory governmental response. DeShaney's no-general-protection principle and Pinder's limited treatment of assurances do not cancel separate equal-protection duties. Police actually received and responded to Hilton's complaints. This claim presents no supported obstruction, retaliation, custody or affirmative-danger predicate.

Individual participation, municipal attribution, conduct-date qualified immunity, causation and the continuing-threat predicate for prospective relief retain their separate work after any reversal. McClendon's immunity method does not establish that a particular defendant's conduct violated clearly established law here.

## Conant: recommendations, participation and the decree

The inquiry concerns the existing decree as construed, not a general exemption for licensed medical speech. A sincere recommendation in a bona fide clinical relationship can communicate medical judgment without purposefully facilitating an unlawful acquisition. The parties distinguish mere discussion from actual unlawful prescribing or dispensing. Rice, unanimously joined, makes purposeful materially effective assistance to an independently unlawful act different from abstract advocacy, foreseeable misuse or knowledge of possible misuse. It does not supply new federal drug-offense elements.

The lower opinion states the applicable aiding-and-abetting elements as specific intent to facilitate another's crime, the required intent of that underlying offense, assistance or participation, and commission of the underlying offense by someone. Conspiracy requires the unlawful agreement and the required knowledge and intent. The question is prospective relief from a speech-targeted policy; no individual physician is being adjudged guilty or innocent. The permanent order's anticipation language therefore cannot be read as an immunity for an agreement, deliberate procurement or a prescription functioning as the means of unlawful acquisition.

The public-interest factors quoted in the source under the then-applicable section 823(f) are the state licensing authority's recommendation; dispensing or research experience; relevant conviction record; compliance with applicable controlled-substance laws; and other conduct threatening public health and safety. Their existence does not itself answer whether protected advice may be treated as disqualifying conduct. The government's registration interest and its ability to enforce sections 841 and 846 and section 2 must be kept separate from the First Amendment classification of the actual speech.

Casey establishes that medical speech remains constitutionally protected while allowing bounded accurate consent information. Rosenberger's private-viewpoint rule is program-specific and cannot be mechanically converted into a licensing holding. Thompson's governing compounding-speech decision supplies scrutiny of genuine informational burdens and actual regulatory fit, while preserving regulation of unlawful transactions. A paid professional relationship does not itself collapse advice into regulable commercial conduct. Rust's distinction between a defined government program and independent private expression remains relevant; the registration policy here reaches independent doctor-patient communication.

Affirmance under the limiting construction and modification of an actually overbroad decree are both supported paths. The strongest enforcement objection is that a prohibition on initiating investigation could prevent officials from discovering purposeful unlawful facilitation. The response must use the decree's actual scope: it protects recommendation as the sole ground, while preserving good-faith investigation supported by substantial evidence of conspiracy, aiding and abetting or other crime. Do not transform that case-specific construction into a universal constitutional prerequisite for commencing all investigations. The source separately mentions probable cause to charge; the threshold for charging and the construction of the administrative-investigation restraint are not identical.

The government's stipulated credible registration threat supports pre-enforcement injury and the reported record supplies no empirical interference with legitimate enforcement. The absence of such proof does not establish that a recommendation could never form part of a crime. If modification is required, identify the actual excess and preserve fitted protection against revocation or investigation solely for protected advice. The existing class was narrowed to specified illnesses/symptoms and treating physicians; the reported description supplies no authority to invent a broader class or publish an unverified exhaustive list. The lower concurrence's scientific and federalism discussion supplies advocacy context, not a finding of universal efficacy or a power to override federal drug law.
