## 59. United States v. Wagoner County Real Estate

### Historical comparison and final component commitments

There is no verified historical Supreme Court merits comparator. The comparator is the unanimous Tenth Circuit judgment by Seymour, with Brorby and Lucero, affirming the homestead and trial rulings and reversing and remanding the excessiveness determination. It supplies the judgment under review and published record, not Supreme Court votes or mandatory authorship.

All eight Associates affirm the two reviewed components of that appellate judgment: the federal forfeiture statute defeats the asserted Oklahoma homestead immunity, and the district court must make adequate findings and conduct a fresh constitutional excessiveness determination. Affirming the appellate remand does not affirm the entire-property exaction. The existing statutory liability verdict stands; the remand concerns constitutional proportionality and its supporting findings.

The shared statutory ground is actual positive conflict under the offense-date 21 U.S.C. §§881(a)(7) and 903. The federal statute reaches qualifying real property, including the relevant interest and improvements, subject to its own owner exception and constitutional limits. An additional categorical state homestead immunity would defeat that remedy. State definition of an ownership interest is distinct from state immunity against its federal forfeiture. Sprietsma confirms why actual conflict must be demonstrated; general uniformity or occupation of property law is insufficient.

The shared constitutional ground is Austin coverage plus Bajakajian's gross-disproportionality boundary and the developed application in Mackby. Statutory connection, punitive coverage and actual excessiveness perform distinct work. The court must relate the exaction to the actual offense and owner involvement on supported findings. Property value, offense gravity, culpability, harm, benefit, connection and authorized or applicable penalties matter when relevant; no compulsory exhaustive checklist, automatic maximum-fine safe harbor, fixed ratio or instrumentality-only immunity is adopted. Appellate courts review the legal constitutional conclusion independently and supported facts for clear error.
