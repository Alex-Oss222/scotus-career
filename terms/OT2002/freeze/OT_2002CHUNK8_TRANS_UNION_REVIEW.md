## Repairs identified and verified complete

1. **Holding 3, operative qualifications: preserve the precise nonfinancial-customer-list exclusion.** At draft lines 140 and 411, replace `Lists of customers of an entity that is not a financial institution` with `Lists of names and addresses of customers of an entity that is not a financial institution`. Rule 313.3(o)(2)(ii)(A), reproduced in `../sources/chunk8_b/operative_text.md`, excludes the identified names-and-addresses list. The present abbreviation can extend the exclusion to arbitrary customer-data lists. The narrower source wording preserves the adopted source-and-content distinction.

2. **Holding 5, operative rule: make the independent exception alternative explicit.** At lines 170 and 441, the phrase `including compliance with the originator's privacy policy and each consumer's actual opt-out and any applicable exception` can make a consumer's ordinary opt-out a condition even for an otherwise exception-authorized transfer. Rule 313.11(b)(2)(ii) separately permits disclosure under the originator's policy as limited by the consumer's opt-out, and disclosure under an applicable rule 313.14 or 313.15 exception, such as disclosure to the recipient's attorneys or accountants. Recommended replacement: `including disclosure under the originator's privacy policy as limited by each consumer's actual opt-out, or disclosure under an applicable rule 313.14 or 313.15 exception`. The general lawful-direct-disclosure comparator remains in the sentence. The shared notice paragraph already preserves these exemptions, but the standalone operative rule must express the alternative accurately.

3. **Holding 6, operative qualifications: distinguish authorization from inability.** At lines 180 and 451, replace `if that recipient cannot directly initiate charges` with `if that recipient is not authorized to directly initiate charges to the account`. Rule 313.12(b)(1) uses an authorization condition for the agent/service-provider exception. Rule 313.12(c)(2), by contrast, excludes a transaction account to which third parties cannot initiate charges. Keeping both conditions in their own terms avoids converting the former into a technical-incapacity test. The frozen neutral addendum uses the correct authorization formulation. The reproduced frozen commitment at draft line 304 uses the abbreviated wording; preserve that frozen copy rather than silently modifying it, and identify the source-precise final formulation as clarification of the same adopted qualification.
