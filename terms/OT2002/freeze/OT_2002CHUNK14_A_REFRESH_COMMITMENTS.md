## 1. American Insurance Association v. Garamendi

### Refreshed material premises

The German arrangement is more specific than a general preference for voluntary settlement. Within the described ICHEIC scope, insurance claims against German companies must be processed through the companies and German Insurance Association under the identified procedures and any additional procedures agreed by the specified participants. The ICHEIC process combines reasonable file review, investigation of filed policies, relaxed claims proof, valuation and payment. The October 2002 supplement provides 200 million deutsch marks for approved claims and part of operating expenses, a further 100 million reserve if needed, and a 350 million contribution to the humanitarian fund. It includes an undertaking to work toward as comprehensive a policyholder list as possible; it does not guarantee a complete list or payment of every asserted claim.

The United States undertakes to file statements favoring the Foundation as the exclusive forum and remedy for the described German claims and to use best efforts, in a manner it considers appropriate, to secure state and local respect for the mechanism. It does not guarantee that foreign-policy interests independently establish a legal dismissal ground; it supports dismissal on a valid legal ground. The claim-processing commitment, the policy undertaking, the limits on that undertaking and the judicial question of legal effect are distinct.

Austria provides a separate $25 million fund for claims processed under ICHEIC procedures, an exclusive-forum understanding for the described Austrian claims, and publicly available policyholder lists to the extent available. France's agreement does not address insurance. No single universal European claims or disclosure rule is attributed to these different instruments.

HVIRA reaches the specified life, property, liability, health, annuity, dowry, educational and casualty policies in effect in Europe between 1920 and 1945, not only policies shown to belong to Holocaust victims. It demands policy status, policyholder location and beneficiary information for a public registry. Its related-company reach includes relationships formed after issuance. The supplied definitions distinguish affiliates, managing general agents and reinsurers; a reinsurer here is a parent, subsidiary or affiliate providing reinsurance, not every independent reinsurer. Default carries mandatory license suspension; false required representations concerning distribution carry the distinct described misdemeanor sanction. Reporting is not itself payment adjudication.

The Commission Act is not an unconditional insurer-production mandate. Section 3(a)(4)(A) directs the Commission to take note of the association's work and encourage its report on the identified insurers and policies issued to listed Holocaust victims. Subparagraph (B) states suggested report contents to the degree information is available: numbers of covered policies, issue-time values, numbers and amounts paid, and present United States assets. That qualification applies to those contents and does not cancel the Commission's duty in (A). Subparagraph (C) separately requires coordination with the identified Washington Conference. Austria's availability qualification belongs to its own agreement, not this statute.

### Components and provisional results

G1 — Federal foreign-affairs displacement: O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer would reverse the judgment sustaining the challenged enforcement because the compulsory reporting scheme, in its application to the identified national insurance-claims arrangements, obstructs the operative negotiated settlement method. Stevens, Scalia, Thomas and Ginsburg reject that preemption ground and would sustain the law against it. The latter four retain the distinction between federal claims processing and state reporting; the former four find that the full information-processing-funding arrangement makes the conflict concrete even though compliance with both sets of literal commands is not impossible.

G2 — Foreign Commerce Clause: Stevens and Ginsburg reject the independent challenge under the actual insurance function, congressional allocation and independent absence of a demonstrated constitutionally essential uniformity requirement beyond the disputed G1 conclusion. Scalia rejects it on his narrower method; Thomas rejects the negative Commerce Clause as a judicial prohibition. O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer do not enter an alternative Commerce holding after their sufficient G1 ground. Their G1 votes are not additional joins to the sustaining G2 rationale.

G3 — Legislative jurisdiction: Stevens, Scalia, Thomas and Ginsburg reject the presented connection objection on the distinction between information about a locally licensed insurance enterprise and adjudication of every foreign policy's payment obligation. The breadth and later-created affiliation matter but do not on this record negate the legitimate connection of the supported information demand. O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer leave this independent constitutional ground undecided because G1 supplies their disposition. They do not find that California lacks authority over all foreign information, and no G3 invalidation receives a commitment here.

The four G1 reversers seek relief confined to conflicting HVIRA enforcement and dependent consequences, with ordinary implementation and any necessary severability questions for remand. They do not adopt a universal prohibition on state information about every foreign insurer or every European policy. The four sustaining Justices would affirm on all three granted grounds. The separate payment and limitations statutes, the ungranted procedural-hearing theory and additional claims in the Gerling petition remain outside the decision.

### Enhanced G1 comparison

**Operative-method ground adopted by O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer.** The national claims-settlement authority recognized in Dames & Moore, Belmont and Pink permits examination of the actual executive arrangements, not merely the existence of negotiations. The refreshed German and Austrian instruments establish interlocking methods of claims handling, information production, funding and intended legal peace. ICHEIC file review and publication are part of the negotiated mechanism rather than incidental details. HVIRA independently compels a materially broader historical inventory and public registry, including nonvictim policies and subsequently related companies, on pain of loss of a current license. Applied to the claims and companies within the actual national arrangements, that sanction imposes a competing information-production method and bargaining pressure on the operation of the settlement mechanism.

These four apply Crosby's demand for an identified operative federal method and a concrete conflicting state duty. They do not rely on diplomatic objections alone, infer a federal privilege from mere nonregulation, or claim that the Commission Act itself forbids state disclosure. The affirmative negotiated allocation of claims processing, information work and resources distinguishes this case from Sprietsma's refusal to prescribe equipment because the evidence did not justify a national requirement. Barclays reserved the legal effect of an actual binding executive agreement and retained independently applicable constitutional review; its rejection of policy-only displacement is not a rule that a nationally negotiated claims-settlement method can never displace a state duty without an express preemption clause.

The strongest contrary argument is substantial: the agreements promise advocacy and best efforts rather than a legal guarantee, process insurance claims rather than bar state reports, and themselves seek more disclosure. The Commission Act contemplates state commissioners' participation, while McCarran-Ferguson affirmatively recognizes state insurance regulation. These four answer that a court must itself find the legal ground and actual obstruction, as the agreement's reservation requires. Their finding does not turn the promised policy statement into an independent dismissal command. Nor does Congress's general insurance allocation affirmatively authorize this interference with the identified international settlement method. They do not hold that McCarran-Ferguson has no work whenever a President objects; the function and statutory trigger remain important, and an actual congressional direction incompatible with the claimed executive authority would present a different question. The Commission's encouragement of a qualified report is not such a direction and does not endorse every compulsory affiliate demand.

**No-demonstrated-incompatibility ground adopted by Stevens, Scalia, Thomas and Ginsburg.** The same exact terms show the limits of the national undertaking. Requiring claims to be processed through ICHEIC, funding that process and seeking a comprehensive list do not expressly or necessarily prohibit independent public information concerning policy obligations. The United States promised legal advocacy and discretionary best efforts, while explicitly leaving legal grounds to courts. A mandatory state report can impose costs and diplomatic pressure without legally defeating the fund or directing a different disposition of claims. The State may seek truthful information from insurers it licenses without deciding the substantive enforceability of every foreign policy.

These four apply their own Crosby joins and the binding-authority distinction in Barclays. They accept that actual executive settlements can have legal force and that impossibility is not the exclusive form of conflict. Their objection is to finding an affirmative federal protection from the combination of voluntarily structured information production and a general legal-peace objective without a demonstrated displaced claims obligation or legally exclusive disclosure arrangement. Sprietsma's nonrequirement distinction remains useful by analogy, not a holding about presidential power.

The strongest contrary argument is that legal peace can be defeated through license pressure without a formal state payment judgment, especially given the Act's broader coverage. These four acknowledge that practical operation rather than labels controls. They nevertheless find the supplied requirements compatible at the legally operative level: supplying the registry need not bypass ICHEIC, withdraw promised funding, alter claim valuation or adjudicate a payment the mechanism reserves. The Commission Act's limited, qualified reporting provisions support the relevance of information gathering without themselves authorizing HVIRA. McCarran-Ferguson reinforces the need to respect the real policyholder-information function; it is not an automatic bar to every executive or constitutional preemption claim. Their conclusion does not immunize a genuinely conflicting future application.

### Enhanced G2 and G3 treatment

The strong G2 invalidation path identifies unusually broad demands about foreign transactions and a subject of active national settlement. Stevens and Ginsburg examine the actual insurance function rather than accept an insurance label: disclosure of policy performance and potential claims relates to the policyholder relationship recognized by Fabe and the state role recognized in Prudential. They independently find no demonstrated need to make all such disclosure nationally exclusive. They do not treat congressional silence, or the failure of G1 in their view, as dispositive of commerce. Scalia's method asks whether the arrangement presents facial geographic discrimination or a materially identical arrangement already invalidated; he finds neither established merely by the historical European policy subject. Thomas's separate ground rejects the inferred negative-Commerce prohibition. O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer regard the full scope as relevant evidence of G1 obstruction but do not turn that observation into an independent G2 holding.

The strong G3 invalidation path argues that a current license cannot expose every historical foreign affiliate transaction to unlimited state control. The four sustaining commitments agree that licensing alone has no such effect. They find the supported reporting demand connected to the licensed enterprise, potential obligations and information about the family of insurers with which residents transact. Hague and Shutts preserve meaningful territorial connection but do not make a request for policy information identical to imposing California payment law. Later affiliation, broad dates and nonvictim policies make the question substantial; they do not eliminate the connection of every identified demand. An unreasonable particular application remains distinct. The four G1 reversers do not decide whether that connection independently suffices. None imports Barclays' particular reporting accommodations into HVIRA or decides the hearing question outside the grant.

### Changed modeling premises and remedial limit

O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer change their provisional G1 commitments from sustaining to invalidating the challenged conflicting enforcement. The material new premises are the specified mandatory claims-processing route, actual ICHEIC information procedures, funded settlement architecture, legal-peace undertakings and the precise breadth and sanctions of the state report. Their ground is obstruction of that identified method under established federal claims-settlement authority. They do not infer a statutory prohibition from the Commission Act, a complete-list obligation from best-efforts wording, or a promised legal result from the agreement's advocacy language. Stevens, Scalia, Thomas and Ginsburg retain affirmance after considering those same premises and their express limits.

The four reversing votes support reversal and remand for appropriate relief against conflicting HVIRA reporting enforcement and dependent consequences within the demonstrated arrangements. The lower courts may address any genuinely necessary scope and severability question under ordinary law; this handoff does not presume a severability clause or impose a new statewide field prohibition. No European policy outside the demonstrated conflict is automatically immunized. The four affirming votes support denial of relief on all three granted grounds, with their G2 rationale differences preserved. ## 2. Green Tree Financial Corp. v. Bazzle

### Refreshed material premises

The integrated clause submits all disputes concerning the contract, resulting relationships, the validity of the arbitration clause and the validity of the entire contract. It selects one arbitrator through the seller-side party with customer consent, identifies the FAA, describes arbitration as the preferred forum subject to its exceptions, waives jury trial, and grants lawful contractual legal and equitable remedial powers. The lender retains the specified judicial or nonjudicial options to enforce the mortgage, deed of trust, related security, secured monetary obligation or foreclosure. The nonwaiver provision preserves arbitration of other covered disputes, including a counterclaim in the lender's permitted suit; it does not eliminate the retained option.

The customer definition reaches the named buyers and guarantors jointly and severally; the seller definition reaches the named seller, successors and assigns. Those definitions neither add unnamed class members to the named buyers nor conclusively prohibit every procedural form the defined parties may have authorized. The Lackey/Buggs variation uses the buyer wording rather than standardizing the Bazzle term. The validity submission is material to delegation, but the scope of that submission and actual class authorization remain distinct questions.

### Components and commitments

B1 — FAA operation in state court: Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer apply the controlling state-court rule. Thomas would affirm on his retained statutory nonapplication position. The contract's reference to the FAA does not itself transform the statute's jurisdictional or forum reach; any state-law incorporation remains distinct from federal statutory displacement.

B2 — Initial decisionmaker: Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer commit to an independent arbitral construction of the disputed collective procedure within the accepted arbitration. The full dispute, relationship, validity and remedy language reinforces their conclusion that this is interpretation within the submitted arbitral undertaking. O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy require independent judicial determination of the actual representative-scope and delegation questions; the express validity submission does not clearly and unmistakably delegate this distinct class-authority question merely because it delegates some validity controversies. Thomas reaches neither federal allocation rule as a reason to disturb the judgment.

B3 — Contract meaning and silence: the seven vacatur Justices reject a categorical authorization or prohibition based solely on the absence of express class words. The actual integrated contract must receive its proper construction. The four arbitral-path commitments do not hold that the definitions or selection mechanism authorize a class; the three judicial-path commitments do not hold that those terms prohibit one. Broad remedies, validity language, FAA selection and jury waiver are not interchangeable forms of class assent.

B4 — Awards and mandate: seven would vacate the state judgment sustaining both awards and remand for the appropriate independent contractual determination, divided 4–3 on B2. The expanded text does not resolve whether either arbitrator independently construed class authority rather than following the judicial silence premise. The different certification sequences therefore remain material but do not independently cure that concern. Thomas would affirm under B1. No Justice decides the separate absent-class-members due-process theory found unpreserved below, the underlying statutory violations, damages or a new federal award-review ground.

### Enhanced comparison

**Arbitral interpretation.** The four supporting this path apply Howsam's distinction between foundational consent and the administration of agreed arbitration. Both named borrower disputes are covered, and the complete clause broadly assigns interpretation-intensive controversies concerning relationships and validity, along with legal and equitable remedies, to the chosen tribunal. Mastrobuono requires reading these provisions together rather than treating a singular term as a self-executing limitation. PacifiCare confirms the arbitrator's initial interpretive role in its separate remedial setting, without itself deciding class procedure. The contractual validity submission strengthens the conclusion that these parties entrusted substantial interpretive work to arbitration; it is not treated as universal delegation of every possible claim about an absent person's agreement.

The strongest contrary argument is that the named-party definitions and one-arbitrator consent mechanism make collective adjudication a change in representative authority rather than an internal procedure. The four recognize that First Options and Specht require actual assent or a valid nonsignatory basis when that is the question. They classify the supported present dispute more narrowly as the procedure those contracting parties permitted for their covered borrower disputes. They do not hold that an absent person is automatically bound, that each customer loses a preserved selection right or that class treatment must be chosen. The arbitrator must independently construe the actual bargain, including its court-remedy reservations and selection mechanism.

**Judicial scope and actual delegation.** O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy give the express validity language full effect without equating validity with every scope or representative-authority question. The disputed issue is whether the particular assent and selection mechanism authorizes adjudication through a class representative; that is not simply whether the clause is legally valid. First Options requires clear and unmistakable delegation of the actual arbitrability question. General relationship language, broad remedies and a specific validity submission do not conclusively establish that distinct delegation. A court must determine it under ordinary applicable contract law rather than infer it from a federal preference for arbitration.

The strongest contrary argument is that this analysis fragments an unusually comprehensive submission and turns ordinary procedural interpretation into a judicial gateway. The three answer that the asserted selection and representative-authority change concerns what the parties submitted, rather than when or how an undisputed submission proceeds. They do not turn every use of the word buyer into a ban on aggregation, classify all imaginable class questions identically or require express class language in every agreement. If ordinary construction establishes actual authorization or a valid delegation covering this dispute, that agreement must be enforced subject to its actual terms and ordinary review.

**Affirmance and the existing awards.** The strongest affirmance path reads the full clause and neutral state procedural defaults as adequate contractual permission and treats the awards as permissible implementation. The new text makes that path more substantial than bare silence alone. It does not, however, settle the preserved selection objection or establish that an authorized decisionmaker independently construed both contracts without the court's premise. The seven therefore retain bounded vacatur. Their remand must be decisionmaker-specific; it does not order automatic bilateral arbitration or forbid reinstatement of a lawfully supported award through the governing procedures. Thomas's affirmance remains antecedent: the FAA cannot supply the ground for federal reversal of this state-court disposition under his retained construction, and the express contractual reference does not by itself alter that conclusion.

### Refreshed topology, mandate and changed premises

No Bazzle judgment vote changes: seven support vacatur, divided four for arbitral interpretation and three for judicial scope/delegation analysis; Thomas supports affirmance on state-court statutory nonapplication. The expanded clause materially strengthens the four-Justice arbitral explanation and requires the three-Justice judicial explanation expressly to account for the validity submission rather than rely on a generic description of a broad clause. Both paths preserve the defined parties, single-arbitrator selection, retained court remedies and nonwaiver qualification. None finds the contract's final class meaning established by the refresh alone.

The four and three allocation rules are not logical subsets: each assigns the disputed initial determination to a different tribunal. Their common vacatur and rejection of bare silence as conclusive cannot be aggregated into a controlling decisionmaker rule. The awards receive no final merits validation or categorical invalidation; the state judgment sustaining them is vacated under the seven commitments for the bounded further determination. The mandate must preserve ordinary statutory review and contract-specific authority and cannot manufacture new arbitral power. Final judgment and rationale coalitions await assembly.
