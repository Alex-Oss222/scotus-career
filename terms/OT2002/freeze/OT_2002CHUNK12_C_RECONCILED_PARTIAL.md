The rule rests on the combination of the enacted maritime prohibition, its express foreign-vessel consent mechanism, the maritime relationship alleged, and fair warning of cooperative enforcement. International condemnation is relevant context but cannot substitute for conduct-time law, under Lanier and Rogers.

Malta's later enforcement consent implements the existing coverage mechanism; the adjudication does not retrospectively invent the drug-conspiracy prohibition. The search consent and enforcement consent remain distinct.

## 152. Star Scientific, Inc. v. Beales — June 16, 2003

### Components and operative rules

**T1 — standing.** All eight Associates accept standing for the presented local escrow injury and the associated Compact Clause challenge. The obligation is concrete; the settlement's substantial financial incentives for adopting and retaining qualifying statutes support traceability despite the intervening enactment. Relief against the challenged scheme could remove the plaintiff's obligation. This establishes the presented case, not standing to litigate every settling State's obligations or entitlement to nationwide relief.

**T2 — due process and equal protection.** All eight affirm dismissal of the pleaded constitutional challenge. The Commonwealth may require cigarette manufacturers selling to its consumers to reserve funds for possible future lawful recovery, distinguishing firms that have made settlement commitments from firms that have not. That legitimate public connection satisfies Beach's economic review; it is not merely preservation of incumbent profits. The manufacturer keeps interest; principal pays the described judgment or settlement, annual qualifying excess can be returned, and otherwise unused principal returns after 25 years. The reserve creates no current tort-liability finding. BMW and State Farm's punitive limits remain law but do not convert this prospective escrow mechanism into an adjudicated punitive award or bar consideration of products actually reaching Virginia through intermediaries.

**T3 — Commerce Clause.** Six Associates join ordinary Commerce analysis rejecting the presented territorial, discriminatory, and excessive-incidental-burden theories. The statute measures cigarettes ultimately sold in Virginia, does not command outside prices or sales in markets outside Virginia, and distinguishes settlement participation rather than local origin. The entire settlement and escrow operation must be considered, but the pleaded mechanism is not West Lynn Creamery's dedicated transfer to local producers. The presented loss-of-use burden does not establish a clearly excessive incidental interstate burden relative to the lawful security and public-health objectives. Scalia and Thomas reach the same judgment on their distinct boundaries below; their votes are not automatically counted for the six-Justice rationale.

**T4 — Compact Clause.** All eight reject the claim. Virginia v. Tennessee and United States Steel ask whether the interstate arrangement enhances state power against federal supremacy. Coordinated bargaining and administration of otherwise available state authority do not suffice. Congress remains able to regulate tobacco and displace conflicting state law; the agreement's cooperation does not supply the States an immunity from that power. Because congressional consent is unnecessary under that rule, no decision on the alternative claim of actual congressional consent is needed.

The strongest competing economic path identifies a settlement cartel that penalizes innocent entrants, ties up capital, and pressures national behavior through intermediary sales. The response must address the actual statutory mechanism: prospective security for lawful local recovery, retained beneficial ownership and release terms, and an in-state consumer-sales measure. A hypothetical more burdensome tax is not independently sufficient to validate this statute. The strongest Compact alternative stresses aggregate power and strong enactment incentives; it confuses power over private bargaining partners with displacement of national supremacy unless an actual federal intrusion is shown. The complaint's local injury supplies neither that missing legal premise nor a blanket nationwide remedy.
