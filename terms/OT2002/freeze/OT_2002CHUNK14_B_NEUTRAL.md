## 170. Nike: supported threshold and merits alternatives

The collection's own listing located docket 02-575, item `micro_IA40385014_0611`. Both merits briefs were retrieved. Nike's statement of the case and Kasky's complete threshold argument at pages 14-19 were read. Other portions and the complete record were not read in full, and no negative archival claim is made.

The parties agree that the California Supreme Court reversed the demurrer dismissal, leaving falsity, liability and relief for further proceedings. Nike describes six classes of challenged statements in public responses and correspondence concerning labor conditions. Kasky invokes six representations in nine communications and argues that a communication-specific reversal may leave others in litigation. The statements' truth and defamatory or deceptive character have not been adjudicated. Neither a corporate speaker nor the public controversy conclusively supplies the classification.

Kasky's threshold argument quotes Nike's reliance on the fourth Cox category: a final federal determination, reversal precluding the relevant litigation, and serious erosion of federal policy from delayed review. Nike invokes the burden on continued public responses, its asserted present legal obligation under the state decision, prospective liability, and the demurrer's declaratory function. Kasky distinguishes ASARCO's judgment altering tangible rights, disputes present concrete injury, and argues that further demurrers, amendment, liability proof and remedies remain. He states that a fault-based reversal would permit an amended negligence allegation and that reversal concerning fewer than all communications would leave litigation. Those are adversarial alternatives, not a judicial finding that amendment will necessarily be permitted or that every speech restriction causes cognizable injury.

The private-enforcement theory and asserted liability standard present a separate preservation question: Kasky says the broad challenge to suits by uninjured private plaintiffs was neither pressed nor passed upon below. The existence of the question in this Court's presentation does not automatically cure that objection. The approved question remains before the Court as scheduled; this is a scope issue for judicial resolution, not a demand to verify the grant.

The finality inquiry is independently substantial. A settled interlocutory legal issue and claimed litigation expense do not alone establish a Cox exception under Jefferson, Florida v. Thomas and Pierce County. Nor may state-court standing rules be mechanically replaced with Article III. Nike's own asserted injury must be assessed separately. A jurisdictional dismissal leaves the state judgment and ordinary further proceedings in place without endorsing its merits. If jurisdiction and preservation permit merits adjudication, each communication and the actual statutory theory must be identified; a blanket rule about all corporate public debate would exceed these supplied facts. Falsity, fault, remedy and classification are distinct.
