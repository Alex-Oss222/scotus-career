## Controlling source premises and boundaries

The enactment is Virginia Acts 1999, chapter 754, approved March 28, 1999, effective July 1, 1999. The inquiry concerns the prospective challenge dismissed under Rule 12(b)(6). The preserved appeal does not include the separately pleaded takings claim. No individualized escrow calculation, judgment on a tobacco claim, adjudicated wrongdoing, forfeiture, release determination or compliance penalty is before this modeling stage.

The statute requires a manufacturer selling cigarettes to Virginia consumers, directly or through the specified intermediaries, either to participate in the MSA and generally perform its financial obligations or to deposit the scheduled, inflation-adjusted amount per Virginia unit by April 15 of the following year. The deposit base is Virginia units, measured through the Commonwealth's excise stamps. A qualified escrow arrangement restricts access to principal; interest or appreciation belongs to the manufacturer as earned. The precise release provisions matter to all three components:

- **B1:** Principal can pay a judgment or settlement on an MSA-defined released claim brought against the manufacturer by the Commonwealth **or an MSA-defined releasing party located or residing in the Commonwealth**. Releases must follow the order of deposit and occur only to the extent and at the time needed for payments required by that judgment or settlement. This is neither a Commonwealth-only clause nor permission to pay any claimant anywhere.
- **B2:** If the manufacturer establishes that its required escrow for a particular year exceeds the **Commonwealth's allocable share of the total payments that this manufacturer would have been required to make for that year had it been a participating manufacturer**, the excess **shall** be released and revert to it. The hypothetical payment is determined under MSA §IX(i)(2), before §IX(i)(3) adjustments or offsets **other than the Inflation Adjustment**. This is an annual, conditional, mandatory excess-return right. It does not compare required escrow with unallocated total MSA payments, eliminate inflation, promise automatic return without establishing the excess, or establish a particular amount here.
- **B3:** Each deposit's remainder, to the extent not released under B1 or B2, must return twenty-five years after that deposit. Neither all principal being unavailable for twenty-five years nor recovery of principal properly paid under B1 is a permissible premise.

The MSA details are used only at the supported level. The source-supported economic comparison has three categories: (1) original participants make continuing nonrefundable payments, undertake conduct restrictions and receive specified releases; (2) qualifying subsequent participants who signed within the described ninety-day period undertake binding conduct restrictions and owe no settlement payment while national market share remains within the greater of 1998 share or 125 percent of 1997 share, with payments due above that grandfathered ceiling; and (3) nonparticipants avoid those settlement conduct restraints, retain current interest and the statutory conditional principal rights, but must fund escrow. Not every participant pays a monetary substitute for escrow. Nothing here finds equal net costs, future liability or risk among these categories.

Star's argument that its entire escrow secures future claims whereas only five percent of participant payments addresses future harms remains part of the comparison. The lower opinion recites the consent-decree allocation underlying that argument. It does not establish that the remaining settlement obligations have no relationship to future conduct or costs, or that the Constitution requires matching contributions to future liability dollar for dollar.

The published source establishes an existing distributor stamp-recording and reporting mechanism. It does **not** establish manufacturers' automatic entitlement to those records, easy access through independent distribution chains, a particular tracing cost, or minimal incremental burden. The lower court's minimal-burden conclusion is an inference supporting its judgment. Star's stated need to monitor interstate sales or alter contractual arrangements is a preserved operational argument; this handoff does not invent additional complaint allegations, cost evidence, incompatible state commands or a denied request for records.

## Entering-law applications common to the component tables

Beach Communications, June 1, 1993, requires a reasonably conceivable legitimate connection for ordinary economic classifications, permits imperfect fit and dispenses with formal legislative findings, while preserving Moreno and Cleburne. Fitzgerald, June 9, 2003, reaffirmed that rule with all eight Associates joining Breyer's opinion: a real economic package can pursue several legitimate purposes and need not confer the largest possible benefit on every affected firm. It did not approve a self-justifying preference merely because favored firms benefit. Craigmiles, January 14, 2003, likewise sustained a concrete public connection and left naked incumbent protection as the sole end unresolved; Stevens's dissent supplies his stronger objection to an inadequately connected entry burden.

Concrete Pipe, June 14, 1993, separates preliminary enforcement from adjudication and sustains a rational economic allocation in its particular pooled-pension setting. Its substantive-due-process portion was joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas. It supplies no rule that every reserve or every deprivation preceding judgment is valid. Eastern Enterprises, June 25, 1998, invalidated its actual tier-three assessments without a controlling affirmative rationale. Its retrospective-assignment facts and divided rationales cannot become a holding against a prospective tobacco reserve. Nor does its majority proposition about unspecified general assets decide a takings claim concerning an identified escrow fund; that claim is outside this appeal.

Pike remains a separate inquiry into whether an evenhanded incidental interstate burden is clearly excessive relative to putative local benefits, considering the nature of the benefit and a supported less burdensome means. Sorrell, March 17, 2003, keeps territorial reach distinct from practical interstate effects and rejects evenhandedness as a categorical answer. Its preliminary-injunction disposition cannot be transposed into a Rule 12(b)(6) requirement that Star already prove final invalidity. Scalia and Thomas withheld affirmative endorsement of Sorrell's general balancing rationale. Walsh, May 19, 2003, applies actual-transaction and competitor inquiries; Scalia's separate position and Thomas's foundational objection remain distinct. Hillside Dairy, June 9, 2003, rejects blanket Commerce immunity and preserves ordinary adjudication of the particular claim; it neither proves discrimination here nor orders a trial whenever a plaintiff invokes commerce.

For the six Associates supporting an incidental-burden remand below, the precise common commitment is limited: vacate the rejection of the preserved Pike theory insofar as it rests on the unsupported inference that existing reports necessarily make manufacturers' tracing burden minimal, and remand for ordinary assessment of the actual pleading and any properly considered material. No new pleading rule, automatic discovery entitlement, compulsory empirical cost study, burden shift, final finding of excessiveness or present injunction follows. Dismissal may remain appropriate on a legally sufficient ground under ordinary procedure. Existing reports and the exact release protections are substantial considerations in Virginia's favor, not conclusive factual answers.

## Due process — prospective security rather than present adjudication of liability

**Competing legal paths.** Sustain the prospective economic reserve because securing eventual lawful recovery on the defined Virginia-connected claims bears a rational relationship to health and fiscal interests; alternatively invalidate, or require further proceedings, if its actual statutory operation is an arbitrary extraction or punishment for refusing to settle rather than rational security. The strongest objection to sustaining it is the immediate loss of access to capital without a present accusation or judgment, potentially for twenty-five years, coupled with the settlement's express competitive objectives. The strongest answer to invalidation is that prospective regulation need not await culpability: B1 requires a judgment or settlement before payment on a claim; B2 requires return of a proved annual excess; B3 returns the remainder; interest remains the manufacturer's. Those safeguards limit the burden without pretending that interest eliminates lost liquidity. The eight rows resolve this component only.

## Commerce Clause — incidental burdens and the actual local escrow

**Competing legal paths.** Sustain dismissal because Virginia regulates only local sales, the statutory protections constrain the capital burden and existing stamp reports provide a practical information mechanism; alternatively vacate the Pike disposition because those propositions do not establish the incremental burden on a manufacturer reaching Virginia through independent interstate distributors. The strongest answer to remand is that ordinary nondiscriminatory compliance costs and a general assertion of more burdensome contracting do not by themselves establish clearly excessive interstate burdens. The strongest answer to affirmance is that a court cannot resolve the factual weight of those asserted burdens against the challenger merely by equating distributors' possession of reports with manufacturers' access to them. The tables distinguish an ultimate burden of proving excessiveness from the legal adequacy of dismissing this claim on the stated ground.

The local benefits are putative rather than adjudicated damages: maintaining resources for eventual lawful payment of the defined Virginia-connected claims and protecting health and fiscal interests. B1's other locally situated releasing parties do not destroy that local connection. The exact B2 release and B3 return reduce the possible extent or duration of retained capital but do not establish an amount released here, eliminate funding costs before release, supply missing distributor access, or settle the separate tracing burden. No row presumes that the entire amount of a neutral local deposit is itself a discriminatory interstate cost.

## Scope of remand, preserved issues and source limits

For each Associate supporting the Pike remand, a complete permissible analysis must use the actual statute rather than a twenty-five-year total-retention shorthand. It may consider the burden of funding and administering escrow, but must identify the claimed interstate aspect, account for current interest and the exact B1–B3 limits, and keep theoretical advantages or disadvantages distinct from supported operational facts. Any asserted less burdensome alternative must be concrete enough to compare while serving the identified legitimate interest; none is found available or equally effective by this handoff. Ordinary pleading rules, properly noticeable statutory material and any later authorized evidence determine how much additional work is necessary. The remand requires neither a new annual assessment nor production of an entire MSA archive.

No new statutory detail changes the independent standing, territorial-reach or Compact premises identified in the neutral packet. Conditional principal-return rights do not erase the initial compelled funding and loss-of-access injury. B1 remains bounded by claims brought by the Commonwealth or defined releasing parties located or residing in Virginia; B2 is a release comparator, not a new command regulating all national transactions. Neither provision establishes out-of-state price control, local-producer preference or federal-supremacy encroachment. Those components are not reopened here. Nothing supplies a ground for restructuring the nationwide settlement.

Nguyen's June 9 rule concerns participation by a statutorily ineligible appellate judge. The Star source describes a recusal followed by a decision of the remaining qualified quorum, not an ineligible participant whose prior participation is erased after judgment. The new statute creates no composition premise and warrants no independent Nguyen adjudication in this affected stage.

The incorporated MSA definitions, numeric allocable share and any individual IX(i)(2)/IX(i)(3) calculation remain unestablished at the complete contractual-detail level. No commitment depends on selecting one, finding an excess proved, determining an actual B1 entitlement or treating a manufacturer as delinquent. A later dispute requiring those propositions must obtain the pertinent date-eligible MSA text and actual assessment or release record. The present published account also does not reproduce the entire complaint; the remand positions therefore do not certify unstated allegations or hold that the complaint necessarily survives every lawful Rule 12(b)(6) ground. They reject the particular unsupported factual shortcut while permitting ordinary adjudication of the preserved theory.
