# Ayers supplementary neutral preservation facts

Published record account, 538 U.S. 135, notes 17 and 19; source provenance retained by control.

The reported record states that Norfolk requested exclusion of cancer-fear damages through the proposed probability-of-cancer and physical-manifestation requirements. It did not request a charge requiring proof that fear be genuine and serious, a particularized verdict form separating fear damages, or plaintiff-specific sufficiency review of that character. The formulation requiring fear to significantly impair everyday life and work was not presented at trial or in this Court. These are affirmative preservation recitations, not inferences from an unrecovered document.

The supplemental facts do not decide whether a new rule, the actual objection, or the available review doctrine supports affirmance, vacatur or retrial. They prevent treating a seriousness-only charge or individual sufficiency challenge as if it had been requested. The original trial instruction and actual rejected instruction remain as copied in AYERS_SOURCE_FACTS.
