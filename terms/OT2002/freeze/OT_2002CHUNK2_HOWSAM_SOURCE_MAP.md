# Howsam — objective source addendum

The Joint Appendix in No. 01-800 was downloaded from the Internet Archive Records and Briefs collection and read in full. It contains the client agreement, pleadings, submission agreements and the identified NASD provisions. The following facts were verified directly from that appendix.

- The client service agreement is dated August 26, 1992. Section 18 selects New York law for construction and enforcement, without reference to choice-of-law doctrine. See printed page 6.
- Section 19 provides for arbitration of controversies concerning the customer's accounts, transactions, or the construction, performance or breach of the parties' agreements, including controversies predating the agreement. The customer may select a self-regulatory organization or exchange of which Dean Witter is a member. See printed pages 6–8.
- The appendix contains signed customer submission agreements invoking the selected organization's constitution, bylaws, rules, regulations and arbitration code. See printed pages 24–38. These do not establish that Dean Witter separately signed the same submission form.
- NASD Rule 10304 appears on printed page 48. It imposes a six-year eligibility limitation measured from the occurrence or event giving rise to the act or dispute, claim or controversy; it does not extend applicable statutes of limitations and does not apply to a case directed to arbitration by a court of competent jurisdiction.
- NASD Rule 10324 appears on the same page. It empowers arbitrators to interpret and determine the applicability of all Code provisions and to take appropriate compliance action; its interpretations and compliance actions are described as final and binding on the parties.
- The July 1997 amended complaint alleges diversity jurisdiction and seeks declaratory and injunctive relief against arbitration. Its account of purchase dates, accrual and eligibility remains a litigant's allegation. See printed pages 39–46.
