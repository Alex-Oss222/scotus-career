# Chunk 14 C STONE

## 171. Alkire v. Irving

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

Apply Bearden, the prompt probable-cause rules, and function-specific municipal attribution. Retrospective relief is the live controversy; settled prospective relief supplies no authority for a new injunction. Entity immunity, statutory personhood, and any claim-specific Heck bar remain antecedent to a damages judgment.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm reinstatement of the two granted claims while leaving denial of Alkire’s own summary-judgment motion intact. Remand under the defendant-specific eligibility and attribution rules; preserve ungranted dispositions.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Determine the County Court’s actual state-arm and statutory status, the eligibility of each damages theory, the custody and contempt grounds, attributable policy, causation, and compensable loss. A valid immunity or eligibility ground disposes only of the affected defendant or theory. No automatic damages, release, class certification, jail-credit formula, or replacement prospective decree follows.

* **Remedial Reach:** The surviving retrospective claims concern Alkire’s proved injury. Official-capacity pleading does not establish a class or a County policy; the settled prospective claims are not reopened. General constitutional guidance does not itself create policy-wide damages.

* **Practical Relief:** Identify the surviving claims and suable entities without reopening the prospective settlement. For any theory alleged to contradict a contempt judgment, identify that operative judgment and compare the civil claim’s necessary legal consequences with it. A compatible theory proceeds without favorable invalidation. For a necessarily contradictory former-prisoner theory, apply Randell to the actual custody periods, notice, available procedures, access, timely efforts and obstacles: absence of a realistic opportunity or its faultless loss permits ordinary civil adjudication; an unexcused failure to use a genuinely available opportunity does not. Resolve genuine material disputes through ordinary procedure rather than assume diligence or an available remedy. A claim still covered by Heck without qualifying invalidation is dismissed without prejudice and accrues only upon that invalidation; independent law determines accrual for claims outside the prerequisite. On eligible claims, distinguish warrantless from actual warrant-based custody and nonpayment punishment from independently lawful nonappearance punishment. Any damages judgment must identify the attributable deprivation and proved causal loss; failure of one theory does not automatically defeat the other.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Imprisonment for inability to pay

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The absent means inquiry and nonpayment language support a live damages theory, but conflicting descriptions of contempt and the court entity’s status prevent final liability. A Heck issue must be resolved claim by claim, including the actual outstanding judgment and available review opportunity.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May government incarcerate for fines and costs without determining ability, reasons for nonpayment, and the adequacy of alternatives?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Bearden’s existing protection and ordinary §1983 attribution, without creating a wealth tier or personal-immunity defense for a governmental defendant.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. A usable pre-incarceration inquiry protects the demonstrated liberty interest while retaining legitimate punishment. A broad automatic-damages rule would ignore eligibility, attribution, and the actual contempt ground. A narrow reliance on the installment signature would erase later inability and the required inquiry.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Before imprisoning a person because fines or court costs remain unpaid, the tribunal must determine the reasons for nonpayment, distinguish willful refusal or inadequate bona fide efforts from inability despite those efforts, and in the latter situation justify incarceration by the inadequacy of lawful alternatives. In the civil action, Alkire must prove by a preponderance the actionable deprivation, the defendant’s attributable policy or decision and required fault, and causal loss; the government cannot establish a lawful nonpayment incarceration merely by pointing to debt or a past agreement. The present evidence permits trial but does not conclusively establish that every term punished nonpayment.

* **Reach Within the Question:** Actual nonpayment-based imprisonment and its attributable institutional cause. Independently lawful punishment for nonappearance remains distinct; the government must show that it was the real basis, not a retrospective label.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Apply Bearden to ability and bona fide efforts at the relevant incarceration, not solely when an installment promise was signed. Apply Heck’s necessary-invalidity comparison and Randell’s adopted opportunity rule to any preserved damages bar. Release alone proves neither eligibility nor a bar; the court must identify whether realistic lawful review existed and, if lost, whether timely pursuit failed for reasons beyond the claimant’s control. This is no exhaustion requirement to pursue every conceivable procedure.

* **Transition:** Not applicable.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Bearden joins liberty and equality by requiring reasons for nonpayment and consideration of alternatives, not automatic conversion of poverty into jail. Alkire’s letters describing disability and treatment and the nonpayment orders support that claim; the mixed contempt descriptions prevent appellate selection of one account as fact. Monell requires the governmental defendant’s own attributable wrong, while personal judicial or qualified immunity does not automatically shield an official-capacity defendant. Randell requires examination of actual custody-review opportunity rather than a categorical former-prisoner bar or an invented exception for every release. Its rule determines whether a necessarily contradictory theory may proceed; it proves neither the deprivation nor municipal responsibility. The actual deprivation and function-specific attribution remain decisive on eligible claims, and neither replaces the separate inquiry into a suable court entity.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Bearden’s inquiry and alternatives rule, Monell’s policy and causation limits, and McMillian’s function-specific attribution. Apply Randell’s resolution of Heck’s former-prisoner reservation, preserving Heck for claims still covered and ordinary accrual rules outside its prerequisite. Official-capacity labels establish neither liability nor immunity.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The actual contempt basis, entity status, and loss need findings. Independent Thirteenth Amendment, jail-credit, and class matters are outside the grant.

##### Issue 2: Prompt judicial probable-cause determination

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The nearly 72-hour interval and alleged weekend practice support the warrantless-detention claim. The outside warrant’s actual role remains disputed and cannot be established for the entire interval by a later holder alone.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Can an attributable local practice delaying warrantless arrestees beyond forty-eight hours for ordinary weekend scheduling support §1983 liability?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the Gerstein–County of Riverside–Powell framework and separate it from municipal attribution and damages proof.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The existing presumptive boundary directly governs a recurring scheduling practice. A broad liability rule for every interval over forty-eight hours would omit actual custody authority and extraordinary circumstances. A narrow routine-weekend exception would contradict the prompt-review guarantee.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** For warrantless custody without a judicial probable-cause determination beyond forty-eight hours, the government must demonstrate a bona fide emergency or other extraordinary circumstance; ordinary weekends and regular court scheduling do not suffice. Within forty-eight hours, the claimant may still show unreasonable delay for an improper purpose. Alkire bears the civil preponderance burden of establishing the pertinent warrantless detention and, for municipal damages, an attributable policy, its required culpability, and causal injury. The unsupported assertion of an outside warrant does not defeat a genuine dispute about the actual basis of nearly seventy-two hours in jail.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The actual warrantless interval and attributable scheduling policy, preserving a genuinely valid independent warrant basis and extraordinary-circumstance defense.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Gerstein’s required judicial determination, Riverside’s time-and-justification allocation, and Powell’s implementation form one prompt-review inquiry. Keep that inquiry separate from Monell’s institutional attribution; compliance or breach by an employee alone does not answer the latter.

* **Transition:** Not applicable.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Gerstein requires a judicial probable-cause determination for extended warrantless custody; Riverside and Powell prevent routine scheduling from replacing promptness with the next convenient session. The alleged inability to obtain weekend review supports a recurrent practice, but McMillian requires identifying which government controlled the relevant function, and Carter requires proof of the actual culpable municipal choice when that theory is asserted. The government’s extraordinary-circumstance burden is decisive for excessive delay once the warrantless premise is proved. The separate warrant, causal duration, and policy facts explain remand rather than an automatic award.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm and restate Gerstein, Riverside, and Powell as the integrated prompt-review test. Apply McMillian and Carter to the separate attribution and culpability inquiry; preserve Monell’s causation requirement without inventing a scheduling safe harbor.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The actual warrant basis, governmental function, attributable duration, and damages need further findings; no general reorganization of Ohio courts is before the Court.

## 172. United States v. Gatewood

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

Apprendi, Angle, and Harris govern facts enlarging binding punishment authority but leave recidivist facts unresolved in entering law. Stone would extend their constitutional protection to those facts, while preserving Almendarez-Torres’s actual statutory element holding and ordinary collateral-validity limits.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate affirmance of all three mandatory life sentences and direct that those sentences be set aside. Remand for lawful resentencing while preserving the convictions and ungranted suppression rulings.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Begin with authority supplied by the existing indictment and verdict: the twenty-year Hobbs Act ceiling and the otherwise lawful kidnapping ranges without mandatory life. A discretionary life sentence for kidnapping requires an actual lawful exercise of discretion and reasons, not automatic carryover of the invalid compulsory result. No new enhancement prosecution is authorized merely by remand; any such proceeding needs independent charging and procedural authority and compliance with finality and double-jeopardy law.

* **Remedial Reach:** The three mandatory sentences are affected. The constitutional rule governs recidivist facts enlarging a maximum or mandatory minimum, while final sentences of absent defendants require a lawful review vehicle. Convictions, ordinary criminal-history consideration within authorized ranges, and collateral attacks on prior judgments remain distinct.

* **Practical Relief:** After the mandate, the sentencing court must vacate the compulsory sentences, identify each independently authorized range, and enter a reasoned new sentencing judgment. It may not delete the nonqualifying-robbery protection to expand mandatory life, infer a valid enhancement admission from sentencing notice, or treat a possible discretionary life sentence as curing the mandatory one. Any request for renewed aggravated proceedings must be adjudicated on an identified independent source of authority before it occurs; absent such authority, resentencing proceeds without the enhancement. No fixed aggregate sentence or immediate release is ordered.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: The factual boundary of qualifying strikes

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Gatewood preserved the burden objection. The sentencing court treated failure to prove exclusion as sufficient for compulsory life; lowering his burden would not answer who must prove the facts creating that additional authority.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May §3559(c) require the defendant to disprove weapon, threat, or injury circumstances determining whether the prior robberies qualify for mandatory life?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Hold the defendant-side clear-and-convincing allocation unconstitutional for these punishment-authorizing facts and extend the constitutional proof rule to recidivist qualification.

* **Reach Calibration:** Broad. The function of the qualifying fact, rather than its statutory label, determines criminal proof protection. A sweeping rule for every mitigating circumstance would exceed facts enlarging authority. An intermediate reduction of the defendant’s standard would still assign him the risk of unproved enhancement elements.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** To obtain mandatory life under §3559(c), the prosecution must prove beyond a reasonable doubt the facts making the necessary prior convictions qualifying strikes, including the weapon use, threat of weapon use, death, or serious bodily injury required to defeat the nonqualifying-robbery exclusion. The defendant’s inability to prove their absence is not proof of their presence. Prior judgments supply admissible evidence of what they necessarily established, not an exemption from the present prosecution’s burden or permission to direct a disputed element finding.

* **Reach Within the Question:** Facts necessary to qualifying-strike status and increased punishment authority, not every mitigating consideration or the defendant’s independent attack on an old judgment’s validity.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Identify the actual prior statute, judgment, and necessarily adjudicated facts rather than infer them from labels such as armed or aggravated. Documentary evidence remains subject to ordinary admissibility and the present adjudicative safeguards stated in Issue 2.

* **Transition:** This new allocation applies to Gatewood and nonfinal criminal cases on direct review under Griffith. It does not itself reopen final sentences. Until this Court decides whether a separately presented collateral claim satisfies an applicable retroactivity exception for this new procedural protection, courts apply Teague and the independent statutory habeas requirements; no exception is presumed merely from severity of punishment.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apprendi, Angle, and Harris locate the constitutional boundary at enlargement of punishment authority. A statutory exclusion cannot transfer the risk of uncertainty about that boundary to the defendant. Patterson’s genuine affirmative defenses and Parke’s collateral-validity presumption address different functions; Cooper’s error-risk reasoning supports but does not replace the criminal element rule. Gatewood’s missing decades-old witnesses neither prove his account nor establish the government’s qualifying facts. The Fifth and Sixth Amendment allocation is decisive, with actual statutory function identifying its application.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Extend Apprendi, Angle, and Harris to recidivist qualifying facts. Distinguish Patterson and Parke’s different allocations; use Cooper only as corroboration of error-risk concerns. Preserve ordinary collateral-validity rules and lawful within-range mitigation.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The complete Arkansas judgments and offense elements are required to identify what was previously adjudicated; the Court does not find Gatewood’s account true or retry the prior crimes.

##### Issue 2: Charging, jury adjudication, and the preserved omission

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The separate indictment-and-jury claim is preserved. Mandatory life raises the Hobbs Act ceiling and removes every lesser kidnapping sentence; both alter legal punishment authority despite different preexisting maxima.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Must the necessary prior-conviction predicates receive indictment, jury, and reasonable-doubt protection, and what follows from the preserved charging omission here?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Adopt a new constitutional recidivist rule and extend Almendarez-Torres’s charge-bound protection to those elements. Do not falsely describe entering law’s reservation as a controlling recidivism exemption.

* **Reach Calibration:** Broad. Both expressly presented questions require protection of the facts enlarging binding punishment. A sweeping rule assigning all sentencing judgments to a jury would destroy lawful selection within a range. An intermediate weapon-only answer would leave the prior-conviction exception and preserved charging omission unanswered.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A prior conviction necessary to increase an authorized maximum or mandatory minimum must be alleged in the federal indictment and proved beyond a reasonable doubt to a unanimous jury, unless validly admitted through a lawful charging and waiver process. The protected facts include existence, identity, and any necessary historical qualification or chronology; judges decide statutory meaning and the legal classification of established facts. A lawful jury waiver permits a judge to decide the facts under the same proof standard but does not expand the charge. Here the preserved absence of the enhancement allegations, with no valid charging waiver, independently requires vacatur of compulsory life.

* **Reach Within the Question:** Federal charging and punishment-authorizing recidivist facts. The jury does not retry guilt of the earlier offense or decide abstract statutory meaning. The rule does not incorporate federal grand-jury requirements against States or forbid history-based discretion within an independently established range.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Use authenticated records and ordinary admissible proof; require a specific informed admission rather than infer one from the current offense verdict or plea. Apply Old Chief’s Rule 403 comparison when an actually available, unconditional and legally adequate admission proves the complete status element and the conviction’s prejudicial name or nature adds no sufficient legitimate value. A partial admission need not be accepted, and a genuine separate evidentiary purpose requires its own balance. An admission adequate to establish one prior status does not establish additional disputed chronology, conduct or identity facts, or cure an omitted indictment allegation. A separately authorized enhancement phase should avoid prejudicial use of criminal status, and is required if a combined proceeding would create an intolerable guilt-determination risk. Until this Court decides a separately presented renewed-enhancement or acquittal-like-finality claim under this new rule, courts apply Monge and the governing charging, sufficiency, and double-jeopardy law; a resentencing mandate supplies no independent permission. Sattazahn leaves Monge’s noncapital holding intact; its completed-capital-life rule cannot itself authorize or prohibit a renewed enhancement here.

* **Transition:** Apply the new recidivist and charge-bound holdings to this and other nonfinal cases on direct review. Final collateral claims remain subject to the procedural-rule treatment and independent gates described in Issue 1. Almendarez-Torres’s statutory construction and Cotton’s nonjurisdictional characterization remain unchanged.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** An earlier judgment establishes liability for an earlier offense, not every present allegation authorizing compulsory punishment. Reliable records may simplify proof but do not transfer the constitutional role to a judge. Almendarez-Torres’s actual statutory holding makes an admission insufficient to enlarge an indictment omitting its aggravated element; Stone expressly extends that protection to the recidivist elements recognized here. Cotton does not transform a preserved omission into forfeiture. Neder and Ring retain their qualified actual-verdict inquiry for errors to which it applies: the government must establish noncontribution beyond a reasonable doubt through the actual adjudication, not a hypothetical verdict. Stone does not declare every omitted finding structural, but the independently preserved charge boundary resolves these sentences. For kidnapping, the availability of discretionary life does not cure removal of all lesser choices. Sattazahn does not settle this case by calling both sanctions life imprisonment. It protects a final life judgment after a completed capital opportunity; Gatewood instead challenges how recidivist facts authorize compulsory noncapital punishment. Its preservation of Monge leaves the distinct indictment, jury and proof issues open. Stone’s broader protection must stand on the element-allocation reasoning stated here, not on his separate, noncontrolling due-process rationale in Sattazahn.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Extend Apprendi, Angle, and Harris to the identified prior-conviction facts and extend Almendarez-Torres’s charge-bound protection while preserving its statutory holding. Reaffirm Cotton’s nonjurisdictional distinction and Neder–Ring’s actual-verdict harmlessness inquiry. Preserve Monge and distinguish Custis’s collateral-validity setting; neither is silently overruled. Daniels’s restriction on using §2255 to reopen expired counseled predicates after meaningful original review likewise does not answer this direct-review claim about current enhancement elements; its counsel-deprivation exception and unresolved faultless-review question retain their own limits. Distinguish Sattazahn’s capital-finality holding and apply its express preservation of Monge to the unresolved renewed-proceeding boundary.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Specific authority for renewed enhancement, the complete prior judgments, and any acquittal-like finality claim require issues or facts outside this adjudication. Final collateral retroactivity is outside this direct appeal and receives the interim rule stated above.

## 173. Eichorn v. AT&T Corp.

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

Rule 56 requires supported inferences for the employees, not final findings of naked allocation or specific interference intent. Antitrust agreement, competitive injury, and classification remain separate from §510 purpose and authorized ERISA relief.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate dismissal of the antitrust claims concerning independent sale participants and remand for supported ancillarity and competitive-effects analysis. Affirm reinstatement of the §510 claims against AT&T and Lucent concerning the specified pre-closing and post-closing restraints, with appropriate class-related discovery. Preserve the distinct earlier internal restriction.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Permit adjudication of the surviving claims under their respective elements. Antitrust damages require compensable antitrust injury and the statutory prerequisites; any equitable relief requires an actual eligible claim. ERISA relief must fit the invoked §502 route, including Great-West’s limits where §502(a)(3) applies. Neither damages, a class, nor liability of an unnamed §510 defendant follows from reinstatement. Any class request following the permitted discovery must satisfy Bridgestone/Firestone’s actual workable-arrangement inquiry; discovery is not certification, and common hiring restraints do not eliminate material differences in employee eligibility, injury or relief.

* **Remedial Reach:** The employees’ preserved claims against the actual defendants return for adjudication. No market-wide injunction or class award is entered before liability, standing, and representative requirements are established.

* **Practical Relief:** The district court must identify the restraint’s actual participants and timing, permit necessary discovery, and assess the 245-day restriction under Issue 1. It must separately decide whether the pension evidence supports prohibited interference by AT&T or Lucent. Before any certification, the court must test an actual proposed arrangement separately for the antitrust and ERISA claims. The discovery must identify which employees and employers were subject to each restraint, how the 245-day restriction affected an employee’s six-month bridging opportunity, and what common or individual proof establishes competitive injury, interference, causation and the relief sought. A single covenant does not answer those differences. If a supported arrangement cannot fairly adjudicate the material differences, the court must deny or limit that requested certification while leaving viable individual claims intact. Bridgestone/Firestone supplies no categorical class prohibition and resolves no independent issue-certification question. At trial the factfinder resolves genuine disputes under the applicable civil burden; the judgment must link the proven injury to an authorized remedy. An employer’s sale rationale alone cannot end both inquiries, and an ERISA claim cannot produce unrestricted compensatory damages by relabeling them equitable. For requested restoration, the court must identify the §502 route, the defendant’s authority over the affected plan status, the protected right and the causal effect of the proved interference. Varity permits genuine individual equitable restoration within the plan’s operative terms; it neither freezes lawful future amendments nor supplies a §510 violation. Where §502(a)(3) is invoked, another adequate ERISA remedy normally makes additional equity inappropriate. A demand for an accrued personal debt remains legal under Great-West; a claimed lien or constructive trust requires specifically identifiable property, equitable entitlement and the necessary connection to its holder, while other equitable categories retain their distinct predicates. The decree must identify the correction or property actually authorized rather than promise all lost wages or benefits as compensation.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Actual ancillarity in a labor market

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Employees selling labor can suffer antitrust injury. Genuine internal corporate coordination remains outside §1 agreement, but the agreement among independent sale participants requires analysis rather than categorical exclusion.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Is the sale-related hiring restraint naked labor-market allocation or a genuinely ancillary restraint subject to the rule of reason?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply existing classification and competitive-effects principles to labor purchasing; reject a transaction-label safe harbor.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Actual relation and reasonable necessity to a legitimate transfer determine classification. A broad per se rule for every sale restriction would ignore lawful ancillarity. A narrow sale exemption would permit naked allocation without scrutiny.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A no-hire agreement among independent labor purchasers is not sheltered by an accompanying sale: a naked market allocation receives per se treatment, while a genuinely subordinate and reasonably necessary transaction restraint receives rule-of-reason analysis. Employees must prove agreement, cognizable antitrust injury, and the applicable competitive-harm predicates by a preponderance; defendants invoking ancillarity must substantiate the legitimate transaction connection and reasonable necessity rather than merely name the sale. Under the rule of reason, plaintiffs establish supported anticompetitive effects, defendants produce the actual procompetitive justification, and plaintiffs may meet it with supported lack of fit or materially less restrictive alternatives. Rule 56 does not permit choosing the defendants’ continuity inference over a genuine contrary inference.

* **Reach Within the Question:** Independent participants’ labor-purchasing restraint, its persons, duration, and function. A wholly owned group’s internal coordination does not become a conspiracy merely because separation is planned.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Use direct effects where sufficient and a market inquiry where needed; neither workers’ individual losses nor elaborate market definition is universally dispositive. A reasonable limited continuity restraint may survive, while transaction language cannot rescue a naked allocation.

* **Transition:** Not applicable.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Copperweld distinguishes a genuine enterprise from independent agreement. NYNEX requires classification by actual agreement and competitive injury; California Dental calibrates scrutiny to the plausible competitive explanation; State Oil preserves horizontal categories while rejecting indiscriminate per se treatment in its different vertical setting. Together these rules supply one staged inquiry: identify the actors and agreement, determine actual ancillarity, then apply the appropriate competitive-effects test. Microsoft confirms that an integration label alone cannot replace supported justification. The duration extending beyond six months and the scope of excluded hiring require actual explanation. Market operation and reasonable transaction necessity are decisive.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Copperweld’s enterprise boundary. Reconcile NYNEX, California Dental, and State Oil in the stated staged classification inquiry without erasing their different settings. Apply Microsoft’s demand for an actual supported justification by analogy from its distinct platform–browser tying holding, not as a decision already classifying this labor covenant.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual competitive effects, participants, and reasonable transaction necessity require disputed record findings; no final illegality or market-wide remedy is declared.

* **Conditional Position or Fallback:** If the complete record conclusively establishes genuine ancillarity and no cognizable anticompetitive effect under the proper labor-market inquiry, affirm antitrust dismissal while preserving the supported ERISA claims. The alternative depends on those predicates, not exclusion of workers from antitrust protection.

##### Issue 2: Purposeful interference with pension bridging

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The duration, internal communications, and pension savings permit an inference requiring adjudication. The §510 defendants are AT&T and Lucent; class discovery does not establish a certified class.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does the evidence permit a finding of specific intent to interfere with prospective pension-bridging rights?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Inter-Modal’s protection of prospective rights and the existing specific-intent requirement at summary judgment.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Circumstantial evidence must remain usable to prove purposeful interference. A broad rule equating all cost savings with liability would eliminate intent. A narrow insistence on a confession would disregard the supported timing and communications.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A §510 claimant may prove specific interference purpose through circumstantial evidence showing that the challenged action was taken to prevent attainment of protected plan rights, even if those rights were prospective. Employees must prove the protected status and right, prohibited action, specific interference purpose, and causation by a preponderance; incidental savings alone do not suffice. At summary judgment, the 245-day restriction, six-month bridging limit, and internal pension discussion support a genuine inference that cannot be rejected merely because workforce continuity is also plausible.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The identified restraints and defendants alleged to act against protected prospective rights, not every lawful plan-design change or the earlier internal restriction with materially different consequences.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Distinguish a genuine alternative explanation from undisputed negation of intent. Once liability is proved, identify the actual §502 remedy; neither an antitrust violation nor a pension-related motive automatically supplies an ERISA damages action.

* **Transition:** Not applicable.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Inter-Modal protects prospective plan interests while retaining purpose, causation, and remedial limits. Hazen Paper distinguishes pension motivation from age motivation rather than immunizing conduct aimed at pension rights. The specific duration and communications go beyond savings alone, while the continuity rationale remains evidence for adjudication. Rule 56’s inference discipline is decisive at this stage; ultimate specific intent must still be proved. Great-West prevents an ordinary payment claim from becoming equitable solely through wording, preserving effective relief only within the authorized route. Bridgestone/Firestone requires a supported workable class arrangement and forbids certification by a common narrative that ignores materially different proof. It supports allowing focused discovery of common restraints and individual bridging conditions before deciding certification. Here the shared duration can support common evidence of purpose while differences in covered employment, actual hiring opportunity and benefit eligibility can affect injury, causation and permissible relief. The court must decide whether the proposed arrangement can resolve those particular differences; it may not treat the common intent inference as proof of every employee’s loss or as a class award. The product-specific differences there do not establish an automatic bar to this employment class.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Inter-Modal’s prospective-right protection and retained elements. Distinguish Hazen Paper’s age-discrimination question. Apply Great-West, Mertens, and Varity according to the actual remedial route, without making survival of §510 equal automatic compensation. Apply Bridgestone/Firestone’s certification discipline to any class request after discovery; distinguish its product and state-law differences rather than importing its certification result.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Intent, causation, class prerequisites, and the precise available relief require facts not yet found; Texas Pacific Group is not added as a §510 defendant through this mandate.

## 174. Bragg v. West Virginia Coal Association

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

Stone proposes a construction of SMCRA preserving an express federal permitting duty after state primacy. Verizon Maryland supplies a connected-officer route, not the existence of that duty. The independent participation-only waiver theory fails under existing consent law.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the categorical dismissal of the pleaded federal §1260 duty and remand. Reject waiver inferred solely from approval of the state program. Leave the separate consent-decree disposition undisturbed.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Determine the nondiscretionary federal duty, the cause and prerequisites under §1270, the connected official, and any ongoing violation before awarding prospective relief. Do not automatically reinstate the general valley-fill injunction. Claims seeking only enforcement of state law remain subject to Pennhurst; no treasury damages or particular permit invalidity is adjudicated.

* **Remedial Reach:** The surviving route permits relief for the actual federal duty and affected persons with standing. A proved common permitting violation may require correction of the practice, but the present construction alone establishes neither that violation nor statewide liability. Stone would require a proved unlawful practice to be cured throughout its demonstrated reach unless the violator shows a narrower lawful order fully cures it; that remedial presumption is a proposed development, constrained by statutory authority and the proved wrong.

* **Practical Relief:** The district court must identify the precise §1260 findings alleged to be nondiscretionary, explain which approved-program requirements that federal command incorporates, determine the actual §1270 prerequisites and connected officer, and decide any ongoing breach. It must use supported, undisturbed findings already made and identify any genuinely unresolved material issue; a fresh trial is not required merely because the categorical immunity premise failed. Only after liability and equitable entitlement are established may it enter an order identifying the permits or practice affected and the legally required corrective action. Any ordered correction must have a reasonable compliance schedule and verification through permit records and required findings; failure permits enforcement of that lawful order. The court cannot simply reissue the vacated blanket prohibition or use its decree to supervise discretionary state choices outside the federal duty.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: An ongoing federal permitting duty after primacy

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The source-of-law question controlled dismissal and the injunction. The substantial federal claim is not automatically outside federal-question jurisdiction merely because a proposed statutory reading ultimately fails.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May a connected official be sued through §1270 for ongoing failure to perform an express nondiscretionary federal permitting duty after state primacy?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the officer-relief framework and resolve the open SMCRA construction in favor of retained federal commands, while preserving statutory prerequisites.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Express federal commands and the citizen-suit reference to the regulatory authority support a usable enforcement route. A broad federalization of every permit objection would exceed nondiscretionary duties and the cause of action. A narrow primacy exemption would erase the federal command without textual support.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** State primacy does not extinguish an express nondiscretionary §1260 duty imposed on the regulatory authority, and an eligible §1270 claim may enforce that duty prospectively against the connected official. Plaintiffs must identify the federal command, satisfy the citizen-suit prerequisites, and establish standing. They must prove the facts constituting an ongoing breach by a preponderance of the evidence; the existence and scope of the statutory command and cause remain legal questions. A permanent injunction also requires actual threatened injury for which legal remedies are inadequate and an order justified by the balance of hardships and public interest, subject to any controlling statutory direction. No breach, eligibility, or equitable entitlement is presumed from survival of the immunity defense. State-program content incorporated by a genuine federal obligation is not automatically a state-only claim, but independent state duties remain subject to Pennhurst. The current record requires that identification and proof before restoration of any injunction.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The actual federal permitting command and necessary incorporated compliance content; no general federal permit appeal, discretionary-policy substitution, or direct treasury compensation.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Identify the source and obligatory content of each claim before invoking immunity or equity. A final injunction requires proven breach and ordinary equitable entitlement; preliminary relief retains its distinct likelihood, injury, and equitable requirements. A claimed policy-wide cure must match the demonstrated federal wrong.

* **Transition:** The statutory construction resolves an open question without displacing an entering controlling construction. The separately proposed cure presumption governs this and other nonfinal equitable proceedings if adopted; it does not automatically reopen final decrees or authorize relief beyond the governing statute and proved injury.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The statute’s reference to the state regulatory authority would do little work if primacy silently erased every express federal obligation. Administration and legal source are distinct. Verizon Maryland supports prospective relief against a connected official while preserving the actual federal violation inquiry; Pennhurst bars enforcement of state law as such, not every federal duty using approved-program content. Text carries decisive weight, and the cooperative arrangement confirms rather than creates the retained command. Missing findings about duty and breach explain why broad injunctive reinstatement is not yet justified. Westside Mothers enforces identified mandatory Medicaid child benefits against responsible officials only after the particular right, statutory conditions and violation are established. Stone applies that provision-specific distinction by analogy: cooperative state administration is not itself a defense, but an enforceable §1260 command and the actual §1270 route must still be demonstrated. The child-service holding supplies neither a general federal right to perfect mine administration nor direct-State damages authority.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Apply Verizon Maryland’s connected-officer inquiry and distinguish Pennhurst’s state-only claim. Resolve SMCRA’s retained-duty question expressly rather than attribute it to those immunity cases. State the proposed remedial presumption as new, not as an existing unlimited policy injunction rule. Apply Westside Mothers’ provision-specific enforcement reasoning by analogy, distinguishing its Medicaid rights and §1983 channel from this SMCRA citizen-suit construction.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Particular permit legality and the properly fitted order need findings absent from the immunity disposition. Claims against federal officials and settled counts are outside the presented questions.

##### Issue 2: Approval is not an undisclosed surrender of immunity

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Participation-only waiver is separately presented as an alternative and can be rejected on the statute’s actual terms.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does voluntary acquisition of primacy itself waive immunity from these citizen suits?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply College Savings Bank’s consent distinction and Bradley’s actual disclosed-condition requirement.

* **Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The enacted condition answers this specific consent theory without denying genuine waiver. An intermediate presumption that regulatory participation always waives immunity would invent consent. A record-only refusal without identifying the missing condition would obscure what a valid waiver requires.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Obtaining federal approval of a state regulatory program does not waive immunity when the statute does not unambiguously condition approval on that surrender and expressly preserves constitutional immunity. The party asserting waiver must identify an unequivocal expression or valid accepted condition; voluntary participation alone does not meet that legal standard. No such condition is established here.

* **Reach Within the Question:** Approval-based consent, preserving an actual express waiver or a separately proved valid benefit condition.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Examine a claimed condition’s actual text and acceptance. Until this Court decides the separately presented effect of consent to the distinct decree, courts apply the governing express-consent and litigation-waiver rules to that decree’s own record, not a general participation waiver.

* **Transition:** Not applicable.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** College Savings Bank rejects constructive consent from engaging in regulated activity. Bradley concerns an actual communicated condition on a benefit, which a recipient can knowingly accept. The Act’s reference to suits permitted by the Eleventh Amendment preserves available officer relief; it does not tell applicants they surrender all immunity. This textual absence is decisive. Florida Prepaid’s affirmative Article I enforcement rule addresses congressional power and unmistakable suit authorization rather than manufacturing consent, and silence in another phase of litigation cannot establish a global waiver here.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Apply College Savings Bank; distinguish Bradley’s disclosed condition. Preserve Florida Prepaid’s separate rule without conflating abrogation, waiver, and officer relief.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The separate decree’s actual consent and any independent litigation waiver are outside the presented approval-only question.

## 175. Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc. v. Commissioner of the Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

The relevant speaker is identified from the operation of this optional-emblem program. Stone extends the private-expression distinction to that function, then applies viewpoint neutrality. Virginia’s actual severability rule supplies the remedial authority.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the injunction against the SCV-specific logo restriction and severance preserving the plate authorization.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Require issuance under the remaining authorization to eligible applicants satisfying lawful design, legibility, fee, and administrative requirements. Preserve separate adjudication of any fees or costs; order no damages, fixed fee amount, compulsory display by others, or governmental adoption of the emblem.

* **Remedial Reach:** The invalid condition must cease governing all eligible applicants under this authorization, rather than be waived for one litigant while continuing the same proved exclusion. Stone’s proposed complete-policy-cure presumption places on the State the showing that a narrower lawful order would fully cure the demonstrated wrong. The relief here is independently supported by the severable condition and does not adjudicate all other plate programs.

* **Practical Relief:** Upon the mandate, officials must process the authorized organizational design and eligible applications under ordinary neutral program requirements without reimposing the forbidden logo condition. The decree should permit compliance verification through the processing and issuance record and a prompt return to the issuing court if the same exclusion persists. Existing lawful production schedules govern unless the court finds they are being used to perpetuate exclusion; the judgment invents no new statutory production date.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Private expression within an official identification program

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The requested emblem, private selection, and the program’s ordinary treatment of organizational designs permit functional attribution. Viewpoint discrimination supplies a sufficient ground without a comprehensive forum classification.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May Virginia single out this otherwise authorized organization’s optional plate expression for an emblem ban?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Extend Rosenberger, Southworth, and Velazquez’s private-expression distinction to the demonstrated program and apply viewpoint neutrality.

* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Function-specific attribution protects the opened private expression while preserving official identification. A broad private-speech rule for every plate feature would erase the State’s own message and safety role. A narrow applicant exception would leave the same discriminatory condition operative.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** When a specialty-plate program invites private organizations to supply optional affiliation messages selected by motorists, the State may not exclude an otherwise eligible participant’s message because of its viewpoint by labeling every optional expression governmental. The claimant must establish the program’s actual private expressive component and the discriminatory treatment; the State must substantiate any asserted independent lawful design or identification ground. Ownership, manufacture, and approval are relevant to attribution but not conclusive. The targeted emblem ban here suppresses the private component opened to other organizations and cannot be justified by disapproval of its message.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The optional private emblem in this program. Registration identifiers and the State’s own marks remain governmental; actual threats, compelled display, and a State’s adoption of an official flag are not adjudicated.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Identify who supplies and chooses the message, ordinary editorial practice, and contextual attribution before applying the appropriate speech rule. Existing viewpoint-neutrality law controls the private component without requiring a decision that every neutral subject limitation or every program feature is a public forum.

* **Transition:** The proposed program-specific extension applies to this and other nonfinal civil cases under Harper. It reopens no final judgments and creates no damages action or exemption from independently valid remedial rules; Reynoldsville separates those consequences from the governing rule.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Rosenberger protects private expression from viewpoint exclusion in the opened benefit; Southworth separates supported private speech from the government’s own message; Velazquez examines what the program actually funds and controls. These overlapping distinctions are restated as one attribution inquiry before applying viewpoint neutrality. The logo prohibition’s formal coverage of every emblem for this one organization does not remove its contextual targeting. The emblem’s association with racial oppression permits governmental criticism and rejection as its own symbol, but supplies no general power to suppress private expression admitted on these terms. Functional attribution identifies the component; viewpoint neutrality is decisive.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Extend and reconcile Rosenberger, Southworth, and Velazquez through the stated functional-attribution inquiry. Preserve governmental control of its own speech and lawful identification requirements; announce no universal license-plate forum rule.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual threats, official state symbols, compelled display, and materially different programs are outside this case. Broader forum classification is unnecessary to the sufficient viewpoint ground, not reserved because of a fictitious preservation defect.

##### Issue 2: Severing the discriminatory condition

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Severability is expressly presented, and the authorization can operate without the emblem prohibition. Relief therefore requires an actual state-law answer rather than an abstract preference to preserve benefits. Because the remaining authorization is severable and operative, the Court need not decide any inherent equitable power to create a benefit contrary to controlling state inseverability.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does Virginia’s severability rule permit condition-only relief preserving the authorized plates?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the then-applicable §1-17.1 presumption and preserve the functioning authorization; state the complete-cure principle as a proposed remedial clarification.

* **Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The statutory presumption and operational independence resolve this authorization. An intermediate inherent power to manufacture an inseverable benefit would exceed the available law. A record-only exemption for one applicant would retain the invalid statutory condition.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Under Virginia’s severability presumption, an unconstitutional logo restriction must be severed when the remaining plate authorization can operate independently and the required inseparability is not established. The party opposing severance must identify the statutory dependence that defeats the presumption; conjecture about some legislators’ motives does not suffice. The remaining authorization is administrable here, so eligible applicants receive it without the discriminatory condition.

* **Reach Within the Question:** This statutory authorization and its severable restriction, not a federal power to create a new plate benefit or close an entire unrelated program.

* **Next-Case Guidance:** Apply the actual severability rule and the practical operation of the remainder. If another statute is genuinely inseverable, determine a lawful cure under its own law and federal equality and speech requirements; do not assume either judicial creation of a benefit or mandatory closure of every program.

* **Transition:** The severability application changes no entering rule. The proposed full-cure presumption applies to this and other nonfinal equitable matters if adopted, with ordinary authority and injury limits; it does not reopen final decrees automatically.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Section 1-17.1 favors preservation unless the provisions must stand together. Issuing the authorized plate without this restriction requires no new benefit design. The State retains dimensions, legibility, and ordinary administration, making condition-only relief both lawful and effective. State statutory text is decisive for severance; constitutional viewpoint neutrality identifies the provision that cannot remain. This combination supplies a concrete remedy without balancing the emblem’s popularity against protected access.

* **Precedent Treatment:** Apply Virginia’s statutory severability rule and the speech holdings identified above. Do not announce an inherent power to manufacture an inseverable authorization.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** None. The necessity-based nonreach of inherent benefit-creation power is explained under Threshold and Merits Reach; it is not treated as an unpreserved or factually missing question.

* **Conditional Position or Fallback:** If controlling state law actually defeats severance, remand for a lawful cure of the unequal access without inventing plate authorization or presuming that the entire program must close. That alternative requires demonstrated inseparability; the present record supports condition-only relief.
