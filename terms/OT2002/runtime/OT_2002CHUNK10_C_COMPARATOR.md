## 123. Los Angeles v. David

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** May 19, 2003: certiorari granted and the Ninth Circuit reversed in a per curiam merits decision with no announced dissent.
* **Historical Holding:** Applying Mathews and Mallen, the Court held this routine twenty-seven-day interval constitutionally permissible for the monetary repayment claim after the automobile was recovered.
* **Historical Authority:** The record-specific result establishes no general timetable for all impound disputes.
* **Historical Sources:** Los Angeles v. David, 538 U.S. 715; No. 02-1212.

## 124. Kansas v. Nebraska

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** May 19, 2003: the Court entered the decree reported at 538 U.S. 720. No numerical vote is announced in the decree; unanimity is not invented.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** The Court issued an implementing decree without an authored opinion or individual join list.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court received the report, approved the Final Settlement Stipulation filed December 16, 2002, and recommitted for the sole purpose of procedural questions in completing the model. Dismissal with prejudice of covered claims arising before December 15, 2002 was made effective on the Master’s certification of adoption.
* **Historical Authority:** The decree’s conditional language does not establish that certification had occurred on May 19.
* **Historical Sources:** Kansas v. Nebraska, 538 U.S. 720; No. 126, Original.

## 127. Nevada Department of Human Resources v. Hibbs

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Supreme Court affirmed on May 27, 2003, by a 6–3 judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist’s opinion was joined by O’Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Stevens concurred in the judgment. Souter filed a concurrence joined by Ginsburg and Breyer. Scalia dissented separately; Kennedy’s dissent was joined by Scalia and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court sustained the family-care provision’s damages remedy against States as appropriate § 5 legislation addressing sex discrimination. It did not decide the validity of every FMLA leave entitlement.
* **Historical Authority:** The five-Justice opinion supplied the historical controlling § 5 rationale. Its historical Article I immunity background is not the governing law.
* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.

## 128. Bryant v. Adventist Health System/West

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

On May 20, 2002, the Ninth Circuit affirmed. Judge Paez wrote for Judges Canby and Graber as well. It required detection and held that good-faith inpatient admission ordinarily ends EMTALA stabilization duties, while preserving a sham-admission limitation. The state claims remained dismissed without prejudice. No historical Supreme Court merits holding is asserted.
