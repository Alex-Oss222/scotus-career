## 11. Popovich v. Cuyahoga County Court of Common Pleas

### Final components

P1: Section 12202 clearly authorizes the private federal action; clarity remains distinct from power and liability. All eight Associates join.

P2: Reasonable measures enabling a hearing-impaired parent to understand and participate in custody proceedings, with effective alternatives and applicable fundamental-alteration/undue-burden limits, are congruent and proportional prevention of denial of a meaningful hearing. P3: Protection against retaliation for requesting that access is fitted enforcement because punishment for requesting the means of participation can defeat the hearing itself. P4: The statutory access claim is not divided into valid and invalid causes solely according to the constitutional rationale advanced for Congress's power. P5: Reconsider the general verdict and trial scope under the valid court-access theories and actual preserved objections, without automatic reinstatement. Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer join P2–P5 in full, subject to the category limits below.

## 13. Syngenta Crop Protection, Inc. v. Henson

### Final judgment and exact joins

All eight Associates affirm the Eleventh Circuit's jurisdictional judgment. The federal merits dismissal remains vacated and the removed Louisiana action returns to state court.

S1: The All Writs Act is auxiliary authority within existing jurisdiction, not an independent source of original jurisdiction or an exception to section 1441. S2: Retained or ancillary power to enforce a federal settlement does not eliminate original jurisdiction as a condition to removing a distinct state action; a federal preclusion defense does not create that jurisdiction. S3: The dependent federal dismissal is vacated and the action returns to state court. Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer join S1–S3 in full.

Rivet supplies the preclusion-defense distinction; Kokkonen preserves only lawfully sourced retained enforcement; Peacock distinguishes auxiliary enforcement from a new jurisdictional anchor; Pennsylvania Bureau of Correction prevents residual writ authority from displacing a governing statutory scheme. The frozen individual rows remain final. In particular Stevens's broader Peacock enforcement position is compatible with the express removal condition, while Thomas's Peacock position preserves genuine decree-effectuation authority within its legal source. Practical settlement protection supplies no new removal jurisdiction.

A separate enforcement proceeding retains its own jurisdictional and remedial predicates. Any restraint on state litigation must independently satisfy section 2283 and the decree's actual scope. This decision adjudicates neither settlement breach nor the availability of an injunction, broadens no release and grants no enforcement remedy.

### Stevens concurrence and precedent treatment

The provisional prediction of no separate writing is revised. Stevens publishes a concurrence, joined by no other Associate, while joining S1–S3 without qualification. He would expressly overrule the expansive All Writs construction in United States v. New York Telephone Co., 434 U.S. 159 (1977). Part II of his own dissent there treats the Act as procedural means for a court to exercise existing jurisdiction, with means consistent with established legal usages; it rejects general power to help the Government or litigants accomplish their objectives. The use of that decision to justify removal supplies his reason to remove its erroneous construction rather than merely distinguish it.

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer do not join the proposed overruling. The Court's rule distinguishes New York Telephone's auxiliary-power holding in an existing jurisdiction and leaves its assistance holding intact. Stevens's proposal is noncontrolling and does not diminish the eight-Associate coalition. Carry this substantive difference in precedent treatment in the Record and public projection. No historical Court-opinion author is imported.
