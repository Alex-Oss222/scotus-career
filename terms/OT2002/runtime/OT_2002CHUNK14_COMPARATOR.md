## 180. Cano v. Davis

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

The historical Supreme Court **summarily affirmed on January 13, 2003**, *Cano v. Davis*, 537 U.S. 1100, No. 02-577. No merits opinion, reasoning, individual vote tally, or dissent accompanied the disposition. It left the three-judge court's judgment intact; it should not be represented as an express endorsement of every passage in that court's opinion. The judgment issued February 12, 2003. ## 165. Green Tree Financial Corp. v. Bazzle

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 5–4 in judgment.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer’s four-Justice plurality included Justices Scalia, Souter, and Ginsburg. Justice Stevens would have affirmed but concurred in the judgment to supply a controlling disposition. * **Historical Authority:** Green Tree Financial Corp. v. Bazzle.

## 168. Limon v. Kansas

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Kansas Court of Appeals affirmed in February 2002. On June 27, 2003, one day after this assigned event, the historical Supreme Court granted review, vacated, and remanded in light of Lawrence. That was not a Supreme Court merits decision resolving all three questions.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** The contemporaneous Kansas opinion was per curiam for Judges Knudson, Pierron, and Green. The later Supreme Court action was a summary remand, with no merits rationale or vote division identified here.

* **Historical Holding:** The original state judgment sustained the classification and juvenile-history use while declining the unraised Eighth Amendment question. The historical remand required reconsideration in light of the actual Lawrence decision. Subsequent 2004 and 2005 state decisions postdate this review.

* **Historical Authority:** Limon v. Kansas.

* **Historical Sources:** Contemporaneous judicial appendix; later state opinion recounting the June 27, 2003 order, used solely to verify that later procedural history.

## 171. Alkire v. Irving

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified. The Sixth Circuit unanimously affirmed in part, reversed in part and remanded in its June 2, 2003 amended opinion.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** District Judge Tarnow wrote for the panel with Judges Moore and Cole; no separate opinion appears. The amended opinion replaced the earlier September 18, 2002 opinion.

* **Historical Holding:** The court reinstated the prolonged-detention and debt-related-incarceration claims because of material disputes and evidence of policy, while preserving denial of Alkire's summary judgment. It affirmed rejection of the separate jail-credit theory and class certification and required further inquiry into the County Court's status.

* **Historical Authority:** Alkire v. Irving supplies the reviewed lower judgment. Its statements about immunity and attribution do not displace the independently controlling holdings.

* **Historical Sources:** Full amended opinion.
