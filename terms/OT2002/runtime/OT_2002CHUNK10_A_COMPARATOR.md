## 117. United States v. Haney

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor and Outcome:** Tenth Circuit, August 29, 2001: affirmed. Judge Ebel wrote for a panel including Judges Anderson and Murphy.
* **Historical Holding:** The panel rejected the commerce challenge and used a militia-connected theory to reject the Second Amendment claim. The latter differs materially from the entering individual-right law.
* **Historical Authority:** The circuit judgment is the decision under review; no Supreme Court merits disposition is identified.
* **Historical Sources:** United States v. Haney, 264 F.3d 1161 (lower-court identifier; no historical Supreme Court docket is assigned).

## 122. Altman v. Bedford Central School District

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor and Outcome:** Second Circuit, March 27, 2001: affirmed in part, vacated in part and reversed in part. Kearse wrote for Walker, Kearse and Pooler.
* **Historical Holding:** The court required dismissal of school-specific claims made moot by moves and graduation, rejected taxpayer standing as shown, and rejected the high-school Earth Day establishment and free-exercise determinations. It addressed the remaining cross-appeal within that posture and removed the dependent relief.
* **Historical Authority:** This is the lower-court comparator. Its 2001 jurisdictional posture and independent Lemon analysis are not automatically current operative facts or governing law in May 2003.
* **Historical Sources:** Altman v. Bedford Central School District, 245 F.3d 49 (lower-court identifier; no historical Supreme Court docket is assigned).

## 125. Hilton v. City of Wheeling

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor and Outcome:** No historical Supreme Court merits decision is identified for this added matter. The Seventh Circuit affirmed on April 20, 2000.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Posner wrote for a unanimous panel with Flaum and Williams.
* **Historical Holding:** The panel rejected an affirmative right to police assistance and required personal, wholly illegitimate animus for the class-of-one unequal-protection claim. That last requirement differs from the Olech rule.
* **Historical Authority:** The appellate decision is a historical lower-court anchor, not a Supreme Court merits holding or an earlier controlling holding.
* **Historical Sources:** Seventh Circuit opinion.

## 126. Conant v. Walters

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Ninth Circuit affirmed on October 29, 2002. All three judges joined the court’s opinion; there was no reported dissent. No historical Supreme Court merits decision in this listed matter is identified.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Judge Schroeder wrote for the panel with Judges B. Fletcher and Kozinski. Judge Kozinski also concurred, emphasizing patients’ informational interests and state regulatory autonomy.

* **Historical Holding:** The policy impermissibly burdened professional recommendations, and the injunction was affirmed under a construction preserving investigation and prosecution of actual criminal participation.

* **Historical Authority:** Conant v. Walters is the lower-court comparator. The concurrence’s federalism discussion is not converted into the court’s independent holding or into governing law.

* **Historical Sources:** Historical appellate anchor.
