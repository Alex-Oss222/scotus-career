## 32. Craigmiles v. Giles

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified. The lower-court comparison anchor is a unanimous Sixth Circuit affirmance on December 6, 2002.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Boggs wrote for Judges Krupansky and Lawson; no separate opinion appears.

* **Historical Holding:** The circuit held this application of the licensing law irrational under due process and equal protection and treated protection of funeral directors from competition as illegitimate. * **Historical Authority:** Craigmiles v. Giles is the Sixth Circuit judgment reviewing the challenged licensing restrictions.

## 33. Johnson v. City of Cincinnati

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified. The Sixth Circuit affirmed by a two-to-one vote.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Judge Martin wrote for himself and District Judge Edmunds; Judge Gilman dissented. The reported text also records denial of rehearing with Gilman favoring rehearing.

* **Historical Holding:** The majority recognized local travel and both asserted intimate-association interests, held the ordinance insufficiently tailored, and affirmed the district judgment and fees. The majority also accepted a broader attorney-relationship rationale and reached the fee dispute after State v. Burnett and cessation of enforcement.

* **Historical Authority:** Johnson v. City of Cincinnati supplies the lower-court anchor. State v. Burnett supplies the separate historical Ohio-law development and is not a federal Supreme Court comparator.

* **Historical Sources:** Full circuit decision.
