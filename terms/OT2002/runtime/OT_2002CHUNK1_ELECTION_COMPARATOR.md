## 4. Georgia v. Ashcroft

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical outcome and structure:** On June 26, 2003, the real Court vacated and remanded, 5–4. Justice O'Connor wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Kennedy and Thomas wrote separate concurrences. Justice Souter dissented, joined by Stevens, Ginsburg, and Breyer.

**Historical holding:** The majority required a broader statewide retrogression inquiry, including influence, coalition opportunities, and legislative support, rather than concentrating too heavily on ability to elect in particular majority-minority districts. It did not itself preclear the original plan. It also rejected the intervention-mootness argument and sustained Rule 24 participation. The dissent favored affirmance because the State had failed to demonstrate preservation of effective electoral power.

**Sources:** *Georgia v. Ashcroft*, 539 U.S. 461, majority; opinion structure and syllabus; Souter dissent. Congress's later 2006 amendment is not operative law in this OT2002 brief.
