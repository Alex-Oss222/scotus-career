## 9. Early v. Packer

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** On November 4, 2002, the Court reversed summarily, with no dissent recorded.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** A state court need not cite Supreme Court precedents; neither its reasoning nor result may contradict them. The state court's rejection of coercion was at least reasonable, and federal supervisory precedents did not supply controlling constitutional rules.
