## 117. United States v. Haney

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would enforce the already established individual right and separately sustain Congress’s stock-regulation authority. Neither militia membership nor valid commerce power decides the unresolved arms application.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reject the commerce challenge; vacate the militia-based arms ruling and remand the resulting §922(o) conviction disposition for the governing two-stage inquiry.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Determine protected conduct, actual burden and the corresponding justification on the preserved record. If the application is valid under that analysis, the convictions may stand on that ground; if it fails, set aside the affected convictions and sentence to the extent required by that constitutional ruling. No immediate acquittal or unrelated sentencing review is directed.

**Remedial Reach:** Relief concerns Haney’s completed weapons and convictions. No statutory exception is established, and no categorical machine-gun right, exclusion or statute-wide invalidation follows.

**Practical Relief:** The lower court must identify the protected activity and practical defensive alternatives, explain the restriction’s severity and test the Government’s actual evidence against the applicable tier. Decide on the existing permissible record when sufficient; any further proceedings require an actual material need and ordinary authority, not an automatic opportunity to rebuild the case. The mandate must identify the consequence for both convictions and the dependent sentence.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Individual right and a weapon-specific restriction

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The preserved arms challenge cannot be rejected by a militia prerequisite that entering law has already displaced.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does lack of militia connection defeat protection, and what inquiry governs these machine guns?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Love, Hale and Wright; do not resolve their expressly open machine-gun application by label.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The recurring two-stage test must govern this application rather than a militia shortcut. Less leaves an erroneous prerequisite intact. Automatic protection or exclusion of the weapon category would outrun the findings.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** An individual need not demonstrate organized militia membership to invoke the Second Amendment; a machine-gun restriction must instead be tested for protected conduct and its actual burden. Haney must identify the protected keeping or bearing implicated. Coverage turns on text, history and precedent, separately from justification. Wright independently forbids abolishing meaningful possession of ordinary functional defensive arms in the home; that boundary supplies no automatic right to these machine guns. For otherwise protected activity, Government must justify a core deprivation by a compelling interest, narrow tailoring and no comparably effective, materially less restrictive alternative; a substantial regulation by an important objective, substantial relationship and no unnecessary or effective elimination of protected activity. Deferential reasonableness governs only an incidental regulation imposing no meaningful interference; arbitrary administration, discrimination and cumulative destruction remain forbidden. Military utility and the statutory mechanism label decide neither stage. The lower court must apply that existing rule to these completed weapons and the stock restriction.

**Reach Within the Question:** The preserved §922(o) application, not unfinished parts, unproved statutory exceptions or an unlimited right to every mechanism.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Determine practical protected use and burden before choosing the justification standard. Until this Court resolves a different weapon application, courts apply Wright’s two-stage rule rather than borrow Emerson’s individual-danger predicate as a category rule.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Love and Wright establish an individual right independent of militia participation; Wright reserved the final machine-gun classification. Fully automatic operation can bear strongly on risk and fit but does not itself supply findings about the actual protected burden and alternatives. Emerson sustains a supported person-specific protective-order disability and cannot substitute for evidence about a weapon-defined restriction. Enumerated power and individual protection remain independent.

**Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Love, Hale and Wright’s individual-right and two-stage rules; distinguish Emerson and Fraternal Order of Police’s conduct-based settings.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual protected-conduct classification, burden and justification require analysis of the preserved record; no unproved exception or unrelated trial issue is added.

##### Issue 2: Local completion within the national stock mechanism

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The independent commerce defense is controlled for the completed converted and assembled weapons described.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Must the Government prove an interstate effect from these particular possessions?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Hale, Kirk and Wright’s stock-regulation holdings.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The actual regulatory mechanism supports inclusion of comparable local completed weapons. A transaction-only exception would defeat that mechanism. A general federal police power would exceed its connection.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Congress may reach possession of completed locally converted or assembled machine guns under §922(o)’s national civilian-stock regime without proof of this defendant’s interstate sale. Exempting that local class would materially undermine the stock restriction; the connection, not aggregate crime costs alone, sustains the application to Haney’s two weapons.

**Reach Within the Question:** The completed-weapon class within the actual regime and its statutory exceptions; the arms defense remains separate.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify the real stock mechanism and how the local class affects it. Until another statutory class is presented, apply the existing connection requirement and Lopez’s enumerated-power boundary rather than a generic dangerousness rationale.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Hale, Kirk and Wright sustain the regime because local conversion and assembly provide the direct means of expanding the restricted stock. Lopez preserves limits but does not displace that concrete mechanism with an individual transaction requirement. A remote component’s past travel is not the rationale, and valid commerce authority does not establish Second Amendment validity.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Hale, Kirk and Wright to local completion; preserve Lopez outside their specific regulatory connection.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Unpresented statutory exceptions and materially different weapon classes require their own facts.

## 118. Pharmaceutical Research and Manufacturers of America v. Walsh

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would require a real Medicaid connection and likely conflict analysis before interim relief, while rejecting unsupported categorical commerce theories. Neither agency silence nor a general public-health aspiration decides the program’s legality.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the appellate disposition and remand for renewed preliminary-injunction analysis on the statutory ground; reject the presented categorical commerce grounds.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Apply likelihood of success, irreparable injury, balance of harms and public interest to the actual threatened leverage, approval posture and agency action. Remand does not itself restore the injunction or finally validate Maine Rx.

**Remedial Reach:** Any interim restraint must target the challenged likely unlawful use of Medicaid authority and the members’ established injury, not ordinary voluntary rebate negotiations. No final common violation or class damages is established on this preimplementation record.

**Practical Relief:** The district court must identify the actual threatened condition, its claimed Medicaid connection, supported predictive beneficiary effects, prior-authorization safeguards and any legally required plan approval. Determine first whether a particular unresolved factual judgment is actually assigned to the Secretary; an agency’s potential interest does not itself transfer the court’s legal inquiry or create a new exhaustion prerequisite. Under Ventura, use the proper agency process for genuinely assigned, unperformed factual work, unless the result is legally compelled and no such judgment remains. Set a case-management schedule for the parties to identify the available process and supply any resulting authoritative record, with a prompt status review if agency action remains pending. For any actual agency interpretation, identify its instrument, scope, delegated authority and stated reasons: apply controlling statutory weight only if both Mead predicates are satisfied, and otherwise independently evaluate its persuasive reasoning under Skidmore. An official litigation position is not itself program approval or delegated interpretive lawmaking; absence of controlling force does not authorize ignoring its actual reasons. Independently decide interim protection under likelihood, irreparable harm, competing hardships and public interest; neither agency silence nor the fact of referral justifies approval, automatic restraint or indefinite deferral of that request. Any restraint must identify the likely unlawful Medicaid condition and supported injury while preserving lawful independent rebate activity. If the association fails the preliminary predicates, deny relief; later authoritative action or supported material facts receive review through the proper procedure.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Use of a federal-program tool and preliminary protection

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The manufacturers assert threatened injury through a prospective officer/preemption route, not an assumed beneficiary §1983 damages entitlement. That lawful channel and the actual statutory conflict precede equity.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does this preliminary record justify protection against using Medicaid authorization to obtain broader rebates?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply program-specific statutory conditions and ordinary preliminary relief, correcting both unrestricted-purpose and every-obstacle rules.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. A real connection is needed to prevent unrelated leverage while preserving authorized state discretion. Any-public-health-benefit review underprotects program beneficiaries. Barring every supplementary rebate program would exceed the statute and record.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Use of Medicaid prior authorization must have an identifiable connection to the authorized program and comply with its safeguards; hypothetical general savings cannot conclusively supply that connection, but modest inconvenience is not automatically preemptive. The association bears the preliminary showing of likely unlawful conflict and irreparable injury, with hardships and public interest independently assessed. Maine must substantiate the program relationship it relies on; courts may not invent findings for it. The present competing categorical rulings require renewed analysis, not final program invalidation.

**Reach Within the Question:** The threatened condition and its actual Medicaid operation, including required approval and statutory access protections, without a general private damages right.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Distinguish authorized program design from a burden used solely for an unrelated objective. Until an actual agency decision or changed plan is presented, apply the governing approval and review rules; silence is not approval.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Suter rejected the asserted reasonable-efforts and reporting rights under the particular child-welfare provisions; Blessing required an intended beneficiary, a binding obligation and a sufficiently definite individual entitlement, followed by a separate foreclosure inquiry. Those §1983 holdings therefore require identifying the claimed right and route rather than treating Medicaid as either wholly enforceable or wholly insulated. Westside Mothers now supplies an actual enforceable category for eligible children’s identified mandatory benefits, so Medicaid is not categorically insulated from private prospective enforcement. The manufacturers must nevertheless establish their own prospective preemption route and injury; the child-benefit holding supplies neither by substitution. Gonzaga’s distinct FERPA result likewise does not decide that route. Choate and Beal permit state design discretion inside Medicaid’s requirements, not unlimited use of its beneficiaries as leverage. Mead requires delegated force-of-law authority actually exercised, while Christensen rejects controlling weight for its merely advisory letter without discarding the letter’s persuasive reasoning. Neither makes form alone decisive. Applied here, the court must distinguish an actual approval or delegated interpretation from litigation predictions, evaluate any supplied reasons on their lawful footing, and supply no nonexistent agency conclusion. Ventura preserves an agency’s genuinely assigned factual judgment; it does not require referral of a purely legal issue or a legally compelled result, and it does not invent an administrative prerequisite where Congress assigned none. Here contestable beneficiary-effect predictions cannot be treated as an agency approval that has not occurred, while the court must independently decide the governing conditions and the present request for interim protection. Statutory connection and likely practical conflict, followed by every interim factor, decide relief.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Suter and Blessing’s provision-specific inquiry and preserve Westside Mothers’ actual child-benefit enforcement holding, distinguishing the manufacturers’ claim. Preserve Gonzaga’s distinct FERPA scope, Choate/Beal discretion, Mead/Christensen agency-source rules and Ventura’s allocation of assigned factual judgments without inventing an agency decision.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Operational effects, required plan posture and any actual agency action lack final findings; no final program-wide merits result is entered.

##### Issue 2: The asserted discriminatory and extraterritorial effects

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The commerce grounds are independently reviewable on the present arrangement; no actual discrimination finding may be invented.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does this record establish a protectionist subsidy or an out-of-state price command?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply the actual competitor and transaction inquiries.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The categorical theories fail on their stated comparisons. Less leaves alternative injunction grounds unresolved. Immunizing every later burden or revised program would overreach.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** The present Maine-sales rebate arrangement does not establish origin discrimination merely because manufacturers are largely outside Maine or pharmacies and residents benefit, and does not itself command prices of separate out-of-state transactions. An in-state manufacturer faces the same condition, and pharmacies are not shown to be subsidized manufacturing competitors. The association has not established the asserted commerce bases for interim relief.

**Reach Within the Question:** The particular discrimination and extraterritorial-control theories, not every possible incidental burden.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify an actual competitive preference or external transaction legally controlled. Until a materially changed discriminatory operation or properly presented incidental burden is proved, apply the governing commerce tests rather than infer immunity from this rejection.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** West Lynn Creamery examines an assessment and dedicated subsidy together but depends on actual competing payors and beneficiaries; Tracy requires a real comparator. Baldwin and Healy concern external price control, not every local bargain with interstate effects. Maine production would not remove the manufacturer’s obligation, and the record does not supply the linked competitor preference those precedents require.

**Precedent Treatment:** Distinguish West Lynn Creamery, Baldwin and Healy on the actual mechanism; apply Tracy’s comparator requirement and preserve a properly presented Pike inquiry.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Different proven operation or incidental burdens require an actual claim and record.

## 119. Breuer v. Jim's Concrete of Brevard, Inc.

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would enforce the enacted distinction between concurrent original jurisdiction and an express bar to removal. The employee’s costs do not authorize a textual exception Congress did not enact.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm denial of remand to state court.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** The FLSA action continues in federal court under ordinary law; award no wages, liquidated damages, fees or sanctions on this interlocutory forum question.

**Remedial Reach:** This is the forum for Breuer’s federal claim, not a rule that all employment actions or all statutes using maintain are removable.

**Practical Relief:** On return, adjudicate the wage claims and defenses without relitigating this rejected removal exception. Any independent procedural defect or statutory exception must be actually raised and established under its own rule. No substantive recovery follows from retention of the case.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Concurrent jurisdiction does not guarantee the initial forum

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Original federal jurisdiction is uncontested and the denial, rather than a grant, of remand is properly under interlocutory review.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does §216(b)’s maintain language expressly prohibit §1441(a) removal?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply the enacted express-exception condition to this federal claim.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The actual FLSA clause and removal text resolve the dispute. Less leaves the forum demand unanswered. A rule for every similarly worded statute would exceed its complete context.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Section 216(b)’s authorization to maintain an action in a competent federal or state court does not expressly bar removal under §1441(a), so this action within original federal jurisdiction is removable absent another applicable express exception. Removal continues the action; it does not negate authority to maintain it.

**Reach Within the Question:** The FLSA and removal provisions then governing this pleaded federal claim, not artful pleading or complete preemption of state claims.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Check both original jurisdiction and any actual express exception. Until a different statute is presented, interpret its complete text and structure rather than treat this word as a universal removal rule.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Congress expressly conditioned the general removal grant on contrary express legislation and elsewhere used direct prohibitions, as §1445 illustrates. Maintain does not unmistakably guarantee the initially selected tribunal. Shamrock Oil requires attention to limits, not erasure of the later affirmative text. Syngenta insists on independent original jurisdiction rather than a federal defense or ancillary enforcement need, consistent with Caterpillar and Rivet. That prerequisite is supplied by Breuer’s own federal wage claim. The case therefore concerns whether Congress barred otherwise authorized removal, not judicial power to create missing jurisdiction. Practical burdens may inform legislation or case management but cannot add the missing prohibition.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Syngenta’s independent-jurisdiction prerequisite and distinguish its missing federal claim from this pleaded FLSA action. Preserve Shamrock Oil’s statutory discipline and Caterpillar/Rivet’s jurisdiction requirements; create no new exception or complete-preemption doctrine.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Wage merits, fees and independent removal defects are outside the presented statutory question.

## 120. Norfolk Southern Railway Co. v. Kirby

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would enforce the actual through-carriage bargains and recognize limited authority to arrange downstream risk, without turning the forwarder into a general agent or COGSA into a universal rail statute.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the categorical denial of contractual limitation and remand for application of the railroad’s protected downstream terms.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Recognize beneficiary status under ICC’s original bill and the limited binding force of the Hamburg Süd downstream arrangement. Determine each bill’s actual limit and remaining package, fault and calculation issues; do not equate both bills with the $500 figure or award damages.

**Remedial Reach:** The holding allocates risk for the authorized international through shipment and the downstream performer. It preserves any actual separate claim against the intermediary and unrelated owner transactions.

**Practical Relief:** The lower court must identify which contractual protection governs each asserted recovery, resolve any material package or amount issue and only then apply a limit to liability otherwise proved. The asserted loss and ten containers are not an adjudicated award or a universal package definition. No limitation can be imported from a bill whose actual terms do not supply it.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Maritime undertaking and the protected performer class

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Choice of substantive maritime law precedes construction; this international through undertaking includes the inland leg and is not a wholly local rail bargain.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does lack of direct privity exclude the contemplated railroad from ICC’s broad clause?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply maritime contract principles and the actual beneficiary language.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The whole undertaking and described class support a rule for contemplated through performance. A direct-privity condition would delete the adopted class. Protection for every inland carrier regardless of text would overreach.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A maritime through bill’s limitation extends to a downstream inland performer when its actual beneficiary language covers persons used to perform the promised through carriage; direct naming or privity is not an additional requirement contrary to that text. ICC’s undertaking reaches Huntsville and expressly includes independent contractors performing it, so Norfolk Southern falls within the described class, subject to that bill’s own substantive limits.

**Reach Within the Question:** The actual international through contract and performer description, not automatic statutory COGSA coverage for all inland carriage.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Read nature of undertaking and clause together before identifying protected actors. Until a separate inland contract or materially different clause is presented, apply its proper law and actual text rather than assume this beneficiary status.

**Transition:** Not applicable to enforcement of the actual clause through existing maritime principles.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Kossick focuses on the undertaking’s maritime character and relevant local interests; the accident’s inland location does not alone detach this promised international delivery. American Dredging distinguishes local forum administration from substantive maritime uniformity. Herd requires contractual support for noncarrier protection, which the broad performance class supplies here; it does not impose a missing privity term. Text carries decisive weight after the proper law is identified.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Kossick and American Dredging at their maritime scope; distinguish Herd’s missing contractual protection from the class expressly covered here.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Different contracts, unresolved package definitions and the original bill’s numerical limit require their own terms and facts.

##### Issue 2: The intermediary’s limited authority to arrange downstream risk

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The second bill independently supplies the invoked downstream amount; absence of general agency does not answer the narrower entrusted-shipment question.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Can ICC’s authorized carriage arrangement bind the owner to the downstream liability limitation?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Extend Great Northern’s shipment-entrustment principle to this limited maritime transaction.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. Reliable downstream risk allocation accompanies authorized procurement of this carriage. Requiring general agency would detach performance from its agreed terms. Authority over unrelated claims or property would exceed entrustment.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** An owner entrusting through carriage to an intermediary is bound, as against the downstream carrier, by the liability limitation the intermediary agreed for that authorized carriage, without making the intermediary a general agent. The railroad may invoke the Hamburg Süd bill’s applicable downstream protection, while any inconsistent promise by ICC remains subject to the owner’s actual recourse against ICC.

**Reach Within the Question:** Negotiated risk for the entrusted carriage, not power to settle unrelated claims, transfer other property or waive every statutory safeguard.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Tie limited authority to the performance entrusted and the downstream agreement actually made. Until an unrelated act or owner-intermediary claim is presented, apply ordinary agency and contractual rules rather than generalize this limited authority.

**Transition:** Harper applies the proposed federal maritime construction to all still-open direct-review claims within its scope, including existing shipments. Reynoldsville permits no reliance-only withholding, while actual contract terms, independent claims and finality remain. No final judgment or unrelated contract is reopened or rewritten.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Great Northern recognizes that entrusting shipment entails authority to arrange carriage and its valuation terms. Extending that confined principle avoids forcing each downstream carrier to investigate a remote owner’s entire contractual relationship while preserving accountability for inconsistent intermediary promises. Maritime uniformity supports the limited scope, not a power to rewrite the first bill or presume liability. The distinction from general agency is essential to the rule, not a label shielding every forwarder act.

**Precedent Treatment:** Extend Great Northern to downstream risk allocation in this through shipment; preserve ordinary general-agency limits and the original bill’s independent terms.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual fault, package calculation and any claim against ICC remain unadjudicated.

## 121. Inyo County v. Paiute-Shoshone Indians of Bishop Community of Bishop Colony

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would reject an unavailable §1983 sovereign-prerogative theory without erasing a separately pleaded federal claim. Jurisdiction, cause, substantive immunity and remedy must remain distinct.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate and remand: reject §1983 insofar as it asserts the sovereign exemption claimed here, and require adjudication of the independent federal theory.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** No damages, general warrant immunity or injunction issues on this record. Resolve the residual claim’s jurisdiction, authorized cause and applicable federal Indian-law rule before deciding its merits and requested prospective relief. The conditional personal-immunity question is unnecessary once this §1983 damages theory fails.

**Remedial Reach:** The judgment concerns these plaintiffs’ asserted sovereign right to withhold evidence. It does not exclude every tribe or tribal corporation from every civil-rights claim, and it adjudicates no absent employee’s private search right.

**Practical Relief:** On return, identify the actual residual federal source and the remedy it authorizes; hear preserved arguments rather than dismiss the whole action solely because §1983 fails. If no authorized claim remains, dismiss on that ground. If one exists, decide its process-immunity merits and any equitable predicates before restraining county investigation. A jurisdiction statute alone cannot produce damages.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: The particular sovereign claim under §1983

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Claimant and right coverage precede the conditional qualified-immunity question; the latter is unnecessary to reject the asserted statutory damages theory.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does §1983 authorize this sovereign exemption from otherwise ordinary probable-cause process?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply context-sensitive statutory authorization rather than universal personhood labels.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The right actually asserted is sovereign withholding of evidence. Less leaves the unavailable remedy intact. Excluding every tribal private-right action would exceed this claim.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** The tribe and sovereign enterprise cannot invoke §1983 to vindicate the particular sovereign exemption from otherwise valid county search process asserted here without a distinct covered private-right violation. Probable cause, particularity and ordinary execution are not challenged; recasting the claimed sovereign privilege as a Fourth Amendment injury does not supply the missing statutory cause.

**Reach Within the Question:** These plaintiffs and this sovereign prerogative, not every tribal entity or ordinary personal search claim.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify the actual right before statutory claimant eligibility. Until a separate private-right claim is presented, apply §1983’s existing source and remedial requirements without treating this holding as categorical tribal exclusion.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Will addresses §1983’s sovereign remedial context, and Vermont Agency requires attention to the particular statutory scheme rather than universal consequences from person language. Neither directly decides all tribal capacities. The actual complaint depends on sovereign immunity from process, not a search defect shared with private persons. Monell concerns municipal attribution and cannot fill that gap.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Will and Vermont Agency’s source-sensitive reasoning; preserve Monell’s distinct defendant-attribution role.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Ordinary private search defects and other corporate capacities were not pleaded and preserved here.

##### Issue 2: The independent federal claim and its lawful remedy

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Failure of one statutory theory does not dispose of a substantial separately pleaded federal claim. Its unresolved authorization is antecedent to a merits decree on process immunity.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Must the residual federal Indian-law route receive separate adjudication?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Require source-specific jurisdiction, cause and merits analysis; do not extend contract-suit immunity by analogy alone.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. Preserving the actual pleaded route avoids accidental forfeiture. An immediate whole-action dismissal would be narrower than the unresolved claim allows. A categorical process-immunity decree would bypass its legal source and scope.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** The lower court must adjudicate the independently pleaded federal Indian-law claim under its own jurisdictional, cause-of-action and substantive requirements before dismissing the whole action or granting prospective protection. Neither §1331 or §1362 alone nor immunity from direct contract suit establishes a damages action or immunity from warrants; nor does conceded state criminal authority automatically defeat a properly sourced sovereign process claim.

**Reach Within the Question:** The residual prospective claim actually pleaded, with no presumption of success or invented alternative enforcement arrangement.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Until this Court decides process immunity on a properly established residual claim, courts apply the existing source-specific federal Indian-law rules and Kiowa’s actual limits, not a general equivalence between suit immunity and evidence immunity.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Kiowa preserves immunity from direct contract suit absent waiver or authorization while separating substantive duties and other enforcement mechanisms. Williams v. Lee’s self-government principle must be fitted to the actual process claim, not treated as a universal warrant bar. The proposed consent alternative explains the parties’ practical dispute but supplies no legal cause or established constitutional violation. Initial source adjudication permits a lawful complete answer without manufacturing one now.

**Precedent Treatment:** Preserve Kiowa’s contract-suit holding and its separation of enforcement mechanisms; no silent extension to searches or rejection of every self-government claim.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** The particular residual source, claimant capacity and relief predicates require adjudication not completed below; no alternative factual search defect is supplied.

## 122. Altman v. Bedford Central School District

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would determine present jurisdiction before issuing any religion merits judgment. The conditional legal analyses below use each school’s own evidence and the current attribution/function framework.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Preserve jurisdictional dismissal of the elementary- and middle-school claims; decide on the opinion below and the record supplied whether the remaining high-school branch presents a live controversy before executing a merits disposition.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** No broad injunction or fee award is reinstated. If the remaining claim is live, adjudicate its two constitutional theories under the rules below; otherwise dismiss and determine vacatur and dependent fee consequences under ordinary law.

**Remedial Reach:** The operative inquiry concerns the identified claimants’ current high-school exposure. No class or absent pupil’s claim supplies jurisdiction, and another school’s activity supplies neither injury nor merits evidence.

**Practical Relief:** Determine the actual enrollment or other redressable interest at the assigned event from the opinion below and the record supplied before deciding merits. If claimants cannot establish a continuing stake, no constitutional mandate issues; fees alone cannot preserve the merits dispute. If jurisdiction exists, use the actual high-school findings and lawful review standards rather than combine records across schools. Identify the challenged act and apply Agostini’s appropriate route: proved official worship, adopted doctrine or delegated religious authority triggers the specific prohibition and a fitted order ending that act, without further secular-purpose balancing. An established secular instructional relationship defeats the establishment claim on that ground; a genuine unresolved attribution or function issue requires only the ordinary record work material to that issue. On the described high-school findings, no such religious act is shown, so no establishment injunction follows. Any distinct free-exercise claim still requires its own burden and justification analysis.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Present school-specific controversy

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Moves and progression already mooted the other schools’ claims, while the 2001 opinion does not establish the high-school stake in 2003.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Can the Court decide this merits dispute without current redressable exposure?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply standing and mootness before constitutional classification.

**Reach Calibration:** Confined to this record. The missing current stake controls adjudicative power. Less would evade Article III. Borrowing absent students or issuing a districtwide advisory rule would exceed it.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A plaintiff must establish a continuing personal, redressable interest in the particular school practice before a merits judgment issues. A prior enrollment statement, general taxpayer assertion or fees dispute does not supply it. Steele’s distinct municipal-taxpayer route requires a supported probable local fiscal effect, which this record does not establish; neither an exact dollar calculation nor a newly invented expenditure is substituted for that showing. The moot branches remain dismissed, and the high-school branch requires a limited jurisdictional determination on the current record.

**Reach Within the Question:** The existing parties and school-specific claims, not a new class or damages theory.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Until current exposure is established, courts apply ordinary standing, mootness and vacatur rules without entering the conditional merits conclusions.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The known changes already eliminated major parts of the action. The time gap makes the remaining stake a real missing fact, not a formality. A constitutional question’s importance does not remedy its absence. Steele rests on a demonstrated probable local fiscal effect from the challenged financing, not objection alone; its route does not cure this missing record by inference from the existence of public schools. A separate school’s religious material cannot support the claimant’s injury.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply existing jurisdictional requirements and Steele’s actual probable-fiscal-effect rule; distinguish its supported financing nexus without creating a general objector or fee-based exception.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Current high-school status and the corresponding vacatur consequences are determined on the opinion below and the record supplied.

##### Issue 2: Conditional establishment analysis of the actual activity

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only if the high-school claim remains live; constitutional classification uses the actual supported trial record.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Do these metaphorical gifts and environmental ceremonies constitute official religious observance?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Agostini’s unified attribution-and-function inquiry, not independent historical Lemon prongs.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The high-school record supports a distinction between secular symbolism and worship. Less would leave the presented activity unclassified if jurisdiction exists. Immunizing every environmental ceremony would exceed the facts.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A public conservation program does not become an establishment merely from ceremonial forms or metaphorical gifts when the supported record shows no governmental devotional function, adopted religious doctrine or directed worship. The challenger must establish the governmental religious act, not only religious resemblance or personal offense. On the described high-school facts, speeches offering knowledge and wisdom do not establish Earth divinity or worship; voluntary attendance would not save an actually devotional event.

**Reach Within the Question:** The described high-school program, not the distinct tape or practices at other schools.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify what government actually organizes and adopts. Until actual prayer, ritual worship or a different ceremony is presented, courts apply Agostini and the school-worship rules to its own facts.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Agostini first identifies the governmental act and then uses the applicable specific-prohibition, established-permissible-relationship or residual attribution-and-function route. Lee’s official worship and Santa Fe’s delegated representative religious choice are specific prohibitions: if their predicates are proved, secular educational aims cannot outweigh the violation.
