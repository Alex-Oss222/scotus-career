Fit governs the unanimous statutory and procedural formulation. Ginsburg's Lawall opinion demonstrates the careful separation of intimate information, recipient and legitimate function, while her Scheidler concurrence insists on the conditions of the particular statute. Those capabilities fit public pseudonymity with confidential defense access and FACE's separate conduct, nexus and private-action predicates. Souter is the strongest equal-count alternative through Meyer and his privacy joins; Ginsburg's demonstrated coordination of disclosure scope and lawful administration gives her the closer fit. Stone-Zsela is fully eligible at the same count, but that concrete comparative advantage favors Ginsburg. The common procedural ground already honors Scalia and Thomas's limits; the assignment neither adds a constitutional-anonymity holding nor secures a new join.
