Fit governs the settled coverage, enforcement, equity, access and discovery grounds. Thomas's Gonzaga opinion distinguishes institutional funding obligations from the private entitlement asserted there, and his Meyer opinion separates the source of a remedy from jurisdiction. Those are the central coordination tasks in this public grant-enforcement case. Ginsburg is the strongest alternative through Owasso's maintained-record definition, but has the higher count; Thomas can state that definition and all disclosure permissions while giving the enforcement-source distinction its independent force. His selection does not make prior authorship an entitlement or omit the other fully eligible Associates. Stone-Zsela's discovery fallback supplies a judgment vote, not the complete Part V rationale, and therefore does not make him eligible for the whole lead.
