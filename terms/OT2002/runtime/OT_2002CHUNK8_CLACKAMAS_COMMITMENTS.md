## Common legal and factual premises

The ADA's circular employee definition calls for the ordinary common-law relationship inquiry. Darden and the unanimous complete-relationship holding in Johnson & Higgins require all relevant incidents, with actual control important but no decisive title, corporate form, ownership interest, compensation label or exclusive factor list. Johnson & Higgins establishes that genuine employment may coexist with substantial collective governance; its particular individual review, compensation-setting and removal controls cannot be imported here. Genuine proprietors remain outside employee status.

The six EEOC considerations usefully direct attention to hiring, firing and work rules; supervision; reporting; influence over the enterprise; expressed intent; and profit, loss and liability arrangements. They supplement rather than replace the complete inquiry. No arithmetic score or presumption against working owners follows. Christensen and Mead supply the controlling distinction between persuasive guidance and delegated authority carrying the force of law; the packet establishes no such force for this guidance. The common-law rule can be adopted independently of any disputed broader account of administrative deference.

The record reports four physicians who apparently control clinic operations, share profits and bear personal malpractice liability. Apparent collective operational control is substantial contrary evidence, but neither individual immunity from direction or removal nor general business-loss exposure is established by those statements. The record also reports actual services for the corporation, written employment contracts, salaries and annual bonuses, corporate premises, required compliance with clinic standards and reporting to a personnel manager. These are concrete relationship evidence. Their existence does not itself establish the manager's independent disciplinary power or the doctors' inability to change the standards.

Clackamas's ERISA employee acknowledgment and reason for incorporating, together with its workers' compensation acknowledgment, bear on its understanding and treatment of the relationships. Darden makes the ERISA comparison particularly pertinent. Neither acknowledgment concedes ADA coverage or creates estoppel. The quoted Oregon language is an incomplete statutory excerpt, not a complete alternative federal definition. Personal malpractice responsibility receives its actual scope; it is not equated with general entrepreneurial loss.

Two supported paths therefore remain: (R) vacate the categorical appellate analysis and return the actual relationships and necessary count for application of the complete inquiry; or (E) affirm reversal of the practice's summary judgment and hold these four physicians employees on the combined reported incidents, rejecting the appellate court's categorical reason. Path E must rest on an assessment of established incidents, not an invented removal restriction. Path R must identify the material unresolved operation of the reported arrangements, not infer missing evidence, litigant default or a need to possess every original document.

## Justice-specific commitments and adversarial comparison

### Stevens — R: vacate and remand classification and necessary counting

**Ground and own authorities:** His joins in Darden's all-incidents rule and both the rule and conclusive application in Johnson & Higgins support actual employee status notwithstanding ownership when the complete relationship establishes it. Here he would apply that same rule but decline a conclusive classification. His Walters join separates genuine status from payroll and daily work; his Mead join permits persuasion without turning guidance into law.

**Strongest alternative:** E has substantial support because contracts, continuing operational service, organizational standards and reporting are present, and the practice recognizes employment elsewhere. His Johnson & Higgins application prevents dismissal of those incidents as merely formal. The decisive difference is that Johnson & Higgins established actual individual review and removal controls; this record does not establish how the manager and standards constrain each physician against his governance power. The alternative's combined evidence is meaningful but does not compel the legal inference Stevens would require to settle that relation now.

**Barrier and scope:** He will not join a proprietor presumption based on apparent collective control, categorical inclusion because of incorporation, or an instruction to restore the practice's summary judgment. He can join a remand explaining and weighing both sets of incidents. Supported individual controls could yield inclusion on return without requiring identical corporate machinery to Johnson & Higgins.

### O'Connor — R: vacate and remand classification and necessary counting

**Ground and own authorities:** Her Darden join, Johnson & Higgins rule and application joins, and Christensen and Mead persuasive-guidance joins support the full common-law inquiry. She would distinguish a proved dual role from uncertainty over whether the supposed supervision operates independently of these four proprietors' own authority.

**Strongest alternative:** E treats the clinic's sustained salaried service, standards, manager reporting and acknowledged employment arrangements as enough, just as ownership did not defeat employment in Johnson & Higgins. That argument does not fail merely because these are professionals. O'Connor would nevertheless require assessment of the actual allocation of control, since the record simultaneously reports apparent control of the entire practice. Conversely, the practice cannot win by converting its governance evidence into individual nonemployee findings.

**Barrier and scope:** No single-control-factor exclusion, benefit-election rule or owner exception. She joins R only if the lower courts consider all reported employment incidents and their actual operation. No demand for a particular form of contract, a new factual concession or a conclusive below-threshold finding follows.

## Count, remedy and coalition boundaries

All eight support the complete-relationship legal rule and reject conclusive corporate-form and ownership rules. Six Associates—Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas—support R. Ginsburg and Breyer support E on the combined relationship. These are non-Stone commitments only; no full Court tally, assignment or historical reconciliation is supplied.

R vacates the appellate judgment and remands for the proper inquiry and proceedings consistent with it; it does not revive the district court's categorical exclusion or direct judgment for the practice. A lower court must determine only those classifications and counts necessary to coverage. If supported other-employee counts already suffice, unnecessary physician classifications need not be decided. A material factual issue concerning control is not a finding that the source documents are absent from the Court's record.

E affirms reversal of the practice's summary judgment, resolves these four relationships as employment and returns the action for genuinely remaining matters. It does not reopen status merely to reproduce Johnson & Higgins's particular review and removal mechanism. It does not adjudicate discrimination, causation, damages or any independent defense.

The reported count is four physician-shareholders plus **at least fourteen** other employees for twenty-eight full weeks in 1996 and thirty-seven full weeks in 1997. For a recited year within the statutory current-or-preceding-year window, inclusion of all four yields at least eighteen during the reported overlap; inclusion of even one can supply fifteen. Those year-specific durations independently exceed twenty weeks. The count concerns genuine relationships on each working day, not actual work or payment that day, and need not use the same fifteenth person throughout if the required total is maintained. Separately insufficient years cannot be combined.

Conversely, excluding all four does not prove fewer than fifteen other employees: a minimum of fourteen supplies no maximum. No Associate would grant final noncoverage on the arithmetic assumption of exactly fourteen. The source excerpts do not independently establish the discriminatory-event date; E resolves status and the stated minimum arithmetic without inventing that date or declaring every other coverage element satisfied. R likewise must use the actual relevant period and supported count. No additional user premise or new research is needed to freeze these bounded commitments.

**Retained material limit:** The force of individual governance, supervision and work-direction arrangements separates R from E; the precise applicable-year nexus and any count needed to establish exclusion remain record questions at their stated scope. These limits support the dispositions above and are not operator or arrival blockers.
