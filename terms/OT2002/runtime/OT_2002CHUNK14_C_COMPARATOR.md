## 171. Alkire v. Irving

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified. The Sixth Circuit unanimously affirmed in part, reversed in part and remanded in its June 2, 2003 amended opinion.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** District Judge Tarnow wrote for the panel with Judges Moore and Cole; no separate opinion appears. The amended opinion replaced the earlier September 18, 2002 opinion.

* **Historical Holding:** The court reinstated the prolonged-detention and debt-related-incarceration claims because of material disputes and evidence of policy, while preserving denial of Alkire's summary judgment. It affirmed rejection of the separate jail-credit theory and class certification and required further inquiry into the County Court's status.

* **Historical Authority:** Alkire v. Irving supplies the reviewed lower judgment. Its statements about immunity and attribution do not displace the independently controlling holdings.

* **Historical Sources:** Full amended opinion.
