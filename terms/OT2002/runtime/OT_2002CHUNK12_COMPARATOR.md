## 141. Entergy Louisiana, Inc. v. Louisiana Public Service Commission

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9–0.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote for a unanimous Court.

* **Historical Holding:** The federal filed-rate principles preempted Louisiana’s order despite the agreement’s use of discretionary inputs. The Court did not decide a hypothetical tariff violation or the exclusivity of FERC’s jurisdiction over such a claim.

* **Historical Authority:** Entergy Louisiana, Inc. v. Louisiana Public Service Commission.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinion.

## 144. Hillside Dairy, Inc. v. Lyons

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded. The Article IV ruling was unanimous; the Commerce Clause analysis had eight votes.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens wrote. Justice Thomas concurred in part and dissented in part, rejecting the dormant Commerce Clause ground while joining the remaining portions.

* **Historical Holding:** The federal provision did not immunize pricing and pooling; absence of facial citizenship language did not dispose of the individuals’ Article IV claims.

* **Historical Authority:** Hillside Dairy, Inc. v. Lyons.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinion.

## 151. United States v. Suerte

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Fifth Circuit unanimously vacated the indictment’s dismissal and remanded on May 14, 2002.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Barksdale wrote for a panel also comprising Duhé and Dennis. No separate writing appears in the reported opinion.

* **Historical Holding:** In the presented MDLEA setting with flag-state consent, due process did not impose the additional nexus demanded by the district court. The court reserved whether the Clause imposes other extraterritorial constraints and permitted the conspiracy prosecution to proceed.

* **Historical Authority:** The lower-court opinion supplies the historical comparator; no Supreme Court merits disposition is identified.

* **Historical Sources:** Reported appellate opinion, especially the discussion of Malta’s consent and the final remand.
