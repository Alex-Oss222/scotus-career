### Alkire: a supported return under existing duties, not acceptance of an attribution shortcut

The June 2 amended Sixth Circuit judgment reinstated the extended-detention and poverty-incarceration claims, retained denial of Alkire's motion, and preserved the distinct jail-credit and class dispositions. The final Associate position matches that reviewed disposition within the granted questions. It does not adopt the lower opinion's suggestion that an official-capacity caption itself identifies County policy. McMillian's unanimous identification rule, Carter's eight-Associate municipal-omission rule, and the unanimous January 14 Gibson opinion require the actual federal deprivation and the particular entity's causal policy to be proved separately.

The competing valid-warrant and nonappearance grounds remain legally sufficient when their facts are established. They do not support defense summary judgment on this record because neither the outside warrant's whole-interval operation nor the actual basis of each contempt term is conclusively established. Powell preserves an unreasonable-delay challenge even within forty-eight hours and requires a bona fide emergency or extraordinary circumstance after that point; routine weekends do not qualify. Bearden requires the means, willfulness, bona fide-effort and alternatives inquiry when poverty causes nonpayment, without immunizing independently lawful punishment for disobedience. These are different grounds governing different incarcerations.

Each Associate has a compatible own-authority basis. Stevens's Gibson opinion and all eight Associates' Carter/Gibson joins establish the demanding policy route. O'Connor's Bearden reasoning and Heck opinion support the separate imprisonment and necessary-invalidity inquiries. Ginsburg's Powell opinion supplies the complete timing rule. Scalia and Kennedy joined that timing-and-remand rule; Thomas's Powell disagreement addressed criminal remedy, not permission for routine unconstitutional delay. All eight joined McMillian Part I and Regents, so differences about Alabama's particular sheriff or Hess's actual compact classification do not preclude an Ohio-specific structural return. Souter and Breyer's Randell joins support its opportunity exception; Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Ginsburg apply that effective rule here without enlarging their former position into a new exception. The claim-specific legal comparison is common ground, and no factual finding about diligence or available review is supplied.

The County Court's arm status and personhood must be determined before retrospective liability against that entity, through the full legal, financial and institutional inquiry. The prospective settlement is preserved at its reported scope; its unrecovered full terms are not used to create a new bar or remedy. ### Gatewood: resolve the reserved allocation question without borrowing the circuit's historical premise

The en banc court's affirmance depended on a constitutional recidivism exception and the characterization of subsection (c)(3)(A) as a defendant's affirmative defense. The supplied Almendarez-Torres decision is instead a statutory aggravated-element ruling; Apprendi, Angle, Harris and Ring expressly left prior convictions undecided. The lower court's historical account therefore cannot settle the present constitutional question. Its ten-to-four count supplies no basis for an eight-Associate distribution.

The five element-rule Associates and the three recidivist-defense Associates have each confronted that open question. Stevens, Scalia, Souter and Ginsburg's Almendarez-Torres avoidance position supplies a concrete prior objection to making prior status indispensable to greater federal punishment while omitting it from the aggravated charge. Their later Angle charging joins and Harris punishment-floor joins support resolving that objection constitutionally when the current statute's express allocation must be tested. Thomas's older Almendarez-Torres dissent is meaningful contrary evidence; his later Apprendi, Angle and Harris joins, and his continued separation of element safeguards from jeopardy finality, support his new answer at the punishment-authorizing level. This is a supported inference from his subsequent own public positions, not a claim that the prior reservation already compelled his vote.

O'Connor, Kennedy and Breyer retain their Almendarez-Torres distinction between previously adjudicated status and present-offense conduct. Their Angle and Harris objections confirm that they do not extend the binding-range principle automatically. Kennedy's Ring join applies settled present-offense maximum law to death eligibility while retaining the prior-conviction reservation. Breyer's separate Eighth Amendment selection rationale is capital-specific. Neither supplies a contrary noncapital recidivist commitment. Cooper addresses competence to undergo trial, and Parke concerns regularity and production in collateral review; neither precedent itself supplies the ultimate standard for this statutory exception. The three accept the heightened burden as an actual recidivist affirmative defense, rather than claiming that either precedent already decides it.

**Exact operation of the robbery exception.** Subsection (c)(3)(A)'s exclusion requires both the absence of firearm or dangerous-weapon use and threatened use, and the absence of resulting death or serious bodily injury to any person. The disqualifying exception thus contains cumulative absence conditions. Under G2's constitutional allocation, the government must establish qualifying facts through the required element adjudication; defeating that exception can rest on the applicable qualifying alternative of weapon use, threatened weapon use, or death or serious bodily injury. G2 does not require proof that every listed aggravating feature occurred. The prior conviction's existence and qualifying identity remain separately necessary. The three contrary Associates leave proof of the complete statutory exclusion on the defendant by clear and convincing evidence. This paragraph makes express the operation of the already frozen text; it changes no vote, factual premise, or legal theory.

The narrower bare-conviction exception and the quantum-only correction were genuine alternatives. The five reject the former because even the bare indispensable predicate performs aggravated charging work; the three regard the broader recidivist-defense arrangement as lawful. The five reject merely reducing defendant persuasion because it leaves an element outside the prosecution's case; the three find no constitutional defect requiring that substitution. No intermediate Associate rationale is manufactured to narrow the rule.

The result must cover all three counts. Mandatory life exceeds the Hobbs Act ceiling and raises the compulsory floor for both kidnapping counts even though kidnapping independently permits life. The five's binding-range analysis addresses both effects. Their retained remedial ground is the preserved omission of an aggravated federal charge and element adjudication on the reported procedure. Almendarez-Torres supplies the admission-versus-charge distinction. Neither sentencing notice, prior labels, nor evidence sufficient to support an unreturned aggravated verdict cures that charge. They do not infer harmlessness or harmfulness from missing Arkansas documents, announce universal structural error, or overrule Neder/Ring's actual holdings. Scalia and Ginsburg retain their stricter actual-equivalent-findings position, which is compatible with this independently sufficient charge-based disposition.

The three's affirmance likewise requires no invented Arkansas finding: on their rationale a valid robbery conviction establishes the recidivist baseline and the defendant must prove the enacted exclusion. Denial of participation does not establish the exclusion or authorize collateral erasure of final guilt. The source limit would become material to a different affirmance based on what a particular judgment necessarily found; that is not any Associate's final ground here. Monge's bounded noncapital renewed-proof permission and Sattazahn's distinct capital-finality rule remain in force at their actual scope. A remand supplies no independent authority for new charges or an enhancement jury. Any further proceeding requires its own charging, waiver, notice, sufficiency, procedure and double-jeopardy authority.

### Eichorn: preserve the mixed judgment without reproducing overbroad antitrust language

The Third Circuit's mixed disposition is consistent with the final Associate position, but neither its outcome nor its phrasing is automatically adopted. California Dental, NYNEX and Microsoft require inquiry into the actual agreement and competitive mechanism. The rule cannot say that only competitors can ever form a Sherman Act agreement, that a sale label proves legality, or that every rule-of-reason plaintiff invariably needs an elaborate market-definition study. The adopted commitments use none of those propositions.

The Preliminary Net's internal-coordination ground is confined to the supported period of common ownership. Prospective separation alone does not create independent actors. The Pre-Closing and Post-Closing Nets require their own analysis; their covered workforce, limited duration, supported continuity function, available competing employers, and deficient claimed pension-dependent market support the actual antitrust dismissal. Loss of an employee's preferred pension arrangement is a real individual disadvantage but does not itself prove injury to competition. The strongest allocation objection remains answered by the covenant's actual ancillary function and this record's competitive showing, rather than by an assumption that labor receives less antitrust protection.

The California Dental divide has been tested Justice by Justice. Souter, O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas's majority positions insist on examining a material commercial explanation. Breyer, Stevens, Kennedy and Ginsburg's contrary application there rejected merely conceivable offsets to demonstrated harm. Here the supported transfer explanation and absence of an adequate competitive-effects showing differ from the advertising record those four found sufficient. Their agreement is an application of their own demand for actual competitive analysis, not capitulation to the other four's result in a different case. All eight joined Inter-Modal's protected-attainment principle and remaining statutory inquiries.

The pension claim asks a different question. Timing beyond the bridging deadline, communications concerning that effect, and direct pension savings together permit a specific-intent inference; the legitimate workforce explanation remains a competing inference. The employees retain the ordinary proof burden on participant status, the right, prohibited action, interference purpose, cause and authorized relief. Footnotes 8–10 of the saved opinion support the named ERISA defendants and exact surviving restrictions: AT&T and Lucent are defendants, the two later Nets survive, and the Preliminary Net remains excluded. Class discovery is reconsidered, while any certification must satisfy Rule 23 under Bridgestone/Firestone. Great-West governs relief where section 502(a)(3) is invoked without converting every remedy into a universal tracing question. No independent new fact or ground was needed.

### Bragg: the text retains the federal duty, and the remedy does not bypass the statutory scheme

The comparator's immunity dismissal rests on treating the contested obligations after primacy as exclusively state law. The final Associates reject that construction. Section 1260(b)(1) itself conditions permit or revision approval on the regulatory authority's finding of compliance with the federal chapter and the applicable state or federal program. Section 1270(a)(2) expressly contemplates a suit concerning the appropriate state regulatory authority's failure to perform a nondiscretionary duty under that chapter, subject to its conditions and constitutional immunity reservation. Together those commands support the retained federal duty identified in B1. Mere federal approval would be insufficient; the statutory command is essential to the result.

The competing primacy argument is substantial: the State administers its approved program, state law states the buffer terms, and Congress supplies federal supervision and possible displacement. But the retained permit-finding command and express state-authority citizen route are not repealed by that administrative arrangement. The adopted opinion need not federalize every independent state rule or treat every discretionary decision as a nondiscretionary federal duty. Nor does it treat the federal respondents' brief as a holding: the saved response verifies the relevant text and reports the counts, while the construction is the Associates' independent legal commitment.

The two contested counts remain distinct. Count 2 challenges missing required findings before valley-fill authorization; Count 3 challenges the supposed authority to allow burial of intermittent and perennial streams under the buffer provision. The preserved buffer terms permit disturbance within one hundred feet only upon the Director's specified authorization and findings concerning normal flow or gradient, fish migration and related environmental values, water quantity or quality, and applicable state or federal water-quality standards. A categorical no-fill conclusion does not follow merely from identifying a federal source. The lower courts must determine the actual nondiscretionary obligation, resolve the preserved statutory and compliance issues, and tailor any prospective remedy. The immunity dismissal direction is vacated; the broad injunction is not automatically restored; the distinct consent decree remains effective.

The prior four-Associate Young objections receive substantive answers. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas's Seminole dissent objected to substituting an officer action for Congress's selected State-party compacting mechanism, not to every detailed statute or affirmative command. This statute expressly names state regulatory authorities and the adopted action retains its nondiscretionary-duty and procedural limits. Their Coeur d'Alene objection concerned a demand functionally replacing sovereign title and comprehensive control; no comparable title decree is entered here, and the broad mining injunction receives further scrutiny. Their later unanimous Verizon Part III and Westside joins support the actual connected-officer, continuing-federal-duty and prospective-remedy route. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer's Seminole/Coeur d'Alene positions support the same route without adding their broader direct-State theories.

All eight's no-waiver conclusion follows the effective consent law. Florida Prepaid Part IV had every Associate's direct join and applied the rejection of constructive commercial consent. Bradley requires an express disclosed forum, claim and remedy condition plus actual acceptance; regulatory primacy supplies no comparable unconditional surrender. Lapides concerns the State's affirmative invocation of adjudication. Consent to the separately settled decree does not establish consent to contested Counts 2 and 3. The constitutional immunity reservation cannot become a waiver simply because the statute names a state authority. No independent abrogation vote is inferred from the officer claim. Statutory notice, standing, actual nondiscretionary duty and proper prospective relief remain requirements; their satisfaction is not invented.

### Sons of Confederate Veterans: the private emblem and the surviving statute support affirmance

The lower panel's affirmance is the reviewed judgment; the later rehearing poll adds no Supreme Court lineup or independent merits judgment. The final Associates' common ground rests on this program's actual function. Official manufacture, continued State ownership, legislative authorization and design control are genuine counterarguments, not facts omitted from the analysis. The optional organizational emblem nevertheless originates with independent organizations in a broadly opened opportunity, with motorists voluntarily selecting it. Those facts support attribution to the organization rather than automatic adoption as the State's own message.

Rosenberger's program-specific viewpoint rule, Southworth's actual independent-expression setting and Velazquez's function inquiry support S1 and S2. The unique ban on every SCV logo targets the Confederate-emblem perspective within this otherwise authorized opportunity; calling that perspective a separate subject does not render the exclusion neutral. The holding needs no universal plate classification or complete forum taxonomy. Wooley's compelled-display rule addresses a different interest and does not do the classification work.

The potentially contrary Associate positions have been considered. Scalia and Thomas's Finley grant-selection theory expressly preserved the rules of a forum actually opened; both joined Rosenberger and Southworth. O'Connor joined those decisions and in Velazquez defended a defined welfare service, not a categorical power to suppress an organization's perspective in a broad expression program. Their three-Justice conditional severability position there also concerned a different federal statute and an expressly conditioned service exception. Stevens, Souter and Breyer's Southworth concurrence declined a universal compulsory-fee framework while preserving Rosenberger's viewpoint-access principle; it does not prevent their direct join here. Souter's Finley dissent gives a separate own-authority basis for rejecting viewpoint conformity; Breyer and Ginsburg's narrow Finley positions preserve review of actual invidious exclusion. Kennedy's Rosenberger opinion directly supports independent-expression attribution. No Associate's current join adopts another's broader subsidy theory.

The applicable Virginia section 1-17.1 presumption, subject to provisions that must operate together, supplies the remedial rule. The first sentence authorizes issuance on application and written membership evidence; the second alone supplies the logo prohibition. Removing the latter leaves the authorization capable of performing its stated function. The possibility that some legislators preferred the restriction does not establish a contrary statutory condition overcoming Virginia's presumption on this record. S3 therefore sustains the actual issuance relief under an already enacted surviving authorization. It does not depend on the district court's alternative inherent-equity theory or a new judicial benefit. The membership/application and otherwise lawful program requirements remain. The deferred fee and cost questions remain in their separate posture.
