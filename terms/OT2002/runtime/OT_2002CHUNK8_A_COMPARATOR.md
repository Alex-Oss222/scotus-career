## 91. PacifiCare Health Systems, Inc. v. Book

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** April 7, 2003: reversed and remanded, 8–0 among participating Justices. Justice Thomas did not participate.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote for all participating Members. Thomas took no part in consideration or decision.

**Historical Holding:** The Court found it premature to invalidate the agreements on the assumption that their remedy limitations prohibited RICO treble damages. Because that interpretation was uncertain, it required arbitration without deciding who would resolve enforceability if the agreements actually eliminated statutory relief.

**Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only. The historical unanimity and later arbitration doctrine are not sources of controlling authority.

**Historical Sources:** PacifiCare Health Systems, Inc. v. Book, 538 U.S. 401.

## 92. State Farm Mutual Automobile Insurance Co. v. Campbell

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** April 7, 2003: reversed and remanded, 6–3.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, and Breyer. Justices Scalia, Thomas, and Ginsburg each dissented.

**Historical Holding:** The historical Court held the award excessive, rejected punishment for dissimilar out-of-state conduct, and stated that few awards exceeding a single-digit ratio would satisfy due process. It also suggested that substantial compensatory awards can support an amount at or near compensation. Those ratio formulations are part of the historical result; Section I states BMW’s governing rule.

**Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only. The historical numerical guidance is distinct from Stone’s territorial and claim-specific ground.

**Historical Sources:** State Farm Mutual Automobile Insurance Co. v. Campbell, 538 U.S. 408.

## 95. Boisson v. Banian, Ltd.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits decision is identified. The Second Circuit's December 3, 2001 three-judge decision unanimously affirmed in part, reversed in part and remanded.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Cardamone wrote for Calabresi and Haight, the latter sitting by designation. No separate opinion appears.

* **Historical Holding:** Original coordination of the quilt elements warranted protection, and the district court's comparison was too atomized. The two Green versions infringed School Days I; the other comparisons did not. Remedies were remanded without an award.

* **Historical Authority:** The lower-court decision supplies the historical anchor and record, not an earlier Supreme Court holding. The later September 2003 remedies outcome is excluded from this pre-event merits analysis.

* **Historical Sources:** Reported opinion.

## 98. Jinks v. Richland County

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9–0, April 22, 2003.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for the unanimous Court; Souter concurred separately.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 1367(d) was upheld as necessary and proper to federal judicial administration and applicable to political subdivisions.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical reasoning about Alden cannot replace the different entering holding.
* **Historical Sources:** Jinks v. Richland County, 538 U.S. 456; No. 02-258.

## 99. Dole Food Co. v. Patrickson

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No. 01-593 dismissed; No. 01-594 affirmed. The timing ground was unanimous; the direct-ownership construction divided 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote the opinion. Breyer, joined by O’Connor, disagreed with the direct-ownership holding while joining the other identified portions.
* **Historical Holding:** The ownership branch required direct majority ownership by the foreign state; status was measured at filing.
* **Historical Authority:** April 22, 2003 historical Supreme Court comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** Dole Food Co. v. Patrickson, 538 U.S. 468; Nos. 01-593 and 01-594.
