## 33. Johnson v. City of Cincinnati

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. LEGAL POSTURE

Stone’s proposed disposition and reasoning follow. Governing law is distinguished from proposed extensions; unresolved facts remain subject to the procedural posture in Section I.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the constitutional basis for the existing fee judgment on ordinary local movement and Johnson’s established parent-authorized caregiving. Remand the prospective decree for a claim-specific determination of continuing enforceability after State v. Burnett and identify the status of any preserved damages claim.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Preserve the valid basis of the $38,500 fee judgment without fixing a new award. If prospective exposure remains, maintain effective protection against the unsupported neighborhood exclusions; if it is moot, apply the governing equitable vacatur principles to that component. Determine whether a damages claim remains and, if so, its causation, compensable injury and defenses; neither award nor silently dismiss damages here.
* **Remedial Reach:** The fee dispute remains live independently of prospective relief. For a live challenge to the proved categorical exclusion policy, Stone would presume relief against that policy for the persons it governs unless the City shows a narrower complete cure. That is a proposed remedial rule, not an assumption that a fee dispute creates jurisdiction to enjoin an abandoned ordinance.
* **Practical Relief:** The remand court must first determine the binding effect of State v. Burnett and whether any damages claim remains. If the City relies on its voluntary cessation, Laidlaw requires it to make absolutely clear that the challenged injurious enforcement cannot reasonably be expected to recur; the plaintiffs need not prove an announced plan to resume. That mootness burden is distinct from the plaintiffs’ entitlement to a continuing injunction. The court must therefore determine actual legal enforceability and remaining exposure before retaining prospective relief. If an injunction is still warranted, it must bar the unsupported exclusion, require withdrawal of operative exclusion notices and corresponding enforcement directions, and permit verification through those records. Any later enforcement contrary to the decree warrants enforcement proceedings. If no prospective controversy remains, the court must dispose of that component without erasing a still-live fee or damages controversy.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Ordinary public passage

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the rights necessary to review the contested fee judgment; that dispute does not establish continuing prospective exposure.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** May the City exclude these persons from all public neighborhood routes solely on the specified arrest or conviction?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Resolve the open local-movement question rather than claim Saenz or Morales already decided it.
* **Reach Calibration:** Intermediate: serious personal neighborhood exclusions require a usable liberty rule. Broader treatment would constitutionalize every traffic rule; narrower review would miss the categorical restraint on ordinary community life.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Ordinary local passage on public streets and sidewalks is protected liberty, and a substantial personal neighborhood exclusion requires a compelling justification with an individualized fit preserving lawful essential access. The claimant must establish the exclusion and its substantial interference; the City then must justify its necessity and scope under strict scrutiny. Proving a past arrest or conviction by a preponderance in an administrative appeal does not discharge that constitutional burden. These exclusions fail because neither their trigger nor their exceptions establish why the individual’s future lawful presence must be banned.
* **Reach Within the Question:** Broad bans on otherwise lawful presence, not ordinary route, hours or place-use management. Arrest or conviction may inform individualized restrictions but cannot replace their justification.
* **Next-Case Guidance:** For comparable personal exclusion zones, demand an individualized necessity and scope showing; ordinary traffic and place-use rules remain distinct. Until any disputed prospective exposure is established, apply Article III and the separate rules for fees and damages rather than infer an injunction from a favorable merits premise.
* **Transition:** Under Harper, this rule governs the parties and all cases still open on direct review. Reynoldsville preserves independent immunity, procedural and remedial limits; reliance alone cannot justify selective prospectivity. Final judgments are not reopened, and collateral relief requires its own governing authorization.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Drug suppression is compelling, but aggregate recidivism does not explain criminalizing each person’s lawful presence. The appeal tests the past offense, not the necessity of the future ban; variances do not repair that defect. Text and liberty precedent combine with operational evidence, with the direct restraint on public passage decisive.
* **Precedent Treatment:** Distinguish Saenz’s interstate-citizenship rule and Morales’s actual holding; extend movement protections to this direct local restraint without treating either case as controlling the open question.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The continued legal and practical operation of the ordinance and the status of damages require remand findings. Double jeopardy is outside the grant.

##### Issue 2: Established parent-authorized caregiving

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Johnson’s caregiving supports the live fee basis. Movement fully resolves Au France’s exclusion, making an additional professional-association classification unnecessary.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does the ban impermissibly interrupt Johnson’s established role raising her grandchildren?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Extend family-direction protection to established parent-authorized care without creating a right against the parent.
* **Reach Calibration:** Narrow: protect the established chosen relationship. Broader grandparent autonomy would conflict with parental authority; narrower treatment would ignore the actual family arrangement disrupted.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Government may not substantially interrupt an established parent-chosen caregiving relationship through a categorical neighborhood exclusion without a compelling, fitted justification. Johnson must show her actual parent-authorized role and substantial disruption; the City must justify that interference, not merely establish her past offense. Her routine assistance in raising five grandchildren is established, and the ordinance supplies neither a caregiving accommodation nor an individualized reason that assistance creates the danger invoked.
* **Reach Within the Question:** Her routine school trips and care for five grandchildren with their mother’s consent; no entitlement to impose visits.
* **Next-Case Guidance:** Comparable state interference must respect the parent’s actual choice. Parental-refusal cases remain governed by Troxel; no attorney-client intimacy holding follows.
* **Transition:** Under Harper, this rule governs the parties and all cases still open on direct review. Reynoldsville preserves independent immunity, procedural and remedial limits; reliance alone cannot justify selective prospectivity. Final judgments are not reopened, and collateral relief requires its own governing authorization.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The City supplied neither a caregiving exception nor an individualized reason this grandmother’s assistance posed danger. Protecting the arrangement reinforces parental choice. Au France’s access problem confirms the geographic burden but needs no separate intimacy rule.
* **Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Troxel’s parental-choice protection and extend it to governmental interruption of the parent’s established caregiving arrangement.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Claims to override a parent’s refusal are outside this record. The attorney-association theory need not be decided because ordinary movement supplies Au France’s complete constitutional protection.
