## 61. Tenafly Eruv Association, Inc. v. Borough of Tenafly

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator is identified. The Third Circuit reversed the denial of preliminary relief on October 24, 2002.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Ambro wrote for the unanimous panel, which included Judges Nygaard and Roth.

**Historical Holding:** The Borough’s selective enforcement was not neutral and generally applicable; the Free Exercise claim supported preliminary relief. Allowing private eruv attachments did not establish religion.

**Historical Authority:** The historical court applied Smith and Lukumi to the challenged exemptions and burdens on religious conduct.

**Historical Sources:** Historical lower-court opinion and record

## 64. Henderson v. Stalder

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** The Fifth Circuit's March 29, 2002 opinion, revised April 4, directed dismissal for lack of standing. On January 9, 2003, it recalled its mandate and modified its rehearing response to allow Keeler a reasonable opportunity to challenge the overall specialty-plate practice. That later order is historical procedural context, not an amendment already before the assigned November 2002 review. The later district-court proceedings concerned an amended complaint and are not a second OT2002 grant here.
* **Historical Supreme Court Merits Comparator:** The Court denied certiorari on December 2, 2002, 537 U.S. 1048, No. 02-523. There was no Supreme Court merits decision on the standing or speech issues. The assigned merits submission replaces that petition disposition counterfactually.
* **Historical Sources:** The official Fifth Circuit opinion; the Supreme Court's reported order; Henderson v. Stalder, 265 F. Supp. 2d 699 (E.D. La. 2003), recounting the mandate recall and later amendment. The official Supreme Court order controls the certiorari date.
