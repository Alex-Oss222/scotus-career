# Jimenez Recio — neutral record supplement for fresh validation

This supplement contains objective procedural recitations only. It supplies no Supreme Court outcome, alignment, rationale, author, or proposed vote. It supplements the approved neutral packet without changing its question or relief.

The second trial instruction required joinder while the conspiracy's objective remained attainable. The Government did not object to that instruction before the jury retired, did not challenge the validity of the circuit's automatic-termination rule in its initial circuit appeal presentation, and first challenged that rule in its petition for rehearing en banc. Its closing argument accepted the burden of proving pre-seizure joinder under the instruction given.

The then-applicable Rule 30 required a party assigning instructional error to object before the jury retired and identify the challenged matter and grounds. Whether the Government's procedural conduct prevents the requested appellate correction is a legal issue, not a fact established by this supplement. Distinguish an instructional-error claim, review of a court of appeals' legal rule, any availability of plain-error review, and the effect of the Government's accepted trial burden on sufficiency and remedy. No alternative is adopted here.
