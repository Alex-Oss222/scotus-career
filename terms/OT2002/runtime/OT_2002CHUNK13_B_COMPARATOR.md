## 163. Krimstock v. Kelly

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits opinion on this judgment is identified. The Second Circuit vacated the dismissal and remanded.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Sotomayor wrote for a panel including Judges Jacobs and Parker.

* **Historical Holding:** The vehicle-retention scheme required a prompt hearing before a neutral factfinder on the probable validity of continued detention, including the City’s likelihood of success and need for retention. Details were left for implementation below.

* **Historical Authority:** Krimstock v. Kelly.
