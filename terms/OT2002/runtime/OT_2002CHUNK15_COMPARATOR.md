## 179. Columbia Union College v. Oliver

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

On June 26, 2001, the Fourth Circuit affirmed. Chief Judge Wilkinson wrote the majority opinion, joined by Judge Williams. Judge Motz concurred in the judgment on the ground that the trial finding under Roemer was not clearly erroneous and declined to treat that precedent as displaced by the broader reading of Mitchell.

The historical judgment permitted Sellinger funding for the specified secular programs under the restrictions and findings. It did not hold that the grants were unrestricted, and it did not invalidate the program. The earlier Columbia Union v. Clarke judgment was followed by denial of certiorari at 527 U.S. 1013 in 1999; that earlier denial is not a verified Supreme Court disposition of the later 254 F.3d 496 judgment. No historical Supreme Court merits decision in the later matter is asserted. Authority: 254 F.3d 496; 159 F.3d 151.
