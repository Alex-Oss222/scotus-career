## 117. United States v. Haney

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would enforce the already established individual right and separately sustain Congress’s stock-regulation authority. Neither militia membership nor valid commerce power decides the unresolved arms application.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reject the commerce challenge; vacate the militia-based arms ruling and remand the resulting §922(o) conviction disposition for the governing two-stage inquiry.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Determine protected conduct, actual burden and the corresponding justification on the preserved record. If the application is valid under that analysis, the convictions may stand on that ground; if it fails, set aside the affected convictions and sentence to the extent required by that constitutional ruling. No immediate acquittal or unrelated sentencing review is directed.

**Remedial Reach:** Relief concerns Haney’s completed weapons and convictions. No statutory exception is established, and no categorical machine-gun right, exclusion or statute-wide invalidation follows.

**Practical Relief:** The lower court must identify the protected activity and practical defensive alternatives, explain the restriction’s severity and test the Government’s actual evidence against the applicable tier. Decide on the existing permissible record when sufficient; any further proceedings require an actual material need and ordinary authority, not an automatic opportunity to rebuild the case. The mandate must identify the consequence for both convictions and the dependent sentence.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Individual right and a weapon-specific restriction

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The preserved arms challenge cannot be rejected by a militia prerequisite that entering law has already displaced.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does lack of militia connection defeat protection, and what inquiry governs these machine guns?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Love, Hale and Wright; do not resolve their expressly open machine-gun application by label.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The recurring two-stage test must govern this application rather than a militia shortcut. Less leaves an erroneous prerequisite intact. Automatic protection or exclusion of the weapon category would outrun the findings.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** An individual need not demonstrate organized militia membership to invoke the Second Amendment; a machine-gun restriction must instead be tested for protected conduct and its actual burden. Haney must identify the protected keeping or bearing implicated. Coverage turns on text, history and precedent, separately from justification. Wright independently forbids abolishing meaningful possession of ordinary functional defensive arms in the home; that boundary supplies no automatic right to these machine guns. For otherwise protected activity, Government must justify a core deprivation by a compelling interest, narrow tailoring and no comparably effective, materially less restrictive alternative; a substantial regulation by an important objective, substantial relationship and no unnecessary or effective elimination of protected activity. Deferential reasonableness governs only an incidental regulation imposing no meaningful interference; arbitrary administration, discrimination and cumulative destruction remain forbidden. Military utility and the statutory mechanism label decide neither stage. The lower court must apply that existing rule to these completed weapons and the stock restriction.

**Reach Within the Question:** The preserved §922(o) application, not unfinished parts, unproved statutory exceptions or an unlimited right to every mechanism.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Determine practical protected use and burden before choosing the justification standard. Until this Court resolves a different weapon application, courts apply Wright’s two-stage rule rather than borrow Emerson’s individual-danger predicate as a category rule.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Love and Wright establish an individual right independent of militia participation; Wright reserved the final machine-gun classification. Fully automatic operation can bear strongly on risk and fit but does not itself supply findings about the actual protected burden and alternatives. Emerson sustains a supported person-specific protective-order disability and cannot substitute for evidence about a weapon-defined restriction. Enumerated power and individual protection remain independent.

**Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Love, Hale and Wright’s individual-right and two-stage rules; distinguish Emerson and Fraternal Order of Police’s conduct-based settings.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual protected-conduct classification, burden and justification require analysis of the preserved record; no unproved exception or unrelated trial issue is added.

##### Issue 2: Local completion within the national stock mechanism

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The independent commerce defense is controlled for the completed converted and assembled weapons described.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Must the Government prove an interstate effect from these particular possessions?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Hale, Kirk and Wright’s stock-regulation holdings.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The actual regulatory mechanism supports inclusion of comparable local completed weapons. A transaction-only exception would defeat that mechanism. A general federal police power would exceed its connection.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Congress may reach possession of completed locally converted or assembled machine guns under §922(o)’s national civilian-stock regime without proof of this defendant’s interstate sale. Exempting that local class would materially undermine the stock restriction; the connection, not aggregate crime costs alone, sustains the application to Haney’s two weapons.

**Reach Within the Question:** The completed-weapon class within the actual regime and its statutory exceptions; the arms defense remains separate.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify the real stock mechanism and how the local class affects it. Until another statutory class is presented, apply the existing connection requirement and Lopez’s enumerated-power boundary rather than a generic dangerousness rationale.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Hale, Kirk and Wright sustain the regime because local conversion and assembly provide the direct means of expanding the restricted stock. Lopez preserves limits but does not displace that concrete mechanism with an individual transaction requirement. A remote component’s past travel is not the rationale, and valid commerce authority does not establish Second Amendment validity.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Hale, Kirk and Wright to local completion; preserve Lopez outside their specific regulatory connection.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Unpresented statutory exceptions and materially different weapon classes require their own facts.

## 122. Altman v. Bedford Central School District

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would determine present jurisdiction before issuing any religion merits judgment. The conditional legal analyses below use each school’s own evidence and the current attribution/function framework.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Preserve jurisdictional dismissal of the elementary- and middle-school claims; decide on the opinion below and the record supplied whether the remaining high-school branch presents a live controversy before executing a merits disposition.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** No broad injunction or fee award is reinstated. If the remaining claim is live, adjudicate its two constitutional theories under the rules below; otherwise dismiss and determine vacatur and dependent fee consequences under ordinary law.

**Remedial Reach:** The operative inquiry concerns the identified claimants’ current high-school exposure. No class or absent pupil’s claim supplies jurisdiction, and another school’s activity supplies neither injury nor merits evidence.

**Practical Relief:** Determine the actual enrollment or other redressable interest at the assigned event from the opinion below and the record supplied before deciding merits. If claimants cannot establish a continuing stake, no constitutional mandate issues; fees alone cannot preserve the merits dispute. If jurisdiction exists, use the actual high-school findings and lawful review standards rather than combine records across schools. Identify the challenged act and apply Agostini’s appropriate route: proved official worship, adopted doctrine or delegated religious authority triggers the specific prohibition and a fitted order ending that act, without further secular-purpose balancing. An established secular instructional relationship defeats the establishment claim on that ground; a genuine unresolved attribution or function issue requires only the ordinary record work material to that issue. On the described high-school findings, no such religious act is shown, so no establishment injunction follows. Any distinct free-exercise claim still requires its own burden and justification analysis.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Present school-specific controversy

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Moves and progression already mooted the other schools’ claims, while the 2001 opinion does not establish the high-school stake in 2003.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Can the Court decide this merits dispute without current redressable exposure?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply standing and mootness before constitutional classification.

**Reach Calibration:** Confined to this record. The missing current stake controls adjudicative power. Less would evade Article III. Borrowing absent students or issuing a districtwide advisory rule would exceed it.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A plaintiff must establish a continuing personal, redressable interest in the particular school practice before a merits judgment issues. A prior enrollment statement, general taxpayer assertion or fees dispute does not supply it. Steele’s distinct municipal-taxpayer route requires a supported probable local fiscal effect, which this record does not establish; neither an exact dollar calculation nor a newly invented expenditure is substituted for that showing. The moot branches remain dismissed, and the high-school branch requires a limited jurisdictional determination on the current record.

**Reach Within the Question:** The existing parties and school-specific claims, not a new class or damages theory.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Until current exposure is established, courts apply ordinary standing, mootness and vacatur rules without entering the conditional merits conclusions.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The known changes already eliminated major parts of the action. The time gap makes the remaining stake a real missing fact, not a formality. A constitutional question’s importance does not remedy its absence. Steele rests on a demonstrated probable local fiscal effect from the challenged financing, not objection alone; its route does not cure this missing record by inference from the existence of public schools. A separate school’s religious material cannot support the claimant’s injury.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply existing jurisdictional requirements and Steele’s actual probable-fiscal-effect rule; distinguish its supported financing nexus without creating a general objector or fee-based exception.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Current high-school status and the corresponding vacatur consequences are determined on the opinion below and the record supplied.

##### Issue 2: Conditional establishment analysis of the actual activity

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only if the high-school claim remains live; constitutional classification uses the actual supported trial record.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Do these metaphorical gifts and environmental ceremonies constitute official religious observance?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Agostini’s unified attribution-and-function inquiry, not independent historical Lemon prongs.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The high-school record supports a distinction between secular symbolism and worship. Less would leave the presented activity unclassified if jurisdiction exists. Immunizing every environmental ceremony would exceed the facts.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A public conservation program does not become an establishment merely from ceremonial forms or metaphorical gifts when the supported record shows no governmental devotional function, adopted religious doctrine or directed worship. The challenger must establish the governmental religious act, not only religious resemblance or personal offense. On the described high-school facts, speeches offering knowledge and wisdom do not establish Earth divinity or worship; voluntary attendance would not save an actually devotional event.

**Reach Within the Question:** The described high-school program, not the distinct tape or practices at other schools.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Identify what government actually organizes and adopts. Until actual prayer, ritual worship or a different ceremony is presented, courts apply Agostini and the school-worship rules to its own facts.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Agostini first identifies the governmental act and then uses the applicable specific-prohibition, established-permissible-relationship or residual attribution-and-function route. Lee’s official worship and Santa Fe’s delegated representative religious choice are specific prohibitions: if their predicates are proved, secular educational aims cannot outweigh the violation. Here the supported high-school activity is secular conservation instruction, and the metaphorical gifts do not establish adoption of Earth divinity or direction of worship. That established instructional character defeats the claim presented; no free-floating balance of religious resemblance against educational value is required. Purpose, effects and institutional relationships remain evidence of the actual function rather than independent prongs. Commack makes clear that the broader constitutional inquiry also reaches government adoption of orthodoxy as a civil standard, even without a devotional ceremony. Neither that authoritative religious function nor directed worship is shown by these conservation speeches, drums and metaphorical gifts. A family’s religious disagreement does not itself turn secular conservation instruction into an official religious standard. Schempp permits education about ideas and Epperson rejects tailoring teaching to a particular creed. The separate-school invocation cannot supply the missing high-school fact.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Agostini’s integrated test and Commack’s prohibition on civil adoption or delegation of religious authority; preserve Lee and Santa Fe’s prohibition of official observance. Distinguish the reported secular instruction without making formal worship the only establishment injury.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** A different school’s tape and unsupported altar, deity or prayer findings are outside this high-school record.

##### Issue 3: Conditional free-exercise analysis

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Religious exercise is a distinct claim; it cannot be absorbed into forum classification or establishment analysis.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does disagreement with the families’ beliefs establish an unconstitutional exercise burden?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply the existing targeting and actual-burden principles separately.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The record shows conflicting ideas, not established coercion or interference. Ignoring practical pressure would underprotect exercise. A general parental veto over secular teaching would overreach.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A secular lesson’s conflict with religious belief does not alone violate free exercise without compelled profession, targeting, prohibited disadvantage or another actual interference with sincere practice. Formal voluntariness is not conclusive when substantial practical religious pressure is proved, but the described high-school record establishes no such compulsion, penalty or targeted burden.

**Reach Within the Question:** The actual exercise effects on these claimants, preserving challenges to real coercion or discriminatory treatment.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Until a supported compulsory profession, practical pressure or targeting claim is presented, courts apply Smith, Lukumi and Lee at their actual scope rather than presume either an exemption or unlimited school authority.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Lukumi tests targeting and comparable secular underinclusion; Shahar preserves a separate religious-exercise inquiry. Lee recognizes real pressure even without formal penalties, so optional attendance is evidence rather than an automatic defense. Here a population remark can conflict with Catholic teaching without prescribing theology or restricting practice. The absence of the relevant burden, not disbelief of the families’ sincerity, defeats the conditional claim.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Smith and Lukumi at their preserved scope, retain Lee’s practical-pressure protection and Shahar’s source separation.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** No actual compelled profession, exclusion or distinct supported exercise burden is established in this record.

## 125. Hilton v. City of Wheeling

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would enforce Olech’s objective protection against intentional irrational administration while rejecting a distinct affirmative entitlement to the police action a petitioner prefers.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm rejection of the affirmative Petition Clause claim; vacate the equal-protection component and remand without a personal-animus requirement.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Assess the summary-judgment record for intent, material comparability and negation of legitimate grounds, then any preserved attribution, immunity, injury and relief questions. No damages or injunction follows now.

**Remedial Reach:** The remaining claim concerns Hilton’s actual treatment and responsible defendants. No neighborhood-wide policing decree or discovery entitlement unrelated to that claim is created.

**Practical Relief:** The lower court must compare relevant complaints and enforcement circumstances rather than count citations, explain any independent ground for judgment and preserve proper defenses. If liability is proved, relief must cure that injury; prospective orders require a continuing threat and established municipal or individual responsibility, not past disparity alone.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Intentional irrational police administration

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The lower court made absence of personal motive decisive; the surviving evidence must be tested under the correct elements, not presumed sufficient.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Must Hilton prove personal animus in addition to intentional irrational treatment?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply Olech’s express objective rule unchanged.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The rule reaches policing without disabling rational discretion. A motive prerequisite would contradict Olech. Treating every unequal outcome as unconstitutional would exceed its comparator and rationality requirements.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A class-of-one claimant need not prove personal animus as an independent element, but must establish intentional different treatment of materially similarly situated persons and negate reasonably conceivable legitimate grounds. At summary judgment Hilton needs evidence supporting those predicates, not numerical disparity or unexplained outcomes; at merits factual liability requires preponderance proof. Probable cause for his citations does not alone decide whether materially comparable complaints against others were irrationally treated.

**Reach Within the Question:** Intentional unequal enforcement or protection, excluding negligence, different evidence, reasonable priorities and mere disagreement with discretion.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Compare severity, proof, credibility and circumstances before inferring irrationality. Until actual attribution or prospective threat is established, apply ordinary §1983 and equitable requirements rather than infer municipal liability from the underlying rule.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Olech expressly rejects spite as a mandatory element of objective unequal administration; its pleading posture does not relieve Hilton of Rule 56 evidence. Approximately eighty responses, varied complaints and citations to both sides on one occasion illustrate why bare asymmetry is insufficient. The existing legal error nevertheless prevents treating absence of vendetta as a complete answer. Olech requires negation of reasonably conceivable legitimate grounds; it does not require officers to have recorded a contemporaneous explanation. Actual comparability and intentional treatment remain factual issues, while legitimate reasons such as different evidence, credibility or severity must be assessed against the choice challenged rather than treated as personal-animus substitutes.

**Precedent Treatment:** Reaffirm Olech and reject the circuit’s incompatible personal-motive gloss; add no policing immunity or presumption of irrationality.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Actual comparability, independent summary-judgment grounds, attribution, immunity and injury require the preserved record.

##### Issue 2: Petitioning versus compelled government service

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Officers received the complaints; the asserted claim demands a favorable enforcement response, not redress for obstructed access or retaliation.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does the Petition Clause require investigation, prosecution or requested protection?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply existing petition and negative-duty principles.

**Reach Calibration:** Narrow. The asserted service demand fails as a legal entitlement. Less leaves an independent claim unresolved. Excluding retaliation or unequal administration would exceed this theory.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** The Petition Clause protects presenting grievances but does not itself require officials to investigate, prosecute, respond favorably or provide requested police protection. Receipt of Hilton’s complaints followed by refusal of his preferred action does not establish that claim; equal-protection obligations remain separately enforceable.

**Reach Within the Question:** Compelled assistance, not obstruction, retaliation, custody or independently created duties.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Until a distinct retaliation, access-denial or protective-duty claim is presented, courts apply its own elements rather than use this service-demand holding as immunity.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Smith v. Arkansas State Highway Employees rejects a general duty to listen or respond through a chosen grievance arrangement. DeShaney and the treatment of Pinder distinguish constitutional restraints from a general duty to prevent private harm. They do not authorize irrational distribution of services actually supplied, so the separate equality remand remains necessary.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Smith and the relevant DeShaney/Pinder distinction; preserve separate affirmative-danger and access claims at their own scope.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** No supported retaliation, access-denial, custody or affirmative-danger claim is presented.

## 126. Conant v. Walters

### SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

#### A. DECISIONAL POSTURE

I would protect sincere clinical communication while preserving enforcement of actual criminal participation. The existing class and stipulated common threat support effective relief against the policy as construed.

#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

**Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the permanent injunction with its limiting construction for protected recommendations and genuinely supported criminal investigation.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** Protect covered physician class members from registration loss or a revocation-directed investigation solely for sincere recommendations within bona fide treatment relationships. Preserve investigation supported by the decree’s good-faith substantial-evidence condition and lawful action for prescribing, dispensing, conspiracy or purposeful assistance.

**Remedial Reach:** The adjudicated common threat reaches the actual narrowed class. I would presumptively cure the policy for those governed unless officials demonstrate a narrower complete cure; that remedial proposal reinforces, rather than enlarges beyond, the established class and protected recommendation category.

**Practical Relief:** Officials must cease recommendation-only registration threats and enforcement while the decree operates. Any investigation claimed outside the restraint must rest on the specified independent criminal evidence, not merely anticipated patient use. The court can assess compliance from the stated grounds and actual conduct without requiring judicial preclearance of every investigation. Proven noncompliance is enforceable through the decree; no drug entitlement or reclassification follows.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Recommendation and purposeful participation

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The injunction’s actual construction, not a hypothetical immunity for crime, defines the challenged conduct.

**Question Stone Resolves:** Does anticipating a patient’s unlawful acquisition make a sincere recommendation aiding and abetting or conspiracy?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply actual criminal participation elements and Rice’s expression/assistance distinction.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The rule protects clinical information while reaching purposeful criminal assistance. Foreseeability alone would underprotect advice. Medical sincerity as immunity for a proved offense would overreach.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** A bona fide recommendation alone does not establish the purpose, assistance or agreement required for the asserted criminal participation theories, even when the physician anticipates possible unlawful patient use. A criminal conviction requires the Government to prove the actual offense’s elements beyond a reasonable doubt; an administrative sanction retains its own lawful statutory predicates and procedures. The injunction does not protect a recommendation functioning as purposeful assistance when the relevant elements are established.

**Reach Within the Question:** Sincere advice within treatment, distinct from unlawful prescribing, dispensing, agreement or materially effective purposeful aid.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Ask what the communication actually does and the purpose it serves. Until a different transaction is presented, apply the actual offense elements rather than infer participation from favorable medical advice.

**Transition:** Not applicable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Rice permits liability for purposeful, materially effective criminal aid but separates advocacy, foreseeability and knowledge of possible misuse. It creates no drug offense; the relevant statutes supply their own elements. Advice has legitimate uses in further treatment discussion, research and efforts to change law. Anticipation cannot automatically attribute a patient’s independent act to the doctor.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Rice’s purpose-and-assistance distinction at its actual scope; preserve the independent criminal statutes rather than make sincerity a defense to completed elements.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** A concrete purposeful acquisition scheme requires evidence not established by this common policy record.

##### Issue 2: Viewpoint-selected regulatory threats and effective relief

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** The stipulated genuine fear and suppression of communication establish a live pre-enforcement injury; the permanent decree is reviewable despite the unappealed preliminary order.

**Question Stone Resolves:** May recommendation alone trigger loss of registration or an investigation toward that penalty?

**Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply speech protection to independently attributed clinical judgment and the proven common threat.

**Reach Calibration:** Intermediate. The policy chills a defined clinical view across the class and requires a common cure. Individual exemptions leave that threat operative. Immunity from genuinely supported criminal investigation would exceed the violation.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Officials may not use DEA registration loss or a revocation-directed investigation as a penalty solely for a sincere medical recommendation within a bona fide treatment relationship. The Government bears the burden of justifying this viewpoint-selected restraint on private clinical speech through a compelling interest and narrow tailoring. Preventing unlawful drug transactions may be compelling, but suppressing recommendations alone is not narrowly tailored when actual prescribing, dispensing and purposeful criminal assistance remain enforceable. The class injunction is warranted by the credible stipulated threat and actual chilling, but preserves the decree’s good-faith investigation supported by substantial evidence of criminal conduct.

**Reach Within the Question:** The narrowed class, sincere recommendation and recommendation-only basis; no view on medical efficacy, necessity defense or all federal drug regulation.

**Next-Case Guidance:** Separate the government’s own message from a clinician’s independent judgment and protected advice from evidence of actual offense. Until a concrete enforcement action is presented, apply the decree’s actual limiting construction rather than infer blanket investigatory immunity.

**Transition:** The speech application uses existing protections. If the complete-policy-cure presumption is adopted, Harper applies it in nonfinal cases within that proven common scope; Reynoldsville permits no reliance-only continuation of the threat. Final judgments and independent class, jurisdiction and equitable limits remain.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Casey permits bounded accurate informed-consent information, not suppression of a clinician’s sincere contrary view or withdrawal of protection because services are paid. Rosenberger’s actual program distinction helps identify private attribution but its funding holding is not a licensing code. The policy tolerates negative advice and threatens favorable advice; public health and enforcement interests do not explain why protected advice alone must be punished. The record’s unsupported prediction of enforcement obstruction does not outweigh the established chilling, while the limiting construction preserves actual criminal work.

**Precedent Treatment:** Apply Casey’s protection of clinical communication and Rosenberger’s attribution distinction without extending their factual settings wholesale; preserve Rice’s genuine criminal-participation boundary.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Clinical efficacy, a medical-necessity defense, classification and a separate federalism theory are outside the grant.
