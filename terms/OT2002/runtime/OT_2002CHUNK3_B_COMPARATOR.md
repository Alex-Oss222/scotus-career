## 30. Pierce County v. Guillen

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** On January 14, 2003, the Court dismissed certiorari as to the tort action and reversed and remanded the public-disclosure judgment unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote for the Court.
* **Historical Holding:** The statute protected program-compiled or program-collected holdings but not independently held nonprogram originals. Both the original statute and 1995 amendment were valid Commerce Clause measures; only the public-disclosure component was reviewably final.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical Supreme Court decision in this same matter, solely as comparator.
* **Historical Sources:** Opinion.

## 34. Barnhart v. Peabody Coal Co.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** On January 15, 2003, the Court reversed the judgments, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer. Justice Scalia dissented with O’Connor and Thomas; Thomas also wrote separately.
* **Historical Holding:** The Commissioner retained authority to make otherwise valid initial assignments after October 1, 1993; the mandatory deadline did not prescribe loss of power.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical Supreme Court decision in this same matter, solely as comparator.
* **Historical Sources:** Opinion and dissents.

## 35. Eldred v. Ashcroft

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** On January 15, 2003, the Court affirmed, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Ginsburg wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas. Justices Stevens and Breyer filed separate dissents.
* **Historical Holding:** The finite extension of existing terms was within the Copyright Clause, and the Act’s preservation of copyright’s traditional speech safeguards defeated the First Amendment challenge.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical Supreme Court decision in this same matter, solely as comparator.
* **Historical Sources:** Opinion and dissents.

## 38. United States v. Jimenez Recio

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** On January 21, 2003, the Court reversed and remanded. Eight Justices joined the Court’s opinion; Justice Stevens agreed with rejection of automatic termination but disagreed with aspects of disposition.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for the Court. Justice Stevens filed an opinion concurring in part and dissenting in part.
* **Historical Holding:** Government frustration does not automatically terminate a conspiracy. The case returned for further proceedings rather than treating impossibility as a complete defense.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical Supreme Court decision in this same matter, solely as comparator.
* **Historical Sources:** Opinion and separate opinion.
