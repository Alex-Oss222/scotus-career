## Controlling holdings

### The actual merchandising and accountability connection satisfies ordinary review

**Holding and operative rule:** An ordinary economic qualification that implicates neither a fundamental right nor a suspect class satisfies due process and equal protection when it has a reasonably conceivable rational connection to a legitimate public objective; the challenger must negate that connection without demanding empirical certainty, formal findings or the least burdensome system. Tennessee's funeral-director qualification may be applied to these merchandise-only retailers because its actual merchandise instruction and examination, together with professional discipline, rationally connect the qualification to competence and accountability in this specialized sales market.

**Authority:** The opinion of the Court, authored by @@LEAD@@ and joined by @@LEAD_JOINERS@@, is supported in full by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Seven Justices adopt this rule, its application to both granted constitutional grounds and dissolution of the injunction.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court holds that the trial findings do not negate every reasonably conceivable legitimate connection. Beach Communications permits supported economic generalizations without proof that the legislature chose the best system. Lee Optical allows imperfect regulatory lines, and Nordlinger permits substantial disparities when an actual legitimate purpose rationally explains them. Heller and Romer retain scrutiny of the relationship to the disadvantage imposed; they do not require a separate, least burdensome scheme whenever one is imaginable.

These stores do not handle bodies, arrange funerals or burials, embalm or make advance-need sales. Much of the required preparation concerns services they never perform. But the curriculum includes merchandise instruction, and 37 of 250 examination questions concern funeral merchandising. A common professional qualification and disciplinary system can rationally serve informed selection, ethical sales and accountability when goods are sold in the funeral market. This is a permissible legislative connection, not a new finding that the qualification measurably improves casket quality.

The dissent's strongest objection concerns the entire entry barrier, not the mere existence of useful instruction. The Court nevertheless rejects its application because ordinary review does not impose proportionality between the curriculum and each specialty's daily work. The findings about casket quality, unrestricted burial choices and the substantial unrelated training defeat some proposed safety explanations; they do not eliminate the actual merchandise and accountability connection. Existing fraud remedies and a possible retail-specific license do not make the common qualification irrational.

Moreno and Cleburne continue to prohibit illegitimate ends and irrational distinctions. The Court does not decide whether incumbent protection standing alone is a legitimate end: the second question's premise that no independent rational public connection exists is not established in this record. The distinct Privileges or Immunities theory lies outside the grant.

## Precedent treatment and present force

- **FCC v. Beach Communications, Inc., 508 U.S. 307:** Applies the reasonably conceivable legitimate-basis rule, challenger's burden and absence of a formal-findings requirement; it supplies no immunity for an irrational or impermissibly motivated line.
- **Williamson v. Lee Optical of Oklahoma, Inc., 348 U.S. 483:** Applies legislative latitude in economic regulation without requiring an optimally tailored occupation or curriculum.
- **Nordlinger v. Hahn, 505 U.S. 1:** Applies its tolerance of substantial disparities supported by legitimate policy differences; protection of an existing business's profits alone is not decided.
- **Heller v. Doe, 509 U.S. 312:** Preserves the required relationship to the actual disadvantage and its separate holding that a less extensive alternative does not alone invalidate a rational classification.
- **Romer v. Evans, 517 U.S. 620:** Preserves real rational-connection review without empirical certainty or least-restrictive-means requirements; distinguishes an unexplained disability from this actual merchandising and accountability connection.
- **Department of Agriculture v. Moreno, 413 U.S. 528, and City of Cleburne v. Cleburne Living Center, 473 U.S. 432:** Preserve the limits against bare harm and irrational prejudice; the Court adds no categorical rule that incumbent protection by itself is legitimate.

* **Other Governing Law:** The same Fourteenth Amendment guarantees. No interstate discrimination or dormant Commerce Clause claim is granted.

* **Material Other Authority:** The circuit relied partly on City of Philadelphia v. New Jersey and Energy Reserves Group, Inc. v. Kansas Power & Light Co. Those received authorities concern different constitutional settings and require a reasoned analogy rather than automatic transplantation.

* **Existing Rule’s Reach:** Supported consumer protection, institutional competence and regulatory costs remain available objectives. A rule condemning interstate economic barriers does not automatically decide local occupational licensing.

* **Open Point:** Whether a transfer to established sellers standing alone is legitimate, and whether the actual licensing arrangement can be sustained independently of that asserted end.

#### C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

* **Reasoning Below:** The courts found no rational connection between training in handling remains and businesses that never handled them. They rejected safety, grief counseling, anti-fraud and advance-need rationales, emphasizing the law's exceptions, existing anti-fraud protections and the absence of advance-need sales. The circuit regarded the targeted 1972 amendment as protection of funeral-home rents. Its alternative suggestions about directly regulating retailers did not themselves establish a least-restrictive-means requirement.

* **Petitioner or Appellant:** Tennessee invokes casket selection, consumer vulnerability, ethical sales and licensing discipline. A common qualification can place a specialized funeral-goods market within a single accountable profession, even if instruction exceeds what each participant uses. The State seeks ordinary deference, not a requirement to prove measurable health gains or explain why a more precise licensing category was not enacted.

* **Respondent or Appellee:** The retailers argue that the conceded business boundaries eliminate the activities the qualification principally teaches. Permitting homemade caskets and unrestricted burial choices defeats the suggested safety mechanism; existing fraud law and direct retail rules can protect consumers. The amendment's concentrated benefits and severe entry costs support their assertion that incumbent protection is the only real objective.

* **Alternatives and Limiting Principles:** Rejecting incumbent protection as a freestanding end does not invalidate every law benefiting an existing business. Conversely, a conceivable rationale must connect to the challenged requirement. A ruling may rest on consumer competence and accountability without relying on disputed federal Funeral Rule coverage or assuming that the plaintiffs will begin advance-need sales. Neither side asks the Court to deregulate handling human remains.

#### D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

* **Threshold Paths:** Resolve the identified threshold objections before any merits ruling; if none succeeds, reach only the preserved questions.

* **Merits Paths, Judgment, and Remedy:** Affirmance would preserve the existing narrow injunction if the retailers have negated every legitimate rational connection and the remaining objective is legally unavailable. Reversal would permit enforcement if merchandising competence and professional accountability rationally support the qualification, notwithstanding overinclusive education and incumbent benefits. A public-purpose ruling alone would not establish which of those factual and legal characterizations controls. Any remand must identify an unresolved preserved issue rather than order another trial merely because the policy is controversial.

* **Plausible Holding Scope:** A decision can govern qualification requirements imposed on a separable retail activity and define the difference between incidental private benefit and private favoritism as the entire objective. It need not recognize a fundamental occupational right, demand tailored curricula for every specialty, or decide regulation of advance payments and handling remains.

* **Record Boundary:** The existing trial findings establish the businesses’ limited activities and the curriculum’s partial merchandising content. No finding establishes that the retailers handle remains or sell advance-need services.

## Public Projection

## Event

Craigmiles v. Giles, 312 F.3d 220. Merits decision, January 14, 2003. ## Holdings

### The actual merchandising and accountability connection satisfies ordinary review

**Holding and operative rule:** An ordinary economic qualification that implicates neither a fundamental right nor a suspect class satisfies due process and equal protection when it has a reasonably conceivable rational connection to a legitimate public objective; the challenger must negate that connection without demanding empirical certainty, formal findings or the least burdensome system. Tennessee's funeral-director qualification may be applied to these merchandise-only retailers because its actual merchandise instruction and examination, together with professional discipline, rationally connect the qualification to competence and accountability in this specialized sales market.

**Authority:** The opinion of the Court, authored by @@LEAD@@ and joined by @@LEAD_JOINERS@@, is supported in full by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Seven Justices adopt this rule, its application to both granted constitutional grounds and dissolution of the injunction.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court holds that the trial findings do not negate every reasonably conceivable legitimate connection. Beach Communications permits supported economic generalizations without proof that the legislature chose the best system. Lee Optical allows imperfect regulatory lines, and Nordlinger permits substantial disparities when an actual legitimate purpose rationally explains them. Heller and Romer retain scrutiny of the relationship to the disadvantage imposed; they do not require a separate, least burdensome scheme whenever one is imaginable.

These stores do not handle bodies, arrange funerals or burials, embalm or make advance-need sales. Much of the required preparation concerns services they never perform. But the curriculum includes merchandise instruction, and 37 of 250 examination questions concern funeral merchandising. A common professional qualification and disciplinary system can rationally serve informed selection, ethical sales and accountability when goods are sold in the funeral market. This is a permissible legislative connection, not a new finding that the qualification measurably improves casket quality.

The dissent's strongest objection concerns the entire entry barrier, not the mere existence of useful instruction. The Court nevertheless rejects its application because ordinary review does not impose proportionality between the curriculum and each specialty's daily work. The findings about casket quality, unrestricted burial choices and the substantial unrelated training defeat some proposed safety explanations; they do not eliminate the actual merchandise and accountability connection. Existing fraud remedies and a possible retail-specific license do not make the common qualification irrational.

Moreno and Cleburne continue to prohibit illegitimate ends and irrational distinctions. The Court does not decide whether incumbent protection standing alone is a legitimate end: the second question's premise that no independent rational public connection exists is not established in this record. The distinct Privileges or Immunities theory lies outside the grant.

## Precedent Treatment

- **FCC v. Beach Communications, Inc., 508 U.S. 307:** Applies the reasonably conceivable legitimate-basis rule, challenger's burden and absence of a formal-findings requirement; it supplies no immunity for an irrational or impermissibly motivated line.
- **Williamson v. Lee Optical of Oklahoma, Inc., 348 U.S. 483:** Applies legislative latitude in economic regulation without requiring an optimally tailored occupation or curriculum.
- **Nordlinger v. Hahn, 505 U.S. 1:** Applies its tolerance of substantial disparities supported by legitimate policy differences; protection of an existing business's profits alone is not decided.
- **Heller v. Doe, 509 U.S. 312:** Preserves the required relationship to the actual disadvantage and its separate holding that a less extensive alternative does not alone invalidate a rational classification.
- **Romer v. Evans, 517 U.S. 620:** Preserves real rational-connection review without empirical certainty or least-restrictive-means requirements; distinguishes an unexplained disability from this actual merchandising and accountability connection.
- **Department of Agriculture v. Moreno, 413 U.S. 528, and City of Cleburne v. Cleburne Living Center, 473 U.S. 432:** Preserve the limits against bare harm and irrational prejudice; the Court adds no categorical rule that incumbent protection by itself is legitimate.

## Law After Decision

The existing rational-basis standard governs the reviewed licensing requirement. A unified occupational qualification may cover a merchandise-only specialty when actual merchandise instruction, examination and common professional accountability supply a rational connection to legitimate consumer interests, despite substantial training concerning other professional services. The decision imposes no curriculum-proportionality test and adopts no fundamental occupational right. It leaves unresolved the legitimacy of naked incumbent protection as the sole end because the actual record supplies a distinct rational public connection; it does not approve an arbitrary credential merely because incumbents benefit.

## Separate Writings

**Stevens, dissenting.** Stevens would affirm the existing-business injunction under ordinary rational-basis review. His approach in Beach and Nordlinger asks whether an impartial legislature could pursue the asserted purpose and whether that purpose rationally explains the actual inequality. He accepts that the curriculum teaches some merchandising; he rejects the conclusion that this limited content explains exclusion from the entire retail activity until the seller completes the substantially unrelated professional qualification. The trial findings concerning the stores' limited work, casket quality and the entry burden supply the basis for that conclusion. A private benefit cannot justify itself merely because the statute creates it. His objection does not demand the least restrictive fraud law or an optimally efficient course: it concerns the relationship of the whole prerequisite to the actual trade. Bare preservation of incumbent rents supplies no independent legitimate justification in his view.

**Stone-Zsela, dissenting.** The Chief Justice would affirm on both due-process and equal-protection grounds. Beach and Lee Optical permit imperfect economic lines, but Moreno and Cleburne preserve a legitimate-public-end requirement. He would extend that limit to bare protection of incumbent sellers from harmless competition. Incidental incumbent benefits, consumer protection, redistribution, aid to a distressed industry and Nordlinger's protection of established commitments remain distinguishable legitimate objectives.

He accepts the merchandising instruction, the 37 examination questions and the possible value of professional discipline. On this combined record, however, the stores perform none of the defining services that principally occupy the education and apprenticeship; the burial choices and quality findings also undermine the proposed casket-safety explanation. Discipline's usefulness does not itself connect those unrelated entry prerequisites to the admitted retail activity. Challengers retain the burden of negating reasonably conceivable legitimate explanations; no empirical proof, least restrictive system or specially optimized curriculum is constitutionally required.

He would retain relief against this separable qualification and presume equivalent protection for materially equivalent merchandise-only activity unless the State establishes that a narrower decree fully cures the proved violation. The trial findings and remedial jurisdiction must define the covered activity. Bodily care, funeral arrangements, advance-need transactions and lawful sales regulation remain outside that invalidation. The State could not obtain narrower relief by merely promising discretionary nonenforcement against the named stores. His rule would govern still-open direct-review cases under Harper, subject to Reynoldsville's independent procedural, immunity and remedial limits; final judgments remain closed and collateral relief requires its own authorization.

## Procedure After Action

The case returns for dissolution of the existing-business injunction. Officials may enforce the reviewed qualification against the covered retail activity consistently with the judgment. The disposition concerns these granted due-process and equal-protection challenges and the existing injunction; the rejected Privileges or Immunities theory remains outside this review.
