## 167. Lawrence v. Texas

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6–3 in judgment.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Justices Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice O’Connor concurred in the judgment on equal-protection grounds. Justice Scalia dissented with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice Thomas; Justice Thomas also dissented separately.

* **Historical Holding:** The historical majority protected the private consensual adult conduct under due process and overruled Bowers; Justice O’Connor relied on the discriminatory classification without overruling it.

* **Historical Authority:** Lawrence v. Texas.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.

## 168. Limon v. Kansas

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Kansas Court of Appeals affirmed in February 2002. On June 27, 2003, one day after this assigned event, the historical Supreme Court granted review, vacated, and remanded in light of Lawrence. That was not a Supreme Court merits decision resolving all three questions.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** The contemporaneous Kansas opinion was per curiam for Judges Knudson, Pierron, and Green. The later Supreme Court action was a summary remand, with no merits rationale or vote division identified here.

* **Historical Holding:** The original state judgment sustained the classification and juvenile-history use while declining the unraised Eighth Amendment question. The historical remand required reconsideration in light of the actual Lawrence decision. Subsequent 2004 and 2005 state decisions postdate this review.

* **Historical Authority:** Limon v. Kansas.

* **Historical Sources:** Contemporaneous judicial appendix; later state opinion recounting the June 27, 2003 order, used solely to verify that later procedural history.

## 169. Stogner v. California

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5–4.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for Justices Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, and Ginsburg. Justice Kennedy dissented with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia and Thomas.

* **Historical Holding:** A statute enacted after expiration of the applicable criminal limitations period cannot revive the barred prosecution consistently with the Ex Post Facto Clause. The dissent regarded the change as outside the historically defined categories.

* **Historical Authority:** Stogner v. California.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.

## 170. Nike, Inc. v. Kasky

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Certiorari dismissed as improvidently granted, 6–3.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** The dismissal was per curiam. Justice Stevens concurred with Justice Ginsburg and, for Part III, Justice Souter. Justice Kennedy dissented; Justice Breyer dissented with Justice O’Connor. The silent votes do not join every stated rationale of the concurrence.

* **Historical Holding:** No Supreme Court merits holding resolved the commercial-speech classification. The separate opinions disputed finality, standing, prudence, and the proper treatment of the speech.

* **Historical Authority:** Nike, Inc. v. Kasky.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.
