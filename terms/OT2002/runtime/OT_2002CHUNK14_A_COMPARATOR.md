## 180. Cano v. Davis

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

The historical Supreme Court **summarily affirmed on January 13, 2003**, *Cano v. Davis*, 537 U.S. 1100, No. 02-577. No merits opinion, reasoning, individual vote tally, or dissent accompanied the disposition. It left the three-judge court's judgment intact; it should not be represented as an express endorsement of every passage in that court's opinion. The judgment issued February 12, 2003. Sources: official docket, January 13 order list, and United States Reports, volume 537, page 1100.

## 164. American Insurance Association v. Garamendi

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5–4.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Kennedy, and Breyer. Justice Ginsburg dissented with Justices Stevens, Scalia, and Thomas.

* **Historical Holding:** The historical majority found HVIRA displaced by interference with presidential foreign policy embodied in agreements and official statements. It therefore did not decide the granted foreign-commerce and legislative-jurisdiction questions. The separate procedural-hearing theory was outside this grant.

* **Historical Authority:** American Insurance Association v. Garamendi.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.

## 165. Green Tree Financial Corp. v. Bazzle

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 5–4 in judgment.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer’s four-Justice plurality included Justices Scalia, Souter, and Ginsburg. Justice Stevens would have affirmed but concurred in the judgment to supply a controlling disposition. Chief Justice Rehnquist dissented with Justices O’Connor and Kennedy; Justice Thomas dissented separately.

* **Historical Holding:** The plurality treated the class-procedure interpretation as a matter for the arbitrator and sought a fresh arbitral determination. Justice Stevens’s judgment concurrence did not supply a fifth vote for every proposition of that rationale.

* **Historical Authority:** Green Tree Financial Corp. v. Bazzle.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.

## 166. Wiggins v. Smith

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7–2.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O’Connor wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice Scalia dissented with Justice Thomas.

* **Historical Holding:** Counsel’s investigation was deficient, the omitted mitigation established prejudice, and the state court’s application of Strickland was unreasonable. The relief concerned the death sentence.

* **Historical Authority:** Wiggins v. Smith.

* **Historical Sources:** Supreme Court opinions.
