## Validated neutral framing and source limits

The public issuance and tax qualification identify an actual governmental act. They do not independently establish an Establishment Clause violation. Conversely, private purchase and borrower repayment do not make the affirmative use of public financing authority disappear. The neutral packet properly separates these questions.

The supported standing finding is that Lipscomb probably would have financed at least part of the expansion through taxable means absent this financing, including testimony concerning accreditation pressure to construct a library and student center. That finding is accepted as supplied. It is not converted into a quantified loss, an established tax increase, or municipal liability on the bonds. The doctrinal disagreement concerns what this finding establishes under the municipal-taxpayer rule.

The institutional evidence includes religious integration of instruction, compulsory Bible study and worship, personnel restrictions, and land-use restrictions. Those are not merely a religious label. They may establish an actual devotional function when connected to financed instructional work. But the packet does not establish that the same connection covers every financed library, academic or administrative improvement, recreation facility, grounds improvement and communications item. Nor does the existence of the loan's religious-use prohibition establish its observance or practical effectiveness. The proper remand gives the evidence its actual scope; it neither presumes every asset devotional nor discounts proved integration to a mere possibility.

The precise contractual and present remedial position of existing bondholders, any completed refinancing, and particular available prospective relief are receiving-court work before altering contractual obligations. These limits do not prevent a final legal ruling on standing, the financing standard, or the invalidity of categorical relief. There is no material unresolved source gap that requires stopping this merits event.

## Entering law and reusable standards

**Lemon test — Religion and public schools.** The supplied entering-law slice's operative Standards title is “Lemon test.” Effective June 23, 1997, Agostini, Holding I, makes purpose, effects and relationships evidence within one governmental-authority-and-function inquiry: a controlling specific prohibition or distinct doctrine; an established permissible relationship; otherwise ordinary attribution and function analysis of a legally sufficient claim. The former independent prongs are not a fallback. Government may purchase identifiable secular work with effective restrictions against devotional use and diversion; religious location, identity and incidental benefit alone are insufficient. Ordinary supervision of secular performance is not itself forbidden entanglement.

**Actual financed use, proof and correction within that standard.** Jackson, March 16, 1999, and Strout, February 28, 2000, reject parental routing as sufficient justification for the demonstrated purchase of integrated devotional instruction. Mitchell, June 28, 2000, has all eight present Associates' direct joins to its substantive actual-use, anti-presumption and lawful-secular-use rules, and all eight to its confined vacatur/remand remedy. A statutory or contractual restriction and its actual observance are distinct. Affiliation, control, fungibility, possible diversion and savings do not establish a violation by accumulation. Actual proved funded religious use requires correction; it is not reclassified as risk on remand. No universal monitoring regime, separate corporation or wholly distinct personnel is mandatory.

**Zelman, June 27, 2002.** Its six-Justice governing opinion preserves the same boundary for restricted provider-redeemed tuition and requires supported provider/payment scope and lawful severability. Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer joined that opinion. Kennedy's dissent, joined by Scalia and Thomas, sought reconsideration at the scope of genuinely individual, independently directed educational benefits; it expressly preserved Mitchell's publicly administered resources rule. That dissent is a published position, not governing law and not automatic approval of institutional project financing.

**Concrete personal interests and procedural citizen-suit standing.** This is the relevant general Standards title identified in the authorized control response; its substantive authority is independently verified here in the public Lujan decision, June 12, 1992. Concrete personal injury, traceability and likely redress require stage-appropriate support. Neither statutory permission nor an undifferentiated interest in lawful administration creates the missing injury. Its procedural-citizen-suit component does not create municipal taxpayer standing; municipal standing requires its own historically recognized financial relationship.

**Municipal financial relationship.** Frothingham, 262 U.S. 447, 486–487, preserves the municipal taxpayer distinction and its direct relation to municipal moneys, citing Crampton, 101 U.S. 601, 609. Flast's federal taxing-and-spending nexus and Valley Forge's rejection of abstract objection remain distinct. Hawley, 773 F.2d 736, and the supplied interlocutory Steele recitation explain the lower court's lost-revenue application; they are persuasive lower-court analysis, not controlling Supreme Court resolution of the present question. The earlier interlocutory ruling does not eliminate independent jurisdictional review.

**Hunt's actual force.** Hunt, 413 U.S. 734, upholds its bounded secular project with use restrictions, inspection and a materially different record. Footnote 7 describes the unusual private-credit assistance but expressly does not decide the argument that the tax exemption independently brings the arrangement within Walz. Hunt therefore supplies a lawful secular-financing example, not an adjudicated universal conduit exemption. Agostini and Mitchell displace any reading that religious institutional character conclusively determines the use of an otherwise separable financed asset. Walz and Mueller retain their actual general-tax relationships.

## Enhanced component forks

These are supported legal alternatives and counterarguments, not fictional exchanges or a record of private deliberation. Each Justice's selection and barriers appear below.

### Standing

**S1 — Recognize this municipal fiscal claim.** The municipal financial relationship recognized by Frothingham includes an affirmative local financing decision with a supported probable effect on local revenue. The identified taxable alternative supplies a concrete fiscal connection and likely preventive redress without requiring a calculation of each plaintiff's future bill. This is not taxpayer standing to supervise every official act.

The strongest counterargument is that no municipal money or credit is committed: the supposed loss is taxation of private transactions that would have occurred under a different arrangement. Treating all forgone taxation as municipal property could eliminate the fiscal limitation. S1 survives only at the supplied finding and challenged local financing relationship; mere exemption status, disappointment with government or speculation about taxable alternatives is insufficient.

**S2 — Require a municipal financial injury this arrangement does not create.** Frothingham's exception concerns misuse of municipal moneys; a neutral private financing transaction with no public repayment obligation does not spend those moneys. Probable taxable alternative borrowing does not by itself give these taxpayers a proprietary interest in tax revenues that were never owed under the actual transaction. The general Article III rule therefore controls and requires dismissal before merits.

The strongest counterargument is the established municipal distinction coupled with evidence, rather than speculation, of lost local revenue. S2 cannot dismiss that evidence as absent or demand a particular dollar threshold. Its objection must be the legal boundary of the recognized taxpayer interest. That is the ground selected by Scalia and Thomas.

### Financing, actual use and safeguards

**M1 — Apply the actual financed-function rule to the affirmative project benefit.** Government creates a favorable financing relationship for identified institutional assets through public issuance and tax qualification. The Constitution examines that relationship's actual function despite private capital and repayment. Proven support of integrated devotional operations without reliable separation is prohibited; independently identifiable secular financing remains permissible.

The strongest counterargument is that this approach makes an exemption equivalent to a purchase and threatens ordinary tax treatment or civic financial services. M1 answers by identifying the affirmative, project-specific issuance and the actual function benefited. It neither treats all private capital as public principal nor displaces Walz's general property-tax relationship. Hunt's qualified project holding and reserved exemption argument permit this inquiry. M1 does not presume a violation.

**M2 — Treat the arrangement as a protected neutral private-financing relationship.** Private investors provide all principal, the borrower alone repays it, and the public entity supplies access to favorable bond treatment under secular criteria. On this view government facilitates private capital rather than purchases the borrower's instruction, and the relationship is closer to protected general tax treatment than public program expenditure. Ordinary restrictions remain enforceable on their own terms.

The strongest counterargument is that government does more than leave private income untaxed: affirmative public issuance qualifies particular financed projects for the advantage, and Hunt examined secular uses rather than granting universal immunity. Moreover, genuine family direction in the Zelman dissent is not present merely because an institution chooses to borrow. This objection leads Kennedy, as well as the other five reaching merits, to M1. Scalia and Thomas retain M2 only as a conditional merits view, grounded in the absence of public capital or credit and the neutral private financing relationship, not in a denial that issuance is government action.

**Proof fork under M1.** Religious integration can establish the prohibited function when it describes the particular work or assets being financed. Institution-wide classification alone cannot establish every asset's use, and a contractual prohibition alone cannot establish effective separation. Thus neither side receives automatic summary judgment on the supplied categorical grounds. The court must use existing evidence to determine the actual financed work, the relation of particular assets to it, and the restriction's practical operation. No new hearing is mandatory when existing materials suffice.

### Remedy

**R1 — Correct both categorical dispositions and return the actual applications.** Remove the institution-wide future-bond prohibition, vacate the defendants' merits-summary-judgment direction and remand for the M1 inquiry. Relief must reach supported unlawful uses and preserve separable lawful financing. The lower court identifies any genuine additional issue and considers affected contractual interests before altering obligations.

The strongest counterargument is that remand postpones correction despite substantial evidence of integrated instruction. R1 expressly requires use of that evidence wherever it establishes the financed function and forbids requiring reproof of established facts. What remains is its actual asset/use scope and reliable separation, not a demand to start again.

**R2 — Retain one categorical merits endpoint.** Reinstating the district injunction would prevent possible devotional support but would also exclude unrelated future secular projects because of institutional character. Sustaining defendants' summary judgment solely because the capital is private would avoid that exclusion but foreclose the function inquiry. The six reaching merits reject both endpoints. The jurisdictional dissenters instead remove all merits adjudications for want of jurisdiction; that distinct disposition supplies no merits vote for either endpoint.

## Eight independent commitments

### Justice Stevens

**Standing:** S1. His own Valley Forge dissent, 454 U.S. 513–515, rejects a formal financial-category distinction that hides the taxpayer's establishment injury. His Lujan separate opinion and his join in the actual Laidlaw opinion likewise credit supported concrete connections and likely redress. Here the longstanding municipal relation and supported taxable-alternative finding furnish a narrower basis than the federal-taxpayer theory he favored in Valley Forge. The S2 objection does not defeat this particular local fiscal nexus. He will not join a holding that all ideological objectors or all federal taxpayers possess standing.

**Financed function:** M1. His direct joins in Jackson, Mitchell and Zelman support examining the institutional work actually financed and prevent private routing from serving as an automatic answer. The no-public-principal argument carries weight in defining the relationship but does not eliminate affirmative project assistance. Against a broader invalidation path, his own Mitchell join rejects character-based category rules and requires an actual-use connection. He would not treat compulsory worship throughout the institution as proof that every recreation or administrative asset performs worship.

**Remedy and joins:** R1, with effective correction of any financed devotional use established by the existing record. His Jackson and Mitchell remedial joins require preserving lawful applications. He rejects both a new presumption that all uses are lawful and restoration of the institution-wide injunction. He can join the complete six-Justice rule and limited disposition; his broader standing views need not become part of the common opinion.

### Justice O'Connor

**Standing:** S1. Her Valley Forge majority join requires a real jurisdictional distinction, supplied here by municipal rather than nationwide taxpayer status and evidence of a probable local revenue consequence. Her Lujan dissent join and authorship of the simulated Laidlaw opinion support favorable reasonable inferences from concrete evidence without eliminating Article III. Unlike Valley Forge's speculative alternative purchaser, this packet supplies an affirmative fiscal finding. She rejects S2's equation of this evidence with a merely abstract constitutional objection, but will not join standing based on public issuance alone or a categorical Establishment Clause exception.

**Financed function:** M1. Her full Agostini join preserves genuine secular separation; her Jackson opinion and Zelman join reject treating financial routing as dispositive. The actual Hunt project illustrates permissible secular assistance, not immunity. The strongest objection to remand is the covenant's express prohibition of religious use. Her Mitchell join supplies the answer: its existence and observance must be distinguished without presuming inevitable diversion. Institutional devotion is relevant evidence, not a conclusive identity test.

**Remedy and joins:** R1. Her Jackson remedy and Mitchell Part IV join directly support vacating categorical relief and resolving supported scope. She requires no automatic new audit, separate corporate structure or wholesale alteration of the college. She will join a function-specific opinion that protects genuinely separable secular projects, uses existing evidence where sufficient and keeps bondholder protection distinct from a merits defense.

### Justice Scalia

**Standing:** S2; vacate the merits adjudications and direct jurisdictional dismissal. His own Lujan opinion and separate redressability analysis, and his Laidlaw dissent, require a demonstrated legally cognizable personal stake rather than converting a claim of governmental illegality into injury. He accepts the packet's probability finding; his objection is that private capital and repayment commit no municipal funds, while an alternative taxable transaction does not create ownership of uncollected revenue. Frothingham's municipal exception is his strongest counterauthority, but he reads its municipal-moneys rationale as a boundary, not authorization to challenge every economically consequential public approval. He adopts no minimum-dollar requirement and does not assert that the fiscal testimony is missing.

**Conditional merits assessment, not an operative vote:** M2 is his preferred lawful merits path. His Agostini and Mitchell joins preserve actual public-service and publicly administered-resource scrutiny, while his joins in Kennedy's published Jackson/Strout/Zelman independent-benefit positions oppose attributing genuinely private direction to the government. The entire capital and repayment obligation here remain private, presenting a stronger private-financing distinction than publicly funded tuition. He would sustain this neutral conduit relationship without announcing that every tax benefit or governmental act is immune. Hunt's reserved exemption argument is a serious limit: he cannot cite it as already deciding M2, and adoption of M2 here would resolve that open scope question. He does not assert proved compliance with the covenant.

**Remedy and join barriers:** Actual position is dismissal, with no merits injunction retained. He will not join the six-Justice standing or merits disposition. Conditional acceptance of the rule against institution-wide religious classification, and of limits protecting nonparty bondholders, supplies no additional vote for R1. A merits-only opinion adopting M1 does not answer his recorded jurisdictional objection.

### Justice Kennedy

**Standing:** S1. His Lujan concurrence with Souter reserves unsupported redressability extensions and recognizes that concrete causal relationships matter; his Laidlaw join accepts likely preventive relief without certainty. The supported probable taxable financing supplies the causal link missing from a purely speculative theory. The municipal relationship recognized in Frothingham supplies the legal stake. He rejects S2 here because the local public financing decision and demonstrated fiscal alternative connect the taxpayers to this governmental transaction. He requires that limitation and will not endorse standing merely because an observer considers the financing unconstitutional.

**Financed function:** M1 at the affirmative, project-specific scope. His Agostini and unanimous Mitchell joins require actual-function analysis when public authority identifies and administers the financed benefit. His strongest contrary path is M2, informed by his Zelman dissent. But that dissent required genuinely individual, independently directed benefits and did not make an institution's own borrowing choice equivalent to family direction. Here the government issues the bonds to obtain favorable treatment for identified institutional assets. Private capital is material to the analysis and remedy; it does not establish the protected relationship conclusively. His join does not retreat from his separate view of genuinely individual benefits or make all religious activity attributable to government.

**Remedy and joins:** R1. His Mitchell Part IV join supplies the direct remedial precedent. The absence of an asset-wide finding makes final institutional invalidation unsupportable, while the institutional integration evidence makes dismissal of actual-function inquiry equally unsound. He can join the complete common rationale only if it expressly preserves the private-capital distinction, requires proof of the particular financed function, and rejects both identity-based exclusion and compulsory separate governance. Those qualifications are included in the common rule above.

### Justice Souter

**Standing:** S1. His direct joins in Lujan's actual-injury rule, Kennedy's concurrence and Laidlaw distinguish supported factual connections from generalized grievances. Frothingham's municipal relation and the actual probable revenue consequence provide the requisite local stake. The strongest S2 argument concerns an unrealized tax rather than expenditure, but the identified local financing decision and supported alternative borrowing avoid the conjectural causal chain that his Lujan commitment refused to assume. He will not join a general power to challenge any government benefit or relax the need for a supported fiscal connection.

**Financed function:** M1. His own Strout and Zelman Court opinions apply the financed-function limitation and require proof at the actual payment/provider scope. His Mitchell join preserves separated secular work and forbids presuming all equipment religious from institutional character. The no-treasury-expenditure defense does not decide what the government's targeted financing assistance supports. Hunt's actual restrictions remain relevant; neither their recital nor institutional integration conclusively resolves all uses.

**Remedy and joins:** R1, applying his Zelman requirement that existing descriptions of integrated teaching receive their actual force. He rejects a remand that orders taxpayers to prove again facts already established or treats an unexamined asset as reason to continue a proved prohibited financed function elsewhere. He also rejects final institution-wide invalidation without scope and severability. He can join the complete common disposition and rule.

### Justice Ginsburg

**Standing:** S1. Her Laidlaw join supports supported localized injury and likely redress without an artificial demand for certainty or a payment to the plaintiff. The separate municipal fiscal doctrine supplies the legally protected relationship here; she does not extend environmental-use standing itself to taxpayer claims. The S2 objection identifies a genuine boundary, but this packet contains a supported fiscal nexus to affirmative local financing rather than the abstract desire to enforce the Constitution rejected by Valley Forge. She will not join standing untethered from that evidence.

**Financed function:** M1. Her direct joins in Jackson, Strout, Mitchell and Zelman support scrutiny of actual subsidized instructional functions while preserving secular separation. Private repayment limits what public assistance exists; it does not nullify the public issuance and project-specific qualification. She gives the integration evidence its actual asset/use force. Her Mitchell join prevents converting the religious employment and worship requirements into a presumption that every funded asset has a devotional function.

**Remedy and joins:** R1. Her Jackson, Mitchell and Zelman remedy joins reject categorical exclusion and require effective conflict-specific relief. A decree may address proven unlawful financing but must respect the legal position of bondholders and distinguish existing transactions from future applications. She will join the complete rule; she rejects automatic cancellation, principal recoupment, mandatory new evidence collection or a categorical ban on religious borrowers.

### Justice Thomas

**Standing:** S2; vacate the merits adjudications and direct dismissal. His Lujan joins, including Scalia's separate redressability ground, and his join in Scalia's Laidlaw dissent support strict identification of the plaintiff's personal financial or other legally protected injury. He accepts the supported probable alternative borrowing and does not substitute an evidentiary-default theory. The municipality neither advances principal nor assumes repayment, and its taxpayers have not shown misuse of municipal moneys within Frothingham's distinctive relationship. The strongest counterargument is the direct local revenue nexus. He concludes that probability of a tax on a different private transaction cannot itself enlarge that recognized interest. He does not require a quantified loss or presume the evidence false.

**Conditional merits assessment, not an operative vote:** M2. His direct Agostini and Mitchell joins concern the actual governmental work or publicly administered instructional resources; his joined independent-benefit positions in Jackson, Strout and Zelman preserve genuinely private direction. A government-created route for borrowing private capital on the borrower's own credit can be a permissible financial relationship without becoming a governmental purchase of instruction. He would preserve this distinction rather than revive an institution-wide religious disqualification. Hunt does not compel his answer to the reserved tax-benefit question; his position applies the preserved private-benefit rationale to that question, with neutral criteria and the absence of public principal or credit doing substantive work. He does not claim every private-capital arrangement is necessarily lawful or that formal covenant language proves actual observance.

**Remedy and join barriers:** Jurisdictional dismissal is his actual result. Conditional opposition to the categorical injunction supplies no merits-remand vote. He will not join a merits opinion on hypothetical jurisdiction, and a narrower asset-specific remedy does not cure his standing objection. He does not authorize retrospective interference with nonparty contracts or create a damages remedy.

### Justice Breyer

**Standing:** S1. His join in the operative Laidlaw opinion evaluates concrete evidence and probable remedial effects rather than demanding certainty. Here the established municipal fiscal relationship supplies the relevant interest and the CFO testimony supports the alternative-financing link. The strongest S2 objection is that no current tax dollars are spent. He treats the supported revenue consequence of this affirmative local financial action as sufficient at this municipal scope, without importing a nationwide taxpayer power or presuming a particular increase in plaintiffs' tax bills.

**Financed function:** M1. His own Mitchell opinion is the most direct authority: actual funded use, not labels or accumulated risk factors, controls; nominal restrictions and their observance are different. His Zelman join rejects routing as sufficient justification for a proved financed religious function. The private-capital argument requires precise identification of the benefit and prevents treating the $15 million as municipal money. It does not foreclose examination of the financing relationship's actual support. Equally, institution-wide integration cannot do the work of connecting every financed asset to devotional operations.

**Remedy and joins:** R1 under the same confined-correction principle he authored in Mitchell. Use sufficient existing materials, identify remaining application questions, preserve lawful separated work and correct demonstrated prohibited financing without an automatic new trial. The precise existing-contract consequences require an appropriate factual and legal basis. He will join the complete common rule and limited disposition; a programwide ban, automatic recoupment or judicial redesign of the college would exceed his commitment.
