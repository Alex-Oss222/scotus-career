## Unresolved discrepancies

### 1. Schaefer: two damaged Holdings formulations and an incomplete Standards qualification

**Locations:** [Holdings candidate, Schaefer](HOLDINGS.candidate.md#shalala-v-schaefer-509-us-292-1993), the second and third Holding fields; [Standards candidate, Social Security remand judgments and EAJA fees](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md#social-security-remand-judgments-and-eaja-fees). **Controlling source:** [Schaefer record](../records/Shalala_v_Schaefer_merits_1993-06-24.md), Controlling holdings; cross-check [chunk 10 render](../output/OT_1992CHUNK10.md).

The second Holding says the application period follows “a properly judgment”; the record and render say “a properly entered judgment.” The third says “a sentence-four remand properly without retained jurisdiction”; the record and render say “properly entered without retained jurisdiction.” Both omissions delete the operative entry condition, although surrounding passages help explain it. Restore the record's wording in an authorized candidate rewrite.

Standards also invokes sentence-six proceedings and “qualifying sentence-six proceedings” without stating their two qualifying routes. The record's controlling explanation supplies them: a pre-answer remand on the Secretary's good-cause motion, or a remand for new material evidence with good cause for its earlier omission. Holdings retains those terms in its limits field. Standards must state them rather than leave the operative qualification to an unspecified statutory reference. Neither repair changes the judgment, coalition or fee award.

### 2. Forfeiture amendment: wrong enacted authority in Standards

**Location:** [Standards candidate, Forfeiture venue, process and protection during appeal](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md#forfeiture-venue-process-and-protection-during-appeal), Authority by component. **Source:** [October 28 admitted source](../records/Forfeiture_jurisdiction_amendment_1992-10-28.md).

Correct the authority label; do not change the amendment's operative text, effective date or unresolved application to earlier events.

### 3. Dunnigan: constitutional permission is blurred with the Guideline's command

**Location:** [Standards candidate, Independent perjury findings for the obstruction adjustment](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md#independent-perjury-findings-for-the-obstruction-adjustment), first sentence. **Source:** [Dunnigan record](../records/United_States_v_Dunnigan_merits_1993-02-23.md), Constitutional permission and the Guideline's consequence; [chunk 3 render](../output/OT_1992CHUNK3.md).

“Section 3C1.1 constitutionally requires its two-level adjustment” does not keep the two sources of legal force clear. The record holds that the increase **does not violate** the right to testify, and separately that the **applicable Guideline requires** the increase after proper independent perjury findings. State those propositions separately. The Constitution is not recorded as commanding this particular sentencing adjustment. The required predicates and the distinction between preferable separate findings and sufficient combined findings otherwise survive in the candidate.

### 4. Hartford: an expressly retained statutory alternative is not stated in terms

**Location:** [Standards candidate, American-market effects and asserted foreign-law permission](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md#american-market-effects-and-asserted-foreign-law-permission). **Source:** [Hartford record](../records/Hartford_Fire_Insurance_v_California_merits_1993-06-28.md), entering-law account and foreign-effects holding.

The current rule uses “the specified United States export effect” without specifying that alternative's terms anywhere in the entry. The record's entering-law summary similarly says “specified U.S. export effect.” The Court reserves §6a's precise applicability while expressly preserving its qualifying-effects and claim-connection requirements; that reservation does not make an incomplete statement of a retained alternative complete.

The missing source detail is the event-eligible text of **15 U.S.C. §6a(1)(B), read with paragraph (2) and the export-only limitation**, and its faithful placement within the already-recorded reservation. A corrective source/projection pass must supply the omitted terms or resolve the omission explicitly. This audit does not infer them, determine §6a's applicability, or adopt a new comity rule.

**Sources and locations:** [Demos record](../records/Demos_v_Storrie_fee_motion_1993-03-08.md), Event and chronology and Law of the decision; Demos blocks in [chunk 4 Render Input](../render-inputs/OT_1992CHUNK4.md) and [public render](../output/OT_1992CHUNK4.md).

Holdings preserves the complete two rules and their qualifications. These are projection/form defects, not grounds to change the fee ruling or certiorari denial.

### 8. Demos and Moreau: source explanations and downstream handoff remain incomplete

AGENTS.md requires a 120–200-word controlling explanation for each controlling proposition and requires the record's Holdings content to enter the Render Input unchanged. Three source explanations fall below that requirement: Demos's prospective-restriction explanation (91 whitespace-delimited words), and Moreau's earlier-hire (109) and later-hire (83) explanations. These are sufficiently below the minimum that ordinary tokenization differences do not cure them. The inputs and public renders abbreviate them further rather than carrying the record explanations unchanged. Moreau's later-hire handoff also drops the express statement that selection of a representative alone does not presume individual consent; the broader no-presumed-consent limit survives elsewhere, but the handoff is not unchanged.

Return these explanations for an authorized record-completeness correction, followed by faithful input and render projection. Any completion must use only adopted reasoning; a renderer or close pass cannot invent reasoning to meet a word count. Blair's short explanation is **not** included in this finding: its equal division creates no controlling holding to which that quota applies.

### 9. Blair: no-precedent disposition is inconsistently labeled as controlling law; public source note exposes reconciliation

**Sources and locations:** [Blair record](../records/Delo_v_Blair_application_to_vacate_stay_1993-07-21.md), Law of the decision; Blair blocks in [chunk 11 input](../render-inputs/OT_1992CHUNK11.md) and [render](../output/OT_1992CHUNK11.md); [workspace, Current-Term Neutral Projection](../workspace.md#current-term-neutral-projection).

Version 2.0 expressly denies precedential effect, supplies no majority opinion, and resolves only A-69. Nevertheless, the record labels both the equal-division consequence and Stone's unjoined statement “Holding and operative rule” with “Controlling explanation”; the public entry uses “Controlling Propositions and Authority” and “Controlling proposition”; and the neutral projection says Blair “adds one holding.” Those labels conflict with the express authority determination. Present the disposition and separate statements without attributing a new holding to this Court. No vote, stay or Herrera rule should change.

The render's Source Notes also say the historical vote was used for the seven non-Stone Justices' positions. That is comparator/reconciliation process, which the Contract excludes from the ordinary public account. Keep procedural source support and unrecovered-record limitations public; keep the vote-modeling account in the audit annex.

The Holdings continuation records express user authorization for a limited Blair **disposition entry**, notwithstanding the template's usual exclusion. That entry accurately states no controlling rationale, has no new Holding or Current force field, and is not an improper promotion of Stone's statement. Standards correctly derives no rule from Blair. The earlier version 1.0 vacatur/coalition defect is not treated as still operative.

## Method and limits

This is a repository consistency and completeness audit, not a new adjudication or reconstruction of historical law. All record files and public chunks were included in extraction, coverage and cross-reference checks; substantive review compared operative propositions, qualifications, authority and procedural effects, with focused examination of fractures and discrepancies. String equality supports preservation; it does not establish legal meaning or validate the historical truth of a source. Existing source limitations, including Blair's unrecovered underlying materials, are not certified as independently cured. No external source was fetched, and no new vote or legal rule was derived.

Working evidence is under `tmp/audit1992/`: `comparison.json`, `baseline-differences.json`, `checks.json`, `coverage.json`, `render-mismatches.txt`, the rule and holding extracts, and `finalchecks.json`. The latter records duplicate checks, publication-location link checks and unchanged hashes for the 161 source/candidate files captured before audit analysis. All findings here rest on the current files, not on an assertion that earlier pass notes remain accurate.

The only durable deliverable of this pass is this Audit. Scratch files were written only under `tmp/`.
