## Controlling holdings

#### 1. Fixed Alabama baseline under the supplemental decree

**Holding and operative rule:** For determining Alabama's Submerged Lands Act grant, the complete paragraph3 baseline incorporated from the United States–Alabama joint submission is fixed on February22,1993 and thereafter does not move with the coast. The whole Mississippi Sound remains state inland waters under the antecedent decrees; each party bears its own costs, and the Court retains authority to implement and supplement the decree and the parties' rights.

**Authority:** The Court's unsigned supplemental decree, paragraphs1–6, entered in its continuing original action. This is binding decretal relief for the parties; it announces no new general coastal-ownership test or opinion rationale.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court gives effect to the parties' agreed completion of the boundary decree. The 1985 decision established the historic inland-water status of Mississippi Sound, and the 1988 decision resolved the remaining Mississippi coastline disagreement. The 1990 supplemental decree fixed the Mississippi baseline and part of Alabama's baseline while leaving the remaining Alabama coast ambulatory. The United States and Alabama now submit the precise remaining description under the jurisdiction retained in that decree. Its adoption fixes the specified Alabama baseline for the Submerged Lands Act grant as of this decree's date. The operative schedule, including its separate line segments and coordinate systems, supplies the geographic rule; a general description cannot substitute for it. The Court does not redraw an interstate boundary, reopen the Sound's status, or announce that consent can create jurisdiction. Costs are borne separately, and continuing authority remains available to give the decree effect.
