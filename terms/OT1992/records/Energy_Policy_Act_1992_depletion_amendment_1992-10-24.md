# Admitted Source Record — Energy Policy Act of 1992, depletion amendment

**Natural source key:** Energy Policy Act of 1992, Pub. L. 102-486, § 1915(a), (d), October 24, 1992, 106 Stat. 3023–3024.

**Record kind:** Controlling noncase-law change; not a Court action or judicial construction. Initial source admission during OT1992 chunk 3; no earlier entry is superseded.

**Authority and provenance:** Congress enacted, and the President approved, Pub. L. 102-486 on October 24, 1992. The official Statutes at Large establishes the enacted language and approval date. This record admits only § 1915(a), together with § 1915(d)'s transition rule. The separate intangible-drilling-cost amendments in § 1915(b) and conforming provisions in § 1915(c) are not silently substituted for the depletion amendment.

## Exact operative text

Section 1915(a), 106 Stat. 3023–3024 (typographic capitalization and line breaks normalized):

> (a) DEPLETION.—
>
> (1) Paragraph (1) of section 57(a) (relating to depletion) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new sentence: “Effective with respect to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1992, this paragraph shall not apply to any deduction for depletion computed in accordance with section 613A(c).”.
>
> (2) Subparagraph (F) of section 56(g)(4) is amended to read as follows:
>
> “(F) DEPLETION.—
>
> “(i) IN GENERAL.—The allowance for depletion with respect to any property placed in service in a taxable year beginning after December 31, 1989, shall be cost depletion determined under section 611.
>
> “(ii) EXCEPTION FOR INDEPENDENT OIL AND GAS PRODUCERS AND ROYALTY OWNERS.—In the case of any taxable year beginning after December 31, 1992, clause (i) (and subparagraph (C)(i)) shall not apply to any deduction for depletion computed in accordance with section 613A(c).”

Section 1915(d), 106 Stat. 3024:

> (d) EFFECTIVE DATE.—The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1992.

## Effective date, scope and transition

The enactment enters the objective statutory chronology on **October 24, 1992**. The amendments apply to **taxable years beginning after December 31, 1992**. These are different facts: the statute existed from enactment, but its amended depletion treatment does not govern a taxable year beginning on or before December 31, 1992. The transition depends on the beginning of the taxpayer's taxable year, not on the date a return is filed, a refund action is adjudicated, or a court decision is issued. January 1, 1993 is the earliest possible beginning date of a covered taxable year; it is not a replacement enactment date.

For covered taxable years, § 57(a)(1)'s depletion-preference paragraph does not apply to depletion deductions computed under § 613A(c). Under the accompanying § 56(g)(4)(F)(ii), the cost-depletion requirement in clause (i) and subparagraph (C)(i) do not apply to those same deductions. No other deduction is exempted by implication. The general cost-depletion language retains its own property-placed-in-service condition, and the new exception retains its taxable-year condition. No taxpayer's qualification under § 613A(c) or amount of allowable depletion is adjudicated here.

The 1981 and 1982 tax years in United States v. Hill remain governed by their applicable pre-amendment minimum-tax law. The source therefore limits the prospective reach of that forthcoming construction; it neither decides Hill nor supplies a retroactive rule for that case.
