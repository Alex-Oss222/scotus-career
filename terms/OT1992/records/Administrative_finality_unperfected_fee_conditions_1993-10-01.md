# Admitted Source Record — Administrative finality of unperfected fee conditions

**Case and dockets:** *Zatko v. California*, Nos. 91-5052, 91-5111, 91-5166, 91-5167, 91-5244, 91-5246, 91-5307, 91-5331, 91-5332, 91-5401, 91-5416, 91-5476, 91-5583, 91-5594, 91-5692, 91-5730, and 91-5732; *Martin v. McDermott*, No. 92-5618.

**Event and date:** Nonadjudicative Clerk docket finality, October 1, 1993.

**Result:** The old paid/form-compliance routes are administratively closed as unperfected filings. No petition is denied on the merits, no certiorari merits determination is entered, no filing restriction is imposed, and no precedent changes.

**Record version and supersession:** Initial continuity record. It supplements the November 4, 1991 *Zatko* fee-motion Record and the November 2, 1992 *Martin* Record only as to later docket stage.

## Docket effect

The November 25, 1991 paid/form opportunity attached to the seventeen *Zatko* submissions and the November 23, 1992 paid/form opportunity in *Martin v. McDermott*, No. 92-5618, remained unresolved in the continuity materials because no later perfected filing had been entered into this timeline. Effective October 1, 1993, those stale administrative conditions are closed for docket-continuity purposes. The Clerk carries no underlying petition from either set of unperfected submissions into October Term 1993.

This is a nonadjudicative finality event. It does not infer why no perfected filing was entered, does not treat silence as a merits default, does not establish a warning breach or abusive act, and does not alter the legal analysis, votes, holdings, or prospective-restriction decisions in the earlier *Zatko*, *Martin*, or *Demos* Records.

## Continuity consequences

- *Zatko*: the seventeen fee-motion proceedings have no remaining paid-docketing condition and are not OT1993 opening matters.
- *Martin v. McDermott*, No. 92-5618: the companion paid/form route is closed without a merits disposition and is not an OT1993 opening matter.
- *Demos v. Storrie*, No. 92-6846: already terminal on March 8, 1993; no change.
- No completed OT1991 or OT1992 adjudication is reopened because this finality event is effective after all supplied merits and application events for those terms and creates no new law.

## Authority and validation

This record is entered under Engine §§1–2 as an expressly established nonadjudicative docket-finality event. It is confined to continuity and carries no holding, vote, opinion, merits judgment, or adverse factual inference. The opening OT1993 Standing State must omit these stale fee-compliance matters.
