## Controlling holdings

#### The comparability limit overrides an unconditional factor entitlement

**Holding and operative rule:** The contracts’ automatic annual adjustment mechanism is subject to §1.9(d)’s overriding prohibition on material differences from comparable unassisted rents, while preserving differences existing at the initial contract date and the protected initial contract rent floor. HUD may use independent comparability studies to enforce that limit; the Court does not determine whether any particular study accurately and lawfully does so.

**Authority:** White’s Opinion of the Court; Stone-Zsela, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas (9 Justices). Direct majority agreement at this level of generality; no Marks inference.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court holds that an adjustment formula cannot be read apart from the contract’s express limitation on its results. Section1.9(b) establishes the annual-factor mechanism, but section1.9(d) applies notwithstanding other provisions and therefore limits the entitlement that mechanism produces. Shomberg supplies the ordinary effect of that overriding language. The owners’ proposed exclusive-formula reading would deprive the comparability provision of practical force whenever the formula itself created a material difference. Neither the contract nor the statutory structure requires that result. An independent study can identify the disparity the limitation addresses; HUD need not rely solely on revising a general factor. This authority does not erase the proviso preserving differences existing when the contract began or permit a reduction below the protected initial rent. Accuracy, comparability and faithful implementation remain contestable. Because the owners possess no unconditional contractual right to the disputed factor increases, the constitutional impairment ruling lacks its asserted predicate. The Court does not otherwise decide the validity of every application of the 1989 legislation.

**Precedent treatment:**

- Shomberg v. United States, 348 U.S.540: applies the overriding effect of notwithstanding language.
- Rainier View Associates v. United States, 848 F.2d988: rejects the lower-court exclusive-factor construction; no Supreme Court precedent overruled.
