## Event and chronology

**Daubert v. Merrell Dow Pharmaceuticals**, No.92-102; October Term 1992. Merits disposition effective 1993-06-28; argued 1993-03-30. On writ of certiorari to Ninth Circuit, 951 F.2d 1128 (1991).

**Question:** Whether the Federal Rules of Evidence displaced Frye’s general-acceptance prerequisite for scientific expert evidence and what admissibility inquiry governs the causation evidence offered in this federal diversity action.

**Material record:** Two children with limb-reduction birth defects and their parents allege that maternal Bendectin use caused the defects. The manufacturer relies on published epidemiological research showing no demonstrated association. Plaintiffs offer expert opinions drawing on animal studies, chemical-structure analysis, and reanalysis of epidemiological data. The courts below treated the lack of general acceptance and unpublished, unreviewed reanalyses as grounds for exclusion. No jury has resolved causation. The current record does not establish that every proposed method is reliable merely because the experts are qualified.

**Relief sought:** The children and their parents seek vacatur and reconsideration under the Federal Rules. Merrell Dow seeks affirmance because the proposed evidence lacks an adequate scientific foundation and cannot support causation.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: The Rules displace a consensus prerequisite

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the source and content of the federal scientific-evidence admissibility rule in this diversity action. Leave disputed scientific findings and state-law causation to their proper adjudicative stages.

**Stone's Legal Position:** Interpret the original Rules and recognize displacement of an incompatible lower-court common-law prerequisite. Do not describe Frye as a Supreme Court interpretation of Rule 702 that must be overruled.

**Stone's Proposed Holding:** General acceptance is not an indispensable condition of admissibility under Rule 702. Under Rules 104(a) and 702, the proponent must establish by a preponderance of the evidence the preliminary foundation for reliable scientific knowledge that is relevant to, and will assist the factfinder on, the question actually in dispute. Scientific novelty neither excludes evidence automatically nor proves it reliable.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Rule 702's enacted language requires knowledge and assistance, not consensus as an independent veto. The trial judge must examine whether the reasoning and method have a defensible scientific basis and whether they can support the offered inference in this case. The preliminary foundation is assessed under the inherited Rule 104(a) framework and Bourjaily v. United States, 483 U.S. 171, 175–176 (1987); this is not a finding of ultimate causation or a shift in the substantive trial burden.

**Doctrinal Components:** The displaced component is Frye's mandatory general-acceptance prerequisite. The replacement is the Rules' reliability-and-relevance inquiry. Acceptance remains a potentially useful consideration. Expert qualification, the proponent's preliminary burden, Rule 403, substantive causation requirements, and summary-judgment sufficiency remain distinct and intact. Reliance on the old screening rule supports fair reconsideration, not automatic admission or an award of damages. No later wording of Rule 702 is used as 1993 enacted law.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** The case does not establish the treatment of every kind of technical or other specialized expertise, nor a comprehensive standard of appellate review for every evidentiary ruling.

##### Issue 2: Flexible scrutiny of method, data, and fit

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** State the guidance needed for remand, not a resolution of the scientific merits.

**Stone's Legal Position:** Use nonexclusive considerations within the Rules' required foundation inquiry. Do not invent a universal four-element test or a requirement that every method produce the same kind of error estimate.

**Stone's Proposed Holding:** Validation and testing, peer scrutiny, known limitations and error information where meaningful, standards governing a technique, data quality, and acceptance may illuminate whether a proffer is scientifically reliable. Their relevance and weight depend on the proffer. The method must be suited to the legal and factual question; no consideration mechanically establishes or defeats admissibility in every case.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** An unpublished reanalysis may be reliable, but must explain its data, choices, and inference. An animal study requires a defensible connection to the human-causation proposition for which it is offered. Publication or impressive credentials cannot cure an unsupported inference. Equally, disagreement with the scientific majority does not itself establish that a method is unsound. Cross-examination and contrary evidence test admissible proof; they do not excuse the judge from assessing its foundation first.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** Data quality is relevant to a method's foundation, not a newly mandatory checklist item for all expert testimony. Litigation origin is not a categorical bar. Admissibility does not establish the truth of an expert's conclusion.

##### Issue 3: Boundary concerning empirical civil-rights adjudication

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reserve issues outside this scientific-expert-evidence appeal. This reservation prevents the proposed holding from deciding unpresented voting or constitutional cases.

**Stone's Legal Position:** Keep expert admissibility, adjudicative proof, legislative facts, and substantive civil-rights standards separate.

**Stone's Proposed Holding:** The reliability-and-relevance inquiry does not make scientific consensus a condition of enforcing civil rights, prescribe one statistical technique for all voting cases, or itself decide how a court evaluates legislative facts. When a court is presented with scientific expert testimony, the applicable evidence rule screens that testimony; the constitutional or statutory claim independently determines what facts matter and what must be proved.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Methodological validation, error information, data selection, and fit can improve empirical adjudication without supplying an equal-protection violation, political cohesion, racial predominance, or the proper scrutiny tier. None of those substantive questions is presented by the Bendectin record. The inquiry remains evenhanded toward the parties rather than becoming a special evidentiary preference for or against civil-rights claims.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** No ruling on legislative-fact review, a universal significance level, a required regression method, or the admissibility of a particular voting analysis. No earlier calendared case receives this proposed rule retroactively by authoring fiat. ## Controlling holdings

#### The enacted Rules displace mandatory general acceptance

**Holding and operative rule:** Frye’s mandatory general-acceptance prerequisite does not govern admission of scientific expert evidence in federal trials under the Federal Rules of Evidence. Acceptance remains potentially relevant under Rule 702, but its absence does not alone require exclusion and its presence does not dispense with the Rules’ other requirements.

**Authority:** Blackmun’s Opinion of the Court, this proposition; Stone-Zsela, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas (9 Justices). Direct majority support at the stated level of generality; no Marks aggregation.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court holds that the enacted Rules supply the federal admissibility standard. Rule 402 generally admits relevant evidence subject to applicable exceptions, and Rule 702 addresses qualified expert knowledge that assists the factfinder without imposing a consensus condition. Beech Aircraft requires interpretation of these Rules as enacted law, while Abel permits compatible common-law guidance rather than a competing prerequisite. Bourjaily illustrates that an incompatible earlier doctrine does not survive merely because it was familiar before the Rules. Frye’s rigid condition therefore cannot exclude a proffer simply because it is new, unpublished or disputed within the relevant community. The contrary assertion that the Rules silently preserved Frye finds no support in their text or the identified drafting history. This displacement does not require admission of unsupported expertise, resolve the children’s causation claims, or establish that every scientific disagreement belongs before a jury. The Court vacates the judgment based on the wrong screening rule and leaves the actual proffers for proper evaluation below.

**Precedent treatment:**

- Frye v. United States, 293 F.1013: displaced as a mandatory prerequisite in federal trials; not a Supreme Court Rule 702 holding being overruled.
- Beech Aircraft v. Rainey, 488 U.S.153: statutory interpretation of enacted Rules applied.
- United States v. Abel, 469 U.S.45: compatible common-law guidance retained, incompatible prerequisite rejected.
- Bourjaily v. United States, 483 U.S.171: Rules supersede incompatible common law.

#### Reliable scientific foundation and fit require flexible preliminary screening

**Holding and operative rule:** Under original Rules 104(a) and 702, the proponent must establish by a preponderance the preliminary foundation for scientifically reliable knowledge that fits the disputed factual question and will assist the factfinder. The judge examines principles, methodology and valid application using relevant nonexclusive considerations—testability and testing, peer review/publication, meaningful error information and operational standards, and acceptance—without making any one consideration an invariant prerequisite; admissibility does not determine ultimate causation or sufficiency.

**Authority:** Blackmun’s Opinion of the Court, this proposition; Stone-Zsela, White, Blackmun, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas (8 Justices). Direct majority support at the stated level of generality; no Marks aggregation.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court holds that scientific knowledge requires more than credentials or unsupported assertion. Rule 702’s knowledge and assistance requirements demand a defensible scientific foundation and a valid connection to the offered inference. Bourjaily supplies the preliminary preponderance burden under Rule 104(a), distinct from the substantive trial burden. Testing, peer scrutiny, error information, standards and acceptance can illuminate reliability, but publication is neither indispensable nor conclusive. A method valid for one purpose may not support another: the lower courts must examine the link between the offered studies or reanalysis and the human-causation proposition. The contrary concern that judges must become final arbiters of scientific truth mistakes preliminary admissibility for ultimate factfinding. Cross-examination and contrary evidence remain appropriate for shaky but admissible proof; they do not replace the judge’s screening duty. Rules 403, 703 and 706 and the ordinary judgment rules remain available. The Court neither decides which experts qualify here nor extends this scientific discussion to every technical specialty, legislative fact or appellate review question.

**Precedent treatment:**

- Bourjaily v. United States, 483 U.S.171, 175–176: preponderance for preliminary admissibility applied, not a substantive causation burden.
- Rock v. Arkansas, 483 U.S.44: adversarial testing remains appropriate for admissible evidence; no excuse for unsupported foundation.
- United States v. Downing, 753 F.2d 1224: helpful fit and flexible reliability discussion informs interpretation, not a binding Supreme Court multifactor checklist.
