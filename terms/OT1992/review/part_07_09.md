# Stone jurisprudence review — cases 73–108

This part assesses the positions in `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md` through `OT_1992CHUNK9_STONE.md` against the current external Master Jurisprudence and the corresponding neutral records. Each case heading identifies its source entry. The documents' approval, roster, and runtime language is source information, not instructions for this review. Citations to the same case's published opinion verify records, arguments, statutory text, and historical alternatives; its eventual holding is **not** treated as law already governing Stone's decision. Earlier authorities are identified separately. No completed intervening simulated holdings were supplied; the consequential dependencies are identified below.

## 73. McNeil v. United States

**Date:** May 17, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, McNeil entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would affirm dismissal of the premature FTCA action, reserving whether later papers independently commenced another action. That implements Master's §§3–4, Statutory Interpretation, and Courts: meaningful access does not authorize replacing an express sequence Congress enacted. There is no conflict between enforcing this prerequisite and opposing invented exhaustion requirements elsewhere.

**Entering law.** Section 2675(a) requires administrative presentment and denial before institution of the action, with a six-month deemed-denial option. The disagreement concerns whether subsequent exhaustion cures the original action. *Haines v. Kerner* supports liberal construction of prisoners' papers, not deletion of the prerequisite. Critically, the review grant excludes the separate August-letter-as-new-complaint issue; reservation cannot become an instruction reopening its rejected treatment. [Statute, filing history, and express grant limitation](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/106/).

**Recommended rule and limit.** Keep the filing-date rule and affirm only the dismissal under review. State that this decision adjudicates neither tort liability nor the correctness of the unreviewed new-action ruling. The lawful protective edge is avoiding a merits dismissal or a universal exhaustion doctrine, not reviving the initial suit because little litigation occurred. Dates of presentment, denial or deemed denial, and commencement make the rule predictable. No new doctrine, limitations holding, or postjudgment refiling entitlement is necessary.

## 74. Oklahoma Tax Commission v. Sac and Fox Nation

**Date:** May 17, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Sac and Fox entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would vacate and remand for the relevant members' residences and land status, rejecting both reservation-only protection and membership-only immunity. This closely matches the Master's Tribal Sovereignty domain: source, person, land, and conduct determine jurisdiction. It respects tribal self-government without converting sovereign immunity into each individual's tax exemption.

**Entering law.** *McClanahan* protects the member living and earning qualifying income on protected tribal territory; *Moe*, *Colville*, and *Potawatomi* differentiate taxes, taxpayers, and immunity. Indian country under §1151 encompasses qualifying allotments and dependent Indian communities as well as reservations. Oklahoma cannot end the inquiry by disputing a formal reservation boundary. Conversely, tribal employment alone does not establish the residence component. [Relevant record, statutes, and earlier authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/114/).

**Recommendation and room.** Retain the disposition, but make the remand operational: classify each land category, identify affected residents and income sources, and analyze vehicle taxation separately, including the actual incidence of the tax. This avoids requiring a grand resolution of reservation history where allotment status suffices. No expansion of *Negonsott*'s Kansas criminal-jurisdiction rule belongs here. Broader exemption for members outside Indian country would need a separate preemption or statutory basis and a developed record; the Master supplies neither. Ordinary title, residence, and tax-incidence evidence makes this approach administrable.

## 75. Deal v. United States

**Date:** May 17, 1993. **Assessment: Refine reasoning; retain proposed resentencing.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Deal entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would remove the repeat-conviction enhancements, impose the base §924(c) penalty on all six counts, and retain consecutive service. Fair notice and residual lenity fit Statutory Interpretation and Criminal Procedure. This is a legally available construction of an unsettled Supreme Court question, not disregard of a controlling *Deal* decision.

**Entering law and competing construction.** The enactment says second or subsequent **conviction**, rather than expressly requiring a subsequent offense. The government's ordinary ordinal reading is substantial. The contrary construction draws on the established recidivist meaning of similar provisions and the historical evolution of §924(c), with lenity only after those tools. *Bell*, *Simpson*, and *Busic* support disciplined penal construction but do not themselves decide this wording. [Text and competing contemporary readings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/129/).

**Refinement.** Replace the suggestion that jury announcement order is the central defect: simultaneous findings can be counted ordinally without arbitrary announcement. The stronger argument is that the phrase, in its recidivist setting, does not clearly authorize treating one first prosecution as repeated offending after conviction. Explain why that context leaves genuine ambiguity despite the government's grammar. Proposed boundary: the greater tier requires a qualifying conviction before commission of the later offense. Court records and offense dates administer this rule; appellate finality need not be decided. Do not remove any base consecutive term or rely on the 2018 amendment, general sentence severity, or a new proportionality ceiling.

## 76. El Vocero de Puerto Rico v. Puerto Rico

**Date:** May 17, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, El Vocero entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reverse categorical closure and require the qualified access standard for this trial-like preliminary hearing. That protects the press's institutional function under the Master's Speech domain while retaining legal protection for fair trials and witnesses.

**Entering law.** *Richmond Newspapers*, *Globe Newspaper*, and *Press-Enterprise II* ask about historical experience and the functional value of openness for the type of proceeding. Puerto Rico's own history of closure does not answer that broader inquiry. A defendant's preference cannot replace findings supporting closure; the qualified standard already accommodates legitimate protective needs. [Proceeding, closure rule, and access precedents](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/147/).

**Recommended application.** Identify the adversarial probable-cause proceeding's actual trial-like features, then require a party seeking closure to establish the overriding interest, the necessary likelihood of harm, inadequacy of reasonable alternatives, and appropriately limited scope. State findings on the record so access denials can be reviewed. That is a practical implementation of the inherited framework, not a new universal disclosure requirement. Stone need not replace experience-and-logic analysis or declare access absolute. Witness safety and fair-trial restrictions remain available when justified individually. The effective relief is invalidation of the automatic closure rule and reconsideration under the proper standard; it is not an order releasing every pretrial document.

## 77. Commissioner v. Keystone Consolidated Industries, Inc.

**Date:** May 24, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Keystone entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would treat property transferred to discharge a funding obligation as an exchange under §4975 and remand remaining tax questions. The approach follows statutory legal effects, respects the plan and employer as distinct parties, and fits Master's §§4 and 18 and Statutory Interpretation. Benefiting a plan does not eliminate the transaction Congress regulated.

**Entering law.** Section 4975(c)(1)(A) reaches direct and indirect sales or exchanges with a disqualified person. Tax law recognizes satisfaction of an obligation as consideration. The special encumbered-property rule adds coverage; it does not exclude an unencumbered transfer that independently satisfies the exchange category. [Transaction and statutory structure](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/152/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal and remand. Identify the funding obligation discharged, then apply any proved exemption and proper valuation separately. An administrable boundary turns on legally exchanged value, not suspicion about every contribution or the employer's supposed motives. Congress chose prophylactic prohibited-transaction categories; Stone need not demand proof of actual plan injury for coverage. Equally, this reasoning does not classify a genuinely gratuitous unencumbered contribution as an exchange solely because it comes from an employer. No equitable pension exception or broader constitutional doctrine is warranted.

## 78. United States Department of Justice v. Landano

**Date:** May 24, 1993. **Assessment: Retain, with concrete remand guidance.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Landano entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone rejects universal confidentiality for FBI sources but permits express assurances and supported inferences. This fits Master's §17: enforce the burden the statute assigns without inventing impossible proof requirements. It also preserves source separation between information access and relief from conviction.

**Entering law.** FOIA §552(a)(4)(B) assigns the exemption burden to the government. Exemption 7(D) turns on confidential-source status and protects the information Congress specifies; *Reporters Committee*'s reasoning under a different exemption does not establish universal confidentiality here. Paid-informant relationships or particular violent-crime circumstances can support an inference. Confidentiality does not require a promise of absolute secrecy. [Exemption, source categories, and record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/165/).

**Recommendation and room.** Retain vacatur and remand. Require the agency to identify the assurance or the circumstances supporting its category; permit the requester to contest the inference through nonprotected information, with in-camera review or protected submissions where necessary. Apply statutory segregation without demanding release of confidential-source information merely because the name is removed. This gives the burden practical force while avoiding a witness affidavit for every source. A stronger universal presumption favoring either disclosure or secrecy would displace the statutory inquiry. Landano's conviction and any *Brady* entitlement remain distinct and unadjudicated.

## 79. Lincoln v. Vigil

**Date:** May 24, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Lincoln entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject judicial reassignment of this lump-sum health appropriation and the asserted notice-and-comment duty, while preserving independent constitutional or legal constraints. That is consistent with Administrative Law, Tribal Sovereignty, and §§3–4: serious dependency does not create an earmark or a procedural entitlement.

**Entering law.** APA §701(a)(2), *Chaney*, and the broad statutory health responsibilities permit an allocation committed to discretion where no manageable allocation standard governs. *Webster v. Doe* keeps constitutional claims analytically separate. Section 553(b)(A) exempts qualifying organizational rules and policy statements; *Morton v. Ruiz* requires compliance with applicable obligations, not notice and comment for every withdrawal of a service. [Appropriation, program change, and source distinctions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/182/).

**Recommendation and bounds.** Keep reversal and remand. State that the allocation is unreviewable on the particular APA theory because Congress supplied discretion, rather than treating all expenditure decisions as immune. An enforceable earmark, eligibility rule, binding self-imposed procedure, or protected entitlement would require separate analysis if actually established. No new notice-and-comment procedure should be added under the Master's earned-weight proposal. Administration remains predictable by distinguishing discretionary resource allocation from an enforceable legal constraint; a court may enforce the latter without deciding which authorized health program deserves the money.

## 80. Keene Corp. v. United States

**Date:** May 24, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Keene entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would affirm the §1500 dismissals because overlapping suits were pending at filing. Master's Courts domain and §§3–4 require respect for actual jurisdictional limits. The awkwardness of split federal remedies does not authorize a court to erase a statutory condition.

**Entering law.** Section 1500 attaches to the Claims Court's jurisdiction, assessed when suit begins. The relevant overlap includes the same operative facts and overlapping requested relief; different legal theories alone do not remove it. *Matson Navigation* and the time-of-filing principle support the analysis. The record does not require adjudication of wholly different relief or a Claims Court-first filing sequence. [Statute, concurrent actions, and reserved questions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/200/).

**Recommendation.** Keep the dismissal and state precisely which claims and requested relief overlapped at each filing. Subsequent dismissal in another forum cannot retroactively create jurisdiction. Preserve unpresented sequence and relief questions rather than extending the bar to everything arising from the same historical episode. Further access protection would require a different statutory construction supported by a different record or congressional action, not a free-standing fairness exception. The comparison of pleadings, relief, and filing dates is administrable; no asbestos-liability ruling or guarantee that a new action remains timely belongs in this disposition.

## 81. Sullivan v. Louisiana

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Retain; articulate the narrow structural category.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Sullivan entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reverse the conviction because the conceded reasonable-doubt defect vitiates the jury's constitutional determination. His separate finality analysis and refusal to reinstate the original death sentence match §§3 and 19. Criminal Procedure expressly protects the jury's function while distinguishing structural defects from ordinary trial error.

**Entering law.** *Cox Broadcasting* permits review of an otherwise finally resolved federal issue despite remaining state proceedings in the pertinent category. *Winship*, *Duncan*, and *Cage* establish the jury and reasonable-doubt requirements; *Chapman* and *Fulminante* distinguish harmless trial error from defects in the adjudicative structure. Classification of this defect remains the question to decide, not an inherited *Sullivan* holding. [Record, practical finality, and governing authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/275/).

**Recommendation and practical rule.** Retain reversal: when the charge invalidates the standard governing the jury's entire guilt determination, an appellate hypothesis about what a correctly instructed jury would find cannot replace the required verdict. That explains automatic reversal by the missing constitutional decisionmaker's valid act, rather than by the seriousness of the accusation or supposed inevitability of guilt. It supplies a workable category and leaves ordinary instruction errors under their own tests. Retrial is available; acquittal requires an independently sustained insufficiency claim. Any earlier simulated *Brecht* holding about collateral review does not silently change this direct-review structural question.

## 82. Smith v. United States

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Retain, with full acknowledgment of the competing text.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Smith entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would remove the firearm-use conviction and its thirty-year sentence because offering the gun as merchandise is not use as a weapon. This fits fair notice, contextual statutory interpretation, and the prohibition on supplying an uncharged element. The matter is a then-open Supreme Court construction.

**Entering law.** *Perrin*, *Timbers*, *Bass*, and *Moskal* direct ordinary meaning, context, and residual lenity. The government's broad reading has real force: using a gun as consideration is ordinary instrumental use, and §924(d) uses related terminology for firearm transactions. The counterargument is that the criminal phrase carries its contextual weapon-employment meaning; satisfying the separate relation-to-drug-trafficking element cannot enlarge use. The indictment charged use alone. [Record, indictment, and competing textual arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/223/).

**Recommendation.** Retain the proposed disposition and say why criminal context distinguishes §924(d)'s transaction-specific forfeiture provisions, before applying lenity to remaining doubt. Weapon employment includes intimidation, threats, or striking; it need not include firing. Possession or transport cannot rescue a conviction on an uncharged carrying theory. This interpretation uses ascertainable conduct rather than a subjective judicial assessment of dangerousness. The narrower lawful outcome leaves the separate drug convictions intact. Later firearm cases and amendments supply no premise, and no general ban on harsh mandatory terms follows.

## 83. Mertens v. Hewitt Associates

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Retain the bounded alternative construction; refine the antecedent holding.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Mertens entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would permit monetary restoration for losses caused by knowing assistance in an ERISA fiduciary breach, vacating only the preserved dismissal. This gives his employee-protection principle a specific statutory and trust-law source. It does not simply infer damages from importance, preemption, or the Master's preferred remedy. The distinction matters under §§4 and 19 and Statutory Interpretation.

**Available law.** Section 502(a)(3) expressly authorizes appropriate equitable relief to redress covered violations. *Firestone* directs attention to the trust-law background, and *Clews* demonstrates that monetary relief within trust litigation can possess an equitable character. *Russell* insists on the enacted remedial architecture but decided §409's claim for extracontractual individual damages; it did not determine this §502(a)(3) question. These authorities make Stone's construction legally credible in June 1993, although not compelled. [Firestone](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/489/101/), [Clews](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/182/461/), [Russell](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/473/134/).

**Strongest objection and answer.** The contrary reading distinguishes remedies traditionally equitable in general from all relief administered by equity courts and emphasizes Congress's express fiduciary-liability provision. Stone should answer in terms of the **specific trust remedy**, not equity's general power to award incidental damages. A knowing participant in the identified breach is different from an ordinary negligent actuary. Section 502(l)'s references to knowing participants support the contextual reading, but are not an independent private cause of action. The record fixes Hewitt's nonfiduciary status, and denial of restitution and several other dismissals were not appealed. [Preservation, relief sought, and contemporary trust-remedy dispute](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/248/).

**Recommended rule and administration.** Permit proven causal restoration of trust loss through §502(a)(3) against a person shown knowingly to have participated in the underlying fiduciary violation; apply traditional equitable limits to the amount and proper recipient. Require breach, knowledge, participation, and causation through ordinary pleading and proof. Do not create status-based professional liability, punitive damages, or an unpreserved restitution theory. Because Hewitt expressly disclaimed the independent lack-of-action ground, Stone may state the minimal coverage construction needed for his remand while reserving unbriefed questions at its edges. Recasting that disclaimer as a jurisdictional defect would be wrong.

This is a substantial, candid choice within open statutory interpretation, not an overruling of the same case's future historical result. The protection should go this far: permit adjudication of the preserved claim. It should not adjudge liability, reopen fixed classifications, or cure every ERISA remedial gap. Traditional knowledge and causal-loss requirements give the construction a more predictable boundary than an undefined power to make beneficiaries whole whenever equity seems desirable.

## 84. Musick, Peeler & Garrett v. Employers Insurance of Wausau

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Refine characterization; retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK7_STONE.md`, Musick, Peeler entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would recognize contribution among persons actually jointly liable under Rule 10b-5. His use of a particular statutory setting rather than a universal professional-liability rule fits §§4, 16, and 19. But calling the decision only application understates the open question: he is defining a previously unsettled incident of an established implied action.

**Entering law.** *Northwest Airlines* and *Texas Industries* reject judicially created contribution in congressionally specified schemes. The received Rule 10b-5 action has a different judicially developed history; *Lampf* used analogous express securities provisions to define one of its incidents. Sections 9(e) and 18(b) provide a close contribution analogy. [Remedy, analogies, and competing authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/286/).

**Recommendation.** Keep affirmance while stating the actual modest doctrinal development: courts defining this recognized implied remedy may follow contribution rules in its closest express statutory counterparts. That is a reasoned exception to a general refusal to invent federal contribution, not disregard of it. The claimant must still establish the proposed contributor's own underlying liability and the permissible allocation; a settlement payment or professional involvement does not establish either. Administration can use established joint-liability and allocation principles. The case supplies no automatic aiding-and-abetting rule and no authority for contribution in unrelated statutes or under later securities legislation.

## 85. FCC v. Beach Communications, Inc.

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Beach entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would sustain the common-ownership franchising distinction under rational-basis review. That is fully consistent with the Master's acceptance of ordinary economic regulation and §17's evidentiary distinctions. Communications equipment does not itself transform an ownership classification into a speech restriction, and disfavored economic policy is not a listed vulnerability.

**Entering law.** *Fritz* and *Stroop* allow legislative generalization and reasonably conceivable legitimate reasons; government need not produce formal findings or empirical certainty. *Moreno* and *Cleburne* preserve meaningful limits concerning forbidden purpose and irrational prejudice. Common management can support an inference about bargaining power and the balance of consumer benefit against regulatory cost. [Classification, asserted rationales, and earlier authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/307/).

**Recommendation and limit.** Retain the result and identify a sufficient rational connection in this ownership arrangement. Avoid importing the historical opinion's sweeping language about virtual unreviewability as a universal rule: the Master permits reasonable inference, not a conclusion affirmatively contradicted by settled facts or an impermissible purpose. Nothing presented establishes such a defect here. A requirement that Congress prove the line with a legislative record would itself change the governing test and lacks justification. The actual ownership and management predicates are readily administered. Separate speech claims and discriminatory applications remain separate cases; no new scrutiny tier or broader economic substantive-due-process rule is needed.

## 86. Nobelman v. American Savings Bank

**Date:** June 1, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Nobelman entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject the proposed modification of a partially secured principal-residence mortgage. This fits Statutory Interpretation and §18's ordinary-law discipline. Bankruptcy's protective purpose does not permit a court to disregard Congress's specific mortgage exception.

**Entering law.** Section 506(a) values and divides claims; §1322(b)(2) protects the rights of a holder secured only by the principal residence. *Butner* supplies state law as the initial source of those mortgage rights. Section 1322(b)(5) separately permits qualifying cure and maintenance. The issue is how these provisions interact where some security remains, not whether valuation is meaningless. [Claim, statutory wording, and mortgage-rights analysis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/324/).

**Recommendation and room.** Retain affirmance. Explain that recognizing an unsecured component does not itself remove this holder's protected contractual rights. Preserve the ability to propose a plan using the enacted cure route. Actual collateral and valuation provide objective predicates: a wholly unsecured junior lien or a claim with additional collateral presents a different question, which Stone should not decide here. A broader power to strip these mortgage rights would need a different textual construction or amendment, not the Master's concern for dependency or housing. This disposition permits the statutory adjustment Congress authorized while maintaining predictable reliance on the exception it enacted.

## 87. Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Lamb's Chapel entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reverse the religious-viewpoint exclusion from an opened after-hours forum. This directly implements both Speech and Religion in the Master: equal private forum access coexists with separation of public government from worship and religious financing.

**Entering law.** *Perry* and *Cornelius* prohibit viewpoint discrimination even where subject-matter restrictions are permitted. *Widmar* and the inherited *Lemon* framework supply an established way to distinguish neutral access from sponsorship. The films addressed family and child-rearing, already eligible subjects; attendance was voluntary and the event was not school-sponsored. [Policy, application, and preceding forum and establishment cases](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/384/).

**Recommendation.** Keep reversal and remand for nondiscriminatory consideration, with declaratory or injunctive relief as needed. The practical rule is straightforward: when a school admits private discussion of a subject, it cannot exclude an otherwise eligible discussion because its perspective is religious. Neutral hours, safety, capacity, and subject limits continue to apply. No replacement of *Lemon* is needed to reach this result, and the school need not make every facility an unrestricted forum. Nor should the opinion decide every separate worship activity's eligibility. The Master expressly distinguishes forum access from financing, so this result cannot be mechanically exported to *Zobrest*'s reimbursement question.

## 88. Minnesota v. Dickerson

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Dickerson entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would preserve suppression while recognizing that lawful touch can reveal contraband. That matches Search and Seizure's attention to actual intrusion, §§3 and 17's factual discipline, and Courts' requirement of live legal consequences.

**Entering law.** *Terry* and *Sibron* limit the protective frisk; *Horton* and *Hicks* distinguish lawful perception from an additional investigative search. Nothing makes touch categorically incapable of establishing probable cause. The accepted facts describe squeezing or manipulation after the officer knew the object was not a weapon. The diversion statute's retained record has identified future legal uses, supporting continued justiciability despite completion and dismissal. [Diversion consequences, factual sequence, and earlier search cases](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/366/).

**Recommended rule.** During an otherwise lawful protective contact, probable cause acquired without an additional search may justify seizure where lawful access exists. Manipulation undertaken to identify a nonweapon object cannot generate its own justification. The inquiry concerns permissible contact and when probable cause arose; it does not require impossible reconstruction of every split-second thought. Require a concrete account of the contact rather than accepting an officer's conclusory label of immediate recognition. Accepted findings already defeat the seizure here, so no second factual opportunity for the State is necessary. This is a bounded extension of an existing principle, not a new evidence-search exception or a categorical prohibition of tactile evidence.

## 89. Gilmore v. Taylor

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Retain, subject to actual intervening simulated habeas law.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Gilmore entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would deny habeas relief because the particular instructional rule was new under *Teague*. This does not conflict with his insistence on a functioning defense. The Master separately requires source, posture, preservation, and collateral-review limits; a broad preference for accurate adjudication does not make every later rule dictated at finality.

**Entering law.** *Teague*, *Butler*, and *Saffle* require identification of the precise rule and the legal landscape when conviction became final. *Cupp*, *Patterson*, and *Martin* did not unambiguously prescribe *Falconer*'s particular sequencing rule for this noncapital mitigating defense. The then-existing substantive and watershed-procedure exceptions must both be considered. A preserved nonretroactivity defense survives a merits concession. [Instructional dispute, finality problem, and earlier authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/333/).

**Recommendation.** Keep the collateral disposition and explain why the rule changes procedure rather than the category of conduct or persons punishable, and why it is not the exceptional watershed change then recognized. Do not add a sweeping rule excluding affirmative-defense instructions from due process on direct review. That extra proposition would be unnecessary and harder to reconcile with the Master. *Estelle v. McGuire* is within the original packets' pre-OT1992 historical baseline; the narrower additional-packet OT1991 baseline creates the global source-state qualification, not a reason to exclude it categorically. Later abolition of *Teague*'s watershed exception is unavailable. Actual earlier simulated *Graham* or *Fretwell* holdings require checking; *Brecht* matters only if an error is reached.

## 90. Good Samaritan Hospital v. Shalala

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Refine explanation; retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Good Samaritan entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject a universal right to above-limit reasonable-cost reimbursement, finding reconciliation of interim payments the better statutory reading. This fits Administrative Law's independent identification of legal authority and genuine respect for delegated cost-setting judgment. The draft correctly resists using every administrative dispute to install the Master's alternative deference system.

**Entering law.** Section 1395x(v)(1)(A)'s corrective-adjustment language operates alongside authorized cost methodologies and interim-payment reconciliation. *Georgetown* prohibits unauthorized retroactive rulemaking; it does not conclusively resolve this different interpretation. *Chevron* remains the inherited framework, and consistency affects the persuasiveness of an agency's construction without making all changes invalid. [Reimbursement regime, statutory issue, and interpretive history](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/402/).

**Recommended refinement.** State whether the text and structure resolve the issue or whether Stone also concludes the construction is permissible at *Chevron*'s second step. Merely calling it the better reading does not establish statutory unambiguity. Identify the existing cost-limit exceptions and assess the agency's shifting explanation; do not bless a litigation rationale or independently unlawful method. The practical rule reconciles payments to amounts due under lawful methods, preserving established exception procedures. There is no demonstrated failure here requiring wholesale abandonment of *Chevron*: the source-based reading already reaches Stone's result. No numerical judicial ceiling, new reimbursement entitlement, or later prospective-payment rule belongs in this case.

## 91. Antoine v. Byers & Anderson, Inc.

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, Antoine entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would remove absolute judicial immunity from the challenged reporting failure. The Master preserves function-specific immunity and refuses to infer it from a court employee's title. This case therefore does not require Stone's more controversial proposed exception for knowing official falsification.

**Entering law.** *Forrester* and *Butz* require historical and functional justification, with the proponent carrying the burden for absolute protection. Section 753's recording and transcription duties do not entail the adjudicative discretion that supports judicial immunity. Years of delay do not by themselves establish deliberate falsification or every element of a constitutional claim. [Reporting function, procedural record, and immunity analysis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/429/).

**Recommendation and limit.** Retain reversal and remand. Ask what function the defendant performed and why that function requires absolute immunity; essential participation in a trial is insufficient. This line can be administered through duties and actions rather than speculation about motives. The claimant must still identify a cause of action, an actionable injury, causation, damages, and defeat any independently available defense. No automatic *Bivens* remedy follows. Broader revision of judicial, prosecutorial, or testimonial immunity would exceed what is necessary when this ministerial conduct falls outside absolute protection under ordinary doctrine. Later *Buckley* proceedings supply no June 7 premise.

## 92. United States National Bank of Oregon v. Independent Insurance Agents of America, Inc.

**Date:** June 7, 1993. **Assessment: Retain.** Source: `OT_1992CHUNK8_STONE.md`, United States National Bank entry.

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject the purported repeal, correct the misplaced statutory punctuation, and remand the regulator's remaining interpretation. This matches Statutory Interpretation's strict allowance for unmistakable clerical correction and §3's duty to decide a necessary antecedent question.

**Entering law.** *Kamen* and *Arcadia* recognize responsibility to identify the governing law. Section 112 gives the Statutes at Large authority over conflicting nonpositive-law codification; *Hammock* allows punctuation to yield to the enactment's demonstrated structure. The relevant sequence of amendments supplies the legal basis for the correction. Fair adversarial briefing remains required even where parties initially framed the dispute differently. [Amendments, punctuation problem, and antecedent-question authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/508/439/).

**Recommendation and room.** Retain reversal and remand. Show the enactment boundary that makes the mistaken quotation mark unmistakable, rather than stating that Congress could not have preferred the apparent policy. That explanation is the rule's administrable constraint: judges cannot repair substantive choices merely because a cleaner statute seems desirable. The surviving authorization does not itself settle how far a small-town bank may market insurance or validate every Comptroller interpretation. No broader deference framework or general power to rescue poorly drafted legislation is warranted. Resolving the actual statutory source is sufficient.

## 93. Rake v. Wade

That conceptual possibility is not by itself the inherited *Boyde* threshold. State expressly that the charge, the evidence, and the whole trial create a reasonable likelihood that the sentencing decision could not reflect this culpability judgment. The competing interpretation deserves a direct answer: youth is transient and can reduce predicted future danger, so the second question does give it some mitigating work. Stone's point is that reduced culpability for the completed act remains significant even where this particular young defendant is predicted dangerous. Explain the evidentiary and instructional basis for finding that aspect outside the jury's effective reach. [Actual youth evidence, charge, and competing contemporary analysis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/509/350/).

**Practical rule and extent.** Require an instruction or special issue authorizing a reasoned sentence below death when the relevant mitigating evidence warrants it, without making the juror falsify an unrelated factual answer. Texas retains structured sentencing; Stone need not demand a particular wording, unbounded mercy, or a categorical age exemption. This is direct review, so *Teague* does not itself prevent the clarification. An actual earlier simulated *Graham* ruling is material: a retroactivity-only ruling can be distinguished, whereas an operative merits rule contrary to this position must be candidly narrowed or reconsidered under §16, with its rule and reason identified. The proposed *Graham* supplement and historical result do not establish that law. These limits make the protection administrable without silently lowering the constitutional instructional-error threshold.

### Principal findings from cases 73–108

- **Brooke Group:** strongest recommended substantive revision. Preserve a recoupment mechanism, but the draft does not justify resolving the competing factual inferences against the verdict holder. Remand remaining grounds rather than automatically reinstating damages.
- **Northeastern Florida:** remove obsolete lane language and any suggestion that individual allocations automatically require a stricter merits rule; preserve competitive-process standing and reserve justification.
- **Johnson:** replace possibility-based phrasing with an express, record-supported *Boyde* reasonable-likelihood finding; check actual earlier *Graham* law.
- **Mertens, Bourland, and Sale:** substantial protective positions already have credible contemporaneous statutory, treaty, or trust-law grounds. Retain their carefully limited dispositions rather than flattening them into historical outcomes or expanding them into universal rules.
- **Zobrest:** the preservation-aware avoidance remand is lawful but discretionary. A later merits analysis must address whether the interpreter service is severable from religious formation, and must confront neutral-aid precedents.
- **Ordinary-law entries:** most already fulfill Stone's method. Demanding new frameworks in tax, bankruptcy, allocation, exhaustion, and ministerial-immunity cases would reduce consistency rather than express the Master more faithfully.
