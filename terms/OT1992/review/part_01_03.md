# Stone review — cases 1–36

## Reading conventions

This review evaluates Stone's proposed disposition and reasoning, not the historical Supreme Court result. The local sources are the captioned entries in `OT_1992CHUNK1_STONE.md` and its `_NEUTRAL` counterpart for cases 1–12, chunk 2 for cases 13–24, and chunk 3 for cases 25–36. References to Master sections and domains identify Stone's commitments, not governing authority. Published opinions in the reviewed cases are used for the record, statutory language, and contemporary arguments; their eventual holdings are not assumed to be law entering their own simulated events. Earlier same-term proposals do not establish precedent. Date-eligible historical authorities remain subject to the report's separate simulated-baseline qualification.

**Retain** means the proposed result and its basic reasoning are sound. **Refine** means retain the result but strengthen or narrow material reasoning. **Revise** means a change in the recommended disposition or operative rule. **Conditional** identifies a missing source or factual determination that prevents an unconditional recommendation. The labels address the supplied proposal, not any supposed approval requirement in the documents.

## 1. Grubbs v. Delo

**Date:** October 20, 1992. **Verdict: Retain, with a jurisdictional refinement.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would grant a temporary administrative stay so that an execution scheduled roughly two hours after receipt does not prevent examination of the papers. This fits Master §§3, 14, 17 and the Remedies domain: irreversible harm can justify preserving a real review opportunity, while capital status creates neither jurisdiction nor automatic merits relief. There is no conflict with his commitment to lawful punishment because the proposal decides no substantive entitlement.

**Governing law and recommendation.** Sections 2251 and 1651 support preservation connected to an actual or prospective jurisdictional proceeding; the All Writs Act supplies no independent jurisdiction. Barefoot's substantial-showing requirements still govern continued relief. Identify the habeas proceeding and potential appellate route in the order's supporting reasons. The reported application establishes urgency and no identified applicant-caused delay, but does not reproduce the substantive claims or the State's incorporated brief. [Application record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/1301/).

**Realistic extent and administration.** Retain a stay pending prompt consideration and further order. Assign immediate examination of jurisdiction, the application, and response; reconsider when that examination is possible. Do not convert missing reading time into a substantive presumption of innocence or a stay through every successive petition. This is a familiar preservation function, not a new capital-stay framework. The missing merits papers limit continuation of relief, not this narrowly justified opportunity to read them.

## 2. Martin v. District of Columbia Court of Appeals

**Date:** November 2, 1992. **Verdict: Refine.**

**Existing position and fit.** Denial of the present IFP motion while declining a blanket prospective civil-filing restriction coheres with Master §§3, 17, 19 and the Courts domain. Stone can respect docket management without treating an indigent person's future, different claim as already adjudicated. His access commitment does not make fee relief an entitlement.

**Governing law.** Section 1915 and Rule 39.8 permit denial of a frivolous or malicious request. McDonald and Sindram establish power to address documented abuse of extraordinary-writ practice; they do not require an all-future-civil-certiorari restriction here. The reported history characterizes the pending filings themselves as frivolous and identifies extensive repetition. [Published filing record and earlier authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/1/).

**Recommendation and boundary.** Retain the disposition, but ground the current denial in this petition's demonstrated frivolousness as well as its relationship to the abusive pattern. A numerical history alone should not substitute for Rule 39.8's applicable condition. State that individual screening sufficiently answers the present abuse, rather than suggesting prospective restrictions are categorically unlawful. If later repetition requires a restriction, define the covered subject matter and preserve a way to present a materially different claim; such a later order needs its own justification. Fee-paid compliance remains available. Do not add a disposition of the unlisted companion petition or treat an unestablished simulated warning as an operative prior order.

## 3. Montana v. Imlay

**Date:** November 3, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would dismiss the writ as improvidently granted because the operative replacement sentence is outside this petition. This is a strong application of Master §3: a serious compelled-admission issue does not license adjudication through a vehicle whose available relief is unclear. His substantive concern about coercion remains intact without becoming an advisory holding.

**Governing law.** Section 1257 requires attention to the judgment actually under review. The later five-year sentence was separately affirmed; the alleged parole consequence is uncertain. The contemporary objection is substantial: Cox Broadcasting's finality exceptions and possible continuing consequences support an argument for review. But a discretionary DIG does not require Stone to declare that every possible controversy is constitutionally moot. [Record and competing jurisdictional arguments](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-supreme-court/506/5.html).

**Recommendation and lawful room.** Keep the altered-vehicle rationale and avoid categorical mootness language. If a properly presented live sentence later conditions liberty on incriminating admissions, Minnesota v. Murphy and Lefkowitz v. Turley furnish a direct route to examining threatened revocation, actual exposure, and adequate immunity. That would implement Stone's voluntariness position without a general constitutional prohibition of rehabilitative treatment. Here there is no lawful need to go further. Dismissal leaves the selected judgment undisturbed without approving its reasoning and grants no relief against the different sentence.

## 4. Church of Scientology of California v. United States

**Date:** November 16, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Vacating the mootness dismissal and restoring appellate review closely matches Stone's distinction between an incomplete remedy and no effective remedy. Master §§3–4 and 19 support treating retained copies separately from information agents have already learned.

**Governing law.** Mills v. Green asks whether effectual relief remains; it does not demand perfect restoration. Reisman permits review of IRS summons-enforcement orders. Ryan and Perlman concern appealability in other discovery settings and do not establish a separate mootness doctrine. A lawful order controlling the Government's retained recordings can still alter the parties' situation. [Record, statutory framework, and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/9/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain vacatur and remand for the enforcement appeal. This fully preserves the core principle through ordinary Article III law; an expanded informational-rights test is unnecessary. On remand, the court must decide the actual privilege or enforcement objection and its remedial authority before ordering return or destruction. The Church does not gain tax immunity, recognition of the asserted privilege, or suppression of all derivative investigative knowledge from winning the threshold issue. Administrability is straightforward: identify copies or tangible material subject to a possible order and ask whether an authorized judgment would provide meaningful benefit. No impossible instruction to erase human memory is needed.

## 5. Hadley v. United States

**Date:** November 16, 1992. **Verdict: Conditional; strengthen the Rule 403 analysis.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone conditionally affirms admission of earlier sexual-abuse evidence for intent, subject to verification of the grant. His rejection of an automatic exclusion whenever the accused denies participation is legally defensible. But Master §17 and Criminal Procedure require an actual nonpropensity inference and actual prejudice analysis; identifying an intent element does not complete either task.

**Governing law.** Rules 404(b), 403 and 105, together with Huddleston, supply the framework. Huddleston permits conditional-fact submission on sufficient evidence; it does not make every similar act admissible or require a defendant to contest intent before Rule 403 operates. [Huddleston](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/485/681/). The lower opinion documents Hadley's denial, his offer not to argue intent, the limiting instruction, the similarity analysis, and the mixed verdict. [Ninth Circuit record](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/918/848/24534/).

**Recommendation.** Preserve the conditional posture. The very short Supreme Court disposition does not authenticate the granted question or explain a merits answer. [Published procedural disposition](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/91-6646.ZPC.html). If the question is properly presented, explain what disputed intent proposition remained after independent proof of the charged conduct, how the prior acts establish it without a character inference, and how the offer reduces probative need. Acquittals do not prove the other convictions were unaffected by propensity prejudice. Affirmance is supportable if that record-specific balance falls within discretion; exclusion or a new trial requires an established error and the applicable harmless-error analysis, not Stone's general distaste for propensity evidence. No Rule 413, Old Chief, or later doctrine belongs in this 1992 analysis. No replacement test is needed.

## 6. Parke v. Raley

**Date:** December 1, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone accepts Kentucky's initial production burden in a collateral challenge to counseled pleas and separately finds the surviving 1981 showing sufficient. That combination is important: a permissible burden scheme does not automatically validate its application. It fits Master §§3–4, 17 and Criminal Procedure, which preserve the State's ultimate element burden without requiring that every historical fact be proved by the same mechanism.

**Governing law.** Boykin's direct-review silence rule does not automatically displace the regularity of a final judgment on collateral attack. Johnson v. Zerbst and Marshall v. Lonberger support the distinction and appropriate respect for historical findings; Burgett prevents use of a constitutionally invalid uncounseled conviction. Kentucky's scheme shifts production after proof of the prior judgment and requires a responsive showing once regularity is rebutted. [Plea record and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/20/).

**Recommendation and limits.** Retain reversal of the conditional-writ judgment. Explain the actual corroboration: the established 1979 advisement, counsel, the 1981 form and acknowledged jury warning, and the absence of contradictory proof. Do not infer understanding merely from having a criminal record. Under pre-AEDPA §2254(d), historical findings receive the statutory presumption; the federal constitutional conclusion remains independently judged. No extra transcript-preservation remedy, universal waiver presumption, or categorical rejection of collateral review is justified. The proposed production/response sequence is administrable and does not require changing the native framework.

## 7. Richmond v. Lewis

**Date:** December 1, 1992. **Verdict: Retain; state the legal-cure requirement directly.**

**Existing position and fit.** Relief from the death sentence, with an opportunity for lawful state correction, fits Stone's insistence on reliable capital sentencing and a remedy tailored to the proved violation. His reasoning should describe whether a valid appellate adjudication cured the defect, rather than foreground vote counting, which is not the focus of this review.

**Governing law.** Godfrey and Maynard require a meaningful limiting construction of capital aggravators. Clemons permits an appellate court in a weighing State to perform constitutionally adequate reweighing or harmless-error review. The supplied record establishes use of a vague aggravator and no operative appellate rationale accomplishing the required cure. A federal habeas court cannot perform missing state sentencing work simply because other aggravators remain. [Sentencing record and governing authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/40/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal and conditional sentencing relief. The practical rule is that the authorized state tribunal must actually conduct the required corrective inquiry on the proper aggravation and mitigation, with reasons adequate to identify the cure. If it does not, the defective death sentence cannot be executed. A lawful new sentencing proceeding or legally sufficient appellate cure remains available within the period fixed on remand. There is no basis here to prohibit appellate reweighing categorically or overturn the guilt judgment. Existing doctrine already implements Stone's core principle predictably; no new reliability test is needed.

## 8. Soldal v. Cook County

**Date:** December 8, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would restore the Fourth Amendment claim because police-attributable interference with possession can be a seizure without a search. This strongly aligns with the Search and Seizure domain and Master §4's separation of interests and remedies. The home was physically removed; an additional invasion of secrecy is unnecessary to identify the possessory injury.

**Governing law.** Jacobsen already distinguishes privacy protected against searches from possession protected against seizures. The alleged police intervention preventing resistance permits joint-action attribution at summary judgment; it is materially different from mere official presence. Whether the conduct was reasonable, who participated, and whether a municipality is responsible remain separate questions. [Record and antecedent seizure/state-action authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/56/).

**Recommendation and lawful room.** Retain reversal and remand. Stone can reject a criminal-investigation prerequisite and a privacy prerequisite as unsupported restrictions on existing seizure law. This is fuller application of the Fourth Amendment's own coverage, not an invitation to impose later property-based search doctrine or a federal landlord-tenant code. He should not decide qualified immunity from the master document's preferred defense: Harlow, Anderson, and the actual presented issue govern any immunity adjudication. The home, interference, and official contribution are concrete adjudicative questions; reasonableness, Monell causation, damages, and personal defenses can be resolved by ordinary proceedings below.

## 9. Farrar v. Hobby

**Date:** December 14, 1992. **Verdict: Refine; retain fee reconsideration.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone recognizes prevailing-party status from an enforceable nominal judgment but remands the amount issue. This fits Master §§4 and 19 and Remedies: a legal victory is real without making every requested litigation expense reasonable.

**Governing law.** Garland concerns alteration of the legal relationship; Carey recognizes nominal relief for a proved violation without demonstrated compensatory harm; Hensley makes actual success central to reasonable fees. The appellate judgment denied eligibility and did not undertake that distinct amount analysis. The record also presents a failed $17 million damages demand and an approximately $280,000 award, so substantial reduction, including zero, is a real possibility. [Fee record and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/103/).

**Recommendation.** Retain vacatur and remand, but describe this as a justified allocation of the unresolved reasonableness inquiry, not a jurisdictional inability of an appellate court ever to determine a fee on a sufficient record. Reject automatic proportionality and automatic substantial fees alike. The lower court should identify successful claims, work reasonably attributable to them, the litigation's actual objectives, and any demonstrated legal significance; exclude unsuccessful unrelated work and explain the award. A documented nonmonetary accomplishment may matter, but cannot be invented. Stone may resist a categorical nominal-damages/zero-fee rule without changing Hensley's framework. His position preserves access through authorized fees while retaining predictable review of actual, rather than asserted, success.

## 10. District of Columbia v. Greater Washington Board of Trade

**Date:** December 14, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone narrowly sustains preemption of the obligation measured by an ERISA-covered plan. This is compatible with his Federalism and Statutory Interpretation domains: express preemption must receive its enacted effect even in a traditional state regulatory field. State regulatory competence does not erase §514(a).

**Governing law.** Shaw, Alessi, Mackey and Ingersoll-Rand provide the entering reference-to-plan framework. The District's concession and the statute's use of the employer plan to measure legally required replacement coverage materially distinguish a separately administered benefit fixed independently by state law. [Statutory operation, concession, and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/125/).

**Recommendation and further room.** Keep the ERISA-covered-plan limitation and do not invalidate the entire workers' compensation system. Stone can confine broad prior language about laws that relate to plans to the legal dependence necessary here, reserving mere economic effects and obligations referencing exempt plans. But replacing an express reference-to-plan rule with actual-conflict-only review would alter settled statutory precedent and is unnecessary to this disposition. Concrete benefits administration and employer reliance strengthen the reason to avoid an unexplained reversal of that framework. The proposed distinction is administrable: ask whether the covered plan determines the legal content of the State's mandate, rather than whether the State's law mentions insurance or increases costs. No new constitutional federalism test is warranted.

## 11. Mississippi v. Louisiana

**Date:** December 14, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Reversal of the district court's interstate adjudication, with remand to identify surviving private claims, directly implements Master §3 and the Courts and Interstate Relations domains. Stone's concern for an effective forum cannot confer jurisdiction Congress reserved elsewhere.

**Governing law.** Section 1251(a) makes this Court's jurisdiction exclusive over controversies between States. Louisiana's claim against Mississippi presents that controversy even though it arose in private title litigation. A prior refusal to exercise original jurisdiction does not confer district-court power. [Jurisdictional record and statutory analysis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/73/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal and remand. Stone should distinguish removing the judgment on the interstate claim from mechanically dismissing every private claim: the remaining parties, jurisdiction, and need for an interstate determination require examination below. If a new original action is sought, it must come through that channel; this appeal does not supply an original boundary decree. The ordinary rule gives a predictable boundary based on the actual sovereign parties and controversy, without sweeping every private land suit near a state boundary into exclusive original jurisdiction. No framework departure is justified, and remedial inconvenience supplies no exception to clear jurisdictional text.

## 12. Republic National Bank of Miami v. United States

**Date:** December 14, 1992. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Restoring the appeal while separating entitlement from payment authority matches Master §§3–4, 19 and Remedies. The Government should not obtain a jurisdictional veto merely by transferring proceeds, but a court also cannot order Treasury payment from fairness alone.

**Governing law.** The Rio Grande and The Little Charles distinguish initial control of the res from uninterrupted postjudgment possession, with a different problem where a judgment truly could accomplish nothing. Section 2465 and applicable judgment-payment authority bear on available relief, while the Appropriations Clause demands a lawful payment source. [Forfeiture record and jurisdiction/payment arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/80/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal of the appellate dismissal and remand for the bank's claim. State that the transfer alone did not destroy jurisdiction acquired over the property and its substituted proceeds. Identify the applicable statutory mechanism before directing any eventual payment, rather than making a general promise that every return judgment is payable from the Judgment Fund. Failure to obtain an appellate stay can carry practical consequences but does not automatically eliminate appellate power. Administrability turns on identifiable attachment, transfer, remaining legal relief, and the particular payment statute. The bank must still prove its property interest and entitlement; no broad forfeiture or appropriations doctrine need be replaced.

## 13. Puerto Rico Aqueduct & Sewer Authority v. Metcalf & Eddy

**Date:** January 12, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reverse the appellate-jurisdiction dismissal without finding PRASA immune. This is precisely the separation Master §§3–4 require: the nature of an asserted protection can justify immediate review without establishing that the claimant possesses it.

**Governing law.** Cohen and Coopers & Lybrand require conclusiveness, separation from the merits, and effective unreviewability after final judgment. Mitchell recognizes the significance of an immunity from suit. A conclusive rejection of a substantial sovereign-immunity claim has that procedural character, while Mt. Healthy requires a separate inquiry into whether a public entity is an arm of the State. [Contract/immunity record and antecedent authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/139/).

**Recommendation and boundary.** Retain remand for consideration of immunity within the collateral-order appeal. Explain the limited category rather than suggesting that every assertion of sovereign status automatically stays every issue in litigation. Ordinary finality, substantiality, and separability requirements remain. Stone's preferred restriction on Article I abrogation does not justify displacing Union Gas in this procedural case, and Puerto Rico's treatment in the litigation does not decide every constitutional question about territorial status. No finding of waiver, abrogation, contractual liability, or entitlement to prospective relief is needed. A categorical appealability rule for the conclusive denial of immunity is administrable without a new substantive immunity doctrine.

## 14. Bath Iron Works Corp. v. Director, Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs

**Date:** January 12, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would keep scheduled hearing-loss compensation rather than impose the retiree occupational-disease measure. His Statutory Interpretation domain supports this result through the enacted injury categories, not an independent preference for larger benefits.

**Governing law.** Section 908(c)(13) specifically schedules hearing loss. The special postretirement provisions concern occupational disease that does not immediately result in disability. Noise-induced hearing loss occurs during exposure; later discovery or filing does not itself convert that injury into delayed disability. Potomac Electric Power distinguishes scheduled compensation from wage-loss compensation. [Statutory provisions and injury record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/153/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain affirmance and the ordinary calculation under the hearing-loss schedule. Separate the date of injury, last exposure, awareness, and filing because each can perform a different statutory function. Retirement does not erase an accrued scheduled injury, but neither does the schedule establish an unproved percentage of impairment. Administrability comes from the statutory schedule and supported medical findings, with the proper wage basis applied to the injury. No constitutional entitlement to a particular benefit, new wage-loss presumption, or replacement disability framework is necessary. This is an example in which ordinary statutory law already permits Stone to preserve practical protection fully.

## 15. Commissioner v. Soliman

**Date:** January 12, 1993. **Verdict: Refine; retain disallowance on this record.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone treats the hospitals as the principal location of an anesthesiology practice despite a necessary administrative home office. That coheres with Master §§2 and 18 and Statutory Interpretation: practical necessity is evidence of business use, not a substitute for an additional statutory condition.

**Governing law.** The then-applicable §280A(c)(1)(A) requires exclusive and regular use as the principal place of business. No entering Supreme Court decision has already imposed Soliman's eventual comparative formulation; Stone is resolving the statute's open application, not mechanically applying a preexisting two-factor Supreme Court test. The record compares more hospital working time and direct patient services with administrative home functions. [Statutory text, record, and contemporary alternative readings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/168/).

**Recommendation and lawful room.** Retain reversal, explaining why importance of the actual business functions and time identify the principal location here. The more generous reading has a real argument: the only location available for managing the self-employed business may be its operational center. Stone should answer that distinction, rather than call all alternatives rewriting. Nevertheless, the word principal permits the comparative conclusion he reaches. Treat service delivery as especially important here, not categorically controlling for every occupation. The rule remains workable when courts compare the taxpayer's actual activities, without requiring every dispersed business to possess a single qualifying site. Do not import Congress's 1997 expansion into these tax years.

## 16. Rowland v. California Men’s Colony, Unit II Men’s Advisory Council

**Date:** January 12, 1993. **Verdict: Retain, with implementation clarification.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would allow an association to attempt an indigence showing. This fits the Dictionary Act, Master §13's source-specific treatment of artificial entities, and his refusal to derive all entity capacities from human personhood. Organizational fee eligibility is statutory capacity, not a personal constitutional entitlement.

**Governing law.** The Dictionary Act includes associations unless context indicates otherwise. Section 1915's references to affidavits, poverty, and counsel create a genuine contextual dispute, but an authorized agent can provide financial facts and counsel can represent an entity. Those familiar practices support an inclusive construction without overruling a controlling natural-person-only Supreme Court rule entering the case. [Text and competing contextual arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/194/).

**Recommendation and administration.** Retain rejection of categorical ineligibility and remand. Require an authorized affidavit explaining the council's assets and its lawful access to resources, and resolve proper representation separately. Avoid mechanically attributing every inmate-member's property to the association or treating corporate insolvency as automatically sufficient: neither rule is necessary here. The strongest contrary position is that organizational poverty and representation produce hard recurring questions. Stone should answer with ordinary financial disclosure and entity law, rather than deny those questions exist. This preserves Congress's presumptive definition while allowing denial for an inadequate showing, sham organization, or improper representation. No automatic appointment of counsel or right to lay representation follows.

## 17. Nixon v. United States

**Date:** January 13, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone dismisses the particular challenge to committee evidence-taking as nonjusticiable while reserving independently determinate constitutional limits. This reconciles the Courts domain's commitment to judicially enforceable boundaries with Master §18's allocation of institutional competence.

**Governing law.** Article I assigns the Senate the sole power to try impeachments and specifies certain procedural commands. Baker distinguishes textual commitment and absence of manageable judicial standards from political importance alone. Powell demonstrates that a legislative judging function need not defeat judicial enforcement of separately fixed constitutional limits. [Record, constitutional text, and contemporary competing readings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/224/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Keep the claim-specific dismissal. The argument for review is credible: courts could ask whether a proceeding meaningfully constitutes a trial without imposing every criminal-trial procedure. Stone may nevertheless conclude that selecting between committee testimony and plenary testimony, where the Senate retains judgment and receives the record, is the committed procedural choice. Explain that functional boundary rather than immunize everything associated with impeachment. A challenge to an express oath or voting requirement would require a separate justiciability analysis; no hypothetical merits ruling is needed now. Deny reinstatement, pay, and procedural supervision on this claim. There is no need to invent a new political-question framework or an alternative merits holding after dismissal.

## 18. Bray v. Alexandria Women's Health Clinic

**Date:** January 13, 1993. **Verdict: Retain, with an explicit account of the limited loss.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone rejects the pleaded federal deprivation-clause theory but preserves examination of independent state-law protection. That fits Master §§3–4, the Personal Liberty domain, and its state-action limits. His strong protection of reproductive autonomy is not itself a federal cause of action against private obstructors.

**Governing law.** Griffin requires the statutory class-based purpose; Carpenters independently requires interference with a right protected against the private encroachment alleged. Interstate travel can have private protection, but the case must establish targeting of that right. The record's nondifferentiation between interstate and local patients does not establish a conspiracy directed at interstate movement itself. Roe and Casey do not independently supply private-action coverage. [Blockade record and statutory arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/263/).

**Recommendation and further room.** Retain reversal on that independent element, vacatur of the associated federal fees, and remand to identify the jurisdiction and scope sustaining the adjudicated state-law injunction. Avoid deciding that anti-abortion purpose necessarily proves or necessarily negates sex discrimination; neither proposition repairs the missing private-right element. The hindrance clause presents a genuinely different statutory route, but was not pleaded or adjudicated with the requisite obstruction-of-authorities purpose. Stone may reserve any procedurally permissible amendment; he cannot enter liability on it now. Bell and Gibbs prevent federal merits failure from retroactively erasing jurisdiction over substantial claims. Relief against unlawful trespass remains administrable through the established state claims. No constitutional state-action expansion or anticipation of later clinic-access legislation is justified by this record.

## 19. Crosby v. United States

**Date:** January 13, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would set aside convictions obtained at a trial begun in Crosby's absence. This fits his Criminal Procedure and Statutory Interpretation domains: flight can be sanctioned through law, but cannot supply an exception that the governing trial rule does not contain.

**Governing law.** Rule 43 requires presence and distinguishes continuation after an initially present defendant voluntarily departs. Diaz and Taylor concern departure after commencement; they do not erase that express boundary. A broad constitutional waiver argument does not decide the narrower rule question. [Rule text, history, and flight record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/255/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal, allowing retrial with Crosby present under Rule 43. The legal rule is easily administered at commencement, while leaving the separate continuation exception operational. Avoid saying the Constitution categorically forbids every trial beginning in voluntary absence; that constitutional question is unnecessary. Stone need not excuse absconding or invent an immunity from lawful flight-related sanctions. Nor should he recast this as structural constitutional error when the enacted procedural rule supplies the adequate basis. The existing framework fully realizes his commitment to a functioning adversarial process without a new attendance doctrine.

## 20. Dobbs v. Zant

**Date:** January 19, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would require reconsideration using the material sentencing transcript rather than grant immediate ineffective-assistance relief. That is strongly aligned with Master §17 and Criminal Procedure: neither an erroneous recollection nor a newly located document should become an untested substitute for the actual record.

**Governing law.** Strickland requires performance and prejudice assessed on the relevant facts. The transcript contradicted recollections relied upon below after the litigation had proceeded on an understanding that it was unavailable. An appellate record-correction mechanism is not unlimited authority to create new evidence, but remand can provide the proper means of consideration. [Published record correction](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/92-5579.ZPC.html).

**Recommendation and administration.** Retain limited review, reversal, and remand to consider the transcript through the appropriate procedure. Specify its material relationship to the prior account and allow the parties to address any factual or procedural issue its consideration raises. The discovery does not itself establish intentional concealment, deficient performance, racial motivation, prejudice, or innocence. Stone's stronger accountability position for knowing official falsification therefore has no occasion to operate without proof of that conduct and a corresponding claim. The administrable correction is an accurate record followed by the existing Sixth Amendment inquiry; no automatic-relief presumption or new exception to all record rules is warranted.

## 21. Herrera v. Collins

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Refine substantially; retain recognition of the claim and a bounded remand.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone proposes a freestanding bar on executing a prisoner who proves factual innocence by clear and convincing evidence. This is his strongest justified substantive extension in this group. It directly implements Master §14's innocence floor and the Criminal Procedure domain, while the proposed remand properly acknowledges that the affidavits are disputed and that exhaustion, abuse, hearing entitlement, and a stay are distinct questions.

**Entering law and legal basis.** Townsend excludes newly discovered evidence alone from ordinary federal habeas relief absent a constitutional violation; Jackson tests trial-record sufficiency. Neither already establishes Stone's freestanding claim. His answer must remain candid: execution after reliable disproof of guilt is itself the constitutional injury. Ford establishes that a valid conviction does not end inquiry into a later substantive obstacle to execution; Beck connects capital reliability to guilt determination. [Ford](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/477/399/), [contemporary Herrera arguments and record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/390/). The best contrary argument is that a second evidentiary inquiry can be less reliable after witnesses disappear and that clemency supplies an established corrective mechanism. Stone should answer that discretionary clemency cannot make an otherwise unconstitutional execution lawful, while preserving the verdict's evidentiary weight and a demanding burden.

**Retroactivity and proposed rule.** Penry's first Teague exception covers substantive limits on a class of persons whom the State may execute. Extending that principle to persons factually innocent of the capital crime is coherent, but the extension should be stated rather than described as an already settled innocence exception. [Penry's substantive category analysis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/492/302/). The practical rule should be: on a properly presented capital habeas claim, the prisoner bears the burden of establishing factual noncommission by clear and convincing evidence assessed with the complete relevant trial and new evidence; a showing merely that another jury might acquit is insufficient. Identify clear-and-convincing proof as Stone's proposed allocation, explaining that it protects finality more than a bare probability test without demanding impossible certainty. It is not Sawyer's different eligibility gateway imported wholesale.

**Administration and remedy.** Separate pleading sufficiency from proof: factual development follows only when allegations, provisionally credited in the setting of the record, could satisfy that merits standard and the existing hearing rules permit it. Assess delay, availability, corroboration and contradictions through findings, not an appellate credibility choice. Use then-existing exhaustion and successive-petition rules; no automatic hearing, indefinite stay, or later stay-and-abeyance procedure follows. Retain remand to address those questions and require a Barefoot/Delo basis for interim relief. If innocence is ultimately established, execution for this crime is prohibited; merely repeating a sentencing hearing cannot cure factual noncommission. The present claim does not adjudicate other convictions or all noncapital custody. This carefully bounded extension preserves the core principle without turning every new affidavit into a retrial.

## 22. Graham v. Collins

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Refine substantially; the proposed relief is defensible only through an explicit Teague analysis.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would grant conditional relief because the special issues cannot express all the mitigating force of Graham's youth and background. That aligns with his capital-reliability commitment. The vulnerable step is calling the required result dictated in 1984 merely because Lockett and Eddings already announced a broad principle. Master §§1, 3 and 16 require a genuine retroactivity analysis, not a conclusion chosen to unlock the preferred merits result.

**Entering law and strongest objection.** Eddings specifically concerns youth, background, and the inability to exclude relevant mitigation as a matter of law. [Eddings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/455/104/). But Jurek had sustained the Texas special-issue mechanism with attention to age and future conduct, making this more difficult than a novel evidence category. [Jurek](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/428/262/). Franklin later upheld the mechanism for prison-conduct evidence, and Saffle distinguishes exclusion of mitigation from every requested direction about its use. Those later opinions may illuminate the dispute; they cannot be inserted into 1984 as its controlling law. [Franklin](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/487/164/), [Saffle](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/494/484/).

**Recommended reasoning.** Retain Stone's proposed relief if he explains why Jurek's facial approval depended on a real avenue for the particular mitigating force and why Eddings had already barred the functional exclusion shown here. Identify that force concretely: reduced culpability from youth or family circumstances may coexist with deliberate conduct and a prediction of future danger. Merely permitting the jury to hear the evidence, or use one aspect of it against future dangerousness, need not allow its full legally relevant mitigating use. Distinguish Franklin's prison-behavior evidence and Saffle's request concerning sympathy. The contemporaneous competing analyses show that this is a disputed application of Teague, not an uncontested statement of old law. [Graham record and arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/461/).

**Boundary and administration.** Do not demand a free-floating mercy instruction whenever evidence has some imaginable independent significance. Examine the submitted evidence, actual questions, instructions and arguments for a reasonable likelihood that a material mitigating ground lacked a lawful route to a noncapital sentence. A supplemental instruction or sentencing mechanism can supply that route without prescribing weight. If Stone instead concludes that this requires a new procedural rule, he cannot relabel it substantive merely because the remedy removes death; he must confront Teague rather than award relief by assertion. The defensible recommended disposition remains conditional sentencing relief through a lawful corrective proceeding, preserving guilt and other valid custody. Nothing here adjudicates a categorical ban on executing seventeen-year-olds or supplies an adopted holding for later Johnson.

## 23. Zafiro v. United States

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** The revised proposal correctly distinguishes Garcia, Soto and Martinez's preserved severance claims from Gloria Zafiro's omitted appellate claim. That is a concrete application of Master §3's petitioner-specific posture discipline. Her testimony can matter to Martinez's claim without reviving hers.

**Governing law.** Rules 8(b) and 14 distinguish proper joinder from prejudicial joint trial. Richardson recognizes the ordinary efficacy of instructions; Bruton identifies a specific risk they may not cure. Mutually accusatory defenses do not themselves shift the Government's burden or require separate trials. Youakim's nonreview practice is prudential rather than jurisdictional. [Supreme Court record](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/534/), [appellate presentation and factual record](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/945/881/289406/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain the petitioner-specific affirmance. Resolve the three preserved claims under abuse-of-discretion review and leave Gloria's judgment undisturbed on the stated review-scope ground. The practical inquiry asks whether a particular trial right or reliable individualized verdict faced an uncured serious risk; severance, redaction, or an effective instruction remains available depending on that risk. Do not treat instructions as invariably curative or absence of a constitutional violation as the only possible Rule 14 concern. On the supplied record, no uncured risk requiring separate trials is demonstrated. Stone's fair-trial commitment needs careful application of the existing standard, not a per se antagonistic-defense rule or an invented hearing on Gloria's omitted issue.

## 24. Spectrum Sports v. McQuillan

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject attempted-monopolization liability without proof of dangerous probability in a relevant market. This coheres with Master §4 and Markets and Private Power: injury to a distributor or competitor is not automatically the statutory market injury.

**Governing law.** Sherman Act §2 and Swift require dangerous probability of successful monopolization; Copperweld distinguishes unilateral conduct threatening monopoly from other restraints. The Ninth Circuit's Lessig approach cannot replace this element with an inference of bad intent. The undifferentiated verdict makes the legal error material even though other theories might remain available. [Business record, instructions, and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/447/).

**Recommendation and administration.** Retain reversal and remand for proceedings under the proper elements and any preserved independent basis. Require anticompetitive conduct, specific monopolizing intent, and a dangerous probability demonstrated through the relevant competitive market, alternatives, entry barriers and the defendant's position. Circumstantial evidence may support multiple elements but cannot abolish one. Avoid a numerical share threshold or immunity for all small firms; neither is needed to make market-power analysis workable. Stone may enforce other valid contract, tort, or statutory obligations through their own elements, but cannot preserve this verdict by guessing which sufficient theory the jury adopted. This is a correction to the lower court's framework from controlling antitrust sources, not a new Stone doctrine.

## 25. Lockhart v. Fretwell

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Refine substantially; retain the conditional remand.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone retains ordinary Strickland prejudice but corrects the assumption that Eighth Circuit precedent bound the Arkansas court. That fits his effective-assistance commitment and Master §§3–4. His protection of competent advocacy is sound; the difficult issue is the legal time frame for the prejudice counterfactual, not simply whether the trial judge might have followed Collins.

**Governing law and strongest objection.** Strickland requires a reasonable probability of a different result but also excludes an advantage depending on an arbitrary or lawless decisionmaker. [Strickland](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/466/668/). Nix rejects prejudice from inability to present perjury; Kimmelman requires a meritorious underlying Fourth Amendment objection. [Kimmelman](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/477/365/). Arkansas therefore has a serious argument that an objection now known to be legally unsound cannot establish the protected disadvantage. The record also identifies an erroneous assumption of binding circuit authority; Collins's subsequent rejection does not itself decide how a conscientious state court would have treated a timely objection at the original sentencing. [Fretwell record and contemporary arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/364/).

**Recommended rule and rationale.** Preserve Stone's result but characterize it as resolving an unsettled temporal application of Strickland, not an obvious consequence of a purely statistical outcome test. A competent, legally supportable argument under then-existing authority is different from perjury or a request for nullification. Stone can conclude that later legal change does not retroactively convert the lost opportunity for such advocacy into an unprotected windfall. The limit is that the argument must have been reasonably available for lawful adoption then; an imagined favorable mistake contrary to binding law does not count. Apply the ordinary reasonable-probability burden to the whole sentencing setting, without either treating Collins as binding or demanding certainty that the objection would succeed.

**Administration and remedy.** Remand to reassess whether the district court's probability finding survives removal of the hierarchy error. Identify then-binding state and Supreme Court law, the persuasive legal argument, and the actual sentencing consequences; do not speculate about a particular judge's tendency to disregard law. This keeps the counterfactual manageable and avoids a universal additional fairness hurdle that would unsettle routine Strickland claims. If prejudice is established, issue conditional sentencing relief. A new proceeding follows applicable law and independent limits on renewed sentencing, rather than automatically awarding the sentence once obtainable under Collins or freely reviving an aggravator already rejected. The proposal changes neither current substantive aggravator law nor the proof standard.

## 26. Bufferd v. Commissioner

**Date:** January 25, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would apply the shareholder's return and extension to the shareholder's assessment. Master §18 expressly leaves tax procedure to its native statutory architecture; no artificial-entity theory or distributive preference is needed.

**Governing law.** Section 6501's assessment period concerns the return of the taxpayer whose liability is assessed. The S corporation's information return supplies data but does not become the shareholder's own return. The shareholder's valid extension therefore need not depend upon a duplicate extension by the corporation. [Assessment record and statutory provisions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/506/523/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain affirmance of the assessment against the asserted corporate-return time bar. Identify the individual taxpayer, return, relevant period, and valid extension. This provides a predictable filing-based rule and respects limitations as enacted rather than treating government audit convenience as a freestanding exception. Stone should not announce that entity-level filings can never control other assessments: Congress can create integrated audit and limitation regimes, and any such regime applies according to its effective date and terms. Nothing in this record justifies a new equitable extension of tax liability or a different rule of repose. Existing law sufficiently explains both the result and the practical limit.

## 28. United States v. Nachtigal

**Date:** February 22, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would reject a jury entitlement under this penalty package while retaining Blanton's rebuttable presumption. That does not conflict with his strong protection of criminal juries: his Criminal Procedure domain protects the jury where the constitutional entitlement attaches, rather than abolishing the established petty-offense category.

**Governing law.** Blanton presumes an offense carrying no more than six months' incarceration petty but allows sufficiently severe additional authorized penalties to rebut that presumption. Classification depends on authorized exposure, not merely the sentence selected. Probation is not itself the equivalent of several years in prison. [Penalty scheme and Blanton application](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/507/1/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal of the jury-trial ruling, evaluating the fine and available probation conditions together with the incarceration ceiling. Do not make six months an irrebuttable rule or say that substantial financial and regulatory sanctions can never establish seriousness. A different future package could require another application, but the present one does not demonstrate a defect warranting replacement of Blanton. Administrability comes from objective authorized penalties and the existing severity inquiry. No new trial follows on this jury ground; any unrelated challenge remains governed by its own posture and law.

## 30. Growe v. Emison

**Date:** February 23, 1993. **Verdict: Retain, clarifying the limited extension of Gingles.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would respect timely state judicial redistricting while rejecting an unsupported federal minority district. This fits Federalism, Elections, and Master §4: a State's responsibility includes its courts, and demographics alone do not establish statutory dilution.

**Governing law.** Germano and Reynolds recognize the State's primary opportunity to redistrict subject to federal requirements. Gingles supplies size/compactness, cohesion, and majority-bloc-voting predicates for its dilution setting. Applying the functional predicates to fragmentation in single-member districts is an extension to the presented mechanism; the draft properly identifies it, rather than treating every detail as already decided. **Recommendation and further room.** Retain reversal and remand. The federal court may retain jurisdiction and act if a genuine election deadline or proved federal violation requires it, but a legislative deadline does not automatically terminate ongoing adequate state judicial work. On §2, Stone should leave a coalition claim legally possible when the groups demonstrate actual political cohesion and common bloc opposition. That is consistent with his Elections domain without aggregating people solely by race or promising proportional representation. Ordinary election evidence may prove the mechanism; no single statistical method is universally mandatory. A challenge to the superseded plan does not prove its replacement invalid. ## 32. Fex v. Michigan

**Date:** February 23, 1993. **Verdict: Revise as a preferred, legally available reading; the present position is defensible, not compelled.**

**Existing position and tension.** Stone measures the 180-day period from actual receipt by prosecutor and court. That respects the named recipients and gives an easily documented date. It is not a direct repudiation of his textualism. But the draft understates the competing textual reading: Article III(b) makes the custodian the prisoner's prescribed transmission channel. His statutory protection can otherwise remain suspended by the official required to activate it, engaging Master §§2 and 15's concern with protections defeated by their operation.

**Legal basis for going further.** Article III(a)'s causation wording can attach the clock to the prisoner's completed action under III(b), while the custodian supplies the certificate and promptly forwards the request. The opposing actual-receipt construction has serious support in the designated recipients, required return receipts, and the prospect of a prosecutor losing time before learning of the request. The contemporary judicial texts expressly recognize interpretive uncertainty; this is not a choice between statutory language and compassion. [Statutory text, dates, and competing interpretations](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/507/43/). Carchman confirms the Agreement's specific detainer procedure and purpose but does not settle this trigger. [Carchman](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/473/716/).

**Recommended rule and extent.** I would have Stone adopt the custodial-delivery reading: once the prisoner gives the responsible custodian the written notice and final-disposition request required by Article III(b), forwarding delay attributable to the statutory conduit does not defer commencement. This is resolution of an open compact question, not overruling an entering Supreme Court actual-receipt holding. It better joins the prisoner's duty, the official transmission duty, and the statutory protection. No negligence finding, arbitrary new grace period, or independent constitutional speedy-trial rule is needed.

**Administration and disposition.** Determine the custodian's receipt from contemporaneous forms, logs, correspondence, or ordinary factfinding. The prisoner must establish the qualifying invocation; the State may establish applicable statutory exclusions or authorized continuances. The record identifies trial 196 days after custodial delivery and 177 after receipt by the prosecutor and court. Reverse and remand to apply the earlier date, with dismissal with prejudice under Article V(c) if no lawful exclusion prevents expiration. Do not resolve the separately unaddressed question or invent damages for the transmission delay. This recommendation goes further than the draft within a plausible text-and-structure interpretation; Stone could retain actual receipt only after squarely answering that alternative.

## 33. United States v. Dunnigan

**Date:** February 23, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would permit an obstruction enhancement for independently found willful, material perjury. This protects the functioning trial while refusing to treat an unsuccessful defense as automatically false testimony. It fits Master §17 and Criminal Procedure's distinction between protected testimony and deliberate corruption of evidence.

**Governing law.** Section 3C1.1, Grayson, and Nix permit appropriate sentencing consequences for perjury; the right to testify includes no right to lie. The predicate requires material falsity and willfulness, excluding confusion, faulty recollection and mistake. Findings must encompass those elements but need not mechanically repeat a prescribed verbal formula. [Sentencing findings and authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/507/87/).

**Recommendation and extent.** Retain reversal of the categorical constitutional prohibition and remand consistent with valid independent findings. Explain why the identified testimony, rather than merely the guilty verdict, establishes the relevant predicate. Do not transform the necessary independent determination into a requirement of a separate perjury prosecution or special heightened proof burden absent a legal basis. Stone's broader preferred jury rule for facts expanding legally authorized punishment does not automatically enter this differently presented challenge; any such claim would require its own preservation, then-governing sentencing law, and candid proposed change. This record permits fair, reviewable application of the existing guideline without a new right-to-testify framework.

## 34. Negonsott v. Samuels

**Date:** February 24, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would recognize Kansas jurisdiction under the specific congressional grant while reserving federal and tribal authority. That is consistent with Tribal Sovereignty: retained sovereignty is source-bound, and a clear congressional allocation cannot be defeated by a general territorial slogan.

**Governing law.** Section 3243 affirmatively grants Kansas jurisdiction over covered reservation offenses and preserves federal jurisdiction; §1153 supplies federal major-crime jurisdiction. Identify the person, land, offense and Kansas-specific statute; do not derive authority from the State's general police power or from a presumed disappearance of tribal sovereignty. Indian-law canons remain relevant to genuine ambiguity, but are not a power to delete clear jurisdictional text. No holding on the separate extent of tribal jurisdiction, double jeopardy, or other conviction claims is needed. The rule is predictably bounded by the enactment's territorial and subject-matter scope and creates no national expansion of state authority. There is no demonstrated framework failure that would justify Stone going further in this case.

## 35. United States v. A Parcel of Land Known as 92 Buena Vista Avenue

**Date:** February 24, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone would permit a donee to invoke the statutory innocent-owner defense and require its adjudication before using relation back to defeat it. This coheres with Property, Takings, and Forfeiture and Master §§4 and 15: a statutory title mechanism should not automatically erase the defense Congress expressly supplied.

**Governing law.** Section 881(a)(6) protects the defined innocent-owner interest without limiting owners to purchasers for value. Section 881(h)'s relation-back provision must be read with that defense; Stowell explains condemnation's role in perfecting relation back. Eligibility, absence of knowledge or consent, and the Government's forfeiture showing are different questions. [Ownership record and statutory arguments](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/507/111/).

**Recommendation and further room.** Retain the eligibility ruling and remand. Make explicit that this is harmonization of enacted provisions and the existing title mechanism, not a general constitutional immunity for all innocent owners. Stone's stronger concerns about forfeiture incentives and process would require their own presented claim and record. The practical rule is straightforward: gift status does not disqualify an actual owner, but the claimant must establish the statutory defense under the then-applicable burden. Do not conclusively determine the temporal knowledge rule beyond what eligibility requires or import later forfeiture reforms. Effective statutory protection is already available without changing constitutional forfeiture doctrine.

## 36. Voinovich v. Quilter

**Date:** March 2, 1993. **Verdict: Retain.**

**Existing position and fit.** Stone rejects the proposition that a State may create majority-minority districts only when a court could compel them under §2, and separately remands the population-equality issue. This is especially faithful to Master §4 and Elections: statutory duty, statutory permission, constitutional injury and a federal court's remedial power are distinct propositions.

**Governing law.** Gingles requires the relevant dilution mechanism; the accepted absence of the necessary majority-bloc-voting showing defeats the presented influence theory without deciding all influence claims. Attention to federal obligations and opponents' speculation do not by themselves establish forbidden racial purpose. Reynolds, Mahan and Brown make deviations above ten percent prima facie problematic, requiring justification rather than automatic invalidity. [Plan record and applicable authorities](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/507/146/).

**Recommendation and administration.** Retain reversal and remand. Assess the actual county-boundary policy, fit, and magnitude of departures under the inherited justification burden. Do not direct a preferred racial map or pretend that the current record already proves population deviations valid. Stone has room to preserve state inclusion efforts by rejecting an invented necessity burden, without deciding a Fourteenth Amendment racial-classification claim never presented here. His later-position rejection of racial attention as injury is therefore unnecessary to this disposition. Any effect of Growe depends on an actually adopted and effective simulated holding, not that case's proposed Stone supplement. Ordinary statutory evidence, purpose review and population analysis suffice; no new combined equality test is justified.

## Prioritized findings for synthesis

1. **Fex is the clearest additional opportunity to go further.** The prisoner's statutory conduit and causation wording support a more protective, administrable custodial-delivery trigger. Recommend changing the proposed disposition to reversal/remand under that reading; explicitly distinguish a legally available preferred interpretation from a compelled result or a clear violation of Stone's master.
2. **Herrera is the strongest justified constitutional extension already proposed.** Keep freestanding capital innocence and bounded remand, but explicitly ground the substantive Teague extension, justify the new burden, separate hearing/stay/exhaustion gates, and state that proven noncommission prohibits execution rather than merely requiring resentencing.
3. **Graham and Fretwell require the most careful reasoning repair.** Graham must answer Jurek, the significance of finality in 1984, and the difference between excluded mitigating effect and general mercy. Fretwell must reconcile the temporal counterfactual with Strickland's exclusion of lawless advantages, beyond simply correcting circuit/state hierarchy. Their proposed outcomes remain legally defensible when these steps are supplied.
4. **Hadley remains conditional.** The exact grant is not authenticated by the short published procedural order. Its tentative affirmance also needs a concrete nonpropensity inference and an honest Rule 403 assessment after the offer not to contest intent. **The Louisiana decree also remains conditional** on authenticating the actual joint coordinate annex; this review certifies no geographic line.
5. **Most other proposals already preserve Stone's principles through existing law.** Particularly strong examples include Soldal, the narrow Bray source separation, Rowland, Crosby, the jurisdictional cases, and the distinct analyses in Growe/Voinovich. Going further in those cases usually means clearer application or retaining an open claim, not inventing additional burdens, rights, or remedies.
