**Stone’s Proposed Judgment or Disposition:** VACATE the Eighth Circuit’s judgment and REMAND for application of ordinary Strickland prejudice without treating circuit precedent as binding on the state court.

**Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** The remand must determine whether the existing finding of a likely sustained objection remains supported independent of the erroneous hierarchy. ##### Issue 1: Prejudice and the relevant legal counterfactual

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the preserved issue within the review posture stated in Section I.

**Stone’s Legal Position and Treatment of Precedent:** Apply Strickland’s received two-part standard while rejecting the proposed additional freestanding entitlement screen; correct the lower court’s mistaken hierarchy. No new burden or proof quantum.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Later rejection of a legal argument does not automatically erase prejudice from counsel’s deficient failure to present it, but prejudice cannot be proved by falsely treating circuit precedent as binding on a state court.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone rejects both an extra universal fairness hurdle after Strickland and an automatic entitlement to the result of persuasive circuit authority. A competent objection under an existing judicial interpretation is not perjury. The correct counterfactual requires a legally supportable probability that the state proceeding would have differed. Because the appellate rationale conflated probability with compulsory obedience, remand is needed rather than invention of the missing finding.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** No revival of Collins as current substantive law and no presumption that every omitted argument changes the outcome.

##### Issue 2: Source and scope of habeas remedy

**Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the remedial issue only to define the consequence of a supported prejudice finding on remand.

**Stone’s Legal Position and Treatment of Precedent:** Apply and explain existing sources; do not announce a general replacement doctrine.

**Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Any established Sixth Amendment violation here warrants a valid replacement sentencing proceeding, not an automatic federal award of the sentence counsel might once have obtained under Collins.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Stone preserves the difference between recognizing injury at the original proceeding and choosing law for a new proceeding. Lawful state sentencing choices remain available unless a separately established rule forecloses them.

**Boundary or Reserved Question:** No judicial invention of new aggravating findings; no treatment of this remand as an authorization to execute before the constitutional claim is resolved.
