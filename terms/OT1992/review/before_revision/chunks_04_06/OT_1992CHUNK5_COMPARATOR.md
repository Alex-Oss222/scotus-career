## Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case:** Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins; No. 91-1600; 1993-04-20. This supplement is introduced only after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. It is not simulated precedent and supplies no Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** VACATED AND REMANDED, 9–0.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor delivered the unanimous opinion. Kennedy concurred, joined by Rehnquist and Thomas.

**Historical Holding:** Age and years of service are analytically distinct; pension interference alone does not establish ADEA disparate treatment, but proxy or dual-motive evidence can matter. ## Withrow v. Williams

## CSX Transportation, Inc. v. Easterwood

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case:** CSX Transportation, Inc. v. Easterwood; Nos. 91-790 and 91-1206; 1993-04-21. This supplement is introduced only after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. It is not simulated precedent and supplies no Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** AFFIRMED. Parts I and II were unanimous; the application in Parts III and IV drew a 7–2 division.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for the Court. Rehnquist, Blackmun, Stevens, O’Connor, Scalia, and Kennedy joined Parts III and IV. Thomas concurred in part and dissented in part, joined by Souter.
