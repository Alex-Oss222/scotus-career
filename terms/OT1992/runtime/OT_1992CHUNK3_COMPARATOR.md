## Voinovich v. Quilter

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case:** Voinovich v. Quilter; No. 91-1618; 1993-03-02. This supplement is introduced only after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. It is not simulated precedent and supplies no Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** REVERSED AND REMANDED, 9–0.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor delivered the unanimous opinion.

**Historical Holding:** Section 2 does not categorically prohibit majority-minority districts, and the plaintiffs failed the necessary bloc-voting showing. The discriminatory-purpose finding was clearly erroneous. The population departures required evaluation of Ohio’s county-boundary justification rather than automatic invalidation.
