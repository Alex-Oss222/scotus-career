## Farrar v. Hobby

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case:** Farrar v. Hobby; No. 91-990; 1992-12-14. This supplement is introduced only after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. It is not simulated precedent and supplies no Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed the denial of fees, 5 to 4 on disposition; the separate opinion agreed that nominal relief established prevailing-party status.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Scalia, and Kennedy. O’Connor concurred separately. White, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, and Souter, concurred in part and dissented in part, favoring remand on amount.

**Historical Holding:** Nominal-damages recipients are prevailing parties, but the degree of success controls the fee. On this record the Court held that the reasonable fee was zero.

**Historical Authority:** 506 U.S. 103 (1992); a historical Supreme Court merits decision. Its authority in the historical legal system does not make it an effective decision of this reboot.
