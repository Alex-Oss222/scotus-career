## Material relevance of the completed earlier law

**Gottshall, June 24.** The actual Court adopts the FELA zone-of-danger boundary, orders first-instance application to Gottshall, and requires judgment for Conrail in Carlisle. Its distinction between a disputed summary-judgment record and an established finding is consistent with the Turner freeze's treatment of contested carriage evidence, but it creates no First Amendment evidence rule. The choice of a substantive FELA injury category is not a rule requiring physical danger before any constitutional injury. Ginsburg's dissent, joined by Blackmun and Stevens, requires serious, substantiated direct employee injury and ordinary negligence predicates; it does not make reported educational distress either legally irrelevant or a constitutional command to create a district. Souter's common-law-development concurrence is confined to the FELA question. No Turner or Kiryas vote changes. [Read public source](../records/Consolidated_Rail_Corp_v_Gottshall_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Davis, June 24.** The actual five-Justice mandatory neutral-clarification rule, unanimous lawful-clarification application and unanimous clear-request protections concern postwaiver custodial counsel. The decision distinguishes the credited record from new appellate factfinding and preserves its military and statutory scope limits. It supplies no speech-content classification, educational accommodation entitlement, or general rule that every uncertain legal issue must receive a new procedural safeguard. The four-Justice no-duty position is noncontrolling; neither it nor the majority supplies a reason to alter these two commitments. [Read public source](../records/Davis_v_United_States_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Dolan, June 24.** The actual individualized-permit rule requires an essential nexus and a government showing of rough proportionality in the nature and extent of identified property dedications. Reasonable estimates suffice; exact equivalence and unique attribution are not required. That property-exaction rule does not replace O'Brien/Ward with a station-by-station imminent-insolvency or individualized-dedication requirement for a generally enacted cable law. The public opinion expressly reserves generally applicable legislative conditions. Stevens's dissent, joined by Blackmun and Ginsburg, and Souter's separate dissent accept the supported planning record in their distinct exaction analyses; those positions do not compel affirmance of a cable judgment with different asserted interests and evidentiary disputes. The original Turner commitments already preserve reasonable prediction and reject scientific-certainty, least-restrictive-means and every-station-failure requirements. They therefore remain coherent. Dolan creates no new establishment rule. [Read public source](../records/Dolan_v_City_of_Tigard_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Heck, June 24.** The actual rule is the bounded favorable-invalidation prerequisite for an in-custody damages claim necessarily negating an outstanding conviction, with deferred accrual and preserved independent injuries. Its distinct rationale coalitions remain distinct. Neither present challenge seeks such conviction-invalidating damages, so Heck changes no threshold, remedy or merits component. Its refusal to manufacture a preserved independent-injury count from a late reply is consistent with preserving the actual presentation limits here; it does not remove the expressly presented Lemon request or establish that every requested theory must be reached. [Read public source](../records/Heck_v_Humphrey_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Honda, June 24.** The actual eight-Justice decision requires meaningful judicial examination and lawful correction of punitive amount where Oregon removed that safeguard without an adequate replacement. It does not require independent appellate reweighing, a universal written-opinion checklist, or a substantive numerical rule. Its institutional-review holding neither makes legislative cable findings conclusive nor establishes a new constitutional standard for every predictive enactment. Ginsburg's whole-process dissent concerns the combined trial safeguards for punitive awards; it is not a position that courts must defer to Congress on compulsory carriage or to the Legislature on denominational allocation. The frozen speech and religion grounds remain unchanged. [Read public source](../records/Honda_Motor_Co_v_Oberg_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Shannon, June 24.** The actual six-Justice decision finds no statutory or general supervisory duty to explain insanity-verdict commitment consequences, while preserving appropriate accurate correction of a particular misleading trial event. It distinguishes permission from command and refuses to infer the jury's actual beliefs. It creates no First Amendment rule and no entitlement to a district based on a predicted response to public schooling. Blackmun and Stevens's published dissent supports a bounded requested explanation and conditional Rule 52(a) remedy, not a general power to invent statutory duties or facts. No commitment changes. [Read public source](../records/Shannon_v_United_States_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).

**Thomas Jefferson University, June 24.** The actual Court applies the existing own-regulation standard to a textually permissible redistribution interpretation and requires reliance on the agency's discernible actual ground. It preserves contrary text, genuine inconsistency and required explanation; it does not create a general deference test or proof burden. The Turner challenge concerns Congress's enactment and First Amendment validity, not simply the permissible meaning of an agency's own regulation. The current home-shopping implementing determination remains a separate source issue if a final ground actually relies on it. Kennedy, Blackmun, Scalia and Souter's joins here do not decide their Turner classification, and Thomas, Stevens, O'Connor and Ginsburg's dissent does not establish strict scrutiny for all communications rules. No constitutional rule in Kiryas is changed. [Read public source](../records/Thomas_Jefferson_University_v_Shalala_merits_1994-06-24.md#public-projection).
