## Kokkonen

The official report's predecision account, 511 U.S. 376–377, identifies Guardian Life as the sole respondent; the two affiliated corporations listed below had been dismissed before trial. Guardian removed the state-law suit to the Eastern District of California on diversity grounds and asserted state-law counterclaims. Settlement occurred after closing arguments but before jury instructions; the agreement was recited in chambers on the record. The parties executed a Rule 41(a)(1)(ii) stipulation dismissing complaint and cross-complaint with prejudice; the judge signed on April 13, 1992. The stipulation/order neither mentioned the agreement nor retained enforcement jurisdiction. The later dispute concerned return of files. Guardian moved to enforce on May 21; the district court's August 19, 1992 order asserted inherent power. Kokkonen's appeal relied solely on subject-matter jurisdiction. The Ninth Circuit's unpublished April 27, 1993 disposition, reported at 993 F.2d 883, affirmed by relying on Wilkinson v. FBI, 922 F.2d 555, 557 (1991), and its inherent supervisory-power formulation. The complete unpublished memorandum and district order are not independently recovered. The source account supports the narrow asserted-jurisdiction issue, not an adjudication of breach, Rule 60 relief or an independent new-action jurisdictional basis.

## Beecham and Jones

The official report's predecision account, 511 U.S. 370, reports Beecham's 1979 federal Tennessee conviction under §922(h), and Jones's prior federal Ohio conviction in 1971 for interstate transportation of a stolen automobile, plus two West Virginia state convictions (breaking and entering; forgery). Jones's West Virginia rights restoration removed his state convictions from consideration; only the effect on his federal conviction is the presented question. Beecham claimed restoration under Tennessee law. The district courts accepted state restoration on different geographical theories: place of Beecham's earlier crime versus Jones's residence when committing the later §922(g) offense. The Fourth Circuit reversed each district ruling. Jones's published opinion is available in full in chunk4-sources/jones-lower.txt, 993 F.2d 1131; Beecham's judgment order is reported at 993 F.2d 1539. Do not describe both appellate dispositions as Supreme Court judgments of guilt or erase surviving proceedings. The complete Beecham table memorandum is not independently recovered.
