## 3. Harris v. Forklift Systems, Inc.

**Case Name:** Harris v. Forklift Systems, Inc.

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 17 (1993); No. 92-1168

**Historical Decision Date:** 1993-11-09

Comparator only. Historical evidence for reconciliation after the modeling freeze; not simulated precedent or a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9–0.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for a unanimous Court. Scalia and Ginsburg filed separate concurrences.

## 7. United States v. James Daniel Good Real Property

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** United States v. James Daniel Good Real Property

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 43 (1993); No. 92-1180

**Historical Decision Date:** 1993-12-13

Comparator only. Historical evidence for reconciliation after the modeling freeze; not simulated precedent or a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded. Pre-seizure due process: 5–4. Statutory timeliness: unanimous.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote Parts I and III for all nine and Parts II and IV for Blackmun, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and himself. Rehnquist, joined by Scalia and partly by O’Connor, dissented in part; O’Connor and Thomas also wrote separately.

**Historical Holding:** Notice and hearing are ordinarily required before civil seizure of real property absent exigency. A timely action is not dismissible merely for noncompliance with §§1602–1604.

**Historical Authority:** Mathews, Doehr, Soldal, Calero-Toledo, Montalvo-Murillo, and the then-operative forfeiture statutes; 510 U.S. 48–65.

**Historical Sources:** [R03](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/43/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.

## 12. Thunder Basin Coal Co. v. Reich

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Thunder Basin Coal Co. v. Reich

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 200 (1994); No. 92-896

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-01-19

Comparator only. Historical evidence for reconciliation after the modeling freeze; not simulated precedent or a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9–0 in judgment.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for seven Justices in full. Scalia and Thomas joined except Parts III-B, IV, and V; Scalia wrote a concurrence in part and in the judgment joined by Thomas.

**Historical Holding:** The Mine Act precluded district-court jurisdiction over this pre-enforcement challenge. Its statutory scheme supplied meaningful review; this record did not show a serious prehearing deprivation requiring a different route.

**Historical Authority:** Block, Abbott Laboratories, McNary, and the Mine Act review provisions; 510 U.S. 207–218. The historical case later became a channeling authority, but its own formulation is not predecision law.

**Historical Sources:** [R06](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/200/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.
