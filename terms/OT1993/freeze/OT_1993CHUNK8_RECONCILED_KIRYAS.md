## Entering-law reconciliation where the historical report differs

**Lemon, Lee and Lamb's Chapel.** Lemon remains governing law. Actual Lee supplies independent school-sponsorship and adolescent-pressure holdings with separate coalitions; it does not replace establishment doctrine. Actual Lamb's Chapel applies Lemon to nonsponsored equal private access, with Kennedy's separate refusal of the formulation and Scalia/Thomas's separate neutral-accommodation rationale. Thus a report reference to a supposed wholesale abandonment of Lemon is not a description of entering law. Blackmun's adherence, O'Connor's more contextual proposal, Kennedy's narrower refusal to rely on Lemon, and Scalia/Thomas's tradition proposal remain distinct present positions. A decision applying a particular denominational-neutrality limit need not decide the test's whole future.

**Zobrest.** The actual case remanded on antecedent statutory and funding questions and decided no Establishment Clause merits. The historical report's references to a holding approving the interpreter cannot be used as a controlling premise by O'Connor, Kennedy, Scalia or Thomas, or by anyone else. The comparison shows that this citation is corroborative to their accommodation arguments, not indispensable: Amos, Wolman, Walz, Zorach, Witters and surviving equal-citizenship authorities supply the narrower propositions each actually needs. The missing historical Zobrest merits holding does not itself support a new judgment here. No broad religious-aid approval is smuggled into current law.

**Shaw.** Actual Shaw rejects the design-only racial territorial equal-protection claim absent an independently cognizable deprivation and preserves the elements of other constitutional claims. It supplies no controlling rule that race-conscious districting alone is unconstitutional. Kennedy's historical citation to Shaw, 512 U.S. 728–729, is therefore unavailable in that role. His frozen deliberate-religious-classification position remains supported independently by the Establishment Clause's prohibition on assigning civil authority by religious identity, Larson, Larkin's underlying boundary, actual Lukumi's objective neutrality principle and his preserved distinction between voluntary religious association and the State's own religious classification. Wright's Douglas dissent may express an analogy but is not a Court holding. No reverse inference from the changed Shaw result permits governmental religious establishment. Conversely, Kiryas's distinct religion rule cannot silently overrule actual Shaw or create a racial-predominance test.

This is a real difference in the precedential foundation of Kennedy's historical analogy, expressly accounted for rather than suppressed. It does not require changing the supported narrow religion judgment: the neutral freeze already stated it as an independent Establishment Clause ground without relying on a historical Shaw majority. Blackmun, Stevens and Souter's support for actual Shaw is likewise compatible with treating denominational civil-power allocation as an independently forbidden establishment, rather than holding all territorial design an injury. No judicially determined religious exclusivity of voting, permanent demographic composition or compelled religious observance is invented.

**Actual earlier OT1993 law.** Carter does not constitutionalize parental placement choices. Ladue and MCI do not decide denominational allocation. June 24 Gottshall, Davis, Dolan, Heck, Honda, Shannon and Thomas Jefferson University provide no new establishment rule, standing bar, or special-district entitlement. ## Justice-specific reconciliation

### Blackmun

Also accept Stevens's separate state-supported religious-separation rationale. Preserve his own express defense of Lemon's general validity, 512 U.S. 710–711. **Historical comparison.** The original judgment and civil-power ground are confirmed. The report makes the additional joins and Lemon explanation precise. His Larkin join, Amos concurrence adopting substantially O'Connor's reasons, actual Lee sponsorship opinion and Lamb's Chapel join all remain effective, Justice-specific support. No changed law or record supports a departure from the historical result or exact operative grounds.

**Strongest contrary ground and response.** The public character of the school, secular teaching, lay election and sincere educational need make the accommodation argument serious. Those features do not answer his objection to the legislature's deliberate choice of a denomination as recipient of continuing general authority. Larkin's direct church veto is not the same fact pattern, but its civil/religious-power boundary informs his analogy; ordinary religious citizenship remains protected by McDaniel. Amos permits general accommodation, not every exceptional allocation. The availability of ordinary local services matters without becoming a requirement to select the least expensive or least separate education program.

**Join barrier and remedy.** No assertion that the board is legally a church, no bar on religious officeholders or all homogeneous districts, and no premise that religious facilitation is invariably forbidden. Affirmance leaves Chapter 748 invalid and preserves children's statutory eligibility. The minimum compatible formulation identifies the State's allocation, preserves neutral accommodation, and does not declare Lemon abandoned.

Additionally explain that affirmative state action arranging this religious separation supports religious attachment, unlike evenhanded public service, voluntary off-site release time, or exemption from a burdensome general rule. Blackmun and Ginsburg are supported joiners in that separate proposition; no one else is inferred. The report's separate writing is at 512 U.S. 711–712.

His Larkin and Amos joins prevent equating every benefit to religion with establishment. His actual Lee sponsorship/pressure and Souter-concurrence joins distinguish public religious advancement from private choices; Lamb's Chapel and Carter preserve equal access and education remedies. **Strongest contrary ground and response.** Secular instruction, outsider staff, public accountability and the parents' real concerns are substantial. The public interest in tolerance supplies his suggested alternative perspective; it is not a remedial order to enroll every child in a prescribed integrated class. **Join barrier and remedy.** Preserve the distinctions among public support, private education and neutral services. ### O'Connor

**Judgment and exact joins.** Affirm; join I, II-B, II-C and III, but not the Part II introduction or II-A Larkin/fusion analysis. Her own Parts I–IV, 512 U.S. 712–722, explain equality, lawful accommodation and a less unitary analytical method. The neutral freeze's limited equality ground is confirmed; it must not be relabeled a full join in the delegation theory.

**Sufficient ground and strongest counterargument.** Absent the most unusual circumstances, legal rights and benefits should not depend on which faith someone follows. Accommodation may relieve a deeply held belief without discriminating by sect. This particular law benefits the selected group because of its religious identity; unlike the village's incorporation under general law, the exceptional legislative route gives no dependable neutral terms for similarly situated groups. She acknowledges the close possibility that this group alone presently needs the accommodation and that the legislature may act without favoritism. The obstacle is structural: a legislature need not decide a later request, and nonaction due to competing business or lack of sponsorship ordinarily yields no reviewable merits response. Her conclusion is not a finding that another community has been refused.

**Permissible narrower choices.** All-village district authority, or neutral eligibility criteria administered through reviewable decisions, could permit a district even for a village deliberately assembled by coreligionists. Neither proposal is mandated. Do not impose a general rule that every lawful accommodation must be enacted in a broad statute. Her objection concerns the enacted sect-specific benefit and absence of neutral assurance here, with history and context informing that assessment.

**Lemon and aid precedents.** Her actual Lee and Lamb's Chapel joins remain. She nevertheless favors freeing establishment analysis from a single rigid Lemon formula and using more precise context-specific rules; abandoning its unitary framework need not abandon its valid insights. Do not portray this as an affirmative vote that every Lemon-derived holding must be overruled. She also maintains her Aguilar dissent and would reconsider that decision in a proper case so neutral special education could be provided at sectarian premises on equal terms. That is a future noncontrolling position, not a present holding authorizing the terminated program; actual Zobrest supplies no merits approval.

**Justice-specific support, limits and remedy.** Larkin join, Amos concurrence's burden/endorsement distinction, actual Lee neutrality-concurrence join and Carter opinion support equal accommodation while preserving this boundary. Religious citizenship and secular education defeat a categorical disability, but not the actual allocation defect. Affirm Chapter 748's invalidation; preserve services and general-law incorporation. Minimum compatible text separates the equality ground from the unjoined fusion theory and retains her methodological reservation. No material departure is adopted.

### Scalia

Leave the expressly reserved state constitutional issue to the state courts. No threshold dismissal or judgment declaring every aspect of the district valid is invented.

**Sufficient grounds.** The State provided a public, secular, normally regulated school district to address a distinctive educational and cultural problem. Larkin transferred authority to religious institutions as such; this law transfers it to public voters, with no religious office or voting requirement. McDaniel protects religious citizens from political disabilities. Secular need and the existing geographic village boundary explain the choice without a finding of denominational favoritism. Denominational favoritism is forbidden, but a case-specific law is not invalid solely because the legislature does not announce every future accommodation in advance.

**Strongest contrary ground and response.** The act's breadth, permanent public powers and known religious homogeneity support the majority's concern. Other special districts illustrate that legislatures can solve special problems; this reconciliation does not assert an unverified new district-history finding as an independent basis. The absence of an actual comparable denial is therefore significant to him, unlike the majority's concern with the structure in advance. Kennedy's boundary rule does not answer his objection because the Legislature used the lawful existing geographic boundary to serve a secular educational need.

**Lemon and precedent reservations.** His actual Lamb's Chapel concurrence with Thomas already rejects Lemon reliance. **Limits.** Do not attribute a view that the Establishment Clause prohibits only formal state churches. The dissent expressly accepts the prohibition on favoring one religion over another.
