## Scope and common record boundaries

All eight Associates reach the presented factor questions on direct review. Both petitioners challenge Section 190.3(a); only Tuilaepa challenges (b) and (i). The two judgments remain distinct. The Section 190.2 eligibility findings are not challenged; their sufficiency is assumed for this review, not newly adjudicated. No habeas new-rule barrier enters, and no unrelated guilt claim, generalized bias finding, or challenge to every statutory factor is added.

Section 190.3 directs the trier to consider the listed factors **if relevant**. Factor (a) concerns the circumstances of the crime of conviction and the existence of special circumstances found true under Section 190.1. Factor (b) concerns the presence or absence of criminal activity involving use or attempted use of force or violence, or an express or implied threat to use force or violence. Factor (i) concerns the defendant's age **at the time of the crime**. The statutory subjects, the California courts' constructions, the actual jury instructions, and the constitutional conclusions remain distinct. The statute's direction to weigh aggravation and mitigation does not turn every absence of mitigation into an aggravator.

Tuilaepa's charge limited the jury to relevant factors, prohibited mechanical counting and arbitrary weighting, permitted moral and sympathetic valuation, and required aggravation sufficiently substantial compared with mitigation to warrant death. The violent-act instruction required proof beyond a reasonable doubt before an act could count in aggravation. That is a reported trial safeguard, not a general constitutional requirement governing every sentencing evaluation. Factor (b)'s evidence included misconduct in custody and during trial; it was not confined to conduct before the capital offense. The state court's no-request and no-prejudice grounds on instructions defining underlying offenses are separate from the general vagueness question.

Proctor's later penalty jury received the full factor list and the older mandatory-language instruction, together with nonmechanical-weighing and mitigation directions. Whole-charge review cannot erase the difference from Tuilaepa's instructions. A defense-requested venue change and new penalty jury establish neither actual juror bias nor an abdication of responsibility. No actual misuse of race, a forbidden factor, or absent mitigation is found merely from the possibilities urged against broad selection discretion.

## Reconciliation of the disputed rows

### Stevens: change the provisional invalidity and vacatur positions

The original row would invalidate the aggravating use of factors (a) and (i), while sustaining (b). His historical own writing, joined by Ginsburg, 512 U.S. 981–984, supplies strong contrary evidence grounded in Zant and Lockett. He accepts the unchallenged narrowing premise, finds the three subjects relevant to an informed individual sentencing decision, and reasons that a State need not label ambiguous relevant evidence exclusively aggravating or mitigating. He also insists that a State may not authorize adverse inferences from constitutionally forbidden or wholly irrelevant considerations. Identifying relevant matters, on his view, can reduce the risk that prejudice replaces individualized judgment.

Each claimed changed premise was tested rather than replacing the provisional row automatically:

- **Graham and Johnson:** Their actual majority rules are stronger than the historical Court's treatment of the Texas claims, but Stevens's own commitment to effective culpability mitigation persists in both timelines. More decisively, the California mechanism here permits an independent mitigating assessment; it does not force a juror to answer deliberateness or future danger falsely to give youth mitigating effect. Neither actual decision guarantees favorable weight or makes age a categorically impermissible subject for the prosecution. Their holdings therefore do not require the provisional invalidity conclusion.
- **Arave, Stringer and Espinosa:** His actual joins demand guidance and correction of a constitutionally invalid factor. They do not establish that these understandable, relevant selection subjects are invalid. The distinction between eligibility and selection does not repeal guidance requirements; conversely, the need to cure a genuine weighing error does not establish one. His historical own no-error position coexisted with his objections to vague aggravators. The same sufficient distinction remains available here.
- **Romano:** His actual join protects the present jury from an irrelevant separate death determination that substantially invites displacement of its own choice. The present factor directions identify relevant sentencing material and do not present another jury's death choice as the answer. Romano's extended responsibility rule does not supply a missing defect in these records or a general capital evidence code.
- **Simmons:** His additional Eighth Amendment instruction ground concerns a requested explanation of a legal sentencing term reasonably likely to be misunderstood. It does not require the judge to assign every item of relevant evidence a favorable or unfavorable label. The fixed legal alternative and express jury question in Simmons have no established counterpart here.
- **Victor/Sandoval and Shannon:** Whole-charge review retains the actual qualifications and does not adopt an isolated-word or boilerplate-cure rule. His Shannon dissent supports a preventive federal supervisory explanation, not a constitutional requirement controlling state sentencing. Neither supplies the necessary changed premise.

The frozen invalidity position was a defensible neutral alternative but lacks a concrete, Justice-specific changed premise sufficient to overcome his directly reasoned historical contrary position. Stevens therefore **affirms both judgments on no-error grounds**, rejecting Tuilaepa's (a), (b) and (i) challenges and Proctor's (a) challenge. He does not find an error harmless. His own historical discussion of Zant's incorrect aggravating label is an explanation for finding no constitutional error here, not a Chapman inquiry or an invitation to transfer Georgia's no-error result to an actually invalid California weighing factor.

### Blackmun: include factor (b), while preserving his independent noncapital ground

Blackmun's historical dissent, 512 U.S. 984–996, objects to **all three** challenged factors, not merely (a) and (i). The original provisional distinction treated violent criminal activity as sufficiently definite because the subject was bounded and a proof instruction protected against unsupported acts. The historical objection is different: even a factually provable and relevant subject can fail to guide the aggravating use made of it on a legally consequential weighing scale. A burden of proof for whether conduct occurred does not itself define the principles governing its sentencing weight. His dissent expressly questions the breadth even with the state narrowing construction; its support is not limited to a new assertion about an omitted instruction.

The actual Arave dissent, joined by Stevens, demands a coherent limiting formulation rather than intelligible words alone. His Stringer and Espinosa joins preserve the separate constitutional significance of weighing after eligibility. Graham and Johnson improve meaningful mitigation but do not, in his view, solve arbitrary selection. Romano and Simmons's actual published concurrences expressly preserve his independent objection to capital punishment as presently administered even while accepting case-specific safeguards and minimum relief. These are the in-world grounds for the broader noncapital position; **no historical Callins action is imported**.

No changed premise in those actual cases explains why Blackmun would now accept (b) while maintaining the same guidance objection to (a) and (i). Accordingly, he continues to oppose both death-sustaining judgments and also regards factor (b) as independently insufficient guidance in Tuilaepa. That reconciles a rationale discrepancy on a lawful path already framed by the neutral materials. It does not add an as-applied finding, a new constitutional burden for every sentencing fact, or a general ban on unadjudicated conduct.

The official dissent's additional discussion of exact jury omissions and examples from other trials is not adopted as a new factual premise. The neutral handoff already records the relevant offense-definition issue and its state disposition, but it does not independently establish every proposed missing instruction or improper trial use. If assembly needs a specific untransmitted definition or prosecutorial statement as an independently necessary ground, that fact must return for neutral verification. The broader guidance conclusion here rests on the factor formulation, actual weighing role and the fully framed competing positions, so no new material source correction is required for it.

Blackmun's preferred remedy remains vacatur of the death-sustaining judgments with lawful noncapital disposition through state proceedings. He also accepts, as minimum case-specific relief, vacatur and a remand correcting the factor errors: genuine reweighing if state law authorizes it, or constitutional harmless-error review with the State bearing Chapman's beyond-a-reasonable-doubt burden; a new sentencing proceeding follows if no lawful cure is available.
