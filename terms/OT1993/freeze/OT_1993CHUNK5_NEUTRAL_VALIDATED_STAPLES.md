## Current-law refresh

The event uses the May 23 start-of-day baseline. Ratzlaf, effective January 11, 1994, requires proof that the charged structuring was known to be unlawful under its particular willfulness provision; it separately adopts residual lenity after ordinary interpretation leaves genuine ambiguity. That holding does not impose knowledge of illegality on every federal offense or settle knowledge of a weapon's characteristics under §5861(d).

Granderson, effective March 22, 1994, independently applies residual lenity to its unresolved penal construction after considering text, sentencing structure, and the companion enactment. It does not make every competing reading ambiguous, supply a universal lowest-punishment rule, or answer this statute's mens rea question. Thompson/Center's actual rule concerns when a firearm is made; neither that negative construction nor its case-specific treatment of a dual-use package decides the knowledge required for possession.

Beecham, effective May 16, resolves which jurisdiction's law governs a conviction-removing event under §921(a)(20); it supplies no knowledge rule for this separate National Firearms Act offense. No other May 23 matter enters this decision merely because it is processed first.

## Neutral issue and remedy clarification

The lawful channel is certiorari review of the Tenth Circuit's criminal judgment. The presented statutory instruction issue is preserved in the approved neutral record. All nine seated Justices are eligible under the supplied record; no additional nonparticipation is established. Quorum is present and an ordinary merits judgment requires five votes, but no vote is supplied here.

Keep three propositions separate: knowledge of possessing the object, knowledge of the physical characteristics that make it a machinegun, and knowledge of the registration duty or of illegality. A public-welfare interpretation may require the first while dispensing with the second or third; the competing ordinary-culpability interpretation may require the first and second without the third. The choice turns on the enacted offense in context and the scope of Morissette, Gypsum, Balint, and Freed, with their different objects and statutory settings preserved.

Do not turn the packet's statement that the rifle could fire automatically into a finding that Staples knew it could do so. His denial is testimony, not an accepted finding. Conversely, the absence of a required jury finding does not establish that the Government could not prove knowledge. Any reversal for instructional error must state the error and leave any properly presented further-proceedings or error-review question to its lawful route; this packet supplies no finding that acquittal is required.

The ordinary-gun and grenade comparison is an argument about statutory culpability, not an adjudication of a Second Amendment claim. A holding should address the charged machinegun characteristics rather than declare a general criminal rule for every firearm or every dangerous object.

These distinct branches and exceptions must not be collapsed into a general collector exemption for machineguns. No record in this packet establishes that either exclusion is asserted for Staples's rifle.
