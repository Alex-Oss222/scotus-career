The neutral freeze's all-eight affirmance therefore creates two candidate departures, each tested rather than presumed permissible.

Thomas: the supposed narrower premise is prospective published cancellation of an incorporated essential component. His own dissent explicitly concedes prospectivity, 511 U.S. 449 n.2, and nevertheless applies American Trucking to any nullification after tariff effectiveness. At 448–454 he tests both express and implied power; he rejects information-prescription power as a source of automatic nullification and insists that a regulation cannot bootstrap its own validity by labeling the tariff incomplete. Thus prospectivity, publication, incorporation, and preexisting regulation are not changed premises. Actual Reiter did not decide this power, and actual Granderson cannot create it by methodological analogy. Correct to reverse/remand.

She finds the numerical information complete, monitoring convenience insufficient, and tariff nullification inadequately fitted where less drastic remedies have not been shown ineffective. The neutral freeze offers those same facts as its narrowing device. No actual prior-simulated holding displaces her premise.
