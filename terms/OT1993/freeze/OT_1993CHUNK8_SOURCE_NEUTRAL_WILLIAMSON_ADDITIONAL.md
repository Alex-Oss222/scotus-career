# Additional predecision primary sources — Williamson

This neutral supplement supplies only date-eligible texts. It adds no Supreme Court outcome, comparator, Stone position, or proposed non-Stone vote. The original source handoff remains frozen.

- `chunk8-sources/A/Rule804_PRE1994_NOTES.txt`: complete original Advisory Committee note, House Report 93–650, Senate Report 93–1277, Conference Report 93–1597, and 1987 technical-amendment note, mechanically copied from the historical notes reproduced at [Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_804). The modern rule and post-1994 notes are excluded. The original note's quoted draft categories are not all enacted law; follow the supplied event-date operative rule and the reports' specific amendments.
- `chunk8-sources/A/Harrell_PRE1994.txt`: complete [United States v. Harrell, 788 F.2d 1524 (11th Cir. 1986)](https://static.case.law/f2d/788/html/1524-01.html), including footnotes. The court required unavailability, a statement against penal interest, and corroborating circumstances indicating trustworthiness. It relied on Rule 804(b)(3) without deciding the alternative coconspirator ground. The opinion's footnote supplies the event-eligible rule text.
- `chunk8-sources/A/Taggart_PRE1994.txt`: complete [United States v. Taggart, 944 F.2d 837 (11th Cir. 1991)](https://static.case.law/f2d/944/html/0837-01.html), including footnotes. It states the circuit's inculpatory corroboration requirement and a separate constitutional harmless-error alternative on its own record. These are circuit propositions; neither the circuit's general rule nor that case's harmlessness finding is automatically a Supreme Court holding or a finding in this case.

The texts were read in full by the root source operator. They have not been rewritten. Minor errors in the historical reproduction remain visible; no altered quotation is supplied.
