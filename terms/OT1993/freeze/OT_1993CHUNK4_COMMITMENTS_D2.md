## Kokkonen

### Sufficient ground, scope, and remedy

Reverse the Ninth Circuit judgment and remand because the district court lacked the asserted ancillary jurisdiction to enforce this settlement. After a Rule 41(a)(1)(ii) dismissal with prejudice that neither retains enforcement jurisdiction nor incorporates the settlement, a dispute alleging breach of the private agreement is not itself enforcement of the court's dismissal order. The court's awareness of the bargain and its role in recording the settlement do not supply jurisdiction. Enforcement therefore requires a separate jurisdictional basis, not shown for the present motion. The legal correction leaves independent contract remedies available and decides neither breach nor entitlement to specific performance.

Retained jurisdiction or incorporation of settlement obligations into the dismissal order would materially change the premise: violation could then implicate an existing order and its administration. No such language appears here. Do not transform that distinction into power for parties to confer jurisdiction merely by agreement. Do not resolve a Rule 60 motion, reopen the original claims, or hold that diversity could never support a properly brought new action.

Actual Willy supports necessary process control with its own source and independence from merits, not jurisdiction over every later contract whose negotiation arose during a case. Wilkinson's broad statement of inherent supervisory power cannot sustain this order without the missing jurisdictional predicate. Its court-supervised production obligations also provide a narrower factual comparison than an unincorporated private settlement.

## Beecham, including Jones

### Sufficient ground, scope, and remedy

Affirm both Fourth Circuit judgments on the sovereign-of-conviction question. Section 921(a)(20)'s instruction to use the law of the jurisdiction where the proceedings occurred governs the immediately related exclusion for expungement, setting aside, pardon, or restoration of civil rights. Federal law therefore determines whether a federal conviction has been removed for these firearms purposes; a State's restoration does not remove the federal predicate. This determines the source of law, not the existence or availability of any particular federal restoration mechanism.

Whenever describing the exception generally, preserve its express qualification: the pardon, expungement, or restoration does not exclude the conviction if it expressly provides that the person may not ship, possess, or receive firearms. No valid qualifying federal relief was established here. The state convictions removed by West Virginia's restoration in Jones are not reinstated as predicates. Core civil rights' administration by States does not authorize choosing a different sovereign for each clause of the same conviction definition.

The two district courts' conflicting geographical theories underscore the function of the statutory sovereign choice, but inconvenience alone is not the ground. Congress listed alternative ways a conviction may lose effect without guaranteeing every defendant every route. The integrated text resolves this question sufficiently that residual lenity does not select petitioners' reading. No constitutional arms, due-process, or equal-protection claim is decided; no statutory restoration procedure is judicially created.

Jones's appellate reinstatement/remand of the indictment remains the procedural consequence, covering the possession and linked false-statement counts as litigated. There is no independent Supreme Court finding of falsity, materiality, knowledge, or guilt, and no new sentencing mandate. Beecham's reversed district ruling likewise remains reversed, with surviving criminal proceedings governed by their ordinary requirements.

## Security Services v. Kmart

### Sufficient ground, scope, and remedy

Affirm the judgment for Kmart because the asserted distance tariff lacked an effective required mileage component for these shipments after published cancellation of the carrier's participation. Sections 10761 and 10762 protect the operative filed rate; they do not furnish an enforceable rate simply because the carrier's monetary schedule remains physically on file. Under the existing participation regulations, a carrier electing to incorporate a governing agent tariff must maintain the required participation/authority. Published cancellation before the transportation removes that legal component from this carrier's asserted rate.

This ground is narrower than an automatic rule that every failure to pay a private bureau invalidates a tariff. Riss's tariff expressly incorporated the guide and its supplements/subsequent issues; the published supplement canceled participation before every shipment in issue. The carrier supplied no proof of authority effective for that period despite the regulation's express proof requirement. No new rule is applied retroactively to shipments performed under then-effective participation. Neither nonpayment alone nor a free-floating equitable objection to collecting the filed rate is sufficient.

Maislin's effective-filed-rate rule remains intact. Berwind-White's treatment of harmless technical defects does not turn a legally unavailable incorporated essential component into an effective rate. American Trucking's limits on retroactive rejection are respected: regulation of operative participation in a required mileage tariff directly implements the specific statutory public-rate scheme and its two-component distance-rate requirement. The Court need not endorse every possible exercise of retrospective tariff rejection or every general agency-law rationale used below.

No rate reasonableness, contract-carrier status, estoppel, or limitations holding is adopted. Kmart waived the limitations defense. The carrier's reasonable-compensation theory was not preserved below; affirmance does not decide its abstract availability. Actual Reiter supplies no automatic recovery, automatic tariff validity, or referral requirement for this resolved legal prerequisite. No earlier guide or unidentified former tariff is substituted. No election or alternative disposition under the Negotiated Rates Act is selected.
