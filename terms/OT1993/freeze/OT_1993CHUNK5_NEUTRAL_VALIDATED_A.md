## 49. BFP v. Resolution Trust Corp.

The corrected posture includes two consolidated appellate routes: district-court affirmance of dismissal as to the private buyers and Bankruptcy Appellate Panel affirmance of judgment for Imperial. The Ninth Circuit rejected the bankruptcy court's alternative no-property-interest premise because the state quiet-title judgment established BFP and the Foremans as fee co-owners. It affirmed on reasonably equivalent value. Do not resurrect the rejected no-interest ground or reduce the lower judgment to a single unexplained summary judgment.

The transfer was the July 12, 1989 foreclosure; the Chapter 11 petition followed on October 25, 1989, within the applicable one-year avoidance period. State-law procedural compliance, adequate notice, and lack of collusion were upheld below. The $433,000 price is the sale price; more than $725,000 is BFP's allegation, not an adjudicated federal valuation or a finding of constructive fraud. Osborne's good faith and lack of notice of the title dispute do not erase the need to identify a statutory consequence when relief is considered.

The quoted §548(a)(2)(A) value requirement and §548(a)(2)(B)(i) insolvency branch remain separate. Sections 101(54) and 548 make foreclosure transfers eligible for the statutory inquiry; their inclusion does not itself choose a value measure. Section 548(d)(2)(A)'s definition of value includes property or satisfaction or securing of the debtor's present or antecedent debt, but excludes an unperformed support promise to the debtor or a relative. The small-capital and inability-to-pay alternatives are not asserted grounds in this record; their full unverified wording must not be supplied by inference or used as an additional basis for judgment.

Dewsnup's Chapter 7 lien-survival construction and Nobelman's Chapter 13 anti-modification construction retain their express settings. Neither exempts a lien or foreclosure from an independently applicable avoidance power. The competing paths are a conclusive regular-foreclosure rule, a rebuttable or all-circumstances federal-value inquiry, and any narrower supported interpretation preserving forced-sale conditions. Do not equate rejection of conclusive treatment with mandatory ordinary-market value or a nationwide percentage. Durrett, Bundles, Madrid and Winshall supply the circuit approaches identified in the source supplement rather than a settled Supreme Court rule.

If further federal valuation is legally required, reversal or vacatur and remand for that inquiry is distinct from an immediate avoidance judgment. The remaining statutory predicates, transferee protections, recovery provisions, and parties' respective remedial positions must be decided only if properly presented and supported; this packet supplies no automatic title transfer, recovery award, or extension to tax sales, collusive sales, personal property, or invalid foreclosure procedure. ## 51. Custis v. United States

The particular judgment is the federal 235-month enhanced sentence. The predicates were the 1985 Pennsylvania robbery, 1985 Maryland burglary, and 1989 Maryland attempted burglary convictions; only the latter two are challenged. The source supplement states the different ineffectiveness and plea/stipulated-facts objections. None alleges a complete absence of counsel. The district court's initial examination of the allegations must be kept distinct from its later ground refusing the channel, and from the Fourth Circuit's channel ruling. An allegation of constitutional error is not a finding of invalidity.

The full quoted §924(e)(1) requires the qualifying §922(g) violation, three qualifying prior convictions for violent felony or serious drug offense or both, and offenses committed on different occasions; it prescribes the fifteen-year floor and stated fine, suspension, probation, and parole restrictions. Whether a predicate falls within the offense definitions and whether a constitutional attack may be heard in this sentencing forum are separate issues. Do not reach a new classification or different-occasions dispute merely because the statute contains those conditions.

Section 921(a)(20)'s conviction-removing events and its firearms reservation retain the actual statutory terms, including shipping, transporting, possessing, or receiving firearms. Beecham, effective May 16, 1994, now supplies the actual jurisdiction-of-conviction rule for that exclusion; it does not decide whether ACCA authorizes present collateral adjudication of a conviction not yet set aside. Parke remains the actual permitted allocation of proof for final counseled pleas in its Kentucky enhancement procedure; it does not enact an ACCA collateral-review channel or eliminate the complete-denial-of-counsel issue.

The §851(c) comparison in the source supplement has express challenge, pleading, proof, and waiver provisions. Those features may support competing statutory inferences but are not ACCA's text and must not be silently imported as its procedure. The parties' constitutional, avoidance, statutory-context and lenity arguments remain available. Ratzlaf and Granderson's actual residual-lenity holdings also remain available where genuine criminal statutory ambiguity survives ordinary interpretation; they do not establish that this forum-allocation issue is ambiguous.

The principal lawful paths are to permit the broader attack and remand for a lawful validity determination, or to sustain refusal of this sentencing channel while preserving whatever direct or collateral remedies the original convictions' review law supplies. Neither path may presume these convictions invalid, constitutionally flawless, or already vacated. Custis's continuing state-custody status at federal sentencing bears on the existence of another route. A later successful predicate challenge may permit an application respecting the enhanced sentence, but the outcome, timing rules, exhaustion rules, and entitlement to reopening are not established here. The record does not present total unavailability of every earlier lawful channel.

## 52. NLRB v. Health Care & Retirement Corp. of America

The complete §2(11) list and all three conditions in source A govern: one or more specified forms of authority or effective recommendation, exercise in the employer's interest, and independent judgment rather than merely routine or clerical action. A nurse's title alone establishes none of them. The Act's professional definition and unit-selection provisions remain separate; these four employees were licensed practical nurses classified as technical employees with duties said to be virtually identical to registered nurses. Do not incorrectly present them as registered professionals or hold that all professional direction is necessarily statutory supervision.

The employer's staffing and supervisory evidence must be read with the ALJ's limited-direction findings, established assignments, aides' assignment choices, patient-care focus, and administrators' availability for nonroutine matters. The court of appeals rejected the Board's employer-interest rationale and found supervisor status. The reviewed agency rationale and actual preserved scope matter: the source does not establish an independent-judgment alternative presented by the Board as a separate basis to sustain its order. A proposed remand on that alternative must address both preservation and the agency-ground requirement; it cannot invent an agency finding.

The Board's patient-care interpretation is defended as a reconciliation of professional/technical coverage with the supervisory exclusion. The employer argues that patient care is the nursing home's business and Congress's list cannot be reduced to a smaller class of personnel decisions. Neither argument is adopted by this packet. Preserve the Board counsel's complete explanation from source A; the response did not concede a categorical unprecedented nurse-only rule. Yeshiva's actual professional/managerial boundaries, including the identified patient-care discussion, and the legal status of the 1974 committee history require attention on both paths.

Actual earlier law now includes John Hancock's statutory-limit ground, ABF Freight's reviewable statutory remedial discretion, and City of Chicago's application of Chevron within the enacted boundary. An agency may adopt or change a permissible interpretation within genuine ambiguity but may not erase a resolved statutory restriction. None of these cases decides §2(11)'s meaning, makes a changed interpretation categorically ineligible for deference, or converts remedial discretion into power to redefine covered employees. Lechmere and Boston & Maine preserve their distinct statutory-access and discernible-agency-ground rules. Thunder Basin governs its review channel and supplies no substantive definition of supervisor.

Lawful paths include sustaining the Board's contextual interpretation and the appropriately supported consequence for its order, or rejecting that interpretation and sustaining the refusal of enforcement, with a narrower supported remand considered only within the actual review and agency-ground constraints. Every path must retain the listed-activity and independent-judgment requirements. No result entails that every nurse is a supervisor or that all patient-care direction falls outside the employer's interest.
