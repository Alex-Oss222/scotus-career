## Verified factual corrections and qualifications

- The judicial header of 498 U.S. 433 says “Decided February 19, 1991.” The packet's January 22 date is incorrect. The frozen original packet remains byte-preserved; this accompanying correction governs the prior decision date. No present-event date, cutoff or prior mandate substance changes.
- Roller below says the October 31, 1992 hearing occurred and parole was denied. That fact does not establish release or moot a continuing prospective frequency claim. No later custody development was verified.
- The Federal Circuit in Izumi states that settlement vacatur is its general rule, but expressly disclaims automatic vacatur in every circumstance. The commitments retain that qualification.
- The Hancock contract has some guarantees and a residual floor; the relevant dispute is the nonguaranteed free-funds component. “No guarantee at all” would be an inaccurate account. The 1977 amendment's cessation of automatic new annuity guarantees is supported by the lower opinion.
- The Middlebrooks operative judgment repeatedly invokes Article I, §16, as well as the Eighth Amendment, and separately states that Lowenfield supplies no rationale for constitutionality under the Tennessee Constitution. The provisional threshold ruling rests on that text.
