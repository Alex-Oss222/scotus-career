#### D. STONE’S REVISED REASONING, APPLICATION, AND LIMITS

**Stone’s Position and Scope:** retain the proposed component dispositions, making totality—not proportionality—the reason liability fails.

The state validation expressly reserved later factual §2 litigation. Section 1738 gives it that actual preclusive scope, not a broader one. The United States was not a losing party seeking appellate correction of its own state judgment. Rooker and Feldman therefore do not bar this original enforcement action. No later formulation of those doctrines is needed, and optional renewed state proceedings are not mandatory exhaustion.

On the merits, Stone's Master disclaims proportional representation while preserving local equal opportunity. Gingles predicates identify a potential mechanism of dilution; they do not make every additional drawable majority-minority district mandatory. Rough proportionality between effective opportunities and the relevant group's voting-age share is consequently probative, but cannot excuse packing, fragmentation, or another proved local barrier. Historical discrimination and polarization retain weight even where the aggregate looks favorable. The analysis cannot simply announce that “other circumstances” lose against a ratio.

The lawful ground for rejecting the House judgment is the absence, on the tried record, of a demonstrated unequal opportunity beyond the maximization inference. A hypothetical eleventh district does not by itself supply that missing conclusion. For the Senate, competing desired additional districts do not establish two proved violations that courts are powerless to remedy. Affirm the operative plan for failure to prove the asserted statutory violation, not impossibility of remedying an acknowledged one. If a full record instead contains a distinct supported dilution finding, rough proportionality cannot support appellate erasure; Stone's result depends on the packet's account of the reasoning actually used.

Use the litigated Dade-area geography without establishing a universal county-level denominator or statewide offset. Define effective opportunity with evidence rather than counting nominal majority districts that cannot function electorally. Preserve unresolved citizenship and coalition issues. The revised Holder recommendation would not prevent this analysis: a geographic district plan with an established body size supplies a different benchmark problem. Dismissed constitutional theories stay dismissed, and removal of the House remedy must proceed through lawful election administration rather than an invented timetable.
