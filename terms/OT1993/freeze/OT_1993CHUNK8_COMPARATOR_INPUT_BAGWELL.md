**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9–0 in the judgment. [R54](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/821/)

**Historical Holding:** The serious fines were criminal and required a jury. Prospective announcement alone did not make them civil; they punished widespread completed violations and were neither compensatory nor purgeable. [R54](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/821/)
