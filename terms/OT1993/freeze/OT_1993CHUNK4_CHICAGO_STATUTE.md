# Chicago neutral statutory source extraction

RCRA §3001(i), 42 U.S.C. §6921(i), enacted in 1984, is reproduced in full below from the statutory quotation at 511 U.S. 334. Only the enacted text is supplied; the accompanying case's result, opinion, interpretation and lineup are withheld. This extraction is for independent neutral validation. The source at https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-511/pdf/USREPORTS-511-328.pdf is available to the later reconciliation context, not to the neutral reader.

> A resource recovery facility recovering energy from the mass burning of municipal solid waste shall not be deemed to be treating, storing, disposing of, or otherwise managing hazardous wastes for the purposes of regulation under this subchapter, if—
>
> (1) such facility—
>
> (A) receives and burns only—
>
> (i) household waste (from single and multiple dwellings, hotels, motels, and other residential sources), and
>
> (ii) solid waste from commercial or industrial sources that does not contain hazardous waste identified or listed under this section, and
>
> (B) does not accept hazardous wastes identified or listed under this section, and
>
> (2) the owner or operator of such facility has established contractual requirements or other appropriate notification or inspection procedures to assure that hazardous wastes are not received at or burned in such facility.

The text's conditions are distinct. A statutory requirement is not itself a finding that a facility met it. The complete lower opinion supplies EDF's stipulation concerning the facility's exclusion procedures; it does not adjudicate that every ash sample is hazardous. The 1991 lower opinion's quotation abbreviated this provision and rendered “subchapter” as “subsection”; the statutory quotation above preserves the complete conditions and the correct word.
