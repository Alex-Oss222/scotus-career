## Holder — critical-path conclusion

**Kiryas Joel changes no Holder premise.** The Court identifies two distinct Establishment Clause defects: deliberate allocation of general civil authority to a community selected as a religious community, and a particular denominational governmental benefit lacking neutral terms or dependable assurance of equal treatment. It expressly rejects religious homogeneity alone as a sufficient defect, preserves equal political citizenship and neutral geographic government, and leaves Shaw's actual racial territorial-design holding intact. The holding does not supply a Section 2 benchmark for governing-body size or make every demographic district choice an independently sufficient constitutional injury.

The different constitutional sources matter. Holder concerns a statutory results claim and a locally offered comparison, with a separate purpose claim not yet reviewed below. No religious-allocation issue is presented. Kiryas Joel's limits cannot be turned into either a new race-neutrality gate for Section 2 or a rule that all deliberate structural accommodation is lawful. The existing Section 2 elements and the difference between state choice and federally ordered relief remain the Holder authorities.
