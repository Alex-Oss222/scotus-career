**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote Parts I, II-B, II-C, and III for Blackmun, Stevens, O’Connor, and Ginsburg; the introduction to Part II and Part II-A had Blackmun, Stevens, and Ginsburg. Blackmun concurred; Stevens concurred with Blackmun and Ginsburg; O’Connor concurred in part and in the judgment; Kennedy concurred in the judgment. Scalia dissented with Rehnquist and Thomas. [R49](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/687/)

**Historical Holding:** The special district violated the Establishment Clause. The opinions differed over delegation, neutrality and future equal treatment, and the independent prohibition on drawing governmental boundaries along religious lines. They did not establish that secular special education for these children was itself forbidden. [R49](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/687/)

**Historical Authority:** Larkin, Larson, Lemon, Amos, and the religion/accommodation precedents; broader reasoning must be attributed by its actual joins. [R49](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/687/)
