## Strong adverse path and why it does not control these components

The strongest current-denial argument is materially stronger than filing volume alone. The paper repeats counsel, trial, and postconviction complaints; its attachments contain adverse Strickland findings and a corrected duplicate burglary conviction. The proposed additional witness's value was unsupported below. The available 1993 appellate pages reject the use of civil admissions to establish perjury and the treatment of counsel's handling of Jencks material as prosecutorial concealment. Federal district rulings apply Swain: denial of relief or inability to use a local remedy does not alone make §23-110 inadequate. The certificate argument also fails to establish that the D.C. Circuit lacked power to require the necessary showing before appeal. Subsequent fee permission and docketing in civil action 94-0022 cut against total permanent foreclosure. All of those objections remain live for independent writ screening.

The selected grant does not disregard these obstacles. Unlike a paper that never addresses its independently dispositive barrier, this petition specifically asserts why ordinary review was unavailable and points to concrete access events, including partial judicial relief concerning a filing injunction. The lower vacatur establishes neither a denial of notice nor continuing obstruction; nevertheless it gives the access allegation content beyond the underlying grievance. Under the actual Demos application, that limited arguability can support fee permission while the extraordinary-writ request fails independently. It is a discretionary, record-bound application of Rule 39.8, not a holding that every pleading with one access allegation necessarily qualifies or that Rule 20 obstacles can never demonstrate frivolousness.

The strongest prospective argument is the concentration of extraordinary-writ filings and their capacity to consume resources without ordinary filing deadlines. It is supported by retained McDonald/Sindram/In re Demos authority. But the sanction would cover unknown future papers, including potential custody-review claims. The actual record must establish abuse and a reason why individualized screening is insufficient for that scope. It cannot borrow historical Sassower's different disposition or the unadmitted Anderson Rule 39.8 orders. The current grant alone does not immunize all future papers; together with the specific evidentiary limits it makes the broad new condition unsupported here.
