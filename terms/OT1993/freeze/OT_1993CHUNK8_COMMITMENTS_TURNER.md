## Validated case and scope

The appeal lies from the three-judge District Court's judgment, 819 F. Supp. 32, under §23 of the 1992 Cable Act. The five consolidated challenges concern §§4 and 5. All eight non-Stone Justices are seated and participating at argument and decision. Each would reach the presented First Amendment challenge. No additional recusal, lack of quorum, mootness, or jurisdictional obstacle is established. The federal defendants moved to dismiss; plaintiffs and intervening defenders made the summary-judgment motions. The entered judgment for defenders is the judgment reviewed; do not describe every defendant as having moved for it.

The source supplement's detailed statutory qualifications accompany this freeze by reference and are substantive premises. In particular: the commercial exemption is for a system with no more than twelve activated channels and no more than 300 subscribers, only while it deletes no broadcast signal; it does not exempt that system from noncommercial duties. Commercial capacity, duplication, network-affiliate selection, payment and signal-delivery conditions are distinct from the noncommercial rules. The commercial channel options include January 1, 1992 carriage; the noncommercial options do not separately include that date. A statutory option to omit a duplicate is not a command to omit it. Required carriage is not an obligation of editorial endorsement.

The principal classification below concerns carriage of the eligible full-power local stations under the general commercial and noncommercial provisions. Low-power eligibility and market adjustment contain additional programming/localism criteria and were not separately analyzed below. The constitutional status of those distinct criteria is reserved; no judicially reconstructed severance or final validation follows from the general classification. Section 614(g)'s initial home-shopping transition alone cannot establish June 1994 administration. A party's contention depending on that current administration requires the actual FCC determination. These reservations do not prevent deciding the legal classification of the general obligations or setting aside the undifferentiated summary judgment. A final operative injunction on a strict-scrutiny path must remain tied to the challenged compulsory carriage, without adjudicating every FCC implementing detail.

## Entering law and independent legal paths

Leathers, 499 U.S. 439, recognizes cable speech and press and permits some medium-specific economic treatment; its broad sales-tax judgment is not a decision about compelled carriage. Preferred Communications, 476 U.S. 488, recognizes editorial selection and rejects disposition of a colorable speech claim through assumed disputed facts, while leaving the exact cable standard open. Blackmun's concurrence there, joined by O'Connor and Marshall, expressly requires examination of cable's actual characteristics before importing another medium's standard. Tornillo protects editorial control; PG&E's fractured compelled-access decision must not become a unanimous categorical prohibition. Red Lion's spectrum rationale concerns broadcasting; a physical cable channel is not a scarce broadcast frequency merely because capacity is finite.

O'Brien and Ward support intermediate scrutiny only for burdens justified without reference to content. The substantial governmental interest must be real, the burden must further it, and the restriction must not burden substantially more speech than necessary. The standard does not demand the least restrictive conceivable alternative. Landmark and Sable preserve judicial responsibility despite legislative findings. Congress may make reasonable predictive judgments supported by evidence; neither legislative labels nor a judicial demand for scientific certainty is appropriate.

Discovery Network (March 24, 1993) makes a benign purpose insufficient to establish neutrality where the operative content distinction lacks a connection to the stated physical harm. It retains its commercial-speech scope and does not supply an automatic strict-scrutiny holding here. Edenfield (April 26, 1993) requires actual advancement in the relevant setting without invariably requiring empirical studies. R.A.V. (June 22, 1992) controls through substantial overbreadth; the Scalia/Kennedy/Souter/Thomas content-selection rationale is a published separate position only. Ladue (June 13, 1994) protects a distinctive communicative opportunity even under assumed neutrality and preserves Ward's rejection of a least-restrictive-means demand. MCI (June 17, 1994) construes a different FCC delegation; it neither decides Congress's carriage power nor turns communications regulation into ordinary economic legislation immune from speech review.

**Path A — structural carriage and intermediate review.** Cable can control a household's practical access to broadcast signals and has economic incentives concerning competing stations. A general rule preserving broadcast access and free service can address this transmission structure without selecting a viewpoint. Unlike a reply requirement, the full schedule is carried without requiring the cable operator to endorse it. The strongest objection is that Congress also extolled local news and educational programming, and the definitions contain distinctions by provider and service. This path must explain why preserving the transmission system is independently meaningful and why content-specific subordinate provisions are reserved, rather than calling every provision neutral because Congress asserted a good purpose.

**Path B — preferred expression and strict review.** Congress reserved private editorial capacity for favored broadcast speakers and protected their local/educational programming. The legal injury is compulsory preference, not simply suppression of one disfavored viewpoint. Leathers does not resolve compelled publication; Discovery Network prevents the structural label from hiding content choice. The strongest objection is that all programs of ordinary local full-power broadcasters are carried, with commercial/noncommercial differences describing distinct broadcast institutions and service availability rather than desired opinions. A strict-review conclusion therefore needs the statutory preference and legislative objectives together, not an assumption that any speaker distinction is necessarily content based.

**Path C — intermediate scrutiny, present judgment affirmed.** Congressional evidence concerning market control, incentives, and carriage losses can support preventive legislation before a broadcaster fails. The capacity caps, local eligibility, duplication exceptions and technical qualifications constrain its burden. Strongest objection: the record as supplied does not resolve the inference from survey drops/repositioning to threatened free service, the duration and impact of those events, or the quantity of displaced cable expression; summary judgment cannot treat each disputed inference as a proved fact. This path is strongest for Stevens but does not ultimately satisfy the present commitment below.

**Path D — intermediate scrutiny, vacatur for a developed record.** Preserve the lawful structural interests but return for proof and assessment of threatened harm, advancement, actual burden and fit. Strongest objection: remand can convert legislative prediction into a requirement of completed injury or trial-type proof of every affected station. The required instruction avoids that error: assess reasonable predictive findings and supporting evidence, disputed contrary material, and actual scope, without requiring station-by-station impending insolvency, conclusive studies, proof of past market collapse, or selection of the least restrictive possible policy. A remand is neither final approval nor invalidation.

## Individual commitments

### Blackmun

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; recognize protected cable editorial selection; reject automatic transfer of Red Lion's spectrum standard; apply O'Brien/Ward to the general full-power carriage provisions; vacate summary judgment and remand on justification and fit. Preserve separate treatment of low-power/market criteria. Reject final invalidation on this record.

**Own eligible support:** His Preferred Communications concurrence expressly keeps cable's distinctive physical/economic facts open for examination. His Leathers dissent join objects to unjustified intermedia discrimination, while preserving regulation tied to cable's special characteristics. His Discovery Network join and separate full-protection position, and his Ladue join, insist that useful information and practical expression remain protected. These authorities support serious review of the burden, not immunity from neutral structural regulation.

**Competing path and answer:** Strict review has force because cable channels are editorial space and governmental preference can distort competition. The relevant distinction here is the asserted transmission bottleneck and continued availability of free television; the Leathers dissent itself preserved special regulation connected to a medium's characteristics. That connection permits intermediate review of the core without assuming the truth of every economic prediction. Conversely, accepting general monopoly findings as sufficient to affirm would leave his editorial-protection concern unanswered. The current disputes require remand.

**Join limits and remedy:** Can join a general content-neutrality conclusion only if it preserves independent judicial review and reserves the separately content-sensitive subordinate criteria. Cannot join broadcast scarcity as a universal cable rule, a conclusion that mandated speech is categorically harmless, or affirmance premised on unproved absence of displacement. Narrower lawful position is to vacate for factual development even if some classification language must be reserved. Remand must preserve reasonable prediction and no universal empirical-study requirement.

### Stevens

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; protect editorial selection; reject automatic Red Lion transfer; treat the general full-power obligations as structural and content neutral. Vacate and remand for the remaining material fit questions, while giving Congress substantial latitude to assess a threatened competitive problem. Do not finally invalidate §§4–5.

**Own eligible support:** Leathers majority join accepts medium distinctions not designed or structured to suppress ideas. His Discovery Network Court opinion requires a connection between classification and actual harms but permits incremental solutions. His Ladue opinion demands adequate expressive opportunities without a least-restrictive-means rule. His MCI dissent recognizes the practical significance of dominant-market structure and agency expertise within actual legal limits; that statutory dissent supplies no substitute for First Amendment review.

**Competing path and answer:** Affirmance is a materially supported alternative: free broadcasting, diversity of sources, and household reception are substantial objectives, and Congress can prevent erosion before observing a completed collapse. The flaw is narrower than disbelief of Congress. The unresolved relationship between carriage decisions and meaningful threats, and the burden on displaced programmers, prevents the supplied record from resolving all material fit issues. Preferred Communications, which he joined, supports developing those disputes. Strict scrutiny overreads the ordinary station-wide obligation as selection of each program's ideas, although content-specific low-power/localism predicates require their own analysis.

**Join limits and remedy:** Will not join an instruction requiring Congress to establish imminent failure of every station, perfect correspondence between burden and benefit, or exhaustion of all conceivable alternatives. Nor does his deference establish that every local programmer contributes unique expression. Supports a bounded evidence/remand ground retaining legislative predictive latitude. A later fully adequate record could sustain the core; this freeze does not prejudge it. A bare dissent from remand merely because others prefer a different margin is not authorized.

### O'Connor

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; protect editorial choice; reject use of Red Lion's spectrum scarcity to dilute cable protection. Regard compulsory statutory preference for local broadcasting, especially the educational/local-programming design, as content based; apply strict scrutiny and reverse the judgment sustaining the challenged compulsory carriage requirements. Remand for declaratory/injunctive relief within the challenge, leaving unrelated Act provisions and unpresented implementation issues untouched.

**Own eligible support:** Her Leathers Court opinion expressly preserves scrutiny of content or censorial classifications while addressing a general tax. Her Preferred Communications concurrence join insists that cable's actual characteristics matter rather than importing broadcast doctrine. Her Discovery Network join rejects benign-purpose neutrality, and her Ladue concurrence says classification ordinarily precedes scrutiny. No proposition depends on treating her as joining Scalia's separate R.A.V. theory; she instead joined White's criticism of that extension.

**Competing path and answer:** Economic bottleneck regulation can be neutral, and compulsory carriage does not require assent. But the Act's preferred categories and congressional programming objectives allocate protected editorial space according to the value assigned to selected speakers' output. Monopoly control does not itself entitle government to make that selection. Preserving free television is substantial, yet the present evidence and alternatives do not establish that this compulsory preference is narrowly necessary. Mere remand under intermediate scrutiny would preserve the wrong classification.

**Join limits and remedy:** Can join a strict-review ground tied to the actual compelled allocation and content-related design; cannot join a categorical rule that all different treatment of media is unconstitutional, or that speaker identity always equals content. Will not base invalidity on the unverified current home-shopping treatment. Her lawful narrower fallback is reversal because the current judgment lacks adequate justification even under intermediate review, but that fallback does not constitute agreement that the provisions are neutral. Relief is against the challenged carriage burden; no damages, wholesale invalidation of the Cable Act, or FCC-rule reconstruction.

### Scalia

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; recognize editorial protection; reject automatic broadcast-scarcity treatment. Adopt the content-preference path and reverse with a bounded remand for relief against the challenged compulsory carriage regime. Agree that the present judgment cannot stand even if the Court instead applies intermediate review; do not join that classification.

**Own eligible support:** Leathers majority join permits noncensorial medium distinctions but not preferential allocation of expression. His Discovery Network join rejects reliance on benign physical objectives when the operative selection concerns content. His published R.A.V. concurrence identifies content and viewpoint selection and carefully delineated exceptions; that writing is personal continuity, not binding law. His Ladue join protects a distinctive channel even under assumed neutrality. His MCI opinion join confirms respect for actual statutory boundaries, without deciding this constitutional question.

**Competing path and answer:** Carriage of entire station schedules appears indifferent to particular opinions; a genuine physical bottleneck supports some differential regulation. But Congress has selected the class whose expression others must publish and has relied on favored local and educational output. Those choices are not eliminated by carrying an entire schedule. The objective of diversity cannot itself permit the government to prefer some private speakers' speech within another's finite editorial space. The present record does not establish the compelling necessity of this choice.

**Join limits and remedy:** No categorical immunity for anticompetitive conduct; no transposition of R.A.V.'s noncontrolling within-unprotected-category doctrine into a Court holding. Could join a narrower conclusion that identified content-based elements fail strict review if severability and actual challenged applications are lawfully established; the present packet does not supply such a replacement scheme. A remand for evidence alone does not resolve his legal objection. Do not find every station healthy or every operator harmless to broadcasters.

### Kennedy

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; recognize operators' and programmers' speech; reject spectrum scarcity as the cable standard. Apply intermediate scrutiny to the general station-wide obligations, identify preservation of free broadcast service and diverse sources against bottleneck suppression as substantial content-neutral interests, and vacate summary judgment for further proceedings on actual support and fit. No final constitutional validation.

**Own eligible support:** Leathers majority join recognizes protected cable activity and allows nondiscriminatory structural treatment. His Discovery Network join requires content-independent justification; his Edenfield Court opinion requires demonstrable advancement without mandatory studies. His R.A.V. separate join protects against selective suppression but preserves genuine secondary-effect and noncensorial distinctions. His Ladue join confirms that scrutiny examines the actual expressive burden.

**Competing path and answer:** Tornillo and PG&E make compelled editorial space a serious concern, and local/educational references require examination. The general duties operate on station-wide transmission and do not depend on agreement with a station's ideas. Cable's ability to prevent effective household reception supplies a distinct physical/economic justification absent from an ordinary newspaper reply law. That distinction supports intermediate scrutiny, not a presumed victory for the government. The 1988 survey does not by itself resolve the challenged chain from carriage loss to economic danger, or the extent of displaced speech.

**Join limits and remedy:** No rubber-stamp acceptance of congressional findings; no requirement of demonstrable censorial intent before a First Amendment injury; no presumption that every broadcaster faces failure. Would join remand directing evaluation of evidence, reasonable predictions, actual channel burden and meaningful alternatives without least-restrictive-means review. Reserve separately content-sensitive low-power and market-adjustment provisions rather than approving them through the broad rule. Strict invalidation requires a premise his core classification rejects.

### Souter

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; protect cable editorial discretion; decline Red Lion transfer. Treat the general full-power obligations as content neutral on their structural justification, and vacate/remand because the supplied summary-judgment record leaves consequential factual issues unresolved. Do not decide final validity or reserve to Congress the ultimate constitutional judgment.

**Own eligible support:** Leathers majority join accepts a broad content-neutral tax without allowing censorship. His Discovery Network and Edenfield joins demand a real link between restrictions and asserted harms. His R.A.V. separate join accepts the content-selection concern together with its carefully confined exceptions. His Ladue join requires examination of the practical communication lost, and his MCI dissent allows expertise to operate within the actual legal framework rather than establishing universal communications deference.

**Competing path and answer:** The strict-review account correctly identifies compelled editorial carriage, and noncommercial eligibility refers to educational function. Still, the source-neutral objective of maintaining an independent broadcast delivery system and the station-wide character of the burden allow a structural analysis; they are not merely labels for disfavored ideas. The record must then establish that the justification is real and sufficiently fitted. A summary assertion that cable can injure competitors does not establish the necessary degree of threatened harm or permissible burden. Conversely, absence of proven past station failure does not preclude supported preventive regulation.

**Join limits and remedy:** Will join only with an express account of predictive evidence and unresolved genuine disputes. No factual determination that unused channel capacity eliminates programmers' injury. Preserve commercial/noncommercial statutory distinctions and leave low-power content criteria to a properly developed treatment. A narrower legal path is remand without a final answer for the ancillary provisions; it is not a finding that they are valid. No damages or prescribed new carriage quota.

### Thomas

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; protect editorial selection equally as expression; do not extend spectrum scarcity automatically. Apply strict review to the compulsory preference for broadcast expression; reverse the judgment and remand for relief within the actual challenge. Do not treat a content-neutrality join as entailed by agreement to set aside summary judgment.

**Own eligible support:** His R.A.V. separate join resists selective speech control while preserving the stated exceptions. His Edenfield join requires substantiated advancement. His Discovery Network dissent join accepts incremental physical regulation and the established commercial/noncommercial distinction; it does not authorize compelled carriage of noncommercial protected speech on cable or replace the majority rule. His Ladue join rejects near-elimination of a protected communicative opportunity, and his MCI joins require giving legal effect to the actual statute rather than a generalized policy label. He was not a participant in Leathers or Preferred Communications; no personal join is invented.

**Competing path and answer:** The medium has physical bottleneck attributes and Congress can regulate market abuse. The decisive concern is whether that authority extends to assigning editorial space to preferred speakers because of the informational output they supply. Structural regulation could address exclusionary conduct without that preference; this record does not establish the narrow necessity of compulsory favored carriage. The Discovery Network dissent involved a settled commercial-speech distinction and removal of physical racks, a materially different basis.

**Join limits and remedy:** Could join strict scrutiny without adopting a universal theory of corporate compelled assent. Does not require proof that every station's programming is politically favored; does require a genuine content-selection basis rather than media identity alone. No retrospective liability or rewriting of statutory caps. If the Court remands under another standard, his agreement that the judgment must be set aside remains judgment agreement only on that narrower action.

### Ginsburg

**Threshold and components:** Reach merits; recognize editorial and programmer expression; reject automatic broadcasting-scarcity review. Adopt strict scrutiny for the statutory preference allocating private cable capacity to designated local/educational broadcast expression; reverse the present judgment and remand for bounded relief. The source record supplies no verified earlier Ginsburg cable merits writing; that evidentiary limitation is retained rather than inventing one.

**Own eligible support and controlling authorities:** Her Ladue join supplies direct published commitment to meaningful protection of an expressive channel and attention to realistic alternatives. Her MCI Court and concurrence joins require analysis of the actual operative allocation and conditions, though those are statutory holdings and do not determine speech scrutiny. Leathers, Tornillo, Preferred Communications and the actual Discovery Network rule supply the governing doctrinal comparison. She is not attributed any pre-accession Supreme Court join.

**Competing path and answer:** The strongest lawful competing path is intermediate scrutiny because Congress regulates a delivery-system bottleneck and requires complete schedules rather than particular opinions. The compulsory reservation nevertheless privileges defined speakers and the output Congress praises at the expense of other protected programmers. Ladue's practical inquiry makes that displacement significant rather than cured by the availability of different outlets. The evidence supplied does not establish strict fit. Because her own supplied cable-specific decisional record is sparse, this row's classification warrants particularly careful comparator reconciliation; sparse evidence does not license a fictitious source or an invented prior position.

**Join limits and remedy:** Supports the actual content-preference ground, not a rule invalidating every medium distinction or a claim that all FCC economic regulation controls ideas. Does not derive a constitutional rule from MCI alone. Can agree separately that material fit questions foreclose summary judgment under any alternative intermediate path, without joining its classification. Preserve legislative authority to enact lawful structural safeguards and do not adjudicate current home-shopping administration without its source.
