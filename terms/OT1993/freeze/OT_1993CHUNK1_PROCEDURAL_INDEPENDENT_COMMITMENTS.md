## Cavanaugh v. Roller — November 30, 1993

### Components and tested alternatives

**Preferred formal disposition of each modeled Justice:** Dismiss the writ of certiorari as improvidently granted. Do not vacate the Fourth Circuit judgment, order a new remand, declare the case presently moot, decide the January 1 effect of the savings clause, or reach the Ex Post Facto Clause or §1983/habeas questions. These are eight independently supported provisional commitments, not a claim that the neutral sources uniquely compel one discretionary choice.

**Sufficient common procedural basis:** The already-enacted future classification rule, Griffin's intervening adoption of annual consideration, and the officials' acknowledged obligation under the outstanding judgments materially alter the practical vehicle for resolving continuing enforcement of the challenged retrospective biennial rule. Discretionary Supreme Court review may be discontinued while an enforceable lower-court judgment remains. That basis does not depend on finding that Griffin is an adequate independent state ground: this is review of a federal appellate judgment under §1254(1). Nor does it depend on an actual completed hearing, the absence of a present injury, legislative bad faith, or a finding that the State will necessarily prevail or lose on the merits.

**Strongest retention path:** The case is fully argued; the statute has not taken effect; Roller had not received his 1993 hearing when counsel last spoke; an order requiring the promised consideration can still give effectual relief. The disagreement over parole procedures extends beyond South Carolina. Church of Scientology prevents treating imperfect or partial relief as no relief. This is a legally supported path. It loses in these provisional choices because a live claim does not compel continued discretionary review, and the extant Fourth Circuit remedy can serve Roller's remaining hearing interest without a new Supreme Court constitutional or remedial holding. A merits preference is not necessary to that conclusion.

**Strongest hold-and-reassess path:** A brief hold could permit adjudication of actual January 1 mootness and the consequent vacatur question on established facts. Its objection is that no new record yet guarantees terminal mootness: the savings clause and any outstanding relief would have to be assessed then. It prolongs this altered vehicle rather than supplying present relief or resolving the merits now. It remains a lawful nonterminal management option, but no Justice below selects it as the preferred action.

**Present mootness/vacatur path:** It fails on this packet. Neither enactment before its effective date nor an undertaking to schedule hearings establishes elimination of effectual relief. No later hearing, release, governing enactment, or comparable mooting fact has been verified. The parties' prediction cannot move January 1 to November 30. Munsingwear therefore supplies no present basis to vacate; its future application and any Karcher limitation are reserved, not rejected categorically.

## Tennessee v. Middlebrooks — December 13, 1993

### Components and tested alternatives

**Preferred formal disposition of each modeled Justice:** Dismiss the writ for want of jurisdiction because an adequate and independent Tennessee constitutional ground sustains the resentencing judgment. Each also accepts the narrower public action of dismissing the writ as improvidently granted without an opinion. These alternatives are explicitly distinct: the first could publish a limited jurisdictional application; the second would announce no substantive Supreme Court holding. Neither is a federal merits affirmance or reversal.

**Sufficient jurisdictional ground:** Middlebrooks Part III grounds its prohibition in Article I, §16, including a Tennessee statutory analogy before its extended federal discussion and its conclusion that Lowenfield provides no rationale for constitutionality under Tennessee's Constitution. Howell then states expressly that the state ground independently required Middlebrooks and that the federal inquiry asked whether duplication also violated the Eighth Amendment. Howell applies that state rule before conducting its own sentence-specific harmless-error review. Correcting the federal discussion therefore would leave the same state-law invalid factor and the same uncorrected weighing error in the Middlebrooks judgment. Under Long and the direct-review principle identified in Coleman and Herb, an advisory correction of federal reasoning cannot provide the State the requested relief.

This is a bounded reading of the original judgment and the highest state court's published explanation. It does not hold that any assertion in a later case may retroactively invent a state ground, that a State may defeat jurisdiction through counsel's characterization, or that every interwoven constitutional opinion is immune from federal review. Howell's actual majority statement supplies the decisive clarification; Reid's separate opinion is corroborative, not a substitute majority holding.

**Strongest retain-or-clarify path:** The original opinion discusses federal narrowing at length and cites Lowenfield; Howell is another case rather than an amended judgment in Middlebrooks. If the state rationale depended on what federal law compelled, Long would permit review; if genuine ambiguity remained, clarification could be appropriate. The difficulty is factual and legal, not a preference for Tennessee's rule: the original text contains its own state basis, and the highest state court has now expressly explained its independence while continuing to apply it. No recovered source supplies a concrete reason to disregard that explanation. A second clarification remand would ask a question already answered.

**Finality and merits:** The independent state ground is sufficient to end review without deciding a Cox exception to §1257 finality. No Justice below reaches federal narrowing, felony-murder eligibility, harmlessness, or guilt. If review is nevertheless proposed, clean additional modeling must first establish finality and exact review scope; then assess Lowenfield's permission for genuine narrowing at either stage, Zant, and the inherited Arave and invalid-aggravator rules. Howell's harmlessness determination cannot establish Middlebrooks harmlessness. Munsingwear vacatur is not the consequence of this state-ground dismissal.
