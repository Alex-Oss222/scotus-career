#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5–4 in the judgment. [R51](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/874/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for Rehnquist and, except Part II-B, O’Connor; O’Connor wrote separately. Thomas, with Scalia, concurred in the judgment on a broader rejection of dilution claims. Blackmun dissented with Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg; Ginsburg wrote separately, and Stevens wrote a separate opinion joined by those same three. [R51](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/874/)

**Historical Holding:** The judgment rejected this §2 size challenge. Kennedy’s three-Justice rationale rested on absence of an objective benchmark; Thomas and Scalia would exclude dilution from §2 altogether. The constitutional claim remained for consideration on remand. The two-Justice proposal to overrule Gingles was not a Court holding. [R51](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/874/)

**Historical Authority:** Gingles, Chisom, Growe, §2, and the distinct §5 precedents; fractured rationales must not be treated as a single majority statutory theory. [R51](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/874/)
