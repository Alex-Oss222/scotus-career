## Turner framing and completeness

The packet's core question is adequate only with the supplement's detailed statutory mechanics. The source handoff properly distinguishes enacted congressional findings, lower-court conclusions and contested evidence. Congress's household penetration, incentives and bottleneck findings are not case-specific adjudications that every cable system threatens every local station. A survey showing that some systems dropped, refused or repositioned a signal does not without more establish duration, financial effect, or the amount of displaced cable speech. Absence of proved station failure does not establish absence of threatened future injury.

The competing paths are genuinely open before modeling: content-based compelled allocation and strict scrutiny; structural content neutrality and intermediate scrutiny; within intermediate scrutiny either an adequately supported predictive judgment, remand for unresolved material questions, or invalidation for insufficient justification. Legislative prediction deserves appropriate consideration but does not remove the reviewing court's duty. No source fact dictates a scrutiny level or the ultimate record assessment.

The following corrections/qualifications are mandatory premises:

- The commercial small-system exemption requires both no more than twelve usable activated channels and no more than 300 subscribers and lasts only so long as no broadcast signal is deleted. It is not a general 300-subscriber exemption and does not erase §615 duties.
- Commercial capacity, station-selection, duplicate/network provisions, delivery/payment conditions and channel placement remain distinct. The three commercial historic/elected channel options and mutually agreed alternative are not a single fixed over-the-air requirement.
- Noncommercial quantities vary by channel capacity; importation, continuation of March 29, 1990 carriage, mutual written waiver, duplication exceptions, and technical/payment conditions have their own text. January 1, 1992 is not separately listed as a §615 channel option.
- Optional noncarriage of an eligible duplicate remains optional. Statutory carriage and technical obligations do not themselves require ideological endorsement.
- Low-power eligibility and market adjustments contain express local-programming criteria. The lower court did not separately analyze their constitutional classification. A conclusion about general full-power carriage must not silently validate those provisions; all modeled paths retain a distinct reservation unless later proper source work establishes a reachable severable question.
- Section 614(g)'s initial sales-programming transition expired before the decision date. Its original interim text is not proof of current home-shopping carriage. The actual FCC implementing determination is necessary if a final rationale depends on that current status.
- Must-carry and retransmission consent are distinct election routes. No right to demand both is inferred.
- The government moved to dismiss; other defenders cross-moved for summary judgment. The posture is review of the summary judgment actually entered, not an invented uniform motion history.

The neutral packet/supplement are sufficient for the modeled core classification and the alternatives concerning this undifferentiated summary judgment. ## Kiryas framing and completeness

The supplement supplies the complete challenged act. It grants all union-free-district powers to an elected public board; it does not give rabbis office, limit voters by religion, restrict power to special education, or contain a sunset. Current secular instruction, outside professional staffing and out-of-district children are substantial counterarguments to an establishment characterization. Conversely those facts do not answer whether the Legislature allocated general public authority on a forbidden religious basis. Both possibilities remain fairly represented.

The village's lawful general-law incorporation is distinct from Chapter 748's special district. Religious homogeneity is not itself unlawful. The invalidation path must identify state religious classification or exceptional denominational allocation, while the sustaining path must address broad permanent authority and the absence of a neutral allocation structure. Neither may replace that inquiry with unsupported findings of religious teaching, rabbinical control, future exclusion, or a faith-based voting qualification.

Wieder recognized local educational discretion, not a mandatory separate school or a free-exercise entitlement. Parents' reports of trauma do not become a finding that ordinary schooling violates religious doctrine. IDEA and Carter preserve appropriate statutory education and remedial rights without deciding establishment. Actual Zobrest supplies no aid-merits holding. The state court expressly reserved state constitutional law, so that issue is no independent affirmance ground; if the federal judgment were reversed, it remains for the state courts. ASARCO supplies the actual threshold route for review sought by a directly injured losing state-court party; the original taxpayers' status does not require a fictitious federal trial-court standing holding.
