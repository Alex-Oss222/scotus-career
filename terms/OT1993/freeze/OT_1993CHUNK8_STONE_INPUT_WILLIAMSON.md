#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

Williamson seeks relief from the convictions based on improper hearsay admission and the Confrontation Clause. The United States seeks to retain the convictions under the exception and any properly applicable harmless-error ground. [P40](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/594/)

#### C. ENTERING LAW BY ISSUE

##### Issue 1: Unit of the statement-against-interest inquiry

**Entering Law:** Federal Rule of Evidence 804(a) and (b)(3) require unavailability and a statement sufficiently contrary to the declarant’s penal interest when made that a reasonable person would not make it unless believing it true. The Rule expressly requires corroborating circumstances for statements offered to exculpate the accused; the Eleventh Circuit’s treatment of inculpatory corroboration, including United States v. Taggart, 944 F.2d 837 (1991), must not be assumed away. Rule 104(a) assigns preliminary admissibility questions to the judge; Bourjaily v. United States, 483 U.S. 171 (1987), supplies the ordinary preponderance approach to preliminary facts. Legal interpretation is reviewed independently, with the usual deference to permissible case-specific admissibility determinations. [P40](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/594/)

##### Issue 2: Confrontation and judgment consequences

**Entering Law:** Ohio v. Roberts, 448 U.S. 56 (1980), Lee v. Illinois, 476 U.S. 530 (1986), Idaho v. Wright, 497 U.S. 805 (1990), and White v. Illinois, 502 U.S. 346 (1992), govern the entering Confrontation Clause reliability and unavailability questions. Accomplice accusations require the applicable scrutiny; a hearsay label alone does not establish constitutional reliability. Rule 52(a) and Kotteakos govern a preserved nonconstitutional evidentiary error; Chapman v. California, 386 U.S. 18 (1967), applies if a constitutional error is actually reached and found. The Government bears the applicable direct-review harmlessness burden. [P40](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/594/)

#### D. STONE’S REVISED REASONING, APPLICATION, AND LIMITS

**Stone’s Position and Scope:** retain assertion-specific review and remand; do not constitutionalize the Master's later preferred confrontation rule.

Rule 804(b)(3)'s reliability rationale concerns what exposes the declarant to liability. A generally inculpatory confession can contain accusations designed to bargain for favorable treatment or relocate principal blame. Reading “statement” at the level of material assertions is a legitimate resolution of the Rule's open meaning, supported by its text and rationale rather than a categorical distrust of every accomplice.

The competing approach permits contextual collateral statements because a confession may be unintelligible when disassembled. Stone should meet that objection by rejecting grammatical atomization. An apparently neutral detail may identify the declarant's criminal participation; naming a partner can itself increase exposure. Conversely, admitting possession does not necessarily make a subsequent identification of a supposedly more responsible actor disserving. The inquiry considers the whole setting while determining whether each offered material assertion actually meets the exception. The same surrounding narrative can supply context without all becoming admissible for its truth.

Here the changing accounts, cooperation incentive, and allocation of roles matter. The Government need not have promised a reward for the declarant to perceive an incentive. Refusal to testify despite immunity and an order bears on unavailability, not trustworthiness. Apply the ordinary preliminary-fact burden and preserve any independently applicable corroboration requirement; do not import a later amendment extending the Rule's express corroboration language.

Stone's simulated Salerno position requiring the particular exception's elements supports this rule. The Master's testimonial-confrontation preference could support a separate proposed change, but Roberts, Lee, Wright, and the inherited White framework remain entering law. Rule correction may resolve the matter without displacing them. Remand to identify admissible assertions and evaluate any preserved error under the appropriate standard: Kotteakos for a purely evidentiary error, Chapman only if a constitutional violation is reached and found. Physical corroboration cannot automatically establish either constitutional reliability or harmlessness. No acquittal or automatic retrial follows solely from rejecting the whole-confession analysis.

**Egalitarian application:** The accused must be able to contest blame-shifting accusations, and the prosecution may use genuinely self-inculpatory evidence that satisfies the actual exception. Context can reveal both reliability and bargaining incentives. Do not let a confession's label admit every accusation or let general distrust of an accomplice exclude every reliable assertion.

#### E. RECORD AND SOURCE SUPPORT

**Independent Record and Threshold Context:** Police found 19 kilograms of cocaine in Harris’s rental car. Harris initially described a Cuban supplier and a dumpster delivery, then admitted those details were false and said he was transporting drugs for Williamson, who had traveled ahead in another vehicle. Other evidence linked Williamson to the rental car and luggage. The agent promised to report cooperation but testified that no reward was promised. Harris refused to testify despite use immunity, a court order, and contempt, so the agent recounted his statements. The lower courts assessed the broader confession without determining the status of each material assertion separately. Harris’s refusal despite an order supplies the Rule 804 unavailability issue; it is not a finding that the accusations were reliable. Resolve the hearsay rule before constitutional adjudication if that suffices. Use the pre-Crawford Confrontation Clause framework. Direct-review harmlessness remains distinct from the admissibility rule. [P40](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/594/)
