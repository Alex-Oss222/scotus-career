#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 7–2. [R30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/700/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for Rehnquist, Blackmun, Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Stevens concurred separately. Thomas dissented, joined by Scalia. [R30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/700/)

**Historical Holding:** Section 401(d) permits limitations on the activity as a whole to ensure compliance with water-quality standards, including designated uses protected through minimum flows. **Historical Authority:** CWA §§303, 401, 510; EPA certification and antidegradation regulations; Chevron; California v. FERC distinguished. [R30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/700/)
