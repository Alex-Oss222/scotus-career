## 61. Farmer v. Brennan

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Farmer v. Brennan

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 825 (1994); No. 92-7247

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-06-06

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 9–0 in the judgment. [R32](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/825/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for eight Justices. Blackmun and Stevens concurred separately. Thomas concurred in the judgment. [R32](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/825/)

**Historical Holding:** Deliberate indifference requires awareness of a substantial risk and disregard, with knowledge inferable from circumstantial evidence, including obviousness. Reasonable response can defeat liability. Discovery and prospective-relief issues required reconsideration. [R32](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/825/)

**Historical Authority:** Estelle, Wilson, Helling, Bivens, Carlson, and Rule 56(f). [R32](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/825/)

**Historical Sources:** [R32](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/825/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.

## 62. Department of Revenue of Montana v. Kurth Ranch

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Department of Revenue of Montana v. Kurth Ranch

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 767 (1994); No. 93-144

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-06-06

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5–4. [R33](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/767/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Blackmun, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Rehnquist and O’Connor dissented separately. Scalia dissented, joined by Thomas. [R33](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/767/)

**Historical Holding:** The unusual drug tax was punitive and impermissible as a successive punishment. The Court did not use Halper’s civil-penalty accounting as the test for ordinary taxation and did not decide the unpreserved separate-offense objection. [R33](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/767/)

**Historical Authority:** Double-jeopardy punishment cases, Halper, Magnano, and the specific Montana Act and arrest-triggered rules. [R33](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/767/)

## 64. Key Tronic Corp. v. United States

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Key Tronic Corp. v. United States

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 809 (1994); No. 93-376

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-06-06

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded, 6-3 on the disputed restriction of recovery. [P30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/809/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Scalia, joined by Blackmun and Thomas, dissented in part, reading the statutory authorization more broadly. [P30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/809/)

**Historical Holding:** Private cost-recovery litigation fees and the settlement-negotiation fees at issue were not recoverable, but necessary nonlitigation work identifying other responsible parties could be response costs. [P30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/809/)

**Historical Authority:** CERCLA §§101(25), 107, 113; Alyeska and Runyon; 511 U.S. 809-824. [P30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/809/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 811-814 for the three categories; 814-821 for source and function; 821-824 for the broader statutory reading. [P30](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/809/)
