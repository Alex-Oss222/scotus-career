## 25. American Dredging Co. v. Miller

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** American Dredging Co. v. Miller

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 443 (1994); No. 91-1950

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-02-23

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 7-2. [P13](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/443/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for six Justices in full; Stevens joined Part II-C and the judgment. Souter also concurred separately. Kennedy, joined by Thomas, dissented. [P13](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/443/)

**Historical Holding:** Federal maritime law does not preempt Louisiana’s refusal to apply forum non conveniens in this state in personam action. [P13](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/443/)

**Historical Authority:** 28 U.S.C. §1333; Jensen, Mayfield, Bainbridge; 510 U.S. 443-464. [P13](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/443/)

**Historical Sources:** 510 U.S. 445-450 for facts and the preemption framework; 450-464 for competing analyses and comparator structure. [P13](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/443/)

## 28. Liteky v. United States

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Liteky v. United States

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 540 (1994); No. 92-6921

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-03-07

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9-0 in the judgment. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Thomas, and Ginsburg. Kennedy, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, and Souter, concurred in the judgment and disputed the majority’s demanding formulation. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)

**Historical Holding:** Extrajudicial origin is a factor rather than an absolute prerequisite. The majority required unusually deep favoritism or antagonism for judicial-source conduct; the concurrence adhered more directly to the objective appearance formulation. The particular conduct did not require recusal. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)

**Historical Authority:** 28 U.S.C. §§144, 455; Grinnell, Berger, Liljeberg; 510 U.S. 540-568. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)

**Historical Sources:** 510 U.S. 541-546 for the record and statutory antecedents; 547-568 for the competing formulations. The latter are comparator material, not entering law. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)
