Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

## 36. McDermott, Inc. v. AmClyde

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** McDermott, Inc. v. AmClyde

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 202 (1994); No. 92-1479

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-04-20

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0. [P17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/202/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for a unanimous Court; no separate opinion. [P17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/202/)

**Historical Holding:** Nonsettling maritime defendants receive a proportionate-share reduction rather than a dollar-for-dollar settlement credit. The rule does not generally abolish joint-and-several liability. [P17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/202/)

**Historical Authority:** Reliable Transfer; Edmonds distinguished; 511 U.S. 202-221. [P17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/202/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 204-207 and the contract discussion for posture; 207-221 for competing common-law rules and the comparator. Ignore the reproduction’s nonjudicial annotation listing Breyer, who was not yet on the Court. [P17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/202/)
