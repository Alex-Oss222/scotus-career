## 27. Campbell v. Acuff-Rose Music, Inc.

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Campbell v. Acuff-Rose Music, Inc.

**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 569 (1994); No. 92-1292

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-03-07

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, 9–0. [R14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/569/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter delivered the opinion; Kennedy concurred separately. [R14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/569/)

**Historical Holding:** Commercial parody can qualify as fair use. The lower court’s commerciality and market presumptions were improper; all factors require context-sensitive analysis, and further consideration was needed. [R14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/569/)

**Historical Authority:** 17 U.S.C. §107; Sony; Harper & Row; the fair-use tradition including Folsom v. Marsh. [R14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/569/)

**Historical Sources:** [R14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/569/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.
