**Citation or Docket:** 512 U.S. 79 (1994); No. 93-489

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-06-13

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9–0. [R36](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/79/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for a unanimous Court. Stevens concurred, joined by Blackmun, O’Connor, and Souter. [R36](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/79/)

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.
