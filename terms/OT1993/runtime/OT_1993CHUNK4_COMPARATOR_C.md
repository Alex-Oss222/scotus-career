## 45. C & A Carbone, Inc. v. Town of Clarkstown

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** C & A Carbone, Inc. v. Town of Clarkstown

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 383 (1994); No. 92-1402

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-05-16

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6–3. [R23](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/383/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for Stevens, Scalia, Thomas, and Ginsburg. O’Connor concurred in the judgment on a different commerce analysis. Souter dissented, joined by Rehnquist and Blackmun. [R23](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/383/)

**Historical Holding:** The exclusive route to the favored private local facility violated the dormant Commerce Clause; majority reasoning treated it as discrimination against interstate processing services. [R23](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/383/)

**Historical Authority:** Philadelphia, Dean Milk, the waste-commerce decisions, and alternative financing and regulation principles. [R23](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/383/)

**Historical Sources:** [R23](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/383/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.

## 48. Security Services, Inc. v. Kmart Corp.

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Security Services, Inc. v. Kmart Corp.

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 431 (1994); No. 93-284

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-05-16

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 7-2. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for seven; Stevens concurred separately. Thomas and Ginsburg filed separate dissents. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)

**Historical Holding:** A tariff rendered ineffective by nonparticipation in its incorporated mileage guide cannot support undercharges; cancellation before shipment is not retroactive invalidation. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)

**Historical Authority:** Interstate Commerce Act; ICC participation rules; Maislin, American Trucking, Berwind-White; 511 U.S. 431-461. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 433-435 for record; 435-443 for tariff and timing analysis; 444-461 for separate views. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)
