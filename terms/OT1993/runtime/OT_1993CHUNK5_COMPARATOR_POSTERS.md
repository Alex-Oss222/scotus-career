### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 513 (1994); No. 92-903

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-05-23

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9-0 in the judgment. [P26](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/513/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, and Ginsburg. Scalia, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, concurred in the judgment with a different reading of the intended-use language. [P26](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/513/)

**Historical Holding:** Section 857 defines paraphernalia objectively but requires knowledge that the goods are likely to be used with illegal drugs; the statute was not vague as applied. [P26](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/513/)

**Historical Authority:** Former 21 U.S.C. §857; Gypsum, Morissette, and Hoffman Estates; 511 U.S. 513-530. [P26](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/513/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 514-516 for record; 516-526 for the statutory provisions, scienter, exemptions, and vagueness; 526-530 for the separate reading. [P26](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/513/)
