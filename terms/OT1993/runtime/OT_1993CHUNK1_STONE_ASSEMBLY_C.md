#### C. ENTERING LAW BY ISSUE

##### Issue 1: Record integrity and conflict analysis

**Entering Law:** Pre-AEDPA 28 U.S.C. §2254(d) presumptively credits qualifying state factual determinations, subject to its enumerated exceptions, including inadequate development of material facts. Burden, 498 U.S. 433, specifically directs treatment of this immunity determination. Cuyler v. Sullivan, 446 U.S. 335, 348-350 (1980), requires an actual conflict that adversely affected counsel’s performance; once those requirements are met, separate Strickland outcome prejudice is not required. Joint representation or a speculative conflict is insufficient. Whether a finding exists must be resolved before assigning it any appellate deference. [P06](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-supreme-court/510/132.html)

#### D. STONE’S REVISED REASONING, APPLICATION, AND LIMITS

**Stone’s Position and Scope:** retain reversal and remand on the accurate record.

Stone's proposed reasoning is especially sound in distinguishing a defective adjudication from a proved Sixth Amendment violation. The earlier *Burden* mandate, 498 U.S. 433, and pre-AEDPA §2254(d) govern; simulated *Dobbs* and *Keeney* support attention to actual evidence without inventing a factual finding or silently adding later habeas restrictions. The court of appeals cannot defer to a finding the district court never made. [Record, 510 U.S. 132](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/132/).

Under *Cuyler v. Sullivan*, 446 U.S. 335, the inquiry is actual conflict and adverse effect. An informal promise not to prosecute may compromise counsel as much as formal transactional immunity. Determine the arrangement's timing, counsel's involvement, the witness's interests, and whether conflicting duties affected a plausible defense course. Once the governing *Sullivan* predicates are met, do not add ordinary *Strickland* outcome prejudice; before they are met, joint representation alone is insufficient.

This is **record correction and application**, not a new conflict test. The proposal's reference to a “plausible defense course” should identify how the evidence demonstrates adverse effect, not impose a newly mandatory evidentiary formula. The hard case is counsel's tactical choice equally explainable by the defendant's own interests and the witness's bargain. Findings must resolve that connection rather than presume disloyalty.

The State's finality argument and the statutory presumption for qualifying state findings deserve their ordinary force. Neither permits substituting an appellate reconstruction for an actual finding. Remand should resolve the disputed record and the statutory exceptions before selecting relief. No unconditional release or automatic finding of conflict is justified at this stage.

**Egalitarian application:** Meaningful representation requires examination of counsel's actual competing obligations and adverse effect, while the State is entitled to adjudication on a real record rather than presumed disloyalty. Remand preserves both reliable conviction review and the ordinary burdens: it does not minimize the conflict or assume that every divided loyalty caused the constitutional defect.

#### C. ENTERING LAW BY ISSUE

##### Issue 1: Willfulness in the operative statute

**Entering Law:** 31 U.S.C. §§5313, 5322(a), 5324(3), and 31 C.F.R. §103.22. Section 5324 supplies the purpose to evade; §5322 separately requires willfulness. Spies v. United States, 317 U.S. 492 (1943), makes willfulness context-dependent; Cheek v. United States, 498 U.S. 192 (1991), and Liparota v. United States, 471 U.S. 419 (1985), address knowledge requirements in particular statutory settings. [R05](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/135/)

#### D. STONE’S REVISED REASONING, APPLICATION, AND LIMITS

**Stone’s Position and Scope:** retain reversal; keep the mens rea rule statute-specific.

The operative §5324 already requires a purpose to evade reporting, while §5322 separately requires willfulness. Reading willfulness as awareness that structuring is unlawful gives it additional work within a coordinated penalty scheme. *Spies*, *Cheek*, and *Liparota* permit context-sensitive treatment; no general constitutional rule requires knowledge of illegality. This fits Stone's enacted-elements and fair-notice commitments. [Operative text and competing arguments, 510 U.S. 135](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/92-1196.ZO.html).

This is **a contestable interpretation of an open statutory question**, not something the Constitution or lenity makes self-evident. The strongest opposing reading is that deliberate evasion supplies wrongful purpose and willfulness confirms intentional conduct, while statutes sometimes repeat related concepts. Stone should explain why this scheme's common penalty language and potential lawful reasons for arranging transactions make the additional knowledge requirement better supported. Use lenity only if the ordinary tools leave genuine uncertainty; do not make surplusage avoidance absolute.

In application, prosecutors may prove knowledge through warnings, communications, concealment directed at an understood prohibition, or other circumstantial evidence. Awareness of the bank's reporting duty and subdivision of transactions alone cannot conclusively establish awareness of the separate anti-structuring law. The difficult defendant knows that bank employees disapprove but reasonably misunderstands whether the customer is prohibited from acting. That remains a factual inference under the proper instruction, not a presumption defeating the element.

Set aside the judgment based on the omitted element, leaving lawful retrial and properly presented remedial questions for the lower court. Do not order acquittal merely from instructional error or import later amendments or later omitted-element harmless-error doctrine into January 1994.

**Egalitarian application:** Fair warning and culpability concern what this defendant understood about the prohibited conduct; effective enforcement remains available through circumstantial proof of that knowledge. Statutory context, the penalty scheme, and the risk of treating lawful money handling as criminal all inform meaning. Lenity resolves genuine residual uncertainty, not a preference to defeat an otherwise established offense.

#### D. STONE’S REVISED REASONING, APPLICATION, AND LIMITS

**Stone’s Position and Scope:** retain affirmance of channeling, with a serious answer to coercive access costs.
