#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0 in the judgment. [P27](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/641/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for eight Justices; Blackmun concurred in the judgment. [P27](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/641/)

**Historical Holding:** Statewide averaging cannot save a use tax that discriminates against interstate commerce within particular localities. The tax is not invalid in every application merely because it exceeds local sales taxes in some places. [P27](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/641/)
