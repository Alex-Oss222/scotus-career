The prospective issue requires two distinct conclusions. A record-specific refusal is **application of inherited law**. Those cases concern extraordinary writs and demonstrated repetitive abuse; ordinary civil certiorari is distinguishable without displacing their core. *Boddie* does not constitutionalize free access to every discretionary civil appeal. Stone can reasonably prefer a free leave-to-file screen for specifically identified repetitive claims: compare parties, transactions, grounds already rejected, and genuinely new facts or law; send doubtful novelty to a judge, not an unreviewable clerical merits decision. Assess the actual petition under the lawful screening rule; neither poverty nor a history of losses substitutes for its merits, and neither access nor fairness requires accepting a currently abusive paper.
