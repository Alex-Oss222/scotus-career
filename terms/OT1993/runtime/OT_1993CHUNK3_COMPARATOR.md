**Citation or Docket:** 510 U.S. 540 (1994); No. 92-6921

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-03-07

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9-0 in the judgment. [P14](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/510/540/)
