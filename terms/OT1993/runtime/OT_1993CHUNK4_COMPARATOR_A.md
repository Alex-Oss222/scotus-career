## 38. United States v. Irvine

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** United States v. Irvine

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 224 (1994); No. 92-1546

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-04-20

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 8-0; Blackmun did not participate. [P19](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/224/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for seven in full; Scalia joined except Part III-A and concurred separately in part and in the judgment. [P19](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/224/)

**Historical Holding:** A disclaimer made after gift-tax enactment can be taxed although the disclaimed interest arose before enactment. State-law relation back does not control the federal tax consequence. [P19](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/224/)

**Historical Authority:** 26 U.S.C. §§2501, 2511; Revenue Act §501(b); Jewett; 511 U.S. 224-243. [P19](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/224/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 226-232 for dates, record, and provisions; 232-243 for the alternative statutory/regulatory analysis and comparator. [P19](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/224/)

## 39. Landgraf v. USI Film Products

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Landgraf v. USI Film Products

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 244 (1994); No. 92-757

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-04-26

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 8–1. [R20](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/244/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Souter, and Ginsburg. Scalia concurred in the judgment, joined by Kennedy and Thomas. Blackmun dissented. [R20](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/244/)

**Historical Holding:** §102’s new damages and associated jury-trial provisions do not apply to the preenactment conduct at issue; the opinion articulated the express-reach/retroactive-effect inquiry. [R20](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/244/)

**Historical Authority:** 1991 Act; Bradley; Bowen; the historical antiretroactivity presumption. [R20](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/244/)
