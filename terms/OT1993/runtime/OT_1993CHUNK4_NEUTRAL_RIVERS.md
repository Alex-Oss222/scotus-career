**Question Presented:** Whether §101 of the Civil Rights Act of 1991 applies to preenactment conduct in a case pending when enacted, notwithstanding the prior construction of §1981 in Patterson v. McLean Credit Union.

**Question Granted / Review Scope:** The primary opinion identifies the sole review question as applicability of §101 to cases pending at enactment. The separate Title VII, labor-contract, and union claims are not before the Court. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

**Court and Judgment Under Review:** Sixth Circuit, 973 F.2d 490 (1992), applying preamendment §1981 under Patterson but remanding for a jury trial on discrimination in contract enforcement, which it found adequately alleged even under Patterson. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

**Relief Requested:** The employees seek application of amended §1981 to the broader discharge claim; Roadway seeks to retain the temporal ruling. The employees already obtained a limited remand below, which must not be erased by describing the judgment as total dismissal. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

**Material Record:** Rivers and Davison, garage mechanics, were discharged in 1986 after disputes over notice for disciplinary hearings. They alleged racially discriminatory discharge and retaliation for insisting on contractual procedures afforded white employees. Patterson issued before trial. The district court dismissed the §1981 claims and separately found for the employer on Title VII. The Sixth Circuit revived the narrower contract-enforcement theory and declined retrospective application of §101. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

**Threshold, Preservation, and Vehicle Matters:** Only the temporal reach of §101 is under review. The limited jury-trial remand under the old statute remains part of the judgment unless independently altered through a lawful review question. Patterson’s validity is not automatically reopened because Congress later amended the statute. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

#### C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The employees argue that §101 restores the intended reach of the older civil-rights guarantee and should govern pending disputes, particularly conduct predating Patterson. They rely on enacted definitions, effective-date provisions, and the character of restorative legislation. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)

Roadway invokes the new legal coverage created by the amendment and the absence of a clear retrospective direction. A later Congress’s disagreement with a judicial construction does not necessarily declare what the earlier enacted law always meant. The employer’s temporal argument does not dispose of the employees’ separate theory that fits within the old enforcement protection. [R21](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/298/)
