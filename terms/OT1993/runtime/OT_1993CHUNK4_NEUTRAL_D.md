The distinction is not between sympathetic and unsympathetic bankrupt carriers but between valid and incomplete tariffs. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)

#### D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

Affirm absence of an effective tariff; enforce the tariff if the alleged defect is legally only technical; or seek appropriate agency determination of a genuinely unresolved regulatory issue. Do not conflate a negotiated-rate equitable defense with the prerequisite that an enforceable filed rate exist. [P24](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/431/)
