## 58. Waters v. Churchill

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Waters v. Churchill

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 661 (1994); No. 92-1450

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-05-31

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 7–2 in the judgment. [R29](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/661/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor announced the judgment in an opinion joined by Rehnquist, Souter, and Ginsburg. Souter also concurred. Scalia, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, concurred in the judgment but rejected a freestanding investigation requirement. Stevens dissented, joined by Blackmun. [R29](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/661/)

**Historical Holding:** The plurality used a reasonably investigated and genuinely held employer account, but found a factual dispute over retaliatory motive. The judgment did not resolve qualified immunity or the ungranted policy/custom issue. [R29](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/661/)

**Historical Authority:** Pickering, Connick, Mt. Healthy; the plurality’s reasonable-inquiry rationale must be distinguished from the narrower agreement on the remand. [R29](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/661/)
