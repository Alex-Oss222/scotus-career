## 53. Dalton v. Specter

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Dalton v. Specter

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 462 (1994); No. 93-289

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-05-23

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0 in the judgment. [P25](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/462/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote for the Court in Part II and for O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas in the remaining portions. Souter, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, and Ginsburg, concurred in part and in the judgment on statutory preclusion. Blackmun also wrote separately to preserve distinct ultra vires and timely procedural challenges. [P25](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/462/)

**Historical Holding:** The challenged recommendations were not final agency action under the majority’s analysis; alleged statutory error did not itself establish a constitutional violation, and the President’s approval decision within the Act’s discretion was not reviewable. The concurrence relied on the Act’s specific preclusion structure. [P25](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/462/)

**Historical Authority:** APA; Franklin; the Defense Base Closure and Realignment Act of 1990; 511 U.S. 462-484. [P25](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/462/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 464-469 for posture; 469-477 for the majority’s analysis; 477-484 for the narrower preclusion ground and limits. [P25](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/462/)
