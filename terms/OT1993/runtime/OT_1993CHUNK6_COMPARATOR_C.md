## 69. Livadas v. Bradshaw

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Livadas v. Bradshaw

**Citation or Docket:** 512 U.S. 107 (1994); No. 92-1920

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-06-13

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9–0. [R39](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/107/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for a unanimous Court. [R39](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/107/)

**Historical Holding:** The nonenforcement policy conflicted with federal labor rights; mere consultation of an undisputed wage rate did not trigger §301 preemption, and §1983 supplied a channel to vindicate the NLRA right. [R39](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/107/)

**Historical Authority:** Nash, Lingle, Allis-Chalmers, Golden State, and the governing federal and California labor statutes. [R39](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/107/)

**Historical Sources:** [R39](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/107/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.

**Case Name:** United States v. Carlton

**Citation or Docket:** 512 U.S. 26 (1994); No. 92-1941

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-06-13

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9-0 in the judgment. [P31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/26/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. O’Connor concurred in the judgment and emphasized temporal limits. Scalia, joined by Thomas, concurred in the judgment on a different constitutional approach. [P31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/26/)

**Historical Authority:** R.A. Gray, Turner Elkhorn, Welch, Hemme, Darusmont; 512 U.S. 26-42. [P31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/26/)
