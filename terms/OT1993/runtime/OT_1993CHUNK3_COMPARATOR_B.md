## 29. Victor v. Nebraska / Sandoval v. California

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Victor v. Nebraska / Sandoval v. California

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 1 (1994); Nos. 92-8894 (Victor) and 92-9049 (Sandoval)

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-03-22

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Both judgments affirmed. Sandoval: 9–0; Victor: 7–2. [R15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/1/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote Part II for a unanimous Court and the remaining Court opinion with differing joinders. Rehnquist, Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas joined fully; Ginsburg joined Parts II, III-B, and IV and separately concurred. Kennedy concurred. Blackmun concurred in part and dissented as to Victor; Souter joined all but Part II of Blackmun’s separate writing. [R15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/1/)

**Historical Holding:** Neither charge, read as a whole, violated the reasonable-doubt requirement, though the Court criticized moral-certainty terminology. [R15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/1/)

**Historical Authority:** Winship, Holland, Cage, Estelle, and Sullivan; the two distinct state judgments and their different instructions. [R15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/1/)

**Historical Sources:** [R15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/1/); United States Reports citation and date above. Opinion alignments are historical only.

## 30. United States v. Granderson

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** United States v. Granderson

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 39 (1994); No. 92-1662

**Historical Event Date:** 1994-03-22

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling is frozen. This material is not simulated precedent and does not control the unapproved Stone position.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 7-2. [P15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/39/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for Blackmun, Stevens, O’Connor, and Souter. Scalia and Kennedy separately concurred in the judgment. Rehnquist, joined by Thomas, dissented. [P15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/39/)

**Historical Holding:** The minimum is one-third of the original Guidelines imprisonment maximum and the maximum is that Guidelines maximum; Granderson had already served more than permitted. [P15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/39/)

**Historical Authority:** Former 18 U.S.C. §3565(a); lenity and the original Guidelines; 511 U.S. 39-75. [P15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/39/)

**Historical Sources:** 511 U.S. 41-44 for facts and text; 44-57 for construction and judgment; 57-75 for separate views. [P15](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/39/)

## 32. Oregon Waste Systems, Inc. v. Department of Environmental Quality

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Oregon Waste Systems, Inc. v. Department of Environmental Quality

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 93 (1994); No. 93-70 (reported with companion No. 93-108)

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-04-04

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7–2. [R17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/93/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for Stevens, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Rehnquist dissented, joined by Blackmun. [R17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/93/)

**Historical Holding:** The facially discriminatory surcharge failed the dormant Commerce Clause; no substantially equivalent intrastate tax or other sufficient justification was shown. [R17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/93/)

**Historical Authority:** Philadelphia, Chemical Waste, Fort Gratiot, and the compensatory-tax authorities. [R17](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/93/)
