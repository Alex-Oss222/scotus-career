## 60. Nichols v. United States

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

**Case Name:** Nichols v. United States

**Citation or Docket:** 511 U.S. 738 (1994); No. 92-8556

**Historical Decision Date:** 1994-06-06

Comparator only. Introduce after provisional non-Stone modeling has been frozen; not simulated precedent and not a proposed Court result.

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6–3. [R31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/738/)

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote for O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Souter concurred in the judgment. Blackmun dissented, joined by Stevens and Ginsburg; Ginsburg also dissented separately. [R31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/738/)

**Historical Holding:** The Court overruled Baldasar and permitted use of a Scott-valid uncounseled misdemeanor to enhance a later sentence. It rejected the future-warning claim and did not reach the government’s alternative waiver argument. [R31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/738/)

**Historical Authority:** Scott, Baldasar, McMillan, and traditional sentencing consideration of prior conduct. [R31](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/511/738/)
