## Exact operative limits for assembly

All eight reverse the judgment sustaining the additional1.5% use tax by statewide aggregation, with remand for locality-specific application and lawful relief. Seven support the full majority framework; Blackmun supports the judgment and narrow actual-discrimination basis without joining that entire framework. This preserves his frozen reason for reversal while correcting any reading of the freeze as an automatic whole-opinion join.

The ordinary matched4.225% statewide levies are unchallenged. Use-tax excess in53.5% of jurisdictions and higher sales taxes on over93% of sales dollars are different statistics; neither proves every plaintiff transaction or refund amount. The rule allows interstate burdens below local burdens and does not compel exact equality by raising interstate taxes. Missouri can delegate taxing power but cannot delegate an authority to discriminate it lacks. A new replacement rate, invalidation in every locality, severability resolution, mandated tax increase, or quantified refund is not adjudicated.

McKesson does not automatically require a refund whenever a tax is invalid: remand must examine whether meaningful predeprivation challenge was available and, if not, what constitutionally adequate backward-looking correction achieves nondiscrimination. Partial or complete refund, lawful additional assessments or another permissible correction may be considered under governing limits; this Court chooses none on the supplied record.
