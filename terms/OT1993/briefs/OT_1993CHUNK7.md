Gardner and Skipper give Stone a stronger existing foundation here than Caldwell alone supplies in Romano. For relief, the jury's question and the blocked rebuttal strongly support finding an effect on selection; eligibility aggravators do not establish harmlessness. But Stone should articulate why the State cannot show harmlessness on the whole record rather than announce automatic reversal for every omitted parole explanation. That preserves his distinction between a substantive protection and the showing needed for its remedy.

**Egalitarian application:** The State may argue danger supported by the evidence, including danger in prison, while the defense may answer with the actual legal effect of life without parole. Jurors need an accurate choice, not reassurance beyond the law or speculation about inevitable release.
