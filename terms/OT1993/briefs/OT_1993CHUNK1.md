**[Zatko v. California, 502 U.S. 16 (1991) — Whether extreme repetitive frivolous filing permits denial of pending Rule 39.8 fee-waiver motions](inherited/HOLDINGS.md#whether-extreme-repetitive-frivolous-filing-permits-denial-of-pending-rule-398-fee-waiver-motions)** — Effective: November 4, 1991.

When the Court is satisfied that pending submissions are frivolous and the applicants' repeated frivolous filings constitute extreme abuse of its process, Rule 39.8 permits denial of in-forma-pauperis status, meaning permission to file without paying fees. Denial may require payment of the docketing fee and compliance with ordinary petition form rules by a stated deadline; it does not decide petition merits or impose a broader prospective filing ban.

**Unresolved boundary:** The ruling is discretionary. It adopts no numerical threshold, does not make earlier denials alone conclusive, and decides no underlying claim or constitutional entitlement to unrestricted free filing.
