## Unresolved findings

### R01 — Medium — Good's statement of mandatory commencement/reporting duties omits operative conditions and exceptions

The [Good Record](../records/United_States_v_James_Daniel_Good_Real_Property_merits_1993-12-13.md), lines 37 and 140, states that §1603 requires prompt reporting and that §1604 requires commencement forthwith whenever the prescribed probable basis appears, then describes these as mandatory duties. The entry does not supply the statutory qualifications needed to apply that account:

- Section 1603(b)'s reporting duty is triggered when the specified seizure or discovery requires proceedings by the United States attorney.
- Section 1604 concerns recovery for which district-court or Court of International Trade proceedings are necessary. It preserves the Attorney General's determination, after inquiry, that proceedings probably cannot be sustained **or** that public justice does not require institution or prosecution. In that event the facts must be reported to the Treasury Secretary for direction. Those are distinct alternatives, not an optional promptness standard.

These conditions appear in the [official 1994 §1603 text](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-1994-title19-section1603&num=0&edition=1994) and [official 1994 §1604 text](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-1994-title19-section1604&num=0&edition=1994); their amendment histories establish that the relevant language predates this decision. Full HTML section text, including notes, was retrieved into audit scratch. The historical opinion's short summary is not a substitute for the statute's own conditions under this repository's completeness rule.

The incomplete account also appears in [chunk 1 Render Input](../render-inputs/OT_1993CHUNK1.md), line 544; [chunk 1 render](../output/OT_1993CHUNK1.md), line 431; [Holdings candidate](HOLDINGS.candidate.md), line 14173; [continuity](../workspace/continuity.md), line 248; and [neutral projection](../workspace/neutral-projection.md), line 250. The Standards entry preserves the narrower no-dismissal consequence without itself restating the overbroad trigger. The finding concerns the complete legal statement, not an unsupported assertion that the nine-vote no-dismissal result must change. No exception is found applicable to Good by this audit.

### R03 — Medium — The Standards candidate blurs Gottshall's two alternative physical-danger branches

[Standards and Tests candidate](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md), line 3204, combines physical impact **or** immediate physical risk with an unqualified following requirement that the emotional injury arise from fear for the employee's own safety. It does not state that the latter qualification attaches to the threatened-impact branch. The [Gottshall/Carlisle Record](../records/Consolidated_Rail_Corp_v_Gottshall_merits_1994-06-24.md), in its first controlling proposition, expressly makes that distinction: actual physical impact is one route; on the threatened-impact route the emotional injury must arise from fear of harm to the employee himself.

At best the candidate is ambiguous; read across both alternatives it adds a condition the Record did not impose on the impact route. This is material in a reusable test and fails the requirement to state alternatives and their triggers in their own terms. The Holdings candidate retains the source rule. This finding supplies neither a new entitlement nor an outcome for any hypothetical impact claim.
