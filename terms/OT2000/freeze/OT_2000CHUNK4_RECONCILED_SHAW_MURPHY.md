## Operative scope for assembly

Turner requires a valid rational connection to a legitimate and neutral penological objective, with alternatives for exercise, accommodation's effects and ready alternatives considered at their proper scope. Its deferential approach remains; it does not make a prison official's unsupported assertion conclusive. Legal-advice content supplies neither a constitutional bonus nor a reason to disregard an actually necessary means of access. Avery and Lewis apply under their own predicates, while Pearson's independently proved fabricated-retaliation wrong does not require loss of a lawsuit. No such access injury or retaliation finding is newly made here.

Murphy was not assigned to Tracy because their security classifications barred the assignment, and another clerk was offered. The letter's actual mixed content, rule construction, legitimate administrative interests, and unresolved as-applied claims remain for ordinary proceedings. The absence of a finding that the letter was directed to Galle or intended to harass him, and the uncontradicted affidavit about anticipated readership, are not affirmative findings that no legitimate prison interest could be affected. Conversely, the fact that officials could read the correspondence is not a finding of intent to harass the officer named in it.

The disposition rejects the Ninth Circuit's enhanced-protection premise, not all inmate speech protection, all necessary inmate assistance, or the preserved due-process claims. It neither approves the sanction conclusively nor orders its cancellation, new damages, or particular prospective relief. Any later prospective remedy remains subject to the original PLRA conditions. Denius supplies no new prison-specific right beyond this analysis.
