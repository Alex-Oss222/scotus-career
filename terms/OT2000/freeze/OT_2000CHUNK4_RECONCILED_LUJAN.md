Historically Rehnquist wrote for all nine Justices to reverse the Ninth Circuit. The Court assumed state action and a protected claim for payment, and held ordinary available judicial contract process adequate for this disputed debt. It distinguished possessed property and occupational interests, described assignment as one route, and treated the apparent ordinary action against the prime contractor as an alternative while expressly reserving final construction of state law to California courts. The future July 1 procedure did no work in the judgment.

The actual American Manufacturers decision preserves fair adjudication of protected claims, existing service-specific entitlements, prior awards, and supported continuation interests. A historical-result match would be inadequate if the frozen rows relied instead on a universal absence of property. They do not. Each assumes the contract claim and identifies actual adjudication as the sufficient protection. An unpaid construction debt has no established prior award, possessed asset, or medically consequential continuation interest on this record. The actual limiting law therefore does not require a different result.

## Operative scope and qualification

Eight Associates support reversing the constitutional declaration and dependent injunction, with ordinary further proceedings under an adequate-contract-process holding. The common rule is that an actually available ordinary judicial procedure capable of resolving and enforcing this disputed contractual entitlement supplies due process; the mere absence of a duplicate statutory administrative hearing does not establish the constitutional violation. It is not a universal permission to withhold possessed property, terminate an established award, or destroy an otherwise protected claim.

The apparent availability of the action against the prime contractor is a supported state-law premise, not an authoritative final construction binding California courts. J & K Painting rejects automatic remedial exclusivity from the lack of assignment; it does not adjudicate every defense the prime may assert. Preserve the frozen objection that an inaccessible action or conclusive state assessment would defeat the ground. Do not infer an assignment, a timely ninety-day suit, or successful recovery. Those limitations are already in the neutral sources and frozen alternative-path test; this reconciliation adds no new fact or ground.

The frozen recognition that the challenged withholding involves a state command distinguishes American Manufacturers. It need not become a separately necessary general state-action holding: property may remain assumed, and the adequate-process ground suffices. No §1983 exhaustion rule follows. The July 1, 2001 amendments remain future law on this event date.
