Ricci's controlling rule concerns a fine-only civil licensing offense. ## Components and strongest supported alternatives

1. **Constitutional rule.** A functional rule may extend Ricci to a known fine-only criminal misdemeanor because a label does not itself justify a greater seizure. Its strongest objection is the administrability and historical legitimacy of ordinary criminal arrest: officers should not need to prove a citation impossible or conduct a detailed penalty and proportionality adjudication at roadside. Alternatively, the ordinary observed-criminal-offense arrest rule may treat probable cause and ordinary enforcement processing as sufficient, retaining independent extraordinary-manner, unrelated-purpose, unreasonable-duration and other applicable constitutional limits. Its strongest objection is that it lets legislative classification accomplish the very automatic authorization Ricci rejects, despite materially identical fine-only consequences.
2. **This custody.** On a functional approach the accepted account supports setting aside defendants' categorical summary judgment: identification was supplied, local residence was known, the children were taken by a friend, and no supported finding ties transport and confinement to a particular appearance, verification or continuing-danger need. This conclusion cannot rest on hostility, an imaginary earlier offense, or a supposed obligation to use the least intrusive method. The competing application treats ordinary booking and securing appearance on the actual criminal charge as a reasonable enforcement function even for an identified local person, without requiring proof that roadside alternatives were impossible. Its objection is circularity if the only reason for custody is the paperwork created by a discretionary decision to take someone into custody. The published Ricci dissent supports the latter path but does not establish this case's processing facts.
3. **Judgment and remedies.** Constitutional error does not supply damages. A disposition correcting the categorical rule and reversing the adverse custody judgment returns existing claims for the actual remaining proceedings, not an automatic new hearing or an invitation to invent justifications. Officer immunity uses March 1997 law; later cases cannot create earlier notice. A municipal claim requires an attributable policy or custom, applicable fault, causation and injury and cannot borrow personal immunity. No disposition disturbs the uncontested seatbelt convictions or awards release, expungement or a specified sum.

On the supported plaintiff-favorable account, the asserted safety and appearance interests do not justify this station custody merely by restating the legitimate purposes of the traffic law. The supplied identification, known residence and completed arrangement for the children remove the factual shortcuts that otherwise might disguise that conclusion.

**Own date-eligible basis:** His direct join in Ricci Part I makes added restraint a distinct constitutional inquiry; his join in Part II allows remand without automatic liability when the record leaves genuine application work. His McArthur dissent gives real weight to the gravity of an offense against an additional seizure, even where probable cause and administratively careful execution exist. His full joins in Whren, Knowles and Wallis preserve initial authority, additional intrusion and remedies as separate questions.

**Adversarial comparison and join barrier:** Ordinary criminal processing is the strongest contrary path, supported by administrability and the absence of extraordinary physical execution. He rejects a rule making an offense's criminal label conclusive where imprisonment is unavailable as punishment and the actual record supplies no comparable enforcement need. He does not require impossibility of citation, infer ill motive or adopt a universal prohibition on minor-offense arrests. He can join an opinion explaining why the supported summary-judgment facts cannot sustain the automatic custody ground; he cannot join a statement that missing papers made her unidentified, that the earlier warning proved recidivism, or that safety and appearance themselves require jail.

**Application and remedy limit:** This is a constitutional sufficiency conclusion on the account required at summary judgment, not a finding that every disputed fact has been tried. His prior refusal to extend immunity in Wilson v. Layne does not establish that this March 1997 application was clearly settled. ## Boundary receipt for reconciliation and assembly
