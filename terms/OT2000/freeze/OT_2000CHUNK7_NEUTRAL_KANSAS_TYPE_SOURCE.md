# Kansas damages components: neutral source addendum

The claimed historical-dollar principal, an inflation adjustment conceded by Colorado, and additional return for lost investment opportunities are distinct. The reported amounts illustrate the pending claim; they are not an adjudicated award. Neither availability nor accrual date of the additional interest is supplied by this factual extract.

The following reported recitation of the Third Report at pages 87?88 was mechanically copied from the saved official report at page 9, footnote 2. The original Third Report remains the direct underlying source.

Source: [official report, page 9 footnote 2](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-533/pdf/USREPORTS-533-1.pdf).
