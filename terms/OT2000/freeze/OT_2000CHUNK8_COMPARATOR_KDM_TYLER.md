# K.D.M. v. Reedsport School District

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator is identified. The Ninth Circuit reversed the challenged judgment on November 15, 1999, rejecting the constitutional challenge as applied by a two-judge majority.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Senior District Judge Schwarzer, sitting by designation, wrote the majority opinion, joined by Judge Hawkins. Judge Kleinfeld dissented from approval of the religious classification.

**Historical Holding:** The majority emphasized that adequate special-education services and safe access were available at the alternative secular location. It rejected the as-applied constitutional objection to withholding onsite services at the religious school. The dissent treated the religious classification as an unconstitutional distinction.

**Historical Authority:** This is the historical lower-court anchor. Its as-applied conclusion does not displace the governing rules of Church of the Lukumi Babalu Aye, Inc. v. City of Hialeah, Agostini v. Felton, or Mitchell v. Helms.

**Historical Sources:** [K.D.M. v. Reedsport School District](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/196/1046/601439/).

# Tyler v. Cain

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for Rehnquist, O'Connor, Scalia, and Kennedy. O'Connor concurred, explaining how multiple holdings can necessarily establish retroactivity. Breyer dissented with Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg.

* **Historical Holding:** Cage v. Louisiana had not been made retroactive by the Supreme Court within the statutory exception. The Court declined to announce retroactivity in this vehicle.

* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparison only; it does not establish entering law.

* **Historical Sources:** [Tyler v. Cain](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-533/pdf/USREPORTS-533-656.pdf).
