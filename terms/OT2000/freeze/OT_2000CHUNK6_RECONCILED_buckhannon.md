## Complete historical comparison

The full official report, 532 U.S. 598–644, was read, including the Court's opinion, Scalia's concurrence, Ginsburg's dissent and every footnote. Historically the judgment was affirmed 5–4. Rehnquist's opinion was joined by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas; Scalia also wrote a concurrence joined by Thomas; Ginsburg dissented with Stevens, Souter and Breyer. These are historical writings, not present assignments.

The historical majority required a judicially sanctioned material alteration of the parties' legal relationship and rejected catalyst eligibility under the FHAA and ADA. It recognized judgments and consent decrees, distinguished voluntary action from judicial relief, and treated practical-policy arguments as insufficient to change its statutory reading. Scalia's additional account emphasized the litigation meaning of prevailing party, judicially sanctioned settlements despite the absence of adjudicated liability, the risk of rewarding pressure from weak suits, and the absence of an earlier controlling catalyst holding. The dissent read prevailing status to include concrete requested relief materially caused by a legally substantial lawsuit, with genuine benefit, legal substance and causation checks; it rejected treating the sufficient judicial routes recognized in previous cases as exclusive.

The actual entering Farrar differs from history on reasonable amount and remedy: its controlling opinion requires explained lower-court consideration rather than immediate zero, while permitting zero and excluding mechanical damages proportionality. Its unanimous eligibility holding is limited to an enforceable nominal judgment directly benefiting the plaintiff. No exclusive judicial-decree rule follows from it. The historical Buckhannon writings themselves recognized that catalyst eligibility had remained open, so the actual Farrar difference does not remove the central premise of either current construction. Laidlaw supplies no catalyst holding and does not keep a moot merits action alive for fees. The current Sandoval and Circuit City positions supply Justice-specific statutory methodology, not a rule awarding fees whenever litigation has practical value.

## Two complete proposition coalitions

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support affirmance and a judicially sanctioned material-alteration requirement. An enforceable merits judgment or court-approved consent decree supplies the requisite legal change; purely voluntary conduct does not, even if the suit helped produce it. Judicial approval and enforceability explain the distinction from an ordinary private resolution. The present rule need not decide every settlement device or interlocutory order. These four do not claim repeal was unrelated to the litigation or that the former residency requirement was lawful.

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support reversal of the categorical exclusion and a remand. A legally substantial, nonfrivolous lawsuit may establish prevailing status when it materially causes the defendant to provide concrete relief sought that actually benefits the plaintiff. Causation must be found; sequence alone does not suffice. The relief must reflect legally grounded success rather than a gratuitous response or surrender to the nuisance and expense of a groundless claim. This is an interpretation of the enacted prevailing-party language, not general equitable reimbursement. A defendant need not admit liability to comply voluntarily, but the claimant still must establish the actual predicates.

The strongest objection to judicial-relief exclusivity is that a meritorious suit can obtain precisely the requested result without a decree, allowing voluntary correction to defeat reimbursement. The four affirming Justices answer that litigation success for these fee statutes requires the legally sanctioned change, with administrability and avoidance of collateral causal litigation reinforcing the text. The strongest objection to catalysts is speculative causation and satellite merits litigation after the original controversy ends. The four reversing Justices answer that courts can determine benefit, causal contribution and legal substance, with ordinary reasonable-fee discretion controlling amount. Neither answer changes the other group's supported commitment.

These remain two groups of four Associates. No final Court majority, equal-division disposition or opinion assignment is inferred. Historical Scalia/Thomas concurrence supplies a supported separate explanatory function if assembly requires it, but historical authorship or a redundant extra writing is not mandated. A published opposition must preserve the actual judicial-relief or confined-catalyst ground, its strongest qualification and its exact remedy.

## Remedy and limits

Under affirmance the fee denial stands without deciding causation or amount. Under reversal the lower courts determine genuine personal benefit, material causal contribution and legal substance before deciding a reasonable amount. Actual success, unrelated unsuccessful work and Hensley's explained discretion remain operative under actual Farrar; no fee award, positive minimum or restored lodestar follows automatically. Neither path revives the moot merits action, decides the former residency rule's legality or disturbs unrelated claims.
