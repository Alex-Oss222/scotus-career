## Controlling changed premise and reconciliation method

The actual entering Ricci decision has no historical counterpart as a governing merits decision. Its five-Justice rule requires an actual enforcement relationship for added custody in a fine-only civil licensing case, rejects probable cause and local permission as automatically sufficient there, and expressly rejects a least-restrictive-means or universal citation requirement. Its four-Justice application reservation is confined to Ricci's record. Its Thomas dissent, joined by Scalia, Kennedy and Souter, rejected unlimited custody yet accepted the actual short enforcement-document processing without requiring proof that citation was impossible. Continuing unlawful operation and the identified documents were express premises; they are not facts of Atwater.

Ricci creates a concrete legal and published-position premise for all eight Justices. It does not compel the same application to criminal misdemeanors, a new case-specific necessity finding, or a new vote. McArthur supplies an additional actual-need and execution precedent in a different, residential setting. Kennedy's authorship and the stated joins matter to continuity, but neither that decision nor its duration authorizes traffic booking automatically.

The refreshed commitments recognize these distinctions. Their division on the extension of Ricci is supportable. All eight disposition commitments survive. The Kennedy and Thomas grounds require the express qualification below: ordinary criminal processing is the legal enforcement interest they regard as sufficient at this scale, not a newly established individualized need to jail this driver. Their agreement in affirmance cannot be used to erase that limit or create a broader unanimous rationale among the four affirming Associates.
