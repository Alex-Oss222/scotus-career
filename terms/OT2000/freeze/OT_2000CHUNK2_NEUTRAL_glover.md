# Glover v. United States

# SECTION I: NEUTRAL MODELING PACKET

## A. CASE, RECORD, AND POSTURE

* **Case Name:** Glover v. United States
* **Citation or Docket:** No. 99-8576; 531 U.S. 198
* **October Term:** 2000
* **Entry Stage and Event:** Merits submission for decision; the historical decision date supplies scheduling only.
* **Simulated Event Date:** 2001-01-09 (historical).
* **Question Presented:** Whether counsel’s failure to pursue grouping of the money-laundering and racketeering offenses denied effective assistance at sentencing, including whether the resulting increase in imprisonment must exceed a significance threshold to establish Strickland prejudice.
* **Question Granted:** Review concerns the ineffective-assistance grouping claim. The contemporaneous merits brief presents that claim more broadly than the appellate minimum-prejudice rule; the latter is the dispositive legal error that can require remand for the remaining application questions. This is a paraphrase of verified briefing scope, not a quotation from the petition. [United States merits brief](https://www.justice.gov/osg/brief/glover-v-united-states-brief-merits).
* **Court and Judgment Under Review:** Seventh Circuit, 182 F.3d 921: affirmed denial of a §2255 motion on the theory that a claimed additional six to twenty-one months was insufficiently significant to establish prejudice.
* **Relief Requested:** Glover seeks reversal of the categorical prejudice ground and consideration of his ineffective-assistance claim. The United States abandons that categorical rule but offers other reasons to preserve the judgment.
* **Material Record:** Glover's money-laundering counts were not grouped with racketeering and tax counts. The resulting two-level increase produced an asserted additional six to twenty-one months; he received 84 months. Counsel did not adequately press grouping or present it on appeal. The lower courts did not decide deficient performance or whether the grouping calculation was actually wrong because they rejected prejudice categorically.
* **Threshold, Preservation, and Vehicle Matters:** The asserted calculation error is an analytic premise, not a Supreme Court factual or legal finding. This federal-prisoner §2255 case does not import §2254(d) deference. New alternative grounds neither raised nor resolved below require preservation and first-instance analysis.

* **Companion or Related Matters:** None requiring a separate module.
* **Participation Matter:** No case-specific recusal or nonparticipation is identified. Historical participation is addressed in Section III.

### Issue 1: Ordinary sentencing prejudice

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Lockhart v. Fretwell preserves ordinary reasonable-probability prejudice except for a lost benefit the law does not recognize. Williams v. Taylor confirms that ordinary lawful sentencing claims require no separate fundamental-unfairness condition.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The sentencing-assistance rules require deficient performance and a reasonable probability of a different lawful result on the supported record, not a judicially invented minimum number of months.
* **Other Governing Law:** Sixth Amendment; 28 U.S.C. §2255; the applicable Sentencing Guidelines grouping provisions.
* **Material Other Authority:** No additional authority is necessary beyond the sources identified here.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** An increase in lawful imprisonment is an adverse result of constitutional significance if caused by constitutionally deficient representation. The performance inquiry and correctness of the underlying objection remain separate.
* **Open Point:** Whether any minimum-duration requirement may be added to prejudice, and which unresolved elements remain for remand.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The Seventh Circuit relied on an expansive understanding of Lockhart v. Fretwell to demand a significant sentence difference. Glover argues that every avoidable increment of imprisonment matters under Strickland v. Washington and that the calculation issue deserves adjudication. The United States now agrees that the categorical threshold is wrong, while disputing grouping error, deficient performance, and other possible grounds. The concession narrows the dispute but does not authorize the Court to decide unpreserved alternatives or grant automatic resentencing.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**Reject the added threshold:** Reverse and remand for the remaining performance, underlying-law, causation, and properly available procedural questions. The rule governs actual increased confinement without presuming prejudice from any attorney error. **Alternative affirmance:** Permissible only on a properly preserved, sufficient ground supported by the record; a new factual determination is not an appellate substitute for remand. The Court need not declare all grouping issues settled or specify a new sentence.
