## Governing law and established record

Love, Hale and Wright foreclose the panel's collective-only premise. The ordinary semiautomatic pistol and necessary ammunition implicate personal arms protection without militia membership. Wright's Stage 1 coverage and Stage 2 justification are separate. A core deprivation requires compelling interest, narrow tailoring and no comparably effective materially less restrictive alternative; substantial regulation requires an important objective, substantial relationship and a restriction that does not unnecessarily or effectively eliminate protected activity; incidental regulation receives reasonableness only when it meaningfully obstructs no right. Government may not generally abolish meaningful possession of ordinary functional defensive arms in the home. That boundary does not immunize every person or use.

Fraternal Order establishes only the developed conviction-based official-use application. Its adjudicated violence, conviction protections and statutory qualifications were material, and it expressly left different private-possession applications open. It neither decides this case nor establishes that a criminal conviction is the exclusive permissible basis for preventive restriction.

Section 922(g)(8) requires an order entered after actual hearing notice and opportunity to participate; its specified intimate-partner or child restraint; and either the credible-threat finding in (C)(i) or the explicit prohibition of the specified injurious physical force in (C)(ii). The alternatives cannot be collapsed. The reported orders found past domestic violence and possible recurrence, and Napier attended the hearings. This is more than an unadjudicated allegation or a bare ex parte order. The plea admits operative orders and the possession charged. His receipt-of-copy objection is not a finding that he lacked hearing notice. The subsequent assault report is not used as a conviction, proof of the alleged new assault or substitute predicate. The private possession actually prosecuted was a pistol and ammunition in a vehicle; no distinct home-possession incident is presented.

## Competing lawful paths

**Affirm on the bounded personal-right ground.** An adjudicated, conduct-based disability operating while these protective orders remain in force is a substantial regulation of particular persons, rather than a general abolition of defensive arms or incidental administration. Preventing armed recurrence of adjudicated domestic violence is important. Actual prior violence, the determination that it may recur, the protected domestic relationship, hearing participation and the order-dependent operation provide the substantial fit. The admission of operative qualifying orders permits this application without adjudicating a new procedural challenge or importing the unpreserved evidentiary ruling. A criminal conviction is not constitutionally indispensable to all preventive protection. On this specific record the serious restriction is justified under intermediate scrutiny, extending Fraternal Order only to these materially described civil predicates and the presented possession.

The strongest objection is that the statute disables all arms and ammunition for the individual, while the civil hearing lacks established criminal safeguards and the precise duration, burden and modification provisions are unrecovered. The six affirmers do not dismiss that severity. They classify the restriction by its adjudicated conduct, constrained predicates and operation against this qualified person, not merely by availability of arms to others. The common rule does not approve every technically qualifying order, erase the force/threat alternatives, establish hypothetical home facts or presume missing safeguards. The supported merits application does not require a transcript of every prior hearing once the valid predicate facts are admitted and no preserved hearing-defect claim supplies a different premise.

**Vacate the appellate arms ruling for actual justification.** The competing path treats complete personal disarmament as demanding a more exact inquiry into dangerousness and duration than the panel performed. Because the panel used a displaced exclusion of the individual right, an admitted order and generic recurrence finding do not supply the missing explanation of why all possession must cease. The court must apply the governing personal-right framework to the actual order's basis and operation, with existing-record resolution or ordinary further proceedings if genuinely needed. The strongest objection is that the two adjudicated domestic findings already provide a direct preventive fit. The two remand proponents accept that relevance, but find it insufficient to resolve the scope and necessity of a total prohibition without the legal inquiry that Wright required. They seek no categorical conviction prerequisite or automatic acquittal.

## Proposition compatibility and remedy

All eight Associates reject collective-only coverage, recognize the pistol/ammunition interest and distinguish this statute from a criminal-conviction disability. All preserve the two statutory alternatives, the accepted-question boundary and the plea's limits. Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer directly join the bounded intermediate-scrutiny classification, substantial-fit application and affirmance. Scalia and Thomas do not join those merits propositions; they support a return under the governing framework and no ultimate constitutional outcome on this record. The latter two remain free to explain their foundational reservations without changing binding law.

Affirmance leaves both convictions in place against this objection, without adopting the panel's collective-right reasoning or issuing a new commerce, fair-warning, state-law or multiple-punishment holding. The remand alternative would vacate the appellate disposition insofar as it rejects the arms challenge and return its actual application, coordinating any necessary conditional-plea consequences below. It would not itself cancel both convictions, adjudicate innocence, order weapons returned or require a particular hearing.

No generally applicable conviction prerequisite, new civil proof burden, prescribed order duration, restoration program or categorical rule governing all section 922(g) disabilities is proposed. No future appellate decision or subsequent historical arms doctrine supplies support. The commitments are complete despite explicit source limits because each selected path states how it lawfully handles those limits.
