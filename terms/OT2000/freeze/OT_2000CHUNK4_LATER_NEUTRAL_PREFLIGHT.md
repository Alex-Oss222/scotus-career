## Texas v. Cobb

**Corrected record and reach.** The burglary indictment and appointment of counsel occurred before the murder interrogation. Counsel permitted two earlier interrogations concerning the disappearances. In November 1995 Cobb's father reported a confession; police obtained an arrest warrant and gave Miranda warnings before the confession now challenged. The capital count is multiple murder in one criminal transaction under Texas Penal Code §19.03(a)(7)(A), not burglary as the sole aggravating element. This matters to any elements comparison. The stated valid custodial waiver does not itself settle whether an attached Sixth Amendment right was asserted and could be waived unilaterally under Michigan v. Jackson. The record does not permit treating prior counsel permission as permission for the later interrogation.

**Entering rule.** McNeil v. Wisconsin, 501 U.S. 171 (1991), makes Sixth Amendment attachment offense-specific and distinguishes it from Miranda/Edwards invocation. Maine v. Moulton, 474 U.S. 159 (1985), bars deliberate circumvention of the attached right in the charged prosecution while preserving use of statements about offenses to which the right has not attached. Patterson v. Illinois, 487 U.S. 285 (1988), concerns informed waiver by an indicted defendant who had not requested counsel; it does not erase Jackson's distinct invocation rule. Blockburger and Brown supply elements and lesser-included-offense definitions in a different constitutional setting. Their application to counsel requires justification, not mere citation. The current Davis rule requires neutral clarification of a reasonable possible present custodial-counsel request; no ambiguous request is identified here. Dickerson preserves Miranda's constitutional force but does not make its waiver dispose of every Sixth Amendment question.

**Alternative paths.** An elements-based attachment boundary can reverse the factually-related-offense ground without deciding the alternative waiver question, if the burglary and actual multiple-murder count are legally distinct. A closely related transaction or anti-circumvention extension could sustain attachment, but then must address Jackson, the actual assertion, waiver and the relevant prejudice/remedy; it cannot simply equate Fifth and Sixth Amendment counsel. A narrower disposition could preserve charged-burglary exclusion while allowing murder investigation and use, provided the governing distinction is expressed. A rule extending attachment must explain an administrable relationship rather than leave factual closeness undefined. An elements rule must answer the risk of strategic charging and elicitation without denying Moulton's charged-case protection.

**Justice-specific anchors for modeling.** Scalia authored McNeil; O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter joined. Kennedy's separate opinion would also make the Fifth Amendment interrogation protection offense-specific and emphasizes workable guidelines; the Court did not adopt that additional rule. Stevens's McNeil dissent and Moulton join support confronting circumvention and the represented relationship. His McNeil dissent expressly questions importing Blockburger into counsel scope and identifies strategic selective charging as a concern. O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy joined Patterson; Stevens dissented. The simulated Davis writing distinguishes O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas's clear-request position from Souter, Stevens and Ginsburg's mandatory-clarification position. Breyer's Dickerson join supports the independent custodial safeguard but supplies no prior personal resolution of the Sixth Amendment offense boundary. Do not pretend it does. An enhanced comparison must examine each Justice's strongest argument on attachment separately from any waiver position.

Sources: [McNeil](https://openjurist.org/501/us/171/mcneil-v-wisconsin), [Moulton](https://openjurist.org/474/us/159/maine-v-moulton), [Patterson](https://openjurist.org/487/us/285/patterson-v-illinois), prior public Davis and Dickerson entries, and control's objective extracts. The existing extracts support a bounded attachment decision. A conclusive alternative waiver ground requires actual Jackson assertion analysis and cannot be filled from the generic valid-Miranda-waiver premise.

## Wallis v. Spencer

**Source and contested record.** The full lower opinion, including Rymer's dissent and all footnotes, is available at [202 F.3d 1126](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/202/202.F3d.1126.html). The exams occurred three days after removal, on Jessie's third birthday. The dependency petition was filed September 25; specialist correction was sent November 25; return to the parents was ordered December 6. The physician's initial report and later correction remain distinct historical propositions.

**Material refinements.** No court order existed. What CPS told individual officers, and what they knew, are contested. The City did not separately argue on that appeal that mistaken reliance alone substituted for an order; its alternative was actual reasonable cause from the information possessed. No allegation implicated Becky in the proposed harm, and the tip did not assert a planned injury to Lauren. The removing officers did not significantly investigate the charge because they believed they were enforcing an order. Police testimony describes the practice of accepting telephone representations without verification. Whether that practice caused this removal is a liability question, not a finding here. The examination report names the city as requesting agency; testimony identifies an examination contract. The majority inferred an examination practice, while Rymer said that theory was never pleaded, argued or proved against the sole remaining defendant. That conflict requires explicit preservation treatment.

**Entering law and pending refresh.** Carter supplies separate deprivation, policy/custom, culpable omission where applicable, and actual causation. It forecloses municipal borrowing of personal immunity. Troxel protects parental direction against mere judicial preference but supplies no comprehensive harm, unfitness, strict-scrutiny or burden rule. Denius addresses justified acquisition of intimate information in employment, not permission for invasive examination. McArthur requires a supported emergency and appropriate scope/duration; it cannot substitute a generalized balancing label for justification. Ferguson's earlier March 21 disposition must be supplied before this event is modeled; medical-search purpose, consent and warrant-exception holdings may materially matter.

**Alternative paths and strongest objections.** Removal may return for trial because supported disputes about reliability, actual knowledge, time for a judge and scope preclude judgment for the City. The City's strongest response is objective urgent protection, not eventual accuracy of the accusation. Its rule must still distinguish the two children, the unimplicated mother, and restraint after the asserted danger expires. A contrary summary-judgment path requires no genuine material dispute under the proper standard; it cannot resolve Pitcher's conflicting accounts adversely to the family. For examinations, preserved bodily and family interests may require prior authorization or consent except genuine medical urgency or threatened loss of material evidence. But recognizing the right cannot create an unpreserved city-policy claim. A narrower remand can resolve removal and direct ordinary preservation and causation analysis before any examination liability. It need not deem the claim forfeited on the dissent alone. Initial removal may cause later injuries without proof of a second custom; the lower opinion expressly reserves that causation theory. Conversely, the later juvenile order does not automatically establish or defeat municipal responsibility for the entire separation.

**Justice-specific anchors.** Carter's municipal rule has the direct support of every Associate. O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer's Troxel writing protects actual parental choice without an all-purpose harm rule; Souter focused on unbounded displacement; Thomas proposed strict scrutiny; Stevens and Kennedy insisted on the actual child interests and relationship; Scalia declined the new visitation extension while preserving established parental domains. Those distinctions do not answer a physical seizure. McArthur's majority joins by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer support particularized emergency/scope review; Stevens's dissent emphasizes serious intrusion and the real government need. Denius's seven-Justice acquisition rule and Scalia/Thomas's source objection must not be mistaken for a bodily-search holding. Enhanced issue-specific treatment is mandatory for removal, examinations, preservation and municipal causation.

No damages or factual liability is established by survival of summary judgment.

## Lujan v. G & G Fire Sprinklers

**Validated facts and law.** Withholding arose from three 1995 projects and alleged wage, recordkeeping and hours violations. The then-applicable contractor-or-assignee action under §§1730–1732 had to be brought within ninety days of completion and acceptance, and was the contractor or assignee's exclusive remedy against the awarding body. That exclusivity does not by itself eliminate an ordinary action against the prime contractor. G & G's account of suits on previous projects does not prove assignment or an available timely action on these three. The July 1, 2001 replacement administrative-review procedure is not operative on April 17 and did not exist for the challenged withholding. It cannot cure past process by assumption.

**Entering standard.** American Manufacturers's limited entitlement holding and the Standards entry `Conditional workers’ compensation medical-payment interests` (effective March 3, 1999) reject an unconditional advance-payment demand while preserving fair adjudication of existing claims. Its private-choice attribution holding does not govern compelled state withholding. Mathews, Logan and James Daniel Good require attention to the interest and available process; a debt claim, possessed property and an existing benefit award cannot be conflated. Availability of a procedure is part of due process here, not a general §1983 exhaustion requirement.

**Two paths.** If ordinary contract adjudication is actually available and can determine and enforce the disputed entitlement, lack of a duplicate administrative hearing may not violate due process. The strongest objection is that a theoretical action, dependent on an unproved assignment or unable to review the wage determination, supplies no real adjudication. If the statutory assessment effectively determines the debt conclusively and all meaningful routes are closed, additional process or a remand on that specific obstacle may be required. The strongest objection to that route is the surviving ordinary contract claim, supported by J & K Painting, 45 Cal. App.4th 1394, 1401 n.7, and absence of a supported categorical bar. A remand must identify a genuine legal or factual obstacle, not presume assignment denial. None of these paths adjudicates the wage violations or exact debt.

**Justice-specific anchors.** Kennedy's American Manufacturers payment opinion had all Associates except Stevens. Ginsburg's separate writing insists on fair determination; Breyer and Souter preserve actual continuation interests; Stevens's distinct process ground rejects automatic advance payment while demanding a genuine hearing. O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas's joins support the limited entitlement distinction without denying every contract-property claim. All require analysis of the actual judicial remedy. These source-based differences support enhanced comparison on remedy availability, with a bounded ordinary-process holding possible without resolving whether property exists.

## United States v. Cleveland Indians Baseball Co.

**Corrected facts.** The settlement is stipulated to be wages. The 1994 payments concern 1986 and 1987 salary damages; none of the recipients was an employee in 1994. All but one recipient had exceeded the relevant earlier wage maxima. Therefore the runtime packet's categorical implication that every payment would generate no earlier-year tax is too broad. The reported employer payments were $99,382 FICA and $1,008 FUTA; the district refund was $97,202. Those figures need not become an unsupported final liability calculation.

**Temporal and interpretive law.** Apply the payment-year statutory versions, rates and wage bases, not modern law. Sections 3111, 3301, 3121(a)(1) and 3306(b)(1) use wages paid and calendar-year limits. The reproduced regulations attach liability when wages are paid and use the rate then effective. Their longstanding application is evidence of construction, not freestanding delegated authority. Nierotko, 327 U.S. 358 (1946), concerns benefit attribution, which must be distinguished from the tax collection provisions. Auer's responsible, fair, considered own-regulation interpretation rule (effective February 19, 1997) and Christensen's advisory-status rule (effective May 1, 2000) remain different. The later Mead case supplies no law. Gitlitz reinforces compliance with each operative statutory account and sequence; it does not create a universal tax-symmetry rule.

**Paths and responses.** Actual-payment allocation can follow statutory text and permissible longstanding regulations; it must answer why benefit attribution does not control this different mechanism. Earning-year attribution must identify a construction of the tax provisions that survives their wording, regulatory history and administration; equitable avoidance of a timing windfall alone cannot displace them. The Court could resolve the text without adopting an unnecessary universal rule for revenue rulings. If an agency interpretation does legal work, identify whether it interprets a statute, regulation or merely advances litigation advocacy. There is no reason to invent a factual remand over agreed dates.

**Justice-specific anchors.** Every Associate joined Auer's bounded own-regulation ground. O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas joined Christensen's advisory-letter rule; Scalia expressly resisted advisory form as a categorical deference barrier while rejecting an impermissible reading; Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer emphasized persuasive agency experience and statutory operation. Breyer's current Gitlitz dissent examines the integrated statutory mechanism but is not a policy veto; the other Associates joined the textual account. These differences require testing the tax and benefit arguments individually rather than predicting a deference bloc.

## United States v. Weston

**Supported record.** Full primary lower text, including the three separate opinions and notes: [206 F.3d 9](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/206/206.F3d.9.99-3119.html). The federal indictment dates to October 9, 1998; incompetence and commitment to April 22, 1999; initial administrative hearing to May 13; judicial remand to June 18; second hearing to July 8; judicial hearing to August 20; authorization to September 9. The order required counsel notice and judicial written approval. The second hearing received the defense psychiatrist's written report and counsel's suggestions but counsel did not attend. The Bureau relied on several grounds including restoration; the district court accepted safety while deferring trial effects. The treating psychiatrist's immediate-containment testimony undermines safety necessity on that record, but other testimony identifies potential staff-contact risk. Neither side may erase that distinction.

**Entering rule.** Riggins and `Involuntary antipsychotic medication of an objecting trial detainee` (effective May 18, 1992) require medical appropriateness, an overriding need and reasonable less intrusive alternatives after objection. They recognize unreconstructable trial risks but adopt no universal strict scrutiny, proof quantum, categorical treatment bar or complete restoration test. Harper permits medically justified safety treatment in its confinement setting; it does not automatically resolve treatment aimed at prosecuting an unconvicted defendant. Seling preserves separate confinement, conditions and remedy predicates; it supplies no medication rule.

**Lawful paths.** A preauthorization judicial assessment of likely trial effects may be required where restoration is an actual purpose and the safety ground lacks independent evidentiary support. Its strongest objection is uncertainty before treatment and the real cost of indefinite isolation; the answer cannot demand perfect prediction. A properly proved independent safety need may justify treatment with distinct later trial limits, but the present safety label is not enough. A bounded remand may demand findings and likely-effect assessment without deciding whether treatment will ultimately be allowed. Reinstatement requires a legally adequate independent justification on this record and a defensible reason to defer all trial-rights inquiry; lack of prosecutorial pretext alone does not remove a genuine restoration purpose. Medication efficacy and trial fairness are not synonyms.

**Justice-specific anchors.** O'Connor authored Riggins; Stevens and Souter joined its justification and trial-risk rules. Kennedy's concurrence demanded an extraordinary showing against significant impairment and recognized possible civil custody if fair trial cannot be achieved. Thomas and Scalia distinguished a liberty violation from proof warranting reversal of a completed conviction. That distinction must be tested anew because this is prospective unwanted treatment, the liberty claim is raised, and no conviction requires reversal. Ginsburg and Breyer's Seling joins support separate actual-operation and remedy analysis but do not establish an individual restoration-medication rule; their choice requires direct application of binding Riggins and the competing supported grounds, without assigning them Kennedy's added test by inference.

**Reserved questions.** The effects on comprehension, consultation, testimony, demeanor and an insanity defense require separate attention. Preserving the ability to mount an effective insanity defense does not necessarily require exhibiting untreated delusions at trial. Tatel's lower concurrence identifies recordings and psychiatric testimony as possible alternatives, and also allows an initial likely-effects assessment followed by reassessment of actual effects. Those are supported possibilities, not established adequate protections. The capital-ethics objection is preserved below but the grant does not require a categorical execution-competency rule. No dismissal, release, competency finding or guilt determination follows from remand.

## Easley / Smallwood v. Cromartie

**Displaced procedural predicate.** Actual Hunt, May 17, 1999, vacated the earlier judgment and limited remand to actually presented independently cognizable claims. It expressly did not require a trial solely to decide racial predominance or deem an unknown claim forfeited. The historical trial and its predominance findings cannot establish that this different mandate was obeyed. The record may establish objective evidentiary findings while leaving their legal relevance and preservation unresolved.

**Entering rule.** `Territorial racial design and independently cognizable injury`, effective in its clarified operation June 29, 1995, rejects predominance, unusual shape and subordination of traditional criteria as sufficient alone. Independent exclusion, dilution, unequal participation or inferior civic status retains its own elements. No universal downstream loss is required where unequal treatment itself completes the injury. Adarand is a justification standard after a cognizable classification, not a replacement cause of action. Rule 52 clear-error review governs factual findings, not their legal sufficiency.

**Paths.** An injunction based only on the displaced predominance cause cannot stand. A bounded vacatur and remand may require identification and adjudication of an actually preserved independent injury, without inventing one or declaring all alternatives forfeited. Outright final rejection of every claim requires reliable preservation and record support not yet supplied. If an independent claim was preserved and proved, apply its own legal rule and ordinary factual review. Four Justices' previously expressed contrary constitutional rule is a genuine jurisprudential position, but affirmance on that basis would require express reconsideration of governing precedent, not a statement that predominance already controls.

**Justice-specific anchors.** Ginsburg authored Hunt's governing remand with Stevens, Souter and Breyer; these Justices joined the same design-only boundary in Miller. Kennedy's Hunt separate writing, joined by O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas, would retain predominant racial assignment subordinating ordinary criteria as a scrutiny trigger, while rejecting automatic invalidity from shape, awareness or percentages. All eight joined Hunt's unanimous vacatur on the supported summary-judgment record. The new trial changes evidentiary posture, not the governing constitutional cause. Enhanced comparison must test the four's preserved disagreement against the actual mandate and identify a lawful remedy for both dockets.

**Exact remaining gap.** No source supplied so far establishes a preserved independent injury after the actual mandate or compliance proceedings under that mandate. This does not bar a narrower legal vacatur with a confined remand, but it bars certifying a final adjudication that no independent claim exists or that one has been proved. Any reconstruction must supply only ordinary proceedings authorized by Engine §14 and must not invent a new claim, evidence, waiver or compliance finding.

## Shaw v. Murphy

**Corrected scope.** Murphy was an inmate law clerk but was not assigned to Tracy because policy separated their security categories; Tracy was offered another clerk. Murphy investigated and wrote anyway. Discipline sustained insolence and interference charges, with a suspended ten-day detention term and demerits. The complaint also raised vagueness and overbreadth as applied; those remain unresolved below. The record has no finding that the letter was directed to Galle or intended to harass him, and Murphy's denial of anticipated readership was uncontradicted. These omissions do not themselves decide the ordinary Turner merits.

**Entering rule.** Turner supplies reasonable relationship to legitimate penological interests, alternatives, impact and ready-alternative considerations. Lewis's operative obstruction requirement, effective June 24, 1996, and `False disciplinary charges in retaliation for prisoner grievances`, effective June 22, 1998 under Pearson, preserve native access and grievance claims. They do not provide a constitutional bonus solely for calling a letter legal advice. Denius protects independent legal consultation in a public-employment condition; it does not remove prison-specific Turner analysis. The PLRA's current prospective-relief conditions operate only after a proved violation and proper remedial claim.

**Paths and objections.** Rejecting categorical enhanced weight can return the actual communication, rule construction and preserved due-process issues for ordinary adjudication. The strongest contrary argument is that suppressing necessary assistance can impair the prison's real access arrangements; that argument must be assessed as actual access or evidence within ordinary scrutiny, not an automatic privilege. A content-sensitive application may recognize legitimate assistance while still applying Turner. Upholding discipline conclusively requires reaching the actual rules, purpose, application and unresolved claims; rejection of enhancement alone does not supply those findings. Neither legal-advice status nor prison administration supplies blanket immunity.

**Justice-specific anchors.** Kennedy's Lewis access and security/remedy ground was joined by O'Connor, Scalia, Thomas, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer; its narrower obstruction ground had O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas. Scalia's separate categorical limits had those three plus Kennedy. Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer preserved practical help in evaluating and pursuing a concrete grievance throughout litigation; Stevens likewise rejected requiring merits proof before necessary help. Thomas's separate institutional doubts did not overrule Bounds. All eight joined Pearson's requirement of actual causal proof of fabricated retaliatory discipline. The enhanced comparison must preserve these differences without converting them into an unexpressed content privilege.
