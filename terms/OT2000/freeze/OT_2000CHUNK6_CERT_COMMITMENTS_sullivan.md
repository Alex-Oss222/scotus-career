## Eight Associate positions

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer each vote to grant review of the finally settled federal suppression ground. The state court expressly rested on federal constitutional law, treated investigative motive as dispositive and asserted latitude to interpret that law contrary to the Court's controlling rules. The current Atwater decision also requires separating added custody from the initial stop. The need to clarify these actual federal premises, while preserving independent inventory and state-law grounds, warrants review. The continuing prosecution does not defeat the packet's otherwise-evading-review channel.

The petition vote commits none of these Justices to finding the arrest lawful, admitting the seized evidence, rejecting a preserved inventory objection or deciding a state constitutional question. It is supported by the issue's present legal importance, not a forecast of a preferred judgment.
