## Supported facts and component-specific legal questions

The 1994 settlement paid salary damages attributed to 1986 and 1987. No recipient was an employee in 1994, and none received other wages from this employer then. The parties stipulated that the payments are wages for FICA and FUTA. They did not stipulate temporal allocation or the authority of a particular administrative interpretation. Damages replacing salary can be wages without making the settlement date irrelevant or making the earlier service period conclusive.

The reported tax payments and $97,202 refund judgment are different amounts. Neither possible disposition computes the exceptional recipient's account or awards the full amount paid by inference.

## Adversarial construction paths

### A. Direct statutory resolution

The specific annual tax provisions can be construed to refer to an actual payment of remuneration that the parties agree is taxable wages. The statutes do not require the recipient to remain currently employed, and the fact that a payment repairs earlier salary loss does not change when the employer actually pays it. On this construction each rate provision applies its own payment-date command, each annual base aggregates actual payments during that year, and the 1994 Medicare regime supplies no wage ceiling. The absence of other 1994 wages matters to that year's aggregation; it is not a distinct exception.

The strongest objection is legal relation back: salary damages replace earlier wages and could enter the annual machinery as a substitute for what was paid in the earlier period. Nierotko demonstrates that payment terminology does not invariably defeat that construction. The answer on A therefore cannot be only a dictionary definition of paid. It must be that these later employer-tax clauses make actual annual payment the tax event and base, whereas Nierotko's benefit account used employment periods to preserve eligibility and benefit measurement. Nierotko's footnote 8 also reproduces older tax-rate language tied to employment during specified years; those are not the later clauses before this Court. Its identical-definitions discussion establishes a substantial analogy, but a common wage category does not compel a common temporal rule under later provisions serving different functions.

Scalia adopts A for every component below. The other seven do not presently join the further claim that those distinctions make the statutory allocation of this damages substitute unambiguous without examining the implementing construction.

### B. Actual interpretive room and a lawful payment-year resolution

The annual wording establishes the normal tax framework but can leave interpretive work concerning a backpay substitute. Nierotko makes the Company's attribution argument legally serious without deciding this later employer-tax question. A responsible administrative construction applying actual-payment treatment can be permissible because it fits the later rate and base language, administers the actual collection event consistently and avoids reconstructing earlier employer accounts, while benefit restoration retains its distinct function. Administrative convenience supports reasonableness only within that interpretive space; it cannot create authority or override a contrary statutory allocation.

The quoted FICA rules provide payment-time attachment and the then-effective rate, and the source identifies corresponding FUTA rules and basic continuity since 1940. Those premises support the proposed payment-year construction. They do not themselves establish the precise terms of an additional wage-base regulation or the content, authority and application of a particular backpay ruling. An own-regulation interpretation under Auer must actually be responsible, fair and considered and fit the regulation. A statutory interpretation or revenue ruling has a different authority inquiry. The statutory validity of a Treasury regulation is yet another question. Calling all three deference would conceal the needed legal work.

**Strongest objections to B:** The rule might merely repeat the disputed statutory concept, the asserted pronouncement might lack binding authority, or the particular interpretation might not extend to this category of salary damages. General administrative continuity does not answer those objections. Nierotko itself rejected an agency's exclusion of legally included backpay: expertise never gives the administrator final control of statutory limits. Auer and Christensen preserve that boundary, and a lawful payment-year outcome cannot depend on an unexamined agency document being declared authoritative by label.

Seven Associates provisionally select B as the appropriate rationale to test, with the exact source predicate stated below. Their proposed judgment is reversal if that construction and its claimed legal force are established. They do not presently freeze an unconditional final rationale or final judgment by counting that conditional conclusion as seven completed votes.

### C. Earlier-period attribution or independent judicial construction

The Company's best argument is that a salary-damage substitute belongs legally to the replaced wage period and that actual receipt identifies collection without necessarily fixing attribution. Nierotko's paid-quarter holding and common wage definitions supply its strongest support. Its substantial obstacle is the later employer-tax framework and any valid payment-year construction within it; the benefits judgment cannot be transferred without explanation. It supplies no damages exemption and does not resolve the exceptional player's calculation.

If the identified administrative interpretation lacks controlling force, its actual reasoning and experience may still be persuasive under Christensen and Skidmore. The court must then resolve the statutory construction rather than automatically award the refund. That is a lawful alternative, not the same as finding either unambiguous text or a controlling agency interpretation. No Associate below assumes that withholding deference necessarily produces earlier-year allocation. The seven who choose B reserve this alternative until the actual administrative premise is established; they do not select it merely to avoid checking a source necessary to their preferred authoritative-construction ground.

## Exact unresolved source predicate and stage consequence

The missing material is narrowly defined: the complete date-applicable Treasury provision, if any, that addresses annual FICA and FUTA wage-base treatment of remuneration for services performed in an earlier year, including its qualifications, and the actual Treasury interpretation treating backpay or salary-damage awards as wages of the payment year, together with its status and responsibility. The full text and applicable authority are necessary before calling that specific construction controlling, fair and considered, or applicable without a material exception to these settlement wages. The quoted attachment/rate provisions and reported regulatory continuity are already established; they need not be researched from scratch. The precise gap is their claimed extension to the disputed substitute and the separate annual bases, not the agreed payment dates.

No revenue-ruling number or unprovided wage-base regulation is guessed. No source is represented as read merely because a neutral summary reports continuity. A primary statutory-construction route remains available and Scalia's commitment is complete on it. The other seven choose an agency-dependent rationale and remain conditional on the missing source; this additive handoff must not be counted as an unconditional eight-Justice reversal or eight direct joins to one statutory holding. A clean neutral supplement supplying that source should return only this affected rationale to independent modeling before reconciliation. The request is an operator source requirement, not a Court filing order or a litigant default.

**Remedial limits that all positions preserve:** No individual benefit award is adjudicated. Any reversal returns for applying the correct component rules and lawful calculation; it does not award or deny a fixed refund amount on the incomplete individual calculation. Earlier-period attribution, if ultimately chosen, likewise must preserve the exceptional recipient and distinct rates and bases. No factual hearing is required merely to repeat the agreed dates, and no later Mead rule or modern tax amendment supplies authority.
