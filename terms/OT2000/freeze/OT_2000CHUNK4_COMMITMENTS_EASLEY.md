# Easley / Smallwood v. Cromartie — independent non-Stone commitments

**Stage:** Frozen independent provisional handoff before comparator or Stone merits. **Event/cutoff:** 2001-04-18. Dockets 99-1864 and 99-1865 review the same three-judge judgment and receive the same disposition. Inputs are the authorized neutral packet, corrected preflight and objective trial findings, exact territorial-design standard and prior public Hunt. All eight Associates participate. No assignment is made.

## Entering law and exact record boundary

The governing territorial-design rule, in its clarified operation effective June 29, 1995, rejects racial predominance, even with subordinated traditional criteria, as a sufficient constitutional wrong by itself. Exclusion, intentional dilution, materially unequal participation, inferior civic membership and other independent claims retain their own elements. An injury completed by unequal treatment needs no universal later electoral or financial loss. Adarand addresses justification for an actual cognizable classification; it does not supply the missing cause of action. Rule 52 governs fact findings, not the legal sufficiency of those findings.

Actual Hunt, May 17, 1999, vacated the earlier judgment and confined remand to actually presented independent claims. It did not require a predominance-only trial. The supplied historical trial findings may be considered as objective evidence of what the trial court found; they do not prove proceedings complying with this different mandate. Neither a preserved independent injury nor the absence, waiver or forfeiture of every independent claim has been established. That exact limit prevents a final merits judgment rejecting all conceivable claims or a finding of liability on an invented one. It does not prevent setting aside a judgment based on the legally insufficient predominance premise and directing a confined actual-law remand. No litigant default is inferred from unavailable procedural material.

## Adversarial paths

**A — apply the actual mandate.** Vacate the invalidation and dependent relief in both appeals. A predominance finding alone cannot support them under Shaw, Miller, Vera and Hunt. Remand to identify and adjudicate only an actually presented, preserved independent claim, if one exists, using its own elements, ordinary procedure and appropriately material existing findings. Do not require a new motive trial, invent a claim or deem an unknown one forfeited. This is a legal vacatur, not a pure GVR or a final validation of the map.

**Strongest objection:** A completed trial now establishes racial predominance, removing the evidentiary dispute that previously defeated summary judgment. The answer is that the five-Justice Hunt rule did not rest merely on that dispute: it expressly rejected predominance as the cause of action. A fuller finding of a legally insufficient fact does not cure the antecedent legal defect. Nor may appellate disagreement with that finding substitute for identifying the actual constitutional question.

**B — retain the four-Justice contrary constitutional position.** Expressly reconsider the governing design-only boundary and permit a claim that predominant racial assignment subordinating ordinary criteria requires constitutional justification. This is a proposed change in law, not a statement that the current rule already requires scrutiny. Its strongest case is the actual racial sorting and unequal-treatment objection articulated by Kennedy's Hunt separate writing and joined by O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas. Its strongest obstacle is the controlling Shaw/Miller/Hunt rule and the need to distinguish a common territorial electorate from actual exclusion or inferior civic status. Mere awareness, percentages or unusual shape remain insufficient even on this proposed path.

The supported bounded remedy on B is vacatur and remand to retain the predominance claim for adjudication under the expressly proposed constitutional rule, rather than a conclusive affirmance based only on the trial court's description of facially race-driven criteria without compelling justification. This handoff does not certify compliance with actual Hunt, independently affirm the entire Rule 52 record, or adjudicate a complete replacement justification analysis from partial source findings. The four B supporters would remove the majority's foreclosure of that claim; they do not join A's constitutional premise or its remand limitation. Their judgment agreement is therefore not eight votes for the same governing law.

**Rejected overbroad endpoints:** Final approval of every aspect of the map requires a reliable account of preserved claims and their resolution. Final invalidation under current law requires a proved independent deprivation. Neither predicate is supplied. Neither court may manufacture the predicate through a hypothetical response or an assumed waiver. A confined remand remains lawful without an invented new claim, deadline, election or replacement map.

**Provisional arithmetic and downstream constraint:** Eight Associates support vacatur with further proceedings, but only Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support A's actual-law remand. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support B's proposed retention of the predominance claim and oppose that limitation. Assembly must decide the governing proposition and remand after adding the authorized final participant; it must not describe an eight-Justice substantive holding or restore B without a majority actually adopting it. Both dockets follow the same resulting mandate.

**Questions left open:** Existence and preservation of an independent claim, its proof and justification, actual compliance proceedings under Hunt, final plan validity, the state-law replacement-plan condition, a new election and particular map relief. No later fact or same-day Shaw v. Murphy rule supplies an answer. Broader disposition requires the precise missing procedural and claim source; narrow legal vacatur does not.
