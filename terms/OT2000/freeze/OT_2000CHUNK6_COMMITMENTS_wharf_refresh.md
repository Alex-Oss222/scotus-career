## Result and exact scope

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer independently retain affirmance of the reviewed federal securities determination. There is no changed vote or sufficient ground. The refresh expressly narrows the described application to the actual option that the Tenth Circuit held enforceable under then-applicable Colorado law, a determination outside the accepted question.

A seller's sale of an actual option while concealing a contemporaneous intention never to honor the undertaking may constitute deception in connection with the purchase or sale of that security. This option's oral form supplies no categorical federal exclusion. The option itself was acquired; the absence of a later share transfer does not turn that purchaser into a person who merely hoped to purchase. The jury's actual-option and initial-intent findings, supported by contemporaneous evidence, govern this limited legal review. An honestly made promise followed by nonperformance, negligence or mere contractual unfairness is insufficient.

The actual state-law enforceability determination remains an express premise. The Court does not construe the repealed Colorado provision, decide federal liability for a state-law-unenforceable agreement, or announce that state enforceability is invariably an additional element of every section 10(b) action. No inference from the 1996 repeal changes the historical agreement or the scope of this grant.

Materiality, required culpability, legally sufficient reliance, causation and recoverable loss retain their separate force. Neither oral form nor breach supplies a presumption establishing them. Kravitz's unanimous knowing-false-undertaking holding supports the fraud/breach distinction without supplying a new option definition or a general primary-recklessness rule. O'Hagan supplies the necessary transaction connection while leaving its separate source-deception disagreements intact. Central Bank's assistance theory is unnecessary to this direct seller claim. Blue Chip Stamps's actual-purchaser limitation and Santa Fe's deception requirement remain fully operative.

## Competing path, remedy and downstream instruction

The strongest contrary path characterizes the transaction as only a disputed oral promise and invokes Blue Chip's proof concerns or the danger of converting breach into securities fraud. On this record that path would require disregarding accepted jury findings, adding an oral-writing exclusion to federal coverage or reopening the state-law determination outside review. The enforceability addendum supplies no supported ground for doing any of those things. A defective instruction could require a different remedy only if a real preserved defect were established; none is supplied by this refresh.

All eight can join the same bounded explanation and affirmance without a separate writing. The added reservation applies equally to all eight. It is a scope clarification, not a change to the fraud rule or an alternative holding deciding state enforceability.

Affirm only the reviewed federal securities determination. The state-law grounds and any unreviewed damages issues retain their lawful existing posture; no new punitive award, damages calculation, share transfer, rewritten contract or new trial is ordered. The final holding and resulting-law sections must not suggest that oral form makes state enforceability irrelevant in every case. State the accepted enforceability premise and reserve the different agreement expressly. No substantive blocker remains.
