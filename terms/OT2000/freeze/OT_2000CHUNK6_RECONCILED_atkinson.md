## Final common non-Stone grounds and remedy

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer reverse the Tenth Circuit's approval of the hotel occupancy tax on the challenged nonmember guest transactions and return for conforming lawful relief. Each directly joins the following fee-land rule, its application and remedial limits.

Montana governs nonmember guest occupancy transactions at this nonmember hotel on non-Indian fee land within the reservation. No identified treaty or statute specifically authorizes the asserted tax. Merrion's sovereign-taxing-power principle is not merely a private owner's exclusion right, but its tribal-land lease relationship does not establish unlimited power over these fee-land transactions. The reservation boundary remains intact; neither title nor geography may be made to answer every distinct question of governing power. Free-ranging territorial balancing cannot replace the applicable source and exceptions.

The consensual-relationship exception requires a meaningful relation between the asserted tax and commercial dealings, contracts, leases or similar arrangements with the Tribe or members. General police, fire and medical benefits do not establish such consent for every nonmember transaction. The federal Indian-trader license is not itself a tribal agreement authorizing this occupancy tax. Even considering a business relationship, a connection in one area does not authorize an unrelated levy on nonmember guests. Because the operator's contacts fail even when considered, the decision need not decide whether those contacts could ever justify a tax whose legal incidence falls on guests. The holding does not require express tax consent or privity universally and does not foreclose a properly grounded fee for a particular service actually rendered; no such fee is adjudicated here.

The second exception retains authority over nonmember conduct threatening or directly affecting tribal political integrity, economic security, health or welfare. Ordinary revenue needs, service provision and business presence do not by themselves supply that qualifying tribal effect for this tax. Their consequences are real but insufficient; the rule adopts no new quantified threshold, does not reduce every limb of the exception to one verbal formula, and does not decide a different demonstrated governmental threat. The Court does not invalidate taxes on members, qualifying tribal leases or transactions, or power supplied by superior federal authorization.

Substantive taxing authority and amenability to suit remain distinct. Under Strate, appropriate conforming relief must fit actual claims, lawful parties, immunity and ordinary equity; reversal does not automatically establish a refund claim or every injunction predicate. C & L enforces a particular clear contractual waiver, not a waiver inferred from location, commerce or services. Its integrated undertaking is absent here. The returned court must give the no-authority ruling its proper effect without inventing a monetary cause of action, unrestricted waiver or new tax arrangement.

## Additional position preserved after comparison

The frozen commitment's statement that no separate writing was necessary did not establish abandonment of a broader lawful position. Full-opinion verification identifies strong same-case evidence that Souter, Kennedy and Thomas favor Montana's general nonmember principle as the first inquiry even on tribal or trust land, with land status relevant to the exception analysis. Nothing in their current Strate joins contradicts that additional starting-rule view: Strate decided the public-highway claim and reserved tribal/internal-road settings rather than deciding the opposite rule for all tribal land. No changed factual premise is required to preserve these three Justices' own legal position.

Those three retain that broader clarification as a noncontrolling additional position while joining the common fee-land holding in full. If the lead opinion remains confined as the frozen common commitments provide, the additional position has a distinct separate-writing function; it must not be silently treated as a holding joined by all eight, a new categorical adjudication of every tribal-land case, or a reason to exclude the historical complete joins. Authorship remains for assembly under the Engine. No other Associate is assigned a join to that broader proposition on the present record. This reconciliation preserves the historical substantive qualification rather than inventing a new dissent or copying historical authorship.

The common fee-land result matches history. Current Strate and C & L independently require the explicit remedy boundaries and preservation of sovereign taxation; matching history does not authorize importing a broader all-land controlling rule, a collapsed land/exclusion theory or automatic coercive relief. The three-Justice clarification changes no judgment vote or common proposition. No unsupported historical departure or material source blocker remains.
