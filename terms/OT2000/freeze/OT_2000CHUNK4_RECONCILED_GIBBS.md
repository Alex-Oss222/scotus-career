## Comparator and changed governing law

The lower majority affirmed summary judgment. It rejected reliance on channels or instrumentalities merely from the wolves' earlier interstate transport, then sustained the regulation through substantial-effects reasoning. Its account combines commercial motives for some takings, interstate tourism and scientific activity, potential future markets, the broader endangered-species program, and the relation between private-land protection and the federal recovery effort. It did not hold that each asserted economic effect was independently substantial. Its reasoning about a comprehensive conservation regime does not establish every element of this Court's necessary-inclusion route. The treaty theory was unnecessary to its judgment.

Judge Luttig regarded the regulated taking as noneconomic, the asserted economic effects as remote or speculative, and the proposed aggregation as unable to establish federal power; he distinguished continued federal spending and federal-land protection. His dissent is a lower-court comparator, not evidence that any particular Justice must adopt his disposition or every premise.

The actual controlling Home Builders rule changes the legal inquiry materially. An owner's commercial motive does not recast the enacted taking class. A necessary-inclusion ground must identify the independently valid interstate regulation, the intrastate class actually covered and a genuine functional relationship in which inclusion is a reasonably adapted and integral means of executing that regulation; it requires no species-by-species, border-crossing or independent-effects proof. An independent-externality ground must identify a concrete substantial cross-border effect, the affected States' inability reasonably to internalize it through ordinary territorial authority, and fit. That route remains an alternative that need not be satisfied where necessary inclusion establishes power. Morrison preserves these requirements and denies a general entitlement to another evidentiary opportunity whenever a theory fails. These rules preclude treating the lower majority's combination of conservation importance and economic consequences as a complete application of governing law.

The frozen broader affirmance proposal is candidly a proposed doctrinal change. It rests on the four Justices' actual Morrison dissent rather than a false claim that the lower rationale already satisfies Home Builders. The Route A affirmance is also a distinct legal choice: it applies Home Builders Part II-A's necessary-inclusion rule to the Section 10(j) regulation within the Act's interstate system, without relying on the lower majority's combination of effects or finding the externality route proved. Immediate confined relief is a third choice, supported by the different Home Builders position of Scalia and Thomas. No lower-court result supplies an arithmetic shortcut among these choices.

## Rule and remedy limits

Any supported Route A opinion must identify the valid federal interstate regulation of listed species, the intrastate class the private-land taking rule covers, and the genuine functional relationship by which the rule preserves the populations and enforceability of that system. Actual interstate visits are not erased by disputes over revenue forecasts, but they are not the connection. Movement from refuge to nearby private land does not become interstate migration. The opinion does not rest on new science, invented failed agreements, a universal findings checklist, or proof that each animal crossed a state line, and it orders no new evidentiary proceedings. The externality route need not be reached.

The broader proposal may not be presented as an application that leaves the connection prerequisite intact. Conversely, a Route A opinion may not announce that tourism, program comprehensiveness, or conservation importance by itself establishes power. The actual Solid Waste majority decided statutory authority and left Commerce power unresolved; its four-Justice ecological position involved a materially different shared mobile interstate bird population. Neither supplies the missing red-wolf facts.

The statutory exceptions remain those established in the validated packet: nonintentional and nonwillful taking under its conditions; protection of human life; taking during an actual livestock or pet attack with freshly wounded or killed animals evident; nonlethal, noninjurious harassment; and the written-approval procedure after abandoned capture efforts, together with applicable reporting conditions. They show the rule's adaptation to its function within the Act's system and remain governed by their own conditions. There is no general cattle-concern exception. Assembly must carry their complete validated terms whenever it relies on them, without replacing distinct conditions with an undefined exception reference.

No disposition decides Mann's earlier conviction, damages or compensation, federal land title, an unpresented treaty or Property Clause ground, or every ESA application. Affirmance authorizes no taking beyond the regulation's exceptions. Confined invalidation is likewise no affirmative permit to kill contrary to other valid law. The adopted merits-submission premise supplies no invented argument date or Supreme Court docket.
