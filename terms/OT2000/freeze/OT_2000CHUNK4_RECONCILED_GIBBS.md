## Comparator and changed governing law

The lower majority affirmed summary judgment. It rejected reliance on channels or instrumentalities merely from the wolves' earlier interstate transport, then sustained the regulation through substantial-effects reasoning. Its account combines commercial motives for some takings, interstate tourism and scientific activity, potential future markets, the broader endangered-species program, and the relation between private-land protection and the federal recovery effort. It did not hold that each asserted economic effect was independently substantial. Its reasoning about a comprehensive conservation regime does not establish every element of this Court's necessary-inclusion route. The treaty theory was unnecessary to its judgment.

Judge Luttig regarded the regulated taking as noneconomic, the asserted economic effects as remote or speculative, and the proposed aggregation as unable to establish federal power; he distinguished continued federal spending and federal-land protection. His dissent is a lower-court comparator, not evidence that any particular Justice must adopt his disposition or every premise.

The actual controlling Home Builders rule changes the legal inquiry materially. An owner's commercial motive does not recast the enacted taking class. A necessary-regime ground must identify valid federal interstate regulation and supported evasion or material impairment from exclusion. An independent-externality ground must identify a concrete substantial cross-border effect, the affected States' inability reasonably to internalize it through ordinary territorial authority, and fit. Morrison preserves these requirements and denies a general entitlement to another evidentiary opportunity whenever a theory fails. These rules preclude treating the lower majority's combination of conservation importance and economic consequences as a complete application of governing law.

The frozen broader affirmance proposal is candidly a proposed doctrinal change. It rests on the four Justices' actual Morrison dissent rather than a false claim that the lower rationale already satisfies Home Builders. The bounded remand is also a distinct legal choice: it applies the actual Home Builders framework to identified existing evidence, without finding either mechanism proved. Immediate confined relief is a third choice, supported by the different Home Builders position of Scalia and Thomas. No lower-court result supplies an arithmetic shortcut among these choices.

## Remand, rule, and remedy limits

Any supported common remand must direct examination of the already existing legislative and adjudicative record. For the regime route, identify the actual valid federal interstate regulation and why excluding this taking class permits evasion or materially impairs it. For the externality route, identify the concrete cross-border effect, supported substantiality, the affected States' inability reasonably to internalize it, and fit. Actual interstate visits are not erased by disputes over revenue forecasts. Movement from refuge to nearby private land does not become interstate migration. The record inquiry does not authorize new science, invented failed agreements, a universal findings checklist, or unlimited new evidentiary proceedings. If neither complete route has adequate support, confined relief follows under the remand position.

The broader proposal may not be presented as an application that leaves the connection prerequisite intact. Conversely, a remand may not announce that tourism, program comprehensiveness, or conservation importance already establishes power. The actual Solid Waste majority decided statutory authority and left Commerce power unresolved; its four-Justice ecological position involved a materially different shared mobile interstate bird population. Neither supplies the missing red-wolf facts.

The statutory exceptions remain those established in the validated packet: nonintentional and nonwillful taking under its conditions; protection of human life; taking during an actual livestock or pet attack with freshly wounded or killed animals evident; nonlethal, noninjurious harassment; and the written-approval procedure after abandoned capture efforts, together with applicable reporting conditions. They may bear on fit only after power is established. There is no general cattle-concern exception. Assembly must carry their complete validated terms whenever it relies on them, without replacing distinct conditions with an undefined exception reference.

No disposition decides Mann's earlier conviction, damages or compensation, federal land title, an unpresented treaty or Property Clause ground, or every ESA application. Remand itself authorizes no prohibited taking. Confined invalidation is likewise no affirmative permit to kill contrary to other valid law. The adopted merits-submission premise supplies no invented argument date or Supreme Court docket.
