# Gitlitz v. Commissioner

# SECTION I: NEUTRAL MODELING PACKET

## A. CASE, RECORD, AND POSTURE

* **Case Name:** Gitlitz v. Commissioner
* **Citation or Docket:** No. 99-1295; 531 U.S. 206
* **October Term:** 2000
* **Entry Stage and Event:** Merits submission for decision; the historical decision date supplies scheduling only.
* **Simulated Event Date:** 2001-01-09 (historical).
* **Question Presented:** Whether income from an insolvent S corporation's discharged debt, excluded from gross income, passes through to shareholders and increases basis, and whether that adjustment precedes corporate tax-attribute reduction.
* **Question Granted:** The issues stated above define the scope of review; their wording follows the judicial record rather than a verified verbatim petition.
* **Court and Judgment Under Review:** Tenth Circuit, 182 F.3d 1143: affirmed the Tax Court's deficiency determination on the view that attribute reduction absorbed the debt-discharge amount before shareholder pass-through.
* **Relief Requested:** The shareholders seek reversal and allowance of the basis increases and resulting loss deductions under the tax-year statute; the Commissioner seeks affirmance.
* **Material Record:** In 1991 an insolvent S corporation excluded discharged indebtedness from gross income. Gitlitz and Winn passed their pro rata portions through, increased stock basis, and used the resulting basis to deduct losses, including suspended losses. The Commissioner denied that treatment. The dispute concerns the statutory characterization and sequence, not whether the corporation was solvent or whether a later amendment should control the earlier year.
* **Threshold, Preservation, and Vehicle Matters:** The tax-year text governs. A perceived double benefit cannot establish an unwritten exception, but the provisions must be read together rather than in isolation. No separate constitutional question or adjudication of Congress's later policy response is required.
* **Relevant Dates:** The returns concern the 1991 discharge year. The event is January 9, 2001; later legislative changes are not entering law.
* **Companion or Related Matters:** None requiring a separate module.
* **Participation Matter:** No case-specific recusal or nonparticipation is identified. Historical participation is addressed in Section III.

### Issue 1: Income character and sequencing

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** The enacted definitions, cross-references, and temporal provisions govern the tax treatment. No controlling decision settles this S-corporation discharge sequence.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** Ordinary statutory construction governs; a tax policy objection must be connected to a plausible reading of the operative provisions.
* **Other Governing Law:** Then-applicable 26 U.S.C. §§61(a)(12), 108(a), 108(b)(4)(A), 108(d)(7), 1366 and 1367.
* **Material Other Authority:** No additional authority is necessary beyond the sources identified here.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** Exclusion from gross income does not invariably eliminate an item's income character. The shareholder-basis limit and corporate attribute reduction perform distinct work.
* **Open Point:** Whether this excluded item passes through, and whether the express after-tax-determination timing places basis adjustment before attribute reduction.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The court below assumed income character but required reduction first. The Commissioner also argues that discharge excluded at the corporate level should not become shareholder tax-exempt income generating deductible losses without an economic outlay. The taxpayers rely on the broad pass-through language, the continuing characterization of debt discharge as income, and the direction that attribute reduction occur after determination of the discharge-year tax. Their reading must still apply all statutory limits and cannot increase basis for an amount the governing text does not recognize.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**Pass-through first:** Reverse and permit basis adjustment before attribute reduction under the applicable year's text; calculate the resulting lawful deductions. **Reduction first or no pass-through:** Affirm if the integrated provisions support that sequence or exclusion. A holding for either side governs the statutory version and mechanism actually presented, not a general exemption from tax or a universal anti-windfall power. No finding concerning later amendments, different tax attributes, or unproved amounts is necessary.
