## Final petition and merits positions

All eight Associates independently retain their separately frozen votes to grant review. That poll is internal and supplies no merits vote or public tally. A public entry states certiorari granted and the public merits action; it must not describe the petition as unanimously granted.

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer each support a reasoned summary disposition vacating the state judgment on its stated federal rationale and remanding under the following rules. This is a legal correction with governing instructions, not an unexplained return that settles nothing.

* An ordinary initial traffic stop supported by objective probable cause is not invalid solely because an officer also investigates a different offense. A state court must follow controlling Supreme Court federal constitutional law; greater protection under actual independent state law remains possible, but no such ground is invented here. Whren supplies the stop rule and Oregon v. Hass the federal-hierarchy distinction.
* A valid stop does not itself authorize every subsequent restraint or search. Effective Atwater extends Ricci to added station transport and confinement for a known fine-only criminal offense: the custody must reasonably serve a supported actual enforcement function. Identification, appearance, safety, a continuing violation, evidence protection and necessary processing are nonexclusive examples, not presumed facts. Neither least restrictive means nor citation whenever conceivable is required. Offense classification and actual supported arrest grounds must be addressed where material; the present decision does not find every Sullivan offense fine-only, a valid weapons predicate, or a failure of identification.
* Impoundment and a claimed inventory retain their own authority, caretaking purpose and standardized-practice requirements under Bertine and Wells as preserved by Whren. Investigative interest does not invalidate every otherwise lawful act, but an investigatory ruse can defeat the inventory exception itself. A policy label cannot establish actual compliance. Knowles keeps search consequences separate from antecedent lawful custody; later-discovered drugs cannot supply antecedent probable cause.

Vacatur removes the erroneous motive-only federal rationale and the asserted power to redefine controlling federal law. It directs consideration of actual, preserved custody, impoundment, inventory and independent state-law grounds under their own standards. It commands no new hearing, speculative reopening of settled facts, admission of evidence, suppression on a new ground, conviction, acquittal or resolution of an unsupported search-incident theory. The finally settled federal question may be reviewed under the otherwise-evading-review route despite the pending prosecution, consistent with the reported Quarles jurisdictional analogy.
