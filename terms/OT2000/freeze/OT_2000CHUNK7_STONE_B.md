# Cedric Kushner Promotions, Ltd. v. King

**Matter type:** MERITS. **October Term:** OT 2000. **Event date:** 2001-06-11. **Date status:** historical.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Precedent effective through June 29, 2000, subject to any intervening decision effective before the stated event date.
* **Conditions:** Subject to intervening controlling precedent and material corrections to the record.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Second Circuit's dismissal judgment on the distinctness issue.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Reinstate consideration of the § 1962(c) claim, requiring proof of the remaining statutory elements and allowing preserved defenses. Award no damages, fees, or injunction at this stage.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Legal separateness under § 1962(c)

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The undisputed corporate structure and dismissal posture squarely present statutory identity. The necessary issue is whether King's corporate role negates distinctness before evidence on racketeering is heard.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does an individual become legally identical to the corporation he owns merely because his alleged racketeering conduct was authorized corporate activity?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply existing element-by-element statutory interpretation and adopt a new application holding on distinctness, which entering law has not expressly resolved.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A natural person and a legally separate corporation satisfy the person-enterprise distinction under § 1962(c), including where that person owns the corporation and acts within his authority. The plaintiff must still prove that the person directed enterprise affairs through the required pattern and caused compensable statutory injury. King's ownership and in-scope conduct therefore cannot sustain this dismissal.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule covers natural owners and employees conducting a corporation's affairs. Corporate existence supplies distinct identity, not liability for ownership. It creates no respondeat superior rule and does not determine a different enterprise configuration.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Congress chose individual and corporate categories whose legal distinction normally matters precisely when individuals act for companies. Treating authorized operation as identity would exempt the conduct that the direction element otherwise reaches. The proposed functional-independence limitation has no support in these enacted words. Preserving all other elements answers the concern that every corporate dispute becomes a successful RICO action; inventing an identity exemption does not.

  A formal distinction is appropriate here because Congress used legally constituted persons and enterprises as separate statutory categories. That textual choice, rather than an assessment of King's economic independence from his company, is decisive. Bestfoods supports respect for corporate identity by analogy; Reves still requires actual participation in directing enterprise affairs. The categorical identity rule answers the recurring legal question without deciding the unproved racketeering allegations.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Corporate-person/association-in-fact identity and vicarious corporate liability lie outside the question presented. Whether the alleged offenses, pattern, and causal injury are proved requires facts this dismissal record lacks.

# Good News Club v. Milford Central School

**Matter type:** MERITS. **October Term:** OT 2000. **Event date:** 2001-06-11. **Date status:** historical.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Precedent effective through June 29, 2000, subject to any intervening decision effective before the stated event date.
* **Conditions:** Subject to intervening controlling precedent and material corrections to the record.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse summary judgment for Milford insofar as it validates exclusion of this otherwise comparable private program because its character teaching includes religious instruction, prayer, and worship.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Direct equal access for the Club under the same genuine neutral operational rules as comparable groups, with declaratory and access relief implementing that entitlement. On the stated record, independent adult control, parental authorization, and after-school use defeat the asserted establishment defense; youth, location, and devotional content do not justify withholding relief. Any remaining damages or fees require their own determinations.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Access and the governmental relationship

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The admitted community subjects and stated religious basis for denial present a speech claim. Because the school invokes its constitutional establishment duty, the permissible governmental relationship must also be decided; it cannot remain an unexamined defense to the remedy. Other independent claims and monetary relief are not reached because the speech ground supplies the judgment.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May a public school exclude comparable private moral instruction because its means are devotional, and does permission-based private use itself establish religion?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply entering Bronx Household of Faith v. Community School District No. 10 and Agostini v. Felton. This position does not represent Stone's earlier separate disagreement as controlling or adopt silently replacing the Court's access rule.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Within this broad private community forum, religious instruction and devotional form cannot disqualify otherwise comparable moral and character instruction. Independent adult sponsorship, parental authorization, and after-school operation constitute permissible private access absent actual official adoption or substantial government-created religious pressure. Location, youth, and prayer alone do not establish those prohibited governmental acts.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule governs the requested private Club use on the same valid terms as comparable groups. It does not authorize teacher-led official devotions, compulsory religious participation, or preferential school recruitment. Parental permission is material evidence of voluntary private choice, not an automatic answer to every institutional-pressure claim.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The school has opened moral instruction to outsiders. Its religious/secular distinction removes a viewpoint and its expressive method from an admitted subject. Bronx Household of Faith v. Community School District No. 10 rejects that distinction for comparable worship. The separate establishment inquiry asks who organizes and controls the religious activity and how the school engages participants. A private meeting on school premises is not converted into governmental religious teaching by the content of its story or closing prayer.

  Stone distinguishes equal private-forum access from governmental financing or sponsorship of devotion. His earlier separate disagreement in Bronx Household does not displace that decision's controlling access rule, which he now applies to the admitted subject. Young age and immediate after-school timing warrant attention to actual official recruitment or compulsion; they do not justify demanding proof that private religious expression can never influence a child. A remand must resolve a supported material issue. Funding and compulsory-school-devotion cases retain their distinct constitutional questions.

  Stone reaffirms the controlling forum rule and distinguishes official devotion by the school's actual role. The decisive speech principle is viewpoint neutrality within the subjects admitted; the functional inquiry into sponsorship and practical pressure separately answers the school's establishment defense. Age and timing are legally relevant circumstances, not automatic disqualifications or a requirement that parents prove religious influence impossible. The stated record establishes private use and identifies no material finding of official adoption or compulsion that defeats access. Remand implements equal access rather than reopens the rejected establishment defense.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Official school devotion and materially different compulsory programs are outside this private-forum question. Any damages or fee amount requires proceedings not completed on this record; the Club's access entitlement is resolved.

# Kyllo v. United States

**Matter type:** MERITS. **October Term:** OT 2000. **Event date:** 2001-06-11. **Date status:** historical.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling precedent, subject to intervening decisions effective before June 11, 2001.
* **Conditions:** Any intervening controlling decision must be incorporated before adjudication.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the no-search ruling and vacate the dependent conviction affirmance to the extent necessary to decide the preserved suppression claim.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Remand for the lower court to determine whether the later warrant and admitted evidence remain supportable without the unlawful scan, and to decide properly presented alternative grounds under their actual requirements. Suppress the derivative evidence if no lawful ground remains; then apply the conditional-plea rule. No automatic acquittal, release, new hearing, or sentence is ordered.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: The thermal search and its consequences

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Kyllo preserved the deliberate home-surveillance challenge. The record identifies the sensor, target, heat pattern, inference, and lack of a scan warrant; no speculative future technology is needed.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** Did agents search the home by technologically acquiring concealed domestic-operation information, and what must be corrected when the conviction was affirmed on a no-search premise?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply United States v. Pinson and United States v. Myers; reject a general-public-use limitation.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Targeted technological reception of a closed home's differential heat pattern is a search when it reveals concealed domestic operation otherwise unavailable to ordinary observation. This scan disclosed the use of indoor cultivation equipment and required a warrant or recognized exception. None justified the scan itself. The later warrant cannot cure it retroactively, while admissibility requires its own preserved, fact-supported determination.

* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule protects concealed domestic information regardless of whether it is expressed as an image, numerical pattern, or inference. It does not convert ordinary external observation into a search merely because it aids investigation. Widespread purchase of a sensor does not itself waive home privacy.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Escape of physical energy is not public disclosure of the domestic facts encoded in it. The distinction between passive and penetrating equipment describes mechanism, not the privacy interest. The lower findings confirm an inference about hidden operation even while denying identifiable interior images. Applying the entering rule therefore requires correction, followed by a genuine causal and remedial inquiry rather than either automatic exclusion or automatic good faith.

  Pinson and Myers control the search characterization. Their functional treatment of concealed domestic information is decisive, supported by the home's constitutional protection; the physical mechanism and commercial availability do not supply competing exceptions. Reaffirming that rule protects future comparable thermal examinations without announcing a rule for every sensor. The distinct remedy inquiry cannot dilute the search holding or excuse the deliberate scan simply because an eventual warrant issued.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The sufficiency of the untainted warrant and particular reliance facts require lower-record findings not established here. Other surveillance technologies and open commercial premises lie outside the question presented.

# Tuan Anh Nguyen v. INS

**Matter type:** MERITS. **October Term:** OT 2000. **Event date:** 2001-06-11. **Date status:** historical.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling precedent, subject to intervening decisions effective before June 11, 2001.
* **Conditions:** Any intervening controlling decision must be incorporated before adjudication.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse rejection of the equal-protection claim and vacate the removal affirmance insofar as it rests on the unconstitutional paternal-recognition condition.

* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Remove the male-only timely-recognition barrier from the applicable transmission scheme and remand for confirmation of all remaining valid prerequisites under Nguyen's available statutory election. If established, recognize statutory citizenship and terminate removal on alienage grounds. If an independent valid prerequisite fails, decide its consequence through the ordinary process. Do not vacate criminal convictions or award damages.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Equality and statutory corrective relief

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The citizen father personally challenges unequal transmission; biological parentage and an actual relationship are established in the described record. Because alienage supports removal, the constitutional condition and its remedial consequence both require decision.

* **Question Stone Resolves:** May Congress add a male-only minority recognition deadline to otherwise established parentage, and can the resulting statutory inequality be cured without withdrawing citizenship from children of mothers?

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply existing demanding sex-classification scrutiny and adopt a new application and severability holding. Miller v. Albright left these points unresolved; this position does not claim its separate opinions already established them.

* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A sex-specific citizenship condition must substantially advance its genuine objective. The additional paternal-recognition deadline fails where the statute already requires reliable proof of biological parentage and its formal acts do not ensure an actual relationship: it excludes an established caregiving father while accepting bare timely acknowledgment. Sever the discriminatory barrier, leaving Congress's underlying citizenship transmission and lawful remaining qualifications in force.

* **Reach Within the Question:** This reaches the challenged male-only recognition requirement and its application to Nguyen's available statutory regime. It leaves legitimate parentage proof, citizen-parent status, and applicable physical presence intact. It does not constitutionalize all parent-child transmission or excuse unrelated valid qualifications.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Birth makes maternal identification easier; it does not justify a permanent male-only loss after parentage is reliably proved. Opportunity for a relationship is important, but a mother can relinquish the child immediately and a father can meet the statutory act without sustained contact. Nguyen's upbringing exposes the mismatch without making an individual hardship exception the rule. Corrective extension preserves the general transmission scheme and settled citizenship interests more faithfully than withdrawing maternal transmission. This is enforcement of a statute after constitutional severance, not free-floating equitable naturalization contrary to a valid statute.

  The Government's strongest distinction is real: birth ordinarily establishes maternal identity and an initial opportunity for contact more directly than it establishes paternity. Intermediate scrutiny permits reasonable generalization from actual differences and does not demand a perfect proxy or the least restrictive measure. Stone nevertheless would reject this additional sex-specific recognition deadline because reliable parentage is already separately required and the formal act can admit a father with no sustained relationship while excluding the established relationship here. Those features bear on substantial fit; underinclusiveness alone is not the rule of invalidity. Removing that barrier does not erase other unreviewed support or residence conditions.

  Demanding intermediate scrutiny supplies the standard. Comparison of the statute's treatment of mothers and fathers and its own recognition requirements tests substantial fit; the statutory design and established parentage supply the decisive reasons without requiring a demographic study. This is a general rejection of the additional sex-based deadline, not a sympathetic exception for one family. Severability implements that conclusion while preserving valid transmission requirements.

* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Separate support and physical-presence classifications are outside this reviewed recognition question. Definitive citizenship depends on any remaining qualification findings absent from the record.
