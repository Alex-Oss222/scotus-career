# INS v. St. Cyr

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Scalia dissented with Rehnquist and Thomas; O'Connor joined Parts I and III of that dissent and filed a separate dissent.

* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court preserved §2241 jurisdiction over the legal claim and held that former §212(c) relief remained available to persons whose preenactment plea convictions would not have disqualified them under the law at the plea. It used habeas-preservation and constitutional-avoidance principles on jurisdiction and retroactivity analysis on eligibility.

* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparison only; it does not establish entering law.

* **Historical Sources:** [INS v. St. Cyr](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-533/pdf/USREPORTS-533-289.pdf).
