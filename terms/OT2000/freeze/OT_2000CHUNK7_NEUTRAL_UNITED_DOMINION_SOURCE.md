# United Dominion neutral source verification

Control checked the supplied neutral packet's statutory and regulatory descriptions against the official report's discussion of the versions governing 1983-1986: 532 U.S. 822, pages 825-826 and 831-834. This receipt reports law and party-position source support only; the same-matter Court outcome and reasoning remain withheld. Fresh neutral validation is required before reliance.

The report expressly identifies its Code and Treasury citations as the versions applicable to the litigated tax years. Section 172(c) defines net operating loss by deductions exceeding gross income. Section 172(j)(1) makes product liability loss the lesser of net operating loss for the year and allowable product liability expenses; section 172(b)(1)(I) permits the special ten-year carryback. Sections 1501 and 1502 authorize consolidated filing and Treasury's detailed rules.

Treasury Regulations 1.1502-11(a) and 1.1502-21(f) build consolidated taxable income or consolidated net operating loss from members' separate taxable income and the additional group-level items. Section 1.1502-12 computes separate taxable income as for a separate corporation subject to modifications, including exclusion of capital gains and losses, charitable contributions, and dividends-received deductions. Those excluded items enter the consolidated computation under the specified regulations. It therefore matters that separate taxable income is a specially modified figure; its relationship to an ordinary separate-return net operating loss cannot be assumed from its name.

Section 1.1502-79 is entitled Separate return years. Its paragraph (a) addresses carryover and carryback of consolidated net operating loss to separate return years. Paragraph (a)(3) defines a separate net operating loss for the purposes of that subparagraph through the section 1.1502-12 computation adjusted for items accounted for in the consolidated loss. The Fourth Circuit used that measure; the Government also advanced separate taxable income as a proxy. Whether either figure governs the initial product liability calculation is the unresolved question for independent modeling. This receipt does not treat the Court's later answer as existing law.

The official recitation supports the neutral packet's account of the competing computations. It does not reproduce every clause of the original tax-year regulations. No direct inspection of the original annual CFR volumes is claimed; exact quotation of an unrecovered clause is not authorized.
