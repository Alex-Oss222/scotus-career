## Common law, disputed application and boundaries

All eight recognize personal protection of the ordinary pistol and necessary ammunition without militia membership. Wright separately requires justification according to actual burden: strict scrutiny for core deprivation, intermediate scrutiny for substantial regulation, and reasonableness only for incidental requirements imposing no meaningful interference. Its categorical prohibition on general abolition of meaningful ordinary defensive home possession remains. The six affirmers directly join intermediate scrutiny for this particular conduct-based disability, the important armed-recurrence objective, substantial fit and affirmance; the two remand proponents do not join those application propositions.

Section 922(g)(8) requires actual hearing notice and an opportunity to participate, the specified intimate-partner or child restraint, and either the credible-threat finding in (C)(i) or the express prohibition of injurious physical force in (C)(ii). These alternatives remain distinct. The reported findings that domestic violence occurred and may recur are not relabeled a statutory credible-threat finding. Napier's participation and admitted operative orders distinguish an ex parte order or mere accusation. His receipt-of-copy objection does not establish absent hearing notice; the subsequent assault report proves no new assault or conviction. The prosecuted possession was in a vehicle, not a separately established home-possession event.

The six affirmers classify the restriction by its concrete adjudicated conduct, domestic predicates and order-dependent operation, not merely because others retain arms. They reject an indispensable criminal-conviction rule while leaving materially different predicates and applications outside this bounded holding. The two remand proponents accept the relevance of those findings but require analysis of the actual full prohibition, its basis and operation before resolving justification. Their path permits existing-record decision or ordinary proceedings if needed; it does not find the hearings defective from missing research materials.
