## Final common disposition and controlling scope

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer each affirm the reviewed federal securities determination and join the same complete rationale. Selling an actual option while concealing a contemporaneous intention never to honor it is deception in connection with that security's sale, subject to materiality, required culpability, legally sufficient reliance, causation and recoverable loss. Oral form alone does not exclude this proved option. The buyer acquired the option through services; failure of the later share transfer does not make it merely a disappointed prospective purchaser. Later nonperformance, negligence, unfairness or breach alone does not establish initial fraud.

The Tenth Circuit's determination that this option was enforceable under then-applicable Colorado law remains an express review premise, not this Court's independent construction of the repealed statute. The Court does not decide federal treatment of an agreement unenforceable under state law and does not make state enforceability an invariably additional element of every section 10(b) claim. Neither the later repeal nor lack of a final writing supplies a new basis for liability or exclusion.

Kravitz's unanimous holding already recognizes primary deception through a knowingly false securities undertaking while preserving independent private-action elements. It supplies direct entering-law support for the initial-fraud/breach distinction, not an option definition, universal execution duty or general primary-recklessness rule. O'Hagan preserves actual transaction-connected deception; its source-deception dispute need not be resolved again where the deceiving seller is the actual purchaser's counterparty. Blue Chip Stamps's purchaser limitation is satisfied by acquisition of the option, and its proof concerns do not create a writing requirement. Santa Fe excludes unfairness without deception. Central Bank's distinct affirmative-assistance action remains in force but is unnecessary to this direct seller claim.
