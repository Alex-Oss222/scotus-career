## Components and common legal baseline

**A — Actual forum exclusion.** Whether Milford may exclude this requested private program because its admitted moral and character instruction includes prayer, biblical teaching, religious songs and invitation to Christian adherence.

**B — Establishment defense on this record.** Whether the supported circumstances of this elementary-school use justify exclusion, or require further adjudication under the actual governmental-attribution and function inquiry.

**C — Judgment and remedy.** Whether to reverse and remand for nondiscriminatory relief with the asserted defense resolved, or vacate and remand for the lower court to decide the remaining establishment application on the existing record under the proper law. These are different remedial commitments even though both displace the judgment sustaining the categorical exclusion.

Lamb's Chapel protects religious viewpoints within an admitted subject. Ladue applies that rule to moral development taught through prayer and biblical instruction in an available after-school period. Bronx Household protects otherwise comparable devotional meetings in a broad educational and community forum and separately protects religious instruction on admitted subjects. A school may still enforce reasonable, viewpoint-neutral genuine subject, activity, scheduling, supervision, safety and capacity limits. Consistent exclusion alone establishes no neutrality, and neither an exact secular counterpart to a religious exercise nor removal of devotion from the program is required.

Agostini supplies the governing Lemon inquiry. Specific prohibitions and distinct doctrines retain their scope; established permissible relationships retain theirs; a legally sufficient residual question receives an attribution-and-function determination under ordinary procedure. Purpose, effects and entanglement are evidence within that inquiry, not independent former prongs. Government worship, official religious solicitation and improper religious control remain forbidden without proof of actual conversion. Lee's independent practical-pressure protection survives; Santa Fe's official representative worship mechanism is distinct from genuine private access. Marshfield requires an actual continuing governmental religious function and preserves private speech in the remedy. None creates liability from possible misunderstanding, location or religious success alone.

## Justice-specific grounds, alternatives and join barriers

### Stevens

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His Ladue dissent treats genuine activities, not a highly abstract common moral purpose, as the proper forum comparison. His Bronx Household partial dissent nevertheless joins the reversal of an instruction exclusion within subjects actually admitted, while distinguishing an integrated recurring church service. Milford's broad instructional and community policy, and the superintendent's acknowledgement that moral instruction through Aesop would be permitted, place this application on the admitted-instruction side of that distinction. The allowance is evidence of forum scope even without proof that an Aesop group actually met. Stevens does not deny the Club's religious exercise or convert it into merely secular discussion. He applies the current Bronx Household holding rather than recreating a categorical worship exclusion through a different label.

**Alternative and answer.** Affirmance under his Ladue actual-activity approach has its strongest argument in adult-led conversion instruction and the absence of a proved identical secular program. But this policy's breadth and express religion-based reason, together with Bronx Household's binding rejection of exact-counterpart reasoning, prevent those facts alone from supplying a genuine neutral activity ground. His narrower sufficient A ground concerns instruction actually admitted; he need not embrace a universal equivalence of every religious and secular activity.

**B and C.** His joins in Souter's Agostini substantive dissent and the Marshfield majority require attention to actual governmental educational support and presentation, not merely the nominal ownership of the message. Here children as young as six move from the compulsory day ending at 2:56 to a 3:00 weekly program on school premises; Milford claims use of the school's ordinary permission mechanism, continuing school-sponsored activities and school audiovisual equipment. The Club contests the elementary-school activity account, stresses private permission and leadership, and explains that Milford assigned the resource room after a cafeteria request. Stevens would have the lower court determine whether those actual arrangements present a continued official educational activity or only private room access. The question concerns the school's supported operational role, not whether Christian teaching is intense or successful. He makes no present finding of government adoption, teacher escort, pressure or increased enrollment.

**Strongest counterargument and join barrier.** Ladue and Bronx Household already protect genuine private uses, and a school address, short interval and parents' permission are not forbidden conduct. Stevens accepts those propositions but does not find that they conclusively decide the disputed operational inference for this youngest audience. He will join A and a rule preserving private access, but not a conclusive B rejection that treats the permission requirement, private designation or absence of a proven conversion as dispositive. His remand is bounded as stated below; it does not authorize a fishing inquiry or revive independent former Lemon prongs.

### O'Connor

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** Her complete joins in Ladue's actual-forum and contextual private-sponsorship analysis and Bronx Household's instruction and worship holdings support reversal. Her Rosenberger concurrence insists that the actual safeguards do the work; her full Agostini join supplies the present method. She evaluates the program as the genuinely private, parent-selected activity described, including its prayers and invitations to adhere, rather than asking whether it is insufficiently religious to count as instruction.

**Alternative and answer.** A contextual remand has force because the children are younger than Ladue's and move directly from school. Her Marshfield join confirms that formal private ownership cannot end an inquiry into continuing governmental presentation. But Marshfield involved the City's continuing integrated presentation of its historical religious centerpiece, not an ordinary community-use program. Here the asserted room assignment, time, permission form and occasional equipment use describe administration and convenience in a broad forum; they do not identify public selection of religious teaching, adoption of it or an official instructional obligation. She can reject their legal sufficiency without deciding every disputed comparator hour, room characterization or attendance statistic.

**B, C and limits.** The defense fails on this actual private-leadership, written-parental-permission and after-dismissal arrangement. Neither child comprehension nor private religious success is a constitutional condition. No quantified pressure or completed penalty is necessary if official worship exists, but those independent prohibitions are not established by the circumstances invoked here. Reverse and remand for appropriate nondiscriminatory relief. She will not join a universal neutrality exemption, a rule making parental permission an automatic defense to school worship, an unlimited facility or equipment entitlement, or a fixed disclaimer requirement. She does not require a new attribution hearing merely to disprove hypothetical misconduct.

### Scalia

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His joins in Kennedy's Ladue concurrence, the Bronx Household access holdings, Rosenberger and Agostini distinguish private religious advocacy from governmental religion and forbid viewpoint discrimination in the actual forum. His Marshfield dissent requires an actual governmental act rather than an association based on surroundings. Milford's acknowledged reason targets religious instruction, while its forum admits the relevant community and moral teaching. The denial therefore cannot stand.

**Alternative and answer.** The strongest alternative treats the immediate elementary transition and use of public classroom resources as continued school education. That inference does not follow from the school's provision of otherwise admitted space and ordinary use facilities to privately led, parent-chosen meetings. A private speaker's invitation to religious adherence is not a public solicitation merely because the school facilitates access. The government's compulsory school day does not compel attendance at this Club. He does not accept attendance growth as evidence of official recruitment; the underlying estimate and causal assertion are disputed.

**B, C and limits.** Reject the asserted establishment justification and reverse for nondiscriminatory relief. He can join a bounded Agostini application because he joined that governing formulation; no earlier withholding of Ladue's pre-Agostini complete explanation bars that join. His principal barrier is an additional inference-driven hearing grounded only in possible child misunderstanding or imagined public supervision. Actual future adoption, preferential control, official worship or substantiated school-created pressure would present different conduct. No general devotional-financing rule or permanent forum mandate follows.

### Kennedy

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His Ladue concurrence with Scalia and Thomas identifies genuinely private advocacy as the core ground, while his Rosenberger opinion protects religious viewpoints within the actual expression program. His joins in Bronx Household and Agostini support this application. His Lee pressure position and Santa Fe join distinguish school-directed religious participation from parents' selection of a private meeting.

**Alternative and answer.** The young audience and near-continuous schedule make a practical-pressure theory materially stronger than in an adult rental. But neither the meeting's availability nor the Club's persuasion establishes substantial government-created pressure to attend or participate. The supported account contains required parental permission and private leadership; Milford's particular allegations concerning school location, forms and equipment do not identify official religious direction. He need not demand proof of punishment, completed conversion or an overt threat to recognize that the asserted facts still lack the governmental connection required by Lee.

**B, C and limits.** Reject the defense and reverse for nondiscriminatory relief. His Marshfield dissent reinforces refusal to infer governmental religious action merely from physical and administrative proximity. He can join the bounded contextual rule with O'Connor and Breyer, because it does not substitute subjective endorsement for the governmental function or dilute independent pressure protection. He will not join a categorical immunity for every parent-approved activity, a rule that young children can never be pressured without legal sanctions, or a claim that actual religious recruitment by officials would be harmless. No standalone pure-worship or financing entitlement outside this forum is required.

### Souter

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His Ladue opinion directly rejects the claim that prayer and biblical instruction remove moral education from an admitted subject. His Bronx Household instruction join confirms the same principle even while his partial dissent preserves a distinct-activity objection to integrated church services. Those positions require rejection of this exclusion: the superintendent's admitted moral-teaching scope and the policy's breadth cannot be reconciled with denying the Club because it promotes the gospel. The current worship holding additionally prevents a categorical devotional-form defense.

**Alternative and answer.** Immediate reversal on the establishment question is supported by his own Ladue explanation and Bronx Household Part I join. But Ladue involved ages 11–15 and express notices of private sponsorship. The current packet identifies written parental permission without establishing the same public-attribution setting. Souter does not convert that distinction into a required disclaimer or infer that no notice existed. His Agostini substantive dissent and Marshfield majority join make the institution's actual continuing educational presentation material, even though he now applies Agostini as governing law.

**B, C and precise reservation.** The existing evidence concerning the four-minute elementary transition, asserted use of the same permission mechanism as official school programs, claimed continued school activities and occasional school-equipment use warrants deciding whether the school actually administers or presents this meeting as part of its own after-school instruction. The competing resource-room and cafeteria accounts matter to that operational inference, not to a rule that any classroom is forbidden. He would vacate and return this application to the lower court under the correct framework and ordinary summary-judgment standards. An ultimate finding of merely neutral access would defeat the defense; an identified official instructional or solicitation function could sustain only relief responsive to that function. He does not decide either alternative now.

**Strongest counterargument and join barrier.** Location, convenience and borrowed equipment can all be ordinary private access. His reservation rests on their asserted institutional operation for young pupils and the competing record accounts, not on the number of listed circumstances or a presumption of indoctrination. He will join A and the current governmental-function rule, but not an appellate conclusion that the private description and parental permission conclusively settle this application. He proposes no compulsory new evidentiary hearing and no general requirement to disprove every possible institutional connection.

### Thomas

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His Rosenberger concurrence and full Court join protect equal participation in the actual civic-expression benefit. His Ladue concurrence join, Bronx Household joins and complete Agostini join support the distinction between the private activity and the government's access function. His Marshfield dissent join rejects attribution from surrounding public property without governmental religious operation. Milford may not exclude the otherwise covered teaching because it asks children to accept a religious account of moral life.

**Alternative and answer.** Treating the program as a distinct worship activity cannot preserve an exclusion premised on devotional form within this broad community forum. Treating access as governmental education fares no better on the supported facts: privately selected content and parental permission are inconsistent with the asserted inference that the school itself conducts the lesson, and the school facilities remain a neutral access benefit. His broader civic-benefit explanation could support additional cases, but no such extension is necessary to this disposition.

**B, C and limits.** Reject the asserted establishment defense and reverse for nondiscriminatory relief. He can join the bounded majority rule without adopting an additional universal theory of benefits. He will not join a hearing requirement triggered by a pupil's possible misunderstanding alone, a demand for an exact secular devotional counterpart, or a mandatory disclaimer as the price of equal access. Genuine government religious instruction remains distinct under the law he joined in Agostini. The judgment does not resolve every financing arrangement or alter independent school-prayer holdings.

### Ginsburg

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** Her complete Ladue join and Bronx Household instruction join support the Club on A. Her Bronx Household partial-dissent join preserves an actual-activity distinction but cannot make the present broad forum's religious instruction exclusion neutral. Her joins in Souter's Rosenberger and Agostini substantive dissents maintain a careful distinction between facility access and governmental instructional support. Her Marshfield majority join reinforces the need to identify actual institutional presentation.

**Alternative and answer.** The strongest contrary path resolves B through Ladue's private-use relationship. The supported age and immediate-transition circumstances differ, and the disputed accounts of school activities and permission practices bear on whether that relationship is actually the same. She does not treat the younger pupils as conclusively unable to distinguish private sponsorship, nor infer actual teacher participation from an amicus assertion. The constitutional question is the school's own operational role, not presumed religious vulnerability.

**B, C and precise reservation.** Vacate and remand for the lower court to determine, on the existing summary-judgment submissions, whether the actual transition and presentation, together with the claimed equipment use, constitute neutral access or an official instructional arrangement. The court must distinguish established facts from counsel's disputed descriptions and draw only legally permissible inferences. The Club's claim that the parties waived trial is not a stipulation to all adverse factual inferences; conversely, advocacy disagreement is not automatically a material factual dispute. Her procedural care does not turn her separate Rule 60 rationale in Agostini into a rule governing this different vehicle.

**Strongest counterargument and join barrier.** Ordinary community use often entails room assignments, permission and common equipment, and those alone cannot revive the religious ban. She accepts that limit but withholds a final B determination until the actual institutional application is adjudicated below. She joins A and a narrow governmental-function rule; she does not join categorical affirmance, a conclusion that no conceivable official role existed, or automatic reopening of discovery. No damages or fees follow from her vote.

### Breyer

**Sufficient ground and own authorities.** His Bronx Household opinion supplies both the bounded worship-access holding and the unanimous rejection of attribution from private worship's location. His complete Ladue join supports integrated religious moral teaching. His Agostini dissent joined the precedent and procedural objections, not Souter's affirmative substantive direct-aid Part I; those reservations do not create a new categorical school-access prohibition. He applies the governing Agostini rule and his Marshfield majority join's insistence on a concrete government function.

**Alternative and answer.** A limited remand deserves attention because elementary pupils may experience a direct transition from ordinary school to a religious lesson. But actual school administration must do the constitutional work. The claimed facts here — immediately available room, school-address permission slip, nearby school activities and occasional audiovisual equipment — remain features compatible with a general private-use forum. Unlike Marshfield's retained municipal religious centerpiece and surrounding official presentation, they do not identify continued official religious presentation. The disputed attendance estimates, comparator hours and room descriptions need not be resolved to reject that legal inference.

**B, C and limits.** He joins the five-Associate path resolving the asserted defense and reversing for nondiscriminatory relief, on the concrete arrangement rather than neutrality or permission alone. Parental choice does not immunize actual governmental worship; official adoption would not require conversion or quantified pressure. The school may enforce surviving neutral operational rules and need not sponsor a religious lesson or guarantee a child's understanding. He will join neither an unrestricted right to elementary classrooms and equipment nor a mandatory fresh factual inquiry when the asserted circumstances do not establish the material governmental function. His separate explanatory function, if needed, is to state this bounded legal sufficiency conclusion without displacing the independent protections of Lee and Santa Fe.

## Exact scope of the remedial disagreement

The five reversal votes decide the asserted establishment defense on the actual arrangement described. Their conclusion accepts the program's devotion and addresses Milford's affirmative contextual contentions at their legally material scope. It does not convert the selected-source packet into proof that no government employee ever acted, find every disputed fact for the Club, or adjudicate every incidental resource use. They regard the supported school conduct invoked as ordinary administration of private access, rather than an identified religious governmental function. The remand determines appropriate relief and otherwise lawful neutral access terms. It supplies no final permit, fixed injunction, equipment entitlement, damages, fee amount, mandatory disclaimer or permanent forum opening. It does not reopen the categorical defense in order to investigate imagined facts.

The three vacatur votes likewise reject the present exclusion. Their reservation is the inference of continuing official instructional administration or presentation from the actual elementary transition, permission practice, asserted continuing school activity and claimed use of school audiovisual equipment. The adverse accounts are identified in final-packet sections 2 and 4 and the sanitized respondent/reply passages; they are not newly posited teacher escorts, official recruitment, public selection of religious content or invented custody findings. The lower court must first determine whether the existing submissions support a legally material inference under Agostini, giving the cross-motions separate ordinary treatment. If they establish only neutral access, the defense fails without further fact gathering. If a genuine material dispute about the identified governmental function survives, ordinary lawful proceedings may resolve it; no new trial, discovery period or second opportunity to supply previously missing proof is commanded. The three do not find establishment established or endorse denial on religious intensity or age alone.

This is a genuine difference over the sufficiency and institutional significance of the supported operational record, not a claim that selected excerpts are absent from the real court file. The school-equipment assertion remains counsel's cited account; it is neither an adjudicated public devotional appropriation nor an independent universal financing issue. Attendance growth is not a supported causal finding; testimony about a guessed earlier count and the contrary cited counts remain distinct. A school's permission slip is not alone adoption, and the presence of a religious invitation within the Club is not public recruitment. No commitment depends on resolving those unsupported propositions in either party's favor.
