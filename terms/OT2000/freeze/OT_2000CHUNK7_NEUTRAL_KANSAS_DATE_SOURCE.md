# Kansas original-action date source

The Court Library catalog identifies the motion for leave, proposed complaint, and supporting brief as filed December 16, 1985. This is the lodging of the initiating motion and proposed complaint; it does not by itself date the later grant of leave. The saved motion PDF cover also bears a printed December 16, 1985 date. The extracted stamp is OCR-uncertain and supplies no independent date. No interest position follows from these facts.
