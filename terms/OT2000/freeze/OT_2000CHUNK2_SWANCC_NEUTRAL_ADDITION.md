## Balanced use under entering law

The material adds identified empirical support that must be considered on its stated scope. It would be inaccurate to describe the neutral materials as supplying no identified ecological report, no regional production estimate, or no site-specific agency habitat findings. It would also be inaccurate to treat the report's percentage or the word migratory as alone establishing every link required by entering Commerce doctrine.

The existing Lopez, Home Builders, and Morrison rules continue to require identification of the enacted regulated class and its asserted interstate connection before aggregation. The recognized necessary-regime route concerns reasonably necessary inclusion to prevent evasion or material impairment of a valid federal interstate regulatory regime. The independent externality route requires a materially supported substantial interstate effect that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize, together with reasonable fit to the regulated class. Eligibility of the mechanism remains a judicial question; predictive magnitude and practical regulatory design receive the latitude those precedents permit after an eligible connection is identified. There is no universal formal-findings requirement, fixed numerical threshold, or requirement to prove an individually substantial effect for every instance.

The new material can inform the identification and evaluation of habitat function, the scale of a broader ecological relationship, and the connection between a class of waters and migratory birds. Evaluation must identify what supports each claimed connection and what remains inferential: regional production, the site's documented habitat use, interstate movement, fit to the regulatory class, and any claimed inability of individual States reasonably to contain or internalize the effect are related but distinct propositions. The record does not become empty because the original report has not been retrieved; the published recitations do not become findings on a constitutional mechanism merely because they are authenticated. This addition resolves neither the sufficiency of either route nor the proper disposition if a route requires further analysis.

Statutory authority remains a separate and logically prior merits question. The new empirical material does not amend sections 404 or 502(7), the other supplied Clean Water Act provisions, the regulation, or the precise 1986 bird criterion. It does not erase Riverside Bayview's reservation concerning nonadjacent waters or convert these ponds into wetlands under the Corps' definition. The existing statutory and environmental positions must be evaluated at their actual scope; ecological importance does not independently establish a delegation.

The handoff identifies Missouri v. Holland, 252 U.S. 416, particularly page 435. This addition does not select a new Holland holding or reproduce its text. Its identification concerns the migratory-bird treaty context and supplies no independent Commerce holding, present-case treaty-power ground, or finding about state regulatory capacity. If a model would materially rely on a proposition from Holland, it must obtain the actual date-eligible authority through a neutral source path before treating that proposition as supplied law.
