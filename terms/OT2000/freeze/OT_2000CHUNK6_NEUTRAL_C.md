## Case-specific validation and neutral corrections

### New Hampshire

The 1977 decree settled the marine line, not every inland point. The earlier jointly proposed middle-main-channel interpretation was nevertheless accepted by the Court after rejection of the Special Master's competing geographical-middle proposal; it was a premise of judicial relief. The new shore theory contradicts that interpretation of the same 1740 words. This supports an estoppel question independent of claim or issue preclusion. The mistake contention must be assessed against the prior inquiry into the historical and international-law materials; the present interpretation rests on materials available then, and no intervening enactment or newly occurring essential fact is identified. These facts do not themselves dictate the equitable conclusion. Cleveland supplies a date-eligible warning against label-based presumed inconsistency, while leaving genuine factual or legal contradiction in other settings open. No universal three-factor rule already governs this case.

### PGA TOUR

The actual 2000 Code, preserved mechanically in the statutory supplement, expressly includes golf courses when their operations affect commerce. Section 12182(a) addresses an individual and the place's goods, services, facilities, privileges, advantages and accommodations. The clients-or-customers definition in (b)(1)(A)(iv) expressly applies to clauses (i)-(iii) of that subparagraph; it must not be silently rewritten as a definition governing every provision. The necessary reasonable-modification duty and fundamental-alteration defense are in (b)(2)(A)(ii), not the auxiliary-aid undue-burden clause. Covered competitor access and justified alteration remain separate questions. Disability and necessity are established; trial findings about fatigue and advantage are findings subject to ordinary review, not statutory commands or universally controlling facts about every golfer. Kirkingburg's federal experimental-program ruling gives private tournament rules no automatic exemption. Garrett's Title I constitutional-enforcement holding does not determine this private Title III claim.

### Pollard

The mechanically preserved Code confirms section 1981a(a)(1)'s additional-remedies structure and (b)(2)'s express exclusion of all relief authorized by section 706(g), not only backpay. Section (b)(3)'s future-loss cap applies to compensation awarded under section 1981a. The question covers both bridging delayed reinstatement and a substitute when reinstatement is inappropriate. The existing awards and liability do not establish an automatic front-pay amount, duration or entitlement.
