# Cleveland Indians — bounded source request

This supplements the source predicate stated in `OT_2000CHUNK4_COMMITMENTS_CLEVELAND_REFRESH.md` and its receipt. The request seeks support for propositions, not possession of every original. Authenticated published quotations or recitations are sufficient under Engine §14 when they establish the operative rule, material qualifications and authority. No target decision outcome, reasoning, lineup, reconciliation or Stone material should enter the neutral return.

1. Identify and supply the relevant terms of any cited Treasury wage-base provision addressing remuneration for services performed in an earlier year, separately for FICA and FUTA, including material qualifications and applicability to the relevant payment year.
2. Identify the actual Treasury backpay or salary-damages allocation interpretation, with its source and date, the operative text or reliable published recitation, whether it concerns rates, bases or both, and any material exceptions. Do not infer that a general backpay rule necessarily addresses every damages variant if the source does not establish that scope.
3. Supply the supported basis for treating the interpretation as Treasury's responsible and established construction, distinguishing a regulation under the statute, interpretation of an existing regulation, and a revenue ruling or other statutory interpretation. A new litigation assertion and an existing agency rule are not interchangeable.
4. For the already quoted FICA attachment/rate provisions, sections 31.3111-3 and 31.3111-2(c), and the identified FUTA counterparts, sections 31.3301-2 and 31.3301-3(b), supply only additional context material to coverage of delayed wage substitutes. Their quoted text and reported continuity need not be researched from scratch.

No ruling number or additional regulatory citation is guessed. No original-source retrieval is required merely for completeness if a supported published recitation answers the material point. The request does not seek new payment facts, an exceptional player's calculation, a complete administrative record or a new judicial filing. Scalia's direct statutory ground remains separately assessed and does not depend on the requested agency premise.
