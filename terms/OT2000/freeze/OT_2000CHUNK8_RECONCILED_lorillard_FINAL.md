The cigarette-preemption division remains four to four among the Associates. ## 2. Governing law, scope, and preserved qualifications

### Actual law rather than the historical report's descriptions of precedent

Actual Cipollone controls by statutory period and predicate duty. The original Act's statement provision, the successor clause's direct coverage of promotional warning and warning-neutralization duties, and the narrower surviving warranty, deception and independent nonpromotional categories retain their distinct authority. No whole amended-Act opinion supplies a uniform majority rationale. The present positive location rules were not decided by Cipollone. Medtronic requires the actual federal and state comparison under its own text; Geier permits independently demonstrated conflict, not displacement from overlap or compliance alone; Buckman and Egelhoff retain their specific enacted predicates. None creates a cigarette field immunity or resolves this express clause automatically.

Central Hudson and Fox remain the governing ordinary commercial-speech framework. Discovery Network, Edenfield, Rubin, actual 44 Liquormart and Greater New Orleans require real advancement and reasonable proportional fit. They impose no universal numerical-study condition, least-restrictive-means rule, or duty to regulate all sources simultaneously. Actual 44 Liquormart's separate advancement and fit coalitions and noncontrolling stronger theories remain distinct. Actual Greater New Orleans's two independent holdings concern its structurally inconsistent lawful-casino scheme; its refusal to reopen causal proof does not mandate the same remedy for every disputed tobacco-tailoring record. The historical report's use of the historical Florida Bar result does not restore that result in this timeline. The existing actual authorities independently supply the applicable evidence and fit standards.

Actual Reno protects adult communication while recognizing the distinct setting of criminal Internet restrictions. Its strict scrutiny and substantial-overbreadth doctrine are not automatically transferred to all commercial claims. June 25 United Foods concerns compelled promotional financing and expressly leaves Central Hudson in its proper field; its separate operational-theory nonreach is not a universal forfeiture rule. Colorado II concerns coordinated spending and demonstrated circumvention, not the evidence or rule governing tobacco advertising. No imported historical quotation of those authorities replaces actual law.

### Product, party, and provision boundaries

1. **Cigarette advertising:** All four cigarette manufacturers challenge §21.04(5)(a) and (b). Section 1334(b) applies only to cigarettes whose packages conform to the federal labeling provisions. Its smoking-and-health and advertising-or-promotion conditions remain express. No finding about noncompliant packages is made.
2. **Cigarette retail access:** Only Lorillard and Brown & Williamson present the First Amendment challenge to §21.04(2)(c)–(d). No corresponding R.J. Reynolds or Philip Morris retail disposition and no cigarette-retail preemption holding is supplied.
3. **Smokeless tobacco:** USSTC separately challenges the advertising and access provisions under the First Amendment and preserves derivative inseverability if cigarette advertising is preempted. The FCLAA itself does not cover smokeless tobacco. USSTC's assertion of an own-advertising, as-applied claim is not erased by the State's facial characterization.
4. **Cigars:** The cigar petitioners challenge §22.06(5)(a)–(b), §22.06(2)(c)–(d), and identify §22.06(1)(a) sampling. Premium-cigar sensory inspection is treated separately from ordinary sealed-package access. The separate cigar-warning Commerce Clause relief is not before the Court and is not extended to §22.06(2)(e).
5. **Different small-sign statuses:** The unappealed severance of §21.04(6) removed the generic sign permission for cigarettes and smokeless tobacco. It is not an operative alternative channel. Separate §22.06(6) remains available for cigars: one sign, at most 576 square inches, outside or visible outside each sales location, black text on white, stating only that tobacco products are sold there. It permits the sign; it does not require it, preserve brand or price advertising, or restore §21.04(6). The historical Thomas discussion at report pp.585–586 expressly recognized the cigar permission, so the corrected neutral treatment is not a new premise warranting a different cigar judgment.
6. **Amended operation:** The January 19, 2000 amendments and February 1, 2000 effective date govern. Outdoor advertising includes enclosed stadiums and indoor material directed or visible outside within the specified 1,000-foot radius of a public playground, playground area in a public park, elementary school or secondary school. It is not triggered by every park or educational institution. The advertising definition includes oral communication, product-identifying matter and specified fixtures, while excluding utilitarian items of 200 cubic inches or less. No general adult-only exception applies outdoors.
7. **Height:** Any portion below five feet triggers the point-of-sale prohibition within the same geographical zones, unless the facility ensures that no person under 18 is present or permitted to enter at any time. Accompanied children are not excepted. Above-height advertising remains subject to the independent outside-visibility rule. No specific guard or costless compliance is found.
8. **Access and samples:** The subsection (4) exceptions qualify the general physical-access provisions. Self-service is permitted in actual adult-only facilities. Clerk-assisted sales and inspection and visible staff-accessible displays are not categorically prohibited. The adult-only sampling exception permits no more than one sample per day to an individual adult: the manufacturer's smallest individual-consumer pack for cigarettes or little cigars, smallest can for smokeless tobacco, and smallest pack for cigars or at most five individual cigars if not sold in packs. This is no unrestricted giveaway permission.
9. **Remaining safeguards:** Mail sales exclude mail coupon redemption and free samples and require age verification, at minimum the recipient's legible valid government identification copy. The vending exception requires the licensed on-premises-alcohol setting, staff-only noncontinuous lockout, immediate staff vicinity and exclusive observation/control, and the specified notice. The source's cigar licensing-subsection OCR difference is not silently repaired or adjudicated. Face-to-face sale, photographic identification except for those appearing at least 27, amended brand-bearing merchandise premiums, and the different cigarette/smokeless and little-cigar unpackaging rules retain their own terms. The prevention duty, training and secret-shopper compliance protections, including the next-year protection after an identified outlet passes an official check, are not invalidated or transformed into a uniform private-testing obligation.
10. **Separate savings rules:** Sections 21.06 and 22.08 preserve their own chapter's remainder and other applications on their terms. They are not a judicial finding of every possible severability result. Other common-law, state and local duties and more restrictive local controls retain their lawful scope; no whole-chapter invalidation follows.

### P — Competing express-clause constructions

**O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas:** For federally conforming cigarette packages, these positive tobacco-specific outdoor and height prohibitions are based on smoking and health and directly concern advertising or promotion. The amended clause's requirements/prohibitions and advertising/promotion language cannot be confined to affirmative health-warning content. Protecting minors from cigarette consumption remains a smoking-and-health objective. A location label does not remove a direct advertising command. The construction preserves genuinely independent duties and generally applicable location/size rules based on other concerns, actual sales regulation and the usual rules governing unlawful solicitation; it does not preempt every tobacco-related action or create field immunity. No additional implied-conflict ground is necessary.

**Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer:** The warning-and-information architecture and Congress's specified concern about diverse and confusing cigarette messages confine the advertising clause. They would construe the phrase concerning requirements or prohibitions with respect to advertising in that setting to preserve these local placement rules; they do not deny that the rules serve smoking-and-health objectives. Traditional location and child-protection authority reinforces that construction where the enacted displacement is uncertain. They acknowledge the broader 1969 words and covered warning-neutralization duties. They do not exempt all positive commands or every regulation called zoning, or rely on a universal impossibility-only rule. No present determination that a complete ban disguised as location control necessarily survives is required.

**Shared limits:** The difference is construction of this clause, not whether States care about children's health or whether common-law damages can ever impose legal requirements. Actual Cipollone's claim-specific limits and later preemption decisions do not force either construction. Both sides preserve the unappealed sign relief and the retail party/ground boundary.

### O — Advancement, tailoring, and the distinct remedies

**Affirmative outdoor-advancement proposition — O'Connor, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer:** On the existing lower record and the summary-judgment assumption, the product-specific reports, marketing history and supported preventive inferences establish more than speculation that limiting youth exposure to smokeless and cigar promotion can materially advance prevention of underage use. The result does not equate the products, declare a universal advertising-demand law, or resolve whether this exact spatial burden reasonably fits that benefit. The reported smokeless decline does not erase continuing use and historical targeting; the cigar materials require their own consideration despite small outdoor expenditures. No later Lodging, precise coefficient, universal controlled experiment, or legal deference to an unauthorized FDA rule is required. O'Connor accepts this bounded proposition and still rejects the rule's fit. The four remand supporters accept it without holding the entire outdoor rule constitutional.

**Scalia and Kennedy withhold this join.** They regard the product-and-medium-specific causal showing as doubtful and unnecessary to resolve because the speech burden fails fit even giving the evidence its strongest permissible reading. A willingness to apply Central Hudson is not an affirmative finding of every prong. They can join a conditional explanation assuming advancement, but not the five-Associate affirmative passage. They reserve rather than find that advertising never affects youth or that no evidence exists. Thomas likewise supplies no affirmative advancement or reduced-protection vote.

**Sufficient fit failure — O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy, with Thomas's expressly conditional assent:** The categorical geographic and communication coverage does not reasonably fit the child-protection interest when applied to the protected advertising presented. It reaches differing sizes and settings, ordinary brands/prices and oral or exterior-visible information, and substantial urban retail communication. The accepted breadth suffices without assigning the disputed percentage to this rule alone. The missing cigarette/smokeless sign and the cigar permission's limited generic content must be analyzed separately. A tiny cigar advertising budget does not make loss of a retailer's principal practical outlet constitutionally insignificant. Other media need not preserve an immediate on-site proposal. The State's substantial existing enforcement and public-information efforts do not prove this speech burden proportionate, but the rule does not impose exhaustion or prescribe a substitute program. Adult access is protected alongside legitimate child protection.

These Justices would reverse rather than send the existing categorical protected-advertising scheme for another tailoring or causal-evidence trial. The statutory breadth and actual substantial burdens are sufficient legal defects even accepting the State's supported advancement account. They do not design a smaller radius or a new sign code. Their implementing return preserves independent proceedings concerning actual deception or unlawful transactions on their own predicates and does not adjudicate every future advertisement.

**Tailoring trial — Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer:** These Justices agree that the State's interest and advancement showing do not themselves answer the adult-channel burden. The existing summary judgment does not adequately resolve the practical adequacy of remaining communication. Disputed incremental coverage, statewide variation, the qualitative location and traffic of permitted sites, and actual alternative media and retailer/manufacturer differences matter to a reasonable-fit determination. A raw percentage cannot establish either final validity or final invalidity in all the presented applications. Nor can the general availability of other media be accepted as a demonstrated substitute for these petitioners' retail communication.

They would vacate the outdoor summary judgment and remand for trial on that specific constitutional tailoring issue. The inquiry may produce different application-sensitive results for manufacturers and retailers and must include USSTC's asserted own-advertising claim. It is a judicial remedy for an inadequately resolved factual basis of constitutional fit, not a source-recovery order, a litigant production default, permission to rewrite the rule, or a prejudgment that the State will prevail. Actual Greater New Orleans involved structural contradictions that stronger causal proof could not cure; it does not make an outdoor-tobacco tailoring trial unlawful. Their proposed return does not reopen unappealed sign or warning relief. The first-step assumption remains limited; these Justices do not find every advertisement misleading or convert their proposed trial into an unconditional right to relitigate every factual matter regardless of ordinary procedure.

### H — Height is independently separable from outdoor regulation

**O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Souter:** The five-foot rule fails independently at advancement and reasonable fit. Evidence that children encounter low signs and the common-sense appeal of reducing exposure do not establish material alleviation by the actual any-portion height prohibition. Older minors are not confined below five feet, younger children can look upward, and the retained above-height channel undermines the asserted mechanism while the outside-visibility rule restricts its practical availability. The conclusion is insufficient demonstrated material advancement, not a finding of literally zero conceivable effect or a universal demand for a height-specific experiment. Separately, the undifferentiated placement rule does not reasonably fit the child's exposure interest and protected adult communication. Its modest claimed burden supplies no exemption from constitutional justification. The adult-only and geographical qualifications are included, not ignored.

They reject treating this advertising provision itself as an expression-independent conduct rule: its purpose operates through the communicative effect of the message. They adopt both sufficient failures under actual Central Hudson/Fox and the restriction-responsive burdens of Edenfield and the later actual cases. Thomas conditionally adopts those failures without endorsing the lesser tier and independently applies strict scrutiny.

**Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer:** Their sufficient ground differs. In this limited indoor setting, with the school/playground radius and adult-only exception, they regard height placement as a modest adjunct to the valid physical-sales and product-display arrangement. State power to require staff-mediated acquisition allows this closely connected arrangement without treating it as the broad suppression imposed outdoors. They acknowledge that, viewed as a direct speech restraint, the asserted means-end connection is doubtful. They therefore do not join an affirmative finding that the five-foot rule materially advances prevention or independently satisfies ordinary speech tailoring. Their conduct-adjunct rationale supplies their judgment and the limit of their disagreement with the other Justices; it creates no general exception for any advertising rule adjacent to sales regulation.

### R — All three products and premium-cigar inspection

**O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas:** Assume that the challenged manner of displaying or inspecting the products has a cognizable expressive component. The regulation nevertheless addresses physical acquisition for an expression-independent reason: uncontrolled access can evade staff age checks and facilitate unauthorized taking. The incidental-conduct analysis permits this substantial interest and appropriately limited means. It does not discount a message merely because it is commercial. Visible information, informative empty packages, staff-accessible actual products, clerk-mediated adult inspection and purchases, and the amended adult-only self-service permission preserve the relevant lawful communication and transactions.

For premium cigars, empty boxes do not reproduce aroma, feel or weight. Assisted examination of the actual cigar supplies the pertinent answer; no prohibition on all pre-purchase inspection is found. The rule need not eliminate every alternative route to underage tobacco to address this route materially. No empirical finding that all or most youth tobacco is shoplifted, or that adult-only admission is costless, is required. No holding that product handling can never communicate is adopted. These five can join this common assumed-speech, expression-independent access analysis. Thomas's rejection of a reduced commercial-speech tier is no barrier to this conduct ground.

**Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer:** They reach the same judgment on the sufficient ground that requiring assistance to reach, inspect or purchase the actual goods regulates physical conduct. A marginal communicative aspect does not turn ordinary placement of restricted products into a direct regulation of a message. They do not join the separately formulated assumed-speech constitutional analysis or an affirmative Central Hudson alternative. Their ground does not deny the real sensory information important to cigar purchasers; the rule changes the manner of obtaining the product rather than suppressing that information. The remaining actual exceptions and assisted access are material to their conclusion.

The lower access judgment therefore stands for Lorillard/Brown & Williamson, USSTC and the cigar petitioners at their respective provisions. No additional party or preemption question is adjudicated. The common judgment does not make every Associate a joiner of either distinct full rationale.
