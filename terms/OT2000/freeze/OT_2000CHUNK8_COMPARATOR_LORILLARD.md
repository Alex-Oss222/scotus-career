# Lorillard Tobacco Co. v. Reilly / Altadis U.S.A. Inc. v. Reilly

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded, with different majorities for the separate regulatory questions. The cigarette-preemption and geographic-advertising conclusions had five votes; the point-of-sale and sales-practice analysis had six.

* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote. Parts I, II-C, and II-D were unanimous. Rehnquist, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas joined Parts III-A, III-C, and III-D. Rehnquist, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer joined Part III-B-1. Rehnquist, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas joined Parts II-A, II-B, III-B-2, and IV. Kennedy concurred in part and in the judgment with Scalia; Thomas wrote separately in the same posture. Souter concurred in part and dissented in part. Stevens concurred in part, concurred in the judgment in part, and dissented in part, joined by Ginsburg and Breyer throughout and by Souter in Part I.

* **Historical Holding:** Covered cigarette advertising restrictions were preempted; the noncigarette outdoor and five-foot advertising restrictions failed commercial-speech review; the challenged sales-access restrictions survived. Cigar giveaways and noncigarette severability received the stated limitations.

* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparison only; it does not establish entering law.
