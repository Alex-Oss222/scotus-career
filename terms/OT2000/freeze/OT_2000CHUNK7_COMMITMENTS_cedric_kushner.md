## Entering law and available paths

Section 1962(c) distinguishes a person from the enterprise whose affairs that person conducts. Sections 1961(3) and (4) expressly encompass natural persons and corporations. Reves requires some role in directing enterprise affairs; Scheidler refuses an extra economic-motive limitation while preserving every enacted element; Holmes independently requires factual and proximate causation. Bestfoods preserves separate corporate personality while distinguishing liability for one's own conduct from derivative ownership liability. Its CERCLA operator test is not imported.

The reversal path treats the natural person and incorporated enterprise as legally distinct even when the individual owns the corporation and acts within corporate authority. Its strongest objection is that incorporation alone could make almost every owner's corporate wrong into a RICO claim. The answer is that legal distinctness supplies only this one element; direction, predicates, pattern, injury and causation must still be proved. The affirmance path demands additional functional separation or action outside corporate authority. Its strongest objection is that it inserts an unexpressed immunity for the people most capable of directing a corporate enterprise through racketeering. Copperweld's antitrust conspiracy inquiry does not justify that addition to a different statutory structure.

All eight can join a common rule limited to a natural owner or employee and an actually incorporated enterprise, including acts within corporate authority. None requires a separate writing on the supplied issue. This is a provisional compatibility assessment, not an assignment or adoption of an opinion text.

## Remedy and reserved matters

Remove the Second Circuit's distinctness dismissal and remand for ordinary proceedings. Do not award damages, fees or liability. Preserve disputes over actual enterprise direction, predicates, pattern, injury, causation and every properly available defense. Do not decide corporate defendants paired with an association containing themselves, affiliated corporations, or sole proprietorships. No historical comparison or material same-term change has been used to generate these commitments.
