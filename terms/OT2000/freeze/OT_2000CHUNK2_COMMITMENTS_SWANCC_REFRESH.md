## 1. Posture, supported facts, and law used

The question concerns statutory jurisdiction over these isolated, nonnavigable intrastate ponds under the Corps' bird-habitat criterion and, if that jurisdiction is authorized, the Commerce Clause. These ponds are not wetlands under the Corps' vegetation definition and are not alleged to adjoin navigable waters. Part (b) of the 1986 explanation is the asserted criterion: habitat used or potentially used by migratory birds crossing state lines. Its treaty-bird, endangered-species, and crop-irrigation alternatives are distinct and are not substituted for the asserted ground. The governing regulation is section 328.3(a)(3). The explanation appeared in a preamble without notice and comment; that fact does not establish an independently preserved procedural challenge.

The separate challenge to the permit denial's merits was abandoned. None of the positions below reopens it, decides mitigation adequacy as an independent claim, issues a permit, or displaces independently applicable state law. There is no supported Article III defect requiring a separate threshold disposition. Federal statutory coverage is a merits question distinct from Congress's constitutional capacity.

## 2. Common alternatives tested

### Statutory exclusion and inclusion

**Exclusion proposition.** Sections 404(a) and 502(7), read within the water-protection scheme, do not authorize jurisdiction over these isolated intrastate ponds solely from their use by migratory birds. The statutory definition expands beyond traditional navigability, but it does not make every connection between a pond and an interstate interest a delegation of water jurisdiction. Section 404(g)(1) identifies waters available for state administration outside the federally retained navigation-related category; it does not conclusively identify every member of that remainder. Section 101(a)'s ecological objective operates consistently with the statute's provisions, while section 101(b) preserves an important state role. The narrow exclusion does not decide every isolated water, require present navigability for every covered water, or undo Riverside Bayview's adjacent-wetland rule.

The strongest opposing construction is that the broad defined term, the biological-integrity objective, and the nonnavigation remainder in section 404(g)(1) make habitat-bearing ponds an ordinary part of the delegated water program. Riverside Bayview accepts ecological rather than solely navigational considerations, and a bird's movement can connect aquatic ecological functions without hydrological adjacency. That argument cannot be answered merely by labeling the land use local or the policy consequential.

**Inclusion proposition.** The enacted definition and scheme permit the Corps to include these actual waters and their demonstrated aquatic habitat function. Section 101(a) informs the meaning of the coverage language rather than supplying power by itself; section 404(g)(1) confirms that federal coverage exceeds waters reserved to direct federal administration. The state role in section 101(b) coexists with federal permit coverage. Riverside Bayview's reservation leaves the question open rather than deciding it adversely. The asserted ecological relationship is consequently a permissible application of section 328.3(a)(3), subject to constitutional limits.

The strongest objection is that broad statutory purpose and a reasonable policy cannot replace a missing delegation, and the 1986 explanation cannot itself enlarge one. Inclusion therefore must stand on text and structure, not on treating the preamble as independently binding. The inclusion positions below would sustain the statutory reading independently of a universal claim to controlling weight for this preamble. Chevron supports permissible delegated interpretation; Christensen does not turn every interpretation outside notice-and-comment rulemaking into an invalid one, and no freestanding procedure judgment is entered.

### Commerce mechanisms and the exact inferential dispute

**Regulated class.** The enacted subject is discharge of dredged or fill material into covered waters. The challenged application reaches isolated intrastate ponds through the actual regulation's commerce language and the bird-habitat criterion. It is neither the municipal consortium's entire business nor all conduct affecting wildlife. A court must evaluate the relationship of this habitat-based water category to the asserted mechanism; it may not insert a new commercial-project element. A present application involving established habitat is distinguishable from a hypothetical assertion based only on a passing bird or unsupported future use, without rewriting the rule.

**Necessary-regime route.** The federal regulation of navigable and interstate waters supplies an identifiable valid interstate core. But neither comprehensiveness nor bird habitat demonstrates that omitting these hydrologically isolated ponds permits evasion or materially impairs that core. No supported causal pathway carries this fill into regulated interstate waters, and none is invented. Nor does identification of a cooperative waterfowl plan establish an operative interstate-commerce prohibition whose enforcement necessarily depends on this application. The cited treaty regime cannot silently be substituted for the valid interstate-commerce regime required by this route. On the supplied grounds, this route independently sustains no commitment. It remains legally available on a materially supported relationship in another application.

**Independent externality route: affirmative argument.** The proposed mechanism is physical loss or degradation of aquatic breeding and other habitat resources used by populations moving across state borders. The exported consequence is diminished support for a shared mobile population, rather than a subsequent reduction in tourist spending or general national prosperity. The report's production account and conversion history support the substantial class-wide importance of such habitat. The agency's actual aquatic-use and interstate-bird findings, together with the breeding colony and the supported displacement concerns, connect this site to a habitat function rather than merely to the word migratory. They do not allocate the report's percentage to this site.

The affirmative territorial inference is that a State can protect the habitat within its boundaries but cannot, through that authority alone, preserve the habitat outside them on which the same mobile populations depend. Destruction in one State can diminish the resource available in another; conserving the latter State's own waters does not restore the lost former habitat. This is a conclusion drawn from the identified migration-and-habitat mechanism, not a new finding that Illinois refuses protection, that all interstate cooperation has failed, or that States lack authority over local filling. The federal permit requirement addresses the source of the asserted loss by reviewing the discharge into the habitat waters themselves. On this view the fit is direct and bounded; the theory does not confer general jurisdiction over every activity with remote environmental consequences.

**Strongest challenge to that affirmative argument.** The regional report does not itself establish that every water reached by part (b) has the same production function or that this artificial pond category bears the same aggregate relationship. Bird use is established, but the exact relationship between the particular habitat function affected and a substantial shared-population effect remains inferential. The States' inability to regulate outside their borders does not alone prove that the asserted effect cannot reasonably be contained or internalized; otherwise every mobile interest could support a national police power. The agency's habitat and mitigation findings are relevant without being constitutional findings. The question is whether their combined substance supports the cross-border inference with sufficient fit, not whether they employ constitutional labels.

**Two permissible evaluations.** An affirming evaluation finds that these combined materials support the eligible shared-habitat mechanism and leave scale and prediction within legislative latitude. It requires neither individual migration tracking, measured site population loss, a comprehensive national census, nor an independently substantial effect from this single discharge. A remanding evaluation accepts the mechanism's eligibility but finds the existing jurisdiction judgment has not adequately tested the specific class relationship and territorial effect; it requests application of the governing rule to the existing record, not a prescribed proof technique or a second opportunity automatically due the government. Lack of an original research document is not the reason for that remedy. A direct constitutional rejection would require a sufficiently supported conclusion that the government's asserted grounds fail, rather than treating the operator's source limits as evidence against a litigant.

## 3. Independent commitments by Justice

### Stevens

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Include these ponds; affirm the jurisdiction judgment. His own joins in Riverside Bayview and PUD No. 1, his Sweet Home joins, and his MCI and Brown & Williamson dissents support reading the operative protective definition in context while enforcing actual limits. Here inclusion preserves rather than displaces a mandatory statutory command. Section 404(g)(1) corroborates breadth but is not conclusive ratification of every agency criterion. The statutory ground is sufficient without making the preamble legally equivalent to every binding regulation.

**Commerce commitment:** Sustain this application under the externality route on the combined evidence and inferences identified above. He can adopt that ground at its complete operative scope without claiming that Home Builders' classification has ceased to govern. His Home Builders dissent, joined by Souter, also preserves the distinct view that actual habitat-destroying construction is an economic application, not an owner's collateral economic association. That remains his additional proposed ground; it is not imported as controlling law or made necessary to the present externality conclusion. He supplies no necessary-regime finding.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Statutory exclusion invokes the legitimate MCI boundary, but the defined waters term and aquatic-protection scheme differ from eliminating mandatory tariff filing. A constitutional remand is lawful if the supported inferences are rejected; he does not prefer it because the report and site habitat findings give the cross-border account concrete support absent from a bare wildlife-value argument. Regional evidence need not make Illinois part of the studied region to be relevant to the same function.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join statutory inclusion and the full bounded externality explanation, not automatic bird-presence jurisdiction, a universal tourism theory, or a declaration that the report proves this site's numerical contribution. He opposes exclusion and unnecessary remand; if remand is selected over his sufficiency view, it must not presume invalidity, prescribe new scientific evidence, or revive permit merits. Affirmance leaves the jurisdiction judgment and unreviewed denial effective without independently endorsing every denial reason.

### O'Connor

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Exclude the asserted bird-only jurisdiction and reverse, with proceedings conforming to that confined statutory ruling. Her PUD No. 1 opinion enforced a particular certification trigger and lawful condition, and her Sweet Home join enforced enacted injury language and causal limits; neither supplies a general aquatic-habitat delegation. Her Brown & Williamson join supports examining the connected statutory structure before deference. Riverside Bayview, which she joined, confirms coverage beyond navigability but expressly leaves this different category open.

**Conditional Commerce commitment:** If the statutory objection is rejected, vacate and remand the jurisdiction judgment for the existing-record externality inquiry. She accepts Home Builders' full externality rule and does not demand an economic transaction. The new report and site findings establish a serious supported ecological basis for the inquiry. She nevertheless would not equate the identified regional production role with the full part (b) class, or regard interstate bird use and unmitigated area-species effects as conclusively identifying the territorial consequence at issue. The proposed inference is credible but its application and fit require adequate assessment. Her Home Builders join and Kennedy-concurrence join support this bounded remedial choice; her Sweet Home concurrence cautions against replacing a concrete causal predicate with undifferentiated population effects. The latter is an analogy about disciplined application, not a new CWA actual-death requirement.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Inclusion has substantial support in section 404(g)(1) and the Act's biological objective. She treats those as confirmation of breadth short of a decision that bird habitat alone supplies the necessary water-program connection. On Commerce, the strongest case for immediate affirmance is that a breeding habitat and shared migratory population already establish the mechanism, with the remaining uncertainty only predictive magnitude. She regards the remaining class-and-territorial relationship as antecedent to that latitude, while rejecting a rule that the original report or an individual bird itinerary must be produced.

**Join limits and remedy:** She can join the narrow statutory exclusion independently of constitutional invalidity. She will not join categorical intrastate-water immunity, a denial of Home Builders' externality route, or the claim that no ecological evidence exists. Conditional remand concerns the existing record and actual asserted mechanism; it supplies no automatic reopening, new proof burden, or permit entitlement. Statutory reversal removes the challenged federal basis while leaving independent law intact.

### Scalia

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Reverse the asserted bird-only jurisdiction. His Sweet Home dissent with Thomas distinguishes an enacted regulatory object from indirect land-use consequences; his MCI opinion joins and additional special-condition objection insist that agency authority have an operative statutory source. His Brown & Williamson join reinforces this approach without a general ban on important agency interpretations. Ponds' aquatic character is relevant but does not make their relationship to mobile birds an enacted substitute for the statute's water-jurisdiction boundary.

**Conditional Commerce commitment:** If statutory inclusion prevails, reject the asserted constitutional justification and seek confined relief from this application. His Home Builders writing accepts the necessary-regime route but rejects an independent externality as a sufficient constitutional source. The supplied material establishes no anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship to a valid interstate-commerce regime. His objection to the externality route is a maintained noncontrolling disagreement, not a description of current law. Even applying that route arguendo, he would not treat regional habitat importance and the existence of migratory bird use as demonstrating the required fitted, noninternalizable effect of the regulated class. The statutory and constitutional grounds remain separate.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** The report and agency findings make the externality case materially stronger than speculation about biodiversity, and controlling Home Builders permits such a route. His response is the retained objection that the need for multistate conservation does not identify commerce or necessary protection of a commerce regime; the stronger evidence of national ecological interdependence does not answer that legal objection. On statutory coverage, ecological purpose and section 404(g)(1)'s remainder do not settle the reach of bird-only jurisdiction.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join narrow exclusion and rejection of unsupported necessity, but not an opinion adopting the externality rule as his own sufficient ground or using a landfill-business label to rewrite the regulated class. He does not automatically deny interpretive weight because a statement appears in a preamble; the interpretation fails his statutory analysis independently. Constitutional relief, if needed, is confined to the asserted grounds and application; he proposes no facial invalidation of the Act or general immunity from environmental regulation. He prefers that relief to a remand directed solely at a route he rejects, while recording that the route remains controlling law.

### Kennedy

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Reverse on the confined statutory exclusion. His MCI full join and special-condition concurrence, and his Brown & Williamson join, give weight to the scheme's operative allocation of authority. His Sweet Home join permits a broad environmental interpretation when the enacted term and structure supply it. His PUD No. 1 join likewise rests on an actual statutory trigger. Those positions support giving effect to the water definition's breadth without treating any interstate ecological relationship as sufficient statutory authorization.

**Conditional Commerce commitment:** If coverage is sustained, vacate and remand for the existing-record Home Builders inquiry. His own Home Builders concurrence recognizes externalities as an eligible independent route and rejects a closed taxonomy. His Morrison opinion preserves the requirement that a concrete interstate mechanism, rather than national prevalence or downstream costs, do the work. Here the new empirical support makes an eligible habitat-loss mechanism substantially arguable; it does not by itself resolve the relationship between the part (b) water category, shared migratory resources, and what individual States cannot reasonably internalize. He would require the actual existing materials to be assessed on that question without adding a fixed evidentiary form.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Immediate affirmance can reasonably identify the mobile shared population, breeding habitat, regional production importance and direct discharge control as a complete mechanism. The strongest answer to his remand preference is that lower courts need not repeat a constitutional formula when the materials already support it. He still regards the territorial limitation and class fit as unresolved applications, not mere estimates of magnitude: the site findings concern habitat and particular agency concerns, while the broad report account addresses different regional waters. Geographic difference is not disqualifying; the needed relationship must be explained from what the materials establish.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join statutory exclusion without announcing that Congress lacks power to reach isolated-water habitat. He cannot join Scalia's rejection of the independent externality route, a state-traditional-function immunity, or a demand for an exact national production share, individual tracking, failed interstate compact, or formal congressional finding. Conditional remand is for applying existing law to the existing record; it does not promise supplementation or presume the outcome. No permit or abandoned permit-merits relief follows.

### Souter

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Include and affirm jurisdiction. His Sweet Home opinion gives both direct statutory and permissible-delegated-interpretation grounds for habitat protection, while keeping operative statutory triggers distinct. His PUD No. 1 join and MCI and Brown & Williamson dissents support contextual breadth when coverage language permits it. The CWA definition, biological-integrity objective and state-program remainder support the present construction; a reserved question in Riverside Bayview is not a statutory prohibition.

**Commerce commitment:** Adopt the bounded externality ground and find the combined materials sufficient for this application. The actual habitat and interstate-use findings, supported breeding function, regional production account and conversion history permit a shared-resource inference with reasonable class fit. He does not substitute the original report's absence for analysis of its authenticated recitation. His Home Builders dissent with Stevens preserves an additional economic-application theory for actual development, but he need not overturn the controlling classification to sustain this different migratory-habitat record. No necessary-regime relationship is found.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Exclusion rightly demands a delegation; the dispute is whether the Act supplies one, not whether agency purpose can create it. His Christensen join prevents assuming the preamble is binding simply because the agency administers the statute. Inclusion can survive that objection through independent statutory analysis. The strongest Commerce challenge concerns the regional evidence's transfer to the water category and the unmeasured site effect. He treats functional habitat findings, rather than matching geography or a site percentage, as the relevant connection, with predictive class effects receiving the latitude Home Builders preserves.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join inclusion and complete externality reasoning without a universal Chevron rule for preambles. He rejects a requirement that the Corps prove the exact route and quantitative fate of particular birds before requiring a permit, and also rejects statutory authority over every land use that attracts wildlife. He would not join a claim that Holland itself resolves Commerce power. Affirmance preserves the jurisdiction judgment; a contrary remand must remain confined to jurisdiction and supply no automatic evidentiary reopening or permit decision.

### Thomas

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Reverse the bird-only assertion of jurisdiction. His joins in Scalia's Sweet Home dissent, MCI's controlling statutory ground and additional special-condition objection, and Brown & Williamson support demanding a delegated rule with a defined statutory object. The water definition's breadth does not eliminate the task of identifying its limit. Riverside Bayview's adjacent-wetland reasoning does not answer these isolated nonwetland ponds.

**Conditional Commerce commitment:** If statutory inclusion is adopted, seek confined constitutional relief. His Home Builders join accepts necessary inclusion within an established valid interstate regime and rejects the independent externality route. Nothing supplied establishes the former mechanism for these ponds. His Lopez and Morrison joins reject constructing the enacted class from incidental economic attributes, so landfill construction and anticipated wildlife-related expenditure are insufficient on their own. The new report and agency findings are real evidence; his retained legal objection is that ecological interdependence does not itself establish the enumerated power. Under the controlling externality rule considered arguendo, he also rejects treating the stated regional importance and mobile-bird presence as conclusive support for the whole required territorial mechanism and fit.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Home Builders makes externality eligibility controlling, and the affirmative mechanism here concerns actual habitat supporting an interstate population, not merely national interest. That is the strongest counterargument to his constitutional position. His maintained objection remains the legal boundary identified in his joined Home Builders writing; it cannot be reported as current law. The statutory affirmative case is substantial because Congress defined navigable waters broadly, but he regards an administrative bird test as going beyond the water-program delegation supplied.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join statutory exclusion without converting it into categorical constitutional immunity for all local waters. He will not join externality sufficiency, a commercial-project reconstruction of the regulated class, or a declaration that every informal interpretation lacks weight. His Christensen join is limited to the actual advisory-letter issue. Relief stays with the challenged jurisdictional application; there is no facial Act invalidation, independent permit-merits decision, or authority to proceed under state law merely from prevailing here.

### Ginsburg

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Include and affirm jurisdiction. Her Sweet Home and PUD No. 1 joins and Brown & Williamson dissent support coverage grounded in the enacted definition and protective scheme. Her contrary MCI join and special-condition concurrence remain important: this reading does not remove a mandatory obligation or disregard an express limitation. Section 404(g)(1) and section 101(a) reinforce a permissible broad waters construction, while section 101(b)'s state role is preserved. The preamble is not an independent grant and no unpreserved process challenge is decided.

**Commerce commitment:** Join the full externality framework and find its requirements satisfied for this application on the supported record and permissible inferences. Her Home Builders position expressly adopted both routes, material support, and predictive latitude while disagreeing with the economic classification. That remand did not commit her to remand every later environmental application. Here the report supplies identified class-level ecological significance, and the site findings connect actual aquatic habitat and breeding to interstate bird use. The resulting shared-habitat effect crosses the ordinary territorial reach of an individual State. Direct review of filling that habitat reasonably fits the asserted loss. The inference is stated as such, not recast as a recovered finding.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** A Home Builders remand is a serious alternative because the regional report does not measure Illinois and the site findings are not a constitutional analysis. She concludes that those limits do not leave the operative mechanism unsupported: the record need not quantify each pond's contribution, duplicate the report in every State, or show failed intergovernmental negotiations. The combination supplies more than a nationwide-interest label. Her earlier remand position guarded against invented facts; affirmance here relies on the added concrete support and expressly bounded prediction.

**Join limits and remedy:** She can join the same complete externality proposition as Breyer, Stevens and Souter, provided it identifies the mechanism and limits in section 2. She will not join an affirmance resting only on the landfill's commercial character as though that were the controlling Home Builders rule, or an assumption that every criterion-(b) application is valid. She supplies no necessary-regime finding. Affirmance leaves the challenged jurisdiction judgment effective without reopening the abandoned permit merits. If a different sufficiency conclusion commands the disposition, she can accept a properly limited existing-record inquiry as a fallback, but her independent vote is to affirm.

### Breyer

**Statutory commitment and judgment:** Include and affirm jurisdiction. His Sweet Home joins and Brown & Williamson dissent favor an operative, context-sensitive account of delegated environmental protection; they do not allow broad purpose to override particular commands. The waters definition and section 404 structure provide the affirmative authority here. Riverside Bayview's ecological reasoning is instructive without deciding this reserved category. The present statutory case does not require transforming the preamble into a universally binding source.

**Commerce commitment:** Sustain the application on the complete externality ground. His own Home Builders separate opinion joined the Court's two routes and evidentiary rule while emphasizing that material support does not require independent judicial environmental science or a fixed quantity of effects. The new report and site material now support a practical cross-border mechanism: filling removes aquatic habitat used by a shared migratory resource, with production and conversion evidence showing that the class's role is substantial. Individual States cannot preserve that resource's habitat outside their territory through ordinary local authority. The inference concerns a concrete shared resource and direct habitat loss, not generic future commercial possibilities. Scale and regulatory design then receive the latitude the governing rule supplies.

**Alternative and strongest objection:** Remand would be appropriate if the supplied materials established only bird visits and nationwide conservation importance. The strongest current objection is the difference between the report regions and this artificial site and the lack of a measured fill-induced population reduction. He regards the agency's habitat and breeding findings as the functional link; a regional production estimate supports the broader class without making every pond equivalent. Requiring a new site percentage or a particular scientific study would confuse material support with a prescribed proof method. His preference for economic-application reasoning in Home Builders remains an additional noncontrolling position, not a substitute for the externality analysis here.

**Join limits and remedy:** He can join inclusion and the complete externality ground, with no necessary-regime holding and no claim that conservation desirability alone supplies power. He opposes categorical exclusion and a remand whose sole purpose is obtaining an original research document. A properly justified existing-record remand remains lawful, but is not his selected disposition. Affirmance decides jurisdiction only and supplies no new adjudication of mitigation, alternative sites, drinking-water risk, or permit entitlement.
