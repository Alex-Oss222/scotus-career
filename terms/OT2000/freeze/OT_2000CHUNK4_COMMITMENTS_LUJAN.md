# Lujan v. G & G Fire Sprinklers — independent non-Stone commitments

**Stage:** Independent modeling; frozen provisional handoff before comparator and Stone merits exposure. **Event/cutoff:** 2001-04-17. Authorized neutral packet, later preflight and objective facts, current entering-law slice and prior public American Manufacturers were used. No withheld material or raw Record was opened. All eight Associates participate. No assignment is made.

## Common legal scope and adversarial alternatives

The state command to withhold is governmental conduct; American Manufacturers' private-carrier attribution holding does not defeat it. Assume a property interest in the disputed contractual debt. The question is meaningful process for deciding that entitlement, not an unconditional right to advance payment and not a general §1983 exhaustion requirement.

**Path A:** Reverse the declaration and injunction because ordinary contract adjudication against the prime contractor is available to determine and enforce the disputed debt, including the justification for withholding. J & K Painting, 45 Cal. App.4th 1394, 1401 n.7, supports that ordinary remedy. A contractor-or-assignee action against the awarding body is a separate route with its own ninety-day period after completion and acceptance and exclusive-remedy provision for that action. No assignment, timely filing or successful suit is presumed. An unpaid debt for construction services is distinguishable from seizure of possessed property, termination of a license or an existing payment award. Mathews permits attention to that difference; Logan prohibits destruction of a claim, not adjudication through an ordinary court. The state need not duplicate this genuine adjudicatory avenue with a separate administrative hearing.

**Strongest objection to A:** The official wage assessment might be conclusive against G & G or the contract claim might fail to reach its merits, making the purported remedy theoretical. That objection would be decisive if supported. The supplied legal materials instead recognize the contract route; the statutory exclusivity clause for contractor/assignee suits against the awarding body does not abolish an action against the prime. The Court does not infer an assignment from G & G's account of earlier lawsuits.

**Path B:** Affirm or require a limited process inquiry if every meaningful means of contesting and enforcing this debt is closed, or if the state determination is conclusive in the purported contract forum. Logan and the protected-adjudication limit in American Manufacturers support that rule. Its strongest objection here is absence of a supported legal bar to the ordinary contract action. An additional hearing cannot be ordered merely because the labor statute does not itself supply one. No current factual dispute establishes the necessary remedial obstacle; a remand to search for an imagined assignment refusal is unwarranted.

All eight choose A at this bounded level. The disposition reverses the constitutional declaration and dependent injunction and remands for proceedings consistent with the contract-process holding. It neither determines violations, debt, penalties or damages nor validates every delay or remedial barrier that could arise in another case. The July 1, 2001 replacement procedure is future law, unavailable for the challenged withholding and no ground of decision.

## Individual commitments and limits

| Justice | Own date-eligible authority and sufficient ground | Strongest competing consideration and join boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Stevens | Reverse on adequate actual process, assuming the contract claim is property. His American Manufacturers separate writing demands a genuine determination but rejects categorical advance payment pending a fair decision. Ordinary contract litigation supplies that determination here. | His earlier insistence on notice and employee input cautions against nominal process. Would not join a no-property rule or a holding that an inaccessible assignee-only action suffices. B fails because ordinary suit against the prime remains available, not because litigants must exhaust §1983 remedies. |
| O'Connor | Reverse on A. Her American Manufacturers payment join distinguishes a protected claim from unconditional payment while preserving Logan and Loudermill. Would require a different assessment if state law barred adjudication of the official wage determination; none is supplied. |
| Scalia | Reverse on A. His American Manufacturers joins preserve actual governmental responsibility and the substantive conditions of payment; a contractual entitlement includes judicial enforcement rather than a guarantee of prompt uncontested payment. | B is legally available if the state destroys the cause of action, but absence of a separate administrative hearing is not that destruction. No invented assignment, exhaustion rule or new no-property proposition. |
| Kennedy | Reverse on A. His American Manufacturers opinion separates attribution, conditional payment and fair adjudication. That separation requires recognizing the state command here and examining the actual judicial route rather than extending private-carrier immunity. | A judicial action incapable of deciding entitlement would not satisfy his stated limit. The supported ordinary prime-contractor action defeats that objection here; assume property and decide process only. |
| Souter | Reverse on A. His American Manufacturers payment join and concurrence with Breyer preserve actual continuation interests and loss that money may not repair. This is an unpaid construction debt, with no established medical continuation or prior payment award. | Judicial delay is not automatically harmless in every benefit case. Will join only a contract-interest holding and preserve B for an identified barrier; cannot infer such a barrier from lack of an automatic assignee right. |
| Thomas | Reverse on A. His American Manufacturers joins reject an unconditional payment premise without defining all protected claims away. | Ginsburg | Reverse on A, assuming property and using only the adequate-process ground. | Breyer | Reverse on A. His American Manufacturers concurrence with Souter preserves genuine individual continuation interests but rejects automatic advance payment. Do not use future administrative reform to cure the old procedure. |

**Arithmetic and continuity:** Eight Associates support reversal and the bounded adequate-contract-process ground. No separate-writing necessity is established by these reservations; a common opinion can preserve them. American Manufacturers remains limited and is applied rather than expanded into a universal no-property or no-hearing rule. No earlier same-term dependency has been identified; no law is imported from other April 17 matters.
