# Frozen independent commitments: Penry v. Johnson

Event: June 4, 2001; review of a Fifth Circuit certificate-of-appealability denial. Argument: March 27, 2001. All nine Justices participate at both milestones. Research cutoff: June 4, 2001. The relevant state merits decision is February 22, 1995, with rehearing denied June 7, 1995; no later merits adjudication supplying a different clearly-established-law date is established for the claims under review. Inputs are the approved neutral packet, validation C, control's copied charge/procedure excerpts and the identified entering law and public prior writings. No present-case historical disposition or Stone position was received. No author assignment is made.

## Common threshold and source conclusions

All eight Associates apply Hohn's reviewability rule notwithstanding any prior disagreement with it. A certificate denial is reviewable under section 1254(1); review does not itself perfect the underlying merits appeal. Section 2253(c) and Slack require an issue-specific substantial constitutional showing, not ultimate success. The section 2254(d)(1) restriction remains separate from constitutional error and ultimate sentence relief. Williams's independent clauses and objective inquiry govern present statutory interpretation; later substantive holdings cannot be inserted into the law established in February 1995.

The relevant pre-state-decision mitigation authorities include Lockett, Eddings, Penry v. Lynaugh, Boyde, simulated Graham (January 25, 1993) and Johnson (June 24, 1993). Graham and Johnson already require an effective route for independent culpability mitigation without a false factual special-issue answer. Buchanan and Weeks confirm the continuing functional method but are not the source of clearly established law at the state adjudication. Shafer is also later and concerns a different due-process information question. Neither a diagnosis-based exemption from death nor a claim to favorable evidentiary weight is involved.

The complete material charge and form are supplied. The jury received the packet, a mitigation direction and an instruction to give a negative answer if life suited culpability; the form still required a reasonable-doubt factual certification for a negative answer. The effect record is not complete. No present conclusion of actual harmlessness, harmfulness or structural error is supported merely by the verdict's affirmative answers or the existence of a constitutional defect.

## Component 1: psychiatric cross-examination

All eight Associates reject ultimate relief on the psychiatric claim and would leave its adverse certificate disposition intact on the presented statutory ground. Estelle v. Smith's compelled examination and adversarial use category does not dictate the result for this defense-requested 1977 competency evaluation concerning an unrelated charge, later relied upon by a defense expert in the present mental-condition presentation. Buchanan v. Kentucky's defense-initiated-examination and rebuttal distinction supplies affirmative date-eligible support for treating those circumstances differently. On this record the state decision is neither contrary to the established compelled-use rule nor an objectively unreasonable application of it. The threshold is assessed through that necessary statutory restriction, not through a broad holding that every direct-review use of any old report is lawful.

The strongest contrary path is that warning-free dangerousness material was used affirmatively in capital punishment, including closing argument, and that the original competency purpose did not authorize a later capital use. That is a serious distinction from an unlimited waiver theory. It does not, however, establish that the state decision fell outside the permissible range of the then-established rule given counsel's request and the defense expert's actual reliance. No general subject-matter waiver, permission to compel uncounseled examinations, absence of objection, absence of privilege or automatic harmlessness holding is adopted. The statutory conclusion supplies the sufficient basis; the Court need not create a new privilege rule or find harmlessness.

## Component 2: mitigation mechanism and statutory gateway

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer find constitutional error and an objectively unreasonable application of the preexisting rule. An instruction authorizing consideration is inadequate when the prescribed expression of its independent culpability force requires a false reasonable-doubt answer to an otherwise proven factual special issue. The supplemental permission and the form never reconcile that conflict. A juror could find all factual answers proven beyond reasonable doubt while concluding that the intellectual impairment, possible organic impairment or abuse warrants life. On the complete supplied charge there is a reasonable likelihood that those supported grounds could not receive effective sentencing use. No exact fourth issue or formula is required, but the actual mechanism must permit lawful expression of the choice.

The State's strongest path reads the supplemental instruction as expressly redefining a negative answer to include a life judgment based on mitigation, with the full packet directing that result. The six accept that as the proper opposing argument; they do not pretend no supplemental permission existed. But the unqualified factual certification on the only answer form and the repeated reasonable-doubt directions continue to require the juror to negate a fact to express that judgment. Under Graham and Johnson, already binding at state review, the contradiction is more than suboptimal drafting or a merely preferable additional charge. The state court's recognition of the general mitigation principle does not make this application reasonable. The shared statutory ground is unreasonable application; no exclusive general allocation of the contrary-to clause or unnecessary alternative contrary-to holding is adopted.

Scalia and Thomas would sustain the mitigation denial. Their strongest path treats the supplemental charge as a lawful, specific authorization to give a negative answer when independent mitigation warrants life. Instructions must be read together, not by isolating the form's description of ordinary factual answers. The jury took the entire packet. They would find no reasonable likelihood of exclusion under Boyde; independently the State's reconciliation of those directions is within the objective range permitted by section 2254(d)(1). They apply Graham and Johnson as governing law while distinguishing their absent effective supplemental route. Their position does not claim those cases were historically decided differently, treat them as nonbinding or make future-danger relevance alone sufficient.

## Component 3: effect and disposition

The six finding error favor reversal of the adverse mitigation certificate determination and return for the issue-specific appeal and remaining effect inquiry with constitutional error and section 2254(d)(1) eligibility settled. This is a corrective appellate disposition, not an immediate conditional writ or sentence vacatur. The lower court may deny relief if the independently required effect condition fails under the governing law ultimately adopted. Only an available harmful error supports conditional sentence relief allowing a reasonable period for adequate capital resentencing or a lawful noncapital sentence. Guilt remains undisturbed.

No preexisting universal effect rule answers the original-charge category. Coleman's Chapman holding concerns commutation misinformation; Weeks reserved its response-error category. The following are actual component positions, not preexisting Court law or fabricated unanimous agreements:

* O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would extend Chapman to this defined interference with the capital jury's lawful nondeath choice: the State must prove beyond reasonable doubt from the whole penalty record that the error did not contribute to the actual death sentence. They would remand for that inquiry, not presume contribution.
* Stevens and Kennedy would require demanding whole-record substantial-influence review, with genuine grave doubt preventing a finding of harmlessness. Death eligibility or sufficient aggravation does not itself answer whether the error substantially affected selection. They would remand without selecting a hypothetical jury outcome.
* Scalia and Thomas, if an available violation is found over their objection, would require the prisoner to establish substantial injurious influence. An evenly balanced showing does not satisfy that burden. They preserve the controlling Chapman categories but oppose adding this one. That conditional effect position supplies no vote for the error finding or the remand judgment.

The standard, burden and treatment of uncertainty must be assembled from actual joins, not summed or synthesized by a mechanical narrowest-ground rule. The four Chapman votes alone establish no Court standard; the two substantial-influence positions are not identical to the two petitioner-burden positions. All eight agree that error identification and actual effect are distinct, no magic wording is required in the lower court's inquiry and the actual penalty record controls. Final opinion topology and any controlling effect rule await assembly.

## Assembly constraints and limits

The six correction proponents can directly join: preserved merits review; substantial-showing/certificate eligibility for mitigation; the pre-1995 functional rule; its application to the actual charge and form; unreasonable application; and a corrective return without immediate sentence relief. All eight can join the bounded psychiatric statutory conclusion, the relevant-law-date rule, continued Hohn authority and the distinction between gateway, error and effect. The two mitigation dissenters are not counted as joining error because they offer conditional effect views. No hypothetical additional join is assumed.

This case establishes neither a categorical disability exemption from death nor facial invalidity of every Texas special-issue system. It prescribes no particular fourth question, guarantees no mitigating weight, reopens no guilt finding and supplies no freestanding hearing entitlement under section 2254(e). The form's numerical concurrence direction is part of the supplied mechanism; no independent jury-unanimity claim outside the grant is adjudicated. No execution date, stay, immediate release, new deadline or subsequent state event is invented. The exact future conditional-writ period belongs below if all relief conditions are met.

Source limit: the official neutral extraction provides the charge, form and material procedure; the state reporter verifies dates and reasons. Neither is claimed to be the entire penalty transcript. That limit is handled by a lawful corrective effect remand, not by presuming a party failed to supply material or demanding a new in-world production order.
