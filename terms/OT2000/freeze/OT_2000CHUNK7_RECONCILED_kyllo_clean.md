## Operative boundaries

The scan is a search because its targeted heat-pattern information reveals otherwise unobservable concealed domestic operation. Passive external reception and lack of a recognizable interior image do not defeat that rule. Equipment use or investigative purpose alone is insufficient. No advance warrant or recognized exception is established, and the later entry warrant does not retroactively authorize the scan.

The six supporting Associates vacate the no-search rejection of the preserved suppression claim and dependent conviction affirmance and remand for lawful disposition. The January 1992 conduct date remains relevant only to an actually available and supported remedial defense. There is no final determination of untainted probable cause, causal dependence, independent source, inevitable discovery or objective reliance. Suppression follows if its governing predicates are met and no available ground defeats it; admission may follow a properly established independent ground. Neither result is assumed.

Preserve Leon's knowing-or-reckless-deception, judicial-neutrality, wholly deficient-affidavit and facial-deficiency limits; its inquiry includes obtaining and supplying officers and assumes execution within scope. Evans concerns qualifying non-law-enforcement court-clerical error, not this deliberate scan. Preserve waiver and forfeiture separately, including Olano's requirements when a genuinely forfeited issue is presented; do not attach those requirements to the preserved claim. Rule 11(a)(2) permits plea withdrawal upon prevailing on appeal; this corrective remand does not itself order withdrawal, release, acquittal, retrial, discovery, an excision hearing or a new defense.

The dissenting position remains limited to the information actually acquired. It adopts neither universal heat abandonment, a contraband-only description, a market test nor an invented good-faith defense. No material new neutral fact is needed for these commitments.
