## Qualifications retained for modeling

Paragraph (a) concerns a ground or issue previously determined on the merits on appeal from the judgment. It expressly excepts a subsequent retroactively effective change in the law controlling that issue. A prior presentation, an unreviewed claim, or a nonretroactive change is not silently substituted for those terms.

Paragraph (c) requires sufficient facts in the underlying record to have allowed adequate appellate review of the particular ground or issue. It further requires that the absence of review or determination resulted from the defendant's unjustifiable failure either to take or perfect a timely appeal or to raise the ground or issue on an appeal actually perfected. The provision is not an unconditional bar on every claim omitted from direct review, every failed appeal, or a claim whose sufficient supporting facts were absent from the record.

The quoted command is denial of a motion when the stated conditions apply, notwithstanding the grounds for vacatur in subdivision one. How that command bears on the distinct federal phrase properly filed remains the submitted question.
