## Historical comparison and actual governing premises

The lower majority affirmed defense summary judgment on the facial and as-applied challenges. It treated the new criteria as sect-neutral accommodation, the reimbursed physical care as a subset of ordinary covered care, and the retained review powers as meaningful public coverage control. It used the former independent Lemon prongs and rejected the argument that physical nursing became devotional because of religious motivation. Its actual-operation conclusions, however, are disputed legal and factual evaluations in the judgment under review; they cannot be treated as stipulations that every payment is secular and every review effective.

Lay would vacate defense summary judgment and grant plaintiffs summary judgment on facial unconstitutionality. His grounds include alleged denominational design, the religiously exclusive financial benefit, and practical transfer of coverage authority despite formal final responsibility. His note 10 identifies actual evidence of nurses' religious training, prayer and Bible reading; note 12 quotes the earlier manual's great-weight instruction while preserving its final-intermediary-responsibility language. Those are concrete source recitations, not proof by themselves that a particular public payment purchased prayer or that revised public review is illusory. Nor does his assertion that nonmedical care cannot be a subset resolve the legal effect of condition-equivalent eligibility.

There is no historical Supreme Court merits vote to assign to any Associate. Each frozen commitment must be justified by actual law and that Justice's prior positions rather than by identifying a lower-court judge as a proxy. Actual Agostini replaces independent Lemon prongs with the unified governmental-act and function inquiry, while retaining distinct accommodation and denominational rules. Actual Mitchell requires identification of the publicly financed function and preserves genuinely secular applications; identity, potential diversion and fungibility alone do not prove forbidden use. Current Marshfield tests continuing public conduct despite a genuine formal change. These premises materially change the lower judgment's method without predetermining every application or requiring a new evidentiary record.

## Boundaries required in the common propositions

E1 permits a limited accommodation: otherwise qualifying physical needs receive the independently identifiable secular component of an existing benefit while the patient declines medical care for religious reasons. It creates neither a constitutional subsidy entitlement nor approval of prayer financing, all religious preferences or every current application. E2 addresses this religious criterion on affirmative payment, not every noncompulsory religious accommodation. Any additional facial remedy requires actual severability analysis; no automatic extension to secular objectors or termination of all lawful services is supported.

D1 concerns what the statute necessarily assigns. Institutional recommendation, utilization review, sufficient-condition information, billed-service review and final governmental responsibility remain distinct. The examination exemption does not repeal the condition standard or eliminate all nonmedical information. Formal retention of authority nevertheless does not establish actual effective review in every case. Larkin and Kiryas Joel do not equate every initial private recommendation with a final civil veto.

O1 directs bounded reassessment of the supported operational challenge under the actual financed-function and public-control rules, with ordinary summary-judgment treatment of evidence and reasonable supported inferences. The Cook and manual evidence must be evaluated for actual linkage and materiality; neither its existence nor an operator's retrieval gap proves the violation. Existing material may suffice for judgment. No new hearing, discovery order, burden reversal, universal audit, repayment or presumed litigant default follows. O2 treats the demonstrated linkage as insufficient on the supplied record while preserving a future proved violation. The disagreement is meaningful; it cannot be erased by describing all eight as finding either actual compliance or actual constitutional harm.

Medicare's condition-equivalent eligibility, effective-election requirement and service coverage are different from an actual payment. Medicaid's substitutions and review exemptions require their own statutory connection; the Medicare election mechanism is not silently made the entire Medicaid regime. The complete enacted terms and validated exceptions must accompany any proposition relying on them, including election/revocation rules, involuntary-or-legally-required treatment, ownership and affiliation exceptions, limited accreditation equivalence and the separate protection of billing review.

One nonmaterial statutory wording precision must be preserved: section 1821(c)(2)(A) requires a proportional payment reduction if the prescribed estimate exceeds the trigger, subject to the Secretary's option under (B) to use alternative coverage conditions sufficient to reduce expenditures to the trigger. The freeze's introductory description that these provisions permit reductions does not authorize ignoring that mandatory consequence. No trigger is found to have occurred, and none of the frozen E, D or O commitments depends on discretion to ignore it. The contingent sunset applies only after the specified three consecutive excessive-expenditure years beginning no earlier than fiscal 2002, with its continuing-election limitation. The separate review restriction concerns the identified estimates and reductions, not every ordinary coverage determination.
