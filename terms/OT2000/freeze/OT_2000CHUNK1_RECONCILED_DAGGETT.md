## Historical comparison and actual entering law

The First Circuit announced affirmance on February 18, 2000 and entered its opinion and judgment on March 7. Its conclusion sustains the presented legislative contribution limits and financing scheme. There is no historical Supreme Court merits position to attribute to any Associate. A difference from this lower judgment is not a departure from an identified historical Justice's vote, and neither its author nor its panel unanimity supplies a Supreme Court author or coalition.

The lower opinion's historical treatment of Russell and Shrink Missouri cannot replace the actual Russell government-burden and close-fit rule or the actual Shrink Missouri application. Its footnote 8 criticism of the historical lower Russell reasoning does not describe this Court's controlling Russell. Its footnote 11 reliance on a historical Breyer concurrence does not establish an additional simulated position. The frozen commitments correctly address the influence evidence, campaign scale, capacity and contrary fundraising evidence under the actual standard. They do not require literal campaign impossibility, make referendum support dispositive, equate donor percentages with dollars lost, or turn one successful special election into a safe harbor. The differing statistical denominators and adjustments in footnotes 12–21 remain expert methodology and projections, not established future causal effects.

The lower opinion rejects the Eighth Circuit's Day reasoning and questions its continued vitality. Actual Day instead is a Supreme Court judgment invalidating its own mechanism, without a controlling responsive-financing rationale: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas supplied the expressive-burden rationale; the distinct fifth ground does not create a Marks rule. The present commitments respect that judgment and assess Maine's enacted mechanism. The lower opinion's categorical treatment of responsive speech is not imported into the four sustaining votes. Neither independent speech nor candidate fundraising becomes a coordinated contribution by being measured in a grant formula.

## Operative qualifications and remedy

The complete enacted qualifications in the original commitment remain part of every row. In particular, the matching trigger measures the greater of expenditures or obligations and funds raised or borrowed, alone or with qualifying independent expenditures; the lower opinion's receipts shorthand does not narrow the statute. Matching is capped at twice the relevant initial distribution. The regulatory deduction applies to matching payments for independent express advocacy favoring the participant or opposing the private opponent, after the private opponent exceeds the initial distribution; it does not reduce the initial grant. No unprovided multiple-opponent formula follows. Initial grants, contested and uncontested elections, the initial 25-percent reduction, seed limits and district qualifying contributions retain their separate terms.

The individual candidate/spouse contribution exception is distinct from a certified participant's restrictions. The shortage provision prohibits overspending the fund and permits, rather than requires, Commission authorization of bounded private financing subject to its amount, seed-money deduction, donor limits and rules. No actual shortage, subsequent fundraising adaptation, completed corruption exchange, or post-decision candidate conduct is found. The statutory validation identifies the August 11, 2000 cleanup of obsolete transitional wording; it does not change the operative legislative amount or exception, and that date-effective statutory source chronology is preserved without inventing a separate Court event or manifest item for the unchanged operation.

The full lower opinion also decides a separate section 1019 reporting challenge and leaves the governor challenge dismissed without prejudice for insufficiently concrete injury. The granted questions here do not independently submit those claims or a distinct party-specific burden. This reconciliation supplies no new disclosure merits holding, governor holding, or party-specific adjudication. The lower opinion's footnote 28 identifies additional primary-allocation and section 1017 reporting theories not pursued on appeal; do not revive them. Any genuine relation between special enforcement and an invalidated component remains for the prescribed severability implementation.

Nor does the statutory name establish a government command to label nonparticipants unclean.
