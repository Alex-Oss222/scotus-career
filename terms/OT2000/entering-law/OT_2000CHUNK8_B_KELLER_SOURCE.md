# Keller: predivergence governmental-speech and compulsory-dues source

Keller v. State Bar of California, 496 U.S. 1 (1990), pages 10–14. The complete official 17-page report was read. This passage is copied exactly from the official text extraction; page markers and extraction artifacts are retained. Keller distinguishes the integrated Bar's particular structure, advisory function and compelled professional dues from ordinary governmental representation and taxation. It does not decide the Mushroom Council's status or message, and its integrated-bar germaneness inquiry does not displace the actual later Glickman and Southworth holdings.
