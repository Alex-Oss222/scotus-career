## Material conclusions and limits

The record supports attributed testimony of differing physical ballot tests and changes over time; it does not support replacing that testimony with a universal finding about every county or counting team. The complete examinations preserve qualifications absent from compressed brief descriptions. The partial Miami-Dade figure has no completed remaining-precinct comparator in these materials. Palm Beach's numerical dispute remains open. Alleged double counting must not be promoted from sorting discrepancies into a demonstrated duplicate vote. Federal preservation and finality remain legal questions despite the identifiable pleadings and leave ruling. Earlier county timing testimony does not prove later statewide completion or impossibility.

The final neutral handoff comprises gore-record-facts.md, gore-main-brief-excerpts.txt, gore-miami-witnesses-2000-12-03.pdf, and gore-clarification-leave-order-2000-12-08.txt, together with the appendix/Burton neutral files. The selected OCR bodies are exact slices; scan witness pages are copied programmatically. No historical federal adjudication, intervention, lineup, or resulting recount total is included as a neutral premise. Independent clean validation remains required before use in modeling.
