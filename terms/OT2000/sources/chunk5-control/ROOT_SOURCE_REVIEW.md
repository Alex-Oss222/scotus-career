## C & L Enterprises

Source: [official report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-411.pdf), saved as `CL.pdf` and `CL.txt`; all thirteen PDF pages, report pages 411–423.

The comparator is accurate: unanimous reversal and remand, Ginsburg writing. The contractual enforcement clause, incorporated arbitration rules and selected Oklahoma law together establish the historical waiver rationale. The opinion does not rest on a commercial-activity exception or require a particular formula of waiver words. Incorporated prescriptions are treated as terms of the agreement, not outside evidence. The opinion leaves the parallelism of state and tribal immunity waiver principles undecided. Footnote 6 identifies unaddressed section 81 and signatory-authority defenses, not aired below and outside the accepted question. No asset-execution decision is made. The historical earlier remand is not independently entered in this timeline by this verification.

## Major League Baseball Players Association v. Garvey

Source: [official report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-504.pdf), saved as `Garvey.pdf` and `Garvey.txt`; all ten PDF pages, report pages 504–513.

The comparator is accurate: petition granted with per curiam reversal and remand; eight Justices support the judgment. Ginsburg joins only the necessary correction of disturbing the award. Stevens dissents, questioning both the substantive boundary of irrational arbitral action and summary treatment without further briefing or argument. His writing does not affirm the arbitral-error finding as his own final merits conclusion. The opinion permits antecedent-ruling review through the timely petition from the later judgment. It treats the accepted collective-bargaining submission as distinct from independent statutory claims. The Court rejects the credibility substitution and directed monetary relief. Further arbitration is not required where the original denial of vacatur is restored; any continuing contractual possibility depends on the agreement. The general remedial discussion must preserve the agreement's authority and not impose another hearing automatically.
