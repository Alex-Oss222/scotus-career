# Mariani comparator and procedural source verification

Control-only receipt. The complete lower-court opinion, paragraphs 1–55 and all five footnotes, was read at https://openjurist.org/212/f3d/761 . The complete October 2000 federal opposition, including all nine footnotes, was read at https://www.justice.gov/osg/brief/mariani-v-united-states-opposition . The latter independently supports the questions, jurisdiction, procedural record and reported lower judgment. No historical Supreme Court event mentioned by a source is admitted by this verification.

The May 18, 2000 en banc judgment rejected both constitutional challenges. It did not determine guilt. Mariani's voter eligibility under section 437h was uncontested. The court treated the corporate-ban challenge as nonfrivolous under either proposed certification standard. It reached the separate conduit challenge without a new frivolousness determination because the government did not object to that omission and the criminal proceeding awaited resolution. The current Supreme Court merits grant does not require a new holding selecting between those certification standards.

The corporate challenge includes both the facial objection to an absolute treasury-contribution prohibition and the claim that soft-money practices make the rule impermissibly underinclusive. The lower court's actual reliance on historical Shrink Missouri must be checked against this timeline's controlling version. Neither that reliance nor its reading of Austin supplies the result here automatically. The published record contains both agreed facts and contested legal or legislative conclusions; do not treat every district-court characterization as a binding adjudicative finding.

Section 441f reaches making a contribution in another's name, knowingly allowing one's name to be used, and knowingly accepting a contribution made by another. The challenge concerns source concealment and underinclusiveness, not an adjudication of criminal intent. The disclosure authorities retain the exception for a reasonable probability of threats, harassment, or reprisals. No such showing is established here. Any later judgment must distinguish this protection from a license to falsify a contributor's identity.

The federal opposition distinguishes direct candidate control and permissible uses of hard money from soft money. Its account identifies corporate administration and solicitation of a voluntary separate segregated fund, and personal contributions, as distinct from treasury contributions. These are existing statutory channels, not compelled individual donations. It notes that some alleged soft-money activity has unresolved legality; the merits judgment should not silently legalize all examples. Allegations of reimbursed contributions remain allegations. The extent and guilt of any criminal violation remain for the criminal proceeding.

This receipt verifies source scope and the lower comparator only. It adds no Justice commitment, author, or Supreme Court merits coalition. Actual entering law must be refreshed after Daggett before independent modeling.
