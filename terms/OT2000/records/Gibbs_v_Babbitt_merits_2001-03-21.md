## Event and chronology

## Holdings

### Part I. The enacted taking class and the required connection

**Holding and operative rule:** The regulated class is the taking of protected wildlife without a commercial element. A landowner's commercial motive, the wolves' past interstate transportation, projected tourism, scientific value and potential trade do not, alone or by accumulation, establish the constitutionally required interstate connection; aggregation measures effects after an eligible connection is identified and cannot supply the missing connection.

**Authority:** Part I of Justice O'Connor's opinion has five direct joins: Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Ginsburg and Breyer accept the governing framework for the remand but do not join this part's restrictive connection premise. The five direct joins control without aggregation of separate rationales.

**Controlling explanation:** Lopez requires identification of the enacted class and its actual interstate mechanism before aggregate magnitude can establish power. Home Builders applies that requirement to wildlife taking; an owner's commercial project does not rewrite the class Congress regulated. Morrison preserves the requirement despite extensive evidence of downstream economic consequences.

The present rule prohibits taking experimental red wolves on private land without making commercial activity an element. Mann's concern for cattle therefore does not transform the regulated class into cattle ranching. Actual refuge visitors and scientific work are relevant evidence, but their economic consequences do not by themselves establish the necessary relationship between this taking class and interstate commerce. Nor does the wolves' earlier transport establish that every later local taking is itself interstate commerce. The government must establish an eligible mechanism under the governing framework rather than combine several incomplete connections. The Court does not insert a new commercial element into the regulation or adopt a categorical rule immunizing all noneconomic local conduct.

### Part II. Examine the existing record under both complete routes

**Holding and operative rule:** The judgment sustaining these private-land applications must be vacated for a confined examination of the existing legislative and adjudicative record. The government must identify and materially support either a valid federal interstate regime that exclusion of this taking class would evade or materially impair, or a concrete substantial cross-border effect that affected States cannot reasonably confine or internalize through ordinary territorial authority, with a reasonable fit between the prohibition and the effect; if neither complete route is supported, appropriate party- and application-specific declaratory or injunctive relief must follow.

**Authority:** Part II of Justice O'Connor's opinion and the vacatur/remand have five direct joins: Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer. Ginsburg and Breyer affirmatively join this complete inquiry and remedy under the controlling connection framework, while preserving their proposed broader ground for affirmance. Those five directly adopt the complete inquiry and remedy; no broader rationale commands a majority.

**Controlling explanation:** Home Builders supplies both routes and the government's obligation to support the mechanism it invokes. Courts independently decide constitutional eligibility while allowing supported legislative predictions and reasonable regulatory design within the power. An actual interstate regime and its anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship must be identified; a comprehensive conservation program and federal refuge ownership do not alone establish that relationship. The externality route requires a supported substantial effect across state boundaries, a real territorial-control limit, and fit. State policy disagreement, national interest and ordinary movement from a refuge to nearby private land are insufficient by themselves.

The existing record reports actual interstate refuge visitors and a population occupying both refuge and private land. Those facts warrant examination under the correct mechanisms; disputes over revenue forecasts do not erase the visitors. Conversely, no interstate wolf migration, failed state cooperation, economic market or causal necessity may be invented. Agency accounts, program records, testimony and reasonable circumstantial inference may supply support without a mandatory statistical study, fixed revenue threshold or proof that every animal crosses a state line.

Morrison denies a general entitlement to repeated opportunities after an inadequate commerce theory. This remand instead identifies evidence already present that the lower courts did not evaluate under Home Builders. It permits no indefinite search for new science or replacement rationales. Solid Waste decided statutory authority, and the separate ecological position there involved a different mobile interstate bird population; neither supplies missing wolf facts. The court on remand must determine whether the existing support completes either route. If not, confined relief is required.

**Limits and remaining work:** The governing legal routes are settled; the remaining task is to evaluate the existing record under them, not to discover an additional legal test. Neither mechanism is found proved in this decision. The Property Clause, treaty theory and any takings-compensation claim lie outside the granted Commerce question. Mann's earlier conviction is not reviewed. Facts needed for a particular future enforcement event are absent and are not found here. Relief remains tied to the parties and challenged private-land applications. The remand itself authorizes no taking contrary to otherwise applicable law, and orders neither damages nor general invalidation of the ESA.

Their earlier view of economic habitat conversion does not establish that direct killing is economic, and their Solid Waste ecological position supplies no interstate red-wolf facts. They do not assert that several incomplete Home Builders theories add up to a complete one. Because their proposed rule supports affirmance, they do not join the existing-law remand.

**Scalia, concurring in part and dissenting in part, joined by Thomas.** These Justices join Part I and would reverse for declaratory or injunctive relief confined to the challenged private-land applications. They accept authority over local conduct genuinely necessary to protect a valid interstate regime, but the offered grounds establish no supported anti-evasion or material-impairment relationship. They retain their Home Builders objection to territorial incapacity as an independent source of commerce authority and do not join Part II's externality route. Morrison confirms that economic consequences cannot supply the missing connection and that failure of the offered theories does not entitle the government to search indefinitely for another. Their proposed relief neither invalidates the ESA generally nor authorizes killing contrary to other valid law, revisits Mann's prior conviction, or awards compensation.

## Procedure After Action

The Fourth Circuit judgment is vacated. The case returns for a confined evaluation of the already existing legislative and adjudicative record on the relevant evidence and theories properly before the lower courts. Adequate support for either complete route can sustain the challenged application; if neither is supported, appropriate party- and application-specific declaratory or injunctive relief must follow. No indefinite new-record search, automatic evidentiary hearing, new science, damages, compensation or affirmative killing authorization accompanies the remand.
