## Event

Rogers v. Tennessee, No. 99-6218. Merits decision, May 14, 2001; argued November 1, 2000. On writ of certiorari to the Tennessee Supreme Court. The question is whether due process permits application of that court's abolition of the year-and-a-day murder limitation to conduct occurring while the limitation remained state law.

Rogers stabbed the victim on May 6, 1994. The undisputed death interval exceeded a year and a day. Tennessee's highest court held that the limitation survived the 1989 statutory revision, abolished it judicially, and applied the abolition to sustain Rogers's second-degree murder conviction. The causal medical account and the legal time limitation are distinct. Notice must be evaluated from law existing when Rogers acted.
