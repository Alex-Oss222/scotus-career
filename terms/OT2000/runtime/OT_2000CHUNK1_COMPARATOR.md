# Bush v. Gore

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

The historical Court entered an interim stay on December 9, 2000. That order is comparator history only; no corresponding stay has been established in this proceeding.

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded; five Justices supported the disposition that ended the recount. Seven Justices identified constitutional problems with the recount procedure, but their remedial positions differed and do not create a single 7-2 judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam disposition supported by Rehnquist, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Rehnquist, joined by Scalia and Thomas, concurred on Article II. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer filed dissents with differing joins. Souter and Breyer supported corrective proceedings rather than the final disposition.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court found unequal recount treatment and treated the December 12 safe-harbor premise as precluding a constitutionally adequate recount in time. The separate Article II analysis had three adherents.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 98, 2000-12-12. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Bush v. Gore](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-98.pdf).
