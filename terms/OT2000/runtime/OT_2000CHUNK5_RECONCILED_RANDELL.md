## Components and alternatives

The civil complaint alleges uncredited parole-warrant confinement and repeated service of that period. Federal habeas concerning the disputed custody was unavailable at the civil filing. It does not follow that Randell deliberately failed to use an earlier opportunity, that no earlier realistic opportunity existed, that an authorized state invalidation channel remains effective, or that the actual confinement was unlawful.

Heck requires comparison of the precise civil theory with the operative criminal or custody determination. A claim that officials executed a valid sentence contrary to its terms may be consistent with that sentence; an attack necessarily defeating an outstanding credit or custody adjudication is different. Edwards does not make every sentence-administration claim independent merely because it is called procedural. The screening judgment does not sufficiently identify the operative determination or decide this necessary-invalidity relationship. All eight commitments require that question to be treated expressly.

Path A rejects a categorical former-prisoner bar and recognizes at least a bounded exception when the person never had a realistic lawful opportunity to obtain custody review, or lost that opportunity through circumstances beyond his control despite timely pursuit. Statutory coordination cannot demand use of an unavailable channel. Its strongest objection is that expiration could reward strategic delay and authorize contradictory judgments. The response does not treat expiration as invalidation and does not exempt a person merely because he allowed a real opportunity to lapse; it preserves claim-specific adjudication, actual opportunity facts, ordinary preclusion and defenses. The supposed need to exhaust every possible state remedy would violate Patsy and is not this rule.

Path B retains the common-law-informed invalidation prerequisite for a necessarily contradictory damages claim after release; loss of federal habeas alone does not make that claim cognizable. Its strongest objection is complete loss of a federal remedy for a person whose custody was too short or whose error was discovered too late. These Justices regard an outstanding adjudication's contradiction as distinct from ordinary exhaustion and do not equate a remedial gap with favorable invalidation. They preserve the need first to identify an adjudication actually contradicted and do not extend the prerequisite to an independent execution injury. Whether genuinely impossible original review warrants a further exceptional rule need not be decided before those missing predicates are resolved; they do not join Path A's affirmative exception now.

A third, broader version of Path A would allow all former-prisoner actions whenever federal habeas is presently unavailable. That is more than required to correct the categorical dismissal on this record and would decide the unsupported deliberate-delay situation. The commitments below do not adopt that unconditional rule. Likewise, Path B cannot affirm on imagined findings that Randell already adjudicated this credit dispute or unquestionably challenges an extant custody decision.

## Compatibility and disposition limits

All eight support vacatur of this categorical screening dismissal and remand for precise classification. Four also directly support the bounded no-realistic-review exception; four withhold that new exception and preserve the common-law-informed release-alone rule for an actually contradictory claim. A shared judgment does not make either additional rationale unanimous, and incompatible grounds may not be combined into an invented controlling rule. There is no completed Court tally or assignment here.

Every Justice can join an opinion stating that existing Heck and Edwards law did not already decide a universal former-prisoner bar, that Spencer adopted no affirmative exception, and that claim-specific necessary-invalidity analysis is required before applying the prerequisite. The four Path B Justices will not join language that uses this remand to confer an exception automatically. The four Path A Justices support an express, independently counted new exception for the genuinely unavailable-review category; an unexplained remand must not falsely represent them as having decided nothing about it.

The lower court should identify the judgment, administrative calculation or custody adjudication the complaint necessarily contradicts, if any, and permit ordinary consideration of the pertinent alleged facts. If the eventually adopted rule makes actual review opportunity material, the court must address its timing and realistic availability without presuming abandonment, deliberate delay or exhaustion of all state procedures. The existence of an authorized qualifying invalidation channel may matter to the prerequisite where applicable; mere theoretical possibility is not a factual finding of availability.

Section 1915(e)(2) retains its mandatory grounds, but failure to satisfy a legal prerequisite is not a finding of factual frivolousness or misconduct merits. A renewed invalidation-prerequisite dismissal must be without prejudice for lack of present cognizability. No custody recalculation, habeas conversion, compelled state reopening, stay, damages award, automatic appointed counsel or particular limitations ruling follows. Individual responsibility, constitutional violation, causation, compensable loss, immunity and independently applicable preclusion remain open. The requested daily amount proves no measure of loss. The operative custody calculation and actual review history would be required for a final factual ruling; they are not required to correct the categorical dismissal on the bounded grounds stated.
