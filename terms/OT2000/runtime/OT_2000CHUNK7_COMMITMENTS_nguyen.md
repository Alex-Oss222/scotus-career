## Supplemental neutral validation and statutory scope

The addendum contains enacted text, amendment and effective-date notes, and the general INA separability provision. It contains no current-matter judicial merits framing. Nguyen was seventeen on November 14, 1986; the enacted transition therefore permits election of the former section 309(a), which required paternity established by legitimation before twenty-one and contained no later written-support promise. The new route requires clear-and-convincing blood relationship, paternal nationality, a written support agreement unless the father is deceased, and one of legitimation, sworn acknowledgment or competent-court paternity adjudication before eighteen. The mother's route separately requires nationality and continuous prior presence of one year. These are distinct conditions, not interchangeable exceptions.

Section 1401's 1986 reduction of the transmitting parent's presence requirement applies to persons born on or after November 14, 1986. It does not itself supply Nguyen, born in 1969, a five-year/two-after-fourteen rule. The earlier ten-year/five-after-fourteen requirement and its actual statutory credit provisions must govern any needed presence determination. An open factual question whether this father's record satisfies the applicable rule is not a new presumption of failure. The 1998 parentage order was late under either recognition deadline; whether it supplies remaining former-route legitimation is an implementation question if not already settled below.

The general separability provision preserves the remainder and other applications when a provision or its application is invalid. It supports, but does not mechanically decide, the statutory-intent choice between extension and withdrawal. The addendum resolves the requested statutory coverage gap sufficiently for a bounded remedial commitment; it does not establish every citizenship fact.

## Competing constitutional and remedial paths

Virginia requires a genuine important objective and substantial fit, with real differences relevant but generalized parental-role assumptions insufficient. Miller supplies no controlling merits or remedy rationale. Fiallo's admission-preference setting cannot simply determine the status of a person claiming statutory citizenship at birth. The father is an actual copetitioner, removing the claimant obstacle decisive for two Miller votes.

The merits-affirmance path treats birth as ordinarily documenting maternity and providing an opportunity for connection that paternity alone does not provide. Recognition during childhood is an administrable means to assure that opportunity; the government need not prove lasting affection in every case. The strongest objection is the mismatch between a male-only legal deadline and either biological certainty or actual opportunity: conclusive later proof is possible, a timely formality need not establish any relationship, and the maternal route demands no comparable continuing bond.

The invalidation path permits evidence rules suited to actual maternity/paternity differences but rejects this additional male-only temporal formality under substantial-fit review. The strongest objection is that the Constitution does not demand individualized relationship inquiries or the least restrictive means. The response must therefore identify failure of substantial fit, not merely point to a conceivable better method: Nguyen's father's years of actual care and unquestioned biological relationship expose the gap between timely legal formality and the asserted opportunity objective; the maternal rule confirms that the chosen proxy is not a general relationship requirement.

If invalid, extension through removal of the discriminatory age barrier preserves the benefit-conferring statute and the remaining parentage, nationality and presence conditions. Mathews requires considering withdrawal as well; Westcott and separability support preserving benefits rather than withdrawing an independently conferred maternal protection. Pangilinan bars equitable citizenship contrary to valid law, but the corrective path recognizes status under the statute remaining after constitutional adjudication. The opposing remedial path treats sections 1401 and 1409 as one conditional grant whose scope courts cannot enlarge under the naturalization power and section 1421(d). It would affirm without deciding the classification. That objection must be addressed independently of merits; a merits vote supplies no automatic remedy vote.

## Precise remedial commitment

The five reversing Associates can join removal of the unconstitutional recognition-age barrier on the available former-law route, with ordinary implementation of the statutory election and determination of remaining valid requirements if genuinely unresolved. No new support promise, imported maternal residence standard or judicial finding that an unverified parentage order satisfies every legitimation rule follows. If the valid remaining conditions establish citizenship at birth, removal as an alien cannot stand; otherwise the responsible tribunal resolves the surviving statutory issues lawfully. No conviction is vacated, no citizenship is withdrawn from another person, and no general ruling addresses all sex distinctions in nationality law. The government may use constitutionally adequate proof safeguards; a late order does not become historically timely by interpretation.
