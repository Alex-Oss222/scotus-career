# Denius v. Dunlap

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Comparator Type:** Seventh Circuit decision, April 11, 2000; no corresponding historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified.
* **Historical Outcome and Structure:** Flaum wrote for a panel with Coffey and Kanne. The court reversed qualified immunity on the attorney-client and medical-information claims and affirmed immunity on the financial-information claim.
* **Historical Reasoning:** The panel treated confidential legal consultation and medical confidentiality as sufficiently established, while financial confidentiality lacked comparable clarity.
* **Authority Caution:** That conduct-date determination is historical comparison material and must be evaluated against controlling precedent.
