# OT_2000CHUNK5

# Lackawanna County District Attorney v. Coss

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor’s Parts I, II, III-A and IV had Rehnquist, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Part III-B, discussing possible additional exceptions, had only Rehnquist and Kennedy; Part III-C, finding no actual sentencing effect, also had Thomas. Souter dissented with Stevens and Ginsburg; Breyer separately dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court extended the general restriction on attacks through enhanced sentences to § 2254 and preserved the complete-denial-of-counsel exception.
* **Historical Authority:** The faultless-unavailability discussion and the factual no-effect rationale lacked five direct joins. They must not be represented as unanimous or as governing law.
