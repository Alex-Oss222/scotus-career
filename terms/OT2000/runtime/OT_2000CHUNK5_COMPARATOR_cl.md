# OT_2000CHUNK5

# C & L Enterprises, Inc. v. Citizen Band Potawatomi Indian Tribe of Oklahoma

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg delivered the opinion for all nine Justices.
* **Historical Holding:** The combined arbitration and enforcement undertaking clearly waived immunity from the state-court action to enforce the award.
* **Historical Authority:** The decision did not recognize a general commercial exception to tribal immunity or require the literal phrase sovereign immunity in every effective waiver. This historical outcome does not govern the present matter.

* **Historical Sources:** [C & L Enterprises, Inc. v. Citizen Band Potawatomi Indian Tribe of Oklahoma](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-411.pdf). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
