# Semtek International Inc. v. Lockheed Martin Corp.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia delivered the opinion for all nine Justices.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court adopted federal common law incorporating the rendering State’s preclusion law in diversity cases, subject to incompatible federal interests, and rejected automatic nationwide preclusion from Rule 41(b) alone.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 497, 2001-02-27. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Semtek International Inc. v. Lockheed Martin Corp.](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-497.pdf).

# Whitman v. American Trucking Associations, Inc. / American Trucking Associations, Inc. v. Whitman

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part, reversed in part, and remanded. All nine Justices agreed in the judgment, with differing rationales on portions of the analysis.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote. Parts I and IV were unanimous. Part II was joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, O’Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, and Ginsburg. Part III was joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Kennedy, Thomas, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice Thomas concurred; Justice Stevens, joined by Justice Souter, and Justice Breyer separately concurred in part and in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court excluded implementation costs from standard setting, upheld the delegation, found implementation reviewable, and rejected EPA’s interpretation eliminating the ozone-specific subpart’s constraints.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 457, 2001-02-27. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Whitman v. American Trucking Associations, Inc. / American Trucking Associations, Inc. v. Whitman](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-457.pdf).

# Cook v. Gralike

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed unanimously in judgment, with different grounds and partial joins.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens wrote. Justices Scalia, Kennedy, Ginsburg, and Breyer joined the opinion; Justice Souter joined Parts I, II, and IV, and Justice Thomas Parts I and IV. Justice Kennedy concurred. Justice Thomas concurred in part and in the judgment. Chief Justice Rehnquist concurred in the judgment, joined by Justice O’Connor.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court invalidated the labels. The controlling Elections Clause analysis treated them as an effort to favor or disfavor candidates rather than regulate election procedure; the Chief Justice’s separate opinion relied on viewpoint discrimination.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 510, 2001-02-28. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Cook v. Gralike](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-510.pdf).

# Legal Services Corp. v. Velazquez / United States v. Velazquez

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Justices Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice Scalia dissented, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court invalidated the condition because the program facilitated private client speech and the restriction distorted legal advocacy and insulated welfare law from challenge. It left the uncontested severability determination undisturbed.
