## Review scope and independent legal questions

All eight Associates participate. The tariff protest presents the legal weight of the headquarters ruling and the classification of the planners. The judicial-review channel is available; no independent threshold defect is established. Customs' ordinary classification duty, its power to issue regulations, the legal effect of this ruling mechanism, and the soundness of this particular interpretation must be examined separately.

Haggar gives effect to an actual delegated interpretive choice implemented through the regulation there, without making either agency form or de novo judicial factfinding dispositive. Christensen treats a genuinely nonbinding advisory letter as persuasive rather than controlling. Appalachian Power requires attention to completed legal consequences for reviewability and forbids regulatory amendment through interpretation. Finality and operative effect do not themselves answer whether Congress delegated authority to make an interpretation binding on the reviewing court.

## Competing paths

**Controlling-delegation path.** Congress entrusted classification and assessment to Customs, and headquarters gave an official explanation in exercising that responsibility. Numerous individual applications, lack of notice and comment, and modifiability need not exclude delegated interpretive authority. The strongest objection is that authority to administer particular transactions is not necessarily authority to establish a rule controlling courts; the scheme's ruling letters bind only their particular administrative setting and do not operate as general regulations for unrelated importers.

**Persuasive-weight path.** The limited operation, numerous issuing offices, transaction-specific effect, and absence of a demonstrated delegation to establish binding law through this mechanism support independent judicial statutory construction with consideration of Customs' actual reasons. The strongest objection is that adjudication and classification can exercise delegated authority without general rulemaking, and Congress need not enumerate every authoritative interpretive form. This path therefore cannot rest on format alone or state that all informal action lacks controlling weight.

**Independent tariff-answer path.** A sufficiently clear enacted tariff category would control regardless of agency weight. Mead's strongest textual argument distinguishes a removable-page organizer from an ordinarily bound diary and gives meaning to the competing residual category. Customs answers that a ring mechanism binds organized pages and that a diary can serve prospective scheduling and notes rather than only a retrospective narrative. Its dictionary and commercial-usage reasons therefore address the actual goods rather than an unrelated policy preference. A court could decide the tariff category directly after applying its own best reading, or sustain the classification as a permissible delegated choice. The seven persuasive-weight Justices select remand because the appellate court excluded an entire relevant source of interpretive assistance, not because missing research papers establish a merits failure. Scalia would resolve the Government's second question by accepting the classification as reasonable within the delegated authority, leaving only ordinary consequential assessment matters below.

## Proposition-level commitments

Seven Associates - Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer - support denying controlling Chevron weight to this transaction-specific ruling mechanism and requiring consideration of the reasons under Skidmore. Their shared operative rule is that a court must identify Congress's actual conferral of authority to make legally controlling interpretations and whether the action exercises that authority; expertise and an administrative assignment alone are insufficient. The facts here do not establish the required conferral through these ruling letters. Formal procedures can evidence such authority, but are not an exclusive prerequisite or sufficient by themselves.

Scalia supports setting aside the importer's appellate victory but would go further and sustain the classification, so he is not an eighth vote for the seven-Justice remand's unresolved-classification scope. His statutory-authority objection is substantive, not a request to count expertise as a vote. He regards official headquarters classification under Congress's assigned responsibility as an authoritative construction eligible for reasonable-choice deference, absent a contrary statutory limit, and finds the stated reading reasonable. He may agree that the Federal Circuit erred in discarding persuasive reasoning, but that does not make him an eighth join to the no-controlling-weight holding.

All eight reject the appellate court's categorical denial of persuasive consideration. Seven support vacating the appellate judgment and returning the classification dispute for the proper legal inquiry. That ground requires evaluating dictionary and commercial reasons, agency experience, consistency and the changed treatment, without predetermined weight or outcome. It is not a delegation to the agency to decide its own authority conclusively. Scalia would reverse on controlling deference and a permissible tariff reading, foreclosing renewed rejection of that classification on the presented interpretive grounds. Neither remedy sets a duty amount or refund, invents a notice procedure or orders a future regulation.

## Current-law and source limits

Appalachian Power remains a reviewability and regulatory-text decision: operational consequences can make agency action reviewable without necessarily giving its legal interpretation controlling force. Haggar's formally promulgated regulation remains governed by its actual delegated-authority rule. Christensen remains binding for its advisory letter, not every informal agency act. None is overruled by the seven-Justice path; it resolves the previously open Customs-letter application. Scalia's alternative remains separately stated.

The 1993 letter must be evaluated under its then-operative legal regime. Later statutory procedures for modifying rulings do not retroactively supply authority or process in this dispute. The record's incompleteness prevents an invented finding about unpublished internal endorsement or every aspect of the agency file, but does not prevent the bounded delegation and remand decision. No material neutral blocker remains.
