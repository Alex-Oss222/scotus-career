# Urofsky v. Gilmore

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Comparator Type:** Lower-court merits decision; no corresponding historical Supreme Court merits decision is identified.
* **Historical Outcome and Structure:** The en banc Fourth Circuit reversed. Wilkins wrote for himself and Widener, Niemeyer, Luttig, Williams, Traxler and Hamilton. Luttig and Hamilton concurred separately; Wilkinson concurred in the judgment. Murnaghan dissented with Michael, Motz and King.
* **Historical Reasoning:** The majority treated the regulated activity as employee speech and rejected the asserted individual academic-freedom right. Wilkinson preferred a narrower approach emphasizing the amended law and approval process.
* **Authority Caution:** The lower-court opinion is the case under review, not a controlling Supreme Court holding. Its categorical employee-speech formulation cannot override the limits of Boring v. Buncombe County Board of Education.
