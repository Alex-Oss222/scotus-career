# OT_2000CHUNK5

# Randell v. Johnson

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling precedent, subject to intervening decisions effective before April 25, 2001.
* **Conditions:** Apply intervening controlling decisions; the record must establish any asserted review opportunity or deliberate bypass.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate and remand; reject categorical dismissal solely because no tribunal previously invalidated the disputed confinement.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Compare the credit claim with the actual sentence and custody determination. If it seeks enforcement of the valid sentence, permit the civil action without favorable invalidation. If success necessarily establishes invalidity, decide from supported facts whether a realistic custody-review opportunity existed and whether its loss is legally excused. Proceed without the prerequisite if no meaningful opportunity existed; otherwise enforce the prerequisite and dismiss without prejudice to a qualifying later invalidation. Preserve ordinary defenses and decide liability and injury if the claim may proceed; award no damages now and direct no state-court reopening.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Former-prisoner civil remedy

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Resolve the legal universality issue and the standard for the reserved no-realistic-opportunity category. Leave supported factual application to the remand court.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether favorable invalidation bars the former prisoner's damages claim when federal habeas relief is unavailable, taking account of whether the claim contradicts a valid sentence and whether a realistic prior opportunity for custody review existed.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Heck v. Humphrey's claim-specific prerequisite and expressly adopt a new, narrow exception within its reservation.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A claim compatible with the valid sentence is not subject to favorable invalidation. If success necessarily contradicts the operative custody determination, a former prisoner who lacked a realistic lawful opportunity to contest that confinement before custody review became unavailable may proceed without prior invalidation. A claimant who lost a genuinely available opportunity through an unexcused failure to use it remains subject to the prerequisite; expiration alone does not satisfy it. These are the governing rules for Randell's remand, not questions postponed to another case.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule covers the formerly confined claimant and the effect of prior availability. It rejects both automatic civil exclusion after release and automatic evasion through deliberate or otherwise unexcused loss of an available review route. It does not require exhaustion of every conceivable state procedure as a universal civil-action condition.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Heck v. Humphrey and Spencer v. Kemna coordinate available statutory remedies without creating an absolute civil disability arising from a conviction. A review route that never realistically existed cannot perform that coordinating function. Randell's allegations establish present federal unavailability but do not settle earlier opportunity or necessary invalidity. The lower court resolved those open matters through an overbroad rule, so remand is required. The decisive reason for the exception is statutory reconciliation: a rule coordinating section 1983 with custody review cannot require a remedy the claimant never realistically could obtain. Patsy's rejection of general state-remedy exhaustion remains in force. Ordinary records of custody, notices, access to the court, and the time available to act can establish realistic opportunity; absence of an earlier successful writ alone proves neither opportunity nor deliberate bypass.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The legal consequences of availability and unexcused bypass are resolved above. Randell's actual review opportunity, the credit calculation, individual responsibility, immunity, limitations, and compensable injury require the undeveloped factual record and ordinary remand adjudication.
