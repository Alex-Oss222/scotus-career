## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas delivered the opinion for all nine Justices.
* **Historical Holding:** Banks for cooperatives remained subject to state income taxation; removal of obsolete stock-related exemption language did not create a broader exemption.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 316, 2001-02-20. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
