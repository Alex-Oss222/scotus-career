# OT_2000CHUNK5

# Atwater v. City of Lago Vista

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4, April 24, 2001.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Justice O'Connor dissented with Justices Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** Probable cause to believe a person committed even a very minor criminal offense in the officer's presence ordinarily permits warrantless custodial arrest.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical majority rejected a fine-only categorical restriction despite criticizing this arrest's practical justification. Ricci v. Village of Arlington Heights presents a distinct question that the historical disposition in Atwater v. City of Lago Vista cannot resolve as governing precedent.

* **Historical Sources:** [Atwater v. City of Lago Vista](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-318.pdf). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
