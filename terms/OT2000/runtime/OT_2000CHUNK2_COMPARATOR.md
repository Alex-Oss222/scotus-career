# Brewer v. West Irondequoit Central School District

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Comparator Type:** Lower-court merits and preliminary-relief decision; this simulation grant has no corresponding historical Supreme Court merits disposition.
* **Historical Outcome and Structure:** Straub wrote the Second Circuit majority opinion; Parker concurred separately; Miner dissented. The court vacated mandatory admission and remanded for trial, while conditionally allowing completion of the ongoing school year.
* **Historical Reasoning:** The majority allowed that reducing racial isolation could be compelling under the historical scrutiny framework and required further examination of tailoring. Parker questioned the available remedy and program efficacy.
* **Authority Caution:** Adarand Constructors, Inc. v. Peña and Eisenberg v. Montgomery County Public Schools govern the applicable equal-protection standard.

* **Historical Sources:** [Brewer v. West Irondequoit Central School District](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/212/738/632474/). This material is quarantined comparison material and has no force as simulated precedent.

# Urofsky v. Gilmore

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Comparator Type:** Lower-court merits decision; no corresponding historical Supreme Court merits decision is identified.
* **Historical Outcome and Structure:** The en banc Fourth Circuit reversed. Wilkins wrote for himself and Widener, Niemeyer, Luttig, Williams, Traxler and Hamilton. Luttig and Hamilton concurred separately; Wilkinson concurred in the judgment. Murnaghan dissented with Michael, Motz and King.
* **Historical Reasoning:** The majority treated the regulated activity as employee speech and rejected the asserted individual academic-freedom right. Wilkinson preferred a narrower approach emphasizing the amended law and approval process.
* **Authority Caution:** The lower-court opinion is the case under review, not a controlling Supreme Court holding. Its categorical employee-speech formulation cannot override the limits of Boring v. Buncombe County Board of Education.

* **Historical Sources:** [Urofsky v. Gilmore](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/216/401/570332/). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
