* **Historical Anchor:** The First Circuit upheld the challenged provisions in its opinion filed March 7, 2000, following an earlier announcement of disposition. The assigned simulation date is December 11, 2000.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Coffin wrote the panel opinion, joined by Judges Selya and Campbell.
* **Historical Holding Below:** The court sustained the contribution ceiling, treated responsive subsidies as additional speech rather than a restriction on opponents, and rejected the coercion challenge to voluntary public financing.
