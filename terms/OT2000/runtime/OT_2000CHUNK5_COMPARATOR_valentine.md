# United States v. Valentine

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

**Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator is identified. The Third Circuit reversed suppression on November 2, 2000; no separate opinion appears in the published decision.

**Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Cowen wrote for a panel with Judges Nygaard and Greenberg.

**Historical Holding:** The court found reasonable suspicion before the command and separately concluded that Valentine had not submitted to police authority before the later conduct. Both grounds supported reversal of suppression.

**Historical Authority:** This is the historical lower-court anchor. Its account of Illinois v. Wardlow does not displace the narrower governing holding described in Section I.
