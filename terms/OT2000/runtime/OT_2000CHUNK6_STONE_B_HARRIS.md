# Harris v. Garner

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Controlling precedent, subject to intervening decisions effective before May 29, 2001.
* **Conditions:** Apply intervening controlling decisions and distinguish the statutory remedy restriction from the constitutional merits.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the en banc judgment on the accepted temporal question.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Preserve dismissal without prejudice for the released plaintiffs' claims barred solely by the provision. Do not direct dismissal of requests outside its coverage or declare any new post-release action timely.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Filing-time coverage

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Decide the filing-status question and the effect of a supplement merely recording release. No alternative constitutional ruling is needed to decide that question.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether later release changes the statutory status of an action brought by a confined prisoner for covered injury.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the preserved statutory text and resolve the temporal issue through a new holding.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Confinement when the federal civil action is brought determines the application of this physical-injury restriction to covered mental or emotional injury claims. Subsequent release and a pleading noting that release do not cure the original action's covered deficiency. Dismissal resting solely on that restriction must not operate as an adjudication of the underlying misconduct or permanently bar a permissible post-release action.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The rule concerns this action and this type of injury. It does not make every monetary claim in a prison case subject to a single undifferentiated bar.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The operative language selects commencement. The definition identifies who is a prisoner; it does not move the selected time to judgment. Supplemental pleading can establish the new event, but it cannot change the legal consequence Congress attached to the earlier one. These six plaintiffs were confined at commencement. The lower court's correction from prejudicial to nonprejudicial dismissal respects both that trigger and the limited nature of the restriction.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The separate treatment of nominal, punitive, prospective or independent economic relief is outside the question presented. Limitations and preclusion in a new post-release action require claims and procedural facts not presented here.
