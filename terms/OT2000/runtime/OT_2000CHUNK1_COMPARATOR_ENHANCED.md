# Wasson v. Sonoma County Junior College

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** The Ninth Circuit affirmed on February 16, 2000. This lower-court ruling is the historical anchor for the organizationally assigned November 27, 2000 merits event.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Schroeder wrote for herself and Judge Hall. Judge B. Fletcher dissented.
* **Historical Holding Below:** The majority required the plaintiff’s own protected expression and rejected the mistaken-attribution claim; the dissent regarded the threatened punishment for anonymous criticism as a First Amendment injury despite her denial.
* **Historical Authority:** The lower-court decision establishes the judgment under review. No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified.

# City of Indianapolis v. Edmond

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote for Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Rehnquist dissented with Thomas and, as to Part I, Scalia; Thomas also dissented separately.
* **Historical Holding:** Suspicionless checkpoints principally directed at ordinary crime control violated the Fourth Amendment; the primary-purpose inquiry was programmatic.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 32, 2000-11-28. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [City of Indianapolis v. Edmond](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-32.pdf).

# Green Tree Financial Corp.-Alabama v. Randolph

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed as to finality unanimously; reversed the cost-based invalidation, 5-4, and remanded.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote. Part II was unanimous; Parts I and III were joined by O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Ginsburg wrote separately with Stevens and Souter, and with Breyer for Parts I and III.
* **Historical Holding:** The completed dismissal was appealable. On the historical record, silence and speculative cost risk did not satisfy the challenger's burden to show likely prohibitive expense.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 79, 2000-12-11. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Green Tree Financial Corp.-Alabama v. Randolph](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-79.pdf).
