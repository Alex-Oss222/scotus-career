# Seling v. Young

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8-1 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote for Rehnquist, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Scalia, joined by Souter, concurred; Thomas concurred in the judgment; Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court rejected an individual as-applied punitive reclassification for Double Jeopardy and Ex Post Facto purposes once the Act was treated as civil. That historical premise differs materially from controlling law.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 250, 2001-01-17. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Seling v. Young](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-250.pdf).
