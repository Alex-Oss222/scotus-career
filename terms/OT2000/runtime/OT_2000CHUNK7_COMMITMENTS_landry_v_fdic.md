# Landry v. Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation - frozen non-Stone commitments

**Event:** Merits, June 15, 2001; judgment below 204 F.3d 1125, No. 99-1230.
**Stage:** Independent modeling before comparator and private Stone material; no assignment.
**Inputs:** Frozen C neutral handoff and source receipt; C entering-law slice, including Edmond, both Ryder components and Hilario; full predecision lower opinion, including its separate opinion; Freytag's date-eligible office-status discussion and Scalia concurrence. The underlying administrative record and original appointment instrument were not independently recovered.
**Research cutoff:** June 15, 2001. No later law or same-matter Supreme Court result was consulted. Mead's later event supplies no authority.

## Reachability and separable components

All eight Associates participate. This is direct review following a timely appointment objection, on the granted officer-status and Board-review questions. It is not a collateral attack or a review of every banking or disclosure issue. The agency's abandoned department-head appointment defense cannot be re-created through inference; assignment through an administrative office is not itself a constitutionally sufficient appointment.

The first component concerns a continuing statutory hearing office exercising substantial discretion over testimony, evidence, discovery and recommendations. The second concerns whether independent Board review of the existing record supplies a lawful substitute for the hearing those powers produced. Independent review is genuine and important; it must not be confused with a new evidentiary hearing.

## Competing paths and governing limits

**Employee path.** The strongest defense stresses that the ALJ cannot enter any final FDIC order and the Board independently finds facts. That limits the ALJ's authority, but does not make the office ministerial. Freytag's significant-hearing-duty ground precedes its independently sufficient final-decision discussion. Edmond and Hilario separately examine subordination to distinguish inferior from principal offices; they do not make all subordinate adjudicators employees. The continuing statutory office and discretionary hearing powers establish officer status here. Ordinary clerical assistance, temporary expert advice and every agency employee remain outside the holding.

**Officer with cure path.** The strongest cure argument combines the Board's de novo duty, its actual reconsideration and reopened disclosure questions, the APA prejudicial-error direction, and powerful evidence from Landry's own statements. This differs materially from the limited higher review rejected in Ryder. Ryder leaves other review structures open, so rejection of cure here is an application extending its remedial reasoning, not a claim that it already resolved every harmless-error question.

**Officer with fresh hearing path.** The ALJ exercised constitutionally significant powers in creating the very record used on review. The Board's freedom to reconsider findings is not proof that the hearing's evidence and procedural choices were recreated by a lawfully appointed adjudicator. Requiring the timely challenger to establish that a hypothetical lawful hearing would change the outcome would ordinarily make the appointment requirement unenforceable in these recommendatory offices. The remedy corrects the defective adjudication rather than deciding guilt or rewarding an untimely objection. The APA does not supply a contrary instruction to validate this timely challenged constitutional hearing defect merely because the final reviewer found the existing evidence persuasive.

## Proposition-level commitments and circulation limits

All eight Associates directly support the significant-hearing-duty classification, rejection of an indispensable-final-decision requirement, treatment of the abandoned alternative appointment defense, and refusal to treat this existing-record review as cure. All eight support vacating the judgment sustaining the challenged order and remanding for a new hearing before a validly appointed adjudicator followed by a fresh Board decision. The remedy reaches this timely challenger and this proceeding.

Souter requires the fuller duties, jurisdiction, tenure and supervision explanation of inferior status. The other seven can join that office-specific explanation if it is presented as the actual structure here, without announcing that every office must satisfy an exclusive Morrison checklist. No new join is implied by this recorded compatibility boundary.

The order does not exonerate Landry, dismiss enforcement charges, adjudicate the privilege or statutory-misconduct merits, reopen other final orders, or decide the validity of the banking agencies as appointing departments. It does not require a specially named replacement individual where a lawfully constituted adjudicator can supply the new hearing. A genuinely new evidentiary adjudication by a lawful decisionmaker remains analytically different from review of the challenged record.

## Source limits and readiness

The full lower opinion confirms the preserved cure contention, the agency's abandonment, the actual record use, and the Board's independent powers. Those facts are sufficient for the bounded questions. No premise that every hearing choice affected the outcome, or that the admissions were false, is required. Freytag was examined for its actual alternative grounds; the current controlling Ryder and Edmond formulations, rather than historical quotations of them in the lower opinion, govern. No material neutral blocker remains.
