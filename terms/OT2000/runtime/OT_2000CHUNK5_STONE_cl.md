# OT_2000CHUNK5

# C & L Enterprises, Inc. v. Citizen Band Potawatomi Indian Tribe of Oklahoma

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Approval Status:** Approved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** Precedent effective through June 29, 2000, subject to any intervening decision effective before the stated event date.
* **Conditions:** Subject to intervening controlling precedent and material corrections to the record.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Oklahoma Court of Civil Appeals’ immunity judgment and remand for further proceedings on confirmation consistent with the agreement’s clear and limited waiver.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Remove sovereign immunity as a bar to the contemplated state-court confirmation proceeding. Do not adjudicate an unpresented award defense, expand the award, or authorize attachment of unspecified tribal assets. The ordinary confirmation process governs any remaining properly preserved issue.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Enforce the actual procedural undertaking

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Resolve the waiver question before the contract merits. The location and commercial purpose explain the background but cannot independently answer immunity.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does express agreement to judicial confirmation waive immunity from that identified proceeding?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Kiowa Tribe of Oklahoma v. Manufacturing Technologies, Inc.’s existing clear-waiver principle to a materially different contract. The construction of this combined clause supplies a new application, not an adopted commercial-activity exception or general abrogation.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The Tribe clearly consented to the state-court proceeding contemplated by its agreement to binding arbitration and judicial entry of judgment under the applicable Oklahoma enforcement regime.
* **Reach Within the Question:** The holding covers this agreement’s confirmation undertaking. It requires actual agreement to judicial enforcement and does not make every arbitration provision, generic law-selection clause or commercial contract a waiver.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Clarity concerns the commitment’s legal content, not a mandatory verbal formula. Here the agreement links an arbitral determination with a court judgment under an identified enforcement regime. Reading that undertaking as allowing arbitration while barring the promised confirmation deprives express judicial language of its practical operation. The Tribe’s own tender of the form supports actual assent but is not an independent waiver by conduct. Sovereign status remains fully relevant to claims and remedies beyond the undertaking. This approach respects both the governing immunity rule and the Tribe’s authority to choose a bounded adjudicative forum.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve immunity from execution against particular assets and liability for unrelated claims because neither is within the question presented or established by the record of this confirmation dispute.
