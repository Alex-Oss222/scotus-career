## Alternatives, remedy and limits

The historical full opinions confirm both live paths were fairly tested. The seven see an existing legal limit whose application cannot become unrestricted trial discretion. They preserve supported historical findings, including culpability and harm; independent review permits no appellate invention of facts. Ginsburg's contrary path emphasizes that the jury's moral appraisal depends on facts and that abuse review already corrects legal error. Her position remains a genuine disagreement about application and institutional allocation, not rejection of every constitutional limit.

The vacatur sends the punitive appellate judgment back for the required review; it chooses no sum, fixed ratio, remittitur or new trial and does not hold $4.5 million unconstitutional. Compensation and the separate functionality judgment remain undisturbed. Lawful useful copying cannot be treated as wrongful advertising; supported actual or potential harm and the wrong adjudicated govern, while wealth cannot replace the necessary relationship. These are existing-law boundaries, not an additional final guidepost adjudication outside the limited question. Oregon's receipt of part of the award is outside that question. No new rule for a materially different legislatively fixed multiple or exact deterrence scheme is decided.
