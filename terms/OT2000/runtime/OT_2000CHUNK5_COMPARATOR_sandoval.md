# Alexander v. Sandoval

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5-4, April 24, 2001.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Kennedy and Thomas. Justice Stevens dissented with Justices Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** No private right of action exists to enforce the disparate-impact regulations promulgated under §602. The validity of those regulations was assumed.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical majority's treatment of private actions does not displace the governing holding of Central Bank of Denver, N.A. v. First Interstate Bank of Denver, N.A.

* **Historical Sources:** [Alexander v. Sandoval](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-275.pdf). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
