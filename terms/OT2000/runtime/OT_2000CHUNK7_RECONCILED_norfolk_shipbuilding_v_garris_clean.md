## Operative law and remedy

The general maritime death action extends to negligent breach of an established maritime duty. Moragne's unseaworthiness facts left this precise application technically open, but its remedial reason does not permit treating the same negligent breach as actionable only when the victim survives. The Jones Act's seaman field does not include this nonseaman. DOHSA does not occupy this territorial-water death. The LHWCA's employer and vessel provisions are distinct from its preservation of claims against this third party, which neither employed Garris nor owned the vessel; its abolition of covered unseaworthiness liability does not repeal negligence claims preserved by the statute.
