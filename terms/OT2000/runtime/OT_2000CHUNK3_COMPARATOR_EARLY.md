# Board of Trustees of the University of Alabama v. Garrett

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5 to 4, on a materially different historical immunity premise.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist wrote for Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Justice Kennedy concurred, joined by Justice O’Connor. Justice Breyer dissented, joined by Justices Stevens, Souter, and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held that Title I private damages actions against nonconsenting States were barred because Congress had not validly abrogated immunity under Section 5. That result and the historical Article I restriction are not the entering simulation law.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 356, 2001-02-21. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
* **Historical Sources:** [Board of Trustees of the University of Alabama v. Garrett](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-531/pdf/USREPORTS-531-356.pdf).

# Buckman Co. v. Plaintiffs' Legal Committee

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

This section is comparison material only. It is not simulated law and does not control Stone.

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed unanimously in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist wrote for Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice Stevens concurred in the judgment, joined by Justice Thomas.

# Lewis v. Lewis & Clark Marine, Inc.

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O’Connor delivered the opinion for all nine Justices.

# United States v. Tank

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits disposition is identified for this matter. The historical anchor is the Ninth Circuit’s January 4, 2000 judgment affirming the convictions and remanding for resentencing.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Pregerson wrote for the panel of Judges Pregerson and Wiggins and District Judge Carter, sitting by designation; no separate opinion is reported.
* **Historical Holding:** The panel found sufficient authentication through Riva’s testimony and corroborating attribution. It also upheld the automobile search and rejected the separately reviewed sufficiency challenge. It required resentencing because exclusion from grouping under one subsection did not forbid grouping under the others and because the leadership adjustment and numerical calculation required reconsideration.
* **Historical Authority:** This is the lower-court anchor for the listed merits matter. It establishes neither a historical Supreme Court ruling on electronic evidence nor a Supreme Court disposition in the present matter.

* **Historical Sources:** [United States v. Tank](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/200/627/521509/). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
