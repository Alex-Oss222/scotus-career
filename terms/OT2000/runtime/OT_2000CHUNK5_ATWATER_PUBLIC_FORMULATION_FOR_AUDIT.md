# Atwater: proposed public formulation for reconciliation audit

This proposed public formulation is not a decision or an assignment. Test it against the frozen neutral refresh, refreshed commitments, comparator and refreshed reconciliation.

The proposed operative disposition is vacatur of defendants' categorical custody summary judgment and remand under the actual-enforcement rule. The proposed Court rule extends Ricci to added station-house transport and confinement for a known fine-only criminal offense: probable cause alone does not justify that added restraint; it must reasonably serve an actual enforcement function, without a least-intrusive-means or universal-citation requirement. The supported identification information and local familiarity must be considered with the missing physical documents. Appearance, safety and processing grounds must rest on the actual record rather than hypothetical facts.

A proposed five-Justice rule portion is distinct from a four-Justice application portion retaining Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer's complete conclusion that the supported plaintiff-favorable facts do not establish a sufficient enforcement function for this custody. Their conclusion remains express and does not become an indefinite reservation. The application portion would have only those four joins; the remaining rule supporter would leave actual verification and custody-need determinations for the lower courts under the governing rule. No majority conclusion would hold this custody either constitutional or unconstitutional on the accepted account.

The question for audit is whether the four Associates can support the common formal vacatur and return while maintaining their complete application position and their preference for reversal. Their substantive conclusion must not be diluted into judgment-only agreement on the rule or agreement that the same established facts might suffice. The actual mandate would correct the categorical rule and return the case for lawful consideration of genuinely supported record disputes and preserved defenses; it would require no new evidentiary hearing, invent no justification, reopen no established fact by speculation, and award no damages. Personal immunity remains governed by March 1997 law, and municipal policy, fault, causation and injury remain separate.

If that common formal disposition is not supported by their own recorded grounds, report the precise barrier. Do not infer a judgment majority from opposition to the existing judgment alone. Any revised disposition commitment needs a Justice-specific explanation that preserves the application and source boundaries. No author, balance count or assignment is proposed.
