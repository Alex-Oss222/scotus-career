## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8 to 1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, and Ginsburg. Justice Souter also concurred. Justice Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court sustained the short, carefully limited seizure based on probable cause, a destruction risk, privacy accommodations, and diligence in obtaining a warrant.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 326, 2001-02-20. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
