Montana governs nonmember guest occupancy transactions at this nonmember hotel on non-Indian fee land within the reservation. No identified treaty or statute specifically authorizes the asserted tax. Merrion's sovereign-taxing-power principle is not merely a private owner's exclusion right, but its tribal-land lease relationship does not establish unlimited power over these fee-land transactions. The second exception retains authority over nonmember conduct threatening or directly affecting tribal political integrity, economic security, health or welfare. Ordinary revenue needs, service provision and business presence do not by themselves supply that qualifying tribal effect for this tax. Their consequences are real but insufficient; the rule adopts no new quantified threshold, does not reduce every limb of the exception to one verbal formula, and does not decide a different demonstrated governmental threat. The Court does not invalidate taxes on members, qualifying tribal leases or transactions, or power supplied by superior federal authorization.

Substantive taxing authority and amenability to suit remain distinct. Under Strate, appropriate conforming relief must fit actual claims, lawful parties, immunity and ordinary equity; reversal does not automatically establish a refund claim or every injunction predicate. C & L enforces a particular clear contractual waiver, not a waiver inferred from location, commerce or services. Its integrated undertaking is absent here.
