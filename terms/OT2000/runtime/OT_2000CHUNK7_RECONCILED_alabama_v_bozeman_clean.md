## Proposition-level compatibility and explanation

All eight Associates adopt the text-based ground: return before trial on the covered charges requires dismissal with prejudice, without a short-trip, arraignment, technical-violation or harmlessness exception. Article IX's construction direction does not delete Article IV(e)'s explicit trigger and consequence. Anderson v. Yungkau supplies the ordinary command conveyed by shall; Montalvo-Murillo involved a statute without an express remedy; the background triviality principle discussed in Wrigley yields to contrary enacted language. Hill preserves a real, right-specific waiver; Reed's collateral-defect threshold remains in its separate channel.

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer also support the independent alternative explanation. Even assuming for argument a purpose-based triviality exception, the violation cannot be called trivial on the theory that a return improves access to rehabilitation: that theory would excuse virtually every early return. Keeping the receiving State responsible for confinement costs can encourage prompt disposition, and avoiding additional shuttles can reduce uncertainty. Those are plausible explanations of the actual provision, not findings of the drafters' precise subjective purpose or a free-standing rehabilitation test. Scalia and Thomas do not join that alternative. If the eventual opinion uses only the common textual ground, all eight may join it; inclusion of the alternative must preserve the two withheld joins.
