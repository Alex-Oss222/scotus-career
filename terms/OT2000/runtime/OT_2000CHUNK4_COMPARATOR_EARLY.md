# Ferguson v. City of Charleston

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6-3, on March 21, 2001.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for O’Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Kennedy concurred in the judgment. Scalia dissented; Rehnquist and Thomas joined Part II of his dissent.
* **Historical Holding:** The nonconsensual searches did not qualify for the special-needs exception because the immediate programmatic objective was obtaining evidence for law enforcement. The consent issue remained for remand.
* **Historical Authority:** Kennedy’s separate reasoning and the five-Justice opinion must not be collapsed into identical propositions.

* **Historical Sources:** [Ferguson v. City of Charleston](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-67.pdf). This material is quarantined comparison material and has no force as simulated precedent.
