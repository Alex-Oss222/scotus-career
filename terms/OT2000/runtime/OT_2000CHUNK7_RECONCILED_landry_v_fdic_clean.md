## Verified comparator and controlling distinctions

No historical Supreme Court merits comparator applies. Williams and Edwards treated the FDIC ALJ as an employee; Randolph classified him as an inferior officer but would sustain the order as harmless after actual de novo Board review. All three denied the petition. Neither a historical denial of review nor later appointments decisions establish merits authority here.

The lower opinion confirms substantial, continuing statutory hearing duties, actual independent Board review, and the use of the ALJ-created record. The Board reopened disclosure questions, rejected some privilege assertions and used its own authority; the current decision may not pretend the review was nominal. It nevertheless did not conduct a replacement evidentiary hearing before a lawfully appointed adjudicator. The agency expressly abandoned its alternative department-head appointment defense. No internal appointment instrument is invented to restore that defense.

Freytag's significant-duty analysis is a sufficient ground distinct from its alternative final-decision ground. Lack of final agency order power does not make this statutory hearing officer ministerial. Edmond and Hilario distinguish superior control and appointment from administrative assignment without reducing every subordinate officer to an employee. Ryder guarantees meaningful relief to a timely direct challenger and rejects its particular insufficient higher review; it reserves other cure and harmless-error settings. The present cure question therefore requires decision rather than quotation of an already categorical rule.

## Cure analysis and exact coalition

All eight Associates support both propositions and the fresh-hearing disposition. The ALJ's continued statutory position and discretionary testimony, evidence, discovery and hearing functions constitute significant public authority despite reserved Board finality. The litigated appointment defense cannot supply a lawful appointment once expressly abandoned. This does not decide whether the banking agencies can qualify as constitutional departments in another properly presented case.

The strongest opposing remedy is Randolph's: real de novo review, reconsidered disclosure questions, the APA's prejudicial-error instruction and powerful admissions make a new hearing seem unlikely to affect the final result. The eight Associates reject that outcome-likelihood substitution here. The same challenged officer created the record and controlled the evidentiary proceeding. A timely challenger need not prove how a hypothetical lawful hearing would differ before receiving one. A declaration coupled with preserving this order would withhold the lawful adjudication sought by this timely challenger; possible future mandamus does not supply it. The holding does not state that every structural error automatically defeats harmless-error review or that a new lawful evidentiary adjudication could never cure a defect.

Souter's fuller classification explanation is compatible with the other seven when confined to the actual combination of this office's duties and hierarchy. The other seven do not agree that every office must satisfy an exclusive checklist. If an opinion instead adopts a review-alone criterion, Souter's refusal must be preserved; no such criterion is needed to sustain these commitments.

**Historical reconciliation:** There is no Supreme Court merits alignment from which the eight Associates depart. The contrary lower result is confronted on both grounds, not ignored. The fresh-hearing rule is a supported application and extension of Ryder, not a claim that Ryder already foreclosed every independent-review cure.

## Remedy and limits

Vacate the judgment sustaining Landry's challenged removal/prohibition order and remand for a new hearing before a validly appointed adjudicator followed by a fresh Board decision. This sets aside the challenged adjudication without dismissing enforcement charges or exonerating Landry. No automatic damages, unrelated disclosure holding, reopening of another person's final order, or adjudication of all banking merits follows. A specially named replacement individual is not required by these commitments; a lawfully constituted adjudicator must supply the replacement hearing. No material source blocker remains for the two granted questions.
