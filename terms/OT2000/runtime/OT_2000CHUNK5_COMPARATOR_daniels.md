# OT_2000CHUNK5

# Daniels v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote; Rehnquist, Kennedy and Thomas joined, and Scalia joined except the discussion recognizing possible rare circumstances for § 2255 review. Scalia concurred in part. Souter dissented with Stevens and Ginsburg; Breyer separately dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Available but unpursued challenges to completed predicates generally could not be revived through § 2255; the complete-denial-of-counsel exception remained.
* **Historical Authority:** The possible faultless-unavailability exception was not adopted as a holding. The historical vote does not determine the alignment of the Court in the present matter.

* **Historical Sources:** [Daniels v. United States](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-532/pdf/USREPORTS-532-374.pdf). The historical disposition is comparison material only and does not govern this case.
