## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded in an unsigned per curiam opinion without noted dissent.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam following the Pennsylvania certified answer; no separate writings.
* **Historical Holding:** The conviction violated due process because the State had not proved the preexisting lack-of-permit element; no retroactivity issue remained.
* **Historical Authority:** 531 U.S. 225, 2001-01-09. This is the historical Supreme Court event, not a simulation holding.
