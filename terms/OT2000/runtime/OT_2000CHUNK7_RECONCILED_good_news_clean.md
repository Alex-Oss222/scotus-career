## Components and fixed record boundaries

**A — Actual exclusion:** Whether this limited community forum may exclude the requested integrated program of prayer, biblical instruction, songs, games and invitation to Christian adherence. Its moral teaching does not make its devotion disappear. The policy admits educational, social, civic, recreational and community uses; the superintendent permits moral teaching through Aesop and distinguishes religious history or moral discussion from instruction promoting the gospel. The Aesop answer defines admitted scope; it does not establish an actual Aesop meeting.

**B — Establishment defense:** Whether the supported operational circumstances establish or permit adjudication of governmental religious instruction, adoption, solicitation or independently sufficient school-created pressure. Agostini's governing Lemon inquiry concerns actual governmental function and attribution. Purpose, effects and entanglement remain evidence, not independent former prongs. Neither neutral availability nor parental permission immunizes official worship. Conversely, a child's possible misunderstanding, location or religious intensity does not alone identify governmental religion.

**C — Judgment and remedy:** Affirmance on A, reversal with B resolved and a relief remand, or vacatur with a bounded B adjudication are distinct positions. The district's October 23, 1998 order granted Milford summary judgment, denied the Club's cross-motion and vacated the injunction. The actual docket controls the date rather than the historical opinion's earlier date. Cross-motions do not establish universal factual agreement. Removal from the trial calendar pending their resolution is not a universal trial waiver.

The four-minute transition from dismissal at 2:56 to the 3:00 meeting, elementary ages, permission practices, continuing school activities, assigned resource room and occasional school audiovisual use remain in their supported and disputed forms. No teacher escort, official recruitment, governmental selection of devotional content, precise attendance growth or causal enrollment effect is found. The earlier attendance estimate was uncertain; the contrary counts are advocacy tied to cited material. No Justice's position depends on converting them into established facts. No new neutral proposition is needed for this reconciliation.

## Historical evidence and the changed legal premises

Historically Thomas's opinion rejected both the viewpoint exclusion and the asserted establishment defense. O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy joined that opinion. Scalia's additional explanation rejected a judicially manageable worship/proselytizing distinction in private expression and would not use endorsement or coercion analysis to suppress purely private speech. His opinion also disputed whether the reasonableness issue had been conceded. That preservation disagreement is not silently decided here.

Breyer agreed that Milford's judgment could not stand but expressly withheld a conclusive establishment determination. His separate opinion treated a reasonable child's perception of school endorsement as potentially decisive, considered neutrality only one factor and insisted that denial of Milford's motion did not establish entitlement to the Club's cross-motion. Stevens would affirm on a distinction between discussing an admitted subject from a religious viewpoint and an activity principally directed to religious inculcation or recruitment. Souter, joined by Ginsburg, would affirm because the actual evangelical service was a different activity; alternatively he objected to first-instance establishment resolution on the record. Each writing and its footnotes has been considered, rather than inferring positions from the historical judgment count.

The specific intervening public decisions are [Ladue, December 11, 1995](../../OT1995/output/OT_1995CHUNK2.md#good-newsgood-sports-club-v-school-district-of-ladue-eighth-circuit-no-93-2148), [Bronx Household, May 11, 1998](../../OT1997/output/OT_1997CHUNK8.md#bronx-household-of-faith-v-community-school-district-no-10-second-circuit-no-96-9633-127-f3d-207), [Agostini, June 23, 1997](../../OT1996/output/OT_1996CHUNK11.md#agostini-v-felton-and-chancellor-board-of-education-of-the-city-of-new-york-v-felton-nos-96-552-and-96-553), and [Rosenberger, June 29, 1995](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK8.md#rosenberger-v-rector-and-visitors-of-the-university-of-virginia-no-94-329). The named entries' relevant holdings, topology and separate writings were checked. Marshfield's current-term public holding and separate position are supplied by the frozen neutral projection.

Ladue is particularly concrete for Souter and Ginsburg: Souter wrote, and Ginsburg joined, the rule and application that prayer and Bible lessons do not remove this form of private moral instruction from an admitted after-school subject. Their later Bronx joins again protect admitted religious instruction, while their partial dissent preserves an objection to a distinct integrated church service. These are their own prior adjudicated choices about the relevant comparison, not a general preference for expression. Milford's younger audience and different attribution evidence affect B; they do not supply an identified difference in the subject comparison that defeats those prior A joins.

Stevens stands differently. He dissented in Ladue on the actual-activity comparison and joined Bronx's integrated-service partial dissent. His Bronx instruction join protects instruction within the actual admitted subject; it does not erase his distinction between discussion and an organized program principally soliciting religious adherence. The frozen commitment treated the Aesop hypothetical, broad policy and religious reason as sufficient to move him across that distinction. His actual Good News dissent considered the same distinction and maintained it. The final neutral packet still preserves the devotional and evangelical program and the superintendent's distinction between discussion and gospel instruction. No case-specific changed premise establishes that Stevens abandoned his activity distinction. His provisional A and first-choice C votes therefore require correction. This does not treat him as free to disregard Bronx: he distinguishes the current integrated elementary program and requested immediate continuation of the school day from the comparable private Sunday meeting covered there, preserves Bronx's instruction rule, and does not purport to overrule its holding.

Agostini changes Breyer's establishment inquiry in a material way. The historical concurrence's independently important reasonable-child endorsement question cannot simply be restored as a separate legal test. Breyer's own subsequent Bronx opinion applied the governing governmental-function inquiry and rejected attribution merely from private worship's school location. His Ladue join likewise approved actual private instructional access; his Agostini dissent joined the precedent and procedural objections, not Souter's affirmative substantive direct-aid objection. His Marshfield join preserves scrutiny of an actual continuing governmental presentation. Together these support his retained conclusion that Milford's stated administrative circumstances, even considered in their adverse supported form, do not supply such a governmental function. His historical Rule 56 distinction remains valid; what changes is the legal materiality of the perception inquiry, not ordinary procedure or the facts being presumed for the Club.

## Compatible propositions and actual disagreement

Seven Associates reject A: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Stevens would affirm on A. The seven can share the bounded admitted-subject rule without pretending that Souter and Ginsburg abandon every integrated-service distinction. They need not adopt Scalia's broader claim that a worship distinction is always unintelligible or resolve an absent pure-worship request divorced from the forum's admitted subjects.

Five Associates — O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer — resolve B against Milford and favor reversal with relief determined below. Their compatible ground is that the actual asserted use arrangements amount to administration of private access, rather than identified governmental religious instruction, adoption or substantial government-created pressure. They can join a common opinion expressed at that scope. Potential objections to a hypothetical broad opinion do not create a current fracture.

Souter and Ginsburg withhold the conclusive B determination and favor vacatur with adjudication of the supported attribution/function question below. Stevens shares that remand only if the Court rejects his first-choice A affirmance. His conditional position is not a third first-choice vacatur vote. Those three do not find establishment established, and they do not demand an evidentiary hearing merely because the audience is young. Under their alternative, the lower court first tests the existing submissions under ordinary summary-judgment rules. If they identify only neutral access, the defense fails without further fact gathering; if a genuinely material dispute about the identified governmental function remains, ordinary lawful proceedings may resolve it. No new trial or discovery period is commanded.

All positions preserve neutral scheduling, supervision, safety, capacity and genuine activity limits. No final access permit, fixed injunction, permanent forum opening, resource entitlement, damages, fees or disclaimer text is supplied. The private group need not remove its prayer or invitation to adherence to obtain the seven's A protection. No financial-support holding, universal religious-facility entitlement or replacement of Lee's independent pressure protection follows.

## Departure accounting and assembly boundary

**Historical departure:** Souter and Ginsburg reject the actual speech exclusion because their own intervening Ladue opinion and join already protect this integrated prayer-and-Bible form of moral instruction within an admitted after-school subject, and their later Bronx instruction joins confirm that specific comparison. Their different establishment/remedy position remains distinct from that departure.

**Historical departure:** Breyer resolves the asserted establishment defense rather than retaining his historical endorsement-based factual reservation because the intervening Agostini rule makes actual governmental function decisive and his own subsequent Bronx opinion applies that rule to private school-space worship. His legal sufficiency conclusion preserves ordinary summary-judgment safeguards and actual official-conduct objections.

Stevens's provisional rejection of A is corrected; no unsupported historical departure is retained merely because a general instruction rule or another Justice's earlier majority could favor access. His conditional B reservation is retained separately. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas have no material disposition departure at the stated bounded scope. Changes in the governing articulation are identified rather than disguised as wholesale adoption of the historical opinion.
