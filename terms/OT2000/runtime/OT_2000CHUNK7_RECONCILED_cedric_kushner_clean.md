## Rule and remedy limits for assembly

Section 1962(c) requires a person and an enterprise that are not merely the same person under different names. Incorporation supplies the necessary distinction here; no additional functional independence or unauthorized-action condition applies. This resolves only that element. Reves's participation-in-direction rule, predicates, pattern, injury, Holmes causation and properly available defenses remain. Corporate defendants paired with an association including themselves, affiliated corporations, sole proprietorships and corporate vicarious liability are not decided. No finding of racketeering, damages, fees or entitlement to final judgment follows.
