His position does not require treating an untimely or misdirected application as proper. The warden's policy concern must be tied to the language Congress enacted.

**Filing-condition construction:** Affirm on the question reviewed; an accepted application complying with rules governing its filing tolls even if individual claims cannot succeed. The rule reaches claim-specific bars, not every timeliness, fee, or jurisdictional restriction. **Relief-eligibility construction:** Reverse only if these particular New York bars constitute conditions on proper filing; do not automatically resolve other independent grounds supporting timeliness. **Uncertainty:** The appellate assumption concerning the written denial is not a Supreme Court finding. # Cleveland v. United States

# SECTION I: NEUTRAL MODELING PACKET

## A. CASE, RECORD, AND POSTURE

* **Case Name:** Cleveland v. United States
* **Citation or Docket:** No. 99-804; 531 U.S. 12
* **October Term:** 2000
* **Entry Stage and Event:** Merits submission for decision; the historical decision date supplies scheduling only.
* **Simulated Event Date:** 2000-11-07 (historical).

### Issue 1: Government licensing and property fraud

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Neder v. United States makes materiality an element of charged financial-deception schemes; material deception does not eliminate the separate money-or-property element. No controlling decision converts regulatory authority into property.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The financial-deception rule keeps materiality, mailing, and the elements of the underlying scheme separate.
* **Other Governing Law:** 18 U.S.C. §§1341, 1346, 1957 and 1962; Louisiana's licensing provisions.
* **Material Other Authority:** McNally v. United States and Carpenter v. United States supply the received money-or-property distinction; they do not resolve this licensing application.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** Federal fraud reaches property schemes, including intangible property under Carpenter v. United States. McNally v. United States preserves a distinction between property and an asserted right to honest governmental administration.
* **Open Point:** Whether the State's power to issue, withhold, renew, and revoke a license is property rather than regulatory authority.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The lower court treated the State's control over issuance and the license's value as sufficient property. The United States stresses exclusivity, allocative control, revenue, and analogies to patents or franchises. Cleveland responds that these are sovereign permissions, not assets the State owns and transfers, and that no unpaid revenue is charged. A rule excluding regulatory permission need not exclude fraudulent acquisition of government money, land, or an actual commercial entitlement. A contrary rule must explain why it does not federalize every dishonest licensing application.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**Regulatory-permission construction:** Reverse the property-fraud ground and remand for consequences on the linked convictions. The holding can cover comparable licenses held only in a sovereign regulatory capacity without deciding every government franchise. **Government-property construction:** Affirm if the identified interest genuinely constitutes property before issuance; materiality and the remaining elements remain required. **Scope and uncertainty:** Licensee property, licenses transferred as commercial assets, and independently charged revenue fraud are different questions. The record does not support treating fees or taxes as missing property merely to save this prosecution.

### Issue 1: Personal stake and design-only claims

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Johnson v. Miller and Bush v. Vera separate personal assignment and standing from ultimate success. Shaw v. Reno, Johnson v. Miller and Bush v. Vera reject territorial racial design alone as the asserted substantive deprivation.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The territorial-design rule requires an independently cognizable constitutional wrong for merits relief; shape is evidence, not a sufficient wrong. Stage-appropriate personal injury, causation and redressability govern standing independently.
* **Other Governing Law:** Article III; Equal Protection Clause; 28 U.S.C. §§1253 and 2284; 42 U.S.C. §1983.
* **Material Other Authority:** No additional authority is necessary beyond the sources identified here.
* **Existing Rule's Reach:** Actual personal exposure can establish standing even when the constitutional theory loses. Neither living near another district nor the unsupported inference that every neighboring assignment was racial necessarily establishes that exposure.
* **Open Point:** Whether these plaintiffs proved the necessary personal relationship to the challenged conduct. If they did, the design-only merits theory is already foreclosed absent a supported distinct deprivation.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

The district court inferred unconstitutional racial design in the seven districts and ordered relief. Appellants challenge personal standing and the legal inference from surrounding racial design. Appellees say their own districts' shapes and the statewide plan demonstrate personal sorting. Under simulated law, appellants also have the independent merits defense that predominant racial design alone is insufficient. Appellees cannot obtain an injunction merely by replacing a rejected design-only theory with an unsupported assertion of status injury; a genuine preserved participation or dilution claim would require its own proof.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

**No personal injury:** Vacate and direct jurisdictional dismissal, without a merits judgment. **Standing established:** Reject the design-only merits theory under controlling precedent and dissolve the dependent injunction; do not label this a standing failure. **Materially different preserved injury:** Adjudicate that claim under its own standard, with a bounded remand only if facts actually require it. The first two routes establish different preclusion consequences. Neither creates a right to maximize minority districts or certifies every aspect of the map. The record does not establish an independent injury sufficient to support the third route.

### Issue 1: Retaliation for attributed protected expression

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The issue determines the challenged judgment and is properly presented on this record.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** May an employee challenge retaliation for protected speech attributed to her although she denies having made it?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Extend the First Amendment prohibition of retaliation to perceived protected expression. Waters does not already decide mistaken speaker identity; its limited genuine-and-reasonable account safe harbor remains intact.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Yes. A public employer violates an employee’s First Amendment right when attributed expression that is protected under the governing public-employment rules substantially motivates an adverse employment action, subject to the employer’s proof that it would have taken the same action for an independent lawful reason. Actual authorship is not a prerequisite to this direct claim.
* **Reach Within the Question:** Protection concerns the employer’s punitive use of perceived protected expression against this employee. Neither erroneous attribution alone nor an imperfect investigation establishes liability; protection, adverse action, motive, and causation remain necessary.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The First Amendment restrains official punishment of a person for the expression officials seek to suppress. Making the victim’s authorship decisive would allow the same censorial employment action whenever the employer selected the wrong speaker. Wasson alleges her own employment injury, so she need not establish a relationship with the anonymous author. Constitutional function and the retaliation precedents together support the extension; the prohibition on official suppression supplies the decisive reason. Waters protects genuine reliance on a reasonable account of disciplinable speech, not hostility to protected criticism. The letters’ content, dismissal notice, chronology, testimony, and other competent direct or circumstantial proof may establish the actual ground under Crawford-El; statistics are unnecessary. The alleged criticism must still satisfy the governing speech rules. A general rule for misattributed expression is warranted because identity error does not change the constitutional reason the retaliation is forbidden.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** None.

### Issue 2: Personal damages and legal reliance

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The issue determines the challenged judgment and is properly presented on this record.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** Does the absence of a previously decided mistaken-speaker case independently defeat personal damages?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Propose replacing Harlow’s clearly-established-law defense, as preserved in Elder and Crawford-El, to the extent it bars an otherwise established violation solely for lack of sufficiently similar prior precedent. Preserve Elder’s independent consideration of relevant authority and ordinary procedural protections.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** No. After the claimant establishes the underlying violation, an official has a distinct defense only by proving actual and objectively reasonable reliance on identified binding precedent or enacted authority directly permitting the challenged conduct. Legal silence, an institutional custom, or general supervisory authority is insufficient.
* **Reach Within the Question:** This change governs the preserved individual-capacity defense; it does not establish a violation, damages, institutional liability, or defeat an independent absolute immunity.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Section 1983 provides a remedy for the deprivation itself. The existing defense can make recurring suppression unredressable merely because officials used a new factual route. Reasonable factual mistakes and legitimate employment discretion already receive their proper protection in the substantive speech inquiry; repeating those allowances after finding a violation needlessly defeats the remedy. Reliance on actual authoritative permission deserves protection and supplies a manageable boundary. This openly changes the prior defense, including decisions Stone previously accepted; the narrower reliance protection addresses legitimate reliance without treating absence of precedent as a license. On the pleaded facts, no authorizing law has been established. Remand permits determination of any properly supported defense and remaining merits, without entering a damages judgment.

**Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate defective procedures and remand for correction.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

**Stone's Holding:** The Equal Protection Clause requires materially consistent treatment of legally comparable votes. A correctable constitutional defect in recount administration warrants corrective relief, not automatic termination of the electoral contest.

**Entering law and application:** This is an open application of equal protection to statewide judicial counting. The districting decisions reject territorial design alone as a constitutional deprivation; they do not permit arbitrary valuation of comparable ballots. The injury here is unequal administration of the vote actually cast. No prior holding makes §5's safe-harbor benefit a universal counting deadline.

**Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Materially inconsistent treatment of comparable ballots violates equal protection. An abstract voter-intent standard is capable of lawful implementation, but the recount needs sufficiently definite instructions and consistent treatment in operation. Uniformity does not require mechanically identical treatment of ballots with legally relevant differences. It requires that equivalent voting marks receive equivalent consideration, including correction of earlier inclusions where that is necessary to remove the established inequality.

The constitutional defect does not automatically invalidate the whole contest. The Florida court must distinguish interpretation implementing existing election law from a materially different post-election rule, identify any state-law deadline, and determine whether a lawful corrective count can actually be completed. Section 5 alone supplies no federal command to terminate the count on December 12. An unfavorable forecast for one candidate supplies no legal reason either to continue or to stop.

**Boundary:** The order determines neither a winner nor how Congress must ultimately treat competing electoral submissions. It neither guarantees completion nor permits counting beyond independently applicable law.

## D. DOCTRINAL DEVELOPMENT AND FUTURE APPLICATION

Equal protection requires consistent treatment of comparable votes and effective correction of material inequality. The rule applies beyond these candidates and this election. Differences reflecting legally relevant ballot characteristics remain permissible; ordinary administrative variation without material unequal treatment does not establish a constitutional violation.
