# Estelle v. McGuire

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 6 to 2; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote the Court's opinion. O'Connor, joined by Stevens, agreed on evidence admissibility but dissented on the instruction and would remand for harmless review.
* **Historical Holding:** Neither admission nor the charge violated due process; the Court applied reasonable-likelihood analysis and found no authorization of propensity use. It reserved a general constitutional propensity question.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 62, comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and separate opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/62).

# Wooddell v. International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 71

# Simon & Schuster, Inc. v. Members of the New York State Crime Victims Board

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 8 to 0 in the judgment; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor for six Justices; Blackmun and Kennedy separately concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The Son of Sam statute imposed an unjustified content-based financial burden and was insufficiently tailored to compensating victims from crime profits.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 105, comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and separate opinions](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/105).

# Ardestani v. INS

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6 to 2; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor for Rehnquist, White, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and herself; Blackmun dissented, joined by Stevens.

* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 129, comparator only.
