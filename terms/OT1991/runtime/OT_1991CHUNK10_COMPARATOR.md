# Chemical Waste Management, Inc. v. Hunt

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8-1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for the Court; Rehnquist dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The additional fee discriminated against interstate commerce, and Alabama failed to justify the origin distinction when nondiscriminatory alternatives could address the asserted concerns.
* **Historical Authority:** The holding addressed the additional charge, rather than constitutionally immunizing waste disposal from evenhanded regulation.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 334](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/334/).

# Fort Gratiot Sanitary Landfill, Inc. v. Michigan Department of Natural Resources

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for White, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas. Rehnquist dissented with Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** The county-based import restrictions discriminated against interstate commerce; treatment of other Michigan counties did not save them, and no adequate justification for the origin distinction had been shown.
* **Historical Authority:** The holding concerns regulation of private disposal facilities and does not decide every waste-planning or public-facility arrangement.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 353](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/353/).

# Morales v. Trans World Airlines, Inc.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part and reversed in part, 5-3; Souter did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for White, O'Connor, Kennedy, and Thomas. Stevens dissented with Rehnquist and Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** The fare-advertising guidelines were preempted, but the injunction extended beyond the threatened enforcement and required limitation.
* **Historical Authority:** The historical opinion read the relational clause broadly while reserving connections too remote or peripheral. Its particular reasoning enters simulation law only through an actual simulated decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 374](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/374/).

# Burdick v. Takushi

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for Rehnquist, O'Connor, Scalia, Souter, and Thomas. Kennedy dissented with Blackmun and Stevens.
* **Historical Holding:** Hawaii's overall ballot-access system imposed a limited burden sufficient to sustain the write-in prohibition; the opinion used a flexible scrutiny framework and described a presumptive validity where other access rules impose only reasonable burdens.
* **Historical Authority:** The majority's burden assessment and presumption were disputed. The historical result does not establish the actual simulated rule entering later cases.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 428](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/428/).

# Eastman Kodak Co. v. Image Technical Services, Inc.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for Rehnquist, White, Stevens, Kennedy, and Souter. Scalia dissented with O'Connor and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Equipment-market competition did not foreclose aftermarket power as a matter of law, and the record presented genuine issues on the tying and monopolization theories.
* **Historical Authority:** A summary-judgment holding permitting litigation, not a final finding of unlawful monopoly or tying.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 451](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/451/).

# United States v. Thompson/Center Arms Co.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter announced the judgment with Rehnquist and O'Connor joining his opinion. Scalia, joined by Thomas, concurred in the judgment on differently located statutory ambiguities. White dissented with Blackmun, Stevens, and Kennedy; Stevens also wrote separately.
* **Historical Holding:** The pistol and conversion package was not taxed as a made short-barreled rifle. Five Justices relied on lenity, without joining one complete account of the ambiguity or all unassembled-parts rules.
* **Historical Authority:** The plurality's full rationale is not a five-Justice holding merely because the judgment had five votes.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 505](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/505/).

# Sochor v. Florida

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 5-4 on the final disposition.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote with different joins: Part I unanimous; Part II joined by Rehnquist, White, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas; Part III-A by Rehnquist, White, O'Connor, Kennedy, and Thomas; Part III-B-1 by all except Scalia; final corrective analysis and disposition by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, and Kennedy. O'Connor concurred. Rehnquist, with White and Thomas, partially dissented; Stevens, with Blackmun, and Scalia also wrote partial dissents.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court declined the barred jury-heinousness claim and sustained the judge's application, rejected the jury-coldness inference under historical Griffin, and required correction of the judge's coldness error because the existing appellate review did not adequately establish harmlessness.
* **Historical Authority:** Joins and separate claims must be tracked; the final vote does not imply five identical positions on every issue. Historical Griffin and Stringer citations do not automatically become simulation precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 527](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/527/).
