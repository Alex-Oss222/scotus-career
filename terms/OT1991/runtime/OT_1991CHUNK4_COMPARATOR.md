# INS v. Doherty

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, with five Justices supporting denial of reopening for both claims; three agreed as to asylum but disagreed as to withholding. Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist's Part I had White, Blackmun, O'Connor and Kennedy; Part II had White, Blackmun and O'Connor; Part III had Kennedy. Scalia, joined by Stevens and Souter, concurred in the judgment in part and dissented in part.
* **Historical Holding:** The Attorney General did not abuse reopening discretion. The specific new-evidence and withdrawal rationales did not each receive the same five-Justice support.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only, with plurality portions distinguished from the Court's common ground.
* **Historical Sources:** [502 U.S. 314, 322–345](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/314/).

# White v. Illinois

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 9–0 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote; seven Justices joined the entire opinion. Thomas, joined by Scalia, concurred in part and in the judgment, declining the rejection of the United States' narrower account of witnesses against the accused.
* **Historical Holding:** No unavailability requirement precedes testimony admitted within the spontaneous-declaration and medical-examination exceptions.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only, not an eligible answer to the open entering question.
* **Historical Sources:** [502 U.S. 346, 352–366](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/346/).

# Rufo v. Inmates of Suffolk County Jail

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 6–2; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for five; O'Connor concurred in the judgment; Stevens dissented with Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** Institutional decree modification requires the movant to show a significant factual or legal change and a suitably tailored revision; Swift's grievous-wrong formulation is not universal.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only. The historical formulation cannot supply entering law for this same event.

# Wyoming v. Oklahoma

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Wyoming's summary-judgment motion granted and Oklahoma's denied, 6–3; Wyoming's exceptions sustained and Oklahoma's rejected.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter. Scalia dissented with Rehnquist and Thomas; Thomas also dissented with Rehnquist and Scalia.
* **Historical Holding:** Wyoming had standing; original jurisdiction was appropriate; the coal preference violated the Commerce Clause; the Act was not severed to preserve application to GRDA. Jurisdiction was retained.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparison only, not entering simulated law.
* **Historical Sources:** [502 U.S. 437](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/437/).

# INS v. Elias-Zacarias

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 6–3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for Rehnquist, White, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas. Stevens dissented with Blackmun and O'Connor.
* **Historical Holding:** Guerrilla recruitment does not necessarily amount to persecution on account of political opinion; the applicant must provide some direct or circumstantial evidence of the persecutor's motive. The record did not compel the statutory conclusion needed to reverse the agency.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only; no simulated holding arises from the historical result.
* **Historical Sources:** [502 U.S. 478](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/478/).
