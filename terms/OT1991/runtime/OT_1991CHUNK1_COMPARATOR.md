# United States v. Ibarra

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court vacated and remanded without recorded dissent.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam; no separate opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** A timely reconsideration motion renewing an abandoned theory invokes the ordinary *Healy-Dieter* finality rule. Bad faith and successive motions were reserved.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 1, a historical Supreme Court disposition only, not simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/1/).

# Mireles v. Waco

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5 to 3, with eight participating Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam; Stevens dissented on functional classification; Scalia, joined by Kennedy, dissented from summary reversal and preferred denial of review.
* **Historical Holding:** Directing officers to bring counsel before the judge was judicial in nature despite alleged excessive force; acting in aid of jurisdiction was not complete absence of jurisdiction.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 9, comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and dissents](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/9).

# Campos v. City of Houston

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The application was denied by one Circuit Justice; there was no historical full-Court vote on this disposition.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia's in-chambers opinion, not a Court merits opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** No legal basis supported the original injunction; the alternative stay was denied in light of delay and uncertainty that stopping the imminent election would advance the public interest.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 1301. Historical Scalia presentation is displaced in the simulation by the express O'Connor/full-Court instruction.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical application opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/502/1301/).

# Zatko v. California

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Fee-waiver motions denied, 6 to 2; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam; Stevens dissented, joined by Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** The repeated frivolous filings constituted extreme abuse warranting Rule 39.8 fee denial; petitioners were given until November 25 to pay and comply with form rules.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 16, an administrative disposition of motions, not a merits resolution of the underlying claims.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/16).

# Hafer v. Melo

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 8 to 0; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor for all participating Justices.
* **Historical Holding:** State officials sued individually are §1983 persons for official conduct; *Will* and the Eleventh Amendment do not prohibit those claims.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 21, comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/21).

# Griffin v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 8 to 0 in the judgment; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia's opinion joined by six others; Blackmun concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** An unsupported factual alternative does not invalidate an otherwise supportable general conspiracy verdict; legally inadequate theories are distinct.
* **Historical Authority:** 502 U.S. 46, comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/502/46).
