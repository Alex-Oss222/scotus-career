# Reynolds v. International Amateur Athletic Federation

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 20, 1992, subject to actual earlier simulated orders.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any relevant earlier stay rule and material change in trials timing before voting.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Grant the application to stay the Sixth Circuit's June 19 stay insofar as necessary for Reynolds's participation in the United States Olympic Trials.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Requested Court order: “The application for a stay presented to Justice Stevens and by him referred to the Court is granted to the extent necessary to restore the District Court's preliminary protection of applicant's participation in the United States Olympic Trials, pending further order.” This is Stone's requested wording, not an issued order. Final eligibility and damages remain below.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Preserve the qualification opportunity

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Address jurisdiction and probable entitlement only as required for interim relief. The district record supplies a preliminary basis, including asserted IAAF-directed relationships and completed review; residence or athletic importance alone would not.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply ordinary emergency equity; create no special constitutional sports-access claim.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** On this preliminary record, the appellate stay demonstrably misapplies the merits-and-equities inquiry by extinguishing a uniquely time-sensitive opportunity despite the supported preliminary showing, when final eligibility can still be adjudicated. Limited restoration is warranted under the demanding appellate-stay standard.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Two proceedings questioning sample reliability and the district hearing supply more than unsupported insistence on innocence. The competing arbitration prevents a final conclusion now, but not provisional relief. Money cannot recreate the qualifying event. Uniform rules remain a legitimate interest, while threats to punish third parties do not conclusively establish that judicial protection is inequitable. The narrow order permits later resolution before any Olympic participation is compelled. Stone regards the forum-jurisdiction question involving an international federation's nationally consequential eligibility decision as sufficiently substantial to make later review likely; that assessment supplies an emergency-relief premise, not a petition-stage vote. His conclusion concerns the objective operation of the stay, not an invented finding about the appellate judges' motives or an unsupported account of their unpublished reasoning.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No final finding of clean testing, invalid arbitration, worldwide jurisdiction, or a right to compete in Barcelona. Master §§3, 12, 17 and 19 support the separation of a private-law claim, provisional proof and bounded relief.

# Sawyer v. Whitley

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 22, 1992, before this matter's disposition.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate actual earlier habeas and aggravator holdings; do not treat the proposal below as adopted law.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the refusal to reopen the barred claims on the narrower ground that these submissions do not establish a fundamental miscarriage of justice even when mitigation is considered.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** No new sentencing proceeding or release on this application of the gateway. Affirmance does not endorse categorical exclusion of mitigation from all future gateway inquiries.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Capital innocence includes the sentencing decision

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The gateway question is presented. Accept the unresolved claims' procedural classification and insufficient cause for this review; decide whether their evidentiary showing warrants further merits review.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Carrier's probability principle and propose its express capital-sentencing extension: the prisoner must show that, absent constitutional error and considering the corrected record, it is more likely than not that no reasonable sentencer would select death under the governing sentencing framework. Include eligibility facts and constitutionally relevant mitigation. For the selection branch, legal eligibility alone does not settle whether any reasonable sentencer would choose death; for the eligibility branch, absence of every lawful eligibility basis independently matters. This is a proposed specification of an open branch, not a previously adopted universal rule or the ordinary Strickland threshold.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A capital gateway may include exceptionally decisive mitigation, but evidence merely supporting a different discretionary sentencing choice does not suffice. Sawyer fails the required exceptional showing.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Eliminating mitigation categorically would preserve only one half of the Eighth Amendment's reliability structure. Finality justifies a demanding gateway, not excluding evidence because it addresses the selection stage. Courts can compare the corrected record with the state-law sentencing requirements without acting as new sentencing juries. The caregiver affidavit addresses who ignited the victim but does not negate Sawyer's participation or the other brutal conduct; witness impeachment does not establish that the prosecution's core account is probably false. The medical material deserves consideration and can support mitigation, but against the surviving evidence it does not make it more likely than not that no reasonable sentencer would choose death on the corrected record. Stone does not decide it is worthless or that a death sentence is mandatory.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The new articulation retains cause-and-prejudice, exhaustion, retroactivity and the separate merits tests. It does not revive every deficient-performance claim. Under Master §16, the source is the habeas miscarriage exception read with Eddings; the defect addressed is exclusion of potentially dispositive mitigation, with final judgments protected by the exceptional probability requirement. No clear-and-convincing quantum is imported from this case's comparator. A record reliably negating every lawful aggravator would stand differently; mere disagreement over the weight of ordinary mitigation would not.

# R. A. V. v. City of St. Paul

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 22, 1992, subject to earlier actual speech holdings.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate the content and overbreadth framework if the preceding simulated cases changed it.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand the judgment reinstating the ordinance charge.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Dismiss that charge under the substantially overbroad provision. Preserve adjudication of independently valid charges; do not create a new criminal prohibition by severance or construction.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Protected offense cannot become fighting words by label

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the preserved facial-overbreadth objection. Its resolution makes the broader category-discrimination issue unnecessary.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the narrow fighting-words doctrine and substantial-overbreadth rule, rather than announce a new system for every distinction within unprotected speech.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The ordinance's injury and resentment coverage, even through the state's Chaplinsky formulation, is not confined to personally abusive provocation of an immediate breach of the peace and reaches substantial protected expression. The state construction therefore does not save the offense.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The ordinance reaches more than intimidation supported by a serious threat or confrontation. Religious criticism and hostile debate over race or gender can cause deep resentment without satisfying fighting-words, threat or incitement doctrine. The defect affects ordinary applications across the ordinance's selected subject matter and is substantial relative to its legitimate protection against immediate violence. The alleged invasion of a family's yard is grave, but a defendant's possibly punishable conduct does not cure a facial speech prohibition. The government may protect the family through valid sources whose elements are met.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve whether carefully confined regulation of a particularly harmful subset of genuinely unprotected speech is permissible; no opinion on sentencing enhancements for independently criminal conduct. Master §§3, 4 and 18 counsel using the already adequate doctrinal ground. Stone's preference for equal citizenship does not exempt a speech restriction from First Amendment analysis or require invalidating antidiscrimination laws not before the Court.

# Medina v. California

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 22, 1992, before this adjudication.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any actual earlier simulated rule concerning trial competence or procedural risk allocation.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate and remand the California judgment insofar as it rests on the challenged competency determination.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Require proceedings under the proper allocation. The state court must determine whether a reliable retrospective inquiry into trial-time competence remains feasible. If it is not, the convictions cannot stand on this competency process; retrial may proceed upon competence. Do not order acquittal or automatically authorize indefinite commitment.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: The State bears competence after a justified inquiry begins

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the preserved burden and presumption questions; do not decide whether a higher proof quantum is necessary.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Propose that after legally sufficient doubt requires a hearing, the State bear persuasion on competence under the preponderance standard at issue. This changes California's allocation and specifies an open federal procedural requirement; it is not merely a different evaluation of the experts.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A presumption may organize ordinary proceedings before genuine doubt arises, but cannot make a defendant bear the risk of equipoise at the competency hearing triggered by that doubt.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Pate and Drope protect the ability to participate in the very process being used to decide guilt. A procedure premised on the defendant's ability to supply persuasive assistance becomes least reliable where the protected capacity is missing. Counsel and examination reduce that risk but do not answer who loses an unresolved close case. The State already participates in a full hearing and can obtain examinations; reallocating persuasion does not require it to disprove every unsupported allegation of incompetence. Ake's procedural inquiry supports weighing this concrete additional safeguard, and the fundamental-fairness concern remains under Patterson. Medina's conflicting experts make the allocation material, though Stone supplies no finding that the jury actually balanced evenly.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Preserve the existing threshold for a hearing, the functional Dusky definition, ordinary factfinding and California's unchallenged preponderance quantum. Do not move the burden for insanity or offense defenses. Master §§3, 16 and 17 require naming the changed burden: the legal reason is protection of established trial capacity, the practical rule is administrable within an existing hearing, and no reliance in completed transactions competes with the need for a valid criminal adjudication. Retrospective feasibility receives a remand rather than an invented finding.

# Estate of Cowart v. Nicklos Drilling Co.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 22, 1992.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any actual earlier simulated LHWCA holding that addresses this trigger.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the Fifth Circuit on the entitlement question.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** No restoration of benefits on the theory that an award or current payments were required. Do not adjudicate an unpresented alternative approval, waiver or settlement-calculation issue.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Enforce the statute's entitlement trigger

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the meaning of entitlement at settlement and its necessary relation to the two subsections.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply text and scheme without adding an employee-protective exception.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A worker whose substantive compensation right has vested is a person entitled to compensation even if the employer disputes liability or no formal award has issued.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A right can exist before acknowledgement; §(g)(2)'s express indifference to payments and acknowledgement reinforces that distinction. The added notice and forfeiture language must operate together with the approval trigger. Retention of one phrase does not silently adopt every Board limitation when Congress simultaneously enacted contrary contextual language. The requirement protects the employer before a low settlement fixes third-party recovery. Cowart's employer's participation makes forfeiture severe, but it does not change the meaning of entitlement or authorize an unpresented waiver ruling. Master §§3, 16 and Statutory Interpretation reject using remedial purpose to rewrite a condition the statute fairly establishes.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No ruling that every injured person is already entitled, that all settlements require approval, or that employer involvement can never matter. No constitutional challenge or new deference framework is necessary.

# Cipollone v. Liggett Group, Inc.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 24, 1992, subject to actual earlier preemption decisions.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate Morales and Gade's actual propositions, if any, without substituting their statutes for these clauses.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm in part, reverse in part, and remand: the 1965 clause does not preempt the common-law damages claims; the successor clause preempts only claims that impose a smoking-health requirement or prohibition with respect to advertising or promotion.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Under the successor clause, bar additional-warning and warning-neutralization duties directed to advertising/promotion. Preserve express warranty, general fraudulent-falsehood and concealment duties outside the covered warning requirement, and conspiracy theories to the extent their predicate duties survive. Leave facts, state-law elements, causation and damages to the lower court.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Read each clause in its own terms

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the federal statutory defense, not the product's legality or an independent speech claim.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply enacted text, context and traditional preemption principles. No new immunity or free-floating anti-preemption doctrine.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The original prohibition on required advertising statements does not encompass all damages obligations. The expanded successor language can include common-law duties but retains its express subject-matter and activity boundaries.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A tort judgment can impose a legal requirement, so common-law form alone cannot defeat the expanded clause. Conversely, Congress did not write complete immunity for tobacco-related wrongdoing. The narrower original wording and broader successor wording must each do their actual work. Uniformity identifies the relevant federal operation; it does not erase duties Congress left outside that operation. Master Statutory Interpretation supports precisely this sequence.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No general holding that an express clause forecloses every independently established impossibility conflict. No position on design-defect liability or later amendments not presented.

### Issue 2: Classify the duty, not the pleading title

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the four disputed claim categories separately.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the construed boundary to each predicate duty.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Additional warning content in advertising and a duty not to neutralize the mandated warning impose covered smoking-health promotional requirements. Enforcing a voluntarily promised warranty does not derive its substantive promise from a state smoking-health command. A general duty not to make false statements is not transformed into such a command by the topic of the falsehood; a warning claim cannot evade the clause by adopting the fraud label. Concealment through independently actionable nonpromotional channels and conspiracy based on surviving deceptive acts remain available.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** These distinctions can be administered by identifying what finding of duty and breach the jury must make. If liability requires deciding that federal warnings should have been supplemented in promotional material, the successor defense applies. If it requires proving a false representation or broken express assurance without prescribing warning content, it does not. The estate still must establish each state-law element and receives no assumed causation finding.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No blanket permission to use nominal fraud to punish conforming warnings, and no blanket exclusion of advertisements as evidence of an otherwise surviving wrong.

# City of Burlington v. Dague

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 24, 1992.
* **Conditions:** Preserve the reviewed scope: contingency enhancement only.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the judgment insofar as it sustains the 25% enhancement and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Reassess any enhancement under the limited market approach below, preserving the lodestar and underlying relief. No predetermined replacement percentage.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Limited, supported contingency compensation

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Resolve permission and its necessary evidentiary boundary; do not reopen merits fees or pollution liability.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Adopt the limited market-based interpretation articulated in Delaware Valley II's concurrence as the resolution of its unsettled permission question, candidly identifying its prior opinion status.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A fee applicant may establish that compensation contingent on success receives different treatment in the relevant market and that, without an adjustment, the client would face substantial difficulty obtaining competent counsel in the relevant market. The adjustment must reflect that demonstrated difference, exclude case-specific speculation and factors already in the lodestar, and account for mitigation of nonpayment risk.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The statutory word reasonable supports attention to the economic character of the services purchased; it does not confine Congress's enforcement mechanism to arrangements inaccessible to the intended clients. A weak claim is not entitled to a larger award simply because winning was unlikely. The district court addressed access but tied its numerical choice to this case's risk and an asserted windfall boundary; its findings do not supply the necessary market basis for 25%. Remand allows the trial court to determine the showing through competent fee-market evidence without this Court inventing an amount.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The applicant retains the burden, the lodestar remains presumptively sufficient, and losing cases receive no independent award. No general judicial power to fund litigation or mandatory fee enhancement. Under Master §16, this resolves an open statutory issue using existing fee precedent and its narrower proposed approach; administrability is preserved by nonduplication and a demonstrated market difference, not a new mathematical risk formula.

# Lee v. Weisman

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 24, 1992.
* **Conditions:** Confirm continued threatened exposure before entering prospective relief.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Preserve the injunction against school officials' inclusion of selected clergy-led invocations and benedictions in the challenged graduation program, without extending it to private prayer or genuinely private forum speech.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: The school cannot organize the devotional exercise

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the live challenge to an ongoing policy; later graduation exposure supplies the material prospective stake.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Engel, Schempp and Lemon's prohibition on official advancement of religion. No replacement establishment test.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** School officials violate the Establishment Clause by organizing the religious exercise as an official part of public secondary-school graduation, notwithstanding nonsectarian content or formally optional attendance.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The selection of a rabbi, request for prayer and guidance over religious content establish governmental adoption. A request that worship be broadly acceptable still requires the State to decide what religious exercise it will present. Engel and Schempp do not require disciplinary punishment for abstention. Graduation's place in school life further exposes students to pressure, but Stone need not invent a quantitative measure of social coercion: the established school-prayer rule already prohibits the sponsorship. Marsh's legislature and Mergens's private forum are materially different institutions and acts. The school's valid interest in solemnity can be pursued with secular ceremony.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No holding against every ceremonial reference to religion, adult legislative practice, or a private speaker's religious viewpoint. Do not decide funding, denominational aid or theology. Master Religion and §§3-4 support resolving official worship through its own source, without making generalized offense the test.

# Doggett v. United States

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** June 24, 1992.
* **Conditions:** Use the existing findings rather than infer flight from the fact of foreign travel.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand for dismissal of the indictment and the consequent vacatur of the conditional conviction.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Apply Strunk's dismissal remedy to the affected prosecution; no sentence-only adjustment.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Years of avoidable neglect defeat a timely prosecution

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** The pending indictment triggers the Sixth Amendment; the conditional plea preserves the claim.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Barker and Moore without making specific prejudice an additional element.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** This extraordinary period of negligent failure to pursue a defendant living openly, unaware of charges and promptly asserting his right upon arrest, violates the speedy-trial guarantee when the resulting inference of defense impairment has not been persuasively answered.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The length far exceeds the threshold for inquiry and includes years during which a routine check could locate him. Negligence deserves less weight than deliberate obstruction, but its sustained duration matters. Unknown charges cannot be knowingly acquiesced in. Absence of jail or anxiety removes two possible harms; it does not eliminate impaired adjudication. The lost ability to reconstruct events may be precisely what prevents naming a vanished defense. The record does not supply a persuasive explanation or answer to that accumulated risk. This is contextual application, not an invented automatic deadline, and no general transfer of the ultimate constitutional burden is announced.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A diligent pursuit, justified foreign obstacle, known deliberate evasion or materially shorter neglect could yield a different balance. No preaccusation rule is decided. Master Criminal Procedure, Evidence and §18 support actual operation within the received factor inquiry.
