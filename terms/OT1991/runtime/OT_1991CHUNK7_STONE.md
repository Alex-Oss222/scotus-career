## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm the availability of §1983 enforcement and the prospective judgment on that ground.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Preserve the case-specific assignment remedy supported by the findings; do not transform three working days into a nationwide statutory command. No treasury damages or revived constitutional claim.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Can beneficiaries enforce §671(a)(15)’s reasonable-efforts duty through §1983 or an implied action?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the statutory enforcement question because the narrower B.H. class and different obligations leave live relief here. Agreement to a defined consent obligation with no admission does not preclude contesting an independent statutory right. Revalidate any actual effective Rufo holding concerning consent obligations; the historical same-term result cannot supply the rule.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Wilder.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** On this claim, §671(a)(15) establishes an enforceable individual right under §1983; the absence of assigned caseworkers can be addressed through the specific supported injunctive obligation.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply Wilder. Children facing removal and separation are the identified beneficiaries, the State undertakes a mandatory duty, and reasonable efforts is a judicially usable obligation despite room for institutional choice. Where the alleged failure is leaving a child without the assigned worker through whom every service is delivered, courts can evaluate whether any reasonable implementation occurred without managing every welfare judgment. A plan-only construction makes the specific in-each-case promise operationally empty. The Secretary’s funding remedy does not, on this scheme, demonstrate incompatibility with §1983. This conclusion comes from enacted §1983 and §671, not Master’s broader family-integrity preferences. No overruling of a preexisting contrary Supreme Court holding is needed.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve an independent implied action because §1983 suffices. Do not constitutionalize all family services or expand the unappealed constitutional count. Safety, actual necessity, causation, and remedy retain their separate requirements.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Do overlapping proof and overt acts make these successive drug prosecutions the same offense?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the distinct-offense precedents and confine Grady to its actual essential-conduct inquiry.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Evidence reused from one offense does not by itself create a second prosecution for that offense, and conspiracy’s agreement remains distinct from its substantive acts on this record.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the distinct-offense precedents and confine Grady to its actual essential-conduct inquiry. Prior evidentiary use did not place Felix in jeopardy for the Oklahoma manufacturing offenses. The conspiracy rests on an agreement with additional conduct and does not become the Missouri attempt because that attempt supplied overt-act evidence. This expressly clarifies Grady’s relation to the established agreement/substantive-offense distinction without overruling Grady or adopting a same-transaction requirement. Reuse of evidence may face ordinary evidentiary limits, but that separate issue is not the double-jeopardy claim.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve any challenge to the admissibility of particular evidence and any different single-incident successive prosecution. Do not import the later historical overruling of Grady.

* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the transfer definition to the property interest actually at stake.

* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the transfer definition to the property interest actually at stake. # National Railroad Passenger Corp. v. Boston & Maine Corp.

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** The entering-law baseline in I.B, not the historical comparator.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any specifically identified earlier simulated dependency when its actual holding becomes effective. No prior approved case-specific position has been supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand the D.C. Circuit’s judgments in both consolidated dockets.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Require review consistent with the statutory need presumption and lawful reconveyance authority; preserve just compensation and any unresolved application question.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: May the ICC condemn track for Amtrak’s service when Amtrak will reconvey ownership?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the statute’s text and structure through Chevron’s ordinary tools.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Section 562(d) permits acquisition required to provide Amtrak service without indispensable permanent Amtrak ownership; the statutory presumption and public-use limits remain satisfied only through their separate terms.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the statute’s text and structure through Chevron’s ordinary tools. A requirement that Amtrak retain ownership cannot coexist with Congress’s express authorization of service-promoting reconveyance. Required property must serve passenger operations, but the presumption prevents the court from adding an indispensable-ownership prerequisite. The ICC’s identified service connection and preserved freight access support its application. A compensated transfer advancing actual rail service satisfies Midkiff; no evidence here warrants treating the transaction as naked private favoritism. Stone need not replace the received agency framework to decide this construction.
The failed-negotiation prerequisite also requires decision. Amtrak made a take-it-or-leave-it purchase offer; B&M offered discussion of upgrading while declining the sale proposal. Stone would read the statute’s inability-to-agree language as requiring a supported finding that further sale negotiations would not produce agreement, rather than an unexpressed mandatory bargaining ritual. The ICC made that finding on the parties’ positions. The record therefore satisfies the prerequisite without treating every unilateral offer as sufficient or replacing the agency’s factual determination with a presumption.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve compensation valuation and distinct regulatory claims. The holding supplies no authority for condemnation unrelated to Amtrak service or for disregarding the conjunctive rebuttal.

# Freeman v. Pitts

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** The entering-law baseline in I.B, not the historical comparator.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any specifically identified earlier simulated dependency when its actual holding becomes effective. No prior approved case-specific position has been supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Vacate the categorical appellate restriction and remand for an integrated assessment of partial release.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Allow release only of components for which the district proves good-faith compliance and elimination of vestiges to the extent practicable, and only where release will not obstruct completion elsewhere. Retain enforceable supervision of remaining violations; require specific findings and a clear order defining what remains.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: When may a district court withdraw supervision over particular parts of a school desegregation decree while retaining other parts?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the existing remedial purpose and specify its operation for partial withdrawal.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Partial withdrawal is permissible upon proven good-faith compliance and practicable elimination of relevant vestiges, provided release will not obstruct completion of the remaining remedy.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the existing remedial purpose and specify its operation for partial withdrawal. Federal equitable power lasts as long as necessary to repair the constitutional violation; it has neither a perpetual administrative mandate nor a permission to abandon a partly repaired injury. Because a decree can contain distinct obligations, completion can justify release of some obligations. The decisive qualification is functional interdependence. A completed transportation arrangement does not automatically prove that an assignment pattern is insulated from discriminatory faculty placement or unequal resources. Conversely, unfinished personnel corrections do not establish that every existing transportation restriction remains needed. The proposed rule requires the district judge to answer those concrete questions on the record.

Stone would treat the demographic findings with their proper appellate respect while refusing to enlarge them. Independent migration can explain imbalance without creating a fresh constitutional wrong under Spangler. It does not prove that every contemporary housing or enrollment pattern lacks a traceable connection to the old system. Where causation was litigated and reliably resolved, a reviewing court must apply clear-error review; where a proposed release depends on a connection never assessed, remand permits the necessary findings rather than an invented conclusion. The school district must demonstrate compliance through conduct, including its implementation of the still-active parts of the decree. A statement of good intentions cannot replace performance.

This is a clarification of how Dowell and Green apply to divisible relief, not an overruling of their affirmative obligations. The strongest contrary argument is that piecemeal release permits the district to lock in racial identities before educational equality is achieved. That concern justifies examination of the decree as a whole and denial of a release that frustrates unfinished work. It does not establish that Article III requires continued control over an obligation proven unnecessary to the remaining remedy. The administrable consequence is component-specific findings, identification of remaining duties, and retained enforcement authority of defined scope. Stone would not calculate an independent racial-balance target or turn broad equal-citizenship commitments in Master into a substitute constitutional test.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve findings of final systemwide compliance, contemporary discriminatory intent, and any proposed new remedy not litigated. Partial release does not adjudicate a future challenge to new official discrimination.

# Jacobson v. United States

# SECTION II: STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** The entering-law baseline in I.B, not the historical comparator.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any specifically identified earlier simulated dependency when its actual holding becomes effective. No prior approved case-specific position has been supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse for insufficient proof overcoming entrapment.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Direct entry of acquittal; do not permit a second trial to fill the evidentiary gap.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Did the Government prove predisposition independent of its inducement beyond a reasonable doubt?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the subjective defense and the criminal proof burden.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The Government did not prove beyond a reasonable doubt that Jacobson was predisposed independently of its inducement; evidentiary insufficiency requires acquittal.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the subjective defense and the criminal proof burden. The lawful 1984 purchase establishes interest in the material then obtained. It does not establish willingness, before the campaign, to commit the subsequently defined crime. The Government’s agents then worked over many months to make the prohibited transaction attractive and defensible through fabricated affiliations and repeated persuasion. Jacobson’s eventual order proves the charged receipt but cannot, without more, answer whether his readiness originated independently of those efforts. Otherwise every successful inducement would retrospectively supply the predisposition necessary to defeat the defense.

Stone would consider the responses on which the prosecution relies rather than declare them legally irrelevant merely because they came after contact. An unsolicited statement revealing an established criminal plan, for example, could rationally illuminate prior readiness. That is not a finding about this record. Here the identified correspondence was part of the campaign’s development, and the search did not produce independent unlawful material supporting the missing inference. Even giving the prosecution its favorable factual inferences, the gap between lawful interest and independent criminal readiness leaves the Government short of its burden. Sherman permits a matter-of-law conclusion without replacing the jury whenever evidence merely conflicts.

The strongest objection is that this analysis makes investigatory persistence itself exculpatory and demands proof that necessarily predates the investigation. The proposed holding does neither. Persistence matters because it can explain conduct the prosecution offers as evidence of prior readiness; temporal origin of evidence and origin of predisposition are different questions. A short or long operation can uncover independently existing readiness, and police need no freestanding constitutional showing of reasonable suspicion merely to offer an opportunity under this defense. What fails here is the particular proof of independence.

No overruling of the subjective line is necessary under Master §16. Stone may object to institutional manufacture of crime, but that methodological concern does not authorize substituting an objective-police-misconduct defense that the controlling majority precedents reject. The disposition instead uses the received defense and its own burden. Because the defect is insufficiency, rather than an erroneous instruction admitting retrial, Burks controls the remedy.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve a separate outrageous-government-conduct claim, criminal-law amendments, and any universal reasonable-suspicion prerequisite for undercover contact. Do not infer that protected interests prove criminal intent or that every repeated solicitation constitutes entrapment.
