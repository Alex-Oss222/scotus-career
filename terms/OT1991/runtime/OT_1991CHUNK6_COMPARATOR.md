# Williams v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor majority; White dissent joined by Kennedy.
* **Historical Holding:** Harmlessness and departure reasonableness are separate; the challenger initially establishes use of an invalid factor, after which a record-supported harmlessness determination is required to avoid remand.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 193 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/193/).

# Holmes v. Securities Investor Protection Corp.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously in judgment, 9–0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter majority with divided participation in Part IV; O’Connor concurrence in part and judgment joined by White and Stevens; Scalia concurrence in judgment.

# United States v. R. L. C.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote the Court’s text and result sections; his legislative-history sections had four votes. Scalia, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, and Thomas separately concurred in part and judgment. O’Connor dissented with Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** The ceiling incorporates the adult Guidelines maximum, subject to authorized departures, without plenary juvenile application of the Guidelines.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 291 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/291/).
