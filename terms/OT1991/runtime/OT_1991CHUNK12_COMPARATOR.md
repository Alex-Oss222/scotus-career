# Nordlinger v. Hahn

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 8–1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote the Court’s opinion; Thomas joined Part II-A and separately concurred in part and in the judgment. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The acquisition-value scheme and exceptions satisfied rational basis; Nordlinger could not assert the unshown travel injuries of others.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 1 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/1/).

# Georgia v. McCollum

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for six. Rehnquist also concurred separately. Thomas concurred in the judgment. O’Connor and Scalia filed dissents.
* **Historical Holding:** Purposeful race-based defense peremptories are forbidden state action; the State may assert the jurors’ rights, and the restriction is compatible with the defendant’s constitutional protections.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 42 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/42/).

# Kraft General Foods, Inc. v. Iowa Department of Revenue and Finance

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for White, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, and Thomas. Rehnquist dissented with Blackmun.

# Gade v. National Solid Wastes Management Association

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for five in Parts I, III, and IV and for four in Part II; Kennedy concurred in part and in the judgment on an express-preemption rationale. Souter dissented with Blackmun, Stevens, and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The OSH Act preempted state occupational standards on federally regulated issues absent an approved plan, including dual-purpose laws to that extent; the precise displaced provisions remained for remand.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 88 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/88/).

# New York v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed in part and reversed in part; 6–3 on invalidating take-title, with all nine joining the portions sustaining monetary and access incentives.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote for six; White, Blackmun, and Stevens joined Parts III-A and III-B. White, joined by Blackmun and Stevens, concurred in part and dissented in part; Stevens also wrote separately.
* **Historical Holding:** Monetary/access incentives were valid; take-title unconstitutionally commandeered States and was severable. The Court assumed without deciding Guarantee Clause justiciability and found no violation from the remaining incentives.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 144 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/144/).

# Wisconsin Department of Revenue v. William Wrigley, Jr., Co.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6–3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for five; O’Connor joined Parts I and II and concurred in part and in the judgment. Kennedy dissented with Rehnquist and Blackmun.
* **Historical Holding:** Entirely ancillary solicitation activities are protected, independent business functions are not, and a de minimis exception exists. Wrigley’s nonimmune activity collectively exceeded that exception.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 214 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/214/).

# Wright v. West

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously in the judgment, 9–0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas announced the judgment with Rehnquist and Scalia joining his opinion. White, O’Connor (joined by Blackmun and Stevens), Kennedy, and Souter separately concurred in the judgment. No single broad habeas-review theory commanded a majority.
* **Historical Holding:** The judgment granting relief was rejected. Eight Justices accepted evidentiary sufficiency; Souter would resolve the case under Teague. The decision did not adopt a majority rule generally replacing independent review of mixed constitutional questions.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 277 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/277/).

# United States v. Salerno

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8–1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for eight Justices; Blackmun concurred separately. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Rule 804(b)(1)’s similar-motive requirement cannot be discarded for adversarial fairness. The case returned for consideration of whether the Government actually possessed that motive.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 505 U.S. 317 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/317/).
