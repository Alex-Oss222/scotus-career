# Barker v. Kansas

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, 9–0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White for the Court; Stevens, joined by Thomas, concurred.
* **Historical Holding:** Military retirement is deferred pay for purposes of §111, and the asserted differences did not justify the discrimination.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 594 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.

# United States Department of Energy v. Ohio

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6–3 on the contested grounds.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter for the Court; Part II-C, rejecting waiver under RCRA’s federal-facilities provision, was unanimous. White, joined by Blackmun and Stevens, concurred in part and dissented in part.
* **Historical Holding:** Neither statute supplied the asserted punitive-fine waiver; coercive compliance sanctions were distinguished.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 607 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/607/).

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

# Keeney v. Tamayo-Reyes

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White majority, joined by Rehnquist, Scalia, Souter, and Thomas. O’Connor dissented, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, and Kennedy; Kennedy separately dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** Cause and prejudice replaced Townsend’s deliberate-bypass standard for failure to develop material state-court facts, subject to a fundamental-miscarriage exception.

* **Historical Authority:** 504 U.S. 1 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/1/).

# United States v. Williams

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia majority, joined by Rehnquist, White, Kennedy, and Souter. Stevens dissented, joined throughout by Blackmun and O’Connor and in Parts II and III by Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Federal courts lack supervisory authority to require presentation of substantial exculpatory evidence to the grand jury; the question was properly reviewable.

* **Historical Authority:** 504 U.S. 36 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/36/).
