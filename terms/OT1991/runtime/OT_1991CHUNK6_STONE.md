* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Allow enforcement of the statutory repayment obligation; order no contract reformation or additional benefit.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Does Michigan’s retroactive restoration of workers’ compensation violate the Contract or Due Process Clause?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply ordinary contract and retroactive economic-law doctrine.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The repayment law impairs no identified contractual promise and rationally restores the statutory compensation arrangement.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply ordinary contract and retroactive economic-law doctrine. A statutory entitlement to coordinate benefits is not automatically a promise that all future legislative adjustments are excluded from an employment bargain. The employers’ agreements supply no such promise. Retroactive repayment rationally restores the compensation compromise and removes unequal treatment between employers that withheld payments and those that did not. Concrete reliance deserves examination, but reliance alone does not constitutionalize the previous statutory schedule. The employers’ alternative incorporation-by-law theory also fails: they identify no Michigan rule at contracting that made completed payment periods constitutionally closed to later adjustment. United States Trust, 431 U.S. at 19 n.17, protects the legal force and enforceability of contractual obligations as well as express terms; this repayment law removes no contractual enforcement remedy and leaves the bargained obligations enforceable. Treating every workplace statute as an immutable contract term would go beyond that source.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Do not announce that retroactive legislation is always valid or deny corporations ordinary property and contract protection. The substantial-impairment balancing branch is unnecessary after the missing-contract-term determination.

# Stringer v. Black

## A. STATUS

* **Version:** 1.
* **Approval Status:** Proposed and unapproved.
* **Current-Through Baseline:** The entering-law baseline in I.B, not the historical comparator.
* **Conditions:** Revalidate any specifically identified earlier simulated dependency when its actual holding becomes effective. No prior approved case-specific position has been supplied.

## B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Fifth Circuit’s Teague disposition and remand.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Direct federal habeas consideration of the vague-aggravator claim under date-eligible law. If the sentence lacks constitutionally adequate correction, a conditional writ should allow the State a lawful resentencing or authorized appellate cure; do not order unconditional release or automatic life imprisonment.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Does Teague bar relief from a Mississippi death sentence resting on an undefined heinousness aggravator?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply Teague rather than replace it.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Godfrey already prohibited the unguided aggravation at issue when Stringer became final on February 19, 1985; Mississippi’s weighing structure does not make the requested protection a new rule.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply Teague rather than replace it. Godfrey supplied a prohibition on unguided aggravation before this sentence became final. The challenged Mississippi factor performs formal work in weighing death against mitigation; that feature strengthens, rather than weakens, the existing requirement of guidance. Zant’s preservation of its distinct nonweighing result cannot become affirmative permission to put an invalid factor on Mississippi’s statutory scale. Maynard describes the same defect in closely equivalent wording. Clemons supplies permissible corrective options, not a new permission to challenge arbitrary sentencing for the first time. The proposed holding is that this particular application is old-rule; it is not that every subsequent capital decision applies retroactively.

The State’s concern about reopening final judgments is real under Teague, but finality does not authorize misidentifying a settled protection as novel merely because a later case explains a remedial branch. Conversely, the existence of the error does not establish that death could never be imposed here. A lawful reviewing state court may perform its own actual weighing if state law authorizes that function; harmless-error analysis asks whether the invalid factor affected this sentencing decision and places the Chapman burden on the State. The sentencing record, including mitigation and the prosecutor’s use of the invalid factor, must support the selected route. Stone proposes no new fact, numerical balancing formula, or presumption that remaining aggravators automatically cure error.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve the ultimate sentencing correction and any unpresented systemic capital challenge. Do not treat an invalid aggravator as necessarily negating death eligibility where valid eligibility findings remain, or conflate eligibility, selection, harmlessness, and reweighing.

* **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Affirm dismissal of the substantive-due-process claim within the verified scope.
* **Stone's Remedy and Remand Position:** Leave the judgment undisturbed on the reviewed substantive claim. Order no permit, damages, or procedural-due-process remand; do not revive the abandoned taking or expand to equal protection.

## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Do the pleaded land-development delays and irregularities state a substantive-due-process claim?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the distinction between ordinary unlawful administration and a federal substantive deprivation.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Assuming the asserted property entitlement, the operative allegations of delay and irregular land-use administration do not establish the additional federal substantive deprivation required; only that claim is reviewed.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** Apply the distinction between ordinary unlawful administration and a federal substantive deprivation. The pleaded delays and irregularities receive favorable treatment, but PFZ’s characterization of review as a sham is not itself an accepted finding of improper purpose; their characterization as illegal or arbitrary under local law is not itself the missing constitutional conclusion. The sources do not establish a fundamental right to this development approval or a federal guarantee that land-use officers correctly exercise every state-law authority. The operative allegations, as treated below, describe a disputed project process without the further supported circumstances that would convert it into the constitutionally oppressive or legally irrational deprivation required by this branch. Stone would therefore affirm on that narrow ground while assuming, without finally deciding, the asserted property entitlement.

He would not adopt the broader suggestion that available state review cures every substantive wrong. That proposition confuses the substance of official conduct with the procedure for correcting it. A properly supported claim of an independently unconstitutional deprivation would require its own adjudication despite available review. Here that distinction does not justify importing Zinermon or Mathews, because the procedural question was expressly excluded. Nor may Stone treat disputed pretrial material as part of the complaint after the unpreserved enlargement was rejected. His concern with sham government action guides scrutiny of the pleaded facts under the existing substantive boundary; it does not turn every allegation bearing that label into a constitutional cause of action.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Reserve the precise property entitlement, procedural due process, equal protection, and officer defenses unnecessary to the dismissal. No immunity exception for knowing falsification is presented by this substantive pleading question.

### Issue 1: Who calculates presentence detention credit under §3585(b), and when?

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach only the question and necessary antecedents identified in I.A, subject to its threshold limitations.
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the new text within the sentence-administration scheme.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The Attorney General through BOP computes §3585(b) credit after federal sentence commencement, and custody already credited to another sentence cannot be counted again.
