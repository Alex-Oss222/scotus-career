# Hudson v. McMillian

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote for Rehnquist, White, Kennedy and Souter. Stevens joined Parts I, II-A, II-B and II-C and concurred in the judgment. Blackmun concurred in the judgment. Thomas dissented with Scalia.
* **Historical Holding:** Serious injury is not an indispensable element of prison excessive force; the good-faith-discipline versus malicious-and-sadistic inquiry applies to force claims. De minimis force ordinarily falls outside the Amendment, subject to its stated exception for force repugnant to the conscience of mankind.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only; the historical extension of Whitley is not entering simulated law.
* **Historical Sources:** [503 U.S. 1](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/1/).

# United States v. Nordic Village, Inc.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for Rehnquist, White, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas. Stevens dissented with Blackmun.

# McCarthy v. Madigan

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9–0 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for White, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter. Rehnquist concurred in the judgment with Scalia and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The federal prisoner seeking only money damages was not required to exhaust the then-existing BOP grievance process; the Court considered the absence of congressional command, lack of monetary relief and limited countervailing exhaustion benefits.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparator only; later statutory exhaustion law and the historical result do not become entering simulated law automatically.
* **Historical Sources:** [503 U.S. 140](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/140/).
