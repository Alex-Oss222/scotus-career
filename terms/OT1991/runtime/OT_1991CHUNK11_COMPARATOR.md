# Lujan v. Defenders of Wildlife

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7–2 in the judgment; six Justices concluded that standing was absent.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for six on injury and procedural standing; Part III-B on redressability had four votes. Kennedy, joined by Souter, concurred in part and in the judgment. Stevens concurred in the judgment on statutory grounds. Blackmun, joined by O’Connor, dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The organizations lacked the requisite personal injury; a generalized procedural citizen-suit theory could not supply Article III standing. The redressability discussion was a plurality, not an independently controlling majority holding.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 504 U.S. 555 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/555/).

# Republic of Argentina v. Weltover, Inc.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed unanimously, 9–0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** Argentina’s bond issuance was commercial in nature, and nonpayment in New York had a direct domestic effect; the Court assumed without deciding sovereign due-process personhood.

# Ankenbrandt v. Richards

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously in the judgment, 9–0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White wrote for six Justices. Blackmun concurred in the judgment; Stevens, joined by Thomas, concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The statutory domestic-relations exception covers divorce, alimony, and custody decrees, not this tort damages suit. Neither Younger nor Burford justified abstention here.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 504 U.S. 689 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/689/).

# Allied-Signal, Inc. v. Director, Division of Taxation

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for White, Stevens, Scalia, and Souter. O’Connor dissented, joined by Rehnquist, Blackmun, and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The unitary-business limitation was retained; the unrelated investment gain could not be included on this record. An operational asset relationship can support apportionment without a unitary payor/payee enterprise.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only, with no force as simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion, 504 U.S. 768 (1992)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/768/).
