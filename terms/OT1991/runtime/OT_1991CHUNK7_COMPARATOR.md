# Suter v. Artist M.

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7–2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist majority; Blackmun dissent joined by Stevens.
* **Historical Holding:** The reasonable-efforts provision was not privately enforceable through §1983 and supplied no implied action.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 347 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/347/).

# Freeman v. Pitts

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8–0 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy majority; Scalia and Souter separate concurrences; Blackmun, joined by Stevens and O’Connor, concurred in the judgment. Thomas took no part.
* **Historical Holding:** Incremental withdrawal is permissible, considering compliance, necessity of continued control, and good faith; independent demographic imbalance does not itself require continued racial balancing.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 467 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/467/).

# Jacobson v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5–4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White majority, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, Souter, and Thomas. O’Connor dissented, joined by Rehnquist and Kennedy, and by Scalia except Part II.
* **Historical Holding:** The prosecution failed to prove predisposition independent of its 26-month campaign beyond a reasonable doubt.

* **Historical Authority:** 503 U.S. 540 (1992), comparator only, with no simulated precedential effect.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical judicial disposition](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/503/540/).
