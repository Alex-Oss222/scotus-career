# Foucha v. Louisiana

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** White's Parts I and II were joined by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, and Souter. Part III's equality discussion lacked O'Connor and was a plurality. O'Connor concurred separately; Kennedy dissented with Rehnquist; Thomas dissented with Rehnquist and Scalia.
* **Historical Holding:** The challenged continued confinement of a non-mentally-ill insanity acquittee violated due process. O'Connor reserved materially different, narrowly drawn detention regimes.
* **Historical Authority:** The majority due process holding and the narrower opinion support are comparison only, not entering simulated law.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 71, opinion structure and text](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/71/).

# Riggins v. Nevada

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 7-2 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote for six Justices; Kennedy concurred in the judgment. Thomas dissented, with Scalia joining except Part II-A.
* **Historical Holding:** Nevada failed to establish justification for compelled medication through trial; the unexplained treatment order and substantial risk of trial impairment required reversal. The Court reserved the unpresented Eighth Amendment claim.
* **Historical Authority:** Comparison only. The historical opinion did not establish every condition under which competency-restoration medication would be permissible.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 127](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/127/).

# Wyatt v. Cole

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 6-3.

# Wade v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for the Court.
* **Historical Holding:** Refusal of a substantial-assistance motion is constitutionally reviewable, but assistance alone or generalized accusations do not justify discovery or a hearing. The Court required a substantial threshold showing and found Wade's proffer insufficient.
* **Historical Authority:** Statutory substantial-assistance and constitutional-review comparator; its discovery threshold must be tracked separately from the underlying power to review an unconstitutional refusal.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 181](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/181/).

# Coleman v. Thompson

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court denied the stay. Blackmun dissented and Souter would grant. The reported order identifies those positions without a complete separately enumerated roll call.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Short per curiam; Stevens concurred in denying the stay and would deny certiorari. Blackmun wrote a dissent; Souter's contrary stay position was noted.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court relied on the district court's rejection of a colorable innocence claim and distinguished another pending case. It did not conduct a new trial or announce a comprehensive freestanding-innocence test.
* **Historical Authority:** An application disposition, not a plenary merits resolution. Historically presented to Chief Justice Rehnquist and referred to the full Court.
* **Historical Sources:** [Official reporter record, 504 U.S. 188](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-504/USREPORTS-504-188); [text reproduction](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-supreme-court/504/188.html).

# Burson v. Freeman

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5-3; Thomas did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun's plurality was joined by Rehnquist, White, and Kennedy. Kennedy also concurred. Scalia concurred in the judgment on nonpublic-forum grounds. Stevens dissented with O'Connor and Souter.
* **Historical Holding:** The challenged 100-foot electioneering boundary survived. Four Justices applied strict scrutiny and relied on the historical need for restricted polling-place approaches; the fifth used reasonableness review.
* **Historical Authority:** The result had five votes; the plurality's entire rationale did not. Comparator only.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 191](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/191/).

# United States v. Burke

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 7-2 in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Blackmun wrote for five Justices. Scalia and Souter separately concurred in the judgment. O'Connor dissented with Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The old Title VII backpay recovery was taxable; the majority treated the restricted remedial range as inconsistent with a tort-like personal injury for the exclusion.
* **Historical Authority:** A statutory holding confined to the applicable recovery and legal version; comparison only.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 229](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/229/).

# Evans v. United States

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote with different joins by part; White, Blackmun, and Souter joined throughout, O'Connor joined Parts I-II, and Kennedy joined Part III. O'Connor and Kennedy wrote separately. Thomas dissented with Rehnquist and Scalia.
* **Historical Holding:** A separate affirmative act of inducement is not an element of official-right extortion; the charge adequately required the exchange, whose promised performance need not be completed.
* **Historical Authority:** Statutory comparator only; the divided opinion support does not convert every supporting argument into a uniform rationale.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 255](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/255/).

# Quill Corp. v. North Dakota

# SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8-1 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for a unanimous Court in Parts I-III and for Rehnquist, Blackmun, O'Connor, and Souter in Part IV. Scalia, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, concurred in part and in the judgment. White concurred in part and dissented in part.
* **Historical Holding:** Due process did not require physical presence on these purposeful contacts; contrary indications were overruled. The Commerce Clause collection rule was retained under substantial nexus and stare decisis.
* **Historical Authority:** The two constitutional grounds and the separate joins must be tracked independently. This historical revision is not entering simulated law.
* **Historical Sources:** [504 U.S. 298](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/504/298/).
