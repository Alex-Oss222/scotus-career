## Controlling holding

A general verdict on a multiple-object conspiracy does not require reversal merely because proof is insufficient as to one lawful object when another object sufficiently supports conviction under the unchanged reasonable-doubt requirement. *Stromberg* and *Yates* address legally unavailable theories; *Turner* governs factual insufficiency. The Court does not decide legal invalidity, failure on every route, element-diluting instructions, independent unanimity questions, or harmless error.

**Authority and law after decision:** Seven participating Justices join the Court's holding; Blackmun supplies an eighth judgment vote. The legal-versus-factual alternative distinction enters simulated law on December 3 without changing *Winship* or *Jackson*.
