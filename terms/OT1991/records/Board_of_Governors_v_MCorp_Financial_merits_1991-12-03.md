# Canonical Decision Record — Board of Governors v. MCorp Financial, Inc.

## Event and chronology

- **Matter/event/date:** *Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System v. MCorp Financial, Inc.*, Nos. 90-913 and 90-914, 502 U.S. 32 (1991); consolidated merits decision, December 3, 1991.
- **Posture/questions:** Whether § 1818(i)(1), the Bankruptcy Code, or *Leedom v. Kyne* permits district-court injunctions against two pending Board enforcement proceedings.

## Participation and entering law

- **Participating:** Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter. **Nonparticipating:** Thomas, because argument occurred October 16 before his oath.
- **Entering law:** 12 U.S.C. § 1818(h)(2), (i)(1); 11 U.S.C. § 362; 28 U.S.C. § 1334; *Leedom v. Kyne*, 358 U.S. 184 (1958). Earlier simulated holdings were immaterial.
- **Snapshot:** Section 1818 expressly barred courts from affecting covered notices and orders while preserving review of a final Board order; the Bankruptcy Code excepted governmental police/regulatory proceedings.

## Stone

- Approved version 1.0 remained compatible. Stone votes to reverse No. 90-913 and affirm No. 90-914; he rejects interim jurisdiction while reserving regulation validity and any situation in which statutory review is genuinely unavailable.

## Public action, judgment, and opinions

- **Judgment:** No. 90-913 reversed; No. 90-914 affirmed. Eight to zero. Both district-court injunctions and their associated filing procedure fall. Review remains available under § 1818(h)(2) after a final order.
- **Opinion:** Opinion of the Court by Stevens, assigned by Stone and joined by all participating Justices.

## Law of the decision

- **Holding 1:** Section 1818(i)(1)'s plain jurisdictional bar deprives a district court of power to enjoin prosecution of the two pending Board proceedings. Unanimous Opinion of the Court.
- **Holding 2:** The Bankruptcy Code neither qualifies nor supersedes that bar: the proceedings fall within § 362(b)(4), and the cited provisions governing estate property do not reach these ongoing nonfinal administrative proceedings. Same authority.
- **Holding 3:** *Leedom v. Kyne* does not permit interim review where § 1818(h)(2) supplies meaningful review of validity and application after a final order and § 1818(i)(1) clearly precludes interim intervention. Same authority.
- **Limits/nonreach:** The Court does not decide the source-of-strength regulation's validity, unsafe-practice allegations, or the § 23A charge. It does not decide a case in which the statutory review route is unavailable.

## Continuity

- Adds the three jurisdiction/review propositions above. No broader presumption or exception is changed. Both Supreme Court dockets terminate; administrative proceedings may continue. Manifest item completed; cursor advances to December 3.
