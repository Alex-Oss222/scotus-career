# Admitted Source Record — Clarence Thomas oath and circuit reallotment

- **Instrument/event:** Clarence Thomas took the judicial oath and entered on duty as Associate Justice; the simulation's complete October 23, 1991 circuit reallotment became operative.
- **Authority and provenance:** The term-opening Standing State and `foundation/COURT_COMPOSITION.md`, validated when OT1991 opened; historical oath date is October 23, 1991. This record implements the already fixed simulated allotment rather than inventing a new Court action.
- **Effective date:** October 23, 1991, before the October 29 *Campos* application.
- **Institutional state changed:** The Marshall seat ceased to be vacant. Thomas became the most-junior Associate Justice. Six Justices remain necessary for a quorum. Thomas may not participate in cases argued or submitted before this date unless reargued or resubmitted.
- **Circuit allotment after change:** Stone—D.C., Fourth, and Federal; Souter—First; Scalia—Second and Third; O'Connor—Fifth; Stevens—Sixth and Seventh; Blackmun—Eighth; Kennedy—Ninth; White—Tenth; Thomas—Eleventh.
- **Scope and transition:** Every allotment except the Eleventh remains as it stood at opening; Kennedy relinquishes the Eleventh to Thomas and retains the Ninth. O'Connor therefore acts as Fifth Circuit Justice in *Campos*.
- **Source location:** `state/STANDING_STATE.md`; `foundation/COURT_COMPOSITION.md`.
- **Validation:** Final and committed. This is an institutional Admitted Source Record, not a judicial holding.
