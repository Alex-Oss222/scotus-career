The Court does not decide consent or suppression.

## Opinion and controlling holding

A per curiam opinion speaks for all eight participating Justices. A timely substantive motion asking a criminal court to reconsider an adjudicated question suspends finality under *Healy* and *Dieter* even when it renews a ground earlier abandoned; the full appeal period begins when that motion is decided. The December 13 motion made the suppression order nonfinal through January 3, so the January 30 notice was timely. Successive motions, bad faith, merits waiver, consent, and the search remain undecided.

**Precedent treatment and law after decision:** *Healy* and *Dieter* control without an abandoned-ground exception. This holding enters current simulated law on October 15, 1991.
