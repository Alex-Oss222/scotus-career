# ADMITTED SOURCE RECORD — Clarence Thomas judicial oath and circuit reallotment

**Source event:** Clarence Thomas takes the judicial oath on the Marshall seat line.  
**Authority and provenance:** OT1991 opening Standing State and `foundation/COURT_COMPOSITION.md`.  
**Effective date:** October 23, 1991.  
**Validation status:** Validated and committed before the next Court event.

Thomas becomes the ninth seated Justice and the most-junior Associate Justice. He may participate only in matters argued or submitted on or after this date unless an earlier matter is reargued. The complete circuit reallotment effective today assigns the Eleventh Circuit to Thomas, retains Kennedy on the Ninth, and leaves every other October 7 allotment unchanged.
