# Canonical Decision Record — Wooddell v. IBEW Local 71

## Event and chronology

- **Matter/event/date:** *Wooddell v. International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 71*, No. 90-967, 502 U.S. 93 (1991); merits decision, December 4, 1991, sharing the uncoordinated start-of-day baseline.
- **Questions/posture:** Whether the Seventh Amendment provides a jury trial on an LMRDA damages claim and whether LMRA § 301(a) reaches an individual member's suit alleging breach of a union constitution.

## Participation and entering law

- **Participating:** Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter. **Nonparticipating:** Thomas, because argument occurred October 16.
- **Entering law:** Seventh Amendment; LMRDA; LMRA § 301(a); *Teamsters v. Terry*, 494 U.S. 558; *Reed*, 488 U.S. 319; *Smith v. Evening News*, 371 U.S. 195; *Plumbers and Pipefitters, Local 334*, 452 U.S. 615. Same-day cases are not entering law.
- **Snapshot:** *Terry* emphasized the legal/equitable character of the remedy. Section 301 reached contracts between labor organizations, including union constitutions, and was not categorically confined to suits by contracting parties.

## Stone

- Approved version 1.0 remained compatible. Stone reverses and remands on both questions while reserving breach, standing not contested, officer liability, damages, and other claims.

## Public action, judgment, and opinions

- **Judgment:** Sixth Circuit reversed and case remanded, eight to zero. The LMRDA damages claim is jury-triable and the § 301 claim lies within federal subject-matter jurisdiction; no merits remedy is awarded.
- **Opinion:** Opinion of the Court by White, assigned by Stone and joined by all participating Justices.

## Law of the decision

- **Holding 1:** A union member seeking the pleaded money damages under LMRDA Title I is entitled to a jury trial. The damages are legal, not restitution incidental to reinstatement, and the LMRDA action resembles a personal-injury action. Unanimous Opinion of the Court.
- **Holding 2:** Section 301(a) extends to an individual union member's suit alleging violation of a union constitution that is a contract between labor organizations where the member is a beneficiary. “Between” describes the contracts, not the permissible plaintiffs. Same authority.
- **Application/rationale:** Wooddell alleged, and the local admitted for pleading purposes, an interunion contractual structure and breach through referral discrimination. Uniform federal construction would be frustrated if the same contract depended on the plaintiff's identity.
- **Limits:** Subject-matter jurisdiction establishes no breach, standing ruling beyond the uncontested posture, officer liability, damages, or resolution of fair-representation and state claims.

## Continuity

- Adds the LMRDA jury-trial and § 301 union-constitution propositions. *Terry*, *Smith*, and *Local 334* are applied, not altered. Matter completed; chronology cursor remains December 4 after the same-day group.
