# Canonical Decision Record — Southwest Marine, Inc. v. Gizoni

## Event and chronology

- **Matter/event/date:** *Southwest Marine, Inc. v. Gizoni*, No. 90-584, 502 U.S. 81 (1991); merits decision, December 4, 1991, sharing the uncoordinated start-of-day baseline.
- **Question/posture:** Whether an occupation enumerated in the LHWCA forecloses Jones Act seaman status, and whether administrative primacy, benefits, preclusion, or estoppel bars the suit.

## Participation and entering law

- **Participating:** Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter. **Nonparticipating:** Thomas, because argument occurred October 21 before his oath.
- **Entering law:** Jones Act; 33 U.S.C. §§ 902(3), 903(e), 905, 913(d); *Wilander*, 498 U.S. 337; *Swanson*, 328 U.S. 1; *Caputo*, 432 U.S. 249; *Tipton*, 375 U.S. 34. No earlier same-term holding materially changed this law.
- **Snapshot:** Crew members were expressly excluded from LHWCA employee status, and Jones Act seaman status turned on the vessel and worker relationship rather than a requirement to aid navigation.

## Stone

- Approved version 1.0 remained compatible. Stone affirms; rejects categorical job-title foreclosure, administrative-first treatment, preclusion, and estoppel; and reserves disputed seaman facts and all underlying liability.

## Public action, judgment, and opinions

- **Judgment:** Ninth Circuit affirmed, eight to zero. Summary judgment remains reversed and the action returns for factual resolution.
- **Opinion:** Opinion of the Court by White, assigned by Stone and joined by all participating Justices.

## Law of the decision

- **Holding 1:** A maritime worker in an occupation enumerated by the LHWCA may be a Jones Act seaman because the LHWCA expressly excludes a master or member of a vessel's crew. Enumeration alone therefore does not establish exclusivity; status is a mixed, fact-specific inquiry into the vessel and the worker's relationship to it. Unanimous Opinion of the Court.
- **Holding 2:** Congress did not require Jones Act suits to await an administrative coverage determination. Voluntary receipt of LHWCA benefits without adjudicated coverage neither precludes nor estops the Jones Act suit; statutory credit prevents double recovery and defeats claimed detrimental reliance. Same authority.
- **Application/limits:** Genuine disputes remain over whether the platforms are vessels in navigation and whether Gizoni's connection suffices. The Court does not find him a seaman or decide negligence, unseaworthiness, maintenance, or cure.

## Continuity

- Adds the two LHWCA/Jones Act propositions above; no general primary-jurisdiction test is created. Matter completed; cursor remains December 4 after same-day processing.
