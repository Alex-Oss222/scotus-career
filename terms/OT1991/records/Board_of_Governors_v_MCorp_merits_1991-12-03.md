## Participation, judgment, opinion, and remedy

Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter participate; Thomas does not participate, with no reason assigned. The Court unanimously reverses in No. 90-913 and affirms in No. 90-914. Stone assigns the opinion to Stevens, who writes for all eight. The injunction against the source-of-strength proceeding is removed and both Board proceedings may continue through the statutory review channels. The Court decides neither regulation validity nor asset enforcement.

## Controlling holdings

Section 1818(i)(1) deprives the district court of jurisdiction to enjoin the pending Board administrative proceedings. Bankruptcy jurisdiction does not supersede that express channel: the Board is not another court for §1334(b), the regulatory proceeding falls within §362(b)(4), and proceeding toward a nonfinal order does not itself seize estate property. *Kyne* does not permit premature district-court review where Congress expressly precludes interference and meaningful review of a final Board order remains available.
