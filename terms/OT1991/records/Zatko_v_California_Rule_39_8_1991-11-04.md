## Participation, disposition, and writings

Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter participate; Thomas does not participate, with no reason assigned. By 5–3, White, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter deny the grouped fee-waiver motions under Rule 39.8. Stone, Blackmun, and Stevens would allow ordinary in-forma-pauperis filing and screening. The applicants may submit the filings with the required docketing fees and compliant petitions by November 25, 1991.

The Court acts per curiam, finding the current submissions frivolous and the repeated filing histories an extreme abuse warranting discretionary fee denial. Stone dissents, joined by Blackmun and Stevens, accepting present frivolousness for purposes of the discretionary decision but concluding that ordinary screening is preferable and preserves later colorable filings. The dissent does not challenge Rule 39.8's validity or establish an unrestricted right to free filing.

**Authority and law after decision:** This is an administrative filing disposition, not a merits decision on any petition. It applies existing Rule 39.8 discretion and creates no controlling merits holding. Stone's joined dissent is a material published noncontrolling position.
