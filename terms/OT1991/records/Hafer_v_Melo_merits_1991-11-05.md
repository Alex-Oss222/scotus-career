Stone, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Souter participate; Thomas does not participate, with no reason assigned. The judgment is affirmed 8–0. Stone assigns the opinion to O'Connor, who writes for every participating Justice. Proceedings may continue on the employees' personal-capacity claims; elements, qualified immunity and other defenses, liability, damages, and reinstatement remain unresolved.

## Controlling holding

A state officer sued in an individual capacity is a “person” under §1983 even when the challenged conduct was performed through official authority. *Will* governs States and officials sued in their official capacities; it does not convert a personal-capacity action into one against the State. State immunity does not independently bar the personal claim. The Court decides coverage, not whether the dismissals violated federal law or whether any relief is due.

**Precedent treatment and law after decision:** *Will* is clarified and preserved for official-capacity/State liability; *Scheuer* and *Graham* govern individual capacity. The holding enters current simulated law on November 5.
