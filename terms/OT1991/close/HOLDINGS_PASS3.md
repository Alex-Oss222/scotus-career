## Incorporation blocker: Wrigley's office and independent-contractor rule

The first controlling-holding block in [Wisconsin Department of Revenue v. William Wrigley, Jr., Co., June 19, 1992](../records/Wisconsin_Department_of_Revenue_v_William_Wrigley_Jr_Co_merits_1992-06-19.md) states that maintaining an office attributable to the company exceeds solicitation even if used for solicitation, subject to §381(c)'s express independent-contractor provision. Neither the record nor its render supplies that provision's operative terms or the definition needed to apply it.

This is a missing qualification on an adjudicated component, not a finding that the Court left the component undecided. Close cannot fill it by inference. The office/independent-contractor component is therefore withheld. The separately established employee solicitation/independent-function rule and the combined-activity triviality rule are incorporated at their actual employee-record scope. Their disposition does not depend on adjudicating an independent contractor's office.

Completion requires an authorized source-supported correction supplying the period-effective §381(c) terms and any necessary definition in the Canonical Decision Record and its downstream projections, followed by candidate completion. The source record was not edited. The candidate must not be treated as a complete synchronized replacement while this component is missing.
