**Audit date:** September 15, 2026.  
**Result:** Unresolved discrepancies remain. The proposed coordinated close is not ready for Commit.

## Scope and method

This fresh audit compares the three October 7, 1991 opening trackers, all 125 record files, the final manifest and Term Continuity Note in `workspace.md`, the three close candidates, and all fourteen public chunk renders. The effective Render Inputs and correction receipts were used to distinguish record defects from downstream projection defects. `STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.BLOCKERS.md` was checked against the current Wrigley record and candidates.

The governing sources are `AGENTS.md`, Engine §§1–3 and 11–15, the Render Contract, the Court Composition register, and the three tracker templates. Records control substance; renders are cross-checks. Casey version 1.5's expressly authorized five-Justice adoption is accepted as the adjudication. Its superseded modeling annex is historical audit material, not current law. The earlier separate-file correction convention for Holmes, Suter/Montana, Alaska and Harris is also respected.

The review combines semantic comparison of adjudicative rules, authority, limits, remedies and procedural state with file-wide extraction and cross-reference checks. It does not re-adjudicate votes or independently reverify every external source or every embedded historical modeling paragraph. Exact-match checks establish projection fidelity only for the fields checked; they do not establish the truth of a source or cure an incomplete audit annex. No external law or later factual event was admitted.

**Concurrent source changes:** The preservation check detected changes during the audit to `foundation/COURT_COMPOSITION.md` and `close/STANDING_STATE.candidate.md`. No audit-team write targeted either file. Their current OT1992 roster/allotment provisions were reread, the candidate was rechecked, and its added future-transition paragraph is included in finding 9. The initial and reconciled source hashes are retained under `tmp/`; this report does not claim that every source remained byte-identical throughout the task.

## Unresolved discrepancies

### 1. Wrigley's office rule and independent-contractor qualification are not consistently preserved

**Controlling source:** [Wrigley record](../records/Wisconsin_Department_of_Revenue_v_William_Wrigley_Jr_Co_merits_1992-06-19.md), lines 54–69, particularly line 56. The corrected holding separately states the company-office rule; §381(c)'s sales/solicitation and office branches; §381(d)(1)'s multiple-principal and holding-out requirements; §381(d)(2)'s exclusion of an independent contractor from “representative”; and the ordinary-home/temporary-meeting-place boundary.

The current downstream documents differ:

- [Holdings candidate](HOLDINGS.candidate.md), lines 505–525, retains only the employee-activity formulation and omits the company-office/independent-contractor distinction and its operative conditions. It also omits the record's material treatment of *Sorenson v. Secretary of Treasury*, which supplies the consistent-meaning basis for that distinction. The added employee-only framing does not supply a second home for the missing controlling proposition.
- [Standards candidate](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md), line 1465, describes an exemption for office maintenance through qualifying independent contractors but omits the office-specific condition that their activities consist **solely of making sales or soliciting orders for tangible personal property**. The multiple-principal definition alone does not satisfy that separate condition. The resulting office exemption is broader than the record's rule.
- [Chunk 12 Render Input](../render-inputs/OT_1991CHUNK12.md), line 838, and [public render](../output/OT_1991CHUNK12.md), line 683, retain the earlier reference to §381(c)'s express provision without supplying its conditions. They do not carry the corrected Holdings block unchanged or satisfy the instruction to state a material exception in its own terms.
- [Standards blocker note](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.BLOCKERS.md), lines 11 and 21, calls the component resolved. The record-level source gap was addressed, but coordinated downstream completeness remains unresolved.

**Needed resolution:** Project the corrected record's distinct office and contractor rules, exact conditions, and material precedent treatment into their proper candidate and public locations. Preserve the six-vote standard and five-vote application. No new vote, rule, or statutory interpretation is needed to identify or repair this mismatch.

### 2. Casey's current workspace still presents superseded law, positions and relief

The [workspace](../workspace.md), lines 310–316, records the authorized version 1.5 adoption and expressly acknowledges that deeper current-state copies remain stale. That acknowledgment is accurate but does not complete the required current-state projection.

Examples of the conflicting current text:

- Lines 322 and 326 still identify version 1.3 as current authority and public handoff.
- Line 3969 still sustains the fixed waiting period and physician-only delivery.
- Lines 5077 and 5288 say the Court does not enjoin the waiting, physician-delivery, referring-source or public-release duties.
- Lines 5274–5284 identify the earlier version, deny adoption of a complete framework, and describe Stone and Stevens's complete proposal as having two noncontrolling supporters.
- Lines 3 and 5290 still state that Casey public rendering awaits replacement, although the operative version 1.5 receipt itself identifies the user's already-edited render as the source of the authorized propagation.

The current [Casey record](../records/Planned_Parenthood_of_Southeastern_Pennsylvania_v_Casey_merits_1992-06-29.md) and the governing designation establish five-Justice support—Stone, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor and Souter—for the complete framework, with four changed provision dispositions at their exact scopes. The current Continuity Note and Neutral Projection contradict that authority. Their older majority/separate-position descriptions can also mislead subsequent Justice-specific continuity.

**Needed resolution:** Regenerate the affected current-law, published-position, remedy, version and render-status projections from version 1.5. Preserve historical receipts and the superseded annex as history. Do not reopen the user-authorized adoption.

### 3. Zatko's expressly open administrative condition has no recorded resolution or carry-forward

The [Zatko record](../records/Zatko_v_California_fee_motions_1991-11-04.md), line 6, denies the seventeen fee-waiver motions while permitting paid, compliant petitions through November 25, 1991. Line 30 expressly calls this an open administrative condition. No underlying petition merits were decided.

No later record in the audited set supplies compliance, noncompliance, a closing event, or petition dispositions for those dockets. The [manifest](../workspace.md), line 18, records the completed fee decision without preserving that condition. The current procedure/open-matter projections omit it, and the [Standing State docket](STANDING_STATE.candidate.md), lines 60–62, contains only Wyoming, Coleman and Reynolds.

The passage of the deadline establishes neither timely compliance nor a terminal Court action. Engine §2 requires open matters to remain accounted for until a dated terminal event or express carry-forward.

**Needed resolution:** Establish the administrative outcome and any ensuing Court action from an admitted source, or expressly carry the unresolved status under the appropriate natural docket identities. Do not invent petition denials, failure to pay, continued merits pendency, or new dates. This finding identifies a missing procedural source and carry-forward decision; it does not decide the petitions.

### 4. Hunter lacks the required audit support for its post-reconciliation commitment changes

The [Hunter record](../records/Hunter_v_Bryant_merits_1991-12-16.md), line 46, says O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter provisionally favored directed immunity, that the comparator confirmed that position, and that circulation then moved them to the narrower remand. Its general explanation is that the remand preserves existing immunity law while correcting allocation errors.

The record supplies no sanitized re-reconciliation audit confirming those changed positions and no Justice-specific, date-eligible basis for those departures. Engine §8 requires that audit when circulation materially changes a non-Stone commitment after reconciliation; the adaptive-annex requirements also require support sufficient to audit a nonobvious coalition change. General citations in the Court's holding do not document that required check.

**Needed resolution:** Return the missing reconciliation/Justice-specific support to the authorized correction process and preserve its conclusions and provenance. The close candidates faithfully repeating this record do not cure the record-validation gap. This audit neither reverses the decision nor selects replacement votes.

### 9. Standing State includes future workflow and institutional transition detail beyond the permitted opening setting

The [Standing State candidate](STANDING_STATE.candidate.md), line 61, correctly carries Coleman petition No. 91-8336 as pending, then adds that a proposed, unadopted certiorari-stage brief has been placed first in OT1992 chunk 1 at a simulated October 5 date.

The Standing State rule permits one line identifying the case, court below, reason for inclusion, established stage and source. It excludes procedural narratives and future workflow. The pending petition is established by its record; the unadopted briefing/scheduling note is not an established Court action or necessary statement of stage.

The paragraph added during this audit at line 50, “Mid-term qualification,” also describes White's June 28, 1993 departure, Ginsburg's August 10 accession, an intervening eight-member Court and a future Tenth Circuit reassignment requirement. Those future institutional events belong in the Composition register under the Standing State template's §1 boundary; they are not the Court's setting at the opening of OT1992. This finding does not dispute the pinned succession dates or extend White's authority after retirement.

**Needed resolution:** Retain the pending-petition docket entry and its canonical source; remove the proposed-input and scheduling commentary. Keep future roster/allotment transitions in the Composition register until effective, retaining the presently operative roster, allotments and general reallotment rule in Standing State.

**Needed resolution:** Limit both selected summaries to the public positions established by their records, or return an intended substantive addition to the authorized record-correction process. These additions do not become controlling law, but they change the published position attributed to a sitting Justice and therefore matter to future continuity. A source note or historical assumption cannot independently supply them.
