## INS v. National Center for Immigrants' Rights

*INS v. National Center for Immigrants' Rights* supports the conditional authority to prohibit **unauthorized** employment while preserving actual individualized work-authorization/status determinations and consideration of existing discretionary relief. Its record does not prescribe the criteria for receiving that discretionary relief. The register therefore states the authorization and required consideration only, expressly disclaims an eligibility test or entitlement, and supplies no omitted regulatory criteria. This is a scope boundary, not an unresolved Court decision or an added exception.
