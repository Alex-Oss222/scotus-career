## Unresolved discrepancies

### 1. Wrigley's Holdings entry still omits both the company-office rule and an operative condition of the contractor exception

**Controlling source:** [Wrigley record](../records/Wisconsin_Department_of_Revenue_v_William_Wrigley_Jr_Co_merits_1992-06-19.md), line 58; authority at line 60. The record states separately that maintaining an office attributable to the company exceeds solicitation **even when the office's purpose is solicitation**. Section 381(c) then supplies two independent-contractor branches: sales or order solicitation, and office maintenance where the contractors' in-state activities consist **solely of making sales or soliciting orders for tangible personal property**. Section 381(d)(1)'s multiple-principal and holding-out definition is a separate requirement; §381(d)(2) distinguishes an independent contractor from a representative.

The [Holdings candidate](HOLDINGS.candidate.md), lines 505–543, now has a separate contractor entry and includes the definitions and Sorenson treatment. However:

- Line 529 exempts office maintenance by a qualifying contractor without the office branch's separate **solely-sales-or-solicitation** condition. This states a broader exception than the record.
- Neither that entry nor the preceding employee-activity entry states the categorical company-office rule. The functional rule for ancillary employee activity does not supply the omitted office rule by implication.
- The [workspace](../workspace.md), line 3256, still reproduces the older office formulation merely “subject to §381(c)'s express independent-contractor provision,” without the provision's operative terms. It is an incomplete current-law projection after the source correction.

The [Standards candidate](STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.candidate.md), line 1465, [Render Input](../render-inputs/OT_1991CHUNK12.md), line 838, and [public render](../output/OT_1991CHUNK12.md), line 683, now state the complete distinction and conditions. Those parts of the prior finding are cleared. The record's corrected lineage and preservation status are also present.

**Required resolution:** Faithfully project the existing record's company-office rule and each distinct exception/definition into Holdings and the current workspace. Preserve the six-vote standard and five-vote application. No new adjudication is supplied by this audit.

### 4. Hunter's added reconciliation does not document the required audit of changed commitments

The [Hunter record](../records/Hunter_v_Bryant_merits_1991-12-16.md), line 46, records that O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter provisionally favored directed immunity, were confirmed in that position through historical reconciliation, and then changed to the remand during circulation. Engine §8 requires a recorded legal objection or narrower position, the material revision resolving it, and a sanitized reconciliation audit confirming the Justice-specific, date-eligible basis for a material post-reconciliation commitment change.

The new “Historical reconciliation” at lines 48–54 adds an explanation, but line 52 treats the absence of a separately stated historical objection as the absence of a recorded objection and describes Stone's opinion as the first individualized analysis presented to these Justices. That does not account for their own already frozen provisional and reconciled commitments. The shared Harlow/Anderson/Malley and Rule 56 explanation addresses final compatibility; it does not identify the prior legal barrier and minimum revision for each changed commitment or establish that the required sanitized reconciliation audit took place. No result location or provenance for that audit is supplied.

The same paragraph calls Blackmun's provisional position a “blind guess,” inconsistent with the record's express single-conversation/true-blindness-unavailable disclosure at line 41 and the governing prohibition on describing this process as blind. The appended material also has no dated correction/lineage statement identifying its relationship to the completed earlier annex.

**Required resolution:** Through the authorized correction process, supply the missing commitment-change audit conclusions, date-eligible support and provenance, reconcile the provisional/reconciled/final account, and preserve truthful mode and correction disclosures. This is a validation-evidence finding. It does not establish that the final votes or judgment must change; the faithful downstream candidates cannot themselves cure the gap.
