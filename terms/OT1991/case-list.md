# OT_1991_CASE_INDEX

**October Term:** 1991. Stone replaces Rehnquist.

**Calendar:** The dates below are the controlling inventory’s historical opinion dates, used for simulation ordering. They are not certiorari-grant dates. Every listed matter is authorized for this run; no matter is designated `[CERT GRANT]`.

**Posture:** MERITS and APPLICATION retain the inventory’s matter labels. Each brief identifies the actual decisional task, including original proceedings, summary dispositions, and interim applications. Every interim application goes to the participating full Court by referral and is decided as an order of the Court.

**Source status:** No prior simulated trackers, effective same-term decision records, or approved case-specific Stone supplements were supplied. New Stone positions are proposed and unapproved. The controlling index supplies the sequence, including among cases sharing a date. Every earlier effective simulated decision must be integrated to the extent relevant before a later matter is simulated. The briefs identify material dependencies for that revalidation; they do not adjudicate those decisions or treat proposed positions and historical outcomes as effective simulated law.
