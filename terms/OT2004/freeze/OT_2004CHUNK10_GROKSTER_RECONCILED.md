## Components and common operative commitments

**A — Intentional inducement.** A distributor who distributes a device with the object of promoting copyright infringement, established by clear expression or other affirmative steps directed to that object, bears secondary responsibility for resulting direct infringement. Sony's protection against imputing culpability from useful product distribution does not immunize that affirmative conduct. Kalem, 222 U.S. 55, 62–63, supplies purposeful promotion as a recognized ground; Napster preserves the distinction between lawful capability and culpable participation. The rule requires the object, affirmative conduct, distribution and resulting infringement; private disapproval of a business or generalized awareness of misuse does not suffice.

The principal protective objection is the danger of treating ordinary innovation as intent. All eight answer it through the culpable-conduct requirement: ordinary distribution, technical support and updates are insufficient in themselves. Infringement volume, advertising receipts and failure to filter may be relevant with supported affirmative evidence but are not independently sufficient. No freestanding monitoring or redesign duty follows. Patent provisions illustrate the distinction between distribution and inducement; they do not create the copyright cause of action.

**B — Current-version judgment and both motions.** The supported promotion, user-assistance and business evidence defeats the distributors' categorical legal immunity, with Grokster and StreamCast assessed separately. Earlier evidence may show the purpose and character of current distribution where an actual connection exists. The relevant culpable act can include distributing the tool with the established infringing object; liability is not restricted to a published message proved to have reached each user. Conversely, an unpublished promotional draft is evidence of purpose, not an established public advertisement or proof of resulting infringement by itself. Current-version review does not adjudicate excluded past-version damages.

All eight would vacate and remand for application of this rule to both actual cross-motions. The underlying direct infringement already established below and uncontested on appeal remains established; the motion court must address each defendant's secondary responsibility, causation and relevant scope under the appropriate inferences for each motion. Rejection of the defense motion's categorical legal ground neither grants the owners' motion nor commands a trial on every issue. Summary judgment remains available if the actual record supports it. The historical Court likewise directed reconsideration of MGM's motion rather than awarding it outright, 545 U.S. at 940–941.

**C — Distribution-based Sony application.** The eight share the continuing capability rule, but the additional application and proposed refinements divide them as stated below. Genuine current and prospective lawful uses matter. Unknown files are not proved lawful, copyrighted status is not necessarily unauthorized use, and available-file percentages are not actual-download percentages. The three protective votes cannot be added to the two adverse-to-summary-judgment votes as support for a common minimum-use test. If no additional supported joins arise in assembly, the common opinion leaves the disputed application without a majority resolution; that is an actual lack of agreement at the relevant level, not a unanimous decision that the claim disappears or is conceded.

**D — Other theories and relief.** All eight preserve Napster's separate knowing-assistance and benefit-plus-practical-supervision elements. They do not adjudicate final vicarious liability by treating inducement as a substitute for control. Any properly remaining independent claim receives application under its own elements. Monetary and equitable relief and any actually invoked section512 protection retain their respective predicates and limits. Further proceedings implement the legal rule; they are not an order to supply materials missing from the operator's research.
