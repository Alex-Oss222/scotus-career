# Johnson objective chronology supplement — neutral eligible

Control extraction under Engine §14 from the factual opening of the published judicial opinion at https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/03-9685.ZO.html and the bound United States Reports at https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep544/usrep544295/usrep544295.pdf . No Supreme Court merits resolution, legal reasoning, diligence standard or lineup is supplied.

- November 29, 1994: federal district court entered judgment after Johnson's guilty plea to cocaine-base distribution. The career-offender guideline enhancement under §4B1.1 relied on two Georgia drug convictions from 1989.
- December 22, 1995: the Eleventh Circuit affirmed the federal sentence. Its footnote said Johnson could apply under §2255 if he later obtained constitutional vacatur of the predicate state conviction; this was not a decision granting future relief.
- April 22, 1996: denial of certiorari made the federal judgment final.
- April 24, 1996: AEDPA took effect.
- April 25, 1997: Johnson's pro se request for a sixty-day extension for a §2255 attack was stamped filed. The district court denied it after the transitional one-year grace period, without prejudice to a motion invoking another statutory timing provision. The record contains no invocation or factual basis for an earlier mailbox date for that request.
- February 6, 1998: Johnson filed his Georgia habeas petition challenging seven guilty pleas entered between 1983 and 1993. One was a predicate of the federal career-offender enhancement. He asserted that waiver of counsel was not knowing, intelligent and voluntary.
- The Georgia court vacated all seven convictions after finding the available records failed affirmatively to show waiver of counsel. The State denied his allegations but did not submit hearing transcripts. This is the published account of the state proceeding, not an inference from missing research materials.
- Johnson filed his federal §2255 motion just over three months after that vacatur, relying on the order as newly available factual support. No exact vacatur or federal-motion date is supplied here because the read opening source states the relative interval.
- Johnson cited lack of education and proceeding without counsel, and said he acted once an inmate law clerk could assist. Those are his explanations, not findings that legally sufficient diligence or its absence has been proved.

The question concerns the later attack on a current enhanced federal sentence after state vacatur. The available materials do not establish that the parties litigated an independent Gideon exception in the original federal proceeding. No waiver, preservation finding or merits entitlement should be invented from that uncertainty.
