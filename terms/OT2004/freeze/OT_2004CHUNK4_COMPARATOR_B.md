## 33. Cherokee Nation of Oklahoma v. Leavitt

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Tenth Circuit reversed; Federal Circuit affirmed; both remanded. All eight participating Justices agreed in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for seven Justices. Justice Scalia concurred in part. Chief Justice Rehnquist took no part in the decision.
* **Historical Holding:** The Government was bound by its support-cost promises where sufficient unrestricted appropriations were available; internal allocation and the later provision did not extinguish these contract claims.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/631/).

## 35. Tenet v. Doe

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed, 9 to 0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist wrote for a unanimous Court. Justice Stevens concurred with Ginsburg; Justice Scalia concurred separately.
* **Historical Holding:** Totten barred the Does’ claims because they were based on alleged covert espionage agreements, notwithstanding their estoppel and due-process formulations.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/1/).
