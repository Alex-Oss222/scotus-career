## 30. Johnson v. California, No. 03-636

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5 to 3 among participating Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O’Connor wrote for Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Ginsburg concurred with Souter and Breyer. Stevens dissented separately; Thomas dissented with Scalia. Chief Justice Rehnquist did not participate in the decision.
* **Historical Holding:** Historical law required strict scrutiny of the racial reception-housing policy, with application left for remand.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/499/).

## 32. Roper v. Simmons

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Stevens concurred with Ginsburg. O’Connor dissented; Scalia dissented with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** The Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments prohibit execution for crimes committed before eighteen; Stanford v. Kentucky was overruled at that scope.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/551/).
