## 91. Mid-Con Freight Systems, Inc. v. Michigan Public Service Commission

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for Justices Stevens, Scalia, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg. Justice Kennedy dissented, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice O'Connor.
* **Historical Holding:** The federal system preempted additional requirements concerning its specified registration subjects, and the separate Michigan fee was outside those subjects.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 440; No. 03-1234.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/440/).

## 93. Kelo v. City of New London

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens wrote for Justices Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Justice Kennedy concurred. Justice O'Connor dissented with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia and Thomas; Justice Thomas also dissented separately.
* **Historical Holding:** The comprehensive economic redevelopment plan qualified as public use despite private implementation and the absence of parcel-specific blight. The opinions disputed how effectively the public-purpose standard constrains private transfers.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 469; No. 04-108.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/469/).

## 94. Gonzalez v. Crosby

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Kennedy, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer. Justice Breyer concurred. Justice Stevens dissented, joined by Justice Souter.
* **Historical Holding:** The limitations-only motion was not successive, but Gonzalez failed to show extraordinary circumstances warranting Rule 60(b)(6) relief.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 524; No. 04-6432.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/524/).

## 95. Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Allapattah Services, Inc.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed the Eleventh Circuit in No. 04-70, 5-4, while also reversing the separate First Circuit companion judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia, Souter and Thomas. Justice Stevens dissented with Justice Breyer; Justice Ginsburg also dissented with Justices Stevens, O'Connor and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 1367 permits sufficiently related claims below the amount threshold when a qualifying named claim and the other jurisdictional prerequisites are present; complete diversity remains distinct.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 546; Nos. 04-70 and 04-79, consolidated within this listed matter.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported consolidated opinion and dissents](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/546/).
