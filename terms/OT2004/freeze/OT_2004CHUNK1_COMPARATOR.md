## 1. Leocal v. Ashcroft

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

## 8. Kansas v. Colorado

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Historically all exceptions were overruled and the matter recommitted; the interest disposition was 8-1, with the other portions joined by all nine.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote; Justice Thomas concurred in part and in the judgment, and Justice Stevens dissented in part on interest.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court accepted the report, including post-1985 interest only on the corresponding later damages and the ten-year accounting approach.
* **Historical Authority:** Supreme Court decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/543/86).

## 13. Brosseau v. Haugen

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8-1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam; Breyer concurred, joined by Scalia and Ginsburg. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court granted qualified immunity because the vehicular-flight circumstances did not fall within sufficiently clear preconduct authority; it did not decide the underlying Fourth Amendment merits.
* **Historical Authority:** Supreme Court decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and separate writings](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/543/194).
