# Scoped historical comparator B

## 96. Orff v. United States

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court unanimously affirmed.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote for the unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 390uu authorizes joinder as a necessary party, not the farmers' direct suit solely against the United States and its agents. The Court did not need to decide intended-beneficiary status.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 596; No. 03-1566.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/596/).

## 97. Halbert v. Michigan

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court vacated and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Ginsburg wrote for Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer. Justice Thomas dissented, joined by Justice Scalia throughout and by Chief Justice Rehnquist except Part III-B-3.
* **Historical Holding:** Indigent plea defendants are entitled to counsel for first-tier Michigan Court of Appeals review, and Halbert had not waived that right by his plea on the described record.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 605; No. 03-10198.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/605/).

## 98. Mayle v. Felix

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed and remanded, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Ginsburg wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer. Justice Souter dissented, joined by Justice Stevens.
* **Historical Holding:** A new claim based on facts differing in time and type does not relate back merely because it challenges the same trial or conviction; a common core of operative facts is required.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 644; No. 04-563.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/644/).

## 99. Green Party of New York State v. New York State Board of Elections

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator exists for this listed lower-court case. The historical anchor is the Second Circuit's November 17, 2004 affirmance of the preliminary injunctions protecting the plaintiff organizations' enrollment information and data access. It was an interim judgment, not a final merits decree or grant of primary eligibility.
* **Historical Sources:** [Green Party, original Second Circuit opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/389/411/495266/).
