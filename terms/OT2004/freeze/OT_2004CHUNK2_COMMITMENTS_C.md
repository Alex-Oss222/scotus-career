## Illinois v. Caballes — January 24, 2005

### Provisional disposition and operative scope

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas and Breyer vote to vacate the Illinois Supreme Court's judgment and remand under a corrected federal rule. Souter and Ginsburg vote to affirm the suppression judgment on the distinct grounds below. The six would hold that the exterior use of a properly trained narcotics dog, confined to detecting contraband, during an independently lawful traffic detention and without unlawful added restraint, does not require separate drug suspicion. The traffic violation supplies detention authority, not authority for every investigation or physical search. The later trunk search still requires its own lawful predicate; the accepted individual reliability finding supports the stated alert's probable-cause use, without establishing universal canine accuracy.

The narrow grant accepts lawful traffic duration. No numerical grace period for extra detention is adopted, no reopening of that accepted duration premise is ordered, and no deliberate delay can be disguised as ordinary traffic business. The mandate removes the categorical independent-suspicion premise and returns any genuinely independent preserved state-law or other live ground; it does not itself order a conviction reinstated, declare every evidentiary objection defeated or create an exclusionary reliance defense from the 1998 conduct date.

### Supported alternatives and decisive objections

**Contraband-confined examination path.** Place and Jacobsen preserve an informational distinction, while Whren and Robinette supply antecedent detention authority and its stopping point. The strongest contrary argument is that actual dogs are not perfect filters and that a seized person experiences a compulsory new investigation. The six answer within the accepted record: constitutional classification does not promise universal evidentiary accuracy, the trial court found this alert reliable, and neither extra restraint nor disclosure of lawful facts by this method is established. The holding remains confined to that type of examination and does not validate all methods advertised as contraband detection.

**Suspicion-required path.** Souter and Ginsburg would recognize a protected added investigative intrusion despite lawful elapsed time, keeping Place's nonsearch exception confined to its distinctive premise. Its strongest objection is that it imposes a new suspicion condition for conduct which Place and Jacobsen exclude from the search category, and thus lets investigative purpose displace Whren. They answer that the initial stop remains valid and objective; the distinct compulsory examination, its asserted privacy exemption and the limited justification for holding this motorist are the objects of scrutiny. No universal empirical finding supplies their legal objection. The six decline this extension.

**Precedent treatment.** Place and Jacobsen are applied by the six at their confined informational level; neither authorizes added seizure. Whren is applied to the traffic predicate. Robinette is preserved for detention after completion. Knowles is distinguished because a physical citation search invokes a different intrusion and exception. Edmond is distinguished because its antecedent seizure lacked ordinary individualized justification and the decision expressly reserved canine classification. Kyllo is distinguished because its device revealed concealed lawful domestic information; its warrant rule is not altered. No same-day case supplies law here.

## Commissioner v. Banks and Commissioner v. Banaitis — January 24, 2005

### Provisional disposition and operative scope

All eight Associates vote to reverse both Courts of Appeals and remand each docket for the lawful calculation under the applicable tax-year rules. When an otherwise taxable recovery belongs to a client who retains ownership and control of the income-producing claim, the contractual contingent-fee portion paid to the lawyer is included in the client's gross income under section 61. Direct payment to the agent, uncertainty of eventual recovery and a lien protecting the fee do not themselves transfer the underlying claim. Oregon's stronger lien receives a separate application: the accepted retained client control and payment-security function do not establish a transfer of the source to counsel.

Inclusion is not disallowance of every deduction. The applicable old-year deduction and AMT provisions must perform their own work on remand; no dollar liability, fair-value recomputation or equitable exclusion is prescribed. Genuine transfer of the income-producing claim, a separately awarded statutory fee entitlement and newly proposed alternative tax theories are not decided. The Banks settlement is not recast as a separate statutory fee award.

### Alternative paths, new enactment and exclusions from the decision

**Agency attribution.** The client's retained control identifies the source; payment to the lawyer satisfies the client's contingent obligation. The strongest counterargument is that uncertain claims lack an assured income stream and counsel creates value through services at risk. Earl's attribution principle does not require that value or receipt already be certain when the arrangement is made. Counsel's contribution makes compensation economically intelligible without making the client cease to own the injury and claim.

**Property-transfer exclusion.** State law can establish actual property ownership, and a genuine transfer of the source would raise a distinct issue. The strongest case here is Oregon's superior lien. The accepted arrangement nevertheless secures payment and leaves the client in control of the claim and settlement; the lien's priority is not source ownership. All eight reject exclusion on this record while reserving materially different ownership arrangements.

**AMT and legislation.** The American Jobs Creation Act of October 22, 2004 is effective noncase law. Section 703's new deduction requires both fees/costs paid after enactment and a judgment or settlement after enactment; these old transactions fail its temporal conditions. Root control confirmed that the enactment will be preserved through an Admitted Source Record. Nothing is read retroactively. Its claim coverage and income cap need no advisory application to these old returns. The public decision can state this short temporal limit without an irrelevant catalogue of covered claims.

**Precedent treatment.** Earl and Horst are applied to retained-source attribution. Schleier and O'Gilvie are preserved as receipt-specific exclusion decisions, not invented assignment precedents. Burke remains a fractured judgment without a controlling general remedial-menu rule. The common rule decides both ordinary contingent compensation and this Oregon lien, with no consolidation of the two tax calculations into one judgment.

## Howell v. Mississippi — January 24, 2005

### Provisional disposition and operative scope

All eight Associates vote to dismiss the writ as improvidently granted. The federal instruction claim was neither meaningfully identified in the supplied state appellate presentation nor actually decided by the Mississippi Supreme Court. A chain of citations from state-law cases to Beck and similarity between the two doctrines do not establish notice on these papers. No mandatory citation formula is imposed: constitutional text, pertinent federal authority, valid incorporation or a sufficiently understandable federal description can identify a claim. The actual submission invoked the distinct state evidentiary instruction rule, and the record does not establish equivalent state and federal rules or actual federal adjudication.

This disposition leaves the state judgment in place without approving the instruction merits, deciding sufficiency of robbery proof, finding an instruction constitutionally adequate, or ordering a sentence correction. The Court need not decide whether its presentation rule is jurisdictional or prudential because no justified exception warrants review under either account. A reasoned explanation of this threshold disposition does not convert the DIG into a new merits holding about Beck or a freestanding binding enlargement of Baldwin.

### Alternative paths

**Threshold dismissal.** Gates's state-review requirement and Baldwin's notice reasoning support dismissal without reaching Beck. The strongest counterargument is that an all-or-nothing capital-verdict description unmistakably identifies Beck in substance. That description must be read in the actual appellate argument, which invoked a generally applicable state evidentiary rule. Neither a direct federal description in those papers nor an established rule of identity is supplied. The current formulation cannot be backdated into the filing.

**Reach the merits through contextual notice.** This is legally available when the papers or valid incorporation give understandable federal notice, and Stevens's Baldwin dissent supplies a concrete example. Its decisive obstacle here is the missing relevant premise: no unified state/federal rule comparable to the one he identified there is established, and the state court did not actually decide the federal issue. The eight decline to use the seriousness of capital punishment as a substitute for that showing. Beck, Schad and Hopkins therefore remain at their existing scope; no new evidentiary or lesser-offense ruling is made.

## Bell v. Cone — January 24, 2005

### Provisional disposition and distinct components

All eight Associates vote to set aside the Sixth Circuit's aggravator-based sentence writ and remand. All reject the proposition that failure to cite Dicks, standing alone, establishes failure to apply Tennessee's narrowing rule or an objectively unreasonable federal adjudication. None votes to affirm the existing writ on that premise. The present neutral record does not justify blanket reinstatement of the district court's complete sentencing judgment or termination of every remaining sentence ground.

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer join the complete remand rule: identify the actual merits adjudication and any preserved state default; apply amended section 2254 to the adjudication actually established, with clearly established law fixed to that adjudication's date; distinguish any actual aggravator narrowing from correction of a separately established jury/weighing defect; and resolve any surviving claim under its governing requirements. The direct-review constitutional cutoff is 1984 for that adjudication. Godfrey and Proffitt are available then. Later Sochor, Richmond and Lambrix do not automatically establish the contents of federal law in 1984, and their current remedial directions do not eliminate the statutory gateway. If review is available and an uncured nonstructural constitutional sentencing error is established, the State must prove harmlessness beyond a reasonable doubt under the governing Brecht rule or provide another actually authorized constitutional cure. Remaining aggravators and proportionality alone do not establish it.

Scalia and Thomas join the judgment and the no-citation/statutory-gateway correction. They reserve the complete contingent actual-cure instructions on this insufficiently specified merits/default record rather than joining an implication that later capital cases themselves defeat section 2254. Their reservation supplies no vote for automatic cure, automatic adjudication, or final denial of all remaining claims. The common judgment is therefore eight votes for vacatur and remand; the full substantive remand instructions have six direct joins. No writ, new hearing, capital resentencing or execution authorization issues now.

### Supported alternatives and their record limits

**Final rejection of this aggravator ground.** The State's strongest path is that its direct court actually discussed the victims' conscious suffering and manner of death in the terms of the authoritative construction; no citation was necessary. Early, Visciotti, the actual-narrowing portion of Sochor and the limited 1984 Godfrey/Proffitt baseline make that path legally substantial. Its present obstacle is that the supplied packet does not finally establish which federal claim was adjudicated on the merits, resolve the preserved default issue, or equate narrowing of one factor with correction of any distinct jury/weighing error. A completed final-denial mandate would therefore require premises this context cannot invent. The eight instead correct the legal inference and return the outstanding inquiries; Scalia and Thomas confine their joins accordingly.

**Preserve the existing writ.** Cone's strongest argument is that a vague jury aggravator infected weighing and the state opinion supplied only factual discussion rather than an authorized cure. Sochor, Richmond and Lambrix prevent automatic correction through surviving aggravators or the label of independent review. The obstacle is separate and decisive: these current rules do not prove a qualifying state merits adjudication, cross section 2254 at the proper historical-law cutoff, defeat actual default or establish this record's uncured constitutional error. Neither a missing citation nor a presumed unperformed cure supports immediate relief.

**Vacatur with bounded remand.** This path removes the proved analytical mistake while preserving actual adjudication of what the supplied record leaves unresolved. Its strongest objection is that a remand would merely prolong a claim if the state analysis already definitively resolves it. The mandate does not require needless fact development: the lower court may resolve any gate or application the actual supported record conclusively settles. It requires identification of the real adjudication and legal operation, not a ritual evidentiary hearing or a demand to repair an operator's source gap.

### Precedent and chronology treatment

Bell (2002) is applied to the amended section 2254 regime and its express preservation of remaining claims, not extended into a disposition of the aggravator issue. Early and Visciotti are applied to citation-free but substantive review. Godfrey and Proffitt are the identified pre-1984 narrowing sources. Sochor, Richmond and Lambrix retain current actual-cure and separate-gateway force without automatic backdating; Lambrix's own old-rule determination concerned November 24, 1986 finality and is not mechanically transplanted. Brecht's State-borne beyond-reasonable-doubt rule governs a properly reached nonstructural error's effect inquiry, not access to merits. Summerlin's contrary-to-history Ring retroactivity category remains law but is outside this claim and does not cancel AEDPA. No same-day case supplies antecedent law.
