## Components and common limits

- **C1 — Reach and protected interest:** Live prospective review of the particular semiclosed-primary condition. Reciprocal invited participation is protected association even when another formal registration remains. No completed election is reopened; no unlitigated registration or ballot-access provision is adjudicated.
- **C2 — Burden and justification:** Both supported current approaches use Anderson/Burdick contextual review. The invalidation approach identifies a substantial but not severe burden and insufficient fit. The validation approach identifies a limited registration-choice burden and adequate fit to identifiable-party, nomination-system and other-party continuity interests.
- **C3 — Judgment and remedy:** Affirm invalidation and remand for precise prospective protection against this application's categorical exclusion, or reverse and remand to sustain the challenged condition. Neither approach commands duplicate voting, entry into an unwilling party's primary, a court-designed complete election code or a new election.

The trial record does not establish confusion, insuperable administrative burdens, actual raiding, actual corrupted results or a need to protect the inviting LPO from its own choice. Nearly 24 percent of the described primaries being decided by five percent or less shows potential outcome changes, not political instability. Preventive interests do not always require a previously completed injury, but the two approaches differ over the adequacy of the actual structural connection here. Neither may replace the rejected findings with contrary factual assumptions.
