## 90. American Trucking Associations

**Supported record and event:** The reviewed charge is the $100 annual fee under Michigan §478.2(1) for each truck undertaking point-to-point intrastate commercial hauling. It is imposed equally on local and interstate carriers undertaking that activity. The record supports local size, weight, insurance and safety regulation and does not substantiate the claimed discriminatory practical effect or clearly excessive interstate burden. The assessment is distinct from §478.2(2) and the federal preemption question in Mid-Con.

**Applicable law:** Jefferson Lines, April 3, 1995, separates the actual local taxable event from interstate transportation and retains independent nexus, apportionment, nondiscrimination and fair-relation requirements. Its five-Justice rationale controls even though Scalia and Thomas joined only its judgment and O'Connor and Breyer dissented on external apportionment. Their actual reasons are available in the prior public render. Scheiner's highway-use flat-charge setting requires comparison with this local-hauling event rather than a categorical invalidation of flat fees. NEMA v. Sorrell, March 17, 2003, rejects a preliminary disparate-impact requirement for Pike review: an evenhanded local measure remains subject to a substantiated clear-excess inquiry. Its public separate position records Scalia and Thomas's assumed-Pike result without an affirmative balancing join.

The standards are **Local retail sales of interstate transportation**, present operation effective April 3, 1995, and **Interstate burdens of evenhanded local regulation**, present operation effective March 17, 2003. A local label does not exempt the fee from review. Conversely, no rule guarantees equal fees per mile when the charged event is participation in regulated local hauling.

**Paths:** Sustain the fee because the local event is genuine and the record establishes neither discrimination nor a clearly excessive burden; or treat the alleged per-mile disparity and multiplication of local fees as a demonstrated Scheiner-type interstate penalty.
