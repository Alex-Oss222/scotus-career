# Clingman — additive remedial compatibility receipt

**Event and scope:** May 23, 2005. This receipt clarifies implementation of the four affirming Associates' frozen remedy. It does not replace `OT_2004CHUNK8_CLINGMAN_RECONCILED.md`, change any judgment position, supply a current Chief position or determine assignment.

**Question presented to reconciliation:** Whether a prospective decree removing the categorical continued-registration exclusion for qualified inviting parties and otherwise eligible invited voters, while preserving lawful single-primary, verification and deadline rules, implements the frozen precise prospective relief; and whether Oklahoma may establish that a narrower party-specific decree fully cures the same common-policy defect.

**Determination:** Stevens, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg each join that formulation in full at the stated scope. It implements their existing C3 commitment rather than enlarging their substantive rule. Each joined the general remedial direction in Anderson v. Spear, November 30, 2004: the decree must correct the adjudicated unlawful operation for those governed by the policy unless the State establishes that narrower relief fully cures the violation. The exact current-term public extraction was reviewed for this clarification. No different remedial join is necessary for any of the four.

The defect they identify is the common categorical condition requiring surrender of an existing registration before otherwise eligible voters may accept a qualified party's voluntary primary invitation. The prospective decree may therefore protect parties and voters governed by that same unconstitutional condition. It must preserve the choice of a party that has not invited outsiders, one voter's single primary vote, lawful verification and lawful deadlines, and every independently valid eligibility condition. It neither compels a party to broaden its electorate nor adjudicates an unpresented challenge to other registration or ballot-access provisions. A deadline that has not been adjudicated unlawful is not removed by this decree.

Oklahoma may demonstrate that narrower party-specific relief fully corrects the adjudicated common-policy violation. The lower court must decide any supported contention under Anderson's actual cure requirement; narrowness is not established merely by identifying the named plaintiffs or preferring a smaller decree. This is a remedial showing, not permission to relitigate the settled defect or to demand a new substantive evidentiary proceeding on the existing record.

The four reversing Associates retain their frozen opposition to merits invalidation and therefore to an injunction predicated on that invalidation. Their earlier general Anderson remedial joins do not convert them into supporters of this judgment.

**Validation:** The clarification is consistent with the unchanged four affirming judgment positions and the operative current-term remedial rule. It adds no facial invalidation of the complete election code, automatic nationwide rule, new election, compelled participation, class-certification finding or hypothetical factual premise.
