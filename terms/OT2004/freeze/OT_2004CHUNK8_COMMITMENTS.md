## 65. Lingle v. Chevron U.S.A. Inc., No. 04-163

**Threshold and judgment.** All eight Associates reach the presented takings question and vote to reverse the Ninth Circuit's judgment sustaining invalidation on substantial advancement, and remand for a judgment rejecting that surviving ground. There is no preserved alternative compensation or due-process claim to manufacture on remand.

**Common sufficient rule, including its precedent consequence.** Whether a regulation substantially advances a legitimate governmental interest is not an independent test of whether property has been taken for purposes of the compensation command. Regulatory efficacy measures the relationship between a governmental means and its object; a taking inquiry identifies the character and burden of an invasion of property. Failure of efficacy alone therefore cannot sustain the challenged invalidation. Agins loses force insofar as it makes lack of substantial advancement a freestanding takings predicate. Del Monte Dunes's accepted purpose-relationship alternative, including its approval as an independently sufficient taking ground and the corresponding allocation of that now-displaced issue, likewise ceases to furnish authority for that independent predicate. That was an actual adopted alternative, not dictum.

**Unchanged components.** The Del Monte Dunes final judgment and single damages award stand. Its independent complete-deprivation sufficiency determination, finality and compensation-route propositions, municipal liability and compensation rules, constitutional jury entitlement in the defined legal action, and division of actual surviving factual and legal tasks remain at their stated scopes. Removing a substantive predicate does not abolish a jury right for an otherwise valid legal claim. Loretto's physical-occupation category, Lucas's total-loss rule with substantiated background-title limits, Penn Central's contextual analysis, and Dolan's demanded-dedication nexus and rough-proportionality rules remain distinct and operative. A dedication's unconstitutional burden is not ordinary review of whether a regulation succeeds economically.

**Alternative and principal objection.** Chevron's strongest path is adherence to the actually applied Del Monte Dunes alternative, with a narrower refusal to extend that particular-denial rule to general rent regulation. That protects reliance on a real holding and avoids treating an unchallenged instruction as immaterial. The objection to that path is doctrinal: the nature of a taking does not turn on whether the ineffective regulatory means is general legislation or a particular final denial. A sufficiently burdened owner may invoke the applicable property rule; a law forbidden for an independent due-process reason is not made lawful by paying compensation. The participating Associates select categorical correction of the mistaken predicate with precise preservation of independent holdings and final judgments. None approves de novo judicial choice among competing gasoline-market predictions as a substitute takings standard.

## 66. Johanns v. Livestock Marketing Association, Nos. 03-1164 and 03-1165

**Components.** Both dockets receive the same disposition. The operational government-message question, targeted funding, and compelled attribution are separate propositions. Unresolved producer-communications, statutory-power and administration claims return through their ordinary proceedings rather than disappearing with the certified First Amendment component.

**Government-control path.** Where the Federal Government determines the promotional policy and actually exercises control over the challenged messages through official selection, supervision and final content approval, the message is governmental for the presented compelled-subsidy objection. Government may fund that message through the lawful targeted assessment; the First Amendment does not require substitution of a general-revenue tax merely because some assessed producers oppose the message. Here the Secretary's appointment and removal powers operate together with content approval and departmental participation in development. This is actual direction of the message, not only regulatory review of an independent speaker. Producer drafting does not defeat that classification.

The statute does not require a particular dissenting producer to endorse a message. A generic identification of the producer community as the funding source does not establish that the law compels personal assent by each objector. A concrete claim of attribution to an identified dissenter retains its distinct factual and legal inquiry. The source of funds, institutional authorship and individual endorsement must not be collapsed.

## 67. Clingman v. Beaver, No. 04-37

**Common threshold.** All eight Associates reach the live prospective challenge. The protected reciprocal association of the inviting party and invited voters exists even while the voters retain another registration. Completed elections are not reopened, and no unlitigated ballot-access or registration provision is invalidated.

**Invalidation path.** The condition imposes a substantial but not severe associational burden: accepting the invitation requires surrender of another formal affiliation, and the party loses its chosen lawful electorate. Under Anderson and Burdick, as actually applied in Timmons, Oklahoma must connect sufficient interests to that actual burden. The supplied findings reject confusion, insuperable administration and protection of the inviting LPO from raiding or swamping. Close elections can change when invited participants vote, but that possibility does not establish destabilization of other parties or corrupted nominations. The other parties retain control of their own unwanted participants; their registrants are not compelled to participate elsewhere. On this record, identifiable-party and stability interests do not justify categorical exclusion from a consenting party's primary where one-person/one-primary administration can preserve the voter's single nominating choice. Affirm the invalidation and remand for precise prospective relief against this application.

**Validation path.** The reciprocal association is real but the required registration choice is a limited condition on formal participation in a nomination system. Oklahoma may use a single declared affiliation to link a voter to the party whose official nominee the voter helps select, and to sustain identifiable, durable parties. That interest reaches the system and other parties, not only paternalistic protection of the LPO. The invitation alone does not eliminate it. The rule permits entry after changing registration and permits the party to invite independents. Preventive design need not await proof of an actually corrupted election. Reverse the Tenth Circuit and remand for judgment sustaining the challenged condition, without reviving the rejected confusion, administration or raiding findings.

**The competing precedent claims.** Timmons is controlling protection of consenting cross-nomination under substantial-but-not-severe review, not a historical affirmance of a fusion ban. The invalidation path applies its demand for an actual fit and rejection of protecting established parties from competition. The validation path distinguishes a candidate's multiple consensual designation from the current voter's election of which formal party nomination to participate in; it must explain why the system-definition interest is stronger here rather than merely repeat Timmons's rejected competitive-protection ground. Jones protects an unwilling party from forced outsider selection and expressly reserves invitations. Tashjian protects invitations to independents and reserves the distinct interests implicated by other-party registrants. Neither case automatically selects the outcome. Anderson v. Spear confirms attention to actual interests and fit in its own speech and contribution settings; it does not transfer strict scrutiny to every primary condition.

## 68. Deck v. Missouri, No. 04-5293

**Components and posture.** The preserved claim concerns visible handcuffs, leg irons and a belly chain throughout the replacement capital penalty proceeding. The sentencing judgment is reviewed directly; guilt remains intact. Visibility and objection rest on the supplied record, not an assumed rule for hidden security or a passing sighting.

**Protection path.** Due process forbids the routine use of visible restraints before the capital sentencing jury unless the trial court determines that circumstances particular to this defendant and proceeding justify them, including actual danger, escape risk or courtroom order. A prior conviction alone and generic juror reassurance do not supply that justification. The judge retains reasonable security discretion and may consider the defendant's supported history; the rule does not demand ignoring an actual threat or waiting for an incident.

The innocence presumption no longer determines guilt, but the jury still must choose punishment fairly. Chains can communicate present dangerousness through the court's own compelled display, impair participation and consultation, and compromise the dignity and reliability of the capital adjudication. Allen and Holbrook supply the distinction between necessary control and an inherently prejudicial measure. Riggins supports attention to actual justification and supported effects that cannot be reconstructed, but its medication holding did not already decide this sentencing-display issue.

**Effect and remedy under that path.** Unjustified visible shackling is constitutional trial error, not automatic structural error. On this preserved direct-review claim, Chapman places on the State the burden to establish beyond a reasonable doubt that the error did not contribute to the death sentence. Deck need not prove the counterfactual jury decision. Smith v. Texas supplies current, closely analogous allocation of harmlessness and sentence-specific relief for a preserved capital error; it is not the source of the new restraint prohibition. Reverse the Missouri judgment and remand under this burden. If the State cannot make that showing on the full penalty record, the death sentence must be vacated and the State must provide a lawful new penalty proceeding or a lawful noncapital sentence. The supplied handoff does not independently establish a completed full-record harmlessness adjudication, so the remand may perform that actual task without retrying the constitutional rule or inventing a security justification.

**Affirmance path.** Conviction has removed the presumption of innocence and has already disclosed serious criminality. The prior guilt-stage authorities do not establish that visible restraints at punishment are intrinsically unconstitutional absent a particularized finding. On this view, an unproved risk of incremental prejudice or impaired consultation is not enough to set aside the sentence; a defendant must establish that the actual sentencing was unfair. This path preserves ordinary security authority and does not find a particular threat that the trial judge never identified.

## 69. Medellín v. Dretke, No. 04-5928

**Common jurisdictional and source premises.** All eight Associates accept reviewability of the Fifth Circuit's judicial certificate denial under section 1254(1), as Slack establishes. ## 71. Arthur Andersen LLP v. United States — May 31, 2005

### Component commitments and operative propositions

**A1 — Culpability.** Reverse the Fifth Circuit's construction. For the charged 2000 version of §1512(b)(2), knowing corrupt persuasion requires conscious wrongful persuasion of another person with the prescribed evidence-withholding or impairment intent. Knowledge of the statute or a legal conclusion that the precise conduct violates it is unnecessary. An instruction permitting conviction for mere interference with government factfinding, even on an honest and sincere belief the persuasion is lawful, does not adequately identify this wrongful awareness. Ordinary document management, legitimate advice and invocation of valid rights are not corrupt merely because they reduce information available to government; their labels likewise do not immunize a knowingly wrongful obstructive use.

**A2 — Official-proceeding object.** Reverse the charge insofar as it permits conviction without the persuader's contemplation of an official proceeding to which the withheld or impaired material would relate. Section 1512(e)(1) expressly permits anticipatory obstruction: the proceeding need not be pending or about to be instituted. Neither a subpoena, a known filing date, certainty that a proceeding will occur, nor success is required. The required proceeding-directed intent cannot be supplied by an abstract possibility that government might someday be interested in the records. This is a construction of the object in §1512(b)(2), not transplantation of Aguilar's §1503 pendency rule or of a separate known-probable-effect requirement.

The statutory qualifications remain distinct. Subsection (e)(2) removes admissibility and absence of privilege as prerequisites; it does not make lawful withholding itself corrupt. Subsection (f) removes knowledge of the proceeding's federal character, not the proceeding-directed intent just described. Subsection (d)'s affirmative defense requires solely lawful conduct and the sole intention of inducing truthful testimony, proved by the defendant by a preponderance; it is not a document-destruction exception or a transfer of the Government's element burden. The 2002 enactments do not supply elements for the 2001 charge.

**A3 — Actual-verdict consequence.** Set aside this conviction for prejudicial instructional error and remand for further lawful proceedings under the correctly stated elements. The jury was expressly allowed to convict without the contested culpability protection and without the required proceeding connection. The knowledge, timing and internal-discussion evidence permits competing inferences. A finding that records were deliberately made unavailable under the charge actually given is not a finding of conscious wrongful persuasion directed toward contemplated official use. Deadlock and subsequent conviction neither prove prejudice independently nor cure it. The Government cannot establish noncontribution beyond a reasonable doubt from this actual verdict and supplied record.

Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer treat these particular instructional errors as trial errors and apply Neder's qualified actual-verdict method by reasoned extension. A jury decided the charged transaction under an adequate reasonable-doubt structure; the defects concern the definition and omission of identified elements rather than elimination of the reasonable-doubt standard itself. They nevertheless reject harmlessness here because the affirmative authorization of the disputed insufficient mental state prevents a reliable verdict connection. Neder and Ring did not already classify every omitted element. Scalia and Ginsburg join the statutory rulings and setting aside the conviction but retain their genuine-equivalent-findings limitation; they do not join the broader inference method or its general extension. Neither approach permits appellate replacement of the jury's missing disputed findings.

The remedy is trial-error reversal, not acquittal. The supplied question and record establish no independent evidentiary-insufficiency disposition. Further prosecution must comply with the correct charge and other independently applicable constraints.

### Alternative-path assessment

The strongest affirmance theory treats deliberate persuasion plus the specific purpose of keeping records from official use as sufficient corruption. Its real strength is the need to reach purposeful obstruction before process issues. It fails on this charge because the definition of impropriety through impeding factfinding, together with express allowance of an honest lawful belief, permits persuasion that the enacted culpability term excludes. Requiring conscious wrongdoing does not require knowledge of statutory illegality or waiting for a subpoena.

The strongest opposing construction would require the pending judicial work and known probable effect described in Aguilar. That would disregard §1512(e)(1) and the difference in statutory objects. The narrower contemplated-proceeding requirement preserves anticipatory reach without criminalizing every routine destruction that might later frustrate investigation.

A first proper harmlessness inquiry below is a genuine alternative, supported by Neder's treatment of its fraud counts. The six qualified-review Justices reject that alternative here because this charge affirmatively removed protections at the center of genuinely disputed culpability, and no required equivalent finding or reliably connected whole-record inference bridges the defect. The two stricter-review Justices reach the same remedy because the verdict contains no genuine equivalent findings. An overwhelming-evidence shortcut is not an available alternative under either position.

## 72. Cutter v. Wilkinson — May 31, 2005

### Component commitments and operative propositions

**C1 — Facial Establishment question.** Reverse the Sixth Circuit's facial invalidation and remand. Section 3's neutral accommodation of private religious exercise under the exceptional restraints imposed by government custody does not, simply by providing protection greater than the Free Exercise minimum or greater than that afforded nonreligious interests, establish religion. Kiryas Joel permits accommodation beyond constitutional necessity while prohibiting denominational allocation of civic power and sectarian privilege. Section 3 relieves government-imposed impediments to private practice across faiths; it neither assigns general civil authority to a denomination nor requires religious adherence.

Agostini supplies the present single inquiry into the governmental act and function, with purpose, effect and entanglement as evidence; its former independent prongs are not a fallback. Agostini reserved general accommodation. C1 applies Kiryas Joel's existing accommodation permission to custody. Doe's April 26 prohibition on school-adopted devotional instruction is consistent with this distinction: a public institution's adoption of instruction as its own differs from allowing confined individuals to exercise their own faith. Neutral coverage is relevant but would not excuse a particular governmental devotional act or denominational preference.

**C2 — Exact statutory operation and constitutional boundaries.** The claimant must establish coverage and a substantial burden on that person's sincere religious exercise. Section 3 then requires government to demonstrate, through production and persuasion, that imposing that burden on that person furthers a compelling interest by the least restrictive means. Exercise need not be compelled by or central to the faith. Institutional expertise, safety, order, resource constraints and effects on other persons must receive their concrete evidentiary weight; they do not replace that defense with rationality review. Sincerity alone does not establish substantial burden or entitlement. Accommodation cannot disregard independent constitutional rights or compel unconstitutional governmental religious activity.

The federal-financial-assistance predicate and enacted commerce predicate are distinct alternatives. If commerce supplies the only basis, §4(g) withholds application where government demonstrates that all comparable substantial burdens, or removal of all those burdens, nationally would not produce an aggregate substantial commerce effect. That exception is not the ordinary §3 compelling-interest defense. Section 4(b)'s text for Free Exercise or §2 claims is not rewritten as a §3 allocation. Section 5 permits elimination of the burden by policy change, exemptions or another means eliminating it; it creates or precludes no right to religious funding, although compliance can require operational expense. The Act's broad-construction direction operates within its text and the Constitution. Its severability, Article III, PLRA and independent Establishment limitations remain operative.

**C3 — Remedy and retained questions.** Return the statutory claims and genuinely preserved defenses for adjudication, including actual coverage, substantial burdens, individual government justifications and lawful relief. This appeal concerns facial establishment, not a final award of the requested practices. Distinct Spending and Commerce power objections remain outside the limited question; Boerne's rejection of RFRA's universal state/local command under §5 neither validates nor invalidates these different predicates. Any former inmate seeking prospective relief must satisfy ordinary standing requirements. Appropriate relief retains its statutory and procedural requirements. No determination of a particular accommodation or a damages rule is made because those issues are outside the resolved facial question and lack the needed adjudication.

### Alternative-path assessment

The officials' strongest facial theory is the unequal protection of religion, reinforced by demands on other inmates and prison resources. Kiryas Joel forecloses the proposition that every accommodation exceeding the constitutional minimum is establishment. Government custody itself makes private practice unusually dependent on official permission, while the statute's neutral scope, substantial-burden trigger, demanding but real government defense and independent constitutional limits leave a substantial lawful operation. Potentially abusive or expensive requests require actual application, not presumed entitlement.

Conversely, an absolute-priority construction would allow sincere religion to displace every security or third-party concern. The text's government defense and constitutional limits defeat it. A deferential rationality replacement would avoid some difficult cases only by deleting Congress's adopted standard. The supported path preserves both the meaningful statutory protection and concrete institutional adjudication.

## 74. Tucson Woman's Clinic v. Eden — May 31, 2005

### Corrected authority and complete governing framework

The `OT_2004CHUNK8_TUCSON_TAFT_NEUTRAL_REFRESH.md` fresh neutral correction and its exact public Taft extraction were read before this commitment was completed. Taft's January 24 holding and individual positions supplement the original B law map. No Ohio statutory feature, clinical finding or unappealed procedural ruling becomes an Arizona fact.

Casey, as reaffirmed in Lawall, Greenville and Taft, supplies the following complete controlling six-step rule. Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer commit to every step and operative qualification below. Kennedy joins the protected-choice and necessary-care baseline and the narrower access ground expressly recorded in X1; he does not join the full residual framework. Scalia and Thomas do not join the constitutional abortion framework. Their separate independent-claim commitments below are not framework joins.

Before viability the patient decides whether to continue pregnancy without proving medical necessity or obtaining official approval of her reasons. The State may not prohibit that choice, confer effective control on another person, or prevent timely necessary life-and-health care. After viability, prohibition requires the life-and-health safeguard, including individualized physical and mental health considerations. Viability is individualized and medically supported, not a fixed constitutional week. Clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable care, not official approval of the patient's reasons; not every preference is a medical emergency.

1. **Identify the duty and protected baseline.** Identify the enacted command, authoritative construction, exceptions, enforcement consequences and relevant medical circumstances. Distinguish an actual obligation from assumed future administration. A previability prohibition, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care violates the baseline and cannot be rescued by justification. Necessary care must remain available before imminent death or catastrophic deterioration. Defining all noncompliant treatment as unsafe does not establish medical necessity.
2. **Identify actual function and purpose.** Identify what the obligation does and the asserted permissible objective, including safety, informed consent, truthful information, permissible parental involvement, health administration or financial accountability. The State may prefer childbirth and communicate truthful information. Moral disapproval does not independently justify obstruction, punishment of protected choice or burdens imposed for their deterrent effect. An obstructive purpose established by objective evidence of text, design, context and operation independently invalidates the requirement; a legislator's opposition to abortion alone does not establish that purpose. Voluntary persuasion differs from prevention through barriers. Evaluate each justification on its terms: medical claims need medical support, and deliberative claims need an explanation linking the particular obligation to informed decisionmaking.
3. **Establish actual interference, comparison and affected population.** Challengers must establish concrete additional interference caused by the challenged obligation. Relevant effects include extra appointments, material expense, consequential delay, loss of usable provider capacity, clinical risk, coercion and disclosure consequences affecting choice or access. Compare the actual system with and without the obligation while holding other relevant law and circumstances constant. For a challenged interacting package, identify the package and compare the system without it. Consider cumulative effects without counting the same burden twice. Define the affected population from the duty's operation before deciding constitutional injury, include evidence-supported subgroups, and keep the population stable. Do not average the burden across everyone pregnant or define the group as only those who prevail. Poverty, distance, transportation, employment and abuse matter insofar as they explain the State's added condition; identify that causal link. Exceptions, bypasses and alternative providers count only if actually usable. Meaningful interference means concrete, nontrivial additional burden on obtaining care or exercising the protected decision. It triggers review rather than establishing invalidity. No universal dollar, mileage or hourly threshold applies. Reliable clinical evidence, records, testimony and reasonable predictions can establish sufficiently demonstrated prospective injury before access is lost.
4. **Apply the substantive protections in order.** First, a baseline violation, unconstitutional obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle is independently invalid. A substantial obstacle seriously obstructs the affected population's practical opportunity for timely, clinically appropriate protected care; absolute impossibility is unnecessary, and success by some patients does not establish adequate access for others. A compelling interest cannot rescue an established obstacle. Second, remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Identify the severe intrusion and explain why the independent prohibitions did not already decide the issue; overlapping protections require no artificial boundary, but an obstacle may not be relabeled as justifiable severe interference. Serious intrusion into clinical judgment, health or protected confidentiality may require this scrutiny despite usable access. Third, for remaining meaningful interference the State must substantiate a permissible justification and its fit to the added obligation. Abstract interests and conclusory assertions are insufficient. When challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it; inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequences may answer that showing. The State need not select the cheapest imaginable policy or disprove every hypothetical alternative. Reliable experience and reasonable predictions may suffice; particular legislative findings, a new study in every case, scientific certainty and numerical benefit calculations are not required. Once justification and fit are established, courts may not substitute their preferred policy. Below meaningful interference this heightened access inquiry ends, while prohibited-purpose claims, independent constitutional protections and ordinary lawful-regulation requirements remain available.
5. **Protect clinical judgment and truthful communication.** Evaluate medical justifications against competent evidence, accepted professional standards, the patient's circumstances and substantial contrary evidence. Substantial professional agreement receives serious evidentiary weight, without a professional association's veto or conclusive weight for a clinician's bare assertion or an official's unsupported preference. Necessary care must remain timely; unnecessary committee vetoes and documentation functioning as prior permission may not displace appropriate clinical judgment. Duties must be sufficiently definite and applicable fault requirements respected. Later reasonable medical disagreement alone does not establish every element of a punishable offense; lawful enforcement against fraud, malpractice and professional misconduct remains. Required medical information must be accurate, nonmisleading and material to informed consent. Concise factual assistance notices may inform choice without ideological assent; clinicians may explain limitations and correct misinformation. Accuracy does not alone validate burdensome timing, delivery or certification. Distinguish useful confidential oversight records from public disclosure and records enforcing invalid obligations.
6. **Identify the violation and tailor relief.** Specify the duty or application that fails, why, and the corresponding relief. Preserve independently lawful requirements under applicable severability law and expressly address dependent certifications, report fields and sanctions. An individual claim does not fail because others comply; broader relief requires a corresponding showing. An indivisible mandatory condition may be invalidated when its ordinary operation imposes demonstrated meaningful interference and the State fails to justify it. Voluntary compliance in some circumstances does not alone validate compulsory compliance. Otherwise confine relief to the established applications or groups. This qualification of a strict no-valid-applications approach does not remove proof of causation, scope or inadequate justification. For cumulative burdens, remedy the challenged obligation's or package's contribution without automatically invalidating all regulation. Replacing the trimester timetable does not dissolve injunctions or authorize enforcement of every challenged provision; the mandate must specify the resulting duties.

The challengers establish the duty, stable population, causation and triggering interference before the State bears the corresponding justification and fit burden. Gestational age may support a medically justified safety requirement but a trimester boundary does not change the standard. The framework creates no general professional immunity, funding entitlement, constitutional fetal-personhood rule, conscience ruling, general entitlement to a particular drug or universal numerical facial-review formula. Taft's current necessary-care application requires competent individualized support for material risk of death or serious health harm, including grave medically established mental danger, and a method necessary for timely safe and suitable care; preference or marginal comparative advantage alone is insufficient. Those propositions do not establish that Arizona has already caused a necessary-care denial.

### Component commitments and operative propositions

**R1 — Power and actual class.** Reverse the Ninth Circuit's Commerce Clause ruling and vacate its direction to enter preliminary relief on that ground. The Commerce and Necessary and Proper Clauses permit including this unauthorized local cultivation and possession within the CSA's independently valid interstate controlled-substance regime because that inclusion is a reasonably adapted and integral means of controlling the supply and enforceability of the regulated substance. The Government identifies the actual interstate drug market and authorization controls; the enacted manufacture, distribution and possession class; and the genuine relationship between unauthorized local supply, demand substitution, fungibility and enforceability of those controls. Courts independently determine those legal boundaries and give Congress reasonable latitude within them.

Lopez requires a fitted connection before aggregation. Home Builders, Gibbs, Rancho Viejo and January 24 GDF supply the necessary-inclusion route; they do not require each included act to cross a border, be motivated by profit or independently have substantial interstate effects. The CSA application satisfies that route on its own supply-control operation, not on the wildlife cases' facts, generalized health costs or the word comprehensive. **R2 — Patient class and statutory/state boundaries.** The local, unpaid, physician-recommended and state-permitted characteristics do not place these activities outside the enacted federal class or render the established functional relationship nominal or pretextual. Congress need not establish that these particular patients sold, crossed state lines or diverted marijuana, and the Court makes no such finding. The Necessary and Proper Clause does not require proof that every medical exemption would measurably collapse enforcement or require the least restrictive possible design.

The enactment's actual exceptions remain. Sections 841 and 844 govern unauthorized manufacture/distribution and possession subject to their enacted authorization routes. Section 802 includes cultivation in production and defines distribution by delivery rather than payment; its separate preparation/compounding and practitioner limitations are not a general cultivation exemption. Schedule I and the research, registration and scheduling paths remain those effective on this date. State permission and a physician's recommendation do not amend federal authorization. Section 903 preserves state law except its positive-conflict condition; sustaining direct federal regulation of private conduct does not require California to criminalize the same conduct or administer the federal system.

**R3 — Preliminary remedy and preserved claims.** Remand for disposition of the remaining properly preserved claims and any request for preliminary relief under their own standards. ## R1 — Federal power: retained operative ground and joins

All eight Associates retain the necessary-inclusion ground stated in the baseline. The Commerce and Necessary and Proper Clauses permit including the challenged unauthorized local cultivation and possession within the CSA's independently valid interstate controlled-substance regulation when that inclusion is a reasonably adapted and integral means of carrying out the interstate system. The Government must identify the valid interstate regulation, the class Congress actually covered and a genuine functional relationship; courts determine those boundaries independently while respecting reasonable regulatory judgments within them.

The actual mechanism remains control of supply and possession of the same fungible substance, including local production's substitution for regulated acquisition and the practical enforceability of the closed authorization system. Sections 801, 841 and 844, read with the applicable definitions and authorizations, identify that relationship. The conclusion rests neither on generalized health or crime costs nor on the description of the statute as comprehensive. Lopez requires the connection before aggregation; Home Builders, Gibbs, Rancho Viejo and GDF supply the controlling necessary-inclusion route. The new decisions neither overrule that route nor qualify the complete GDF joins of the eight Associates.

Cutter does not provide a different Commerce route. Its statutory aggregation limitation does not adjudicate constitutional sufficiency, and the decision leaves the power objections open. The possibility of legislatively permitted accommodation also does not prove that the Constitution requires the medical subclass exemption sought here. Necessary inclusion is not a command to regulate every conceivable act without exception; it permits Congress's reasonably adapted actual inclusion within the independently established relationship. No rule requires proof that every conceivable medical exemption would collapse the interstate regime.

## R2 — Proposed patient subclass and statutory/state boundaries: retained

All eight retain the conclusion that local cultivation and possession, absence of payment, physician recommendation and California permission do not by themselves place this activity outside the enacted federal class or make the supply-control relationship nominal or pretextual. The patients' medical motivation does not become a sale or commercial finding; their activity nevertheless produces, holds or delivers the substance whose unauthorized supply the national regime controls. The necessary-inclusion ground does not require patient-specific border crossing, sale, diversion or independently substantial interstate effects. No such fact is newly found.

The CSA's actual authorization limits remain operative. Cultivation falls within production; delivery rather than payment defines distribution; the manufacture definition's preparation/compounding and practitioner limitations are not a general cultivation exemption. The date-effective Schedule I classification and research, registration and scheduling routes receive their own statutory operation. State permission and a medical recommendation do not supply federal authorization. Section 903's positive-conflict boundary remains distinct from a general occupation of state law; direct federal regulation of private conduct neither requires California to criminalize that conduct nor commands state administration of the federal system.

Andersen reinforces the need to preserve the particular statute's elements, but it supplies no basis for adding its wrongfulness or contemplated-proceeding elements to the CSA. Cutter's statutory accommodations do not amend federal drug authorization. Tucson's independent search and notice holdings do not establish a newly presented search or vagueness claim concerning Monson's seizure. Actual statutory liability remains separate from Congress's power; the present preliminary-injunction review establishes neither criminal guilt nor a comprehensive construction of every possible CSA defense.

## Strongest competing paths after the actual refresh

The strongest opposing Commerce path remains a confined, administrable class of local, unpaid, physician-supervised and state-permitted medical production and use whose exclusion would leave federal interstate controls materially intact. It properly insists on independent identification of a real relationship rather than deference to comprehensiveness or national health costs. The four new decisions do not supply the missing factual or legal separation from the CSA's actual substance-supply controls. The proposed subclass still produces and possesses that same fungible regulated substance, and a claimed medical destination outside the federal authorization system does not itself remove the practical enforcement and supply-substitution relationship. The original rejection of the exemption therefore stands for each Associate on the sufficient necessary-inclusion ground.

Cutter's recognition that some statutory accommodations are permissible does not establish that exclusion is constitutionally required or that the necessary-inclusion standard is a least-restrictive-means test. Tucson's medical protections arise from a distinct established right and do not convert serious need into an enumerated-power exception. The original boundaries remain real: an outside-class application, invalid underlying interstate regime, or nominal or pretextual relationship can defeat the asserted basis. The record and new law establish none of those defects here.

A broader government path based on treating all home production or consumption as economic and aggregating its national effects remains unnecessary. The specific supply-regime relationship suffices without adopting that general characterization. A commerce-fact remand remains available if genuinely material facts about that relationship are missing, but none is supplied by the intervening decisions. A discretionary dismissal likewise finds no new vehicle change in this record; Tory's and Medellin's materially different procedural circumstances do not establish a general preference for avoiding a properly presented merits question.
