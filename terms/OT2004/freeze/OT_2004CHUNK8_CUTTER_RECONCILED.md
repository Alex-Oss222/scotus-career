## Reconciled C1–C3 and operative qualifications

**C1 retained in full:** Section 3's generally available protection of private religious exercise against exceptional burdens of custody does not establish religion merely by exceeding the Free Exercise minimum or by protecting religious interests more generously than other interests. The actual function is relief from government-created constraints on private practice, with no necessary assignment of civil authority to a denomination, compulsory adherence or sect preference. Particular unconstitutional governmental devotion or denominational preference would remain independently prohibited. The proper disposition on this facial Establishment ground is reversal and remand.

**C2 retained in full:** The claimant must establish statutory coverage and substantial burden on sincere exercise. Section 3 then requires the government to demonstrate, with production and persuasion, compelling interest and least restrictive means concerning the burden on that person. Religious exercise need not be central to or compelled by a theology; sincerity remains a legitimate inquiry. Institutional experience, security, order, costs, resources and burdens on others receive their concrete evidentiary weight. They are neither automatically conclusive nor irrelevant. An accommodation cannot disregard other persons' constitutional rights or compel unconstitutional governmental religious activity. An excessive or unsafe demand must be assessed under the actual statute and constitutional limits, not through a presumption that sincerity guarantees victory.

The alternative assistance and commerce predicates remain distinct. Section 4(g)'s commerce-only aggregate-effect limitation is separate from section 3's justification defense. Section 4(b)'s Free Exercise/section 2 burden text is not rewritten as section 3. Section 5 permits government to eliminate the burden through policy change, exemption or another eliminating means; it neither creates nor precludes a religious funding right, although compliance can entail operational expense. Broad construction remains bounded by text and Constitution. Article III, the PLRA including applicable exhaustion, severability and the independent Establishment limitation retain their actual force. No particular accessory, staffing arrangement or expense is awarded.

**C3 retained in full:** Return the statutory claims and preserved defenses for ordinary adjudication of coverage, actual burdens, personal government justifications and lawful relief. The inquiry is facial establishment, not individual accommodation merits. Boerne's invalidation of RFRA's universal state/local command under section 5 does not decide RLUIPA's separate Spending or Commerce predicates. Those distinct power questions and other unresolved federalism objections are reserved at their actual scope. Former inmates' requests for future relief remain subject to standing; the existence of live claims does not adjudicate every released inmate's entitlement. No new damages construction, land-use holding or universal security rule follows.

## Thomas's additional position and exact historical reconciliation

The historical separate writing does substantive work beyond its criticism of Lemon: it rejects the contention that the Establishment Clause excludes every federal law concerning religion. It also distinguishes that Clause objection from Congress's enumerated-power authority. No record, question or legal change justifies erasing that entire additional explanation merely because the independent model did not predict a separate writing.

Thomas therefore retains a bounded additional position: federal legislation concerning religion does not necessarily constitute legislation establishing religion; this general accommodation provision neither necessarily compels a State to impose religious observance or support nor requires preference for a sect. A general claim that religion is exclusively a state subject cannot itself establish the facial violation. That is an additional sufficient explanation of his rejection of the categorical objection, not a decision that Congress possesses unlimited power concerning religion. Spending, commerce and other unadjudicated power challenges remain for their proper presentation.
