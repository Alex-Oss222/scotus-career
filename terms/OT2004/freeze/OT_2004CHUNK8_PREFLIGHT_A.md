## 65. Lingle v. Chevron U.S.A. Inc., No. 04-163

**Cutoff and posture.** May 23, 2005; argument February 22. Review concerns the Ninth Circuit's affirmance of a takings invalidation of Hawaii's service-station rent cap. The surviving theory is the law's failure substantially to advance its objectives. It is not a claim of compelled occupation, surrendered title, total loss or an independently preserved due-process claim. Neither Kelo nor San Remo has been decided and neither supplies law here.

**Neutral factual and remedial boundaries.** Competing economic predictions about dealer rents and gasoline prices are material to the lower court's efficacy approach. They do not establish the severity or distribution of a property loss under another test. The cap and economic record are accepted as supplied; no oral-argument concession or abandonment beyond the packet is added. Disposition of the selected test does not authorize adjudication of an absent Penn Central or due-process claim. Reversal of this invalidation need not declare every rent cap valid.

**Exact entering-law selections.** `state/holdings/property-and-economic-rights.md`, case headings Lucas, Dolan, Del Monte Dunes, Tahoe-Sierra. The compact slice retains their rules and direct authority. The operative Standards heading is **Juries in municipal §1983 taking actions**, present operation effective **Del Monte Dunes, June 16, 1997**. Its general jury rule is not the issue here, but its reserved preservation of the received Agins formulation is material. Lucas's complete-loss rule and substantiated antecedent-property exception remain separate from Penn Central's contextual inquiry. Dolan's government burden concerns identified compelled dedications. Tahoe-Sierra rejects manufacture of a total deprivation by selecting the precise moratorium interval and refuses to revive an abandoned contextual claim.

**Critical current-law distinction.** Del Monte Dunes is not merely a repetition of Agins language: its majority sustained an undifferentiated award after finding the evidence sufficient on both the received purpose-relationship and complete-deprivation alternatives. It expressly withheld comprehensive approval of every substantial-advancement formulation, but the accepted alternative did decisional work. Any categorical rejection of efficacy as a taking predicate must expressly explain the resulting limitation or displacement of that alternative and of Agins. The statutory damages vehicle, jury entitlement, total-loss rule and actual final judgment do not disappear automatically with a change in the substantive predicate.

**Supported competing paths.** The State's strongest ground is the mismatch between regulatory efficacy and the nature or severity of a property burden. A law could fail its purpose while imposing little property loss; compensation cannot make constitutionally forbidden arbitrary action legitimate. Chevron's strongest answer is the operative prior purpose-relationship holding, not a bare quotation from dicta. A Court retaining that independent test must explain the connection to the compensation command and the permissible degree of efficacy review. A Court rejecting it must preserve the distinct physical, total-loss, contextual and exaction fields and identify exactly what earlier precedent loses force. A narrower general-economic-regulation distinction could leave the particular permit-denial application intact, but needs a legal distinction grounded in the claim and authority rather than a factual label. The question does not require a new judicial definition of every due-process limit.

## 66. Johanns v. Livestock Marketing Association, Nos. 03-1164 and 03-1165

**Cutoff and posture.** May 23, 2005; argument December 8, 2004. Both dockets concern the same assessment and certified First Amendment component. Government speech was raised and adjudicated below. The case differs procedurally from United Foods's unpresented operational defense. Other producer-communications, statutory and administration claims are not silently disposed of by the certified judgment.

**Neutral record and statutory operation.** The 1985 Beef Act and implementing order impose a dollar-per-head assessment and authorize promotional and other program activities. The Secretary appoints the Board, retains the stated committee-removal power, approves advertising content, and department representatives participate in development. Producer participation and funding lines are part of the attribution inquiry. The statute does not command a particular dissenter to endorse an advertisement. The packet supports operational control facts; it supplies no finding that the public identifies each advertisement as governmental or associates a particular objector with it.

**Exact entering-law selections.** `state/holdings/first-amendment.md`: Glickman and United Foods. Standards: **Generic promotion assessments in collective agricultural marketing**, present operation effective **United Foods, June 25, 2001**, with Glickman's **June 25, 1997** collective-program permission retained. Glickman permits germane nonideological promotion within its substantially collective system, without attribution to dissenters and with independent expression intact. United Foods invalidates selected private promotion as its own compulsory-association justification in an otherwise independent market. Common benefit, free riding, majority approval and counteradvertising do not cure that defect. Actual government speech and independently justified functions remain expressly open.

**Independent questions.** Government's authorship and operational control, the constitutional consequence of targeted funding, and personal attribution are distinct. A legislature's promotional purpose and reserved veto alone need not conclusively establish whose message the actual operation conveys; conversely, producer drafting does not itself make speech private when a public official determines the message and its content. A valid government-speech classification resolves the private-subsidy objection only at that scope. It does not eliminate separate personal-endorsement, statutory-power, religious-establishment or discrimination constraints. No such additional violation is presumed here.

**Supported paths.** The governmental side can defend this campaign on actual executive selection and supervision and on the ordinary power to finance public messages through a lawful assessment rather than a general tax. The objectors can insist that operational authorship, public accountability and attribution cannot be replaced by nominal government status. If the campaign is private, United Foods requires an independently sufficient common function; commercial usefulness alone is inadequate under its controlling rule. A specific attribution injury could support a different remedy without invalidating the whole statute. The new holding must define the significance of a generic producer-funding line without declaring individual endorsement proved. Any reversal or vacatur must return unresolved claims for ordinary adjudication.

Consult Opinion Topology and Separate Positions. Keller, Barnette and Wooley supply the surviving pre-divergence distinction between government operation, germane compulsory support and compelled individual expression.

## 68. Deck v. Missouri, No. 04-5293

**Cutoff and posture.** May 23, 2005; argument March 1. Direct review of the replacement capital penalty proceeding. Guilt remains finally adjudicated for this review. Counsel repeatedly objected to the jury's seeing handcuffs, leg irons and a belly chain. The supplied record supports actual visibility. The trial court referred to the conviction and juror reassurance, without particularized current danger or escape findings. No concealed-restraint or passing accidental-sighting case is presented.

**Exact law.** `state/holdings/criminal-procedure.md`: Riggins and Sell. Standards: **Involuntary antipsychotic medication of an objecting trial detainee**, present operation **Sell, June 16, 2003**, with distinct Riggins and Weston components retained. Riggins requires actual overriding justification and reasonable alternatives for compelled medication, and allows relief for supported substantial defense risk that cannot be reliably reconstructed without proof of a counterfactual unmedicated trial. It did not establish a universal Chapman holding or decide the unpreserved sentencing-display theory. Sell concerns prosecution-only restoration treatment and does not itself authorize or prohibit restraints.

**Surviving pre-divergence authorities and functions.** Illinois v. Allen permits necessary courtroom control and recognizes the disadvantages of chaining an accused. Estelle v. Williams addresses compelled trial appearance and its timely-objection distinction. Holbrook v. Flynn distinguishes inherently prejudicial measures from ordinary security presence and asks about unacceptable risk rather than requiring jury admissions of prejudice. Chapman supplies the general direct-review harmless-beyond-a-reasonable-doubt rule for preserved constitutional trial error. Applying that rule to this new capital-restraint holding must be stated rather than misattributed to Riggins. These citations are authority functions, not quotations or invented holdings about this precise penalty procedure.

**Supported legal forks.** The conviction eliminates the presumption-of-innocence interest at guilt determination; it does not itself answer the separate reliability, defense-participation and dignity interests while punishment remains unresolved. Visible chains may communicate current danger at the point when the jury chooses life or death. The State's strongest argument retains real safety and escape-control authority, including supported facts from the defendant's history; a valid justification need not disregard that history merely because it predates the hearing. Deck's strongest answer is that conviction alone and routine reassurance supply no individualized necessity for every capital defendant. A rule extending protection to capital sentencing must retain justified security measures and identify the permissible exercise of trial-court discretion.

If no violation is found, the sentence survives this claim. If unjustified visible restraint violates due process, the Court must separately determine the harmlessness rule, its burden and the consequence for the sentence. It may not assume structural error or put the burden on Deck to reconstruct the jury's unseen alternate decision. A remand or new penalty proceeding must follow the adopted direct-review rule and any record-based harmlessness determination. The convictions are not disturbed by a penalty-only violation. No general noncapital, concealed-restraint or emergency rule is necessary.

## 70. Lofton v. Secretary of the Department of Children and Family Services

**Cutoff and posture.** May 23, 2005 scheduled merits review of 358 F.3d 804. The supplied route and timely review are accepted. Later Florida-law changes do not enter. The challenge seeks removal of the categorical adoption barrier and ordinary consideration, not an adoption decree, removal order or adjudication that every named applicant is fit.

**Material factual distinctions.** Florida permits gay adults to foster and serve as guardians but bars adoption under section 63.042(3), while allowing unmarried heterosexual applicants. The lower court's proposed future-marriage rationale is a legal justification, not a finding that these particular households harm children. Lofton's care of Doe from infancy and Houghton's longstanding care and co-guardianship of Roe matter to the pleaded existing-family claim. Houghton's placement followed the biological father's act and later consent to termination. Lofton's concern that a guardianship would cost foster payments and Medicaid protection explains his refusal without proving that every alternative is inadequate. The plaintiffs' disclaimer of a general fundamental right to adopt does not waive their distinct existing-family theory.

**Exact controlling law.** `state/holdings/civil-rights.md`: Shahar, Equality Foundation, Romer, Troxel and Lawrence. Standards **Intermediate scrutiny for sexual-orientation classifications**, present operation **Shahar, March 2, 1998**; **Fit-parent visitation choices and judicial preference**, present operation **Troxel, June 5, 2000**; **Private consensual adult intimacy and criminal prohibition**, present operation **Lawrence, June 26, 2003**. Equality Foundation applies Shahar outside civilian employment, December 3, 1998. No new scrutiny tier is needed. The State must demonstrate a genuine important objective and substantial fit to the actual classification. Moral disapproval, private bias, stereotype and merely conceivable post hoc reasons do not carry that burden. Experience and supported professional prediction may suffice without scientific certainty; neither least restrictive means nor an individual exception in every case is required.

**Equality application and remedy fork.** Child welfare and stable permanent care are genuine important objectives. The dispute is the relationship between those objectives and every orientation exclusion, given actual foster and guardianship reliance and heterosexual-single eligibility. Florida may distinguish permanent adoption from temporary placement, but must show why that distinction substantially supports the categorical line. The possibility that heterosexual single applicants may marry cannot perform the entire burden without a supported connection to the actual class and policy. Summary judgment based only on conceivable rational grounds uses the wrong standard. The lawful alternatives are a return for genuine unresolved material application questions, an adjudication that the developed record fails substantial fit followed by relief against the barrier, or affirmance only if the actual intermediate burden is satisfied. An invented factfinding requirement is as impermissible as a presumed failure from an unrecovered document. Relief removes the unconstitutional eligibility condition while preserving neutral fitness, parental-rights and child-interest adjudication; no adoption is automatically awarded.

**Independent family-liberty claim.** Troxel protects fit legal parents against ordinary judicial-preference substitution but has no comprehensive scrutiny rule and does not automatically transform foster status into adoptive parenthood. Smith v. Organization of Foster Families distinguishes the source and expectations of foster care while recognizing the significance of real bonds; it supplies no categorical rule that every such relationship is irrelevant. Lawrence decides private adult criminal punishment and expressly reserves civil status. The actual claim therefore requires identification of the existing protected relationship, the interference caused by the adoption barrier, and a corresponding remedy. It is not answered merely by rejecting a universal right to obtain a child. If equality supplies the complete requested opportunity for consideration, the independent substantive-due-process theory need not be reached. If the Court denies equivalent equality relief, it must address the preserved family-integrity theory rather than silently replace it with a disclaimed general adoption right. No absent custody, visitation, marriage or removal controversy is decided.
