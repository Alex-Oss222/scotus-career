## Cutter: keep statutory aggregation and constitutional power separate

Section 3's statutory alternative reaches a burden affecting commerce or whose removal would affect commerce. Where that is the only basis, section 4(g) withholds application if government demonstrates that all comparable burdens, or removal of all of them, nationally would not lead in the aggregate to a substantial commerce effect. Cutter gives those enacted provisions their distinct work while reserving the constitutional objections. It neither replaces the governing connection-before-aggregation rule nor accepts comprehensiveness as sufficient authority.

Thomas's published concurrence rejects the categorical proposition that any federal legislation concerning religion establishes religion. It expressly preserves enumerated-power questions. It cannot be converted into either a categorical federal police power or a new categorical immunity for local conduct. Each Justice's later GDF necessary-inclusion join remains the direct current public authority relevant to Raich.

## Tucson: preserve scope, independent claims and actual remedies

Its baseline bars, severe-interference scrutiny and meaningful-interference review are abortion rules grounded in Casey and reaffirmed by Taft. They do not follow merely from serious medical need in a different field. Those positions do not predetermine a drug-liberty, necessity or commerce conclusion, and no new search or privacy claim may be invented for Raich from an incidental analogy.
