## 17. United States v. Booker, consolidated with United States v. Fanfan

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Booker's judgment was affirmed and remanded; Fanfan's was vacated and remanded. The constitutional and remedial holdings each had a different five-Justice majority.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote the constitutional opinion, joined by Scalia, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg. Breyer wrote the remedial opinion, joined by Rehnquist, O'Connor, Kennedy and Ginsburg. Separate writings disagreed with the constitutional or remedial portions.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court applied the jury principle to mandatory Guidelines, then excised §3553(b)(1) and the incompatible appellate-review provision, producing advisory Guidelines and reasonableness review.
* **Historical Authority:** Supreme Court decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/220/).

## 24. Bell v. Cone

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded without a recorded dissent.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam; Ginsburg concurred, joined by Souter and Breyer, emphasizing the assumed actual state merits adjudication.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court rejected the inference from the missing Dicks citation and found the state court's actual analysis consistent with its narrowing law.
* **Historical Authority:** Supreme Court decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and concurrence](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/543/447).
