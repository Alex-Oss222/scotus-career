## Historical evidence and changed-law test

The historical judgment reversed the facial Establishment Clause invalidation and remanded, with all eight present Associate Justices joining the Court's reasoning. Thomas also supplied a separate historical federalism explanation. The majority treated institutional dependence as an exceptional government-imposed impediment to private exercise, required account of nonbeneficiaries and institutional security, and preserved neutral treatment among faiths. This is strong case-specific evidence for retaining every Associate's C1–C3 result and the substantive accommodation boundary.

The constitutional methodology has changed. Current Agostini retains a modified Lemon inquiry into the actual governmental act and function, with purpose, effects and entanglement as evidence. It did not decide prison accommodation; current Kiryas Joel supplies permission to accommodate beyond the minimum, and Cutter applies that permission to custody. This distinction applies individually. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas directly joined the current Agostini methodology. ## Reconciled C1–C3 and operative qualifications

**C1 retained in full:** Section 3's generally available protection of private religious exercise against exceptional burdens of custody does not establish religion merely by exceeding the Free Exercise minimum or by protecting religious interests more generously than other interests. The actual function is relief from government-created constraints on private practice, with no necessary assignment of civil authority to a denomination, compulsory adherence or sect preference. Particular unconstitutional governmental devotion or denominational preference would remain independently prohibited. The proper disposition on this facial Establishment ground is reversal and remand.

**C2 retained in full:** The claimant must establish statutory coverage and substantial burden on sincere exercise. Section 3 then requires the government to demonstrate, with production and persuasion, compelling interest and least restrictive means concerning the burden on that person. Religious exercise need not be central to or compelled by a theology; sincerity remains a legitimate inquiry. Institutional experience, security, order, costs, resources and burdens on others receive their concrete evidentiary weight. They are neither automatically conclusive nor irrelevant. An accommodation cannot disregard other persons' constitutional rights or compel unconstitutional governmental religious activity. An excessive or unsafe demand must be assessed under the actual statute and constitutional limits, not through a presumption that sincerity guarantees victory.

The alternative assistance and commerce predicates remain distinct. Section 4(g)'s commerce-only aggregate-effect limitation is separate from section 3's justification defense. Section 4(b)'s Free Exercise/section 2 burden text is not rewritten as section 3. Section 5 permits government to eliminate the burden through policy change, exemption or another eliminating means; it neither creates nor precludes a religious funding right, although compliance can entail operational expense. Broad construction remains bounded by text and Constitution. Article III, the PLRA including applicable exhaustion, severability and the independent Establishment limitation retain their actual force. No particular accessory, staffing arrangement or expense is awarded.

Before viability the patient decides whether to continue pregnancy without proving medical necessity or obtaining official approval of her reasons. The State may not prohibit that choice, confer effective control on another person, or prevent timely necessary life-and-health care. After viability, prohibition requires the life-and-health safeguard, including individualized physical and mental health considerations. Viability is individualized and medically supported, not a fixed constitutional week. Clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable care, not official approval of the patient's reasons; not every preference is a medical emergency.

1. **Identify the duty and protected baseline.** Identify the enacted command, authoritative construction, exceptions, enforcement consequences and relevant medical circumstances. Distinguish an actual obligation from assumed future administration. A previability prohibition, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care violates the baseline and cannot be rescued by justification. Necessary care must remain available before imminent death or catastrophic deterioration. Defining all noncompliant treatment as unsafe does not establish medical necessity.
2. **Identify actual function and purpose.** Identify what the obligation does and the asserted permissible objective, including safety, informed consent, truthful information, permissible parental involvement, health administration or financial accountability. The State may prefer childbirth and communicate truthful information. Moral disapproval does not independently justify obstruction, punishment of protected choice or burdens imposed for their deterrent effect. An obstructive purpose established by objective evidence of text, design, context and operation independently invalidates the requirement; a legislator's opposition to abortion alone does not establish that purpose. Voluntary persuasion differs from prevention through barriers. Evaluate each justification on its terms: medical claims need medical support, and deliberative claims need an explanation linking the particular obligation to informed decisionmaking.
3. **Establish actual interference, comparison and affected population.** Challengers must establish concrete additional interference caused by the challenged obligation. Relevant effects include extra appointments, material expense, consequential delay, loss of usable provider capacity, clinical risk, coercion and disclosure consequences affecting choice or access. Compare the actual system with and without the obligation while holding other relevant law and circumstances constant. For a challenged interacting package, identify the package and compare the system without it. Consider cumulative effects without counting the same burden twice. Define the affected population from the duty's operation before deciding constitutional injury, include evidence-supported subgroups, and keep the population stable. Do not average the burden across everyone pregnant or define the group as only those who prevail. Poverty, distance, transportation, employment and abuse matter insofar as they explain the State's added condition; identify that causal link. Exceptions, bypasses and alternative providers count only if actually usable. Meaningful interference means concrete, nontrivial additional burden on obtaining care or exercising the protected decision. It triggers review rather than establishing invalidity. No universal dollar, mileage or hourly threshold applies. Reliable clinical evidence, records, testimony and reasonable predictions can establish sufficiently demonstrated prospective injury before access is lost.
4. **Apply the substantive protections in order.** First, a baseline violation, unconstitutional obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle is independently invalid. A substantial obstacle seriously obstructs the affected population's practical opportunity for timely, clinically appropriate protected care; absolute impossibility is unnecessary, and success by some patients does not establish adequate access for others. A compelling interest cannot rescue an established obstacle. Second, remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Identify the severe intrusion and explain why the independent prohibitions did not already decide the issue; overlapping protections require no artificial boundary, but an obstacle may not be relabeled as justifiable severe interference. Serious intrusion into clinical judgment, health or protected confidentiality may require this scrutiny despite usable access. Third, for remaining meaningful interference the State must substantiate a permissible justification and its fit to the added obligation. Abstract interests and conclusory assertions are insufficient. When challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it; inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequences may answer that showing. The State need not select the cheapest imaginable policy or disprove every hypothetical alternative. Reliable experience and reasonable predictions may suffice; particular legislative findings, a new study in every case, scientific certainty and numerical benefit calculations are not required. Once justification and fit are established, courts may not substitute their preferred policy. Below meaningful interference this heightened access inquiry ends, while prohibited-purpose claims, independent constitutional protections and ordinary lawful-regulation requirements remain available.
5. **Protect clinical judgment and truthful communication.** Evaluate medical justifications against competent evidence, accepted professional standards, the patient's circumstances and substantial contrary evidence. Substantial professional agreement receives serious evidentiary weight, without a professional association's veto or conclusive weight for a clinician's bare assertion or an official's unsupported preference. Necessary care must remain timely; unnecessary committee vetoes and documentation functioning as prior permission may not displace appropriate clinical judgment. Duties must be sufficiently definite and applicable fault requirements respected. Later reasonable medical disagreement alone does not establish every element of a punishable offense; lawful enforcement against fraud, malpractice and professional misconduct remains. Required medical information must be accurate, nonmisleading and material to informed consent. Concise factual assistance notices may inform choice without ideological assent; clinicians may explain limitations and correct misinformation. Accuracy does not alone validate burdensome timing, delivery or certification. Distinguish useful confidential oversight records from public disclosure and records enforcing invalid obligations.

## Historical evidence and the genuine fork

The historical judgment was vacatur and remand, 6?3. Stevens?s opinion was joined by Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Scalia concurred in the judgment alone. O?Connor dissented, with Thomas and the historical Chief joining all except Part III; Thomas also wrote separately. Part III?s personal initiative preference belongs only to O?Connor. Historical authorship supplies no present assignment.

The five-Justice historical opinion treated production, distribution and consumption of a commodity with an interstate market as an economic class and relied on Wickard and the operation of the broader drug regime. Scalia instead emphasized the Necessary and Proper Clause, including its application to noneconomic conduct and its independent constitutional limits; he expressly did not join the economic characterization of simple possession. O?Connor and Thomas pressed both the appropriate class and the adequacy of the functional showing. They did not claim that every local drug prohibition is categorically invalid. Their argument was that an identifiable medical-use class could be administered without an established substantial effect or demonstrated need for federal inclusion. Thomas additionally maintained a narrower original meaning of commerce and a stricter necessity/propriety account. The strongest historical objection is therefore an actual constitutional boundary and evidentiary objection, not a bare preference for state policy.

That objection must be tested against current law rather than dismissed by describing the CSA as comprehensive. Home Builders, Rancho Viejo and January 24 GDF establish a necessary-inclusion route with an independently valid interstate regulation, the actual enacted class, and a genuine functional relationship independently assessed by the court. Rancho?s necessary-inclusion holding and remedy were joined by all eight Associates, including O?Connor and Thomas. GDF?s complete rule, application and remedy were also joined by all eight, and rejected a fresh independent-effects or evidentiary requirement for each local noncommercial species within the genuinely connected class. These are Justice-specific affirmative commitments, not merely precedents the two historical dissenters are asked to accept against their own recorded positions.

GDF does not decide the CSA application by analogy alone. An endangered-species system and a controlled-substance system have different objects and mechanisms, and the validity of one cannot supply the missing functional explanation for the other. Here the independently valid interstate drug regime governs the actual supply and authorized handling of the same substance. The enacted manufacture, distribution and possession controls operate together to distinguish authorized from unauthorized supply. Cultivation creates that substance, and possession retains it within that supply system; local source, absence of payment and medical destination do not alter its identity. Supply substitution, fungibility and the operation of those authorization controls furnish the concrete relationship. Section 801?s practical-differentiation and supply-control findings support that mechanism, rather than conclusively declaring federal power. General health costs, possible productivity effects and the label comprehensive are insufficient independent grounds.

### Why the medical subclass does not compel a different result

The strongest medical-subclass argument should be considered at its full force: take the accepted patient circumstances and physician recommendations seriously; do not assume diversion, sale, interstate movement or pretext; and allow that a State can identify medical patients and enforce its own safeguards. None of those propositions establishes a federal constitutional exemption. Under the current necessary-inclusion rule, Congress need not prove that each purpose-defined subclass independently substantially affects commerce or that every conceivable exemption would cause measurable failure. The operative question is whether inclusion of the actual cultivation/possession class is reasonably adapted and integral to the separately valid interstate supply regime, with the asserted local application genuinely within that relationship.

The relationship here survives even the proposed medical limitation: it concerns production and control of the regulated substance itself, and the authorization boundary for its supply. Medical purpose describes why the patient produces or possesses the substance; it does not remove production or possession from those controls. A State?s ability to administer a medical exception can be evidence bearing on the functional argument, but it does not transform the constitutional means inquiry into a requirement that Congress adopt that State?s alternative authorization system. The conclusion does not find California?s controls ineffective and does not require a patient-specific diversion inference. It finds the accepted activities directly within the federal supply mechanism at the level the current inclusion rule permits Congress to regulate. State permission remains effective within its own legal sphere, subject to the actual positive-conflict rule; it does not amend the federal exceptions. Direct regulation of private conduct neither requires the State to criminalize it nor requires state officers to administer the federal system.

This is also the answer to the historical household-production objection. Substitution for a purchased good, standing alone, would sweep too broadly. The reconciled ground does not declare every household activity economic or derive power from demand substitution alone. It requires a valid interstate regime, its actual production/authorization class and a genuine direct supply or enforcement relationship. A remote chain through general health, safety or productivity, an outside-class application, an invalid interstate regime or a nominal or pretextual relationship would fail. The CSA?s operation and the accepted cultivation/possession facts establish the needed relationship here; the Court does not treat Congress?s selection of any class as self-validating.

### Component commitments and operative propositions

**R1 — Power and actual class.** Reverse the Ninth Circuit's Commerce Clause ruling and vacate its direction to enter preliminary relief on that ground. The Government identifies the actual interstate drug market and authorization controls; the enacted manufacture, distribution and possession class; and the genuine relationship between unauthorized local supply, demand substitution, fungibility and enforceability of those controls. The Necessary and Proper Clause does not require proof that every medical exemption would measurably collapse enforcement or require the least restrictive possible design.

The enactment's actual exceptions remain. Sections 841 and 844 govern unauthorized manufacture/distribution and possession subject to their enacted authorization routes. The strongest patient path identifies a confined class of local, unpaid, medically supervised production and use, arguing that federal inclusion is unnecessary because that class can be administered without materially affecting interstate enforcement. The strongest broader government path would classify all production and consumption as economic and aggregate nationwide effects. The eight Justices need not adopt that more general characterization: the identified supply-regime connection is sufficient and preserves an enforceable boundary. Generalized national drug costs or a patient's motive alone cannot do the work. ## Retained operative grounds, statutory boundaries and remedy

**R1 remains the necessary-inclusion ground.** The Government must identify the independently valid interstate controlled-substance regulation, the enacted cultivation/manufacture, distribution and possession class, and a genuine functional relationship. Courts determine those legal boundaries independently while respecting reasonable congressional judgments within them. Inclusion must be reasonably adapted and integral to the interstate system. The CSA application remains supported by its actual control of the same substance's production, possession and authorized supply, with fungibility, substitution for regulated acquisition and practical differentiation supporting that direct mechanism. General health costs, comprehensiveness or demand substitution alone do not supply authority. An invalid underlying regime, outside-class application or nominal or pretextual relationship remains challengeable.

Lopez's connection-before-aggregation requirement and the actual Home Builders, Rancho and GDF necessary-inclusion holdings remain the controlling premises. GDF's unanimous current rule and application do not decide a different statute automatically: the separate CSA mechanism must still be established. That independent application was performed in the original reconciliation and is not weakened by any new decision. Cutter's reserved power questions provide no substitute route in either direction.

**R2 remains bounded by the actual class and enacted authorizations.** Locality, nonpayment, physician recommendation and state permission do not alone sever the direct substance-supply relationship or place the accepted cultivation and possession outside the federal class. No patient-specific sale, border crossing or diversion is found. The current rule does not demand separate substantial-effects proof for every purpose-defined subclass, require that every conceivable medical exemption would collapse the system, or impose least restrictive means as the constitutional inclusion test.

The strongest historical medical-subclass objection remains real: the patients can contend that an identifiable, medically supervised local class is administrable without materially impairing interstate controls. Neither the original nor this refresh finds California's safeguards ineffective. The answer remains the actual production and authorization relationship, assessed under the current rule each Associate has joined, rather than an assumption of failed state enforcement or a general assertion that Congress controls all local conduct. Legislative permission for accommodation, as illustrated by Cutter, does not establish a constitutionally compelled exemption from this otherwise sufficient inclusion.

The CSA's date-effective definitions, preparation/compounding and practitioner limitations, research/registration/scheduling channels and actual exceptions retain their own work. Cultivation is within production; delivery does not require payment. State permission and medical recommendation do not amend federal authorization. Section 903's positive-conflict condition is not general displacement of state law. Sustaining direct federal regulation of private conduct does not require California to criminalize it or administer the federal system. Andersen supplies no new mens rea or statutory-liability ruling for the patients.

**R3 remains correction of the Commerce ruling and its preliminary-relief direction, followed by remand.** The appellate judgment and its direction to grant relief on that ground cannot stand. There is no additional Commerce-evidence hearing requirement where the accepted production/possession facts and actual statutory operation establish the relationship; the new decisions supply no material factual defect in that analysis. Remaining genuinely preserved claims and requests for relief receive their own legal, factual and equitable adjudication.

Oakland Cannabis remains confined to the distributor's asserted statutory medical-necessity exemption and impermissible equitable recreation of it. Its individual-possession, patient-necessity and independent-constitutional reservations remain intact. The power ruling does not decide the separately preserved due-process, Ninth Amendment, necessity or other distinct theories outside that question. Tucson and Taft do not decide those patient claims. That limit is equally not an adjudication that Raich's independent patient claims fail. No new search, privacy, notice or equality claim is invented from Tucson.
