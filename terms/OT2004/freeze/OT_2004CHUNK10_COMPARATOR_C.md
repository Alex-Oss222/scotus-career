## 100. Van Orden v. Perry

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 5-4 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist wrote a plurality joined by Justices Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Justice Breyer concurred in the judgment on a contextual rationale. Justices Scalia and Thomas wrote separately; Justices Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and O'Connor dissented through separate writings.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical judgment sustained the Texas monument, with no single majority adopting the plurality's entire reasoning.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 677; No. 03-1500.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported plurality and separate opinions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/677/).

## 101. Town of Castle Rock v. Gonzales

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer. Justice Souter concurred with Justice Breyer. Justice Stevens dissented, joined by Justice Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court found no protected property interest in police enforcement of the restraining order, emphasizing retained discretion and the character of the asserted benefit. The dissent favored recognition of the specific statutory interest and authoritative state-law consideration.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 748; No. 04-278.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/748/).

## 102. Bell v. Thompson

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas. Justice Breyer dissented, joined by Justices Stevens, Souter and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** Assuming the relevant power existed, the Sixth Circuit abused its discretion through the prolonged unannounced withholding and amended merits disposition after Supreme Court review ended.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 794; No. 04-514.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/794/).

## 103. McCreary County v. American Civil Liberties Union of Kentucky

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer. Justice O'Connor concurred. Justice Scalia dissented, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice Thomas, with Justice Kennedy joining specified portions.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court used the secular-purpose component of Lemon and the full sequence of official acts to sustain the injunction.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 844; No. 03-1693.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/844/).

## 104. Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer Studios Inc. v. Grokster, Ltd.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court unanimously vacated and remanded.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for the Court. Justice Ginsburg concurred with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice Kennedy; Justice Breyer concurred with Justices Stevens and O'Connor. The concurrences differed about Sony's application and lawful-use evidence.
* **Historical Holding:** Affirmative purposeful promotion of infringement can create inducement liability despite lawful product uses. The Court did not resolve the competing broader Sony formulations or award final damages.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 913; No. 04-480.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and concurrences](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/913/).

## 105. National Cable & Telecommunications Association v. Brand X Internet Services

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Breyer. Justices Stevens and Breyer concurred separately. Justice Scalia dissented, with Justices Souter and Ginsburg joining Part I.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held the FCC's information-service-only classification permissible under Chevron and allowed displacement of a prior judicial construction that had not established the only unambiguous statutory meaning. It also rejected the asserted arbitrary-and-capricious differential-treatment challenge.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 967; Nos. 04-277 and 04-281, consolidated within this listed matter.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/967/).
