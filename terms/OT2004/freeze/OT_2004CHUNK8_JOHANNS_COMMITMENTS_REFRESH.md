## Material changes screened

1. The commercial-speech argument was consistently advanced below. The district court rejected its applicability; the appellate court adapted its framework but ultimately relied on the legal insufficiency of advertising as its own compulsory-association justification. Presentation is established. The actual United Foods majority likewise did not find its offered commercial argument waived; its decision not to reach the separate operational government-speech defense is a different procedural holding.
2. The lower record establishes mandatory payment tied to livelihood, actual objection by at least some plaintiffs, producers' retained marketing autonomy, promotion as the program's principal object, and the stated spending allocations. At least 50 percent of assessments collected and paid to the Beef Board supported advertising, and 10–12 percent of that same denominator supported research. Those are not findings about demand growth, voluntary-campaign failure, comparative financing effectiveness or every producer's position.
3. The published opinion supplies no distinct empirical disposition of all predicates under the independent commercial framework proposed in the United Foods dissent. That does not establish absence of evidence in the trial file or failure of a litigant's factual burden. It also does not permit importing mushroom findings or presuming the beef program satisfies the framework.
4. The certified judgment declared the Act and Order unconstitutional and prospectively restrained all further checkoff collection. It did not merely prohibit advertising expenditures. The appellate court affirmed that reach, treating deletion of the former severability clause together with advertising's central role as establishing nonseverability. The deletion is an enacted change; the asserted conclusive inference is judicial interpretation, not an affirmative statutory command of nonseverability.

## Common legal and remedial distinctions

**Actual governmental message.** The original control-path commitments retain their exact basis: the approved facts show actual official determination, participation and final content approval, not merely a public label or dormant veto over an otherwise private speaker. A lawful targeted assessment can finance that governmental message without replacement by a general tax. The refresh's report that the lower court characterized oversight as limited does not eliminate any approved control fact. Neither the spending shares nor producers' independent marketing decisions contradict actual government control of the particular challenged campaign.

**Private campaign.** If the message remains selected private promotion under the accountability objection, United Foods supplies an independently sufficient constitutional defect absent an independently sufficient common function. Glickman's integrated-program permission remains valid on its conditions. Actual private marketing autonomy and advertising's principal role support the private-program comparison; research spending alone does not establish a larger function to which the challenged advertising is germane. Other sufficiently supported common functions remain legally possible without universal requirements of price controls or an antitrust exemption.

**Preserved commercial alternative.** Preservation permits consideration; it does not decide substance. Under the actual United Foods majority, even assumed effective promotion and reasonable economic design do not cure the independent private-support defect. A new independent commercial justification for the same category would therefore require an express doctrinal change, rather than a declaration that the lower courts merely failed to apply existing law. O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer's prior dissent supports that proposed change. Their framework places on government the burden of establishing a substantial interest, direct advancement and reasonable fit. It demands neither a prescribed study, a particular coefficient, certain producer profit nor the least restrictive imaginable means. A remand can resolve genuinely unresolved application on the existing bench-trial record, with supplementation only as ordinary procedure requires. The published opinion's limited recitation is not itself a reason to order a new trial.

**Personal attribution.** The refresh adds actual compelled funding and actual commercial objections. It supplies no finding that a named objector was made an apparent personal endorser. The original limited facial-attribution commitments and distinction between governmental identity and individual endorsement remain. No additional individual-attribution holding follows from the new evidence.

### Exact disposition G: governmental-message ground succeeds

Vacate the reviewed First Amendment judgment in both dockets and remand. Remove the dependent declaration that the Act and Order are unconstitutional on this compulsory-financing ground and the whole-checkoff collection injunction resting on that declaration. The approved government-message facts defeat the reviewed private-subsidy objection; the statutory program does not require personal endorsement by each objector.

Advertising-related collection is no longer prohibited by that reviewed constitutional ground. The same judgment supplies no independent basis to forbid nonadvertising collection. Because the constitutional predicate for the challenged whole-program remedy falls, no severability ruling is necessary to this disposition: it neither approves the lower nonseverability interpretation nor holds the statute severable in every future setting. Pending producer-communications, referendum, statutory, administration and accounting claims remain for their own adjudication, including the lawful effect of any independently supported relief. The judgment does not itself award an assessment amount, order a refund or resolve all causes in the action. An old appellate stay is not presumed to remain operative or to expire automatically on this date.

### Exact disposition P: private-advertising defect remains, remedy needs correction

Affirm the constitutional judgment insofar as it protects against compelled financing of the challenged private generic advertising; vacate the final determination that all remaining statutory functions necessarily fall with that defect and the corresponding categorical nonseverability declaration; remand for a lawful determination of the Act's separability and the injunction's appropriate reach. This is **affirmance in part, vacatur in part and remand in both dockets**, not full affirmance of the decree actually entered.

The existing prospective restraint on the integrated checkoff collection remains in place during that remedial remand unless and until the lower court enters a conforming order permitting collection for a constitutionally lawful, legally separable operation. This continued restraint prevents collection for the adjudicated unconstitutional advertising while the proper court determines the operation of any lawful remainder. It is not a final holding that research, consumer information, industry information or every other nonadvertising activity is itself unconstitutional.

The lower court must examine whether the enacted scheme permits an independently lawful remainder to function and whether it can separate the unconstitutional financing without rewriting the assessment or statutory program. Advertising's centrality and deletion of the earlier severability provision are material to that legal inquiry, but neither supplies an affirmative textual command invalidating every remainder. A 10–12 percent research allocation does not itself create legal authority to collect that fraction, just as a principal advertising purpose does not automatically prove every separate function incapable of survival. If a lawful remainder can be separated under the Act, the decree must permit that remainder while fully protecting against unconstitutional compulsory promotion; if none can, the whole-collection restraint may continue on the resulting severability determination. No new rate, earmark, refund entitlement or funding protocol is prescribed here.

The continued remedial protection addresses the programmatic compulsory-advertising defect; it is not limited merely by the names of the particular objectors when the same adjudicated operation governs collection. Separately pending claims retain their own predicates. The old appellate stay supplies no present relief premise.

## Individual screening

### Stevens

**Government-message and attribution components — retain.** He retains the accountability objection in his original row, accepts the unchanged operational control facts, and does not infer individual endorsement. The refresh does not supply public governmental identification or resolve the objection by changing authorship facts. His United Foods concurrence and full majority join protect against forced financing of competitors' selected private promotion, while his Glickman join permits genuinely ancillary collective-program expenses.

**Private-function and commercial components — retain ground; clarify the preserved alternative.** The new findings strengthen the actual analogy to an advertising-centered independent market. He expressly considers and rejects commercial advancement and reasonable fit as an independently sufficient answer under United Foods, even assuming those economic predicates in the proponents' favor. He finds no waiver and makes no empirical finding that advertising failed. The research allocation does not demonstrate that the selected advertising is ancillary to another sufficient common function.

**Remedy — revise to disposition P.** His original advertising-protection ground does not sustain the newly clarified decree's categorical declaration that every statutory remainder is invalid. United Foods Part III, which he joined, preserves actual operation, separability and independent nonadvertising assessments for lawful implementation. He supports the stated partial affirmance, remedial vacatur and remand, including continued protection against integrated compulsory-advertising collection during that remand. He does not fully affirm the actual whole-program declaration.

**Strongest contrary path and join barrier.** The best complete-affirmance argument combines advertising's central role with deliberate deletion of the severability clause; it warrants serious analysis rather than an assumption of severability. It still does not answer whether actual lawful functions can operate under the enacted scheme. He will not join either automatic total invalidation or automatic fractional collection. The best merits objection remains real executive control; the added commercial presentation does not resolve his different governmental-accountability requirement. A substantive opposing writing remains necessary if the control ground prevails, and must accurately state his narrower remedial position.

### O'Connor

**Government-message, targeted-funding and facial-attribution components — retain.** She continues to join the original actual-control ground and its limited facial-attribution conclusion. Her Glickman opinion allows justified collective funding; her United Foods dissent recognizes meaningful expression interests without an absolute prohibition on commercially justified support. The unchanged actual official selection supplies the sufficient present ground, regardless of whether the program would qualify as a private collective enterprise.

**Commercial component — retain nonreach as an additional present holding; add explicit supported alternative.** The defense is preserved and available, not forfeited. Her United Foods dissent supports allowing independent commercial justification even when selected private advertising lacks the common function the United Foods majority demands. If the governmental-message ground is unavailable, she would consider that expressly proposed revision and return a legally material unresolved commercial application for decision under government's substantial-interest, direct-advancement and reasonable-fit burden. She would not sustain the beef assessment now merely by importing mushroom effectiveness or voluntary-campaign findings. This records an available alternative, not a second sufficient factual holding or an actual commercial remand in her preferred disposition.

**Remedy — retain vacatur and remand; specify disposition G.** The whole-checkoff injunction and constitutional declaration lose their reviewed predicate. She joins removal of those dependent restrictions in both dockets without deciding whether research would be severable from an unconstitutional campaign. Other claims continue independently.

**Strongest contrary path and join barrier.** The accountability objection concerns how the public identifies the speaker, but the record establishes actual governmental message choice sufficiently for her current rule. If the campaign is treated as private, United Foods is a real contrary holding; her commercial view must be stated as a proposed change, not as an unnoticed exception. She rejects a finding of either empirical success or failure from silence in the published lower opinion. No separate writing is required merely to mention an alternative that the sufficient government ground makes unnecessary.
