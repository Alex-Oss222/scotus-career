# Ballard / Kanter reporter citation: verified clerical correction

The Seventh Circuit judgment in Estate of Kanter v. Commissioner is reported at **337 F.3d 833 (7th Cir. 2003)**. The supplied neutral packet and historical syllabus/header use 337 F.3d 883. That is a reporter-page transcription error, not a different judgment.

The official United States Reports text of Ballard cites Estate of Kanter at 337 F.3d 833, 840–841, on 544 U.S. 51; the following page cites the judgment's merits discussion at 873–874 and Cudahy's separate discussion at 874 and 884–888. Source: [official volume 544](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/544bv.pdf), printed pages 51–52 (PDF pages 145–146). Cornell's full majority and dissent text independently carry the 833 citation even though the surrounding slip-page header and syllabus disposition line contain 883. Source: [Ballard majority](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/03-184.ZO.html) and [Ballard dissent](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/03-184.ZD.html).

Use 337 F.3d 833 in the new Decision Record and its Public Projection. No brief, inventory, original frozen handoff, historical result, current commitment, remedy or adjudication is changed. This receipt records the authorized public-fact correction and its verified source; the A reconciliation handoff remains frozen as written.
