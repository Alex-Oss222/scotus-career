**Verified lower record:** United States v. Small, 333 F.3d 425 (3d Cir. June 23, 2003), affirmed the denial of dismissal and resulting conditional conviction. **Completeness correction to the compressed packet:** If the foreign-inclusive path relies on the lower court's screen, its actual structure must be stated.
