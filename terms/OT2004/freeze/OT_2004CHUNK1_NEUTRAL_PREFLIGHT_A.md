**Channel and scope.** Section 1254 review of the Sixth Circuit judgment, 356 F.3d 651 (January 16, 2004). **Primary-record clarification.** [The appellate opinion](https://openjurist.org/356/f3d/651/anderson-v-e-spear), Parts II, III, VI and VIII, recites the operative provisions and claims. Section 117.235(3) reaches election-day electioneering at polling places and within 500 feet of the county clerk's office or an entrance to a building containing a voting machine. Its coverage includes signs, campaign literature, cards, handbills, petition signatures and solicitation of votes; exit polling is excluded. The private-property exception covers electioneering within a private residence or establishment other than the polling premises by persons having an ownership interest. It does not supply a general outdoor, invited-speaker or public-sidewalk exception. The enforcement interpretation reached write-in voting instructions.
