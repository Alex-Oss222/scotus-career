## Reconciled propositions and remedy

**L1 — Independent efficacy test rejected.** Lack of substantial advancement of a legitimate governmental objective does not independently establish a taking requiring compensation. Efficacy concerns the relationship between means and ends; the taking inquiry concerns the nature, magnitude and distribution of the burden on property. An ineffective slight burden and an equally ineffective confiscatory burden cannot be classified by efficacy alone. Compensation does not authorize action independently forbidden for want of lawful public purpose or by due process.

**L2 — Precise overruling and preservation.** Agins is displaced insofar as it supplies that independent taking predicate. Del Monte Dunes is overruled to the extent its purpose-relationship alternative authorizes compensation as a freestanding taking ground and assigns that displaced substantive issue for decision. The change does not reopen its final judgment or single award, erase its independently sufficient complete-deprivation determination, or disturb its finality, compensation-route, municipal-policy, damages or constitutional-jury holdings for surviving legal claims. Its factual/legal allocation remains operative for surviving issues at the stated scope. No general attack on civil juries follows from rejecting one substantive predicate.

Loretto's physical-occupation rule, Lucas's complete-beneficial-use rule and substantiated antecedent-property/nuisance exception, Penn Central's contextual property-burden inquiry, and Nollan/Dolan's nexus and rough proportionality for demanded property dedications remain distinct. A required surrender of identified property is different from judicial assessment of the success of ordinary rent regulation. Rejection of the efficacy test supplies no blanket validity rule for economic regulation and no new substantive-due-process test.

**L3 — Judgment and remand.** Reverse the Ninth Circuit judgment sustaining invalidation on the selected substantial-advancement theory and remand for a judgment rejecting that surviving ground. No compensation, revived Penn Central claim, newly adjudicated due-process claim or declaration that Hawaii's economic predictions are correct is ordered. The independent source and preservation requirements for any other case remain unchanged.

## Historical departure and separate-writing function

**Historical departure:** All eight Associates support the historical reversal and substantive rejection but must expressly overrule the actual purpose-relationship taking ground adopted in this Court's Del Monte Dunes, a holding the historical Lingle opinion said had never existed. For Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas the change confronts their own verdict-sustaining joins; for O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer it distinguishes their common substantive agreement and jury-remand position from the prior majority's operative holding. Each retains the independently sufficient total-loss determination and final award while rejecting efficacy as a freestanding compensation predicate.

The changed premise warrants this additional stare-decisis explanation: the earlier alternative measures regulatory validity rather than the property burden the compensation command addresses; restricting it to particular denials does not cure that mismatch; independent constitutional routes remain; and the completed award and independent ground are not undone. No special reliance finding, new economic fact or post-event doctrine is invented.

Kennedy's historical concurrence performs a substantive reservation, not an additional taking rule. His current commitment does not require a separate writing if the common opinion expressly preserves independently applicable due-process protection against arbitrary regulation and declines to decide an unpresented claim. If a draft omits that reservation or implies economic regulation is immune from separate constitutional review, the omission must be repaired or his distinct reservation retained in a short separate writing. No other additional writing is required by the reconciled commitments. Current authorship remains for assignment.
