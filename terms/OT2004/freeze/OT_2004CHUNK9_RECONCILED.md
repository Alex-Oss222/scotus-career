## Original independent commitment, preserved verbatim

## 79. Johnson v. California, No. 04-6964

**Question and disposition:** Seven Associates would reverse the first-stage threshold ruling and remand for completion of Batson's process. Thomas would affirm California's first-stage rule and the reviewed judgment. All reach the granted merits question on the supplied final-judgment posture.

**Common reversal ground:** A defendant triggers the prosecutor's explanation duty by facts sufficient to permit an inference of purposeful racial exclusion. Requiring proof that discrimination was more likely than not before the prosecutor explains the challenged strikes improperly demands an ultimate persuasion showing at the production stage. Striking all three qualified Black prospective jurors from this venire satisfies the required inference on the supplied record. The trial court must receive and evaluate the actual reasons and determine purposeful discrimination with the ultimate burden remaining on Johnson. A retrospective hearing must permit a reliable determination; if the lapse or state of the record prevents that constitutionally adequate adjudication, the state court must provide a new trial. Section 271(e)(1) protects the enumerated making, using, offering to sell or selling within the United States, and importation into the United States, of a patented invention solely for uses reasonably related to developing and submitting information under the covered federal regulatory law. Its animal-product exception concerns a new animal drug or veterinary biological product primarily manufactured through recombinant DNA, recombinant RNA, hybridoma technology or other processes involving site-specific genetic manipulation. The reasonable-relationship condition includes qualifying preclinical work on drug safety, efficacy, mechanism of action, pharmacology and pharmacokinetics. At least where the drugmaker reasonably believes that a patented compound may act through an identified biological process to produce the intended physiological effect, and uses it in experiments that, if successful, would yield information appropriate for FDA submission, the use meets the statutory relationship. ## Regulatory explanation and preserved qualifications
