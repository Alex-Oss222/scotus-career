Wilkinson v. Dotson provides the material additional public position. O'Connor wrote for all nine Justices that courts must classify a section 1983 claim by the necessary legal consequence of its actual requested relief, not by the litigant's hoped-for eventual result. The Court permitted claims seeking lawful parole reconsideration that preserved discretion to deny release, while leaving proof of the asserted constitutional right and independent procedural and remedial requirements for further proceedings. ## Clarified statutory and remedy boundaries

The statutory qualifications in the pending handoff define covered assistance at their actual stage and scope. The definition's active-treatment condition concerns the qualifying services for which payment is sought. It must not be converted into a requirement that an eligible waiting-list claimant already be receiving the very treatment allegedly withheld before asserting a prompt-assistance claim. Similarly, section 1396a(a)(31)'s written care plan before admission or authorization of benefits and periodic professional review remain applicable conditions; this Court does not invent a finding that a claimant failed those steps or a new ground to dispute the eligibility accepted below.

Paragraph (a)(10)'s adopted coverage and individual eligibility, paragraph (a)(8)'s reasonable promptness, and section 1396d(a)'s dated cost-payment definition do different work. The enforceability decision can recognize those particular personal obligations without deciding that furnishing services themselves is always required or that merely making funds nominally available always satisfies the statute. Either proposed categorical remedial rule would go beyond the reserved question and undeveloped violation record. Actual lawful compliance and any effective injunction remain for the District Court after identification of breach, causation, responsible official, relevant persons and available lawful arrangements.

The opinion below's description of plaintiffs as a class supports preserving the representative nature of the action. It does not establish the terms of an operative certification order or authorize a new statewide decree without the ordinary findings. Those matters belong to further adjudication and are not a general record-gap blocker. The scheduled event remains properly before the Court without proof of a historical petition event.
