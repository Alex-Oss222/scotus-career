The monument's age helps explain its preserved commemorative role; it is not an exemption from review. The dissent would affirm on the full contextual account, without a separate rule allowing religious acknowledgment, favoring monotheism or requiring coercion before governmental adoption can be prohibited.

## Procedure After Action

The case returns to the district court for remedial proceedings. It must evaluate Texas's actual proposed arrangement, select an effective lawful cure and retain authority to enforce compliance with the resulting decree.
