## Decision kernel and posture

## Controlling holdings and precedent treatment

### Local production and possession are integral to this interstate supply regime

**Holding and operative rule:** Congress may include unauthorized local marijuana cultivation, caregiver supply and possession in the Controlled Substances Act's independently valid interstate drug-control regime because that inclusion is reasonably adapted and integral to control of the same fungible substance's supply and distribution. The Government must identify the valid interstate regime, enacted covered class and genuine functional relationship; courts independently determine those boundaries while respecting reasonable congressional enforcement judgments within them.

**Authority:** The opinion of the Court, joined by all nine Justices, controls this necessary-inclusion rule, its application and the following statutory-boundary and remedial holdings.

**Controlling explanation:** Lopez requires an eligible interstate connection before aggregation. Home Builders supplies the necessary-inclusion route, and Gibbs, Rancho Viejo and GDF apply it without demanding border crossing, commercial motive or a separate substantial interstate effect for each local member of a genuinely connected class. Those decisions supply the rule, not facts about drugs.

The CSA controls production, distribution and possession through an integrated supply-and-authorization system for the substance traded interstate. Cultivation creates that substance, caregiver delivery transfers it, and possession retains it. Local unauthorized supply and its substitution for market acquisition bear directly on the regime's objects and enforceability. Section 801's supply and practical-differentiation findings support that concrete mechanism; generalized health costs or the description of the Act as comprehensive cannot supply the constitutional connection by themselves. The accepted local cultivation and possession remain within the mechanism even without a sale or interstate movement by these patients. The rule preserves independent judicial scrutiny of an application outside the enacted class, an invalid interstate regime or a nominal or pretextual relationship. It requires no least-restrictive-means showing or proof that every conceivable exemption would cause measurable enforcement failure.

### Medical purpose and state permission do not remove the functional connection

**Holding and operative rule:** The accepted local, unpaid, physician-recommended and state-permitted setting does not sever the relationship between these production and possession activities and the federal substance-control system. California permission and a physician's recommendation do not amend federal authorization; the CSA's enacted definitions, authorization provisions and state-law limits retain their actual operation.

Section 841(a) prohibits knowing or intentional manufacture, distribution, dispensing and the related possession-with-intent conduct except as authorized by the subchapter. Section 844(a) governs knowing or intentional possession, preserving substances obtained directly, or under a valid prescription or order, from a practitioner acting professionally, and other authorization under the applicable subchapters. Those are federal statutory conditions, rather than an exemption created by state permission alone.

Section 802 includes planting, cultivation, growing and harvesting in production. Distribution is delivery other than administering or dispensing; delivery includes actual, constructive or attempted transfer regardless of an agency relationship and does not require payment. The manufacture definition excludes preparation, compounding, packaging or labeling in conformity with state or local law by a practitioner as an incident to professional administration or dispensing; that limited exception is not a general cultivation exemption. Marijuana remains governed by Schedule I and the federal authorization, research, registration and scheduling routes in force. Section 903 leaves otherwise valid state law in place unless a positive conflict prevents the federal provision and state law from consistently standing together. Direct federal regulation of private conduct does not require California to criminalize the same conduct or administer the federal program.

**Controlling explanation:** The patients' strongest boundary would define a separately administered local medical class and require proof that excluding it would defeat interstate enforcement. Their medical needs, lack of sales and state supervision are accepted at their proper record status. But medical purpose explains why production or possession occurs; it does not change the substance produced or remove the activity from the authorization and supply controls. A State's ability to administer an exception may inform the functional inquiry without obliging Congress to adopt that State's alternative system. The Court need not find patient-specific diversion or failure of California safeguards to identify this direct relationship. Household substitution alone would be too broad; the holding depends on the actual class and same-substance supply mechanism within an independently valid regime. The statutory operation and accepted activity facts settle that relationship here, so a further Commerce evidentiary proceeding is unnecessary.

## Holdings

### Local production and possession are integral to this interstate supply regime

**Holding and operative rule:** Congress may include unauthorized local marijuana cultivation, caregiver supply and possession in the Controlled Substances Act's independently valid interstate drug-control regime because that inclusion is reasonably adapted and integral to control of the same fungible substance's supply and distribution. The Government must identify the valid interstate regime, enacted covered class and genuine functional relationship; courts independently determine those boundaries while respecting reasonable congressional enforcement judgments within them.

**Authority:** The opinion of the Court, joined by all nine Justices, controls this necessary-inclusion rule, its application and the following statutory-boundary and remedial holdings.

**Controlling explanation:** Lopez requires an eligible interstate connection before aggregation. Home Builders supplies the necessary-inclusion route, and Gibbs, Rancho Viejo and GDF apply it without demanding border crossing, commercial motive or a separate substantial interstate effect for each local member of a genuinely connected class. Those decisions supply the rule, not facts about drugs.

The CSA controls production, distribution and possession through an integrated supply-and-authorization system for the substance traded interstate. Cultivation creates that substance, caregiver delivery transfers it, and possession retains it. Local unauthorized supply and its substitution for market acquisition bear directly on the regime's objects and enforceability. Section 801's supply and practical-differentiation findings support that concrete mechanism; generalized health costs or the description of the Act as comprehensive cannot supply the constitutional connection by themselves. The accepted local cultivation and possession remain within the mechanism even without a sale or interstate movement by these patients. The rule preserves independent judicial scrutiny of an application outside the enacted class, an invalid interstate regime or a nominal or pretextual relationship. It requires no least-restrictive-means showing or proof that every conceivable exemption would cause measurable enforcement failure.

### Medical purpose and state permission do not remove the functional connection

**Holding and operative rule:** The accepted local, unpaid, physician-recommended and state-permitted setting does not sever the relationship between these production and possession activities and the federal substance-control system. California permission and a physician's recommendation do not amend federal authorization; the CSA's enacted definitions, authorization provisions and state-law limits retain their actual operation.

Section 841(a) prohibits knowing or intentional manufacture, distribution, dispensing and the related possession-with-intent conduct except as authorized by the subchapter. Section 844(a) governs knowing or intentional possession, preserving substances obtained directly, or under a valid prescription or order, from a practitioner acting professionally, and other authorization under the applicable subchapters. Those are federal statutory conditions, rather than an exemption created by state permission alone.

Section 802 includes planting, cultivation, growing and harvesting in production. Distribution is delivery other than administering or dispensing; delivery includes actual, constructive or attempted transfer regardless of an agency relationship and does not require payment. The manufacture definition excludes preparation, compounding, packaging or labeling in conformity with state or local law by a practitioner as an incident to professional administration or dispensing; that limited exception is not a general cultivation exemption. Marijuana remains governed by Schedule I and the federal authorization, research, registration and scheduling routes in force. Section 903 leaves otherwise valid state law in place unless a positive conflict prevents the federal provision and state law from consistently standing together. Direct federal regulation of private conduct does not require California to criminalize the same conduct or administer the federal program.

**Controlling explanation:** The patients' strongest boundary would define a separately administered local medical class and require proof that excluding it would defeat interstate enforcement. Their medical needs, lack of sales and state supervision are accepted at their proper record status. But medical purpose explains why production or possession occurs; it does not change the substance produced or remove the activity from the authorization and supply controls. A State's ability to administer an exception may inform the functional inquiry without obliging Congress to adopt that State's alternative system. The Court need not find patient-specific diversion or failure of California safeguards to identify this direct relationship. Household substitution alone would be too broad; the holding depends on the actual class and same-substance supply mechanism within an independently valid regime. The statutory operation and accepted activity facts settle that relationship here, so a further Commerce evidentiary proceeding is unnecessary.

### Independent patient claims retain their own adjudication

**Holding and operative rule:** The preliminary-injunction direction cannot stand on the rejected Commerce theory. The case returns for genuinely preserved independent patient claims and any request for interim relief under the substantive law, evidence and equitable requirements governing those claims.

**Controlling explanation:** Oakland Cannabis rejected the distributor's statutory medical-necessity exemption and an equitable recreation of it, while leaving individual possession, patient necessity and independent constitutional claims outside that disposition. The present grant resolves federal power, rather than the distinct due-process, Ninth Amendment, patient-necessity or commandeering theories. Those matters remain because they fall outside the question decided and require their own legal and factual adjudication. The physicians' evidence keeps its proper significance for medical risk and any applicable claim or interim-relief requirement; the Commerce decision does not decide those claims' merits or guarantee relief. The lower courts must address the actual preserved requests promptly under their proper standards.

## Law After Decision

The existing necessary-inclusion rule permits the CSA to reach unauthorized local medical cultivation, caregiver supply and possession within its genuine same-substance interstate control system. State permission and absence of payment do not create the claimed Commerce exemption. Courts must continue to identify a valid regime, enacted class and actual functional connection; statutory authorization and the distinct preserved patient claims retain their own requirements.
