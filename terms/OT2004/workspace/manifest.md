For every scheduled row, entering law is the law effective before its Event date: the opening trackers plus all earlier-effective validated current-term Decision Records and Admitted Source Records. An uncoordinated same-date group uses one common pre-date baseline; its decisions combine only prospectively. Processing order and a chunk boundary do not make one same-date decision law for another. Only an established coordinated or expressly dependent same-date sequence permits a different treatment.
