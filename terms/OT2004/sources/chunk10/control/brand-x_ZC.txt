    Justice Stevens,
concurring.

     While I join the Court’s opinion
in full, I add this caveat concerning Part III—B, which
correctly explains why a court of appeals’ interpretation
of an ambiguous provision in a regulatory statute does not
foreclose a contrary reading by the agency.  That explanation
would not necessarily be applicable to a decision by this Court
that would presumably remove any pre-existing ambiguity.
