    Justice Breyer,
concurring.

     The majority explains that a proper
Rule 60(b) motion “attacks, not the substance of the
federal court’s resolution of a claim on the merits, but
some defect in the integrity of the federal habeas
proceedings.”  
Ante
, at 8.	This is consistent with
Judge Tjoflat’s description of the standard in his opinion
below, see 366 F.3d 1253, 1297 (CA11 2004) (specially
concurring in part and dissenting in part), and I agree with
it.  I fear that other language in the majority’s opinion,
especially its discussion of the significance of the word
“claim,” could be taken to imply a different
standard, with which I would disagree.	With that
qualification, I join the majority’s opinion.
