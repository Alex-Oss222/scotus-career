[May 23, 2005]

    Justice Kennedy,
dissenting.

     I join Justice Souter’s
dissenting opinion, which demonstrates with persuasive analysis
why the speech at issue here cannot meaningfully be considered
government speech at all.  I would reserve for another day the
difficult First
Amendment questions that would arise if the government were
to target a discrete group of citizens to pay even for speech
that the government does “embrace as publicly as it
speaks,” post, at 11.
