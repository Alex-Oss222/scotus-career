## Karaha Bodas recognition review

One signed Opinion of the Court resolves the distinct recognition judgment after full merits briefing. A substantive treaty construction and contested construction of the actual arbitration clauses require a reasoned signed merits opinion. There is one common nine-Justice rationale and one affirmance; no separate-writing function is established. The question is distinct from the earlier anti-suit judgment and from the other matters decided the same day, so the cases issue separately.

The governing propositions are that Article V(1)(e)'s governing-law country means the country whose national arbitral procedural law governs the award's making, rather than every country whose substantive contract law is applied; the resisting party must establish the qualifying authority and annulment or suspension. Particular contractual incorporation is construed at its actual scope. ## Smith v. Massachusetts

One signed Opinion of the Court and one dissent resolve the substantive completion dispute. Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Souter and Thomas support reversal and the complete common rationale. Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer support affirmance on common provisionality reasoning. Their genuine constitutional disagreement warrants a dissent; its author remains undecided. A single disposition with a straightforward dissent permits a compact judgment and uncomplicated opinion account.

The governing proposition is that an unqualified insufficiency determination completed under the applicable procedure and whole trial episode bars renewed guilt adjudication on the count, including within the same jury trial despite legal error. Here the allowance, implementation, absence of a reserved question and completed defense phase establish completion; ordinary reconsideration power is insufficient to keep this substantive acquittal provisional. Remove the firearm conviction and dependent punishment, preserve the independent assault convictions, and prohibit renewed guilt adjudication on the acquitted count. A genuinely established expressly provisional procedure lies outside this record. The dissent accepts completed-acquittal finality but regards the general state reconsideration rule and uninterrupted trial as leaving this ruling nonfinal.

## Stewart v. Dutra Construction Co.

One signed Opinion of the Court resolves the vessel question and its fitted mandate. A statutory definition, its operational application and separate treatment of the two claim judgments require reasoned signed merits treatment. All nine support the actual common ground. The resulting mixed disposition requires a component judgment account: reverse the Jones Act vessel-based judgment and remand; leave the independent section 905(b) capacity judgment operative outside the grant. No separate writing is necessary merely to repeat earlier disagreements about the worker-duration framework, which the actual holding does not renew.

The governing vessel rule is practical use or capacity for carrying people or things over water, without a primary-transportation-purpose, self-propulsion or injury-time-motion condition. Permanently fixed or withdrawn structures with only theoretical transportation capacity remain outside the rule. The Super Scoop's actual movements carrying equipment and crew satisfy it. Worker connection and the actionable employer or vessel capacity retain separate functions. On the surviving Jones Act branch, the lower court must determine the genuinely remaining employment, proper defendant-capacity, negligence and remedy questions under the corrected vessel premise while honoring Dutra's motion-specific acknowledgments. Further summary judgment requires an independent legally sufficient ground; otherwise ordinary factfinding follows. The Chief's express command to reopen claims affected by the erroneous vessel premise does not reopen the independent section 905(b) judgment reached with vessel status assumed. Its surviving status is stated as a scope consequence, not a new merits endorsement of its capacity rationale.
