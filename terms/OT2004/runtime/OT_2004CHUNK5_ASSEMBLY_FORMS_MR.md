# March 22: Muehler and Rancho opinion forms

These forms are fixed before either assignment count or author selection. The earlier March 22 cases must be completed before the counts below are taken.

Muehler: one signed plenary lead, with a unanimous part resolving detention authority, the questioning error, post-search custody, present question scope and verdict-sensitive mandate, and a five-member part requiring renewed review of continued handcuffing. The Chief's approved continued-force position joins Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. A partial concurrence and dissent states O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas's conclusion that the identified search-period cuffs were reasonable. The four's concrete limits on harmful restraint and apparent safe removal are preserved. No separate immunity merits part is formed: the frozen scope supplement limits the present adjudication, while ordinary surviving defenses remain subject to preservation and finality. One author can carry both lead parts because five fully join each. The division follows actual merits disagreement, not counts.

Rancho: one signed plenary opinion unanimously reversing the additional statutory section 1983 channel, including derivative fees, while retaining direct permit relief. The right is assumed; the holding rests on actual incompatibility of the thirty-day expedited personal judicial route, distinct from administrative supervision. A common formulation incorporates all eight Associates' supported limits and the Chief's approved position. It supplies no general express-remedy presumption and no categorical direct-TCA damages prohibition. The common reasoning requires no separate writing. The form and propositions reflect the statutory question and reconciled commitments, not assignment totals.
