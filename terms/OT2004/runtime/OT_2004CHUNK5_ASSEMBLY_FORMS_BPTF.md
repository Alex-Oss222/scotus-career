# Black Political Task Force — form and propositions before assignment

Fixed before the March 22 assignment recount: one signed lead opinion after full merits submission resolves this final statutory injunction appeal. Its legal work is to distinguish the continuing judicial restraint from completed candidate relief, apply the existing equal-opportunity framework without the lower court's numerical and presumptive errors, decide whether independent supported local findings suffice, and fit the continuing remedy to the enacted replacement. These related questions require one opinion; its parts reflect actual joins, not separate assignment units.

Part I addresses the live appeal and the actual final judgment. All nine may join that common procedural ground under the frozen commitments and approved Chief position. Part II states existing statutory coverage, retained causal requirements, independent totality, actual crossover opportunity and rejection of maximization or a presumption; it applies the supported preliminary findings under the majority-district route. Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support that ground. Part III affirms on the independently sufficient practical packing injury and preserves the Count I declaration, bounded permanent injunction and lawful derivative fee basis. Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support that complete disposition and its reasoning.

O'Connor and Kennedy require reassessment of totality and dependent relief. Their common partial concurrence and dissent joins Parts I and II while rejecting Part III and proposing vacatur and remand. Thomas and Scalia maintain statutory noncoverage, join only Part I, and seek reversal and dismissal of Count I with removal of its dependent federal relief. Their distinct partial concurrence and dissent does not join a reassessment mandate. The Chief adds no separate writing because his approved rule, scope and remedy fit the five-member affirmance formulation. No commitment changes, new accommodation, hypothetical common mandate or fractured-opinion synthesis is proposed.

The complete lead can be carried by the five full-opinion joiners; one author will be selected after the required fresh recount. Each proposed join will be checked against its actual scope in the final Record. Same-date matters supply no entering law to this event.
