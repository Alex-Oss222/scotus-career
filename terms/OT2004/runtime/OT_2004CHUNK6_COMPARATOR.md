## 48. Smith v. City of Jackson

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 8-0 in the judgment; five Justices recognized disparate-impact claims. Chief Justice Rehnquist did not participate.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote Parts I, II and IV for five Justices and Part III for four. Scalia separately supported recognition through agency deference. O’Connor, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, concurred only in the judgment and rejected impact liability.
* **Historical Holding:** The ADEA permits disparate-impact claims, but these employees had not adequately isolated a practice and the plan rested on reasonable nonage factors.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 228.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/03-1160); [official docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/Search.aspx?FileName=/docketfiles/03-1160.htm).

## 49. Rhines v. Weber

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 9-0 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O’Connor wrote the Court’s opinion; Souter concurred in part and in the judgment, joined by Ginsburg and Breyer. Stevens also concurred, joined by Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** District courts have limited stay-and-abeyance discretion upon good cause, potentially meritorious claims and absence of intentionally dilatory tactics, with reasonable time limits.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 269.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/03-9046).

## 50. Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Saudi Basic Industries Corp.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for the Court, with no separate opinion.
* **Historical Holding:** The jurisdictional doctrine was confined to suits by state-court losers complaining of injuries caused by earlier state judgments and seeking their federal rejection. Parallel litigation and ordinary preclusion remained distinct.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 280.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/544/280); [official docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/Search.aspx?FileName=/docketfiles/03-1696.htm).

## 52. Johnson v. United States

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Thomas and Breyer. Kennedy dissented, joined by Stevens, Scalia and Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** Vacatur qualified as a fact, but the Court required diligence in seeking it after federal judgment and held Johnson’s unexplained delay insufficient.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 295.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/295/).

## 55. Dura Pharmaceuticals, Inc. v. Broudo

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded unanimously, 9-0.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for the Court; no separate opinions.
* **Historical Holding:** Inflated purchase price alone does not establish economic loss or loss causation, and the complaint gave inadequate notice of the alleged causal loss.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 336.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/336/).

## 56. Monsanto Co. v. McFarling

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** The Federal Circuit affirmed liability and rejected the antitrust and misuse defenses, while vacating the $780,000 liquidated-damages award and remanding for actual damages. It relied on patent scope in rejecting antitrust liability.
* **Comparator Limit:** No Supreme Court merits comparator is identified; the cited appellate judgment is the historical anchor.
* **Historical Sources:** [Federal Circuit opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/363/1336/531948/).
