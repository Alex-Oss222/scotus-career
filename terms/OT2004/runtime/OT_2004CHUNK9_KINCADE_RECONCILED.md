## Competing paths and operative relief

The strongest affirmance position emphasizes the government's continuing correctional responsibility, objectively selected offenders, reliable identification and modest medical means. The five reversing Associates regard those considerations as insufficient under the specific Rise holding. Their rule respects the actual safeguards while rejecting the inferential step from safeguards and reduced privacy to search authority. Their reply does not depend on an invented finding of genomic misuse or dangerous collection.

The strongest reversal objection to the three affirming Associates is the close controlling Rise precedent and the ordinary investigative use of retained biological information. Their published Rise dissent supplies a concrete basis for proposing the defined exception despite that precedent. Their position cannot be described as a simple application of Knights, a factual distinction based only on active supervision, or an already controlling DNA exception.
