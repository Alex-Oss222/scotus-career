## 37. Shepard v. United States

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed and remanded, 5-3 among participating Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Souter wrote for the Court except Part III; Stevens, Scalia and Ginsburg joined throughout, and Thomas joined except Part III and separately concurred. O'Connor dissented, joined by Kennedy and Breyer. Rehnquist did not participate.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court limited the plea inquiry to charging documents, written plea agreements, plea colloquies and comparable judicial findings assented to by the defendant. Police reports and complaint applications were insufficient. The four-Justice constitutional-avoidance discussion did not have the same support as the statutory holding.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Shepard opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/13/).

## 38. Ballard v. Commissioner

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed both appellate judgments and remanded, 7-2.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer. Kennedy concurred, joined by Scalia. Rehnquist dissented, joined by Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Rule 183 did not authorize the Tax Court's practice of withholding the initial report while attributing the final revised opinion to the special trial judge. The Court required a reviewable report and did not resolve the constitutional objections.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Ballard opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/40/).

## 39. Wilkinson v. Dotson

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court affirmed and remanded, 8-1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Breyer wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg. Scalia concurred, joined by Thomas. Kennedy dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court permitted section 1983 claims for new parole proceedings because success would not necessarily imply invalid confinement or require speedier release.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Wilkinson opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/74/).

## 41. Muehler v. Mena

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court unanimously vacated and remanded, with different reasoning among the Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Rehnquist wrote for O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Kennedy concurred. Stevens concurred in the judgment, joined by Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical majority upheld the handcuff detention during the weapons search and rejected a separate violation from immigration questioning absent added detention. It left the alternative claim of detention beyond the search for the lower court.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Muehler opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/93/).

## 42. Rancho Palos Verdes v. Abrams

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed and remanded unanimously in judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Scalia wrote for eight Justices. Breyer concurred, joined by O'Connor, Souter and Ginsburg. Stevens concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held the TCA's express expedited remedy incompatible with section 1983 enforcement of the same siting rights and rejected the saving-clause argument for a parallel route.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Rancho Palos Verdes opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/113/).

## 43. Brown v. Payton

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed the Ninth Circuit, 5-3 among participating Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for O'Connor, Scalia, Thomas and Breyer. Scalia concurred, joined by Thomas; Breyer also concurred. Souter dissented, joined by Stevens and Ginsburg. Rehnquist did not participate.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held that the state court's contextual application of Boyde was not objectively unreasonable under AEDPA, despite the prosecutor's erroneous statement about postcrime mitigation.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Brown opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/133/).

## 44. Black Political Task Force v. Galvin

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** The three-judge court, with Selya writing and Woodlock and Ponsor participating, found a section 2 violation on February 24, 2004. The June 21 final judgment permanently barred the challenged plan after approval and enactment of Plan C, and dismissed the other counts. There is no Supreme Court merits comparator.
* **Supreme Court Merits Comparator:** None. The historical anchor is the identified lower-court judgment.
* **Historical Sources:** [Liability opinion](https://clearinghouse-umich-production.s3.amazonaws.com/media/doc/59181.pdf); [final judgment](https://clearinghouse-umich-production.s3.amazonaws.com/media/doc/8380.pdf).

## 45. Jackson v. Birmingham Board of Education

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** O'Connor wrote for Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Thomas dissented, joined by Rehnquist, Scalia and Kennedy.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held that retaliation against a person because he complains of sex discrimination is intentional discrimination within Title IX's private action, even if he was not the initial victim.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Jackson opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/167/).

## 46. City of Sherrill v. Oneida Indian Nation of New York

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Supreme Court reversed and remanded, 8-1.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Ginsburg wrote for Rehnquist, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer. Souter concurred. Stevens dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court relied on long delay, settled governance and disruptive consequences to deny unilateral revival of sovereign tax immunity through repurchase. It identified the federal trust-acquisition process as the appropriate route and left the earlier Oneida damages holding undisturbed.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Sherrill opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/197/).

## 47. Sabree ex rel. Sabree v. Richman

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Anchor:** The Third Circuit's May 11, 2004 judgment recognized enforceable rights under the specified Medicaid provisions and reversed dismissal. It expressly left the available remedy for initial district-court consideration. That lower-court result is the historical anchor, not a Supreme Court merits disposition.
* **Supreme Court Merits Comparator:** None. The historical anchor is the identified lower-court judgment.
* **Historical Sources:** [Sabree, 367 F.3d 180](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/367/180/495852/).
