## California Public Employees' Retirement System v. WorldCom, Inc.

Its single governing ground is statutory coexistence: section 1452(a) does not override section 22(a)'s prohibition where the enacted section 77p(c) exception does not apply. It requires state-court return of the covered individual actions. The legal-authority appeal is distinct from section 1452(b)'s equitable decisions; actual bankruptcy relatedness remains assumed for this certified question. The eight reconciled Associates and the approved Chief Justice position support every governing proposition and that remedy. All nine are tentative full-opinion eligible authors. The case requires neither separate leads nor a per curiam form. This form is based on the statutory question and complete common rationale.
