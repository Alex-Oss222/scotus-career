## Historical comparison and operative current law

The historical five Associate reversers are the same five here. Their common historical grounds were direct statutory intentional discrimination, Sullivan's personally injured advocate, a distinction between broad Title IX language and Title VII's detailed drafting, adequate funding notice, and a remand requiring proof of retaliation. The historical three Associate affirmers are the same three here. They joined the dissent's independent statutory causation, protected-beneficiary, implied-action and funding-notice objections. Neither side's result depends on reproducing every historical description of another decision.

Actual Powell, Sandoval and South Camden recognize a bounded Title VI prospective effects-regulation action and preserve the distinct section 1983 limits. The historical Jackson majority's categorical description of Sandoval is therefore displaced. That change supports using the actual enforcement precedents accurately; it does not itself establish Title IX retaliation damages. The reversal group rests on section 1681 itself, and the affirmance group can retain its statutory and notice objections without asserting the displaced historical rule.

Actual Franklin permits compensation for intentional statutory discrimination. O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter joined its controlling reasoning with Stevens; Scalia and Thomas agreed with the judgment on the narrower significance of the 1986 enactment. Those different grounds remain material: a remedy for an established right does not decide retaliation's statutory classification for Kennedy, Scalia or Thomas. For the reversal supporters it supplies relief only after that classification and proof of the intentional wrong.

Actual Gebser does not make corrective-official actual notice the exclusive attribution rule. Its fractured positions and the controlling common ground must retain their actual scopes. Davis likewise concerns the recipient's own culpable response to third-party harassment and preserves its particular severity and attribution requirements. Jackson alleges the recipient's own retaliatory decision; neither an allegation of Board action nor reporting's practical usefulness proves attribution. The historical discussion of actual notice as universally necessary is not adopted. This changed attribution premise does not remove the historical disagreement about whether retaliation is statutory sex discrimination.

Back's March 7 holding rejects a mandatory opposite-sex comparator where actual sex-stereotype evidence supports an equal-protection claim. It supplies no broader rule about retaliation, advocates or a claimant's statutory beneficiary status. Rancho's March 22 holding separately tests an established right and an asserted incompatible enforcement channel; it does not decide whether section 1681 includes the disputed conduct. Ballard, Shepard, Dotson, BPTF, Brown and Muehler were checked in the public supplements; their distinct record, sentencing, prison-remedy, voting, habeas and search rules provide no new Jackson entitlement or changed Associate disposition.

## Pleading facts, qualifications and work on remand

The complaint alleges unequal treatment of the girls' team, Jackson's complaints, his removal from coaching and resulting loss of supplemental pay while he remained a teacher. Those allegations are not findings of athletics discrimination or Board liability. The five reversal supporters hold that a personally injured reporter need not first obtain a completed adjudication of the girls' underlying claim to survive this categorical dismissal. They do not decide every mistaken or bad-faith complaint, a new causal quantum, or a burden-shifting system.

On the reversal mandate, ordinary proceedings must establish covered-program status, legally attributable recipient action, the complaint's sex-discrimination subject, retaliation, any supported defense, and causal injury. Franklin permits appropriate compensation for a proved intentional statutory violation, including proved causal pay loss. Employment or equitable relief requires its own lawful basis. Remand is not immediate damages, compulsory trial, automatic reinstatement or a fixed award.

The longstanding regulation supports construction and notice; its separate interference, testimony, assistance and participation terms are not wholesale statutory damages holdings. The United States' proposed subject-neutral all-complainers exclusion remains advocacy, not an adopted categorical defense. The historical majority's captain hypothetical and the dissent's reasonable-good-faith, but-for and association examples were read as arguments about the disputed category, not as new facts or holdings on every retaliation variant. Sullivan's standing and statutory-protection arguments were distinguished throughout. The dissent's fourth footnote supplies section 1982's text, not a transplant of its entire scope into Title IX.
