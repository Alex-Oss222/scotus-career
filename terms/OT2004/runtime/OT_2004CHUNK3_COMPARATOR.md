## 27. Smith v. Massachusetts

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5 to 4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia wrote for Stevens, O’Connor, Souter and Thomas. Justice Ginsburg dissented, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Kennedy and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The midtrial insufficiency ruling was an acquittal, and its later reconsideration violated double jeopardy where no preexisting rule or case authority expressly permitted reconsideration after the defense proceeded.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/462/).

## 28. Stewart v. Dutra Construction Co.

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 8 to 0 among participating Justices.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote for all participating Justices. Chief Justice Rehnquist took no part in the decision.
* **Historical Holding:** The dredge was a vessel under the LHWCA because it was practically capable of maritime transportation. Primary purpose and actual motion at the injury were not prerequisites.
* **Historical Authority:** The reported Supreme Court merits decision.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/543/481/).
