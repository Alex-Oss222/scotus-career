## 57. Pasquantino v. United States

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Thomas wrote for Rehnquist, Stevens, O’Connor and Kennedy. Ginsburg dissented, joined by Breyer and, for Parts II and III, Scalia and Souter.
* **Historical Holding:** The domestic wire-fraud prosecution targeting Canadian tax revenue fell within §1343 and was not barred by the revenue rule.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 349.
* **Historical Sources:** [Opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/349/).

## 60. Pace v. DiGuglielmo

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed, 5-4, on April 27, 2005.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist wrote for O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Stevens dissented, joined by Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The Court held an untimely state application not properly filed despite exceptions and, assuming equitable tolling could apply, found Pace insufficiently diligent.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 408.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical opinion and dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/408/).

## 61. Bates v. Dow Agrosciences LLC

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, with seven Justices joining the Court’s opinion and two concurring in the judgment in part and dissenting in part.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Stevens wrote for Rehnquist, O’Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Breyer concurred separately. Thomas, joined by Scalia, wrote separately in partial concurrence and dissent.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court rejected the inducement test, recognized genuinely parallel state labeling duties, and remanded the Texas-law comparison. It distinguished nonlabel duties and voluntary warranties from different or additional labeling requirements.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 431.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/431/).

## 63. Granholm v. Heald

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Michigan judgments were affirmed and the New York judgment reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Kennedy wrote for Scalia, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Stevens dissented with O’Connor. Thomas separately dissented with Rehnquist, Stevens and O’Connor.
* **Historical Holding:** The historical Court held both discriminatory systems unconstitutional and rejected the §2 justification; neutral regulation remained available.
* **Historical Authority:** 544 U.S. 460.
* **Historical Sources:** [Historical Supreme Court opinions](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/544/460/).
