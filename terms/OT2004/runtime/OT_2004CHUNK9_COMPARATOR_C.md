# OT2004 chunk 9 — COMPARATOR C

## 86. San Remo Hotel, L.P. v. City and County of San Francisco

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court unanimously affirmed the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens wrote for five Justices. Chief Justice Rehnquist concurred in the judgment, joined by Justices O'Connor, Kennedy and Thomas, and questioned the continuing justification for the state-litigation requirement.
* **Historical Holding:** Section 1738 contains no exception for takings plaintiffs who litigated in state court to satisfy Williamson County. The historical concurrence's criticism did not overrule that requirement.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 323; No. 04-340.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and concurrence](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/323/).

## 87. Dodd v. United States

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court affirmed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O'Connor wrote for Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Justice Stevens dissented with other dissenters joining portions; Justice Ginsburg also dissented, joined by Justice Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The one-year period begins at initial Supreme Court recognition, not the later determination of retroactive applicability.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 353; No. 04-5286.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and dissents](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/353/).

## 88. Rompilla v. Beard

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter wrote for Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer. Justice O'Connor concurred. Justice Kennedy dissented with Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Scalia and Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Counsel unreasonably failed to inspect the prior-conviction file the prosecution would use, and the omitted mitigation established sentencing prejudice.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 374; No. 04-5462.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/374/).

## 89. Graham County Soil & Water Conservation District v. United States ex rel. Wilson

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court reversed and remanded, 7-2 in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Thomas wrote the principal opinion, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy, and by Justice Souter except for one footnote. Justice Stevens concurred in the judgment. Justice Breyer dissented, joined by Justice Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** The six-year fraud-based provision does not govern §3730(h) retaliation; the appropriate state period must be borrowed and selected on remand.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 409; No. 04-169.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and separate writings](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/409/).

## 90. American Trucking Associations, Inc. v. Michigan Public Service Commission

### SECTION III: HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

#### A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The historical Court unanimously affirmed in the judgment.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer wrote for seven Justices. Justices Scalia and Thomas separately concurred in the judgment.
* **Historical Holding:** The evenhanded local-hauling fee did not violate the dormant Commerce Clause on the record presented; the Court distinguished flat interstate highway charges.
* **Historical Authority:** 545 U.S. 429; No. 03-1230.
* **Historical Sources:** [Reported opinion and concurrences](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/429/).
