## Standards and Tests selection: Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects

### Commerce connection, mechanism, and class-wide effects

**Governing question:** How courts determine whether a regulated class falls within Congress’s Commerce Clause authority, including when aggregation, a case-specific nexus, or inclusion within a broader federal regime may perform constitutional work.

**Current rule:** The Court first identifies the class Congress actually regulated and the interstate mechanism asserted to connect that class to commerce among the several States. Interstate commerce itself, channels, instrumentalities/persons/things genuinely in commerce, and properly defined economic classes participating in or materially affecting actual interstate markets remain established routes. Congress may also include intrastate conduct that is neither itself interstate commerce nor independently shown to have substantial interstate effects when inclusion of the regulated class is a reasonably adapted and integral means of executing an otherwise valid federal regulation of interstate commerce. That necessary-inclusion route requires a genuine functional relationship between the independently valid interstate regulation and the intrastate class actually covered; a declared national objective or an assembly of unrelated provisions in one statute does not establish it. No separate proof is required that every included act or species crosses state boundaries, participates in interstate commerce or would independently produce a substantial interstate commercial effect. A defendant may show that a purported application is outside the relevant statutory class, that the supposed interstate regime is not independently valid, or that the asserted integral relationship is merely nominal or pretextual. Independently, Congress may also reach noneconomic intrastate conduct when it reasonably identifies, on a materially adequate legislative or adjudicative basis, a substantial interstate externality generated by the regulated class that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize, and the federal prohibition reasonably fits that demonstrated spillover; that alternative need not be satisfied where necessary inclusion establishes power. Aggregate magnitude and substantiality may measure the effect of a class only after a constitutionally sufficient connection has been identified; generalized chains through crime, education, health, insurance, consumption, productivity, public expenditure, or similar downstream consequences cannot themselves create the missing connection.

The Government must identify the interstate mechanism it invokes. For necessary inclusion, it identifies the independently valid interstate regulation, the intrastate class actually covered and a genuine functional relationship between the two, which may appear from the statute's operation and any supporting legislative or adjudicative basis; for the externality route, it supports each part of the cross-border mechanism and its fit with a materially adequate legislative or adjudicative basis. The court independently determines whether the asserted interstate mechanism is constitutionally eligible and decides those legal boundaries. Once that mechanism exists, Congress receives substantial latitude on predictive judgment, magnitude, regulatory necessity and practical regulatory design; the Necessary and Proper Clause is not a least-restrictive-means provision, and the Government need not prove that every conceivable exemption would cause a quantitatively demonstrated failure of enforcement. A court may not invent a market, externality, causal pathway, regime or relationship that the statute and its supporting legislative or adjudicative basis do not supply. A reasonable and judicially identifiable fit must exist between the class Congress enacted and the mechanism supporting authority. The limiting-principle inquiry tests whether the asserted mechanism leaves an enforceable distinction between the enumerated commerce power and a general police power; it is not a free-form balance of federal and State interests.

The externality route requires identification of the regulated class; a concrete cross-border ecological, commercial or comparable interstate effect; why that effect is substantial rather than speculative or merely nationally interesting; why the affected States cannot reasonably internalize or control it through ordinary territorial authority; and the reasonable fit of the federal prohibition to that effect. Biodiversity's importance, speculative scientific or future economic value, tourism, development pressure, the statute's comprehensiveness, and the occurrence of similar problems in many States are insufficient by themselves. The rule requires no fixed number of States, incidents, dollars or species, market percentage or scientific threshold. The route is an independent alternative; it need not be satisfied where necessary inclusion establishes congressional power.

A court may not save an unambiguous federal offense by inserting an omitted commerce element. A statutory jurisdictional element is relevant only insofar as the connection it requires actually performs constitutional work; the mere existence of jurisdictional words is not itself a safe harbor. Remote historical movement may satisfy a statutory nexus, but it does not by itself supply a constitutional basis for regulating a possession class; Congress cannot evade Lopez merely by adding a jurisdictional element.

For Hobbs Act robbery, direct impairment of the operating assets of an active business participating in interstate commerce supplies the required commercial connection when the enacted commerce element is proved. An individual robbery need not have a substantial effect; aggregation measures this connected class's magnitude. A business label, a victim's potential consumption, or money's eventual interstate use cannot replace the connection. The commerce element remains an element of the offense, not subject-matter jurisdiction.

Under ESA §9(a)(1)(B), the regulated class is the taking of listed wildlife, including habitat-caused injury, without a commercial element. The economic character of particular development projects does not by itself supply a constitutionally sufficient connection fitted to that enacted class; the asserted mechanism for these applications must be identified independently of those commercial features. Within the Act's integrated system regulating interstate exploitation, movement and commercial disposition of protected wildlife, the unauthorized taking of listed wildlife is a reasonably adapted and integral means of preserving the populations and enforceability of that system; §9(a)(1)(B), as applied to the listed Delhi Sands flower-loving fly, falls within the Commerce and Necessary and Proper powers without proof that the species is traded or crosses state boundaries. That constitutional conclusion establishes no actual taking and leaves the statutory conditions for liability, permits and relief intact. The distinct statutory requirements for habitat-based harm and conditional permission remain governed by Habitat injury within the Endangered Species Act’s harm prohibition.

The recipient-possession restrictions in §§922(g)(1) and (g)(9) have a distinct fitted connection to the corresponding §922(d)(1) and (d)(9) prohibited-recipient supply restrictions: preventing the disqualified recipients’ acquisition and retention from evading or materially impairing those valid supply restrictions supports inclusion under the Commerce and Necessary and Proper Clauses. The enacted interstate conditions remain separately provable offense requirements; historical interstate movement alone is not the constitutional mechanism. Section 922(g)(1) requires neither contemporaneous movement nor an individually substantial or direct interstate effect beyond its retained nexus construction. The prosecution must prove the enacted nexus beyond a reasonable doubt.

**Sequence or decisionmaker:**

1. Define the enacted regulated class.
2. Identify the asserted interstate-commerce connection.
3. Identify the mechanism connecting the class to interstate commerce.
4. Determine whether the mechanism is constitutionally eligible.
5. Only then use aggregation or substantiality to evaluate class-wide magnitude.
6. Give Congress substantial latitude on prediction and regulatory design within the valid power.
7. Test whether the statutory class reasonably fits the asserted mechanism.
8. Confirm that the theory leaves an enforceable constitutional boundary.

**Exceptions and limits:** Noneconomic intrastate conduct is not categorically immune. Traditional State subject matter is not categorically immune. Formal legislative findings are neither universally required nor sufficient by themselves. Wickard and Perez retain their economic-class scope. Garcia’s rejection of traditional-governmental-function immunity remains intact. Remote movement and generalized social costs independently establish none of the current Commerce grounds. Mussari/Schroeder directly regulates the private obligor: it commands no State administration and gives no federal power to issue or modify support, custody, parentage or domestic-status orders. The cross-border payment rule adds no flight, travel, voluntary-crossing, bank-wire or evasion-motive element. Not every protester is an interstate enterprise; the service-market ground reaches conduct directed at provision or receipt of the covered services. Section 922(o)(2) preserves transfer to or by, and possession by or under the authority of, the United States or its departments or agencies, or a State or its departments, agencies or political subdivisions. It separately preserves lawful transfer or possession of a machinegun lawfully possessed before May 19, 1986; earlier manufacture alone is insufficient. No Second Amendment, amended-statute or independent indictment rule is established by these commerce holdings.

**Open question:** Purely personal or household robbery, every asserted depletion theory, a universal dollar floor and other Hobbs Act applications remain unresolved. Home Builders determines no actual taking, section 10 permit or independent property claim and does not apply the externality route to the fly; it announces no categorical immunity for intrastate takings, and a defendant retains the outside-class, invalid-regime and nominal-or-pretextual showings. Calling a statute comprehensive does not itself establish the required relationship, which for the ESA rests on the taking prohibition's function within the Act's valid interstate regulation of listed wildlife; no universal formal-legislative-findings requirement, defendant-specific congressional-findings requirement, clear-and-convincing burden or numerical benchmark is adopted.

**Related current rules:** Habitat injury within the Endangered Species Act’s harm prohibition supplies the full statutory scope, causal limits and conditional permissions retained in Home Builders. Property-function connection under the federal arson statute states Hinds's separate statutory inquiry; it does not amend Commerce doctrine.

## Standards and Tests selection: Reproductive liberty and abortion regulation

## Reproductive liberty and abortion regulation

### Protected choice and the six-step standard for previability regulation

**Governing question:** Whether a legal obligation regulating previability abortion impermissibly interferes with the patient's protected decision or access to timely, clinically appropriate care.

**Current rule:** Before viability the patient decides whether to continue pregnancy without proving medical necessity or obtaining official approval of her reasons. The State may not prohibit that choice, confer effective control on another person, or prevent timely necessary life-and-health care. After viability, prohibition requires the life-and-health safeguard, including individualized physical and mental health considerations. Viability is individualized and medically supported, not a fixed constitutional week. Clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable care, not official approval of the patient's reasons; not every preference is a medical emergency. The following complete standard governs throughout previability:

1. **Identify the duty and protected baseline.** Identify the enacted command, authoritative construction, exceptions, enforcement consequences and relevant medical circumstances. Distinguish an actual obligation from assumed future administration. A previability prohibition, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care violates the baseline and cannot be rescued by justification. Necessary care must remain available before imminent death or catastrophic deterioration. Defining all noncompliant treatment as unsafe does not establish medical necessity.
2. **Identify actual function and purpose.** Identify what the obligation does and the asserted permissible objective, including safety, informed consent, truthful information, permissible parental involvement, health administration or financial accountability. The State may prefer childbirth and communicate truthful information. Moral disapproval does not independently justify obstruction, punishment of protected choice or burdens imposed for their deterrent effect. An obstructive purpose established by objective evidence of text, design, context and operation independently invalidates the requirement; a legislator's opposition to abortion alone does not establish that purpose. Voluntary persuasion differs from prevention through barriers. Evaluate each justification on its terms: medical claims need medical support, and deliberative claims need an explanation linking the particular obligation to informed decisionmaking.
3. **Establish actual interference, comparison and affected population.** Challengers must establish concrete additional interference caused by the challenged obligation. Relevant effects include extra appointments, material expense, consequential delay, loss of usable provider capacity, clinical risk, coercion and disclosure consequences affecting choice or access. Compare the actual system with and without the obligation while holding other relevant law and circumstances constant. For a challenged interacting package, identify the package and compare the system without it. Consider cumulative effects without counting the same burden twice. Define the affected population from the duty's operation before deciding constitutional injury, include evidence-supported subgroups, and keep the population stable. Do not average the burden across everyone pregnant or define the group as only those who prevail. Poverty, distance, transportation, employment and abuse matter insofar as they explain the State's added condition; identify that causal link. Exceptions, bypasses and alternative providers count only if actually usable. Meaningful interference means concrete, nontrivial additional burden on obtaining care or exercising the protected decision. It triggers review rather than establishing invalidity. No universal dollar, mileage or hourly threshold applies. Reliable clinical evidence, records, testimony and reasonable predictions can establish sufficiently demonstrated prospective injury before access is lost.
4. **Apply the substantive protections in order.** First, a baseline violation, unconstitutional obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle is independently invalid. A substantial obstacle seriously obstructs the affected population's practical opportunity for timely, clinically appropriate protected care; absolute impossibility is unnecessary, and success by some patients does not establish adequate access for others. A compelling interest cannot rescue an established obstacle. Second, remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Identify the severe intrusion and explain why the independent prohibitions did not already decide the issue; overlapping protections require no artificial boundary, but an obstacle may not be relabeled as justifiable severe interference. Serious intrusion into clinical judgment, health or protected confidentiality may require this scrutiny despite usable access. Third, for remaining meaningful interference the State must substantiate a permissible justification and its fit to the added obligation. Abstract interests and conclusory assertions are insufficient. When challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it; inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequences may answer that showing. The State need not select the cheapest imaginable policy or disprove every hypothetical alternative. Reliable experience and reasonable predictions may suffice; particular legislative findings, a new study in every case, scientific certainty and numerical benefit calculations are not required. Once justification and fit are established, courts may not substitute their preferred policy. Below meaningful interference this heightened access inquiry ends, while prohibited-purpose claims, independent constitutional protections and ordinary lawful-regulation requirements remain available.
5. **Protect clinical judgment and truthful communication.** Evaluate medical justifications against competent evidence, accepted professional standards, the patient's circumstances and substantial contrary evidence. Substantial professional agreement receives serious evidentiary weight, without a professional association's veto or conclusive weight for a clinician's bare assertion or an official's unsupported preference. Necessary care must remain timely; unnecessary committee vetoes and documentation functioning as prior permission may not displace appropriate clinical judgment. Duties must be sufficiently definite and applicable fault requirements respected. Later reasonable medical disagreement alone does not establish every element of a punishable offense; lawful enforcement against fraud, malpractice and professional misconduct remains. Required medical information must be accurate, nonmisleading and material to informed consent. Concise factual assistance notices may inform choice without ideological assent; clinicians may explain limitations and correct misinformation. Accuracy does not alone validate burdensome timing, delivery or certification. Distinguish useful confidential oversight records from public disclosure and records enforcing invalid obligations.
6. **Identify the violation and tailor relief.** Specify the duty or application that fails, why, and the corresponding relief. Preserve independently lawful requirements under applicable severability law and expressly address dependent certifications, report fields and sanctions. An individual claim does not fail because others comply; broader relief requires a corresponding showing. An indivisible mandatory condition may be invalidated when its ordinary operation imposes demonstrated meaningful interference and the State fails to justify it. Voluntary compliance in some circumstances does not alone validate compulsory compliance. Otherwise confine relief to the established applications or groups. This qualification of a strict no-valid-applications approach does not remove proof of causation, scope or inadequate justification. For cumulative burdens, remedy the challenged obligation's or package's contribution without automatically invalidating all regulation. Replacing the trimester timetable does not dissolve injunctions or authorize enforcement of every challenged provision; the mandate must specify the resulting duties.

**Authority by component:**

- Protected previability choice, individualized viability and necessary life-and-health protection: Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, “The framework retaining protected choice”, June 29, 1992; Stone's opinion joined by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter.
- All six steps, including the independent obstacle bar, residual severe-interference scrutiny, meaningful lesser-burden substantiation, proof allocation, clinical safeguards and relief: *Casey*, “The standard governing previability regulation after this decision”; Stone's opinion joined by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor and Souter. These five direct joins supply the whole rule; no *Marks* synthesis is needed.
- Integrated displacement of the independent trimester timetable: *Casey*, “The trimester timetable and the timing of regulation”; the same five-Justice rationale. The other four votes against continuing the timetable do not join the replacement framework.

**Present operation effective:** *Casey*, June 29, 1992.

**Burdens and proof:** Challengers establish the duty, population, causation and triggering interference. The State then bears the applicable justification and tailoring or fit burden. This does not alter ordinary proof of disputed historical facts or the enacted burden for a separate statutory defense. No State substantiation burden arises merely because a law concerns abortion.

**Exceptions and limits:** Gestational age may support a particular medically justified safety requirement; crossing a trimester boundary does not itself change the standard. The rule creates no general professional immunity, funding entitlement, constitutional fetal-personhood rule, conscience ruling, general right to a particular drug, or universal numerical facial-review formula. It does not decide every postviability restriction.

**Material development:** The independent trimester timing restrictions are displaced only with adoption of this complete substitute. Protected choice, viability and necessary life-and-health care survive. Absence of a substantial obstacle no longer ends the inquiry into demonstrated meaningful interference.

**Holdings navigation:** Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Scope of prospective relief for abortion requirements; Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Reproductive liberty and constitutional precedent.

### Spousal notification that gives practical control over protected choice

**Governing question:** Whether compulsory spousal notice and certification may condition an adult patient's protected previability decision.

**Current rule:** A notification requirement is invalid when its proved operation gives spouses practical control over protected decisions for a large fraction of the independently defined women compelled to disclose against their wishes, after accounting for usable exceptions. Voluntary consultation by other patients does not cure that restriction. Identify the compelled group before deciding injury; do not count willing disclosure as a constitutionally valid restrictive application or impose a universal numerical threshold. Formal notice rather than consent does not answer practical prevention. Severance and relief against dependent certification, enforcement and notice-compliance reporting must address the unconstitutional duty; a separately lawful confidential demographic field does not fall merely because it shares a subsection.

**Authority by component:**

- Effective control, affected-group review and notice-specific facial qualification: Casey, “Spousal notice and effective control over the protected decision”, June 29, 1992; Stone, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter directly support the rationale.
- Dependent reporting versus independently severable demographic information: *Casey*, “Notice-compliance and nonnotification-reason reports” (the same six) and “The independently severable marital-status field” (all nine).

**Present operation effective:** *Casey*, June 29, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** The invalidated §3209 allowed substitute signed statements that the spouse was not the father, could not be located after diligent effort, that pregnancy resulted from spousal sexual assault under §3128 reported to an agency with jurisdiction, or that the woman had reason to believe notification was likely to cause bodily injury to her by her spouse or another person; medical emergency separately excused compliance. The incorporated §3128(c) condition required personal reporting by the victim or her agent within 90 days of the assault. Statements required no notarization but had to warn against punishable false statements. These exceptions did not generally reach psychological coercion, financial retaliation, harm to children or others, exposure of private information, or divorce and custody threats. The holding does not independently adjudicate §3128(c)'s criminal-law reporting requirement. That reporting condition does not limit the distinct rape-omission permission for paternal-support information.

**Related current rules:** “Protected choice and the six-step standard for previability regulation” supplies the five-Justice independently dispositive obstacle rule. Kennedy joins this notice-specific rationale without joining that whole standard.

**Holdings navigation:** Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Abortion reporting and public disclosure; Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Spousal control of reproductive choice.

### Medical emergency and the separate disclosure-health defense

**Governing question:** How Pennsylvania's emergency, emergency-explanation and harmful-information provisions operate independently.

**Current rule:** Section 3203 defines emergency through a physician's good-faith clinical judgment that immediate abortion is needed to avert death or that delay creates serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function. It is sustained under the prospective construction that compliance cannot create a significant threat to life or health, permitting treatment of preeclampsia, inevitable abortion and prematurely ruptured membranes at onset rather than after catastrophic deterioration. Under §3205(b), the physician must explain the medical indications before emergency abortion only if possible; explanation is not advance permission or a reason to delay necessary care. Separately, §3205(c) protects a physician from liability for omitting §3205(a) information upon proof by a preponderance of a reasonable belief that furnishing it would severely adversely affect the patient's physical or mental health. Actual occurrence of the feared injury is unnecessary. That defense neither supplies a waiting-period waiver nor replaces emergency treatment protection.

**Authority by component:**

- Emergency construction: Casey, “Medical emergency and the protective construction of §3203”, June 29, 1992.
- Conditional prior explanation: *Casey*, “Emergency explanation under §3205(b).”
- Separate defense and enacted proof burden: *Casey*, “The distinct information-omission defense in §3205(c).” All three are unanimous direct holdings.

**Present operation effective:** *Casey*, June 29, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** The significant-threat interpretation is the Court's adoption of the Third Circuit's prospective construction, not extra statutory language or a binding Pennsylvania Supreme Court construction. No separate emergency-vagueness holding or decision about every clinical risk or postviability restriction follows. The statutory defense's burden remains distinct from the constitutional proof allocation.

**Holdings navigation:** Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Abortion emergency care; Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Abortion informed consent.

### Accurate abortion-consent information, optional materials and receipt certification

**Governing question:** What Pennsylvania may require patients to be told, offered or asked to certify in obtaining informed consent, including independent First Amendment limits.

**Current rule:** Section 3205(a)(1) may require accurate, medically material information about the procedure, material risks and alternatives, probable gestational age and childbirth risks. The three §3205(a)(2) notices may state truthfully: state materials concerning fetal development and alternatives are available and a free copy will be supplied if chosen; medical assistance for prenatal care, childbirth and neonatal care may be available; and the father is liable to assist in support even if he offered to pay for abortion. Assistance and legal liability are not promises of eligibility, payment or collection. Paternal-support information **may** be omitted in a rape pregnancy, including spousal rape, without a police-report condition; omission is permitted, not required, and changes no support obligation. Section 3208 publication and §3205(a)(3) chosen receipt are permissible at their objective, accurate, nonjudgmental scope. Notice of availability is distinct from receipt: patients need not accept, read or endorse materials or listed agencies. Clinicians may correct or criticize them. Section 3205(a)(4) certification may record actual receipt of lawful information, not ideological agreement, unchosen reading or compliance with an invalid prerequisite. The §3205(c) disclosure-health defense remains available on its own terms.

The First Amendment independently permits these bounded medical and ancillary disclosures, voluntary receipt and factual documentation. Payment does not strip professional speech of protection, and truth alone does not justify every compulsory statement. False, irrelevant, coercive or ideological scripts and materially different implementation remain outside the sustained duties. Accurate content does not establish the validity of a timing or exclusive-speaker condition; invalid timing and delivery obligations cannot survive through a certificate.

**Authority by component:**

- Medical content, objective publication/chosen receipt and basic certification: Casey, “Accurate material medical information,” “Statutory publication and chosen receipt of materials,” and “Certification of lawful receipt”, June 29, 1992; unanimous direct holdings at those scopes.
- Each ancillary notice and its qualification: *Casey*, “Notice of available free materials,” “Notice of potentially available medical assistance,” and “Paternal-support notice and the rape omission permission”; Stone, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter supply five rationale joins. The additional judgment votes do not broaden that rationale.
- Independent speech rules: *Casey*, the three “Independent speech challenge” holdings; Souter with White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas for medical content, chosen materials and basic certification; the same group without Blackmun for ancillary notices. Stone's judgment-only agreements supply no speech rationale.
- Dependent timing and delivery links: *Casey*, “Certification of lawful receipt,” with the five-Justice invalidation grounds in “Physician delivery of medical information” and “The mandatory 24-hour interval.”

**Present operation effective:** *Casey*, June 29, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** The rape omission permission has no §3209/§3128 reporting or 90-day condition. No finding establishes universal accuracy of actual publications or agencies. No general commercial-speech classification or professional-speech immunity is adopted. Required content remains subject to the separate access framework.

**Material development:** Akron and Thornburgh are qualified only insofar as they categorically prohibit the sustained concise, accurate ancillary notices because they may encourage childbirth; their protection against misleading, coercive and unjustifiably burdensome implementation remains.

**Holdings navigation:** Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Abortion informed consent; Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992) — Abortion information and compelled speech.

### One-parent informed consent with an independent judicial bypass

**Governing question:** The permissible operation of Pennsylvania §3206's parental-consent structure for unemancipated minors under eighteen.

**Current rule:** The minor's informed consent and one parent's informed consent ordinarily suffice, with emergency treatment excused from compliance. Only the mother's consent is required when the father participated in incest. #### United States v. Lopez

**Docket or dockets:** No. 93-1260.
**Decided:** April 26, 1995.

##### The Commerce Clause requires a constitutionally identifiable connection before aggregate magnitude can establish federal authority

**Holding:** When Congress regulates conduct that is neither interstate commerce itself nor a channel, instrumentality, person, or thing in interstate commerce, the Court must identify the regulated statutory class and the interstate mechanism connecting that class to commerce among the several States before aggregation and substantiality may perform constitutional work. Connection before magnitude: aggregation may measure the effects of a class already connected to interstate commerce; it may not manufacture the missing constitutional connection. Congress may regulate a properly defined class of economic conduct materially participating in or affecting an actual interstate market, but generalized downstream consequences through crime, education, health, insurance, consumption, productivity, or public expenditure cannot themselves supply the missing connection. The original §922(q)(1)(A) school-zone possession prohibition fails at the connection stage: it regulates no interstate transaction, channel, instrumentality, economic class, or identified anti-evasion component of a valid interstate regulatory regime, and the chain from local firearm possession through crime costs, impaired education and reduced productivity to the national economy cannot supply that connection.

**Proposition-level authority:** Chief Justice Stone-Zsela's Opinion of the Court, joined by Justices O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas: five Justices.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court's sequence is: (1) identify the regulated statutory class; (2) identify the asserted Commerce connection or anchor; (3) identify the interstate mechanism that connects that class to commerce among the several States; (4) only after such a constitutionally sufficient connection exists may aggregation and substantiality determine the magnitude of the class-wide effect; (5) Congress receives substantial latitude over factual prediction and regulatory design once it acts inside the enumerated power; (6) there must be a reasonable, judicially identifiable fit between the class Congress actually regulated and the mechanism invoked; and (7) a proposed theory must leave an enforceable boundary between the Commerce power and a general police power, so generalized downstream consequences cannot themselves supply the missing connection. The Court independently determines whether the asserted mechanism is constitutionally adequate. The fit requirement is not strict scrutiny or a least-restrictive-means test, and the boundary inquiry is a diagnostic of the asserted mechanism, not an independent balancing factor. The rule is not an economic/noneconomic categorical switch, a traditional-governmental-function immunity, a mandatory-legislative-findings rule, a closed list of metaphysical Commerce categories, or a rule that every individual instance must substantially affect interstate commerce.

**Material application:** The regulated class is knowing firearm possession in a defined school zone. It is not paid firearm delivery, interstate trafficking, interstate shipment or participation in the commercial firearms market merely because the record contains evidence that Lopez expected $40 for delivery or that the handgun had previously been manufactured outside Texas. The Court accepts that violent crime carries economic costs and need not dispute every empirical step of the education-and-productivity chain; neither theory explains why this statutory class constitutes commerce among the States, and neither leaves an enforceable stopping point.

**Treatment of earlier authority:**

- Wickard v. Filburn, 317 U.S. 111: preserves aggregation at its economic-class scope; the school-possession/productivity chain does not extend that holding to this stand-alone offense.
- Perez v. United States, 402 U.S. 146: preserves Congress’s regulation of an economic class without requiring each instance to have a substantial effect; its regulated commercial setting remains material.
- Allied-Bruce Terminix Cos. v. Dobson, January 18, 1995: distinguishes actual interstate commercial operation from remote-object history; its statutory breadth and reserved constitutional ceiling remain intact.
- Hodel v. Virginia Surface Mining & Reclamation Assn., 452 U.S. 264: preserves broad review of congressional commercial regulation without treating deference as a grant of general police power or imposing a universal formal-findings requirement.
- New York v. United States, June 19, 1992: distinguishes compelled state administration from direct regulation of private persons; neither its anti-compulsion rule nor the traditional local subject alone decides this case.
- Garcia v. San Antonio Metropolitan Transit Authority, 469 U.S. 528: remains undisturbed in its generally applicable regulation of state activity; traditional-governmental-function immunity is not revived.

**Limits and questions not reached:** No categorical exclusion of noneconomic intrastate conduct, reserved State subject area, universal formal-findings requirement or rule that only conduct physically crossing State lines is regulable is adopted. Findings may support legislative facts concerning an identified interstate mechanism, but they cannot create the constitutional connection. No particular form of jurisdictional element is held automatically sufficient. The holding creates no Second Amendment rule and decides no amended possession statute, separate discharge offense or materially different federal firearms offense containing a commerce element.

##### Intrastate conduct necessary to a valid interstate regulatory regime remains potentially regulable

**Holding:** Congress may sometimes include intrastate conduct where inclusion is genuinely necessary and proper to prevent evasion or material impairment of an otherwise valid interstate regulatory regime. That authority derives from the Commerce Clause together with the Necessary and Proper Clause and requires an identifiable relationship between the local class and the valid interstate regime. It cannot save original §922(q)(1)(A): the Government identifies no national firearms-market prohibition whose operation materially depends on criminalizing all possession within 1,000 feet of schools, and the enacted offense does not define its class around concealment, retention, acquisition, trafficking or another mechanism by which school-zone possession undermines a larger federal market regime.

**Proposition-level authority:** Chief Justice Stone-Zsela's Opinion of the Court, joined by Justices O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas: five Justices.

**Limits and questions not reached:** The Court recognizes this route without fully defining it; fuller definition of its requirements is reserved for a case that actually presents it. Lopez does not formulate a separate interstate-externality doctrine: whether materially different intrastate conduct imposing direct and substantial interstate spillovers that individual States cannot practically confine or internalize may be reached remains reserved for a later case actually presenting it.

**Later-authority backlinks:** United States v. Hale, February 12, 1996, Eighth Circuit No. 91-3830, supplies the first operational rule for the reserved anti-evasion/material-impairment route: Congress may include possession within §922(o)'s direct prohibition of additions to the post-May 19, 1986 civilian machinegun market when excluding it would materially impair enforcement of that valid transfer/stock regime; see Constitutional Structure — Federal regulation of economic activity. National Association of Home Builders v. Babbitt, June 8, 1998, Parts II-A and III, gives this reserved route its fuller definition: Congress may include intrastate conduct that is neither itself interstate commerce nor independently shown to have substantial interstate effects when inclusion of the regulated class is a reasonably adapted and integral means of executing an otherwise valid federal regulation of interstate commerce; the Government identifies that regulation, the covered intrastate class and a genuine functional relationship between them, without species-by-species, crossing or independent-effects proof. Part II-B resolves the interstate-externality reservation: the regulated class must generate a materially supported substantial cross-border effect that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize, and the federal prohibition must reasonably fit that effect. That independent route need not be satisfied where necessary inclusion establishes power.

##### The original possession text cannot be saved by adding a new commerce element

**Holding:** The Court will not insert an unexpressed commerce element into original §922(q)(1)(A) to sustain this charge. Constitutional avoidance permits a fair narrowing construction of enacted language; it does not permit the judiciary to enact a different federal offense. The stipulated facts that the handgun was manufactured outside Texas and that Lopez allegedly expected $40 for delivery do not become statutory elements. Because no commerce element is judicially inserted and the constitutional ground requires affirmance of dismissal under the enacted prohibition, the Court does not decide the separate grand-jury question that would arise if such an element were created and then supported only by trial evidence rather than an indictment allegation.

**Proposition-level authority:** Chief Justice Stone-Zsela's Opinion of the Court, joined by Justices O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas: five Justices.

**Treatment of earlier authority:**

- United States v. Bass, 404 U.S. 336: preserves federalism-sensitive construction of ambiguous firearms language, without authorizing addition of an element absent from this unambiguous provision.
- Gregory v. Ashcroft, 501 U.S. 452: preserves its clarity principle within permissible statutory interpretation; it supplies no power to rewrite this offense.
- United States v. X-Citement Video, Inc., November 29, 1994: distinguishes interpretation of an enacted knowledge term from adding a new jurisdictional condition; its separate rules for defending a favorable judgment remain intact.
- Stirone v. United States, 361 U.S. 212: supplies the grand-jury allegation boundary invoked against substituting trial proof for a necessary indictment allegation; the renewed objection remains conditional and is not decided after affirmance on another sufficient ground.
- Russell v. United States, 369 U.S. 749: supplies the grand-jury allegation boundary invoked against curing omission of a necessary offense allegation through later proof; that conditional indictment objection is left unresolved because the commerce ground is sufficient.

**Operative remedy or transition:** The Fifth Circuit's reversal of the original §922(q)(1)(A) conviction and dismissal mandate are affirmed. No prosecution reinstatement, new commerce allegation, sentencing decision or merits remand is ordered. Supervised-release, special-assessment and conditional indictment objections are unnecessary to decide.


## Authority excerpt: constitutional-structure, source line 2576

#### National Association of Home Builders v. Babbitt

**Docket or dockets:** D.C. Circuit No. 96-5354; no separate Supreme Court docket assigned.

**Decided:** 1998-06-08.

##### I. The enacted taking class is not made economic by the projects' commercial features

**Holding:** For this challenge to section 9(a)(1)(B), the regulated class is the taking of listed wildlife, including habitat-caused injury, without a commercial element. The economic character of the reviewed development projects does not by itself supply a constitutionally sufficient connection fitted to that enacted class; the asserted mechanism for these applications must be identified independently of those commercial features.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part I, joined by Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas: five Justices. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer do not join this part, although they join Parts II-A, III and IV and the judgment; Ginsburg and Breyer also join Part II-B.

##### II. Necessary inclusion in a valid interstate scheme sustains the taking prohibition; an independent externality route remains available

**Holding:** Congress may regulate intrastate conduct that is neither itself interstate commerce nor independently shown to have substantial interstate effects when inclusion of the regulated class is a reasonably adapted and integral means of executing an otherwise valid federal regulation of interstate commerce. The Court identifies the independently valid interstate regulation, the intrastate class actually covered, and a genuine functional relationship between the two; Congress may not establish that relationship merely by declaring a national objective or assembling unrelated provisions into one statute. Where Congress has established an integrated system regulating interstate exploitation, movement and commercial disposition of protected wildlife, it may include the unauthorized taking of listed wildlife as a means of preserving the populations and enforceability of that system. The Constitution does not require separate proof that every species crosses state boundaries, participates in interstate commerce, or would independently produce a substantial interstate commercial effect if left unprotected. A defendant may show that a purported application is outside the relevant statutory class, that the supposed interstate regime is not independently valid, or that the asserted integral relationship is merely nominal or pretextual.

The section 9(a)(1)(B) taking prohibition, as applied to the listed Delhi Sands flower-loving fly within the Act's integrated system, falls within Congress's Commerce and Necessary and Proper powers. This constitutional conclusion neither establishes an actual taking nor eliminates the statutory conditions for liability, permits or relief.

Congress may also regulate noneconomic intrastate conduct when it reasonably identifies, on a materially adequate legislative or adjudicative basis, a substantial interstate externality generated by the regulated class that individual States cannot reasonably confine or internalize, and the federal prohibition reasonably fits that demonstrated spillover. That route is an independent alternative. It need not be satisfied where necessary inclusion establishes congressional power, and it is not applied to this fly.

The second route requires identification of the regulated class; a concrete cross-border ecological, commercial or comparable interstate effect; why that effect is substantial rather than speculative or merely nationally interesting; why the affected States cannot reasonably internalize or control it through ordinary territorial authority; and the reasonable fit of the federal prohibition to that effect. Biodiversity's importance, speculative scientific or future economic value, tourism, development pressure, the statute's comprehensiveness, and the occurrence of similar problems in many States are insufficient by themselves. The rule requires no fixed number of States, incidents, dollars or species, market percentage or scientific threshold.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part II-A's necessary-inclusion rule and its application are joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer: seven Justices. Part II-B's independent externality rule and its complete limits are joined by Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer: five Justices. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer join Part II-A while preserving their disagreement with Part I.

##### III. The Government identifies the regime, the covered class and a genuine functional relationship; courts decide those boundaries independently

**Holding:** The Government must identify the independently valid interstate regulation, the intrastate class actually covered, and a genuine functional relationship between the two. Courts determine those legal boundaries independently while respecting reasonable congressional judgments about regulatory necessity and practical enforcement. The Necessary and Proper Clause is not a least-restrictive-means provision: the Government need not prove that every conceivable exemption would cause a quantitatively demonstrated failure of enforcement. A court may not invent a regime or relationship that the statute and its supporting legislative or adjudicative basis do not supply.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part III, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer: seven Justices.

**Limits and questions not reached:** For necessary inclusion, the relationship appears from the statute's operation and any supporting legislative or adjudicative basis. Here the Act's text and structure identify each element: its valid regulation of interstate trade in listed wildlife, the taking class section 9(a)(1)(B) actually covers, and that class's function in preserving the populations the trade provisions regulate and keeping those provisions enforceable. General claims that endangered species are valuable, that people travel to see wildlife, or that research might produce future commercial uses do not perform that work by themselves. No universal formal-legislative-findings requirement, defendant-specific congressional-findings requirement, clear-and-convincing burden or numerical benchmark is imposed. The obligation does not presume that every individual act must cross a state boundary or produce a separately substantial effect.

##### IV. The judgment sustaining the challenged applications is affirmed

**Holding:** The D.C. Circuit's judgment sustaining application of section 9(a)(1)(B) to the challenged habitat modification against this Commerce Clause objection is affirmed. Part II-A resolves the constitutional question presented, so no Commerce Clause evidentiary remand follows. The builders receive no constitutional exemption from section 9 and no injunction against its enforcement on the Commerce Clause theory; the Government may enforce the statute subject to its actual elements and exceptions.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part IV, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer: seven Justices.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Sweet Home requires significant habitat modification or degradation that actually kills or injures protected wildlife by significantly impairing essential behavior, including breeding, feeding or sheltering. Proximate causation and ordinary foreseeability remain required; speculative future harm or bare but-for linkage does not suffice. An omission requires an independently existing legal duty, and this decision creates no general habitat-maintenance duty. No actual injury, liability or permit entitlement is newly found. Otherwise lawful development alone is not an exemption. Section 10(a)(1)(B) permission remains confined to taking incidental to, rather than the purpose of, otherwise lawful activity. A conservation plan must state likely impacts, minimization and mitigation measures and funding, alternatives considered and reasons rejected, and other necessary appropriate measures. After public comment the Secretary must find incidental taking, maximum-practicable minimization and mitigation, adequate funding, no appreciable reduction in survival-and-recovery likelihood in the wild, fulfillment of other required measures and receipt of assurances. Those findings require issuance with necessary or appropriate conditions and reporting; noncompliance requires revocation. The substation's existing permit decides no entitlement concerning the separate intersection/corridor controversy.

The distinct threatened-species protections, section 7 agency and incidental-take provisions, scientific permissions, experimental-population provisions and enforcement channels receive no new application here. Sweet Home's reserved section 6(g)(2) question remains reserved; deciding its terms or application would require the operative official text and applicable State-agreement record. The Court inserts no commercial element into section 9 and announces no categorical immunity for traditional State subjects.

**Operative remedy or transition:** Affirmed, 7–2; Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support the disposition, and Scalia and Thomas would reverse and remand for relief confined to the reviewed applications. The D.C. Circuit's judgment sustaining application of section 9(a)(1)(B) to the challenged habitat modification against this Commerce Clause objection stands, and no evidentiary remand follows. ##### Whether a housing project's commercial character replaces the enacted wildlife-taking class

**Holding:** The commercial character of a housing project does not by itself establish Congress's authority to apply the Endangered Species Act's wildlife-taking prohibition. **Holding:** Under National Association of Home Builders v. Babbitt, Congress may regulate intrastate conduct that is neither itself interstate commerce nor independently shown to have substantial interstate effects when inclusion of the regulated class is a reasonably adapted and integral means of executing an otherwise valid federal regulation of interstate commerce. The Endangered Species Act's integrated system regulating interstate exploitation, movement and commercial disposition of listed wildlife may include the unauthorized taking of listed wildlife as a means of preserving the populations and enforceability of that system, without separate proof that each species crosses state boundaries, participates in interstate commerce or would independently produce a substantial interstate commercial effect. The section 9(a)(1)(B) taking prohibition, as applied to the listed arroyo toad within that system, falls within Congress's Commerce and Necessary and Proper powers; Rancho Viejo has not shown that the application is outside the statutory class, that the Act's interstate regulation is not independently valid, or that the relationship is nominal or pretextual.

**Proposition-level authority:** Kennedy's Parts II and III, joined by all nine Justices.

**Material application:** The statute's own operation identifies its valid interstate wildlife regulation, the taking class and the population-protection and enforceability relationship. Courts independently determine those boundaries while respecting reasonable congressional judgments. The project's commercial character, general biodiversity value, speculative future value, tourism and development pressure do not substitute for that relationship.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Home Builders's independent externality route remains available but need not be satisfied. Statutory liability still requires Sweet Home's actual killing or injury from significant habitat modification impairing essential behavior, proximate causation and ordinary foreseeability; speculative injury and bare but-for causation do not suffice, an omission requires an independent legal duty, and otherwise lawful development supplies no exemption. Section 10 permission remains governed by its own terms.

**Operative remedy or transition:** The constitutional judgment sustaining the application is affirmed on the necessary-inclusion ground, without a Commerce Clause evidentiary remand; the text-and-structure relationship does not depend on further species-specific facts. The Government may enforce section 9 subject to its actual elements and exceptions. The decision determines no actual taking or permit entitlement.

**Treatment of earlier authority:** Home Builders's necessary-inclusion holding is reaffirmed and applied to another listed single-State species, with its outside-class, invalid-regime and nominal-or-pretextual-relationship limits intact. Holston illustrates the method, but its image-supply facts establish no wildlife market or ecological spillover. Hale and Kirk remain distinct necessary-inclusion applications. Sweet Home's statutory causation rules remain separate.

##### The standard governing previability regulation after this decision

**Holding:** The Court adopts a complete six-step standard governing previability abortion regulation. Substantial obstacles and violations of the protected baseline are independently invalid. Remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring. Remaining meaningful interference requires the State to substantiate its justification and fit and answer identified practicable, substantially less burdensome alternatives. The complete standard replaces the independent trimester timetable in the same holding.

The adopted inquiry has the following complete operative scope:

1. Identify the enacted duty, authoritative construction, exceptions, enforcement consequences and relevant medical circumstances, separating actual commands from assumed administration. A previability prohibition, effective third-party veto or denial of timely necessary life-and-health care independently violates the protected baseline and cannot be justified later in the inquiry. Necessary care must remain available before imminent death or catastrophic deterioration makes intervention unavoidable. Clinical appropriateness concerns safe, suitable care, not official approval of the patient's reasons or a legislative definition declaring all noncompliant care unsafe.
2. Identify actual function and purpose. Safety, informed consent, truthful information, permissible parental involvement, health administration and financial accountability may supply permissible functions. Truthful persuasion and a preference for childbirth are permissible; moral disapproval, punishment or deterrence through blocked access are not independent justifications. Obstructive purpose established objectively from text, design, context and operation independently invalidates the duty; a legislator's opposition to abortion alone does not establish it. Medical and deliberative justifications require support addressing their actual functions.
3. Challengers must establish a concrete, nontrivial additional interference attributable to the duty, including demonstrated expense, appointments, delay, provider-capacity loss, clinical risk, coercion or disclosure consequences. Compare the system with and without that obligation, or the identified challenged package, holding other law and circumstances constant. Consider cumulative operation without double counting. Define the affected population from the duty's operation before deciding injury, retain that group throughout, and use evidence-supported subgroups where appropriate. Do not average burdens over all pregnancies or define the group only as successful claimants. Poverty, distance, work, transportation and abuse matter through their causal connection to the State's added condition; exceptions, bypasses and providers count only if usable. No universal monetary, distance or time cutoff applies. Reliable evidence and reasonable predictions may establish prospective interference without waiting for lost access. Meaningful interference triggers review but does not itself establish invalidity.
4. Apply the protections in sequence. A violation of the baseline, obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle ends the inquiry in invalidity. An obstacle seriously obstructs practical opportunity for timely, clinically appropriate protected care in the affected population; absolute impossibility is unnecessary, and successful compliance by some does not establish access for others. No compelling interest rescues an established obstacle. Remaining severe interference requires a compelling permissible justification and narrow tailoring; identify the severe intrusion and why the first inquiry did not resolve it. The protections may overlap, but an obstacle cannot be relabeled as justifiable severe interference. Serious intrusion into clinical decisionmaking, health or protected confidentiality may require exacting justification even where practical access remains usable. For remaining meaningful lesser interference, the State must substantiate its permissible justification and the fit of the additional duty. If challengers identify a concrete, practicable, substantially less burdensome alternative adequately serving the objective, the State must answer it, including any inadequacy, unavailability or material countervailing consequence. Reliable experience and reasonable predictions may suffice: no particular legislative finding, new study in every case, scientific certainty, numerical benefit calculation or disproof of every hypothetical alternative is required. A court may not substitute its preferred policy once justification and fit are established. Below meaningful interference, the heightened access inquiry ends, but prohibited-purpose claims, independent constitutional protections and ordinary legal requirements remain. Challengers bear duty, population, causation and trigger; the State bears the applicable justification, tailoring or fit burden. Ordinary proof rules for historical facts and separate statutory defenses do not change.
5. Protect clinical judgment and truthful communication. Assess medical reasons against competent evidence, accepted standards, individual circumstances and substantial contrary evidence. Substantial professional agreement has serious evidentiary weight but no association holds a veto; neither a bare clinical assertion nor unsupported official preference is conclusive. Necessary care must remain timely, without an unnecessary committee veto or documentation used as prior permission. Duties must be definite and applicable culpability requirements respected; reasonable later professional disagreement alone does not establish every element of an offense. Fraud, malpractice and misconduct remain enforceable. Required medical information must be accurate, nonmisleading and material. Concise factual assistance notices need not entail ideological assent; clinicians may explain limitations and correct misinformation. Accurate content does not itself justify burdensome timing, delivery or certification. Analyze useful confidential records separately from public disclosure and enforcement of invalid conditions.
6. Identify the particular violation and tailor relief. Preserve independently lawful requirements under severability law and address dependent certifications, reports and sanctions expressly. Another patient's ability to comply does not defeat an individual claim. Broader relief requires a corresponding showing. An indivisible mandatory condition may be invalidated when its ordinary operation causes demonstrated meaningful interference and the State fails to justify it; voluntary compliance in some cases alone does not justify compulsion. Otherwise limit relief to proved applications or groups. This qualification of a strict no-valid-applications approach does not dispense with causation, scope or justification analysis. Cumulative-burden relief addresses the challenged contribution, not all existing regulation. Replacement of the timetable neither dissolves injunctions nor authorizes every challenged duty.

- Carey v. Population Services International — Retains the connection between protected reproductive choice and meaningful access to its exercise; its existence undercuts a claim that Roe's foundation has been doctrinally displaced.

- Akron v. Akron Center for Reproductive Health and Thornburgh v. American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists — No wholesale overruling follows from this stare-decisis holding; each actual qualification, retained protection or changed rule is identified in the provision-specific holdings.

#### Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, 505 U.S. 833 (1992)

**Docket or dockets:** Nos. 91-744 and 91-902.
**Decided:** June 29, 1992.

For the other questions in Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey, June 29, 1992, Nos. 91-744 and 91-902, see Civil Rights — Reproductive liberty and constitutional precedent (retained protection, governing framework and timetable), Abortion emergency care, Abortion informed consent, Minor consent and independent authorization, Spousal control of reproductive choice, and Abortion reporting and public disclosure; Federal Courts — Scope of prospective relief for abortion requirements; and First Amendment — Abortion information and compelled speech. Each question has one primary entry.

##### Spousal notice and effective control over the protected decision

**Holding:** Section 3209 is invalid because its compulsory notice and certification condition gives spouses practical control over protected previability decisions for a large fraction of the independently defined women whom it requires to disclose against their wishes; voluntary consultation does not cure that operation. Its exceptions do not adequately protect that group, so the notice requirement and dependent enforcement are severed and enjoined; no general numerical facial-review formula is adopted.

**Proposition-level authority:** Stone's opinion, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter: 6 direct rationale joins including the author.

**Material application:** For the five framework Justices an established substantial obstacle independently ends justification. Kennedy joins the notice-specific effective-control and affected-group rationale but not the complete framework. No compelling-interest assertion rescues that obstacle and no universal numerical facial formula follows.

**Limits and questions not reached:** The affected population is married women compelled to notify against their wishes after the actual exceptions, defined independently of the constitutional conclusion. The record establishes effective obstruction for a large fraction of that group without assigning a percentage. Under §3209, a signed substitute statement may certify that the spouse is not the father; diligent effort failed to locate him; pregnancy resulted from reported spousal sexual assault under §3128; or there is reason to believe notice would likely cause bodily injury to the woman by the spouse or another individual. Medical emergency independently excuses notice. Statements need no notarization but must warn that false statements are punishable. The incorporated assault involves sexual intercourse or deviate sexual intercourse through force, threat of force preventing resistance by a person of reasonable resolution, or an unconscious spouse. The victim or her agent must personally report to an agency with jurisdiction within ninety days of commission, not discovery of pregnancy or the abortion request. That statutory clock is distinct from district finding 297's explanation of its practical narrowing and is not independently adjudicated as a criminal-law rule. The exceptions do not generally cover economic or psychological coercion, threats to children or others, publicity, divorce or custody retaliation. Failure to qualify for one substitute does not establish safety; another substitute may remain available. None of these reporting conditions applies to the separate rape-information omission permission in §3205(a)(2)(iii). Independent spousal speech, equality and generalized privacy grounds are not reached by a controlling rationale.

**Operative remedy or transition:** Affirm the Third Circuit in No. 91-902. Sever and enjoin notice, its certifications and dependent enforcement, while preserving voluntary consultation. The invalid enforcement includes professional discipline and the knowing-violation civil action available to the spouse who is the father, with resulting damages, $5,000 punitive damages and prevailing-plaintiff fees. Remove dependent notice-report fields through the separately stated judgment.

**Treatment of earlier authority:**

- Danforth, 428 U.S. 52 (1976): extended from formal spousal consent to notice proved to give effective control; voluntary consultation remains lawful.
- Ohio v. Akron Center, 497 U.S. 502, 514 (1990): its no-valid-applications formulation is qualified for this notice condition; voluntary disclosure by unaffected women does not defeat the demonstrated facial defect.
- Bellotti II, Ashcroft and Hodgson: distinguished because parental responsibility and effective bypass for minors do not establish marital authority over an adult.
- Roe and Doe: supply the protected decision and its life-and-health boundary.

#### Women’s Medical Professional Corp. v. Voinovich

**Docket or dockets:** Sixth Circuit Nos. 96-3157 and 96-3159; no separate Supreme Court docket supplied.

**Decided:** 1998-04-20.

##### The complete Casey framework governs the facial and access claims

**Holding:** Casey’s complete six-step standard, as applied in Fargo, governs the challenged previability regulation. Neither Salerno’s no-valid-applications barrier nor an independent numerical large-fraction threshold displaces that inquiry, and failure to establish a substantial obstacle does not end review of remaining severe or meaningful interference.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part I; Souter, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O’Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer. These six directly adopt the governing standard, including its proof allocation and qualifications:

**Limits and questions not reached:** The complete governing framework is the six-step rule in Casey, as applied in Fargo: identify the duty and protected baseline; identify actual function and purpose; establish attributable interference and a stable affected population; apply the ordered substantive protections; protect clinical judgment and accurate communication; identify the violation and tailor relief. Challengers prove duty, population, causation and triggering interference; the State bears the corresponding justification and fit burden. No substantiation burden arises from the subject of abortion alone.

##### An act-defined method and professional clinical criteria are not categorically unintelligible

**Holding:** Ohio’s method definition and specified good-faith, reasonable-medical-judgment criteria cannot be invalidated on the categorical ground that statutory and medical procedure labels overlap or that every subsidiary clinical judgment lacks an express culpability term. Courts must examine notice and enforcement guidance, actual fault for the relevant element, criminal and civil liability, and substantive access as distinct questions; this holding neither supplies a universal fault rule nor validates every enforcement.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part II; Souter and all seven other Associates. Stone-Zsela dissents.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Purposeful skull suction is the defined act, with suction curettage and suction aspiration expressly excluded; knowingly performing or attempting it supplies the separate prohibition. Overlap with some D&E does not itself establish vagueness, ban every D&E or prove substantive validity. Criminal and civil fault remain distinct; no universal Ohio default fault level, professional immunity or knowledge-of-illegality rule is supplied.

##### Postviability prohibition must preserve the presented necessary mental-health care

**Holding:** A State may prohibit abortion after viability, but it must permit abortion necessary in competent individualized medical judgment to avert grave, medically established danger of severe, lasting and potentially irreversible mental or emotional injury. The patient need not await catastrophe; transient distress, unsupported assertion or mere preference does not establish the protected necessity.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part III.A; Souter, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O’Connor, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer: seven direct joins. Part III.B’s particular construction and relief directions receive the same joins except Stone-Zsela’s: six direct joins.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Part III.B requires an Ohio-law-supported protective construction of §§2919.17(A), 2919.16(F)/(J) and genuinely dependent duties if available; otherwise the established unconstitutional exclusion remains unenforceable. Courts may neither invent an unlimited exception nor assume all protective constructions impossible. Other postviability duties are not automatically invalidated.

##### The method prohibition requires application to actual conduct, care and alternatives

**Holding:** The final method-ban disposition must be vacated where categorical notice reasoning or the displaced facial formula substituted for Casey’s substantive inquiry. On remand, the lower court must assess actual affected applications, attributable interference, usable alternative care and the distinct statutory defenses before deciding invalidity and matching relief; previability and postviability applications remain distinct.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part IV; Souter, joined by Stevens, O’Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer: five direct joins.

**Limits and questions not reached:** The physician’s prima facie showing that every available alternative poses greater health risk triggers the prosecutor’s burden to prove beyond a reasonable doubt that at least one available alternative would not. The civil affirmative defense remains separate and receives no imported criminal burden shift. Alternatives must be usable for the patient and circumstances; no new evidence hearing is automatic.

##### Relief must follow Ohio severability law and the component judgments

**Holding:** Independently operable provisions and applications must receive Ohio’s state-law severability analysis; invalidity of one command does not itself eliminate the Act. The lower court must conform relief to the affirmed necessary-care protection, determine the unresolved applications and dependent duties separately, and promptly decide interim protection under ordinary equitable standards without automatic blanket enforcement or permanent blanket invalidation.

**Proposition-level authority:** Part V.A’s ordinary severability principles receive all eight Associates’ direct joins. Part V.B’s full remaining mandate receives Souter, Stevens, O’Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer; Part III.B separately supplies the six-Justice health-linked construction directions. Stone’s decision to leave precise relief and severability below does not add a join in these particular analyses.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Ohio §1.50, Geiger and Maurer require independent operation, fidelity to legislative intent and no judicial insertion or fundamental disruption. Viability testing begins at week twenty-two subject to its emergency exception; the week-twenty-four presumption is rebuttable and is no fixed constitutional boundary. Certifications, facility requirements, method selection, second-physician presence and dependent sanctions require their own treatment; no whole-Act validation or invalidation follows.

Under §2919.17(A), purposeful performance, inducement or attempted performance or inducement of an abortion on a viable fetus is prohibited unless a physician, acting in good faith and using reasonable medical judgment, determines either that abortion is necessary to prevent maternal death or a serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function, or that the fetus is not viable after the assessment required by §2919.18(A). The latter provision requires, beginning with the twenty-second week, a good-faith determination of nonviability using reasonable medical judgment, after examining the patient and performing or arranging the viability tests a reasonable physician would use. Its medical-emergency exception excuses that testing obligation. The separate twenty-four-week viability presumption is rebuttable.

Section 2919.16(F) defines the emergency through the physician’s good-faith, reasonable medical assessment of a pregnancy complication: immediate abortion must be needed to prevent the patient’s death or to avoid a serious risk, created by delay, of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function. Section 2919.16(J) separately defines that serious-risk category by a medically diagnosed condition complicating the pregnancy that directly or indirectly causes such impairment. Its nonexclusive examples are preeclampsia, inevitable abortion, prematurely ruptured membranes, diabetes and multiple sclerosis. These enacted categories remain distinct from the constitutional protection and the method-prohibition defenses addressed below.

The five postviability duties are the attending physician’s written necessity certification; a second physician’s written certification after reviewing records and tests; a facility with access to neonatal services; a survival-favoring method unless it creates significantly greater maternal death risk or serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function; and a second physician present to care for the fetus. Their emergency exemption permits noncompliance when its terms are satisfied; it does not require omission. The method-choice exception is distinct from the alternative-method affirmative defense.

**Operative remedy or transition:** The categorical notice/scienter ground is vacated 8–1, all Associates supporting. Necessary-care protection is affirmed 7–2, Scalia and Thomas opposing. The method and remaining bounded mandate are remanded 5–4 by Stevens, O’Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Protection must conform to the health holding; unresolved-method interim relief requires prompt ordinary equitable consideration without automatic prior dissolution or permanent blanket affirmance.

#### Stenberg v. Carhart, 530 U.S. 914 (2000)

**Docket or dockets:** No. 99-830.

**Decided:** June 28, 2000.

##### 1. The complete Casey standard governs the enacted duty and proved applications

**Holding:** The complete six-step Casey standard, as applied in Fargo and Voinovich, governs Nebraska's method prohibition; an established substantial obstacle and denial of timely necessary life-and-health care are independently invalid, and the absence of an obstacle does not end review of remaining severe or meaningful interference. The Court applies that existing rule without substituting a numerical facial threshold, a no-valid-applications barrier, a physician-preference exemption or a new bodily-autonomy framework.

**Proposition-level authority:** Souter's opinion of the Court, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer; six direct joins. Kennedy does not join the completed applications.

**Limits and questions not reached:** The complete six-step standard remains in the Casey entry. This decision applies baseline, obstacle and necessary-care protections; it makes no additional obstructive-purpose finding or new residual-interference application. A different precise method restriction with usable care safeguards remains subject to the complete framework.

**Material application:** Before viability the patient decides whether to continue pregnancy without proving medical necessity or obtaining official approval of her reasons. The State may not prohibit that choice, confer effective control on another person, or prevent timely necessary life-and-health care. After viability, prohibition requires the life-and-health safeguard, including individualized physical and mental health considerations. Viability is individualized and medically supported, not a fixed constitutional week. Clinical appropriateness concerns safe and suitable care, not official approval of the patient's reasons; not every preference is a medical emergency.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Challengers establish the duty, population, causation and triggering interference. The State then bears the applicable justification and tailoring or fit burden. This does not alter ordinary proof of disputed historical facts or the enacted burden for a separate statutory defense. No State substantiation burden arises merely because a law concerns abortion.

##### 2. The enacted definition reaches ordinary D&E and creates a substantial obstacle

**Holding:** Nebraska's definition reaches the supported ordinary dilation-and-evacuation sequence when the physician deliberately and intentionally brings a living fetus or a substantial portion into the vagina to perform a procedure the physician knows will cause death and that does cause death before delivery is completed. Prohibiting those ordinary D&E applications removes practical access to the predominant second-trimester method for the affected previability patients and creates a substantial obstacle, independently sustaining the existing bounded declaration and injunction.

**Proposition-level authority:** Souter's opinion of the Court, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer; six direct joins. Kennedy does not join the completed applications.

**Material application:** The enacted substantial-portion alternative reaches the described living-limb D&E sequence when all intent, knowledge, life and timing elements are met. The Court does not prohibit every D&E by label or treat removal after death as satisfying the living-fetus requirement. The Attorney General's nonenforcement position is not an authoritative state-high-court construction or established control over every prosecutor.

**Independent alternative holding:** Even if the prohibition could lawfully be confined to an intact procedure, it cannot be enforced in supported circumstances where compelling substitution for that method creates a material risk of death or serious health harm and the method is medically necessary for timely safe and suitable care. Nebraska's specified life-only exception and post-charge life-necessity evidence do not supply a usable exception for those necessary serious-health applications; this independent defect supports the existing protection at its proved nonviable scope.

**Proposition-level authority for alternative holding:** Souter's opinion of the Court, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer; six direct joins. Kennedy does not join the completed applications.

**Material application:** Credited clinical evidence supports necessary risk reduction in identified recurring cases, not universal D&X superiority, every physician preference or a professional veto. Competent disagreement and the absence of controlled comparative studies do not erase those supported applications.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Section 3(1), codified at section 28-328(1), permits the procedure only when necessary to save the mother's life endangered by physical disorder, illness or injury, including a life-endangering physical condition caused by or arising from the pregnancy. It supplies no independent serious-health exception. The separate emergency-situation definition turns on the physician's good-faith clinical judgment concerning a condition requiring immediate abortion to avert death or delay creating serious risk of substantial impairment of a major bodily function; section 3(1) does not invoke or incorporate that broader definition. A hearing after charges permits evidence on the same life criterion, not permission to provide the otherwise forbidden necessary-health treatment. Neither admissibility of the Director's findings nor a possible motion to delay trial supplies the missing substantive defense. Voinovich's Ohio alternative-method protections cannot be imported. This conclusion adds no general medical immunity and does not decide every future, precisely drawn method restriction with genuinely usable care safeguards.

**Limits and questions not reached:** After criminal charges, the Attorney General must also petition for suspension or revocation, and a hearing must be set on the same life-necessity criterion. The defendant may move to delay the trial until the Director's order; delay is not automatic. The findings are admissible in the criminal case, not conclusive. None of these procedures supplies a preprocedure medical-board veto or the missing necessary-health defense.

##### 4. Affirmance preserves the actual nonviable injunction and bars its evasion through dependent sanctions

**Holding:** The existing declaration and injunction remain in force for Carhart, his patients and similarly situated persons regarding his D&E and D&X methods on nonviable fetuses, against the defendants and persons bound under Rule 65(d), with the existing county-attorney service direction. Criminal and licensing consequences cannot enforce those same unconstitutional applications, while independently lawful requirements and applications remain subject to Nebraska's section 8 severability direction and the judgment's actual limits.

**Proposition-level authority:** Souter's opinion of the Court, joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Ginsburg and Breyer; six direct joins. Kennedy does not join the completed applications.

**Operative remedy or transition:** The Eighth Circuit's judgment is affirmed. The existing declaration and permanent injunction protect Carhart, his patients and similarly situated persons regarding his D&E and D&X methods on nonviable fetuses. The defendants and their agents, servants and employees remain bound; Stenberg must serve the decision and judgment on Nebraska county attorneys, who and whose agents, servants and employees are bound as directed under Rule 65(d). Dependent criminal and license sanctions cannot enforce those protected applications. The Court adds no whole-statute facial invalidation, viable-fetus injunction, new hearing, damages award or separate fee decision. Independently lawful provisions and applications remain subject to section 8 and the actual judgment's limits.

**Limits and questions not reached:** Nebraska section 8 preserves the validity of remaining portions when a section or part is unconstitutional. It supplies no power to insert an intact-only definition or health exception and does not erase the sanction provisions for every lawful application. The enacted Director, petition, hearing and evidentiary procedures remain material to what the law does; the Court neither converts the life-only hearing into a health defense nor decides the unnecessary relationship between section 3(4)'s mandatory wording and the general sanction menu. Patient immunity remains intact. No new hearing, administrative veto, prosecution, damages award or statewide medical-policy revision is ordered. Section 3(3) prohibits prosecution of the woman under this section or for conspiracy to violate it; that patient immunity is distinct from a physician defense.

**Treatment of earlier authority:**

- Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey retains protected choice, individualized viability, timely necessary life-and-health care and the complete six-step rule. Both independent invalidity grounds are applications of that rule; residual severe- and meaningful-interference protections remain, and no obstacle-only substitute is adopted.
