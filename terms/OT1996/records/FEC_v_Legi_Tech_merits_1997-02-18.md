## Holdings

### Actual lawful reconsideration permits this enforcement action to continue

**Holding and operative rule:** After unconstitutional congressional participation has ended, a lawfully constituted Commission with available authority may independently adopt the necessary factual and legal determinations and authorize continued enforcement on an existing investigative and conciliation record. That cure is sufficient here because the lawful Commission actually reconsidered the matter, found probable cause and authorized continuation; it cannot replace an unperformed mandatory statutory opportunity, cure a lack of capacity, or evade an expired statutory deadline.

**Authority:** Part I of Justice Thomas's opinion, joined by all eight other Justices; nine direct votes.

**Controlling explanation:** The Court accepts the original structural defect and gives the timely challenge effect. A prediction that the same commissioners inevitably would reach the same result would not supply lawful authority. The reported new recommendations, deliberation, probable-cause finding and authorization establish an actual lawful enforcement decision instead. The Commission may use existing evidence and adopt the completed attempted conciliation without replaying each step merely because the earlier body was defective. No distinct mandatory opportunity is shown to have been omitted here.

Ryder requires a real replacement for the lawful decision-making function denied; higher legal review there could not replace lost fact, credibility and sentence review. Here the properly constituted body itself performs the executive probable-cause and prosecution function, while the court retains the liability decision. FEC v. NRA prevents ratification beyond an expired period for authorized Supreme Court review; it establishes no categorical bar to a presently authorized enforcement decision. No such expired period is established in this case. Neither lack of personal bias nor administrative convenience is an independent basis for cure, and judicial examination of whether lawful reconsideration actually occurred remains available.

### Retroactive governing law and valid curative relief remain distinct

**Holding and operative rule:** A governing structural rule applies to this still-open case, but its equal application does not require dismissal after an independently valid lawful decision supplies the authority needed to proceed. Reliance on the former arrangement alone would not justify withholding the rule or preserving defective authority.

**Authority:** Part II of Justice Thomas's opinion, joined by all eight other Justices; nine direct votes.

**Controlling explanation:** Harper requires the same governing rule on open direct review; Reynoldsville Casket prevents a remedy label from concealing selective prospectivity. The original Commission is therefore not treated as constitutionally valid. The action can continue because the subsequent lawful Commission itself made the required enforcement decision, an independent curative ground rather than reliance protection. The district court erred in treating the earlier remedy as mechanically mandatory despite this different remedial fact. Whether Legi-Tech violated the commercial-use restriction, and whether any particular remaining statutory or evidentiary defense succeeds, remain for the enforcement proceeding.
