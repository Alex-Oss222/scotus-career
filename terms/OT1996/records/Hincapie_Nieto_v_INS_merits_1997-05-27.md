## Holdings
The enacted withdrawal of ordinary direct review applies to the pending petition where it changes the review channel rather than the elements of the underlying conviction or completed agency consideration. The Court preserves a qualifying §2241 channel for substantial legal and constitutional challenges, but a claimant must invoke that channel and satisfy its actual predicates; the present record does not establish its failure.
## Precedent Treatment
Landgraf supplies the temporal inquiry, and Felker supplies provision-specific treatment of review restrictions. Neither creates automatic retroactivity or a guaranteed merits review of discretionary relief.
## Law After Decision
Direct review remains unavailable under §440(a), subject to the retained legal and constitutional review channel described above.
