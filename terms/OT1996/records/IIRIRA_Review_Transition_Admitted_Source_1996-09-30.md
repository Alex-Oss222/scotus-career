## Effective date, scope and issues left for adjudication

Section 309(a) generally sets April 1, 1997 for Title III-A and its amendments, subject to its stated exceptions for §309 itself and §§303(b)(2), 306(c), 308(d)(2)(D) and 308(d)(5). The October 11 technical correction expressly takes effect September 30, 1996. It alters §306(c)(1), §309(c)(1) and §309(c)(4). This admission preserves those distinct commands without treating the general date as a blanket delay of every review provision.

The admitted text includes new §242(a)(2)(B)'s separate permanent limits and §242(g)'s enumerated executive actions, §306(c)'s applicability and judgment-saving clauses, and §309's transition provisions. The new permanent discretionary-review language is not interchangeable with §309(c)(4)(E)'s specific list. This admission establishes no new Yang final order, criminal conviction, agency election, waiver of review or litigation default. Its inclusion of enacted §242(g) language does not decide commencement, scope, relation to §309, or whether this legal challenge arises from an enumerated action. Those remain judicial questions.
