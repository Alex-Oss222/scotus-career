## Holdings
ERISA does not preempt the testamentary transfer of the deceased spouse’s community-property interest on this record where the claim does not direct plan administration, redefine a plan beneficiary, or transfer undistributed plan benefits contrary to the plan’s governing allocation. The Court does not decide claims concerning post-distribution assets or independent obligations.
## Precedent Treatment
ERISA’s survivor and anti-alienation provisions remain controlling within their terms; they do not automatically displace every state-law incident of a separate community-property interest.
## Law After Decision
The challenged testamentary claim may proceed within the limits stated; plan administration and prohibited alienation remain governed by ERISA.
## Separate Writings
Stevens’s dissent would treat the testamentary transfer as an impermissible attempt to alter ERISA’s allocation of the pension benefits and would reverse.
