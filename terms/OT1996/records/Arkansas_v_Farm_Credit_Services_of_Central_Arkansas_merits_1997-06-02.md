## Holdings
A federally chartered but privately owned cooperative is not the United States for the Tax Injunction Act’s exception merely because federal law created and regulates it. Where the State provides a plain, speedy, and efficient remedy, §1341 bars the federal injunction action. The statutory exception and any underlying tax immunity remain distinct questions.
## Precedent Treatment
The Court applies the Tax Injunction Act according to the plaintiffs’ legal status and the adequacy of the state remedy, without equating federal regulation with sovereign identity.
## Law After Decision
The associations’ federal tax-injunction action cannot proceed on the United States exception presented here.
