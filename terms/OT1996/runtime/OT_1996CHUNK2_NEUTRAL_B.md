## F. VERIFIED SIMULATED-LAW APPLICATION

### Separation of the statutory and speech inquiries

Harris v. Forklift Systems (1993) unanimously rejects psychological injury, physical threat, and lost productivity as indispensable elements. The recurring workplace distribution and asserted professional humiliation therefore must be assessed together under the favorable-to-verdict standard. Harris does not resolve the First Amendment boundary for a labor association’s own publication. Nor does failure to prove retaliation negate a separately supported hostile-environment finding. Because all described publication conduct predates November 21, 1991, Landgraf’s actual damages restriction is relevant background; the supplied preservation statement expressly keeps that unraised issue outside this review.
