# 2. California v. Roy

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court vacated and remanded in a per curiam opinion. Justice Scalia concurred, with Justice Ginsburg joining Part I of his concurrence; no dissent from the judgment was reported.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam opinion plus a separate Scalia concurrence.
* **Historical Holding:** The omitted-intent instruction was trial error, and the federal habeas court was required to apply Brecht rather than the Ninth Circuit’s special standard.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [California v. Roy, 519 U.S. 2](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/519/2/).
