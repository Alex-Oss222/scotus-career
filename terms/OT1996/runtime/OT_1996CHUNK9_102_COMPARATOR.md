# 102. Boggs v. Boggs

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court reversed and remanded, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy announced the judgment and wrote the controlling opinion. Justices Stevens, Scalia, Souter, and Thomas joined the principal preemption portion; Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice Ginsburg joined the portion addressing the statutory conflict. Justice Breyer dissented, joined by Justice O’Connor and, except for one subsection, by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justice Ginsburg.
* **Historical Holding:** ERISA preempted the testamentary transfer under Louisiana community-property law because it conflicted with ERISA’s survivor-annuity and anti-alienation scheme. The Court rejected the sons’ asserted interest in the pension benefits.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Boggs v. Boggs, 520 U.S. 833](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/520/833/); [Cornell historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/96-79.ZO.html).
