# OT1996 chunk 13 — mechanically scoped COMPARATOR C

# 152. Faragher v. City of Boca Raton

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL LOWER-COURT OUTCOME

The en banc Eleventh Circuit rejected liability because it found neither employment-scope conduct nor agency aid, and held the trial's constructive-notice reasoning insufficient. It distinguished actionable hostility from what management should have known. Dissenting reasoning treated delegated command power as a sufficient agency connection. This was the reviewed lower judgment, not governing simulation Supreme Court law. [Opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/111/1530/630363/).

## B. LATER HISTORICAL SUPREME COURT COMPARATOR ONLY

Historically, Faragher v. City of Boca Raton, 524 U.S. 775, No. 97-282, decided June 26, 1998, reversed and ordered reinstatement of the district judgment. The Court recognized employer vicarious responsibility for a supervisor's actionable hostile environment. Without a tangible employment action, it allowed an employer to prove both reasonable preventive/corrective care and the employee's unreasonable failure to use available protection or avoid harm; a tangible employment action precluded that defense. The City's complete failure to communicate its policy defeated the defense, leaving no reason for further litigation over the nominal award. Thomas, joined by Scalia, opposed vicarious liability absent an adverse employment consequence and would remand on negligence.

That later rule, including its affirmative defense and disposition, is comparison material only. [Supreme Court opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/97-282).
