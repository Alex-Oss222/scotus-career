## B. ANSWERS AND APPLICATION

**Question1.** Give the intrinsic instrument primary explanatory force, without an artificial rule excluding useful technical evidence whenever a court announces it sees no ambiguity. Stone's preference for public meaning is compatible with consulting technical usage; his disagreement with Markman PartII is not governing law.

**Question2.** Extrinsic evidence cannot justify a construction that contradicts the patent's demonstrated definition and integrated operation. On this record the specification supports peak reflow temperature; adopting liquidus temperature by isolating expert statements disconnects the term from that explanation. This is a record-specific conclusion, not a claim that every embodiment must fall within every claim regardless of text.

**Question3.** Permit technical materials to illuminate terminology and genuine usage disputes. Under binding Markman PartII the judge resolves those construction disputes, including embedded credibility questions. Keep separate infringement facts with their proper finder and do not announce universal de novo appellate review of all subsidiary findings. Stone may restate his individual allocation concern, clearly separate, but no departure is necessary to resolve this record.

Affirm the Federal Circuit's reversal and remand for infringement analysis using the supported peak-temperature construction, while declining an absolute intrinsic-evidence exclusion rule. Decide no infringement, validity, damages or equivalents question. No mandatory additional hearing is proposed when the existing record permits proper construction.
