# 97. Vitronics Corp. v. Conceptronic, Inc.

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

The Federal Circuit reversed the noninfringement judgment and remanded under the peak-reflow construction. Its opinion gave strong priority to intrinsic evidence and warned against using outside material to change an otherwise clear claim. No historical Supreme Court merits outcome in this case is established. [Opinion](https://openjurist.org/90/f3d/1576).
