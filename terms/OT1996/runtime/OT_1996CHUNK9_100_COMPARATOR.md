# 100. Ayeni v. Mottola

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

The Second Circuit held that the pleaded media intrusion violated sufficiently established Fourth Amendment limits and affirmed the denial of dismissal. It treated genuine assistance in executing a warrant differently and kept unresolved factual claims separate. Historically the Supreme Court denied Mottola's petition on April 17, 1995, No. 94-1202, 514 U.S. 1062; there was no Supreme Court merits opinion in this matter. The simulation replaces that denial with an express hold and grant. [Appellate opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/35/680/605586/); [official denial](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/514bv.pdf).
