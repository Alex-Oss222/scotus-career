## B. JUDGMENT, REASONING, AND REMEDY

- **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand.

### Issue 1: Suspicionless drug testing of candidates

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the Fourth Amendment merits. Mootness is resolved by Chandler's unchallenged representation of future candidacy.
- **Question Stone Resolves:** Whether a generalized desire to demonstrate that officeholders are drug free is a special need substantial enough to displace individualized suspicion.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the received special-needs doctrine. The testing method is limited, but reduced intrusiveness cannot replace the missing governmental need. Georgia identifies no drug crisis among candidates, no shared safety-sensitive function, and no circumstance in which ordinary investigation is impracticable. The law operates principally as a symbolic badge of fitness, and symbolic reassurance is not a special need that authorizes a bodily search.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A State may not require every candidate for public office to undergo suspicionless drug testing absent a substantial, concrete special need connected to the duties and risks of the covered offices. Georgia's candidacy requirement violates the Fourth Amendment.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The covered offices range across functions with no common immediate safety risk. Candidates remain ordinary citizens until elected, and holding or seeking office does not waive bodily privacy. The State can investigate evidence of unlawful drug use, enforce criminal law, and disclose relevant convictions through ordinary means. Because the program has no demonstrated operational problem to solve, balancing cannot transform a generalized preference into constitutional reasonableness.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not decide medical fitness examinations, financial disclosure, targeted testing based on particularized suspicion, or security screening tied to a real and immediate safety risk.

## D. VERIFIED PRECEDENT APPLICATION AND POSITION STATUS

Stone’s approved rejection should rest on the mismatch between the symbolic qualification and a concrete special need in this record. It should not convert his separate Vernonia preference into a Court requirement that every testing program prove an existing catastrophe or exhaust every alternative.
