# 18. United States v. Watts; United States v. Putra

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Both Ninth Circuit judgments were reversed and remanded, 8-1, per curiam.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam opinion. Justice Scalia and Justice Breyer filed separate concurrences. Justice Stevens dissented.
- **Historical Holding:** A sentencing court could consider acquitted conduct proved by a preponderance because an acquittal establishes only failure to prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt and § 3661 permits broad sentencing information.
- **Historical Authority:** Per curiam Opinion of the Court.
- **Historical Sources:** United States v. Watts, 519 U.S. 148 (1997).

- **Official comparator source:** [U.S. Reports](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/519bv.pdf#page=318).

# 19. Old Chief v. United States

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 5-4.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Souter delivered the Opinion of the Court, joined by Stevens, Kennedy, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice O'Connor dissented, joined by the Chief Justice, Scalia, and Thomas.
- **Historical Holding:** When felony status is the only evidentiary purpose and the defendant offers to admit the full status element, Rule 403 requires the court to compare the offered record with the admission and exclude the name and nature of the offense when its added prejudice substantially outweighs its negligible added probative value.
- **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court.
- **Historical Sources:** Old Chief v. United States, 519 U.S. 172 (1997).



## Historical reasoning references preserved from the source

- **Other Authority Used:** The historical Old Chief analysis is date-eligible persuasive authority for comparing evidentiary alternatives under Rule 403.

- **Official comparator source:** [U.S. Reports](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/519bv.pdf#page=342).

# 20. Walmer v. Department of Defense

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits comparator. The historical anchor is the Tenth Circuit's affirmance of the preliminary-injunction denial.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Baldock wrote for a panel joined by Judges McKay and Ebel.
- **Historical Holding:** On the preliminary-injunction record, Walmer had not shown a likelihood of success under binding circuit precedent, and the executive regulation was not a legislative bill of attainder. The court affirmed denial of preliminary relief and dissolved the stay pending appeal.
- **Historical Authority:** Lower-court judgment only.
- **Historical Sources:** Walmer, 52 F.3d 851.
