# 74. Board of County Commissioners of Bryan County v. Brown

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Vacated and remanded, 5-4.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O'Connor delivered the Opinion of the Court, joined by the Chief Justice and Justices Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Justice Souter dissented, joined by Justices Stevens and Breyer. Justice Breyer separately dissented, joined by Justices Stevens and Ginsburg.
- **Historical Holding:** A facially lawful single hiring decision creates municipal liability only when inadequate scrutiny reflects deliberate indifference to a plainly obvious consequence that the hire would commit the particular constitutional violation and the decision directly causes the injury. The record did not satisfy that standard.
- **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court.
- **Historical Sources:** Board of County Commissioners v. Brown, 520 U.S. 397 (1997).

- **Official comparator source:** [U.S. Reports](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/520bv.pdf#page=492).

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Stevens delivered the Opinion of the Court for a unanimous Court.

# 80. Harbor Tug & Barge Co. v. Papai

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court reversed, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy wrote the majority, joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Souter, and Thomas. Justice Stevens dissented, joined by Justices Ginsburg and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** The record could not support seaman status because vessels linked only by a hiring hall were not an identifiable fleet under common ownership or control and Papai’s actual duties did not show the required substantial seagoing connection. The Court did not reach administrative preclusion.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [Harbor Tug & Barge Co. v. Papai, 520 U.S. 548](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/520/548/); [Cornell historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/95-1621.ZO.html).
