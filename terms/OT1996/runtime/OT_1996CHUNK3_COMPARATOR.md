# 27. In re Deuel

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL APPELLATE COMPARATOR

On March 28, 1995 the Federal Circuit reversed the rejection of claims 4–7, emphasizing the absence of art suggesting the specific compounds, genetic-code redundancy and the difference between familiar cloning and contemplation of a product. It reversed the genus-claim rejection because no separate sufficient ground was supplied and reserved §112 examination. Its language treated general isolation methods as essentially irrelevant without further art suggesting the molecules. Stone’s proposed result agrees with reversal on this record but declines that categorical formulation.

# 34. Ingalls Shipbuilding, Inc. v. Director, Office of Workers' Compensation Programs

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Affirmed. The compensation issue was unanimous; the Director-participation issue was 7-2.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice O'Connor delivered the unanimous portions and the Court's opinion on participation, joined there by the Chief Justice, Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer. Justice Scalia, joined by Thomas, dissented from the participation ruling.
- **Historical Holding:** A spouse is not a person entitled to death benefits before the worker dies, so predeath settlements do not trigger § 33(g)'s approval forfeiture. The Department of Labor, represented by the Director, may be named respondent under Rule 15(a).
- **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court at each stated scope.
- **Historical Sources:** Ingalls Shipbuilding, Inc. v. Director, OWCP, 519 U.S. 248 (1997).
