# 105. McMillian v. Monroe County

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court affirmed for Monroe County, 5-4.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist delivered the opinion joined by Justices O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, and Thomas. Justice Ginsburg dissented, joined by Justices Stevens, Souter, and Breyer.
* **Historical Holding:** Under Alabama law, a sheriff represents the State rather than the county when acting in a law-enforcement capacity. Monroe County therefore could not be held liable on the challenged policymaker theory.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
* **Historical Sources:** [McMillian v. Monroe County, 520 U.S. 781](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/520/781/); [Cornell historical opinion](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/96-542.ZO.html).
