- **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand.

### Issue 1: ERISA preemption of the generally applicable health-facility tax

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the preserved preemption question. The assessment's coverage, rate, and treatment of the Fund's centers are undisputed.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies Travelers. A generally applicable tax does not relate to an ERISA plan merely because the plan chose to own the taxed provider and therefore remits the assessment itself. The relevant inquiry is whether state law governs plan benefits, administration, enforcement, or choices with a coercive force equivalent to regulation. This tax does none of those things.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** ERISA does not preempt New York's Health Facility Assessment as applied to the Fund's treatment centers because the assessment is a generally applicable facility tax that neither refers to ERISA plans nor dictates their benefit structure, administration, or enforcement.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** Had the Fund purchased care from an independent treatment center, the facility could pass the same tax cost through its prices. Ownership changes the route of the economic burden, not its legal character. Treating every direct cost paid by a plan as preempted would displace taxes, property law, utility charges, and other ordinary state rules Congress did not make ERISA-specific. The assessment enters general revenues and does not condition benefits or plan procedure.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A tax or charge that singles out ERISA plans, operates as an alternative enforcement device, or is structured to compel a particular benefit or provider arrangement may present a different case.

## D. VERIFIED PRECEDENT APPLICATION AND POSITION STATUS

Stone’s approved reversal extends Travelers’s reasoning to this directly paid provider tax after examining its actual trigger and operation. It should not be expressed as though Travelers had already sustained every tax collected from a plan or as though only taxes formally naming ERISA could ever be preempted.
