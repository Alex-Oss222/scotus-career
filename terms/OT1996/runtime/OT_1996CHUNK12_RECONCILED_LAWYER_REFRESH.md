## Result and correction of provisional join scope

All eight Associates reach the appeal and vote to **vacate the March 19, 1996 approval and decree and remand for the confined proceedings stated below**. The proposed judgment is retained. No Associate directs a finding that Plan 330 is unconstitutional, holds Plan 386 unconstitutional, or supplies a replacement map or election timetable.

The refreshed provisional handoff's common reasoning needs one material qualification after reading the complete historical dissent. **O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas do not abandon the districting-specific objection that federal judicial replacement of state boundaries requires a legally established federal violation and a proper opportunity for the State to respond.** Their acceptance of Firefighters's general settlement principles is not a vote that litigation consent always supplies authority to bypass that requirement in a reapportionment case. Nor do they join a conclusive holding that the participation of authorized litigation representatives necessarily exhausts the State's normal redistricting opportunity. They can join the narrower ground that this approval lacks a legally sufficient identified basis under the controlling law and that the proposal was not itself enacted state law. Their additional districting-authority position remains separate; it supplies no different map, deadline or present disposition.

This qualification narrows the proposed common opinion scope; it changes no neutral fact, claimed injury or judgment vote. It corrects any contrary implication of the provisional statements that all eight accepted the same no-adjudication or state-opportunity rationale. There is no supported reason to turn the four historical dissenters into adherents of the historical majority's districting-specific consent theory. There also is no need to decide that larger disagreement to vacate the present approval. An eight-Associate join is available only for the common propositions identified below, not for an unqualified application of the historical majority's consent/state-opportunity holdings.

## Common operative scope and actual disputes

### 1. Reachability

All eight accept Lawyer's established personal relationship to the electoral assignment and decree, the live challenge to that decree and the disposition of his claims, and the direct appellate channel. Miller's actual standing holding and Thomas's unanimous-Associate threshold opinion in Vera govern. Neither receiving part of the requested practical relief nor losing on the substantive design-only theory eliminates that stake. This is not general resident standing. No supported jurisdictional dismissal, mootness dismissal or insubstantiality dismissal follows from the merits rule. The historical dissent's jurisdictional terminology does not override the actual standing/merits distinction.

### 2. Consent, state choice and the nonconsenter

All eight accept that Firefighters permits lawful consent obligations beyond the relief obtainable after trial and gives no objector an absolute veto over other parties' lawful settlement. A consent decree must resolve a dispute within subject-matter jurisdiction, remain within the general scope of the pleadings, advance the underlying law's objectives and remain lawful. Others' consent cannot extinguish a nonconsenter's properly raised valid claim or impose obligations on that person. There is no universal trial-of-liability requirement for every consent decree.

That common negative proposition does **not** settle the additional conditions for a federal decree substituting state legislative boundaries. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer preserve the historical majority's distinction between a lawful settlement and a contested liability judgment: an adjudicated violation is not invariably necessary for a properly supported districting compromise. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas maintain the stricter districting-specific objection explained above. All eight agree that consent alone cannot turn a legally rejected predicate into governing law or convert the officials' proposal into an independently enacted plan.

The authorized litigation participation is a genuine state choice and is not fabricated or collusive on this record. Plan 386 nevertheless obtained its compulsory force from the March 19 order. No legislative joint resolution or superseding state judgment adopting it is supplied. The historical-majority Associates do not require a formal session merely because an authorized settlement is proposed. The historical-dissenting Associates preserve the distinction between authority to litigate or propose and the normal authority to enact or judicially replace state boundaries. Neither position permits treating the proposal as the actual superseding state judgment addressed in Growe.

### 3. The present approval's legal defect

The lower majority expressly tested for a materially and fairly contestable constitutional dispute and found it by applying the historical predominance theory. The concession was for settlement only; defendants denied liability. The separate lower concurrence is not a majority adjudication or an unconditional admission. Under actual Shaw, Miller and Vera, race-conscious shared territorial design, even when predominant, does not alone establish the asserted deprivation or trigger scrutiny. No independently sufficient adjudicated claim is identified in the approval.

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer personally endorse that substantive rule. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas acknowledge its present force without endorsing it; their proposed replacement rule is neither existing law nor an automatic finding of Plan 330 invalidity. All eight agree that the approval must be reconsidered on a legally available basis, with the nonconsenter's actually retained claims properly treated.

The strongest affirmance argument is consent's independent legal force: a compromise need not duplicate a successful trial remedy; authorized state actors favored it; Plan 386 need not itself be unlawful; and settlement reduces disruption. Those propositions remain substantial. The error is not merely absence of a liability finding or the prospect that the plaintiff would lose. It is the court's express assessment of the requisite dispute under a constitutional rule that is unavailable in this timeline. The Court need not substitute a new appellate rationale about the scope and objects of this agreement when the approval used the wrong legal predicate and the existing claims' precise procedural scope matters. Vacatur permits that legal assessment; it does not announce a categorical bar on settling every weak, disputed or ultimately unsuccessful claim.

The strongest immediate-termination argument is Vera's rule ending relief founded solely on its rejected adjudicated claim. That determines the fate of the design-only theory but does not establish that every allegation in this differently structured consent case was adjudicated or abandoned. A confined return is supported without a finding that an independent claim exists. The original record's limitations, rather than a new factual premise, define that boundary.

### 4. Confined remand and limits

On remand the district court identifies whether a distinct claim was actually preserved within the complaint, operative pleadings and pretrial definition; if so, it determines that claim's legal sufficiency, personal basis and relation to the requested relief under its own requirements. It reconsiders decree authority and the disposition of Lawyer's claims using current law. It may resolve the question on the available record and ordinary proceedings when sufficient. No new hearing, amendment entitlement or discovery is ordered.

Complaint paragraph 9 remains an allegation about street-side boundaries preventing potential opponents from challenging the plan's author. It is neither erased nor treated as an established personal candidate-exclusion, ballot-access or partisan-dilution claim. The complaint identifies resident voters rather than a named excluded candidate. Representation allegations dependent on composition and shape alone do not avoid Miller. Polarization evidence about remedial alternatives does not itself establish an original §2 claim; actual 1992 administrative mistakes do not by themselves identify a preserved plaintiff's continuing federal claim supporting this decree. No evidence is relabeled nonexistent, and no allegation is promoted to a finding.

If no legally sufficient, properly preserved basis supports the decree, approval and dependent compulsory-redistricting relief end; the rejected design-only claims receive the appropriate merits disposition. This is not an invitation to create a different injury, revive abandoned or dismissed claims, or search for a new suit. A separately adopted state plan must be established through its lawful source; none is presumed.

Vacatur of this approval does not adjudicate either plan comprehensively unlawful. No conclusive decision concerning Plan 386's predominance finding or the disputed restriction on examining DOJ counsel is needed. Ordinary clear-error review remains the rule for actual purpose findings. No Justice finds that geometry alone reverses such a finding or requires counsel's examination. There is no new map, racial target, election cancellation, special election, deadline or immediate mandate. Ordinary mandate procedures govern. Any actual transition request requires its own lawful basis and operative instruments; no automatic stay, preservation order, unverified later map or continuing supervisory jurisdiction is supplied. Fees and costs retain their own requirements; retained fee jurisdiction is not entitlement.

## Eight exact final commitments

Each following commitment includes the common reachability, confined-remand and remedial limits above. Agreement on judgment does not erase the stated join barriers.

### Stevens

**Final commitment:** Reach; vacate the approval/decree and remand. Join the governing substantive rule, the full consent safeguards, and the limited basis for reconsidering the approval. Authorized state settlement can be meaningful without formal enactment, but it does not dispense with lawful judicial authority. Do not order Plan 330 invalidated or Plan 386 condemned.

**Sufficient ground and authorities:** His Firefighters join preserves settlement beyond trial remedies. His own Shaw concurrence and Miller/Vera joins reject the racial-design premise actually used below. His Richards join protects actual retained claims, while his Bossier position requires a proved statutory wrong rather than demographic inference. These grounds allow a lawful consent theory to be reconsidered without manufacturing another cause of action.

**Join barriers:** No categorical liability trial, ban on voluntary racial awareness, heightened pleading, automatic independent injury from fragmented representation, or remand to create a new claim. He can join no-adjudication latitude at the districting-settlement level subject to the full legal conditions; the four historical dissenters do not thereby join him.

**Historical departure:** Stevens historically affirmed on the basis of a settlement addressing a legally cognizable racial-design dispute and a lawful replacement. Here his own controlling Miller and Vera joins foreclose the exact asserted design-only predicate. Consent's breadth survives, but the legal assessment supporting approval does not; his prior distinction between territorial composition and actual unequal treatment supports vacatur for an available basis rather than automatic affirmance or invalidation of Plan 386.

### O'Connor

**Sufficient ground and authorities:** Her Firefighters concurrence expressly preserves substantive predicates and individual challenges despite negotiated relief. Her historical Lawyer dissent join applies a distinct federal districting-authority limit. Her Miller/Vera writings preserve practical voluntary compliance latitude and her predominant-assignment objection, without converting that proposed constitutional rule into present authority. Her Matsushita opinion distinguishes representation and lawful binding from the existence of a settlement.

**Join barriers:** Neither an exact federal command for a voluntary state map nor a prior trial for every consent decree is required. Those negative propositions do not waive her specific objection to this federal substitution of district lines. She does not join a statement that authorized litigation participation conclusively satisfies every state redistricting-opportunity requirement, or that denial of the old claim destroys standing.

**Historical comparison and narrower disposition:** Her historical vote was reversal/remand on authority and state opportunity, with Plan 386 unexamined. Vacatur/remand here preserves that practical direction. The changed controlling merits law means a return cannot simply seek a design-only unconstitutionality finding under the historical trigger; her published Miller/Vera dissents already recognize the need expressly to replace that rule. Her additional authority condition is retained, not silently abandoned in favor of a universal consent rationale.
