## B. JUDGMENT, REASONING, AND REMEDY

- **Stone's Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse and remand.
- **Stone's Remedy and Remand Instruction:** Dismiss without prejudice the damages and declaratory claims whose success necessarily invalidates the outstanding good-time decision in the in-custody review setting presented. Remand prospective date-stamping and genuinely separable claims for ordinary §1983 adjudication. Do not announce that expiration or loss of a habeas opportunity automatically makes an invalidity-dependent claim cognizable; Heck reserved those claimant categories and requires qualifying invalidation for the claim it decided.

### Issue 1: Section 1983 claims that necessarily invalidate prison discipline

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the cognizability of the deceit-and-bias claims and the proper disposition. He does not decide the merits, standing for future relief, or whether every alleged procedural defect necessarily affects the credits.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies simulated Heck. A procedural label does not avoid channeling when success on the specific defect would necessarily invalidate the discipline. Deceitful suppression of all exculpatory evidence and adjudication by a biased hearing officer would vitiate the good-time decision. A prospective rule governing future receipt of witness statements ordinarily does not invalidate the past judgment and may proceed if standing and equitable requirements are met.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Balisok's damages and declaratory claims based on hearing-officer deceit and bias are not presently cognizable under § 1983 because success would necessarily imply invalidity of the outstanding deprivation of good-time credits. A genuinely prospective or separable procedural claim remains cognizable.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The channel turns on legal consequence, not on whether the complaint asks directly for restoration. A declaration that the officer intentionally suppressed exculpatory evidence and decided the case with bias would undermine the disciplinary judgment itself. By contrast, an injunction requiring future date stamps can operate without altering past credits. The lower courts must classify each remaining allegation rather than use a categorical rule for all procedural claims.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Failure to provide a written statement or another defect that can be remedied without invalidating the result may be separable. The decision does not extend Heck to a claimant lacking a legally available habeas route through no fault of his own.

### Issue 2: Dismissal rather than stay

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the proper procedural disposition because the district court stayed the action pending state proceedings.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Stone applies the registered consequence. A claim either proceeds under § 1983 or is temporarily unavailable because another lawful channel must first invalidate the judgment. The district court may not create an exhaustion stay that turns a noncognizable claim into a pending federal action.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** The channeled claims must be dismissed without prejudice rather than stayed for exhaustion.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** Dismissal preserves the habeas sequence and avoids an unauthorized federal hold over claims that cannot yet be adjudicated. It also leaves immediately cognizable prospective claims free to proceed.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A stay may remain available for an independently authorized reason, but not merely to impose state exhaustion on § 1983.
