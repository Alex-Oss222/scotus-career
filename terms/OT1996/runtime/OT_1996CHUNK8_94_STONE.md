## C. ISSUE POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Accepted pleas are not freely withdrawable because agreements remain pending

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the Rule question because it controls the judgments and presents no threshold bar.
* **Question Stone Resolves:** When a district court accepts a defendant’s guilty plea but defers acceptance of the associated plea agreement, may the defendant withdraw the plea before sentencing as of right, or must the defendant show a “fair and just reason” under former Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 32(e)?
* **Stone's Legal Position:** Apply the enacted procedural text without creating an equitable exception that collapses the plea and agreement into one event.
* **Stone's Proposed Holding:** When a district court accepts a guilty plea after the required Rule 11 inquiry but defers acceptance of the plea agreement, former Rule 32(e) requires the defendant to show a fair and just reason to withdraw the plea before sentencing.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The plea is the defendant’s admission and waiver; the agreement is the proposed disposition. Rule 11 separately authorizes deferral of the agreement and specifies the consequence if the court rejects it. Treating deferral as nonacceptance of the plea would make the separate provisions largely meaningless and would diminish the seriousness of the sworn colloquy. Fairness runs through the Rule’s express protections rather than an unwritten unilateral option.
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** Stone does not decide whether Hyde's preserved challenges establish a fair and just reason, whether any plea was involuntary, or whether the court must reject an agreement.
