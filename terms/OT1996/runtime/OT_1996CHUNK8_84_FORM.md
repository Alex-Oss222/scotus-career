# Bossier opinion form before assignment

One signed lead Opinion of the Court after plenary argument, for both consolidated direct appeals. No divided authorship is necessary because Ginsburg and Breyer each support every governing proposition. Form and scope are selected from the legal grounds before any assignment count is consulted.

Common disposition: vacate the ultimate preclearance judgment in both appeals and remand; all nine support that formal disposition. The reason for remand and the directions have distinct proposition coalitions.

Part I: Section 5 effect remains the retrogression comparison; a proved Section 2 violation does not independently defeat preclearance. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer support the complete proposition. Stone supports only the effect comparison and separate Section 2 enforcement, not the general nonincorporation proposition. Stevens and Souter reject the latter.

Part II: Dilution-related evidence may not be excluded from purpose analysis merely because of its Section 2 relevance; all nine support the evidentiary correction and no appellate purpose finding. Evidence remains nonconclusive and the existing burden unchanged.

Part III: Unconstitutional purposeful racial vote dilution is within Section 5 even when the jurisdiction does not intend retrogression. Stone, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer supply five direct votes for that proposition. The remaining four reserve the larger purpose-scope question and join only the bounded evidentiary remand. Stone's full enacted-text position is preserved without making unsupported broader applications Court law. No same-decision defense, burden shift, maximization, proportional-representation rule, liability finding or required map follows.

One lead with partial joins, a Stevens/Souter separate writing preserving their incorporation-on-proof view, a Thomas reservation retaining statutory dilution and deference objections, and a Stone writing only to preserve the exact scope of his broader text-based proposal if the common limited holding does not fully state it. No fictional draft exchange or changed Associate commitment is used.
