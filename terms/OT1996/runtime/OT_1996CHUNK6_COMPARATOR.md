# 60. Oncale v. Sundowner Offshore Services, Inc.

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL LOWER-COURT COMPARATOR

The Fifth Circuit treated Garcia's same-sex discussion as an alternative holding binding subsequent panels, despite arguments that it was unnecessary to Garcia's result. It therefore affirmed without adopting Oncale's and the EEOC's proposed interpretation on a clean slate. Its opinion acknowledged the statutory language, Meritor and Harris arguments and emerging disagreement elsewhere. The lower-court judgment and the later historical Supreme Court decision below are comparators only; neither later outcome supplies entering simulated law.

This records the historical lower-court disposition only. It does not establish a completed simulated Supreme Court outcome or supply governing post-divergence Supreme Court law.

[Primary opinion](https://openjurist.org/83/f3d/118); [Primary opinion](https://www.laed.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/200th/notablecases/57%20Oncale%20-%205th%20Cir..pdf)

## B. LATER HISTORICAL SUPREME COURT COMPARATOR — OUTSIDE GOVERNING SIMULATION LAW

On March 4, 1998, the historical Supreme Court unanimously reversed and remanded in Oncale v. Sundowner Offshore Services, 523 U.S. 75, No. 96-568. It held that same-sex harassment can violate Title VII when it is discrimination because of sex. The Court required proof of sex causation and sufficiently severe or pervasive conditions judged in their social context; it did not make every sexual or offensive workplace act actionable or require sexual desire as the only evidentiary route.

[Historical Supreme Court opinion](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/523/75/). This subsequent historical result supplies no simulated holding, vote, or effective precedent for this packet.
