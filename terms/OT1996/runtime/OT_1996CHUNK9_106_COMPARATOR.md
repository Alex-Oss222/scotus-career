# 106. Saratoga Fishing Co. v. J.M. Martinac & Co.

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court reversed and remanded, 6-3.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Breyer delivered the opinion joined by Chief Justice Rehnquist and Justices Stevens, Kennedy, Souter, and Ginsburg. Justice O’Connor dissented separately. Justice Scalia dissented, joined by Justice Thomas.
* **Historical Holding:** Equipment added to the vessel by the initial user was other property rather than part of the product itself under East River. The subsequent purchaser could pursue tort recovery for its destruction.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
