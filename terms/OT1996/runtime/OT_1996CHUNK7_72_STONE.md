## B. ANSWERS AND APPLICATION

**Question1.** Retain a fairness inquiry after the contacts determination; due process does not stop at counting transactions. But continuous and systematic voluntary forum activity substantially reduces the force of an ordinary claim that litigation there is unexpected or inconvenient. Stone should require a concrete burden before defeating general jurisdiction on that second step.

**Question2.** Lack of a Vermont connection to this Florida dispute can inform the interests analysis but cannot do the decisive work alone: jurisdiction over unrelated disputes is the reason general jurisdiction is a separate category. The domestic parties and commercially active defendant do not present Asahi's exceptional international setting. Selecting a favorable limitations period is not itself unconstitutional. The supplied fairness rationale gives the missing nexus excessive weight.

**Question3.** Examine the multiyear pattern reasonably preceding filing, including actual sales, local relationships and deliberate solicitation. Aggregate quality and regularity matter, not a mechanical dollar threshold or a requirement that the defendant maintain every kind of physical establishment. On this record propose accepting the appellate contacts conclusion while rejecting its dispositive fairness analysis.

## C. PRECISE PROPOSED DISPOSITION

Vacate the judgment affirming Rule12(b)(2) dismissal and remand for further proceedings under those limits, without finding product liability, waiving other preserved defenses or requiring a new evidentiary proceeding on questions the existing record resolves. Do not silently adopt a future “at home” test or eliminate fairness in all general-jurisdiction cases.
