# 101. Arkansas v. Farm Credit Services of Central Arkansas

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** The Court reversed unanimously.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Kennedy delivered the opinion for a unanimous Court.
* **Historical Holding:** Production Credit Associations were not the United States for the Tax Injunction Act exception. Their federal suit was barred where a plain, speedy, and efficient state remedy existed.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
