# 68. In re Vey

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** IFP denied with a May 5, 1997 cure deadline. Future extraordinary-writ petitions required payment under Rule 38 and compliance with Rule 33. Stevens dissented; no nonparticipation is recorded.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Per curiam order with Justice Stevens's dissent. The source's statement that no separate opinion was reported is incorrect.
- **Historical Holding:** Vey's pattern of frivolous extraordinary-writ filings justified prospective fee-and-format conditions for further extraordinary-writ petitions, without a noncriminal limitation.
* **Historical Authority:** Historical comparator only; it is not simulated precedent.
