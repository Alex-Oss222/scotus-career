## 48. Andrade v. Mayfair Management, Inc.

**Channel, reach and disposition:** Review under the stipulated §1254 merits grant of 88 F.3d 258. Vacate the employer-attribution judgment and remand for application of agency principles, preserving genuinely unresolved Rule 50, instructions and preservation questions. Do not reinstate the $25,000 verdict directly. Do not reopen constructive discharge. All eight Associates support this disposition and the common ground below.

**Common propositions:** Title VII employer attribution is not confined to negligence in learning of and correcting harassment. An employer may be responsible where delegated supervisory authority actually aided the supervisor's creation or maintenance of the unlawful environment, although higher management lacked actual or constructive notice. A supervisor's title or mere opportunity to encounter an employee is insufficient by itself; the inquiry concerns the authority actually delegated and its connection to the misconduct. Making or threatening employment decisions is a strong route, but an express threat, completed discharge or measurable economic loss is not an indispensable condition of every agency-supported claim. The employer's notice and response remain relevant to a distinct negligence route. Harris and DeAngelis determine whether the environment crosses the statutory threshold; neither resolves attribution.

The administrator's hiring/firing authority and actual supervision require examination with verdict-favorable permissible inferences. Reporting to a corporate officer does not itself negate agency. This is a correction of the no-notice barrier, not a finding that this record necessarily establishes the relevant connection or that every allegation was proved. The mandate leaves available ordinary review of the verdict under the correct legal standard, including properly preserved alternative grounds and any instruction problem. It orders no new trial automatically.

**Adversarial paths:** The employer's strongest lawful route is affirmance because misconduct for personal ends may fall outside ordinary employment scope and because Meritor rejects status-based automatic liability. That objection warrants a genuine agency connection, not a universal notice defense: misuse of delegated power can explain how an otherwise unauthorized wrong became possible. The competing employee route would impute every supervisor's harassment or restore the verdict outright. It exceeds the established connection and unresolved trial-predicate record. The corrected-standard remand answers both objections without introducing later affirmative defenses.

## 49. In re Rhone-Poulenc Rorer

**Channel, reach and disposition:** Review of the Seventh Circuit's mandamus judgment, not an ordinary interlocutory appeal from certification. Reverse the grant of mandamus and remand with instructions to deny this writ petition. No class verdict, liability judgment or damages award exists to restore. The extraordinary decertification command falls; the District Court must conduct further proceedings consistently with current Rule 23 law, including Castano, and retains ordinary authority to revise certification. This judgment does not independently certify the class or approve its proposed instructions. All eight Associates support the disposition and the threshold ground below.

**Mandamus rule and application:** A certification objection supports mandamus only upon clear and indisputable entitlement, inadequacy of ordinary remedies and circumstances warranting extraordinary intervention. Serious potential Rule 23 error is insufficient. The supplied record assumes eventual final judgment for named plaintiffs and final review; the certification and trial arrangements remain revisable. Ordinary settlement pressure, even at substantial aggregate stakes, cannot alone make final review inadequate. The appellate figures about defense victories and aggregate exposure neither adjudicate the claims nor show that the defendants lack an adequate ordinary avenue. No finding that the manufacturers failed to file some particular request is made.

Beacon Theatres and Dairy Queen preserve writ protection for an established jury entitlement. Gasoline Products forbids later redetermination of inseparable jury issues. Here the appellate account identifies a serious risk of overlap between negligence, comparative fault and proximate cause, but no final common verdict or unavoidably fixed sequence requiring reexamination. That risk must be addressed in the trial structure; it does not establish that ordinary correction and review are inadequate. A later demonstrated unavoidable jury deprivation would present a different writ record. No universal bar to mandamus in class litigation follows.

**Certification law relevant to the threshold, without an ordinary merits affirmance:** Castano demands an actual law-and-proof showing before certification. Erie, Klaxon and O'Melveny prohibit averaging materially different state duties into a national negligence standard. Material equivalence, lawful groupings or genuinely separable common questions require demonstration. The district court's issue label does not perform that work, and Rule 23(c)(4)(A) does not authorize dilution of substantive rights. Representation and preclusion must separately satisfy Matsushita and Richards. These requirements do not themselves decide every issue class, every negligence instruction or the full relation between action-wide predominance and issue certification. Castano expressly leaves that latter question open. The Court can state these continuing obligations while reversing the unauthorized writ.

**Enhanced paths and remedy comparison:**

- **Affirm a confined writ:** Its strongest basis combines state-law displacement with threatened jury reexamination, rather than settlement pressure alone. Mandamus historically protects an actual jury right, and a single erroneous nationwide liability issue could influence many later proceedings. The decisive objection is the absence of a showing that this revisable plan already makes such loss unavoidable and uncorrectable on the assumed named-plaintiff final review. Mandamus cannot become ordinary abuse-of-discretion review simply by aggregating potential harms.
- **Reverse the writ:** Allied Chemical, Kerr, Mallard, Blodgett, Digital Equipment and Swint preserve ordinary routes and distinct extraordinary thresholds. The strongest objection is that defendants may settle rather than risk the initial trial, making later review practically unlikely. Coopers & Lybrand and Castano reject that ordinary pressure as a sufficient jurisdictional or categorical certification substitute. The seriousness of an eventual constitutional violation remains relevant but must be demonstrated, not inferred from certification alone. This route leaves certification duties intact and resolves the actual appellate judgment.

## 51. Arizonans for Official English v. Arizona

**Channel, authority and disposition:** The Court reviews the Ninth Circuit's exercised appellate jurisdiction under §1254, for the limited purpose of correcting jurisdictional error and disposing of the resulting judgments. Bender, applying Corrick, recognizes appellate authority to correct a lower federal court's exercise of jurisdiction even when authority to decide the underlying merits is absent. That limited review power, and §2106's judgment-disposition power, must be identified before applying mootness or equitable vacatur. It is not a provisional assumption that these sponsors possess Article III standing to prosecute the constitutional merits. On this record, vacate the Ninth Circuit judgment, remand with directions to vacate the District Court declaratory judgment and dismiss the action as moot. All eight Associates support this disposition and the limited authority ground. The sponsor-standing question remains unresolved; there is no general initiative-proponent standing holding.

**Chronology and the jurisdictional distinctions:** The District Court left only Yniguez and the Governor in her official capacity and entered declaratory relief, denying an injunction for the stated absence of a demonstrated enforcement threat. It denied postjudgment intervention and certification on April 3, 1990. The sponsors' April 9 notice challenged denial of intervention and contemplated a subsequent merits appeal; the Attorney General's notice was filed the same day, but its complete scope is not supplied. Yniguez resigned April 10, apparently effective April 25, before the later appellate merits adjudication. The July 19, 1991 order let the sponsors proceed and allowed only limited Attorney General argument, denying him party status; that nonparty designation was never revised.

The Court therefore cannot call Arizona a damages party, assume the Attorney General perfected an independent merits appeal, or treat his statutory appearance as a cure for sponsor standing. Equally, the fact that notices preceded resignation does not establish lawful merits jurisdiction over every order. The decision rests instead on the established appellate duty and limited power to remove continued federal adjudication of a case that no longer supplies a plaintiff controversy. It adjudicates neither the correctness of the earlier intervention denials nor the full scope of §2403(b). The reported notices and appellate judgment suffice to identify the judicial proceeding subject to that corrective review; no unknown notice designation is used as a merits-jurisdiction premise.

**Mootness and damages:** An individual prospective challenge becomes moot when the employee leaves the public employment to which the challenged rule applies and no supported return or other ongoing application remains. Yniguez alleged no intention to return. This is not a class action with a continuing representative claim or a supported capable-of-repetition exception. A general prayer for relief cannot maintain this action through nominal damages against a legally unavailable defendant: §1983 does not make the State or an officer sued only in official capacity a damages person. Hafer's personal-capacity route cannot restore the dismissed individual defendants or their unpursued claims. Even construing a general prayer liberally cannot cure those independent defects. No broad rule requiring the words nominal damages in every complaint is adopted; a properly supported personal-capacity damages action presents a different case. Cooperation over a nominal payment supplies no statutory defendant or live cause of action.

**Equitable disposition and state-law issues:** Bancorp makes vacatur equitable rather than automatic and distinguishes a review-seeking party's voluntary settlement from unilateral action by the prevailing plaintiff. Yniguez's departure, the continued appellate adjudication after that departure, and the absence of a lawful damages residue favor clearing both constitutional judgments. No finding of strategic concealment or improper resignation is made. The result prevents an unreviewable constitutional declaration in an extinguished individual controversy from continuing as the resolution of the amendment's merits. Anderson supports clearing judgments when their live predicate has disappeared, without creating mandatory vacatur outside settlement. A later live action remains possible. Certification can assist avoidance of unnecessary constitutional decisions when a genuinely unsettled state-law construction matters in a live case; it cannot revive this one. No authoritative construction, First Amendment holding, mandatory certification in every case or operative certification order follows.

**Enhanced paths and objections:**

- **Decide sponsor standing first, then dispose of the appeal:** Diamond, Karcher, Lujan and Brown Group supply a substantial objection to treating initiative sponsorship or political commitment as concrete injury or State representation. The record supplies no personal injury or express representative authorization. The countervailing concern is that a general determination of proponents' representational status could reach beyond what is necessary once the original action is extinct. The selected path retains those standing limits without deciding the sponsors' ultimate status, using only jurisdiction-corrective authority and not hypothetical merits jurisdiction.
- **Leave standing unresolved and dismiss only the Supreme Court proceeding:** This avoids every merits question but leaves standing an appellate constitutional judgment entered after the only plaintiff controversy ended. The Court's limited power to correct that exercise of jurisdiction is sufficient to remove the judgments without recognizing sponsor standing. Bancorp's lawful-review limitation is respected by identifying the corrective review power independently rather than inferring it from §2106 alone.
- **Preserve a damages controversy or send the merits for certification:** The strongest argument is liberal relief pleading and the value of an authoritative state construction. The remaining party capacities independently defeat the suggested §1983 damages remedy, and certification supplies no live plaintiff. Both paths would require adding a claim, defendant, State-party status or future exposure the packet does not establish. They therefore cannot support adjudication here.
- **Vacate only the appellate judgment:** This is a possible narrower equitable remedy and would be necessary if review concerned only an improperly invoked appellate merits dispute without an intervening loss of the underlying live claim. Here the plaintiff's departure extinguished the entire individual prospective action during the pending postjudgment/intervention proceedings. The same established mootness that precluded appellate merits review warrants clearing the trial declaration as part of terminating the action. The Court does not treat the original declaration as void at entry or invoke automatic vacatur.

## 52. United States v. Gonzales

**Channel and disposition:** Review under §1254 of the Tenth Circuit concurrency ruling. Reverse that ruling and remand for further proceedings consistent with the specific prohibition on concurrent service of the §924(c) firearm terms with the preexisting New Mexico terms. The District Court's choice to make other federal drug terms concurrent is not invalidated by this holding. No ruling on the convictions, a later state sentencing court, the administrative order of service, credit or an independent constitutional objection follows. All eight Associates support this disposition on the stated text and limited scope.

**Operative construction:** The later-sentencing federal court may not direct a §924(c) term to run concurrently with any preexisting state term of imprisonment. The unqualified words covering any other term include state as well as federal terms. The nearby restriction to a predicate crime prosecutable in federal court defines the federal firearm offense; it is not repeated in the separate concurrency command. The phrase including the predicate crime's sentence illustrates rather than exhausts the other terms covered. The prohibition's notwithstanding clause makes §3584's general discretion subordinate for this firearm term.

Section 3584 otherwise permits concurrent or consecutive terms when multiple terms are imposed together or the defendant is already subject to an undischarged term; its same-time concurrency and different-time consecutiveness defaults remain subject to lawful contrary orders and statutory commands. Its separate exception forbids consecutive terms for an attempt and another offense that was the sole objective of that attempt. When exercising lawful concurrency discretion the court must consider §3553(a)'s factors as to each offense. None of those provisions authorizes concurrent §924(c) service contrary to the specific command. No finding establishes an attempt/sole-object pair here, and that exception is not recast as a firearm exception. Section 3584(c)'s aggregate administrative treatment does not decide which sentence must be served first.

**Enhanced construction paths:** The federal-only reading has a serious structural argument: §924(c) punishes a federal predicate, dual systems can create prosecution-order differences, and Witte recognizes coordinated punishment for related conduct. Its decisive difficulty is that it inserts a federal qualifier into the very clause in which Congress omitted one while using one elsewhere. Witte makes coordination conditional on applicable law; it does not authorize disregarding a more specific command. The broader reading's strongest objection is potentially uneven aggregate punishment depending on prosecution sequence. That does not make the operative language indeterminate, and this case requires no rule governing a later state court. Lenity has no residual work after text and context resolve the specific later-federal-sentencing question. No legislative-history conclusion beyond the supplied arguments is invented.

## 53. Warner-Jenkinson Co. v. Hilton Davis Chemical Co.

**Channel and disposition:** Review of the Federal Circuit's en banc affirmance of the nonliteral-infringement verdict and injunction. Reverse and remand for application of element-specific equivalence and prosecution-history limits, including determination of the reason for the lower pH boundary. Neither abolish equivalents nor declare infringement/noninfringement finally. All eight Associates support this disposition; their common estoppel rule includes an actual opportunity to explain the amendment before an unexplained-amendment presumption is applied.

**Continued doctrine and boundaries:** Graver Tank's recognized nonliteral-infringement doctrine survives the 1952 Act; the Act does not establish the requested wholesale displacement. Equivalence must be assessed for each claim element, within the context of the patent and relevant art, not by asking only whether the accused process achieves the invention's overall result. The theory cannot erase an element or expand the patent into subject matter surrendered to obtain it. Functional comparison can assist but is no exclusive mechanical formula; technical proof and known interchangeability bear on whether a difference is substantial. The doctrine does not add a bad-intent requirement to the infringement claim merely because earlier explanations discuss copying.

**Amendment and burden rule:** A narrowing amendment made to meet a patentability objection prevents recapturing through equivalents the subject matter surrendered for that purpose. The patentee invoking equivalence must establish a legally sufficient explanation for an otherwise unexplained narrowing amendment if it seeks to avoid that consequence. If the explanation remains absent after a fair opportunity to present the relevant prosecution history and supported explanation, the court presumes a patentability-related reason for the narrowing limitation and applies estoppel at that limitation's surrendered scope. This is not a finding that the examiner actually demanded pH 6.0, nor an irrebuttable presumption that every amendment extinguishes every possible equivalent. A demonstrated reason unrelated to patentability must be evaluated on its actual terms; the Court does not invent that reason or predetermine its sufficiency.

Markman's judicial claim-construction rule includes genuine technical-usage disputes embedded in construction, while preserving infringement facts outside that task. The present decision does not settle a general judge-jury allocation for all equivalence issues or appellate review of every subsidiary fact. Claim meaning and the legal effect of prosecution history must be settled without letting a factfinder erase the pH limitation. The reason for the lower boundary is unknown; the remand addresses it rather than treating incomplete present explanation as an already adjudicated surrender.

**Enhanced paths:**

- **Abolish or categorically bar equivalents:** Literal claim notice and the 1952 Act's claim requirements support taking the public boundary seriously. But Graver Tank supplies a continuing doctrine that can coexist with those requirements when applied to each limitation. Wholesale abolition or an automatic bar after any amendment discards the distinction between a particular surrender and permissible equivalents remaining outside it. No textual repeal or particular established surrender of all possible pH equivalents is supplied.
- **Affirm broad overall equivalence:** A jury found infringement, and the doctrine protects against insubstantial variants. The objection is that an overall-result comparison can nullify a limitation and the unexplained prosecution amendment may foreclose the very territory asserted. Markman does not allow the jury to change the public claim, and Exhibit Supply preserves actual surrender. Affirmance would resolve an unknown legally material premise.
- **Remand with a rebuttable allocation:** The patentee seeks protection beyond literal terms and can identify why its claim was narrowed; silence cannot leave public notice unprotected indefinitely. The strongest objection is unfair surprise for an old prosecution record not created under an express explanation requirement. A real opportunity for a supported explanation, without deeming prior silence misconduct or demanding a nonexistent contemporaneous recital, answers that objection while retaining the patentee's ultimate burden. No preferred result in the prosecution-history inquiry is assumed.

## 54. Wright v. MetroHealth Medical Center

**Channel and disposition:** Review of the Sixth Circuit's affirmance of summary judgment. Affirm the federal constitutional judgment and the consequential dismissal of pendent state claims; decide no state claim's merits. All eight Associates support affirmance on the actual assignment record.

**Common rule and application:** Marriage remains a fundamental liberty, but a public-employment classification does not receive heightened scrutiny merely because it attaches an incidental consequence to marital status. Heightened review of this asserted burden requires direct and substantial interference with deciding to marry, assessed by the rule's actual operation. The supplied policy and application concern close-interaction assignments in a small emergency-flight unit; a comparable transfer was offered, no discharge or pay reduction is established, and no legal or practical inability to marry is shown. The record therefore presents an ordinary employment classification rather than the substantial marriage barriers addressed in Loving and Zablocki.

Under rational-basis review, the hospital's interests in safety, supervision, conflicts and efficient emergency teamwork rationally support separating close-interaction family assignments. Marriage may create practical divided-loyalty or supervisory concerns without any finding that this couple is unsafe. The State need not establish the best policy, prove that every married pair causes an accident or disprove every possible scheduling alternative. The temporary ability to avoid shared flights does not itself negate a rational general assignment policy. Reaching a contractor's pilot rationally relates to operation of the same team; formal payroll separation does not require treating operationally connected personnel as strangers.

Romer and Heller still require a relationship between the offered interest and the actual disadvantage; the record does not show the blanket withdrawal of ordinary protection invalidated in Romer or an unrelated explanation for the burden invalidated in Heller. Tracy provides an analogous insistence on the actual operational distinction, not a new marriage rule. No genuine factual dispute about discharge, lost pay or inability to marry is supplied. A policy operating through discharge, major material loss, coercive marriage prevention or a different unsupported classification would require its own record and is not validated here.

**Alternative paths:** Heightened scrutiny's strongest argument is that the policy is triggered by the couple's planned marriage and pressures them to choose between their preferred joint assignment and marriage. Jobst distinguishes an incidental economic/administrative consequence from a state prohibition; the present transfer and unchanged-pay facts do not establish the required direct substantial burden. Ordinary-review invalidation has the stronger alternative argument that nonoverlapping schedules already address the risk and that one spouse works for a contractor. Rational-basis review permits imperfect categories and relevant operational predictions; neither narrower schedules nor a different nominal employer disproves the relationship to the common emergency unit. These conclusions preserve review of a genuinely different deprivation.
