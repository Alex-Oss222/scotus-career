### Receipt-specific personal-injury income exclusion

**Governing question:** The return-year §104(a)(2) conditions for excluding damages from gross income.

**Current rule:** The taxpayer must satisfy both the regulation’s tort-or-tort-type-right requirement and the independent statutory requirement that the particular damages be received on account of personal injuries or sickness. Amounts attributable to §213 deductions allowed in a prior year remain excepted. ADEA backpay and willfulness-based liquidated damages do not satisfy the required causal connection merely because the underlying claim is assumed tort-type.

**Authority by component:**

- Receipt-specific causation under the return-year exclusion: Receipt-specific causation under the return-year exclusion, 1995-06-14. Stevens's Opinion of the Court, joined by Stone-Zsela, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer; five directly support the causal holding. Scalia concurs only in the judgment and supplies no vote for this explanation.

**Present operation effective:** 1995-06-14; the component authorities identified above.

**Exceptions and limits:** This is the personal-injuries-or-sickness text, without a later physical-injury amendment. No categorical ADEA tort classification, physical-or-mental-health-only test, general punitive-damages rule or allocation for a distinct settlement is decided.

**Holdings navigation:** Receipt-specific causation under the return-year exclusion.

### Automatic-death jurors and requested capital voir dire

**Governing question:** Inquiry and qualification of a jury provided by the State to determine a capital sentence.

**Current rule:** A juror who would automatically impose death upon conviction regardless of the facts and required mitigation is subject to challenge for cause. If such a juror serves and death is imposed, that sentence cannot be executed. On the capital defendant's request, voir dire must adequately inquire into that disqualifying view; general assurances of fairness or willingness to obey instructions do not suffice when they leave automatic-death bias unexamined. The inquiry protects impartial consideration and does not require jurors favorable to either side, prescribed mitigation weight or any particular questionnaire.

**Authority by component:**
- Disqualification: Morgan v. Illinois, “An automatic-death juror is disqualified from capital sentencing”, June 15, 1992; White, joined by Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy and Souter (six).
- Requested inquiry: Morgan, “Requested inquiry must expose the disqualifying view,” June 15, 1992; same six Justices.

**Present operation effective:** Morgan v. Illinois, June 15, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** No constitutional entitlement to jury capital sentencing, rule for unrelated voir dire omissions, new proof quantum, collateral-review rule or retroactivity holding is adopted. Stone's condition requiring jury-only renewed sentencing does not control.

**Holdings navigation:** Morgan v. Illinois, 504 U.S. 719 (1992) — Capital sentencing.

### Independent Miranda custody review under the former habeas statute

**Governing question:** Classification of ultimate Miranda custody and historical facts in proceedings governed by former §2254(d).

**Current rule:** Whether established interrogation circumstances would make a reasonable person understand freedom of movement as restrained to the degree associated with formal arrest is a mixed constitutional question requiring independent federal habeas review. Former §2254(d)’s factual presumption does not attach to that ultimate conclusion. Historical events and supported credibility findings retain statutory protection. A competent state court’s factual determination after a merits hearing involving the applicant and State or its officer or agent, evidenced by written findings, opinion or other reliable adequate written indicia, is presumed correct subject to the statutory exceptions. Those exceptions are an unresolved factual dispute; factfinding procedure lacking a full and fair hearing; inadequately developed material facts; absence of jurisdiction over subject matter or applicant; unconstitutional denial of counsel to an indigent applicant; denial of a full, fair and adequate hearing; other denial of due process; or a pertinent produced state record, as a whole, failing fairly to support the determination. At a federal evidentiary hearing, after due proof of the state determination, the applicant must establish error by convincing evidence unless one of the first seven circumstances is established, otherwise appears or is admitted, or the federal court finds the eighth. A ruling on papers does not itself establish an exception or require a fresh hearing.

**Authority by component:**

- Ultimate classification: Ultimate Miranda custody requires independent federal review; factual conditions and exceptions: The statutory protection of historical factfinding remains unchanged, 1995-11-29. O’Connor joined by Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer (eight).

**Present operation effective:** Thompson, 1995-11-29.

**Effective transition:** This entry states the former §2254(d) regime. AEDPA separately enacted §§2254(d) and (e) on April 24, 1996. Their temporal application to particular pending proceedings remains unresolved; Thompson does not displace a genuinely applicable new statutory restriction.

**Related current rules:** “Communicated circumstances and Miranda custody” supplies the substantive custody inquiry; “State-court merits decisions and factual development under amended §2254” supplies the distinct amended text.

**Holdings navigation:** Thompson v. Keohane, 516 U.S. 99.

### Proof before a potentially indefinite complete parental-contact cutoff

**Governing question:** The sexual-abuse finding triggering Louisiana Revised Statutes §9:364(D)'s severe parental-contact restriction.

**Current rule:** Sexual abuse of the parent's child or children must be proved by clear and convincing evidence before §9:364(D) imposes mandatory loss of all visitation and contact until an adversarial hearing establishes both successful completion of a program designed for sexual abusers and that supervised visitation is in the children's best interests. The statutory treatment requires evaluation and psychotherapy specifically designed for perpetrators of family violence and conducted by licensed mental-health professionals. Bare preponderance cannot supply the predicate for that complete, potentially indefinite exclusion.

**Authority by component:**

- Heightened proof for the specified deprivation: Clear and convincing proof must precede this statutory all-contact restriction, 1995-11-20; Stone-Zsela, joined by Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer (five).

**Present operation effective:** A. St. P. C. v. B. C., 1995-11-20.

**Exceptions and limits:** Ordinary investigation, emergency intervention, temporary separation and lesser custody or supervised-contact measures retain their existing requirements. The rule supplies no universal custody burden, periodic-review schedule, restoration procedure, treatment-admission condition or entitlement to contact.

**Holdings navigation:** A. St. P. C. v. B. C..

### Protection against serious avoidable harm in compulsory custody transitions

**Governing question:** The manner of state-compelled severance of an established caregiving relationship to implement otherwise lawful custody entitlement.

**Current rule:** The child has an independent liberty interest against serious, avoidable injury attributable to the manner of compulsory implementation. Before implementation, the child's representative must receive a meaningful, prompt opportunity to present concrete evidence of that transition harm and practicable means of avoiding it; the entitled parent must be able to respond, and the court must consider the issue under Mathews and give a reasoned implementation decision. Mathews requires attention to the identified private interest, error risk and value of further procedures, and governmental interests and burdens. Procedures and protective measures must fit the actual showing, proceed promptly and preserve lawful parental custody entitlement.

**Authority by component:**

- Bounded child interest: I. The child's own interest limits the manner of compulsory implementation; fitted process and relief: II. The required opportunity and relief must fit the actual transition claim, 1996-06-21; Stone-Zsela Parts I–II, joined by Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer (five).

**Present operation effective:** Baby Richard v. Kirchner, 1996-06-21.

**Exceptions and limits:** Caregiving duration does not itself prove serious injury. No right to preserve an invalid adoption, defeat a fit parent through a best-interests comparison, or remain indefinitely with nonparents follows. Unsupported requests may be rejected and adequate consideration may support immediate implementation. There is no automatic evidentiary hearing, fixed delay, appointed-clinician requirement, prescribed proof burden or renewed custody contest. Independently lawful emergency protection remains available.

**Holdings navigation:** Baby Richard, by his guardian ad litem, v. Kirchner (In re Petition of Kirchner).
