# Enacted-law supplement: IIRIRA §633

Current law entering the first scheduled OT1996 event includes the following amendment enacted September 30, 1996, Pub. L. No. 104-208, Division C, Title VI, §633, 110 Stat. 3009-701. This is the enacted text, not a judicial interpretation or case outcome.

INA §202(a)(1), 8 U.S.C. §1152(a)(1), is amended by inserting “(A)” after the nondiscrimination heading and adding:

> (B) Nothing in this paragraph shall be construed to limit the authority of the Secretary of State to determine the procedures for the processing of immigrant visa applications or the locations where such applications will be processed.

Section 633 states no separate delayed effective date or express pending-case instruction. Section 309(a)'s delay is expressly limited to Title III-A, “this subtitle and the amendments made by this subtitle”; it does not delay Title VI §633. The text's case-specific meaning and temporal reach, its effect on prospective versus reparative relief, and any APA or constitutional claims are undecided. An effective enactment is not necessarily retroactive to completed conduct.
