## Supported reported facts

The control-extracted published record recitation reports that Mississippi officials used the Provisional Plan to register voters for forty-one days and that about one-third of the State's voter-registration officials had begun using it. It reports that no election occurred before the Plan was abandoned and that no election was then imminent. It attributes the officials' abandonment to the point when it became clear that the legislature would not enact the state-law changes they expected and considered necessary for the Plan. The reported events occurred within a few weeks.
