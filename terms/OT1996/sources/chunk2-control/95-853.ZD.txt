Chief Justice Rehnquist,
 dissenting.
 
 

I join all but Part II of Justice Thomas' dissenting opinion.
 For the reasons stated in that opinion, I would not extend the Griffin-Mayer
 line of cases to invalidate Mississippi's refusal to pay for petitioner's
 transcript on appeal in this case.
