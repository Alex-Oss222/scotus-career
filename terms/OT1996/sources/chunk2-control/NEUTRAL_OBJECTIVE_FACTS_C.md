## Atherton

Argument occurred November 4, 1996. City Federal Savings Bank was a federally chartered, federally insured savings association that entered receivership in 1989. The RTC sued in the bank's name against officers and directors over development, construction and business-acquisition loans, alleging gross negligence, ordinary negligence and fiduciary breaches. The district court dismissed the claims other than gross negligence under its reading of §1821(k). The Third Circuit reversed on a certified interlocutory appeal under §1292(b), allowing pursuit of federal common-law negligence/fiduciary claims because the institution held a federal charter. Source recitation: 519 U.S. 216–217, citing 57 F.3d 1231, 1243–1249. FDIC succeeded RTC as receiver.

The reviewed issue is the source and governing standard of care. Neither the supplied packet nor this record recitation independently establishes a litigated retroactivity objection. Do not infer such an objection merely from receivership in 1989, or announce a retroactivity holding without source support. The underlying breach, causation, damages and business-judgment defenses have not been adjudicated by this extraction.
