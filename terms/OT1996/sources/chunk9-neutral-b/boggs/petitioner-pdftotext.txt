       QUESTIONS PRESENTED FOR REVIEW

                                    I.

Whether the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of

1974 (ERISA), 29 U.S.C. 1001 et seq., preempts states’ com-

munity property laws that afford an inheritance right to heirs
of a predeceased non-employee spouse of the pension plan par-
ticipant to receive ERISA covered pension plan benefits (in-
cluding those rolled over in an IRA) from the participant's
estate and surviving spouse.

                                   II.

Does ERISA preemption of states’ laws extend to pension

benefits ‘rolled over’ into an Individual Retirement Account
under 26 U.S.C. 401, et seq.?
