Before the court is the Motion of Defendants to Dismiss 
for Lack of Jurisdiction. Plaintiff has filed an opposition. The 
matter is before the court on briefs, without oral argument. 


Defendants argue that this court lacks subject matter 
jurisdiction over the declaratory judgment action brought by 
Plaintiff. They deny that the matter involves an interpreta- 
tion of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 
(ERISA). This court finds, however, that the scope of ERISA 
is at the heart of this matter. Accordingly; 


IT IS ORDERED that the Motion to Dismiss for Lack 
of Jurisdiction be DENIED.

STIPULATION OF FACTS 
The parties hereto stipulate to the following facts: 


1. Isaac E. Boggs, Jr. died February 16, 1989 leaving 
a Last Will and Testament. 


2. He is survived by his widow, Sandra Jean Dale Boggs, 
the plaintiff in this matter, and three sons, Thomas Frank 


Boggs, Harry Maurice Boggs, and David Bruce Boggs, the 
defendants in this case. 


3. His succession was opened under No. 89-30055 of the 
22nd Judicial District Court for the Parish of St. Tammany, 
State of Louisiana, and Sandra Jean Dale Boggs was con- 
firmed as Testamentary Executrix in that proceeding. 


26 


4. Isaac E. Boggs, Jr. was married first to Dorothy 
McDonald Boggs, who died on August 14, 1979. 


5. Under Dorothy Boggs’ will, Isaac E. Boggs, Jr. was 
left one-third of her estate and the lifetime usufruct of the 
remaining two-thirds of her estate which was inherited by 
her three children, Thomas Frank Boggs, Harry Maurice 
Boggs and David Bruce Boggs. 


6. Dorothy Boggs’ succession was opened under No. 
8627 of the 22nd Judicial District Court for the Parish of St. 
Tammany, State of Lonisiana, and a Judgment of Possession 
was signed on Febru»ry 5, 1980. 


7. The Sworn Descriptive List of asserts filed in Dorothy 
Boggs’ succession included the Bell Systems Savings Plan 
for Salaried Employees with a value of $42,388.57 at the time 
of her death; Dorothy’s one-half interest was valued at 
$21,194.29. Bell Systems provided the value of the Savings 
Plan as of Dorothy Boggs’ death. 


8. Isaac E. Boggs, Jr. married Sandra Jean Dale Boggs 
on April 5, 1980, ¢ no children were born of their marriage. 


9. Isaac E. Boggs, Jr. was employed by South Central 
Bell Tel & Tel from June 18, 1949 until September 1, 1985, 
when he retired or a period of thirty-six years and two 
months. 


10. He was married to Dorothy McDonald Boggs from 
the date of his employment on June 18, 1949 until her death 
on August 14, 1979. 


27 


11. Upon his retirement he received the sum of 
$151,628.94 which was the distribution from the BellSouth 
Management Savings Plan, (formerly called BellSouth 
Systems Savings Plan for Salaried Employees) and all of 
these funds were “rolled over” into an IRA account at 
Delaware Charter Guarantee Trust Co. 


12. He made no withdrawals from the IRA account prior 
to his death. At the date of his death this account was 
$180,778.05. 


13. In addition to the distribution from the Savings 
Plan, he received monthly retirement payments from 
September 1, 1985 through the date of his death on February 
16, 1989 in the amount of $1,777.67 per month. 


14. Thereafter the survivor’s lifetime annuity pension 
has been paid to his widow, Sandra Jean Dale Boggs, and 
she was named as the beneficiary of the IRA plan. 


15. Under the terms of the BellSouth ESOP, Isaac Boggs 
acquired 96 shares in AT&T which were distributed to him 
when he retired. 


16. In addition, the Plan included life insurance policies 
payable to Sandra Jean Dale Boggs as the named beneficiary. 


17. The BellSouth Management Savings Plan, the 
ESOP Plan and all retirement benefits and employee benefit 
plans of South Central Bell Tel. & Tel are qualified plans 
subject to the provisions of The Employee Retirement Income 
Security Act of 1974 and The Retirement Equity Act of 1984. 


28 


18. The IRA account at Delaware is a qualified In- 
dividual Retirement Account under IRC Section 401, et seq. 


19. Two of the defendants herein, Harry and David 
Boggs, have filed an action in the 22nd Judicial District Court 
for the Parish of St. Tammany, State of Louisiana, No. 
92-14434, claiming that they are entitled to the following: 


a. An accounting of the usufruct enjoyed by Isaac E. 
Boggs, Jr. over that portion of the retirement payments at-
