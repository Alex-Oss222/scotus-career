ORDER 


This case was argued May 6, 1996, and our opinion 
affirming the district court was filed August 6, 1996. A 
petition for rehearing by the plaintiff-appellants was denied 
September 3, 1996, and our mandate was filed September 11, 
1996. 


On July 26, 1996, the Maryland Court of Appeals 
deed the case of Sheets v. Brethren Mutual, 342 Md. 634, 
679 A.2d 540 (1996), which case did not come to the 
attention of the plaintiff-appellants before August 30, 1996. 
The plaintiff-appellants take the position that Sheets should 
have required a different disposition of this case than the 
disposition contained in our said decision filed August 6, 
1996. The defendant-appellee does not agree that Sheets so 
requires.

It is accordingly ADJUDGED and ORDERED that all 
of the said petitions and motions above shall be, and they
