PER CURIAM: 


On June 23, 1994, we entered a judgment and filed an 
opinion, NYSA-ILA Medical & Clinical Servs. Fund vy. 
Axelrod, 27 F.3d 823, which reversed a summary judg- 
ment entered February 23, 1993 by the United States 
District Court for the Southern District of New York 
(John S. Martin, Jr., Judge). The district court had held 
that the Employee Retirement Income Security Act 
(“ERISA”), 29 U.S.C. § 1001 ef seqg., did not preempt 
New York’s Health Facility Assessment (“HFA”), N.Y. 
Pub. Health Law § 2807-d, reasoning that the tax im- 
posed by the HFA “is not great enough to pose a serious 
economic threat to the plan.” In reversing, we held that 
the HFA directly affected and was therefore “related to” 
the Fund in its principal role as an employee welfare plan.
