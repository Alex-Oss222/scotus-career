A notice of appeal has been filed from the final order of 
the district court denying a petition for writ of habeas corpus 
filed pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2241 or § 2254, in a case in which

State court." 28 U.S.C. § 2253 (as amended April 24, 1996). An 
appeal may not proceed to consideration of the merits unless a 
"circuit justice or judge" grants a certificate of appealability. 
Id. 


As appellant has not submitted a formal application for a 
certificate of appealability, appellant’s notice of appeal will 
be deemed to be such an application, and will be submitted by the 
Clerk of this Court to a panel of the Court for consideration. 
cf. ird Circuit Local Appellate Rule (3rd Cir. LAR) 22.1. 
Appellant may file a memorandum of law in support of the 
application for a certificate of appealability within fourteen 
(14) days of the date of this letter. Any formal application for 
a certificate of appealability must be submitted at the same time 
as the memorandum of law. Any memorandum in opposition to 
issuance of a certificate of appealability must be filed within 
twenty-one (21) days of the date of this letter. 


A memorandum in support of or in opposition to an 
application for a certificate of appealability may not exceed 
fifteen (15) pages in length. Cf. 3rd Cir. LAR 22.1. The Court 
requires an original and three (3) copies of any memorandum, and 
a certificate of service showing that all interested parties have 
been served with a copy of the document. 


If the Court grants the application for a certificate of 
appealability, the Clerk will issue a briefing schedule. The 
appeal will be heard on the original record. 3rd Cir. LAR 30.2. 
If the appellant is indigent, counsel may be appointed on behalf 
of the appellant under the Criminal Justice Act. 18 U.S.C. § 
3006A(a) (2) (B).
