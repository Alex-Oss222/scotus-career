In Segura v. United States, 468 U.S. 796 (1984), the police

illegally entered the defendant’s apartment without a

seized in the execution of the search warrant. The
defendant argued that the evidence seized under the

that the legality of the entry was irrelevant where the
source of the information on which the warrant was

warrant.
