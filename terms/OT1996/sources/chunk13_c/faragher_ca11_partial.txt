L251: cite230†¶87 With respect to the City’s involvement with the lifeguards, the court found that “the lifeguards’ contacts with higher city officials ... were almost non-existent,” and the City admits that, “Marine Safety headquarters was located at the City beach and was thus physically remote from City Hall.” The lifeguards operated under an extensive chain of command, with at least six levels of management between the lifeguards and the City Manager.
