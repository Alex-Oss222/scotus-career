                                                  CONCLUSION

                  No reason exists to torture the meaning of § 905(a),
               ignore the principles of collateral estoppel, and greatly
                expand the seaman status test in order to allow a finding
               that Papai was a seaman. The Court of Appeals decision
               below should be reversed, and Papai should be precluded
               from further pursuing seaman remedies.
