# Printz/Mack — final form fixed before assignment

The controlling merits proposition has five direct joins: Stone-Zsela, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas. Congress may not command unwilling state executive officers to perform affirmative investigative, screening, record-processing or enforcement duties as administrators of a federal regulatory program directed at private persons; the defect is the compulsory assignment of executive responsibility, not its cost, and §922(s)(2)'s mandatory checks, with the acceptance of the dealer's notice and the purchaser's statement they imply, fall within it. Stone's approved position states that rule as an extension of his New York position from compulsory legislation and ownership to compulsory executive administration. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas join the rule at its full stated reach, as reconciliation §5 records. O'Connor joins it except its reach to purely ministerial reporting or record-processing requirements, which she reserves; that reach has four joins and is marked as the opinion of four Justices.

No majority adopts the contrary source-and-function proposition: Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer would affirm, and Souter rejects categorical incapacity only together with his fiscal remand. The conditional judgment-only alternatives in the postfreeze audit are therefore not activated; under its §9 the reconciled structural reversals remain operative. With Stone's approved reversal they supply five votes to reverse in both dockets. Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer would affirm; Souter would vacate and remand on his fiscal ground. The eight Associates' commitments alone were not a four-to-four equal division and supplied no judgment majority.

One signed opinion of the Court carries the complete structure for both consolidated dockets: a jurisdictional section, joined by all nine; Part I, stating and applying the structural rule, with its reach to purely ministerial record-processing separately identified as the opinion of four Justices; and Part II, stating reversal, declaratory relief for Printz and Mack and remand for conforming judgments, with no damages, reimbursement or budgetary hearing. The opinion contains no blanket dealer severance, presumed receipt, unrestricted data-use right, automatic residual remand, balancing test, Take Care or unity-of-executive holding, Second Amendment discussion or contraction of commerce authority, so every joiner's recorded limits are met. No Marks synthesis or formal partial affirmance is selected. The related dockets share the same components and disposition; one opinion covers both.

The final judgment has one disposition in each consolidated docket. The Chief Justice is in that judgment majority and assigns under Engine §8. Only majority members joining every proposition of the opinion in full can carry it; O'Connor's withheld join to the ministerial reach leaves her outside that group. Assignment accounting follows next, from actual prior Records. No opinion is split or combined to alter the census. Separate writings will state O'Connor's short concurrence reserving purely ministerial reporting, the Stevens dissent joined by Ginsburg and Breyer, Souter's separate dissent on his fiscal ground and Breyer's additional functional dissent.
