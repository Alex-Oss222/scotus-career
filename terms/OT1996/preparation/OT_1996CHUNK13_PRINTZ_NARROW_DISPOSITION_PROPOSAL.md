# Printz/Mack — sanitized narrower-disposition proposal for isolated review

This is a proposed public legal formulation and limited alternative disposition, not a new commitment, a completed decision, an authorship selection or a statement of any controlled Justice's private position. The frozen reconciled handoff remains controlling unless the isolated audit independently validates a changed join or disposition.

## Proposed issue treatment

The Court would decide the source-and-function question only to this extent: the Constitution does not categorically disable Congress, when implementing an otherwise valid direct regulation of private transactions, from requiring appropriate, temporary records-based assistance within an executive officer's proper function. The proposed Brady construction would require receiving prescribed forms, consulting available state/local and designated federal records, interpreting those records for eligibility, and the statute's actually triggered explanations and information protections. It would authorize no independent field investigation, arrest, prosecution or transfer of general regulatory policymaking. This is a proposed new boundary under New York, not a rule already supplied by Voting Rights Coalition. Actual work and resource use are acknowledged; smallness and duration alone are not constitutional authority.

That limited proposition would leave open whether the asserted fiscal and unauthorized-expenditure objections prevent sustaining this compulsory arrangement without further development. It would not adopt an unqualified fair-value compensation rule, establish a damages cause, find a taking, fix an amount, direct an appropriation or adjudicate State-law liability. It would not convert the officer's oath alone into an affirmative duty without enacted direction. Statutory meaning and every conditional trigger remain as in the frozen handoff.

## Proposed narrow judgment and remand

Vacate the Ninth Circuit judgments insofar as they unconditionally sustain the challenged officer obligations, and remand for development and consideration of the already asserted lack-of-budget-provision and unauthorized-expenditure objections under the Court's stated limited-assistance rule. Require no fresh theory, new money claim or transaction-specific factual assumption. The courts would consider those existing objections and their legal consequences; the Supreme Court would neither award compensation nor hold a constitutional compensation entitlement established.

The proposed disposition would not categorically invalidate the checking command, direct an injunction for nonparties, adjudicate independent dealer/purchaser duties, revive every theoretical residual issue or require factfinding about hypothetical receipt, determinations or requests. It would issue no new Supreme Court interim injunction. Further interim relief, if properly sought, would require its own ordinary predicates. Lower dispositions outside the grant remain outside this action. The treatment of actual conditional duties would follow their proved predicates and the bounded fiscal inquiry, not a generalized finding of nonparticipation.

## Affected reconciled commitments and audit question

Souter already requires development and consideration of these fiscal objections and rejects unconditional affirmance. He supports appropriate executive assistance only with his own fair-value qualification. The audit must determine whether reserving that legal question for the stated remand adequately preserves his ground; it cannot count him as having abandoned the qualification or endorsed unconditional assistance.

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas currently seek the stronger disposition of bounded reversal because their structural ground invalidates mandatory inquiry and implicit acceptance regardless of a quantified expense. They would continue to reject the proposed limited-assistance merits rule. The possible narrower disposition is a judgment-only vote to vacate the unconditional appellate judgments and permit the already identified fiscal objections to be developed **if the Court adopts the contrary source-and-function rule**. Such a vote would preserve their structural dissent while permitting the remaining existing objection to be examined under the rule that would govern the case. It would not make any of them join a compensation entitlement, accept State law as a veto of valid federal law or endorse a renewed inquiry into every ancillary provision.

The proposed changed setting is thus the legal ground governing the remand and the lesser relief actually proposed, not new evidence, a desired numerical result or the asserted inconvenience of a divided Court. Their current objections remain substantial: each regards further fiscal consideration as unnecessary because the command is already structurally invalid; the final handoff specifically rejected an automatic residual-remand instruction. This narrower proposal concerns the actual budget/expenditure objection rather than hypothetical ancillary claims, but that difference does not compel a new vote.

Please determine separately for each affected Associate whether their date-eligible reasoning supports this alternative judgment at precisely that scope, and identify a concrete legal basis for any changed disposition. If the sources do not support it, retain the frozen position. Do not infer a majority from all five rejecting unconditional affirmance, do not treat a request for reversal as automatic assent to every lesser remand, and do not invent draft dialogue or a compensation holding. Any resulting judgment-only join must remain distinct from a join to the source-and-function or fiscal rationale.
