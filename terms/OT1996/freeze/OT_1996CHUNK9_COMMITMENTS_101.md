## Component commitments and operative grounds

**A — Forum:** Section 1341 bars this federal declaratory and injunctive action. These borrower-owned production credit associations sue for their own financial interests. Congress's designation of an entity as a federal instrumentality, and the possibility that federal law exempts its obligations or activities from particular taxes, do not make its independent suit one by the United States. The Farm Credit Act provides the associations' own sue-and-be-sued capacity and distinct statutory privileges; it does not identify this litigation as litigation of the sovereign. The associations' lending role, regulation and statutory designation do not establish sovereign identity sufficient to defeat section 1341 here.

*Department of Employment* supplies the United States' exception where the sovereign sues to protect itself and its instrumentalities; the United States and Red Cross were plaintiffs there. Its substantive instrumentality holding cannot be transposed into a holding about every instrumentality's independent federal forum. *Nash-Finch* concerns a federal regulatory agency protecting its regulatory system under section 2283; it provides the strongest analogy for dispensing with the United States' formal joinder but does not turn these private lending institutions into a regulatory agency. The Farm Credit associations fail even the functional-sovereign-interest path described in the United States' argument. It is unnecessary to adopt an absolute rule requiring the United States as named plaintiff in every possible instrumentality action, to resolve the treatment of the Federal Reserve or FDIC, or to settle every possible congressionally created exception.

**B — Adequacy and merits reach:** The statutory state-remedy condition is satisfied on the associations' express concession. *National Private Truck Council* and *Reich* retain their actual-adequacy and honored-postpayment-remedy limits, but no present dispute requires independent construction of Arkansas's refund or deficiency procedures. Section 26-18-507's refund route and section 26-18-406's deficiency-review route remain distinct. The Court reaches neither the validity of these taxes nor a general constitutional or statutory immunity of production credit associations. Section 2077's limited exemption for notes, debentures and other obligations, including its surtax, estate, inheritance, gift and holder-income qualifications, is not adjudicated into an unlimited exemption or a rejection of every substantive immunity theory.

**C — Judgment and remedy:** Reverse the Eighth Circuit's affirmance and remand for proceedings implementing dismissal of this federal action for lack of jurisdiction under section 1341. No federal merits judgment of immunity survives that disposition. Dismissal does not adjudicate the underlying exemption claim or foreclose its ordinary state-court presentation. The Court orders no state refund, assessment, new factual hearing, United States joinder, filing deadline or disposition of the parallel state action.
