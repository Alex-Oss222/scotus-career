## Enhanced comparison and principal objections

**Affirmance after lawful reconsideration:** A validly constituted executive body may itself exercise the discretion to proceed on an existing evidentiary record. The reported acts establish an actual new exercise rather than inevitable-result harmlessness. The strongest objection is that earlier participation may have tainted discretionary investigation and conciliation; an endpoint authorization might fail to restore an opportunity Congress protects. Here Legi-Tech identifies the unconstitutional composition and alleges a rubber stamp but the supplied record does not establish an unperformed conciliation requirement, continuing congressional participation, or a distinct statutory opportunity denied by adopting the earlier materials. The Court need not require proof of subjective reconsideration by each member or treat absence of newly recovered minutes as a failure of proof.

**Dismissal/restart or remand for additional cure:** Ryder guarantees real lawful replacement of a constitutionally defective adjudicative proceeding; a timely challenger cannot be denied relief simply by de facto treatment. That principle supports insisting that the valid Commission genuinely perform the relevant decisional function. Its strongest application here stops short of an automatic restart: the Commission prosecutes rather than adjudicates liability, and a lawful probable-cause/enforcement determination has occurred. The separate fact weighing, credibility and sentence-review opportunities missing in Ryder were not supplied there by higher review; the record here identifies the responsible lawful body actually exercising the enforcement judgment. The district court's automatic retroactivity theory supplies no independent finding that the cure failed.

**De facto or selective prospectivity:** Neither supplies an alternative holding. Harper and Reynoldsville Casket require the governing rule for this open case. The result does not immunize prior action because officials relied on the old arrangement. It rests on valid subsequent action. Buckley's limited treatment of past FEC acts does not itself defeat a timely structural challenge after Ryder.
