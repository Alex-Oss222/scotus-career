The precise permanent schedule, bargaining constraints, overtime consequences, and any later vacancy remain relevant to relief.

### Issue 1: Reasonable accommodation and undue hardship

* **Controlling Simulated Law:** Simulated *Beadle v. City of Tampa* applies the then-governing *Hardison* standard: an employer need not provide an accommodation imposing a real more-than-de-minimis operational burden, but speculative inconvenience, coworker hostility, or an unavailable swap does not carry the employer’s burden. No categorical public-safety exception exists.
* **Current Reusable Doctrine:** The employee must identify a sincere religious practice, a conflict with an employment requirement, employer notice, and adverse action. The employer must establish that available reasonable accommodation would impose an actual more-than-de-minimis burden on business operations. The statute does not guarantee the employee’s preferred accommodation when another effective one exists.
* **Other Governing Law:** Title VII, 42 U.S.C. §§ 2000e(j) and 2000e-2(a); *Trans World Airlines, Inc. v. Hardison*; *Ansonia Board of Education v. Philbrook*; ordinary appellate review of findings after trial.

* **Open Point:** The record does not establish a universal obligation to conduct a trial accommodation, and it does not resolve every remedial or later-vacancy issue.

## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Accommodation and proof of hardship

* **Reasoning Below:** The District Court accepted the asserted burden on coworkers, morale, and station operation. The Ninth Circuit regarded those findings as unsupported or insufficient under the governing legal standard and emphasized the Department’s failure to explore feasible combinations.
* **Petitioner or Appellant:** California argues that a continuously operating inspection station requires equitable rotating availability, any permanent Sabbath exemption would shift undesirable work to others, recurring exceptions could impair morale and administration, and courts should not redesign public staffing.
* **Respondent or Appellee:** Opuku-Boateng argues that he offered extensive flexibility, the employer never tested or meaningfully evaluated available schedules, the anticipated burdens were speculative, and Title VII requires proof of actual operational hardship rather than generalized coworker dissatisfaction.
* **Material Alternative or Unreached Ground:** The Court may affirm because the present record fails to carry the employer’s burden without creating a categorical requirement that every employer run an experimental schedule.
* **Necessary Concession or Limiting Principle:** Title VII does not require violation of a bona fide seniority system, payment of more-than-de-minimis overtime, understaffing a safety function, or imposition of material transferred burdens on identified coworkers.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

A lawful path applies simulated *Beadle* and affirms the Ninth Circuit because California relied on anticipated scheduling and morale effects without sufficient evidence that available combinations would produce actual more-than-de-minimis hardship. The Court can leave exact relief and any materially changed staffing conditions to the District Court. A broader rule requiring every employer to operate a trial accommodation is unnecessary and unsupported.

## F. VERIFIED SIMULATED-LAW APPLICATION

### Application of Beadle without a trial-accommodation requirement

Beadle’s eight-Justice holding allows concrete noncash efficiency, training, and safety burdens to be proved through professional evidence and reasonable inferences without implementing a trial schedule or awaiting an accident. It rejects treating a concession about one arrangement as dispositive of materially different supported proposals. California therefore must address nights, Sundays, holidays, swaps, and combined scheduling on this actual station record. Coworker hostility does not become a business necessity, but real involuntary burdens, bargaining restrictions, or overtime costs cannot be discarded merely because no trial was attempted.

## G. RECONCILED QUESTIONS, LAW, AND AVAILABLE PATHS

Title VII’s applicant protections and its definition of religion do not begin only after hiring; rejection because of a known sincere scheduling conflict triggers the same accommodation inquiry. Beadle preserves Hardison’s then-effective more-than-de-minimis standard, including actual nonmonetary burdens and reasonable, evidence-supported professional predictions. Ansonia permits any effective reasonable accommodation without a duty to exhaust every option. Thus the absence of an implemented trial accommodation is not alone liability. California must connect its scheduling, overtime, coverage or other concrete constraints to the proposed available arrangements; coworker hostility or abstract future requests do not supply that connection. Those are the parties’ actual competing showings.
