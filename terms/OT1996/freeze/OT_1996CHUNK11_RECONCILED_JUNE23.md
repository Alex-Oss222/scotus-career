## 127. Agostini v. Felton; consolidated Nos. 96-552 and 96-553

### Constitutional rule, Rule 60 channel and limits

Lemon remains the governing Establishment Clause test. Its purpose, effects and entanglement inquiries supply evidence within one inquiry: a specific prohibition or distinct doctrine controls when it resolves the conduct; an established permissible relationship defeats the claim on that ground; otherwise a legally sufficient unresolved allegation receives attribution-and-function analysis under ordinary procedure. These are routes within the updated Lemon test, not freestanding prongs or an alternative test.

Religious-school location, presumed indoctrination from location, incidental institutional benefit and ordinary secular monitoring do not alone establish prohibited sponsorship, financing or institutional control. Public control, independently secular work and enforceable protection against devotional use and diversion remain material. The rule overrules Aguilar's categorical treatment of this public Title I service and displaces Ball's corresponding onsite rationale to that extent, without reopening Ball's judgment or approving its separate Community Education program. Specific prohibitions, distinct doctrines and the actual holdings of Kiryas Joel, Rosenberger, Pinette, Chabad, Witters and Mueller remain intact. Zobrest supplied no Establishment Clause merits holding.

Rufo requires a significant change in fact or law making continued prospective enforcement inequitable and relief suitably tailored to that change. Actual anticipation and acceptance of a relevant risk can impose a heavy burden; foreseeability alone is no absolute bar. Costs, a cheaper alternative or anticipated judicial disagreement alone do not suffice. Rodriguez de Quijas required the lower courts to apply Aguilar. The Court's decision supplies the present legal change; no second motion is required solely to invoke it. Rule 60(b)(5) does not generally reopen completed judgments or extinguish independently supported statutory, contractual or decree obligations.

### Remedy and source limits

The four Associates joining the Court support mandatory removal of the onsite prohibition insofar as it rests on religious location, presumed indoctrination from that location or ordinary secular monitoring. If no independent basis supports that prohibition, it must be dissolved. Any unresolved issue must identify a supported prohibition material under the updated Lemon test; ordinary procedure applies, without an impossible-misconduct guarantee or presumed inseparability. Existing lawful services may continue during prompt implementation. Administrative delay cannot perpetuate the categorical rule indefinitely, and the City cannot disregard a still-operative injunction before the district court acts under the mandate. The four dissenters would preserve the injunction by affirming denial of relief. The consolidated dockets have the same disposition.

The Rule 60 motions were filed in October and December 1995; the record does not allocate each month to a particular filer or establish precise filing days. No factual transformation, unproved diversion, new damages entitlement, compulsory onsite funding, fixed implementation period or extinguishment of an independent obligation supplies the decision.

### Historical comparison and Justice-specific premises

The Associates divide as in the historical judgment, but the governing premises and the scope of the Court's method are stated independently. Zobrest is an antecedent-law remand, and Kiryas Joel preserved Aguilar and Ball; neither supplied a prior constitutional change. O'Connor's own public-control and monitoring analysis, Scalia's distinction between governmental religion and private activity, Kennedy's governmental-function and private-responsibility analysis, and Thomas's equal-benefit and funded-activity positions support their direct joins to all four holdings. Those joins do not depend on an absent Zobrest merits ruling or a judicial headcount as a legal reason.

**Historical departure:** The Court adopts a single three-route inquiry within Lemon and supplies the significant legal change in this decision. O'Connor's Aguilar and Ball writings support retaining safeguards while rejecting their treatment as a categorical disability; Scalia's Kiryas Joel and private-access positions support specific prohibitions and actual governmental action rather than observer inference; Kennedy's Lee, Kiryas Joel and Rosenberger positions distinguish official religious functions from secular services and private responsibility; Thomas's Kiryas Joel, Rosenberger and private-access positions support the distinction between religious identity and the function publicly supported. Their complete joins preserve the concrete precedents and unadjudicated limits stated above.

Souter's Part I is joined by Stevens and Ginsburg; Part II also by Breyer. Ginsburg's procedural dissent is joined by Stevens, Souter and Breyer. The stare-decisis and vehicle grounds do not convert Breyer's limited join into a full direct-aid rationale.

## 128. Cohen v. Brown University

### Common components and remedy

All eight Associates affirm the First Circuit's liability and remedial disposition. The components are: validity and actual application of the three independent accommodation routes; rejection of the identified governmental-classification challenge on its actual objective and fit; and preservation of preliminary protection with the four-team final directive remaining vacated while Brown proposes a lawful effective compliance plan. The original preliminary protection for gymnastics and volleyball differs from the later final directive concerning gymnastics, fencing, skiing and water polo.

Actual Kelley Parts I and II have all eight Associates' direct joins. Title IX permits effective accommodation through substantial proportionality, continuing responsive expansion, or full effective accommodation of the underrepresented sex's interests and abilities; any one is sufficient. Section 1681(b) forbids compelled preference merely from imbalance but permits relevant statistics. Separate facilities, coaching, equipment, scheduling and benefits obligations survive. Brown's failure rests on absent responsive expansion and actual competitively viable unmet interests, not enrollment percentages or a conclusory assumption of equal aggregate preferences.

### Alternative-path and comparator audit

Brown's strongest path is that a nominal safe harbor becomes a quota if the other routes are illusory or actual interest is presumed from enrollment. The reconciled rule rejects such an interpretation. It nonetheless rejects reversal here because the source record supplies actual competitively viable interests and absence of responsive expansion. Comparative interest evidence remains relevant to genuine demand; it does not authorize leaving proved opportunities unserved merely because the overrepresented sex may have greater aggregate demand. No new survey rule, participant formula, fixed roster floor or later compliance fact is found.

The governmental act is federal implementation and judicial enforcement, not Brown's mere receipt of funds. Demonstrated denied opportunity is the actual important objective; independent alternatives, nonstereotyped demand findings and effective rather than numerical-only correction supply fit. Kelley requires that inquiry independently of statutory validity. Its symmetry for the demonstrably disadvantaged sex survives.

The lower court must permit a lawful plan, including permissible reductions, with prompt effective correction. A court may eventually prescribe specific relief if institutional choices do not cure the violation or that relief proves necessary; the present ruling does not categorically prohibit team-specific orders. The preliminary gymnastics/volleyball protection remains until the lawful final remedial process replaces it. No final four-team mandate is reinstated, no budget selected, and no damages or funding sanction created.

There is **no Supreme Court merits departure to assess**. A historical certiorari denial has no merits rationale to import. The lower majority and dissent supply competing legal arguments, not eight Supreme Court positions. Kelley is materially different existing in-world authority: every Associate actually joined its statutory and constitutional rules. That direct continuity, together with this record and remedy distinction, supports the unanimous Associate commitment; it is not a prediction from the historical denial or a fabricated petition vote.

## 129. Idaho v. Coeur d'Alene Tribe of Idaho, No. 94-1474

### Components and law

All eight leave the direct-State, agency and direct quiet-title dismissals undisturbed. Four would reverse the prospective-officer ruling at the actual comprehensive prayer's scope; four would affirm the limited officer route and permit ordinary merits/remedial proceedings. None decides ownership, navigability, prestatehood disposition, the alternative aboriginal-title theory, or the separate United States action.

Actual Seminole permits the connected Governor's prospective IGRA action within that statute's full limits and rejects categorical exclusion by its detailed scheme in that case. It does not decide sovereign submerged lands or supply a universal rule preserving every officer remedy. Its State-amenability judgment has no controlling rationale; Union Gas was not overruled. A historical statement that Seminole already bars all Article I abrogation, or already adopts a general Bivens/special-factors restriction on Young, cannot enter this decision as current law.

The actual prayer seeks exclusive use, occupancy and quiet enjoyment of the disputed beds, banks and waters and exclusion of State regulatory action, not a hypothetical isolated permit correction. The Ninth Circuit's express limitation avoids a judgment binding the immune State's title. Whether that limitation preserves a real officer remedy despite the prayer's practical scope is the dispute; it cannot be resolved by pretending that the complaint was amended or that title has been proved.

### Paths, history and remedial limits

The reversal path requires the unusual combination of a demand effectively transferring all practical ownership/control and eliminating the State's sovereign regulation of navigable waters. Ordinary prospective relief involving land, substantial compliance costs, or an important governmental interest is not enough. Tindal's ordinary possession/title distinction and Treasure Salvors's officer-property reasoning do not, for these four, establish that the State can be stripped of this regulatory jurisdiction without an effective sovereign-title decree. Young remains valid, Pennhurst's state-law limit remains separate, and the narrow rule supplies no title merits holding.

The strongest objection is that every Young injunction displaces asserted official authority, sometimes very important authority, and the State cannot defeat a federal-right allegation by claiming property ownership. The affirmance four accept that objection: federal title may be a necessary premise of an officer judgment without binding the immune State. The Rule 12 posture and Ninth Circuit's limits warrant adjudication before relief is fixed. They do not approve every word of the prayer; relief remains confined to proved federal rights against officers actually connected to the alleged violation, with no retrospective treasury award or final quieting of the State's title.

The historical result and these eight judgment positions match, but the authority comparison must be accurate. Historical Kennedy Parts I, II-A and III had five direct joins. The narrow Part III rationale was an actual majority, not a Marks construction from the concurrence. Kennedy/Rehnquist's broader Parts II-B–D did not command those joins. O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas rejected that generalized approach; Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer rejected it too, while disagreeing on the narrow exception. This evidence confirms the present four-to-four narrow issue divide, not an automatic opinion assignment or present majority.

**Historical departure:** The historical Seminole premise barring Article I abrogation and rejecting the IGRA officer route is absent: actual Seminole permits that connected-officer route and leaves direct-State amenability without a controlling rationale. Kennedy's broader historical analogy from Seminole to discretionary special-factors balancing consequently cannot be reproduced as governing law. His independently supported functional sovereign-title ground remains available, as do O'Connor, Scalia and Thomas's narrower limits and Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer's opposing federal-officer ground. No Associate judgment change is required by this concrete legal difference.

A different, genuinely narrower officer claim could present another issue, but no amended/severed pleading is found. The separate United States action covers only part of the geography and is neither consolidated nor decided. Its presence supplies no completeness finding, sovereign waiver, exhaustion requirement or basis for treating the Tribe as without need for relief.

### Due-process paths and historical qualification

Foucha governs the end of a conceded illness predicate and the need for new lawful grounds/proceedings; it reserves materially narrower preventive regimes. Sahhar rejects a hypothetical sentence ceiling for independently lawful §4246 commitment while requiring current disease/defect, danger and effective safeguards. Neither holds that every legislative abnormality is sufficient or that conventional illness terminology is indispensable. Heller supplies no nationwide reasonable-doubt minimum. Kansas's own high initial burden does not cure a missing substantive limiting condition.

For the seven affirmative commitments, the causal condition does work beyond a prior offense and a risk prediction: recognized pedophilia, the evidence of serious impaired control, and the connection to dangerous conduct distinguish this record from ordinary recidivism or Foucha's ended predicate. The strongest objection is that the definition merely redescribes antisocial propensity. The answer is therefore limited to this application and evidence; no absolute inability under all circumstances, new jury instruction, or universal personality-disorder rule is prescribed.

Stevens, Souter and Breyer's historical due-process writing did not subscribe to all majority reasoning and expressly left the treatment due-process question to its Ex Post Facto analysis. Those qualifications remain. Ginsburg's historical omission of Part I is not an affirmative constitutional objection, but equally is not a join. Sahhar deals with independently lawful disease/defect custody rather than deciding this Kansas definition, and the new original-treatment precision affects a different issue. The proper reconciliation retains her reservation rather than manufacturing a changed premise.

### Punishment paths and original-record sufficiency

All use the governing distinction between civil legislative intent and a sufficiently strong showing of punitive purpose/effect; binding Ursery adopts the clearest-proof approach within its actual forfeiture field. Its application to this personal-confinement scheme requires analysis. Civil designation is rebuttable; ordinary protective confinement, an offense nexus, duration or criminal-style protections is not individually decisive. Actual Morales does not supply the historical general speculative-risk rule, and Lynce decided exhaustion, not withdrawn-credit merits.

The civil path relies on the narrow current-condition/risk class, SRS administration, segregated setting, care/treatment duty, original procurement and interim-professional evidence, and actual judicial/release mechanisms. Preventing harm from the qualifying condition is an independently civil purpose; difficult or unsuccessful treatment does not necessarily require release. This does not certify care was adequate or every later retention lawful. The strongest objection is that confinement began after the prison sentence while the specialist program was still prospective. The four reverse votes consider those deficiencies serious but insufficient as clearest proof of criminal purpose/effect when the full original evidence and independent preventive purpose are considered. They do not need later habeas findings or oral representations to fill a treatment gap.

The punitive path considers the cumulative original application: offense-linked selection and additional potentially indefinite confinement, restrictive setting, absent completed specialist contract/dedicated clinical program, and the October court's acceptance of future possible treatment while leaving commitment in force. Stevens's Allen and Ursery positions make personal punitive operation directly material; Souter's Foucha/Sahhar positions insist that actual current grounds and the character of confinement remain connected; Ginsburg's Ursery/Sahhar commitments require actual function rather than a label; Breyer's practical statutory analysis weighs the relationship of confinement, protection and treatment. Their historical dissent supplies direct evidence that original implementation and retrospective application could sustain this conclusion without a universal treatment requirement.

The contrary original evidence must be included in that assessment: Davis described procurement efforts and proposed interim arrangements; House described available Larned professionals, some relevant experience, individualized assessment/planning and the first-patient context; Hendricks described an offered prison program, pay choice and a qualification about parole-board involvement. No reason for failed contracting, effective therapy deliberately withheld, complete interim plan or actual post-transfer services is established. The four affirmance votes infer punitive operation from the combined established features and judicial acceptance of a future program, **not** from proof of no clinicians, deliberate deferral, contractual impossibility or complete absence of any treatment opportunity. Lay ignorance cannot prove a clinical plan did not exist elsewhere.

The historical dissent itself acknowledged earlier offered/taken/refused treatment at various points and cited House's original testimony while disputing the later record.
