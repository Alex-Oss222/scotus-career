## Decisional components and common limits

### A. Reachability and personal stake

All eight reach the appeal. The decree grants injunctive districting relief after a required three-judge proceeding. The supported resident-assignment relationship is sufficient under the actual Miller threshold holding and Thomas's unanimous Associate opinion in Vera. Lawyer contests the decree that governs his own district and the disposition of his own claims; he is not appealing merely as a stranger dissatisfied with a settlement. The continuing decree and requested relief supply a live controversy. Neither agreement that the original map should change nor receipt of some desired relief settles the lawfulness of the judicial means or disposes of a properly retained claim.

The rejection of a substantive racial-design theory does not itself eliminate Article III standing or make the controversy wholly insubstantial. Miller, Vera, Bell v. Hood and the limited Goosby rule distinguish those questions. Steel Co. requires attention to actual constitutional power, not conversion of every merits defect into a jurisdictional one. No jurisdictional dismissal is supported here.

### B. Consent, state choice and the nonconsenter

All eight accept these limited propositions from Firefighters, 478 U.S. 501, 525–531: a prior adjudication of liability is not invariably required for a consent decree; lawful consent can support obligations broader than relief obtainable after trial; withholding consent does not give one plaintiff a veto over the other parties' lawful compromise. The decree must nevertheless arise from a dispute within jurisdiction, remain within the pleadings' general scope, advance the objectives of the underlying law and remain lawful. Other parties cannot settle away a nonconsenter's properly raised valid claim or impose duties on him through their consent. The opportunity to object is relevant but does not independently supply authority to extinguish a claim.

The record supports state officials and both chambers acting through authorized litigation representatives. Their participation is a genuine state settlement choice, and Florida was not denied an opportunity merely because its political branches declined formal legislation. That is different from finding that Plan 386 became state law through a joint resolution or a superseding state judgment. Neither event is supplied. The March 19 order itself immediately substituted the six district descriptions. Growe's duty to respect an operative state judgment cannot be applied to a proposal as though the proposal were such a judgment.

No general holding that state officials can never settle districting litigation, or that every such settlement requires a legislative session, is supported. Conversely, litigation authority and a lawful map do not alone furnish the federal judicial basis for compulsory replacement of the operative plan.

### C. Why this approval cannot stand on its stated basis

The district court required a fairly contestable federal dispute, then identified that dispute by applying racial predominance and the related shape and composition inferences as the underlying constitutional wrong. The defendants made a settlement-only concession and expressly denied liability; the concurrence's contrary characterization is not a majority adjudication or an unconditional admission. The court's only accepted predicate thus uses the very substantive theory that current Shaw, Miller and Vera reject. It did not identify a distinct established wrong or explain an independently sufficient settlement basis under the governing rule.

Firefighters does not require a plaintiff to prove that the settled claim would win at trial. Nor does the fact that prospective terms exceed constitutional minima invalidate them. The defect here is more specific: the operative approval expressly depends on the legal sufficiency of a territorial-design theory foreclosed by the governing cases, and the supplied judgment does not identify a different preserved federal dispute supporting the judicial substitution. A reasonable compromise of contested facts, valuation or remedy is different from treating an unadopted legal rule as the rule already governing the court. The possibility of asking this Court to reconsider precedent is not an intervening holding on which the lower court could act.

The four Associates who joined the earlier design-only merits holdings support that analysis as their own substantive position. The four who dissented retain their objection to those holdings. They support vacatur of this approval at the narrower level that the court must identify and decide its legally available basis, distinguish the authorized compromise from a purported state enactment, and dispose of the nonconsenter's claims under the law it is bound to apply. They do not join an opinion announcing renewed personal endorsement of the substantive design-only boundary. Their present procedural vote does not adopt their proposed replacement rule as law or silently abandon it.

### D. The actual remaining allegations and the confined remand

An unconditional direction to dismiss every conceivable claim is not justified by the account of the record. Complaint paragraph 9 alleges that street-side boundaries prevented potential opponents from opposing the plan's author. Paragraphs 12–13 assert fragmented interests and deficient representation. Those allegations must be described accurately. Paragraph 9 is not simply erased as a racial-percentage allegation, but it also is not an established individual ballot-access, candidate-exclusion or partisan-dilution claim. The complaint identifies the plaintiffs as residents and registered voters; it does not identify a plaintiff as one of those excluded candidates. The final order addresses the racial-design theory and supplies no resolution of a separately preserved claim on that allegation.

The representation allegations, insofar as they infer incapacity to represent a shared constituency from its racial composition and geometry alone, are within the merits rule of Miller. Actual discriminatory treatment, if properly within this case, would have its own standards. No such treatment is found. The declarations describing effective representation cannot become a universal finding of equality either.

Polarization evidence is present. It concerns comparative electoral opportunity under the proposed remedy and alternatives and does not establish the Gingles prerequisites and totality inquiry for a Plan 330 claim. Bossier preserves independent §2 enforcement but neither creates such a claim here nor turns preclearance into an affirmative decree predicate. The 1992 election-administration mistakes were actual harms, including wrong elections and candidate-qualification problems. They are not merely hypothetical inconvenience; neither the source memorandum nor the final order, however, connects them to a preserved plaintiff's continuing federal claim that supports this decree.

The remand therefore concerns the existing case: identify whether any distinct claim was actually preserved within the complaint, operative pleadings and pretrial definition; determine its legal sufficiency, personal basis and relation to the relief, if it exists; and reconsider decree authority and the disposition of Lawyer's claims accordingly. The quoted pretrial insistence that only a Miller remedy remained bears on preservation but does not permit invention of the complete pretrial statement's contents. No new claim, amendment entitlement, proof failure, waiver or hearing is presumed. The remand neither orders a search for a different injury nor directs the parties to supply materials solely to fill an operator's archive gap.

If no legally sufficient, properly preserved basis supports this federal decree, the approval and dependent compulsory-redistricting relief must end, and the rejected design-only claims must receive their appropriate merits disposition. Other properly presented matters follow ordinary procedure. The court may not revive dismissed or abandoned claims merely because the rejected theory no longer works. Any separate state adoption must be established through its own lawful source; it is not inferred from the federal settlement.

### E. Plan 386, evidence and remedy

Vacating approval of the decree does not adjudge Plan 386 unconstitutional. The plan's shape, crossing of water or county boundaries, percentages, racial awareness and negotiation context do not themselves supply a recognized violation under the current rule. The accepted ordinary review rule applies to actual findings of districting purpose. The four earlier merits-majority Associates would reject a freestanding predominance-only invalidation; the four dissenters retain their different proposed scrutiny rule. No majority or individual commitment is inferred from historical Lawyer reasoning or from a historical Lawyer join.

The renewed decree-authority ground makes a conclusive appellate decision about Plan 386's predominance finding and the disputed limits on examining DOJ counsel unnecessary. The supplied declarations and hearing are not treated as nonexistent, and counsel's participation in settlement is not presumed to prove racial predominance. No Justice commits here to reversing a factual finding from shape alone, requiring examination of that attorney, finding a new injury, or certifying the plan under every constitutional and statutory provision.

**Formal provisional judgment:** All eight vote to vacate the March 19, 1996 approval and decree and remand for the confined current-law proceedings above. There is no instruction to find Plan 330 unconstitutional, no new map, no judicial racial target, no election cancellation, special election, deadline or special mandate date. Ordinary mandate procedure applies. The legal consequences of vacatur and any properly supported request concerning transition must be addressed under the actual operative instruments and applicable law, without inventing an election schedule or treating vacatur as an independent grant of continuing supervisory authority. No automatic stay, preservation order or restoration of an unverified later map is supplied. Fees and costs remain subject to their own lawful predicates; retained fee jurisdiction establishes no entitlement.

## Enhanced Justice-specific commitments

These are supported decisional conclusions and alternative-path assessments, not private conference dialogue or a transcript of deliberation. Each Justice's entries cover the same separable components A–E; a common judgment does not imply eight votes for every substantive proposition.

### Stevens

**Commitment:** Reach the appeal; vacate and remand as specified. Join the consent and nonconsenter rules, the distinction between state litigation choice and enactment, and the governing-law rejection of the stated design-only predicate. Do not direct a Plan 330 liability finding or a final finding that no other claim could remain. Do not adjudicate Plan 386 unconstitutional.

**Grounds and sources:** His join in Firefighters supports meaningful settlement latitude without a compulsory liability trial. His Shaw concurrence distinguishes reviewable arbitrary treatment from an inference of forbidden segregation drawn from attention to actual racial voting patterns; his Miller and Souter/Vera joins reject this territorial theory and its dependent relief. His unanimous Richards join preserves a claimant's actual rights despite others' aligned interests. His Bossier separate position recognizes effective statutory protection but expressly requires an independently established violation.

**Competing paths:** The strongest affirmance path invokes lawful voluntary inclusion, the State's unified participation, the benefits of a timely compromise, and relief broader than a trial judgment. Those considerations support settlement capacity, but none explains how this approval advances a preserved right under the rule Stevens actually joined once its sole stated injury is removed. The strongest direct-termination path is Vera; it controls the design-only claim, but its sole-adjudicated-claim record does not justify deleting paragraph 9 or conclusively interpreting an unreproduced operative pretrial definition here.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No categorical liability-finding prerequisite; no constitutional ban on voluntary racial awareness; no heightened pleading or inference that fragmented interests necessarily defeat representation. A confined remand for an actually preserved claim is permissible. A mandatory evidentiary expedition or invitation to invent a replacement claim is not.

### O'Connor

**Commitment:** Reach the appeal; vacate and remand on the limited approval and preservation ground. Join Firefighters's general settlement rule and substantive safeguards. Acknowledge Shaw/Miller/Vera as the law the lower court must use, while withholding renewed substantive assent to their racial-design boundary. No determination that either plan is unconstitutional.

**Grounds and sources:** Her own Firefighters concurrence, 478 U.S. at 530–531, makes both propositions material: consent avoids §706(g)'s trial-remedy limit but does not eliminate independent legal restrictions or affected persons' substantive claims. Her published Miller and Vera writings distinguish voluntary compliance latitude from the constitutional question posed by predominant racial assignment; they reject an exact-map judicial command as the necessary basis of every permissible state choice. Her Matsushita opinion and Richards join separately protect lawful authority to bind. Her Agostini join supports the Court's express program-specific change to precedent and does not authorize a lower court to anticipate that change.

**Competing paths:** Her strongest affirmance route is particularly substantial: a State may reasonably compromise a serious dispute without a finding of guilt, and Plan 386's legality need not depend on proof that federal law compelled it. The difficulty is the actual judicial instrument. It treats the officials' proposal as effective legislative redistricting and assesses the settlement's predicate through a rule contrary to governing law, without resolving the boundaries of Lawyer's retained claims. Her prior proposed predominance rule could make the original claim viable, but it has not been adopted; this approval cannot be validated by importing that proposal as existing law. Vacatur permits the actual legal basis to be assessed without turning every settlement into a trial.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** She will not join a rule that a prior violation must invariably be adjudicated, a rule forbidding all districting settlements outside a legislative session, or a substantive reendorsement of the design-only holding. She can join the narrower common explanation identifying the incorrect lower-court premise and requiring a lawful current basis. She retains practical latitude for otherwise valid voluntary state remedies and reserves the Plan 386 merits.

### Scalia

**Commitment:** Reach the appeal; vacate the judicial approval and remand at the confined scope. Join the distinction between settlement and enacted law, the limits on the nonconsenter's claims, and the requirement to use operative precedent. Do not personally endorse the design-only merits rule; do not supply an alternative dilution count or order a map.

**Grounds and sources:** His Growe opinion requires respect for actual timely state redistricting and an operative state judgment, not judicial preference dressed as state legislation. His Lujan and Steel Co. positions insist on the actual judicial task but do not make a failed merits claim jurisdictionally nonexistent. His joins in Miller's standing, permission, evidentiary and review portions, and Thomas's Vera threshold opinion, preserve those distinctions. His Miller/Vera separate positions maintain an individual-classification objection and separate statutory reservations; neither furnishes a new pleaded claim or authorizes a federal court to substitute a plan outside its lawful case.

**Competing paths:** Firefighters's consent breadth is a serious objection to treating the absence of an adjudicated violation as dispositive. Scalia accepts that no universal trial prerequisite follows. But it does not make litigation representatives into the constitutionally prescribed state redistricting body or supply an operative federal merits premise. Conversely, immediate dismissal of the entire action would require a conclusion about preserved distinct claims beyond the ground the lower court decided. Paragraph 9 is not enough to sustain the decree, but its legal and procedural treatment cannot be invented from missing portions of the record.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No broad federal power to impose a preferred consensual map merely because it is lawful. No automatic §2 theory derived from demographics, polarization or minority intervenors. The remand may resolve a claim genuinely already in the case; it may not solicit a new case. A properly supported merits disposition under current precedent can be entered without pretending Scalia has abandoned his published constitutional objection.

### Kennedy

**Commitment:** Reach the appeal; vacate and remand on lawful judicial authority, the status of the state proposal and the disposition of retained claims. Maintain the prior proposed predominant-assignment scrutiny rule, but do not import it as governing law or use it to find Plan 330 invalid without the required justification inquiry. Reserve the replacement plan's ultimate constitutional status.

**Grounds and sources:** His own published Miller and Vera dissents identify individual racial assignment as the reason to propose changing Shaw, while expressly recognizing that Shaw remained the law and allowing reasonable compliance choices without an exact federal map command. His joins in Growe protect the State's primary role; they do not convert the parties' proposal into a state enactment. His unanimous Plyler opinion distinguishes negotiated obligations from the underlying federal right and preserves actual legal predicates, without applying the PLRA to this litigation. His Richards and Matsushita joins protect adjudication of a person's retained claim rather than fictional consent.

**Competing paths:** Affirmance can draw force from the State's authentic wish to settle and the fact that an objector cannot privately control public litigation. That answers a veto claim, not the question whether the court may give this proposal compulsory force using a legal predicate the governing cases reject. His competing preferred constitutional rule could support a different assessment of the original claim, but this case's approval and preservation defects do not require its adoption. At the other extreme, an order to find Plan 330 unconstitutional would bypass the settlement's actual nonadmission and the majority's absence of such an adjudication.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** He will not join a finding that agreement itself proves a federal wrong, a universal requirement that lawful state consideration of race be compelled, or a new endorsement of Shaw's merits boundary. He can join the limited existing-law and record disposition. No new election timetable or independent claim follows from his alternative constitutional position.

### Souter

**Commitment:** Reach; vacate and remand. Join the existing-law rejection of the accepted design-only predicate, the consent rules and the confined preservation inquiry. The design-only basis cannot sustain compulsory relief; this record does not justify declaring every conceivable participation allegation extinguished. Do not decide Plan 386 unconstitutional.

**Grounds and sources:** His Shaw concurrence rejects the inference of legally segregated participation from a common constituency. His Miller joins preserve actual unequal treatment without demanding a later loss. His Vera merits opinion terminates relief dependent solely on a rejected theory, expressly distinguishing merits from jurisdiction and forbidding manufacture of another injury. His Richards join and Bossier separate position preserve genuine individual claims and claim-specific statutory proof. His Agostini dissent distinguishes the lawful operation of existing obligations from use of a Rule 60 appeal to create its own changed-law premise.

**Competing paths:** Consent is not merely a trial remedy in another form; the strongest affirmance argument correctly invokes obligations exceeding constitutional minima. It still must meet Firefighters's jurisdiction, pleading-scope and underlying-law requirements. A court cannot identify only a legally rejected racial-design injury and sustain its order by generalized approval of electoral fairness. Vera is the strongest case for an immediate merits end, but Lawyer's unadjudicated paragraph 9 allegation and the disposition of a nonsettler require examination of what this case actually preserved before treating the two records as identical.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No renewed finding of predominance is necessary to dispose of the legal theory. No universal downstream-loss requirement, no heightened pleading and no evidentiary search for an invented injury. A remand that first identifies the existing claim and applies its own rules, ending relief if none supports it, fits his commitments; an open-ended new-injury proceeding does not.

### Thomas

**Commitment:** Reach; vacate and remand at the same confined scope. Join the threshold and lawful-authority rules without endorsing the territorial-design merits boundary. Preserve his independent constitutional and statutory objections; neither becomes a new cause or a new remedy here. Do not infer that a consent decree can settle Lawyer's own retained claim without adjudication.

**Grounds and sources:** His own Vera threshold opinion, joined by all eight Associates, is especially decisive against converting the failed merits theory into lack of standing or an insubstantial appeal. His Growe join requires the statutory dilution predicates and respects actual state process. His Miller and Vera joins in Scalia's separate writings maintain his objection to governmental racial political allocation. His Bossier concurrence accepts a bounded remand under existing dilution precedent while retaining his statutory objection; it illustrates application of existing law without personal adoption of every underlying premise. His unanimous Plyler join distinguishes agreed terms from a self-created underlying federal right.

**Competing paths:** The best affirmance route relies on the State's choice and the absence of a valid veto by one plaintiff. Thomas accepts those limits on the appellant's theory, but consent does not make federal redistricting an independently authorized state legislative act or cure the lower court's use of a rejected substantive predicate. The strongest termination route would treat the case as exclusively design-only. The actual candidate allegation must first be given its supported procedural status; its presence authorizes neither a presumed candidate claim nor an order developing one.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No inferred right to proportional officeholding, no new §2 action from polarization alone, and no extension of the PLRA to districting. He will join a remand restricted to actual preserved matters and lawful termination if no basis remains. He will not join an unrestricted search for another constitutional wrong or an opinion portraying his prior racial-classification objection as abandoned.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment:** Reach; vacate and remand. Join the merits-rule application, settlement safeguards and distinction between legal representation and enactment. Resolve any actual preserved independent claim in the ordinary manner; do not make an appellate finding that such a claim exists or that it fails for want of an unrecovered document. Reserve Plan 386's comprehensive legality.

**Grounds and sources:** Her Miller and Vera joins distinguish personal exposure and substantive entitlement and reject the original design-only theory. Her Matsushita reservations and unanimous Richards join require constitutional authority over another person's claims rather than treating procedural opportunities as sufficient consent. Agostini preserves Rufo's distinction between voluntary obligations and an adjudicated constitutional injunction. Her dissent there insists on the legal conditions of the Rule 60 proceeding; it does not join the Court's opinion. Her Steel Co. concurrence favors deciding the actual authorization question at its proper scope rather than announcing an unnecessary larger constitutional rule. Her Bossier joins preserve separate statutory channels and the difference between relevant evidence and proved liability.

**Competing paths:** A settled defendant need not admit liability, and the breadth of a negotiated obligation does not by itself invalidate it. Those are the strongest reasons against an automatic reversal modeled on a contested remedial trial. They do not validate the present approval's expressly displaced premise or its treatment of the nonsettler. Conversely, refusing affirmance does not establish that no other preserved claim exists. The distinction between a deficient stated basis and an affirmative finding of absent claims warrants a confined remand.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No categorical jurisdictional dismissal, no conclusive waiver inferred from consent by others, no new state-law enactment and no guaranteed amendment or hearing. She can join a disposition that rejects the stated ground and leaves only properly preserved questions for ordinary resolution. An assertion that the settlement was necessarily collusive or that the State lacked all capacity to settle has no basis.

### Breyer

**Commitment:** Reach; vacate and remand. Join the existing-law application and the full consent qualification. Reject mandatory liability trials and needless repetition of evidence. The remand must do identifiable legal work: determine the preserved basis, if any, and whether it supports this judicial order. Do not invalidate Plan 386 on its geometry or displace genuine lawful state choice.

**Grounds and sources:** His Miller and Vera joins reject the territorial injury while preserving state inclusion and ordinary factual review. His own Bossier opinion separates statutory tasks, evidence and ultimate liability and orders a remand only for a consequential legal error; it supplies no original §2 claim here. His unanimous Plyler join respects negotiated protections but distinguishes their source and enforceability. Agostini retains the lawful voluntary-obligation qualification in Rufo; his dissent there rests on the Rule 60 vehicle and stare decisis, not a join in the Court's opinion. His Steel Co. position permits a narrowly justified easier resolution, but not assumed facts or an unresolved merits question masquerading as an easy one.

**Competing paths:** Breyer's strongest affirmance grounds are concrete: consent reduced litigation risk, protected electoral continuity, reflected the State's choice and offered a plan supported by evidence. Firefighters permits those benefits to accompany relief beyond a litigated award. Nevertheless, administrative usefulness cannot replace the legally sufficient dispute that the district court itself made the basis of approval. A remand simply to retry whether race predominated would waste proceedings because that fact alone supplies no current constitutional predicate. Immediate unconditional dismissal is likewise premature if it assumes the status of the actual candidate allegation and retained claims.

**Join barriers and permissible accommodation:** No obligatory new hearing, expert study, new map or inquiry into every possible injury. A court may decide the confined issue on the available record and ordinary submissions when legally sufficient. A valid later or independent state adoption must receive its proper effect if established, but practical preference cannot establish it. He joins no broad rule that every negotiated term must duplicate a constitutional command.
