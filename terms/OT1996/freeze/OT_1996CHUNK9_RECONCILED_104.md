## Common jurisdictional component

Under *Bowman* and *Idaho Power*, an untimely rehearing request postpones finality only if actually entertained; a merely filed or unentertained application for leave does not. The court requested a response, received it, recited the competing positions on *Sheets*, and said it considered and found the petitions and motions without merit. In this combination the denial of leave does not erase the actual substantive entertainment. The December 27 petition is therefore timely from December 16. Neither the mandate date nor §2106 independently extends the deadline.

No Justice relies on an unsupported independent recall-review doctrine or an invented extension.
