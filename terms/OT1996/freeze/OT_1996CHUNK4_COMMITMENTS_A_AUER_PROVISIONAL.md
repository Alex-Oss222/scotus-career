## Provisional judgment and components

All eight Associates support **vacating the final exemption judgment and remanding for bounded further proceedings**. They agree on the following legal components; vacatur does not imply rejection of the salary-basis rule or automatic overtime liability:

1. The supported statutory and institutional account does not make the St. Louis Board an arm of Missouri entitled to the State's federal-forum immunity in these employee actions. State creation, appointment and statutory employment regulation do not alone overcome the locally financed and locally budgeted operation and the absence of state operational control of the kind asserted in Hess. No finding of authorized waiver or Article I abrogation is necessary.
2. Congress's express delegation in §213(a)(1) permits the Secretary to use the salary-basis requirement for these public executive/administrative employees. The special public-sector hours and leave provisions do not repeal or create an unwritten disciplinary exception to that requirement. The presented public-safety policy objection does not establish the rule's invalidity.
3. An employee can be practically subject to impermissible reductions without having suffered a deduction personally. An actual practice or an effectively communicated policy creating a significant likelihood of such deductions can establish that exposure. A merely theoretical possibility in a manual covering both exempt and nonexempt employees does not suffice by itself. No required number of deductions, indispensable form of notice, or actual-deductions-only rule is adopted.
4. The correction provision's inadvertence and reasons-other-than-lack-of-work clauses are alternatives. The isolated intentional disciplinary deduction described here is eligible for correction; intentionality alone does not foreclose it. Eligibility does not satisfy the separate cumulative requirements of reimbursement and a promise of future compliance. A settled practice of impermissible reductions presents a different salary-basis question that cannot be erased by labeling every deduction a correctable isolated event.
5. The lower courts must apply that practical-exposure standard to the properly preserved record, resolve any material preservation/application dispute as necessary, and secure actual satisfaction of both correction conditions before entering a final judgment resting on restored exempt status. The uncontested duties and salary-amount predicates remain intact. No specific overtime amount or immediate entitlement for every claimant is determined.

## Enhanced comparison by issue

### A. Entity status, waiver and constitutional immunity

**Reach the FLSA issues on non-arm status.** The supplied account combines local salary/claim financing, certified city budgets and appropriations, municipal constraints on budget transfers and certain personnel additions, Board selection of its own officers, and the absence of an asserted state operational veto. Four gubernatorial appointees plus the mayor, state creation and statutory compensation limits are substantial state connections; they are not the equivalent of making every municipal agency a State. Canonical Hess requires the combined actual relationship. The packet's quoted trial description of municipal financing is admissible source support under the Engine's proposition rule, while the model makes no universal finding about every hypothetical payment source.

**Treat the Board as a state arm or remand status first.** The strongest contrary position rests on Missouri's appointment power, detailed legal regulation and the public-policing function; O'Connor's Hess dissent expressly rejects making immunity depend solely on who writes a damages check. The distinction here is institutional as well as financial: local operations and budget authority differ from the governors' and legislatures' retained control over PATH's projects, actions and spending. Applying Hess therefore does not require proof that state treasury liability is impossible under every imaginable circumstance. No material competing predicate is supplied that requires a threshold fact trial before applying the supported legal structure.

All eight reject an automatic waiver from litigation participation or a generic jurisdiction admission. Edelman permits a belated immunity contention; the actual trial motion preserved the Garcia coverage objection and did not itself assert an Eleventh Amendment theory. Neither point decides an authorized waiver. Ford requires the separate authority inquiry. Because the Board is not an arm on the supplied structure, those unresolved waiver sources are unnecessary. Seminole Tribe left Union Gas intact and supplied no majority State-amenability rationale; neither a universal Article I prohibition nor a universal abrogation permission is imported. Naming commissioners does not turn backpay into prospective officer relief.

### B. Validity and the basis of interpretive authority

**Sustain the salary criterion.** Section 213(a)(1) expressly delegates definition and delimitation of the exemption. Fixed compensation reasonably distinguishes salaried executive/administrative work from compensation varying with work quality or quantity. Congress's distinct §207(k) treatment of law-enforcement hours does not eliminate that delegated exemption requirement. The Department considered the different public-accountability absence setting and enacted §541.5d's particular exceptions; it did not thereby adopt a general exception for police discipline. The source also identifies the earlier proposed broader disciplinary allowance that was omitted from the final rule. The Court need not pretend these histories make every policy choice mandatory; they refute treating discipline as a subject inherently outside the delegated definition.

**Invalidate or refuse the rule's application.** The employer's strongest contention is that paramilitary discipline requires short suspensions and that the private-sector salary criterion is irrational for public safety. The regulatory distinction remains reasonable: an employer may discipline employees while retaining salary, use lawful full-week/no-work arrangements within the text, or accept the consequences for overtime exemption. The Court supplies no advisory approval of a particular alternative schedule or sanction. The major-safety exception has its own narrow terms; a residency rule is not transformed into a serious-danger rule merely because police work generally involves public safety. No finding about an APA §553(e) petition or failure to petition is needed, and no exhaustion/default rule is adopted.

**Agency interpretation versus factual application.** Canonical TJU, Seminole Rock and Tallman supply the own-regulation framework, bounded by contrary text, promulgation intent and the actual administrative position. Stinson confirms that interpretation cannot amend operative text but retains its distinct Sentencing Commission setting. Martin and Bowen require examining who speaks and the legal and administrative support, not assigning automatic authority to any litigation submission. The Department's explanation here addresses the practical meaning of its salary rule and the express disjunctive correction language, with the identified administrative letter/history supporting that interpretation. It does not supply adjudicated facts about this police department. The Court accepts those text-consistent legal constructions without deferring to an uninvestigated factual conclusion that every officer actually met them.

### C. Practical exposure and the individual record

The employees' actual-deductions-only objection is sound: salaried status concerns what the compensation arrangement exposes them to, not merely which risk has already materialized. The opposite rule, under which every abstract disciplinary option defeats salary status for everyone, disregards practical application of a manual covering different employee classes. Actual practice or a effectively communicated policy creating significant likelihood gives operative meaning to subject-to reduction while preserving both sides of the text. Neither a single exceptional negotiated event nor the absence of any personal deduction conclusively answers every employee's case.

The lower court's account supports treating Guzy's negotiated residency suspension as isolated. The later termination-only residency policy prevents repeating that particular sanction under that rule; it does not prove every other non-safety suspension authorization disappeared. Additional examples and employee-understanding evidence carry a preservation/application dispute. This Court should not resolve credibility or turn the source extractor's limits into forfeiture. On remand, the lower court need consider only material evidence properly available under ordinary preservation principles. The remand is not a direction to reopen the unchallenged duties element or to conduct new proceedings for their own sake.

### D. Correction, timing and actual relief

The regulation uses **or** for inadvertence/non-lack-of-work eligibility and **and** for reimbursement/promise. The employees' insistence that the intentional disciplinary choice must automatically close the window cannot be reconciled with that grammar. The employer's opposite claim that amending the single residency penalty itself completes correction omits two express conditions. Neither the text nor the supported administrative interpretation supplies an immediate-upon-discovery deadline; lack of such a deadline does not make repayment optional or permit final dismissal while a cure on which the exemption depends remains unperformed.

A mere promise to consider reimbursing is not reimbursement, and a changed particular disciplinary rule is not necessarily the required promise of future compliance with the salary rule. The lower court must assure both conditions in the proceedings implementing its judgment. If correction is not completed, it must decide the affected period, employees and resulting compensation issues under the ordinary exemption and overtime rules. No remedy reinstates the district court's $9,284 duties-based award automatically, predetermines a new amount, or assumes Guzy's situation establishes all other claimants' exposure.

## Operative exceptions retained

The full salary is ordinarily due for any week with work, but none is required for a week without work. Personal full-day absences under (a)(2), sickness/disability arrangements under (a)(3), jury/witness/military restrictions and specified offsets under (a)(4), and good-faith major-safety penalties under (a)(5) remain distinct. Sickness/disability treatment retains its bona fide-plan/insurance, before-qualification and exhausted-leave conditions. The safety category addresses serious danger to plant or other employees, not all discipline.

Section 541.5d(a)'s public-accountability partial-day exception retains its established pay-system requirement and the distinct unused-leave circumstances: permission not sought or denied, exhausted leave, or the employee's choice of unpaid leave. The budget-furlough rule in (b) preserves loss of salary treatment for the particular workweek with the furlough and reduced pay. Neither provides the general disciplinary exception sought. No unused exception is made dispositive from a paraphrase.
