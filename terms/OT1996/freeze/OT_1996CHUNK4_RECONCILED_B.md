## 46. Dunn v. Commodity Futures Trading Commission — February 25, 1997

**Reconciled result:** All eight reverse the statutory-coverage judgment and remand from the interlocutory receiver appeal. No final fraud finding, restitution, blanket dismissal of every conceivable claim or immunity from other law is entered.

**Common proposition:** Off-exchange options to buy or sell foreign currency are transactions in foreign currency within the Treasury Amendment's exclusion.
