## Exact propositions and limits

**V1 — Current-paper nonfrivolousness is distinct from extraordinary entitlement.** Under Rule 39.8 and Martin, the Court must assess the complete present paper; past unsuccessful filings do not establish its frivolousness. Vey's reopening attachment identifies a particular asserted conflict between her psychiatrist's statement and the prosecutor's contrary submission, alleged reliance on the latter without resolving the conflict, and alleged denial of access to the records. Those allegations supply an arguable access/reopening contention rather than only an assertion that her convictions were wrong. The supplied materials do not conclusively dispose of that contention or establish independent malice. The Court therefore should not deny financial leave under Rule 39.8 on this showing.

That conclusion does not establish falsification, withheld records, a constitutional violation or entitlement to reopening. It does not accept the claim that unchallenged evidence must be treated as conclusive. Her assertions of insanity, abuse-based justification, a firearm-law exception, medical conditions and tampering remain allegations; no unidentified statute or medical record is authenticated. Denton's distinction between clearly baseless allegations and ordinary disbelief informs the screening inquiry without importing its entire former §1915(d) regime into Rule 39.8. Unsupported or legally insufficient aspects of the requested relief do not automatically erase the distinct current contention under Jones, Shieh, Anderson and Gaydos.

**V2 — A future extraordinary-writ condition requires its own supported predicates.** A prospective fee-and-form condition must rest on applicant-specific abuse, a justified fit to the filing category restricted and a supported connection to the inadequacy of treating particular papers. Section 1651 aids existing jurisdiction and supplies no free-standing basis to demand dismissal of all charges.
