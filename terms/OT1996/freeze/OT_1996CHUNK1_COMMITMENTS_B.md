A completed summons-enforcement judgment is a final disposition of that proceeding, including its presently binding condition on circulating, copying or transferring the produced records to other IRS divisions. This uses ordinary finality, not an enlarged collateral-order exception or a general hardship right to interlocutory review.

Leave the validity of the condition, the scope of district-court enforcement powers, any particular future criminal use, and the ultimate summons merits to the Court of Appeals. Church of Scientology reserves those questions; Zolin's equal division supplies no controlling answer.

## 11. Humane Society v. Babbitt — December 9, 1996

### Proposed threshold judgment and components

Affirm the D.C. Circuit's direction to dismiss for want of Article III standing, on the independent causal and remedial defects in this challenge to the August 1991 ESA certificate amendment. The November 1990 transfer count was abandoned on appeal; the July 1991 CITES certificate was not challenged. Those distinct acts cannot be restored to the case through standing analysis.

**H1 — Injury question:** Actual impairment of an individual's concrete observation or study of a particular animal can be an aesthetic injury; species-wide depletion or extinction is not a prerequisite. But the hypothetical regular observer in Question 1 is not an established member in this record. Assume, without resolving, sufficient injury from Lota's absence at the Milwaukee Zoo; do not rest the judgment on a categorical inability to suffer injury from one animal's removal, a purchased-ticket rule, or general emotional disagreement with animal treatment.

**H2 — Traceability and redressability:** The challenged amendment concerns uses after Hawthorn acquired Lota. Loss of zoo access preceded it, and no supported causal mechanism links that later amendment to the continued loss rather than the independent donation. Invalidation leaves Hawthorn able to retain her in Illinois and supplies no demonstrated likelihood of return or other concrete improvement in an established observer's activity. Complete restoration is unnecessary, but some likely beneficial relief to the member is required. Third-party involvement alone is not a categorical bar; the actual chronology and available remedy defeat this claim.

**H3 — Procedural and associational grounds:** Assuming an enforceable conservation-permit procedure for analysis does not establish the required connection to a member's concrete interest or cure the causal/remedial defect. A plaintiff need not show that proper procedure certainly changes an agency's ultimate decision, but must establish a threatened personal interest protected by that process. Brown Group preserves the constitutional requirements for represented members; WARN's particular abrogation of a prudential limit supplies no ESA exemption. Do not decide whether section 1539 actually imposes the procedure asserted here.

No commercial-activity merits interpretation, ESA/CITES certificate cancellation, return order, mistreatment finding, damage award or Chaney holding follows. The lower court's statutory merits judgment remains vacated as part of its jurisdictional disposition.

## 12. Caterpillar Inc. v. Lewis — December 10, 1996

### Proposed judgment, rule and remedy

Reverse the Sixth Circuit's jurisdiction-based vacatur and state-court remand direction, and return the case to that court for the five unaddressed merits-appeal issues. Do not finally affirm the defense verdict against those challenges.

**C1 — Error and preservation:** Removal was unauthorized because complete diversity was absent when Caterpillar removed; Liberty's claim kept Whayne, the Kentucky defendant, in the case. Lewis timely sought remand and preserved that error. The district court should have remanded at that stage. Later events neither make that ruling correct nor create a waiver, and no requirement of an extraordinary pretrial petition is imposed on Lewis.

**C2 — Consequence after actual pretrial cure:** On the supplied premise that complete diversity genuinely existed before trial and through judgment after Whayne's dismissal, the earlier removal error does not require destroying the completed federal judgment solely to repeat the litigation in state court. Section 1332's original-jurisdiction requirements were satisfied when the merits were tried and decided. Finn's necessary jurisdictional barrier persisted through its judgment; here it did not. Grubbs establishes a final-judgment distinction in its no-objection setting; applying that distinction here is an express extension to a preserved removal error after actual pretrial cure, not an assertion that Grubbs already decided this precise case. Newman-Green confirms that party dismissal can cure statutory diversity, with its prejudice limits; this case needs no appellate dismissal or fictional creation of jurisdiction over a trial conducted without diversity. Existing subject-matter jurisdiction at trial/judgment and the final-judgment interest do the work, not consent or judicial economy alone.

**C3 — Limits and remand:** The result neither licenses courts to disregard section 1447(c) while a defect persists nor cures a judgment entered without subject-matter jurisdiction. It does not announce a universal cure for every removal defect or override section 1447(d)'s bar on review of actually entered covered remands. There is no established collusion, fraud or other manipulation finding to decide, and no sanction is imposed. The remaining discovery, sanction-request, excluded-exhibit, excluded-witness and failure-to-warn-instruction issues are for the Sixth Circuit. A jurisdictional decision does not establish their merits or insulate the verdict from ordinary appellate correction.

Each Justice accepts C1 and rejects a waiver rationale. The dispositive response is that the jurisdictional impediment ceased before the merits were adjudicated; preservation makes the old ruling reviewable but does not by itself dictate destruction of a judgment subsequently rendered with original jurisdiction. Finn's contrary outcome depended on the uncured trial/judgment defect. This reasoning must be stated as the present decision's limited extension, never attributed wholesale to Grubbs.

**Automatic cure whenever diversity exists on appeal:** Rejected. The adopted predicate is actual complete diversity before trial and at judgment, with no need for an appellate court to create it. Newman-Green has its own dispensability and prejudice discipline and cannot be turned into a universal relation-back device. Scalia and Kennedy's own prior dissent makes that limit a genuine join barrier, rather than a recital added for symmetry.

**Unqualified restoration of the verdict:** Rejected by all eight. The Sixth Circuit did not resolve the five other appellate issues, and this narrow grant supplies no basis for deciding them. C3 therefore returns the ordinary appeal and does not manufacture a discovery, sanctions, evidence or instruction ruling.

**Source limit:** The actual lower opinion and the adopted packet establish the timing and complete-diversity premise. No ruling resolves Lewis's separate partial-settlement assertion at removal because Liberty's surviving claim already defeated diversity. No collusion or prejudice finding beyond the supplied record is added. A materially disputed assertion that Whayne remained a jurisdictional party at trial would require correction of the neutral premise and affected-stage remodeling.
