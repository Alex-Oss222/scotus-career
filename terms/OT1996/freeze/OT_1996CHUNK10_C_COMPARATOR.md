## A. MERITS COMPARATOR

* **Historical Outcome and Vote:** No historical Supreme Court merits decision exists. The historical anchor is the divided Fourth Circuit judgment affirming summary judgment for Tulon.
* **Historical Opinion Structure:** Judge Motz wrote for herself and Judge Michael; Judge Niemeyer dissented.
* **Historical Holding:** The majority held that Chalmers gave inadequate advance notice and that the letters were not susceptible to accommodation. The dissent would have remanded for trial because the prima facie and hardship issues were disputed.
