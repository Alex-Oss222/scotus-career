## Actual ProCD law now entering

Justice Ginsburg's opinion, directly joined by all eight other Justices, establishes assent under Wisconsin UCC §2-204 on this communicated offer and review/use/refund sequence. Reasonable advance notice, full terms before meaningful use, practical rejection and refund, and objective assent matter. The Court neither treats payment as necessarily completing every retail bargain nor permits unilateral alteration of an already completed agreement. Acceptance of goods remains distinct from assent to terms; materially different exchanged forms and other transactions remain outside the ruling. Genuinely preserved ordinary defenses survive, but no developed defense warrants reopening merely to search for one.

The decision separately construes §301: fixed factual compilations can fall within copyright subject matter despite lack of protection, while the genuine party-specific promise here changes the right asserted and is not equivalent to an exclusive copyright right against nonassenting persons. A contract label does not create categorical immunity. The Court preserves Feist's factual-public-domain rule and the independent scope of Wolens and Cipollone. It supplies no public entitlement over facts, no universal license rule and no answer to an independent federal prohibition of a materially different covenant. Relief remains confined to the proved undertaking and parties.
