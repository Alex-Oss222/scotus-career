### Protection against serious avoidable harm in compulsory custody transitions

**Governing question:** The manner of state-compelled severance of an established caregiving relationship to implement otherwise lawful custody entitlement.

**Current rule:** The child has an independent liberty interest against serious, avoidable injury attributable to the manner of compulsory implementation. Mathews requires attention to the identified private interest, error risk and value of further procedures, and governmental interests and burdens. Procedures and protective measures must fit the actual showing, proceed promptly and preserve lawful parental custody entitlement.
