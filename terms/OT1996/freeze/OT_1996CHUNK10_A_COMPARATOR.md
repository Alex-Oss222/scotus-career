# 109. Bracy v. Gramley

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Chief Justice Rehnquist delivered the Opinion of the Court for a unanimous Court.
- **Historical Holding:** Bracy's specific allegations, combined with the established corruption record, showed good cause for discovery into actual bias. The district court retained discretion over the scope of discovery.

# 110. Gilbert v. Homar

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL COMPARATOR

- **Historical Outcome and Vote:** Reversed and remanded, 9-0.
- **Historical Opinion Structure:** Justice Scalia delivered the Opinion of the Court for a unanimous Court.
- **Historical Holding:** The arrest and formal felony charges supplied an objective basis for immediate temporary suspension of a police officer without a prior hearing. The lower court had to determine whether the post-suspension opportunity to respond was sufficiently prompt.
- **Historical Authority:** Opinion of the Court.
- **Historical Sources:** Gilbert v. Homar, 520 U.S. 924 (1997).

- **Official comparator source:** [U.S. Reports](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/520bv.pdf#page=1019).
