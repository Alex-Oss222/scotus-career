### 126. Alaska

The normal coastline/claimed historic inland waters, Dinkum Sands classification, Petroleum Reserve reservation, and Arctic Range reservation are four separable issues. Their instruments and governing legal predicates must remain separate; a general reservation purpose cannot replace the necessary intent inquiry. Geographic classification, legal title, and a later implementing decree are also distinct.

### 128. Cohen

The reviewed First Circuit decision is 101 F.3d 155, decided November 21, 1996 after argument April 1, 1996. The liability decision and remedial disposition are separable: liability was affirmed, specific relief requiring elevation of four named women's teams was vacated, and the case was remanded for reconsideration of remedy. The continuing preliminary relief must not be collapsed into the vacated final team-selection order. The four teams in the specific order were women's gymnastics, fencing, skiing, and water polo; the original demotions concerned women's gymnastics and volleyball and men's golf and water polo.

One chronology clarification is required: March 29, 1995 is the District Court's merits opinion, not the eventual final judgment. The appellate opinion records the May 4 modified compliance-plan order, the August 17 specific-relief order, final judgment on September 1, 1995, and denial of the additional-findings/amendment motion on September 27, 1995. These are lower-court events and do not add a Court action to the simulation calendar.

The First Circuit treated prior legal rulings as law of the case while reviewing final liability and remedy; that is its procedural reasoning, not a restriction on this Court's granted questions. Brown's status as a private institution and recipient of federal funds must be kept distinct from the governmental acts challenged in its constitutional theory.

The primary appellate opinion was checked at [OpenJurist](https://openjurist.org/101/f3d/155/cohen-v-brown-university), particularly paragraphs 11–23 for posture and facts. Independent original-source support is recovered in [the archived petition packet](https://archive.org/details/micro_IA40386013_0293). The complete separate appendix is saved as `terms/OT1996/research/chunk11_b/cohen/micro_IA40386013_0293 02. Appendix.pdf` and its `_djvu.txt` derivative; petition and opposition are also saved. This appendix, rather than the historical petition-stage disposition, is the source route for the lower opinions. The historical petition number 96-1321, appearing on the filing, does not create a new simulation merits docket number. The supplied May 19 and June 23 dates remain the authorized scheduling premises.
