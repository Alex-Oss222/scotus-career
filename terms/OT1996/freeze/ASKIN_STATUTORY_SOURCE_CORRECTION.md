## Exact source and recovered qualification

The official [1994 United States Code, title 18, section 2510](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-1994-title18/pdf/USCODE-1994-title18-partI-chap119-sec2510.pdf), printed page 441, defines oral communication with the exhibited and justified expectation requirement and also excludes electronic communication. Printed page 443's 1986 amendment note identifies that concluding exclusion as the addition made by Public Law 99-508 section 101(a)(2). It therefore predates the interception presented here. The 1994 main text must not be mistaken for the unamended wire/electronic definitions at interception: it already incorporates the later cordless amendment.

The official [Public Law 103-414](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-108/pdf/STATUTE-108-Pg4279.pdf), section 202(a), printed page 4290, deletes the cordless-radio exclusion from section 2510(1) and deletes former section 2510(12)(A), redesignating the remaining subparagraphs. It does not amend section 2510(2). The Code's printed pages 442–443 reproduce the deleted text and 1986 amendment history. The four-year delay in this Act's section 111(b) names only sections 103 and 105 of Title I; section 202 is in Title II. No conclusion about retrospective application or later use is supplied by this source receipt.
