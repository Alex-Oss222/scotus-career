INTRODUCTION 


1. This Complaint (i) alleges, inter alia, violations 
under (a) The Organized Crime Control Act of 1970, Pub. 
L. No. 91-452, Section 901(a), 84 Stat. 941, Racketeer Influ- 
enced and Corrupt Organizations (“RICO”) and (b) The 
Nevada Racketeering Act, Nevada Revised Statutes 
207.350, et seq (“Nevada RICO”), and (ii) is brought by 
Plaintiffs Tom Grimmett as Trustee for the Bankruptcy 
Estate of Vincent Siragusa and Joanne M. Siragusa in 
connection with multiple schemes knowingly, inten- 
tionally, willfully and unlawfully devised, conspired, 
conducted, implementea and/or participated in by, 
among others, Defendants Patricia L. Brown, Patricia L. 
Brown, Ltd., and Beckley, Singleton, Delanoy, Jemison & 
List, Chtd. and Participants Vincent Siragusa; Vincent 
Siragusa, M.D.; Ltd.; V. Siragusa, M.D., Chartered; Paul 
Heeren; Paul Heeren, M.D.; Ltd., John A. Bowers; Cardi- 
ology Associates of Nevada, John A. Bowers, M.D., Ltd.; 
Heart Institute of Nevada; Heart Institute Cath Lab; and 
Heart Institute Property, each of whom (c) through a 
pattern of racketeering activity acquired and/or main- 
tained, directly or indirectly, an interest in or control of 
the Heart Institute of Nevada, the Heart Institute Cath 
Lab, and Heart Institute Property (collectively the “Medi-
