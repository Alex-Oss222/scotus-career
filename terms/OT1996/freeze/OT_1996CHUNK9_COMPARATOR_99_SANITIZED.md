# 99. Kinman v. Omaha Public School District

# SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

## A. HISTORICAL LOWER-COURT COMPARATOR

The Eighth Circuit allowed Title IX proceedings under a knew-or-should-have-known standard drawn from Title VII, found disputed welcome and institutional response, and rejected a same-sex exclusion. It affirmed the §1983 judgments because the officials investigated and the record did not show deliberate indifference or a widespread District practice. It thus used different standards for statutory and constitutional responsibility. It does not establish a completed simulated Supreme Court outcome or supply governing post-divergence Supreme Court law.
