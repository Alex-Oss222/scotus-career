## The controlling law actually added

All eight Associates join Auer's controlling-weight rule: the responsible Secretary's fair and considered interpretation of the salary regulation controls unless plainly erroneous or inconsistent with the regulation. The amicus form alone neither defeats that weight nor establishes authority; an agency lawyer cannot amend the text, exceed lawful authority or establish disputed employer facts through a legal submission. Courts still test the regulatory reading against text, consistency and the agency's actual interpretive responsibility. The Court preserves Thomas Jefferson University, Seminole Rock, Stinson, Martin and Bowen within those bounds.

The Chief Justice's proposal for independent judicial interpretation plus special persuasive weight receives no Associate join and is noncontrolling. No later commitment is revalidated by treating that proposal as governing law or as an Associate's position.

The unanimous portions validate the salary-basis rule within the express statutory delegation, distinguish an actual practice or effective particular policy from theoretical exposure, and enforce both actual reimbursement and a future-compliance promise before the employer may rely on correction. Eligibility for correction is not completed correction. The Board's non-arm status rests on its particular combined local relationship and does not determine other entities' status. These holdings do not add a universal deference doctrine for statutory exclusions, a presumption that all agency factual assertions are true, or a general requirement to reopen evidence after every legal ruling.

## Dunn: own-regulation interpretation does not enlarge statutory coverage

The strongest agency-side implication of Auer is substantial but bounded: the CFTC may invoke the appropriate interpretive authority when it actually interprets its own trade-option regulation, and a court may not reject that authority merely because the interpretation appears in litigation. The frozen Dunn ground does not require rejecting such an interpretation. It keeps the agency's representation that interbank options qualify under its regulatory exemption intact for the limited argument made, without adjudicating every commercial-actor or business-purpose condition.

The granted Dunn issue is the Treasury Amendment's statutory category and exception. The independent question is whether off-exchange currency options are transactions in foreign currency within the enacted exclusion. Construing a regulation that exempts some transactions from rules otherwise within agency authority cannot turn that regulation into the source of statutory authority over transactions Congress excluded. Auer expressly retains lawful-delegation and text limits, and its salary-rule validity holding rests on Congress's express power to define and delimit that different exemption. No comparable power to alter the Treasury Amendment is supplied by the new decision.

Thus all eight retain the ordinary statutory text-and-context ground: options to buy or sell foreign currency fall within the broad transaction category; the exception operates only when its future-delivery and board-of-trade conditions are satisfied. No bank-only restriction is added. The relevant regulatory trade-option exemption remains distinct rather than being treated as invalid, irrelevant to every issue, or proof that the CFTC claims authority over all interbank options. Fraud allegations and enforcement need cannot substitute for statutory coverage. This is compatible with Auer's controlling rule, without adopting the Chief Justice's noncontrolling replacement or a general anti-agency presumption.

The remedy remains reversal/remand of the reviewed temporary-receiver authority premise. The receiver cannot rest on the rejected statutory coverage ground; any genuinely remaining ground must satisfy its own law. There is no final fraud adjudication, restitution calculation, invented direct sale of an option to investors, automatic resolution of every claim, or new factfinding order. Auer's correction-dependent affirmance supplies no authority to manufacture a curable defect or an additional remand condition in Dunn.
