## Threshold component: reachability of the August judgment

All eight Associates accept jurisdiction over the underlying judgment on this record. They apply the existing actual-entertainment doctrine of Bowman, 311 U.S. at 266, and Idaho Power, 344 U.S. at 21 n.1, rather than import later Rule 13 language or invent an extension. The decisive combination is the court's request for an adversarial response to the September rehearing petition, its recital of the competing positions on the effect of Sheets, and its statement that it considered and found the requests without merit. The later denial of leave is part of that composite disposition; it does not conclusively establish that the merits were never entertained. This is a record-specific legal determination, not a rule that filing any second motion restarts the clock. With actual entertainment, the December 27 petition is timely from December 16.

The strongest contrary path applies Bowman's warning about denied leave literally: no formal allowance of a second rehearing appears, the December order can be read as declining reopening, and the ordinary deadline from September 3 expired December 2. That path would bar direct review absent an unestablished valid extension and would require a distinct, supported recall-review basis for any relief. The Associates reject it here because the supported adversarial treatment and merits consideration supply more than the mere unentertained filing Bowman excludes. They do not derive jurisdiction from §2106. No Justice relies exclusively on an independent recall route, and no indefinite power to revive expired review is adopted.

Actual entertainment for finality means the court considered the merits of the renewed request. It is not consistent with a claim that Sheets was never presented or wholly unconsidered. The separate remedial dispute is whether the existing unexplained denial, against the still-articulated August rationale, leaves a sufficiently probable unresolved error about the new state-law rule to justify this limited remedy. The commitments below preserve that distinction.

## Remedial component and common law

Lawrence's controlling reasonable-probability and independent-equities rule is the starting point for every Associate. LAVAS demonstrates all eight Associates' willingness to return a case for a legally cognizable, material development, without deciding the development's ultimate effect. It does not answer whether a pre-judgment authority already presented on rehearing warrants a second return. No Associate adopts the Chief's noncontrolling reasonable-possibility standard.

The state-law probability analysis concerns the legal meaning of accident. Sheets's majority expressly disapproved the natural-and-ordinary-consequences rule used in the August opinion and identified I.A. Construction's reliance on it. Its duty-to-defend disposition does not restrict every part of its legal definition to defense cases. Conversely, Sheets did not decide actual indemnity, and neither absence of a negligence cause of action nor a verdict under issue 5 resolves the insured's expectation of the physical harm. The itemized statutory qualifications and separate policy issues remain operative.
