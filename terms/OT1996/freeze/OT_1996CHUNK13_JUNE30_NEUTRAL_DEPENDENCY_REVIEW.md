## Printz's actual addition and authority limits

The final delta's Printz **jurisdiction holding** has all nine Justices: a command assigning concrete work to these officers presents a redressable official injury, without standing to obtain an advisory ruling on independent duties of absent dealers or purchasers. It applies Raines's explicit distinction between concrete official duties and unauthorized legislators' abstract institutional dilution.

Printz **Part I — Appropriate temporary executive records assistance is not categorically forbidden** is a five-Justice Court holding: Souter, Stone-Zsela, Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer. When implementing otherwise valid direct regulation of private transactions, Congress is not categorically barred from requiring appropriate, temporary records assistance within the state executive officer's proper function under affirmative statutory direction, with the federal transaction rule and federal responsibility for coercive enforcement. The rule does not finally sustain the actual compulsory arrangement; the existing fiscal and unauthorized-expenditure objections remain open. It does not establish a cheap-command exception, permanent records power, state-law veto or payment entitlement.

Printz **Part II — The limited mandate binds without a majority fiscal rationale** rests on a different five-Justice judgment coalition: O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas. The mandate vacates unconditional appellate sustainment and returns the existing fiscal objections for confined consideration under Part I. Souter alone supplies the affirmative fair-value rationale. The other four reject Part I but accept that disposition under its independently controlling rule. Their judgment votes add no Part I joins; conversely, Part I's other four supporters would affirm. There is **no majority compensation holding and no Marks synthesis**. No monetary cause, award, measure of compensation, taking, state-law liability or automatic hearing is established.

These separate propositions and coalitions must remain distinct in any June 30 use. The structural dissent, the opinions favoring unconditional affirmance and the fair-value view remain published noncontrolling positions at their stated additional scope.
