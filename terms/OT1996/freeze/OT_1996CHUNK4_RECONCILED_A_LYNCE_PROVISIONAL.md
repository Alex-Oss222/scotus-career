## Proposition-level commitments

**Common direct-credit rule — all eight Associates.** Florida cannot escape the Ex Post Facto Clause by calling an overcrowding-credit system administrative. Retrospective cancellation of the already awarded and used credits implementing the legally available offense-date overcrowding-release framework increases punishment by restoring custody; that core cancellation violates the Clause. Credits attributable only to a later expansion beyond that framework are not automatically protected. The lower courts must determine the protected portion and lawful custody under the correct rule. Actual rearrest and added imprisonment supply the concrete punitive effect; no vested-right formula, plea-bargain expectation finding or right to every later benefit is adopted.

**Opportunity and Weaver explanation — Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.** These six also support explaining the direct-credit result through Weaver's protection of a legally available route to reduced confinement, the operation of the release provisions and the fact that statutory credits determine the effective term. The motivation for creating the original credit program does not change that consequence. Existing simulated Morales and Cavanaugh govern at their recorded scope; their opportunity protection does not require proof that a denied discretionary release would certainly have occurred. No new holding about all future credits or all procedural disadvantages follows.

**Scalia and Thomas's exact reservation.** Both support the common direct-credit rule, statutory-continuity conclusion at the core level, reversal and bounded remand. They do not join an unnecessary discussion extending this decision's rationale to prospective acquisition of future good-conduct credits. Their historical partial join and Thomas's concurrence provide strong direct evidence on that scope, and their actual simulated Morales dissent remains compatible with it. Collins v. Youngblood's punishment boundary and the concrete cancellation of credits already earned and used suffice. A draft limited to that direct-credit ground can receive their full join; a severable Weaver/future-opportunity discussion receives only the six Associates listed above. No author or separate-writing form is preassigned.

It does not automatically restore all 1,860 provisional days, reinstate the separately unchallenged 335 administrative days, undo unrelated forfeitures or prescribe an immediate release date.
