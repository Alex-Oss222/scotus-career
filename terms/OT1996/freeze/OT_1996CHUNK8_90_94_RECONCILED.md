### Breyer's separate-writing substance for assembly

Breyer agrees that the Constitution creates no automatic immunity for private conduct and that this President must explain the actual need for postponement. The absence of that explanation makes the categorical trial stay premature. He does not decide that trial could never seriously interfere with presidential work.

His constitutional ground is Article II's concentration of the entire executive responsibility in one nationally elected individual.
