## Statutory premises that must accompany the application

The full validated addendum supplies the operative qualifications. The following distinctions are material to any explanation relying on limited capacity, duplication, eligibility or burden; they are descriptions of enacted duties, not new freestanding constitutional holdings.

| Provision | Exact premise and consequence for the explanation |
|---|---|
| Commercial capacity, §534(b)(1) | Systems with 12 or fewer usable activated channels carry at least three local commercial stations. Only systems with 300 or fewer subscribers that do not delete any broadcast signal receive the stated exemption, and that exemption concerns §534. Systems with more than 12 channels carry eligible commercial stations up to one-third of aggregate usable activated channels. Do not say all systems of 12 or fewer channels are exempt, or that one-third caps combined commercial and educational obligations. |
| Educational capacity, §535(a)–(c), (e) | Educational duties are additional. The general requesting-qualified-local-station duty has separate small- and intermediate-system rules. Systems of 12 or fewer channels carry one, with the statutory importation rule where no qualified local station is present; the protected March 29, 1990 programming need not be removed for that importation, but the first available channel must be used. Systems of 13–36 channels carry at least one and need not carry more than three, subject to continued carriage; additional carriage is permitted. Above 36 channels, the additional-station duplication rule is not a general three-station cap. Continued carriage of every qualified local educational station carried on March 29, 1990 remains required, with waiver requiring both operator and station written consent. |
| Selection and duplication | Commercial selection among excess eligible stations cannot substitute low power for a local commercial station and has the closest-headend network-affiliate condition. Commercial duplication and multiple-network-affiliate carriage may be omitted on their own terms; voluntary duplicates count. Educational same-State-network duplication on 13–36-channel systems and the additional-station rule above 36 are different provisions. Their permissions do not prohibit carriage and cannot be exchanged for one another. |
| Signal, copyright and carriage burden | Eligibility and good-quality delivery conditions remain operative. Educational distant-signal indemnity applies to adding a signal not already carried under §535(c), rather than authorizing deletion of continued-carriage stations. Required primary video, audio, captions, program-related material, placement, availability and technical-quality terms make carriage an actual programming obligation, not reservation of empty capacity. Commercial and educational payment, placement, notice and enforcement exceptions differ. Preserve the addendum's terms whenever a rationale invokes them. |
| Low power and market modification | Low-power carriage depends on the full-power shortfall and separate statutory qualifications; the one-station/two-station split uses the 35-channel boundary. Its programming criterion includes local needs inadequately served because of full-power stations' geographic distance. Market modification is a separate discretionary community inclusion/exclusion process with localism factors. Its disputed factor asks whether another eligible station supplies the listed community news, sports or events; it does not impose that programming duty on the station requesting modification. |

Neither the historical Court's loose small-system description nor lower-court or advocacy shorthand can displace the enacted qualifications. The general model's assertion that the Act has limits is supportable only with these terms. The 1.2 percent statistic still concerns channels occupied by added must-carry stations, not the total legal restriction, all foregone editorial decisions or the combined statutory capacity ceiling. The affirming four weigh the enacted burdens and contrary evidence; they do not make the incremental statistic decisive by itself. The reversing four may rely on the actual breadth without demanding certainty, industry collapse or the least restrictive imaginable alternative.

Section 534's February 8, 1996 market-definition and decision-deadline amendments apply as specified to pending and later requests. Do not substitute the superseded market definition or general expedited-consideration wording. The sales-presentation and advanced-television clauses depend on their own proceeding or triggering facts; the bare statute proves no such facts here. These limits do not produce additional adjudicated components.
