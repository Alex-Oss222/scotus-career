The limited-carriage framework is confirmed, with qualifications necessary to assess its actual operation. Commercial carriage on systems of 12 or fewer channels is **at least three**, and the 300-subscriber exemption is conditional on **no broadcast-signal deletions**. Educational duties are additional, with separate one-station, 13–36-channel, and over-36-channel rules. The March 29, 1990 continued-carriage rule requires both parties' written consent to waiver. Duplication provisions permit omission on different conditions rather than forbid carriage. The addendum also preserves qualification, signal quality, copyright, placement, payment, notice, and remedy differences.

One material precision point requires attention if the reserved market component is reached. Section 534(h)(1)(C)(ii)(III) concerns whether **another eligible station** supplies the listed community programming. It is not a requirement that the station seeking market modification itself broadcast that programming, and it is distinct from §534(h)(2)(B)'s low-power programming condition. The clean packet attributes earlier descriptions to the lower opinion and party submissions; those source accounts cannot replace the enacted text. The 1996 market-definition and 120-day decision amendments also govern the temporal statement, including pending requests as specified by the note.
