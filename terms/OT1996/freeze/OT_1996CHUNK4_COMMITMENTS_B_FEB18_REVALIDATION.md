## Material effect of the new law

**Uhlrig to Brown.** Uhlrig's eight-Associate Court opinion resolves only individual qualified immunity at the time of the administrators' acts. It expressly leaves constitutional culpability, the affirmative-danger merits and the lower five-part test undecided. Its acceptance of the estate's supported favorable account is not a finding of reasonable care, no awareness, no causation or waiver. It preserves Collins's limited no-general-workplace-duty rule without extending that rule to all affirmative governmental conduct. The Chief Justice's proposed merits framework and application are a sole noncontrolling concurrence; none of the Associates adopted them.

Brown accordingly receives no new controlling merits test, compulsory-schooling immunity, requirement of a personal assurance, or automatic immunity disposition. Its complaint alleges direct compulsory exposure by school actors, not merely the workplace/private-violence chain considered in Uhlrig. The frozen analysis continues to acknowledge compulsory attendance and the alleged aftermath, to examine the claimed deprivation directly, and to reject the specific parental-liberty and personal-privacy claims on their own bounded grounds. Nor may the February 18 decision establish conduct-time notice for the earlier assembly. If a different scope later requires Brown's immunity question, the actual actor, conduct date, claim and notice law would need their own analysis; this addendum supplies no such holding.

**Other February 18 decisions.** Robinson's context-sensitive construction of a statutory category supports reading a term in its actual provision; it does not create a universal broad-reading rule or decide Title IX discrimination, currency-option coverage or criminal materiality. Ingalls keeps substantive entitlement, party capacity and litigation posture distinct and does not decide state-arm immunity or CFTC statutory power. Legi-Tech permits actual lawful reconsideration where the agency already has available authority; it expressly cannot cure missing capacity, evade an expired deadline or replace an unperformed mandatory opportunity. It gives Dunn no agency power over statutorily excluded transactions.

Dillingham distinguishes economic influence from a direct or demonstrated effective plan command within ERISA. Its approval of a wage application is no general rule validating coercion, school programs or speech injunctions. Tracy's specific Commerce Clause comparison and separate rational-basis holding neither supply a police-safety standard nor lower scrutiny of speech or personal liberty. Bibles addresses the proper statutory disclosure interest and leaves the actual Exemption 6 balance open; it does not create a constitutional listener right or authorize an injunction. Its undisclosed Associate roll call supplies no individual vote evidence. Brockamp's incapacity ruling rests on the particular integrated tax scheme and preserves the separate unreviewed deposit alternative. It neither creates a universal immunity or jurisdiction rule nor abolishes statutory or equitable remedial authority in other settings.

These decisions' careful separation of an appellate legal correction from unreviewed merits is consistent with the frozen remedies. That consistency is corroboration, not a new freestanding doctrine displacing the actual source of each remand or question-presentation limit.
