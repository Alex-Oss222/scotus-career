## What the proposed mandate actually does

The relevant judgment is the January 3, 1996 Eleventh Circuit panel judgment. Vacatur is confined to its disposition of the Espinosa claim. It removes that adverse appellate disposition and returns the claim for the remaining lawful inquiries. It does not itself vacate the state death sentences, grant a writ, or disturb unrelated guilt or habeas determinations.

The old-rule direction is conclusive on remand: this claim does not request a new rule at November 24, 1986 finality, and the lower court may not again reject it on that ground. The statement is an adjudicated legal instruction in the proposed disposition, not a suggestion to reconsider Teague, an unresolved question, or an assumption made solely to discuss default. Thus the proposal supplies the operative rejection of the appellate Teague ground that the reversing Associates require. If the unresolved state ground or another genuinely applicable gate ultimately defeats relief, that outcome follows from the separate gate; it does not undo or dilute the old-rule determination.

The rule being classified is the same rule classified in the final freeze. Where Florida law gives the capital jury's recommendation a legally consequential contribution to weighing, inadequate guidance of that contribution can infect the final death sentence despite the judge's independent duty. Godfrey supplies the federal guidance principle. The state decisions establish the contribution to which that principle applies. They do not themselves create the federal rule, establish an actual constitutional violation here, or require identical power or weight for life and death recommendations. No narrower rule is derived from substituting the word “vacated” for “reversed.”

The proposed direction that default be resolved first on remand governs the remaining inquiries in this case. It is separate from the decision to reach Teague now. It neither announces a universal mandatory order for all nonjurisdictional habeas defenses nor finds that this Court was obliged to decide Teague first. On remand, the preserved default defense requires resolution of the independent state ground, its adequacy when compliance was required, any genuinely unresolved preservation issue, and any applicable cause-and-prejudice or miscarriage-of-justice exception. A barred and unexcused claim receives no relief. A claim that remains available proceeds to its actual merits, effect and lawful correction, subject to genuinely applicable unresolved statutory conditions before an operative writ.

The lower court's assumption of inadequate jury guidance for its retroactivity analysis remains an assumption, not a finding of factor-specific vagueness, juror reliance, harmful transmission or failed cure. The supplied HAC and cold/calculated/premeditated instruction issues remain within the supplied claim; the change in mandate wording silently deletes neither. If an available claim establishes a violation, existing Espinosa/Stringer correction requires genuine authorized reweighing or Chapman harmlessness beyond a reasonable doubt addressing transmitted influence. Remaining valid aggravators, bare proportionality review or an independent-judge label alone does not establish either cure. If no lawful cure is available and relief is otherwise warranted, the existing law requires a constitutionally adequate new sentencing proceeding. None is presently ordered.

Cause and prejudice remain distinct from Sawyer's death-eligibility gateway, which requires clear and convincing evidence that no reasonable juror would find death eligibility absent constitutional error. A selection error alone does not establish that showing. No AEDPA transition, amended §2254(d) predicate, chapter 154 qualification, exhaustion waiver or statutory exception is presumed. No future Lindh rule is imported. No new evidentiary hearing follows merely from missing research material. There is no present conditional writ, reinstated deadline, new penalty proceeding, life sentence, release, stay, or execution authorization.

## Source-specific grounds retained without dilution

The public proposal's decision standards expressly retain these qualifications. They are part of the formulation tested here, rather than unstated conditions added to secure joins:

- **LeDuc, 365 So.2d 149, 150–152:** A death recommendation had operative weight before finality, conditioned on consideration of all relevant data. The recommendation was not binding, and the case's consideration of psychiatric material and affirmance prevents treating every information defect as requiring a new jury. The condition is not a new finding that Lambrix's jury possessed adequate information, and an inadequately guided recommendation does not lose all legal influence merely because the protection of a sound recommendation is qualified.
- **Messer, 330 So.2d 137, 141–143:** The jury's informed contribution is a distinct safeguard under state sentencing procedure; later judicial consideration did not replace that contribution when relevant accomplice-sentence and proposed psychiatric mitigation were excluded. Its particular new-jury remedy does not constitutionalize every state procedure, establish that every judicial cure is impossible, or direct the remedy in this habeas case.
- **Ross, 386 So.2d 1191, 1197–1198:** The judge must exercise independent reasoned judgment while giving the recommendation serious weight, and LeDuc's relevant-data condition remains. Judicial experience supplies a real narrowing safeguard. Ross's judicial remand corrected excessive deference to the recommendation, a different error from the challenged jury guidance; it establishes neither lack of influence nor automatic cure here.
- **Eutzy, 458 So.2d 755, 757–760:** Great weight and judicial independence coexist. Its instruction no-harm conclusion rested on the favorable life recommendation and did not decide the instructions' merits. Its evidentially unsupported robbery factor and its absence-of-equal-culpability reasoning do not decide a vague-factor death-recommendation claim.

The strongest contrary argument remains that those state protections did not compel the precise federal transmission rule in 1986, given the judge's independent responsibility recognized by Ross, Spaziano and Baldwin. The old-rule supporters answer that argument through Godfrey's protected guidance function operating in the established state allocation, with current Stringer and Gray supplying the finality method and their own bounded applications. Postfinality Maynard, Clemons, Walton, Florida Smith and Grossman are not recast as decisions available in 1986. The historical comparator supplies no additional in-world holding.

## Justice-specific formulation audit

### Stevens

**Frozen requirement and objection tested.** His reversal/remand requires rejection of the appellate Teague ruling and preservation of a legally available route to consideration of the sentencing claim, without deciding default, actual harm or present relief. A vacatur that merely invited renewed Teague consideration, withheld the old-rule legal conclusion, or supplied a remand on an unrelated procedural ground would not satisfy that commitment.

**Why this formulation is acceptable.** This proposal expressly decides old law and prohibits reuse of the new-rule ground. That supplies the operative legal consequence of his reversal even though the formal treatment of the appellate judgment is vacatur. His Stringer and Espinosa joins distinguish removing the erroneous legal obstacle from deciding all remaining gates and corrections; his Gray joins likewise keep claim availability and relief distinct. His historical Lambrix reasoning expressly separates an infected recommendation from the further question of lawful cure. The proposal retains that separation and his insistence that independent judicial judgment does not erase the jury's required function. His Caspari presentation concern is answered by the already accepted Teague question; no new question or default finding is introduced.

**Default-first remand and remaining limits.** Once the accepted Teague issue has been resolved, addressing genuine default before merits implements his frozen remand for default and, if the claim remains available, correction. It does not reverse his position on reaching Teague now or require a universal priority rule. No recorded ground requires a formal reversal term independently of those legal consequences. He accepts the precise vacatur with binding instructions, not a general equation of all vacaturs with reversals.

**Change assessment.** New express assent to the proposed mandate's form; no material change in the required legal result, rationale, scope or available remedy. No new historical departure and no new factual or doctrinal premise.

### Souter

**Why this formulation is acceptable.** The proposal removes precisely that possibility. His Gray approach gives separate legal effect to a controlling old-rule classification, claim availability, actual constitutional injury, effect and implementation of relief. Vacating an appellate denial with an express binding old-rule instruction preserves every one of those distinctions. No part of his supported rationale requires that the unresolved default defense disappear or that a sentence be replaced now. Default first among the remaining inquiries is consistent with the claim-specific gates he retained; it does not reconsider Teague or add an excuse for default.

**Changed-premise analysis preserved.** His own Stringer dissent and historical Lambrix new-rule join remain strong contrary evidence. The four Florida opinions and current Stringer existed in the historical landscape; retrieving them does not itself justify a departure. The difference retained from the final reconciliation is the legal content and controlling force of simulated Gray Part II. It treats supported loss of an established capital protection's real function as an old-rule application despite a formally different implementation. For Souter, LeDuc's qualified death-recommendation weight and Messer's distinct jury safeguard connect Godfrey's guidance protection to the actual contribution. Ross's independent judge and Eutzy's life-only instruction analysis do not remove that contribution. Gray concerns opportunity to answer, not this exact allocation, and the independent-judge distinction remains substantial; his conclusion follows his supported functional application, not automatic extension or authorship alone.

**Historical departure:** Souter rejects the Teague bar, rather than joining the historical new-rule affirmance, because simulated Gray Part II now makes functional protection of an established capital safeguard a controlling old-rule application; together with LeDuc's qualified death-recommendation weight and Messer's distinct jury safeguard, that changed legal premise leads him to apply Godfrey and controlling Stringer to this legally consequential weighing contribution while preserving Ross's independent judge, Eutzy's life-recommendation limit, and all separate default, cure and statutory conditions.

**Change assessment.** New express assent to this mandate's form, without changing the supported old-rule departure or its bounds. Vacatur neither supplies the Gray premise nor broadens it. No additional historical departure is created by the wording.

**Own-law and historical check.** Her Stringer and Espinosa joins support the old-rule/cure distinction, and her Gray Part II join with withheld final merits/effect/remedy joins directly supports separate treatment of classification and relief.
