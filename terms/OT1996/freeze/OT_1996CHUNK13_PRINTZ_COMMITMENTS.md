## Added premises and their exact effect

The petitioners asserted that their budgets made no provision for the federal work, that unauthorized expenditures could expose them to liability, and that state law prohibited their undertaking these federal responsibilities. The source provides the predecision brief locators, No. 95-1478, pages 4–5, and No. 95-1503, pages 6–7. Those passages have not newly been recovered and read in this context; the sanitized authenticated recitation supports the existence of the assertions, not findings about the State laws, actual expenditures, costs, personal liability, a taking, damages, or a preserved compensation cause of action. The reported posture also establishes that Montana and Arizona did not require their chief law-enforcement officers to implement the Act and that Printz and Mack chose not to implement it. Absence of a State requirement and an asserted State prohibition remain different propositions.

The added fiscal and legal objections must be answered in evaluating the Government's limited-assistance defense. It would be inaccurate to characterize the record as containing no fiscal objection, to assume costless implementation, or to assume the States had authorized the work. Conversely, the assertions do not by themselves prove a large burden, a constitutional spending threshold, a conflicting State-law rule of known scope, or an entitlement to reimbursement. They give no basis to turn the reasonable-effort command into a voluntary request or to supply a new payment mechanism.

Reported nonparticipation confirms the officers' objection to compulsory implementation. It does not establish whether a particular dealer notice or purchaser statement reached either officer, whether either possesses information derived from one, or whether an eligibility determination and a request for reasons occurred. Those distinctions matter especially to §922(s)(6)(B) and (C). The revised handoff therefore does not describe every residual issue as necessarily live, and does not treat a remand as permission to invent missing triggers. The actual severability appeals were dismissed as moot because the Ninth Circuit sustained the mandatory inquiry; a change in that legal foundation restores the need to dispose of those existing appeals correctly, including their actual justiciability and scope.

## Components, sufficient grounds, and operative alternatives

### T1. Reach, injury, and the questions actually presented

All eight Associates reach the challenge to the mandatory inquiry imposed on these officers through their offices. Their asserted compelled work and associated fiscal/legal exposure differ from the unauthorized legislators' abstract institutional dilution in Raines v. Byrd, June 26, 1997. Raines expressly reserves concrete official injury; it neither requires a criminal prosecution here nor creates a universal rule against official plaintiffs. The challenged command and relief from it have a direct relationship. No new finding about a specific receipt, expenditure, liability or conditional duty is needed to accept the packet's direct challenge to the mandatory inquiry; standing and relief for separate conditional provisions still require their own scope.

The grant concerns the mandatory interim executive commands and their statutory consequences. It does not reopen the Fifth Amendment vagueness rulings, the unraised civil-vagueness theory, Mack's separate Thirteenth Amendment theory, or an individual Second Amendment claim. The Government's disavowal of criminal prosecution is retained. The burden of a compulsory civil command is not eliminated by that disavowal, but a contradictory threat of criminal prosecution cannot be invented. Congressional authority to regulate commercial firearm transactions is distinct from whether these particular implementing means are proper.

### T2. Section 922(s)(2): compulsory reasonable-effort inquiry

The statutory trigger is a transferor's provision of the notice specified in §922(s)(1)(A)(i)(III). An officer receiving that notice must make a reasonable effort within five business days to ascertain whether receipt or possession would violate law, including research in whatever State and local recordkeeping systems are available and in the designated national system. The available-resource reading bears on what effort is reasonable, but does not erase an affirmative obligation. The statute does not require an adverse notice to the dealer, an arrest, a prosecution, or prevention of the transfer. The ability to use a designee does not turn an office's compulsory federal responsibility into a State choice to decline it.

**Invalidating path — O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas.** These four invalidate the mandatory inquiry and reverse the contrary constitutional judgment in each docket. New York v. United States, June 19, 1992, protects an unwilling State from being made responsible for administering federal regulation of private persons. Its structural distinction extends to direct commands to the State's executive officers; otherwise the allocation of federal program responsibility could be changed merely by addressing an officer rather than a legislature. Congress has regulated the private transaction but has also conscripted the State office to perform federal implementation. The assertion that State budgets and law do not authorize that work makes the actual allocation of responsibility salient; the holding does not depend on proving those assertions, a minimum cost, or a long duration.

This path preserves direct federal regulation of private persons, valid preemption, genuinely voluntary cooperation, and otherwise lawful spending incentives. Garcia's generally applicable regulation of State activities and Baker's rules governing State activity remain intact. FERC's retained cooperative/preemptible-field setting and Testa's State-court obligations do not establish ordinary-commerce power to demand this executive program. Voting Rights Coalition v. Wilson, February 26, 1996, rests on distinct congressional-election and presidential-registration sources, not an exception for inexpensive State work. A federal promise to reimburse, if there were one, would not by itself cure a structural transfer of responsibility; none is enacted or ordered here. State opposition alone does not invalidate a valid federal law, but whether this command is a constitutionally proper federal means must be answered before invoking supremacy against that opposition.

**Sustaining path — Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer.** These four uphold §922(s)(2) and affirm the constitutional judgment in both dockets. Congress directly governs an identified commercial transaction and requires a limited information task reasonably connected to that rule. It does not require a State to legislate, adopt federal policy as State policy, take private property or liabilities, establish a general regulatory regime, arrest, prosecute, or send adverse notices. The command is compulsory, but its defined task, available-information basis, reasonable-effort limitation and retained policy/enforcement choices distinguish it from New York's transfer of regulatory or ownership responsibility. Garcia, Baker and the cooperative arrangements retained by New York rebut a categorical exemption for every task performed by State personnel. Those authorities do not independently decide this precise task; the conclusion is a confined application of commerce power and necessary implementing authority.

The added fiscal objections make it necessary to reject any premise that the assistance is free, State-authorized, or harmless merely because temporary. They do not make the task a compulsory appropriation of a specified amount or prove the claimed personal liability. Reasonable effort permits account to be taken of available resources; it is not read to authorize no effort whenever the State has omitted a budget line. Nor may Congress obtain unlimited administration by calling it a small burden. The affirmative ground remains the defined informational relationship to direct federal transaction regulation and the responsibilities left with the State. If the command is constitutionally valid, a contrary State prohibition cannot alone defeat it under the Supremacy Clause. The Court need not, and these commitments do not, construe the two States' particular laws or decide that an unidentified liability is preempted in every application. Section (7) supplies no blanket immunity for all expenditures. Neither payment nor a compensation remedy is made a condition of these four Justices' merits votes.

### T3. Conditional information duties must be separated

**T3B — §922(s)(6)(B).** The relevant officer is one to whom a purchaser statement is transmitted under §922(s)(1)(A)(i)(IV), a trigger distinct from the notice in §922(s)(2). Unless that officer determines that the transaction would violate Federal, State or local law, the officer must destroy the statement, any record containing information derived from it, and any record created as a result of the required notice within twenty business days after the date the purchaser made the statement. Under the same statutory exception, the information may be conveyed only to a person needing it to carry out the subsection and may be used only to carry out the subsection. The destruction clock does not begin upon an officer's election to participate. A nonparticipating officer could receive a dealer's required statement; neither receipt nor nonreceipt is established by the general nonparticipation posture. Affirmative record destruction and restrictions on use/disclosure are different duties even though contained in one subparagraph.

**T3C — §922(s)(6)(C).** Written reasons are due only if the officer determines that an individual is ineligible to receive a handgun and the individual requests the reasons. They are due within twenty business days after receipt of that request. Neither receipt of a statement nor objection to the program establishes either predicate. There is no unconditional duty to make an adverse determination, send an adverse notice, or furnish explanations to every purchaser.

**Position if the inquiry is sustained.** Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer reject the asserted structural challenge to these provisions as bounded protections concerning the information and decisions generated within the valid interim system. They would affirm the judgment sustaining the program and leave the dismissal of the severability appeals in place because no constitutional invalidation requires severance. They neither find a particular trigger satisfied nor direct compliance with an untriggered duty. Their disposition establishes no new monetary entitlement, no independent liability ruling, and no unconditional explanation requirement. Their grounds must preserve the actual duty and exception of each subparagraph; a reference to ancillary safeguards alone is insufficient if used to conceal their affirmative administrative work.

**Position if the inquiry is invalidated.** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas vacate the dismissal of the existing severability appeals as moot and remand their disposition under the structural holding. This is a concrete return of issues already appealed and displaced by the appellate merits ruling. It does not conclusively sustain or invalidate every residual duty. The lower courts must separate (i) actual injury and the statutory trigger, (ii) whether the challenged residual requirement itself transfers federal administration to an unwilling office or instead permissibly regulates information handling on an independently lawful basis, and (iii) severability and the proper plaintiff-specific relief. If the existing record suffices, no new evidence is required; if a particular claim is not justiciable, the remand does not manufacture it. No automatic evidentiary hearing or invitation to add claims is ordered.

Passive receipt of a privately transmitted form is not consent to undertake the federal program. Thus these four cannot join an opinion sustaining compulsory destruction merely because the plaintiffs chose not to implement the Act, or treating every recipient as a volunteer. Conversely, the structural holding does not give a person unrestricted use or disclosure rights in information received through a federal statutory channel. Restrictions on such information and an affirmative administrative task require distinct analysis. A requested explanation following an actual voluntary eligibility determination has different predicates from a demand that an unwilling officer undertake the inquiry in the first place. State-authorized action, voluntary action, federal compulsion, and mere receipt must not be conflated. The added posture strengthens the need for those distinctions without resolving the missing transaction-specific facts.

Two narrower lawful alternatives were tested. A direct constitutional decision about a residual compulsory task could follow if the record establishes its trigger, live application and basis; direct preservation of a specific information restriction could follow if its independent legal basis and proper application are established. Neither conclusion may be obtained by substituting general nonparticipation for those premises. On this supplied posture the four choose the bounded remand, because the Ninth Circuit dismissed the existing appeals on a premise their §922(s)(2) judgment removes. They do not adopt a standing rule that requires every plaintiff to violate a duty first, or decide that receipt is the only way to establish a sufficiently imminent application.

### T4. Remainder, severability, and the decree

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas apply New York's ordinary Alaska Airlines severability rule: preserve a remainder capable of operating independently unless it is evident Congress would reject it without the invalid provision. Removing compulsory State inquiry does not itself authorize erasing the independent federal restrictions on dealers and purchasers, and these plaintiffs' direct-officer injury supplies no general standing to obtain relief against duties borne solely by others. The permanent federal system is not invalidated. The remand does not automatically suspend every privacy restriction pending further proceedings, and does not enter a new nationwide decree. It must protect these plaintiffs from §922(s)(2)'s compulsory inquiry and address the actual restored residual claims. The Government's unresolved objection that the actions are not class actions remains material to injunction scope. Ordinary precedential force is distinct from the parties protected by an injunction.

The enacted private transfer routes remain distinct, and no new exception is supplied. For the ordinary route after the latest proposed transfer, the dealer receives the required statement, verifies identity through the presented identification document, and within one day after the purchaser furnishes the statement provides its contents and transmits its copy to the chief officer of the purchaser's residence. Transfer may follow five business days from that notice without information from the officer that receipt or possession would violate Federal, State or local law, or earlier receipt of the officer's notice that the officer has no information indicating such unlawfulness. Business days are days on which State offices are open. Separate alternatives are a written statement by the chief officer of the purchaser's residence, issued during the ten-day period ending on the latest proposed transfer, that access to a handgun is required because of a threat to the life of the purchaser or a household member; a permit to possess or acquire a handgun issued no more than five years earlier by the State where transfer will occur, under a State law requiring an authorized official first to verify that the available information does not indicate unlawful possession; a State law requiring that verification by an authorized official before the dealer completes the transfer; the Secretary's approval under Internal Revenue Code §5812; or the Secretary's impracticability certification on the dealer's application. That last certification concerns the required notice and requires together State officer density no greater than 0.0025 per square mile, extremely remote business premises in relation to the chief officer, and absence of telecommunications facilities in the area. The exception for return of a handgun to the person from whom it was received also remains. Stating these operative alternatives does not adjudicate an absent nonparty's independent challenge to them.

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would affirm without severance. If a contrary Court majority invalidates the inquiry, they accept that a decree requires proper party scope and that severability does not automatically destroy independent private duties; this conditional agreement does not become a join in the structural invalidation. They do not substitute a new federal administrator, accelerate the permanent system, convert mandatory text into an optional request, or enact reimbursement.

### T5. Fiscal assertions, compensation, and matters reserved

All eight distinguish a financial objection relevant to the constitutional characterization of a command from a cause of action seeking money. The supplement supplies the former. It establishes neither the preservation nor the legal sufficiency of the latter. These commitments adjudicate no taking, compensation entitlement, amount of reimbursement, appropriation, claim-processing route or proper money defendant; they also make no ruling of waiver, forfeiture, failure to incur costs or universal absence of a remedy. No separate compensation ruling is needed to decide the granted executive-command challenge on either sufficient merits path.

Section 922(s)(7) protects the chief officer or other person responsible for background information from damages actions for failure to prevent an unlawful handgun sale or transfer and for preventing a lawful one. It is not compensation for doing the work, and it does not by its terms settle the asserted State-law exposure for unauthorized expenditure. The invalidating four would not cure their structural objection by presuming payment available. The sustaining four would not convert uncertain payment into a necessary saving construction or decide that all possible fiscal claims fail because the structural claim fails. Any proposed decision on a separate compensation or actual State-liability claim would require its actual presentation, governing source, factual predicates and requested relief; this is a path-specific source limit, not a blocker to the bounded constitutional decision.

No new criminal threat, civil-vagueness claim, Thirteenth Amendment judgment, individual Second Amendment holding, State-law construction or general unfunded-mandates rule is supplied. Validity under the selected structural rationale, exact statutory obligation, actual trigger, and remedy remain separate questions.

## Join and remedy audit

The four invalidating Associates can join one opinion at the complete T1–T5 scope stated here. Its sufficient ground is structural executive administration, not a factual finding of expenditure, a State-law prohibition, or an implied compensation condition. A proposal that automatically invalidates all residual information restrictions, treats passive receipt as consent, orders a nationwide injunction, or rewrites the statute exceeds their recorded joins. Their return of the existing residual appeals leaves questions for lawful disposition; it does not publish an unconditional merits holding about duties not yet shown applicable to either plaintiff.

The four sustaining Associates agree on affirmance and the confined assistance ground. A shared opinion must preserve New York, distinguish direct private-transaction regulation from compelling a State regulatory regime, and answer the added fiscal and State-law assertions without reducing the rule to smallness of burden. Souter's join does not extend to general federal power to command State executive programs. All four reject the structural challenge to the distinct conditional safeguards within the sustained scheme, but no shared vote permits presumed determinations, requests or possession. A writing proposing a separate compensation remedy or immunity determination would require a new supported component; none is committed here.

All eight agree on the limited reach, nonidentity of the statutory duties, absence of an adjudicated compensation entitlement, and need for proper party/remedy scope. That common ground does not supply eight votes for either T2 result, a final residual-duty judgment after invalidation, or a universal rule that every ancillary duty is valid. Provisional arithmetic is four to invalidate §922(s)(2) and four to uphold it; the residual and remedial positions track the separately stated paths.
