## 2. Entering law and propositions that must remain separate

1. **Actionable environment.** Harris, November 9, 1993, establishes severe-or-pervasive, objectively and subjectively abusive sex-based conditions without a psychological-injury or tangible-loss prerequisite. Its exact Standards title is **Hostile employment conditions without a psychological-injury prerequisite**, present operation effective Harris, November 9, 1993. DeAngelis, January 6, 1997, preserves that contextual inquiry without adding hierarchy, physical contact or lost-output requirements. The lower court accepted Faragher's hostile-environment finding. Attribution, rather than reconsideration of that finding, is the issue here.
2. **Actual authority and material aid.** Breyer's unanimous Andrade, February 26, 1997, requires a demonstrated connection between actual delegated supervisory authority and creation or maintenance of the environment. Title and proximity are insufficient; lack of higher-management notice is not a complete defense. Actual daily control of working conditions may supply the connection without an express threat, completed personnel decision or tangible economic loss. Thomas's unanimous Oncale, March 19, applies exactly that rule while retaining the distinct causation, environment and attribution predicates.
3. **Other agency and negligence routes.** Meritor, 477 U.S. 57, 69–73, directs an agency inquiry and rejects both automatic liability for every supervisor's misconduct and an invariably sufficient no-notice defense. Scope of employment, actual-authority aid, apparent authority and negligence are distinct. A conclusion that the acts pursued a personal object does not foreclose the authority-aid route. Negligence depends on its own knowledge, reasonable-care and causal predicates; severity and constructive notice are not identical legal conclusions.
4. **Policy and employee conduct.** Meritor rejects a defense made conclusive merely by a written grievance policy and nonuse. A meaningful, accessible complaint procedure and actual reasonable corrective measures can matter to the appropriate agency, negligence or remedy inquiry. Andrade expressly adopted no new general affirmative defense. No general two-part defense, tangible-action gateway, supervisor classification or mandatory complaint prerequisite enters this case as existing law.
5. **Relief.** The Standards title **The 1991 Civil Rights Act’s new liabilities and jury election**, with present operation effective Rivers, April 26, 1994, preserves Landgraf's rule that §102 damages do not attach to this pre-November 21, 1991 conduct and its dependent jury condition. The specific City nominal judgment is the reviewed relief, not a request to create new compensatory or punitive liability. **Nominal civil-rights relief and reasonable fees**, present operation effective Farrar, December 14, 1992, separates §1988 eligibility from amount; it supplies at most a limited analogy for the different Title VII fee statute. No fee amount or entitlement is determined here.

## 3. Record application and rival paths

### A. Existing findings support restoration under Andrade

The selected sufficient ground is actual authority materially aiding the maintenance of the accepted hostile environment. The relevant facts are the trial-established allocation and exercise of workplace authority, not the supervisors' titles alone. Terry supervised all aspects of assignments, gave reprimands and placed discipline in personnel files, and selected recruits subject to higher approval. Silverman supervised daily duties, assignments and fitness routines. The two controlled the work in a geographically separated unit operating through a defined command structure. The City's harassment policy and an effective alternative complaint route were not communicated to that unit. The district court made an agency determination based on the actual supervisory powers and the overall structure, separately from its disputed constructive-notice ground.

Those findings describe the authority through which the supervisors maintained the working conditions in which the repeated abuse occurred. Their delegated control was not merely an employment opportunity to be near Faragher: it governed her assignments, daily duties and the on-site environment she encountered in performing them. The remote chain of command and ineffective communication of a route beyond that command reinforced the practical force of that control. They are contextual facts supporting the connection, not mandatory elements every future plaintiff must prove. The determination attributes the established course of hostile working conditions; it does not declare every isolated personal act by any employee with authority attributable to the employer.

The court of appeals' contrary legal application emphasized absent firing or demotion threats, absent employment decisions based on response to advances, and the personal object of the misconduct. Andrade makes none a necessary condition of the selected route. Its surviving warning against mere proximity remains sound, but does not require dismissal where the findings identify this actual and extensive control of the environment. Restoration does not depend on a new finding that Faragher feared discharge, was ordered to submit, lost wages, was constructively discharged, received a particular threat or made a complaint to a designated official. None is invented.

**Strongest objection:** A list of supervisory powers proves capacity, not actual aid; the district court's agency conclusion could have treated status as sufficient. Because no specific personnel-power use or retaliation for refusal was found, a remand might be required to distinguish enablement from ordinary access.

**Disposition of that objection:** This is a serious limit on the permissible rule, but the existing bench findings go beyond a rank label. They link the supervisors' actual control of daily working conditions with the structure in which they created and maintained the unlawful environment. The legal significance of that supported connection may be determined on review without adding a subjective-fear finding or demanding the very express-action evidence Andrade says is not invariably needed. The complete record also contains the trial determination of agency responsibility, not only an unresolved allegation or a verdict returned without a known agency standard. Anderson's lower separate writing identifies the especially broad workplace authority and ineffective supervision as sufficient without deciding every supervisor's minimum authority; that reasoning illustrates this lawful application but supplies no Supreme Court precedent. Barkett's overlapping authority-aid ground is separable from her broader scope and notice theories.

### B. Focused vacatur and remand is lawful if the findings are held inadequate, but is not selected

A lawful competing disposition would vacate the employer judgment and return only the unresolved actual-material-aid determination under Andrade, with the supported historical findings reviewed under the ordinary standard and any truly necessary further proceedings determined below. It could not simply order a new trial or demand proof of an express threat. Its strongest support is Andrade's refusal automatically to restore a Rule 50 damages verdict when attribution, instructions, preserved grounds and sufficiency remained unresolved, together with Meritor's remand on insufficient findings.

The distinction here is that the relevant supervisory powers, their daily exercise, workplace structure, policy failure and agency determination emerged from a completed bench trial. Meritor had unresolved harassment and unwelcomeness questions; Andrade had a categorical notice bar and unresolved verdict/instruction issues. Neither establishes a general rule requiring factfinding again whenever an appellate court corrects its agency analysis. The ordinary restoration path is supported without curing the separate constructive-notice reasoning. No additional preserved independent employer-liability objection requiring a new proceeding is identified in the validated packet. An identified material omitted fact would require a focused source return, but a possible fact that might exist supplies no present reason for exploratory remand.

### C. Affirmance for the City is not selected

The City's strongest surviving case combines the personal nature of the acts, the absence of personnel consequences tied to advances, remoteness from City Hall, informal rather than designated complaints to Gordon, and prompt discipline after Ewanchew's letter. That account may defeat particular scope, notice or negligent-response theories. It does not defeat the independently supported actual-authority route. Treating either higher-management ignorance or prompt correction after eventual notice as conclusive would restore the categorical restriction Andrade rejected. The City remains entitled to the material-aid limitation; it is the proved workplace relationship, not a repudiation of that limitation, that defeats affirmance.

### D. Question 3 and the policy question

Three legally distinct options were examined. A complete care-and-nonuse defense could be considered in a case of proved effective prevention, a usable complaint route, reasonable correction and demonstrated unreasonable nonuse; Meritor's attention to effective procedures supports considering that possibility, but neither Meritor nor Andrade supplies its complete rule, burdens or exceptions. A narrower rule could leave established authority-aid liability intact and account for avoidable injury in the legally available remedy. Stevens's Meritor concurrence, joining Marshall, directly supports that distinction. A third option resolves the present policy contention and reserves whether a complete defense exists on materially different facts.

The common selected position is the third. A policy not communicated to the relevant workforce does not demonstrate an accessible preventive or complaint system, and failure to invoke that unknown route cannot by itself constitute unreasonable nonuse. Eventual correction after another employee's letter does not establish that the City had supplied effective prevention or an available route during the existing abuse. The City therefore cannot defeat this judgment by combining its paper policy, Faragher's failure to complain to distant higher management and its eventual response. No finding about the policy's unprovided exact text, an undisclosed promise, actual fear of retaliation or a particular employee's understanding is necessary.

**Strongest argument for deciding a complete defense now:** The grant expressly asks about it; a general answer could clarify the incentives and limits of supervisor attribution and avoid treating reasonable employers as insurers.

**Answer and operative limit:** The proper agency connection already limits attribution. This City's policy failure makes a complete defense based on effective precautions unavailable on the facts even if such a defense can exist elsewhere. A new burden-shifting formula or tangible-action exception would not perform necessary work here and would decide conditions the case does not present. Reservation is supported by that absence, not by a general preference for narrow opinions. Stevens additionally records the broader position below; the other seven do not adopt it as a general rule.

## 4. Justice-specific commitments

Each Justice reaches the employer-attribution merits within the four granted questions. Each preserves the accepted hostile-environment finding, the pre-1991 remedy limits and all individual or state-law components outside the grant. The common proposed operative propositions are: **P1**, Andrade actual-authority/material-aid rule and rejection of categorical notice or tangible-action prerequisites; **P2**, these trial findings meet that rule; **P3**, the actual undistributed policy, noncomplaint and eventual response do not defeat this liability, with any general defense reserved; **P4**, reverse the reviewed Title VII appellate disposition and return for restoration of the existing one-dollar judgment against the City, without reopening other components or fixing fees. No majority is declared without assembly.

### Stevens

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4 and restoration. He would additionally reject a complete reasonable-care/noncomplaint defense once abuse of actually delegated day-to-day responsibility has established attribution. Effective procedures and unreasonable failure to use them may affect a remedy on its own legal predicates; they do not erase the attributed discrimination.

**Own authorities and basis:** His express double join in Meritor, 477 U.S. 73–78, adopts both the Court's agency limits and Marshall's account of delegated workplace control, rejecting a special notice rule for hostile environments and separating liability from constructive-discharge/backpay remedies. His full joins in Andrade and Oncale establish the current requirement of actual material aid; his earlier broader language is applied consistently with that binding qualification. His Harris join and DeAngelis dissent reinforce attention to actual working conditions rather than a tangible-loss requirement, without turning those opinions into attribution holdings.

**Alternative and barrier:** He rejects the City's no-threat argument for the reasons his Meritor concurrence states. He would accept a narrowly confined remand if a material finding were actually absent, but the existing findings satisfy his own delegated-environment account. He cannot join a rule making uncommunicated procedures a complaint duty or creating a complete liability defense from reasonable care alone. He can join P3's express reservation without surrendering his separate answer. A separate concurrence would have a substantive function: explain why a hypothetical effective policy may influence relief without extinguishing established agency liability. No author assignment is made.

### O'Connor

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4. Reserve a general affirmative defense; no separate ground is needed.

**Own authorities and basis:** She joined Meritor's Court opinion, including its rejection of both automatic supervisor liability and conclusive reliance on a formal policy, and authored the unanimous Harris standard that separates unlawful conditions from tangible harm. Her full Andrade and Oncale joins establish that meaningful day-to-day authority can supply aid without a completed personnel decision. The recorded assignment, discipline and daily-control facts distinguish this case from liability based on title alone.

**Alternative and barrier:** A focused agency remand would be proper if the trial had merely attached an agency label to a title. Here the independent structural and operational findings support the legal application. The City's prompt response is significant for its response once informed, but does not undo actual authority-aided conditions or establish communicated prevention. She will not join automatic liability for all supervisors, an equation of severe harassment with notice, or Stevens's general rejection of every complete defense on facts not presented. An opinion that reserves that broader issue and states the actual findings is fully joinable.

### Scalia

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4 at their stated, record-dependent scope. No opinion author is selected and no dissent is manufactured from disagreement with an unadopted broader rule.

**Own authorities and basis:** His full joins in Andrade and Oncale accept the statutory agent relationship, actual authority materially aiding the wrong, the absence of a mandatory personnel action and retention of the plaintiff's distinct predicates. His Harris join confirms that noneconomic conditions can be the statutory injury; his DeAngelis join preserves the statutory threshold without adding hierarchy or tangible loss. The application here rests on found operational control, not Title VII's general purpose or an automatic inference from a title.

**Alternative and barrier:** His strongest objection is that potential power plus opportunity would empty Andrade's material-aid requirement. He requires P2 to identify the actual allocation and exercise of daily control, the supported workplace structure and their connection to the continuing environment; a statement merely that supervisors are more intimidating would not suffice. Those findings support restoration without an invented threat. He would favor a targeted remand if the opinion required an unmade coercion or fear finding, but the selected ground requires neither. He withholds a join from free-standing prevention duties, automatic imputation of all supervisory knowledge, or a new general care defense unsupported by the deciding ground. Reserving the latter creates no such rule.

### Kennedy

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4; leave the general defense question open on these facts.

**Own authorities and basis:** His Harris, Andrade and Oncale joins preserve both the employee's protection against discriminatorily imposed work conditions and the independent connection needed to attribute them. His DeAngelis join applies that separation without an economic-injury floor. The City entrusted these supervisors with the actual organization of the remote unit's work, and the accepted findings describe the use of that responsibility in maintaining the environment, rather than a personal encounter unrelated to assigned work.

**Alternative and barrier:** The strongest City position is that it lacked knowledge and remedied the problem when it learned of it. His Andrade join prevents making that account dispositive of agency responsibility; it remains relevant to negligence and an actually available remedial argument. He can accept different future legal treatment for a genuinely effective procedure without attributing unreasonable conduct to this employee. He will not join a rule erasing ordinary agency limits or denying any possible legal relevance to effective prevention. P3 expressly avoids both. No residual factual issue in the selected sufficient ground calls for the alternative remand.

### Souter

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4; retains a distinction between the present policy holding and an unanswered general defense.

**Own authorities and basis:** His full Andrade and Oncale joins directly accept authority-aided attribution without a notice or tangible-action prerequisite. His Harris join keeps the statutory environment distinct from injury. His controlling-rationale join in Farrar separates an existing nominal judgment from unresolved collateral fees, while his Landgraf join prevents converting this review into new §102 compensation. These remedial commitments support precise restoration of the reviewed judgment rather than a larger award.

**Alternative and barrier:** Meritor's reservation and Andrade's remand make adequate findings a genuine concern. Unlike those procedural records, this bench trial resolved the operative structure and attributed the hostile environment through it. A remand is unnecessary merely to ask the court to restate those findings in Andrade's words. He requires the opinion to preserve separate negligence and apparent-authority questions and not characterize constructive notice as established by severity alone. He would not join a purported comprehensive defense whose burden, trigger and relationship to agency are left unstated. Reservation on the materially different effective-policy facts allows a full common join.

### Thomas

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4 with the actual attribution connection explicit. No new supervisor definition or automatic liability rule.

**Own authorities and basis:** His own unanimous Oncale opinion requires independent proof of sex discrimination, actionable conditions and Andrade attribution; it expressly rejects job title as sufficient and absence of employment action as a categorical bar. His full Andrade join is direct current authority. His Harris and DeAngelis joins similarly require the statutory predicate without an extra injury prerequisite. Here that predicate was tried and accepted, and the operational authority facts were found; this is not the allegation-only situation his Oncale opinion returned for adjudication.

**Alternative and barrier:** A mere inference that every supervisor can make misconduct easier would not pass his Oncale qualification. The complete control of actual daily assignments and duties, combined with the trial's specific workplace-structure determination, establishes the materially aided environment at this narrower scope. He does not require a new personnel consequence that Oncale expressly says is not invariably necessary. A fact-dependent application is therefore possible without remand. He refuses any categorical nondelegable-duty rationale making every supervisor's private wrong the employer's act, any presumed negligence from abuse alone, or any policy defense eliminating the accepted separate agency route merely for want of notice. The common propositions impose none of those rules.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4. Her position on a complete defense in a materially different effective-policy case remains reserved; no broader join is inferred from agreement with Stevens here.

**Own authorities and basis:** Her Harris join and her DeAngelis joinder with Stevens emphasize the actual disadvantageous conditions of work without measurable output loss. Her full Andrade and Oncale joins require a real agency connection. The proof here identifies that connection through daily control of the workplace, not simply the conclusion that abuse occurred. Her Landgraf join preserves the existing pre-1991 remedial boundary.

**Alternative and barrier:** The strongest remand argument points to defects in the trial's constructive-notice analysis. Those defects do not eliminate its independent agency ground. Her DeAngelis preference for correct-rule review of that jury record does not create a remand preference when the necessary findings already establish this different sufficient ground. She rejects an employee-complaint prerequisite inferred from an inaccessible procedure and a relabeling of the abuse as personal conduct that bypasses Andrade. She can join an opinion reserving a general defense while explaining why this ineffective policy cannot protect the City. No separate writing is necessary to preserve an unselected future view.

### Breyer

**Commitment:** Joins P1–P4; no general affirmative-defense regime is adopted.

**Own authorities and basis:** He authored unanimous Andrade, expressly distinguishing authority materially aiding misconduct from title and proximity, and distinguishing that route from negligence and management notice. His full Oncale join confirms the rule's separate application; his DeAngelis join applies the hostile-environment threshold without adding injury or hierarchy elements. The supported record now supplies the specific daily-control connection Andrade required the lower courts in that case to examine.

**Alternative and barrier:** The strongest opposing argument is that allowing liability on this structure discourages useful complaint procedures and approaches automatic responsibility. Actual aid remains necessary, and the City did not supply an operative procedure to the unit; neither rationale establishes immunity here. Effective care and genuinely unreasonable nonuse may require separate treatment in a properly presented future record, but the new defense's precise structure is unnecessary to this judgment. His Andrade remand was tied to that verdict's unresolved sufficiency and procedural issues. It does not require return of adequate bench findings solely because the appellate court applied a categorical barrier. He rejects an unexplained restoration that omits the connection, and equally rejects an exploratory remand that identifies no missing predicate.
