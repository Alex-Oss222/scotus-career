* **Reasoning Below:** The Eighth Circuit treated federal-instrumentality status as sufficient to invoke the United States exception.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

## Reviewed judgment, relief, and threshold

The Eighth Circuit affirmed summary judgment holding the four borrower-owned production credit associations immune from the disputed state income and sales taxes. Its merits judgment is not merely a ruling allowing litigation to proceed. The district court rejected the Tax Injunction Act objection under the federal-instrumentality theory; the appellate opinion did not decide that objection expressly. The United States is not a plaintiff. Its merits amicus supports Arkansas, seeks jurisdictional dismissal, and alternatively disputes substantive tax immunity.

Respondents expressly concede that Arkansas provides a plain, speedy and efficient remedy (merits brief page 22 note 7). The model may not replace that concession with a hypothetical inadequacy branch. The record describes a parallel August 1994 Pulaski County chancery refund action; the status described by a party is not a newly issued Court order. The pending merits and procedural issues of that state suit must not be inferred from the federal judgment.

The question is whether these particular corporations suing in their own right may use the United States exception to 28 U.S.C. 1341. Congressional designation as a federal instrumentality, a substantive immunity from taxation, and entitlement to the sovereign's federal forum are separate propositions. Department of Employment involved the United States and Red Cross as co-plaintiffs; its sovereign exception does not itself settle every privately owned instrumentality's independent suit. The United States amicus discusses agency regulatory litigation, identity of sovereign interest, and competing circuit rules; those arguments preserve both the narrower joinder position and a functional exception analysis, without certifying either as governing law. A jurisdictional disposition can leave substantive immunity undecided. If the jurisdictional exception is sustained, any treatment of the merits must identify what is properly presented and distinguish the statutory exemptions from constitutional instrumentality arguments.
