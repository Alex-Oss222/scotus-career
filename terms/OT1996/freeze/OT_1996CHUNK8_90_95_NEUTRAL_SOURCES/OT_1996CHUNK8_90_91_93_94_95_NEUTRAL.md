## C. DECISION-MATERIAL LITIGATION POSITIONS

### Issue 1: Statutory maximum

* **Reasoning Below:** The First Circuit accepted the Commission’s view that recidivist enhancements should be excluded to limit prosecutorial control over the career-offender range.
* **Petitioner or Appellant:** The maximum legally authorized for the offender includes every applicable statutory enhancement. The Commission cannot substitute a lower policy maximum for Congress’s command.
* **Respondent or Appellee:** Section 994(h) addresses a generic offense maximum and permits the Commission to avoid making prosecutorial charging decisions determine the guideline ceiling.
* **Material Alternative or Unreached Ground:** If Amendment 506 is invalid, remand for ordinary resentencing under the lawful guideline without deciding every issue concerning prosecutorial discretion.
* **Necessary Concession or Limiting Principle:** The statutory construction does not require prosecutors to seek enhancements or eliminate discretion Congress otherwise conferred.

## D. LAWFUL PATHS AND UNCERTAINTY

If §994(h)'s maximum term includes the applicable recidivist enhancement, the Court may reverse the approval of Amendment 506's contrary definition and remand for sentencing proceedings consistent with the statute, including the required enhancement procedures. If Congress left the Commission discretion to use an unenhanced benchmark, the judgment may be affirmed. The interpretation must separate the meaning of the maximum from charging choices, actual §851 notice and the lawful sentence for each respondent.

Simulated MCI rejects agency replacement of a statutory command; Neal preserves the statute's actual LSD carrier-weight command separately from the Commission's dose-based guideline measure. Those holdings require examination of §994(h)'s own maximum-term direction here. If it includes applicable recidivist enhancements, a Commission policy preference cannot erase them. Neal does not itself interpret §994(h), and MCI supplies no universal denial of lawful Commission discretion.
