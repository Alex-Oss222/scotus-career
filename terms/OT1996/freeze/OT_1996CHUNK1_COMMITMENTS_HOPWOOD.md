The controlling inquiry comes from current Adarand's common substantial-justification standard, with Croson's retained evidentiary demands, Wygant, and Bakke's actual divided reasoning and express joins. Current Adarand (June 12, 1995) adopts that standard for federal, state and local racial classifications and overrules Metro's lower federal tier. Its five-Justice merits holding supplies no exception for this state program and does not establish the historical universal strict-scrutiny rule. The same decision's equal-competition holding, supported on that point by all eight Associates, separates the injury of unequal competition from entitlement to admission or damages.

Bakke supplies three distinct points: the Davis reserved-seat system failed; Powell's educational-diversity analysis was not itself an opinion of five; and Powell's Part V-C, expressly joined by Brennan, White, Marshall and Blackmun, rejected a categorical injunction against competitive consideration of race. This handoff makes no Marks claim. The present Court can adopt, reserve or reject a more specific constitutional proposition on direct review; a lower court could not treat every part of Bakke as already displaced. None of the commitments imports a later admissions formula, numerical endpoint, or later Supreme Court decision.

Current Fordice/Ayers (June 26, 1992) requires removal of traceable policies with continuing segregative effects when practicable consistently with sound education. The State bears the discharge showing; good faith and facial neutrality alone do not discharge an established dual-system duty. Racial imbalance alone proves neither a violation nor a particular remedy. Current Jenkins (June 12, 1995) preserves violation-linked educational repair and causally bounded relief, not a general warrant for preferences. The State's institutional responsibility cannot be decided merely by drawing an administrative boundary around the law school. Conversely, no statewide history alone establishes that these preferences repair a still-operative wrong.

The explicit use of different racial thresholds and decisional channels supplies a state racial classification and unequal treatment; no separate hostile motive is needed to identify that classification. The constitutional inquiry still concerns justification and fit. The presumption bands contained individual exceptions. The record does not establish a fixed reserved-seat count, mechanical rejection of every person below a threshold, or that all preferred applicants were interchangeable. Those qualifications do not erase the separate discretionary committees and unequal competitive process found below.

The four damages claims keep a live controversy concerning 1992. Prospective relief has its own exposure and equitable requirements. The changed later process is only partly described, and neither its validity nor a future violation is established. The grant reaches the three supplied questions, not the intervenors' independent Title VI theory. No presently missing original filing is treated as a litigant's default.

## Frozen component commitments

The following tables state decisional conclusions and competing legal grounds, not private deliberations. The strongest objections are tested at the relevant component; support for the same result is not treated as agreement on every reason.

### 1. Threshold, review scope and scrutiny

All eight reach the supplied merits questions to the extent needed to govern the 1992 process and its remedies. They reject whole-case mootness from discontinuance because the concrete retrospective claims remain, while requiring separate prospective standing. They apply Adarand's governing standard to this explicit state allocation; O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas record their preference for strict scrutiny. Stevens does not embrace a universal assertion that identical scrutiny describes every inclusionary and exclusionary governmental act; he can join the demanding examination required for this program and its individual burdens. The other three Adarand majority Associates do not export federal congressional competence into an exception for Texas.

The competing threshold path is to decide only the discontinued committee practice and leave every justification question open. Its strength is the four-person suit and the incomplete later record. Its limit is that the appellate categorical rules govern the lawful counterfactual and instructions on remand, while the supplied grant expressly presents diversity and remediation. No Justice favors dismissing this grant merely because the operative procedure changed. Kennedy's narrower merits reservation and Stevens's insistence on program-specific scope remain below.

### 2. Educational diversity and Bakke

**Available paths:** A substantive educational interest in a broad mix of individual experiences can justify some competitive consideration of race, subject to Adarand's governing standard; or diversity cannot justify allocating an opportunity by racial preference. A third, narrower path assumes the asserted interest and decides this process on fit. The first path's strongest objection is that generalized racial categories can become proxies for beliefs and create guaranteed advantages. The second path's strongest objection is Bakke Part V-C, the distinction between a diverse educational community and a broadcast stereotype, and the absence of any earlier controlling overruling. The assumption path avoids an unnecessary general rule but cannot silently leave the panel's categorical remand instructions governing this case.

### 5. Causation, lawful counterfactual and individual relief

**Operative commitment:** Each Justice supports retaining the appellate correction of the causation burden. Once these applicants establish the unconstitutional unequal admissions treatment, the school must prove by a preponderance of the evidence that the individual applicant would not have been admitted under a constitutional process. The same-decision defense concerns the denied admission and consequent loss; it does not erase the completed unequal-competition injury. The plaintiffs must establish the existence and amount of actual compensable loss under an available cause against a proper defendant. Failure of the school's defense is not an automatic damages amount or unconditional admission order.

Mt. Healthy, 429 U.S. 274, 287, and Arlington Heights, 429 U.S. 252, 270 n.21, support that allocation. Carey, 435 U.S. 247, 254–257, preserves actual injury and nominal recognition. Bakke's admission remedy involved the university's concession that it could not carry the burden; it does not independently establish that these four have proved the same counterfactual. The trial court's Title VII analogy used to leave ultimate nonadmission persuasion with the applicants does not fit the already proved unconstitutional treatment.

### 6. Statutory intent, immunity, punitive damages and prospective orders

The two statutory/remedial paths are a comprehensive award or injunction on proof of unconstitutional classification, and a bounded disposition preserving claim-specific requirements. The first path fails because intentional treatment is not all the elements of a remedy, the defendants are state entities and officials in official capacities, and the panel expressly left immunity undecided. The prospective choice is a new injunction against all race consideration or leaving the refusal intact subject to a properly supported future request. The latter preserves review of later conduct without adjudicating an incompletely described program.

For punitive damages the strongest contrary path is remand because the panel says the District Court's precise legal standard was unclear and the applicants invoked reckless indifference. Smith v. Wade, 461 U.S. 30, 56, includes evil motive or intent **or** reckless or callous indifference to federally protected rights. Nonmalicious motivation alone is therefore not a complete legal answer. The accepted good-faith finding in this record and the unsettled authority nevertheless support retaining the denial, without declaring that benevolent purpose always defeats punitive liability. This conclusion is limited to the supplied adjudicated request; no finding about a later official's state of mind is made.

## Readiness and precise limits

Independent commitments are complete for reconciliation. There is no all-path blocker.
