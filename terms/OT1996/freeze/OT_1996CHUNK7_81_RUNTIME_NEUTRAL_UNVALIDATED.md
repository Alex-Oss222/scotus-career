### Application of ERISA’s function-specific limits

Varity (1996) attaches fiduciary duties to actual discretionary plan administration and deliberate disloyal benefit-security deception; it expressly leaves corporate restructuring and plan amendment distinct and adopts no §510 alternative. Curtiss-Wright and Lockheed preserve lawful plan-design powers within their statutory settings. Thus a lawful right to amend or terminate a welfare plan does not answer whether a discharge was intentionally used to defeat a participant’s protected attainment under §510. Conversely, a benefits-reducing restructuring does not itself establish specific interference intent. Remand must decide the actual §510 elements and remedy, not treat Varity as having found this transaction fiduciary.
