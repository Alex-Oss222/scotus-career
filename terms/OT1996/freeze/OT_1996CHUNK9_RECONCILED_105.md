## Law and posture that enter this event

The permitted §1292(b) appeal is the supported route. Swint's earlier jurisdictional vacatur does not decide the later, properly presented attribution question. The granted review concerns Tate's criminal-investigation and law-enforcement acts as final County policy, not the individual defendants' immunity, the truth of the allegations, jail administration, or every sheriff function. The lower court found no final County policymaking authority and expressly reserved whether Tate was a final State policymaker. Its reservation must not be reported as a lower-court finding of State status.

Monell's institutional-attribution requirement remains controlling. Pembaur allows a single authorized final policy decision; its four-Justice Part II-B should not be misreported as the whole Court's reasoning. Praprotnik's relevant plurality respects positive law while preserving legally effective custom, genuine delegation and ratification. Jett Part IV supplies the later majority instruction to identify the official who speaks with final policymaking authority for the particular governmental entity concerning the challenged action. Operational finality and the identity of the represented entity are separate questions.

Collins preserves the distinction between constitutional injury and institutional attribution. The actual May 27 Kinman institutional holding, joined by every Associate, requires policy/custom or an attributable final decision and specifically preserves the single-final-policymaker route. It does not decide which entity an Alabama sheriff represents. Its personal-supervisor culpability holding and distinct Title IX recipient rules do not replace Monell here. The pending Bryan County reargument supplies no merits rule; the complete historical McMillian writings do not depend on a Bryan County merits holding. No June 2 peer decision is antecedent authority. The other May 27 public decisions provide no sheriff-attribution rule.

These differences in the surrounding docket therefore do not remove a premise supporting any historical Associate position. Kinman is consistent with both application paths and cannot be used to manufacture a change in alignment. ## Proposition and remedy boundaries for assembly

1. **Common method and reachability:** All eight Associates support the properly presented §1292(b) route, the separate Monell attribution requirement, and the function-specific identification of the represented entity through state/local law and legally effective custom. A title, County funding, local election, geography, or absence of commission control is not independently dispositive. The case does not overturn Pembaur's single-decision rule or Jett's method.
2. **Affirmance application:** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support retaining the County dismissal because the relevant Alabama legal arrangement places the challenged criminal-investigative authority outside the County. They can explain the State character of that function without deciding whether the sheriff can establish every form of State policy or whether the State could be sued for damages. County payment does not supply an institutional policy link.
3. **Reversal application:** Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support reversing the attribution dismissal because the sheriff's own final local law-enforcement office supplies County authority without a commission-delegation requirement. Their common ground is Monell attribution for this office and function, not employer liability, statutory agency or a general preference for compensation. Further proceedings must address the remaining elements rather than assume a proved constitutional injury.
4. **Other components and remedy:** All eight leave individual-capacity claims and immunity, unreviewed evidentiary rulings, State damages liability or immunity, jail functions, different States' arrangements, and independently proved County policies/customs outside the present disposition. Affirmance does not approve the alleged misconduct. Reversal does not compel a trial or award money. No new factual inquiry, presumed State instruction, missing custom or litigant default is manufactured.
5. **Separate-writing function:** If both applications remain represented after lawful assembly, the contrary application must explain why the independently elected sheriff can himself embody County authority and why State classification/oversight does not defeat that conclusion. The other application must answer that independent-office argument by the actual legal allocation rather than by assuming County government means only its commission. Whether either is a majority, dissent or concurrence, and who writes it, remains for assembly.
