# Gasperini — authenticated operative text excerpts

Control extraction for neutral validation; June 24, 1996 cutoff. These are text quotations only. No same-case Supreme Court result, reasoning, vote or author is supplied. Ellipses remain express and are not represented as a complete provision.

New York CPLR §5501(c), as contemporaneously reproduced in the public opinion text:

> In reviewing a money judgment . . . in which it is contended that the award is excessive or inadequate and that a new trial should have been granted unless a stipulation is entered to a different award, the appellate division shall determine that an award is excessive or inadequate if it deviates materially from what would be reasonable compensation.

The complete lower opinion independently quotes the operative last clause at [66 F.3d 427, 430](https://static.case.law/f3d/66/html/0427-01.html). The quoted direction names the state appellate division; its relevance or permissible allocation within the federal system remains a legal question. This text does not itself prescribe a numerical cap.

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 59(a), pertinent jury-trial clause, as contemporaneously reproduced:

> [a] new trial may be granted . . . for any of the reasons for which new trials have heretofore been granted in actions at law in the courts of the United States

This is the pertinent text of the rule, without adopting any source opinion's construction of it. No timing issue is presented by this excerpt; no modern 28-day deadline is supplied. The quotation does not purport to reproduce the nonjury clause or the remainder of Rule 59.
