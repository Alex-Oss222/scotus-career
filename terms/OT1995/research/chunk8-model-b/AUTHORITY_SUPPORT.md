## Jaffee

- [Trammel v. United States, 445 U.S. 40, 47–53 (1980)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/445/40): Burger's opinion, joined by Stevens among the current Court, confirms Rule 501's authority for common-law development in light of reason and experience, examines state developments, and insists on a sufficiently important public benefit to justify withholding evidence. It narrows adverse-spousal-testimony privilege while preserving confidential marital communications. Its explanation distinguishes confidential professional communications from a privilege excluding all a spouse's adverse testimony. It neither creates a general physician privilege nor decides psychotherapy coverage.
- [University of Pennsylvania v. EEOC, 493 U.S. 182, 189–195 (1990)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/493/182): Blackmun's unanimous opinion was joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy among present members. It recognizes Rule 501's development authority but rejects a peer-review privilege where Congress specifically extended Title VII and broad investigatory access to universities, considered confidentiality and supplied limited confidentiality protection. It requires a defensible boundary and more than assumed benefits. The enactment-specific reason does not itself decide treatment privilege.
- The actual Daubert and Williamson authorities and exact joins are in `OT_1995CHUNK8_B_OPENING.md` and the Williamson entry in `terms/OT1993/output/OT_1993CHUNK8.md`. They provide evidence of each participant's handling of enacted Rules and context, not a psychotherapist-privilege vote. Kennedy's Williamson concurrence, joined by Thomas, permits related collateral assertions only with its two separately stated unreliability/favor-seeking exclusions. Ginsburg's application concurrence, joined by Stevens and Souter, does not establish a categorical exclusion of custodial confessions.
- [United States v. Butt, 955 F.2d 77, 80–87 & n.3 (1st Cir. 1992)](https://openjurist.org/955/f2d/77/united-states-v-butt): Coffin's opinion on a panel including Breyer addresses relevance, psychiatric-record privacy, in-camera review and opportunity to cross-examine. It does not decide that a federal psychotherapist privilege exists. Any analogy must preserve the distinction between excluding irrelevant/stigmatizing material and withholding relevant privileged treatment communications.
- Rule 501's complete contemporary text, including the state-law sentence, is already in the validated packet and statute extracts. Proposed Rule 504 remains unenacted. The packet supports widespread state recognition, not identical coverage or exceptions in every State. Neither these authorities nor partial disclosures establish wholesale waiver in this record.

## Egelhoff

- [Martin v. Ohio, 480 U.S. 228, 233–236 (1987)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/480/228): White's opinion was joined by Stevens, O'Connor and Scalia among present members. Its approval of defendant persuasion on self-defense expressly depends on the jury's ability to consider all the evidence when deciding whether the State proved the actual offense elements. It expressly distinguishes an instruction placing that evidence aside for purposes of the State's case. Powell's dissent was not joined by Stevens; no contrary personal join may be attributed to him.
- [Patterson v. New York, 432 U.S. 197, 201–215 (1977)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/432/197): the Court's opinion, joined by Stevens, preserves substantial legislative control over offense/affirmative-defense definitions, but requires proof of actual offense ingredients and recognizes limits on presumptions or relabeling. Rehnquist did not participate. The majority's reasoning is not authority for complete legislative freedom to remove the burden of proving an enacted element.
- [Rock v. Arkansas, 483 U.S. 44, 49–62, 62–67 (1987)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/483/44): Stevens joined Blackmun's opinion invalidating the wholesale exclusion of a defendant's hypnotically refreshed testimony, subject to legitimate nonarbitrary evidentiary restrictions. O'Connor and Scalia joined Rehnquist's dissent favoring state reliability rules in that unsettled scientific setting. Neither side treated every evidentiary exclusion as an offense-definition rule; reliability and the right to testify are distinct from the present retained-element dispute.
- [Hopt v. People, 104 U.S. 631, 633–634 (1881)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/104/631) distinguishes the traditional absence of a voluntary-intoxication excuse from consideration of intoxication under Utah's enacted particular-intent/degrees-of-murder provisions. It construes the territorial statute; it supplies no freestanding constitutional rule compelling every State to retain those provisions.
- Actual Medina preserves Kennedy/Scalia/Thomas's principal-opinion method and O'Connor/Souter's separate California-specific concurrence; it supplies no majority exclusion of practical-fairness analysis from criminal procedure. Actual Cooper (April 16, 1996), authored by Kennedy and joined by all current members, confirms the actual higher-burden limit without an exclusive methodology. Actual Gaudin, Staples, Ratzlaf and Victor retain their statutory scope and exact proposition-level joins.
- The complete §45-2-203 unknown-intoxicant qualification and the actual purpose/knowledge charge remain as in the packet. Neither the source work nor the statutory characterization determines an exact impairment finding, harmlessness, or the unresolved state assignments.

## Koon/Powell

- [Pierce v. Underwood, 487 U.S. 552, 558–563 (1988)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/487/552): Scalia's Part II, joined by Stevens among present members, makes issue-specific institutional competence, statutory context and limited generalizability relevant to deferential review of EAJA substantial justification. O'Connor joined White's separate opinion seeking independent review of legal substantial justification. Kennedy did not participate. Do not attribute a whole-opinion or Part II join to O'Connor.
- [Cooter & Gell v. Hartmarx Corp., 496 U.S. 384, 399–405 (1990)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/496/384): O'Connor's review Part IV was unanimous, including Stevens, Scalia and Kennedy. It protects fact-dependent Rule 11 judgments while treating erroneous law or clearly erroneous factual assessment as abuse. It does not make every mixed issue deferential and does not immunize categorical Guidelines permission errors.
- [United States v. Rivera, 994 F.2d 942, 946–952 (1st Cir. 1993)](https://openjurist.org/994/f2d/942/united-states-v-rivera): Breyer's opinion distinguishes forbidden, discouraged, encouraged and unmentioned considerations. Legal permission and textual scope receive independent review; genuinely case-specific unusualness and degree receive respect for trial competence. The suggested analytical sequence is not mandatory. Its application must yield to actual Williams and Stinson, including the actual same-sentence burden and prohibition on appellate replacement reasons.
- Actual Williams was written by O'Connor and joined by Stevens, Scalia, Souter and Thomas among present members. Actual Stinson was written by Kennedy and joined by those five. Ornelas's current eight-Justice review holding and Scalia's contrary position preserve issue-specific allocation; neither imports the Fourth Amendment review standard into every departure decision.

### Exact additional 1992 Chapter 5 provisions

Source: [official archived 1992 Chapter 5](https://www.ussc.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/guidelines-manual/1992/manual-pdf/Chapter_5.pdf), printed pages 325–326 (PDF pages 39–40). The local PDF/text is a downloaded source copy. These are full operative provisions, excluding only historical amendment notes.

**§5H1.5. Employment Record (Policy Statement)**

> Employment record is not ordinarily relevant in determining whether a sentence should be outside the applicable guideline range.
>
> Employment record may be relevant in determining the conditions of probation or supervised release (e.g., the appropriate hours of home detention).

**§5H1.10. Race, Sex, National Origin, Creed, Religion, and Socio-Economic Status (Policy Statement)**

> These factors are not relevant in the determination of a sentence.

**§5H1.11. Military, Civic, Charitable, or Public Service; Employment-Related Contributions; Record of Prior Good Works (Policy Statement)**

> Military, civic, charitable, or public service; employment-related contributions; and similar prior good works are not ordinarily relevant in determining whether a sentence should be outside the applicable guideline range.

These provisions distinguish categorical exclusion from discouragement; the second paragraph of §5H1.5 concerns conditions rather than a prison-range departure. They do not establish that loss of a police position is independently sufficient mitigation. Category I's lower bound and §5K2.10's complete threshold, five extent considerations and offense qualifications remain as in the full selected extracts. No later sixth factor is added.

## Rise

- Actual Vernonia, including Ginsburg's concurrence and O'Connor's dissent joined by Stevens/Souter, was read in the public OT1994 chunk 8 output. Its controlling search/no-waiver rule has all current Justices. Its five-Justice program rule is tied to documented athletic safety, objective selection, restricted collection and information, school confidentiality and nonpolice consequences. Ginsburg's concurrence is a full join emphasizing those limits, not a different controlling rule. O'Connor's dissent allows the possibility of proper suspicionless exceptions but finds that record insufficient to displace workable suspicion.
- [Skinner v. Railway Labor Executives' Association, 489 U.S. 602, 616–633 (1989)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/489/602): Kennedy's opinion was joined by O'Connor and Scalia; Stevens joined except portions of Part III and separately relied on the railway accident/safety record. Both bodily collection and chemical analysis are searches. Its nonprosecutorial railroad purpose and tightly described program matter; it does not announce a universal low-intrusion exception to suspicion for crime solving.
- [National Treasury Employees Union v. Von Raab, 489 U.S. 656 (1989)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/489/656): Kennedy authored the Court's opinion, joined by O'Connor. Scalia's dissent was joined by Stevens. Further use must identify the security/employment purpose and the specific disagreement over demonstrated need, rather than treat the title as an automatic rule for offender identification.
- Actual Hafer requires each individual defendant's own federal violation. Actual Sandin supplies the sufficient, nonexclusive entitlement rule and preserves direct constitutional liberty; it does not supply absent segregation conditions, causal acts by Knox/Pearce, or liability of unsued disciplinarians. The actual Whren and eventual Myers public updates require their own limited scope.
