## Alternative-path comparison

**A — Grant and separately vacate the mootness dismissal summarily.** If successful appeal could remove the still-unperformed retrial obligation, effectual relief remains possible. Commencing compliance with an unstayed order while maintaining the appeal does not, without more, extinguish the controversy. Vacatur returns the actual dismissed appeals for ordinary consideration; it does not automatically restore a constitutionally valid conviction or adjudicate the cross-appeal. The strongest objection is that granting a new trial spent the writ's release alternative and might make the State's chosen course irrevocable. The present record establishes neither completed retrial nor legal irrevocability, and a continuing obligation could still be removed. Whether this State complied sufficiently is separate and remains for its actual lawful disposition.

**B — Deny discretionary review or leave a genuinely established prudential ground intact.** Moore emphasizes the unpublished, unusual ruling and lack of a demonstrated split, and invokes equitable discretion. Lack of a split is a substantial institutional consideration but is not indispensable where the dismissal prevents consideration of a live federal habeas appeal on a simple recurring misconception. W.T. Grant recognizes discretion in granting equitable relief, not permission to deem every disputed continuing obligation constitutionally moot. The present two-line order contains no established independent prudential ruling. No such ruling should be invented to affirm. On this supported record A survives that objection; a different prudential question may be addressed below if properly available, not resolved in the State's favor by silence.

**Financial motion, independent of A/B.** The signed motion says no lower-court IFP leave was sought and identifies counsel's District Court appointment under the CJA. Under the actual 1995 Rule39.1, a qualifying statutory appointment excuses the ordinary affidavit/declaration if the motion cites that statute. The filed assertion invokes the correct route; no challenge to it or other filing defect is established in the supplied record. Grant leave and permit the Rule33.2 opposition. The appointment statement remains a filed assertion, not a newly verified order or account balance. Ordinary accompanying-document, copy, legibility, service and timeliness requirements remain; the packet establishes no defect in them, and this assessment claims no exhaustive docket inspection. Rule39.8's specified petition screening is not a finding that this represented respondent's opposition is frivolous. No modern appointment-order-copy or prisoner-account requirement is imported.
