### Age motivation, pension tenure and willfulness

**Governing question:** What instructions govern age motivation, pension tenure and willfulness?

**Current rule:** ADEA disparate treatment requires age actually to motivate and have a determinative influence on the employment decision. Service-based pension interference alone does not establish it; deliberate use of pension status as an age proxy or mixed age and pension motivation may. Age-triggered vesting and disparate impact remain unresolved. A disparate-treatment violation is willful if the employer knew or recklessly disregarded whether conduct violated the Act, for individual decisions as well as formal policies. Once shown, liquidated damages require no additional outrageousness, direct evidence or predominant-age motive. A good-faith nonreckless mistake about a statutory permission is not willful.

- Service-based vesting is distinct from age motivation: [Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins — Service-based vesting is distinct from age motivation](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1992/records/hazen_merits_1993-04-20.md), 1993-04-20. O'Connor’s Opinion of the Court, I; Stone-Zsela, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas (9 Justices). Direct majority agreement at this level of generality; no Marks inference.
- Thurston governs individual acts as well as formal policies: [Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins — Thurston governs individual acts as well as formal policies](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1992/records/hazen_merits_1993-04-20.md), 1993-04-20. O'Connor’s Opinion of the Court, II; Stone-Zsela, White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas (9 Justices). Direct majority agreement at this level of generality; no Marks inference.

**Present operation effective:** 1993-04-20; Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins, for the components identified above.

### Rejected employment explanations and circumstantial discrimination proof

**Governing question:** What instructions govern rejected employment explanations and circumstantial discrimination proof?

**Current rule:** After an employer produces admissible nondiscriminatory explanations, a proven prima-facie case and rejection of those explanations do not automatically command judgment for the employee. They may support intentional discrimination without a mandatory additional category of direct or independent proof. Burdine's persuasion allocation is unchanged; Hicks adopts no replacement allocation, universal Title VII/§1983 equivalence or determination of the ultimate finding. Stone's proposed shifted burden is noncontrolling.

**Authority by component:**

- Pretext alone does not command judgment, and circumstantial evidence need not be supplemented by a special category of proof: [St. Mary's Honor Center v. Hicks — Pretext alone does not command judgment, and circumstantial evidence need not be supplemented by a special category of proof](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1992/records/St_Mary_s_Honor_Center_v_Hicks_merits_1993-06-25.md), 1993-06-25. Scalia’s Opinion of the Court, this proposition; Stone-Zsela, O’Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Thomas (5 Justices). Direct majority support at the stated level of generality; no Marks aggregation.

**Present operation effective:** 1993-06-25; St. Mary's Honor Center v. Hicks, for the components identified above.

### Associations seeking statutory fee relief

**Governing question:** What instructions govern associations seeking statutory fee relief?

**Current rule:** An association is not categorically excluded from then-effective 28 U.S.C. §1915: the Dictionary Act's inclusive definition applies because ordinary agency and representation permit the affidavit, financial-inability and counsel provisions to operate. Context can displace that definition, but these features do not. An authorized affidavit must establish actual inability to pay costs or give security and asserted entitlement to redress. Courts may examine legally available resources and sham concealment without automatically aggregating every member's assets. Inability to hold an entity account or a balance-sheet deficit alone does not compel relief. Lawful representation and screening remain necessary; §1915 neither licenses lay entity advocacy nor guarantees appointed counsel. No universal organizational means test is established.

**Authority by component:**

- Holding 1 — associations may apply.: [Rowland v. California Men’s Colony, Unit II Men’s Advisory Council — Holding 1 — associations may apply.](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1992/records/rowland-v-california-mens-colony-merits-1993-01-12.md), 1993-01-12. Thomas’s Opinion of the Court, joined in full by Stone-Zsela, Blackmun, Stevens and Kennedy: five votes for this construction and its operative limits. Kennedy’s separate account of permissible workability analysis adds no condition to the Court’s holding.
- Holding 2 — eligibility does not establish the showing.: [Rowland v. California Men’s Colony, Unit II Men’s Advisory Council — Holding 2 — eligibility does not establish the showing.](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1992/records/rowland-v-california-mens-colony-merits-1993-01-12.md), 1993-01-12. The same five-Justice Opinion of the Court supplies this remand rule and limitation. The dissenters would require denial because of entity status and do not join the Court’s application process.

**Present operation effective:** 1993-01-12; Rowland v. California Men’s Colony, Unit II Men’s Advisory Council, for the components identified above.

### Factual frivolousness and review of fee-screening dismissals

**Governing question:** Dismissal as factually frivolous under then-effective 28 U.S.C. §1915(d), and appellate review of that screening determination.

**Current rule:** A court may dismiss a claim proceeding without prepayment when its factual allegations are clearly baseless—irrational or wholly incredible—even without judicially noticeable contrary facts. The initial assessment must favor the plaintiff; mere improbability, extraordinary circumstances or ordinary disbelief is insufficient, and screening cannot decide genuine factual disputes. Review is for abuse of discretion, with legal errors independently correctable. The reviewing court must address relevant circumstances, including pro se status, resolution of genuine factual disputes, legal errors, adequacy of reasons, prejudice and whether more specific pleading could cure the defect. Such dismissal is not a merits adjudication barring a paid complaint with the same allegations; its possible effect on later fee-screening decisions makes prejudice and curability material.

**Authority by component:**
- Irrational-or-wholly-incredible threshold and plaintiff-favorable limit: [Denton v. Hernandez](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1991/records/Denton_v_Hernandez_merits_1992-05-04.md), May 4, 1992, “Factual frivolousness under section 1915(d).”
- Review, relevant circumstances and nonmerits character: the same record, “Appellate review and the consequences of dismissal.” O'Connor for Stone, White, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas controls both propositions; the dissenters' agreement with the factual standard is not a whole-opinion join.

**Present operation effective:** Denton, May 4, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** Corroborating affidavits are not a pleading prerequisite. Rule 12(b)(6) merits pleading remains distinct from factual-frivolity screening.

**Open question:** No absolute notice-and-amendment entitlement or comprehensive preclusion rule for later fee applications is adopted.

**Holdings navigation:** [Denton v. Hernandez, 504 U.S. 25 (1992) — Fee-waiver screening and appellate review](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/state/HOLDINGS.md#denton-v-hernandez-504-us-25-1992).

### Equitable delay independently of a habeas procedural bar

**Governing question:** Whether an execution stay may lack equitable support even assuming the challenge is cognizable under §1983 and not barred as abuse of the writ.

**Current rule:** A court considering an execution stay may weigh the State's interest in enforcing its judgment and unjustified delay in presenting a long-available claim independently of a habeas procedural bar. A supported serious constitutional claim and the irreversible consequence of execution require consideration but do not alone establish entitlement to last-minute relief; later evidence strengthening the claim does not necessarily explain withholding the underlying challenge. Equitable vacatur on that ground may proceed while assuming an open §1983 channel and without adjudicating every McCleskey abuse element. The rule supplies a contextual equitable inquiry, not an automatic bar for late claims or an automatic capital stay.

**Authority by component:**
- Independent equitable ground, relevant considerations and procedural assumptions: [Gomez v. United States District Court](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1991/records/Gomez_v_United_States_District_Court_application_to_vacate_stay_1992-04-21.md), April 21, 1992, “Equitable basis for vacating the execution stay,” per curiam for White, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas, six votes. The unexplained postponement through four earlier habeas proceedings supplies the record application, not a minimum numerical prerequisite.

**Present operation effective:** Gomez, April 21, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** Delo's abuse-of-discretion stay review, Barefoot's substantial-ground requirements and McCleskey's rules within their proper habeas domain remain intact. Gomez does not prohibit later federal stays. The subsequent A-766 and A-768 unexplained orders create no reusable rationale; A-768's Harris-specific Court-authorization condition does not enlarge this general instruction.

**Open question:** The §1983/habeas boundary for execution-method relief, a full McCleskey application here, Younger abstention, final constitutionality of cyanide gas and a universal alternative-method requirement remain undecided.

**Holdings navigation:** [Gomez v. United States District Court for the Northern District of California, 503 U.S. 653 (1992) — Execution stays](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/state/HOLDINGS.md#gomez-v-united-states-district-court-for-the-northern-district-of-california-503-us-653-1992).

### Capital eligibility innocence as a gateway to barred habeas claims

**Governing question:** Review of successive, abusive or procedurally defaulted capital habeas claims without otherwise required cause and prejudice.

**Current rule:** The prisoner must show by clear and convincing evidence that, absent constitutional error, no reasonable juror would find the prisoner eligible for death under applicable state law. Examine the offense, required aggravating facts and every other necessary eligibility condition. Additional mitigation affecting only the discretionary selection of death does not satisfy this gateway. Opening the gateway permits consideration of an otherwise barred claim; it does not itself establish the claim or grant relief.

**Authority by component:**

- Eligibility-only scope, no-reasonable-juror inquiry and clear-and-convincing burden: [Sawyer v. Whitley, “Capital innocence as a gateway to otherwise barred claims”](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1991/records/Sawyer_v_Whitley_merits_1992-06-22.md), June 22, 1992; Kennedy's opinion joined by White, Scalia, Souter and Thomas. Broader judgment concurrences supply no component.

**Present operation effective:** *Sawyer*, June 22, 1992.

**Exceptions and limits:** Ordinary cause and prejudice, exhaustion, retroactivity, merits standards and proper factual-development rules remain distinct. Neither ordinary sentencing prejudice nor discretionary mitigation replaces this gateway. Freestanding innocence claims remain unresolved. No categorical exclusion of impeachment evidence follows from its insufficiency on Sawyer’s record.

**Holdings navigation:** [Sawyer v. Whitley, 505 U.S. 333 (1992) — Habeas procedural gateways](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/state/HOLDINGS.md#sawyer-v-whitley-505-us-333-1992).

**Related current rules:** Crime innocence as a gateway to otherwise barred habeas claims states Schlup’s distinct January 23, 1995 offense-innocence inquiry; it does not change this death-eligibility quantum.

### Capital habeas counsel before a merits petition and discretionary stays

**Governing question:** When an indigent capital prisoner may obtain qualified counsel and a stay while preparing federal habeas review.

**Current rule:** A defendant financially unable to obtain adequate representation or reasonably necessary services is entitled to commence the §848(q)(4)(B) representation proceeding before filing the merits application by requesting counsel to prepare an identified §2254 or §2255 challenge seeking to vacate or set aside a death sentence. For this postjudgment appointment, at least one attorney must ordinarily have five years’ admission to the relevant court of appeals and three years’ experience handling felony appeals there. For good cause the court may appoint another attorney whose background, knowledge or experience permits proper representation, considering the penalty’s seriousness and the litigation’s unique and complex nature. Upon an ex parte finding that investigative, expert or other services are reasonably necessary concerning guilt or sentence, authorization and payment are required at rates or amounts the court finds reasonably necessary, notwithstanding general criminal-case rates and maxima. The court may authorize services and payment retroactively if timely procurement could not practicably await prior authorization; it need not do so. Appointment alone establishes no entitlement to unidentified expenses.

**Authority by component:**

- Prepetition representation and statutory qualifications: [McFarland v. Scott — Qualified capital counsel may be appointed before the merits application](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1993/records/McFarland_v_Scott_merits_1994-06-30.md#public-projection), 1994-06-30. Part I of Justice Stevens's opinion, joined by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Blackmun, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg. Seven Justices directly adopt the statutory rule and its application to the October 22 motion.
- Pending preparation proceeding and express anti-injunction exception: [McFarland v. Scott — The statutory preparation proceeding satisfies §2251 pendency; Section 2251 supplies the express statutory exception to §2283](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1993/records/McFarland_v_Scott_merits_1994-06-30.md#public-projection), 1994-06-30. Part II of Justice Stevens's opinion, joined by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Blackmun, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg. Six Justices adopt this construction; O'Connor does not join it. Part III of Justice Stevens's opinion, joined by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Blackmun, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg. Six Justices directly adopt this statutory conclusion; it is distinct from the appointment entitlement and from the exercise of stay discretion.
- Reasonable preparation and individualized stay discretion: [McFarland v. Scott — Reasonable preparation and individualized stay discretion govern the remand](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1993/records/McFarland_v_Scott_merits_1994-06-30.md#public-projection), 1994-06-30. Part IV of Justice Stevens's opinion, joined by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Blackmun, Kennedy, Souter and Ginsburg. Six adopt the stay-discretion rule and common remand; O'Connor separately joins the appointment direction under Part I only.

**Present operation effective:** McFarland v. Scott, 1994-06-30.

**Sequence or decisionmaker:** Representation continues through available judicial and postconviction proceedings, stays and appropriate motions, and available competency and clemency proceedings unless similarly qualified counsel replaces the attorney on the attorney’s or defendant’s motion; the full reach into later state proceedings remains open. The statutory preparation proceeding is pending for §2251 purposes. The judge before whom it is pending may stay state action against the detained person concerning a matter involved in the habeas proceeding, before final judgment, after final judgment of discharge or pending appeal. Covered state action after a stay is void; without a stay, it remains valid. Section 2251 supplies §2283’s express statutory exception, without finding its other exceptions satisfied. A stay is discretionary, not automatic or indefinite. The prisoner must receive a reasonable preparation opportunity, while proved inexcusable disregard of that opportunity and obstruction can justify denial. The distinct prejudgment counsel qualification—five years in the trial-court bar and three years’ actual felony-trial experience there—does not replace the postjudgment standard.

### Crime innocence as a gateway to otherwise barred habeas claims

**Governing question:** The showing needed to overcome successive or abusive habeas barriers through innocence of the offense.

**Current rule:** The prisoner must present reliable new evidence and show, considering the whole probative record, that it is more likely than not that no reasonable, properly instructed juror would find guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. The inquiry includes the new evidence together with the existing record rather than deferring conclusively to the original jury’s credibility choices. Passing this demanding procedural gateway establishes neither innocence as a freestanding claim nor the underlying constitutional violation and shifts no ultimate merits burden. The court considers all relevant probative evidence, including evidence excluded at trial or becoming available afterward; trial-admissibility rules do not categorically delimit the gateway record, and even improperly admitted trial evidence may have probative force subject to reliability. The court must assess reliability and likely credibility, not accept every submitted statement as true. Timing, delay and inconsistency may matter; no categorical rule excludes impeachment evidence or discredits a witness solely because the witness is a prisoner.

**Authority by component:**

- Crime innocence invokes a demanding probability gateway: [Crime innocence invokes a demanding probability gateway](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1994/records/Schlup_v_Delo_merits_1995-01-23.md#public-projection), 1995-01-23. Justice Stevens's Opinion of the Court, gateway and all-evidence portion, joined by Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. Six of nine participants directly adopt this rule and explanation. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support a stricter crime-innocence quantum and would affirm.
- This legal error requires a bounded remand despite the statutory refusal permission: [This legal error requires a bounded remand despite the statutory refusal permission](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/main/terms/OT1994/records/Schlup_v_Delo_merits_1995-01-23.md#public-projection), 1995-01-23. Justice Stevens's Opinion of the Court, statutory-refusal and remand rationale, joined by Justices O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. These five directly adopt this bounded rationale. Chief Justice Stone-Zsela supplies a sixth vote for vacatur and the same gateway-assessment remand on his stated ground, without joining the separate §2244(b) analysis. No aggregation of different rationales is needed for the five-Justice holding.

**Present operation effective:** 1995-01-23; the component authorities identified above.

**Exceptions and limits:** Sawyer’s clear-and-convincing death-eligibility gateway is distinct. Section 2244(b) permits refusal of a subsequent habeas application by a person held under a state-court judgment after a federal court, justice or judge denied release or another habeas remedy on an earlier habeas application following either an evidentiary hearing on the merits of a material factual issue or a hearing on the merits of an issue of law, unless the later application alleges and rests on a factual or other ground not adjudicated in the earlier hearing and the court is satisfied that the applicant did not deliberately withhold the newly asserted ground on the earlier application or otherwise abuse the writ. Both exception conditions are required. New evidence is not automatically a new ground, and absence of deliberate withholding does not eliminate every other kind of abuse. When both conditions are met and other barriers, including nonexhaustion and procedural default, are absent, McCleskey requires consideration. The qualified refusal permission does not itself sustain refusal based on the rejected clear-and-convincing crime standard. Other lawful independent barriers and necessary factual-development rules remain.

**Burdens and proof:** The State must plead abuse with clarity and particularity by identifying prior writ history and newly raised claims and alleging abuse before the prisoner’s burden arises. Cause and prejudice and the exceptional innocence route remain separate.

**Open question:** Schlup does not decide whether every successful innocence showing compels review, a general appellate standard for refusal, or an automatic hearing, writ, retrial, release or stay.

**Related current rules:** Capital eligibility innocence as a gateway to barred habeas claims supplies the separate eligibility inquiry and proof quantum.
