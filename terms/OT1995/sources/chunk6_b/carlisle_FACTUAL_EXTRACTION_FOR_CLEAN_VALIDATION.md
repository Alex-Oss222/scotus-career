## Exact rule text recovered in government's brief

Rule29(a): court on defendant's motion or its own motion shall order acquittal after evidence on either side closes if insufficient to sustain conviction; title is Motion Before Submission to Jury. Rule29(b): permits reservation and later decision on trial motion, on evidence when reserved. Rule29(c): motion may be made or renewed within seven days after discharge, or further time fixed during that seven-day period; on that motion a guilty verdict may be set aside; no prior trial motion prerequisite. Rule29(d) addresses conditional ruling on a new-trial motion when postverdict acquittal granted. US brief2–3.

Rule45(a): exclude event day; include last day unless weekend/legal holiday/inaccessible clerk; under11 days excludes intermediate weekends/holidays. Rule45(b): general excusable-neglect enlargement expressly excludes actions under Rules29,33,34,35 except extent/conditions stated there. US app2a–4a. Rule2 requires just determination, simplicity, fairness and elimination of unjustifiable expense/delay. Rule57 version reproduced US app4a–5a permits practices only in cases not provided for and not inconsistent with Rules. Version caution: this brief reproduces trial-era Rule57; any Dec1,1995 amendment must be independently checked before quotation as event-date text. 28USC1651 permits necessary/appropriate writs in aid of jurisdiction agreeable to usages and principles of law. US app1a.
