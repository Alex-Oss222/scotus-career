offeror to treat all target shareholders equally.

that Matsushita, as part of its 1990 acquisition of MCA Inc.,

This case arises out of the acquisition of MCA Inc. in late 1990 
by Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., Ltd. and its subsidiaries 
("Matsushita") for consideration of over $6 billion.' 


Under the formal terms of its tender offer, Matsushita offered 
$66 per share in cash to every shareholder except Lew Wasserman, 
the chairman and owner of approximately 5 million shares of MCA 
common stock. In return for Wasserman’s backing of their tender 
offer, Matsushita entered into a special agreement with Wasserman, 
under the terms of which Wasserman exchanged his MCA stock for 
preferred stock in a Matsushita subsidiary (the "Wasserman 
transaction"). The Wasserman transaction was structured to be a 
tax-free transaction that would provide Wasserman with at /east

avoid ever paying over $113 million in income taxes.’
