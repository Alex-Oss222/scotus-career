# Libretti — bounded remedial requests

Objective source supplement only. These passages are party positions, not judicial findings. Scope inspected: petitioner merits brief printed pp. 49–50, reply printed pp. 19–20, government merits brief printed p. 34 and its footnote 12. No claim to a full reading of the reply.

The petitioner’s merits conclusion requests reversal and remand for inquiry into the factual basis for the forfeiture component and whether he knowingly waives a special forfeiture jury verdict. The reply §5 clarifies that he seeks a remand to determine whether he is now willing to waive; it expressly leaves relief if no waiver can be obtained undecided, because agreement over forfeiture scope may produce waiver.

The government’s brief p. 34 argues that rejecting a forfeiture stipulation does not itself undermine the acknowledgement of substantive guilt; the court could accept guilt and determine which assets are forfeitable at sentence. Footnote 12 separately preserves the right to withdraw a plea made contingent on acceptance of a specified sentence under then-Rule 11(e)(1)(C) when the court rejects that agreement. This is not a finding that this agreement is such an agreement.
