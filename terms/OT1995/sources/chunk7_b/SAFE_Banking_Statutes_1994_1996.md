## Exact support and limits

**Section 43.** Subsection (a) requires advance Federal Register notice describing each State law, at least 30 days for written comments, and consideration of comments before the specified final action. Its subject is an opinion letter or interpretive rule, on request or on the agency's own motion, concluding that federal law preempts application to a national bank of State law concerning community reinvestment, consumer protection, fair lending, or establishment of intrastate branches; it also covers a determination under §36(f)(1)(A)(ii). Subsection (b) separately requires Federal Register publication of the specified final actions.

Preserve the distinctions among exceptions: (c)(1) removes essentially identical previously resolved preemption issues and requests containing no significant legal basis; (c)(2) removes materials prepared for judicial proceedings, congressional submission, or intragovernmental use. These exceptions address the entire section. Under (c)(3), the agency may make exceptions only to subsection (a), upon the specified written serious-and-imminent safety-and-soundness determination or an opinion/rule connected to the specified default/danger-of-default acquisition or FDIC-assisted acquisition. This source alone does not establish that a particular action fits the trigger, satisfies an exception, or complied with procedure; it does not itself decide the underlying preemption issue.

**Section 93a.** The Comptroller may prescribe rules and regulations to carry out the responsibilities of the office, except to the extent authority has been expressly and exclusively granted to another regulatory agency. The conferred authority does not apply to §36 or national banks' securities activities under the Glass-Steagall Act. All these qualifications are part of the saved operative sentence.

**Section 93.** Subsection (a) requires judicial determination in a suit brought by the Comptroller before dissolution for the specified knowing violations, and addresses directors' personal liability. Subsection (b) gives civil-penalty machinery, including assessment by the Comptroller, differentiated culpability/harm tiers, maximums, a 20-day hearing-request period, mitigation/collection cross-references, and procedural regulations. Subsection (c) preserves jurisdiction over former institution-affiliated parties only if notice is served within the stated six years. The final paragraph, also designated (d) in the official source, permits the Comptroller to act in the Comptroller's own name and through the Comptroller's own attorneys in the listed enforcement and other proceedings. Preserve the official duplicate (d) and (b)(12) numbering; editorial notes identify them. The official Title 62 classification note includes §§85 and 86. These enforcement provisions do not themselves settle a disputed substantive construction.

**Sections 1818 and 1813.** Section 1818(b)(1) supplies notice-of-charges, hearing, findings/consent, cease-and-desist, and corrective-action authority on its stated violation/practice predicates; (b)(2) supplies effectiveness and stay/modification limits. Paragraphs (b)(3)–(5) preserve institutional and agency scope, including the Federal Reserve limitation for specified holding-company matters and the express extension to Comptroller-chartered national associations, including uninsured associations. Paragraphs (b)(6)–(10) preserve corrective-action qualifications, activity limits, examination-rating conditions, savings-and-loan scope, and the Rule 65 qualification. The excerpt also retains temporary-order predicates/review, court enforcement, APA hearing/review arrangements, civil-penalty procedures and limits, post-separation timing, and ancillary rulemaking tied to the stated proceedings/claims/examinations/investigations. Section 1813(q)(1) identifies the Comptroller for national banking associations, District banks, and federal branches/agencies of foreign banks; the full subsection warns that more than one agency may be appropriate. Definitions (u) and (x) retain their own participation, culpability, harm, and default qualifications.

## Timing and amendment check

Section 43 was added by Pub. L. 103–328, §114, September 29, 1994, 108 Stat. 2366–2368, without a special delayed effective date. The Act's §101(e), 108 Stat. 2343, delays amendments made by “this section” for one year; it does not extend that delay to §114. The full Act was checked for a title-wide delay affecting §114; none appears.

The operative text of §§43, 93a, 93, and 1818 is unchanged in the 1994 Code, 1995 Supplement I, and 1996 Supplement II (the latter through January 6, 1997). The selected §1813 definitions (q), (u), and (x) are likewise unchanged. Thus these checks found no intervening amendment before June 3, 1996. Later-edition material was used only to verify that interval, not as new substantive authority. Older conduct-dependent effective-date provisions remain in the saved §§93 and 1818 notes; do not erase them when applying a penalty provision.
