Uniform Code of Military Justice Article 18 
10 U.S.C. § 818 
Jurisdiction of general courts-martial 


Subject to section 817 of this title (article 17), general 
courts-martial have jurisdiction to try persons subject to 
this chapter for any offense made punishable by this 
chapter and may, under such limitations as the President 
may prescribe, adjudge any punishment not forbidden by 
this chapter, including the penalty of death when 
specifically authorized by this chapter. General courts- 
martial also have jurisdiction to try any person who by the 
law of war is subject to trial by a military tribunal and 
may adjudge any punishment permitted by the law of war. 
However, a general court-martial of the kind specified in 
section 816(1)(B) of this title (article 16(1)(B)) shall not 
have jurisdiction to try any person for any offense for 
which the death penalty may be adjudged unless the case 
has been previously referred to trial as a noncapital case.

Murder 


Any person subject to this chapter who, without justifica- 
tion or excuse, unlawfully kills a human being, when he— 


(1) has a premeditated design to kill; 
(2) intends to kill or inflict great bodily harm; 


(3) is engaged in an act which is inherently dangerous
