# FY1996 Medicaid appropriations through March 18, 1996

This is a separate, bounded statutory-source check. It establishes the appropriations continuation chain, without deciding any pending case, importing a later enactment, or admitting a source into the simulated chronology.

## Official source chain

1. **Pub. L. 103-333, approved September 30, 1994, 108 Stat. 2539.** The title identifies the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995. The account headed **Grants to States for Medicaid**, 108 Stat. 2554–2555, separately appropriates $27,047,717,000 for the **first quarter of FY1996**, to remain available until expended. The relevant prior-year general condition is **§ 509, 108 Stat. 2573**. Its full official text is preserved in `pl103_333.txt`, lines 1912–1917, and in the source PDF. Section 503, 108 Stat. 2572, supplies the fiscal-year obligation limit except where expressly provided otherwise. Do not infer from the Act's FY1995 title alone that all Medicaid authority ended September 30, 1995.
2. **Pub. L. 104-91, title I, approved January 6, 1996, 110 Stat. 7, 10–14.** Section 101(a), 110 Stat. 10, appropriates necessary amounts “under the authority and conditions provided in the applicable appropriations Act for the fiscal year 1995” for its listed activities. The list expressly includes **all projects and activities funded under “Grants to States for Medicaid”** at 110 Stat. 12. Section 102, 110 Stat. 13, preserves the extent and manner supplied by the pertinent appropriations Act; § 104, 110 Stat. 13–14, excludes newly proposed general conditions absent from the applicable FY1995 Act. Section 106, 110 Stat. 14, makes the authority available until the earliest of a replacement appropriation for the activity, enactment of the applicable appropriations Act without that activity, or **September 30, 1996**. Section 110, 110 Stat. 14, deems the covered period to have begun **December 16, 1995**. This is the direct Medicaid continuation provision relevant on March 18, 1996.
3. **Pub. L. 104-99, title I, approved January 26, 1996, 110 Stat. 26–27.** Section 101(a) continues additional Labor/HHS/Education and Interior activities under FY1995 authority and conditions, but expressly **excepts activities already provided for in Pub. L. 104-91 or 104-92**. Its § 106(c) originally names March 15, 1996. That shorter general deadline therefore must not be substituted for the distinct § 106 deadline of Pub. L. 104-91 when describing the Medicaid account. The relevant amendment to Pub. L. 104-91 in § 211, 110 Stat. 37–38, concerns the Justice Department paragraph, not the Medicaid paragraph.
4. **Pub. L. 104-116, approved March 15, 1996, 110 Stat. 826.** The complete one-section resolution extends the identified Pub. L. 104-99 deadlines from March 15 to **March 22, 1996**, and does the same for Pub. L. 104-92 § 106(c). It also specifies the House bill numbers in Pub. L. 104-99 § 101(a). It does not amend Pub. L. 104-91's Medicaid paragraph or September 30 outside deadline. It supplies the general continuing-resolution context as of March 18.

Thus the current-event source description should identify **the FY1995 condition continued for the Medicaid account by Pub. L. 104-91**, rather than merely quote an expired annual title. The preceding account-level advance appropriation and § 110 backdating also matter if the operator must trace continuity across the start of FY1996. This note does not independently reconstruct every funding measure or every account between October 1, 1995 and January 6, 1996; it gives the express source texts needed for the March 18 event.
