SB  969 Capital litigation; relating to prisoners sentenced to death.
Introduced by:
Thomas K. Norment, Jr.
|
all patrons
...
notes
|
add
to my profiles
SUMMARY:
Capital litigation.
Makes the Supreme Court of Virginia the exclusive
jurisdiction for filing habeas corpus petitions by a prisoner sentenced to
death. The bill also sets time limitations upon the filing of such petitions
following appeals to the Virginia and United States Supreme Courts and upon
requests for appointed counsel to represent an indigent prisoner sentenced to
death in a habeas corpus petition.
The bill also prohibits motions by the Attorney General objecting to
appointment of counsel for the petitioner and establishes a procedure for the
setting of execution dates.
