## Lower judgment

Read: `barnett_lower.txt`, opinion paragraphs 1–44 and both numbered footnotes plus visiting-judge note, from https://openjurist.org/43/f3d/631. Only the actual January 30, 1995 lower opinion was used; later citation-network metadata is not authority.

43 F.3d 631, No.93-3508, affirms the Middle District of Florida judgment after a bench trial. The panel is Fay, Cox and visiting district judge Carnes; Fay writes, and no separate writing is printed. The court found federal-question jurisdiction for prospective relief against enforcement of allegedly preempted state law, citing Shaw v. Delta Air Lines and Ex parte Young; statutory interpretation received de novo review and bench findings clear-error review.

Barnett is a subsidiary of Barnett Banks, Inc.; principal office Ocala, branch Belleview (under 5,000 inhabitants in last decennial census). It purchased Linda Clifford's agency, and she became a bank employee working inside the bank. The Department's Immediate Final Order, issued four days after the complaint, prohibited agency activities except credit life and credit disability sales. Complaint sought authority to market insurance regardless of customers' location.

The lower court held Florida §626.988 regulates the business of insurance because of its protection of insurer/potential-policyholder relations against coercion, concentration and solvency risk. It held §92 did not specifically relate to the business of insurance, emphasizing the banking purpose and pre-1944 conception of commerce power. These are the lower court's conclusions, not a new Supreme Court rule.

Two cross-appeal questions were expressly NOT reached: whether the Belleview branch satisfies §92's located-and-doing-business requirement, and whether qualifying bank sales may reach customers nationwide. Reversal of the McCarran premise alone cannot silently decide those preserved questions. The source packet's unqualified qualifying-location premise requires this distinction.
