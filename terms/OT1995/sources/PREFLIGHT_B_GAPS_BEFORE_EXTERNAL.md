# Neutral preflight B: gaps identified before external same-matter research

Scope: Citizens Bank v. Strumpf (1995-10-31), Louisiana v. Mississippi exceptions (1995-10-31), Libretti v. United States (1995-11-07), American Life League v. Reno (1995-11-13). This source-research context has read only Engine rules, assigned neutral-packet sections, the current neutral projection, and public tracker headings/substance. It makes no commitments or predictions.

- **Strumpf:** Verify the actual Fourth Circuit disposition and its review path; bankruptcy chronology, precise amount and duration of hold, request for relief and later account events; parties' concessions and preserved statutory theories under sections 362(a)(3), (6), and (7), 542(b), 553; statutory versions; petition, both merits briefs, joint appendix. The packet's generic relief description and absent conflict treatment are insufficient for a source-grounded remedy and live-path check.
- **Louisiana:** Obtain Master report and exceptions/response, including the evidence on continuity, Louisiana's proposed island definition and legal theory, burden/review formulation, and precise unexcepted private-title disposition. The numerical survey is needed for the later decree unless the exceptions depend on a particular segment. Do not invent coordinates or cancel private title.
- **Libretti:** Verify petition/question scope, Rule 11 and Rule 31(e) versions, plea agreement and full colloquy, property categories and objections, direct-appeal preservation, remand/final-order boundaries, statutory versus constitutional jury claims, and factual-basis issue. Obtain petition, both briefs and joint appendix before inferring what the record does not contain.
- **American Life League:** Read the full Fourth Circuit judicial text, complaint-based factual premises and preservation limits; authenticate FACE as enacted (all challenged language, definitions, exceptions, criminal/civil relief), RFRA operative provisions and enacted effective scope. Preserve distinct commerce, expression/vagueness/overbreadth, free-exercise, and RFRA paths under current Lopez/Harris, Lukumi, Swanner and Clearwater law. Established reconstructed merits review requires no invented Supreme Court docket or historical petition disposition.

Source gaps may be cured by objective source evidence. Any same-matter historical Supreme Court result, rationale, lineup or petition treatment exposure must be separately logged and excludes this researcher from affected neutral framing repair or independent modeling.
