### Henderson enacted text and transition source limits

- Official Pub. L. 97-462, 96 Stat. 2527–2530, recovered at `frcp1982_act.pdf/.txt`, was read in full. Congress itself enacted former Rule 4(j): dismissal without prejudice if service is not made within 120 days and the serving party cannot show good cause; notice before sua sponte dismissal; exception for foreign-country service under former subdivision (i). Section 4 made the amendments effective 45 days after January 12, 1983; §5 prevented the separately delayed 1982 proposals from taking effect. This was not solely a judicial rulemaking act.
- Existing official SAA sources are `../A_Henderson_SAA_41Stat525_1920.txt/.pdf` and `../A_Henderson_SAA_amend_74Stat912_1960.txt/.pdf`. The 1960 amendment changes only §742's first sentence; its §4 applies that amendment to proceedings brought after enactment. The valid-service sentence remains the 1920 text “United States **and** such corporation.” The 1960 first sentence ends “any corporation mentioned in section 1 of this Act.” The briefs' variant wording is not the exact enacted text.
- The 1920 §743 includes private-party principles/practice, costs and four-percent interest (or higher contractual rate), appeal/revision, election to proceed on in-rem principles without foreclosing proper in-personam relief, and government bond/stipulation exceptions. The private-party-practice sentence is not the whole section.

### Casarotto exact statutory scope

Official 1994 FAA §§2 and 3 are recovered as `faa_1994_sec2.html` and `faa_1994_sec3.html` and read fully. Section2's saving clause is for grounds existing in law or equity for revocation of **any contract**; its covered writings/transactions remain predicates. Section3 addresses “courts of the United States,” requires the court to be satisfied of referability, operates on a party's application, stays until arbitration in accordance with the agreement, and conditions the stay on the applicant not being in default in proceeding with arbitration. The state district court invoked §3, but the entering simulated *Allied-Bruce* expressly reserves wholesale state-court incorporation of §§3–4. Do not infer that incorporation from the lower order.

The full contemporaneous Montana §27-5-114, reproduced and visually verified in `casarotto_record_receipt.md`, distinguishes (1) existing controversies, (2) future controversies and its exclusions for personal injury, individual acquisitions of $5,000 or less, insurance/annuity agreements except insurer-to-insurer contracts, and workers' compensation, (3) agreements among trade/professional-organization members, and (4) the precise typed, underlined, capital-letter, first-page notice for arbitration “pursuant to this chapter.” Subsection2 is expressly qualified by subsection3. The actual decision rests on subsection4, not an adjudication applying the listed subject-matter exclusions. This text is a party-filed contemporaneous reproduction checked against the PDF, not independent official Montana-code authentication.
