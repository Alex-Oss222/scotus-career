# Seminole Tribe of Florida v. Florida — primary-source archive

517 U.S. 44 (1996); docket 94-12; decision date March 27, 1996.

Historical comparator-only. These files may be supplied to a fresh reconciliation context only after the independent non-Stone commitment freeze. They are not neutral modeling inputs.

## Official U.S. Reports

- [Original PDF](usreports-517-44.pdf): GovInfo, 142 pages, printed reporter pages 44–185.
- [Complete layout extraction](usreports-517-44.layout.txt): `pdftotext -layout -enc UTF-8 -eol unix`, with no page restrictions or content filtering.
- [PDF retrieval and extraction metadata](usreports-517-44.metadata.json), [pdfinfo output](usreports-517-44.pdfinfo.txt), and HTTP response headers are retained.

HTTP status, PDF magic, PDF parsing, case title/docket identity, page count, every expected printed page header, and nonempty text on every extracted page were checked. The source PDF is preserved byte-for-byte. The complete unmodified layout extraction preserves the tool's output; PDF-to-text conversion cannot be represented as perfectly lossless.

## Scope

This is an archival record, not a substantive legal analysis. The syllabus inventory, writing headers/tails, official PDF identity, and extraction coverage were checked. No complete substantive reading of the opinions is claimed, and no votes were modeled or reconciled. No neutral packet, commitments, Stone material, Decision Record, or term workspace was consulted.

See [machine-readable inventory](inventory.json) and [SHA-256 manifest](SHA256SUMS.txt). Hashes cover all archive files other than the hash manifest itself.
