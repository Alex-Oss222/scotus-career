SEC. 2. (a) Chapter 91 of title 28 of the United States Code is 
amended by adding at the end thereof the following new section: 
"§ 1506. Transfer to cure defect of jurisdiction. 
"If a case within the exclusive jurisdiction of the district courts 
is filed in the Court of Claims, the Court of Claims shall, if it 
be in the interest of justice, transfer such case to any district court 
in which it could have been brought at the time such case was filed, 
where the case shall proceed as if it had been filed in the district 
court on the date it was filed in the Court of Claims." 
(b) The analysis of chapter 91 of title 28 of the United States Code 
is amended by adding at the end thereof the following: 
"Sec. 1506. Transfer to cure defect of jurisdiction."
