## OT1995-050 — O’Connor v. Consolidated Coin Caterers Corp.

**Event:** 1996-04-01 | **Category:** MERITS | **Action:** Argued merits decision

**Citation:** 517 U.S. 308

**Docket(s):** No. 95-354

### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

**Position status:** Approved by the user as written on October 2, 2026; no Court vote or outcome entered.

- **Stone’s Controlled Judgment:** Reverse and remand.
- **Stone’s Remedy / Remand Position:** Permit the claim to proceed under the ordinary ADEA burden framework without deciding pretext or ultimate liability.
- **Stone’s Threshold Position:** Construe the ADEA’s forbidden reason before applying the prima facie evidentiary framework; do not turn the age-forty coverage line into an element the statute does not state.
- **Supporting Authorities:** 29 U.S.C. §§623(a)(1) and 631(a); *McDonnell Douglas Corp. v. Green*, 411 U.S. 792; *Hazen Paper Co. v. Biggins*, 507 U.S. 604.
- **Stone’s Proposed Holding for This Case:** An ADEA plaintiff need not show replacement by a person under forty; replacement by a substantially younger person may support an inference of discrimination because the statute forbids adverse action because of age, not preference across the statutory coverage boundary.
- **Stone’s Reasoning:** Section 631 identifies who may invoke the Act, while §623 identifies the prohibited reason. A fifty-six-year-old may be displaced because of age by a substantially younger worker who is also over forty. The prima facie inquiry must follow that causal logic while leaving the employer free to articulate a lawful reason and the plaintiff with the ultimate burden of proving age discrimination.
- **Stone’s Operative Limit:** A minor age difference or materially different circumstances may fail to support an inference, and the holding does not alter the plaintiff’s ultimate burden of proving that age caused the challenged action.
