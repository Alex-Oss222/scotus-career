#### B. JUDGMENT AND REMEDY

* **Stone’s Controlled Judgment or Disposition:** Reverse the Eleventh Circuit’s judgment and remand. This is Stone's approved vote.
* **Stone’s Remedy and Remand Position:** The lower court shall provide relief preventing application of the conflicting prohibition to the bank’s qualifying §92 activity. Compatible state insurance rules remain available and are not adjudicated in the abstract.

#### C. ISSUE POSITIONS

##### Issue 1: Federal permission and the state prohibition

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the conflict between the two enacted provisions; do not infer an independent banking power broader than §92.
* **Stone’s Legal Position:** Enforce the specific federal authorization against the categorical contrary prohibition while preserving compatible state regulation.
* **Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Florida may not apply this prohibition to prevent a national bank from exercising the insurance-agent authority conferred by §92. The categorical ban conflicts with the federal grant.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** The federal permission would do little work if a State could negate the authorized activity altogether. That does not make all regulation of the activity incompatible: neutral supervision of sales can coexist with the power to sell. This case presents the prohibitory end of the spectrum, so a comprehensive catalog of permissible state requirements is unnecessary. [P1, 31–37; S1, Myrick]
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** No blanket displacement of insurance licensing, consumer protection, or contract law. A different state requirement must be tested for its actual conflict.

##### Issue 2: McCarran-Ferguson

* **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Reach the enacted exception because it determines whether the ordinary conflict has legal effect.
* **Stone’s Legal Position:** Apply the operative subject matter of §92, not only the institutional label of the statute containing it.
* **Stone’s Proposed Holding:** Section 92 specifically relates to insurance within §1012(b); McCarran-Ferguson therefore does not preserve Florida’s conflicting prohibition.
* **Essential Reasoning and Record Application:** A provision expressly identifying who may act as an insurance agent regulates the business of insurance even though it appears in a banking enactment. Reading specifically relates to require specifically preempts would insert different words and erase the exception’s independent function. [P1, 37–42]
* **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not weaken McCarran-Ferguson for federal enactments that do not specifically relate to insurance.
