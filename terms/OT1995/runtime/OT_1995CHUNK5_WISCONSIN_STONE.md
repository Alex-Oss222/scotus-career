### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

**Position status:** User-selected or delegated position; procedural and legal implementation authorized October 1, 2026; no Court vote or outcome entered. The user's selections control; the three roadmaps provide context rather than predetermined outcomes.

**Stone’s disposition:** Reverse the Second Circuit and uphold the Secretary's refusal to adjust this census on the reviewed record.

**Stone’s reasoning and application:** An actual enumeration requires a reasonable relationship between the chosen method and the constitutional task. Improving a nationwide net count and improving the distribution of population among jurisdictions are not identical. The Secretary considered the distributional effects and uncertainty of the proposed adjustment; the Court may not replace that choice merely because another technique could improve one measure. Differential undercount is serious, but it does not by itself establish discriminatory intent, an unreasonable enumeration or a judicially required statistical method. Apply that existing source-specific standard without inventing a presumption of either perfect accuracy or unreviewable discretion.

**Relief and limits:** Reject the judicially compelled adjustment while preserving challenges to proven discrimination, an unauthorized method or an actual unsupported reason. Simulated Franklin preserves the distinction between reviewable legal conduct and treating the President or a nonfinal report as an ordinary final agency action. No general exemption from constitutional review and no rule forbidding all statistical adjustment follows.

**Limit:** Equal opportunity requires lawful census administration, not adoption of a preferred statistical technique regardless of the governing source and record.
