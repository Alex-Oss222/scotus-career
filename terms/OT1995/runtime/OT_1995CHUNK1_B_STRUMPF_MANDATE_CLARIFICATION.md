## Boundary that all eight retain

An acceptable formulation is:

> The judgment is reversed, and the case is remanded for proceedings consistent with this opinion. This mandate does not identify any further issue as procedurally open, direct a new evidentiary inquiry, or reopen the unappealed contempt disposition, the later setoff authorization, the unpreserved confirmation contention, or another final matter. Any otherwise properly remaining question may be addressed only through its ordinary lawful channel.

The shorter proposed formulation — 'reverse and remand for proceedings consistent with this opinion, preserving only properly remaining Code/setoff questions through ordinary channels' — has the same permitted scope **only if** its surrounding opinion carries those boundaries. 'Properly remaining' is conditional: it does not find that any new issue actually remains in this litigation, overcome preservation/finality, or revive the earlier independent direction to investigate timing, validity, excess, coercion or confirmation.
