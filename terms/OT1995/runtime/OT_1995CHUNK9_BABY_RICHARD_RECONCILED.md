## Reconciled positions and material join limits

**Limited-process position.** The child has an independent liberty interest against serious, avoidable injury from the State's manner of abruptly severing an established caregiving relationship to implement an otherwise lawful custody transfer. In the supported circumstances, the State may not categorically reject the child's request to present concrete evidence confined to transition harm and practicable implementation measures. The child's representative must receive a meaningful, prompt opportunity to present that proffer and the father must be able to respond before compulsory implementation; the state court must consider the issue under Mathews and give a reasoned implementation decision. This is a proposed doctrinal development, not a holding already contained in Smith, Parham, Flores, Heller or Pinder.

The process is fitted to the actual showing. It prescribes no automatic evidentiary hearing, full trial, clinician, proof burden, visits, fixed delay or optimum-welfare outcome. Unsupported requests may be rejected. A meaningful inquiry can result in immediate implementation; it cannot become indefinite nonparent custody or a renewed choice between lawful custodians. Genuine emergency authority remains, but no emergency is invented in this case.

**Affirmance position.** The supplied caregiving relationship, lawful turnover and possible transition trauma do not establish the proposed independent constitutional liberty predicate for additional process. Mathews cannot create that predicate, and institutional confinement differs materially from restoration to a fit parent. Humane state-law measures remain available. This position does not declare every transfer method immune from constitutional scrutiny, infer harmlessness from absent clinical evidence, or decide an independently supported arbitrary affirmative-injury claim not established here.
