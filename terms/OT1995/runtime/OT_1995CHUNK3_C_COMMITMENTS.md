## United States v. Hale — February 12, 1996

### Validated scope and entering law

The Eighth Circuit affirmed thirteen §922(o) machinegun-possession convictions and three §5861(d) unregistered-firearm convictions. Its source supplies the constitutional and registry-proof objections, but does not supply a preserved Staples instruction defect, an actual rejected registration application, or a count-by-count finding of when each weapon became a machinegun. None is inferred. A separate modern due-process impossibility claim is not silently substituted for the presented constitutional-source argument.

The complete §922(o) arrangement matters: the prohibition covers transfer and possession, subject to the governmental-authority exception and the exception for lawful transfer or possession of a machinegun lawfully possessed before the provision's effective date. This is a limitation on the lawful machinegun supply and transfer market, not merely a location-based possession ban. The NFA registration system separately serves making and transfer taxes. Its continuing tax function must be identified, rather than upheld under a freestanding federal police power.

Lopez forbids the school-possession/crime-cost/education/productivity chain and forbids adding a commerce element the statute does not contain. Harris, adopted on its commerce ground by all eight Associate Justices, permits economic-class regulation within the actual interstate vehicle-theft scheme. Neither supplies a universal jurisdictional-element requirement. Staples requires knowledge of whole-weapon machinegun characteristics in its stated setting and preserves Freed's separate nonregistration rule; it establishes no error in Hale's unproduced charge.

The lower opinion's statement that Miller upheld a conviction is incorrect. Miller reversed a dismissal on demurrer and remanded. Its militia-related inquiry does not establish Hale's proposed automatic immunity for every weapon useful in war. Hale supplied no evidence relating this possession to preservation or efficiency of a well-regulated militia. This permits rejection of his actual argument without adopting the lower court's broadest collective-right language or resolving the full individual-right question.

The two ATF certifications state that a diligent search disclosed no application by Hale to register his weapons. Counsel conceded the identified exception's firmly rooted character and Rule 902(1) authentication. A conceded evidence-rule predicate does not itself waive every constitutional objection. Under the inherited Roberts framework, however, that established exception and the administrative-record character of this proof support admission. Dutton and Inadi prevent transforming Roberts's former-testimony discussion into an unavailability prerequisite for every hearsay category. The 1979 Senate material was offered for the truth of disputed claims about registry reliability; the trial judge rejected it as hearsay and stale, not because registry accuracy is conclusively immune from challenge. Rule 201 does not permit judicial notice of those disputed conclusions merely because a hearing occurred.

## Sherman v. Community Consolidated School District 21 — February 12, 1996

### Validated scope and entering law

The authorized assigned review concerns the school-led Pledge containing “under God,” on a record permitting abstention. The complete lower text confirms that pupils need not speak, stand, place a hand over the heart, or leave the room; silence and remaining seated are unpunished. The teacher's evidence said she prohibited hazing and had observed none. The father's contrary assertions were rejected because his affidavit did not establish personal knowledge; no finding of actual coercive conduct or peer harassment may be added.

The lower disposition separately ordered dismissal of the Illinois Attorney General: the official-capacity damages claim was barred and no enforcement connection supported prospective relief against that officer. The assigned constitutional issue against the school defendants does not reopen that distinct disposition. The child's actual exposure to the ongoing official exercise supplies a live prospective controversy; no injury to the unappealing Society is needed. No unverified Supreme Court docket or argument date is added.

Lee's controlling sponsorship holding forbids the actual official graduation worship without requiring a separate quantified-pressure showing. Its independent pressure holding forbids the substantial religious participation pressure identified there. These grounds do not already classify the Pledge as worship. Good News, effective December 11, 1995, and Pinette distinguish actual private expression from governmental religious action. This Pledge is governmental speech: calling pupils' participation voluntary does not make the program private. Its constitutionality therefore requires examination of its own civic content and context under inherited establishment law, not a forum immunity.

### Two lawful paths and controlling distinction

**S1 — provisional adopted merits path:** Affirm the merits judgment on the particular program. The Pledge addresses allegiance to the flag and Republic; the short traditional acknowledgment in that civic declaration, without a prayer, religious instruction, worship leader, or required religious profession, does not turn the whole exercise into school worship. Its civic educational and ceremonial function and the actual absence of compelled affirmation matter together. This is a bounded classification under existing law, not a new exception for anything labeled patriotic. Barnette still prohibits compulsory affirmation, gestures of assent, or punishment; if officials add devotional instruction, direct prayer, or use the phrase to require belief, this holding does not authorize it.

**S2 — strongest contrary path:** Reverse because the State selects and repeatedly presents a proposition concerning God to young compulsory-school pupils. A nominal opt-out cannot cure official religious sponsorship under Lee, Engel or Schempp. That path would support prospective relief against the official religious component without suppressing genuinely private speech. Its obstacle on this record is the character of the exercise: those authorities address actual worship, prayer or devotional instruction, while Engel's retained patriotic/historical distinction and the pre-divergence Lynch and Allegheny discussions leave room for civic acknowledgments. The religious words must be taken seriously, but their occurrence is not a rule that every such civic phrase is worship. Silence alone would be insufficient if S2's religious-program classification were established.

**Framework support:** Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer support contextual examination under the existing purpose/effect/entanglement principles. They find a civic purpose and function, no primary governmental religious advancement in this bounded use, and no religious-institutional entanglement. Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas support the concrete civic-acknowledgment conclusion, the distinction from official worship, and Barnette's protection; they do not join a comprehensive Lemon/endorsement explanation. These latter three votes cannot be counted for that methodology.

No Justice's commitment rests on the proposition that the phrase literally has no religious meaning to anyone. The five contextual supporters may describe its established ceremonial function without directing believers to treat it as meaningless. All eight reject a universal historical-practice immunity and a rule that voluntariness alone validates official school prayer.

## Fulton Corp. v. Faulkner — February 21, 1996

### Validated record and entering law

North Carolina's annual intangibles tax applies to residents' shares at twenty-five cents per $100 of market value, reduced according to the issuing corporation's income apportioned to North Carolina. For a corporate shareholder the linked dividend-deduction proportion determines the reduction, without the ordinary $15,000 dividend-deduction cap. The corporation's income tax and shareholder's property tax concern distinct taxpayers and taxable events. The lower court's discussion of common price/earnings ratios is a justification for its judgment, not a legislative command or a finding that every affected taxpayer faces matched taxes. It describes a 7.75% corporate rate but expressly notes the pre-1991 rate was 7%; no 7.75% finding is attributed to the 1990 tax year claimed here.

The intermediate court invalidated the discrimination but severed the deduction and denied Fulton a refund. Both sides sought review. The state Supreme Court sustained the tax and expressly did not decide refund and fee questions. Its reversal does not establish that the intermediate court's severability choice is constitutionally required. The other state/federal constitutional theories were not properly presented there; this handoff decides the Commerce Clause question and preserves lawful remedy proceedings.

The operative Standards entry is “Compensatory taxes and the actual equivalent local burden,” effective May 23, 1994 through Oregon Waste and Associated Industries. The State must identify a real intrastate burden, demonstrate approximate and nonexcessive equivalence, and establish substantially equivalent taxable events. Each condition does independent work. West Lynn requires examination of the linked operation, but does not make every fiscal relationship a constitutional offset. Reich v. Collins, the actual tax-refund decision copied in the entering-law slice, governs a promised postpayment remedy; the unrelated Thunder Basin material under the packet's broad “Reich” label supplies no tax rule.
