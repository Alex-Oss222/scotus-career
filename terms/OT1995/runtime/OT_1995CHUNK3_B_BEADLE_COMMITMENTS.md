## Governing rule and components

Section 2000e(j) defines religion to include all aspects of religious observance, practice and belief unless an employer demonstrates inability to accommodate the employee's or prospective employee's observance or practice reasonably without undue hardship on the conduct of its business. Section 2000e-2(a) forbids the relevant religious discrimination in employment. The enacted duty applies to the City; a neutral employment rule and absence of religious animus do not alone satisfy the distinct accommodation obligation.

**E1 — Legal standard:** Preserve Hardison, 432 U.S. 63 (June 16, 1977), and Ansonia, 479 U.S. 60 (November 17, 1986). Under their event-date rule, the employer need not incur more than a de minimis cost. Concrete lost efficiency, materially deficient training and consequent operational or safety burdens may qualify without an additional cash expenditure. Once an employer supplies an effective reasonable accommodation, it need not furnish the employee's preferred accommodation or disprove every alternative. Here the ordinary schedule and one isolated Saturday leave did not resolve the continuing conflict; the separate hardship justification therefore matters. No categorical public-safety exemption, automatic right to a fixed Sabbath schedule, cash-only test or new heightened hardship threshold is adopted.

**E2 — Error and application:** Vacate the adverse Title VII accommodation judgment. The district court expressly relied on the ordinary neutral system and the general nature of police duties despite finding no demonstrated deprivation of another trainee's scheduling benefit. The court of appeals' training rationale treats a concession concerning one trainer as answering a record presenting multiple-trainer alternatives. Legitimate training and safety concerns must be evaluated as to the supported alternatives and actual operations. General respect for police administration and loss of one-trainer educational variety do not independently establish hardship for all materially distinct alternatives. The defect is in the inquiry actually conducted, not merely the absence of a transcript in the preparation packet.

**E3 — Remedy and boundaries:** Remand through the Eleventh Circuit for application of the proper standard, with any necessary further proceedings in the district court. Preserve the employer's ability to prove actual training, efficiency, safety or legally protected coworker burdens, and the factfinder's ordinary role and standards of review. The existing record may suffice if properly evaluated; no automatic new trial or new evidentiary hearing is commanded. No proposal is ordered, no permanent downtown vacancy is found, and no damages or final accommodation entitlement is awarded. The distinct release determination remains outside review. RFRA and constitutional free exercise are not substituted for Title VII, and no later statutory or judicial standard is applied.

Eight non-Stone Justices support E1–E3. Their common rationale is a bounded Title VII remand, not recognition of a Sabbath entitlement at any operational cost.

## Complete alternative and qualification audit

**Affirmance on a sufficient accommodation:** A neutral rotating schedule may accommodate some workers or some conflicts, but it did not resolve this credited conflict; one Saturday off was not a continuing solution. Ansonia does not convert every neutral rule into an accommodation. The Court recognizes no separate liability for failure to discuss alternatives if the employer in fact supplies a reasonable accommodation or proves the required hardship.

**Affirmance on actual hardship:** Training deterioration and consequent safety risk can exceed de minimis cost even without new expenditure. The City may use concrete professional evidence and reasonable operational inference; it need not await an accident or express every burden in dollars. The remand follows because the identified findings and concession do not settle the different multiple-trainer proposals, not because the Court has disbelieved all the command testimony or imposed a cash-only proof rule.

**Mandatory accommodation:** The petition's alternatives are supported proposals, not established entitlements. Later changes do not prove original availability. A possible downtown assignment is not an available guaranteed vacancy. Extensions offered to other recruits bear on comparative feasibility but may still create actual costs here; secular extensions do not automatically make a religious extension costless. Nor does §2000e(j) require invalidating actual contractual seniority rights. Here the particular training clause and no-required-swap finding prevent simply assuming such a conflict.

**Constitutional objection:** The City invokes religious preference and its public duties. This limited application of a statute bounded by reasonable accommodation and hardship neither establishes an absolute Sabbath veto over coworkers nor resolves a separately presented constitutional challenge. The Court need not decide the constitutionality of a hypothetical unconditional exemption to apply the governing Title VII rule.

**Remedy:** The proper judgment is vacatur of the accommodation judgment with a bounded remand, not judgment for Beadle, an order of reinstatement, a damages award, wholesale restructuring of police schedules or reopening of the release. The lower courts must make the legally material assessment on the actual record; further fact development is only as lawfully necessary. The City may again prevail.
