## Reconciled result

Retain all eight provisional commitments: Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg, and Breyer would affirm and join the same sufficient statutory ground. Waiting incident to return transportation after the assignment is completed, when no additional train-movement responsibility or other service is required, falls within §21103(b)(4)'s return-transportation category. It counts as neither on-duty time nor the off-duty time required before a new assignment. Actual service remains on duty under §21103(b)(2)–(3). No Justice requires a separate writing or judgment-only qualification on that ground. This does not determine Stone's position, the Court's final count, or any opinion author.

The neutral commitments' full statutory conditions and remedial limits remain part of this handoff. In particular, the ordinary rest requirements, subsection (c)'s distinct emergency permission, and subsection (b)(7)'s separate interim-rest exception are not excused or merged into the return-transportation rule.
