## Reconciled operative positions

**A — monetary immunity applies here.** Sections 504(a), 505(a)(2) and the 1986 State-remedies provision do not unequivocally consent to compensatory damages against the United States for this federally conducted academy program. Agency coverage and prospective enforcement remain, but a reference to federal assistance providers does not clearly reach all agency-operated programs. Actual Nordic Village and Ohio require consent to the particular money remedy; Franklin's ordinary-remedy rule does not provide that missing federal consent, and Meyer keeps waiver, cause and ultimate recovery distinct. Section 505(a)(1)'s employment route, §505(b)'s discretionary fee route and §1003's State-directed operation retain their own scope.

**B — the combined enactments sufficiently authorize this remedy.** Congress's express extension of §504 to federal programs, the established remedial context, the linked enforcement provisions and later acknowledgment of legal remedies together suffice for compensatory relief in this statutory action. A standalone phrase covering all federal operators need not recur in the assistance-specific remedial channel. Franklin supports the available remedy once consent is established; neither remedial purpose alone nor the violation itself waives immunity. B rejects the categorical damages bar and remands without deciding entitlement, amount or any remaining lawful defense.
