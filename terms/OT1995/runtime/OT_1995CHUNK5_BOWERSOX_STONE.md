## OT1995-053 — Bowersox v. Williams

**Event:** 1996-04-09 | **Category:** APPLICATION | **Action:** Application to vacate execution stay

**Citation:** 517 U.S. 345

**Docket(s):** application No. A-828

### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

**Position status:** User-selected or delegated position; procedural and legal implementation authorized October 1, 2026; no Court vote or outcome entered. The user's selections control; the three roadmaps provide context rather than predetermined outcomes.

**Stone’s disposition:** Request an immediate supplemental record and preserve the ability to decide the application through a short administrative stay. Do not yet grant or deny Missouri's request to dissolve the Eighth Circuit stay on its ultimate grounds.

**Stone’s reasoning and application:** Execution before the Court can identify the lower court's substantial grounds would make review irretrievable. The lack of an explanation is a reason to demand one, not a finding that a successive habeas claim is substantial. A tightly limited administrative interval respects both the State's judgment and the Court's responsibility to decide the actual application on a reviewable basis. Delo's equal division supplies no controlling rationale; McFarland's first-petition counsel rule and Schlup's gateway have their own predicates. AEDPA is not yet enacted on April 9, 1996.

**Mechanism and proposed interim order:** Justice O’Connor, the Eighth Circuit Justice under the August 3, 1994 allotment, presents and refers the application to the full Court. Stone proposes that, upon service of the Court's order, the lower court supply its stay order, reasons, identified claims and relevant successive-petition findings within two hours; the parties may submit concise responses within two further hours. The execution is administratively stayed until the full Court resolves the pending dissolution application or twelve hours after service, whichever comes first, unless the Court expressly extends protection. Require a Court review before the twelve-hour expiration, with the Clerk confirming actual notice to the executing authorities. These are proposed relative deadlines, not assertions that such an order has issued or that it changes the event date.

**Order form and consequence:** “The application presented to Justice O’Connor and by her referred to the Court is held pending receipt of the requested supplemental record. Execution of the sentence of death is temporarily stayed on the terms stated in this order.” Make clear that administrative protection neither dissolves nor adjudges the underlying Eighth Circuit stay. After the record arrives, apply the substantial-grounds standard to the actual successive-petition posture; Stone selects no final grant or denial in advance. All nine participate, and the Court's majority decides. No merits opinion or new innocence holding is required.

**Limit:** Urgency supports only the bounded protection stated here, not an automatic stay rule. The presentation route does not resolve the application’s legal predicates.
