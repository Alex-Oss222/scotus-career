## Channel and component map

Section 1254(1) permits review of the certified interlocutory Federal Circuit judgment; section 1292 supported the intervening appeal. An unliquidated damages amount does not defeat this channel. Section 1491(a)(1) supplies jurisdiction but no contractual duty. The en banc court found an express Winstar agreement and did not independently resolve implied-in-fact formation. The three contract sets and the separate Statesman goodwill and capital-credit terms require distinct application.

The components are (A) express formation and continuing scope, separately for Glendale, Winstar and Statesman; (B) actual contracting authority; (C) unmistakability and reserved sovereign powers; (D) sovereign acts and ordinary impossibility; and (E) the partial-liability judgment and remaining proceedings. FIRREA's validity, a damages amount, and the unadjudicated takings theories are not automatically before the Court for decision.

## Committed legal conclusions and opposing paths

### Actual contracts and what was promised

The stronger affirmative construction is that the integration provisions preserve contemporaneous resolutions and letters as terms of the bargain. The accounting treatment was specified transaction by transaction in exchange for assuming failing institutions' obligations. That is more than a regulated business's expectation that background rules will continue. Romein requires identification of a promise; it does not exclude an actual negotiated promise merely because it addresses regulation. Hercules demands an actual authorized undertaking and preserves genuine warranties; it does not turn the absence of the asserted third-party-cost promise there into a universal government-contract defense.

For **Glendale**, the preserved Board documents and accountant treatment identify $18 million amortized over twelve years and $716.666 million over forty years, subject to satisfactory accounting and R-31b compliance. Those terms identify what the Government furnished in exchange for the acquisition. A finding that the accounting condition was satisfied comes from the lower judgment, not a newly invented concession. A ten-year SAA termination provision must be reconciled with the more specific treatment periods rather than silently deleted or treated as an automatic forty-year sovereign freeze.

For **Winstar**, section 16 preserves the merger documents and contemporaneous resolutions/letters. The July 13 letter confirms the understanding that purchase-accounting intangibles may be amortized, for regulatory reports, over no more than thirty-five years. This is permission to use the agreed treatment, not an obligation to use the maximum period. The affirmative construction distinguishes changes to the required level of net worth from repudiation of the agreed components countable toward it. It does not convert the reporting provision into immunity from every later capital rule. Section 13 ordinarily terminates the Assistance Agreement after two years except as otherwise specifically provided; section 15 bars required unlawful action; those provisions are real interpretive counterarguments. On the affirmative construction, the specifically preserved multi-year accounting undertaking would otherwise be emptied of its negotiated duration, and payment for its breach does not compel unlawful accounting after FIRREA.

For **Statesman**, the $26 million capital-credit promise is particularly explicit: $21 million of the FSLIC contribution plus $5 million debenture principal shall count as regulatory capital under the identified regulation. Its approximately $25.8 million acquisition goodwill is a different asset and promise; the goodwill allowance is no more than twenty-five years, straight line, with the satisfactory accountant-analysis condition within ninety days. The five-year assistance term, reference to the regulation, integration and lawful-action provisions must be read together. FIRREA and the ensuing regulation changed treatment while the five-year period was still running; the later expiration issue can affect damages without making the initial promise nonexistent. Congress's supervisory-goodwill requirements and OTS's treatment of capital credits under section 567.1(w) must not be attributed to the same statutory words.

The strongest Government construction is different: the writings consent to treatment lawful when approved, while compliance, changing-capital, lawful-action and termination provisions retain subsequent-law effects. Neither incorporation nor a long amortization ceiling expressly says the Government will insure the thrift against later legislation. Hercules and Cisneros require enforcement of the undertaking actually made, including qualifications, rather than an economically desirable guarantee. The meaningful narrower alternatives are a differentiated construction for each instrument, or a remand addressing a specifically unresolved contract question; a remand cannot be justified merely because an operator has not read every appendix page.

### Authority, sovereign power, and risk

Former section 1725(c)(3), the transaction-effective section 1729(f) assistance powers, and the actual Board resolutions authorize contracts and these merger-assistance transactions. Their applicable conditions matter. The packet supplies no actual failure of the statutory determinations or approval. The affirmative position recognizes monetary responsibility for an authorized contractual undertaking; it does not assume power to forbid Congress from legislating. Sutton's rule cannot be satisfied by labeling relief damages, but it also cannot be defeated by inventing a delegation defect.

Bowen, joined by Stevens and O'Connor, retains sovereign legislative power and enforces the express reservation in its statutory social-insurance arrangement. It also distinguishes genuine bargained debts and contractual rights. These thrifts claim particular negotiated consideration, not merely the right every participant received under a amendable welfare statute. The difference is material, although a money demand alone never establishes that a sovereign-power claim is absent. Tax-immunity and sovereign-surrender authorities remain available when enforcement would confer immunity rather than hold the sovereign to a financial undertaking.

Two affirmative routes must remain separate. One treats unmistakability as a protection against surrender of sovereign authority and holds it inapplicable to these particular monetary risk undertakings after establishing their actual scope. The other retains an exacting construction presumption and finds the incorporated transaction-specific accounting terms sufficiently definite to allocate the risk of their withdrawal. Agreement in the liability judgment does not combine those rationales into a single rule. Neither route should announce that every claim seeking money escapes unmistakability.

Horowitz recognizes protection for genuinely public and general sovereign acts and places the Government in the position of an ordinary contractor for that purpose. The source involved an embargo preventing shipment; it did not hold that a specifically allocated risk always disappears upon legislation. The adequate narrower affirmative rationale here is: even assuming the challenged measures qualify as public and general, ordinary impossibility does not discharge liability for the very regulatory-treatment risk these contracts allocated. A law's validity and compliance with it are different from contractual discharge. No conclusion about legislative bad faith or a predominant congressional purpose to evade contracts is needed or supplied. A broader theory treating FIRREA's particular impairments as non-general requires its own demonstrated basis and is not an additional committed holding here.

The strongest defense is that broad financial-system reform is public and general and lawful performance became impossible without any assumption of legislative-change risk. If the contract-scope premise fails, this defense gains force. Conversely, finding an actual promise does not itself answer which later risks were assumed. Scope must do the legal work; neither fairness nor the availability of money can substitute for it.

## Exact terms incorporated into every commitment

**Winstar.** Section 10, JA 108–109, expressly addresses later clarification, interpretation and amendment of governing regulations and accounting principles. It also expressly gives the Board's transaction resolution or action priority when it conflicts with those regulations. The latter sentence cannot be omitted when assessing the former. The affirmative reading treats the tailored, preserved accounting treatment as contractual consideration while leaving ordinary changes in generally applicable standards operative. The July 13 letter permits regulatory-report amortization over a period **not exceeding thirty-five years**; it neither requires use of the full period nor establishes a thirty-five-year damages award. Its exclusion of GAAP financial statements and continuing reporting/recordkeeping requirements remain operative. The letter reserves powers outside the enumerated waivers and their authorizing statutory provisions insofar as waived. That reservation supports retention of the Government's other regulatory powers; it cannot accurately be paraphrased as an unqualified reservation to withdraw every specified benefit without contractual consequence. It also is not an express promise that Congress lacks legislative power.

The Net Worth Maintenance provisions must be read with the definition that computes net worth under the agreement's accounting principles, not only with the provision that maintains the legally required level. A changed minimum level and a repudiated promised component are analytically distinct, although the particular loss must still result from breach of the actual promise. The express integration treatment of contemporaneous documents answers a threshold incorporation objection; it does not establish every asserted promise merely because a regulatory document exists.

Section 13(a), JA 110–111, ordinarily ends the Assistance Agreement after two years, except as otherwise specifically provided, or at another date agreed in writing. It separately terminates the agreement and FSLIC obligations upon the stated liquidation-receiver or loss-of-insurance events. The affirmative construction reads the integrated, specified forward accounting benefit as an undertaking whose particular duration is not reduced to two years solely by the general assistance term. The competing construction treats the two-year term as controlling unless a sufficiently specific exception actually applies. That competing reading is substantial and must be confronted with the incorporated documents; merely observing that a longer benefit would make business sense is insufficient. Section 13(b)'s qualified surviving indemnities concern their identified liabilities and litigation; they cannot silently become a general survival clause for all claims. The lawful-action provision does not compel illegal reporting after FIRREA under the affirmative damages construction, but it remains relevant to what was promised and cannot be erased by the label “damages.” No newly inspected clause establishes when a receiver was appointed, insurance ended, or an accrued breach claim was extinguished.

**Statesman.** The section 17 priority sentence, JA 402–403, likewise gives the relevant transaction resolution or action priority over conflicting regulations, while retaining its express changing-accounting/regulatory language and the distinct real-estate-valuation provision. Its actual wording is reproduced with the scan's grammatical irregularities in the source addendum. The affirmative reading gives effect to both provisions: ordinary regulatory and accounting evolution continues, while the incorporated negotiated exceptions retain contractual significance. The Government's strongest contrary construction is that a priority among agency instruments does not unmistakably assume the risk of a later congressional command. The answer must rest on the nature of the actual promised consideration and its agreed scope; agency priority is not a claim that an agency can disable Congress.

The capital-credit provision is an express, separate undertaking that the identified $26 million shall be credited to regulatory capital under the specified definition; the goodwill provision permits the specified amortization, no more than twenty-five years, on the accounting conditions already identified in the packet. Neither eliminates the distinction between regulatory reports and GAAP statements. Congress's treatment of supervisory goodwill and OTS's regulatory treatment of capital credits remain separate legal acts. The source addendum supplies no new concession that either act falls outside reserved sovereign powers.

Section 20, JA 406–407, requires material compliance with applicable law, but contains an express qualification while applicability is contested in good faith. The initial draft's reference to compliance must therefore not be read as an unqualified promise to accept every asserted rule's applicability. Conversely, the qualification does not itself prove that a particular contest occurred, was in good faith, excused compliance under public law, or preserved any claim. The narrower affirmative construction can recognize the duty to comply with valid law and the separate monetary consequence of withdrawal of promised treatment; it need not rely on the contest qualification as an independent defense.

Section 19(a), JA 405, ordinarily provides a five-year term or another written agreed date and separately supplies liquidation-receiver and loss-of-insurance triggers. Sections 19(b)–(d) and the referenced section 16 make the full scheme more qualified than a single five-year sentence. The particular goodwill duration must be reconciled with that scheme, and the capital credit cannot automatically borrow goodwill's twenty-five-year ceiling. FIRREA's enactment within the ordinary five-year period answers only the assertion that ordinary expiration had already eliminated every promise; it does not eliminate a distinct termination event if proved or settle the recoverable duration of any loss. No sale, receiver event, insurance termination, good-faith contest or damages cutoff is newly found here.

**Glendale.** The approved and preserved accountant treatment distinguishes the $18 million/twelve-year and $716.666 million/forty-year components and retains the stated satisfactory-accounting and R-31b conditions. Section 9 of the Supervisory Action Agreement, JA 598–599, provides a ten-year assistance term and distinct receiver/liquidation and loss-of-insurance consequences. Section 10, JA 599–600, preserves contemporaneous resolutions and letters and requires the specified written agreement for modification; the applicable lawful-action language also remains relevant. The affirmative construction reads the specific forward accounting undertaking together with, rather than as indistinguishable from, the term of ongoing assistance and payments. It does not delete the ten-year provision. The Government's contrary construction would confine continuing benefits to the assistance term or to their lawfulness under later legislation. The integrated specific duration supplies the stronger contractual answer, but does not establish a maximum damages period irrespective of other terms, actual loss or termination. The lower court's construction is the issue under review, not an independent binding rule that resolves that disagreement by itself.

**Common reachability and remedy.** These are construction questions on the interlocutory liability judgments, not a reason to announce a new trial merely because the operator sought fuller pages. The affirmed liability premise would establish withdrawal of an authorized, identified contractual benefit, while leaving causation, recoverable losses, mitigation and applicable temporal limits below. No goodwill balance or capital-credit amount is itself a damages award. A broader promise of immunity from regulation, protection from all capital increases, or insurance against institutional failure is outside the committed rationale. The unaddressed Winstar implied-contract alternative and other preserved theories do not become adjudicated simply by choosing an express-contract construction.


**Statesman section 16's separate sale scheme.** The fresh neutral validator read the complete section across JA 401–402. Defined Sales during seven years require prior written approval and a net-proceeds percentage agreed at the time, capped at fifty percent with the specified proportional investment-return adjustment. The Sale definition aggregates sales consummated or agreed during ninety days having an aggregate sales price of $20 million. A defined Sale during five years without approval triggers immediate termination notwithstanding section 19; approval may be granted or withheld in the Corporation's sole discretion. The exception covers transfers to an affiliate of the Acquirer of stock or assets and liabilities of Federated Financial, Combined Mortgage Corporation, Homestead Mortgage Corporation or Perpetual Financial Corporation, not every affiliate transfer. The seven-year proceeds/approval arrangement and five-year termination consequence are not interchangeable. No qualifying Sale, approval, named transfer or termination is newly found. These conditions limit the construction and any later relief if applicable; they are not presumed events defeating or extending present liability.
