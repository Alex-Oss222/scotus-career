# Assembly requirements — OT1995 chunk 1

This is an operator checklist, not authority or an opinion template. Follow foundation/ENGINE.md, foundation/RENDER_CONTRACT.md and the applicable user AGENTS instructions.

- Never run Git. Never access or enumerate top-level stone/. Write only under terms/OT1995/ or tmp/. Use Python -B when importing local tools. Do not edit the approved brief, foundation or state. Do not render output: Run and Render are separate tasks.
- Receive only the validated frozen neutral packet, entering-law slice, frozen reconciled commitments, and the assigned Stone supplement, plus public date-eligible source evidence and assignment statements. Preserve the stage handoffs. No independent non-Stone remodeling in assembly. A new material source or legal fork returns through clean stages.
- Revalidate Stone against the corrected factual record and actual entering law. An unapproved changed material premise stops the affected matter; do not fill a missing choice. The standing fallback covers only a genuinely silent component, supplies a vote rather than new reasoning, and must be disclosed by component in the Record and Public Projection. No routine separate Stone account in the projection.
- Resolve every judgment component and separate rationale at its supported scope. Record source-supported exceptions, qualifications, proof burdens, triggers and remedies in their own terms. State all material separate positions fully enough to understand their legal work. Never convert a party allegation into a finding or a judgment vote into an opinion join.
- Make assignment under Engine §8 from the actual judgment coalition; evaluate Stone against the strongest appropriate alternative when he assigns. OT1994_ASSIGNMENT_REVIEW.md establishes that no prior-term author exceeded half. Current assignments must be counted from actual current Records. No invented drafting dialogue.
- Draft each proposed Record initially under terms/OT1995/runtime/assembly/ using its natural case/event/date filename. The root operator validates and preserves it under records/ in effective order. Do not label an unvalidated draft committed, finalized, or passed. Every Record begins with the four exact required labels. Lineage: initial adjudication, no prior Record superseded, operator Git commitment pending. No fabricated version hash.
- Each Record needs event/argument dates and date provenance, review channel, lower judgment, questions, participants at submission/argument and decision, quorum and threshold, entering law with exact Standards titles and operative authority/dates, Stone core and final compatibility, judgment/remedy, opinion topology, full controlling law, published continuity positions, cutoff, source map and performed validation. Assignment and every non-Stone ground/departure remain auditable through the frozen handoffs.
- Controlling holdings must contain the complete operative rule, named proposition-level authority, explanation (ordinary ceiling 350 words), application, principal contrary argument, reserved matters and precedent treatment. Holdings in the kernel and Public Projection must be identical. Do not pad. Keep the separate precedent-treatment and resulting-law functions.
- End with `## Public Projection` followed by exactly these level-two headings, in order: Event; Participation; Public Action; Judgment & Remedy; Opinion Topology; Holdings; Precedent Treatment; Law After Decision; Separate Writings; Procedure After Action; Source Notes. Source Notes carry only public support and legally material source limits, never workflow or modern retrieval dates. No correction/approval/model/history language enters public blocks. Record research cutoff internally, before Public Projection.
- Supply companion JSON in runtime/assembly/ for clerical review: record filename, case, date, disposition summary, participants, `judgments` (component, disposition, support names, oppose names, other positions if any, printed tally), `writings` (author, joiners, scope, controlling true/false), and blockers. This data checks arithmetic and identity; it does not decide law. Exact justice spellings: Stone-Zsela, Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg, Breyer.
- An exact source/Stone-choice blocker belongs in a separate assembly blocker report. No Canonical Decision Record or merits vote is created for an unresolved matter. Complete unaffected matters.
- For the small workspace projections, add companion JSON fields `law_summary` (complete but compact controlling rule and key limits), `published_positions` (array of substantive noncontrolling public positions naming authors and joins; empty if none), and `next_stage` (remedy and any remaining Court event). These must project the proposed Record only; root checks them before using them. They never replace the full Record.
