### Operative issue and the two retained grounds

Section9:364(D) requires prohibition of **all visitation and contact** after a finding of parental sexual abuse, until a contradictory hearing establishes both successful completion of treatment and that **supervised** visitation serves the children's best interests. Section9:362(6)'s treatment is evaluation and psychotherapy designed for perpetrators of family violence by licensed mental-health professionals. The text states no burden of proof and no mandatory admission-of-abuse condition. Evidence concerning the actual OCS plan and the effect of continued denial does not establish that every treatment program demands an admission.

The earlier CINC finding used bare preponderance. The lower opinion positively establishes that the father lacked notice that this finding could invoke §364(D)'s more severe regime; it therefore did not make the old finding conclusive. The existing supervised-contact disposition and the later request for all-contact prohibition remain distinct. The reviewed state decree affirmed invalidation on the federal proof ground; it did not itself find abuse by clear and convincing evidence or prescribe a new schedule.

**Heightened-trigger ground:** Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer require clear and convincing proof before this all-contact regime may be triggered. They apply Santosky's Mathews-based attention to the private interest, consequences of error and governmental protection interest to the complete scope and potentially indefinite duration of lost family contact. This extends the method to the identified statutory consequence; it does not declare that Santosky already controls every visitation restriction or that all parental limitations require that burden. Child protection can continue under otherwise lawful temporary measures and supported supervised restrictions. Those alternatives answer the contention that accurate heightened factfinding necessarily leaves the child exposed.

**Protective-procedure ground:** O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas reject a categorical constitutional requirement of intermediate proof for this statute. Retained parental status, a treatment route and judicial best-interests determination permitting supervised contact distinguish irrevocable termination. Erroneous permission can expose a child to renewed abuse; the full protective procedure and both directions of error matter. They do not approve the actual notice defect, declare all indefinite restrictions adequate, or make periodic review sufficient for every liberty deprivation. Actual duration and process remain subject to constitutional review.

The heightened group supports affirmance of the reviewed federal proof determination with return for appropriate state implementation, possible conforming construction and remedy. It does not unconditionally hold that the statute can never constitutionally operate. The protective-procedure group reverses that federal proof ground and remands without authorizing immediate enforcement on the earlier unnoticed finding. Neither path restores the independent state separation-of-powers command requiring greater weight for the child's therapist; that invalidation remains untouched. No exact present contact, particular program, fee entitlement, new abuse determination, severability choice or unrestricted visitation is supplied.

### Common reliance rule; separate application and mandate

All eight Associates retain the independent **actual and justifiable reliance** rule for the actual-fraud claim presented under §523(a)(2)(A). Congress's common-law fraud terminology carries its established legal content; §523(a)(2)(B)'s express reasonable-reliance requirement for the defined materially false, intentionally deceptive written financial-condition statement neither imports ordinary-prudence investigation into (A) nor eliminates reliance and justifiability from actual fraud. Justifiability considers this recipient's knowledge, capacities and circumstances. Failure to investigate is not automatically disqualifying, but the recipient may not ignore known or personally obvious falsity or a discovered warning requiring attention under that standard. Ordinary objective reasonableness may bear on whether actual reliance is credible without becoming a separate universal prudent-creditor duty.

The rule concerns actual fraud here. No different case involving only false pretenses or representation without actual fraud is independently adjudicated. The result incorporates the general common-law standard, not any one State's tort law, and it does not enlarge the Restatement propositions beyond the question presented. The statutory “to the extent obtained by” limitation remains separate; no Justice declares every dollar of the note nondischargeable from an interpretation victory.

**Six-Associate application:** Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg vacate and remand. The bankruptcy court explicitly invoked an objective prudent-person investigation standard. The lower courts must apply the chosen standard to the existing findings, address any properly available obtained-by and amount issues and ordinary preservation questions, and may not relitigate actual reliance merely because the legal standard changes. ### Retained operative rule and remedy

All eight Associates retain the independent rejection of a **categorical** exclusion based on union compensation and organizing obligations. Under §152(3), and the common-law service inquiry recognized in Darden, a worker may serve two masters where service to one does not require abandonment of compatible service to the other. Union pay, organizing purpose and an obligation to leave on union instruction do not by themselves negate the contractor's control over the manner and means of electrical work while the employment lasts. The Board's inclusion is permissible consistently with that inquiry. Neither absence of a union-pay exclusion nor labor-policy purpose alone supplies the holding.

The statute remains broader than employees of a particular employer unless the subchapter explicitly provides otherwise, and includes the specified labor-dispute/unfair-labor-practice cessation category subject to the substantially equivalent employment qualification. Its exclusions remain agricultural labor, domestic service in a home, employment by parent or spouse, independent contractors, supervisors, employment by a Railway Labor Act employer, and employment by a person outside the Act's employer definition. No additional paid-organizer exclusion is supplied, and none of those existing exclusions is deleted.

The authentic salting resolutions require organizing assignments and departure on union notice, preserve list status and impose disciplinary consequences for unauthorized nonsignatory work. The control objection is therefore real. But the terms do not establish that every covered worker cannot perform the contractor's electrical work under its direction; neither organizing intent nor a potential later departure makes current dual service inherently incompatible. No actual sabotage, refusal of assigned work or other disqualifying misconduct is found. Lechmere concerns access by established nonemployees and cannot decide this antecedent classification by assumption.

The final formal commitment is **vacate the Eighth Circuit judgment and remand** after rejecting its categorical employee exclusion. This expresses the frozen reversal of the legal ground without directing unconditional enforcement of every Board determination. It does not change any Justice's merits or remedial position. Ameristaff and Town & Country remain distinct; two full-time officials and nine other members are not collapsed into eleven identical workers. Hansen was hired/referred; the other ten were not interviewed. Unreached discrimination inferences, qualifications, lawful work-time limits and other defenses remain for proper adjudication. The Board's compliance process determines how many would have been hired; no automatic ten-person backpay award, amount or bargaining-unit determination follows.

### Retained statutory distinction and opposed disposition

Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer retain **independent federal review of ultimate Miranda custody**, while preserving statutory treatment of actual historical findings. What officers and the person said and did, the setting, time and supported credibility determinations are factual inputs. Given the established circumstances, whether a reasonable person would understand restraint equivalent to formal arrest is the ultimate legal application. It requires independent judgment under Miller's functional distinction and the objective arrest-equivalence rule in the actual Stansbury holding. The conclusion is not that all mixed or ultimate inferences always receive de novo review. Patton/Witt and competency classifications retain their actual scope and their comparative observational rationale.

Under the pre-AEDPA §2254(d), the factual presumption requires a competent state court's merits hearing on the factual issue in proceedings involving the applicant and State/state officer, supported by adequate written indicia. Preserve all eight exceptions: unresolved factual merits; inadequate factfinding procedure; inadequately developed material facts; lack of jurisdiction over subject matter or applicant; unconstitutional denial of appointed counsel to an indigent applicant; lack of a full, fair and adequate hearing; another due-process denial; or lack of fair support in the produced pertinent state record considered as a whole. The statute's convincing-evidence burden at a federal evidentiary hearing applies on its stated conditions. The present disposition finds none of those exceptions; the ultimate legal application is simply outside this factual presumption. A paper suppression proceeding does not alone prove failure of the statutory hearing condition.

**Thomas retains the opposed classification.** He treats the concrete reasonable-person inference as presumptively factual under the statute while reviewing the legal definition of custody independently. Patton and Witt show that applying a legal standard does not by itself make the resulting assessment independently reviewable. The state judge's task includes interacting circumstances and inferences even on documentary material; lack of live testimony does not categorically alter the inquiry. Miller's independent voluntariness history and function do not decide every warning predicate. Thomas would affirm the reviewed judgment on this challenged classification ground, without a new plenary custody finding.

The seven vacate/remand for independent custody review. Voluntary arrival, accusations, duration, setting, communicated freedom to leave and actual departure must receive their proper legal significance, not be resolved here from selected facts. Private police intentions or later surveillance allegations do not become communicated custody facts. No Justice in the reconciled commitment decides ultimate custody, suppression, harmlessness, an independent involuntariness claim or entitlement to the writ. The abandoned initial default is not restored. The lawful review path remains subject to ordinary habeas and remedial requirements.
