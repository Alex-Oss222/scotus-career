## Material common predicates and competing rules

The formal components are distinct. No. 94-2107 seeks reversal of the Fourth Circuit's initial liability ruling, which vacated a district judgment rejecting liability. No. 94-1941 seeks reversal of the later approval of VWIL. The initial remand allowed Virginia to propose a lawful cure; it did not adjudicate every possible separate program adequate or order immediate coeducation. The merits reach is supported on both submitted components. No new standing, exhaustion, waiver, mootness, or default ground is manufactured.

Craig and O'Connor's Hogan opinion supply the State's burden to justify an express sex classification by an important governmental objective and a substantial relationship between that objective and the means. Hogan's “exceedingly persuasive justification” describes that existing burden; it is not a new strict-scrutiny tier or a requirement that every distinction use the least restrictive means. Hogan also reserves the general question of separate undergraduate institutions. Its footnote 17 explains why providing a choice to the favored sex does not itself justify denying the choice to the other. The statutory admissions exemption discussed there does not grant constitutional permission.

Actual J.E.B. applies heightened scrutiny to sex as a proxy for individual juror capacity. Its jury holding is not an automatic decision about every educational classification. Actual Kelley permits sex-conscious program decisions under a genuine important objective and substantial fit, with actual disadvantage supporting its compensatory application; fiscal pressure, ratios, or stereotypes alone do not suffice. Its athletics rule is not a college-admissions exemption. Actual Klinger requires comparison tied to the disputed benefit: whole-institution dissimilarity is neither a threshold immunity nor proof of liability. An express sex bar here does not require additional proof of discriminatory intent of the kind needed for a genuinely neutral policy.

The State's strongest liability ground is substantial: the lower courts accepted findings that single-sex education can benefit both sexes, that VMI's integrated method has educational value, and that coeducation would change privacy, physical training, adversative interaction, and the method as a whole. The parties agree that some women meet existing physical standards; that fact does not erase those institutional predictions. Virginia can identify the VMI board's present educational purpose without proving that the original legislature used modern terminology. Nor does a Governor's disagreement nullify the actual authorized institutional position. The legal issue is whether these supported benefits justify reserving the State's distinctive opportunity to men, given the available alternatives and actual opportunity denied. The purpose cannot be established simply by redescribing the sex bar as homogeneity, but genuine pedagogical evidence cannot be dismissed merely because it concerns sex.

The remedy must be assessed at its real scope, including an assumption of faithful implementation of its promises. VWIL provides public subsidy, four years of ROTC, leadership coursework, an externship, service, and physical education at an established accredited women's college. Its expected ordinary student cost is no greater and the annual public payment per student equals VMI's. But VWIL does not provide the pervasive barracks/rat-line method, on-campus engineering, or the same degree and institutional resources. Its three-plus-two engineering option requires two final private-tuition years without the proposed discount. Mary Baldwin's endowment is reported as approximately $19 million in one adopted comparison and $20.5 million in another, compared with approximately $131 million for VMI; the separate $5.4625 million VWIL endowment commitment is not Mary Baldwin's entire endowment. Faculty credentials, facilities, and degree prestige are relevant in combination, not constitutional numerical minima or proof of an individual woman's capacity.

The VMI Alumni Association's express pledge of the same placement-network access, recruitment help, externships and advancement support must be credited. No row treats alumni access as absent or accepts a contrary prediction as a superseding finding. The pledge does not supply a VMI degree, institutional history, or demonstrated equality of outcomes. The program's approval record does not establish unreported 1995-96 implementation success or failure. Mary Baldwin's independent educational value is not in dispute; whether this State-selected alternative remedies the particular exclusion is a different question.

Sweatt supplies a useful measure of actual educational opportunity, including tangible and intangible benefits. Its racial setting does not automatically dictate that all public sex separation is unconstitutional. Fordice and Jenkins preserve effective, violation-linked relief, lawful institutional differences, and State choice among adequate means. Installation, equal annual payments, or good intentions alone need not establish cure; institutional identity, numerical balance, general educational improvement, and judicial management of every detail do not follow either.

## Stevens

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm the liability judgment in No. 94-2107. Reverse the approval judgment in No. 94-1941 and remand for effective relief against the categorical unequal exclusion. The State has not justified limiting this distinctive public opportunity to men; VWIL does not cure the demonstrated inequality even if its alumni and funding promises are fully performed.

**Own eligible positions.** His Craig concurrence, 429 U.S. at 211-214, treats equal protection as impartial governance and rejects using broad sex statistics to impose a birth-based disadvantage on individuals who do not share the supposed characteristic. He joined Hogan's Court opinion. His actual J.E.B., Kelley and Klinger joins preserve substantial-fit review and attention to the actual opportunity. His Jenkins join and his Lewis v. Casey separate position preserve demonstrated injury while correcting excessive remedial terms; neither treats every program expenditure as a constitutional entitlement.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest contrary path accepts the method's real benefit and the prediction that women would change it, sustaining an educational choice within a diverse system. That is stronger than an unsupported stereotype. It nevertheless does not explain why Virginia may make the distinctive leadership, training, degree, and advancement opportunity unavailable to otherwise qualified women while supplying it to men. His Craig position gives weight to the capable individual and to the exclusion's practical disadvantage; it does not let the aggregate preference of most women settle the rights of the rest. Hogan's choice argument and Kelley’s actual-fit requirement defeat the idea that preserving one favored class's choice supplies its own sufficient justification. He need not reject every lower pedagogical finding or declare that sex differences never matter.

**Remedy comparison.** Affirming a sincerely designed, equally subsidized women's program avoids judicial standardization and credits its substantial educational benefits. The contrary evidence remains decisive for him: different substantive training, degree options and resources leave the particular opportunity unequal, rather than merely different in style. Sweatt makes intangible opportunity relevant; no claim that alumni access is absent is needed. An additional open-ended experiment would continue the current exclusion without an adequate cure established by this record.

**Scope and joins.** Join the established heightened-scrutiny rule, present liability, failure of this plan, and a remand requiring the exclusion to end through an effective lawful arrangement. If Virginia continues this public VMI program without an adequate alternative established, qualified women must be considered without the categorical sex bar. Appropriate operational adjustments may be designed below; this is no order to admit a named applicant regardless of qualifications. He would not join strict scrutiny for all sex classifications, a racial-segregation analogy doing all the legal work, uniform facilities ratios, or a universal ban on single-sex education. He can join an opinion using Craig's settled formulation despite his broader impartiality explanation. A separate writing is needed only if an opinion rests on automatic institutional identity or understates the continuing individual denial.

## O'Connor

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm in No. 94-2107; reverse approval in No. 94-1941 and remand for prompt effective correction. Apply her existing Hogan rule, not a newly elevated scrutiny tier. The record establishes pedagogical value but not sufficient justification for reserving the unique public opportunity to men, and this remedial substitute does not provide an adequate cure.

**Own eligible positions.** O'Connor authored Hogan's state-burden, actual-purpose and substantial-relationship analysis, including its limited nursing-school holding and reservations. Her actual J.E.B. concurrence joins the prohibition on using group experiences as an automatic proxy while preserving legitimate institutional functions. She authored actual Kelley and Klinger, both joined by the entire Court. Her Jenkins dissent and Lewis v. Casey joins insist on proved remedial connections and a genuine State design role; her Settle join preserves substantial legitimate educational discretion while withholding immunity from an unlawful categorical rule.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest affirmance-of-policy ground distinguishes Hogan's male auditors, who already participated fully, from VMI's integrated residential method and accepted predictions of material changes. She accepts that distinction as factual and rejects any assertion that Hogan already decided every parallel-college question. But the State still must justify unequal access to the benefit at issue. Merely preserving an effective male institution does not establish why women can be denied its distinctive opportunity when some are capable and interested. Kelley permits genuine compensatory restructuring on demonstrated inequality; this male-only reservation is not supported by comparable remedial disadvantage. Nor does she require a compensatory purpose for every lawful sex distinction: the asserted noncompensatory educational purpose must stand on its own fit, which is inadequate here.

**Remedy comparison.** The best alternative would affirm the professionally designed VWIL plan with enforceable funding and alumni commitments, acknowledging that equal protection does not demand institutional duplication. Its genuine design and new remedial purpose are not disqualified merely because adopted after suit. Even on full implementation, however, the plan leaves material differences in the actual public opportunity, including the kind of training and the accessible engineering education. Substantive equality cannot be reduced to matching general aspirations, a subsidy, or a degree label. She would reject an additional remand solely to relabel the existing plan comparable.

**Scope and joins.** Join present-record invalidity and inadequacy, but reserve lawful single-sex programs serving supported objectives with adequate opportunities and fit. Join an effective-admissions requirement if the same public VMI remains without an established adequate alternative; permit practical implementation adjustments without an indefinite continuation of the violation. Actual withdrawal of state involvement remains legally different from retaining the same public institution under a new label, and is not preapproved. No forced physical-standard revision, quota, individual admission guarantee, automatic closure, Title IX expansion, or general judgment against private women's colleges follows. Refuse language treating every empirical sex difference as a stereotype, requiring a least-restrictive-means test, or finding bad faith from litigation timing alone. No separate writing is necessary if these limits appear in the shared opinion.

## Scalia

**Independent primary result and grounds.** Reverse the liability ruling in No. 94-2107. The actual educational purpose and accepted findings sufficiently connect sex homogeneity to preservation of VMI's integrated educational method under the existing intermediate standard. In No. 94-1941, vacate the compulsory-remedy approval and remand for removal of orders resting only on the reversed constitutional-liability predicate. This primary position does not prohibit Virginia from voluntarily operating VWIL. It is distinct from his conditional view below if liability is retained by the Court.

**Own eligible positions.** His actual J.E.B. dissent accepts ordinary heightened sex scrutiny but resists mechanically extending a particular equality doctrine in a way that destroys a legitimate institution's function. He joined the complete actual Kelley and Klinger opinions: these commitments foreclose rational-basis review, mere tradition as sufficient reason, blanket whole-institution comparator immunity, or reliance on pretext. His Jenkins dissent and Lewis v. Casey concurrence/joins preserve the distinction between a proved wrong and general institutional improvement. His unanimous Settle join excludes legally impermissible categorical reasons from pedagogical discretion, while preserving genuine evaluative judgment. His Romer dissent's rational-basis latitude does not replace the governing sex test here.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest opposing path uses Hogan, capable women's exclusion, and the actual-opportunity comparison to conclude that preserving a favored sex's choice cannot itself justify denying the other sex the opportunity. He accepts that statement as a limit, and does not define the protected benefit as “men only” to make the answer automatic. He instead treats the supported integrated training method, and the finding that coeducation would materially change its educational operation, as affirmative evidence of substantial fit to an important actual purpose. Hogan's auditing practice undermined its asserted necessity; VMI's record affirmatively supports the relevant distinction. Klinger permits materially justified differences after benefit-specific comparison, and Kelley demonstrates that a sex-conscious educational arrangement is not automatically invalid because some individuals would prefer the excluded option. In his application the State need not duplicate every distinctive institutional method for both sexes or prove that no other educational arrangement could work. That conclusion preserves the real burden of justification; it does not claim VMI's age or popularity alone supplies it.

**Conditional remedy comparison.** If the Court retains liability because women lacked an adequate corresponding public opportunity, he would affirm the later approval of VWIL subject to the existing appellate monitoring requirements. This is an independent conditional commitment, not agreement with liability and not the primary paired-docket disposition. The plan supplies subsidized education, leadership, ROTC and professional access under a genuinely chosen alternative method. He credits the actual alumni pledge and continuing enforceable commitments. The strongest objection is that VMI's degree, curriculum, facilities and historical prestige remain unequal. He regards those differences, including the engineering-cost limitation, as insufficient to make exact institutional replication constitutionally obligatory under intermediate review where the alternative program's educational value and resources have been adjudged adequate. Sweatt's comparison is instructive but its racial exclusion setting does not establish the identity requirement proposed here. He would not deny the differences, dismiss the need for performance monitoring, or treat future funding promises as completed performance.

**Scope and joins.** Join only a merits rule that respects the existing important-objective/substantial-fit standard and grounds this result in the accepted pedagogical record. Refuse a majority invalidation resting on an effective demand to preserve every feature of VMI while changing its defining residential composition, or an inference that individual eligibility automatically defeats every sex classification. Conversely, he cannot join a rationale that tradition immunizes the policy, that Klinger bars comparison across institutions, or that the State may simply assert rather than establish an educational purpose. If liability survives, he can join an affirmance requiring faithful implementation of VWIL's actual terms; he would oppose compelled VMI admission and the reversal of this plan. A dissent or conditional separate writing must keep those two positions distinct. No count or authorship is assigned here.

## Kennedy

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm the liability judgment in No. 94-2107. Reverse remedy approval in No. 94-1941 and remand for an effective cure of the individual's unequal exclusion. The State may recognize educational differences but cannot make sex itself conclusive of who may seek this public opportunity on this showing.

**Own eligible positions.** His actual J.E.B. judgment concurrence rests on each citizen's individual equality, not representation of presumed group viewpoints. His Kelley and Klinger joins accept supported sex-sensitive choices while requiring attention to the actual opportunity and actual fit. He joined Fordice's effective-dismantlement rule with its institutional limits and dissented in Jenkins on the required connection between the proved wrong and the challenged expenditure. His newly completed Lewis v. Casey Court opinion requires meaningful opportunity and violation-linked relief while preserving government choice of effective methods. His Settle join likewise distinguishes genuine program boundaries from constitutional immunity.

**Liability comparison.** Preserving an integrated, successful residential method is a genuine objective, and findings that coeducation will change it deserve recognition. That alternative does not settle who may be categorically denied the accompanying public education and citizenship-training opportunity. Some women can meet the standards and desire that experience. Treating the preferences or needs of most women as their conclusive identity repeats the proxy problem his J.E.B. writing rejects. His ground is not that men and women must display the same preferences, or that the institution must be organized to represent both groups; the equal right belongs to the excluded individual. The broader public system's availability does not supply the particular opportunity denied under Klinger's comparison.

**Remedy comparison.** The strongest affirmance argument preserves a State-selected alternative, actual funding, accredited education and equal promised network access. Lewis v. Casey makes genuine State design choice significant. Virginia has received that opportunity here and proposed this plan; choice among adequate means does not certify an inadequate one. The differences in actual training, affordable curriculum, resources and credential remain material even assuming the alumni promise is fully honored. No separate entitlement to each facility is required before those combined facts can show failure to remedy the established exclusion.

**Scope and joins.** Join present liability and the failure of this alternative, with remand requiring nondiscriminatory access if Virginia continues the same public VMI without an adequate lawful cure. Permit operational adaptation and ordinary qualification requirements; do not prescribe training details or guarantee a place to every interested applicant. He withholds assent from a rationale making equal group representation the constitutional end, claiming that all separate programs are necessarily demeaning, or treating equal subsidy alone as equality. He also rejects applying Casey's prisoner-claim or PLRA conditions as new prerequisites here. His individual-right explanation can coexist with the shared Hogan rule; a separate writing is useful only if the opinion rests on group representation or institutional identity rather than actual denied opportunity.

## Souter

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm liability in No. 94-2107; reverse approval in No. 94-1941 and remand for effective, violation-linked relief. The State's asserted educational purpose must justify the actual exclusion, and the proposed remedy must correct it in substance. Neither inquiry is resolved by attaching a benevolent purpose to a sex-defined arrangement.

**Own eligible positions.** His actual J.E.B., Kelley and Klinger joins supply the sex rule and actual-benefit comparison. His authored Jenkins opinion separates necessary educational repair from a freestanding entitlement to each means, and separates installing programs from eliminating the relevant wrong. His authored Romer opinion examines the connection between the claimed justification and the real disadvantage without assuming every classification invalid. His authored Settle opinion preserves genuine pedagogy but rejects constitutional immunity for impermissible categorical grounds. His Lewis v. Casey separate opinion accepts supported common proof, meaningful assistance and State choice while rejecting unsupported statewide scope; these remain their own legal settings.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest contrary conclusion credits institutional autonomy and accepted evidence of different educational methods. A legitimate present purpose need not have existed in identical form in 1839, and he would not declare a new remedial purpose pretext simply because it followed litigation. But the record's evidence that single-sex education can help both sexes, coupled with some women's capacity and interest, leaves the reservation of this distinctive public opportunity to men unjustified. The important-objective test cannot stop with the abstract desirability of diversity. The connection must account for the particular disadvantage, as his Romer reasoning illustrates at a different scrutiny level. He accepts predicted changes without treating preservation of every feature as an independently constitutional objective overriding equal access.

**Remedy comparison.** Affirmance with monitoring has genuine force: VWIL is a real program, and constitutional remedies need not make institutions identical. Yet equal support promises and a shared leadership objective do not remedy the combined loss of training, affordable degree opportunities and institutional benefits. Jenkins rejects treating installation alone as completed cure. On this approved design, further monitoring cannot by itself supply the missing substantive opportunity; that is why reversal rather than a finding of bad implementation is required. Sweatt's tangible/intangible inquiry identifies the relevant deficit without extending racial doctrine wholesale.

**Scope and joins.** Join the existing intermediate standard, present liability, and reversal of the plan assuming faithful implementation. Join an enforceable remand requiring the unequal exclusion to end, ordinarily through consideration of qualified women at a continuing public VMI unless an actually adequate lawful alternative is established. The lower court retains the work of practicable implementation and necessary safeguards, with no indefinite postponement justified by mere promises. He refuses an automatic institutional-merger rule, a permanent resource-equality decree, an inference of malicious intent, or rejection of every genuine sex-sensitive educational adjustment. No new factual hearing is required merely to reweigh settled findings; proceedings are for lawful implementation and any genuinely material remedial proposal.

## Ginsburg

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm liability in No. 94-2107; reverse the VWIL approval in No. 94-1941 and remand for effective relief. The State's showing does not justify making sex an absolute bar to the distinctive public opportunity, and the alternative does not correct that concrete disadvantage even when all pledged assistance is credited.

**Own eligible positions.** Her authored actual Harris concurrence focuses on whether sex creates disadvantage in the conditions of the actual opportunity, rather than requiring an extraneous tangible-loss measure; that statutory workplace analysis is an individual-position anchor, not the constitutional rule here. Her actual J.E.B., Kelley and Klinger joins establish the governing sex-classification and comparator rules. Her Jenkins join, Romer join, Settle join and Lewis v. Casey join in Souter's separate writing preserve supported substantive opportunity and effective repair without automatic liability from every disparity. No later opinion or advocacy has been substituted for those date-eligible judicial positions.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest State path treats single-sex composition as a real teaching method rather than a judgment of female inferiority. She accepts the distinction and the findings that the method benefits its students. But describing the interests of women as a group does not justify excluding those willing and able to undertake this public training. The State must answer the denied opportunity, rather than direct all women to the curriculum judged suitable for most. Hogan's burden and Klinger’s benefit-specific comparison require that answer. She does not need to prove that every founder or current official acted from hostility, or that women and men have identical physical characteristics.

**Remedy comparison.** A professionally designed women's leadership program can confer substantial benefits and could be a legitimate educational project. The strongest affirmance path credits precisely that value, the per-student subsidy, ROTC, and the promised VMI network access. Its difficulty is remedial: it leaves the excluded women with a different, materially less complete substitute for the State's distinctive opportunity, including engineering access, facilities and institutional credential. Her Harris explanation supports assessing real disadvantage rather than allowing a nominal resource or benefit to end the inquiry. Sweatt confirms that educational opportunity includes material intangible advantages. The result does not require denying the alumni pledge or predicting that its members will violate it.

**Scope and joins.** Join a Hogan-based holding with its existing burden; “exceedingly persuasive” supplies no new strict-scrutiny rule. Join relief requiring otherwise qualified women to receive nondiscriminatory consideration if Virginia continues this public program without an adequate lawful substitute. Permit necessary practical adjustments; reject preserving the absolute exclusion merely to avoid them. Reserve legitimate programs directed at supported unequal opportunity and the unpresented question of private single-sex education. She will not join language judging women's interest or capacity by the typical member, excusing the present exclusion through general access to other colleges, or allowing an indefinite period of promised equality. A separate writing is unnecessary if the shared opinion states those limits and the actual injury plainly.

## Breyer

**Independent result and grounds.** Affirm the liability judgment in No. 94-2107; reverse approval in No. 94-1941 and remand for practical, effective correction. Give the supported pedagogical findings weight while applying the existing heightened scrutiny to the opportunity actually denied. The proposed alternative fails that remedial task at the level of the complete educational opportunity, not because every institutional difference independently violates the Constitution.

**Own eligible positions.** His actual Kelley and Klinger joins are direct adopted judicial positions concerning sex and educational/programmatic differences. His Jenkins join adopts effective, violation-linked educational repair and meaningful institutional discretion. He joined Souter's Romer and Settle opinions and Souter's Lewis v. Casey separate writing, including concrete opportunity, proper common proof, State remedial choice and limits on unsupported scope. These are expressly joined writings; this handoff does not invent a personally authored pre-1996 sex-admissions opinion for him.

**Liability comparison.** The strongest sustaining path credits VMI's tested success and the institutional costs of changing an integrated method. Courts need not choose their preferred teaching method or insist that educational experts are infallible. But deference is not an answer to the State's reservation of the whole unique opportunity to one sex. The accepted capable-women evidence and the absence of a corresponding opportunity make the categorical bar broader than the demonstrated educational justification supports. Kelley allows actual-fit judgments rather than categorical indifference to sex; it does not establish that resource variety alone justifies this exclusion. Settle reinforces the distinction between respecting pedagogical choices and abandoning constitutional review, without transferring its causation test.

**Remedy comparison.** The strongest alternative is conditional approval with serious monitoring, permitting different methods aimed at comparable leadership formation. He credits the actual ROTC, subsidy and alumni commitments and does not presume future failure. But this approved design's combined differences in training, available degrees, financial burden and resources leave more than harmless variation. Adequacy cannot be inferred from a shared goal or equal annual state payment. Jenkins and Casey permit necessary implementation measures once the wrong is established; they do not require a freestanding constitutional right to each facility, yet forbid general educational improvement unrelated to the denial.

**Scope and joins.** Join the shared standard, present-record liability and plan inadequacy. Join a remand with enforceable correction and an opportunity for lawful operational design; if the public VMI continues without an adequate alternative, sex cannot remain the categorical admissions barrier. Do not force the lower court to recreate identical campuses, equalize every budget figure, invent new physical standards, or guarantee equal career outcomes. He will not join a categorical repudiation of educational expertise, strict scrutiny, a universal least-intrusive-remedy formula drawn from the PLRA, or a judgment against all single-sex programs. A separate writing is unnecessary if the opinion preserves these practical and doctrinal bounds.
