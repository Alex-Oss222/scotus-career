## Adversarial and remedial audit

The strongest denial route reads the petition as merely asserting an unlimited right to proceed pro se after proven abuse. The actual petition and attached order establish a narrower arguable question about mandatory counsel as the practical condition of access, alleged inability to obtain counsel and claimed custody-related effects. The Court can screen or deny the petition under the appropriate petition rules later. It need not resolve those merits now to grant financial leave.

The strongest future-control route relies on unusually extensive state abuse, notice and the preservation of paid/criminal channels. Those facts matter but do not establish either particular present frivolousness or the inadequacy of particular-filing controls for the category of future submissions here. Refusal therefore rests on the missing legal predicates of the proposed order, not on a claim that one must always await more abuse or give another warning.

Certiorari itself remains unadjudicated because it is outside this event, not because this freeze makes a concealed merits choice.
