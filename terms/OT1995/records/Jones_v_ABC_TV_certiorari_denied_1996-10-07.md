## Basis

The [February 26, 1996 filing-management order](Jones_v_ABC_TV_filing_order_1996-02-26.md) kept the financial-leave motion pending with eligibility reserved, declined a present Rule 39.8 denial and a future noncriminal-certiorari condition, and left the petition pending. It provided that a later final financial ruling requires actual support for eligibility, an established deficiency or an applicable Rule 39.8 ground. Both the motion and the petition remained pending when the term closed.

Pending petitions are acted on at conference. The Court's end-of-summer conference took up matters held over from October Term 1995, and its orders were announced on the order list of Monday, October 7, 1996. The Court denies certiorari. A denial disposes of the petition without reaching the reserved financial question, so the motion is dismissed as moot. That makes no finding of eligibility or ineligibility, applies no Rule 39.8 ground and imposes no future filing condition. It is consistent with the February 26 order and with Stone's approved position, which treats financial eligibility and the petition separately and rejects identity-based restrictions; that position does not call for a grant of certiorari.

## Participation and vote

Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg participate. Justice Breyer takes no part, as on February 26; no reason is stated. Four votes are required to grant certiorari. No Justice is recorded as voting to grant, and no dissent is noted.
