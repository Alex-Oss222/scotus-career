## Basis

The [April 1, 1996 filing-administration order](Shieh_and_companions_filing_order_1996-04-01.md) granted L. Shieh's three individual financial motions, 9–0, declined the proposed Rule 39.8 denial for each petition and the proposed future condition, and left all three petitions pending for their ordinary independent consideration. They remained pending when the term closed.

That ordinary consideration takes place at conference. The Court's end-of-summer conference took up matters held over from October Term 1995, and its orders were announced on the order list of Monday, October 7, 1996. Each petition is considered separately. Each seeks review of a fact-bound reopening or prefiling ruling; the April 1 order found their particular notice contentions sufficient to avoid a Rule 39.8 denial but expressly granted no entitlement to review. The Court denies all three. This is consistent with the April 1 order and with Stone's approved position and concurrence, which treat financial leave as an opportunity for consideration, not an endorsement of a petition or a claim to certiorari.

## Participation and vote

Chief Justice Stone-Zsela and Justices Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer participate. Four votes are required to grant certiorari. No Justice is recorded as voting to grant any of the petitions, and no dissent is noted.
