## Authority and exact operative provisions

Congress enacted the [Telecommunications Act of 1996](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-104publ104/html/PLAW-104publ104.htm), Public Law 104-104, on February 8, 1996. The official enacted text, rather than an amended current compilation, controls. Section 302(b), 110 Stat. 124, provides:

> (1) Repeal.—Subsection (b) of section 613 (47 U.S.C. 533(b)) is repealed.

Section 302(b)(3) separately provides:

> The Commission's regulations and policies with respect to video dialtone requirements issued in CC Docket No. 87-266 shall cease to be effective on the date of enactment of this Act. This paragraph shall not be construed to require the termination of any video-dialtone system that the Commission has approved before the date of enactment of this Act.

The repeal has no delayed effective date in section 302. The adjacent six-month deadlines for adopting open-video-system regulations concern new section 653; they do not postpone section 302(b)(1)'s repeal. Section 302(a) separately adds the new sections 651–653, with their distinct regulatory classifications, acquisition restrictions and exceptions, and open-video-system certification rules. This admission does not treat those provisions as the old prohibition continued under another number or determine any party's rights under them.

Section 601(c)(1) provides:

> This Act and the amendments made by this Act shall not be construed to modify, impair, or supersede Federal, State, or local law unless expressly so provided in such Act or amendments.

Section 601(c)(2) separately preserves state and local tax law except as provided in Communications Act sections 622 and 653(c) and section 602 of the 1996 Act. Section 601(a) replaces the specified AT&T, GTE and McCaw consent-decree obligations for future conduct with the amended statutory regime, subject to its stated terms. None of those provisions is an adjudication of a particular pending case or an instruction automatically to vacate an existing judgment.
