## Exact source and scope

[Public Law 104-91, title I](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-104publ91/html/PLAW-104publ91.htm), approved January 6, 1996, is preserved in [official HTML](../sources/chunk4_c/pl104_91.html) and [text](../sources/chunk4_c/pl104_91.txt). Section 101(a), 110 Stat. 10, provides necessary amounts “under the authority and conditions provided in the applicable appropriations Act for the fiscal year 1995” for the listed continuing activities. The list expressly includes “All projects and activities funded under the account heading ‘Grants to States for Medicaid’” at 110 Stat. 12. Section 101's rate limitations, section 102's extent-and-manner rule, section 103's restriction on newly initiated or resumed activities, and section 104's exclusion of newly proposed general conditions remain part of the source. This admission does not calculate a payment or expand an account.

Section 106 makes the authority available until the earliest of replacement appropriation for the activity, enactment of the applicable appropriations Act without provision for it, or September 30, 1996, unless the specified statutory sources otherwise provide. Section 110 states that the period covered by title I is considered to have begun December 16, 1995. Sections 105 and 107–109 retain their enacted expenditure, charging, authorization and apportionment terms. The complete title I text controls; its other accounts are not converted into new case-specific findings here.

The applicable prior-year condition is Public Law 103-333, section 509, **108 Stat. 2573**; 108 Stat. 2539 is the Act's opening page. [The official statute](../sources/chunk4_c/pl103_333.pdf) and [text](../sources/chunk4_c/pl103_333.txt) preserve the exact restriction and all its exceptions. The Medicaid account at 108 Stat. 2554–2555 separately provides an advance appropriation for the first quarter of fiscal year 1996, available until expended. The annual title alone therefore does not establish that every relevant account authority expired September 30, 1995. No new interpretation of section 509's effect on a participating State is adopted by source admission.

The [bounded statutory source review](../sources/chunk4_c/FY1996_MEDICAID_CONTINUATION.md) also verifies that Public Law 104-99's additional continuation excepts activities already provided for in Public Law 104-91. Public Law 104-116's March 15 extension of the specified general deadlines to March 22 does not replace the Medicaid-specific section 106 outside deadline. Those later source checks support the March 18 event's temporal inquiry; they do not enter earlier events as facts known before their enactment. No post-March-18 enactment is relied upon.
