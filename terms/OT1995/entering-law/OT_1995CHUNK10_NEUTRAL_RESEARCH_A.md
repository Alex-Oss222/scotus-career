## Additional verified pre-divergence authority precision

The full [Scalia dissent in Rutan, including its notes](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/88-1872.ZD.html), was checked for the validator's requested join distinction. O'Connor joins Parts II–III, not Part I. Part I gives dispositive force to a longstanding tradition when interpreting ambiguous constitutional language. Part II instead tests patronage under deferential employment balancing, considering party stability, integration, incentives and coercion; Part II-C expressly argues that Elrod/Branti should be overruled because they are wrong and unworkable. Part III alternatively confines their reach to actual loss of a position, title and salary rather than nonhiring, nonpromotion or a substantial-equivalent test. Thus exclusion of Part I does not erase O'Connor's join in Part II-C, and joining Part II does not adopt Part I's distinct method. The validator independently checked the official report's lineup. No historical post-divergence NTEU separate writing was substituted for simulated grounds absent from the supplied slice.
