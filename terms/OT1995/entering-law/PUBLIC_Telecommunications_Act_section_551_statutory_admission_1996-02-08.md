## Law After Decision

Section 551 establishes a statutory program for parental information and blocking technology, including the 13-inch receiver threshold, the carrier exception and the conditions for alternative technology. Its conditional television-rating amendment takes effect one year after enactment only upon the specified FCC determination about the absence of acceptable voluntary rating rules and voluntary rating signals. The FCC must set applicability of the covered manufacturing requirement no earlier than two years after enactment. The statute does not establish that a nationwide rating or receiver-blocking system was operational in March 1996. Congressional findings are not judicial findings about a pending case. No later rule, determination or implementation fact is established here.
