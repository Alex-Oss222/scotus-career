## Pre-divergence primary authorities available locally

Files below are in `../research/chunk9_neutral_authorities/`; each text begins with its official Library of Congress source URL and includes PDF page markers. Downloading a file is not a representation that every page was read. A model should read the portions on which its own commitment depends.

- `Smith_OFFER.txt` / `.pdf`, 431 U.S. 816, especially 839–856 and separate concurrence: nature of protected interest precedes Mathews weighing; emotional bonds can matter, but state-created foster arrangements and the natural family's rights materially qualify the comparison. The Court did not definitively recognize the claimed foster-family interest; it assessed procedure on an assumption. Do not convert its assumption into a holding or its treatment of foster arrangements into a decided rule about every adoptive relationship.
- `Michael_H.txt` / `.pdf`, 491 U.S. 110, especially 130–136: children's asserted symmetrical liberty remains a stated reservation; Scalia's rationale, O'Connor/Kennedy's refusal to join footnote 6, and Stevens's separate account of the actual opportunity for discretionary visitation are distinct. Neither that opportunity nor a reservation creates a right to displace a lawful parent.
- `Lehr.txt` / `.pdf`, 463 U.S. 248, especially 260–268: developed responsibility, opportunity and the actual notice mechanism; biological connection alone and an opportunity obstructed by deception are different predicates. The Court does not prohibit a State from protecting a father beyond the federal minimum.
- `Stanley.txt` / `.pdf`, 405 U.S. 645: parental fitness and process before the State's removal; apply the actual relationship and state-action setting.
- `Santosky.txt` / `.pdf`, 455 U.S. 745: parental termination and risk allocation. Do not export its proof standard without examining the particular deprivation.
- `Parham.txt` / `.pdf`, 442 U.S. 584, especially 599–607 and Brennan/Marshall/Stevens at 625 onward: independent child liberty in institutional confinement can coexist with broad parental authority; the nature of confinement, clinical assessment, form and timing of process remain material.
- `Mathews.txt` / `.pdf`, 424 U.S. 319, especially 332–349: framework for required procedures after identifying a protected interest and an attributable deprivation. The magnitude of alleged harm alone cannot do that antecedent work.
- `DeShaney.txt` / `.pdf`, 489 U.S. 189: private harm, state restraint and the limits left open; read with the actual current simulated Pinder public projection, not as a categorical answer to every affirmative state act.
