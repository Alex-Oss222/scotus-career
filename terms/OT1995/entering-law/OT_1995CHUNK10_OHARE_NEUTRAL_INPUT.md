**Waters**

**Current rule:** An employer acting on a genuine, reasonable account of speech that would be disciplinable under the governing public-employee-speech rules is protected even if that factual account is mistaken. Conversely, an unreasonable and incorrect account does not defeat a claim when the employee actually spoke protected speech and that speech caused the adverse action. The employee must show protected speech was a substantial or motivating factor; the employer may establish by a preponderance that it would have taken the same action absent the protected speech. These limited propositions do not impose liability for every investigation defect or adopt the plurality’s complete investigative procedure.

**Authority by component:**

- Seven-Justice genuine-and-reasonable-account safe harbor: [Waters v. Churchill — A genuine, reasonable account of disciplinable speech supplies a limited safe harbor](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/87e62b7612c15409039d524fd66f743d0ad75e2a/terms/OT1993/records/Waters_v_Churchill_merits_1994-05-31.md#public-projection), 1994-05-31. O'Connor's plurality opinion, joined by Stone-Zsela, Souter and Ginsburg, adopts this sufficient condition; Scalia's concurrence in the judgment, joined by Kennedy and Thomas, expressly accepts it under their broader genuine-ground rule. Seven Justices agree at this precise level; only four adopt the full objective-inquiry framework.
- Six-Justice unreasonable-and-false-account limit: [Waters v. Churchill — An unreasonable false account cannot defeat actually protected speech](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/87e62b7612c15409039d524fd66f743d0ad75e2a/terms/OT1993/records/Waters_v_Churchill_merits_1994-05-31.md#public-projection), 1994-05-31. O'Connor, Stone-Zsela, Souter and Ginsburg accept this proposition under objective factual care; Stevens's dissent, joined by Blackmun, expressly accepts it under their broader actual-speech rule. Six Justices agree on this proposition, but the two dissenters do not join the reasonable-error safe harbor or the judgment vacating the appellate rule.
- Motive and same-decision proof: [Waters v. Churchill — The motive dispute requires remand without restoring defendants' summary judgment](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/scotus-career/blob/87e62b7612c15409039d524fd66f743d0ad75e2a/terms/OT1993/records/Waters_v_Churchill_merits_1994-05-31.md#public-projection), 1994-05-31. O'Connor's four-Justice plurality and Scalia's three-Justice concurrence in the judgment support this common vacatur and limited remand. Blackmun and Stevens would affirm the appellate reversal under their different actual-speech rule; their votes are not counted as support for vacatur.

**Open question:** Unreasonable belief where the actual speech was unprotected, and a universal investigation checklist, are not resolved. The four-Justice complete reasonableness framework is noncontrolling.
