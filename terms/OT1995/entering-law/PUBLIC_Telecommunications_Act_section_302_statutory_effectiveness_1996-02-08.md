## Law After Decision

Section 613(b) of the Communications Act, 47 U.S.C. §533(b), is repealed. The FCC's video-dialtone regulations and policies in CC Docket No. 87-266 cease to be effective on enactment, but that termination does not require termination of a video-dialtone system approved before enactment. The new statutory regulatory scheme and its separate requirements remain distinct from the repealed provision. No particular pending case's mootness or remedy is determined.
