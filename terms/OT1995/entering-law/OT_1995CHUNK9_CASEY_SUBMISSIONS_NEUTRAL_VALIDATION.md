## Operative qualifications for independent modeling

1. **Legal concession is not a factual admission.** The State expressly disclaimed an initial-standing challenge while maintaining its challenge to proof warranting relief. That position does not admit a constitutional violation, causal injury to each claimant or class subgroup, classwide liability, or the appropriateness of the decree. Nor does it establish all jurisdictional facts by stipulation. Preserve the concession's actual procedural significance rather than describing the State as having contested initial standing in its Supreme Court merits submission.
2. **Jurisdiction remains independent of party agreement.** A legal concession cannot supply otherwise absent Article III authority. Initial standing, proof at the applicable litigation stage, entitlement to prospective relief, merits causation, class scope, and the breadth of the remedy must remain distinct inquiries. The nonwaivable jurisdictional duty does not by itself erase preservation limits on a separable merits argument. Any decision to reach or decline that argument needs its own supported ground.
3. **Preservation remains a live legal question, not an invented default.** Respondents' account of the appellate briefs and their request to decline the general actual-injury question are supported submissions; they are not binding findings of forfeiture. The panel's narrower footnote is a verified lower-court statement. Existing positive injury findings cannot be silently replaced with a premise that no inmate ever suffered or faced a concrete impediment. Conversely, identified injuries do not alone establish the reach of every classwide remedy.
4. **Paging is a conditional alternative.** Respondents acknowledge the possibility of constitutional adequacy where prisoners receive an inventory and prompt delivery and are able to use a library independently. They continue to challenge the actual restrictive system. This is neither approval of that system nor agreement that the alternative serves prisoners who cannot independently use legal materials. Physical entry is not conceded to be invariably necessary. Any selected alternative still requires record-supported adequacy and the applicable remedial findings.
5. **Mediation has a limited procedural meaning.** Declining appellate mediation establishes that event only. It supplies no finding of bad faith, relinquishment of preserved objections, liability, or reason to dispense with the statutory limits on prospective relief.
