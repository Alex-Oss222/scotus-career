## Exact source and source limits

The [official promulgation order, 514 U.S. 1161, and Rule 57, 1165–1166](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-514/pdf/USREPORTS-514-BackMatter-4.pdf), were independently retrieved and read. The [House Document 104-65](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CDOC-104hdoc65/pdf/CDOC-104hdoc65.pdf), preserved at `sources/chunk6_a/rule57_1995_manual.pdf`, and [dated-source receipt](../sources/chunk6_a/RULE57_DATED_SOURCE_NOTE.md) supply persistent corroboration. `sources/chunk6_a/rule57_1996_code.html` and its extraction contain the actual Code text and 1995 note. The similarly named `rule57_1996_uscode_official.html` is a govinfo landing/error page and was not used as substantive text.

The exact transition is: “shall take effect on December 1, 1995, and shall govern all proceedings in criminal cases thereafter commenced and, insofar as just and practicable, all proceedings in criminal cases then pending.” The pending-case qualification is indispensable. The amendment does not itself decide whether it applies to an earlier procedural act in a pending case.

The exact substantive changes concern local-rule consistency/nonduplication and uniform numbering, protection from loss of rights for nonwillful violation of a **local form requirement**, and actual notice before a sanction or other disadvantage for breach of a requirement **not found in federal law, federal rules or local district rules**. The power to regulate practice is expressly conditioned on consistency with controlling law. The complete text and the 1995 note were read, including the notice and publication provisions. Neither the notes nor this receipt create an exception to a national rule. The source is a noncase rule instrument; no historical Supreme Court merits judgment is used as governing law.

The relevant criminal holdings, remedial qualifications and procedures, and the habeas and filing-control provisions described below, received substantive review beyond the keyword screen.

No Stone input, private section, provisional commitment, neutral modeling packet or reconciliation handoff was read for this task. Public published separate writings remain part of the permitted public-law record. This context had historical comparator exposure from its previous source-verification assignment; it performed no fresh neutral modeling and imported no historical outcome as simulated authority. No source documents underlying every prior case were reopened, and no absent fact is inferred from the targeted search.
