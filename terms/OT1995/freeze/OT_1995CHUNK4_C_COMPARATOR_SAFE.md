**Event:** 1996-03-18 | **Category:** MERITS | **Action:** Summary review on certiorari petition

**Citation:** 516 U.S. 474

**Docket(s):** No. 95-1025

### SECTION III — HISTORICAL COMPARATOR SUPPLEMENT

- **Historical Outcome:** Certiorari granted as to the second question only; judgment reversed insofar as it affirmed the scope of the injunction, and remanded for entry of an order enjoining Amendment 68 only to the extent it imposes obligations inconsistent with federal law; per curiam.
