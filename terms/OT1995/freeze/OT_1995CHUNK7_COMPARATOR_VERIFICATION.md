### Henderson and Casarotto

[First-pair full-read and legal verification receipt](../sources/chunk7_comparators/FIRST_PAIR_VERIFIED.md).

- **Henderson:** [official report PDF](../sources/chunk7_comparators/USREPORTS-517-654.pdf), [complete text](../sources/chunk7_comparators/USREPORTS-517-654.txt), pp.654–680. Preserves the irreconcilable conflict between Rule 4's extendable 120 days and forthwith service; this service direction's procedural/nonjurisdictional character; federal-right/timely-commencement limits; state-law commencement distinction; and Scalia/Kennedy's reservation of Congress's power to make procedure a waiver condition. Crucially, former Rule 4(j) was enacted by Congress, not promulgated by the Court under the Rules Enabling Act. The majority separately explains Congress-enacted-rule supersession and uses §2072(b) as evidence of intent; do not describe §2072(b)'s direct operation alone as the rationale. Distinguish former good-cause-only extension from then-current Rule 4(m)'s discretionary extensions without good cause. Thomas's dissent addresses both immunity-condition and direct-enactment/implied-repeal objections. No independent alternative disposition of personal-injury merits.
- **Casarotto:** [PDF](../sources/chunk7_comparators/USREPORTS-517-681.pdf), [text](../sources/chunk7_comparators/USREPORTS-517-681.txt), pp.681–689. Preemption extends to arbitration agreements covered by FAA; generally applicable contract defenses survive, arbitration-specific disabilities do not. Volt's agreed procedural coordination differs from invalidation. Footnote 3 declines the proffered general informed-consent/reasonable-expectations alternative because it was not the state court's actual ground; does not decide it against respondents categorically. Thomas maintains FAA §2 does not apply in state court. Remedy includes remand, omitted by runtime's shorthand.
