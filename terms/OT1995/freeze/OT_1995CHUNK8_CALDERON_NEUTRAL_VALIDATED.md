## Financial-leave source and actual exception

The signed May 8 motion seeks leave to proceed in forma pauperis and file the opposition under Rule 33.2. It states that Moore sought no such leave in either lower federal court and that no affidavit is attached because the District Court appointed counsel under the Criminal Justice Act of 1964. This is counsel's filed assertion of statutory appointment, not a newly recovered appointment order or independently verified account balance.

Contemporaneous Rule 39.1 ordinarily requires the prescribed notarized affidavit or §1746 declaration and a statement of earlier leave requests and outcomes. When the District Court or Court of Appeals appointed counsel under the Criminal Justice Act, 18 U.S.C. §3006A, or another applicable federal statute, **no affidavit or declaration is required**, but the motion must cite the appointment statute. The recovered motion identifies the CJA route. Absence of the ordinary affidavit is therefore not a defect if that appointment premise is accepted. The 1995 rule does not impose the later appointment-order-copy formulation.

Rule 39.2 requires accompanying filing, Rule 21 compliance, and ordinarily an original and ten copies; only a confined unrepresented inmate may submit the original alone. Rule 39.3 ordinarily uses Rule 33.2 preparation, subject to express Rule 33.1 requirements and impossibility, and preserves substance and jurisdictional-timeliness requirements. Paragraph 4 requires Rule 29 service proof for fee-free docketing. Paragraph 5 permits a challenge to leave grounds. Paragraph 8 concerns frivolous or malicious certiorari petitions, jurisdictional statements or extraordinary-writ petitions; it is not a finding about this represented respondent's opposition. The source supplies no filing defect or challenge requiring one to be invented.

Acceptance or rejection of the asserted appointment route is an independent filing determination. It cannot be derived from the appeal's mootness merits. Do not demand modern prisoner-account materials or manufacture financial amounts. Any genuinely disputed service, appointment or timeliness fact must be sourced if material, not presumed against a party because the research packet lacks a document.
