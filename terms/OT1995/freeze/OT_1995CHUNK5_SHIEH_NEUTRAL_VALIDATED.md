## Entering law and operative distinctions

The preparation baseline is coordinated opening law through June 29, 1995, supplemented by actual public current-term decisions through March 18, 1996. The April 1 matters share their proper common pre-group baseline. Later prepared dispositions do not supply law. The Rules effective October 2, 1995 control; PLRA's later enactment supplies no three-strikes rule here.

The exact operative Standards entries are reproduced in [ENTERING_STANDARDS_VERBATIM.md](../sources/chunk5_d/ENTERING_STANDARDS_VERBATIM.md):

* **Associations seeking statutory fee relief** — `Present operation effective: 1993-01-12; Rowland v. California Men’s Colony, Unit II Men’s Advisory Council, for the components identified above.` An association is not categorically excluded by then-effective §1915. An authorized affidavit must establish actual inability to pay or give security and asserted redress; legally available resources and sham concealment may be examined without automatic aggregation of every member's resources. Lack of an entity account or a balance-sheet deficit is insufficient by itself. Lawful representation and screening remain distinct; no lay advocacy license, counsel entitlement, or universal organizational means test follows.
* **Factual frivolousness and review of fee-screening dismissals** — `Present operation effective: Denton, May 4, 1992.` Its §1915(d) standard allows rejection of clearly baseless, irrational or wholly incredible allegations, with the initial assessment favoring the claimant; ordinary disbelief or improbability is insufficient, and genuine disputes may not be tried through that screen. Corroborating affidavits are not required merely to plead. This does not make every Rule 12(b)(6) failure frivolous, and does not itself provide the complete Supreme Court Rule 39.8 standard.

No dedicated Rule 39.8, Day, Sassower, Zatko or Martin Standards entry was found in the current register by exact-text searches. Do not invent a Standards title or effective-date entry. Their public holdings, plus current Jones, govern the filing inquiries.

Rule 39.1 requires the prescribed motion and notarized affidavit or §1746 declaration, subject to its actual qualifying appointed-counsel exception. Rule 39.8 permits, rather than requires, refusal for a frivolous or malicious **particular** filing. Zatko permits a supported present-paper denial; Martin requires examining each current petition and keeps its fee disposition distinct from certiorari. Martin, Day, Sassower, Anderson, Attwood and Jones require a separately supported applicant-specific and category-specific justification for future controls, including why ordinary individual treatment is inadequate. None establishes a universal warning prerequisite, numerical threshold, categorical immunity from prospective controls, or duty to exhaust every conceivable lesser measure.

Jones's actual February 26 order **leaves both financial leave and certiorari pending**, declines the proposed current Rule 39.8 denial on the present showing, and declines the proposed future condition. It makes no indigence/ineligibility finding and imposes no refiling duty, payment deadline, or default. It supplies no extra Shieh warning, filing history, or automatic present disposition. Shieh has now supplied different financial evidence and different present papers; Jones's missing-proof premise must not be mechanically transplanted.

The official Rules adopted July 26 and effective October 2, 1995 have now been checked in `sources/chunk4_a/SCOTUS_Rules1995_515US1195_1254.txt` (matching official PDF), including full Rules 33 and 39 and Rule 38's fees. Rule 38(a) sets the docket fee at $300. Rule 39.1 requires the motion and notarized affidavit or §1746 declaration in the prescribed form, identifying the disposition of any other-court financial application; an actual qualifying federal statutory counsel appointment supplies its stated exception, with the statute cited. Rule 39.2 normally requires an original and ten copies with the document and service; an unrepresented institutional inmate may file the original alone. Rule 39.3 ordinarily uses Rule 33.2 and requires legibility, with due allowance for pro se presentation but adherence to timeliness and substance. Rule 39.4 directs docketing on submission of the required documents and proof of service without prepayment; Rule 39.5 permits an opposition to financial leave. Rule 39.8 says the Court **may** deny leave when satisfied that the certiorari petition, jurisdictional statement or extraordinary-writ petition is frivolous or malicious. It supplies no automatic prospective bar or automatic compliance deadline. Rule 33.1 governs booklet format and forty copies; Rule 33.2 permits its specified paper format and ordinary forty-page certiorari-petition limit, with the actual listed exclusions. Any paid-filing direction must accurately apply the rule and the Court's actual order rather than inventing a deadline or nonfiling default. The standalone then-effective §1915 text remains unverified by this worker; its simulated construction is supplied by the authorized public law. ## Lawful paths for independent assessment

1. Accept an adequately established individual financial showing and determine each current petition under ordinary filing and petition procedures. Financial leave grants neither review nor underlying relief. The presence of weak allegations does not erase a materially distinct access or notice argument without assessing it.
2. Preserve one or more financial motions while resolving the identified income/resource tension or another genuine financial uncertainty. Any direction must identify the actual needed information. No adverse finding, retroactive nonfiling, invented warning, or automatic deadline follows from uncertainty itself.
3. Deny a particular motion on an actually supported Rule 39.8 finding, specifying the filing-specific grounds and any proper paid-filing opportunity under the effective Rules. If a paper contains a distinct potentially nonfrivolous question, the analysis must explain why that question does or does not change the current-paper finding. No underlying certiorari denial follows automatically.
4. Adopt or decline a precisely bounded future condition only after separate examination of its support, covered filing category, exceptions, and inadequacy of ordinary treatment. The current source set does not establish a sequence of prior Supreme Court denials/warnings in the simulated timeline; historical Court orders cannot fill it. Ordinary lower-court abuse findings alone do not establish all predicates for Supreme Court future certiorari restrictions.
