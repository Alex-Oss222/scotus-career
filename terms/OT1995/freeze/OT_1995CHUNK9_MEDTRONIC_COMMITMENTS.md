## Questions, claims and common remedial boundaries

The 1982 substantial-equivalence clearance of the Model 4011 lead was not PMA or substantive FDA approval of its safety. The Activitrax pacemaker and lead allegedly failed after implantation; defect, breach and causation are not established. Classification, permission to market, required submissions, actual manufacturing controls and an approved substantive design are distinct.

No. **95-754** concerns Medtronic's challenge to restoration of negligent design and strict liability insofar as based on design. No. **95-886** concerns the Lohrs' challenge to preemption of negligent manufacture, negligent failure to warn, and manufacture/labeling-based strict liability. The consortium claim follows the surviving underlying claims without an independent finding of entitlement. The separate Florida-law warranty dismissal was not appealed and remains undisturbed.

The lower court expressly reached the parallel-duty argument despite its first appearance in reply. There is no procedural forfeiture commitment. It assumed, without deciding, that Florida recognizes such a duty and that the complaint could encompass it. Restoration of that possibility does not decide either question. The late FDA submission in Talbott was rejected below; its treatment does not mean that no FDA view existed. No unexamined present-case amicus position is attributed to the agency.

All eight Justices reach the submitted express-preemption questions. Each rejects preemption based merely on grandfathering, class III classification, §510(k) clearance, general oversight, or the submission of proposed labeling. Section 807.97 expressly separates substantial equivalence from official approval. Section 820.100's requirement that manufacture conform to an original design or approved changes is not itself FDA substantive adoption of that design. None decides preemption for an actual PMA approval, an unestablished device-specific standard, or a different device.

All eight preserve a genuinely equivalent, independently available state duty from **§360k(a) express preemption**. An additional damages remedy does not alone make the substantive requirement different. Equality must concern the actual obligation and application, not a pleading's use of the word parallel. A theory demanding additional warning content, different production conduct, or liability notwithstanding satisfaction of the identified federal duty cannot secure equivalence merely by invoking federal law. State elements, pleading, actual violation, causal connection and available relief remain for ordinary proceedings.

Section 337 does not create a private FDCA-enforcement action; its State-enforcement exception concerns enumerated food provisions, with its own notice and federal-enforcement limitations, not these private device claims. This preserves the distinction between an independent Florida duty and direct enforcement of the federal Act. No unpresented standalone §337 defense or later implied-preemption doctrine is decided. Freightliner leaves room for an independently established actual conflict; none is established merely by clearance or regulatory overlap here. No universal immunity from a proven conflict is declared.

## Statutory and regulatory qualifications integral to the commitments

1. **The enacted comparison.** Section 360k(a) requires a state requirement respecting a human-use device, an applicable federal requirement, a difference or addition, and the requisite relation to safety, effectiveness or another federally included matter. Common-law liability can impose a requirement; it is neither categorically excluded nor automatically displaced. The actual duty is compared, rather than the negligence or strict-liability label. The Justices divide over the sufficiency and operation of the asserted federal manufacturing and warning requirements, as stated below.

2. **Separate exemption mechanisms.** Section 360k(b) permits, on State or local application and after notice and opportunity for an oral hearing, an exemption by regulation under prescribed conditions if the state requirement is more stringent, **or** if compelling local conditions require it and compliance would not violate an applicable federal requirement. The no-federal-violation condition belongs to the second alternative; it is not silently added to the first. Section 808.1(c) preserves federal requirements despite an exemption. No pertinent granted exemption is established. Nonpreempted equal duties do not first need an exemption. Section 360j(f)(2)'s manufacturer/device GMP exemption or variance is different: exemption requires that compliance be unnecessary to assure safety and effectiveness; a variance requires adequate proposed methods, facilities and controls, subject to necessary conditions and the statute's petition, decision and hearing provisions. No such exemption or variance is found here, and no agency-exhaustion requirement for the Lohrs is invented.

3. **Section 808.1 in full.** Subsection (b) includes force-of-law court decisions. Subsection (d) identifies specific counterpart regulations or other specific requirements applicable to a particular device before divergent state requirements are displaced. Its examples preserve general requirements whose purpose reaches other products as well as devices, including the stated commercial-law example, and equal or substantially identical requirements. Those provisions are not read without (d)(6)(ii): general adulteration/misbranding prohibitions may survive, but their application as a divergent substantive requirement for a specific device can be preempted; actual interpretation and enforcement, rather than identical wording alone, matters. The further exceptions remain intact: professional-practice licensing subject to conflicting federal §520(e)/(g) restrictions; state procurement contracts; Medicaid or similar payment criteria; general enforcement, inspection, registration and licensing subject to conflicting federal records, reporting and GMP requirements; administrative delegations; revenues and fees; and the identified atomic-energy/radiation measures until applicable specific FDA requirements intervene. None is factually applied to create an exemption here. Subsection (e)'s FDA responsibility, subject to judicial review, to determine equality or difference through the prescribed procedures does not establish an unpresented exhaustion bar. Subsection (f) preserves applicable federal duties whether or not a state rule is preempted or exempted.

4. **Manufacturing.** Original Part 820 imposes real obligations, not advice: quality systems, personnel and facilities, component acceptance, production controls and records, inspection, failures, complaints, labels, packaging and distribution have operative terms. Its scope concerns finished human-use devices; an accessory suitable for use can be a finished device, while the provision does not generally apply to component/part manufacturers and blood products have the separate Part 606 treatment. The everyday word component does not decide the lead's regulatory status. Additional critical-device duties require their own applicability basis; class III is not silently equated with every critical-device condition. Where compliance with two regulations is impossible the specific device regulation supersedes the general rule under §820.1; the exemption/variance route remains. No specific command, granted exception, violation, critical-device factual classification or substantive design approval is supplied by inference. Section 820.100's conformance requirement and device-master records do not themselves select the safe design for this lead.

5. **Warnings.** Section 801.109 conditionally exempts qualifying prescription devices from the consumer adequate-directions requirement, preserving its lawful possession and prescription/order conditions. Subsection (b)'s prescription caution and method-of-use label requirements have the surgical-instrument exception. Subsection (c)'s practitioner-use information includes indications, effects, routes, methods, frequency/duration and relevant hazards, contraindications, side effects and precautions; information **may** be omitted only if commonly known to licensed practitioners, with the written-request/Commissioner-opinion route retained. No qualifying omission is found for this lead. Subsection (d) separately governs labeling purporting to give use information, with its name-only reminder-piece exception. Subsection (e)'s issuance/revision-date requirement excludes labels and cartons. These are different exceptions, and permission is not obligation. Submission under §807.87(e) does not establish substantive FDA approval of particular warnings. No later amendment is treated as an earlier clearance condition.

6. **Savings and agency authority.** Section 360h(d) preserves liability despite compliance with an order **under that section**; it is not an unrestricted savings clause for every device suit. Section 371(a)'s efficient-enforcement rulemaking grant, together with the agency's device-regulation responsibilities and express §360k(b) task, supplies relevant authority for interpreting and administering this scheme, not an unlimited power to create or withdraw preemption. The formal §808.1 regulation receives consideration on its actual terms. Actual MCI requires statutory boundaries first; Sweet Home's six-Justice Chevron ground remains controlling; Smiley applies that sequence and assumes without deciding that courts always independently determine preemption's scope. Smiley therefore does not itself command deference to every direct preemption pronouncement. No separate litigating interpretation is automatically controlling, and neither its litigation setting nor age alone conclusively disqualifies a reasoned interpretation.

## Decisive actual precedents and individual evidence

**Cipollone (June 24, 1992),** as reproduced in the entering-law slice, has proposition-specific authority. Its 1965-Act construction had seven supporters. Under the successor provision, Stevens's analysis joined by White, O'Connor and Stone and Scalia's separate analysis joined by Thomas supplied six supporters for inclusion and displacement of the covered promotional warning duties. No whole amended-Act opinion had five joins. The surviving warranty, independent nonpromotional and deception categories rest on Stevens's narrower category-specific grounds with Blackmun, Kennedy and Souter's broader nonpreemption position where the subset test is met. The broader no-common-law theory is not controlling. Those actual positions, not invented formal joins, supply individual evidence here. The cigarette wording does not decide the MDA's entire scope.

**Easterwood (April 21, 1993)** requires the applicable federal predicate and actual coverage rather than regulatory overlap; its railroad-warning predicates and speed holding remain distinct. Its statutory term covering does not replace §360k's different language. **Freightliner (April 18, 1995),** the public entry in `terms/OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK4.md`, is Ginsburg's full opinion joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas and Breyer among the Associates. Scalia agreed only in the judgment without an explanation, from which no reason is invented. It requires an operative federal basis and rejects converting a regulatory absence into an affirmative right to avoid state liability, while preserving actual-conflict analysis.

**Sweet Home (June 29, 1995),** public entry in `terms/OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK8.md`, supplies Souter's bounded statutory and six-Justice deference grounds joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Ginsburg and Breyer. O'Connor's own concurrence insists on the real causal and application limits. Scalia's dissent, joined by Thomas, insists that interpretation remain within enacted authority; it does not establish a general rejection of Chevron. **Smiley (June 3, 1996),** `../entering-law/PUBLIC_Smiley_v_Citibank_South_Dakota_NA_merits_1996-06-03.md`, supplies the actual current application, joined in full by all eight Associates, of delegated interpretation, independent statutory limits, particular federal permission, and carefully bounded use of an interpretation for antecedent transactions. Its specific banking permission is distinguished from this clearance. Souter's actual unanimous **Travelers** opinion supplies attention to the particular statutory connection and the continuing state role, without converting that ERISA decision into a general medical-device immunity from federal law.

## Individual issue-specific commitments

The following dispositions incorporate the common claim mapping and qualifications above. Each Justice independently supports the design disposition and the conditional parallel-duty restoration. Their manufacturing/warning grounds are separated because identical ultimate relief would not establish agreement with a broader rationale.

### Stevens

**Commitment:** Affirm in No. 95-754 as to negligent design and design-based strict liability. Reverse the manufacture, warning and corresponding strict-liability preemption barriers in No. 95-886 and remand, including the independently sufficient correction of the parallel-duty bar.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His Cipollone claim-by-claim approach requires both the actual state duty and the federal subject Congress made preemptive. The general GMP and prescription-information provisions here do not establish a counterpart federal decision prescribing this lead's safe manufacture or adequate warning in a way that displaces the presented traditional duties. Section 808.1's specificity and general-applicability terms reinforce that reading. His Sweet Home and Smiley joins support respect for the authorized regulatory interpretation within the statutory boundary. No holding that damages can never impose an MDA requirement is necessary.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The strongest contrary path treats every enforceable GMP or warning instruction applicable to this device as sufficient, with a jury's further standard necessarily additional. The provisions' breadth does not make them optional, but their legal force does not alone establish the requisite counterpart to these duties. Conversely, a categorical immunity for all common-law actions would disregard his actual Cipollone inclusion holding and §808.1(b). He joins the bounded absence-of-counterpart ground and equivalent-duty rule; he does not join blanket displacement from manufacturing/labeling subject overlap or an adjudication of Florida tort elements.

### O'Connor

**Commitment:** Affirm in No. 95-754. In No. 95-886 affirm the legal preemption of manufacture, warning and corresponding strict-liability theories **insofar as they demand different or additional conduct from the applicable federal manufacturing or labeling requirements**; reverse the categorical rejection of genuinely equivalent duties and remand to identify the actual duty and remaining Florida claim. This is not affirmance of complete dismissal of a count that could support a parallel theory.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** Her Cipollone join recognizes that juries can impose additional substantive warning requirements. Her Easterwood position and Freightliner join require an operative applicable federal rule; unlike the absent antilock standard, GMP and practitioner-information commands are present. Neither §360k nor §808.1 requires every operative command to name one device model. The specificity inquiry can be satisfied by an actual manufacturing or warning command applicable to the device, despite its application to other devices. Her Sweet Home concurrence and full Smiley join support the agency's bounded authority and exact application limits, not a universal exemption based on a state rule's general form.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The strongest nonpreemption path uses §808.1(d)'s counterpart and general-applicability language to deny displacement by these generic rules. She reads that language with (b) and (d)(6)(ii)'s actual substantive-application qualification; the rule does not remove every court-imposed divergent device requirement. Yet the opposite path barring even an equal duty fails the enacted different/additional condition and (d)(2). She refuses both a categorical all-generic-rules-are-insufficient proposition and a categorical all-manufacturing/warning-suits-are-barred proposition. No factual finding of regulatory compliance, applicability of a particular exception, or breach is made.

### Scalia

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; in No. 95-886 sustain preemption of the divergent manufacturing/warning duties described in O'Connor's disposition, reverse the equal-duty bar, and remand for the duty-specific application. Strict liability tracks the actual underlying obligation.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His own Cipollone separate analysis, joined by Thomas, gives operative effect to requirements imposed by damages without preferring enacted state commands over judicial ones. Section 360k asks whether a federal requirement applies, not whether it is written for this manufacturer alone. Actual substantive GMP and labeling rules can meet that description. His MCI position and Sweet Home dissent require statutory authority before deference; his subsequent full Smiley join also forecloses treating deference as categorically unavailable. Section 808.1 can be read consistently with the statute by preserving equal/general state rules while recognizing their divergent device-specific applications. This does not depend on an invented reason for his unexplained Freightliner judgment-only agreement.

**Competing paths and barrier:** A narrow construction based on the historic state tort role has textual support in the required comparison and §808.1's examples; it does not justify adding a universal device-model-specific condition. The strongest broader preemption claim treats an additional civil remedy as an additional requirement even when conduct is identical. The word requirement concerns the substantive duty here; equal conduct is not made different by damages alone. He will not join a free-floating policy presumption overriding the enacted comparison, a grant of FDA power to contradict it, or automatic immunity for an actually noncompliant manufacturer.

### Kennedy

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; reverse the contested manufacture/warning and associated strict-liability preemption rulings in No. 95-886, including the parallel-duty ruling, and remand.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His actual Cipollone support for Blackmun's broader common-law preservation is individual evidence of his attention to the distinction between compensation and affirmative regulation; it does not repeal that case's controlling inclusion of particular common-law duties. His Freightliner, Sweet Home and Smiley joins support a narrower sufficient ground here: no substantive safety approval or counterpart manufacturing/warning choice has been shown to displace these state duties, and §808.1 reasonably preserves their operation in this setting. A genuine equal duty plainly survives the express clause.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The operative-force argument for generic federal commands is substantial, and he does not deny their enforceability. It does not establish that these commands make the asserted state safety duty different from a federally selected requirement for this lead. The broader path excluding every common-law action is unnecessary and cannot be generalized from his noncontrolling Cipollone position. He can join a duty-specific ground that acknowledges potential common-law preemption; he withholds assent from converting regulatory applicability or permission to market into comprehensive tort displacement. His Smiley join preserves specific statutory federal options, but this record supplies no comparable right to market a defectively manufactured or inadequately warned device free of the asserted liability.

### Souter

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; reverse the disputed No. 95-886 barriers and remand on all the challenged manufacturing/warning theories, with separate protection for a genuinely equivalent duty.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His actual Travelers opinion examines what state obligations do under the particular federal text, rather than deriving displacement from connection alone. His Sweet Home opinion and Smiley opinion/position require statutory authority before respecting the responsible agency's reasonable construction. Section 808.1's specificity and general-applicability provisions fit the absence here of a device-specific substantive approval or demonstrated counterpart requirement. His broader Cipollone common-law position supports caution but remains noncontrolling; this commitment rests on the MDA comparison, not on that broader theory becoming law.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The alternative treats detailed general controls as the federal counterpart and any additional state assessment as divergent. Their detail establishes enforceable federal duties; it does not establish equivalence of the regulated subject and the asserted state duty at the level needed to displace these claims. Nor does a state tort label itself protect a claim against a proven divergent device-specific application under §808.1(d)(6)(ii). He joins the bounded application and reasonable regulatory reading, refuses automatic FDA supremacy over the judicial statutory question, and leaves a later case involving an actual substantive counterpart open. He rejects the lower court's remedy-alone reason for barring equal duties.

### Thomas

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; in No. 95-886 affirm preemption of actually divergent manufacturing/warning requirements, reverse the rejection of genuinely equal state duties, and remand as specified for O'Connor and Scalia. Preserve the strict-liability and derivative-claim distinctions.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His join to Scalia's actual Cipollone analysis supports the ordinary breadth of requirement and inclusion of jury-imposed duties. His full Freightliner join preserves the demand for an operative federal predicate; that predicate is absent for substantive design approval but present in actual applicable manufacturing and labeling commands. His Sweet Home dissent join insists on the enacted delegation, while his full Smiley join accepts reasonable interpretation within it. These positions support giving legal effect to specific commands without requiring that they name only the 4011 lead.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The opposing construction reads the FDA's specificity language as an exclusion for every general federal standard. Read with the whole regulation, including court decisions, equal requirements and divergent substantive device applications, that construction goes farther than necessary. The broad pro-preemption alternative also fails when it treats any damages remedy as an additional substantive duty; §360k(a) retains an actual difference/addition condition. He does not join a rule that all tort law is exempt, all regulation displaces tort law, or FDA may dispense with Congress's conditions. No current assumption of compliance or later rule fills a missing historical predicate.

### Ginsburg

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; reverse the challenged No. 95-886 preemption rulings and remand, separately rejecting the categorical parallel-duty bar.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** Her Freightliner opinion makes the actual aspect regulated decisive and refuses to manufacture a protected federal choice from permission or absence. Her Sweet Home and Smiley joins retain both statutory limits and reasonable agency interpretation. The existing GMP and information provisions are enforceable, but the record identifies neither substantive FDA approval of this design nor a sufficiently corresponding federal determination of the manufacturing/warning duty to displace these traditional claims. Section 808.1 confirms the bounded comparison; its equal-duty protection independently defeats the lower court's remedy rationale.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The strongest opposing path distinguishes Freightliner because real regulations, rather than an invalidated standard, exist here. That distinction makes examination necessary; it does not make the entire regulated subject a federal ceiling. She does not import Freightliner's statutory wording, savings clause or unresolved common-law question into §360k. A state-law duty may be a requirement under this clause, and an actual different device-specific application remains possible. She refuses both the categorical absence of common-law preemption and the lower court's presumption that a generic negligence standard necessarily demands additional conduct. Warranty remains outside the appeal despite Cipollone's treatment of voluntary promises elsewhere.

### Breyer

**Commitment:** Affirm No. 95-754; reverse the challenged No. 95-886 barriers and remand, including equivalent-duty restoration. His manufacturing/warning agreement is at the application level described here, not a categorical rule that a generally applicable federal regulation can never preempt.

**Sufficient ground and own authority:** His actual Freightliner, Sweet Home and Smiley joins support comparing actual obligations and giving weight to the administering agency within its authority. A common-law judgment can function like a state regulatory command. But these generally framed controls leave substantial practical choices with manufacturers; their existence does not show a federal selection of the particular safe manufacturing or warning obligation that the Lohrs' claims would contradict or supplement in the preemptive sense. The FDA's formal specificity and equal-duty rules support that conclusion. The present clearance supplies no substantive design rule, and the independent parallel-duty route supplies no regulatory conflict by itself.

**Competing paths and barrier:** The strongest contrary path stresses that a broad federal requirement can have a concrete device application and a jury can impose a different one. He accepts that possibility; he rejects its categorical use to dispose of these claims without the required comparison. The converse categorical rule requiring a regulation to single out an individual model would also be too broad: an otherwise general rule can establish a precise applicable command whose divergent state counterpart is displaced. He can join the result, the common-law-inclusion proposition, the parallel-duty rule, and the present absence of a sufficient contrary federal requirement. He does not join either an all-common-law exemption or a categorical rule that only individually negotiated/device-model-specific commands can ever preempt. This is a material join barrier if proposed language exceeds the application resolved here.
