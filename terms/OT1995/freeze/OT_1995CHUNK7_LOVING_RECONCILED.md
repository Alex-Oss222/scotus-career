## Historical comparison and material corrections

Historically, Kennedy's opinion received complete joins from Rehnquist, Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer. O'Connor and Scalia joined Parts I, II, III, IV-B and IV-C, withholding IV's introductory discussion and IV-A. Thomas joined the judgment alone. Stevens's concurrence, joined by Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer, preserved the substantial capital service-connection question. Scalia's concurrence, joined by O'Connor, rejected English constitutional history as a basis for the allocation and distinguished permissible executive assignments from delegation of legislative power. Thomas's separate ground combined the unresolved military application of civilian aggravator requirements with the special constitutional setting of congressional and presidential military regulation. All historically affirmed; there was no historical plurality problem.

The independent freeze correctly resolves the presented authority challenge for all eight, but agreement on that result did not establish eight joins in a common rationale. Three corrections are required:

1. **Thomas remains judgment-only.** Replace his provisional direction to join the common textual-authorization and actual-delegation-sufficiency propositions with a separate military-specific ground. He accepts the lawfulness of this enacted scheme and its implementation, but does not adopt the common opinion's use of nonmilitary delegation doctrine or a general capital-delegation framework. His *Weiss* historical-practice route and his *Ryder*, *Neal* and capital-case joins do not supply a concrete changed premise overcoming the historical nonjoin.
2. **Preserve the four-Justice service-connection reservation.** Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer do not merely avoid deciding an unpresented claim. Their agreement expressly leaves open the substantial question whether capital military prosecutions require a service connection notwithstanding *Solorio*'s noncapital holding. The actual military connections are sufficient for this case. The final neutral already establishes Fay's active-duty status and killing on Fort Hood and Sharbino's retired Army status and killing in Killeen. No additional pickup-location fact from the historical concurrence is needed or imported. The reservation establishes no constitutional service-connection requirement and does not reopen tribunal jurisdiction here.
3. **Preserve Scalia and O'Connor's allocation explanation and historical nonjoin.** Their affirmance rests on Congress's enacted assignment of implementation to an officer with genuine executive and Commander-in-Chief responsibilities. Legislative power itself is not lawfully transferable; an assignment can become unconstitutional when its extent and lack of constraints amount to a transfer. Their acceptance of the confined propositions below does not join the historical IV introduction/IV-A, English Parliament–Crown analogies, or a free-ranging institutional balance. This explanation has a separate legal function even where the common propositions use compatible language.

These are reconciliations of provisional conclusions after authorized comparator exposure, not retroactive edits to independent modeling and not invented circulation events.

| **P1 — Actual statutory authorization** | Articles 18 and 56 authorize presidential restrictions on the death punishment Congress already selected for Article 118(1) and (4); Article 36 supplies additional statutory confirmation and the implemented procedural authority. Read together they authorize these RCM1004 restrictions. The statutory word “limits” is not confined to a numerical maximum. The President cannot make Article 118(2) or (3) capital, create a new capital offense, exceed or contradict Congress's punishment choice, or derive substantive authority merely by calling a factor procedural. | **P2 — No additional express-factor formula** | The existing enacted authorization is sufficient without a capital-specific formula expressly naming aggravating factors. | **P4 — Adequate bounds for this delegation** | Congress's selected capital offenses, death-or-life alternatives, express presidential limiting role and consistency restraints, operating in the President's actual military executive sphere, adequately bound this assignment. No military exemption from structural review or general rule about a newly created actor or unrelated executive sphere follows. |
| **P5 — Limited no-error disposition and unresolved Article II alternative** | The presented authorization, clear-authorization, categorical-delegability and guidance challenges fail. Affirm CAAF's judgment against those challenges without harmless-error review, a new sentence proceeding or an execution order. This reservation neither recognizes nor rejects that freestanding power. |
