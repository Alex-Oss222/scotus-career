## The condition and its application

Stone requires a valid informed waiver, leaves the constitutional source unresolved, preserves the guilty plea and conviction, and requires vacatur of an **identified unsupported portion** of the property order. His text expressly allows actual admissions and existing evidence to establish a basis without a separate hearing for every asset. It rejects both presumed universal eligibility and presumed universal invalidity. It also retains ordinary preservation, statutory conditions, and third-party interests. Thus neither the absence of a Rule 11(f) duty nor the general agreement can discharge his statutory limitation; equally, the limitation does not create a blanket new asset-by-asset proceeding.

The strongest contrary argument is substantial: Libretti personally objected to the factual basis of the whole forfeiture (JA154); the later court found that the government's aggregate-proceeds theory had not demonstrated §853(p)'s conditions (JA303 n.1); and the circuit used broad all-assets language (JA325–326). Those matters cannot be erased, relabeled as an unpreserved objection, or answered by saying that consent authorizes punishment. They support Stevens's frozen demand for an independent statutory-assurance remand. They do not, on these sources and the questions reviewed, identify an own-property portion that Stone has directed must be vacated. His conditional remedy differs from a categorical demand for that additional proceeding.

No prior final judgment of invalidity is a prerequisite to Stone's relief. An appellate court could identify a statutory defect from the properly presented record itself. Nor must a defendant disprove hypothetical statutory theories that the government has never supported. This review relies instead on the actual scope of the findings, the still-unresolved tracing and ownership matters, the operative order's stated treatment of the check, and the procedural questions presented. It invents no alternate statutory basis to save a particular item.

## Assembly guardrails

Stone may affirm on the reviewed questions while expressly withholding the circuit's any-property-by-agreement rationale. The operative sentence remains in place at its lawful scope, subject to the actual third-party amendments and the circuit's power-of-disposition qualification. This is the practical consequence of affirmance, not a declaration that uncertainty itself validates forfeiture or an independent finding of statutory eligibility for every item. No unestablished future reopening or collateral remedy is promised.

Suggested substance for his published position: An informed waiver permits resolution without deciding the constitutional source of the jury right. An agreement cannot enlarge §853. The order and record support rejecting the demanded general new proceeding, and the reviewed claims establish no identified remaining portion requiring statutory vacatur. The Court does not thereby validate every substitute-property predicate, undo third-party relief, or decide whether an actual pension interest is legally inalienable. A demonstrated unsupported portion would require separation and lawful treatment, preserving supported portions and the conviction.

This preserves Stone's fixed core because the conditional remedy remains operative if its factual/legal trigger is established; it does not change the trigger to consent, lack of objection, or inconvenience. Assembly must not join him to a categorical denial of a constitutional jury source, an all-assets statutory sufficiency holding, or a rule that an existing unlawful sentence is immune simply because Rule 11(f) is inapplicable. His constitutional reservation and statutory limitation require explicit part-level joins or a separate writing as the actual final topology warrants.
