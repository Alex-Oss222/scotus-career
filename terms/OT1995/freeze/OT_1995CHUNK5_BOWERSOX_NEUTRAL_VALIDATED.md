Rule 4 calls for prompt examination and facial dismissal only when nonentitlement plainly appears, otherwise responsive pleading or other appropriate action, with service. The exact relevant Standards titles/effective authorities are:

* **Equitable delay independently of a habeas procedural bar** — *Gomez*, April 21, 1992. The State's enforcement interest and unjustified withholding of a long-available challenge can matter independently of a habeas procedural bar. Serious constitutional issues and irreversible consequences must be considered but do not alone compel a late stay; later strengthening evidence does not necessarily excuse delay. This is contextual discretion, not a categorical late-filing rule, minimum number of previous petitions, or universal bar to later federal stays. It leaves the *Barefoot*, *Delo* and proper *McCleskey* rules intact. Its later unexplained orders and case-specific authorization condition are not general precedent.
* **Capital habeas counsel before a merits petition and discretionary stays** — *McFarland v. Scott*, June 30, 1994. An identified statutory preparation proceeding can be pending for §2251 purposes; the statute supplies the express anti-injunction exception. Stays remain discretionary; reasonable preparation must be protected, while proved inexcusable disregard and obstruction can justify denial. They are neither automatic nor indefinite. Qualification and good-cause rules for appointed counsel, necessity findings for services and possible retroactive authorization remain separate; appointment authorizes no unidentified expenses. This does not make every later petition a new preparation entitlement.
* **Crime innocence as a gateway to otherwise barred habeas claims** — *Schlup v. Delo*, January 23, 1995. Reliable new evidence plus the whole probative record must make it more likely than not that no reasonable properly instructed juror would convict. Reliability and likely credibility must be assessed; delay and inconsistency may matter, but neither impeachment evidence nor inmate evidence is categorically excluded. A possible acquittal, ordinary merits prejudice or the reviewing judge's own doubt is insufficient. The gateway does not prove a constitutional violation or order a hearing, writ, retrial, release or stay. Exhaustion, default, abuse, retroactivity and a sufficient independent merits ground remain distinct. The then-effective §2244(b) new-ground and no-deliberate-withholding/other-abuse conditions must both be respected; a particular State abuse pleading precedes the prisoner's burden.
* **Capital eligibility innocence as a gateway to barred habeas claims** — *Sawyer v. Whitley*, June 22, 1992. Death-eligibility innocence requires clear and convincing evidence that absent constitutional error no reasonable juror would find the necessary eligibility facts under state law. Additional discretionary mitigation alone does not qualify. Cause and prejudice, offense innocence and the underlying merits remain separate; no automatic relief or freestanding innocence right follows.
