# Peacock neutral validation — terminology clarification

This explicit addendum supplements the neutral validation frozen at `fde2181`; the frozen supplement and receipt are unchanged.

The **$205,182.01** award described as the “fee amount” in the supplement comprises **attorneys' fees and expenses**, not attorneys' fees alone. The Fourth Circuit source expressly uses that combined description. The lower court's fee entitlement, its treatment of costs, the vacated amount, and the requested recalculation remain distinct from the underlying **$187,628.93** judgment. This clarification does not change the neutral jurisdiction, liability, choice-of-law, or remedy forks.
