## Exact qualifications and competing constructions

**Winstar.** Section 10, JA 108–109, expressly addresses later clarification, interpretation and amendment of governing regulations and accounting principles. It also expressly gives the Board's transaction resolution or action priority when it conflicts with those regulations. The latter sentence cannot be omitted when assessing the former. The affirmative reading treats the tailored, preserved accounting treatment as contractual consideration while leaving ordinary changes in generally applicable standards operative. The July 13 letter permits regulatory-report amortization over a period **not exceeding thirty-five years**; it neither requires use of the full period nor establishes a thirty-five-year damages award. Its exclusion of GAAP financial statements and continuing reporting/recordkeeping requirements remain operative. The letter reserves powers outside the enumerated waivers and their authorizing statutory provisions insofar as waived. That reservation supports retention of the Government's other regulatory powers; it cannot accurately be paraphrased as an unqualified reservation to withdraw every specified benefit without contractual consequence. It also is not an express promise that Congress lacks legislative power.

The Net Worth Maintenance provisions must be read with the definition that computes net worth under the agreement's accounting principles, not only with the provision that maintains the legally required level. A changed minimum level and a repudiated promised component are analytically distinct, although the particular loss must still result from breach of the actual promise. The express integration treatment of contemporaneous documents answers a threshold incorporation objection; it does not establish every asserted promise merely because a regulatory document exists.

Section 13(a), JA 110–111, ordinarily ends the Assistance Agreement after two years, except as otherwise specifically provided, or at another date agreed in writing. It separately terminates the agreement and FSLIC obligations upon the stated liquidation-receiver or loss-of-insurance events. The affirmative construction reads the integrated, specified forward accounting benefit as an undertaking whose particular duration is not reduced to two years solely by the general assistance term. The competing construction treats the two-year term as controlling unless a sufficiently specific exception actually applies. That competing reading is substantial and must be confronted with the incorporated documents; merely observing that a longer benefit would make business sense is insufficient. Section 13(b)'s qualified surviving indemnities concern their identified liabilities and litigation; they cannot silently become a general survival clause for all claims. The lawful-action provision does not compel illegal reporting after FIRREA under the affirmative damages construction, but it remains relevant to what was promised and cannot be erased by the label “damages.” No newly inspected clause establishes when a receiver was appointed, insurance ended, or an accrued breach claim was extinguished.

**Statesman.** The section 17 priority sentence, JA 402–403, likewise gives the relevant transaction resolution or action priority over conflicting regulations, while retaining its express changing-accounting/regulatory language and the distinct real-estate-valuation provision. Its actual wording is reproduced with the scan's grammatical irregularities in the source addendum. The affirmative reading gives effect to both provisions: ordinary regulatory and accounting evolution continues, while the incorporated negotiated exceptions retain contractual significance. The Government's strongest contrary construction is that a priority among agency instruments does not unmistakably assume the risk of a later congressional command. The answer must rest on the nature of the actual promised consideration and its agreed scope; agency priority is not a claim that an agency can disable Congress.

The capital-credit provision is an express, separate undertaking that the identified $26 million shall be credited to regulatory capital under the specified definition; the goodwill provision permits the specified amortization, no more than twenty-five years, on the accounting conditions already identified in the packet. Neither eliminates the distinction between regulatory reports and GAAP statements. Congress's treatment of supervisory goodwill and OTS's regulatory treatment of capital credits remain separate legal acts. The source addendum supplies no new concession that either act falls outside reserved sovereign powers.

Section 20, JA 406–407, requires material compliance with applicable law, but contains an express qualification while applicability is contested in good faith. The initial draft's reference to compliance must therefore not be read as an unqualified promise to accept every asserted rule's applicability. Conversely, the qualification does not itself prove that a particular contest occurred, was in good faith, excused compliance under public law, or preserved any claim. The narrower affirmative construction can recognize the duty to comply with valid law and the separate monetary consequence of withdrawal of promised treatment; it need not rely on the contest qualification as an independent defense.

Section 19(a), JA 405, ordinarily provides a five-year term or another written agreed date and separately supplies liquidation-receiver and loss-of-insurance triggers. Sections 19(b)–(d) and the referenced section 16 make the full scheme more qualified than a single five-year sentence. The particular goodwill duration must be reconciled with that scheme, and the capital credit cannot automatically borrow goodwill's twenty-five-year ceiling. FIRREA's enactment within the ordinary five-year period answers only the assertion that ordinary expiration had already eliminated every promise; it does not eliminate a distinct termination event if proved or settle the recoverable duration of any loss. No sale, receiver event, insurance termination, good-faith contest or damages cutoff is newly found here.

**Glendale.** The approved and preserved accountant treatment distinguishes the $18 million/twelve-year and $716.666 million/forty-year components and retains the stated satisfactory-accounting and R-31b conditions. Section 9 of the Supervisory Action Agreement, JA 598–599, provides a ten-year assistance term and distinct receiver/liquidation and loss-of-insurance consequences. Section 10, JA 599–600, preserves contemporaneous resolutions and letters and requires the specified written agreement for modification; the applicable lawful-action language also remains relevant. The affirmative construction reads the specific forward accounting undertaking together with, rather than as indistinguishable from, the term of ongoing assistance and payments. It does not delete the ten-year provision. The Government's contrary construction would confine continuing benefits to the assistance term or to their lawfulness under later legislation. The integrated specific duration supplies the stronger contractual answer, but does not establish a maximum damages period irrespective of other terms, actual loss or termination. The lower court's construction is the issue under review, not an independent binding rule that resolves that disagreement by itself.

**Common reachability and remedy.** These are construction questions on the interlocutory liability judgments, not a reason to announce a new trial merely because the operator sought fuller pages. The affirmed liability premise would establish withdrawal of an authorized, identified contractual benefit, while leaving causation, recoverable losses, mitigation and applicable temporal limits below. No goodwill balance or capital-credit amount is itself a damages award. A broader promise of immunity from regulation, protection from all capital increases, or insurance against institutional failure is outside the candidate rationale. The unaddressed Winstar implied-contract alternative and other preserved theories do not become adjudicated simply by choosing an express-contract construction.

## Reconsidered Ginsburg candidate and the other Associates

The original Ginsburg row proposed a contrary future-change construction and expressly reserved reconsideration if the terms allocated that risk. Further inspection materially weakens its factual-textual premise: the regulatory-priority sentences and qualified reservation/compliance clauses make the bargain more particular than a bare permission subject to an undifferentiated retained power. Her actual **Hercules** join requires identification of the obligation actually undertaken; it does not establish a special requirement for explicit “future legislation” words that her fellow joiners rejected. Her actual **Cisneros** join similarly supports enforcing the agreement's precise terms. Neither source supplies a Justice-specific basis to treat these fuller promises as nonexistent while the same duty-first reasoning is satisfied for her fellow joiners.

The revised provisional Ginsburg candidate is therefore **affirmance of the partial express-contract liability judgments for Glendale, Winstar and Statesman, with Statesman's goodwill and capital-credit undertakings separately identified; damages and preserved remaining proceedings below**. This is a limited construction of the actual integrated undertakings, actual statutory authority and assumed regulatory-treatment risk. It does not depend on program fairness or on a categorical rule that seeking money defeats the unmistakability doctrine. The strongest counterposition remains that a regulatory exception and a long amortization ceiling do not themselves assign legislative-change risk. Her affirmative path must answer that position through the incorporated bargain, the priority/reservation wording and the actual duration terms, not simply through the desirability of compensation. The narrower available rationale assumes the challenged acts public and general and rejects impossibility only because the construed contract assigned this particular risk. A broad legislative-purpose characterization or general immunity for regulatory agreements is unnecessary. If fresh neutral validation changes the material wording or its incorporation, this candidate must be reconsidered.

This change is independent preliminary reasoning, not a correction of an adjudication or a response to any historical comparator. The original contrary candidate remains documented in the unchanged first draft. It is not carried forward as a settled separate position merely to manufacture disagreement.
