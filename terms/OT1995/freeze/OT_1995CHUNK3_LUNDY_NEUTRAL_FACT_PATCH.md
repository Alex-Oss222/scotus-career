# Lundy: supplemental neutral source facts for fresh validation

Source: Fourth Circuit judicial opinion, Lundy v. Internal Revenue Service, 45 F.3d 856 (January 30, 1995), https://static.case.law/f3d/45/html/0856-01.html ; lower-opinion text also at https://openjurist.org/45/f3d/856 . Treat this as a factual supplement to the approved neutral packet, not a completed preflight or a commitment. Verify the lower-opinion body directly; website editorial summaries and later-citation material are not source law.

The lower opinion identifies Fourth Circuit docket 94-1260. It reports that Lundy did not file the 1987 return by April 15, 1988. The deficiency notice was mailed September 26, 1990. He mailed a joint return December 22, 1990, received December 28, showing an overpayment of $3,537, and filed his Tax Court petition December 28. The parties stipulated liability was $778 greater than stated and a $369 addition to tax. They also stipulated that the Commissioner had indicated her understanding that a settlement involved a refund. An IRS notice dated February 3, 1992 described a $3,537 amount to be refunded subject to other obligations or pending matters.

The Commissioner initially answered February 19, 1991 without a limitations defense. On March 17, 1992 she moved to amend the answer to add that defense, and the Tax Court granted the motion. On June 28, 1993 the Tax Court ruled that section 6512(b)(3)(B) permitted refund only of payments within two years before mailing of the notice. Section 6513(b)(1) deemed the withholding paid April 15, 1988. The Fourth Circuit reversed and remanded. The parties agreed below that proceeding through an ordinary refund suit would have permitted recovery; whether that premise establishes any Court rule is open.

This supplement supplies source facts only. No inference of waiver, estoppel, enforceable settlement, jurisdictional classification, or general district-court refund eligibility is made. The original neutral packet identifies statutory lookback as the submitted issue.
