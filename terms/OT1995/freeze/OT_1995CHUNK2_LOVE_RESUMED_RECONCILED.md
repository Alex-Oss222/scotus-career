## 4. Arms: common five-Associate rationale and three-Associate disagreement

### Exact common propositions available for an opinion portion

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas directly join each proposition below and its limits:

1. **Personal invocation.** The guarantee protects an individual's keeping and bearing of arms within its constitutional scope. Formal enrollment or current service in an organized militia is not a prerequisite to invoking it. Miller's militia-related inquiry concerning the weapon and record retains force; it is not an express organized-membership condition. This proposition does not decide every weapon, defensive purpose or manner of carrying.
2. **State application.** Under the received right-specific incorporation method, the Fourteenth Amendment's Due Process Clause applies that personal guarantee to state action with the same constitutional standards as federal action. Cruikshank and Presser are overruled only insofar as they foreclose that application. Presser's actual regulation of unauthorized armed military organization and the other unaffected holdings remain. No total direct application of the Bill of Rights and no new Privileges or Immunities holding follows.
3. **Acquisition.** A necessary acquisition incident of constitutionally protected keeping cannot be categorically excluded merely because government obstructed purchase rather than seized an existing weapon. This does not decide that every handgun is protected, guarantee purchase from every seller, or prohibit investigation or licensing. The protected weapon and conduct, the actual constitutional restriction, and state-law authorization are separate inquiries.
4. **Disposition and unresolved application.** Neither inherited nonapplication nor lack of a pleaded formal militia affiliation sustains this dismissal. Vacate the Fourth Circuit judgment insofar as it affirms dismissal of the arms claim and remand for assessment of the actual purchase restriction under the recognized guarantee, with constitutional scope, lawful regulation, personal responsibility, properly presented defenses and relief addressed separately. No present determination that the policy infringed the guarantee, no damages, no restored procedural claim, and no injunction or automatic trial.

The five do not adopt the Fourth Circuit's threshold requiring this individual to show that her possession improves an organized militia. At the same time, Miller is not converted into either an exhaustive military-weapons-only code or a general self-defense rule independent of all militia-related reasoning. The remand leaves the actual weapon and constitutional application open. No modern scrutiny tier, closed regulatory list, new evidentiary burden, or universal firearms test is supplied.

## 5. Substantive due process: two preserved grounds and their remedies

### Four-Associate affirmance ground

O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas assume the asserted state-created interest and accept the alleged deliberate, knowing and malicious arrest-only practice. They find this concrete eligibility-screening restriction constitutionally insufficient to establish the distinct substantive wrong. Unresolved arrest dispositions concern the screening program's eligibility subject even where officials knowingly exceeded the statute. That is a limited legal characterization of the alleged conduct, not a finding that the applicant was disqualified, the policy lawful, the officials benevolent, or investigation impossible. State-law violation and correction expense alone do not establish federal substantive arbitrariness. Ordinary contracting adds no independently heightened right.

Collins Part C supports the distinction between a state duty and the additional constitutional wrong; its workplace-omission result is not mechanically made an executive-licensing rule. Romein and Beach supply limited economic-rights analogies, not transplantation of legislative-classification presumptions or burdens into every executive-misconduct action. These four reject dismissal based on an absolute-discretion entitlement test, later cure, lack of confinement or financial injury, or the unsupported assumption that deadline pressure forced the policy.

The strongest opposing argument is that describing an eligibility connection risks substituting a benign public-safety justification for the assumed malicious affirmative barrier. The four preserve their conclusion only as a constitutional-sufficiency assessment accepting actual alleged culpability, not factual resolution of motive or necessity. They do not hold that no affirmative licensing act can violate substantive due process. Their selected formal action is affirmance of the due-process dismissal on this bounded ground, not approval of every explanation below.

### Four-Associate vacatur and remand ground

Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer find the lower courts' legal and pleading errors material to disposition. Zinermon and Stevens's Daniels concurrence distinguish a substantive wrong, complete when committed, from adequacy of procedure; later correction cannot by itself cure the former. At dismissal, the knowing affirmative-policy allegation cannot be replaced by official-friendly necessity or caution findings. Collins leaves the separate right and substantive-arbitrariness questions necessary but does not itself complete their application to this affirmative restriction.

The strongest opposing argument is that correcting overbroad language need not require vacatur if the accepted allegations independently fail. These four do not deny that principle. They decline to complete this constitutional application through the lower court's disputed factual characterization and return the claim for assessment under correct premises. This is not a holding that malice establishes the substantive wrong, that a state interest necessarily exists, that the complaint ultimately must succeed, or that every deliberate statutory breach is actionable.

Their selected formal action is vacatur of the Fourth Circuit judgment insofar as it affirms this due-process dismissal, with remand through that court for the remaining entitlement, constitutional sufficiency, attribution, properly raised defenses and relief inquiries. No categorical adoption of Gardner as Supreme Court law, procedural-due-process count, automatic discovery or trial, damages finding, or retrospective notice rule follows.
