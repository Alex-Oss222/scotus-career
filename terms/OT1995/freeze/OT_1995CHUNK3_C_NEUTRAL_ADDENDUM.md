## Fulton

[Complete authentic state opinion, 338 N.C. 472](https://static.case.law/nc/338/html/0472-01.html) states the .25% stock-value tax and the deduction linked to the issuer's North Carolina income apportionment. It discusses a 7.75% corporate income rate but notes the pre-1991 rate was 7%; this suit claims relief for 1990. Do not treat 7.75% as the relevant 1990 rate or the opinion's price/earnings comparison as a finding of matching burdens in every application.

The intermediate court invalidated the deduction, severed it and denied a refund; both sides sought review. The state Supreme Court sustained validity and expressly left refunds and fees undecided. Reversal of its constitutional ruling therefore does not itself decide the state severability issue or a refund amount. Other constitutional theories were not properly brought before that court.

[Darnell, 226 U.S. 390](https://static.case.law/us/226/html/0390-01.html) was decided December 23, 1912, not 1913. Its actual text concerns the tax on residents' foreign-corporation shares and a substantial-equality comparison to domestic corporate property taxation, with separate standing limits. The neutral packet's tax-remedy authority is Reich v. Collins; the additional Thunder Basin text under its “Reich” label is a different case and supplies no tax-refund rule.
