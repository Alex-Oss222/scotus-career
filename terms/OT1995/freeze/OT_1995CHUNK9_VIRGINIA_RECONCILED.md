## Historical comparison at its actual scope

The official judgment affirms the liability judgment in No. 94-2107, 976 F.2d 890, and reverses and remands the remedial judgment in No. 94-1941, 44 F.3d 1229. Ginsburg's historical Court opinion was joined throughout by Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Breyer. Rehnquist concurred in the judgment. Scalia dissented. Thomas did not participate. These historical relationships establish what each participating Associate actually accepted in this matter, without assigning any present opinion or supplying the absent Chief Justice's simulated view.

The historical Court opinion applies the State's demanding burden of showing an important governmental objective and a substantial relationship between the sex classification and that objective. Its exceedingly-persuasive-justification language describes that burden; it does not require the handoff to adopt strict scrutiny or a least-restrictive-means test. Justification must be genuine, and averages about women's preferences or capacities cannot conclusively exclude women able and willing to undertake VMI's program. The opinion acknowledges real differences and accommodations and reserves broader questions about private institutions and other single-sex education. Its analysis of this publicly maintained, distinctive opportunity is not a universal holding that no sex-conscious educational arrangement can ever survive.

On remedy the historical majority examines the whole opportunity, including method, curriculum, resources, credentials, institutional standing and connections. The official text acknowledges the alumni pledge before expressing doubts about the resulting practical equivalence. This handoff accepts the pledge as the frozen record requires. It does not adopt predicted alumni disloyalty or employer refusal as an adjudicated fact. The plan can remain inadequate even on the assumption that every promised recruitment, placement, externship and advancement benefit is faithfully supplied.

Rehnquist's complete concurrence supplies a serious narrower alternative: conventional intermediate scrutiny; no necessary demand for a written purpose declaration; no condemnation of an 1839 policy simply because current doctrine developed later; and the possibility of a genuinely comparable women's institution without a clone of VMI. He nonetheless regards this particular program as insufficient. His assertion about lack of proof of adversative benefit is not substituted for the frozen record's accepted pedagogical findings. His writing is a historical counterargument and source of distinctions, not the current Chief Justice's position or a substitute for the seven individual audits.

Scalia's dissent supplies independent tradition-based and conventional-intermediate-scrutiny arguments. It stresses the trial findings, preservation of an integrated educational method, actual institutional diversity, imperfect fit permitted by intermediate scrutiny and the difference from Hogan's male-auditing record. Its conditional discussion also defends VWIL as a professionally designed, substantially comparable alternative. Those propositions support the frozen primary and conditional paths at different premises. His predictions about consequences for other public or private single-sex education are predictions, not additional judgments or objective record facts.

No Associate's historical disposition must be changed because a material historical premise is absent. The operative reasons nevertheless must be expressed in actual entering law. ## Common legal and record constraints

### Scrutiny, proof and pedagogy

This is an express sex classification in public admissions. The State bears the burden of supplying a genuine important objective and a substantial relationship between its discriminatory means and that objective. The challenger's ultimate-purpose burden for a facially neutral policy is not transferred to this express classification. Nor does J.E.B.'s staged peremptory-strike proof procedure govern college admissions. The demanding justification inquiry is not rational-basis speculation, an automatic invalidation of any sex distinction, a perfect-fit requirement, or a least-restrictive-alternative test.

Hogan, 458 U.S. 718, 723–731 and notes 1, 7, 9, 16 and 17, supplies the actual-purpose and substantial-fit inquiry, protection of either sex, and rejection of a benefit-to-one-sex rationale that never explains the discriminatory allocation itself. It reserves other schools and the generic parallel-undergraduate-institution question. Its Title IX discussion at 731–733 establishes that a statutory admissions exception is not constitutional permission. Actual Kelley confirms both genuine sex-conscious opportunity measures and the separate constitutional burden. Actual Klinger requires examination of the particular denied benefit rather than either institution-wide immunity or mechanical equalization of selected programs. Neither decision supplies a right to every preferred course or school.

The case has affirmative evidence of single-sex benefits and VMI's integrated adversative method. Some women can meet current male physical standards and wish to undertake the opportunity. Trial predictions concerning privacy, physical-training arrangements and adversative interaction are preserved as predictions/findings at their stated scope. The legal question is what those premises justify, not whether an appellate court may pretend the evidence never existed. A conclusion that the categorical exclusion is unconstitutional does not require finding that coeducation produces no change. Conversely, the need for some change does not by itself establish constitutionally sufficient grounds for excluding every woman.

Virginia may pursue a genuine current purpose even if it was not the institution's original 1839 purpose. No rule requires an APA-style contemporaneous written explanation. Failure to establish an adequate present justification is not automatically a finding of deceit, antifemale animus or litigation misconduct. The six liability-affirming commitments conclude that the supplied diversity and method grounds fail to justify this allocation of the distinctive public opportunity. Scalia concludes that the accepted benefits and institutional arrangement establish the requisite connection. The disagreement is a real legal evaluation of supported facts and predictions.

### This remedy, including the promised alumni benefits

The record distinguishes VMI's approximately 1,300-student public military college and pervasive barracks/rat-line method from VWIL's proposed 25–30-student leadership program at private Mary Baldwin. VWIL includes four years of ROTC, leadership study, seminars, community service, externships and physical education. Its cooperative design is a professional educational choice, not no education or no leadership training. The comparison nonetheless includes the missing pervasive adversative experience, on-campus engineering, differences in curricula and facilities, credentials, resources and institutional reputation. The lack of a scientific demand survey does not erase the finding that some women want and can undertake VMI's program; general female preferences do not establish every individual's choice.

The promised annual public payment per student equals VMI's, and ordinary VWIL student cost is no greater. Those facts do not establish identical total resources. The separate $5.4625 million VWIL endowment commitment is not Mary Baldwin's entire endowment. The latter is reported as approximately $19 million in one comparison and $20.5 million in another, against approximately $131 million for VMI; no unproved timing reconciliation or precise common-date ratio is manufactured. The engineering alternative requires three Mary Baldwin years and two years at Washington University, with private tuition and no proposed public discount in those final two years. The cohort SAT and faculty-credential comparisons are institutional evidence; they do not establish an individual woman's inability or make any numerical threshold constitutional.

The VMI Alumni Association expressly promises recruitment assistance and the same access to its placement network, externships and advancement opportunities. Every audited path credits that promise, including the six positions finding the remedy insufficient assuming full performance. Access to that network is neither absent nor an assurance of identical individual outcomes. It is also not a VMI degree or a demonstrated identity of the total educational opportunity. No premise depends on predicted repudiation, a later implementation failure, or a judicially found universal difference in employers' responses. The supplied approval record and future-oriented monitoring are not rewritten as proof of what happened in 1995–1996.

Sweatt, 339 U.S. 629, 631–636, supports attention to concrete and intangible institutional opportunity rather than labels alone. Its racial setting does not automatically establish a ban on every public single-sex institution. Actual Fordice/Ayers likewise informs effective repair while preserving educationally sound alternatives; it does not command institutional identity or numerical balance. Remedial differences must be materially tied to the denied opportunity, not collected into an unbounded demand for every desirable educational improvement.

### Remedy and appellate review

The first appellate liability remand offered lawful alternatives, including admitting women, creating an adequate parallel program, ending public support, or another lawful response. It did not predetermine VWIL's sufficiency or require immediate coeducation regardless of alternatives. The later appellate court approved the proposed plan subject to monitoring. Both judgments are independently before the Court, in different dockets.

Actual Jenkins permits measures necessary to repair proved injury without demanding that each implementing expenditure be independently unconstitutional. It does not guarantee maximal educational potential or equal national scores. Actual Lewis v. Casey preserves effective access, demonstrated scope, State design opportunity and necessary implementation, while rejecting unsupported institutional improvement. Its PLRA requirements are prison-conditions statutory law, not a universal least-intrusive-means test for this educational remedy. Settle preserves genuine educational judgments while denying constitutional immunity to every decision described as pedagogical; its speech and mixed-motive burdens are not the equal-protection test here.

Clear-error constraints preserve underlying findings. Whether accepted facts establish constitutionally adequate justification or relief remains a legal question. The six remedy-rejecting Associates do not deem the State's opportunity to design the cure forfeited by proposing an insufficient plan. Scalia's conditional approval does not dispense with implementation or existing monitoring. No path orders a new evidentiary hearing, special master, fixed resource ratio, immediate award, admissions outcome or retained Supreme Court proceeding absent a supplied basis.

### Scalia — accept the primary and conditional commitments separately

**Own eligible basis.** His actual J.E.B. dissent accepts ordinary heightened review for sex but resists transplanting Batson's individual-strike rule into a traditional institution with its own function. That is a contextual legal position, not a general immunity for sex classifications. His full Kelley and Klinger joins are stronger direct evidence of his current sex-classification standard: actual important objective, substantial fit, and relevant institutional differences. His Jenkins dissent and Lewis v. Casey Court/concurrence joins insist on a proved wrong, supported remedial reach and genuine State choice. His full Settle join confirms that the word pedagogy alone cannot excuse a legally forbidden classification.

**Strongest opposing path.** Hogan places the burden on the State and asks why only one sex receives the benefit. Some women can meet existing standards. Klinger forbids disposing of the claim by declaring whole institutions incomparable. The public plan's substantial differences persist even with full alumni assistance. On this account, intermediate scrutiny itself invalidates the exclusion and an effective remedy must address the denied opportunity.

**Resolution of liability.** His frozen primary path remains reasonable under his own actual precedents because it identifies a genuine institutional function and supported relation, rather than tradition or a litigation label alone. He gives substantial weight to concurrent findings concerning the integrated adversative method, single-sex benefits and the changes coeducation would bring. In his view, the current arrangement supplies real diversity within higher education, and conventional intermediate scrutiny tolerates an imperfect fit. A qualified exception does not automatically eliminate a class-wide connection. He distinguishes Hogan's auditing concession, which contradicted its pedagogical rationale, from VMI's actual integration of method and residential structure. His conclusion is that Virginia has carried its burden, not that it has no burden or may posit any conceivable rationale.

**Resolution of remedy.** On that primary conclusion, reverse No. 94-2107 and vacate the compulsory remedial judgment in No. 94-1941 insofar as it depends on the rejected liability, with remand to remove those obligations. Separately, assuming liability survives, he would affirm the present remedy approval: expert-designed cooperative leadership education and four-year ROTC can pursue similar ends by different methods; equal annual State subsidy, endowed support, promised alumni assistance and the approved monitoring supply a substantial real opportunity. He credits the engineering, resource and credential differences but regards the constitutional demand as meaningful overall comparability, not reproduction of VMI. His conditional path requires faithful implementation; it does not declare every separate program adequate or render monitoring optional.

**Historical audit and join ceiling.** The historical dissent confirms primary rejection of liability and contains the distinct conditional defense of VWIL. Its tradition-based constitutional approach is not imported as a substitute for his actual Kelley/Klinger commitments. His independent historical intermediate-scrutiny ground supplies the relevant confirmation. This is no departure in outcome; the supporting path is bounded by a concrete changed legal baseline in which he has already directly joined those two sex-classification decisions. He cannot join an invalidation rationale asserting that the method findings never existed, treating every individual exception as dispositive, or requiring the least restrictive means. Any necessary separate explanation belongs to later assembly; authorship is not decided here.
