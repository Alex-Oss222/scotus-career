## Material reconciliation

**Appealability preserves two rationales.** Historical Quackenbush applies both Moses H. Cone grounds: effective surrender of the federal forum and, independently, satisfaction of Cohen's collateral-order conditions, 517 U.S. at 712–715. The independent A correctly supplies both sets of reasons but expressly identifies only Cohen as the selected route. Final A should retain the limited effective-surrender basis as well. This does not declare every remand conventionally final under Catlin; the historical opinion expressly says this remand does not end litigation on the merits in that traditional sense.

No actual intervening premise requires abandoning the first basis. Digital Equipment's two barriers concern an ordinary private settlement right, not a completed federal-forum surrender. Wilton itself preserves Cone's effectively deferring stay. O'Connor's older Cone dissent regarded its stay as revocable case management; the present remand leaves nothing on the federal docket and supplies a concrete distinction already recognized in her independent commitment. Stevens and Breyer's Behrens concern about successive interlocutory review likewise does not require waiting for a later federal judgment that will never issue here.

Thermtron's statement that remands are reviewable only by mandamus is disavowed **to the extent inconsistent with this application of the later Cone holding**. Its construction of §§1447(c)–(d) remains intact. This limited precedent treatment makes A's rejection of mandamus exclusivity express; it creates neither a general remand appeal nor an assumed entitlement to mandamus.

**Kennedy and Scalia retain materially different supplemental positions.** The independent statement that no separate opinion or partial join is required under A–C understates the verified writing functions. Kennedy historically joined the whole opinion while preserving the possibility of an extension permitting damages dismissal where a serious federalism affront could be averted in no other way, 517 U.S. at 733–734. He emphasized comity and federalism as foundations additional to equitable discretion and the State's substantial insurance-regulation interest. He regarded a retained-jurisdiction stay as the preferred course and found no occasion to decide that hypothetical exceptional dismissal.

Scalia expressly joined on the understanding that such discretionary surrender was foreclosed, id. at 731–732: lawful congressional assignment of nondiscretionary jurisdiction cannot itself constitute the federalism affront authorizing refusal. The court therefore need not determine whether this record otherwise presents extraordinary circumstances. This is a response to Kennedy's proposed reservation, not a rejection of every stay or of independently governed tax-comity and forum-non-conveniens doctrines.

Their actual sources supply no concrete changed premise for omitting either position. Their full NOPSI joins coexist with those different views of a possible extension; actual Wilton is confined to declaration-only statutory discretion. Kennedy's actual Things Remembered concurrence reserves a distinct remand-review question rather than renouncing federalism-based remedial concerns. Scalia's actual Digital Equipment reasoning and NOPSI authorship remain consistent with insistence on a lawful jurisdictional source. No new authorship or generalized later analogy displaces either rationale. Ginsburg's join in Kennedy's Things Remembered reservation concerns Cohill reviewability and does not supply a join in this different proposed dismissal reservation. No other Associate is assigned to either supplement.

## Source and remedial boundaries for assembly

Affirmance is of the **actual lower judgment**, which vacates remand and returns for proceedings consistent with its opinion. The historical Supreme Court report's recitation that the Ninth Circuit sent the matter to arbitration is not adopted against the validated lower-source text. No lower federal court decided Allstate's FAA motion. Likewise, the Commissioner's reply preserves its broader §1447(d) nonconcession; the historical description of party agreement supplies no contrary present concession.

The complaint contains a declaration as well as the independent contract claim. The rehearing treatment and the actual coercive claim remain as the frozen sources establish; there is no finding that the complaint seeks no declaratory relief, and no wholesale conversion into Wilton's declaration-only case. Allstate's requested stay/setoff does not make the Commissioner's legal recovery discretionary. Permission to sue elsewhere remains alongside the liquidation court's asset-control orders.

NOPSI's two Burford branches are alternatives, subject to timely and adequate state review; complex regulation or potential conflict alone does not suffice. No holding finds those predicates met or absent here. Prudential and Imperial resolved particular questions, not every setoff defense. The substantial federal interest in the FAA motion does not establish that it succeeds, that McCarran-Ferguson is inapplicable, or that arbitration must be ordered first.

The independent reservations concerning an actual stay, Fair Assessment, Thibodaux and unchallenged Colorado River grounds remain. Fair Assessment's state-tax/§1983 setting supplies no decided general damages-dismissal exception here. Forum non conveniens has a distinct pedigree and considerations, including the surviving foreign-alternative-forum setting; B does not overrule it. The Ninth Circuit's broader per se exclusion of abstention in actions at law is not adopted: discretionary declaratory relief and properly supported stays remain distinct.
