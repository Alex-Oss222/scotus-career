## What changes after comparison

Three independent formulations need correction or clarification:

1. The common reference to obstruction of a **nonfrivolous** claim cannot become an eight-Associate jurisdictional test. O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas adopt that requirement. 2. The independent reservation of all further scope questions cannot conceal the four stricter Associates' actual disagreement with the other four. Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer retain assistance during litigation and reserve access concerning other important rights. Stevens declines the unrequested narrowing. No rule that all resources must be cut off immediately upon filing is supplied by the narrower capability formulation.
3. The separate constitutional and remedial components require precise judgment language. Seven Associates support setting aside the unsupported classwide approval and the overbroad decree and remanding, while retaining the actual findings and demonstrated access wrong. Stevens supports preservation of the adjudicated wrong and a narrower remand to correct the excessive remedy. ## Component grounds and exact join boundaries

### A. Preserved evidence and actual access duty

All eight reject a rule that adequate books alone discharge the duty when a prisoner who needs assistance cannot present a court claim. Bounds permits effective libraries, trained assistance and other effective arrangements evaluated as a whole; it does not mandate physical browsing, appointed counsel for every prisoner, a universal staffing credential or the decree's numerical program. An efficient inventory-and-prompt-delivery system may suffice for prisoners able to use the materials independently. Respondents' conditional concession does not establish that the actual paging system worked or that it meets illiterate or non-English-speaking prisoners' different needs.

All eight preserve the distinction between an actual obstruction and an ultimate loss on the merits. A prisoner need not first win the obstructed suit. Standing, substantive liability, the represented group, geographic reach and the necessity of particular relief remain separate. The State's brief page 33 note 23 concession of initial standing cannot bind the Court on Article III; it also cannot be rewritten as a concession of every merits element. The Ninth Circuit's statement that the general actual-injury issue was not before it supports the presentation objection to broader merits rulings, not a fictional blanket forfeiture ruling.

The demonstrated obstruction findings survive at their supported scope. Existing findings, testimony and common practices remain evidence on remand. Neither the acceptance of a named plaintiff's injury nor preservation of findings itself affirms injury throughout every institution. Conversely, rejecting the whole-system decree is not an adjudication that no prisoner was denied meaningful access.

### B. Four-Associate injury and substantive-scope ground

**O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas** require actual or sufficiently imminent hindrance of a nonfrivolous legal claim within the protected sentence/conditions categories. A technical dismissal caused by inability to meet a filing requirement, inability to file an actionable contemplated claim, or sufficiently concrete threatened obstruction can qualify. Ultimate victory is not required; a dismissal for a correctable procedural deficiency does not make the underlying claim substantively frivolous. Inadequate books, research ability or staffing without the required connection is not an independent Article III injury.

For these four, the affirmative capability required by Bounds is the ability to bring contemplated direct or collateral challenges to a sentence or challenges to prison conditions before a court. The State need not supply the general legal sophistication to discover unspecified grievances or litigate effectively once in court, and the Bounds affirmative-assistance theory does not require provision for every other form of private civil litigation. This is not an authorization to obstruct an independently protected right, a ruling on every freestanding confidentiality or petition claim, or an unrequested overruling of Bounds. Any independently presented communications claim must be identified on its own legal predicate; it cannot acquire statewide scope merely by being attached to this access decree.

Standing is not conferred in gross. After trial, a named plaintiff must have proved personal injury from the type of administrative deficiency he seeks to remedy. These four regard the concrete findings supporting Bartholic's literacy-related obstruction and Harris's evidence as insufficient to sustain separate broad remedial entitlements for non-English speakers, lockdown prisoners and all prisoners simply because those categories appear within the certified class. Bartholic's custody in a lockdown unit does not by itself prove injury caused by lockdown status as distinct from inability to obtain needed assistance.

The further failure to prove an illiteracy-related violation throughout the entire system is a **merits/evidentiary scope failure**, not an absence of all standing. The record identifies the concrete instances and recurring resource concerns but does not establish that each institution denies the minimal capability despite its available assistance and alternatives. No requirement that every class member already have lost a lawsuit follows. Common evidence may prove a common wrong; the present findings do not establish the unrestricted statewide scope granted below. The actual Jenkins rule still permits necessary implementation of a demonstrated wrong without making each implementation detail a standalone constitutional violation.

### C. Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer's distinct access and class ground

**Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer** require a concrete personal stake, not an abstract wish for a better library. Ordinarily the prisoner must have a concrete grievance about confinement or conviction that he would seek to pursue if research, consultation, a complaint form or other access showed a legal claim, and the access system must prevent research, consultation, filing or litigation as pertinent. They refuse to make preliminary proof that the underlying claim is nonfrivolous a jurisdictional gate to the assistance needed to evaluate it. They reserve the exceptional case of substantial systemic denial of every means of court access; they do not use that reservation to presume a statewide violation here.

Bartholic provides the named representative's concrete anchor. Class-member representation remains subject to Rule 23's requirements, including typicality and adequacy, rather than a demand that every unnamed member separately prove Article III standing. Separate types of injury still require proper represented scope; the failure of proof concerning non-English speakers and lockdown conditions can be resolved as a failure of the merits showing without deciding the broader standing doctrine. They accept that the supplied findings do not justify the present systemwide decree.

They reject treating filing as the end of every affirmative Bounds obligation. Necessary assistance between complaint and disposition can be part of meaningful access. They also reserve whether important other rights, such as family status or other retained fundamental interests, can require limited access; they adopt neither a universal subsidy for all civil litigation nor the categorical sentence/conditions exclusion.

The district court may use common evidence and relief for a proved common barrier. The actual Jenkins rule protects necessary means of repair and does not constitutionalize every resource specification. These three preserve the real access wrong while requiring proof supporting the particular breadth, institution and means of future relief.

### D. Stevens's narrower route

**Stevens** accepts the established personal denial of meaningful access and the existing class controversy. He rejects using the case to require a preliminary nonfrivolous-merits showing or to narrow Bounds to specified claim categories or initial presentation. His Bounds join, full Murray dissent and actual Lujan separate position support an affirmative ability to bring a grievance to a court even when the court ultimately finds the grievance legally deficient. Personal interference remains necessary; a desired improvement does not become constitutionally required simply because standing exists to test it.

He would preserve the adjudicated constitutional wrong on the actual obstruction findings and remand because the decree exceeds the identified injuries and must satisfy the original PLRA. He will not join the broader jurisdictional and substantive-scope rulings, an unqualified systemwide liability affirmance or a constitutional guarantee of every term. His willingness to correct remedial overbreadth does not support every premise of the seven-Associate remand.

### E. Security, intrusiveness and the State's opportunity to devise a remedy

**O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer** adopt Turner as the governing qualification: a prison regulation impinging on access is valid if reasonably related to legitimate penological interests. Bounds's administrative choice and Turner must be applied together. Substantial deference has particular force for dangerous or otherwise specially restricted prisoners. Delay caused by a valid regulation does not become unconstitutional merely because an access injury results. The record requires examination of the actual rule and its relationship to a legitimate interest; an assertion of security is not a finding that every restriction is valid. Neither ordinary constitutional deference nor Bounds itself supplies the PLRA's distinct statutory least-intrusive-means command.

These seven regard the comprehensive decree as insufficiently fitted to the established wrong and insufficiently respectful of the State's remedial choice. State officials must have a genuine first opportunity to devise an effective program within constitutional limits; an opportunity merely to rebut a presumptive statewide Gluth template does not discharge that obligation. This is a legal assessment of the constrained remedial procedure already described in the record. It is not a finding that officials never participated, that the master rejected every objection or that the court lacked authority to obtain qualified assistance. Earlier resistance in Gluth does not automatically establish obstruction in this case or strip the State of that opportunity. Preserved objections are not erased for lack of the most vigorous possible advocacy.

**Stevens** agrees that excessive remedial breadth warrants remand and accepts lawful security limitations, but refuses to characterize this process as denial of a fair opportunity to be heard. The prior approved Gluth remedy could reasonably supply a starting point; the State had opportunities for proposals and objections, and several objections were accepted. The competing assessments of those opportunities remain a substantive disagreement. His criticism of the State's participation is not a neutral finding of bad faith or a forfeiture ground. His agreement that a genuine breakdown in negotiation could justify remand does not join the seven's assessment that the State was denied its required initial role here.

### F. Original PLRA — retained independent statutory ground

All eight retain the independent commitment to reconsider ongoing prospective relief under the original enacted section 802, applicable to existing as well as later relief. Historical silence about this statute supplies no contrary individual vote and does not establish that it did not exist in June 1996. The present handoff does not posit a counterfactual statutory enactment, a party default or a historical constitutional ruling on the PLRA.

The operative statutory limits and exceptions in the original commitments remain incorporated in full. In particular:

- Section 3626(a)(1)(A) requires findings that the prospective relief extends no further than necessary to correct the violation of the federal right of particular plaintiffs, is narrowly drawn and is the least intrusive means necessary, with substantial weight given to adverse effects on public safety or criminal-justice-system operation. Necessary implementation may qualify even though each implementation measure is not itself a freestanding right. Existing findings may be used for what they establish; required substance cannot be replaced by labels or presumed from a desirable program.
- Relief requiring an official to exceed authority under state or local law or otherwise violate that law must meet all the distinct (a)(1)(B) conditions: federal law permits it, it is necessary to correct the federal violation, and no other relief will correct it. Section (a)(1)(C) confers no power to order prison construction or taxation and does not create a new prohibition on otherwise lawful remedial means.
- Preliminary relief remains subject to otherwise-existing authority, need, narrowness, least intrusiveness, safety and comity; it expires after 90 days unless the required findings are made and the order is made final before that expiration. No prisoner-release order is authorized or presented here.
- Termination is not automatic upon enactment. Section (b)(1) specifies the applicable two-year, one-year-after-denial and preenactment-relief periods and permits an earlier agreement. Section (b)(2)'s immediate-termination entitlement for relief lacking the required findings remains subject to (b)(3)'s written, record-based determination of a **current OR ongoing** violation and the required need/narrowness/least-intrusiveness findings. The original text is not replaced by a later “and” formulation; (b)(4) preserves otherwise-available earlier modification or termination.
- The automatic stay requires an actual pending qualifying motion: the original (e) periods are 30 days for (b)(1)/(2) motions and 180 days for other-law motions, ending when the final order resolves the motion. No such motion, stay, termination or automatic dismissal is invented in this record.
- A special master must satisfy the statute's impartiality, objectivity, safety consideration, complexity, selection, compensation and continuing-need requirements. The original procedure permits up to five nominees per party and up to three strikes by a party against the opposing list, followed by selection from the remaining names; the impartiality determination is subject to the prescribed interlocutory review. The CJA-rate limit and Judiciary payment provisions, six-month need review, record-based findings, no ex parte communications, permitted assistance in developing plans, and termination with the relief remain distinct. The preexisting fee-objection remand and any actual temporal question are not erased or decided by assuming all earlier master work was invalid.

No constitutional challenge to section 802 is decided. No new prospective decree, numerical minimum, funding source, appointment, immediate dissolution, filing deadline or enforcement stay is invented. The remand must allow lawful effective relief for established or properly proved current violations while applying the actual statutory predicates.
