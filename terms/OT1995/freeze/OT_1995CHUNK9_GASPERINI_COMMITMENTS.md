## Material text, record status and entering law

CPLR5501(c)'s published text directs the Appellate Division, when reviewing a money judgment challenged as excessive or inadequate in connection with conditional new-trial relief, to find excessiveness or inadequacy if the award deviates materially from reasonable compensation. Its direction names that state appellate institution. It creates no numerical ceiling. Rule59(a)'s pertinent jury clause permits a new trial for reasons for which new trials have historically been granted in federal actions at law. The supplied quotations contain express ellipses and are not certifications of complete original code volumes; no timing dispute requires an omitted deadline.

The Second Circuit's conclusions concerning at most fifty especially unique transparencies and the photographer's earning power are appellate assessments whose legal role is disputed. They do not become Supreme Court findings merely through repetition. Industry-standard evidence was held admissible with other evidence, not conclusive at $1,500 per transparency. The Court must not select its own amount or turn the lower court's conditional choice into a compulsory $100,000 judgment.

Hanna requires deciding whether a valid Federal Rule actually governs the disputed point before undertaking the unguided Erie choice; where no direct coverage controls, forum shopping and inequitable administration inform the inquiry. Byrd preserves the federal distribution of trial functions even when state procedure could affect outcomes. Donovan establishes federal allocation and the prohibition on appealing an accepted remittitur; it does not decide this unaccepted appellate remittitur or mandate full appellate factual review. Browning-Ferris§IV recognizes state damages confines, federal Rule59 functions and deferential appellate review, but footnote25 expressly reserves the Seventh Amendment appellate-review question. It must not be cited as already deciding that question.

Actual Markman distinguishes the historical jury right from allocation of particular legal and factual tasks; its patent construction holding and reserved subsidiary appellate-review question do not control compensatory excessiveness review. Actual Honda requires meaningful review of punitive amount or an adequate functional substitute, without a mandatory appellate tier, independent appellate reweighing or fixed number. Actual BMW's constitutional punitive guideposts, actual remedy and separate institutional positions remain within their punitive and state-court setting. They do not supply a compensatory damages formula or resolve the federal Reexamination Clause.

Actual Koon, June13 1996, Part I, was also reviewed in this snapshot. All eight Associates join its abuse-of-discretion method: independently correct legal error, protect historical findings unless clearly erroneous and defer substantially to supported case-specific sentencing judgment. Kennedy authors that explanation; the other seven Associates join it despite differences on individual departure factors. Section3742(e) and the sentencing function supply its authority. It strengthens the distinction between real deference and appellate substitution but does not decide what the Seventh Amendment permits after a civil jury verdict. Scalia and Thomas's Gasperini position therefore does not deny their Koon commitment; it invokes an additional, issue-specific constitutional limit absent from that sentencing-review allocation. The other six apply the same distinction between legal correction and substituted factual judgment, while separately defending civil appellate review under the Reexamination Clause.

## Enhanced comparison by issue

### A. State compensation standard and Rule59

**Path A — accommodate the state standard within federal procedure.** CPLR5501(c)'s materially-deviates rule has a substantive effect on the permissible recovery for a state claim. Ignoring it would allow materially larger recoveries solely because diversity makes federal court available. Rule59 preserves the federal new-trial function and recognizes excessiveness as a traditional ground; it need not freeze every state compensation constraint into an immutable federal shock-the-conscience measure. Apply the state standard through the district court's federal new-trial authority while retaining federal allocation and review limits.

**Strongest counterargument.** The enactment directs a particular state appellate court to exercise a more searching review, while Rule59 expressly refers to historical federal practice. A court cannot invoke Erie policy to rewrite a governing Federal Rule or transport a state institutional reassessment of facts into federal court. Separating standard from named actor arguably creates a hybrid that neither legislature enacted.

**Response supporting Path A.** The state allocation and state measure do different work. Enforcing reasonable-compensation limits does not oblige federal courts to reproduce the state's reviewing hierarchy. The traditional ground for new trial remains excessiveness; state law supplies its operative compensation constraint, just as it supplies substantive damages criteria. Hanna's direct-coverage inquiry therefore permits the coexistence, and Byrd requires preserving federal functions. The conclusion is about this damages standard, not a general license to rewrite rules or import every state appellate practice.

**Path B — Rule59 controls the federal excessiveness standard.** Treat the provision as a procedural change in the degree of judicial review, not a new measure of recoverable injury. Rule59's historical-federal-practice language then governs and excludes the materially-deviates substitution. Erie cannot trump a valid Federal Rule that covers the issue. This does not permit a federal jury to disregard actual state liability elements, damages categories or fixed substantive ceilings.

**Strongest counterargument.** A difference that systematically changes permissible recovery can be substantive even without a dollar ceiling; excluding it encourages choice of forum for a larger award. The federal reference to reasons for new trial need not choose the degree of excessiveness. Scalia and Thomas nevertheless select Path B because they regard the disputed provision as directing the judicial reexamination function already covered by Rule59, while the other six select Path A for the stated separation of substantive limit from federal method.

### B. Trial application and appellate review

Under Path A the district judge applies the state measure on a Rule59 motion. The judge's opportunity to evaluate the trial evidence and historically recognized new-trial authority preserve the jury relationship. An appellate court reviews for abuse of discretion, correcting an erroneous legal rule or an unreasonable exercise of the district court's assigned judgment; it does not independently fix compensation from its own cold-record comparisons. A legally mistaken standard is not insulated by the label discretion.

The Reexamination Clause presents a real additional issue: the Amendment permits reexamination only according to common-law rules, and the historical new-trial power belonged to the trial court. Browning-Ferris expressly left the appellate constitutional question open. The six Justices accepting review conclude that reviewing the trial court's exercise of its lawful authority, with actual deference and no fresh valuation, respects that separation. Neither functional convenience nor a changed label could validate independent appellate factfinding. No claim is made that an abuse-of-discretion label by itself cures a forbidden second valuation.

Scalia and Thomas take the competing historical position. They permit appellate correction of legal error and unsupported verdicts through established legal channels, but refuse to transform the district judge's factual assessment of excessiveness into an appellate factual reassessment by calling it abuse-of-discretion review. Dimick's insistence on preserving historical jury functions and the express reservation in Browning-Ferris prevent treating modern practice alone as constitutional authorization. They accept traditional trial-court new trial and conditional remittitur; they do not invalidate every reduction or treat remittitur as additur.

### C. Disposition and the existing $100,000 calculation

For the six Justices adopting Path A, vacate the Court of Appeals' judgment and remand for the district court to apply the materially-deviates standard in the first instance through Rule59, followed by appellate review only within the stated limit. Its unexplained denial does not establish use of the required standard. The Court of Appeals' separate valuation cannot substitute for that initial district-court judgment. The Supreme Court does not adopt $100,000, reinstate $450,000 as substantively correct, require a new jury automatically or find how many slides were unique.

An alternative is to affirm by characterizing the Second Circuit's calculation as review of the legal outer boundary of permissible compensation. Its strongest support is the lower opinion's use of state comparator cases and its insistence that it is policing bounds rather than selecting an ideal award. The six reject that characterization here because the appellate court made the first operative valuation under the more searching state standard, without a district-court exercise under that standard to which it deferred. A conditional new trial protects the plaintiff's choice but does not eliminate the distinct allocation problem.

Scalia and Thomas would reverse the appellate remittitur and restore the district judgment against the reviewed excessiveness challenge: state appellate-review practice cannot supply the federal standard, and disagreement with the district court's factual excessiveness determination is not a permissible appellate basis. They do not adjudicate a new damages amount or preclude ordinary appellate correction of an independently established legal error. The neutral record supplies no such error in the distinct industry-evidence ruling. The final mandate must reflect the actual controlling disposition after Stone enters, not sum these distinct remedies.
