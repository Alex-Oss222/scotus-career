## Questions that remain separate

1. **Article III and presentation.** The State's merits brief p.33 n.23 expressly concedes initial standing while disputing proof of injury and causation sufficient for relief. This legal position does not stipulate jurisdictional facts or bind the Court's nonwaivable jurisdictional inquiry. Respondents' preservation argument is advocacy; the Ninth Circuit's footnote3 actually states that the particular general actual-injury issue was not before it. That statement must be acknowledged without inventing a comprehensive forfeiture ruling. A jurisdictional inquiry does not automatically authorize decision of every unpreserved merits theory. The classwide remedy and its connection to proved constitutional injury are themselves presented.
2. **What was proved.** Bartholic is a named representative plaintiff: the complaint caption and petitioners' own roster establish status. His alleged injury in the complaint remains an allegation; the separately verified district finding that deficient assistance caused a dismissal with prejudice is a finding. Harris is a witness supplying additional class evidence, not an established named plaintiff. Neither source establishes that exactly two legally sufficient injuries exist or that every alleged resource defect injured every inmate. Concrete obstruction of a nonfrivolous claim need not await an ultimate loss on the merits, and proving an access injury does not require proving that the obstructed case would have succeeded.
3. **The substantive right.** Bounds requires meaningful access, through adequate libraries or adequate assistance evaluated as a whole. It does not guarantee one prescribed library program or professional representation for every inmate. The present claims concern prisoners' efforts to present prison-condition and related protected court claims. A general rule for unrelated private civil litigation is unnecessary and reserved. Training, language assistance, supplies, copying and communications may be necessary means in particular circumstances; a preferred administrative feature is not itself a constitutional entitlement.
4. **Common proof and reach.** The findings identify recurring barriers, unit variations, literacy and language difficulties, inadequate assistance and concrete consequences. Common practice can support common relief without requiring that every class member already lose a lawsuit; the findings must still establish the group affected by the particular barrier and the remedy needed. Different literacy populations cannot be merged into a single current statewide statistic. A resource inventory, low educational attainment alone or one prisoner's past injury does not establish every element of a statewide decree.
5. **Present remedy.** Changes after filing, continuing need, alternative methods and actual security restrictions matter. Respondents acknowledge that an efficient paging system with an inventory and prompt delivery might suffice for prisoners able to use legal materials independently; they contest the actual system and do not concede adequacy for others. The record does not show that officials were excluded from all remedial participation. The decree incorporates administrative approaches and the master worked with the parties. Declining appellate mediation supplies no bad-faith finding or forfeiture.

## Existing decree and original PLRA

The injunction under review is the decree as modified by the Ninth Circuit. That court had already vacated the $46 indigency threshold for lack of the necessary current-standard finding and vacated the unopposed typewriter provision. It remanded copying price to consider the proposed increase from five to eight cents and master fees to afford the State an opportunity to object. They are not newly removed or restored here.

Libraries are required in facilities with population or capacity of150 or more except Aspen DWI, Flamenco, Papago DWI, Maricopa and Picacho; those exceptions do not eliminate access obligations. Individual library access may be postponed for documented safety/security inability or a medical determination preventing use. Requested use is at least ten actual hours weekly, with additional time for legal deadlines. Opening requirements are50 hours without advance scheduling, or60/70/80 hours with advance scheduling according to the decree's population/capacity bands. Librarians may hold a library-science, law or paralegal degree. These details are lower-court measures to be tested, not constitutional minima adopted here.

The telephone provision allows at least three twenty-minute calls weekly to an attorney, designated attorney representative or legal organization, with additional emergency calls. The prisoner pays or calls collect; calls uncompleted for technical reasons, no answer or refusal to accept do not count. The State may verify numbers and duration but, absent documented abuse, may not deny calls to those categories or demand information about their content. Calls must be private and not recorded or overheard. Copying likewise has a separate confidentiality rationale below. The access-injury rule proposed here does not decide that a distinct confidentiality injury requires a lost lawsuit; properly presented independent grounds retain their own elements on remand, without automatic endorsement of every claimed privilege or the numerical call minimum.

PLRA §802(b)(1), effective April26, expressly applies amended §3626 to prospective relief granted before, on or after enactment. This prison-conditions decree falls within that provision; it is not excluded as habeas merely because access assistance can facilitate habeas petitions.

- Section3626(a)(1)(A) permits prospective relief only upon findings that it is narrowly drawn, extends no further than necessary to correct the federal-right violation of the particular plaintiff or plaintiffs, and is the least intrusive means necessary. The court must give substantial weight to adverse effects on public safety or operation of a criminal-justice system. Necessary implementation measures need not each independently constitute a constitutional right, but each must meet these limits. The adequacy and actual feasibility of less intrusive alternatives matter; an imaginary ineffective alternative does not defeat necessary relief.
- Section3626(a)(1)(B)'s separate state-law limitation permits an order requiring excess official authority or violation of state/local law only if federal law permits it, the relief is necessary to correct a federal-right violation, and no other relief will do so. No such conflict is found here. Subsection(C) supplies no authority to build prisons or raise taxes. No prisoner-release remedy under(a)(3) is sought or authorized in this case.
- Section3626(b)(2)'s immediate-termination entitlement for relief entered without the required findings is subject to(b)(3): record-based written findings that the relief remains necessary to correct a **current OR ongoing** federal-right violation, extends no further than necessary, is narrowly drawn and is least intrusive prevent termination. Do not substitute the later conjunction AND. The ordinary(b)(1) termination schedules are distinct, including the pre-enactment two-year rule. Section(b)(4) preserves otherwise lawful earlier modification or termination.
- Enactment alone supplies no termination motion or automatic dissolution. Section(e)'s automatic stay requires an actual pending motion, with the original30-day or180-day trigger applicable to the kind of motion; no such filing is established here. The appellate remand positions below rest on review of the decree under governing law, not a fabricated termination event. They do not adjudicate an unpresented constitutional challenge to the statute.
- Any new preliminary relief on remand must be otherwise authorized and comply with(a)(2)'s need, narrowness, least-intrusiveness, safety and comity requirements. It expires90 days after entry unless the court timely makes(a)(1)'s findings and makes it final. This is no automatic reentry, extension or90-day conversion of the old permanent decree.
- Continued special-master arrangements must be examined under(f): disinterested/objective service and public-safety regard; sufficient remedial complexity; the prescribed party-list/strike selection procedure when appointing under the subsection; partiality appeal; the CJA-rate ceiling plus reasonable costs paid from Judiciary appropriations; six-month review; on-record hearings/findings, no ex-parte findings or communications; and termination of the appointment with termination of relief. These are statutory duties, not findings of misconduct by the existing master. The unresolved earlier-fee objection and temporal treatment of particular accrued charges require determination on remand, not an invented reimbursement or retrospective invalidation of all work.

The statutory findings require substance, not ritual use of new words. Existing supported findings may be used; new or changed conditions require appropriate consideration. The lower judgments did not conduct the newly enacted least-intrusive review, and several remedial explanations relied on reasonableness, little cost or absence of demonstrated hardship rather than the full necessity inquiry. Accordingly, the individual remand positions below do not declare every underlying measure unlawful; they require the governing standard to be applied to the actual measures and circumstances.

## Independent Justice commitments

### Stevens

**Judgment and components.** Affirm the determination that the Department denied meaningful access through the proved restrictive paging and inadequate assistance barriers, rather than vacating liability on a new abstract no-injury premise. Recognize initial jurisdiction on the supported personal obstruction and class controversy, while requiring current prospective entitlement and remedial breadth to remain supported. Vacate the appellate approval of prospective relief to the extent necessary for reconsideration under the original PLRA and remand for that limited task, retaining the adjudicated constitutional wrong and supported findings. Preserve the Ninth Circuit's prior partial vacaturs and remands.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** Stevens joined Bounds's affirmative meaningful-access rule. His Murray dissent, 492 U.S.15–32, treats actual capacity to prepare a meaningful claim as central, rather than requiring an inmate to establish a winning case before receiving assistance needed to identify it. His actual Lujan separate position accepts concrete personal injury and distinguishes jurisdiction from ultimate statutory entitlement. His actual Jenkins join permits necessary implementation measures repairing an established wrong without requiring each salary or program detail to be an independent constitutional right. His actual Baby Richard join likewise requires concrete injury and fitted implementation; it does not supply a general program entitlement. These positions support the liability determination on the concrete findings while accepting the new statute's separate remedial constraints.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** The strongest contrary path treats the Ninth Circuit's core-Bounds formulation as dispensing with injury and vacates all liability. Stevens would decline that broad correction here: the State concedes initial standing, respondents identify a presentation objection supported in part by the panel's own footnote, and the district actually found obstruction and dismissal. He would decide the presented remedy and sustain liability on those findings, not endorse an abstract right to any missing book. The strongest objection to keeping the decree is its mismatch with variable conditions and the intervening statute; that requires remedial reconsideration, not an inference that the constitutional wrong never existed.

**Joins, alternatives and limits.** Would join a rule that actual or sufficiently concrete threatened inability to prepare or present a protected, nonfrivolous claim is injury, without proof of ultimate success. Refuses a final-loss requirement, an assumption that only two inmates were injured, or vacation of supported liability merely to impose a categorical new pleading rule. Accepts effective paging for capable users and assistance for those who need it. Refuses a constitutional guarantee of every decree detail, but would not treat ordinary cost or administrative preference as a complete defense to necessary access. Supports an expedited lawful remedial reconsideration, with no automatic termination or unconditionally preserved decree.

### O'Connor

**Judgment and components.** Vacate the appellate judgment insofar as it approves classwide liability and the prospective decree on the broad core-Bounds premise; remand to identify the represented injury, the affected group and the relief justified under Bounds and the PLRA. Preserve factual findings, including the concrete named-plaintiff obstruction, rather than order dismissal for lack of all standing or negate the findings. The State's concession does not waive jurisdiction, but the available named-plaintiff evidence supports reaching the properly presented dispute; it does not resolve the extent of relief.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** Her Murray concurrence, 492 U.S.13–14, and join in Kennedy's14–15 concurrence preserve meaningful access while leaving States substantial choice among effective methods; they do not require appointed counsel generally. Her Lyons join requires a real threat for prospective relief. Her actual Lujan dissent accepts reasonable factual inferences rather than turning a supported claim into an artificial evidentiary impossibility. Her actual Jenkins dissent requires a demonstrated connection between the wrong and the challenged remedy, while accepting lawful implementation costs and actual findings. Her Madsen opinion likewise requires fit, although its speech-specific standard is not the PLRA's text. These authorities support remand for proper legal and remedial scope, not a conclusion that literacy barriers can never require assistance.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** Affirming liability on the concrete findings and remanding only the decree is plausible. O'Connor would find the lower courts' unqualified core/non-core distinction too important to the classwide scope to leave intact: the affirmative duty remains, but the existence of a program deficiency is not itself every class member's constitutional injury. Conversely, a dismissal based solely on the absence of a completed lost lawsuit would conflict with the real access duty and the positive record. The appropriate intermediate disposition preserves evidence and permits a properly bounded determination.

**Joins and limits.** Would join a nonfrivolous-claim obstruction standard allowing sufficiently concrete threatened harm and reasonable inference; no ultimate-success requirement. Requires attention to post-filing improvements, security qualifications and effective alternatives. Will not join a mandatory physical-library-entry rule, judicial management justified merely as better policy, automatic forfeiture from mediation, or automatic PLRA termination. State discretion does not permit an actually ineffective substitute; the court must evaluate effectiveness and necessity under the statute.

### Scalia

**Judgment and components.** Vacate the affirmed classwide liability and prospective-relief portions and remand. Article III must be independently satisfied notwithstanding the State's initial-standing concession. The named-plaintiff causal finding prevents treating this as an established absence of any possible access injury; remand must distinguish the actual personal obstruction from other classwide claims and unsupported program entitlements. Do not order outright dismissal of every claim on the present record.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** His actual Lujan opinion requires concrete personal injury and stage-appropriate proof rather than a generalized interest in lawful administration. His join in Murray's plurality, 492 U.S.7–13, rejects expansion of Bounds into a general appointed-counsel entitlement while preserving a remedy for an actual access deprivation. His actual Jenkins dissent rejects a remedial purpose detached from the proved constitutional wrong. His actual Sandin dissent's hardship framework is noncontrolling and supplies no new access rule; it illustrates his objection to creating constitutional entitlements from administrative detail, not a license to overrule Bounds by analogy. Actual Baby Richard's dissent likewise refuses to derive a substantive right from a proposed procedure; here the established Bounds right still governs.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** Concrete findings and an initial-standing concession support the narrower affirm-liability/remand-remedy path. Scalia would reject the concession as dispositive and require the legal sufficiency and represented reach of the findings to be evaluated under the correct injury standard, because the core-Bounds rule could support liability without that connection. The strongest objection to a broader dismissal is the named representative's supported lost-action finding and the program evidence; that evidence cannot be erased or reduced to mere allegations. The proper disposition is vacatur and bounded reconsideration, not a factual declaration of no harm.

**Joins and limits.** Joins a rule requiring demonstrated hindrance or sufficiently imminent obstruction of a nonfrivolous protected claim, with no need to prove that the claim would win. Refuses injury based solely on a deficient library inventory or a generalized right to litigate all private matters at public expense. The latter category is not decided here. Does not require every class member to have a completed lost lawsuit, but requires evidence connecting the represented group and remedial reach to the wrong. Joins the PLRA remand and its actual exceptions. No assumed exclusion of officials from designing relief, no invented motion, and no unrequested overruling of Bounds.

### Kennedy

**Judgment and components.** Vacate the classwide liability scope and decree approval and remand, preserving the concrete findings and the availability of targeted relief. Reach the presented access and remedial questions because an identified representative's obstruction supplies a concrete controversy; independently test each proposed extension of the remedy. Do not treat the State's legal concession as a stipulation of statewide injury.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** His Murray concurrence, 492 U.S.14–15, turns on actual available assistance and preserves administrative choice among effective solutions. This record differs materially: the district found inability to file and dismissal attributable to assistance barriers. His actual Lujan join and concurrence with Souter require personal injury and a real causal connection without treating every new form of concrete injury as constitutionally impossible. His actual Jenkins dissent requires explicit lawful remedial purpose and supported fit; ordinary implementation measures can be permissible once that connection exists. His actual Rufo join permits responsive institutional-decree modification rather than mechanical retention or dissolution. The PLRA now adds its own mandatory findings.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** The proved impediments make affirmance of an actual Bounds violation a substantial alternative. Kennedy would nevertheless require reconsideration of the classwide liability scope under the proper functional injury standard because the lower core-Bounds reasoning treated program adequacy as sufficient across the system. He would not permit that reconsideration to presume the actual barriers harmless. The opposite categorical denial of affirmative assistance is inconsistent with Murray's reliance on effective institutional assistance and Bounds's retained force.

**Joins and limits.** Joins practical access, concrete actual or threatened obstruction, and supported common relief. Refuses both a universal browsing mandate and a claim that all paging systems suffice. Requires consideration of the inventory/prompt-delivery alternative for capable users and different assistance needs for others. No general right to counsel is created. Joins original PLRA review, with current OR ongoing and the termination/stay predicates intact; no federal-state comity objection eliminates an established federal right.

### Souter

**Judgment and components.** Affirm the determination of an actual Bounds violation on the supported obstruction findings, while expressly refusing to make every core-resource defect an injury by definition. Vacate the prospective-decree approval for reassessment of its coverage and terms under the PLRA; remand without requiring the plaintiffs to relitigate the existence of the already established wrong. The represented class and each remedial extension still require supported scope.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** His actual Lujan join requires concrete injury, but his concurrence with Kennedy preserves Congress's ability to identify real injuries and causal chains; it does not reduce injury to a final adverse judgment. His actual Jenkins opinion protects measures necessary to repair a proved injury, with full attention to causal scope and limits on continued control. His actual Rufo join treats a changed governing legal setting through a responsive modification rather than erasure of the underlying wrong. His actual Sandin join and concurrence with Breyer insist on the real operation of the challenged deprivation, not administrative labels alone. His Baby Richard join likewise separates recognition of an interest from proof of serious injury and fitted relief. Here the named plaintiff's status plus the separate district causal finding supply the concrete anchor; Harris's testimony is additional evidence, not a substituted representative.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** Vacating liability because the lower legal formulation was overbroad is the strongest alternative. Souter would correct that formulation without vacating the demonstrated violation: appellate affirmance may rest on the actual obstruction found, and the State's presentation does not require adjudicating an abstract no-injury case this is not. The strongest objection to preserving the complete decree is that its breadth and justifications do not establish every new statutory requirement. That objection warrants a remedial remand, with factual findings retained only to their actual scope.

**Joins and limits.** Would join proof of actual or sufficiently concrete threatened interference, no abstract library right, meaningful administrative alternatives, and a statute-compliant remedy. Refuses a presumption that a named prisoner's injury proves every facility's liability, a requirement of a separate completed loss by every class member, or replacement of actual findings with a fixed historical injury count. No join in automatic reopening of all liability, mandatory termination, or a principle that each implementation detail must itself be a standalone constitutional right.

### Thomas

**Judgment and components.** Vacate the classwide liability and decree approval; remand for the correct personal-injury, causal-scope and statutory-remedy inquiries. Do not infer a total absence of jurisdiction from the source gaps, and do not treat an initial-standing legal concession as binding. Preserve the existing testimony and findings for their proper legal evaluation, including Bartholic's established representative status.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** His actual Lujan join adopts the requirement of an individual concrete injury and stage-specific proof. His actual Jenkins dissent rejects broad judicial administration resting on an unproved or impermissible remedial objective, while accepting relief tied to an established constitutional wrong. His actual Sandin dissent supports limiting constitutional claims to genuine deprivations; its proposed test did not become current law and cannot displace Bounds. His actual Baby Richard dissent insists that a desirable safeguard does not generate the necessary substantive predicate. These authorities support enforcing the access right already recognized while refusing to constitutionalize a prison's entire legal-services administration. No personal pre-appointment join in Bounds or Murray is invented.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** The concrete dismissal finding and recurring practices support the argument for affirming liability and correcting only the remedy. Thomas would find the lower core-Bounds standard insufficient to sustain the full adjudicated classwide reach and therefore require legal reconsideration of that reach. A broader course overruling Bounds is not necessary to these judgments and is not supported here merely by extrapolating an ideological preference. The strongest objection to outright dismissal is the record of an actual named prisoner's obstruction; preserving that evidence is compatible with requiring a proper constitutional and classwide showing.

**Joins and limits.** Would join Scalia's injury and scope propositions at the stated level, without a rule that all private civil litigation must be subsidized, a final-loss requirement, or a finding that every prison practice caused injury. Requires concrete justification for hours, staffing, calls, copying, assistance and supervision as means of correcting the proved violation. Joins statutory need/narrowness/least-intrusiveness, safety and master review. Does not treat existing administrative exceptions as absence of any federal duty, and does not invent bad faith or waiver from refusal to mediate.

### Ginsburg

**Judgment and components.** Affirm the determination that the proved assistance and access barriers violated Bounds; vacate the prospective-decree approval for a tailored PLRA reassessment. Preserve current source-supported findings and the Ninth Circuit's prior modifications. Do not require fresh litigation of the already established constitutional obstruction merely because the Court clarifies that library defects alone are not the protected injury.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** Her actual Jenkins join sustains practical remedial measures linked to an established wrong while retaining limits on purpose, causation and duration. Her actual Sandin concurrence with Stevens emphasizes the actual deprivation experienced by a prisoner rather than the administrative label; that is individual evidence of attention to effects, not a new Bounds test. Her actual Madsen joins require injunction terms to fit their predicate and remove invalid predicates without evasion. Her Baby Richard join accepts a supported implementation claim while requiring concrete proof and fitted relief. Applying controlling Bounds and Lujan here, the named representative's adjudicated obstruction gives the case a personal basis; the witness Harris and systemic findings may support common proof within their actual reach.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** The strongest contrary argument is that the core-Bounds distinction infected liability and requires total vacatur. Ginsburg would distinguish the legal shortcut from the actual proof: denying a real opportunity to present a nonfrivolous claim is injury even when assistance is needed to articulate its legal contours. The proper response to excessive scope is to confine relief to what the findings support, not to disregard those findings. Conversely, preserving every numerical and staffing term without the statutory inquiry would mistake useful administration for demonstrated necessity.

**Joins and limits.** Joins actual/threatened obstruction rather than abstract resource entitlement, effective paging alternatives for capable users, assistance fitted to literacy/language need and proper security qualifications. Refuses attribution of named-party status to Harris, an exact two-injury premise, or a requirement that the underlying litigation first be won. No inference of default from mediation or of invalid special-master conduct. Joins the original statutory remand, preserving present-right and current OR ongoing distinctions and all genuine motion predicates.

### Breyer

**Judgment and components.** Affirm the actual constitutional liability established by the obstruction findings, and vacate the prospective-decree approval for reconsideration of coverage, alternatives and terms under the PLRA. Retain the established factual basis; require any broader measure to be justified rather than presumed. No automatic declaration that every unit needs the same assistance or schedule.

**Sufficient grounds and own authorities.** His actual Jenkins join permits workable measures reasonably needed to repair the identified wrong without constitutionalizing every implementation component. His actual Sandin opinion examines the scheme's real constraints and the deprivation's seriousness instead of treating either a label or generalized administrative preference as conclusive. His concurrence there with Souter applies that inquiry to the actual record, while accepting a narrower remand. His Baby Richard join requires a concrete implementation injury and a measured procedure, not an ideal government program. Those own positions, together with controlling Bounds and Lujan, support recognition of the proved access deprivation and a separate practical examination of effective, least intrusive remedies. No fictional pre-appointment votes in the earlier access cases are assigned to him.

**Alternative and strongest objection.** The strongest alternative vacates liability because the lower core-Bounds formulation can operate without proof of injury. Breyer would reject the automatic link while sustaining the independently supported application: barriers to preparing or presenting a real claim are not transformed into harmless resource imperfections merely because the existing legal explanation was too broad. The opposite path of affirming the entire decree fails to test current alternatives and need under the intervening statute. Cost and administrative feasibility inform what works and what is least intrusive, but cannot alone extinguish the established access right.

**Joins and limits.** Would join a concrete obstruction rule allowing proof of imminent interference and common barriers, without final-success proof or one completed dismissal per class member. Joins reconsideration of exact hours, staffing qualifications, phone access, copies, supply eligibility and master arrangements against their proved functions and statutory limits. Refuses either universal physical browsing or categorical acceptance of ineffective paging. Supports prompt lawful relief where justified, not automatic continuation, termination or a new interim order without its own predicates.

## Remand and compatibility boundaries

The individual dispositions distinguish liability from the remedy. Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg and Breyer would preserve the actual liability determination on the proved-obstruction ground while remanding prospective relief; O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas would vacate the adjudicated classwide liability scope as well as decree approval and require reconsideration under the correct injury rule. Preservation of underlying factual findings is not agreement to preserve the same classwide legal judgment. Agreement on a remedial remand cannot be counted as agreement on liability, jurisdictional scope or every substantive injury proposition.

All stated PLRA remands concern the decree actually appealed and the new governing law. They neither turn original enactment into an automatic termination motion nor decide a constitutional challenge to §3626. The district court may use still-valid findings, must hear the parties on current need and statutory fit, and must give prison officials' effective alternative methods and public-safety considerations their proper weight. It may grant only otherwise lawful interim relief on the statutory conditions already set out. No final nationwide access-code standard, universal hours minimum, mandatory new special-master appointment, present fee amount or new Supreme Court filing is prescribed.

The merits and remedy holdings of actual Bounds, Lujan, Jenkins, Rufo and the PLRA retain their respective scopes. There is no proposed overruling of Bounds, no importation of historical Sandin, and no automatic extension of the Baby Richard implementation rule. No Stone vote, final Court count, opinion assignment or historical comparator has entered this model.
