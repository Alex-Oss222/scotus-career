## A. St. P. C. v. B. C., No. 94-7810

### Components and live paths

The merits component is the federal proof requirement for the finding that invokes §9:364(D)'s mandatory prohibition of **all visitation and contact**. The prohibition continues until a contradictory hearing establishes both successful completion of the defined treatment and that **supervised** visitation serves the children's best interests. The statute states no proof burden. It does not enact an admission-of-abuse condition. The actual OCS treatment/admission evidence does not establish the requirements of every program.

**Path A — heightened proof for this trigger:** Require clear and convincing evidence before invoking that all-contact regime. The family interest, complete scope of lost contact and absence of a fixed endpoint make erroneous classification sufficiently serious to warrant an intermediate burden. The strongest objection is that Santosky concerns irreversible termination, while this law protects a child through a conditional, reviewable restriction; proof error can expose the child to renewed abuse. The answer sustaining this path is confined to the specified trigger: a heightened burden does not bar temporary protection or supported supervised limits under other law, and accurate classification protects the child as well as the parent. It is an extension of Santosky's method, not a claim that Santosky already decided visitation.

**Path B — no categorical intermediate burden here:** Permit preponderance within constitutionally adequate, noticed protective proceedings because parental status survives, treatment and judicial best-interests review can restore supervised contact, and erroneous permission can directly harm the child. The strongest objection is that conditional restoration may remain remote and all contact can end indefinitely on a bare preponderance. The answer sustaining this path is that duration and actual procedure remain reviewable, but the risk balance of a protective visitation restriction is not the termination balance and does not itself constitutionalize one burden for every such proceeding. This path cannot validate the old finding as conclusive despite the positively established notice defect.

**Distinct remedial component:** The lower decree upheld invalidation of §364(D) on its federal proof ground. The earlier CINC finding used bare preponderance without notice that it could trigger this regime. No Justice supplies a new abuse finding, an exact current contact schedule, a particular treatment order or fee disposition. The independent state separation-of-powers ruling against mandatory greater weight for the child's therapist is not undone. The Court may resolve the proof question without deciding every visitation safeguard.

## Field v. Mans, No. 94-967

### Components and live paths

Section 523(a)(2)(A)'s reliance standard and the separate “to the extent obtained by” debt nexus are distinct. Section (B) expressly requires reasonable reliance for its specified written financial-condition misrepresentations; its other conditions do not migrate into (A). The bankruptcy court found actual reliance, detriment and an effective extension through nonacceleration, then applied an objective prudent-person investigation requirement. There is no separate justifiability finding. Petitioners request entry of judgment on remand; respondent preserves a competing nexus argument in the identified briefing.

**Path J — common-law justifiability:** The enacted fraud terminology carries the usual requirement of actual, justifiable reliance, assessed in light of this recipient's knowledge and circumstances. Failure to investigate is not by itself disqualifying; known or obvious falsity cannot be ignored. The strongest textual objection is that Congress mentioned reliance only in (B). But that contrast identifies the heightened objective standard in (B); it does not erase reliance inherent in the fraud terms in (A). The strongest policy objection is that creditor carelessness undermines a debtor's fresh start. Grogan rejects a fundamental discharge entitlement and does not authorize rewriting fraud around an ordinary-prudence requirement.

**Alternative paths:** An actual-reliance-only construction would give the (A)/(B) contrast greater force but discard the background legal limitation of justifiability. An objective reasonable-investigation rule would protect debtors but erase the different statutory formulation and import a stricter criterion into (A). Both remain arguments to test, not additional independent grounds for the chosen result. The relevant Restatement §§537, 540 and 541 propositions are the ones identified and discussed in the filed merits sources, not a new expansive tort holding.

**Remedial fork:** Rejecting the legal rule below warrants vacatur and remand, not a fresh Supreme Court declaration that every dollar of the note is nondischargeable. Actual reliance is already found and is not to be relitigated merely because the legal standard changes. Justifiability under the correct standard, the preserved obtained-by argument, the extent of any qualifying debt and ordinary preservation rules must be properly addressed below. No conclusion that the record lacks a nexus follows from the reservation. Grogan's footnote 2 reinforces the distinction; it supplies no answer on the amount here.

## NLRB v. Town & Country Electric, Inc., No. 94-947

### Components and live paths

The threshold issue is whether union pay and organizing obligations **categorically** prevent an applicant or worker from being an employee of the hiring employer. Section 152(3)'s breadth, its particular cessation-of-work provision and its seven express exclusions remain as set out in the validated packet. Neither statutory purpose nor absence of an express union-pay exclusion by itself disposes of the common-law employment inquiry.

**Path I — permissible inclusion consistent with common law:** A person may serve more than one master when the services are compatible. Union pay and organizing purpose do not necessarily eliminate the contractor's control of the manner and means of the electrical work. The Board may reject a categorical exclusion for such persons. The strongest contrary facts are the authentic resolutions requiring organizing assignments and immediate departure on union notification, preserving list status and imposing discipline. Those are real control terms, not merely a subjective hope to organize. They still do not demonstrate that the hiring employer lacks control over the work while the relationship lasts: an obligation or decision to leave is not necessarily an incompatible manner of performing the employer's work. No finding of actual refusal, sabotage or other misconduct is supplied.

**Path E — categorical exclusion for divided control:** The employer argues that a union-directed organizer cannot loyally serve the nonsignatory employer, particularly where the union can end the placement. Darden, common-law control and Lechmere provide the strongest legal basis. The decisive objection is that this treats all simultaneous union service as incompatible without examining the actual employment relationship and uses Lechmere's rule for established nonemployees to decide the antecedent classification. Darden demands all relevant incidents, not an irrebuttable rule from one pay or tenure feature. Health Care's dissent forbids an agency-created exclusion inconsistent with enacted predicates; that concern does not support creating a different exclusion here.

**Separate remaining components:** Eleven persons are involved, including two full-time union officials and nine other members. Ameristaff hired and referred Hansen; the ten others were not interviewed. A threshold ruling does not establish that all eleven had identical arrangements or actually began work. Other employer objections to discrimination inferences, qualifications and work-time rules remain unreached below. The Board's stated compliance procedure must determine how many would have been hired; no ten-person automatic backpay award follows.

## Thompson v. Keohane, No. 94-6615

### Components and live paths

The governing statute is pre-AEDPA §2254(d), whose complete filed Appendix A was read by the validator. The presumption covers qualifying factual determinations after the required state merits hearing and written indicia, with all eight statutory exceptions and the statutory convincing-evidence allocation preserved. The abandoned initial default is not the present judgment. A paper suppression proceeding does not automatically establish a statutory exception.

**Path M — independent ultimate custody determination:** Separate what occurred from the legal significance of those facts. Historical events, words, circumstances and supported credibility findings remain presumptively correct when §2254(d) applies. Whether those established circumstances amount to arrest-equivalent restraint for a reasonable person is an ultimate mixed determination requiring independent federal review. Miller's functional distinction and Stansbury's objective legal rule support this path. The strongest objection is that reasonable-person custody is a highly concrete estimate of an encounter and resembles factual inferences that remain presumptive. The answer is that the normative degree of restraint activating federal warnings requires consistent legal elaboration, while the state court's observational advantage is preserved for facts rather than converted into deference on the rule's application.

**Path F — factual ultimate estimate within a legal framework:** Treat the state court's reasonable-person custody inference as factual under the then-statute, while reviewing the definition of custody independently. Patton and Witt show that a constitutionally consequential inference is not automatically a legal question. The strongest objection is that Miranda custody applies a federal legal threshold, and this paper record gives the state judge no special credibility advantage. The answer sustaining this alternative is that a concrete estimate of freedom of movement can remain factual without live credibility disputes; Miller's distinct history of independent confession-voluntariness review need not classify every warning predicate. This path cannot impose a general reasonable-state-legal-decision standard, overrule Withrow, or eliminate the eight exceptions.

**Distinct application and remedy:** The parties brief actual custody as an alternative, but the present commitments do not resolve it. Voluntary arrival, the accusations, duration and setting, communicated freedom to leave and actual departure must be evaluated in their proper legal roles. Private intentions and later surveillance assertions cannot be converted into communicated custody facts. No present ruling on harmlessness, an independent involuntariness claim, suppression, or entitlement to the writ is made. The source support permits decision of the review rule without pretending that selective extracts establish every merits circumstance.
