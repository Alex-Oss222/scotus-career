## Common commitments and the actual scope revision

The [independent commitments](OT_1995CHUNK5_BARNETT_COMMITMENTS.md), released at `c769139`, remain the source for unchanged participation, vehicle, affirmative McCarran ground, state-purpose reservation, statutory conditions, remedy and source limits. This handoff changes ordinary-preemption generality and the individual explanations supporting it; it does not rewrite that freeze.

**Revised rule, adopted by all eight:** A congressional grant of national-bank power ordinarily protects its exercise against state laws that prevent or significantly interfere with that exercise. This includes lawfully conferred enumerated and incidental powers; the particular federal scheme determines the power's existence and limits, including any congressional incorporation of state restrictions. States retain authority under this ground to regulate where their laws do not prevent or significantly interfere with exercise of the federal power. The rule does not establish banking-field immunity or make every federal permission preemptive.

Section 92 affirmatively adds insurance-agency power. Florida's challenged affiliation prohibition prevents a qualifying association from exercising that power; it is preempted to that extent. The ability to comply by abandoning the activity does not remove the conflict. Equal treatment of state and national banks likewise does not reconcile a prohibition with the federal grant. Franklin supplies the protected banking-power principle; de la Cuesta confirms that a protected option need not be compulsory; McClellan preserves compatible state regulation. The branching cases enforce Congress's express state-law conditions, which §92 does not reproduce as a veto on bank affiliation. Its insurer-authorization condition remains effective.

**Actual change:** Significant interference short of an outright prohibition is now within the adopted national-bank rule. The frozen formulation had resolved denial of this particular option without adopting that reusable generality. No other state requirement is adjudicated here. Ordinary costs, general applicability and regulatory labels are not mechanically conclusive; a different law must be assessed against the actual federal power and its operation. No numerical threshold, new balancing formula, evidentiary procedure or categorical licensing safe harbor is added.

**Retained federal exception:** Section 92 specifically relates to the business of insurance through its express agency, policy-sale, premium, compensation and insurance-related restrictions. Banking purpose, placement in a broader enactment, the 1916 date and absence of express-preemption words do not defeat §1012(b)'s exception. The exception removes reverse-preemption protection; ordinary conflict remains independently necessary. Fabe's functional state-law protection and John Hancock's insurance-specific federal direction retain their actual simulated scope. The distinct antitrust proviso and §1013(b) boycott qualification do no work here. Assume Florida's insurance-regulatory purpose in its favor without deciding it; no protectionism finding, least-restrictive-means requirement or alternative first-clause holding is adopted.

**Retained disposition and limits:** Each Associate reaches the statutory merits through §1254(1), reverses the Eleventh Circuit's judgment sustaining denial of prospective relief, rejects the ordinary-preemption defense, and remands for the preserved association-location and customer-geography cross-appeals and corresponding relief. Shaw's §1331 treatment and Ex parte Young support the prospective vehicle. No damages action, unconditional injunction or nationwide sales entitlement is established. Belleview's population does not settle whether the Ocala-headquartered association qualifies through its branch.

All §92 conditions remain: a place with **no more than 5,000 inhabitants under the last preceding decennial census**, state authorization of the insurer, Comptroller rules, and the prohibitions on assuming or guaranteeing premium payment and guaranteeing the insured's application statements. Agency activity supplies no underwriting or insurer-obligation guarantee. Florida's independent-bank exclusion separately uses **less than 5,000** and excludes holding-company affiliates; Barnett's affiliation defeats it. Credit life and credit disability are separate activity exclusions.
