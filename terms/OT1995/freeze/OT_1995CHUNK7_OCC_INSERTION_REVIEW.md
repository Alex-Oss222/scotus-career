Section 7.4001(a) defines §85 interest to include compensation for extending or making credit available and borrower default or breach, with listed periodic rates, late fees, NSF fees, overlimit fees, annual fees, cash-advance fees and membership fees; its ordinary exclusions include appraisal, specified credit-insurance, finder, document/notarization and credit-report charges. Subsection (b) permits the maximum rate available to a state-chartered or licensed lender while retaining loan-class provisions material to permitted interest, including the example's loan-size limits. Subsection (c) preserves state definitions and uses solely for state-law purposes, including its late-fee/state-usury-calculation distinction. Subsection (d) permits the agreed corporate-borrower rate where the bank's State denies that borrower the usury defense. These are the verified conditions assessed below. No express special retroactivity provision appears in the bounded source.

## Material connections considered

**Agency interpretation.** Holly Farms independently identifies both statutory agricultural requirements before accepting the Board's permissible, supported application. An OCC construction of a separate banking statute supplies neither a new Court-wide deference holding nor a new NLRB rationale. Its issuance therefore does not displace that Record's reasoning or require an agency remand. A later Court construction in Smiley is a distinct, uncompleted event and cannot enter any earlier baseline.

**Interest, fees and money relief.** Noland's administrative tax penalties and retained tax-interest priority arise under the Bankruptcy Code; BMW's postjudgment interest accompanies an existing state judgment; Shieh's financial-leave inquiry concerns Court filing charges. The fact that these matters involve money, interest or fees does not make them national-bank compensation for extending credit. The reviewed Records establish no borrower transaction or §85/§86 issue that would supply that missing connection. Brown Group's statutory back pay, benefit and penalty limits and the other monetary judgments likewise remain governed by their own sources.

**Earlier banking decision.** [Barnett Bank](../records/Barnett_Bank_of_Marion_County_v_Nelson_merits_1996-03-26.md) is dated March 26, before the verified April 1 effective date. It concerns §92 insurance-agency authority, McCarran-Ferguson and unresolved association-location/customer-geography questions. Its public law expressly preserves incorporated state restrictions and supplies no new deference holding. Section 7.4001's §85 interest provisions do not settle those insurance questions. No post-March 26 lower-court act or new stage is inferred. Publication on February 9 is not conflated with effectiveness on April 1; whether a court could make some permissible interpretive use of the published rule before effectiveness is not decided. No broader national-bank insurance or non-interest-charge rule is part of this insertion review.
