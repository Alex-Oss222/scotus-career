## 2. Governing transaction and law

The Town purchases residential collection **and disposal** in the improvement districts. The payment includes the disposal allowance; actual performance of the purchased disposal remains incomplete when the hauler merely collects the waste. Itemization, the hauler's retained volume savings and its excess-volume risk do not by themselves convert that purchased component into an unrelated postperformance transaction. Conversely, a Town payment or contractual label alone cannot immunize a restriction on unrelated business. The facility's owner remains Ogden/CE Huntington Limited Partnership: public land, financial guarantees and purchased capacity are not an ownership finding.

The ordinance separately directs covered acceptable waste to the designated facility, including independently arranged commercial and other transactions beyond the Town's paid residential service. Its criminal enforcement identifies the use of sovereign authority to compel those purchases. Criminal penalties alone are not a complete constitutional test; otherwise lawful neutral health regulation may also carry them. The relevant distinction is the conduct governed and the service actually purchased.

SSC's strongest opposing contract argument is that distinct suppliers and prices identify a separate disposal market, and that the combined program protects a private processor through public leverage. South-Central Timber's plurality supplies substantial support for scrutinizing that boundary. The selected answer does not deny that disposal is a recognizable commercial market: a government can genuinely buy services in more than one market. Here it pays for both. White, 460 U.S. 208–215 and n.7, rejects an inflexible privity boundary while preserving limits on unrelated conduct. South-Central Timber, 467 U.S. 93–99 and n.11, concerns a seller's restriction on a later transaction after the State's direct commercial performance ended and insists on substance over retained-title labels. Even its narrower approach does not compel rejection of this actual paid disposal service. Reeves, 447 U.S. 436–447, supplies the general participant distinction, not unlimited permission for every government contract or natural-resource restriction.

The operative register remains **“Discriminatory routing, charges and linked subsidies in interstate commerce,” present operation effective West Lynn Creamery, June 17, 1994**, including Oregon Waste's April 4 component and Carbone's May 16 private-processing component. Carbone requires a legitimate local purpose that reasonable nondiscriminatory alternatives cannot adequately serve when the Town compulsorily reserves independent private processing business to a designated local private operator. It expressly leaves genuine government purchases and materially different public systems separate. Financing, sanitation and a substantial public stake do not alone meet the justification requirement; ordinary Pike balancing does not displace that controlling classification.

**“Proprietary public purchases and labor preemption,” present operation effective Boston Harbor, March 8, 1993**, informs the distinction for genuine purchased performance but remains an NLRA holding. It does not independently decide this Commerce Clause question. **“Compensatory taxes and the actual equivalent local burden,” present operation effective Lohman, May 23, 1994**, as applied in Fulton on February 21, 1996, creates no financing exemption. No compensatory-tax claim, congressional authorization for this ordinance, antitrust immunity determination or actual CERCLA liability is established. Potential disposal liability supports the lower court's account of the Town's continuing interest, not a new statutory holding.

## 3. Justice-specific reconciliation

### Stevens

**Retain affirmance on both components and the bounded remaining-proceedings position.** His Reeves dissent joined Powell's distinction between procurement for government needs and public enterprise favoring private local customers. His later White majority join permits the city's paid project condition despite its effect on contractors' dealings; his subsequent South-Central Timber plurality join preserves the limit on regulation beyond the transaction. Those positions supply a coherent boundary rather than an immutable opposition to all public purchasing preferences. The strongest contrary application would classify SSC's independent disposal supplier as a downstream market secured for Ogden. The admitted payment for final disposal distinguishes that application: the Town's service remains in performance. He sustains only the purchased component, not an unpaid obligation governing subsequent unrelated business.

His actual Carbone Court join and authored West Lynn opinion support invalidation of the ordinance's separate reservation of private demand. Examining the whole program does not erase a genuine purchase or convert the contract's destination into compulsory routing of all other waste. His ACT dissent's acceptance of the broadcast record is specific to that record and protected nighttime access; it creates no exception to Carbone for financial efficiency. He joins neither a universal government-contract immunity nor a breach finding, money award or rescission.

### O'Connor

**Retain the contract affirmance and precedent-based ordinance affirmance, with her Carbone disagreement express.** Her White join and South-Central Timber dissent join offer direct support for a proprietor's choice of contracted performance. The presently purchased disposal falls within that position even without adopting the broader proposition that every contractual condition is proprietary. The strongest expansive alternative would also sustain the ordinance because it supports the Town's financial program. That alternative fails the actual purchase boundary: the ordinance commands independent customers' transactions through sovereign power.

Her actual Carbone concurrence rejected the majority's discrimination classification and instead found a clearly excessive Pike burden, particularly the compulsory stop for already processed residue. SSC does not acquire that factual finding merely because both cases involve waste. Her selected sufficient ground is a narrow application of the now-controlling private-processing rule, while preserving her personal objection to the original classification. Her Oregon Waste and West Lynn joins establish no contradiction: they involved materially distinct express-origin and integrated-fiscal preferences. Her Hercules dissent allows inquiry into a disputed authorized undertaking; it does not require reopening SSC's admitted purchase of disposal because complete contract documents are absent. No new Pike findings or unconditional adoption of Carbone's original theoretical account are attributed to her.

### Scalia

**Retain both affirmances.** His Boston Harbor join supports identifying the service actually bought, and his Carbone Court join directly supports the ordinance's discrimination classification. White supplies governing authority rather than a fictional earlier personal vote. The strongest contrary contract position treats the linked contract and ordinance as one exclusionary scheme. That scrutiny is legitimate, but their different reach and payment predicates identify a separable actual purchase; invalidity of sovereign routing does not itself eliminate it.

His narrower West Lynn concurrence does not undo his full Carbone join or require rejection of market-participant doctrine. His Hercules majority join likewise distinguishes an actual contractual undertaking from consequences asserted from control or fairness. Here disposal is an affirmative purchased component; no reimbursement promise or breach is inferred from it. He accepts no universal downstream power, new Pike formula, automatic antitrust result or award of the disputed withheld payments. If an enforcement claim instead depends on the invalid ordinance, its legal basis must be addressed in the properly remaining proceedings, not declared valid by the procurement holding.

### Kennedy

**Retain both affirmances on the distinct transaction grounds.** His authored Carbone opinion expressly separates compelled private purchases from actual government purchasing. His Boston Harbor join reinforces attention to genuine proprietary performance without making that labor-preemption rule dispositive here. The strongest objection is that Ogden operates in a distinct disposal market and the contract helps reserve that market. Recognizing that market does not answer who purchases its services: the Town actually buys disposal, and White permits payment through the hauler rather than direct privity with every performer.

The ordinance reaches business that the Town does not purchase. His Carbone service-market analysis does not depend on the waste's eventual physical destination, exclusion of other local processors or a minimum number of favored firms. His Hercules majority join adds no government-contract immunity and no contract-liability finding. He retains the private-facility and actual-purchase limits, refuses an ownership fiction, and leaves lawful public systems, other municipal franchises and unresolved payment questions to their own records.

### Souter

**Retain both affirmances, preserving his objection to Carbone's original classification.** His actual Carbone dissent, joined by Blackmun, accepted private operation, compulsory routing and interstate effects. It viewed the arrangement as a single municipally guaranteed service and found no clearly excessive burden under Pike, giving weight to sanitary reliability, financing and allocation of costs. It did not claim categorical market-participant immunity or deny that outside processors could lose business. His Boston Harbor join and that concern for the actual service strongly support the still clearer paid-disposal purchase here.

The strongest ordinance alternative is to retain his earlier municipal-service/Pike result or seek overruling. The selected commitment instead applies Carbone's existing private-service rule to materially comparable compulsory private transactions, without pretending his earlier dissent has disappeared. The record supplies no established government ownership, new justification or other premise warranting displacement of that rule. This is a present precedent-following judgment, not a claim that stare decisis mechanically requires every Justice to abandon an earlier dissent. His actual West Lynn majority join distinguishes compulsory funding of a local preference from genuine purchasing; his authored Hercules opinion identifies actual contractual obligations rather than governmental labels. His ACT renewed-review position concerns unresolved broadcasting analysis and supplies no requirement to reopen this admitted purchased-service fact. He can join the narrow application and remedy while withholding renewed endorsement of Carbone's original classification rationale.

### Thomas

**Retain both affirmances.** His Boston Harbor join and governing White support the contractual specification of purchased disposal. His own Oregon Waste opinion and direct Carbone join support the ordinance's distinct discrimination analysis; neither his narrower West Lynn ground nor anyone else's older South-Central Timber position displaces those actual commitments. The strongest anti-contract argument would treat every local disposal preference as discrimination regardless of government payment. **Retain both affirmances with substance and scope controlling.** Her Carbone majority join expressly leaves ordinary government purchasing outside that compulsory private-route holding. **Retain both affirmances and ordinary remaining proceedings.** He did not participate in Carbone, Boston Harbor or the older participant decisions.
