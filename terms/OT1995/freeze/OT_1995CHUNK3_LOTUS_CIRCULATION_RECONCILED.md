## Proposed formulation and audit result

The sanitized proposal is to vacate the First Circuit judgment and remand to address preserved section 107 defenses and remaining statutory questions. O'Connor, Kennedy, Thomas and Ginsburg would preserve their own disagreement with the First Circuit's categorical section 102(b) exclusion in a plurality writing. The common decree would not declare the command hierarchy copyrightable, adjudicate fair use or order a defense victory. Scalia, Souter and Breyer would retain affirmance under section 102(b).

**Result:** Each of the four X Justices can independently support the bounded vacatur/remand decree below, with her or his substantive X position retained and no substantive copyrightability join manufactured. This is a supported revision of the formal relief from reversal of the categorical ruling to vacatur of the appellate judgment; it is not a switch to E, a concession that the First Circuit's categorical reasoning is sound or adoption of a free-standing procedural reason supplied by an undisclosed Justice. The four remain judgment supporters on their own merits-error grounds. This audit establishes four non-Stone judgment votes only. Assembly must separately determine whether an independently supported fifth vote adopts the identical operative relief; this file does not establish that vote or its grounds.

The proposed decree may read in substance:

> The judgment of the Court of Appeals is vacated, and the case is remanded for further proceedings addressing Borland's properly preserved fair-use defenses and the remaining statutory issues, as necessary to resolve the claims concerning the Emulation Interface and the Key Reader. The judgment does not determine that the command hierarchy is copyrightable or that either use is fair, and it does not direct reinstatement of the district court's injunction or final liability judgment.

This is merits vacatur following review of the judgment, not a summary GVR based on an intervening event. The decree requires genuine further consideration of the preserved issues; it does not merely invite the same appellate judgment to be reissued without the required work. It does not impose a universal fair-use-first sequence or require decision of an unnecessary issue. No automatic new trial, evidentiary hearing or reopening of unpreserved matters follows. Ordinary appellate authority, the actual record and applicable burdens govern what further proceedings are needed. No ultimate copyrightability, infringement, fair-use, damages or injunction result follows from vacatur itself.

## Existing support and the material change

The original X commitments already reject immediate restoration of Lotus's liability judgment or injunction and preserve Borland's properly remaining defenses. Their disagreement with the First Circuit concerns its categorical treatment of the entire operative hierarchy; they do not hold that every command, every arrangement or all 469 command choices are protected. Each retains the distinction between excluded functions/methods and any original expression, then the distinct inquiry into infringement and defenses. Setting aside the appellate judgment and returning the unresolved issues therefore supplies relief each already supports without awarding Lotus anything the original freeze withheld.

The actual change matters: a judgment of reversal accompanied by a majority X rule would foreclose the categorical legal route as Supreme Court law. A vacatur accompanied by only four X votes does not establish that substantive Supreme Court rule. The audit does not treat that difference as meaningless. It permits the four to retain and publish their own legal conclusion while joining the common operative disposition, rather than falsely attributing their substantive conclusion to the Court. They do not retract the conclusion or assent to an opposing one merely because their reasoning lacks a fifth join. Their own ground for vacatur is the error they identify; the decree's further-proceedings limits are independently supported by their preserved-defense commitments and their own Campbell joins.

The strongest rejection path is that every X Justice would insist on an operative Supreme Court declaration rejecting the categorical exclusion and therefore refuse vacatur without that declaration. The frozen record does not establish such an insistence. It expressly withholds final copyrightability/infringement relief and makes further consideration of defenses part of each X remedy. No cited own writing makes support for that lawful relief conditional on obtaining a majority for all of the Justice's reasoning. Conversely, preservation of defenses alone would not prove a vacatur join if the proposed remand adjudicated fair use, adopted E or withdrew X; none is authorized here.

Their distinct reasons in the original freeze remain. They need no fair-use decision once the copied subject matter is excluded. They do not become vacatur supporters because further defense litigation would be permissible under the contrary X premise. Stevens remains a nonparticipant.

The X writing's function is to explain why potentially original selection and arrangement require separate treatment from the underlying method and why compatibility can matter within preserved defenses. Its remaining limits are essential: no ownership of common terms/functions; no holding that the hierarchy is wholly protectable; no infringement determination; no automatic fair use; no privilege for all interoperability; no reopening of screen displays, long prompts or copied source/object code. The E writing's function is to defend the actual method classification while preserving those distinct kinds of material. Neither group adopts Boudin's possible new privilege as a holding.
