## Common supported disposition and propositions

Each Associate independently supports **affirming the Ninth Circuit's judgment vacating the District Court's abstention remand and returning the action for further federal proceedings**, with the qualifications below. The affirmation does not adopt an absolute prohibition against every form of abstention in every action at law.

**A. This remand is reviewable on appeal under §1291 through Cohen.** The order conclusively surrenders the federal forum rather than regulates the progress of a case that remains there. The question of the court's authority to surrender jurisdiction is separate from whether Allstate owes reinsurance payments or has a valid setoff. It has the public importance of the statutory allocation of adjudicative responsibility, rather than merely the private interest in avoiding litigation that failed in Digital Equipment. No later federal final judgment will provide review of the surrender. Those features satisfy conclusiveness, separateness, importance and effective unreviewability for this category of abstention remand.

Moses H. Cone's distinction between effective surrender and ordinary delay supports that conclusion. This actual remand presents the conclusive loss of the federal forum even more directly than the retained-jurisdiction stay disputed there. The Associates need not additionally hold that every remand is an ordinary final judgment. Thermtron's use of mandamus does not make that remedy exclusive for a later order meeting the independently applicable collateral-order requirements. Neither §1292(b) certification nor mandamus is needed to sustain this appeal; neither route is presumed available if its own requirements fail.

Section 1447(d) does not defeat this particular channel. The District Court expressly accepted jurisdiction and remanded under Burford, not for a timely raised removal-procedure defect or lack of subject-matter jurisdiction. Things Remembered's covered-ground restriction and Thermtron permit that classification without reviewing the correctness of a covered remand. The Commissioner's broader nonconcession does not supply a reason to decide all discretionary remands or the reserved Cohill category. No new §1447 exception is created.

**B. Burford does not authorize the outright remand of these coercive contract-damages claims.** The equitable discretion described in Burford and NOPSI allows a court to withhold relief whose grant is discretionary in the relevant sense; it does not confer a general power to abandon the statutory duty to adjudicate this damages action. Procedural merger did not convert an enforceable legal claim into discretionary relief. California's important liquidation interests do not themselves enlarge that remedial authority.

The complaint includes a declaration, and this decision must not inaccurately describe the pleading as containing no declaratory request. The Ninth Circuit declined the Commissioner's distinct declaratory-character theory as first raised on rehearing. In any event, the independent claim for money due under the contracts prevents treatment of the entire action as Wilton's declaration-only controversy. No ruling on the discretionary handling of a genuinely separable declaratory claim is necessary. Allstate's request for a litigation stay, disputed setoff defenses and participation in liquidation do not establish that the Commissioner's contract claim is itself a discretionary equitable remedy. The state asset-control orders must also be read with their permission to sue elsewhere; they supply no established exclusive-jurisdiction ground overturning the District Court's accepted jurisdiction.

This is a limit on **surrender**, not an adjudication that courts can never postpone a damages action under an otherwise applicable abstention doctrine. A stay retains responsibility and permits resumption; the District Court chose remand after declining that alternative. The present judgment neither approves nor requires a Burford stay, supplies its missing predicates, nor determines every abstention doctrine's treatment of legal claims. It does not disturb distinct state-tax comity rules or decide the unpresented application of Thibodaux. Nor does it reopen the unchallenged Colorado River ruling.

**C. Ordinary further proceedings, with no adjudication of the pending FAA motion or actual Burford predicates.** The legal limit in B suffices to affirm the judgment. The Court need not determine in the first instance whether either NOPSI branch otherwise supports abstention: difficult state questions of transcendent public importance and disruptive interference with coherent state policy are alternative grounds, subject to timely and adequate state review, not cumulative requirements. Prudential and Imperial cannot be treated as resolving all setoff disputes or all Allstate defenses. Neither a new abstention hearing nor a direction to grant a stay follows from this reservation.

Allstate's arbitration request remains for lawful initial treatment below. FAA coverage, assent, scope, defenses, the conditions for a federal §3 stay, and the interaction with insurance-liquidation law have not been decided here. Casarotto supplies its actual §2 rule and limits, not automatic enforcement of this agreement or a ruling on McCarran-Ferguson. Affirmance awards no damages, compels no arbitration, directs no distribution of insurance assets and orders no particular new evidentiary proceeding.
