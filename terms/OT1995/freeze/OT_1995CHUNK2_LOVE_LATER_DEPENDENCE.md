## Actual Love law relevant to the comparison

- **Personal arms protection:** Six directly adopt the proposition that the Second Amendment protects an individual's keeping and bearing within its constitutional scope. Formal enrollment or current organized-militia service is not a condition of invoking protection. The Court distinguishes the holder of the right from its purposes and scope. Miller retains its **weapon-and-record inquiry**; it is not an exhaustive self-defense code.
- **State application and acquisition:** The same six apply the guarantee to States through the Fourteenth Amendment's Due Process Clause on the same constitutional standards governing federal action. Cruikshank and Presser lose only their contrary state-application barrier. Necessary acquisition is protected as an incident of protected keeping. Love decides no comprehensive weapon category, scrutiny tier, general licensing prohibition, or actual infringement on the pleaded policy. Five separately adopt the bounded arms vacatur and remand.
- **Due process:** All nine agree that state-law illegality does not alone establish a substantive federal wrong and that ordinary contracting does not create a heightened entitlement to every lawful transaction. The five-vote affirmance has **no common sufficient application rationale**. The four-Justice screening rationale, Stone's specific-source ground, and the contrary remand position do not create a general executive-licensing test, a rule that later state correction cures substantive injury, or a general specific-source displacement rule.
- **Immunity and review:** Eight decline first-instance adjudication of an unpleaded, undecided personal defense on this presentation. Exceptional review remains discretionary. Love does not decide immunity entitlement, abolish or replace Harlow/Anderson, select a universal merits/immunity sequence, change Elder's conduct-date inquiry, or supply retrospective notice by its new holding. The inactive conditional proposals in the audit annex are not law or published judicial positions.
- **Published positions:** Stone's ordinary lawful home-defense and adjudication-based disqualification propositions remain his alone. Stevens, Ginsburg and Breyer's arms disagreement concerns retention of state-nonapplication precedent; it does not adopt a collective-only rule or formal-enrollment requirement. Souter joins the controlling arms holdings. Those exact scopes matter to Hale; none may be replaced by a general supposed view about all firearms.

## Material blocker: Hale's arms explanation

The [Hale Record](../records/United_States_v_Hale_merits_1996-02-12.md), Public Projection Holding 3, contains this controlling application sentence:

> Hale supplies no evidence connecting this possession with preservation or efficiency of such a militia, and military usefulness alone does not make the necessary case.

That is part of the explanation adopted by the recorded nine-Justice coalition. It supplies an absence-of-proof reason tied to **this possession and such a militia**, in addition to the proposition that military usefulness is not categorical immunity. Love, effective two months earlier, expressly separates the personal holder's ability to invoke protection from formal militia enrollment or service and explains Miller as asking about **the particular weapon on its record**. Love also rejects withholding all protection outside an organized militia. Hale must therefore distinguish the permitted weapon/scope inquiry from an impermissible requirement that this individual demonstrate a qualifying militia connection before invoking the guarantee.

The strongest reading favoring unchanged Hale is substantial: Holding 3 rejects only automatic immunity for every militarily useful weapon; it already refuses an exclusively collective-right formulation, and it leaves the full scope of personal protection open. Love preserves Miller, lawful regulation, and unresolved weapon coverage. It does **not** hold that a machinegun is protected, that usefulness in war makes regulation invalid, or that the Chief's ordinary-arms/automatic-fire distinction controls. Accordingly, neither the narrow operative rule nor the affirmance is logically displaced merely because Love exists.

That compatibility of the narrow rule does not establish compatibility of every adopted reason. The current application sentence does not say that it concerns only a failure to establish the weapon's constitutional characteristics. It relies on the absence of a connection between **this possession** and the militia while conceding that military usefulness alone is insufficient. Reading it now as nothing but the retained weapon-and-record inquiry would add a limiting construction to controlling explanation. Reading the lack of an individual militia connection as a necessary coverage predicate would conflict with Love. Deleting the first clause, treating it as legally inoperative, replacing it with a regulation justification, or distinguishing Love's holding within the explanation would likewise change the recorded grounds. The review cannot silently select one of those substantive repairs and then call the insertion harmless.

**Exact unresolved task:** Determine, under Love's now-effective personal-holder rule and retained Miller inquiry, whether the existing Hale H3 explanation and each recorded join remain supportable at their actual scope; if they do, preserve the compatible holding through a truthfully authorized correction that states the proper scope. If the supported application changes, record that changed reasoning and any resulting join, writing, judgment or remedy change through the correction protocol. This review neither authorizes that work nor supplies a substituted merits rationale.

### What the blocker does and does not reach

Hale's **full-scope reservation is not independently false**: Love also leaves many scope questions open. Likewise, omission of a Love citation would not alone establish a material defect. The blocker is the specific adopted application reason in a directly governed arms controversy, not a rule that every later opinion must cite every relevant precedent.

Hale's separate-writing and continuity treatment must be checked with that correction. Love has already made the personal holder and absence of a formal-service prerequisite controlling, with O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas joining Stone at that level. Hale cannot treat that general personal character as merely Stone's proposed law. But Hale's more specific ordinary-lawful-civilian-arms and sustained-automatic-fire distinction remains beyond what Love controls. Thus Love does **not** automatically create an Associate join in Hale's concurrence, eliminate that concurrence, or change the recorded topology. The current combined description of a distinct individual-arms premise and functional distinction needs to be separated at those legal levels if the arms explanation is corrected. Prior adherence to the generic personal-right proposition is evidence for that distinction, not permission to infer assent to the Chief's broader formulation.

Love's incorporation holding does not turn Hale into a state-action case; Hale is a federal prosecution. Its acquisition principle does not establish a categorical license to acquire regulated automatic weapons. The new right leaves the actual commerce and tax powers questions distinct. No material Love effect is identified in Hale H1's Lopez/Harris market-scheme analysis, H2's Sonzinsky/Freed tax-registration ground, H4's Roberts/Dutton/Inadi/Wright confrontation analysis, or H5's evidence and judicial-notice ruling. Love supplies no new Staples claim, registration-impossibility fact, evidentiary objection, harmlessness finding or remedy. Those components are not independently reopened by this report.

The existing conviction result cannot be used to conceal the reasoning dependency. Conversely, this dependency does not establish a new result, dissent, author or vote. **Hale is the one later event that is not cleared for an entering-law-only reconciliation.** Its Record, generated Public Projection handoff and public render have not been changed.

## Behrens: related subject, no material dependency

The [Behrens Record](../records/Behrens_v_Pelletier_merits_1996-02-21.md) concerns a defense actually asserted, a later summary-judgment denial, and a categorical one-appeal jurisdictional dismissal. Love concerns whether this Court should decide an affirmative defense not invoked in the dismissal motion or decided below. Love's discretionary nonreach creates no numerical limit on appeals and no exception to Cohen/Mitchell's cumulative collateral-order conditions. It neither supplies nor removes Behrens's authorized appellate channel.

Behrens Part II already confines legal review to properly assumed facts, forbids evidentiary reweighing, and states that Elder supplies neither independent jurisdiction nor later notice. Love expressly preserves all three distinctions. It does not change Johnson's assumed-fact method or Kimberlin's separation of a legal rule from factual selection. Love's acceptance of pleaded deliberate-policy allegations is appropriate to its dismissal posture; it does not require a summary-judgment court to treat every allegation as supported evidence.

Behrens adjudicates no underlying employment entitlement, constitutional violation, damages, or actual immunity. Love's common state-law/federal-wrong propositions therefore displace no Behrens merits conclusion. Its fractured due-process application cannot be imported as a general licensing or property rule. The inactive immunity-replacement proposal has no effect at all.

Behrens Part III's surviving-different-claim rule assumes an otherwise authorized appeal. Love neither contradicts claim-specific immunity nor creates review of unpresented claims. The first mandate, preservation rules and exclusion of unrelated issues remain. Breyer and Stevens's one-ordinary-appeal/finality objection is also untouched: refusing an unpleaded defense in Love says nothing about whether one prior appeal exhausts an actually asserted one. Their conditional joins remain coherent.

**Finding:** No material change to Behrens's judgment, controlling explanations, precedent treatment, exact joins, separate writing, remedy or continuing stage. A truthful internal note can distinguish Love's unpresented-defense posture and record its express preservation of the existing immunity framework. No public correction or rerun is supported by this review.
