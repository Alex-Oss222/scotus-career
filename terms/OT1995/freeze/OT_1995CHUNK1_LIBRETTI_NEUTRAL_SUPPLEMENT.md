## Statutory and procedural boundaries remain live

The neutral packet's separate treatment of §853(a)(1) proceeds, (a)(2) facilitation, (a)(3) enterprise-control interests, and (p) substitute property remains necessary. Section 853(p) requires qualifying §853(a) property made unavailable by a defendant's act or omission under one of the five statutory conditions. For the commingling condition the source's exact formulation is property commingled with other property that cannot be divided **without difficulty**; it should not be silently replaced with an unconditional tracing rule. Lawful acquisition alone does not decide substitution, and the existence of a negotiated all-assets term does not independently prove each predicate.

The April order quotes §853(n)(6)'s preexisting/superior-interest and bona-fide-purchaser-for-value branches, but also invokes the civil §881(a)(6) innocent-owner rule and civil authorities. Report that as the district court's reasoning. Do not adopt a generic innocent-owner defense as the text of §853(n), collapse §853(n) into a defendant's postjudgment remedy, or assume that a third party's successful claim decides the defendant's constitutional or procedural claim. This supplement neither approves nor revises the correctness of the third-party rulings.

Libretti's personal December factual-basis objection is express (JA154); the judge notes it and refers to trial evidence. The new source completion does not convert it into a forfeited objection or establish what counsel privately advised about the forfeiture jury. The two presented procedural questions, right-specific waiver conditions, competing Rule11(f) readings, and the distinction between a Rule11 duty and statutory sentencing authority remain those in the frozen packet. A procedural ground may be resolved without a new asset-by-asset finding only when that ground truly does not depend on one. The source additions may inform a modeler's assessment of the procedural dispute; this validator makes no commitment-level materiality conclusion.
