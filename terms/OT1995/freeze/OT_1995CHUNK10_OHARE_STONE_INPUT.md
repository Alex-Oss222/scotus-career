## OT1995-097 — O’Hare Truck Service, Inc. v. City of Northlake

**Event:** 1996-06-28 | **Category:** MERITS | **Action:** Argued merits decision

**Citation:** 518 U.S. 712

**Docket(s):** No. 95-191

### SECTION II — STONE POSITION SUPPLEMENT

**Inherited-law qualification:** The supplied patronage-extension position does not require an assumed prior same-day Umbehr outcome. The two matters share their baseline absent a separately established effective sequence.

**Position status:** Approved by the user as written on October 2, 2026; no Court vote or outcome entered.

- **Stone’s Controlled Judgment:** Reverse the Seventh Circuit and remand.
- **Stone’s Remedy / Remand Position:** Permit the claim to proceed under political-association, retaliatory-motive, same-decision, immunity, and damages principles.
- **Stone’s Threshold Position:** Limit the decision to an existing recurring contractor relationship allegedly withdrawn to compel political support.
- **Supporting Authorities:** *Elrod v. Burns*, 427 U.S. 347; *Branti v. Finkel*, 445 U.S. 507; simulated *Waters v. Churchill*, 511 U.S. 661; *Mt. Healthy City School District Board of Education v. Doyle*, 429 U.S. 274.
- **Stone’s Proposed Holding for This Case:** Government may not terminate or materially disadvantage an existing independent contractor because the contractor refuses political support, unless political affiliation is an appropriate requirement for the contracted function; ordinary motivating-factor and same-decision rules govern causation.
- **Stone’s Reasoning:** The City’s power to allocate towing referrals cannot become a mechanism for compelling campaign loyalty. Towing work does not formulate policy, occupy a confidential political role, or speak for the administration, so political allegiance bears no lawful relation to performance. Neutral removal for service, cost, safety, or integrity remains available and can be tested through *Mt. Healthy*.
- **Stone’s Operative Limit:** The holding does not constitutionalize every lost bid or contract dispute, protect deficient performance, treat contractors as employees for every purpose, or prevent political criteria for a genuinely policymaking or confidential function.
