## Material premise for reconsideration

CPLR §5501(c) names the Appellate Division in its text. Published New York appellate decisions issued before June 24, 1996 also apply its materially-deviates standard to trial judges deciding motions to set aside jury damage awards. This construction is material to whether the state enactment is solely an appellate allocation rule, and to the analysis of applying the measure within federal trial procedure. It does not itself resolve Erie, Rule 59, the Seventh Amendment, the standard of federal appellate review or Gasperini's disposition.

## Verified primary state texts

1. **Inya v. Ide Hyundai, Inc., 209 A.D.2d 1015, 619 N.Y.S.2d 440 (4th Dep't 1994).** The court states that the trial-level Supreme Court may set aside a jury damage award under the material-deviation measure and holds that using the shocks-the-conscience test on the inadequacy motion was error. The court then applies the material-deviation measure to the injury record and orders conditional new-trial relief. This is a state-law construction, not an inference from an enactment's purpose. [Full primary text](https://static.case.law/ad2d/209/html/1015-01.html); [local copy](../research/chunk9_neutral_authorities/Gasperini_Inya.txt).
2. **Cochetti v. Gralow, 192 A.D.2d 974, 975–976, 597 N.Y.S.2d 234 (3d Dep't 1993).** The court recognizes trial judges' sparingly exercised discretion over damage awards and applies §5501(c)'s material-deviation standard to a trial order setting aside the jury's refusal to award future damages. It directs a new trial on all damage issues because of the possible compromise/inconsistent verdict. [Full primary text](https://static.case.law/ad2d/192/html/0974-01.html); [local copy](../research/chunk9_neutral_authorities/Gasperini_Cochetti.txt). The complete opinion was also cross-checked against [OpenJurist's primary reproduction](https://openjurist.org/192/ad2d/974/cochetti-v-gralow-6096454).
3. **Shurgan v. Tedesco, 179 A.D.2d 805, 806 (2d Dep't 1992).** The short memorandum preserves sparing use of trial-court discretion, then approves the trial judge's setting aside an inadequate damage award under the material-deviation measure. It confirms application, without an extended statutory-construction discussion. [Full primary memorandum](https://static.case.law/ad2d/179/html/0805-02.html); [local copy](../research/chunk9_neutral_authorities/Gasperini_Shurgan.txt).
