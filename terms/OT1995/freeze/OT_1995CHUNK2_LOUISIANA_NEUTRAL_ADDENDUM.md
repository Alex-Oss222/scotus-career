# Louisiana decree: authenticated coordinate source

Neutral source verification for the December 4, 1995 implementation event. This supplies no Court disposition, Stone position or historical comparator.

The Supreme Court Library's original-jurisdiction records index identifies Mississippi's Answer filed December 1, 1993. Its attached counterclaim paragraph 5, printed pages 22–23 (PDF pages 24–25), states Point 3 at north latitude **32°48′47″**, west longitude **91°09′37″**. This matches the Special Master's Appendix A, A-1, which expressly cites that counterclaim paragraph as a source for the Austin Smith line. Appendix E's proposed decree instead prints 32°49′47″. The source counterclaim therefore independently authenticates Appendix A's call; this conclusion does not rest on copying a historical Supreme Court decree or inferring a coordinate from the shape of the line.

The remaining twenty-one calls in counterclaim paragraph 5 match Appendix A. The counterclaim's introductory reference to Points 1 through 21 is followed by an expressly listed Point 22; the schedule, not that introductory count, supplies all twenty-two points. Its separate description calls Point 21 the 1975 downstream end of the fixed thalweg and beginning of the 1975 live thalweg; that descriptive statement should not be silently replaced by an invented new survey or a post-1975 movement finding. Louisiana's February 7, 1994 answer denies the counterclaim's boundary claim generally; it does not offer a competing transcription of Point 3. The completed October 31 decision controls which line is to be implemented; these pleadings authenticate the description, not a new merits concession.
