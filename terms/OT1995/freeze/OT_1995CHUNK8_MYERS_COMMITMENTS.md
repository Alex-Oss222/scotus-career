## Common threshold and governing rules

The examination concerned Myers's own residence; no vicarious Fourth Amendment theory or unsupported standing defect is supplied. Souter's Doggett threshold opinion and the unanimous review-channel portion of O'Connor's Ornelas opinion support giving the actual reservation effect.

Pinson, May 8, 1995, is the direct controlling authority: targeted technological examination of a closed home's heat patterns is a search when it reveals otherwise unobservable information concerning concealed domestic operation. External reception and absence of an identifiable interior image do not defeat protection. Scalia authored the search and remedial portions, joined by Stevens, O'Connor, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg among the present Associates. ### The actual fork under Pinson

**Six-Associate controlling-rule path:** The reported heat patterns were directed at the residence and used to disclose concealed grow-light activity that ordinary outside observation did not reveal. Pinson already addresses the lack of wall penetration and lack of an ordinary interior image. A roof vent does not make the underlying domestic information analogous to garbage intentionally delivered for collection, and a thermal report concerning lawful as well as unlawful activity does not acquire Place's contraband-only character. The investigators' inference concerning missing curbside garbage is not a finding about what occurred inside or about exposure of the thermal information. An unaided observation of visible smoke would present a different record; none is substituted here.

The strongest contrary argument is that the scanner reports only heat already at the exterior and police reason from it rather than directly see an object inside. Pinson considered and rejected that physical-medium answer for the demonstrated closed-home information. No material capability or exposure distinction supports affirmance under the existing majority rule. These six therefore retain and apply Pinson rather than create a new threshold based on device sophistication, public availability, intimate detail, or a forecast about future technology.

**Kennedy and Breyer's contrary path:** They maintain their published objection that this comparative exterior reading, without identification of a person, object or exact activity, does not itself obtain Karo's protected interior fact. The supplied facts offer no honest distinction from Pinson's controlling application. Their preferred affirmance therefore entails rejecting that application and replacing it, to this extent, with their earlier content-specific understanding of Katz and Karo. This is an express disagreement with present law, not a claim that lower courts may disregard it or that their dissent already controls. It preserves protection for materially more revealing acquisition and adopts no universal heat-abandonment, outside-receiver, contraband-only or good-faith rule.

Their strongest objection from the other path is the directly controlling, recent Pinson holding and the absence of any intervening source undermining it. Their contrary commitment rests on the same specific constitutional distinction they published there; no changed factual premise or new authority is invented. Their mere disagreement cannot be counted as a majority holding. Neither commits to a separate broad overruling of Katz, Karo or all technological privacy protection. If the majority retains Pinson, their conditional agreement with lawful remedial limits below is not a vote joining its search holding or vacatur.

## Separate suppression/remedy comparison

For Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg, correction of the search ruling is sufficient to set aside the categorical ground actually sustaining denial of the preserved motion. It is insufficient to decide whether the evidence recovered under the later warrant must ultimately be excluded.

**Selected remedy:** Vacate the Seventh Circuit's judgment insofar as it sustains the suppression denial and conviction affirmance on the categorical no-search ground; remand for lawful disposition of Myers's preserved suppression claim under Pinson. The later warrant cannot retroactively authorize the earlier scan. The derivative claim is genuinely preserved here; Pinson's appellate failure to challenge affidavit sufficiency is not transferred to Myers. The lower courts may address the actual preserved derivative-warrant contention and other independently supported, procedurally available grounds, applying their own legal predicates.

**Direct-suppression alternative:** Myers's argument runs from unlawful scan to invalid warrant to suppression. The first conclusion does not establish the next two automatically. Karo Part IV preserves a distinct inquiry whether untainted information supports a warrant, but the complete affidavit and a corresponding finding are not supplied. No independent-source or inevitable-discovery facts are established. Direct suppression would require the very record determination this Court cannot invent.

**Final-admission alternative:** Other investigative evidence and issuance of a warrant do not establish untainted probable cause or objective reliance. Leon remains available only if factually supported and procedurally open, with its exclusions for knowing or reckless deception, abandonment of neutrality, an affidavit wholly lacking probable-cause indicia, and facial deficiency defeating reasonable reliance. The inquiry includes officers obtaining or supplying the information and assumes execution within scope. Evans adds no exception for a deliberate thermal examination; it concerns qualifying court-clerical error. Neither a general reasonableness label nor an officer's subjective innocence fills these gaps.

**Proceedings allowed, not compelled:** The mandate does not order a new affidavit hearing, grant discovery, presume a new defense, impose an automatic excision procedure without examining the actual claim, or reopen waived matters. Ordinary preservation controls each additional ground. Olano's error, plainness, ordinary outcome-prejudice and separate fairness/integrity/public-reputation requirements apply only if a genuinely forfeited issue calls for Rule 52(b); they are not added to the claim Myers preserved. Rule 11(a)(2)'s consequence if he ultimately prevails remains; this limited appellate correction does not alone order plea withdrawal, acquittal, release, retrial or a replacement sentence.

Kennedy and Breyer would avoid these unresolved suppression grounds by affirming on their no-search view. Conditional on the Court retaining Pinson, they accept the same distinction between violation and remedy and the same actual-preservation boundary, consistently with their Pinson conditional position and their Evans/Ornelas joins. That conditional compatibility supplies no unconditional vote for vacatur, final admission or suppression.
