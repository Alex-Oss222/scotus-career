## Reconciliation result

All eight Associates support dismissal of the ordinary certiorari component for want of jurisdiction, preservation of the distinct original habeas jurisdiction, denial of this original application without a further return, and denial of the pending A-890 request. These are eight-Associate commitments, not a nine-member Court judgment. All eight permit the successive statutory restrictions to inform original-writ discretion but reserve whether §§2244(b)(1)–(2) bind this Court directly. They do not adopt a general freestanding-innocence burden or merge innocence, the procedural gateway, trial error and extraordinary-writ discretion.

Two independent-model differences do not survive source review: Stevens and Souter's proposed further return/protection, and Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas's proposed conclusive direct-application holding. The latter three retain their textual argument as an unresolved alternative, not a proposition they insist be decided or published here. Neither correction treats a historical vote as a command; the specific changed-premise tests appear below.

## Reconciled common components and legal limits

**J/T — ordinary review, original jurisdiction and timing; all eight.** Dismiss the certiorari request directed to the Eleventh Circuit's authorization denial under §2244(b)(3)(E). The May authorization proceeding and requested review follow April24 enactment; the channel rule applies without determining the temporal reach of every TitleI amendment. The frozen Landgraf method distinctions remain: Stevens, O'Connor, Souter and Ginsburg use the controlling sequential approach; Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas their relevant-activity approach; Breyer applies controlling law without an invented earlier join. Retained §2241 original jurisdiction is not impliedly repealed. Its constitutional character is appellate although filed in the first instance here. Yerger's separate-source principle and the actual text support that result. Section1651 is not a freestanding jurisdictional enlargement. This last proposition does not adjudicate every possible writ in aid of independently existing jurisdiction, certified question, or interlocutory route not presented for decision here.

**S — statutory consideration and constitutional scope; all eight.** Paragraph(3)'s district-court authorization process does not govern filing an original petition here. Paragraphs(1) and (2) address distinct repeated/new claims and inform original consideration; whether they bind this Court is reserved. A newly requested rule is not an already announced, previously unavailable rule made retroactive by this Court under (2)(A). Paragraph(2)(B) separately requires both due-diligence unavailability and clear-and-convincing whole-evidence proof of no reasonable guilty finding but for constitutional error. Neither a Schlup showing nor an assertion of innocence supplies those cumulative predicates automatically. The present claims do not establish those conditions; this assessment does not create a new diligence, waiver, state-remedy or credibility finding.

The retained channel and independent traditional denial answer the asserted loss of constitutionally sufficient review in this case. The successive restrictions are considered within the existing statutory and abuse-of-writ framework, not as a direction to falsify a fact or apply an unchanged rule to a legislatively predetermined result. The original application's independent inadequacy means these claims establish no suspension through the operative successive scheme. This is not a declaration that survival of a nominal but unusable remedy always satisfies the Constitution. Questions concerning exclusion of established innocence, all avenues of supervisory review, §2254(d)/(e), chapter154 qualification, or every TitleI provision remain outside the necessary decision. No majority-level general freestanding-innocence burden is adopted.

**C — instruction; all eight.** The complete charge does not create a reasonable likelihood of conviction below proof beyond a reasonable doubt. Its evidence/lack/conflict definition, unshifting State burden, circumstantial-proof qualification and repeated duty to acquit supply the relevant context for moral certainty and the voir-dire statements. This is actual Victor whole-charge review, not an isolated-word rule or automatic cure by boilerplate. Sullivan's structural consequence would control an actually vitiated proof standard, but the predicate is absent. No cause, retroactivity, procedural-default or newly unpleaded instruction ruling is needed.

**P — trial-process claim; all eight.** The presented nonphysician/reliability ground does not establish constitutionally fundamental unfairness. Ake, Ford and Eddings do not supply the requested automatic physician-only rule. The additional expert criticism has substantive forensic content, but on this record it does not itself prove knowing false evidence, concealed qualifications, deprivation of a constitutionally required expert or another established trial-process defect under Lisenba. Tillman's credentials were exposed, contrary medical testimony was offered, Whitaker supplied independent qualified testimony, and the jury controlled expert weight. The finding concerns the constitutional claim, not the truth of every forensic conclusion.

**I — original innocence/gateway assessment; all eight accept the narrow sufficient denial ground.** The detailed later opinions are considered with Whitaker's estimate and concession of possibility, the disappearance, car, call and clothing evidence, and the appropriately limited use of other evidence. The affidavits substantively contest chronology; they are not dismissed merely because Whitaker's qualifications remain unchallenged. Their asserted certainty nevertheless is not a judicial finding. The combined showing does not establish the Schlup gateway or exceptional circumstances warranting this Court's original intervention on the separately asserted innocence claim. Denial at this discretionary threshold does not require a new universal merits burden, a finding that the affidavits are false, or a ruling that another forum is conclusively adequate. Neither the gateway nor new evidence alone establishes the underlying constitutional violation. No transfer, further return, evidentiary hearing, retrial, sentencing proceeding or release is ordered by these commitments.
