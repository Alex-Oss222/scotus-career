## Proposed formulation and audit result

O'Connor, Kennedy, Thomas and Ginsburg would preserve their own disagreement with the First Circuit's categorical section 102(b) exclusion in a plurality writing. **Result:** Each of the four X Justices can independently support the bounded vacatur/remand decree below, with her or his substantive X position retained and no substantive copyrightability join manufactured. This is a supported revision of the formal relief from reversal of the categorical ruling to vacatur of the appellate judgment; it is not a switch to E, a concession that the First Circuit's categorical reasoning is sound or adoption of a free-standing procedural reason supplied by an undisclosed Justice. The four remain judgment supporters on their own merits-error grounds. This audit establishes four non-Stone judgment votes only. The proposed decree may read in substance:

> The judgment of the Court of Appeals is vacated, and the case is remanded for further proceedings addressing Borland's properly preserved fair-use defenses and the remaining statutory issues, as necessary to resolve the claims concerning the Emulation Interface and the Key Reader. The judgment does not determine that the command hierarchy is copyrightable or that either use is fair, and it does not direct reinstatement of the district court's injunction or final liability judgment.

This is merits vacatur following review of the judgment, not a summary GVR based on an intervening event. The decree requires genuine further consideration of the preserved issues; it does not merely invite the same appellate judgment to be reissued without the required work. It does not impose a universal fair-use-first sequence or require decision of an unnecessary issue. No automatic new trial, evidentiary hearing or reopening of unpreserved matters follows. Ordinary appellate authority, the actual record and applicable burdens govern what further proceedings are needed. No ultimate copyrightability, infringement, fair-use, damages or injunction result follows from vacatur itself.
