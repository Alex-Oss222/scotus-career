## Law and source qualifications that govern every row

1. *FCC v. Pacifica Foundation*, 438 U.S.726 (1978), sustains the particular contextual broadcast ruling; it does not prescribe a universal schedule. Stevens wrote Parts I–III and IV-C for the five-Justice Court; only three joined IV-A and IV-B. Powell and Blackmun expressly refused the general lower-value-speech premise. Time, program, audience and medium matter; the disposition did not decide criminal prosecution, an isolated expletive or every work with serious value. *Sable*, 492 U.S.115, Part IV, unanimously protects nonobscene adult speech, distinguishes broadcast accessibility, requires careful tailoring, and retains independent constitutional review. Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia and Kennedy personally joined that portion. *Butler*, 352 U.S.380, prohibits reducing adults to children's material, without prescribing this broadcast boundary.
2. The standard **“Cable-carriage rules and review of legislative predictions,” present operation effective Turner, June 27, 1994**, governs its content-neutral cable subject. Its intermediate scrutiny is not the test here. Its general predictive-review proposition receives five direct agreements only at the limited level stated in the slice: Kennedy, Souter and the other two Part III-B joiners, plus Stevens's express concurrence. Stevens did not join the plurality's record-insufficiency application. Ordinary content-based review, with the medium's actual characteristics and *Pacifica*'s continuing limits, supplies the present inquiry. Protecting children and assisting parental supervision are substantial constitutional interests; recognizing them does not itself establish fit.
3. Audience statistics establish relevant access and risk, not universal actual exposure, causal harm from each program, effective supervision in each household, or interchangeable radio/television findings. Quarter-hour radio audience and cumulative reach remain distinct. The FCC considered alternatives; the question is their treatment's sufficiency, not an invented absence of agency analysis. The contextual definition recognizes clinical/instructional and bona fide news circumstances but has no blanket serious-merit exemption.
4. FCC paragraphs37–38 tentatively retain a defense based on no actual risk in the station's **market** at the time. Market-wide data showing no appreciable child audience may raise a viable defense. Station/program ratings are not accepted as sufficient pending convincing evidence of their relevance despite audience grazing. This is neither a ready-made exception for every low-child station nor an absolute bar to contrary proof. It qualifies the rule but does not supply an adjudicated defense here.
5. Enacted section551 is relevant statutory context, not a presently operational substitute or an adjudicated factual concession. The television-rating amendment takes effect one year after enactment only if the FCC, after the required consultation, determines that distributors have not established acceptable voluntary rating rules and agreed voluntarily to transmit ratings. Its rating authority does not permit political or religious ratings. Covered manufacturing requirements begin on an FCC-selected date no earlier than two years after February8,1996 and concern sets with screens at least13 inches diagonally. Carriers merely transporting receivers without trading in them are excepted. If the FCC finds alternative technology that blocks programs without ratings, is comparably priced, and blocks a broad range on a multichannel system as effectively and easily, its rules must permit either technology; no such finding is admitted. No implementation, household penetration, cost, comparative effectiveness or equivalent radio system is admitted. The statute does not repeal section16 or establish mootness. No row treats future equipment as a present least restrictive means.
6. The standard **“Presented questions, subsidiary arguments and exceptional Supreme Court review,” present operation effective Lebron, February21,1995**, retains *X-Citement Video*'s November29,1994 favorable-judgment boundary and the earlier *Izumi/Madsen* scope rules. A new midnight restraint, sanctions judgment, blanket obscenity ruling or universal definition ruling is not silently introduced through an alternative rationale.

### Precedent and remedy forks

**Distinguish or overrule Pacifica.** The strongest overruling account questions pervasive-access exceptionalism and government substitution for parents, as the lower Edwards dissent argues. The strongest answer is that the received decision was deliberately narrow, *Sable* preserved it, and current §551 does not establish disappearance of its factual premises. No Associate here commits to overruling *Pacifica* or *Red Lion*. The invalidating and remanding positions distinguish the present general schedule; the affirming positions apply the received contextual allowance. This preserves the constitutional fork without manufacturing support for its broadest branch.

**Severability and source of remaining FCC authority.** The original classification can be removed without restoring midnight, but extending10 p.m. treatment does not answer an independent First Amendment defect in the whole mandatory schedule. The four final-invalidity commitments would reverse the approval of that schedule and remand through the D.C. Circuit for relief removing the corresponding rule and any dependent section16 command as enforced here. They do not strike the entire1992 Act, invalidate §1464 in every application, judicially select a replacement hour or restore the repealed24-hour provision. §1464 remains subject to *Pacifica*, *Sable* and the resulting constitutional ruling. The agency may exercise independently lawful authority through lawful procedures; it cannot reimpose the same invalid restriction under a different label. Implementation of statutory severability is confined to the challenged command; no unexamined severability clause or new statutory exception is invented.

The three affirming commitments preserve the lower decree's uniform10 p.m. opening; none adjudicates a later sanction. Souter's vacatur is independently narrower in merits reach but is **not** a join in the final-invalidity remedy. No logical-subset or opinion-majority conclusion follows from adding those positions.
