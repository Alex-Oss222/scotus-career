## Source key and common issue boundaries

- **Ake:** *Ake v. Oklahoma*, 470 U.S. 68, 76–87, particularly 83–84; Court text in `../sources/A_VALIDATOR_Ake.txt`. The significant-sanity threshold and the capital future-danger branch have their respective predicates. Relevant assistance includes examination, evaluation, preparation, presentation, and assistance in answering the State's psychiatric case. There is no entitlement to a personally preferred psychiatrist or to funds for one's own selection. Stevens and O'Connor joined this opinion.
- **Zant:** *Zant v. Stephens*, 462 U.S. 862, 884–891 and notes 23–24; Court text in `../sources/A_VALIDATOR_Zant.txt`. Stevens authored; O'Connor joined. Its nonweighing conclusion concerned a defective narrowing label with underlying evidence properly before the jury, accurate and open to explanation, together with the identified state safeguards. It did not declare one surviving aggravator a cure for every independent constitutional defect.
- **Brecht:** [Prior public decision and separate positions](../sources/A_PRIOR_Brecht.md), April 21, 1993. Its controlling Chapman rule is the preserved-Doyle category. O'Connor's concurrence, Souter's joins, Kennedy's dissent joined by Scalia and Thomas, and Stevens's separate dissent supply the individual positions used below; none is silently substituted for the Court's actual rule.

The preserved personal-intent instructional/argument claim receives Chapman review under Parts II and IV. O'Connor and Souter supported Part III's broader proposal; Ginsburg and Breyer reserved it. Stevens, joined by Kennedy, supported demanding Kotteakos review with grave doubt preventing harmlessness. Scalia, joined by Thomas, required prisoner proof of substantial injurious influence. Neither separate approach is already the governing Ake rule.
- **Capital correction and procedure:** The relevant *Stringer*, *Richmond*, *Simmons*, *Romano*, *Schiro*, *Caspari*, and Standards selections in [the entering-law slice](../entering-law/OT_1995CHUNK1_CORRECTION.md), with the proposition-level limits stated in the fresh neutral packet.
