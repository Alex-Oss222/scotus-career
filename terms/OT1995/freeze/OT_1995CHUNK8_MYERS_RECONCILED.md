## Comparator level and concrete changed premise

**There is no historical Supreme Court vote or writing for this assigned Myers event.** The comparator is the February 1, 1995 Seventh Circuit affirmance. It supplies neither a historical certiorari poll nor individual Supreme Court commitments. No Associate can be described as changing a historical Myers Supreme Court vote.

The lower panel relied on the historical Eighth Circuit Pinson and Eleventh Circuit Ford. It treated Myers's failure to contain heat and roof ventilation as negating a subjective expectation, and independently treated escaped waste heat as objectively unprotected, by analogy to discarded garbage, visible smoke and drug scent. It emphasized the Agema 210's passive external measurement and lack of penetration. Its footnote reserved more advanced, materially intrusive equipment. It resolved neither an excised affidavit nor an alternative suppression ground.

**The concrete changed legal premise is the completed May 8, 1995 Pinson decision.** It rejects those physical-medium answers for a targeted technological examination whose demonstrated heat-pattern information reveals otherwise unobservable concealed domestic operation. Myers's March12 scan produced the reported pattern indicating indoor cultivation lights. Nothing establishes that ordinary observation disclosed that information. Roof vents do not convert the underlying domestic information into discarded garbage; lawful heat-producing activity defeats a contraband-only characterization. The absence of curbside garbage establishes an investigative inference, not exposure of the scanned interior activity. No new device capability, penetration, image, flight fact or finding of marijuana cultivation before entry is assumed.

The six Associates who joined Pinson's controlling application have a concrete, individual prior commitment to applying the displaced legal premise correctly. Kennedy and Breyer have a concrete published contrary position, but it remains a dissent from governing law. Their retained position is a proposal to reject Pinson's corresponding application, not a material distinction that permits a lower court to avoid it. The recent precedent and absence of an intervening undermining authority are the strongest objections to that proposal; neither is concealed by relabeling their approach an application of Pinson.

## Exact remedy and remaining source limits

The six-Associate disposition is to **vacate the Seventh Circuit's judgment insofar as it sustains denial of the preserved suppression motion and the dependent conviction affirmance on the categorical no-search ground, and remand for lawful disposition of that claim under Pinson**. The lower court must recognize the scan as a search unsupported by a warrant or an established exception on the supplied record. That resolves the constitutional premise actually used below; it does not itself decide admissibility of everything later seized.

Myers expressly argued that the later warrant was invalid because it used the scan. His conditional plea preserved review of the suppression denial. Pinson's separate appellate nonchallenge to affidavit sufficiency is therefore not transferred to Myers. This does not establish preservation of every newly formulated affidavit objection, remedy or defense. The actual claim and ordinary procedural rules govern each remaining ground.

The full affidavit, untainted probable-cause determination, independent-source facts, inevitable-discovery facts and Leon application are not supplied by the reviewed published opinion. Direct suppression cannot follow just from classifying the scan as unlawful; Karo Part IV preserves the distinct possibility that untainted information sustains a warrant. Conversely, the described purchase, electricity evidence, absence of garbage and later warrant issuance cannot be assembled into a new judicial finding of sufficient untainted probable cause or objective reliance. Neither final suppression nor final admission is supported on this record. Those gaps do not establish litigant default or absence of documents from the Court's file.

Leon is a potential ground only if independently supported and procedurally available. It does not protect knowing or reckless deception of the magistrate, abandonment of judicial neutrality, reliance on an affidavit so lacking probable-cause indicia that reliance is wholly unreasonable, or reliance on a facially deficient warrant precluding reasonable reliance. The inquiry includes officers who obtained or supplied information and assumes execution within the warrant's scope. No qualifying reliance finding, general police-technology exception or later-developed good-faith rule is adopted. Evans addresses qualifying non-law-enforcement court-employee clerical error and objectively reasonable reliance; it does not justify this deliberate police scan.

The mandate permits disposition of the actual preserved derivative-warrant contention and independently supported, procedurally available alternatives. It orders no new evidentiary hearing, discovery, automatic excision proceeding, new defense, reopening of waived issues, or specified finding on an absent affidavit. Ornelas supplies no contrary hearing entitlement and does not displace the distinct review of an issuing magistrate's warrant determination. Scalia's disagreement with Ornelas's ultimate application review supplies no barrier to correction of this legal rule.

Olano applies only if a genuinely forfeited additional issue invokes Rule 52(b): error must be plain and affect substantial rights, ordinarily through outcome prejudice, and discretionary correction separately requires serious effect on fairness, integrity or public reputation. Rule 11(a)(2)'s consequence if he ultimately prevails remains available according to its terms; this limited correction alone orders no plea withdrawal, acquittal, release, retrial or replacement sentence.
