## Remedy, source boundaries and writing functions

**The mandate is reversal and ordinary remand.** The state courts may not again deny the existing stay solely for the missing first-page notice. If no genuinely available, preserved independent ground defeats the stay, the rejected statutory objection cannot prevent restoration of the June 2, 1993 stay. The mandate does not unconditionally reinstate it or direct arbitration of every claim and person. This matches the historical remand's legal scope; it does not convert the comparator's narrative shorthand into additional findings.

**Footnote 3's two limits remain together.** The Montana majority relied on the statute and announced no independently sufficient general informed-consent, adhesion or reasonable-expectations rule. The present disposition therefore decides that statutory ground only. A proper general defense may still operate within actual preservation rules, but arbitration's uniqueness cannot itself supply the unconscionability or other invalidity ground. No cross-the-board conspicuousness rule is adopted, no genuine general defense is adjudicated, and no new hearing is ordered. Respondents' asserted *Chor* actual-knowledge exception remains unestablished; Paul's affidavit and contractual acknowledgment remain evidence, not new findings of knowledge, ignorance or unconscionability.

**Party and procedure limits survive without reopening settled matters.** Paul signed; the incomplete pleading record does not establish Pamela's entire position. Lombardi remains the alleged agent in the validated record, with his nonsignatory entitlement contested; no actual agency finding is added. Existing clause-scope findings remain undisturbed, but are not expanded into decisions of every distinct enforcement theory. The Hudson defendants and D&D Subway remain outside the stay and appeal. *First Options* preserves independent antecedent-assent determination, clear and unmistakable actual delegation, ordinary legal/factual review and its unaddressed nonsignatory theories; the AAA reference does not establish an adjudicated delegation here.

Section 3's separate statutory terms remain intact if invoked: “courts of the United States,” written agreement, referable issue, court satisfaction, party application, arbitration according to the agreement and absence of applicant default. The district's invocation supplies no wholesale state application of §§3–4, no default finding and no mandatory default hearing. The state courts implement §2 through lawful procedure. Neither the historical comparator nor this reconciliation decides every procedural mechanism.

The ruling concerns subsection (4) as applied to covered arbitration agreements, not all of §27-5-114. The independent freeze preserves subsection (2)'s specified personal-injury, $5,000-or-less acquisition, insurance/annuity and workers' compensation exclusions and subsection (3)'s trade/professional-organization qualification in their actual statutory locations; none is rewritten as an exception to subsection (4) or adjudicated here. No liability, damages, actual arbitral expense, award, constitutional jury-waiver rule or outer Commerce Clause boundary is decided. The agreement's forum, costs, severability and integration terms are not rewritten.

**Writing functions:** A common explanation can carry C0–C4 with the seven specified merits joins and all eight C0 joins separately recorded.
