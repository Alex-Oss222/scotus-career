There is one judgment component: whether the statutory interbank action can proceed in federal court and the resulting treatment of the Seventh Circuit's jurisdictional judgment. Its supporting propositions are separately identified below to keep statutory authorization, agency construction and remedy from being conflated.

1. **Statutory route.** A depository institution may bring in federal district court an action enforcing interbank liability established by the Board within §4010(f)'s delegation. Section 4010(d)'s authorization of any action under the section includes that action. Section 4010(a)'s exclusion of another depository institution withholds that subsection's customer remedy; it does not remove the distinct interbank liability arrangement from the same section's forum provision.
2. **Source and limits of authority.** This conclusion rests on the enacted relationship of subsections (a), (d) and (f), with §4008(c)'s payment-system responsibility providing context. A regulation or the Board's preference cannot create jurisdiction withheld by Congress. No rule of judicial deference concerning jurisdiction is needed to decide this case, and no general rule approving or forbidding such deference is adopted.
3. **Consequences for the appeal.** Reverse the Seventh Circuit's jurisdictional judgment and remand for consideration of properly remaining challenges to the district judgment. Do not affirm the bank's substantive liability, award damages anew, direct an injunction, or order an administrative damages proceeding. Recognition of the action does not decide negligence, causation, loss, bad faith, timeliness, a defense, or an unexamined state cause of action.

The §4010(a) individual/class additional damages and successful-action costs and reasonable attorney fees do not follow merely from recognizing the subsection (f) action. ## Enhanced alternative-path comparison

### Statutory authorization and the customer exclusion

**Path A — enforce the separate interbank regime.** Subsection (f) expressly authorizes the Board to impose or allocate loss and liability among depository institutions and limits that liability. Subsection (d) supplies a judicial forum for any action under the section. Reading those provisions together gives effect to interbank liability without extending subsection (a)'s expressly different customer remedy. The strongest objection is that subsection (f) is phrased as a rulemaking authorization and contains no separate sentence saying that an injured bank may sue; subsection (a), where Congress directly imposes liability, expressly excludes interbank plaintiffs. Touche Ross therefore demands more than pointing to a jurisdictional clause or an agency rule.

The answer is the combined enacted allocation: subsection (f) concerns the very interbank loss and liability absent from subsection (a), and subsection (d) refers to the whole section rather than only subsection (a). The action enforces liability Congress expressly empowered the Board to establish, through a forum Congress expressly supplied for the section. This construction does not infer a damages right from a reporting requirement, statutory beneficence, or agency regulation alone. The customer exclusion retains full work in its own remedial arrangement.

**Path B — treat subsection (a) as the only privately enforceable liability.** The strongest form preserves the express interbank exclusion and treats subsection (f) as regulatory allocation for administrative enforcement or otherwise available state remedies. Its principal authority is Touche Ross's refusal to let jurisdictional language and regulation supply absent substantive authorization. Its principal obstacle is that it narrows subsection (d) to one subsection without a corresponding textual restriction and discounts subsection (f)'s express interbank liability language. Section 4009(c)(1)'s residual administrative enforcement command does not expressly establish exclusive adjudication of one bank's damages against another. The Board reported that no such administrative mechanism existed. That absence alone cannot create a remedy, but it also cannot substantiate the lower court's proposed exclusive administrative route. Transamerica supplies an additional boundary: legal consequences can be supplied by the actual statutory structure without an explicit private-action sentence, and an exclusive state-forum premise needs support.

All eight Associates adopt Path A at the stated level of generality for the Justice-specific reasons below. They reject Path B's construction of this statute, not the congressional-authorization requirement on which it relies. A hypothetical rejection of the private action would also require distinguishing failure to state a claim from subject-matter jurisdiction, as Transamerica's footnote 4 illustrates. Because none selects that rejection here, no additional §1331 ruling or unsupported alternative decree is made.

### Agency role and remedial reach

**Textually sufficient route.** The Court independently determines that the statutory arrangement permits judicial enforcement. The Board's account of its available processes explains why the proposed administrative route lacks the factual support asserted below; it is not jurisdictional law. NationsBank and Town & Country require independent statutory boundaries before permissible agency construction and remain intact.

**Additional deference route.** An argument for respecting the Board's reading within delegated payment-system authority is supported by those cases' treatment of genuine delegations. Its strongest objection is that Congress, not an agency, supplies the federal forum, and those precedents did not adjudicate agency power to expand jurisdiction. The independent textual ground resolves this case without answering that broader dispute. Each Associate accepts the textually sufficient route and leaves the general deference issue open; none conditions the result on deference.

**Bounded remand versus immediate merits disposition.** Recognition of the forum removes the only ground the Seventh Circuit decided. The strongest argument for reinstating the district award outright is that it already rests on summary judgment. That does not establish that all properly remaining appellate challenges fail; the reviewing court never reached them. Each Associate therefore supports remand to that court, without a new negligence finding or unconditional damages award. Zicherman reinforces respect for the particular remedial regime; it does not supply the EFAA cause of action or resolve the bank's liability.

## Enhanced booking-statement comparison

### Common facts, governing distinctions, and available paths

Custody is established by arrest, handcuffing and station booking. Stansbury's objective custody rule is satisfied; the case is not resolved by an officer's uncommunicated view of suspect status. The officer asked what was the matter with an observed limping leg. The report does not identify that questioner as Rhodes, attribute Rhodes's crash knowledge to the questioner, describe a question asking how the injury happened, or establish investigative follow-up. The District Court found an actual booking purpose of identifying a need for medical attention, and that finding survived clear-error review. Those facts are accepted on both paths.

The response that the injury occurred in an accident is a factual assertion, and therefore testimonial, even though the physical limp itself is not. Medical purpose is relevant context, not a conclusive answer to constitutional admissibility. Rhode Island v. Innis, 446 U.S. 291, 300–03 and nn.5–9, includes express questioning as well as its functional equivalent; the latter inquiry concerns reasonably foreseeable incriminating responses, not just subjective intent. Pennsylvania v. Muniz, 496 U.S. 582, 600–02 and n.14, Part III-C, is a four-Justice administrative-booking rationale, not an existing majority holding creating a blanket medical exception. Its Court portions distinguish physical evidence and limited communications attendant to legitimate procedures. No waived right, volunteered accident narrative, medical emergency or public-safety necessity is found here.

**Path sustaining admission:** Recognize and apply the narrow administrative-booking ground to a question reasonably related to assessing necessary medical care for an observable condition, where its actual content and setting do not show an investigative demand or an objectively reasonably likely means of extracting incriminating offense information. Muniz's administrative rationale and its limited-procedure holding support this bounded application. The short condition inquiry and credited medical setting satisfy it; the later usefulness of the responsive answer does not alone defeat it. This path recognizes that an administrative question can call for testimonial content; it does not pretend the answer becomes physical evidence. The statement may be used at this trial without a harmless-error ruling.

The strongest objection is that this was express custodial questioning eliciting a factual response, rather than a biographical entry or an instruction-understanding check, and that an injury explanation could connect Stokes to the crash. The sustaining path answers with the actual question's condition-focused content, the observed limp and the accepted intake context, not with an assumption of ignorance, pretextlessness, or subjective good faith alone. The reported facts support that restricted administrative classification. This is a case-specific doctrinal application and modest extension of the Muniz plurality's rationale, not a claim that its medical extension was already a majority holding.

**Path finding error:** Treat the question as express interrogation that calls for Stokes's substantive account of his condition, with the accident statement a responsive factual assertion. The genuine need to ask about health does not by itself authorize later criminal use of the unwarned answer. Muniz's controlling physical and procedural-communication portions do not cover that substantive assertion, and a Justice need not adopt or extend its four-vote booking exception. This path finds an identified legal error in permitting evidentiary use on a medical-purpose exception; it does not negate the actual medical-purpose finding or infer knowledge of the crash.

## 2. Component commitments and operative grounds

The following propositions state the grounds actually supportable for commitment; they are not a final opinion or assignment.

### A. Section 922(o): enumerated power

**Commitment of each Associate:** Reject the presented Commerce Clause challenge to the thirteen §922(o) counts. This is agreement on constitutional power, not agreement that every application survives the Second Amendment.

**Sufficient rule and application:** Congress may control possession as an integral part of the enacted prohibition on additions to, and transfers within, the private machinegun supply market. Section 922(o) directly regulates the traded articles and their transfer and possession; its government exception and preservation of the existing lawful stock define the remaining permitted channels. Permitting unregulated creation and possession of additional private stock while forbidding its ordinary transfer would leave the regulated supply outside the same control. That direct relationship to the regulated machinegun market distinguishes the unrelated school-location restriction in Lopez. The conclusion rests on the statute's actual transfer-and-possession design, not on a chain from guns to crime, drugs, productivity or national prosperity.

**Decisive authority:** Lopez, Harris, American Life League and Bishop/Stokes at their stated scopes; the retained economic-class principles of Wickard and Perez. American Life League confirms that the absence of an actor-specific crossing element is not invariably fatal. Harris's enacted vehicle-history condition does not become an unwritten element in §922(o).

**Alternative tested:** Reverse these counts because Hale's immediate possession is local and noncommercial. Its strongest point is that describing an object as traded cannot federalize every local crime involving it. The adopted ground answers that objection with the same subsection's direct transfer restriction and control of the market's available stock, not the object's remote history. No universal “anything marketable” rule, general police power, new nexus element or finding of a particular interstate transfer is accepted. Every Associate's prior direct join in American Life League and the Harris/Bishop economic-class ground supports this bounded distinction; O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy and Thomas also retain their actual Lopez joins.

### B. Section 5861(d): independent constitutional authority

**Commitment of each Associate:** Reject the presented enumerated-power objection to the three registration convictions on the independent taxing-and-necessary-administration ground. Do not use a general power to maintain public order.

**Sufficient rule and application:** Congress may enforce registration of firearms within a genuine making-and-transfer tax system as an aid to assessment, collection and tracing taxable transactions. Prohibiting possession of a covered firearm unregistered to the possessor supports that system by preventing its record requirements from being bypassed after making or transfer. A separate transaction-specific interstate element is unnecessary to that tax ground. Sonzinsky sustains a genuine NFA tax and registration in aid of revenue despite regulatory effects; Miller rejects the general police-power objection to the then-present NFA provisions. Freed explains the revised lawful-transfer and registration structure, while Staples preserves its registration law and requires the appropriate factual knowledge for its particular rifle category.

The strongest contrary path concerns prosecution for nonregistration where the very weapon cannot lawfully be approved or taxed. The supplied record does not establish that premise for any of the three counts. No Associate adopts a holding either validating or invalidating that hypothesized application, nor orders an exploratory remand to create it. ### C. Section 922(o): personal arms guarantee and appropriate appellate disposition

**Commitment of each Associate:** Vacate the appellate judgment insofar as it affirms the thirteen §922(o) convictions against the Second Amendment challenge, and remand that component for application of Love to the actual weapons, conduct and prohibition. Reject Hale's asserted automatic immunity based solely on military utility. Do not uphold the prohibition through the removed affiliation prerequisite or decide its ultimate constitutional application on a new unsupported classification.

**Sufficient rule:** A federal possession conviction challenged under the personal arms guarantee cannot be sustained merely because its possessor did not prove membership in, service to, or preparation for an organized militia. Conversely, military utility alone establishes neither an unrestricted immunity from regulation nor the invalidity of a particular possession prohibition. Where the lower court's application depends materially on that personal-affiliation prerequisite and supplies no adequate independent application of the surviving weapon-and-regulation inquiry, its judgment must be reconsidered under the governing personal-right rule.

**Concrete remand:** The lower court must determine the actual §5845(b) basis and object for each affected count and decide the preserved arms challenge under Love and Miller, keeping constitutional coverage distinct from permissible regulation. It must account for the admitted military character rather than treating lack of formal membership as dispositive. The restrictions' actual exceptions and application remain relevant. The remand does not presume that automatic arms or their regulated parts are protected, that they are excluded, or that a protected arm is immune from control. It requires no invented civilian-use survey, mandatory scrutiny tier or new categorical evidentiary burden. It permits lawful decision on the existing record or ordinary further proceedings as that record and the governing inquiry require.

**Why affirmance is not independently adopted:** A rule permitting a categorical prohibition on the particular automatic arms is a legally available competing path, not a result already dictated by Miller, Love, or the statutory label. The serious capacity for group harm supports regulation, but neither this observation nor grandfathering alone supplies a complete constitutional distinction between permitted regulation and destruction of protected keeping. Miller treated the relationship of the actual weapon to militia function as relevant and remanded the dismissal before it; it did not exclude weapons because they have military utility. Love's original evidence recognizes both private keeping and regulation. It does not decide this prohibition. Rejecting absolute immunity therefore does not exhaust the preserved claim that these arms fall within the guarantee. An independent merits affirmance here would require an additional scope or regulatory rule and an adequate account of its fit, not merely the lower opinion's conclusion. The present source set supports correcting the identified legal barrier and returning that actual application more strongly than adopting such a new categorical rule.

**Why outright reversal is not adopted:** Military suitability is not itself the complete constitutional inquiry; neither Love nor Miller creates immunity for all military-capable objects. Count/object, statutory-branch, date and exception details are not supplied. The Court cannot adjudge all thirteen applications unconstitutional or order acquittals from that premise. The underlying registration record would exist independently of prosecution if the legally recorded event occurred. The certificates concern a search and nonentry; they do not purport to reconstruct possession, classify weapons by expert analysis, narrate observed criminal acts, or decide the jury's ultimate element. Their preparation for this prosecution is a reason to examine the function of the certification, not a reason under existing law automatically to displace a conceded rooted method of proving absent public entries. Calling the custodian could test the search, but no particular faulty search, altered record, or pertinent contemporary omission is established by the reported objection. This is not a finding that questioning could never help, that government records are infallible, or that every formal assertion satisfies the Constitution.

**Strongest contrary path:** Treat these prepared affidavits as replaceable assertions offered to establish an offense element, require live testimony or a justified substitute, and remand any affected registration count for appropriate constitutional error-effect review. Unlike conspiracy, spontaneous and treatment declarations, the searcher's report can be given at trial without losing its original contextual value. The common admission position does not deny this distinction: it rests instead on the accepted longstanding public-record exception and the limited search/nonentry function, within the received framework. Wright distinguishes an ad hoc residual exception from a rooted one. Coy concerns altered presentation of an actual trial witness and preserves the relevance of established exceptions. Neither those cases nor White already decides that every prepared public certification needs the former-testimony gateway. The record establishes no additional basis for rejecting this conceded exception in this application. No new general test of testimonial hearsay is adopted.

**Thomas and Scalia's exact boundary:** Their published White concurrence is relevant because these are signed formal materials. It does not commit them to rejecting every such document, and it joined the existing exception analysis. Both accept the bounded disposition under that governing analysis and the conceded predicates. Neither joins a statement that formalized prosecutorial affidavits categorically fall outside confrontation, that Rule 803 automatically decides constitutionality, or that their White reservation has been rejected anew. They leave possible reconsideration of the Clause's application to formalized accusations open. This reservation supplies neither an additional ground nor a competing controlling test; a separate concurrence is optional only if the final text otherwise obscures it.

**Preserved limits:** Authentication alone is not constitutional reliability. No finding determines whether the actual certificates proved every legally relevant registration route; the shorthand “application by Hale” does not establish a new preserved wording or sufficiency defect. The Government retains the burden of proving every statutory element to the jury. The admission holding supplies neither an irrebuttable nonregistration presumption nor a finding of sufficiency or harmlessness.

### F. The 1979 Senate excerpts: admission and judicial notice

**Commitment of each Associate:** Sustain the separately reported exclusion and refusal of judicial notice. The limited record identifies no demonstrated abuse of discretion or constitutional defense-presentation error; do not invent an error and then declare it harmless.

**Grounds:** A congressional hearing's existence does not render the truth of its disputed assertions an indisputable adjudicative fact under Rule 201. Hale sought to use the excerpts to establish unreliability; neither their governmental setting nor their age makes their contents judicially conclusive. Ordinary admission is a separate question: the reported offer relied on older out-of-court criticism, with no supplied exception, relevant nonhearsay use, passage-specific foundation, or demonstrated application to the registry searched here. The actual hearsay and relevance ruling is therefore sustained within its adjudicated scope. Age alone is not a categorical exclusion rule, and a properly founded reliability challenge remains legally available.

**Alternative tested:** Vacate exclusion because testing the registry could expose error in the negative certificates. That purpose is important, but the possibility of useful criticism does not authenticate the missing offer, supply a hearsay exception, show its bearing on these records, or require notice of disputed truth. Bishop/Stokes's recent eight-Associate ruling on the separately reported video objection reinforces the distinction between absent detail in an appellate account and affirmative proof that the trial court committed an evidentiary error. Its medical and physical-evidence doctrines are not imported. No freestanding right to introduce all government criticism, no prohibition of effective rebuttal, and no new error-effect rule is adopted.
