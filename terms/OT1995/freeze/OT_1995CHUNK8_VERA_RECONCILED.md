## Final shared rule and claimant-specific application

All eight Associates retain the independent commitments. They exercise the supplied direct review in all three dockets, resolve the Article III question in favor of the five personally assigned residents, recognize Chen's distinct limit, and retain the appeals without adjudicating the excluded constitutional merits.

Actual Miller Part I-A separates an established personal relationship to challenged governmental assignment from success on the claimed legal entitlement. A voter personally subjected to the challenged assignment may litigate the asserted unequal treatment when exposure, causation and redressability are established. Residence alone, statewide concern or dislike of racial consideration does not suffice. Nor does standing require proof of a changed election winner, a lost contract, a particular replacement district or ultimate substantive success.
