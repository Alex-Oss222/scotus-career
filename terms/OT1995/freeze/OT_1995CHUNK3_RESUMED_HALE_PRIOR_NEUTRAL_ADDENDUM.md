# Previously verified neutral source material

## Hale

[Complete authentic Eighth Circuit text, 978 F.2d 1016](https://static.case.law/f2d/978/html/1016-01.html) establishes thirteen §922(o) convictions and three §5861(d) convictions. The material objections are constitutional power, the asserted military-utility protection, the two ATF negative-record certifications, and exclusion/judicial notice of the 1979 Senate material. Counsel conceded the hearsay exception's firmly rooted character and authentication under Rule 902(1); these concessions are not a finding of general waiver of the constitutional issue. No preserved Staples instruction defect or count-specific registration refusal is established by this source.

The lower opinion inaccurately says Miller upheld a conviction. [Miller's actual Supreme Court text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/307/174) reversed the district court's dismissal on demurrer and remanded. Use its actual holding; do not carry the lower court's procedural misdescription forward. The individual/collective characterization remains distinct from Hale's argument that military utility automatically defeats regulation.
