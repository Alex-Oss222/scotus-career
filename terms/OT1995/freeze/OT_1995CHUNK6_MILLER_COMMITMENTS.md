## Controlling law and application common to the comparison

### The actual rule, rather than a substitute facial formula

The governing Standards entry is **“Protected choice and the six-step standard for previability regulation,” present operation effective Casey, June 29, 1992**. Casey's five direct joins establish the whole rule. Fargo, February 13, 1995, Part I, applies that complete rule without an independent Salerno no-valid-applications barrier or numerical facial threshold. Kennedy's Casey spousal-notice join does not constitute a join to the complete framework. His and Scalia and Thomas's contrary public positions remain contrary positions, not alternate governing rules for the lower courts.

**“One-parent informed consent with an independent judicial bypass,” present operation effective Casey, June 29, 1992**, sustains Pennsylvania's actual consent structure. Maturity and capacity with actual informed consent, or alternatively best interests, authorize a confidential and expedited independent route without first requesting parental consent. Its three-business-day trial deadline, five-business-day appeal deadline, counsel and family-substitution details were sustained; they were not all declared indispensable in every notice law. **“Spousal notification that gives practical control over protected choice,” present operation effective Casey, June 29, 1992**, rejects formal notice as a conclusive answer to proved practical control, while leaving minors' distinct parental-welfare setting intact. Fargo's emergency-definition ruling concerns the specified vagueness claim and supplies no blanket approval of narrow substantive health protection.

Dalton, March 18, 1996, requires effective relief against the proved violation while preserving severable lawful applications. It expressly permits broader relief for an established defect in an indivisible condition and creates no new facial-review formula. Its funding holding answers no parental-notice question.

The relevant application of all six steps is as follows. The five Associates supporting invalidation adopt this application; the contrary commitments identify the points they dispute.

1. **Duty and baseline.** Section 34-23A-7 prohibits abortion until at least 48 hours after written parental notice is delivered personally by the physician or an agent, or by certified mail with return receipt and restricted delivery. Mail is deemed delivered at noon on the next day of regular delivery after mailing; this is not simply 48 hours from mailing. For the minors' question the starting population is unemancipated minors seeking protected previability abortion subject to this command. The comparison removes this notice condition and its interval, retaining other law and actual family, provider and access circumstances. The protected baseline forbids an effective third-party veto, but does not abolish permissible parental involvement in minors' care.

   The actual exceptions matter. A physician may certify, in good-faith clinical judgment, an immediate need to avert death or a delay-created serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function, **with insufficient time to provide notice**. A written certification by the person entitled to notice acknowledges actual notice. A minor's declaration or information indicating abuse or neglect, followed by the physician's report, activates the abuse route; recipient authorities must protect abortion confidentiality. None supplies an independent maturity or best-interests determination. The abuse definition reaches threatened substantial harm, injurious environments and substantial emotional or mental impairment as well as completed physical abuse. The analysis does not construe it as confined to an earlier assault.

2. **Function and purpose.** Parental advice, medical history, practical assistance, informed consideration and protection of minors are permissible interests. The defect does not rest on a finding that legislative opposition to abortion proves obstructive purpose. No independent purpose holding is necessary. The added disclosure and interval must nonetheless serve these interests within the retained protected choice.

3. **Causation and population.** Within the statutory population, identify before assessing injury the minors who would not voluntarily disclose to a parent and who lack an actually usable exception. Examine the record-supported circumstances of mature minors, minors in previously nonabusive homes, and minors reluctant to initiate abuse reporting separately rather than defining a group as everyone whose constitutional claim succeeds. Neither every minor nor every unwilling discloser is assumed to encounter the same burden. The honor-student episode, reported parental obstruction, evidence of new abuse following disclosure, reluctance to report, delay and the supported confidentiality concerns establish concrete additional interference for relevant applications. Existing parental hostility alone is not state action; the additional compulsory disclosure and opportunity for resulting interference supply the causal connection. The 18% single-parent-household figure rebuts an assumption of a universally safe second parent; it is not an injury rate or facial threshold. The court-recited predictions are evidence, not findings that every threatened event is inevitable.

4. **Substantive protections in sequence.** For minors exposed by mandatory disclosure to demonstrated practical prevention or serious obstruction of timely care, the effective-control or substantial-obstacle prohibition is sufficient; an important parental interest cannot justify that obstruction. For mature or other minors whose added delay, forced disclosure or coercive consequences cause meaningful interference without an established obstacle, the inquiry continues. The State must substantiate the fit of requiring disclosure in those circumstances and answer the concrete independent-authorization alternative. An effective route assessing maturity and informed decisionmaking, or best interests, preserves justified parental involvement for other minors while protecting the situations where compulsion frustrates the interest asserted for it. The record does not provide an adequate answer to that alternative.

   The residual severe-interference branch remains distinct: a proved serious intrusion into health, clinical judgment or protected confidentiality that does not already amount to an obstacle requires compelling justification and narrow tailoring. No commitment below treats every parental disclosure as such an intrusion or uses that branch to justify an established obstacle. The proposed invalidation is sufficiently supported by the obstacle findings and the separate failure of justification and fit for demonstrated remaining meaningful interference; it does not require a new categorical severe-confidentiality holding. Meaningful interference alone would not suffice without the State's inadequate answer to the identified alternative.

5. **Clinical and communication safeguards.** A clinical emergency exception with an insufficient-time condition does not answer the nonemergency mature-minor and best-interests problem. The abuse route has a real confidentiality command, but its reporting trigger and the supported possibilities of disclosure through juvenile proceedings affect practical usability. No inevitable breach or particular completed disclosure is found. The separate general information provision's 24-hour requirement is not a new waiting period inserted into §7's abuse exception. This case need not resolve an independent emergency-health claim, general culpability rule, compulsory-information claim or medical-immunity question.

6. **Relief.** The defect is the mandatory previability minor-notice condition without a usable independent route, not the whole abortion act or every possible parental-notice arrangement. Its operative text supplies no maturity or best-interests exception capable of separate enforcement. A decree merely stating that mature minors are exempt would not supply a usable method of obtaining that protection; preserving enforcement by assuming such a method would invent the missing safeguard. On this supported record, the challenged compulsory condition may therefore be enjoined in its previability minor operation without proof that every minor is injured. Voluntary consultation, actual notice and ordinary informed consent remain possible. Broader minor relief rests on the indivisible condition and its demonstrated unjustified operation, not the bare existence of an injured individual.

   The supporting commitments would affirm the invalidation of that minor-notice condition and return the case for conformance of declaratory and injunctive relief to this scope. The remand is for implementation, not a new merits trial or proof assignment. It must protect providers and affected minors against enforcement of the invalid notice prerequisite and dependencies resting solely on its violation, while preserving the separate lower dispositions of the information and penalty questions. It supplies no new ruling on those penalties, report fields not established in this packet, or independently lawful requirements. It does not direct enactment of a particular tribunal, procedure or set of deadlines; the State may abandon the condition or provide a constitutionally adequate independent route. Adult-incapacity and unadjudicated postviability applications receive no merits determination. Any decree language reaching those distinct applications must be confined to the adjudicated minor violation rather than treated as constitutionally invalid by association. There is no new approval of those other applications and no automatic revival of enforcement barred on another unreviewed ground.

## Justice-specific commitments and alternative-path comparison

The result descriptions below concern only each Associate's position. Identical positions do not establish an assembled Court disposition.

### Justice Stevens

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; apply the full Casey/Fargo method; affirm invalidation of the compulsory previability minor-notice condition without an effective maturity or best-interests route; remand for the bounded decree conformance stated above. Join the obstacle/effective-control application, the independently sufficient remaining-meaningful-interference justification-and-fit ground, and the explanation of the indivisible condition. Do not declare every parental-notice law equivalent to consent.

**Authority and operative level.** His [public Casey concurrence and joins](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) require a supported connection between added burdens and permissible purposes, recognizing that access surviving for some patients does not answer avoidable barriers for others. His own Hodgson discussion, 497 U.S. 417, 450–58 (1990), and Akron dissent, 497 U.S. 502, 520–23 (1990), as supported in the frozen packet, give particular force to actual family circumstances and an adequate independent mature/best-interests mechanism. His Fargo Part I join and unanimous Dalton join supply current method and remedial limits.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** Sustaining notice would recognize legitimate parental guidance, the legal absence of a consent veto, and the broad abuse exception. The strongest objection to invalidation is that this turns a consent safeguard into an automatic notice requirement despite Matheson's permissible notice application. Stevens does not adopt that automatic equation: the supplied evidence of practical control and unsupported compulsion supplies the additional premise. Conversely, the strongest objection to sustention is that notice can create the obstruction even without an express legal power to refuse consent, and neither actual-notice certification nor a report of abuse answers a mature, nonabused minor's situation. He finds the record adequate under Casey without another merits remand.

**Join barriers and remedy.** No join to a universal numerical fraction, automatic strict scrutiny of every notice duty, a finding of obstructive legislative purpose on this record, or a decree extending to adult guardians or the whole act. He can join the limited affirmative requirement of a usable independent route for this compulsory structure, while declining to constitutionalize Pennsylvania's every deadline and counsel detail. A separate writing would be useful only if the assembled rationale omits this justification-and-fit ground or proposes broader relief.

### Justice O'Connor

**Commitment.** Reach the merits and join the full Casey/Fargo analysis and invalidation, with the same implementation remand. The record establishes substantial practical obstruction for supported compelled-disclosure applications, and the remaining concrete interference requires a substantiated answer to the usable independent-authorization alternative. Neither universal injury nor an additional numerical threshold is required.

**Authority and operative level.** Her [Casey concurrence](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) insists on determining the burden independently before considering the asserted benefit, preserves the obstacle bar and distinguishes residual scrutiny from automatic strict scrutiny of an inconvenience. Hodgson, 497 U.S. at 458–61, supplies her attention to actual family circumstances and a functioning bypass. Her Akron join sustained an available bypass, without answering its universal necessity. Her Fargo Part I and Dalton joins reinforce record-specific application and matched relief.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** A reasonable one-parent-notice law may serve important welfare interests, and the abuse definition reaches threatened harm. The best argument for sustention is that these exceptions could protect the dangerous cases while ordinary parental advice remains beneficial. The record nevertheless identifies minors outside an actually usable reporting route and explains the reporting and confidentiality problem with specificity. A statutory promise of confidentiality must be credited, but is not conclusive proof of a usable substitute for independent authorization. Her strongest objection to a categorical invalidation rationale would be its failure to distinguish one-parent notice from two-parent notice and consent. She therefore joins the supported functional conclusion and full Casey application, not a categorical transfer of Bellotti's entire procedure.

**Join barriers and remedy.** No speculative additional visit, automatic 48-hour-per-se rule, assumption that all parental opposition equals abuse, or elimination of the challengers' initial burden. She rejects a remand merely because the lower court described the wrong facial formula: the existing record supports resolution under the correct one. She accepts decree conformance and protection against the indivisible compulsory condition, but not an invented judicial or physician bypass, a mandatory new hearing, or adjudication of the adult-guardian clause.

### Justice Scalia

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; reverse the facial invalidation of the minor-notice requirement and remand to conform the decree on that component, leaving the unrelated dispositions untouched. Retain his objection to Casey's heightened constitutional framework and apply the reasonable-regulation position already publicly taken. The one-parent notice duty, finite interval and actual statutory exceptions reasonably serve parental responsibility and minors' welfare; the Federal Constitution does not require this State to add the asserted maturity or best-interests route on his view.

**Authority and operative level.** His [Casey joins in White's separate opinion](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) reject the constitutional foundation and the complete scrutiny sequence, while sustaining the actual parental structure and reasonable participation requirements. His [Fargo separate opinion](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md), joined by Thomas, preserves that objection rather than joining an access remand under the majority framework. His pre-divergence joins in Kennedy's Hodgson opinion, 497 U.S. at 461–501, and Akron opinion, 497 U.S. at 510–13, support substantial room for parental involvement and distinguish approval of an available bypass from requiring one in every notice law. Dalton supplies the scope limit on any decree, not agreement that a violation exists here.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** The strongest case for invalidation is the complete controlling Casey rule together with concrete evidence that compelled disclosure can produce practical prevention, with no independent authorization route. Scalia does not answer that ground by pretending Casey contains only a large-fraction obstacle test or by declaring a missed source to be a failure of proof. His disagreement remains constitutional: parental involvement is reasonably regulated even when some minors would prefer secrecy and some parents act wrongfully, and the stated exceptions address emergencies and threatened abuse without a mandatory judicial substitute. The strongest objection to his sustention is precisely that foreseeable additional coercion can qualify as a state-caused constitutional burden under law he has rejected. He maintains that disagreement; it is not converted into a purported application of the majority's full framework.

**Join barriers and remedy.** Cannot join the protected-choice framework, compulsory-bypass entitlement, residual justification burden or notice invalidation. Approval of Casey's existing bypass establishes permissibility, not indispensability. He can join accurate statutory descriptions, the question limits and the prohibition on using the minor judgment to invalidate adult-incapacity or unrelated provisions. He does not commit to a vacatur merely to apply Casey below; his Fargo position supports reversal on his contrary sufficient ground. No wider constitutional question about every emergency or particular wrongful enforcement is resolved.

### Justice Kennedy

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; reverse the categorical facial invalidation of this minor-notice condition and remand for corresponding decree conformance. Maintain his Casey/Fargo reasonable-regulation approach rather than join the complete residual-interference framework. On this record he does not require an independent maturity or best-interests procedure as a condition of this notice-only law's operation. Preserve the possibility of relief for an established practical veto or concrete denial of necessary care in a supported application; do not treat reversal as approval of such conduct.

**Authority and operative level.** His own Hodgson opinion, 497 U.S. at 461–501, supports parental notice without converting its absence of a bypass into a conclusive defect. His Akron opinion, 497 U.S. at 510–13, expressly leaves universal notice-bypass necessity unresolved. His [public Casey separate opinion](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) retains protected choice and joins the spousal-notice practical-control holding, but rejects the general duty to answer less burdensome alternatives for every meaningful lesser burden. His [Fargo separate opinion](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md) sustains reasonable access regulation on its actual terms while acknowledging the majority framework governs lower courts. His Dalton join requires a decree corresponding to the actual established violation.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** The strongest contrary path applies his own effective-control principle to the honor-student incident, feared new abuse, and reporting limitations, yielding a targeted independent route even though consent is not formally required. Those facts are material, not erased by the word notice. He nevertheless distinguishes a spouse's authority over another adult from the State's permissible reliance on a parent's responsibility for a minor. The broad threatened-harm and emotional-injury terms and the express confidentiality duty give the exceptions more reach than a completed-assault-only account would allow. The supplied predictions and individual episodes do not, for him, make the whole one-parent notification structure an effective veto or show that all mature minors must be exempt from notification. That conclusion does not deny that a proved unprotected instance of practical prevention could cross his retained baseline.

**Join barriers and narrower alternative.** His opposition to a required general bypass is not a join to Salerno as a rule displacing controlling Casey/Fargo, nor a denial that the actual governing law includes residual scrutiny. He cannot join invalidation merely for failure to answer a generally less burdensome authorization alternative, a conclusion that all parental notification transfers decisional control, or a categorical severe-confidentiality rule. A narrower judgment protecting a separately established application where notice creates an effective veto outside a usable exception is within his retained Casey position; he does not find that proposition supports the facial decree offered here. Any such narrower route must identify the protected application and effective relief, rather than announce an unenforceable paper exception. He does not commit to joining the five invalidating Associates' facial remedy through that reservation.

### Justice Souter

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; join the complete Casey/Fargo application, affirm invalidation of the compulsory previability minor-notice condition, and remand for the bounded remedial implementation. Both the practical-obstruction application and the inadequately justified remaining meaningful interference support the result at the scope stated.

**Authority and operative level.** His [Casey concurrence and parental-content opinion](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) separate actual commands from assumed administration and permissible information from independent burdens. His [Fargo Part I](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md) requires the full inquiry, supported causal burdens, stable affected classes and corresponding relief. His Dalton join rejects overbroad decrees while preserving adequate protection. Those sources support applying the entire rule here without inventing an automatic equivalence between consent and notice.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** A framework-only vacatur and remand is a genuine alternative: Fargo used it where application and the State's responsive justification required renewed assessment. The strongest objection to immediate invalidation is that the lower court used the historical large-fraction description and the statute's abuse route is broader than a simple past-abuse exception. Here, however, the published record identifies the operative absence of independent authorization and material applications the existing routes do not effectively reach. The legal defect and concrete alternative can be resolved without treating unrecovered declarations as nonexistent evidence. Unlike Fargo's unresolved access assessment, another merits remand would not identify a necessary factual or remedial finding left unanswered by the record used for this limited conclusion.

**Join barriers and remedy.** He requires the explanation of why current exceptions do not do the work, why the reporting concerns are supported predictions rather than assumed breaches, and why the operative condition is indivisible. He cannot join a new numerical formula, automatic invalidation from one burdened patient, or a broader constitutional holding based on the mere presence of a guardianship clause. He can join the bounded independent-route requirement without prescribing a court system or equating judicial authorization with official approval of an adult patient's reasons. The implementation remand does not reopen the information and penalty dispositions.

### Justice Thomas

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; reverse the facial minor-notice invalidation and conform the decree on that component. He independently retains his constitutional objection to the heightened abortion framework and regards this parental-notice regulation, including its real exceptions, as reasonably related to the State's legitimate interests. No federal constitutional command to enact the proposed bypass follows on his ground.

**Authority and operative level.** His [Casey joins in White's separate opinion and in the actual parental-consent holding](../../OT1991/output/OT_1991CHUNK14.md) distinguish opposition to the asserted constitutional right from agreement that an enacted bypass structure is permissible. His join in [Scalia's Fargo separate opinion](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md) specifically preserves the constitutional objection and opposes access vacatur under the majority rule. His unanimous Dalton join confines relief to established invalidity. No pre-appointment Supreme Court join is attributed to him.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** The strongest invalidation path is supported prospective interference under the binding six-step framework, including practical obstruction despite the absence of a legal consent veto. His contrary conclusion does not rely on ignorance of that evidence, a fictitious lack of declarations, or an independent numerical facial bar. He rejects the constitutional premise that requires the State to justify these notice burdens at that level and regards the emergency and abuse provisions as reasonable accommodations within legislative regulation. The strongest objection to his position is that parental wrongdoing made foreseeable by state-compelled disclosure violates the majority's retained practical-choice protection; his existing constitutional disagreement answers that objection for his own vote but cannot authorize a lower court to disregard Casey.

**Join barriers and remedy.** He will not join an independent maturity/best-interests entitlement, the six-step access test, or facial invalidation on its residual branches. Sustaining the actual Pennsylvania bypass does not make it mandatory here. He can join the limited review scope and the refusal to invalidate unrelated provisions or adult-incapacity applications through the minor question. His position neither creates a universal scienter rule nor resolves every hypothetical life-or-health application. His Fargo commitment supplies no basis to manufacture a conditional join to an access remand.

### Justice Ginsburg

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; join the full Casey/Fargo analysis and the limited facial invalidation, with decree conformance. Compelled disclosure can undermine the protected decision through practical control; the absence of a usable maturity or best-interests route is material to both serious obstruction and the failure to justify remaining meaningful interference. The remedy must be effective for minors whose protected position the enacted exceptions do not reach.

**Authority and operative level.** Her direct join in [Fargo Part I](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md) is date-eligible public support for the complete six-step rule, its proof allocation and its rejection of an independent facial threshold. Her Dalton join supports preserving lawful independent operation without leaving the established violation unremedied. She did not sit in Casey; its controlling rule is authority, not a fictional personal join. These actual simulated positions are sufficient without importing later abortion decisions or attributing an unexpressed independent equality holding.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** The strongest sustention argument recognizes a parent's assistance and the law's express confidentiality protections, contrasting notice with a veto. But a minor able to make an informed decision is not made safer simply by an unqualified disclosure command, and a reporting condition may deter use of the supposed alternative in the very family circumstances identified. The record supports those consequences without assuming universal parental abuse. The best objection to broad relief is that many minors consult willingly and other applications may be lawful. Under her Fargo and Dalton positions, that requires careful definition of the command and remedy; it does not cure the indivisible compulsion or create the missing independent procedure.

**Join barriers and remedy.** No equation of all families with abusive families, no new generalized equality ground, and no exemption justified solely by an unwillingness to comply. She requires the supported causal showing and the State's inadequate answer at the applicable trigger. She can join the same bounded minor-notice remedy, retaining lawful consultation and declining to decide adult guardianship or all postviability care. A merely nominal exemption unavailable in practice is not adequate relief. No distinct separate writing is required if those operative grounds and limits are represented.

### Justice Breyer

**Commitment.** Reach the merits; join the whole controlling Casey/Fargo inquiry and affirm invalidation of the previability minor-notice condition, with the implementation remand described above. The material practical burdens and a concrete authorization alternative establish the constitutional deficiency without a new quantitative test or a mandated legislative study.

**Authority and operative level.** His direct join in [Fargo Part I](../../OT1994/output/OT_1994CHUNK2.md) supports reasonable predictions, attention to actual operation, proof of added burdens and a substantive answer to concrete less burdensome alternatives. His Dalton join supports effective but bounded relief. He did not participate in Casey; no such personal position is assumed. The inference concerns this record's application of rules he has actually joined, not a later-career position.

**Competing path and strongest objection.** Remand for application of the correct framework could be justified if the State's responsive showing, the statute's actual administration or a disputed material fact remained essential. The strongest objection to immediate invalidation is that limited episodes and predicted disclosure do not establish a statewide incidence. Casey/Fargo do not require a numerical incidence threshold, and this record establishes the actual compulsory mechanism, supported serious consequences, lack of independent authorization and the relevant practicable alternative. A new study or trial is not a prerequisite merely because prospective evidence includes reasonable predictions. A merits remand premised on the operator's unavailable originals would improperly turn a research limit into party failure.

**Join barriers and remedy.** He requires acknowledgment of the State's experience-based parental-welfare rationale and of the broad abuse definition; neither receives conclusive weight against the supported gaps in usability and coverage. He will not join a cheapest-policy requirement, categorical strict scrutiny of all notice, or an injunction covering adults merely because they appear in the same sentence. He supports relief against the indivisible challenged minor condition, preserving voluntary notice and leaving the State its lawful choices. The decree-conformance remand does not demand a fresh hearing, legislatively specified institution or automatic enforcement of unreviewed provisions.
