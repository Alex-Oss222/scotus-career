## Actual law and its independent gates

**Teague:** Actual Graham requires identifying the rule at the relevant level of generality and whether prefinality authority dictated its application; different facts or disagreement alone do not make it new. Caspari applies the finality-specific rule and retains both contemporary exceptions: substantive limits on state power over conduct/persons/punishment, and watershed procedures essential to accuracy and fundamental fairness. Neither exception is eliminated. The State presented Teague below here; Schiro's permission to decline a late defense does not erase it.

**Notice, opportunity and deception:** Gardner supplies a pre1987 capital opportunity to deny or explain material sentencing information. Weatherford rejects general criminal discovery and distinguishes surprise, an absence of deliberate misrepresentation and lack of demonstrated prejudice. A universal timetable for advance disclosure differs from State-created impairment of an effective opportunity to respond. Lankford's1991 majority and dissent inform individual jurisprudence but do not become1987 law. Stevens's majority, joined by O'Connor/Kennedy, emphasizes misleading issue definition; Scalia's dissent, joined by Souter, requires reasonable reliance and an actual supported limitation rather than assumed misunderstanding. Actual Simmons applies the opportunity-to-answer principle to future danger and legal parole ineligibility, not a universal discovery deadline.

**Fair presentation and claim default:** Picard and Anderson require the substance of the federal claim, not facts alone or a merely similar legal complaint; identical phrasing is not indispensable. Vasquez allows supplementation that does not fundamentally alter the presented claim, reserving deliberate withholding. A supported adequate independent claim bar invokes Coleman and its own exceptions; it is not established merely by a research gap, a different claim's default, or a state order's generic label. Cause/prejudice and the applicable miscarriage gateway, if actually implicated, require their own supported findings. No gateway showing is invented.

**Factual development:** Actual Keeney retains Townsend's fifth hearing circumstance and deliberate-bypass qualification for a properly presented claim. Ordinary negligent development does not by itself bar a necessary federal hearing. O'Connor wrote that rule; Stevens and Kennedy joined. Hall's historical cause/prejudice substitution cannot sustain dismissal under this timeline. This correction does not prove fair presentation, material fact inadequacy, absence of deliberate bypass, or entitlement to a new hearing. A distinct state hearing-issue default requires its own supported rule and presentation facts.

**Brady:** Actual Kyles requires collective lawful uses, including preparation and impeachment, to create a reasonable probability undermining confidence; it includes knowledge of police acting for this prosecution and excludes a second duplicative prejudice test for a completed material violation. Suppression, favorability, actual or known evidence, reliability and lawful uses remain separate. Wood is only a grant, not a merits restriction on inadmissible material. Neither Kyles nor Wood resolves Sorrell appellate preservation or establishes another person's guilt.

**Error effect, remedy and temporal law:** Actual Brecht and O'Neal prescribe Chapman for their stated preserved Doyle and personal-intent categories; they announce no universal habeas rule. Tuggle prevents a surviving aggravator from automatically defeating an independently available injury but selects no universal standard here. A due-process merits path must separately identify any needed error-effect rule rather than silently importing historical Brecht or automatically reinstating relief. A material Brady finding incorporates its own materiality requirement. AEDPA admission establishes enacted text, not pending-case reach or a qualifying Virginia chapter154 system. ## Competing paths and their strongest objections

**A — Vacate the challenged appellate dismissal for a claim-specific inquiry under the old meaningful-opportunity rule.** The actual complaint and supported lower findings concern alleged impairment of the defense's ability to meet materially different evidence at capital sentencing. Gardner's core cannot be satisfied solely because the information eventually appeared in open court and counsel could ask questions. The operative rule is no fixed disclosure interval: a defendant must have a meaningful opportunity to deny or explain material capital sentencing information, assessed against the supported preparation circumstances and any misleading assurance. Actual Graham requires testing that existing function rather than treating every application involving delay as new. The strongest objection is that Gardner concerned wholly secret information and Weatherford denies a discovery entitlement. A answers by requiring demonstrated impairment beyond surprise; it does not create general pretrial disclosure, guarantee an exclusion remedy, infer a continuance request, or establish deception as fact. The present appellate reasoning does not properly perform that narrower inquiry.

**B — Preserve the new-rule bar to the ordinary advance-notice theory, but separately remand the materially different deception/preservation issues.** Gardner does not dictate a right to earlier notice of evidence disclosed before the hearing; extending its secret-information rationale to preparation time was at least reasonably open in1987. Neither substantive nor watershed exception permits that newly formulated discovery rule. The strongest objection is that the complaint and lower findings cannot fairly be reduced to a request for more discovery. B addresses that objection by withholding any global dismissal: alleged misleading assurances, their presentation, their actual effect and any distinct Brady theory receive their own analysis. That remand does not concede the old-rule status or merits of a new substantive theory, and does not revive a finally disposed claim without a lawful basis.

**C — Affirm all habeas dismissal on an independent bar, or reinstate the entire writ now.** Either terminal alternative needs more than the supplied support. The first requires a claim-specific preserved procedural ground or constitutional rejection, not Hall's displaced hearing rule or an assumed appellate omission. The second requires proper availability, review of actual findings, the precise violation and applicable error-effect/materiality analysis. The appellate treatment of the actually presented Sorrell due-process challenge and the displaced factual-development rule require the stated legal correction; neither establishes an across-the-board alternative bar or the predicates for immediate relief. Original-document limits qualify what this modeling context can assert, but do not supply a judicial reason to remand or defeat a claim. The receiving court can decide the actual remaining legal tasks on the litigation record without an operator-driven production order or fabricated response.
