## Finding requiring repair

**Spink, Public Projection, Holdings III (Record line 133):** The collective-bargaining transition is described as covering agreements **in effect before March 1, 1986**. Section 9204(a)(2) instead requires agreements **ratified before March 1, 1986** and applies the substituted effective date to benefits pursuant to, and individuals covered by, those agreements. Ratification and taking effect are different triggers. Replace the trigger and state that covered-benefits/individuals scope. The preserved official Statutes at Large extraction supplies the exact text at [obra1986.txt](../sources/chunk8_a_control/obra1986.txt), lines 6349–6366 (100 Stat. 1979–1980). The date formula itself is accurate. No supplied allegation invokes this transition, so the repair does not change the case's disposition, coalition, or remand.

> (2) SPECIAL RULE FOR COLLECTIVELY BARGAINED PLANS.—In the case of a plan maintained pursuant to 1 or more collective bargaining agreements between employee representatives and 1 or more employers ratified before March 1, 1986, paragraph (1) shall be applied to benefits pursuant to, and individuals covered by, any such agreement by substituting for “January 1, 1988” the date of the commencement of the first plan year beginning on or after the earlier of—
>
> (A) the later of—
>
> (i) January 1, 1988, or
>
> (ii) the date on which the last of such collective bargaining agreements terminate (determined without regard to any extension thereof after February 28, 1986), or
>
> (B) January 1, 1990.
