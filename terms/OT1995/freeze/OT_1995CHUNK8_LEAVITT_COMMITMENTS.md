Casey's complete six-step framework governs any properly reached federal merits: enacted duty/baseline; function/purpose; attributable interference and stable affected population; independent invalidity for baseline violation, objectively proved obstructive purpose or substantial obstacle, then compelling justification/narrow tailoring for remaining severe interference and substantiated justification/fit for remaining meaningful interference; clinical/communication safeguards; and corresponding relief. Challengers first establish the triggering interference. Individualized viability and timely necessary physical and mental health care survive. Fargo and Miller preserve that entire structure without a separate no-valid-applications barrier. Dalton requires relief matched to the established conflict and preservation of independently lawful applications; it does not settle Utah severability or constitutional validity. Spink preserves genuinely independent grounds rather than converting rejection of one theory into complete dismissal. IBM preserves discretion whether to decide a fairly included argument.

The first petition question cannot be treated as authorization to reopen unappealed invalidation of section302(2). Section302(3) retains only the life, grave-medical-health and grave-fetal-defect circumstances after twenty weeks from conception; rape/incest are not independent exceptions there. Section302(2)'s rape/incest exceptions themselves require pre-abortion police reporting. Sections307–308 use a reasonable-possibility-of-survival trigger and grave-health qualifications rather than silently importing the complete Casey safeguards. The clinical and legal consequences must be assessed from those actual terms, not a presumed universal viability finding.

HB206 supplies a separate procedure prohibition conditioned on physician-determined viability and an exception when all other available procedures threaten life or health. Its definitions exclude specified other procedures. Section314 still names sections307–308; section315's emergency list does not name them; section4 confines the new viability method to section310.5 and disclaims effect on other provisions/litigation. These provisions rebut an assumption of automatic repeal, but admission decides neither implied repeal nor mootness. No direct HB206 challenge is before the Court.

## Live petition and disposition paths

**A — Limited Q1 grant and separate summary correction.** The express severability clause says each provision and application survives others' invalidity. Section302(3) can independently prohibit the abortions within its own terms even after section302(2) falls; cross-reference identifies the exceptions rather than making its legal operation impossible. The reenactment provision has a separate contingency and does not withdraw ordinary severability. This supports correcting the contrary state-law conclusion without deciding the unreached federal validity of section302(3). The strongest objection is Utah's interdependence doctrine and the obvious relevance of alternative federal grounds. The response is a confined remand for those grounds, with no holding that the provision is constitutionally enforceable. Pennhurst does not categorically forbid examining state severability to define relief for an established federal violation, so the broad Eleventh Amendment theory is not adopted.

**B — Deny discretionary review.** The first issue is a disputed state-law severability application; review would leave serious independent federal questions concerning the fixed week and health limitations. The second seeks reconsideration of a method-duty ruling amid current Casey safeguards and an intervening enactment. No established conflict or sufficiently clean need for plenary review is supplied. The strongest objection is that a clear severability error need not persist merely because further issues remain. This path regards the state-law/federal-ground interaction as making summary intervention unsuitable and the petition an unattractive vehicle, not as jurisdictionally unreviewable or conclusively moot.

**C — Grant for ordinary briefing, with question-specific scope.** If the Court wants the second question or an extended constitutional resolution, plenary presentation is preferable to treating a certiorari petition as a completed merits record. The concrete issues are the actual health qualification, fetal-survival trigger, relationship to the governing complete standard and HB206's effect. No later outcome, schedule or filing is invented. This route is a distinct petition position, not enough by itself to order a summary judgment.

**Remedial boundary for A:** Correct only the appealed severability disposition concerning section302(3); remand its unadjudicated federal validity and any legally relevant HB206 effect. Do not disturb unappealed section302(2) invalidation, the sections307–308 judgment through a Q1-only ruling, or other unreviewed provisions.
