## Reconciliation result and further source review

All seven provisional judgment positions remain: O'Connor, Scalia, Souter, Thomas and Breyer affirm; Kennedy and Ginsburg reverse and remand. The verified historical disposition was affirmance 6–2 with Stevens absent; those same seven Associates divided five to two, with Rehnquist historically supplying the remaining affirmance vote. That historical count supplies no Stone vote.

The comparison disclosed a material **scope risk**, despite matching judgments. The historical majority retained Thames & Mersey and rejected the nondiscrimination argument while expressly leaving a general reconsideration of the goods/services boundary for another case. Several provisional rows elected to consider the narrower argument. The independent freeze also permitted a common opinion applying existing precedent and reserving other services; this is the supported final scope.

Further primary source review directly read the preserved [Cornell Court text](../sources/chunk8_a_control/ibm_court.txt), particularly the passages corresponding to 517 U.S. 854–856, 861–862 and notes 3–5. The majority identified both the direct precedent and the petitioner's deliberate litigation choice; it did not assert a jurisdictional inability to consider an included argument. Its stare-decisis discussion and the absence of a demonstrated workability defect were separate grounds for not reopening the classification. The verified receipt supplies the complete dissent, which treats the narrower question as fairly included and sufficiently supported by the stipulated insurance facts.

Actual Lebron permits discretionary consideration of an included legal argument despite disavowal; it creates no duty to decide it. Itel expressly preserved direct-export questions, Jefferson Lines concerns a different taxable event under a different constitutional prohibition, and Fulton identifies a particular doctrinal contradiction when overruling Darnell. None displaces Thames & Mersey or supplies a concrete changed premise requiring these five affirming Associates to reopen the service classification. The historical majority's limited scope therefore remains strong evidence. The reconciled affirming ground is retention and application of the direct insurance precedent after rejecting the asserted interclause/nondiscrimination reason for overruling it, not a newly announced universal test of all export-related services.

The distinction is substantive: willingness to examine a proposed reason for overruling is not adoption of an independent new holding classifying every indemnity service from first principles. Nor may the reversing pair's refusal to endorse a nondiscrimination safe harbor be counted as direct joins in an unnecessary broad holding. They resolve the case on the narrower service ground and would leave the broader goods-tax question undecided.

## Proposition-level assembly boundaries

The five affirming Associates directly support these propositions together: nondiscrimination does not itself authorize a federal tax on goods in export transit; changes in the distinct Commerce and state Import-Export doctrines do not establish the asserted reason to discard the direct federal precedent; Thames & Mersey remains governing for this export-transit-insurance application; and the challenged refund judgment should stand. Their sufficient ground does not require choosing whether every other export-related service is equivalent to the articles. Ordinary pre-export transactions, insurer-business taxation, income taxation and sufficiently distinct/tangential services remain outside what this application decides.

All seven recognize Lebron's legal availability of an included argument as distinct from the prudential election to decide it. The five affirming Associates can join a reservation that the broader classification is not reconsidered here. O'Connor's initial prudential election is the clearest; the other four's willingness to test the argument does not produce a required separate holding. No opinion may say the Court lacks jurisdiction to address the included argument or that disavowal always forbids its consideration.

Kennedy and Ginsburg require the narrower service ground to be reached and the direct precedent expressly displaced to the stated extent. They disagree with leaving the classification unreconsidered here and with preserving this refund. Their rationale can preserve the constitutional prohibition on genuine goods taxes without resolving the Government's entire nondiscrimination theory. Agreement on that limit is not an automatic joinder in the affirming opinion.

No Associate commits to a new tax amount, an exemption ruling, a facial invalidation or validation of section 4371, or adjudication of the period-specific statutory qualifications beyond the presented application. On affirmance the existing refund judgment stands; on the reversing pair's approach the constitutional ground is rejected and conforming further proceedings occur, with no new assessment or unrelated issue supplied. No final Court count is stated without Stone.
