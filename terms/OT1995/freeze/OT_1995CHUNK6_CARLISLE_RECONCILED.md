## Governing premise and departure test

The submitted question concerns authority to grant this new postverdict motion, not the existence of federal criminal subject-matter jurisdiction. Counsel filed July 23, 1993, after the July 22 deadline calculated from the July 13 discharge. The Government timely objected. No timely trial motion, reservation, extension fixed within the seven-day period, or predeadline judicial assurance inducing delay is established. The judge's later changed view of sufficiency supplies neither factual innocence nor a different timely motion.

The historical majority's core Rule 29(c)/45(b) premise remains: a new postverdict motion cannot be granted after the specified period on ordinary excusable neglect, no prejudice or generalized fairness. Rule 29(a)'s trial power and Rule 29(b)'s reservation mechanism cannot automatically supply the same forbidden relief. The guilty-verdict provision's “on such motion” language matters. Smith's completed-appellate posture differs, but its reasoning against an unrestricted judicial substitute for a restricted party motion remains material.

Actual Williams differs from historical Williams by recognizing bounded grand-jury supervision. The [entering-law B holding](../entering-law/OT_1995CHUNK6_B.md) therefore prevents using historical Williams as an indiscriminate prohibition of supervision. It nevertheless preserves Bank of Nova Scotia's constraints and does not hold that inherent power overrides a controlling procedural rule. Actual [Lonchar](../entering-law/PUBLIC_Lonchar_v_Thomas_merits_1996-04-01.md) protects process genuinely required by governing law while rejecting a free-standing equitable substitute for its conditions. It neither identifies a still-owed Carlisle proceeding nor determines whether Rule 29 withdrew this own-motion authority. Actual Pioneer permits flexibility where authorized and preserves express enlargement restrictions. Libretti, Olano and Cooper distinguish authority, waiver, adequate process and available correction; none changes Rule 29's text or decides this motion.

Those authorities support serious consideration of both constructions. They do not supply a sufficiently concrete changed premise for the two provisional historical departures:

- **Kennedy:** Manypenny's timely-motion limit, Pioneer, and the distinction between a pending timely adjudication and a new late motion already existed historically. His Carlisle dissent accepted that distinction yet concluded that Rule 29 did not withdraw the underlying judicial power. His actual Lonchar necessary-opportunity join and Cooper capacity opinion do not newly reject that approach. A reason to favor the majority construction in the abstract is not a changed Justice-specific premise sufficient to reverse the direct historical evidence. His reconciled position therefore returns to the bounded inherent-power route, with actual simulated Williams and all rule-consistency limits expressly considered.
- **Breyer:** His actual Lonchar and Cooper joins protect identified legal process rather than create every desirable correction mechanism. Williams's changed grand-jury holding is available to him, but its rule-consistent scope does not resolve the contrary-rule objection central here. His historical full Court join and Ginsburg-concurrence join directly reject extending the deadline to sentencing. AEDPA requires a more qualified account of collateral remedies; it does not establish that Carlisle has no adequate avenue or confer the proposed district power. The modeled reversal is therefore unsupported as a departure and is corrected to a bounded affirmance.

The two corrections are independent assessments, not preservation of a preferred count. No unsupported historical departure in judgment remains.

## Reconciled grounds and eight individual checks

**Affirming ground A:** Rules 29(c) and 45(b) withhold authority to grant this new, untimely postverdict motion on the supplied facts. Rule 2 guides construction rather than overrides the specific limit; former Rule 57 supplies only consistent gap-filling practice. Neither inherent supervision nor coram nobis supplies an established alternative here. The result does not decide all court-initiated action within the allowed period, later reconsideration of a timely motion, valid reservations, Government waiver, or every possible judicial-assurance case. It does not abolish inherent trial authority generally or label the deadline a loss of subject-matter jurisdiction.

**Reversing ground B, refined:** Stevens and Kennedy recognize a confined pre-sentence judicial power to prevent judgment on legally insufficient evidence, which they regard Rule 29 as not having withdrawn. The source is the court's retained judicial authority, informed by the acquittal duty reflected in Rule 29(a), rather than Rule 29(a) alone conferring an unrestricted postverdict motion remedy. They accept that Rule 29(c)/45 bars the defendant's late motion entitlement, but read those provisions as not extinguishing this separate own-motion power. Smith's later procedural posture is material to their distinction. Their ground is not a one-day/no-prejudice exception, an adjudication of innocence, or an indefinite power to reopen completed criminal judgments.
