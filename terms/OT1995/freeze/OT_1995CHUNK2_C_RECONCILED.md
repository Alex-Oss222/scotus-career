### Reconciled legal commitments

The six supporting Associates accept §2106 authority for a bounded remand when an intervening or sufficiently recent development creates a reasonable probability that the lower decision rests on a premise the lower court would reject on reconsideration, and reconsideration may affect the ultimate outcome. That is a necessary justification for considering a GVR, not an automatic command. The Court must also consider finality, lower-court independence, delay and expense, and whether a party is manipulating litigation. A changed executive position does not control judicial judgment. An agency reinterpretation may warrant reconsideration without a prior merits decision that it is valid, entitled to deference, or dispositive. The record must still support an independently assessed material relationship to the judgment.

The proposed order concerns that reconsideration alone. It decides neither benefit eligibility nor the constitutional validity of a state intestacy provision, awards no benefits, and establishes no categorical deference to interpretations adopted during litigation. McCarthy, Darby, MCI, and Thomas Jefferson University retain their distinct rules and limits. Deference to an agency's own regulation under Thomas Jefferson University cannot simply be treated as statutory Chevron deference here.
