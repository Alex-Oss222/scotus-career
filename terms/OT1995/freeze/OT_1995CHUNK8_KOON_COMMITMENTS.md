## Components and two supported paths

**A. Review.** All eight accept an abuse-of-discretion framework for a departure decision, with independent resolution of statutory/Guidelines interpretation and legal permission, clear-error protection for historical findings, and due deference to supported case-specific unusualness and degree. Erroneous law is an abuse of discretion. Section 3742(e), Cooter & Gell and the functional allocation in Pierce support this formulation. The competing national-uniformity path would independently determine every heartland application; its strongest point is that accumulating departures defines the national guideline's reach. It loses where the question is a sentencing judge's concrete comparison of an unusual encounter, but remains powerful on legal scope and adequate Commission consideration. Actual Ornelas does not make all mixed questions independent. Actual Stinson governs interpretive commentary; it does not elevate contrary commentary over enacted text. No mandatory Rivera four-step sequence is imposed.

**B. Separate five-level victim-misconduct departure.** Section 5K2.10 requires wrongful victim conduct significantly contributing to provocation of the offense behavior. It does not say that the victim must remain dangerous at every instant of the resulting crime. Its extent considerations nevertheless make the end of resistance and danger material. The lawful-force phase, preceding resistance and rapid transition may support significant provocation and unusual degree; simply observing that an arrest preceded the crime would not. The district court supplied the former explanation, not chronology alone. The rival path treats excessive force after danger ends as the ordinary official-assault case already reflected in §2H1.4 and its underlying-assault calculation. This is a serious limit on both unusualness and the size of the reduction, but it does not justify a categorical rule excluding the court's complete dynamic-arrest explanation.

All eight reject the categorical legal exclusion. Stevens, Scalia, Kennedy and Breyer would also sustain the five-level departure as within the sentencing court's supported discretion. O'Connor, Souter, Thomas and Ginsburg would return the extent/application question for appellate review under the correct allocation, leaving the five levels neither categorically prohibited nor automatically sustained. This latter position is a review/remedy reservation, not a finding that King remained dangerous or that significant provocation is impossible. No current opinion assignment or Court majority is selected.

The full 1992 §5K2.10 is retained: comparative size/strength; persistence and efforts to avoid confrontation; reasonably perceived danger including reputation; actual danger; and other victim conduct substantially contributing to danger. Criminal sexual abuse ordinarily supplies insufficient victim misconduct; nonviolent offenses usually do not fit, although unusual extended provocation followed by property retaliation may. No later sixth consideration is imported. None of those qualifications supplies a new fact here.

**C. Extraordinary prison vulnerability.** All eight permit genuine unusual vulnerability to be considered under §3553(b)/§5K2.0. Police status alone is not an entitlement, but police status combined with the identified extraordinary publicity can produce a qualitatively unusual confinement burden. The strongest objection is that this rewards occupational standing and assumes future prison treatment. The permissible basis is the actual vulnerability, not social prestige; the record does not establish inevitable continuous solitary confinement. The legal ground survives, but it does not independently establish the whole three-level combination or a particular prison prediction.

**D. Employment/professional consequences.** All eight reject the described loss of law-enforcement employment as a sufficient departure ground on this record. Sections 5H1.5 and 5H1.11 discourage employment/public-service considerations; they do not categorically forbid every unusual circumstance. Section 5H1.10 categorically excludes socioeconomic status and the other listed classifications. The strongest alternative is that unusually severe collateral consequences may exceed ordinary consideration. Here the record supplies job/professional loss following this official-abuse conviction, without a distinct unusual-degree showing that avoids occupational preference. The §5H1.5 permission to consider employment for probation/supervised-release conditions does not authorize a prison-range reduction.

**E. Very low recidivism.** All eight reject it as a basis to go below category I. Section 4A1.3 expressly puts the lowest-risk first offender at that lower bound. The strongest individualized-sentencing argument cannot displace this actual Commission consideration. This conclusion concerns the identified recidivism ground; it does not make every other offender circumstance a criminal-history departure.

**F. Successive prosecutions.** All eight reject an automatic departure for lawful succession alone and reject the opposite categorical inference that legality makes every attendant circumstance irrelevant. An unmentioned circumstance may be considered if genuinely unusual and inadequately considered; lawful dual sovereignty supplies neither an entitlement to mitigation nor a conclusive prohibition. The described burden must be tested under that standard on remand. No finding of double-jeopardy violation, punishment already discharged against the federal sentence, prosecutorial misconduct, or extraordinary burden beyond the stated record is invented. No final allowance of the entire three-level reduction follows from the availability of this inquiry.

**G. Remedy in both dockets.** The combined three levels cannot simply stand because some grounds survive. Actual Williams places on the sentence defender, after invalid reliance is shown, the burden of establishing from the whole record that the same sentence would have been imposed without the invalid grounds; reasonable extent must independently rest on actual valid reasons. The record supplies no finding that each factor independently warranted all three levels and no assurance of the same 30 months. All eight require renewed proceedings under that rule. The independent five-level departure follows each Justice's B position. The affirmed convictions and bodily-injury adjustment remain outside the submitted departure review.
