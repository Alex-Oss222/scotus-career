## Exact common merits and remand scope supported by the six

**Federal rule.** When an automobile is readily mobile and probable cause supports belief that it contains contraband, the automobile exception permits its search without a warrant and without a separate showing of unforeseen circumstances, imminent departure, or insufficient time to seek a warrant. Ready mobility supplies the relevant exigency; reduced vehicle privacy arising from regulation further supports the exception. Police control of the vehicle or an earlier opportunity to obtain a warrant does not itself add another federal prerequisite. Ross retains its rule that the search's object and the places capable of containing it limit scope; no universal authority to search on probable cause follows.

**Application and judgment.** The state courts accepted probable cause in both matters. Labron's street transactions and use of the Lincoln's trunk, and Kilgore's controlled-purchase circumstances and connection to the truck, supply their accepted applications. These automobile searches are not rendered unconstitutional by the separate absence of the exigency the Pennsylvania court demanded. The six apply the automobile exception to that adjudicated basis and support **reversal of both reviewed judgments and remand for proceedings consistent with the federal decision**. This is a reasoned merits disposition and application, not a GVR, an instruction merely to consider whether the rejected urgency rule should survive, or a vacatur leaving that automobile-exception application entirely undecided.

**Labron's failed start.** Preserve that the Lincoln would not start when officers attempted impoundment, that its exterior appeared highway-legal, and that timing relative to the trunk search remains unspecified. The six do not infer a working engine or police knowledge at a particular instant. The record's ordinary automobile setting and accepted observations support the case-specific legal application; no general rule that appearance always defeats known mechanical incapacity is adopted. The Court need not manufacture a factual answer in order to reject the separate advance-opportunity requirement actually imposed below. Nor does this handoff direct a new mechanical-condition hearing or guarantee a newly raised theory's availability.

**Kilgore's setting.** Preserve the private farm location, the actual custody sequence, the respondent's location-based automobile argument and the limited house-consent testimony. Parking the pickup there and having officers available to guard it do not supply the rejected additional automobile-exigency condition. The Court's automobile-exception conclusion is not a finding that the location was outside protected curtilage, that any protected boundary was lawfully crossed, that the farmer authorized every outdoor approach, or that Kilgore consented to the truck search. A distinct entry/access theory's merits and procedural availability are not adjudicated by the added-exigency holding. Do not describe that theory as waived, conceded, preserved, successful, or necessarily requiring further proceedings without its own support.

**Remaining law and remedy.** Long permits the six to correct the federal premise without preventing Pennsylvania from applying a properly available, actually independent state rule. The six do not dictate such a state disposition, and the dissenters' prediction that it will occur is their stated reason for restraint, not an entered future event. Reversal and ordinary remand direct neither an automatic evidence-admission order nor a conviction, new trial, new hearing or particular state judgment. Scope qualifications preserve questions genuinely outside the decision; they may not be used in assembly to restore the rejected provisional rule-only vacatur under a different name.
