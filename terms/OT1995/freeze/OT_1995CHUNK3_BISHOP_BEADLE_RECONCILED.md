**B2 — Commerce rule for both defendants.** The same eight apply the completed simulated Harris holding: Congress may regulate the economic class of armed vehicle takings within the interstate motor-vehicle theft-prevention scheme. The offense's actual vehicle-history requirement remains necessary. A currently interstate trip or an individual resale motive is not required; remote interstate movement alone and generic insurance, crime or productivity costs do not supply the constitutional rationale. No independent sufficiency dispute over the vehicle-history element is decided.

The Third Circuit supplies three alternative commerce routes: aggregate economic effects, past movement under Bass/Scarborough, and vehicles as instrumentalities. Becker contests all three, treating local violent takings as noncommercial, remote movement as insufficient, and mere potential interstate travel as inadequate. The reconciled eight do not mechanically affirm all three lower rationales. Their own Harris commitments already select the economic-class/scheme ground and its limits; neither a freestanding past-movement rule nor an all-vehicles instrumentality rule is needed. Becker's strongest objection receives an answer under the actual simulated premise: this is the class of economic vehicle takings already sustained in Harris, not firearm possession supported only by downstream social costs. The historical lower court's account of Lopez and Robertson is not imported as simulated law.

**B3 — Stokes's same-prosecution cumulative punishment.** The same eight reject the Double Jeopardy challenge to the offense-date sections 2119 and 924(c). Under Hunter and simulated Harris, section 924(c)'s additional, consecutive punishment direction supplies the necessary legislative authorization even assuming Blockburger overlap. No residual ambiguity about this pairing calls for lenity. This does not decide successive prosecution, erase the firearm statute's distinct elements or make possession equivalent to use/carrying. Effective Bailey requires active employment for use. The reported pistol-to-head conduct constitutes active employment, but no independent Bailey instruction, sufficiency, carrying or harmless-error issue is manufactured. The later amendment substituting intent to cause death or serious bodily harm in section 2119 is not the offense-date text.

**B4 — Stokes's identifications.** The same eight reject the asserted due-process basis for excluding the two witnesses' out-of-court and in-court identifications. Manson/Biggers require reliability assessed against the corrupting effect of the suggestion. Both witnesses observed the gunman during the taking; separate identifications occurred within ninety minutes; the trial court found independent recollection, and the court of appeals sustained the findings and reliability conclusion. The police's caught-the-thief comment and station showup are weighed as suggestive. The supported findings do not establish the substantial likelihood of misidentification necessary for exclusion on this challenge. Neither suggestion alone nor a bare declaration of independence supplies a categorical rule. No absent precise description, observation duration, lighting, certainty quotation or factor finding is invented. Other evidence of guilt cannot replace reliable identification.

## Beadle: retain bounded Title VII vacatur and remand

**E1 — Statutory standard, eight joins.** Stevens, O'Connor, Scalia, Kennedy, Souter, Thomas, Ginsburg and Breyer retain the event-date Hardison/Ansonia framework. Section 2000e(j) includes religious observance, practice and belief unless the employer demonstrates inability to accommodate reasonably without undue hardship on its business. Under then-governing law, more than de minimis cost is not required of the employer; concrete lost efficiency, deficient training and consequent operational/safety burdens may suffice without extra cash expenditure. Once the employer provides an effective reasonable accommodation, it need not provide the employee's preferred one or disprove every alternative. The ordinary conflicting schedule and isolated Saturday leave did not resolve this continuing Sabbath conflict, so the hardship branch matters. Neutrality and lack of animus do not alone discharge the distinct accommodation duty. No later hardship standard, public-safety exemption, absolute Sabbath right or cash-only test is adopted.

**E2 — Identified error, eight joins.** Vacate the adverse accommodation judgment because the actual inquiry did not adequately address the supported, materially distinct alternatives. The trial court relied on the neutral system and general police responsibilities while finding no demonstrated deprivation of another trainee's scheduling benefit. The appellate opinion relies on Beadle's concession that one trainer throughout would be educationally deficient; the verified record presents alternatives preserving multiple trainers. The conceded defect of one arrangement is not a finding about every proposed arrangement. Professional training and safety evidence can support hardship, but it must address the relevant alternative and actual operations. The legal defect is affirmatively identified from the lower opinions and verified party/lower record; it is not a remand because the operator lacks a transcript.

The three supported alternatives remain proposals: multiple qualified trainers while retaining Dawson's squad; qualified trainers on other Sabbath-compatible nontraining squads; and a four-to-eight-week extension addressing rotation deficits. No one is found costless or adequate. Later departmental practice does not prove 1988 availability. Extensions for secular recruits are relevant but do not make this extension automatically costless. The actual training clause prevents assuming an obligatory seniority bar; it does not eliminate all legitimate coworker interests. A possible permanent downtown assignment is neither a training assignment nor a guaranteed vacancy. The City's professional evidence need not predict an actual accident or translate every burden into dollars. Beadle's acceptance of tangible lost efficiency and demonstrably inadequate training is retained, not enlarged into concession of all hardship.

**E3 — Remedy, eight joins.** Vacate the Title VII accommodation judgment and remand through the Eleventh Circuit for proper application, with such further district-court proceedings as lawfully necessary. The factfinder and ordinary review standards remain. A correct evaluation of the existing record may suffice; no automatic new trial or evidentiary hearing is ordered. The City may prove the necessary actual operational, training, safety or protected coworker hardship and may again prevail. No specific schedule, training program, vacancy, reinstatement, damages or ultimate entitlement is ordered. The distinct rejected release defense remains outside review. RFRA and constitutional free exercise do not replace Title VII; no hypothetical unconditional religious exemption's constitutionality is decided.
