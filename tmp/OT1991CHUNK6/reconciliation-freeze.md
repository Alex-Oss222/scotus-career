## Supplemental neutral facts and issue gates

- Dawson: Blackmun's harmless-error reservation concerns whether this particular First Amendment error can be harmless at all. The remand leaves availability and application open; the Court does not order harmlessness or find it. No categorical exclusion of association evidence follows.
- Williams: the old, nonsimilar convictions question is not clearly presented or briefed and is not decided. The prior same-offense conduct and threats were used to choose the sentence after departure, not established independent departure grounds. Remand is to the circuit for causal harmlessness and separate reasonableness, not necessarily district resentencing. A policy statement expressly forbidding a sentencing action is authoritative guidance to the applicable Guideline; no universal policy-statement rule is needed.
- Stringer: February 19, 1985 finality remains decisive. The district court also found a procedural bar, which the circuit has not decided. Reversal of the new-rule ruling leaves that defense and actual adequacy of state cure open. No writ is issued now. Godfrey and Zant supply the prefinality law; later Maynard and Clemons are tested for novelty, not imported as automatically retroactive.
- Germain: the district court already certified the interlocutory question, but circuit permission and the statutory prerequisites remain for remand. No jury-trial entitlement is adjudicated. Section 1292's text reaches district courts acting in appellate capacity, not necessarily bankruptcy appellate panels.
- PFZ: the full official argument and operative-complaint/rehearing record confirm the vehicle difficulty. Historical Chief Justice questions are not attributed to Stone. Unidentified questions are not votes. The writ can be dismissed without a merits holding or endorsement of the lower court's state-remedy rationale.
- Holmes: the grant covered purchaser/seller status; proximate cause is fairly included and was actually briefed and argued. The denied coconspirator question remains assumed. A distinct asserted SIPA §78eee(d) basis for SIPC's administrative advances must be addressed if deciding its recovery: the statute confers party-in-interest status and a right to be heard in a pending liquidation, not an independent damages action and not relief from §1964(c)'s causation requirement. SIPC did not advance its separate parking-scheme theory in the merits brief; potential direct customer injury under that theory remains open. Broker trustees' own claims are outside the challenge. Neutral reassessment: White, Blackmun, Stevens, O'Connor, Kennedy, Souter and Thomas reject the independent SIPA route on its text; Scalia reaches the same judgment without joining Souter's explanation. This adds a ground to be tested against Stone's approved scope, not an assumed Stone choice.
- R.L.C.: certiorari No. 90-1577, argued December 10, 1991. The Government sought no stay of the circuit mandate; the district court has already imposed 18 months on remand and that term has been served. Possible restoration of the original three years keeps the case live. Affirmance preserves the circuit judgment rather than ordering a new, fictitious resentencing. Seven support the cap and only four historical votes supported legislative history; lenity methodology is not a majority rule. Refresh on March 24 must use the actual Williams disposition and lawful-departure constraints, without assuming an additional departure here.
- Darden: No. 90-1802, argued January 21, 1992. The denied cross-petition over the Extended Earnings Plan remains outside review. The agency test has no single decisive factor and is general federal common law, not one State's agency law. ERISA's benefit expectations do not decide whether this claimant is an employee.
- Wilson: No. 90-1745, argued January 15, 1992. Federal sentencing preceded state sentencing and custody transfer; 429 days were credited by Tennessee before the federal sentence commenced. The case concerns actor/timing and the no-double-credit constraint, not a new numerical award. McCarthy's damages-only exception does not excuse a credit process able to provide the requested relief. Stevens's cited historical Wolas must be checked against the actual simulated Wolas; no unadmitted postdivergence premise controls.
