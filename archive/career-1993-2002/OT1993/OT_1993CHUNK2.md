#### Issue 1: Employer factfinding before disciplining protected speech

- **Question Stone Resolves:** What factual basis a public employer may use when disciplining an employee for speech, and whether the employer must reasonably investigate what was said before applying the public-employee speech balance.

The holding does not protect speech made pursuant to assigned duties as such or excuse viewpoint retaliation under the label of workplace efficiency; a sham or deliberately one-sided inquiry remains relevant evidence that the employer lacked a genuine or objectively reasonable factual basis.

A conviction entered with no counsel at all may not be used because that defect is apparent and categorically fundamental under controlling precedent.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** At ACCA sentencing, a defendant may challenge a predicate conviction for complete denial of the Gideon right to counsel, but other constitutional attacks must first succeed through a lawful direct or collateral channel before the conviction ceases to count.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The statute counts convictions not set aside by the issuing jurisdiction and supplies no procedure for broad collateral review. Adjudicating fact-intensive plea and performance claims in federal sentencing would displace the courts holding the relevant records. Preserving the Gideon exception follows existing authority without converting the enhancement hearing into a general postconviction forum.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not foreclose later sentence relief after a predicate is vacated, decide a case where no realistic review channel exists, or permit use of a conviction obtained after complete denial of counsel.
