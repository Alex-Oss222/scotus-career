**Current stage:** The Court retains jurisdiction while the antecedent state-law question certified to the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania remains pending.

**Operative grant or order:** On November 30, 1999, the Court certified the question stated below and reserved the federal due-process issue.

**Last Supreme Court action or material milestone:** Certification order entered November 30, 1999.

**Court below:** United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit; its judgment remains under Supreme Court review.

**Question before the Court:** Whether Fiore was convicted without proof of conduct prohibited by 35 Pa. Stat. § 6018.401(a), if the interpretation later stated in *Commonwealth v. Scarpone* was the statute’s correct meaning when Fiore’s conviction became final.

**Grant or order scope:** The Court certified this question to the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania: Whether the interpretation of 35 Pa. Stat. § 6018.401(a) stated in *Commonwealth v. Scarpone* was the correct interpretation of Pennsylvania law when Fiore’s conviction became final. The federal due-process issue remains reserved.

**Next nonroutine act or controlling condition:** The Supreme Court of Pennsylvania must provide its authoritative response before this Court resumes disposition; no response date is supplied.

**Active procedural dependency:** The retained matter depends on the certified-question response identified in Section 5.

**State source:** OT1999 completed render, certification in *Fiore v. White*, No. 98-942, November 30, 1999.
