- **Stone’s Reasoning and Record Application:** The Eighth Amendment distinction between a good-faith effort to restore discipline and punishment imposed after the need for force has ended made the violation clear. The Eleventh Circuit nevertheless granted immunity solely because no earlier case reproduced the hitching-post facts. Simulated *Saucier* and *Elder* reject that categorical demand. The qualified-immunity judgment is therefore reversed, while personal participation, causation, and damages remain defendant-specific matters on remand.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** A policy violation alone does not establish constitutional liability, brief restraints reasonably used to restore discipline remain distinct, and each defendant's participation, causation, and damages must be proved separately.
