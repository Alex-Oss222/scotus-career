- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A State violates the Establishment Clause when it uses public money or a public monetary equivalent to pay for religious-school tuition, worship, devotional instruction, theological formation, or inseparably sectarian education, even when neutral eligibility and an intervening private selection determine the provider.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** Wisconsin created the benefit, limited it to approved schools, and satisfied tuition through a payment instrument delivered for provider endorsement. The selected parent could not take the value outside the program as ordinary private funds. Its practical incidence is therefore State financing of the provider's integrated religious instruction.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not exclude religious institutions from a genuine neutral forum or from a severable, publicly controlled secular service supplied on the same civil terms to all eligible participants.

#### Issue 2: Neutral forum access, secular rules, and anti-circumvention

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the attribution boundary necessary to decide the payment program.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Religious and nonreligious speakers receive equal access to a genuine neutral forum, nothing more by virtue of the forum. Once admitted, every participant is governed by the same secular rules concerning use, safety, administration, and conduct. Public financial support cannot be created through a third-party loophole, and vouchers, credits, reimbursements, tax mechanisms, and redeemable instruments remain public financing when they satisfy a religious provider's charge.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Neutral forum access without financing is constitutionally distinct from public financial aid. Private selection does not break attribution when public money or a public monetary equivalent is restricted, routed, credited, reimbursed, or redeemed to pay for religious exercise or instruction; forum participants remain subject to neutral secular rules.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The program is not an open forum in which speakers receive access to space or channels under common rules. It is a payment system satisfying educational charges. The intervening parent therefore does not sever governmental attribution, and the form of the instrument cannot do what direct payment could not.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The Court does not decide every neutral transportation, health, meal, safety, or other genuinely severable public service, but no form of public money may finance religious exercise or instruction through an intermediary.
