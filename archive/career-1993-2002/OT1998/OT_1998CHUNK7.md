#### Issue 1: Constitutionally sufficient remedial predicate

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches the constitutional merits of the granted promotion judgments. Review is confined to the driver-engineer, lieutenant, and captain promotions and treats the racial and sex predicates separately.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** Dallas had a strong basis in evidence that its own racial discrimination produced effects persisting in the relevant promotion structure. The record's near-total historical exclusion, the Justice Department's 1972 discrimination finding, the 1976 consent decree, continuing upper-rank disparities, and the internally populated promotion pipeline establish that predicate. The record supplies no comparable finding, history, qualified-pool comparison, or causal evidence concerning women; numerical absence from upper ranks alone is insufficient under simulated *Kelley*.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** A public employer has a sufficient racial remedial predicate when objectively grounded evidence connects its own relevant discrimination to continuing effects in the challenged promotion structure; Dallas established that predicate for race on this record. Numerical absence without comparable historical, qualified-pool, or causal evidence does not establish the predicate for sex-conscious promotions, and Dallas did not establish it here.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The racial evidence concerns Dallas's own department and the pipeline feeding the ranks at issue, not generalized societal discrimination or racial balance for its own sake. By contrast, the sex evidence does not identify the qualified pool, a discriminatory practice, or a causal mechanism tying Dallas's conduct to the upper-rank numbers. The two classifications therefore cannot share one remedial predicate.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not require a prior liability judgment or proof of completed individual discrimination, and it does not permit generalized disparity or representation goals to substitute for a source-linked remedial showing.

#### Issue 2: Fit of the race- and sex-conscious promotions

- **Threshold and Merits Reach:** Stone reaches fit after resolving the distinct predicates. Strict scrutiny governs the discrete race-conscious promotions; intermediate scrutiny governs the sex-conscious promotions, but the absent sex predicate is independently fatal.
- **Stone's Legal Position:** The race-conscious promotions satisfy strict scrutiny on this record because the selected candidates were qualified and fell within score bands treated as equivalent, the measure lasted five years, terminated upon elimination of the rank-specific imbalance, covered no more than half of annual promotions, followed attempted race-neutral reforms, and imposed neither discharge nor an absolute advancement barrier. The sex-conscious promotions fail because no sufficient remedial predicate supports them, regardless of the mechanism's asserted fit.
- **Stone's Proposed Holding:** Dallas's challenged race-conscious promotions are narrowly tailored to its compelling remedial interest on this record. Its sex-conscious promotions are unconstitutional because the City did not establish the important remedial objective necessary for intermediate scrutiny.
- **Stone's Reasoning and Record Application:** The racial mechanism operated within qualification and equivalence constraints, remained temporary and rank specific, included a termination condition and numerical ceiling, followed race-neutral efforts, and left nonbeneficiaries able to compete and advance. Those limits answer for the burden imposed on identified candidates. The same operational limits cannot cure the missing evidentiary foundation for sex-conscious action.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The position does not approve the plan as a whole, the deputy-chief appointment, selections outside the established score-equivalence bands, permanent racial administration, or sex-conscious promotions supported only by numerical imbalance.

The association’s written policy is relevant but not conclusive without evidence of training, reporting, enforcement, and corrective operation.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** The holding does not make punitive damages automatic upon liability, impose respondeat superior for every employee, excuse acts implementing company policy, or eliminate statutory caps and constitutional review.
