Isolated references to litigiousness do not justify a systemwide injunction. The remand must identify the protected activity, causal role, same-decision proof, and lawful remedial scope.
- **Boundary or Reserved Question:** This does not protect frivolous litigation or permit systemwide relief based only on generalized references to litigiousness or isolated employee conduct.
