- **Question Granted:** Whether interest generated by client funds placed in a mandatory IOLTA account is the private property of the client for Takings Clause purposes.
- **Legal Field:** Property and takings, IOLTA interest.
- **Court and Judgment Under Review:** The Fifth Circuit held that the interest was client property and remanded the remaining takings questions.
- **Requested Supreme Court Judgment and Relief:** Texas officials seek reversal of the property determination; the challengers seek affirmance and further takings adjudication.
