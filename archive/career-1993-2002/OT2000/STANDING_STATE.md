### *Arizona v. California*, 531 U.S. 1 (2000)

**Current stage:** The unresolved Fort Yuma component remains before the Special Master for further boundary, title, water-entitlement, riverbed-interest, and related private-right proceedings. The Court retains jurisdiction for further administration, but no return or new submission is supplied.

**Operative grant or order:** On June 19, 2000, the Court remanded the unresolved Fort Yuma component to the Special Master. On October 10, 2000, the Court entered the supplemental decree implementing the approved Fort Mojave and Colorado River Reservation settlements, left the prior decrees in force except as expressly amended, retained jurisdiction, and did not adjudicate the Fort Yuma component.

**Last Supreme Court action or material milestone:** Supplemental decree entered October 10, 2000, in *Arizona v. California*, 531 U.S. 1 (2000).

**Question before the Court:** No question is presently submitted for decision. The remaining original-action phase concerns the disputed Fort Yuma reservation boundary, title, water quantity, riverbed interests, and related private rights not resolved by the June 19 decision or the October 10 supplemental decree.

**Grant or order scope:** The Fort Mojave and Colorado River Reservation settlements are incorporated into the governing decree. Earlier decrees remain in force except as expressly amended. Only the unresolved Fort Yuma component and later decree administration remain within the retained original jurisdiction.

**Next nonroutine act or controlling condition:** The Special Master must complete the remaining Fort Yuma proceedings and return the matter for further Court action. No return date is supplied.

**Active procedural dependency:** The original action depends on the Special Master proceedings identified in Section 4.

**State source:** OT1999 completed render, original-jurisdiction decision in *Arizona v. California*, 530 U.S. 392 (2000), June 19, 2000; OT2000 completed render, supplemental decree in *Arizona v. California*, 531 U.S. 1 (2000), October 10, 2000.
