# Stone planning and research files

These files describe Stone's proposed methods, dispositions, and longer-run doctrinal roadmaps. They are planning material, not entering law. The term-opening state, effective current-term Records, and the approved case-specific Section II control adjudication.

A case-specific Section II may cite one of these files to explain Stone's method or planned disposition. A build never fills a missing Stone choice, never binds another Justice, and never becomes a holding merely because a later roadmap anticipated it. Planning labels such as Essential, Recommended, Optional, deliberate silence, or Stone roadmap stay in this directory and do not enter Records, state registers, or public renders.
