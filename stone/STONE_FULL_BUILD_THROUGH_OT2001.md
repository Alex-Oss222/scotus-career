# Justice Stone: Working Summary and October Term Case Tracker (OT1992–OT2050)

> Working navigation document. It records the questions attached to the cases this Stone build follows, the questions actually resolved in completed simulated decisions, and future case questions awaiting consideration. It is **not** a source of controlling law or a prewritten set of Stone dispositions.

## Scope

- **Completed baseline:** October Terms 1992–1999. The completed decisions are fixed by their Canonical Decision Records and the controlling Holdings and Standards and Tests registers.
- **Current point:** OT2000 is next. Its cases below are **proposed questions**, not decisions or confirmed grants.
- **Working horizon:** OT2000 through OT2050. Cases already identified for OT2000 and OT2001 appear below; OT2002–OT2050 remain unfilled until cases are selected.
- **Scope of this file:** Cases included in Stone's original working build. It is not a comprehensive inventory of every Supreme Court event or the Court's controlling-holdings register.
- **Source of law:** [HOLDINGS.md](../state/HOLDINGS.md), [STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md](../state/STANDARDS_AND_TESTS.md), the relevant term's [Canonical Decision Records](../terms/), and the Court's actual term records control over an earlier proposal in this tracker.

**Updating a case:** Record its historical Question Presented when identified; change status from **Proposed** to **Selected** when accepted for the term, to **In progress** when under consideration, and to **Completed** only after a Canonical Decision Record exists. Once completed, state the actual answer and explicitly note any presented question not reached or left open. A remand can complete the Supreme Court case while leaving issues for the lower court. If a case is no longer available or not taken, mark **Removed** rather than inventing its outcome.

## Working summary

Stone's Court has completed OT1999. The controlling registers are processed through **June 29, 2000**, after the completed OT1999 Court events. The next simulated term under consideration is OT2000.

The established body of law includes significant decisions on electoral participation and districting, governmental racial treatment, voting-rights enforcement, congressional powers, state immunity, statutory remedies, education, speech, civil rights and evidence. These results are already recorded in the Court's decision files. This tracker does not restate their full tests or anticipate later outcomes.

The original build mixed completed holdings with Stone's anticipated decisions. This version preserves the historical legal questions, records actual outcomes where a decision exists, and leaves future answers blank. For a question omitted from a holding or expressly reserved, the controlling decision record, not a prior prediction, determines what remains open.
